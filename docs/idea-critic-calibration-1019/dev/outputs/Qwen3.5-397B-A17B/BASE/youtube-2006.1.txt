The proposal presents an unprecedented opportunity to acquire the dominant market leader in online video for $1.65 billion. While the price appears steep given the target's lack of revenue and high operational costs, the strategic value lies in its position as the largest destination for user-generated content. The buyer's current video service is smaller and struggling to gain traction; acquiring the market leader instantly resolves this competitive disadvantage.

The primary risks involve copyright litigation and unproven monetization. However, the threat of lawsuits from media companies is a manageable legal challenge rather than a fatal flaw. History suggests that platforms facilitating user content can negotiate licensing deals or implement content identification systems post-acquisition to mitigate these liabilities. The existence of copyrighted material currently on the site proves immense user demand for professional content, signaling a future revenue stream once proper agreements are in place.

Regarding the unproven ad model and high bandwidth costs, these are scaling issues typical of hyper-growth infrastructure plays. The buyer possesses the capital and technical expertise to optimize bandwidth efficiency and integrate a robust advertising engine that the target currently lacks. Waiting for the target to prove profitability before buying would likely result in missing the window entirely, as the network effects are already cementing its dominance.

The alternative is to continue building a smaller, inferior product while the competitor solidifies its hold on the market. In the fast-moving internet sector of 2006, speed and market share often outweigh immediate financial metrics. The potential to define the future of online video distribution justifies the premium price tag and the associated legal hurdles. The risks are significant but calculable, whereas the cost of inaction is the permanent loss of the category leadership. The strategic imperative to control the primary pipeline for digital video content overrides the current financial deficits and legal threats.

DECISION: GO
