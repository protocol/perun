Steelman: Leveraging existing, underutilized infrastructure to create a high-margin revenue stream that diversifies income beyond thin-retail margins, turning a cost center into a profit center.

However, the attack on this claim reveals fatal flaws in the current proposal context. First, the "unproven market" for hourly server rental is not merely a risk; it is an unvalidated assumption. Building a developer platform requires distinct competencies in API design, developer support, and SLA management that the retailer explicitly lacks. Entering a market with large incumbents without a proven differentiator or internal expertise invites catastrophic failure.

Second, the financial timing is dangerous. Investors are already questioning heavy capital spending. Doubling down on infrastructure to serve external clients—whose demand is unproven—dilutes focus and capital from the core retail business, which operates on thin margins. If the new venture fails to gain traction, the company faces stranded assets and heightened investor skepticism, potentially jeopardizing the core business's valuation.

Third, reliability expectations for enterprise developers differ vastly from internal retail spikes. A failure in the retail site during a spike is an internal outage; a failure in a sold service is a breach of contract and reputation damage in a new, unforgiving market. The proposal offers no mechanism to bridge this operational gap.

The "do nothing" alternative preserves capital and focus. A "better way" would be to first validate the market with a minimal, low-capacity pilot using existing spare capacity without new build-out, proving demand before seeking further investment. The current proposal asks for a massive, distinct business line build-out based on unproven assumptions while the core business faces investor scrutiny. This violates the principle of preserving the core while experimenting. The risks of distraction, margin dilution, and competitive failure outweigh the theoretical benefits without prior validation.

DECISION: NO-GO
