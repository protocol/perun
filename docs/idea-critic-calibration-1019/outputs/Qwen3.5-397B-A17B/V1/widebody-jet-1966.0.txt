The claim is that committing to a massive, unproven wide-body jet will secure market dominance despite existential financial and technological risks. The steelman: this specific design (raised cockpit, high-bypass engines) creates a dual-use asset (passenger/freight) that locks in the largest customer and preempts competitors, justifying the unprecedented capital outlay.

However, the failure modes are catastrophic. The development cost exceeds all prior company history; a single technical delay or engine failure could bankrupt the firm. The "supersonic replacement" threat is not hypothetical but an industry expectation that could render subsonic giants obsolete before they pay back their debt. Relying on a single launch customer for such a scale-up violates basic portfolio theory; if that airline cancels, the factory becomes a stranded asset. The "unproven engine" adds a critical path dependency outside direct control.

Alternatives exist: stretching existing models incrementally, waiting for engine maturity, or partnering to share the financial burden rather than bearing it alone. The proposal bets the entire company on a specific prediction of subsonic growth and freight conversion utility. While the raised cockpit is a clever hedge, it does not mitigate the immediate cash-flow cliff or the supersonic disruption risk.

The strongest attack is the concentration of risk: one customer, one unproven engine, one massive factory, all while a technological shift (supersonic) looms. The proposal acknowledges these but offers no kill criteria, no phased funding gates, and no fallback if the supersonic timeline accelerates. It asks for a binary "commit now" decision without evidence that the market growth is guaranteed or that the supersonic threat is overstated. The potential reward is high, but the probability of total failure given the constraints (no tools, no network, single customer) is unacceptably high for an immediate "GO" without further validation of the supersonic timeline and engine readiness.

DECISION: NO-GO
