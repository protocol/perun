**Steelman:** Launch a clear‑cola brand nationwide now, backed by a large, high‑visibility marketing push, to capture consumers seeking a “purer” alternative and to grow market share for the second‑largest cola maker.

**Attack & Alternatives:**  
1. **Do‑nothing / modest extension** – keep the current portfolio and monitor the clear‑cola trend. Low cost, no risk of cannibalisation.  
2. **Limited regional rollout** – expand the test markets gradually, using a scaled‑down campaign and measuring repeat purchase before a national push. This reduces waste if novelty fades and limits brand‑confusion risk.

**Fit with Strategy:** The goal is to offset slowing soft‑drink growth. A clear‑cola could attract health‑conscious buyers, but the core brand’s identity is tied to traditional dark cola; a stark visual shift may dilute that identity.

**Premortem:**  
- Consumers associate “cola” with brown colour; a clear product may be perceived as “not a real cola,” leading to low repeat rates.  
- Large upfront spend could be sunk if novelty wanes.  
- Rivals could quickly imitate, eroding any first‑mover advantage.  
- Cannibalisation could pull sales from the flagship, reducing overall revenue.

**Kill‑criteria (proposed):**  
- If repeat purchase after 3 months < 20 % of initial trial volume → halt national rollout.  
- If flagship sales drop > 5 % in any quarter post‑launch → pause marketing spend.  
- If a rival launches a comparable clear cola within 6 months → shift to niche positioning.

**Recommendation:** The current proposal’s scale and marketing intensity expose the company to significant financial risk without proven repeat demand. A staged, data‑driven rollout with clear kill‑criteria is a safer path that still allows capture of the emerging clear‑cola niche.

**Decision:** The proposal, as written, should not be approved for immediate nationwide launch.

DECISION: NO-GO
