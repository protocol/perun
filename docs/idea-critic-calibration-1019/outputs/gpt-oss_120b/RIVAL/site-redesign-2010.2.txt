The proposal aims to replace a mature community‑driven product with a brand‑new version that re‑weights story surfacing toward publishers. While a fresh UI could attract new traffic, the plan ignores three critical safeguards:

1. **Community core value** – Users stay because the democratic voting system signals relevance. Removing or heavily altering that mechanic risks alienating the loyal base that differentiates the site from the rival community already gaining momentum.

2. **Engineering risk** – A complete rewrite from scratch carries high uncertainty: unknown performance, hidden bugs, and the need to rebuild every ancillary service. Historically, such “big‑bang” migrations have a low success rate without extensive incremental validation.

3. **Launch strategy** – Moving **all** users to the new platform in a single cut‑over eliminates any fallback. If the new ranking algorithm under‑delivers or introduces regressions, the site could suffer a catastrophic traffic drop with no easy recovery path.

Given these factors, the prudent approach is to **incrementally** evolve the product: preserve the existing voting core, introduce the new ranking algorithm as an optional view, and pilot it with a small user segment. This allows data‑driven validation, mitigates risk, and retains the community’s trust while still testing the publisher‑focused surfacing.

**Recommendation:** Defer the full rewrite and all‑users launch until the new mechanics are proven in a controlled rollout.

DECISION: NO-GO
