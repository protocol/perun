The plan targets a dramatic shift in brand perception by introducing a premium burger at a price point above the core menu and backing it with an unprecedented advertising spend. While the ambition to capture adult diners is clear, the risks outweigh the potential upside given the chain’s established child‑centered image.

1. **Brand mismatch** – Adults already associate the brand with inexpensive, kid‑focused meals. A premium offering will clash with existing expectations, making it hard to convince diners to pay more for a product they do not view as “premium.”
2. **Price elasticity** – Raising prices on a core‑brand customer base that values low cost is likely to alienate regular patrons, reducing foot traffic and average transaction size.
3. **Demand uncertainty** – Deploying the largest industry‑wide advertising budget before any market validation commits massive spend with no guarantee of lift, increasing financial exposure.
4. **Opportunity cost** – Resources devoted to this launch could be used to enhance existing menu items or develop modest adult‑focused options that align better with the brand.

A more prudent approach would be to pilot a modestly priced, adult‑oriented item within the current menu, test acceptance, and scale advertising only after demand is proven. Until that validation, the current proposal is too risky.

DECISION: NO-GO
