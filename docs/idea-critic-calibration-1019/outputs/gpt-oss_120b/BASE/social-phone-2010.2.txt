**Assessment of Proposal**

**Market Fit**  
- Target: teenagers active on social networks.  
- Current competitor phones already offer extensive app catalogs, which is a key driver for teen adoption. Without an app store, the proposed devices lack a critical engagement channel.  

**Pricing**  
- Mandatory monthly data plan costs as much as a full‑smartphone plan. For the teen demographic, price sensitivity is high; a plan of this cost is likely to be prohibitive and could deter adoption or push users to cheaper, carrier‑agnostic options.

**Technology Positioning**  
- The phones will run a brand‑new in‑house OS, not the company’s existing mobile platform. This adds development risk, longer time to ecosystem maturity, and higher support burden. It also prevents leveraging any existing developer community or assets the company already possesses.

**Sunk Cost Consideration**  
- Two years of development have already been invested. While sunk costs should not dictate continuation, they do indicate that the project is already deep in the pipeline and that abandoning it now would waste resources. However, sunk costs do not outweigh clear market mismatches.

**Competitive Landscape**  
- Rival smartphones with large app catalogs and flexible carrier options already dominate the teen segment. Entering with a closed, higher‑priced offering is unlikely to capture meaningful market share.

**Strategic Risks**  
1. **Adoption Risk** – Lack of an app store limits the device’s appeal to the core teen user base.  
2. **Pricing Risk** – The mandatory data plan price is misaligned with teen purchasing power.  
3. **Platform Risk** – A brand‑new OS delays ecosystem development and increases operational complexity.  
4. **Opportunity Cost** – Resources tied up in this product could be redirected to a more competitive offering (e.g., leveraging the existing mobile platform, partnering with multiple carriers, or introducing an app store at launch).

**Recommendation**  
Given the misalignment of price, ecosystem, and carrier strategy with the target market, the proposal presents high execution risk and low probability of achieving the desired teen market penetration. A revision is needed—either redesign the offering to include an app store and flexible, affordable data options, or pivot to a platform that can leverage existing assets.

**Decision**  
DECISION: NO-GO
