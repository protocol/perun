**Failure Modes & Halt Triggers:**
*   **Technical Failure:** The third-party API integration for the dashboard exceeds $20,000 or 3 weeks of dev time. *Action:* Drop the automated dashboard; revert to weekly manual reporting.
*   **Revenue Failure:** Pilot sites show a revenue uplift of less than 3% by 2024-09-20 (mid-pilot). *Action:* Immediately revert pricing to baseline; do not wait for the October end date.
*   **Occupancy Collapse:** Overall network occupancy drops below 70% during the pilot window. *Action:* Halt the rollout entirely; the 8% booking boost assumption is invalid.
*   **Churn Spike:** Member cancellations in pilot sites exceed 4% (double the expected reduction benefit) within the first 30 days. *Action:* Pause new FlexPass sales and initiate member retention outreach.

**Note:** The 8% booking boost and 2% churn reduction are assumptions based on Q2 surveys, not realized data; treat them as hypotheses to be disproven. The unknown integration cost is the highest risk to the budget; require a fixed-price quote from the vendor before committing the $15,000 license.
