**Subject:** Decision Memo: Rooftop Rays Rollout Execution

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a staggered rollout starting with Greenview (12 units) as the pilot, followed by Oakridge (18 units), then Hillcrest (15 units), conditional on correcting the budget deficit and securing the software license. Do not authorize procurement until the software cost is fixed and the labor gap is funded.

**Figures:**
*   **Budget Gap:** Stated $2.5M vs. Required $2.92M (based on $3.6M revenue / 1.30 margin). **Deficit: $420k.**
*   **Competitor Variance:** Stated $2.5M vs. SolarCo $1.8M (scaled to 150kW = $1.35M). **Overage: $1.15M.**
*   **Labor Omission:** Unpriced. If overtime exceeds 15% of hardware costs, margin drops below 20%.
*   **Software Risk:** Unknown cost. If >$250k total, the project breaches the corrected budget.

**Execution Order & Human Gates:**
1.  **Design & Software Procurement:** Finalize architecture and **lock software license cost**.
    *   *Gate:* CFO sign-off required on final software price before PO issuance.
2.  **Permitting:** Submit applications immediately.
    *   *Gate:* Legal counsel sign-off on permit completeness before submission.
3.  **Hardware Procurement:** Order inverters and panels.
    *   *Gate:* COO sign-off required confirming budget re-base ($2.92M+) is funded before shipping.
4.  **Installation:** Greenview → Oakridge → Hillcrest.
5.  **Commissioning:** Target completion by 15 Oct 2024 (buffer for rebate deadline).

**Primary Failure Modes:**
*   **Permit Delay:** City approval exceeds 45 days (current assumption: 30), pushing installation past the rebate window.
*   **Cost Overrun:** Software license or labor overtime erodes the 30% margin to <15%.
*   **Revenue Miss:** State rebate rules change or eligibility is denied due to late commissioning.

**Halt & Fallback Triggers:**
*   **Halt Trigger:** Permit not received by **15 July 2024** (45-day mark). *Action:* Pause hardware procurement; re-evaluate rebate eligibility.
*   **Fallback Trigger:** Software license cost exceeds **$250k total** (not per unit). *Action:* Re-pivot to alternative inverter vendor or renegotiate revenue share to cover cost.

**Note:** The current $2.5M budget is unrealistic against the $3.6M revenue target for a 30% margin; the plan above assumes an immediate budget re-base to $2.92M. The SolarCo comparison suggests our hardware estimates are inflated by ~85% unless scope differs significantly.
