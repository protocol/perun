**Decision:** Build the AI matching engine first; defer the dashboard pilot until legal sign-off is complete; implement pricing tiers only after the engine stabilizes. The single number driving this order is the **$22,000/month revenue upside** from the engine, which funds the other two initiatives.

**Figures:**
*   Revenue Lift: 12,000 students × 40% adoption × ($22,000 / 4,800 adopted) ≈ $4.58/student/mo lift. Total stated $22k/mo is consistent if applied to net new value.
*   Pricing Impact: 1,200 students × 12 months × (Avg Tuition × 5% discount). *Missing Avg Tuition.* Stated enrollment lift (1,200) cannot be converted to revenue without this rate.
*   Timeline: 3 weeks (Engine) + 2 weeks (Pricing) + 1 week (UI) = 6 weeks total dev. Legal review (5 days) runs parallel to Engine sprint.

**Failure Modes & Triggers:**
1.  **Compliance Block:** If legal identifies state-level prohibitions on third-party data sharing, the Dashboard pilot fails entirely.
    *   *Trigger:* Legal memo cites "unresolvable data residency conflict" by [Date: 5 business days from now]. Action: Kill dashboard pilot; reallocate UI budget to engine hardening.
2.  **Adoption Shortfall:** If tutor adoption of the new engine stays below 25% (vs. the 40% assumption), the revenue model breaks.
    *   *Trigger:* Week 4 post-launch adoption report shows <25% active usage. Action: Halt pricing tier rollout; focus engineering on tutor UX barriers.
3.  **Match Rate Degradation:** If the AI engine causes a **>5% drop** in successful match rates (falling below 63%) due to algorithmic bias or latency.
    *   *Trigger:* Daily ops report shows match rate <63% for 3 consecutive days post-launch. Action: Immediate rollback to legacy matcher; freeze engine updates.

**Sign-offs Required:**
*   **Senior Legal Review:** Mandatory before *any* code merges for the Dashboard pilot (data privacy).
*   **CEO Approval:** Required for the 5% discount structure (revenue impact validation).

**Halt Trigger:** Cumulative burn rate exceeds $85,000 (34% of budget) before the Engine achieves 80% of projected match rate improvement.
**Fallback Trigger:** Legal review exceeds 7 business days. Action: Decouple Dashboard from Q4 release; proceed with Engine and Pricing only.

**Note:** The pricing revenue projection is currently unverifiable without the average tuition figure; do not commit to the 1,200 student enrollment lift in investor communications until this variable is sourced. The 5-day legal assumption is aggressive for K-12 data; treat the Dashboard as "at risk" until the memo arrives.
