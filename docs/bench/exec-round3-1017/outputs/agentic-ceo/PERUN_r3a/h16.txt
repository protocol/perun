**To:** Maya Patel, Board Chair
**From:** Project Lead
**Date:** 24 October 2024
**Subject:** Decision Memo: Fall Season Launch Strategy

**Recommendation:** Prioritize the Autonomous Navigation Module immediately, defer final Sponsor Pricing validation until Week 6, and freeze the KPI Dashboard to three core metrics only. We must sequence development to hit the 2 March expo prototype; pricing can be adjusted post-demo based on actual build performance.

**Figures:**
*   **Total Dev Cost:** $48,000 (4 engineers × 12 weeks × $1,000/week avg).
*   **Sponsor Revenue Potential:** $25,500 (sum of 3 tiers), unvalidated.
*   **PCB Risk Variance:** $6,000 gap ($9,000 max - $3,000 min) not yet reserved.
*   **Timeline Buffer:** 11 days between Expo (2 Mar) and Rollout (15 Mar); currently insufficient for post-expo fixes if the 20% build-time reduction fails.

**Order of Work:**
1.  **Weeks 1–8:** Build Navigation Module. Treat the vendor's "20% build time reduction" as unproven; design the prototype to function *without* this efficiency to ensure expo readiness.
2.  **Weeks 4–9:** Define KPI Dashboard. Track only: (1) Prototype Reliability, (2) Actual Build Time, (3) Sponsor Commitment Count. Drop "Sponsor ROI" and "Team Retention" until post-launch data exists.
3.  **Weeks 9–12:** Validate Sponsor Pricing. Use expo demo data to confirm willingness to pay before finalizing the $5k/$8.5k/$12k tiers.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Sensor Reliability Gap:** The vendor's 20% efficiency claim is false, causing the prototype to miss the 2 March demo deadline.
2.  **PCB Cost Overrun:** Custom PCB costs hit the $9,000 upper bound, blowing the $48,000 budget by 12.5% with no allocated contingency.
3.  **Pricing Mismatch:** Market rejection of the $12,000 tier leaves a revenue hole that undermines the business case before launch.

**Hidden Risks:**
*   **Integration Debt:** Parallel work streams often collide at Week 10; no time is currently allocated for system integration testing before the expo.
*   **Liability Exposure:** Autonomous navigation implies safety risks; no legal review budget is listed for potential liability at the expo.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers:**
*   **Halt Trigger:** If sensor reliability testing shows <90% success rate by 15 January, stop module development and pivot to a tele-operated fallback for the expo.
*   **Fallback Trigger:** If confirmed PCB costs exceed $7,000 by 1 December, freeze feature scope and revert to off-the-shelf components, accepting a 10% performance reduction to protect the budget.

**Note:** We are assuming the $48,000 covers all labor; if the PCB hits $9,000, we need an immediate $4,000 contingency approval.
**Note:** Legal review for autonomous operation at a public expo must be initiated by 1 November, outside the current engineering budget.
