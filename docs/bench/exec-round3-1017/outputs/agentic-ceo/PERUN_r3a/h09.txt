**Recommendation:** Start product development and packaging redesign immediately; defer the full marketing push until pilot churn data is validated; require explicit sign-off from Legal and Finance on the biodegradable pouch cost variance before any material is shipped.

**Decision Memo:** Proceed with caution. The plan rests on a single fragile number: the unquantified cost increase of the new biodegradable pouch beyond the quoted $0.08/unit.
Figures: 150,000 units x $4.25 price = $637,500 revenue (stated target implies profitability). Costs: $500k (dev) + $300k (pkg) + $400k (marketing) = $1.2M total budget. Unit COGS: $0.45/oz protein + $0.08 pouch base. If the pouch premium exceeds $0.12/unit, gross margin drops below 40%, threatening the $1.2M recovery within Q1. The stated $1.2M budget leaves zero contingency for a cost overrun >15% ($180k).

**Sequence of Activities:**
1.  **Development & Review (Weeks 1–8):** Finalize pea-protein blend and lock pouch specifications. Conduct immediate cost audit on the biodegradable material.
2.  **Stakeholder Briefing (Week 9):** Present cost audit and initial churn projections to the Board.
3.  **Measurement (Weeks 10–14):** Run a limited consumer panel to measure churn rate against the current format.
4.  **Pricing & Marketing (Week 15+):** Adjust pricing strategy based on final COGS and churn data; launch pilot marketing only if churn <5%.

**Three Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Material Cost Escalation:** The biodegradable pouch costs >$0.20/unit (vs. $0.08 assumed), eroding margins by 25% and making the $4.25 price point unprofitable.
2.  **Customer Churn:** Existing customers reject the new texture/format, causing a churn rate >12% in the pilot, reducing Q1 volume below 130,000 units.
3.  **Supply Chain Delay:** The new pouch supplier cannot scale by Jan 15, 2027, forcing a delay that misses the Q1 sales window entirely.

**Halt Triggers:**
*   **Pause Condition:** If pilot unit sales project below $300,000 for Q1 (approx. 70,500 units) OR if total project cost overrun exceeds 15% ($180,000).
*   **Fallback Action:** Revert to current poly-foil packaging and delay launch to Q3 2027 to renegotiate supplier contracts.

**Sign-off Required:** CFO must approve any pouch cost variance >$0.02/unit before tooling begins.
