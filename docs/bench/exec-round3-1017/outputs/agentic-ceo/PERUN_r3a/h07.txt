**Decision:** Start the PMS rollout immediately; defer the Lakeside lobby renovation until Q3; launch marketing only after PMS is live. This sequence rests on one number: **$300k** (the mid-point of the vendor's $250k–$350k range), which leaves a **$50k exposure** if integration hits the maximum estimate.

**Figures:** $1.5M (Lobby) + $1.2M (PMS) + $1.3M (Marketing) = $4.0M total. The PMS line item assumes $300k, but the vendor range is $250k–$350k. If costs hit $350k, the plan exceeds the allocated $1.2M by $50k unless trimmed elsewhere. The assumed 5% ADR rise to offset renovation costs is unverified against current occupancy trends and supply chain volatility.

**Order of Work & Sign-offs:**
1.  **Start Now:** PMS Rollout. It is the foundational layer for both the renovation tracking and marketing data. **Sign-off:** CTO must approve the final integration scope before the $300k commit.
2.  **Defer:** Lakeside Lobby Renovation. Push start to Q3 to avoid peak construction material volatility and allow Q1/Q2 revenue data to validate the 5% ADR assumption. **Sign-off:** CEO must re-approve the $1.5M budget in August based on H1 performance.
3.  **Conditional:** Digital Marketing. Do not spend the $1.3M until the PMS is live to ensure accurate attribution. Launch targeted campaigns only after system go-live. **Sign-off:** CMO must confirm PMS data integrity before releasing funds.

**Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Integration Overrun:** PMS integration costs hit $350k+, blowing the $1.2M cap and forcing a cut to marketing or renovation scope.
2.  **Supply Chain Delay:** Lobby renovation materials are delayed >6 weeks, pushing completion past the high-season window and invalidating the 12-month ROI calculation.
3.  **ADR Stagnation:** Average Daily Rate fails to rise by 5% within 6 months post-renovation due to market saturation, leaving the $1.5M investment unrecovered.

**Halt Triggers:**
*   **Value:** Occupancy drops below 70% for two consecutive months post-PMS launch. **Action:** Freeze all marketing spend immediately.
*   **Date:** PMS go-live slips beyond 15 July 2024. **Action:** Cancel the "Spring Refresh" marketing campaign and re-allocate funds to retention only.

**Fallback Trigger:**
*   **Value:** PMS integration quote exceeds $325k. **Action:** Re-baseline the PMS budget to $325k, reduce marketing allocation by $25k, and require CFO approval before proceeding.
