**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **Phased Validation Sequence**: (1) Weeks 1–4: Finalize measurement framework and secure legal sign-off on pricing/terms; (2) Weeks 5–10: Build MVP push feature ($80k) and execute beta with 12 churches; (3) Weeks 11–12: Analyze beta engagement data to validate the 8% usage lift before committing to full design spend ($45k) or finalizing the $5 premium tier.

**Decision Logic:** The plan rests on the **$125k committed cost** ($80k dev + $45k design) fitting within the **$150k budget**, leaving only a **$25k contingency** for pricing experiments and communication.
*Figures:* $80k (dev) + $45k (design) = $125k stated committed; $150k (total) - $125k (committed) = $25k remaining.
*Correction:* The stated assumption of an immediate full rollout risks a **$20k+ overrun** if the beta fails and requires rework, as the $25k buffer is insufficient for a second design sprint. Re-base the design spend to a **$15k "beta-only" visual pass** now, deferring the remaining $30k until the beta validates the 8% lift. This creates a **$40k total buffer** ($25k + $15k deferred) to absorb failure.

**Sequence of Tasks:**
1.  **Immediate (July 1–15):** Define event taxonomy for the 8% lift/2% churn targets; obtain **Legal/Compliance sign-off** on push notification consent and the new $5 pricing terms.
2.  **Build & Beta (July 16–Sept 15):** Develop core push logic; deploy to 12 churches; measure actual engagement vs. the 8% hypothesis.
3.  **Go/No-Go (Sept 16):** If beta engagement <4% (half the target), **halt** full design spend and pivot to retention fixes. If >4%, release remaining design budget and prepare broad rollout.
4.  **Defer:** Full marketing deck and broad pricing announcement until beta results are in.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Adoption Mismatch:** Less than **5% of beta churches** activate the feature, invalidating the 8% usage lift hypothesis and rendering the $125k spend inefficient.
2.  **Pricing Delusion:** Zero uptake of the $5 tier in the beta group, revealing that push notifications are an expectation, not a premium differentiator.
3.  **Budget Creep:** The $25k contingency is consumed by integration delays, forcing a choice between shipping broken code or cutting the design quality.

**Halt Trigger:** If **premium tier conversion is <1%** among beta users after 30 days of availability, **stop** the pricing rollout and revert to the existing model.
**Fallback Trigger:** If **beta engagement lift is <4%** by August 30, **pause** the remaining $30k design spend and re-allocate funds to user interviews to diagnose the value gap.

**Hidden Risk:** The **$45k design estimate** likely excludes the cost of creating distinct "premium" vs. "free" UI states required to enforce the new pricing tier, potentially adding **$10k–$15k** in unseen scope.
