**Decision:** Proceed with a gated 90-day launch, conditional on isolating the regulatory unknown before marketing spend begins. The plan rests on one number: **$20,000** (the unallocated buffer remaining after known costs).

**Figures:** $120k (Dev) + $80k (Marketing) + $30k (Legal) = $230k stated costs; $250k budget - $230k = $20k buffer. The 10% conversion assumption on 1.2M users implies 120k new Pro users; at $3/mo, this yields $360k/mo new revenue, covering the $250k investment in <1 month if realized. However, the unquantified regulatory cost threatens the $20k buffer and the $30k legal line item.

**Sequence of Work & Sign-offs:**
1.  **Days 1–30 (Build & Isolate Risk):** Develop the core feature. **Mandatory Sign-off:** CFO must approve the specific "anonymized data sharing" protocol before any partner integration code is merged. This is the gate for the regulatory unknown.
2.  **Days 31–60 (Beta & Validate):** Release to 5% of users. Measure conversion against the 10% hypothesis. **Defer:** Broad marketing rollout until beta conversion hits ≥8%.
3.  **Days 61–90 (Launch):** Full rollout only if beta metrics hold and legal clearance is documented.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Shock:** A partner requirement triggers a compliance redesign, burning the $20k buffer and delaying launch past the 90-day window.
2.  **Conversion Miss:** Free-to-Pro conversion lands at 4% instead of 10%, extending ROI payback from 1 month to 3+ months and invalidating the revenue model.
3.  **Churn Spike:** The new feature confuses existing Pro users, causing churn >2% among the base, netting negative revenue despite new conversions.

**Halt Triggers (Stop/Pivot Conditions):**
*   **Beta Churn > 15%:** Immediate pause on rollout; revert to research mode.
*   **Marketing ROI < 2×:** Halt ad spend; reallocate remaining budget to retention fixes.
*   **Legal Cost Estimate > $50k:** Project termination; the risk premium exceeds the allocated buffer and potential margin.

**Note:** The regulatory unknown is the single point of failure; do not commit the $80k marketing budget until the data-sharing protocol is legally signed off. The 10% conversion assumption is aggressive; treat the 5% beta as a go/no-go gate for the full spend.
