**Primary Failure Modes**

*   **Integration Cost Overrun:** If custom LMS integrations exceed $2,500 per school, the pilot becomes unprofitable.
*   **Low Adoption/Engagement:** If schools purchase but teachers do not run simulations, renewal rates will collapse.
*   **Compliance Gaps:** Failure to meet student data privacy regulations (e.g., FERPA/COPPA) in new districts could halt the rollout legally.
*   **Budget Breach:** The lesson builder exceeding the 8-week/$120k cap, delaying the October 15 start date.

**Stop or Pivot Triggers**

*   **Pilot NPS < 60:** Indicates the product experience is fundamentally flawed; pause general rollout to redesign.
*   **Pilot Revenue < $200k:** If conversion from pilot to paid full-year subscriptions falls below ~65% of the $275k target, revisit pricing or value proposition.
*   **Integration Costs > $225k total:** If total integration spend exceeds this cap, stop signing new custom-LMS districts until automation is built.
*   **Adoption Lag:** If <40% of pilot schools have launched a simulation by 15 Dec 2026, the "60-day adoption" assumption is false; shift strategy to heavy-touch onboarding.
