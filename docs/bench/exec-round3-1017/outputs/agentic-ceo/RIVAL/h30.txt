**Recommended Order of Work**

1.  **Immediate (Week 1): Secure Licensing & Validate Economics.** Before any development begins, pay the $18k licensing fee to lock in the new games. Simultaneously, halt the assumption of a 12% ticket lift. Run a 3-day simulation using last quarter's actual 6% lift data against the new labor costs. 2.  **Parallel Track A (Weeks 2–6): Menu Development & Staff Training.** Develop the three premium games while training staff on "Game-Guided Service." These are interdependent; the service protocol is useless without the products, and the premium products will fail without the service. 3.  **Parallel Track B (Weeks 2–10): Loyalty App Beta.** Begin app development only after the economic model is validated in Step 1. The app enhances retention but does not drive the immediate Q4 revenue spike required to offset costs. Target the Nov 1 beta for the existing customer base, not as a customer acquisition tool for the new menu launch.

**Three Biggest Failure Points**

1.  **The Revenue Assumption Gap:** The plan relies on a 12% ticket lift, but historical data supports only 6%. If this gap is real, the new labor costs will erode margins, turning the "expansion" into a loss leader that drains cash from the app and lease negotiations.
2.  **Operational Dilution:** Launching a complex new menu, a new service protocol, and a new app simultaneously creates a "change saturation" point. Staff may fail to master the "Game-Guided Service," leading to poor customer experiences that negate the premium pricing strategy.
3.  **Capital Misallocation:** The budget is fully allocated with no contingency. If the app development encounters bugs (common in beta) or the license fee reveals hidden costs, there is no liquidity to fix issues without cannibalizing the training or menu quality.

**Stop/Change Course Triggers**

*   **Traffic Warning:** If foot traffic drops below **90%** of projected levels for two consecutive weeks post-launch (Oct 15–31), pause the app beta development and redirect those funds to immediate marketing or menu adjustments.
*   **Margin Warning:** If the actual ticket lift averages **below 8%** by Nov 1, halt the lease renegotiation aggression; do not commit to higher fixed costs until the unit economics of the new menu are proven profitable at the lower lift rate.
*   **Adoption Warning:** If less than **15%** of eligible customers enroll in the loyalty app by Nov 15, pivot the app strategy from "points for spend" to "points for engagement" (e.g., attending events) before further investment.

*Note: The $18k licensing fee is a sunk cost risk; ensure the contract allows for partial rollout if the economic validation fails. Do not proceed to full marketing spend until the Week 1 economic simulation confirms profitability at a 6% lift.*
