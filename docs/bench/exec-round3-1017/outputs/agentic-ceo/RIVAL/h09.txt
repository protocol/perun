### Green Crunch Rollout Plan: Executive Summary for Board Presentation

**Recommended Sequence of Activities**
1.  **Immediate Start (Week 1):** Lock the pea-protein supply contract at $0.45/oz and initiate the packaging redesign ($300k budget). Simultaneously, launch a rapid A/B taste test with 500 existing customers to establish a baseline churn risk before full production.
2.  **Development & Review (Months 1–3):** Complete prototype manufacturing. Conduct a "fail-fast" cost review; if the biodegradable pouch material cost exceeds $0.08/unit by more than 10%, trigger an immediate design pivot.
3.  **Stakeholder Briefing & Pricing (Month 4):** Present final unit economics to the board. Confirm the $4.25 price point ensures margin stability even if material costs rise by 5%.
4.  **Pilot Marketing & Measurement (Months 5–6):** Deploy the $400k pilot in two test markets. Measure actual churn and repeat purchase rates weekly.
5.  **Explicit Sign-Off Gate:** Required before the pilot marketing spend is released. Sign-off is contingent on pilot unit economics matching the $0.08 packaging cost assumption and churn remaining below 8%.

**Three Main Failure Modes**
1.  **Cost Overrun:** The biodegradable pouch material cost exceeds estimates, eroding margins below viability at the $4.25 price point.
2.  **Churn Spike:** Existing customers reject the new texture or format, causing a churn rate >10% that outweighs new customer acquisition.
3.  **Supply Chain Fragility:** The single-source pea-protein contract fails to deliver volume, halting production during the critical Q1 launch window.

**Pause/Change Course Triggers**
*   **Financial:** Q1 unit sales revenue falls under **$300,000** (approx. 70,000 units sold) OR total project cost overrun exceeds **15%** ($180,000 over the $1.2M budget).
*   **Customer:** Measured churn rate among existing customers exceeds **8%** during the pilot phase.

***

*Note: This plan assumes the $0.08/unit packaging cost is a hard cap; any variance requires immediate re-approval. The Jan 15, 2027 launch date allows ample time for a iterative pilot approach rather than a "big bang" release.*
