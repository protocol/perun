**Recommendation: Order of Work & CEO Sign-offs**

1.  **Secure Variable Costs (Week 1):** Finalize freight contracts for mugs and licensing fees for blends. *CEO Sign-off:* Approval of final unit economics if total variable costs exceed $18/unit.
2.  **Software Build & Pricing Test (Weeks 2–6):** Develop the "Seasonal Roast" feature and integrate the $24 pricing tier. Run a closed beta pricing test with 5% of current premium users. *CEO Sign-off:* Go/no-go on full price rollout if beta churn exceeds 6%.
3.  **Onboarding Redesign (Weeks 4–7):** Parallel track to software build. Focus on highlighting the new mug and seasonal options.
4.  **Mug Production & Logistics (Weeks 5–9):** Begin manufacturing only after cost certainty. Allow 4 weeks for shipping.
5.  **Marketing Launch (Week 10):** Deploy the committed $30k ads synchronized with inventory arrival.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Margin Collapse:** If freight and licensing push the cost per unit above $19, the $2 price increase yields no net profit gain.
*   **Churn Spike:** The price hike combined with changes to onboarding could confuse users, pushing churn from 5% to over 8%.
*   **Inventory Mismatch:** If mugs arrive after the launch date or software bugs prevent ordering the seasonal roast, ad spend will be wasted on broken experiences.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Churn > 7%:** Immediately revert pricing to $22 for affected cohorts and pause the onboarding redesign.
*   **Cost Overrun > $10k:** Halt the mug rollout entirely; launch software features only with standard packaging.
*   **Beta Conversion < 80% of Control:** Delay the general launch to rework the value proposition before spending the ad budget.

*Note: Do not commit to mug manufacturing until freight costs are locked in writing. The $50k contingency is insufficient to absorb a 20% freight variance.*
