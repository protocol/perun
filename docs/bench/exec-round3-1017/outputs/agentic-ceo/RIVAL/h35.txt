**Executive Summary & Recommendation**
The current financial projection contains a critical error: $480k (Revenue) - $200k (Dev) - $300k (Marketing) equals **-$20k**, not +$80k profit. We are currently projected to lose money on this pilot. Furthermore, the plan relies on unverified external dependencies (legal sign-off, API costs, partner pricing approval).

**Recommendation:** Do not proceed to full build immediately. Adopt a **"Compliance-First, Phased Rollout"** strategy.
1.  **Weeks 1–3 (Gatekeeper Phase):** Halt UI/Integration dev. Prioritize the Compliance Audit and finalize Third-Party API costs. Secure written confirmation from pharmacy partners regarding the 20% fee hike. *Go/No-Go Decision Point:* If legal sign-off is not imminent or partners reject the hike, pivot immediately.
2.  **Weeks 4–8 (Core Build):** Once compliance is cleared, build the API integrations and Pricing Model logic.
3.  **Weeks 9–11 (Frontend & Comms):** Develop the Subscription Toggle UI and draft stakeholder decks based on confirmed metrics.
4.  **Week 12 (Soft Launch):** Release to a 5% user segment to validate the 15% conversion assumption before full go-live.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Regulatory Block:** The 20% delivery fee hike triggers a compliance violation or requires a lengthy review, missing the March 1 deadline.
*   **Partner Revolt:** Pharmacy partners reject the fee increase, forcing a re-architecture of the pricing model mid-sprint.
*   **Unit Economics Collapse:** The actual conversion rate is <10% or the third-party API costs exceed estimates, turning the projected -$20k loss into a significant deficit.
*   **Technical Debt:** Integrating three distinct pharmacy APIs simultaneously causes latency issues, degrading the user experience for the core free tier.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers**
Immediate cessation or strategic pivot is required if:
1.  **Regulatory Hold:** Legal cannot sign off on the pricing model by **November 15, 2023**.
2.  **Adoption Failure:** Soft launch conversion is **<8%** (more than 45% below target) after two weeks.
3.  **Cost Overrun:** Third-party verification API costs exceed **$50k** annually, invalidating the margin model.
4.  **Partner Rejection:** Any major pharmacy partner formally objects to the fee structure, threatening the supply chain.

**Note:** The math must be corrected in the stakeholder deck before presentation; projecting a profit when a loss is likely undermines credibility. Secure the legal opinion before spending a single dollar on development.
