**Spring Refresh Execution Plan: Lakeside First, PMS Parallel, Marketing Locked**

**Immediate Start (Week 1):** Commence the Lakeside lobby renovation ($1.5M). This is the critical path item with the longest lead time and highest physical disruption risk. Do not wait for PMS finalization.
**Parallel Track (Week 1-4):** Initiate PMS vendor contract negotiation immediately to lock the integration cost. Do not authorize the full $300k spend until the integration scope is fixed at a maximum of $325k (mid-point of their range plus buffer).
**Deferred Start (May 15):** Execute the digital marketing push only after the Lakeside renovation is at least 40% complete and the PMS contract is signed. Launching marketing before physical improvements are visible wastes budget on a product not yet ready for the promised "refresh" premium.

**Required Human Sign-offs Before Spend:**
1.  **PMS Integration Cap:** Maya must sign off on the final integration cost if it exceeds $325k. If the vendor demands >$350k, pause the entire PMS rollout for re-bid.
2.  **Supply Chain Contingency:** Maya must approve a 10% contingency fund ($150k) drawn from the marketing budget if renovation material costs rise >5% due to shortages.

**Three Main Failure Modes & Stop-Loss Triggers:**
1.  **Renovation Delay:** Supply shortages push Lakeside completion past 4 months.
    *   *Trigger:* If occupancy at Lakeside drops below 65% for two consecutive months during construction due to noise/access issues, halt non-essential renovation work and redirect funds to temporary guest compensation.
2.  **PMS Integration Bloat:** Vendor integration costs exceed $400k, consuming marketing funds.
    *   *Trigger:* If the final integration quote exceeds $350k, cancel the custom integration module and revert to standard API only, even if it delays full feature rollout by 6 months.
3.  **ADR Stagnation:** The 5% ADR increase fails to materialize post-refresh.
    *   *Trigger:* If ADR has not increased by at least 3% within 90 days of the Lakeside re-opening, immediately pause the remaining marketing spend and commission a pricing elasticity audit before further investment.

**Decision:** Prioritize physical asset improvement (Lakeside) as the foundation, secure the operational backbone (PMS) with a hard cost cap, and time the demand generation (Marketing) to match the improved product availability.

*Note: This plan assumes the 5% ADR lift is achievable based on comparable market refreshes; validate this assumption with a quick competitor rate check before May 15.*
