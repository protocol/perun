# Copperkettle "Seasonal Series" Rollout Plan

**Prepared for:** Maya Patel, Founder
**Launch Target:** 2024-10-01
**Total Budget:** $120,000

### 1. Recommended Order of Work (Critical Path)

1.  **Immediate (Week 1): Compliance & POS Definition**
    *   Submit alcohol-service permit applications immediately (3-week variable timeline is the primary schedule risk).
    *   Demand a fixed written quote from the POS vendor before proceeding. If the cost exceeds **$18,000**, the project budget is compromised.
    *   **Maya Sign-off Required:** Approval to proceed with permit fees and POS contract.

2.  **Procurement (Weeks 2–3): Tap Line & Labels**
    *   Authorize the tap line installation ($45,000 + $5,000 contingency).
    *   Approve label design/printing ($12,000) only after the physical tap installation date is confirmed to ensure label timing matches stock availability.
    *   **Maya Sign-off Required:** Final release of funds for tap line hardware and label printing.

3.  **Execution (Weeks 4–9): Installation & Integration**
    *   Complete tap installation.
    *   Integrate POS module concurrently with label arrival.
    *   Staff training on new sales tracking.

4.  **Launch (Week 10): Go Live**
    *   Soft launch to test POS tracking; full marketing push for Seasonal Series.

### 2. Financial Reality Check
Your current projection double-counts revenue. The $30,000 (traffic lift) and $40,000 (label premium) are likely overlapping, not additive.
*   **Corrected Conservative Q1 Upside:** ~$55,000 (assuming 75% capture of combined potential).
*   **Remaining Budget Buffer:** $58,000 (after known costs). This buffer must cover the unknown POS cost and any permit delays.

### 3. Failure Modes (How This Rolls Out Wrong)
*   **The "Blind" Launch:** The POS vendor delays pricing or integration, forcing a launch where you cannot track which labels are selling, rendering the data strategy useless.
*   **The Compliance Gap:** Permits take 5 weeks instead of 3. You have the taps and labels ready but cannot legally serve the new series, wasting marketing momentum.
*   **Budget Creep:** The POS module costs >$20k, eating into the contingency needed for the tap line installation overruns.

### 4. Stop-Loss & Re-prioritization Triggers
**Abort or Pause the project if:**
*   **Cost Overrun:** Total committed costs exceed **$135,000** (leaving insufficient buffer for operational shocks).
*   **Revenue Miss:** Week 1 post-launch traffic lift is <**8%** (half of projected 15%), indicating the tap line is not driving the expected footfall.
*   **Timeline Slip:** Permits are not secured by **2024-09-15**. If permits are pending past this date, delay the marketing spend immediately to preserve cash.

***

**Note:** Do not sign the POS contract until the price is fixed; "priced later" is unacceptable for a fixed-budget project. Ensure the permit application references the specific "Seasonal Series" branding to avoid re-filing delays.
