**Subject:** Go-Forward Plan: Smart Season Pass

### Executive Recommendation: Sequence & Scope
We must immediately **descopethe dynamic pricing engine** from the initial launch to meet the $150k budget and 8-week timeline. The current plan exceeds the budget by $10k and carries high technical risk for a single sprint.

**Phase 1 (Launch - March 15): "Static" Season Pass**
*   **Build:** Unlimited 6-month pass product, UI purchase flow, and backend entitlement logic.
*   **Defer:** Dynamic pricing engine (weather/demand adjustments).
*   **Human Sign-off Required:** Legal/Finance approval on pass terms (liability caps, blackout dates) before code freeze.
*   **Budget Impact:** Reduces backend/testing scope, bringing total estimated cost to ~$135k.

**Phase 2 (Post-Launch - Q2): Dynamic Pricing Integration**
*   **Build:** Weather API integration, demand algorithms, and real-time rate adjustment.
*   **Trigger:** Initiate only if Phase 1 pass uptake exceeds 3% of active users.

### Three Main Failure Modes
1.  **Revenue Cannibalization:** High-value users (top 10% of spenders) switch to the pass, reducing total revenue despite increased booking volume.
2.  **Fleet Saturation:** Unlimited access creates a supply crunch during peak weekends, alienating non-pass paying customers and damaging marketplace liquidity.
3.  **Technical Debt Rush:** Attempting to force the dynamic pricing engine into the 8-week window results in unstable pricing logic, requiring costly hotfixes post-launch.

### Pivot/Stop Criteria
We will **pause Phase 2 development** and reconsider the pass model if:
*   **Uptake < 2%** of active users (240 purchases) within the first 14 days.
*   **Cancellation rate > 5%** within the first 30 days (indicating price/value mismatch).
*   **Peak weekend availability drops below 15%** for standard vessels due to pass-holder booking hoarding.
