# Northwind Kids Health: Behavioral-Health Video Visit Rollout Plan

## 1. Recommended Order of Work

**Immediate Actions (Now – Sept 15):**
*   **Secure Reimbursement Clarity:** Before any technical build, obtain written confirmation of reimbursement rates from top 3 insurers. This is the primary financial variable.
*   **Negotiate Vendor Timeline:** The Sept 15 EHR integration deadline is impossible given the Oct 1 start date. Immediately negotiate a phased rollout or a manual workaround for the pilot to avoid delaying clinician training.
*   **Recruit & Train Clinicians:** Begin the $180k training program for the 12 clinicians immediately. Do not wait for full EHR integration if a standalone secure video platform can be used temporarily.

**Deferred Actions:**
*   **Full EHR Automation:** Defer deep bi-directional EHR integration until after the pilot validates demand. Use manual charting or interim APIs for the first 3 months.
*   **Marketing Scale-Up:** Limit marketing strictly to the 500-family pilot cohort until unit economics are proven.

**Required Human Sign-offs:**
*   **CMO (Dr. Patel):** Must sign off on the clinical safety protocol for the interim (non-integrated) video workflow.
*   **CFO:** Must approve the revised break-even analysis including the $120k integration fee and training costs before funds are released.
*   **Legal/Compliance:** Must validate that the interim video solution meets HIPAA/state telehealth regulations without full EHR integration.

## 2. Primary Failure Modes

*   **Reimbursement Collapse:** If insurers classify these visits as "audio-only" or deny telehealth parity, revenue could drop by 40–60%, making the model insolvent.
*   **Integration Bottleneck:** If the vendor misses the Oct 1 start or the 6-week timeline slips, clinician utilization will sit idle, burning the $180k training investment without generating revenue.
*   **Adoption Overestimation:** The 80% adoption assumption is aggressive for behavioral health. If actual adoption is <50%, visit volume will not cover fixed clinician costs.

## 3. Stop/Change Course Trigger

**Trigger:** If **Pilot Net Revenue after Month 2 is < $30,000**.

**Action:**
1.  **Pause Clinician Onboarding:** Halt training for the remaining clinicians immediately to prevent further sunk costs.
2.  **Pivot or Kill:**
    *   *If the cause is low adoption:* Pivot to a hybrid model (async messaging + video) or discontinue the service.
    *   *If the cause is reimbursement denial:* Renegotiate payer contracts or shift to a cash-pay model before proceeding.
3.  **Financial Review:** Conduct a forensic review of the unit economics. Do not proceed to full rollout until the path to profitability is recalculated with actual data.
