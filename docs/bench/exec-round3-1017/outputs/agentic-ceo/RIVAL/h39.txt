Set a *target* price now, but make the *final* offer contingent on pilot validation.

**Three Main Failure Modes**
1.  **Integration Latency:** The third-party SDK fails to deliver true "real-time" sync on standard kitchen Wi-Fi, causing staff to revert to manual entry due to lag. This nullifies the 20% efficiency gain.
2.  **Budget Overrun on Customization:** The $120k budget assumes a standard SDK implementation. If the SDK requires expensive custom middleware to fit our existing database schema, development costs will spike, forcing cuts to the mobile UI quality.
3.  **Adoption Friction:** Kitchen staff find the mobile interface too cumbersome during rush hours. If the UI adds even 10 seconds per item scan, adoption will collapse regardless of backend efficiency.

**Specific Kill/Pivot Criteria**
Stop or fundamentally change course if **pilot adoption is under 40% of kitchen staff by Week 3 of the pilot (late July)** OR if **measured labor savings are less than 8% (less than $12,000/quarter)** after the first 30 days of live use.
