**Recommendation for COO Maya Patel: Harvest Deluxe Rollout Strategy**

**Executive Summary**
Proceed with a **"Packaging-First, Price-Validated" sequence**. Immediate priority is securing the GreenBox contract by 30 December 2026, as this is the critical path item with the hardest external deadline. Simultaneously, initiate a rapid 2-week price elasticity test before finalizing the recipe scale-up.

**Proposed Sequence of Work**
1.  **Immediate (Now – 30 Dec 2026):** Finalize and sign the GreenBox packaging contract. This locks in the $80,000 variable cost and ensures the refrigerated capability required for the premium tier.
2.  **Parallel Track (Jan 2027):** Launch a limited A/B price test in two markets comparing the $28 vs. $39.99 price points. Do not commit to the full $250,000 herb sourcing budget until this data confirms demand at the higher price.
3.  **Finalization (Post-Validation):** Upon confirming price elasticity, execute the full herb sourcing scale-up to meet the 15 January 2027 deadline.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Margin Erosion:** The plan assumes a 12% margin, but the unpriced $80,000 refrigerated box cost could reduce actual margins to ~9.6% if the $39.99 price point fails to hold.
*   **Supply Chain Bottleneck:** The assumption that the current supplier can scale to 5,000 units/week without extra charge is unverified. If organic herb availability tightens, costs could spike, destroying the premium tier profitability.
*   **Adoption Friction:** Core customers may reject the $12 price premium ($28 to $39.99) if the perceived value of "organic" and "refrigerated" does not clearly outweigh the cost increase.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Pivot Condition:** If the A/B test shows **less than 15% conversion** to the $39.99 tier among existing loyal customers, immediately pivot to a "Deluxe Add-On" model (selling the herbs/box separately) rather than a full kit replacement.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the uptake of the premium kit is **below 5% in the first month** post-launch, halt further marketing spend on the Deluxe line and revert resources to optimizing the standard $28 kit volume.

**Note:** The $4M revenue forecast is highly sensitive to the unverified elasticity assumption; the recommended A/B test is essential to de-risk this before full capital deployment.
Ensure the GreenBox contract includes a clause allowing volume flexibility to mitigate the risk of over-committing to the 5,000 unit/week target.
