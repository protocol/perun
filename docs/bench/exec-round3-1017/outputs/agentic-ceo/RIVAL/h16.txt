**Subject:** Actionable Plan for Fall Season Launch (Deadline: March 15, 2025)

**Executive Recommendation: Parallel Execution with "PCB First" Critical Path**
We must execute all three work streams in parallel, but the **Autonomous Navigation Module** is the critical path. The unknown custom PCB cost ($3k–$9k) and unverified sensor reliability claims pose the greatest threat to our March 2 Expo demo.

**Immediate Order of Work (Weeks 1–4):**
1.  **Validate Hardware Reality (Week 1):** Before full engineering allocation, spend $500 on a rapid prototype or third-party audit to verify the vendor's "20% build time reduction" claim and narrow the PCB cost variance. If the sensor fails reliability checks, the $48k development budget is at risk.
2.  **Lock Sponsor Pricing (Weeks 2–3):** Simultaneously, validate the drafted pricing tiers ($5k/$8.5k/$12k) with five anchor sponsors. Do not print the pricing sheet until we confirm willingness to pay at the $12k level; if uptake is low, we must adjust tiers before the March launch to avoid revenue shortfalls.
3.  **Define KPIs (Weeks 2–4):** Finalize the 5-metric dashboard (Build Time, Retention, Sponsor ROI, etc.) only after the sensor data pipeline is proven. Building the dashboard before confirming data availability risks creating a "vanity metric" tool that cannot track the promised 20% efficiency gain.

**The Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **The "Paper Tiger" Prototype:** The sensor package fails reliability testing in January, forcing a redesign that misses the March 2 Expo demo. This destroys credibility with sponsors and the board.
2.  **Revenue Gap:** The market rejects the $12k tier, and the $5k tier fails to cover the actual cost of goods (especially if PCB costs hit $9k), resulting in a loss-leader launch.
3.  **Budget Overrun:** The custom PCB cost lands at $9,000 (3x low estimate), consuming 19% of the development budget and forcing cuts to engineering hours or marketing.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers:**
*   **Pivot:** If sponsor validation shows <40% interest in the $8.5k+ tiers by December 1, immediately pivot to a lower-cost "basic" navigation package to match market willingness.
*   **Stop:** If the sensor reliability test fails to show *any* improvement over current manual builds by January 15, halt the $48k development spend. Do not throw good money after bad; revert to manual operations for the season.

**Hidden Costs & Risks:**
*   **Integration Debt:** The "20% faster" claim likely ignores the software integration time required to make the sensor talk to existing systems. Budget an extra 2 weeks for debugging.
*   **Liability Exposure:** Autonomous navigation introduces safety risks not present in manual builds. Ensure legal reviews the liability waivers for the Expo demo specifically.
*   **Opportunity Cost:** Allocating 4 engineers for 12 weeks removes them from maintenance. If the module fails, we lose both the new feature and critical system upkeep.

**Note:** This plan assumes the PCB quote is the primary variable; secure a fixed-price contract immediately to cap exposure.
**Note:** The March 2 Expo is a hard deadline; any slip in Week 4 requires cutting features, not extending time.
