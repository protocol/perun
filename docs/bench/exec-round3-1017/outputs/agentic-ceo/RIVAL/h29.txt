# Riverbend Gazette "Deep Dive" Implementation Plan

**Executive Summary:** Launch the premium investigative series and pricing tier immediately to validate revenue assumptions. Defer the "Story Builder" mobile feature to post-Q3. Human sign-off required on ad network compliance before publishing sensitive investigations.

### 1. Order of Work (45-Day Sprint)

**Phase 1: Foundation & Revenue (Days 1–20) – DO NOW**
*   **Pricing & Paywall:** Implement the revamped pricing tier and paywall upgrade first. This is the revenue engine; without it, traffic converts to nothing.
*   **Editorial Launch:** Publish the first two "Deep Dive" articles simultaneously with the paywall go-live to drive immediate conversion.
*   **Marketing:** Activate the email campaign to the 13,500 subscribers immediately upon launch.

**Phase 2: Optimization (Days 21–35)**
*   **Conversion Tuning:** Analyze initial signup data. Adjust paywall messaging and email copy based on real conversion rates (currently modeled at 15%, industry avg is often 2–5%).
*   **Ad Network Integration:** Finalize ad network contracts *after* the first articles are live to demonstrate content quality, mitigating the approval risk.

**Phase 3: Deferrals (Post-Q3)**
*   **DEFER:** "Story Builder" mobile feature.
    *   *Reasoning:* It consumes ~28% of the development budget ($90k) and critical engineering time but does not directly drive the Q3 subscription goal. Launch this in Q4 as a retention tool for existing subscribers.

### 2. Required Human Sign-Offs
*   **Legal/Compliance:** Final review of investigative content against potential ad network policies before publication.
*   **COO (Maya Patel):** Approval to shift "Story Builder" to Q4 and reallocate its contingency funds if conversion lags.

### 3. Top Three Failure Modes
1.  **Conversion Delusion:** The 15% conversion assumption is overly optimistic. If the 13,500-list yields only 5% (675 subs), you miss the Q3 target by 1,825 subscriptions.
2.  **Ad Revenue Gap:** If the ad network rejects the investigative content due to "sensitivity," the projected dual-revenue stream collapses, leaving the $70k editorial spend unsupported.
3.  **Technical Friction:** A buggy paywall upgrade during launch causes churn among existing users, offsetting new gains.

### 4. Halt or Pivot Trigger
**The Metric:** **Day 14 Conversion Rate.**
*   **Trigger:** If the conversion rate from the email list is **<7%** by Day 14.
*   **Action:** **HALT** further editorial spending on the series. **PIVOT** immediately to a lower-price point or a bundled offering. Do not wait for Day 45; the math will not recover in time for Q3.

***

*Note: This plan prioritizes cash flow (subscriptions) over feature completeness to meet the tight Q3 deadline. The "Story Builder" feature is a value-add, not a revenue driver, making it the logical cut.*
