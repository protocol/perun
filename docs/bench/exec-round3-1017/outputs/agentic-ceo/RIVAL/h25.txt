**Executive Plan: Dynamic Ad Pods Initiative**

**Recommended Execution Order**
1.  **Integrate SDK (Weeks 1–6):** Secure the audio-fingerprinting foundation first. Without accurate pod detection, dynamic insertion is impossible. *Sign-off required: CTO on SDK vendor contract.*
2.  **Build Dynamic Insertion Engine (Weeks 7–18):** Develop the core logic to swap ads based on fingerprint triggers. This is the highest technical risk and consumes the bulk of the $250k dev budget. *Sign-off required: VP Engineering on architecture review.*
3.  **Design Measurement Dashboard (Weeks 10–20):** Parallel track to ensure data capture is ready before pilots launch. Must track fill rate, latency, and distinct advertiser metrics.
4.  **Run Pricing Experiment (Weeks 21–24):** Execute with pilot hosts only after the tech stack is stable. Use this to validate the $12 CPM assumption before full rollout.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Technical:** The existing ad server crashes under the projected 30% traffic spike during live insertion, causing audio dropouts. *Mitigation:* Load test to 150% capacity by Week 16.
*   **Financial:** The third-party SDK final cost exceeds $120k, eroding the ROI margin. *Mitigation:* Cap contract at $125k or pause integration.
*   **Adoption:** Existing advertisers churn >8% due to perceived loss of control over ad placement, offsetting new revenue. *Mitigation:* Create a "legacy lock" option for top-tier spenders.

**Pivot/Stop Criteria**
Initiate immediate pivot or stop-work order if, after the first month of pilot operation (August 2027):
*   **Revenue:** Net yield is <85% of the $42k/month/pod projection (i.e., <$35.7k) after accounting for SDK costs.
*   **Stability:** Audio interruption incidents exceed 0.5% of total impressions.
*   **Churn:** Advertiser cancellation rate attributable to format changes exceeds 5%.

**Required Human Sign-Offs Before Code Ships**
1.  **Legal/Procurement:** Final SDK licensing agreement.
2.  **CTO:** Architecture approval for the insertion engine.
3.  **Head of Sales:** Agreement on pilot host pricing terms.
4.  **COO (Ravi Deshmukh):** Final go/no-go for pilot launch on 15 Jul 2027.

*Note: The churn impact on existing advertisers remains a critical unknown; a dedicated survey of current partners is recommended before Week 10 to de-risk adoption.*
*Budget contingency of 15% is advised given the unfinalized SDK cost.*
