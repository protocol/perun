**Recommended Path: "Legal-First Sequential Launch"**

**Sequence of Execution:**
1.  **Immediate Halt & Legal Sign-off (Weeks 1–2):** Pause all development and creative work. Legal must approve the new contract terms and quantify the insurance premium increase before a single line of code is written. This is the only mandatory human gate; if legal rejects the terms or the insurance cost exceeds $8,000 annually, the project stops immediately.
2.  **Parallel Build & Creative (Weeks 3–6):** Once legal clears, run subscription development (3 weeks) and ad creative production (2 weeks) simultaneously. Begin field crew training in Week 5 to align with QA.
3.  **QA & Pricing Finalization (Week 7):** Complete QA on the subscription feature. Finalize "Premium Wash" pricing based on the confirmed insurance costs.
4.  **Pilot Launch (Week 8 – Target Feb 25, 2025):** Deploy to the 12 pilot locations. Initiate the $25,000 ad spend only after the first location goes live.
5.  **Buffer (Feb 26 – Mar 1):** Reserve these days for critical hotfixes before the hard March 1st date.

**Item Waiting on Human Sign-off:**
Legal sign-off on contract terms and insurance impact. No shipping, spending, or coding occurs until this is written and approved.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **The Margin Trap:** The third-party API fee ($20k) combined with the unquantified insurance hike erodes the "Premium Wash" margin, making the 30% conversion volume profitable only on paper but cash-flow negative in reality.
2.  **The Integration Bottleneck:** The 3-week development estimate assumes clean APIs; if legacy systems require complex middleware not yet identified, QA spills into March, missing the launch window and wasting the $25k ad spend on airtime.
3.  **Competitor Price War:** A major competitor drops prices by 15% in February, forcing us to discount the new tier immediately, invalidating the revenue model used to approve the $150k budget.

**Kill Switch Metric:**
Stop or radically pivot if **pilot revenue from the new tier is below $30,000 in the first 30 days** (approx. $2,500/location). This indicates either the conversion rate is below the required 30% or the average transaction value is insufficient to cover the new fixed costs (API + Insurance).

*Note: This plan assumes the $150k budget absorbs the $20k API fee; if legal/insurance costs exceed $12k combined, the marketing budget must be cut to compensate.*
