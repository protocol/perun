**2. Primary Failure Modes**

*   **Unit Economics Collapse:** If the translation API costs settle at $0.025/min rather than $0.015/min, our margin per session disappears. At 20% DAU growth, variable costs could outpace the $350k capex runway within three months.
*   **Regulatory Blockade:** If EU data-privacy clearance exceeds 4 weeks, we miss the 5 Dec global launch. Launching without full clearance risks fines exceeding our entire project budget.
*   **Demographic Mismatch:** Our assumption of a 20% DAU lift relies on a competitor's data from a user base 30% older than ours. If our younger demographic prefers asynchronous text over live video, adoption will flatline despite perfect execution.

**3. Kill/Pivot Criteria**

*   **Stop Condition:** If the 6-week pilot (ending 28 Nov) shows a translation cost per user session exceeding $1.50, or if EU clearance is not granted by 15 Nov.
*   **Pivot Condition:** If DAU lift is <5% but session duration is >15 minutes, pivot from "mass market rooms" to "premium tutoring add-ons" to justify higher API costs.
