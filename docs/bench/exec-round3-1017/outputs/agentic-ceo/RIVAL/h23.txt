# SmartRide Go/No-Go Plan: Recommendation & Execution Path

**Recommendation: CONDITIONAL GO**
Proceed immediately with a **Phased Rollout** strategy. Do not attempt a full simultaneous launch of hardware, pilot, and pricing. The current budget deficit ($210k) and FCC uncertainty make a "big bang" launch too risky.

### 1. Order of Work (Critical Path)
1.  **Immediate (Week 1): Legal & Privacy Sign-off.**
    *   *Why:* If legal rejects the data model, the GPS feature is dead. Do not spend hardware dollars until data usage is approved.
2.  **Parallel Track A: FCC Certification Submission.**
    *   *Action:* Submit immediately. The 4–8 week window is your primary schedule risk.
3.  **Parallel Track B: Budget Correction & Pilot Prep.**
    *   *Action:* Cut marketing spend by $150k (shift to organic/pilot-focused) and defer 110 bikes from the initial hardware order to close the $210k gap. Prepare the Seattle pilot with only 50 bikes initially.
4.  **Month 2: Seattle Pilot Launch.**
    *   *Condition:* Launch only if Legal is green and FCC submission is confirmed. Run for 8 weeks (accelerated from 3 months to save time/cost).
5.  **Month 4: Pricing Finalization & Full Rollout.**
    *   *Action:* Set the $15/mo tier based on pilot conversion data, not assumptions.

### 2. Primary Failure Modes
*   **The Budget Trap:** You are already overspent before the pilot starts. If the FCC process hits the 8-week mark, you will burn through your contingency while waiting, leaving no funds for the actual rollout.
*   **The Revenue Assumption:** The plan assumes 100% adoption of the $15/mo add-on to justify costs. If only 20% of riders opt-in (industry standard for add-ons), monthly revenue drops from $120k to $24k, destroying the ROI case.
*   **Hardware Lock-in:** Integrating GPS before validating user demand means you are stuck with $450k of specialized hardware if the feature fails.

### 3. Kill Switches (When to Pull the Plug)
Advise Maya Patel to issue a **STOP** order immediately if any of the following occur:

*   **FCC Delay > 6 Weeks:** If certification is not secured by mid-June, the July 15 launch is impossible. Defer to Q3 to avoid launching with non-compliant hardware.
*   **Pilot Churn > 15%:** If more than 15% of pilot users disable the GPS or cancel the subscription within the first 30 days, the value proposition is invalid.
*   **Legal Conditional Approval:** If Legal requires user opt-in *before* ride activation (rather than passive tracking), the friction will likely kill adoption. Defer the feature.
*   **Pilot Adoption < 10%:** If fewer than 10% of pilot riders voluntarily choose the $15 tier, the revenue model fails. Pivot to a bundled pricing model or scrap the hardware.

**Note:** The current budget math assumes full fleet adoption; the pilot must validate the *attach rate* before committing the remaining $2M.
**Note:** Shift the "Go/No-Go" decision gate to the end of the 8-week accelerated pilot, not today.
