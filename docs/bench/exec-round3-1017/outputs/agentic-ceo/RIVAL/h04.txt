### Push‑Notify Rollout Plan (July 1 – Sept 30, 2024)

**1. Recommended Sequence & Budget Allocation ($150k Total)**
*   **Phase 1: Validation & Design (Weeks 1–4) | Cost: $45k**
    *   **Action:** Finalize UX/UI for push notifications and draft the premium pricing landing page.
    *   **Human Sign-off Required:** Board approval on the final $5/month pricing structure before engineering begins.
    *   **Deliverable:** High-fidelity prototypes and a "measurement framework" baseline (current MAU/churn).
*   **Phase 2: Core Development (Weeks 5–10) | Cost: $80k**
    *   **Action:** Build the mobile push infrastructure and integrate with the existing app.
    *   **Constraint:** Do not build billing integration yet; hard-code the "premium" flag for beta users only.
    *   **Deliverable:** Functional push feature ready for internal QA.
*   **Phase 3: Controlled Beta (Weeks 11–13) | Cost: $15k (Contingency/Comms)**
    *   **Action:** Deploy to 12 churches (0.1% of base). Monitor delivery rates and opt-in percentages.
    *   **Deliverable:** Beta report and Stakeholder Communication Deck for Ravi.
    *   **Decision Gate:** Go/No-Go for full launch based on beta engagement data.

**2. Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Adoption Mismatch:** Churches refuse the $5 premium tier because they view push notifications as a basic utility, not an add-on.
2.  **Technical Fatigue:** Users disable notifications due to poor timing or relevance, negatively impacting app sentiment before value is proven.
3.  **Budget Overrun:** The $80k engineering estimate excludes backend scaling costs for high-volume delivery, blowing the $150k cap before launch.

**3. Kill/Pivot Trigger**
*   **Stop Condition:** If **<1% of beta churches** activate the premium feature or if **opt-out rates exceed 15%** within the first 14 days of the beta.
*   **Action:** Immediately halt billing integration work and pivot to making push a free retention tool to stabilize churn.

**4. Hidden Risks & Costs**
*   **Platform Compliance:** Apple/Google strict guidelines on notification spam could require expensive re-work if the messaging strategy isn't vetted by legal/compliance early.
*   **Support Load:** A sudden influx of "how do I turn this off?" tickets from non-beta users if the feature flag leaks, straining current support staff (unbudgeted labor cost).

***

**Note:** This plan assumes the $80k/$45k estimates are fixed; if engineering discovers API complexity during Phase 1, the beta scope must be reduced to stay within $150k.
**Note:** The "measurement framework" must be finalized in Week 1, or you will not have clean data to present to Ravi in September.
