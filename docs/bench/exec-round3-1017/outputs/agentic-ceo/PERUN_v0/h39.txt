**Pantry Pulse Phase 2 Execution Plan (For COO Sign-off)**

**1. Recommended Order of Work & Deferrals**
*   **Weeks 1–2 (Foundation & Validation):** Finalize the barcode SDK licensing (confirm the ~$8k cost) and lock the pilot measurement framework. Do not write code until the SDK contract is signed.
*   **Weeks 3–7 (Build):** Develop the real-time stock-level API first, followed immediately by the mobile-first UI. These are technical dependencies; the UI cannot function without the API.
*   **Weeks 8–9 (Pilot Launch):** Deploy to the pilot group.
*   **Defer:** Finalizing the commercial pricing model for mid-tier clients. While a hypothesis is needed for the pilot, formal pricing packaging should wait until pilot data validates the value proposition. Do not lock pricing before seeing actual adoption and waste reduction metrics.

**2. Three Main Failure Modes**
*   **Integration Blocker:** The third-party barcode SDK fails to integrate with our legacy database within the first 10 days, causing a cascade delay that misses the June 15 launch.
*   **Workflow Friction:** Kitchen staff reject the mobile UI because it adds steps rather than saving time, resulting in low adoption regardless of the backend's speed.
*   **Economic Miscalculation:** The projected 20% labor savings relies on 100% staff compliance; if actual usage is sporadic, the $30,000 quarterly saving will not materialize, undermining the ROI case for wider rollout.

**3. Kill/Pivot Criteria**
Stop or fundamentally change course if **pilot adoption is under 40% of shifts** after the first three weeks OR if **measured waste reduction is less than 5%** by the end of the 9-week period. These thresholds indicate the core value hypothesis (labor savings and waste reduction) is invalid.

***

**Note:** The competitor's 15% revenue boost is not a reliable benchmark for our labor-saving goal given the market size difference; focus strictly on our internal efficiency metrics. The $120k budget is sufficient provided the SDK cost does not exceed $15k.
