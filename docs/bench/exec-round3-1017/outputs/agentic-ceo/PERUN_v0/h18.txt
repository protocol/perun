**Recommendation: Sequence, Sign-offs, and Pivot Triggers for "Smart Season Pass"**

**1. Work Sequencing & Human Gates**
*   **Weeks 1–4 (Build Core Value):** Prioritize the **Pass Logic & UI** ($30k UI + $20k Backend). Defer the full dynamic pricing engine; launch with static "Peak/Off-Peak" tiers manually configured. This secures the revenue vehicle within budget.
*   **Weeks 5–8 (Iterate Pricing):** Allocate remaining $20k Backend + $90k Testing to integrate the dynamic pricing engine *only if* Week 4 pass sales exceed 200 units. If not, use testing budget to harden the static tier model.
*   **Human Sign-off Required:** CEO approval is mandatory before Week 5 to unlock dynamic pricing development, contingent on Week 4 uptake data. Legal review is required immediately for "unlimited" liability clauses regarding weather cancellations.

**2. Three Main Failure Modes**
*   **Margin Erosion:** Heavy users dominate the fleet during peak weather, displacing higher-margin single rentals, causing net revenue to drop despite higher booking volume.
*   **Supply Shock:** Dynamic pricing spikes rates during high demand, but inventory remains fixed; users pay more for the same boats, triggering churn rather than lifting conversion.
*   **Budget Blowout:** The $90k testing allocation suggests over-engineering; if the dynamic engine requires complex integration not scoped, the $10k overage becomes a $50k deficit, delaying launch past the March 15 peak season start.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Uptake Threshold:** If pass sales are **<1.5% of active users (180 units)** after two weeks, pivot to a "5-Ride Bundle" (lower price point, capped usage) immediately.
*   **Cannibalization Limit:** If single-ride revenue drops **>20%** week-over-week while total fleet utilization stays flat, disable dynamic pricing surges and revert to flat pass pricing.
*   **Operational Halt:** If weather-related cancellation requests exceed **10% of total pass redemptions** in the first month, suspend the "unlimited" clause and institute a fair-use policy.

**Note:** The current budget exceeds limits by $10k; the recommended deferral of the full dynamic engine to Phase 2 resolves this without cutting testing coverage. Competitor benchmarks (Breeze Boats) should be treated as aspirational, not predictive, given their 2x fleet size.
