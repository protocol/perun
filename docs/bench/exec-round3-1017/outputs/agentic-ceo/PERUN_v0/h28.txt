# FlexPass Rollout Plan for Maya (COO)

**Recommended Order of Work**
1.  **Immediate Start: Dashboard Integration (Work Stream 3).** Resolve the unknown API integration cost immediately. Without a measurement baseline, the pilot (Stream 2) and feature launch (Stream 1) cannot be validated. Budget $15k license + contingency for integration.
2.  **Parallel Start: Pilot Pricing Experiment (Work Stream 2).** Launch September 1st in Downtown/Midtown using *existing* manual tracking if the dashboard lags slightly, but full dependency on Stream 3 is required for day-one accuracy.
3.  **Deferred Start: Mobile App Build (Work Stream 1).** Begin development only after Week 2 of the pilot confirms the pricing elasticity hypothesis. If the pilot fails to show a path to the 5% lift, the $120k dev spend is wasted.

**Deferrable Items**
*   **Mobile App Feature (Stream 1):** Defer full build until pilot data validates the 5% price lift potential.
*   **Full Fleet Rollout:** Defer beyond Downtown/Midtown until churn reduction (target 2%) is proven over the 6-week pilot window.

**Mandatory Human Sign-Offs**
*   **Before Pilot Launch:** Maya must sign off on the specific pricing tiers for Downtown/Midtown to ensure compliance with member contracts.
*   **Before Mobile Release:** Product Lead must sign off on the API integration stability test; Engineering Lead must sign off on the "kill switch" capability to revert pricing logic instantly.
*   **Before Full Spend:** Finance must approve the final integration cost for Stream 3 before the $120k Stream 1 budget is unlocked.

**Primary Failure Modes & Stop-Loss Triggers**
Maya must halt or pivot the rollout immediately if:
*   **Occupancy Collapse:** Average occupancy in pilot sites drops below **70%** for two consecutive weeks (indicating price sensitivity is higher than modeled).
*   **Revenue Miss:** Pilot sites fail to achieve a **3% net revenue uplift** (after churn adjustment) by October 1st.
*   **Churn Spike:** Member cancellation rate in pilot sites exceeds **4%** (double the baseline assumption) within the first 30 days.
*   **Technical Block:** The third-party API integration for the dashboard cannot be resolved within 10 business days, rendering the pilot unmeasurable.

**Core Assumption Validation**
This plan assumes the Q2 survey data (8% booking boost, 2% churn reduction) holds true under real pricing pressure. The pilot is designed specifically to falsify this assumption before major capital deployment.

*Note: The unknown integration cost for Stream 3 is the highest immediate risk to timeline and budget accuracy.*
*All financial projections assume no changes to underlying site operational costs during the pilot window.*
