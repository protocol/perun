**Recommendation for Maya Patel: Turbo Match Initiative Plan**

**1. Order of Work & Legal Gates**
*   **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–3): AI-Driven Pairing Engine.**
    *   *Rationale:* Highest immediate ROI ($22k/mo) and core value prop improvement (68% → 85% match rate).
    *   *Legal Gate:* **None required before dev start**, but require legal sign-off on *data usage terms* before enabling the model on live student data.
*   **Phase 2 (Weeks 4–5): Tiered Pricing Model.**
    *   *Rationale:* Low engineering lift (2 weeks), captures Q4 enrollment growth.
    *   *Legal Gate:* **Mandatory Senior Legal Review BEFORE code merge.** Contractual discounts and 12-month commitments carry binding financial liability and potential regulatory scrutiny in K–12 procurement.
*   **Phase 3 (Week 6): Live Performance Dashboards (Pilot).**
    *   *Rationale:* Highest risk, lowest immediate revenue impact.
    *   *Legal Gate:* **Hard Stop.** Do not write code until state compliance for third-party data sharing is confirmed in writing. If compliance is unclear by Day 5, defer indefinitely.

**2. Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Regulatory Blockade:** State laws (e.g., COPPA, state-specific student privacy acts) prohibit sharing granular performance data with third-party analytics without explicit parental consent, killing the Dashboard pilot.
*   **Adoption Mismatch:** The $22k revenue projection assumes 40% tutor adoption. If the new AI matches tutors to students outside their preferred subjects/grades to maximize "match rate," tutor churn could spike, negating revenue gains.
*   **Billing Leakage:** Implementing tiered pricing without a robust billing audit trail could result in unrecoverable revenue loss if the 5% discount applies incorrectly to legacy contracts.

**3. Kill Criteria (Stop/Re-prioritize Triggers)**
*   **AI Engine:** If live A/B testing shows a **>5% drop in session completion rates** (indicating bad matches despite high "match rate" scores) or a **>10% increase in tutor cancellation requests** within 14 days of launch.
*   **Dashboards:** If Legal cannot identify a compliant data-sharing framework for at least one target state within 5 business days.
*   **Pricing:** If the engineering estimate for secure discount application exceeds 2 weeks, defer to avoid sprint spill-over.

**Note:** The 5-day legal assumption is a critical single point of failure for the Dashboard; treat it as high-risk until confirmed. Do not commingle student PII with the analytics pilot until compliance is verified.
