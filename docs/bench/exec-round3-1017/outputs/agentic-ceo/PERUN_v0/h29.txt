**Recommendation: Order of Work & Critical Gates**

**1. Immediate Execution (Days 1–20):**
*   **Paywall Upgrade & Pricing Revamp:** Deploy first. This infrastructure must be live to capture the projected 2,500 subscribers. Without this, content delivery and revenue collection are impossible.
*   **Editorial "Deep Dive" Production:** Begin immediately alongside tech work. Content lead time is the highest risk to the July 1 launch.
*   **Human Sign-off Required:** Final pricing tier structure and legal terms for the new paywall must be signed by the COO before development starts.

**2. Secondary Execution (Days 21–40):**
*   **"Story Builder" Mobile Feature:** Develop only after the paywall is stable. If delays occur, this is the primary candidate for scope reduction (e.g., launch as a web-only feature first).
*   **Marketing Campaign:** Launch teasers once the payment gateway is verified.

**3. Defer or Descoped (If behind schedule):**
*   **"Story Builder" Advanced Interactivity:** If Day 30 arrives without a beta, strip interactive elements and launch a static "premium reader" view instead.
*   **Ad Network Integration:** Do not wait for approval to launch. Launch with existing ad inventory or a temporary "sponsor-free" premium experience.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Conversion Math Failure:** The 15% conversion assumption is aggressive. If the email list yields <1,800 subs by Day 30, the $320k budget becomes unsustainable without immediate pivot.
2.  **Technical Bottleneck:** Integrating the new paywall with legacy subscriber data often causes login failures at launch, churning users before they pay.
3.  **Content Delay:** Investigative journalism is unpredictable. If the lead "Deep Dive" story isn't ready by June 25, the launch marketing loses its hook.

**Halt/Pivot Trigger**
*   **Metric:** If paid subscriptions do not reach **850 by July 15** (35% of the Q3 goal), halt all "Story Builder" development and reallocate the remaining $45k design budget entirely to performance marketing to salvage the subscription target.
*   **Event:** If the new ad network denies approval by **June 20**, pivot the revenue model to rely 100% on subscription volume rather than hybrid ad/sub revenue, requiring an immediate re-forecast of the Q3 financial target.

**Note:** The ad network uncertainty should not block the July 1 launch; treat it as upside potential, not baseline revenue. Ensure the COO signs the pricing tier document before any code is written.
