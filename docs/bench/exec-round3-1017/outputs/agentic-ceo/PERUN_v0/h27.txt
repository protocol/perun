**Recommended Rollout Plan for Safe Class Phishing Module**

**1. Order of Work & Deferrals**
*   **Phase 1 (Immediate):** Develop the interactive lesson builder. This is the core value prop; without it, there is no product to pilot.
*   **Phase 2 (Parallel to Dev):** Finalize pricing at $3,200/school. Do not wait for the pilot to end to set the commercial framework, but mark the $1,500 LMS integration cost as a variable risk to be validated during pilot onboarding.
*   **Phase 3 (Pilot Launch - Oct 15, 2026):** Execute the 150-school pilot.
*   **Deferrals:** Defer the **optional analytics dashboard** ($30k) until post-pilot validation proves users need it beyond basic reports. Defer **teacher-training webinars**; replace with self-serve documentation for the pilot to test if schools can onboard without high-touch support. If pilot churn is high due to confusion, reinstate webinars for the full rollout.

**2. Mandatory Human Sign-offs**
*   **Legal/Compliance Gate:** Before the Oct 15 pilot launch, Legal must sign off on data privacy handling, specifically regarding the simulation of phishing attacks on minors within school networks.
*   **Financial Gate:** The COO must approve the final unit economics model once the first 10 LMS integration costs are confirmed, verifying the margin holds at the $1,500 integration estimate.

**3. Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Integration Cost Blowout:** If actual LMS integration exceeds $2,500 per school, the unit economics collapse, turning the $275k projected revenue into a loss leader.
*   **Low Adoption/Engagement:** If schools purchase but teachers disable the simulations due to perceived disruption, renewal rates will hit zero.
*   **Compliance Incident:** A single false-positive phishing simulation that causes a real security breach or data leak during the pilot could terminate the program and invite liability.

**4. Stop/Change Triggers**
*   **Stop Pilot:** If pilot NPS is below 60 or if more than 15% of schools request to opt out within the first 30 days.
*   **Change Course (Pivot):** If confirmed LMS integration costs average over $2,000, immediately pause full rollout to re-architect the integration layer or shift to a higher price tier ($4,000+).
*   **Revenue Gate:** If committed pre-orders from the pilot do not reach $200k (approx. 73 schools converting) by the end of the pilot phase, do not proceed to general availability.

*Note: The $275k revenue figure assumes 110 conversions (not 150) based on your $2,500 pilot price vs $3,200 target; ensure the conversion math aligns with the go/no-go criteria.*
*All integration cost assumptions must be stress-tested with three live district IT directors before Phase 1 completion.*
