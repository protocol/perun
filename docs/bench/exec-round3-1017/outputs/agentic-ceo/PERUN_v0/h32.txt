**Gentle Hours At-Home Tele-Monitoring Rollout Plan (Q4 2024)**

**1. Recommended Order of Work**
*   **Weeks 1–2 (Discovery & Gate):** Finalize third-party analytics pricing; secure legal/compliance sign-off on data handling (Mandatory Human Gate before device procurement).
*   **Weeks 3–6 (Build):** Complete device integration and EMR scheduling sync (validate "no extra fee" assumption immediately).
*   **Weeks 7–8 (Train):** Staff training on verified integrated system.
*   **Weeks 9–14 (Pilot):** Launch at 3 sites (150 clients total, correcting the 200 count error).
*   **Week 15 (Review):** Analyze pilot satisfaction; iterate if <90%.
*   **Weeks 16–21 (Go Live):** Company-wide rollout.

**2. Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Budget Breach:** Unanticipated analytics subscription fees or EMR licensing costs exceeding the $150k cap.
*   **Integration Block:** Scheduling platform fails to sync with EMR without paid middleware, delaying training and pilot.
*   **Pilot Dilution:** Attempting to serve 200 clients instead of the feasible 150, overwhelming support staff and tanking satisfaction scores.

**3. Stop/Change Metric**
*   **HALT CONDITION:** Pilot client satisfaction drops below **85%** after the first 30 days OR if total projected cost exceeds **$145,000** (leaving 5% buffer) before pilot launch.

*Note: Do not order devices until the analytics cost is confirmed in writing to protect the budget. The mandatory human sign-off is required by compliance before any client data flows to the third-party aggregator.*
