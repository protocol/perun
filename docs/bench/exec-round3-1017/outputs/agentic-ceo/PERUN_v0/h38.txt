**Recommendation for Maya Patel: Festival Pass Launch Plan**

**1. Order of Work & Budget Allocation ($1.2M Total)**
*   **Phase 1: Foundation & Validation (Immediate – April 2025)**
    *   **Action:** Secure content licensing ($450k) and initiate vendor contract correction for the recommendation engine.
    *   **Budget:** $450k (Content) + $5k (Contingency for vendor discrepancy).
    *   **Milestone (Sign-off Required):** Finalize vendor contract to resolve the $5k split discrepancy ($120k software vs. $85k integration) before payment. Total engine cost confirmed at $205k.
*   **Phase 2: Build & Integrate (May – June 15, 2025)**
    *   **Action:** Deploy recommendation engine and hire two contract QA analysts.
    *   **Budget:** $205k (Engine) + $75k (QA) = $280k.
    *   **Milestone (Sign-off Required):** QA Certification. No public rollout until QA confirms the engine correctly surfaces the 120 indie titles without latency issues.
*   **Phase 3: Market Test & Launch (June 16 – July 15, 2025)**
    *   **Action:** Execute marketing push with gated spend.
    *   **Budget:** $150k (Marketing).
    *   **Strategy:** Release 20% of budget ($30k) initially to validate the 0.8% conversion assumption. Release remaining 80% only if metrics hold.
    *   **Remaining Budget:** $45k (Reserve for operational overruns).

**2. Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Conversion Delusion:** The assumed 0.8% impression-to-conversion rate is unsubstantiated; if actual conversion is <0.4%, the $150k spend yields fewer than 2,000 users, failing to cover the $450k content cost.
2.  **Integration Bottleneck:** The vendor's $85k integration line item is underestimated; if technical debt delays the engine past June 15, the July 15 launch window is missed, wasting the Q4 strategic timing.
3.  **Retention Cliff:** Users convert via free trial (estimated 12%) but churn immediately if the "indie titles" library lacks depth beyond the initial 120 licensed films, rendering the recommendation engine irrelevant.

**3. Stop/Re-prioritize Metric**
*   **Trigger:** If the **free-trial-to-paid conversion rate is <5%** after the first 10,000 trial sign-ups (post-launch), or if the **cost-per-acquisition (CPA) exceeds $60**.
*   **Action:** Immediately halt the remaining marketing spend and re-prioritize resources toward content acquisition or pricing model adjustment before further engineering investment.

**Note:** The $5k vendor discrepancy must be resolved contractually before Phase 2 begins to prevent billing disputes. The 12% trial conversion estimate is a critical unknown; consider a small pre-launch A/B test to validate this before committing the full marketing budget.
