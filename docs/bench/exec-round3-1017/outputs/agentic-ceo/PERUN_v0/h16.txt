**To:** Maya Patel, Board Chair
**From:** CEO
**Date:** 24 October 2024
**Subject:** Action Plan: Fall Season Launch & Risk Mitigation

**Executive Summary**
We will prioritize the **Autonomous Navigation Module** immediately to secure the 2 March Expo demo, while deferring final pricing validation until prototype reliability is confirmed. The custom PCB cost variance ($3k–$9k) represents our primary financial exposure.

**1. Recommended Order of Work**
*   **Phase 1 (Now – 15 Jan): Critical Path Build.** Allocate all 4 engineers to the navigation module. We must treat the vendor's "20% build time reduction" claim as unverified; our schedule assumes zero gain until internal testing proves otherwise. The goal is a functional, albeit expensive, prototype for the Expo.
*   **Phase 2 (16 Jan – 28 Feb): Validation & Pricing.** Once the prototype demonstrates basic reliability, we will pressure-test the sponsor tiers ($5k/$8.5k/$12k). Selling unvalidated specs risks reputation; selling a working demo secures commitment.
*   **Phase 3 (1 Mar – 15 Mar): Integration & Dashboard.** Finalize the KPI dashboard using actual data from the Expo demo rather than theoretical projections. Execute full rollout.

**2. Three Biggest Failure Modes**
*   **Hardware Latency:** The custom PCB arrives late or exceeds the $9,000 cap, starving the software team of test units and missing the 2 March demo.
*   **Reliability Gap:** The sensor package fails to deliver the promised 20% efficiency in real-world conditions, rendering the "autonomous" value proposition false during the Expo.
*   **Market Rejection:** Sponsors reject the $12,000 tier after seeing the prototype, leaving us with a high-cost build and insufficient runway.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Technical Pivot:** If the prototype fails >2 times during Expo dry-runs, we halt the "autonomous" marketing angle and pivot to "assisted navigation" to manage expectations.
*   **Financial Stop:** If confirmed PCB costs exceed $7,500 *and* soft-commitments for the $12k tier are below 40% of target, we pause the full rollout to re-architect for cost.
*   **Commercial Stop:** If sponsor pull-back exceeds 30% post-Expo, we cancel the general launch and restrict the season to a pilot program.

**4. Hidden Costs & Risks**
*   **Integration Debt:** Rushing the 2 March demo often creates fragile code that requires a 3-week "stabilization sprint" post-Expo, delaying the 15 March rollout.
*   **Liability Exposure:** Deploying autonomous features without verified reliability data increases insurance premiums and legal liability during the Expo.
*   **Opportunity Cost:** Engineering time spent manually fixing sensor errors is time not spent on the KPI dashboard, potentially leaving us blind to operational inefficiencies at launch.

**Note:** We are proceeding with the assumption that the PCB lead time fits the current critical path; procurement must verify this within 48 hours.
No external market data was used; pricing strategy relies entirely on the Expo demo performance.
