**Board Brief: Skyline Survey Drone Inspection Phase II**
**Date:** 2024-11-12 | **Presenter:** Maya Patel, COO

**Executive Summary**
We request approval to deploy $500k over 90 days (starting Jan 3, 2025) to acquire five autonomous drones and integrate next-gen LiDAR sensors. Targeting three regional utilities ($3.2M revenue base), we project Q3 2025 breakeven based on 85% renewal and 15% upsell uplift. Success hinges on resolving altitude regulatory gaps and finalizing operational cost models.

**1. Recommended Order of Work & Deferrals**
*   **Phase 1 (Days 1–30): Regulatory & Cost Validation.** Secure written confirmation on >1,000ft operations and finalize insurance/training quotes (est. 8% of capex). *Do not purchase hardware until these are confirmed.*
*   **Phase 2 (Days 31–60): Procurement & Integration.** Purchase drones/sensors and complete technical integration.
*   **Phase 3 (Days 61–90): Pilot Launch.** Deploy to the three partner utilities.
*   **Deferred:** Full marketing launch and hiring additional pilots. Wait for pilot validation before scaling headcount or public messaging.

**2. Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Regulatory Block:** Inability to secure >1,000ft waivers renders the new sensor's accuracy advantage unusable for key infrastructure.
*   **Cost Underestimation:** Insurance and training costs exceed the 8% historical estimate, eroding margins before revenue scales.
*   **Integration Latency:** LiDAR integration delays push the pilot start date beyond Q1, missing the utility maintenance window.
*   **Adoption Friction:** Utility partners reject the new data format or workflow, preventing the projected 15% upsell.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
We will halt or pivot the initiative immediately if:
*   **Regulatory Denial:** Formal rejection or >6-week delay on altitude waivers.
*   **Financial Breach:** Pilot setup costs exceed $550k (including hidden ops costs) or projected pilot net loss exceeds $50k in the first 45 days.
*   **Technical Failure:** The new LiDAR sensors fail to demonstrate ≥20% efficiency gain over current models during internal testing.

***

*Note: The 8% ops cost estimate should be treated as a floor, not a ceiling, given the autonomous nature of the new fleet. A contingency reserve of $40k is advised within the $500k budget to cover regulatory legal fees.*
