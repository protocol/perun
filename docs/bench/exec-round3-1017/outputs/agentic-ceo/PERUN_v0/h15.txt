**Recommendation to COO Maya Patel: Parallel Execution with Pricing Gate**

**Sequence of Work**
Execute all three work streams immediately in parallel, but institute a hard "Pricing Gate" before finalizing the GreenBox contract.
1.  **Immediate (Now – 15 Dec 2026):** Launch the pricing/positioning analysis to validate elasticity between the $28 standard and $39.99 premium tiers. Simultaneously, finalize the herb recipe and initiate non-binding talks with GreenBox.
2.  **Decision Point (15 Dec 2026):** Review pricing data. If the model confirms >15% conversion to the premium tier at $39.99 (absorbing the $80k refrigerated box cost), proceed to sign the GreenBox contract.
3.  **Finalization (16 Dec 2026 – 30 Dec 2026):** Sign the GreenBox contract and lock herb sourcing.
4.  **Completion (Jan 2027):** Complete recipe scaling and packaging prototype integration for launch.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Margin Erosion:** The $80,000 variable cost for refrigeration combined with lower-than-expected premium uptake reduces the 12% margin to break-even or loss.
*   **Supply Chain Bottleneck:** The assumption that current suppliers can scale to 5,000 units/week without extra charge proves false, delaying the January 15 deadline.
*   **Contract Lock-in:** Signing the GreenBox contract before validating price elasticity locks the company into high fixed costs for a product customers reject at the $39.99 price point.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Stop:** If pricing analysis shows less than **8% uptake** of the premium kit in the first month of testing, or if the effective margin drops below **5%** after accounting for refrigeration costs.
*   **Pivot:** If uptake is between 8–12%, pivot to a hybrid model: offer the refrigerated box as a paid add-on ($12) rather than bundling it into the $39.99 base price, preserving the $28 entry point.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes validating the $80k cost risk before signing the binding December 30 contract. The January 15 herb deadline is secondary to securing unit economics.
