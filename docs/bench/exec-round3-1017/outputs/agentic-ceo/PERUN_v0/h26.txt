1.  **Immediate (Now – July 14): Vendor & Data Lock.** Renegotiate hardware to strict $12,000/bus cap ($540k total). Secure cellular data contract ($8/bus/mo). **Defer:** Marketing campaigns and full driver training until hardware installation begins.

3.  **Training & Soft Launch (Oct 1 – Oct 31):** Train drivers on the 10 active buses. Launch limited marketing to "occasional riders" only.
4.  **Full Fleet & Evaluation (Nov 1 – Dec 1):** Install remaining 35 buses if adoption >20% on pilot group. Finalize go-live reporting.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Backend Collapse:** The existing ticketing system cannot handle the spike in API calls from 1,200 daily transactions, causing fare gate timeouts and rider frustration.
2.  **Adoption Stall:** Occasional rider adoption stays below 20% due to poor signage or confusing UX, making the unit economics unsustainable.
3.  **Connectivity Gaps:** Cellular data plans have coverage holes on specific routes, causing readers to fail offline without a proper store-and-forward mechanism.

**Stop/Pivot Metrics**
*   **Adoption:** <20% of target demographic using contactless by Nov 15.
*   **Cost:** Total project cost exceeds budget by >10% (driven by hardware overruns or unexpected integration hours).
*   **Reliability:** Transaction failure rate >2% during the 10-bus pilot phase.
