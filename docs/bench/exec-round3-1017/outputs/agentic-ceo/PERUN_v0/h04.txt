**Push‑Notify Rollout Plan (July 1 – Sept 30, 2024)**

**1. Recommended Sequence & Budget ($150k Cap)**
*   **Phase 1: Validation & Design (Weeks 1–4) | Spend: $25k**
    *   *Action:* Finalize premium tier value prop; design push UI/UX.
    *   *Gate:* **Human Sign-off Required** on pricing hypothesis before any build starts.
    *   *Deliverable:* Beta test protocol for 12 churches.
*   **Phase 2: Core Build & Beta (Weeks 5–10) | Spend: $95k**
    *   *Action:* Develop MVP push engine ($80k dev allocation); integrate with beta group.
    *   *Gate:* **Human Sign-off Required** on beta security/privacy review before enabling notifications.
    *   *Deliverable:* Live beta with 12 churches; initial engagement data.
*   **Phase 3: Measurement & Go/No-Go (Weeks 11–12) | Spend: $30k**
    *   *Action:* Analyze beta data against 8% MAU / 2% churn targets; finalize stakeholder deck.
    *   *Decision:* Proceed to full launch, pivot pricing, or halt based on metrics.
    *   *Remaining Budget:* $0 (Contingency absorbed in design/dev estimates).

**2. Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Revenue Miss:** Premium adoption stays <1% after 30 days, failing to cover the $125k build cost within a reasonable ROI window.
2.  **Churn Backfire:** Poorly timed or irrelevant notifications increase opt-outs or uninstalls, worsening the very churn metric we aim to fix.
3.  **Scope Creep:** Custom requests from the 12 beta churches bleed into the core build, exhausting the $150k budget before general availability.

**3. Stop/Pivot Trigger**
*   **Hard Stop:** If beta data shows **<1% conversion to premium** OR a **net increase in churn** among the 12 beta churches after 4 weeks of active usage. Do not launch broadly; return to discovery.

**4. Hidden Risks & Costs**
*   **Platform Compliance:** Apple/Google push notification guideline violations could delay launch indefinitely (no budget allocated for legal/compliance review).
*   **Infrastructure Load:** A sudden spike in concurrent connections during a "blast" notification may exceed current server capacity, causing outages.
*   **Support Overhead:** No budget included for increased customer support tickets regarding notification settings or premium billing disputes.

**Note:** This plan assumes the $80k/$45k estimates are fixed; any variance requires immediate scope reduction. The pricing hypothesis is the single largest unknown and must be validated in Phase 1.
