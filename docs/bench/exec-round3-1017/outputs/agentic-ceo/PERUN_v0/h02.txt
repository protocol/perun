**Recommendation: Phased Launch Plan for Lantern Players**

**1. Immediate Actions (Weeks 1–2)**
*   **Secure Licensing:** Halt all marketing spend until licensing fees for "The Nightwatch" and "Midnight Revue" are confirmed in writing. This is the critical unknown; proceeding without it risks immediate budget overrun.
*   **Platform Verification:** Obtain written confirmation from the streaming vendor that simultaneous streams for five productions incur no extra fees beyond the standard 10% revenue share.
*   **Production Sprint:** Allocate the $45,000 production budget to finalize the first two shows only. Do not start production on the remaining three until the first two are live and licensing is settled.

**2. Deferrals**
*   **Full Marketing Push:** Defer the full $25,000 marketing spend. Release $5,000 now for pre-launch teasers; hold the remaining $20,000 until licensing is signed and the platform test is successful.
*   **Staff Overtime:** Freeze the $15,000 overtime budget until the first two shows are locked. Use existing staff hours for the initial launch prep.

**3. Required Human Sign-Offs (Gatekeepers)**
*   **Legal/Finance Director:** Must sign off on the final licensing contracts before any content is uploaded.
*   **Board Chair (Maya Patel):** Must approve the revised cash-flow projection showing the impact of the confirmed licensing fees before the marketing budget is unlocked.
*   **Technical Lead:** Must certify the platform's simultaneous stream capacity in a live test environment before go-live.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Licensing Cost Shock:** If "The Nightwatch" or "Midnight Revue" fees exceed $10,000 each, the $120,000 budget breaks, leaving no funds for marketing or staff overtime.
2.  **Subscriber Shortfall:** Falling below 2,000 subscribers in Month 1 yields only ~$10,000 revenue (vs. the $15,000/month target), creating a $5,000 monthly cash burn that depletes reserves by Month 3.
3.  **Platform Fee Misinterpretation:** If the 10% fee applies to gross revenue *before* payout adjustments or if there are hidden bandwidth costs, net revenue drops, extending the break-even point beyond the 6-month window.

**Stop/Change Course Trigger**
If subscriber count is **below 2,000 after Month 1**, immediately pause production on Shows 4 and 5. Reallocate the remaining production and marketing budget to a targeted retention campaign for existing subscribers. Do not spend further on acquisition until the conversion rate from trial to paid subscriber is understood. If Month 2 also falls below 2,000, cancel the remaining rollout and negotiate a refund or exit clause with the streaming platform.

*Note: All revenue projections assume the 10% platform fee is deducted from gross subscriber revenue; verify this calculation with Finance before presenting. The $90,000 six-month revenue estimate is only valid if the 3,000-subscriber target is hit by Month 2, not Month 6.*
