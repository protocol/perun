**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, conditional pilot ("Go-If"), not the full plan as written.**

Launch the July 15 stream only if you secure a written royalty cap at 7% of gross *before* marketing spend begins. Do not proceed with the current financial model; it is mathematically impossible and hides a $13,000+ deficit.

**Three Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Financial Collapse:** Your revenue math is flawed. 80% of 3,000 donors is 2,400 viewers, not 2,500. At $15/ticket, maximum gross is $36,000, not $40,000. With a $45,000 total budget ($12k platform + $8k marketing + $25k production), you are already $9,000 in the hole before paying playwright royalties (5–10% of $36k = $1,800–$3,600). The true deficit ranges from $10,800 to $12,600.
2.  **Rights Dispute:** Launching without a fixed royalty rate invites a post-revenue demand for 10%, worsening the deficit. If the agency audits gross vs. net definitions, legal costs could exceed the pilot's remaining budget.
3.  **Technical/Adoption Risk:** Assuming 80% donor turnout is aggressive for a first-time stream. If actual turnout is 50% (1,500 viewers), gross drops to $22,500, doubling the loss to ~$22,500+.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the pilot immediately if **net revenue after platform fees but before royalties falls below $13,000** (the break-even point to cover production and marketing). This must be measured after the first 48 hours of access; if tickets sold do not project to meet this threshold by Day 3, cancel the marketing push and refund pre-sales to minimize reputational damage.

**Hidden Costs & Flawed Assumptions:**
*   **The "Off" Math:** You acknowledged the math was off, but the error is structural, not rounding. You cannot present a $40k revenue figure against a $45k cost base to a board chair without explicitly framing this as a "loss-leader strategy" rather than a profitable pilot.
*   **Platform Fee Volatility:** The $12,000 estimate assumes no overage charges for bandwidth or concurrent viewers. If the stream spikes, variable costs could eat another 10–15% of gross.
*   **Donor Fatigue:** Assuming existing donors will pay $15 *on top* of their donations assumes no cannibalization of future giving. If donors feel they are "paying twice," renewal rates may drop post-pilot.

**Note:** Reframe the board presentation from "profitable pilot" to "strategic investment in digital reach with a hard loss cap of $13k."
**Note:** Secure the royalty rate in writing; a verbal range is insufficient for a fixed-budget project.
