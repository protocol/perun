**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped pilot ("Go-If"), not a full launch.**
Launch the tier to a maximum of 100 photographers (4% of base) for 90 days. Do not build the custom analytics dashboard yet; offer manual weekly reports instead. Proceed to full build only if the pilot achieves a 5% paid conversion rate from trial and maintains <3% churn in month 2.

**Figures & Arithmetic Check:**
Your revenue math is overstated. 8% of 2,500 photographers is 200 users. At $299/month, that equals $59,800 MRR, not $150,000. To reach $150,000 MRR, you need ~502 subscribers (20% conversion), which contradicts your 8% assumption. Additionally, a 12% lift on $2.2M quarterly bookings implies $264k in *new* booking volume; ensure your priority algorithm can actually drive this demand shift without cannibalizing existing paid placements.

**Failure Modes & Risks:**
1.  **Cannibalization:** Priority placement may simply reshuffle existing bookings rather than growing the total pool, inflating the "lift" metric while angering non-premium photographers who see their visibility drop.
2.  **Development Sink:** The $80k estimate likely excludes ongoing maintenance for the "custom" dashboard and the algorithmic tuning required to prevent gaming of the search results.
3.  **Value Mismatch:** Photographers may value immediate booking volume over analytics. If the trial doesn't demonstrate a direct revenue lift within 14 days, conversion to paid will likely collapse regardless of dashboard quality.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the initiative immediately if:
*   **Trial-to-Paid Conversion** is below 3% after 60 days.
*   **Net Revenue Retention** of the pilot group drops below 90% in the first renewal cycle.
*   **Total Platform Bookings** do not show a statistically significant increase (p<0.05) compared to the control group by Day 45, indicating pure cannibalization.

**Notes:**
The "custom analytics dashboard" is the highest risk for scope creep and lowest driver of immediate willingness-to-pay; defer it to validate core demand first.
