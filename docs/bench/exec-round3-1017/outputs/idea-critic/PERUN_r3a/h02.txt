2.  **Adoption Lag:** The assumption that all 12 hospitals will adopt within three months is overly optimistic; if adoption drags to 9–12 months, the realized ROI drops below the cost of capital before year 2 benefits materialize.
3.  **Data Quality Trap:** The rough $50K estimate for data cleansing is severely underestimated; if historical inventory data is fragmented or inconsistent, the predictive model produces inaccurate forecasts, leading to increased stockouts rather than reductions.
