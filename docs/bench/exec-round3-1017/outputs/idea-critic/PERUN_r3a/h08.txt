**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped 90-day pilot (Go-If), not a full 12-month commitment.**

The full build-out is a one-way door with unverified demand elasticity. Instead, convert only **one** room immediately for a 90-day term. This caps construction exposure at ~$125,000 (pro-rated) and validates the $45/day rate against actual startup uptake before committing the second room or the full furniture budget.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Demand Illusion:** The "pilot-only" niche is too small; occupancy stalls below 25 desks, failing to cover the $257,000 total capex within 18 months.
2.  **Cannibalization:** Startups downgrade from higher-margin standard memberships to the flexible rate, reducing net revenue despite higher desk count.
3.  **Timeline Mismatch:** Relying on the metro line (May 2025) creates a 6+ month valley of low foot traffic that burns cash before the projected 12% lift arrives.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the pilot and revert the space to standard use if **Day-45 average occupancy is below 18 desks** (40% of the 45-desk target) OR if **net new revenue** (excluding downgraded existing members) is less than **$15,000** in the first full month of operation.

*Note: The $520k revenue figure assumes 100% utilization of the 45 desks for 365 days; real-world variance on this single input swings the ROI from positive to negative.*
*Note: Verify current churn data before launch; if churn exceeds 5%, the flex model may accelerate member instability.*
