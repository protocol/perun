**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** The vague "around $20k" fee balloons to $50k+, destroying the Year 1 ROI and indicating hidden technical debt in your payroll system.
2.  **Adoption Friction:** The 30% time savings assumes perfect user adoption; if hiring managers resist the new interface, manual workarounds will persist, leaving you with high software costs and unchanged labor hours.
3.  **Error Value Mismatch:** The $120k error-reduction benefit assumes every prevented error equals a full re-hire cost; if most errors are minor fixes worth only $50 each, the actual financial gain will be negligible, failing to justify the capital outlay.

**Kill Criterion:** If first-quarter onboarding time does not drop at least **10% compared to baseline** by the end of Q1, immediately halt further rollout phases and revert to the manual workflow while negotiating an exit or remediation plan.
