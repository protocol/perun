In enterprise security, a 30–40% attach rate is typical for add-ons; expecting 80% ignores budget fatigue and competing priorities, potentially leaving you with $5M+ in stranded capacity and marketing spend.
2.  **Regulatory Bottleneck:** The "unknown" duration for EU-GDPR content creation is a critical path risk. If this delays the pilot launch by even 8 weeks, you burn $100k+ in fixed costs before generating a single dollar of the projected $750k, skewing the Year 1 ROI negative.
3.  **Adoption vs. Churn Mismatch:** You target 30% user adoption within pilot accounts but forecast 15% account churn. If users don't adopt the tool (missing the 30% target), the perceived value drops, likely driving account churn well above 15%, eroding the recurring revenue base needed to justify the $500k development cost.
