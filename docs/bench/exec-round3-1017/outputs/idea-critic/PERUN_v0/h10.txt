3.  **Notification Fatigue:** Assuming a 25% open rate based on industry averages is dangerous; if your specific customer base treats push notifications as noise (common in food service), the "weekly specials" engine fails, and retention lifts never materialize.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the project if, after 6 months post-launch, **monthly active users (MAU) who redeem points fall below 8% of the total downloaded base**, or if the **cost per acquired returning visit exceeds $4.00** (calculated as total app spend divided by incremental visits).
