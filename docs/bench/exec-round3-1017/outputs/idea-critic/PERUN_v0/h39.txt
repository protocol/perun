**Recommendation: Go-Forward (Conditional)**
Proceed with the Cedar City satellite only if a binding letter of intent securing ≥75% of projected reimbursement rates is obtained by March 1, 2025, and regulatory pre-approval is confirmed by April 1, 2025. Without these, the risk of a three-month delay combined with unverified pricing renders the $1.225M outlay unsafe.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Reimbursement Shortfall:** If actual negotiated rates average below $155 per visit (vs. the $180 assumption), gross revenue drops to ~$1.86M, likely failing to cover the high fixed costs of three full-time therapists and the build-out amortization within the first 18 months.
2.  **Regulatory & Timeline Slip:** A three-month delay pushes operations to October 2025, burning ~$187,500 in fixed salaries and overhead with zero revenue, while potentially missing the Q4 seasonal demand peak critical for hitting the 12,000-visit target.
3.  **Utilization Gap:** Achieving 80% therapist utilization requires ~160 visits per week immediately upon opening; if ramp-up takes six months instead of three due to slow provider referrals or churn exceeding 5%, cash burn will exceed the $200k/month safety threshold before positive cash flow is reached.

**Walk-Away Trigger**
Terminate the project if, by September 30, 2025 (three months post-target launch), cumulative patient visits are below 2,400 (averaging <20 visits/day) OR if confirmed insurance contracts cover less than 65% of the local market population required to sustain the $180 average revenue assumption.

*Note: The current math assumes immediate full utilization which is historically rare for new clinics; a phased hiring plan for the three PTs is strongly advised to match actual patient ramp-up. The $50k marketing spend must be tied directly to verified referral source activation, not general brand awareness, to ensure immediate volume.*
