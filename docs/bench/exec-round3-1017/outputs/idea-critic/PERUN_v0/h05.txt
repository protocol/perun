**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the rollout only if you first secure a binding commitment from Engineering that the current ad server requires **zero** additional bandwidth or latency overhead to handle dynamic tier logic, and if you establish a hard kill switch triggered by **>8% churn** among affected podcasters or a **<10% net revenue lift** after 90 days. Without verifying the infrastructure constraint, the $120k engineering estimate is likely a floor, not a ceiling.

**Primary Failure Modes & Risks**
1.  **Infrastructure Hidden Costs:** Your assumption that the ad server handles complexity "without extra bandwidth" is the weakest link. Dynamic CPM requires real-time decisioning per impression. If this forces a database lookup or external API call per request, latency will spike, causing ad timeouts. This could lower effective fill rates, negating the projected 20% lift and actually reducing total revenue despite higher CPMs.
2.  **Churn Underestimation:** You flagged 15% of podcasters dropping to the $20 tier. If churn exceeds 8% (not just the total 15% affected, but 8% of your *entire* base leaving), the loss of long-tail inventory will degrade network density, making the platform less attractive to premium advertisers and creating a negative feedback loop.
3.  **The "Average" Trap:** With an average of 12,000 downloads, a significant portion of your 3,200 podcasts likely sits just above 10,000. If the new pricing incentivizes creators to artificially suppress growth or split shows to stay in lower tax brackets (gaming the system), your inventory mix shifts downward, capping upside potential.
4.  **Engineering Scope Creep:** The $120k estimate assumes a clean implementation. If the ad server requires significant refactoring to support dynamic tiers, costs could double, pushing the break-even point beyond the six-month horizon and consuming budget needed for other growth initiatives.

**Kill Criteria (Tripwires)**
Execute the plan, but terminate immediately if either of these occurs:
*   **Churn Trigger:** Net loss of **>8% of total active podcasts** (approx. 256 shows) within the first 90 days post-rollout, specifically driven by dissatisfaction with the new tier placement.
*   **Revenue Trigger:** Net quarterly revenue increase is **<10%** (i.e., less than $150k gain over the $1.5M baseline) after three full months of operation, accounting for all engineering overruns and churn losses.

**Leading Metric to Watch**
Monitor **Effective CPM (eCPM) vs. Fill Rate Correlation** daily. If eCPM rises but Fill Rate drops by more than 5% simultaneously, the complexity is causing ad delivery failures, and the model is destroying value.
