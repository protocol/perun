**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Pilot, Not a Full Launch.**
Do not commit all 12 boats for June 1. Instead, launch a 45-day pilot on **two** boats starting May 15, 2025. Commit to the full fleet rollout only if the pilot achieves a **60% occupancy rate** and maintains a **gross margin above 55%** after variable costs. This approach validates the unknown occupancy demand and exposes hidden operational frictions (fuel/crew) without risking the full $20k video spend or 12-boat capacity on an unproven time slot.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **The "Empty Seat" Margin Trap:** Your $150k revenue projection assumes 100% seat occupancy per boat. If sunset charters run at 40% seat occupancy (common for premium niche slots), revenue halves while fuel and crew costs remain fixed, potentially turning a projected profit into a loss per trip.
2.  **Crew Overtime & Retention Shock:** Sunset slots inherently trigger overtime pay and disrupt standard crew rotations. If you cannot secure reliable part-time captains willing to work irregular summer evenings at $4,500/month, you may face last-minute cancellations or burnout among full-time staff, degrading the "premium" experience.
3.  **Marketing Efficiency Decay:** The assumption that the existing $30k budget absorbs this launch without dilution is risky. If "Sunset Charter" requires a distinct customer acquisition channel, you may cannibalize bookings from your core high-margin products, resulting in net-zero growth despite the 25% booking volume increase.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the expansion immediately if, after the 45-day pilot: (1) Average occupancy per trip is **below 55%**, or (2) Total variable costs (fuel + overtime) exceed **35% of gross revenue** for the pilot group.

*Note: Recalculate your $150k projection using a conservative 60% seat-fill rate rather than total booked hours to stress-test cash flow. Ensure the $20k video production is contractually tied to pilot success metrics before spending.*
