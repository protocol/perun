**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the pilot only if the Hotel Alliance team provides written confirmation by **October 15, 2025**, that the $150 surcharge is treated as a direct guest amenity fee (100% margin to Seaglass) and does not trigger a recalculation of base room commissions. Without this, do not build.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Partner Risk (Critical):** If hotels classify the upgrade as a room-rate increase rather than an add-on, commission payouts could rise by 10–25% on the surcharge amount, instantly erasing the $120 projected profit margin per upgrade and potentially making the feature net-negative.
2.  **Conversion Cannibalization:** The assumption that lead time and conversion remain static is untested. Introducing a high-friction monetary decision at the final confirmation step may cause cart abandonment, reducing total booking volume enough to offset the 12% value lift.
3.  **Financial Miscalculation:** The $250k build cost assumes a 3-month timeline. If integration with the Property Management Systems (PMS) of Miami/Portland partners requires custom middleware not currently accounted for, costs could double before launch.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Criteria**
Terminate the project immediately if either of the following occurs during the first 30 days of the pilot (Nov 1–30, 2025):
*   **Metric Failure:** The net uplift in Average Booking Value (ABV) is **< 5%** after accounting for any increased commission payouts.
*   **Volume Failure:** Overall booking conversion rate drops by **> 1.5%** compared to the control group, indicating the offer is causing checkout friction.
