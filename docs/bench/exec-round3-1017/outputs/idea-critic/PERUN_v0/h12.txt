**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with CareSync only if a binding addendum caps total Year-1 hidden costs (training + compliance) at $15,000 and verifies 85% caregiver device compatibility before signing. Do not sign the current quote.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Hidden Cost Explosion:** The vendor's "no extra charge" support likely excludes regulatory compliance configuration (HIPAA/GDPR). If third-party audit fees or data residency setups exceed $5,000, the projected $30k annual savings evaporates, turning the ROI negative for over two years.
2.  **Device & Adoption Fragmentation:** Your 80% smartphone assumption is unverified. If 20–30% of caregivers lack compatible devices or data plans, the organization faces an unplanned $20,000+ hardware procurement bill and a bifurcated workflow (paper vs. digital) that increases, rather than reduces, administrative overtime.
3.  **Implementation Timeline Slip:** A fixed July 1, 2025 end-date for a three-month implementation is aggressive for 40 staff members. If training delays push go-live into Q3, you lose the summer efficiency window while paying full license fees, creating immediate cash-flow strain without operational relief.

**Kill Criteria & Triggers**
*   **Compliance Cost Trigger:** If the written estimate for regulatory compliance setup exceeds **$5,000**, halt signing and redesign the scope to exclude cloud storage or renegotiate the vendor fee.
*   **Adoption Trigger:** If the pilot phase (first 30 days) shows less than **70%** of shifts logged digitally (not 60%, due to network effects), suspend the rollout and revert to paper for non-compliant teams.
*   **Training Budget Trigger:** If internal training hours required exceed **120 hours** (approx. $6,000 in lost care time + trainer fees), freeze the project to simplify the workflow before continuing.
