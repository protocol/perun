**Recommendation: Do not move forward with the full launch yet.** Proceed immediately to a 4-week priced beta test with 5,000 users to validate willingness-to-pay before committing the $230,000+ in capital. Your current arithmetic contains a critical error that masks a likely net loss in the first year.

**Critical Arithmetic & Assumption Errors**
1.  **Revenue Miscalculation:** You calculated $57,600 for six months, but $9.99 × 9,600 users (8% of 120k) equals **$95,904 per month**. Over six months, conversion revenue alone is **~$575,424**, not $57,600. While this improves the top line, it does not guarantee profit.
2.  **Churn Ignored:** You applied an 8% conversion rate as a static block. With a 5% monthly churn rate, you will lose ~480 premium subscribers every month unless you constantly re-convert free users. Your net subscriber growth is likely overstated by 30–40% in the first year.
3.  **Hidden Licensing Cliff:** You estimated $200,000 for licensing but treated it as a one-time budget item. Premium content usually requires recurring royalties (e.g., 15–30% of premium revenue). If this is a recurring cost, your margin evaporates. If it is one-time, you have not budgeted for *ongoing* content acquisition to keep premium users from churning.
4.  **Partnership Risk:** The 5,000 new users/month from IndieWave is a gross addition. If those users churn at the standard 5% rate and cost you a revenue share, the net value may not cover the integration and support costs.

**How This Plan Could Fail**
*   **Cannibalization:** Existing users who would have eventually converted organically might slip into the new tier early at a discount you didn't model, or free users might simply leave if the free tier is degraded to force upgrades.
*   **Content Cost Overrun:** If premium users demand more content faster than anticipated, licensing fees could scale linearly with revenue, capping your upside.
*   **Platform Instability:** The $150,000 platform upgrade is a fixed cost estimate for a variable engineering challenge. Delays here push back revenue recognition while burn continues.

**Pre-Agreed Stop/Pivot Condition**
**"If the paid beta test (5,000 users) fails to achieve a 4% conversion rate at $9.99/month with a monthly churn rate below 3% after 30 days, halt the full launch and pivot to a lower-priced 'ad-free' tier at $4.99."**

This condition tests both price sensitivity and content retention before you risk the full $230,000+ investment.
