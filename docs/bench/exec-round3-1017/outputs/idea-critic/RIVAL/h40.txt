Do not sign the final agreement until the payroll integration cost is firm, as variable integration fees are the highest risk for budget overruns in HR tech implementations.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Integration Cost Escalation:** The "around $20k" fee balloons to $40k+ due to legacy payroll system incompatibilities, destroying the Year 1 ROI.
2.  **Adoption Friction:** The 30% admin time reduction assumes immediate proficiency; if HR staff struggle with the new interface, productivity may drop initially, delaying breakeven.
3.  **Error Reduction Mismatch:** The projected 40% error reduction (valued at $120k) relies on the platform catching errors the current manual process misses; if most current errors are human data-entry mistakes not prevented by software, the financial benefit will be significantly lower.

**Pivot Trigger Metric:** If **average onboarding cycle time per hire** does not decrease by **10% within the first 90 days** of full deployment compared to the pre-implementation baseline, initiate a process audit to determine if the issue is software limitation or training deficiency. If the audit confirms software limitation, pause further rollout and renegotiate support terms.
