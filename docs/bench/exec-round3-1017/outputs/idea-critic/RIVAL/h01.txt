**Recommendation: Proceed with a Phased Pilot, Not a Full Rollout**

Do not commit to the full $135,000 outlay yet. The undisclosed API licensing fee is a critical variable that could destroy your unit economics. Instead, negotiate a 90-day pilot with TapTech using a single 12-tap module ($45k hardware + ~$5k install). This caps your initial risk at ~$50k while validating the 8% sales lift and app adoption assumptions. If the pilot hits your metrics, exercise the option to expand; if not, you salvage the remaining capital.

**Top Three Failure Modes**

1.  **Hidden Cost Structure:** The undisclosed API fee could be a per-pour tax or a high monthly SaaS cost. If the fee exceeds $0.20 per pour, your projected $0.75 margin uplift evaporates, turning volume into lost profit.
2.  **Friction-Induced Abandonment:** Self-serve requires a behavior change. If the app download, registration, and payment linking take longer than 45 seconds, customers will revert to asking staff for drinks, leaving the expensive taps idle while you still pay for the hardware.
3.  **Regulatory & Liability Gaps:** Self-serve alcohol faces strict local scrutiny regarding age verification and intoxication monitoring. If the system cannot legally guarantee a cut-off when a patron is visibly intoxicated (a hard technical challenge), you risk severe fines or license suspension that outweighs any revenue gain.

**Kill Switch Triggers**

Your proposed triggers are sound but need one financial guardrail. Execute the kill switch and revert to staffed service if, after six months of operation:
*   App adoption is under 10% of weekly patrons; **OR**
*   Incremental weekly revenue is less than $2,000; **OR**
*   **New:** The fully loaded cost per pour (including the revealed API fee) exceeds 60% of the sale price.

**Stress Test Verdict**
Your break-even math assumes the $135k is the only cost. However, an 8% lift on $45k weekly sales is $3,600/week. At a $0.75 margin uplift, you need 4,800 additional pours weekly just to cover the *opportunity cost* of the capital, ignoring the unknown API fee. If the API fee is significant, your 12-month payback period likely extends to 18–24 months. The pilot approach is the only way to validate the "unknown" before betting the full amount.

*Note: Ensure your legal counsel reviews local self-serve alcohol statutes before signing even the pilot agreement.*
