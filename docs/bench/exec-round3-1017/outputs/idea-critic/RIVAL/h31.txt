**Recommendation: Do not push forward on 15 June.**
Pause for a 4-week "Financial & Technical Validation" sprint. The current plan relies on a critical unknown (licensing costs) and a fragile revenue assumption (doubling revenue solely via session time). Without a capped licensing contract and a validated correlation between session time and revenue, you risk launching a feature that scales losses rather than profits.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Financial (The "2% Trap"):** If "2% of monthly active-user revenue" refers to *total* company revenue rather than *feature-attributed* revenue, your cost basis is uncapped and potentially catastrophic. If it applies only to the new revenue, the math holds, but the definition is ambiguous. At $1M quarterly revenue, a 2% cut of the total pool is $20k/month ($240k/year), which may be acceptable, but if the user base grows to 50k, this becomes a massive line item unrelated to actual feature usage.
*   **Technical (Latency & Cost):** Real-time speech-to-text for 12,000 new users/month implies heavy concurrent load. If the engine averages >500ms latency, the "coach" feels broken, destroying adoption. If the vendor charges by audio-second rather than a flat fee, high-engagement users could make the unit economics negative.
*   **Adoption (The Session Time Fallacy):** Assuming session time jumping from 7 to 10 minutes automatically doubles revenue is dangerous. If users spend 3 extra minutes struggling with the tool or listening to bad advice, churn could spike above your 4.8% baseline, netting a revenue loss despite higher engagement.

**Kill/Pivot Triggers**
1.  **Financial:** Vendor contract cannot cap licensing fees at **$15,000/month** (1.5% of projected new revenue) or define "revenue" strictly as "feature-generated upsell."
2.  **Technical:** Beta testing shows average inference latency **>400ms** or error rates **>5%** in noisy environments.
3.  **Adoption:** Post-launch **Day-30 retention** for feature users is lower than non-feature users, or **NPS < 45** (not 60; 60 is elite, 45 is solid for beta).

**Hidden Costs & Flawed Assumptions**
*   **Flawed Revenue Math:** You assume revenue doubles because session time increases ~43%. This implies monetization is perfectly linear with time, which is rarely true. Are you selling ads? Subscriptions? If subscriptions, why does longer usage trigger payment? You need a specific monetization mechanic (e.g., "Premium Coach Tier") rather than hoping time equals money.
*   **Hidden Integration Debt:** The $45k fee likely covers only "happy path" integration. Error handling, fallback modes when the API fails, and data privacy compliance (GDPR/CCPA for voice data) often double initial dev costs.
*   **Support Overhead:** Voice features generate unique support tickets ("it didn't hear me," "it transcribed wrong"). Budget for a 20% increase in customer support volume for the first 3 months.
