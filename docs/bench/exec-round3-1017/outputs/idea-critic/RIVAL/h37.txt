**Three Biggest Failure Modes**

1.  **Data Sparsity & Cold Start:** With only 12,000 sellers and niche textbook inventory, the AI may lack sufficient interaction data to generate accurate recommendations, leading to irrelevant suggestions that decrease rather than increase conversion.
2.  **Margin Erosion:** The $20,000/month hosting cost plus unknown licensing fees could consume 40–60% of the *gross* revenue gain. If the model drives sales of low-margin books or requires expensive compute for real-time inference, the net profit impact could be negligible or negative.
3.  **Implementation Lag:** Integrating AI into an existing marketplace often reveals technical debt. If the six-month timeline slips to nine or twelve months due to integration complexity, the cost overrun could exceed the projected annual gain before the feature ever launches.
