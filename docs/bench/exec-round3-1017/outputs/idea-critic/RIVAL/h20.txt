**Recommendation: Do not move forward to build yet.** Proceed only to a 2-week "paper pilot" to validate the hotel commission model and true conversion impact before writing code.

**Why:** The unit economics are currently fragile. At $150 revenue per upgrade, you need just a **$30 increase in partner commissions** (20% of the surcharge) to erase the margin needed to cover your $30k/month ongoing API fees at current volumes. Building a $250k feature without knowing if the hotel alliance will treat this $150 as "room revenue" (standard ~15% commission) or "ancillary service" (often 0-5% commission) is a financial gamble, not a product strategy. Furthermore, assuming a 14-day lead time allows for a "final confirmation" upsell without friction is untested; introducing price anxiety at the very last step often increases cart abandonment, potentially negating the 12% booking value lift.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Partner/Financial Risk (Critical):** The hotel alliance classifies the $150 as taxable room revenue, triggering a 15-20% commission ($22.50–$30) that destroys the contribution margin required to sustain the $30k monthly API overhead.
2.  **Conversion Friction:** Displaying a paid upsell at the "final confirmation" step creates price shock, causing a >2% drop in overall booking completion rates, which costs more in lost base revenue than the upgrade generates.
3.  **Technical/Inventory Sync:** The "instant" nature requires real-time inventory locking. If the API latency exceeds 2 seconds or fails to lock the specific balcony suite, the user pays for an upgrade they cannot receive, leading to immediate customer service escalations and brand damage.

**Pre-agreed Kill Conditions:**
Terminate the project immediately if **any** of the following occur during the pilot or paper validation:
*   **Commission Structure:** The hotel alliance confirms the $150 surcharge incurs a commission rate **greater than 10%** ($15 per upgrade).
*   **Conversion Impact:** Overall booking completion rates drop by **more than 0.5%** compared to the control group.
*   **Uplift Performance:** The attach rate for upgrades is **below 4%** (generating less than $6,000/month in gross surplus in Miami alone) after 30 days of live traffic.
*   **Operational Load:** Customer support tickets related to upgrade failures exceed **0.1% of total bookings** in the first two weeks.

**Note:** Run the "paper pilot" by manually offering the upgrade via email/call center to 500 recent bookings in Miami to test price sensitivity without engineering cost. Secure the commission ruling in writing before allocating engineering resources.
