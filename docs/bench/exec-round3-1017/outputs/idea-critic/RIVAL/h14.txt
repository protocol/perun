**Recommendation: Proceed with a Controlled Pilot, Not a Full Launch**

Do not roll out to all 12,000 subscribers immediately. The current projection assumes a **$0.00 impact** from third-party delivery fees, yet you flagged a potential **$0.30/bag increase**. If this fee materializes, it adds **$8,640 annually** in hidden costs (2,400 users × 24 bags × $0.30), slashing your projected net profit by over 10% before accounting for any churn spike. Furthermore, assuming churn remains flat at 15% while doubling fulfillment frequency is a high-risk bet; operational friction usually drives early attrition in frequency upgrades.

Launch a **4-week pilot** to 500 users (approx. 4% of base) starting August 1st. This validates the true delivery fee structure and measures real-world churn impact before committing the full $5,000 marketing budget or risking the core subscription base.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes**

1.  **The "Hidden Fee" Erosion:** The third-party carrier classifies the new packaging as "oversized" or "special handling," triggering the $0.30/bag surcharge. This turns a margin expansion play into a margin-neutral or negative exercise, especially if volume discounts don't apply to the new SKU.
2.  **Frequency-Induced Churn:** Subscribers receiving coffee every two weeks feel overwhelmed by inventory or cash-flow strain compared to monthly deliveries. If churn spikes from 15% to 20% among this tier, the lifetime value (LTV) collapses, making the acquisition cost impossible to recover.
3.  **Operational Bottleneck:** Doubling the roast and pack cycle creates a logistical bottleneck that delays shipments for the *original* monthly subscribers. Collateral damage to the core 9,600 users would threaten the primary revenue stream ($86k/month) to chase an incremental $19k.

**Pre-Agreed Kill/Pivot Conditions**

Trigger an immediate pause and re-evaluation if **either** of the following occurs during the pilot:

*   **Churn Threshold:** First-month churn for the new tier exceeds **18%** (a 20% relative increase over baseline).
*   **Cost Threshold:** Confirmed delivery fees rise above **$0.20 per bag** (capturing 2/3rds of your feared $0.30 risk).

If either triggers, pivot to a "Monthly Premium" blend upgrade instead of a frequency change, or renegotiate carrier contracts before resuming.
