**Recommendation: Proceed with a Phased Pilot Launch**

Move forward with the flex-lab conversion, but structure it as a 6-month validated pilot rather than a permanent immediate commitment. The projected net gain of approximately $263,000 in Year 1 ($520k revenue minus $257k total costs) represents a strong return on investment, provided the 45-desk occupancy target is met. The upcoming metro line opening provides a tangible catalyst for the required foot traffic increase, de-risking the demand assumption slightly. However, given the unverified elasticity of "pilot-only" memberships, you must treat the initial rollout as an experiment to validate pricing and uptake before scaling operations or signing long-term leases for the space.

**Primary Failure Modes**

1.  **Demand Elasticity Miss:** The core risk is overestimating the market for "pilot-only" memberships. If startups prefer standard terms or the price point ($45/day) is above their willingness to pay, occupancy will stall below the 45-desk breakeven threshold.
2.  **Cannibalization:** Existing members may downgrade from higher-margin standard plans to the new flexible pilot rate, eroding the net incremental revenue. This is particularly likely if the "flex" offering is not strictly gated to new startup entities.
3.  **Cost Overruns & Timing:** Construction delays could push the launch past the May 15 metro opening, causing you to miss the initial 12% foot traffic surge. Additionally, any increase in the 5% churn rate during the transition would disproportionately impact cash flow in a high-fixed-cost remodel scenario.

**Kill Trigger**

Establish a hard stop clause based on Month 2 performance (allowing Month 1 for stabilization): **If net revenue in Month 2 falls below $17,300 (40% of the monthly pro-rated forecast of ~$43,300), immediately halt marketing spend and revert the space to standard hot-desk usage.** This threshold accounts for a slow ramp-up while protecting against sustained low utilization that would make the $257k sunk cost unrecoverable.

*Note: Ensure the "pilot-only" designation includes strict eligibility criteria to prevent current members from downgrading.*
