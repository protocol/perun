The proposal attempts to shift a proven, cash‑generating licensing model to an untested direct‑to‑consumer (DTC) business. The company currently earns “billions” from licensing its library and theatrical releases; that revenue stream is both stable and highly profitable. Removing its content from rival platforms would immediately sacrifice that cash flow, creating a large financing gap that must be covered by the projected multi‑year operating loss.

A low‑priced subscription offering could attract price‑sensitive viewers, yet the market leader already commands well over 100 million subscribers. The target of “tens of millions” within five years represents a modest share of the total market, but achieving it would require rapid subscriber acquisition, aggressive marketing spend, and a compelling user experience—areas where the company has no operational history. Building and maintaining a streaming technology platform involves significant expertise in content delivery networks, recommendation algorithms, device integration, and ongoing security and compliance. Failure in any of these domains would erode user trust and accelerate churn.

The strategic upside—greater control over distribution, data, and long‑term brand equity—must be weighed against the immediate loss of licensing cash, the uncertainty of subscriber uptake, and the risk of cannibalising existing cable and theatrical revenue streams. Those legacy businesses could be further weakened if audiences migrate to the new service before the company can offset the loss with DTC earnings.

Given the scale of cash burn required, the lack of internal streaming capabilities, and the high probability that licensing revenue and legacy channels will decline before the service reaches scale, the proposal carries a material risk of net negative cash flow and impaired profitability. A more prudent approach would be to pilot a smaller‑scale DTC offering, retain key licensing deals, and build technology capability incrementally before committing to a full‑scale launch.

**Recommendation:** Hold the current plan, revise the financial assumptions, and develop a phased implementation strategy that preserves licensing income while testing the streaming platform.
