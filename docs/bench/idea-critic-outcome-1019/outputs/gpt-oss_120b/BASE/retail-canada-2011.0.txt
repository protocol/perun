The retailer’s brand strength and the opportunity to acquire ready‑made leases give it a clear cost advantage for an initial foothold. However, the proposal couples three high‑impact uncertainties:

1. **Speed of rollout** – Opening 130‑150 stores in two years means commissioning, staffing, and launching roughly one store per week. That tempo leaves little margin for learning curves in local labor practices, permitting, and store‑fit standards. Even a modest 5 % delay per site could cascade into a multi‑month schedule slip, inflating launch costs and eroding the first‑year profit outlook.

2. **Unproven supply‑chain and inventory system** – The new logistics network and inventory‑management platform have not been field‑tested at the required scale. Cross‑border freight, customs clearance, and bilingual labeling add layers of complexity that can trigger stock‑outs or excess inventory, directly harming the discount model’s razor‑thin margins.

3. **Price perception vs. entrenched competitor** – The local market already carries higher price points on many categories, but the dominant rival enjoys deep brand loyalty and a pervasive store network. If the newcomer cannot consistently undercut prices—especially on the higher‑priced items—consumers may default to the familiar incumbent, limiting traffic and sales velocity.

A more prudent path would be a phased entry: secure a representative sample of 20‑30 lease sites, launch them over 12‑18 months, and use the results to validate the supply chain, refine pricing tactics, and fine‑tune regulatory compliance. Successful pilots could then fund a staggered expansion, preserving capital and reducing operational risk.

Given the current scale, timeline, and system uncertainties, the proposal as written exposes the company to significant execution risk that could outweigh the strategic upside of a rapid market entry.

**Recommendation:** Hold the current plan and require a revised, phased rollout with pilot validation before committing to the full 130‑150‑store launch.

DECISION: NO-GO
