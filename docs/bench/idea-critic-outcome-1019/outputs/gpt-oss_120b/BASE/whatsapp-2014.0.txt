The acquisition offers a strategic foothold in a market the buyer fears could erode its core services, but the economics and execution risks are extreme.

**Valuation gap** – The target was valued at $1.5 bn a year ago; the proposed $19 bn price is more than twelve times that figure. Even with a “large user base,” the premium cannot be justified without a clear path to monetize the hundreds of millions of monthly users.

**Revenue and monetisation** – Current revenue is negligible (a $1‑year subscription) and the company has explicitly refused advertising, eliminating the most common scale‑economy model for messaging platforms. The buyer has no disclosed plan to introduce new revenue streams, leaving the purchase price largely speculative.

**Regulatory exposure** – Messaging apps are increasingly subject to privacy and data‑protection regulations (e.g., GDPR, emerging US state laws). Integrating a platform that has not demonstrated compliance could expose the buyer to fines, litigation, and reputational damage, especially given the buyer’s existing global footprint.

**Founder and talent risk** – With only ~50 employees, the target’s ongoing innovation and product roadmap are tightly coupled to its founders. Retention after a $19 bn cash‑stock deal is uncertain; loss of key talent could rapidly erode the platform’s value.

**Strategic fit vs. financial discipline** – While the acquisition would be the buyer’s largest ever and could pre‑empt a competitive threat, the size of the deal would dominate the buyer’s balance sheet and likely strain cash flow and shareholder returns. A more measured approach—such as a minority stake, partnership, or phased acquisition contingent on meeting monetisation milestones—would preserve strategic insight while limiting exposure.

**Conclusion** – The strategic rationale does not outweigh the valuation disparity, lack of a proven business model, regulatory uncertainty, and talent‑retention risk. Approving the deal as proposed would commit the buyer to a high‑cost, low‑certainty investment.
