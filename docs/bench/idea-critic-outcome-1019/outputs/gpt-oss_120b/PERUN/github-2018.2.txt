**Steelman claim:** Acquire the code‑hosting platform for $7.5 B in stock, keep it operationally independent, and leverage its market position to accelerate the buyer’s open‑source strategy despite community distrust, leadership vacuum, and regulatory exposure.

**Skeptic attacks**  
- **Community trust:** Assumes independence will neutralise distrust, yet no evidence that a former hostile owner can regain credibility; history shows similar acquisitions (e.g., Microsoft‑GitHub) required extensive outreach and product‑level guarantees.  
- **Financial justification:** Target is loss‑making; a $7.5 B price implies a valuation far above current earnings. Without a clear path to profitability, the deal risks a negative‑EV acquisition.  
- **Regulatory/antitrust:** Dominant code‑hosting service plus a major platform vendor creates a “two‑sided market” concentration that regulators have flagged in similar contexts (e.g., Google‑Fitbit). No assessment of likely remedies is provided.  
- **Leadership vacuum:** No permanent CEO for months suggests governance instability that could exacerbate integration risk.

**Better‑way alternatives**  
1. **Strategic partnership or minority stake** – lower cash outlay, preserves community perception, and allows joint road‑mapping without full ownership.  
2. **Build an in‑house alternative** – leverage existing developer tools to create a competing service, mitigating antitrust concerns and aligning culture from the start.  
3. **Acquisition of a smaller, profitable niche platform** – provides a foothold with less market concentration.

**Kill‑criteria** (must be met before proceeding)  
- **Defection threshold:** If >15 % of active developers migrate within 6 months, abort.  
- **Regulatory block:** If any antitrust authority issues a “non‑consistent” ruling, abort.  
- **Valuation cap:** Deal price must not exceed 4× projected 3‑year EBITDA; current numbers breach this.

**Next concrete actions**  
- Commission an independent antitrust risk assessment.  
- Conduct a quantitative developer‑sentiment survey to estimate defection risk.  
- Build a detailed 3‑year financial model comparing acquisition vs partnership.

Given the unverified assumptions, high valuation, and significant regulatory exposure, the proposal does not satisfy the required kill‑criteria.

DECISION: NO-GO
