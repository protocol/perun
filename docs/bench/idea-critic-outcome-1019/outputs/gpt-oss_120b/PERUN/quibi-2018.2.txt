**Steelman**: Launch a $1 B+ mobile‑only subscription service delivering professionally produced, sub‑10‑minute episodes with premium talent, using a UI that adapts seamlessly to portrait or landscape orientation.

**Skeptic attacks**  
1. **Demand uncertainty** – No market evidence that viewers will pay for short‑form premium content; existing free short video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts) satisfies the appetite at zero cost.  
2. **Price elasticity** – Premium short‑form pricing must compete with longer‑form services (Netflix, Disney+) and with ad‑supported free tiers; a $10‑$15/mo fee may appear unjustified for <10‑minute episodes.  
3. **Attention scarcity** – Mobile users have <5 minutes of discretionary time per session; multiple apps vie for that slot, raising churn risk.  
4. **Cost vs. scale** – High production budgets and A‑list talent demand subscriber base >30 M to break even; reaching that scale without an existing brand is unproven.

**Better‑way alternatives**  
- **Pilot with limited budget**: Produce a small slate (5–10 titles) at modest cost, test pricing on a niche audience, and iterate.  
- **Leverage existing studio platforms**: Bundle short‑form premium content as an add‑on to current streaming services, reducing acquisition cost and using established subscriber bases.  
- **Hybrid ad‑supported model**: Start with a free tier supported by limited ads, converting engaged users to a paid tier only after proving value.

**Kill‑criteria (pre‑mortem)**  
- If average monthly active users (MAU) < 5 M after 12 months, abort.  
- If churn > 15 % quarterly after launch, cease further investment.  
- If cost‑per‑acquired‑subscriber (CAC) exceeds $30, stop scaling.  
- If the adaptive‑orientation feature adds > 15 % to engineering budget without measurable engagement lift, drop it.

**Concrete failure scenario**: Launch with 12 M projected subscribers at $12 /mo, but actual uptake stalls at 2 M, yielding $24 M monthly revenue versus $120 M needed to cover production and marketing. The shortfall forces cash burn > $200 M in year 1, triggering investor pull‑back and service shutdown.

**Recommendation**: The proposal carries high financial and market risk without validated demand. Proceed only after a low‑cost pilot validates willingness to pay for premium short‑form content and demonstrates sufficient engagement to meet the kill‑criteria thresholds.

DECISION: NO-GO
