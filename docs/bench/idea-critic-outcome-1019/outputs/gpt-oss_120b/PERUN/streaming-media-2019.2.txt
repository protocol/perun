**Steelman:** Launch a low‑price, loss‑making subscription service, pulling all content from rivals, to capture tens of millions of subscribers within five years and eventually replace licensing, cable and theatrical revenue streams.

**Skeptic attacks**
- **Revenue loss:** The company currently earns billions annually from licensing. Removing that income creates a cash‑flow gap that must be covered entirely by venture funding or debt, increasing financial risk.
- **Cash‑burn feasibility:** Funding a multi‑year operating loss at scale (hundreds of millions per year) requires deep reserves or sustained capital markets access. No data on balance‑sheet strength is provided.
- **Technology execution:** Building a streaming platform from scratch entails massive engineering, security, and scaling challenges that the firm has never managed; delays or outages would erode brand trust.
- **Cannibalisation:** Pulling content harms existing cable and theatrical partners, potentially triggering contract penalties and accelerating churn in those higher‑margin businesses.

**Better‑way alternatives**
1. **Hybrid model:** Retain licensing while co‑developing a platform with an experienced tech partner, reducing upfront loss and tech risk.
2. **Phased rollout:** Start with a premium tier of exclusive content, then add a low‑price tier once subscriber base and platform stability are proven.
3. **Strategic partnership:** License the platform to an existing streamer, sharing risk and capital.

**Kill‑criteria**
- Cash‑burn exceeds 15 % of cash reserves in any fiscal year.
- Subscriber acquisition lags 20 % behind the 2‑year target (≈2 M subs) after launch.
- Platform launch delayed >12 months beyond the announced date.

**Dissent ledger:** The proposal underestimates cash‑flow impact, over‑relies on aggressive subscriber growth, and ignores proven alternatives that mitigate risk.

**Remaining risk:** Even with mitigations, market saturation and entrenched competitors make the target ambitious; early‑stage losses could threaten solvency.

DECISION: NO-GO
