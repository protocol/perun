The proposal aims to revive a struggling brand by launching a hybrid console that costs as much as far more powerful rivals, relies on a modest‑power mobile chip, and offers only a thin first‑year game slate from internal studios.  

**Key concerns**  
1. **Hardware competitiveness** – A modest‑power chip will be out‑performed by existing consoles, making the product unattractive to core gamers and difficult to justify the price.  
2. **Brand confusion** – The previous home console’s poor launch already damaged consumer confidence; a hybrid with ambiguous positioning risks repeating that failure.  
3. **Third‑party ecosystem** – Without strong hardware specs or a compelling developer program, third‑party support will be minimal, limiting the game library and long‑term sales.  
4. **Supply chain and timing** – A short window to recover financial losses leaves little margin for production delays or inventory shortfalls.  
5. **Revenue upside** – Relying on a thin internal lineup offers limited differentiation; rivals can easily out‑produce and out‑price the offering.

**Potential mitigations** – Secure a stronger SoC, lower the price to match the hardware value, commit to a robust multi‑year third‑party support plan, and stagger the launch to allow a richer game pipeline.

Given the high risk of repeating past missteps and the limited upside under current specs, the proposal should not be approved in its present form.

DECISION: NO-GO
