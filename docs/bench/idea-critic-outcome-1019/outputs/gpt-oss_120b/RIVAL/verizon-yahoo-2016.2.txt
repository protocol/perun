The carrier’s ambition to create a mobile‑first media and advertising platform that can rival the two dominant online ad giants hinges on two fragile pillars:

1. **Target weakness** – The portal’s traffic and ad revenue have been on a multiyear decline, and it has suffered repeated management turnover. Those trends indicate a product that is losing relevance with users and advertisers. Buying a business that is already shrinking offers limited upside unless the carrier can reverse the decline quickly.

2. **Carrier inexperience** – The carrier’s core competence is network operations, not consumer‑facing media. It has no track record of building, selling, or scaling ad technology, audience data, or editorial content. The learning curve for acquiring and operating a media stack is steep, and the carrier will need to develop new talent, processes, and culture.

**Risks** – The proposal calls out ad‑revenue decline and integration as the main concerns. Both are severe: a falling revenue base reduces cash flow to fund the integration, while the cultural and technical integration of a legacy media company with a declining portal is likely to be costly and disruptive. Without proven expertise, the carrier may underestimate the resources required to stabilize the target and to build the necessary sales and programmatic infrastructure.

**Strategic fit** – While the goal of a mobile‑scaled ad business is attractive, the path to scale is unclear. Competing with entrenched platforms will require not just size, but differentiated data, inventory, and technology—assets the carrier currently lacks.

**Recommendation** – Given the target’s deteriorating fundamentals and the carrier’s lack of relevant experience, the acquisition presents a high‑risk bet with uncertain return. The carrier should either seek a healthier, more synergistic target or first develop internal media capabilities before committing $4.8 billion to a declining portal.

**Conclusion** – Approve a pause for further analysis and alternative sourcing rather than proceeding as proposed.
