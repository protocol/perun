The proposal aims to revive a struggling brand with a hybrid console that mirrors competitors’ form‑factor but sacrifices performance, pricing it competitively while relying on a thin first‑year game slate from internal studios. This creates three core concerns:

1. **Hardware‑vs‑Value Gap** – A modest‑power mobile chip will struggle to meet the graphical and frame‑rate expectations set by rival consoles. Early adopters will compare specs directly, likely perceiving the product as a downgrade rather than a complementary device.

2. **Brand Confusion** – The previous home console’s poor launch already damaged brand equity. Introducing another “hybrid” without a clear differentiation strategy risks repeating that confusion and alienating both core gamers and casual handheld users.

3. **Ecosystem & Supply Risks** – Limited third‑party support reduces the library depth that drives console adoption. Coupled with tight supply constraints and a short recovery window, the product may fail to achieve the sales momentum needed to offset the initial loss.

To make the plan viable, the team should consider:
- Upgrading the SoC or offering a scalable performance tier.
- Defining a distinct value proposition (e.g., exclusive motion‑controlled experiences or seamless cross‑play) that separates the hybrid from existing rivals.
- Securing early commitments from external developers and establishing a robust launch window with staggered releases.
- Extending the timeline to allow hardware validation, supply chain stabilization, and a richer first‑year catalog.

Given the current risk profile and insufficient mitigation, the proposal should be revised before proceeding.
