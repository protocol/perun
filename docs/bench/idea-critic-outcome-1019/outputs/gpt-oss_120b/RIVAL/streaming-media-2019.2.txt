**Executive Summary**  
The company’s core business now generates billions by licensing its content to rivals and by theatrical releases. The proposal pivots to a low‑price, subscriber‑based streaming service, funded by a multi‑year operating loss while it builds an audience of “tens of millions” in five years. The market leader already serves >100 M subscribers.

**Key Risks**  

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Needed |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Loss of licensing revenue | Immediate cash‑flow hit; the company forfeits a proven, high‑margin income stream. | Secure alternative revenue (e.g., premium tiers, ad‑supported tier) before pull‑back. |
| Cash burn to fund loss | Multi‑year negative EBITDA strains balance‑sheet and may limit other investments. | Clear runway analysis, capital infusion plan, and staged loss limits. |
| Technology platform execution | No prior experience building, operating, or scaling a streaming stack (CDN, DRM, recommendation engine). | Partner with an established platform provider or acquire a proven tech team. |
| Cannibalisation of cable/theatrical revenue | Pulling content from rivals could reduce ancillary revenue and weaken relationships with distributors. | Phase‑in pull‑back, retain some licensing windows, or bundle with premium cable bundles. |
| Competitive scale gap | Targeting “tens of millions” vs >100 M for the incumbent suggests a long ramp‑up and high churn risk. | Aggressive content exclusivity, original productions, and clear differentiation. |

**Strategic Fit**  
A direct‑to‑consumer service aligns with industry trends (e.g., Disney+, HBO Max) and could capture higher margin subscriber revenue. However, the proposal omits concrete mitigation for the four high‑impact risks, especially the loss of a proven licensing cash‑flow and the technology deficit.

**Recommendation**  
Approve only after a detailed mitigation plan is presented: (1) phased licensing withdrawal, (2) partnership or acquisition for platform technology, (3) financial runway with staged loss limits, and (4) a content‑exclusivity roadmap to justify subscriber acquisition targets.

**Decision**  
DECISION: NO-GO
