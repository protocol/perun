There is no publicly available or industry-standard data point for the "average discount percentage" that Estonian SaaS vendors specifically offer to public universities on three-year contracts. This level of granular, region-specific, and sector-specific pricing data is proprietary, negotiated on a case-by-case basis, and not aggregated in any public report.

**Strategy for Your Email:**
Since a specific number does not exist, providing a fabricated figure would be risky. Instead, frame your response around **global SaaS benchmarks for multi-year public sector deals** and the **strategic levers** available.

**Recommended Response Approach:**
1.  **State the Reality:** Clarify that Estonian-specific averages are not published.
2.  **Provide Global Benchmarks:** Standard SaaS discounts for 3-year commitments typically range from **15% to 25%** off the monthly list price. For public sector/education, vendors often add an additional **5% to 10%** "mission-based" or "education" discount on top of the multi-year commitment.
3.  **Total Potential Discount:** A aggressive but defensible total discount for a 3-year public university deal often lands between **20% and 30%** annually, usually contingent on upfront payment or strict payment terms.
4.  **Contextual Factors:** Remind your boss that the final number depends on:
    *   **Deal Size:** Larger ACV (Annual Contract Value) commands higher discounts.
    *   **Payment Terms:** Upfront annual payment vs. quarterly invoicing significantly impacts the discount depth.
    *   **Strategic Value:** Is the university a reference account? If yes, discounts may be deeper.

**Drafting the Email Line:**
Instead of a fake number, use a range based on global norms:
*"While specific Estonian market data is proprietary, global SaaS benchmarks for 3-year public sector contracts typically yield total discounts between 20-30% off list price, combining multi-year commitment and education pricing."*

**Why this data is missing:**
*   **Privacy:** B2B contracts are confidential.
*   **Fragmentation:** The Estonian SaaS market is small; sample sizes for 3-year university deals are too low for statistical averages.
*   **Negotiation Dynamics:** Public procurement laws in Estonia (and the EU) often dictate tender processes where price is just one factor, making "standard discounts" less relevant than "best value" scoring.

**Actionable Advice:**
If you need a concrete number for a specific negotiation, analyze your own company's **marginal cost of service**. If your marginal cost to serve an additional university user is near zero, you have room to push toward the 30% range to secure the long-term commitment. If you have high implementation costs, stay closer to 15-20%.
