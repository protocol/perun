**The Numbers**
The formula for Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is:
$$ \text{CAC} = \frac{\text{Total Sales \& Marketing Spend}}{\text{Number of New Customers Acquired}} $$

Since you requested a specific input for a model without providing your own data, I cannot compute your specific CAC. Industry benchmarks for B2B fintech vary wildly based on sales motion (inside sales vs. enterprise field sales).
