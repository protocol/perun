**The call:** I cannot state a specific 2031 TAM dollar figure or CAGR for AI dental imaging in Southeast Asia because no verified, public dataset exists with that granularity; presenting a fabricated number would be misleading to investors. Instead, the defensible approach is to build a bottom-up model using current dental clinic counts, imaging adoption rates, and estimated software ARPU, clearly labeling every assumption.

**The numbers:**
The formula for a bottom-up TAM is:
$$TAM = (\text{Target Clinics}_{2031}) \times (\text{Penetration Rate}) \times (\text{Annual ARPU})$$

Since specific 2031 inputs are unavailable, we must bracket them based on current proxies (marked **ASSUMED** for illustration only):

*   **Target Clinics:** Southeast Asia has ~60k–80k dental clinics today. Assuming a 3% annual growth in formalized clinics:
    *   *Low:* 90,000 clinics
    *   *Base:* 110,000 clinics (**ASSUMED**)
    *   *High:* 130,000 clinics
*   **Penetration Rate:** % of clinics adopting AI imaging software by 2031.
    *   *Low:* 15% (regulatory lag)
    *   *Base:* 35% (**ASSUMED**, based on mature market S-curve lag)
    *   *High:* 55%
*   **Annual ARPU:** Price per clinic/year.
    *   *Low:* $1,200
    *   *Base:* $2,500 (**ASSUMED**, tiered pricing)
    *   *High:* $4,000

*Illustrative Result (Base Case):* $110,000 \times 35\% \times \$2,500 = \mathbf{\$96.25M}$.
*Range:* ~$16M (Low) to ~$286M (High).

**CAGR Derivation:**
CAGR cannot be stated without a confirmed 2024 baseline. If we **ASSUME** a 2024 TAM of $5M (early adopter phase):
$$CAGR = (\frac{96.25}{5})^{\frac{1}{7}} - 1 \approx 52\%$$
*Note:* This 52% is purely illustrative based on the assumed base; changing the 2024 baseline to $10M drops CAGR to ~38%.

**What flips it, and the trigger:**
The **Penetration Rate** is the primary variable. Regulatory approval timelines in key markets (Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam) will dictate this.
*   *Trigger:* If any major SEA market delays AI medical device clearance beyond 2027, revise the Penetration Rate down by 40% and extend the timeline.
