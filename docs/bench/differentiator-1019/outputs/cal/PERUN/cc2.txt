**Context on the Decoy Effect.**
The "decoy effect" (asymmetric dominance) is well-documented in general behavioral economics. The seminal works are:
*   Huber, J., Payne, J. W., & Puto, C. (1982). Adding Asymmetrically Dominated Alternatives: Violations of Regularity and the Similarity Hypothesis. *Journal of Consumer Research*.
*   Ariely, D. (2008). *Predictably Irrational*. (Popularized the concept for business audiences).
