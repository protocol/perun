**Steelman:** A mandatory client solves critical update/cheat failures and creates a high-margin platform moat, outweighing short-term friction.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Status quo guarantees worsening piracy and support costs as games age; retail dependence limits margin.
*   **Optional Patcher:** Fails to stop offline cheating or capture platform value; fragmented user base.
*   **Broadband Constraint (2003):** Valid but transient. Dial-up users are a shrinking segment; the core demographic (PC gamers) leads adoption. Mitigation: LAN caching at retailers, small delta updates.
*   **Retail Backlash:** High risk of short-term shelf loss. However, digital distribution eliminates manufacturing/shipping costs, offsetting volume drops. The "box" becomes a license key carrier, not the product itself.
*   **Competitor Copying:** First-mover advantage in user library lock-in is the defensible asset, not the code.

**Pre-mortem:** The initiative fails if the client is unstable at launch (confirming user bias against online requirements) or if broadband adoption stalls unexpectedly. If the client crashes or blocks play, trust evaporates instantly.

**Fit:** Aligns with the north star of recurring revenue and IP protection. The "store" vision transforms a cost center (support) into a profit center.

**Blockers:** None. The risks (adoption, retail pushback) are execution challenges, not fundamental flaws. The cost of delay is high: every month without anti-cheat erodes the multiplayer community; every month without digital sales cedes ground to emerging competitors.

**Kill Criteria:**
1.  **Metric:** Client crash rate > 2% of sessions in first 30 days.
2.  **Action:** If triggered, revert to optional patcher for 90 days while stabilizing.
3.  **Leading Metric:** % of active users running the latest version within 48 hours of release (Target: >80% by Q4).

The proposal addresses an existential threat (cheating/obsolescence) with a scalable solution. The friction is the feature that ensures security and ecosystem control.

DECISION: GO
