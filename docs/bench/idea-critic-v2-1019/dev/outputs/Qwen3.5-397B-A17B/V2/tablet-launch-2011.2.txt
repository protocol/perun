Steelman: Leveraging vertical integration of hardware and a newly acquired OS to create a differentiated ecosystem, using marketing to bridge the initial app gap.

**Blockers:** NONE. The proposal contains no internal mathematical contradictions or factual impossibilities. The risks cited (small app catalog, low OS share, leadership transition) are market conditions, not logical blockers preventing execution.

**Risks:** High. The "chicken-and-egg" problem is severe: users want apps, developers want users. Heavy ad spend may burn cash without retaining users if the app gap remains wide. Leadership transition introduces execution volatility. Competitor dominance creates a high barrier to entry.

**Better Ways:** A lower price point could offset the app deficit, but the proposal explicitly targets price parity with the leader, betting entirely on brand and marketing rather than cost leadership. Doing nothing avoids capital risk but cedes the mobile form factor to competitors.

**Fit:** Assumes the company's north star is ecosystem control rather than short-term unit profitability. If the goal is immediate market share via price, this fails. If the goal is long-term platform ownership, the heavy investment is consistent.

**Pre-mortem:** The device launches, early adopters buy it for the brand, but churn is high because key apps are missing. Developers ignore outreach because active install bases remain too low to monetize. The product is perceived as a premium hardware shell with a hollow soul, discontinued in two years.

**Decision Logic:** This is a two-way door (product can be iterated or discontinued), but the financial commitment is massive. However, in the context of 2011, the window to establish a third ecosystem is closing rapidly. Waiting for a larger app catalog guarantees irrelevance. The only path to success is a "field of dreams" launch: build it, market it aggressively, and force the ecosystem into existence. The risks are inherent to the strategy, not flaws in the plan's construction. The plan addresses the primary weakness (apps) with the only viable lever available at launch (developer outreach + ads).

DECISION: GO
