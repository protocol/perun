**Steelman:** A hybrid device leveraging first-party IP can redefine the market rather than compete on raw power, creating a unique value proposition that phones and high-end consoles cannot match.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
The primary blocker hypothesis is that "modest power" prevents third-party support and sales. However, history shows unique gameplay (motion, portability) often outweighs fidelity for mass markets. The alternative "do nothing" guarantees continued erosion by mobile devices. A "high-power only" console repeats the previous failure mode of high cost and niche appeal. A "handheld only" strategy cedes the living room. The hybrid approach is the only path that attacks both erosion vectors simultaneously.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
This fits a survival strategy where differentiation is the only moat. If this fails, it will be due to supply constraints or a lack of compelling launch software, not specs. The "confusing positioning" risk is real but mitigated by the physical clarity of "dock vs. handheld" compared to abstract marketing messages of the past.

**Evidence & Constraints:**
The proposal relies on the unverified assumption that first-party studios can deliver a "thin but sufficient" lineup immediately. If they cannot, the hardware has no reason to exist. However, the pricing parity with powerful rivals is a massive advantage if the experience is distinct. The risk of weak specs is accepted as a trade-off for battery life and form factor.

**Blockers vs. Risks:**
*Blockers:* NONE. No fact presented proves the plan fails as proposed; weak specs are a feature, not a bug, in this specific context.
*Risks:* Third-party abandonment, supply chain failure, software delays. These are execution risks, not fundamental flaws.

**Decision:**
The plan beats "do nothing" and avoids the traps of previous strategies. It requires a capped, time-boxed execution focus on software readiness. The potential upside of creating a new category outweighs the risk of competing on specs where the company has already lost.

DECISION: GO
