**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only viable channel to demonstrate the integrated hardware-software music strategy, as third-party staff lack the incentive or expertise to sell a premium, loss-making niche product.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
The proposal faces severe blockers: high fixed lease costs during a recession, historically poor industry retail margins, and immediate channel conflict with existing resellers who may retaliate. The "do nothing" alternative preserves cash but guarantees continued margin erosion and brand dilution in current channels. A "shop-in-shop" concession model within existing department stores was not evaluated; this could offer demonstration capabilities with lower fixed costs and reduced channel hostility. However, given the specific need to control the full customer experience for a complex new strategy, partial measures may fail to achieve the necessary brand rehabilitation.

**Pre-mortem:**
Assume failure in 18 months: The recession deepens, mall foot traffic collapses, and the high fixed costs of prime leases bleed cash faster than the stores can generate profit. Resellers drop the brand in retaliation, leaving the company with no volume channel and unsustainable fixed overhead.

**Decision Logic:**
The risks are substantial and the industry track record is negative. However, the core claim rests on a unique strategic necessity: the "digital-music strategy" requires an environment that third-party retailers cannot provide. If the product differentiation relies entirely on an experience that cannot be sold through existing channels, the high cost of retail is not an expense but a prerequisite for survival. The "recession" argument is valid but static; if the current trajectory is losses due to an inability to differentiate, staying the course is also a path to failure. The proposal lacks a specific cap on initial rollout or a defined kill criterion for the pilot, which is a procedural gap, but the strategic imperative overrides the financial caution in this specific context of turning around a differentiated product.

**Kill Criteria:**
Success requires a pilot of no more than 5 stores with a strict 12-month cash-burn cap. If same-store sales do not exceed the operating cost of the lease plus inventory carrying costs within 12 months, the program must halt.

DECISION: GO
