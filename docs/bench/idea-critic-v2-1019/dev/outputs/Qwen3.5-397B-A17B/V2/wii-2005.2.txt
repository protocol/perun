The claim is that a third-place console maker can win by abandoning the graphics arms race to target non-gamers with low-cost, motion-controlled hardware. Steelman: By redefining the market from "processing power" to "accessibility," the proposal creates a blue ocean where existing rivals' strengths (HD fidelity, hardcore libraries) become irrelevant liabilities due to high cost and complexity.

Alternatives considered:
1.  **Do nothing:** Continue fighting for third place in the saturated hardcore market; likely financial decline given current trajectory.
2.  **Match rivals:** Attempt to build HD hardware; high risk of failure due to late entry, higher costs, and inability to secure exclusive content against market leaders.
3.  **Hybrid approach:** Release two consoles; dilutes focus and confuses the value proposition.

The "do nothing" option guarantees continued irrelevance. The proposed path is the only one offering a distinct value proposition.

Pre-mortem: The console fails if third-party publishers refuse to support the unusual architecture, leaving a content drought, or if the "toy" perception prevents adoption by the very casuals it targets. However, the low price point ($50–$100 less than rivals) mitigates the "toy" risk by lowering the barrier to entry for gift-givers. The lack of HD is a feature, not a bug, for the target demographic who may not own HD TVs.

Blockers: None. The risks cited (skepticism, untested audience) are inherent to disruptive innovation, not factual contradictions. The hardware margin is thin, but the strategy relies on volume and software attachment rates, a proven model if the audience expands. The "untested" nature of the casual market is the opportunity, not a blocker; competitors are ignoring this segment entirely.

Risks: Third-party resistance and brand perception. These are managed by first-party software launch titles and aggressive pricing. The cost of delay is catastrophic, as rivals will eventually lower prices or simplify interfaces, closing the window.

DECISION: GO
