**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only viable channel to demonstrate the integrated hardware-software music strategy, as third-party staff lack the incentive or expertise to sell a premium, loss-making niche product.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
The proposal faces severe blockers: high fixed lease costs during a recession, historically poor industry retail margins, and immediate channel conflict with existing resellers who may retaliate by deprioritizing the brand. The "do nothing" alternative preserves cash but guarantees continued margin erosion and brand invisibility in stores. A "shop-in-shop" concession model within existing retailers was not evaluated; this cheaper, lower-risk alternative could provide demonstration units without the burden of full leases, inventory ownership, or staffing.

**Pre-mortem:**
Assume failure in 18 months: The recession deepens, mall foot traffic collapses, and the high fixed costs of empty stores accelerate bankruptcy before the music strategy gains traction. The resellers, feeling undercut, drop the brand entirely.

**Evidence & Fit:**
The claim relies on the assumption that third-party staff *cannot* be trained or incentivized to demonstrate the music strategy. No evidence is provided that a concession model would fail to achieve the same demonstration goals at 20% of the cost. The proposal treats "retail store" as the binary solution to a "demonstration" problem, ignoring the middle ground.

**Blockers:**
1. **Unverified Necessity:** The proposal asserts full stores are required for demonstration without proving that lighter-touch, lower-cost retail formats (concessions, kiosks) cannot achieve the same strategic goal.
2. **Financial Mismatch:** Launching a high-fixed-cost physical expansion while reporting recent losses and facing a recession contradicts basic capital preservation principles unless the ROI is proven superior to all alternatives, which it is not.

**Risks:**
Recessionary demand shock, reseller channel war, inventory obsolescence, and brand dilution if stores feel empty.

**Decision:**
The plan fails the "cheaper alternative" test. Until a shop-in-shop or kiosk pilot proves insufficient for the music strategy demonstration, committing to prime mall leases is financially reckless.

DECISION: NO-GO
