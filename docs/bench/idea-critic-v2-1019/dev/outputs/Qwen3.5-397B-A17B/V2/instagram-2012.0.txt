**Steelman:** Acquire the leading mobile-native photo network immediately to neutralize a existential threat to the buyer's mobile relevance and secure the primary user-generated content pipeline for the next decade, accepting that financial metrics are irrelevant compared to strategic survival.

**Attack & Options:**
The valuation ($1B for 13 staff, $0 revenue) is indefensible on standalone financials; it fails every traditional ROI test. However, the "do nothing" option carries catastrophic risk: if this app becomes the default camera for the dominant mobile OS, the buyer's core advertising business becomes obsolete. Competitors could acquire it instead. Building a rival internally has already failed (the buyer's "weak mobile product").

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
This fits the north star of "connecting the world" only if the buyer cannot dictate mobile photo sharing organically. If we wait for regulatory approval or a business model, the app's growth may plateau, or a competitor may intervene. Conversely, if we buy and destroy the culture, the product dies, and we waste $1B. The cost of delay is likely total loss of mobile photo dominance.

**Blocker Test:**
*Blockers:* None. The lack of revenue is a feature of the strategy (buy growth/defense), not a bug. Regulatory risk is present but manageable given the timeframe; the deal must close before the IPO locks in the app's independence. Cultural risk is real but mitigable by keeping the team autonomous.
*Risks:* Overpayment if mobile trends shift; regulatory block; cultural integration failure. These are risks of execution, not blockers to the thesis that the asset is strategically vital.

**Decision:**
The proposal is a defensive monopoly play disguised as an acquisition. While the price is astronomically high relative to current output, the cost of the competitor owning this distribution channel is infinite. The "no business model" argument is irrelevant; the business model is the buyer's existing ad engine fed by this new data stream. The only path to secure the mobile future is immediate acquisition.

DECISION: GO
