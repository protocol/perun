**Steelman:** Acquiring the portal's core business for $4.8B creates immediate scale in mobile media, theoretically enabling the carrier to challenge the two dominant ad platforms by combining assets with its recent legacy purchase.

**Attack:** The claim relies on the assumption that combining two declining, non-core assets generates growth. The target suffers from years of falling traffic and revenue, repeated management instability, and lacks a turnaround plan. The acquirer (a wireless carrier) has zero proven history in consumer media operations. Integrating a second distressed asset into a recently acquired legacy company multiplies execution risk rather than diluting it. The "scale" achieved is merely scale in decline.

**Alternatives:**
1.  **Do Nothing:** Preserve $4.8B capital; avoid integrating a culture of failure.
2.  **Organic Build:** Invest in internal mobile capabilities where the carrier has domain expertise.
3.  **Targeted Acquisition:** Buy a specialized, growing ad-tech firm instead of a dying general portal.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:** This contradicts the likely north star of sustainable profitability, chasing a "me-too" scale play in a winner-take-most market. *Pre-mortem:* One year post-close, the combined entity misses revenue targets by 20% as legacy traffic erodes faster than synergies are realized. Key talent flees due to cultural clashes between telco and media teams. The $4.8B is written down as goodwill impairment.

**Evidence & Logic:** The proposal admits the target is declining and the buyer is inexperienced. No evidence suggests the carrier can reverse the target's trajectory or that the sum of two struggling parts exceeds the whole. The cost of delay is low; the cost of a failed $4.8B integration is catastrophic.

**Blocker:** The acquirer lacks the core competency (consumer media management) required to execute the turnaround, and the target's fundamental metrics (traffic/revenue) are structurally negative. Buying decline does not create a competitive platform against dominant, growing incumbents.

**Kill Criteria:** If the combined mobile ad revenue does not grow quarter-over-quarter within 12 months, divest immediately.

**Verdict:** The plan buys scale in a dying category without the operational skill to fix it. It is a capital destruction event disguised as a strategic pivot.

DECISION: NO-GO
