**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only viable channel to demonstrate a complex product and integrated music strategy when third-party retailers actively undermine the brand due to misaligned incentives.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
The proposal fails the "cheaper alternative" test. Before committing to high-fixed-cost leases in prime malls during a recession, the company must validate demand and the "experience" hypothesis via low-capital methods:
1.  **Shop-in-shop kiosks** within existing department stores (lower rent, existing foot traffic).
2.  **Temporary pop-up locations** to test specific markets without long-term lease liability.
3.  **Enhanced reseller training/incentives** to align third-party staff behavior.

The "do nothing" option (fixing the current channel) is safer given the industry's poor retail track record. The proposal treats a strategic necessity (demonstration) as synonymous with a specific, high-risk execution method (owned real estate).

**Pre-mortem:**
Assume failure: The company signs 10-year leases just as the recession deepens. Foot traffic drops, but rent remains fixed. Inventory becomes obsolete before sale. Resellers, feeling threatened, drop the brand entirely. The cash burn from stores accelerates bankruptcy rather than preventing it. The "digital music strategy" fails to gain traction because the hardware volume collapses under the weight of retail overhead.

**Fit & Evidence:**
The proposal cites "management argues" but offers no data proving that third-party staff cannot be retrained or that customers will travel to mall locations specifically for this brand. The assumption that high lease costs are justified by margin recovery is unproven; low-margin retail combined with high fixed costs is a lethal combination for a company already running losses.

**Decision Logic:**
This is a one-way door with massive irreversible costs (leases). The risks (recession, channel conflict, inventory) are immediate and quantifiable, while the benefits are theoretical. A cheaper, reversible alternative (kiosks/pop-ups) exists that tests the core hypothesis without betting the company's remaining cash on real estate. The proposal lacks a capped, time-boxed pilot; it jumps straight to a full chain.

**Kill Criteria Missing:**
There is no defined metric for store success before scaling, nor a pivot plan if the first location misses targets.

DECISION: NO-GO
