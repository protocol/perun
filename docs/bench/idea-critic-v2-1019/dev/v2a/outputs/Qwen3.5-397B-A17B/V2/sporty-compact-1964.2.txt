**Steelman:** Leverage existing compact mechanics to rapidly deliver a stylish, affordable sports car for emerging young buyers, maximizing margin via options and scale via a massive launch.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
1.  **Unproven Demand:** The risk of zero volume is real. However, the "do nothing" alternative cedes the emerging youth segment to competitors. A clean-sheet sports car is too costly and slow. The proposed path is the cheapest viable test of this hypothesis.
2.  **Image Contamination:** Sharing parts with a "dull" economy car risks brand dilution. Yet, the distinct styling (long hood, short deck) and customizable options list directly counter the "cheap" perception, targeting buyers who value style over pedigree.
3.  **Financial Exposure:** Heavy upfront ad spend is dangerous without sales data. But in consumer goods, visibility drives trial. A muted launch guarantees obscurity; a bold one creates a cultural moment. The low base price lowers the barrier to entry, mitigating the financial risk per unit.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:** This aligns with capturing a new demographic (North Star). If it fails, it will be because the styling missed the mark or the economy association was too strong, not because the price was wrong. It is a two-way door: production can be halted if initial sales lag, limiting losses to the marketing spend and tooling adjustments.

**Evidence & Logic:** The logic rests on a demographic shift (baby boomers aging) and a gap in the market (no sporty low-price options). The mechanics are proven (reliable), reducing technical risk. The options list shifts margin risk to the consumer's willingness to pay for extras.

**Decision:**
There is no factual blocker. The risks are executional, not structural. The alternative (waiting for perfect data) ensures missed opportunity. The proposal bets on a clear demographic trend with a capped downside (existing parts) and high upside (new segment creation).

**Kill Criteria:** If first-year sales do not cover the launch marketing cost by 20%, halt further ad spend and reduce production volume immediately. Monitor the attachment rate of high-margin options as the leading metric for profitability.

DECISION: GO
