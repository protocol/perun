**Verdict: HOLD (Internal Recommendation to Executive Team)**

**Claim:** Acquiring the declining portal for $4.8B will create a scaled mobile media competitor to dominant ad platforms.

**Steelman:** Combining two legacy media assets under a wireless carrier's umbrella creates sufficient scale and data synergy to pivot from declining desktop traffic to a viable mobile advertising duopoly challenger.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The proposal fails the "better-way" and "fit" tests immediately. The strategy relies on merging two assets with proven, multi-year declining trajectories in traffic and revenue. There is no evidence that combining two failing models yields a successful one; rather, it compounds integration risk with revenue erosion. The acquiring carrier has zero history in consumer media, suggesting a high probability of cultural clash and operational mismanagement, evidenced by the target's own repeated leadership churn.

The "do nothing" or "cheaper alternative" option is stark: $4.8 billion represents a massive capital outlay for a shrinking market share. A cheaper path would involve organic mobile development or acquiring smaller, growing mobile-native entities rather than a legacy portal in freefall. The cost of delay is negligible compared to the risk of immediate capital destruction; the ad market is shifting to programmatic and walled gardens, not legacy portal aggregations.

**Pre-mortem:** One year post-acquisition, the combined entity misses revenue targets by 20% due to faster-than-expected traffic decay. Integration costs balloon as the carrier attempts to impose telecom processes on media operations. The $4.8B purchase price is written down as goodwill impairment.

**Kill Criteria:** The proposal lacks a specific metric proving that traffic decline can be reversed post-merger. Without a verified mechanism to stop the bleeding of the core asset, the ROI calculation is negative by default.

**Strongest Attack Survived:** The only potential defense is data synergy from the wireless carrier. However, without a proven track record of monetizing that data in media (which the prompt states they lack), this remains an unproven assumption, not a strategy.

**Decision:** The risks of integrating a declining asset into an inexperienced organization, at a premium price, outweigh the speculative benefit of scale. The plan does not beat "do nothing" or a targeted smaller acquisition.

DECISION: NO-GO
