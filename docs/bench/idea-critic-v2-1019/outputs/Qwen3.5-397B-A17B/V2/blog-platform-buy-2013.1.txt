**Blockers:** NONE. The high price ($1.1B) against low revenue is a strategic premium for market position, not a mathematical failure of the core claim. Integration struggles and founder retention are execution risks, not proof the acquisition itself fails to deliver the audience. **Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the parent company forces immediate heavy monetization, triggering a user revolt similar to previous platform migrations. The "independent" clause is the critical safeguard.

Leading metric: Daily Active Users (DAU) retention rate among 13–24 demographic, reviewed quarterly.

The proposal addresses the core strategic deficit (lack of youth relevance) more effectively than any alternative. The risks are manageable through the proposed independence clause and do not constitute blockers to the decision itself. The cost is high, but the cost of obsolescence is higher.
