**Steelman:** Launching in May 1995 secures a critical four-month head start to establish market presence and developer mindshare before the competitor arrives, justifying a limited, high-price "soft launch" to manage supply constraints.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The proposal fails the blocker test on three verified facts provided:
1.  **Developer Readiness:** The machine is "hard to program for 3D," and developers "did not plan for the early date." A console without games is a non-starter; launching with "few games" when the core value proposition (3D) is difficult to realize guarantees a poor user experience that cannot be patched post-launch.
2.  **Channel Alienation:** Shipping "only to a few retailers" while excluding others invites immediate retaliation from major partners who will prioritize the competitor's full launch in September. In a new market entry, distribution leverage is more vital than early units sold.
3.  **Price Disadvantage:** At $399, the unit is $100 (33%) more expensive than the rival. Without a robust library to justify the premium, early adopters will wait four months for the cheaper, better-supported alternative.

**Alternatives:**
*   *Do Nothing (Wait for September):* Launches alongside the rival but with full retail support, a complete game lineup, and competitive pricing parity.
*   *Staged Announcement:* Announce in May to claim "first mover" marketing rights but ship in September. This captures the headline without the operational disaster of shipping an incomplete product.

**Pre-mortem:**
If launched in May, reviews will cite the lack of games and high price. Retailers excluded from the initial batch will refuse prominent placement in September. Developers, rushed by the date change, will deliver broken or shallow titles, cementing a reputation for poor quality. The four-month lead becomes a four-month period of negative brand equity.

**Blockers:**
*   **Fact:** Developers are unprepared and the hardware is difficult to program.
*   **Fact:** Price is significantly higher than the competitor with no content justification.
*   **Fact:** Partial distribution guarantees channel conflict.

**Risks:**
*   Competitor dominates the holiday season if we wait.
*   Marketing momentum is lost without an early physical presence.

**Verdict:** The lack of software and developer readiness constitutes a fundamental product failure that no amount of early availability can overcome. The channel retaliation risk threatens the entire US launch.

DECISION: NO-GO
