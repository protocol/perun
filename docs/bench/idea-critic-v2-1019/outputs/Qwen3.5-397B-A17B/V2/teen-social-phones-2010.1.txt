**Steelman:** Launching two carrier-bound, data-plan-dependent social phones without apps or games leverages an acquired team's momentum to capture the youth market before the main platform arrives.

**Attack:** The proposal fails the "fit" and "options" tests immediately. The target demographic is explicitly price-sensitive, yet the cost of entry (device + full data plan) equals a standard smartphone, which offers superior value (apps, games, utility). Removing apps and games—the primary drivers of youth engagement—while charging a premium price creates a product with negative perceived value.

**Better Way:** The "do nothing" option is superior. Waiting for the main platform launch later in the year allows the company to integrate social features into a competitive, app-enabled device, avoiding the cost of supporting a fragmented, doomed hardware line. Alternatively, launching a software-only social layer on existing cheap devices would test the market without hardware risk.

**Pre-mortem:** Assume failure. Why? Parents refuse to pay smartphone rates for a "dumb" social phone. Kids reject a device with no games or third-party apps. The carrier demands subsidies the company cannot afford due to low volume. Internal conflict between the core and acquired teams escalates as sales miss targets, distracting from the main platform launch.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Economic Mismatch:** Price-sensitive users cannot/will not pay smartphone data rates for a limited device.
2.  **Value Deficit:** No app store and no games remove the primary utility for the target segment.
3.  **Cannibalization/Timing:** Launching a weak product shortly before a superior main platform confuses the market and dilutes brand equity.

**Risks:** Internal team conflict; carrier relationship strain; inventory write-offs.

**Conclusion:** The plan requires users to pay more for less functionality in a segment driven by trends and cost. It contradicts basic market logic for the stated demographic. No amount of execution excellence can fix a value proposition that is negative by design.

DECISION: NO-GO
