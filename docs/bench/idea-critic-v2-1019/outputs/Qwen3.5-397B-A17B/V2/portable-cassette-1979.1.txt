**Steelman:** A low-cost, portable, playback-only cassette device targeting youth can create a new personal music market despite dealer skepticism and lack of prior research.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The proposal faces four critical hurdles: dealer resistance, perceived antisocial behavior, unproven demand, and easy copyability. However, these are not blockers but execution risks.
1.  **Dealer/Staff Doubt:** This stems from viewing the product as a compromised recorder rather than a new category. The "no recording" feature is a cost-saving advantage, not a deficit, if positioned correctly.
2.  **Antisocial Concern:** In 1979, privacy and personal space are emerging values. Headphones offer an escape, not isolation. The risk is marketing, not product fit.
3.  **No Market Research:** Historical data cannot predict a category that doesn't exist. Waiting for research guarantees missing the first-mover window. The "do nothing" alternative yields zero growth in this segment.
4.  **Copyability & Volume:** Competitors can only copy once the market is proven. The large initial run is necessary to hit the youth price point; without that price, the concept fails. The risk is real but unavoidable for this strategy.

**Alternatives:**
*   *Do nothing:* Safe, but cedes the emerging portable music space to future entrants.
*   *Add recording/speaker:* Increases cost, misses the youth price target, and dilutes the unique value proposition of portability.
*   *Small pilot run:* Fails to achieve the cost structure required for the target demographic.

**Pre-mortem:** If this fails, it will be because the price was too high or the marketing failed to explain the "personal music" benefit, not because the technology was flawed. The lack of recording is a feature for cost, not a bug.

**Decision Logic:**
There are no factual blockers. The numbers (cost vs. price) work only at scale. The risks (copying, acceptance) are inherent to innovation and cannot be resolved by delay or modification without destroying the core value (price/portability). The "antisocial" and "no record" objections are biases of the current market, which this product intends to disrupt.

Blockers: NONE.
Risks: Market acceptance, competitor response, inventory exposure.
Kill Criteria: If sales do not reach 30,000 units in the first 6 months, halt production and liquidate.

DECISION: GO
