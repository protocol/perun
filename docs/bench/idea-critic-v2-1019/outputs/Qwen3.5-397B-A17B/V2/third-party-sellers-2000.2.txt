**Steelman:** Transforming from a pure retailer into a hybrid marketplace creates immediate inventory depth and variety without capital expenditure, turning fixed inventory risk into variable commission revenue while capturing traffic that would otherwise leave for competitors.

**Attack & Options:**
The primary blocker hypothesis is that margin erosion from undercutting and cannibalization of new sales by used copies will accelerate losses rather than stem them. However, given the company is *not yet profitable*, the current model of bearing full inventory risk is already failing. The alternative, "do nothing," guarantees continued cash burn on limited SKUs. A "curated marketplace" (slow rollout) delays the liquidity needed to make the platform attractive.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
This aligns with a survival-to-scale strategy. If we assume the site fails in 18 months due to cash constraints, the pre-mortem shows it was because selection was too narrow to retain customers. The cost of delay is losing the window to become the default search destination before a competitor locks in network effects.

**Evidence & Logic:**
The proposal trades high-risk inventory ownership for low-risk fee income. While suppliers may object, the retailer has no leverage if it remains unprofitable; diversifying supply is a defensive necessity. Customer trust risks are mitigated by the fact that the retailer still controls the checkout and can de-list bad actors, whereas the current risk is simply running out of money.

**Blocker Test:**
Is there a fact proving this fails immediately? No. The fear of cannibalization assumes customers only buy new items; data suggests price-sensitive customers would leave entirely if used options aren't present, resulting in zero revenue rather than reduced margin. The risk of supplier retaliation is real but secondary to the existential threat of insolvency. The "used vs. new" conflict is a feature, not a bug, capturing the full demand curve.

**Decision:**
The plan beats "do nothing" because the status quo is unsustainable. The risks are manageable operational challenges, whereas the current path leads to certain failure.

Blockers: NONE.
Risks: Supplier relations, quality control (mitigated by policy).
Kill Criteria: Halt if marketplace GMV does not exceed 10% of total sales within 12 months or if customer complaint rates triple.

DECISION: GO
