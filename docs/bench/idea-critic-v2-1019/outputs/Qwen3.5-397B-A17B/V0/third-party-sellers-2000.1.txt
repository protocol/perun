**Steelman:** Transforming from a pure retailer into a hybrid marketplace creates immediate inventory depth and variety without capital expenditure, turning fixed inventory risk into variable commission revenue while capturing demand the retailer cannot currently satisfy.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
The primary failure mode is brand erosion: if third-party sellers provide poor service or sell counterfeit/used goods alongside new items, customers blame the platform, not the seller. This directly threatens the "trust" asset required for future profitability.
*Alternative 1 (Do Nothing):* Maintain full control and margin but cap growth at inventory capital limits.
*Alternative 2 (Segregated Pages):* List third-party offers on separate pages to protect the primary product page's integrity, though this reduces conversion.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
For an unprofitable retailer, the "grow at all costs" pressure may override necessary quality gates. Assuming this works risks a race to the bottom on price, destroying margins for both the retailer and suppliers. If publishers retaliate by withholding new titles, the core business collapses. The proposal lacks a specific mechanism for vetting sellers, handling customer service disputes, or enforcing price/quality standards. Without these, the "fee per sale" is negligible compared to the cost of lost lifetime customer value.

**Evidence & Constraints:**
The proposal admits "less control over outside sellers' service quality" and "customer trust may suffer" but offers no mitigation plan. It treats these as acceptable risks rather than existential threats requiring strict operational guardrails before launch. There is no mention of a pilot program, seller insurance, or a dedicated trust-and-safety team.

**Decision:**
The proposal asks to approve a fundamental business model shift that introduces unmanaged reputational risk and supplier conflict without defining the operational controls necessary to prevent them. Approving "as proposed" means launching without safety nets. The risks of cannibalization and brand damage are acknowledged but not solved.

DECISION: NO-GO
