**Steelman:** Secure a generational talent early to dominate basketball culture, using controversy over uniform rules as a catalyst for brand identity and sales, accepting high upfront cost for long-term market leadership.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
The primary failure mode is total capital loss if the rookie fails physically or statistically. However, the "unproven" status is precisely why the acquisition cost, while high relative to peers, is lower than it would be post-success. The alternative—signing an established star—guarantees availability but caps upside and costs significantly more. Doing nothing cedes the basketball segment to competitors indefinitely.

The risk of league fines for rule-breaking colorways is not a bug; it is the marketing engine. Fines generate news cycles that money cannot buy, framing the brand as rebellious and player-centric. Historical data on signature shoes failing after one season applies to commodity players, not unique cultural phenomena. The proposal explicitly bets on the latter.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
If we assume the player stays healthy, the only way this fails is if the market rejects the "rebel" narrative. Given the stagnation of current basketball shoe sales, the status quo is already a slow failure. The cost of delay is losing the specific athlete to a rival, rendering the entire strategy impossible later. This is a one-way door; once the contract is signed, the budget is committed.

**Evidence & Kill Criteria:**
The proposal relies on the assumption that this specific rookie possesses transcendent talent and charisma. Without external scouting tools, we must accept the premise that internal evaluation has confirmed this "generational" potential. The financial risk is capped at the five-year term.

**Decision:**
The strategy transforms high risk into high leverage by monetizing the controversy and banking on elite talent before the market corrects the price. The alternative is safe irrelevance.

**Kill Criterion:** If the player misses more than 50% of games in Year 1 due to injury, terminate endorsement activation immediately. Leading metric: Sell-through rate of the banned colorway within 30 days of launch.

DECISION: GO
