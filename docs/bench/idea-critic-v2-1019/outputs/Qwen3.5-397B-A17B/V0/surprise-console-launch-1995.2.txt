The proposal to launch four months early in May 1995 at $399 fails the fundamental test of market readiness and strategic coherence. While the intent to seize first-mover advantage is understandable, the execution plan guarantees failure on three critical fronts.

First, the software library is insufficient. A console is useless without games; launching with "few games ready" alienates early adopters who will quickly lose interest, creating negative word-of-mouth that will poison the well for the full September launch. Developers, caught off guard by the date change, cannot produce the necessary 3D titles for a machine already deemed "hard to program." This creates a hardware shell with no soul.

Second, the pricing strategy is suicidal. At $100 above the competitor's announced price, the value proposition collapses. Consumers will not pay a premium for an inferior library on a complex machine when a cheaper, potentially better-supported alternative arrives shortly after. This cedes the mass market to the rival immediately.

Third, the distribution strategy invites channel conflict. Shipping only to "a few retailers" while excluding others guarantees retaliation from frozen-out partners. These retailers will likely prioritize the competitor's console in September, reducing shelf visibility and marketing support exactly when the full launch needs momentum.

The "first-mover" benefit is illusory here; being first to disappoint is worse than being second to deliver. The pre-mortem is clear: by September, the brand is associated with high cost, lack of games, and retailer animosity. The competitor launches into a vacuum of consumer trust, capturing the holiday season unchallenged.

There is no kill criterion that saves this; the foundational constraints (no games, high price, angry retailers) are baked into the May launch date itself. The only viable path is to maintain the September date, use the interim to secure developer commitments, finalize the price strategy, and ensure broad retail alignment.

DECISION: NO-GO
