The readout indicates a valid sample split (50,200 vs 50,100) consistent with the planned even split, ruling out SRM. The analysis occurred only at the planned end date, eliminating PEEK. Both arms exceeded the required 25,000 users, so POWER is not an issue. Results are consistent across platforms and the total, avoiding SIMPSON. The denominator matches the randomized unit (users), so DENOM is clear.

However, the readout explicitly states that the lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades thereafter, yet the team projects the day-one lift for a full year. This is a classic NOVELTY error: mistaking a short-term reaction for a sustained behavioral change. Projecting a transient spike as a permanent annual gain will lead to significantly overstated business value. Because the justification for the rollout relies on this flawed long-term projection, the decision cannot be made until the forecast is corrected to reflect the fading effect.
