The readout presents multiple critical flaws. First, the arm sizes (60,400 vs. 50,100) deviate significantly from the planned even split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Second, the results exhibit Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON): while the pooled data shows a +12.3% lift, the treatment actually loses in both mobile and desktop segments individually. Third, the test was stopped early based on daily interim looks when significance was first reached, which is a clear case of Peeking (PEEK) that inflates false positive rates.

Although the readout claims the sample size matched power calculations and the denominator was correct, the presence of SRM, SIMPSON, and PEEK invalidates the statistical conclusion that the treatment wins. The contradictory segment data and biased stopping rule mean the observed lift is likely an artifact of analysis errors rather than a real effect. Therefore, the recommendation to roll out cannot be accepted without fixing these issues.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
