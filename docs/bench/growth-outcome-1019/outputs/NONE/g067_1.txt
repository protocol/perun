The readout reveals two critical issues preventing a safe rollout. First, there is a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Second, the analysis exhibits a Novelty (NOVELTY) error. The lift dropped sharply from 12% on day one to 1% by day fourteen, indicating the effect is likely a short-lived reaction to the new interface. Despite this decay, the forecast incorrectly uses the initial 12% figure to project long-term gains.

While the readout correctly notes that the test ran to the planned end date (avoiding PEEK), had sufficient sample size for the target lift (avoiding POWER), showed consistent results across segments (avoiding SIMPSON), and used the correct denominator (avoiding DENOM), the presence of SRM and NOVELTY is fatal. The SRM implies the data collection mechanism is flawed, and the NOVELTY issue means the business case is based on inflated, unsustainable numbers.
