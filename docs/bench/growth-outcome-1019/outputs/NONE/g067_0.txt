The readout presents several critical issues preventing a safe rollout. First, the arm sizes (60,400 vs 50,100) deviate significantly from the planned even split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Second, while the study had enough users for a power calculation, it claims 25,000 users per arm despite reporting total traffic of over 110,000; if the analysis only used a subset without justification, this could imply selection bias, but strictly looking at the discrepancy between reported total visitors and the "25,000 users" claimed for power suggests a data inconsistency or potential Denominator issue if the metric wasn't calculated on all randomized units. However, the most glaring error is NOVELTY: the lift dropped from 12% to 1% over two weeks, yet the forecast relies entirely on the initial Day 1 spike, ignoring the decay. The readout explicitly states the forecast uses the 12% figure despite the drop, confirming the novelty error is present and unaddressed. SRM is also explicitly present due to the split deviation.

ERRORS: SRM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
