The readout presents multiple critical flaws preventing a safe rollout decision. First, the arm sizes (52,300 vs. 47,700) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) where assignment or logging is likely broken. Second, the test was explicitly stopped early based on daily interim looks when significance was first reached, which constitutes Peeking (PEEK) and inflates the false positive rate. Third, while the sample size matched power requirements initially, the actual imbalance suggests the effective power may be compromised, though the primary power issue here is overshadowed by the SRM. Finally, the readout admits the lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades thereafter, yet it projects this day-one lift over a full year. This is a clear Noveltiy (NOVELTY) error, mistaking a short-term reaction for a lasting improvement.

Because the assignment mechanism appears broken (SRM), the statistical significance is invalid due to early stopping (PEEK), and the long-term impact is falsely projected (NOVELTY), the results cannot be trusted.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, NOVELTY
