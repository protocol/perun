The readout indicates several critical issues preventing a safe rollout. First, while the sample sizes (50,200 vs 50,100) appear balanced and the power requirement was met, the metric definition is flawed. Conversions are divided by sessions, yet randomization occurred at the user level. Since heavy users generate more sessions, this creates a denominator mismatch where the analysis unit differs from the randomized unit. This flags DENOM.

Additionally, the forecast relies entirely on the day-one lift of 12%, ignoring the decay to 1% by day fourteen. Projecting the initial spike as a lasting effect despite the rapid decline suggests a short-lived novelty effect is being misinterpreted as a permanent gain. This flags NOVELTY.

The readout explicitly states there were no early looks (ruling out PEEK), the split matches the plan (ruling out SRM), and the treatment leads consistently across segments (ruling out SIMPSON). However, the presence of denominator mismatch and the reliance on novelty-driven projections constitute significant errors that invalidate the current recommendation.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
