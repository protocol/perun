The readout explicitly states that while users were the unit of randomization, the primary metric (conversion) was computed per page view. This violates the requirement that the metric's denominator must match the randomized unit, creating a risk of bias if users in different arms generate different numbers of page views. This corresponds to the DENOM error category.

Other potential issues appear to be addressed within the text: the arm sizes (50,200 vs 50,100) are consistent with the planned split and user counts (25,000 each), ruling out SRM. There were no early looks, ruling out PEEK. The sample size matched the power calculation, ruling out POWER. The treatment won in both mobile and desktop segments, ruling out SIMPSON. The lift remained stable over four weeks without fading, ruling out NOVELTY.

However, because the denominator mismatch (DENOM) is present, the statistical validity of the 7.4% lift is compromised until the metric is recalculated per user. Therefore, the rollout cannot be approved based on this data alone.

ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
