The readout indicates a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The planned split was 50/50, yet the arms received 52,300 and 47,700 users respectively. This significant deviation suggests a potential issue with assignment or logging that invalidates the test results.

Additionally, the readout highlights a Novelty effect error (NOVELTY). The lift dropped sharply from 12% on day one to 1% by day fourteen, yet the forecast for the rollout relies on the initial 12% figure. This projects a short-lived spike as a sustained improvement, which is methodologically unsound.

The readout explicitly states that other common errors were avoided: there were no early looks (not PEEK), the sample size matched the power calculation (not POWER), results were consistent across segments (not SIMPSON), and the metric denominator matched the randomization unit (not DENOM). However, the presence of SRM and the misapplication of novelty-driven data are critical flaws.

ERRORS: SRM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
