The readout presents two critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation to roll out the change.

First, the analysis suffers from **SIMPSON**'s paradox. The text explicitly states that while the overall treatment group wins, the treatment actually loses within both the mobile and desktop segments. This indicates that the aggregated result is misleading and likely driven by an imbalance in segment sizes rather than a true positive effect of the feature.

Second, there is a **DENOM**inator error. The users were randomized as the unit of assignment, yet the conversion metric is computed per page view. This mismatch between the randomization unit and the analysis unit can lead to biased estimates and incorrect inference.

Other categories are not flagged because the readout confirms they were handled: the arm sizes (50,080 vs 49,920) are consistent with a 50/50 split given the sample size (no SRM); the test ran to the planned end date (no PEEK); the sample exceeded the power calculation requirement (no POWER); and the lift remained stable over 28 days (no NOVELTY).

Due to the presence of Simpson's paradox and the denominator mismatch, the current results cannot support a rollout decision.
