The readout explicitly describes a classic case of Simpson's Paradox. Although the treatment leads in the pooled total, it actually trails within each specific platform segment. The aggregate lift is an artifact of the treatment group receiving a disproportionate amount of high-converting desktop traffic, masking the fact that the change performed worse on both mobile and desktop individually. This indicates that the overall positive result is misleading and driven by traffic composition rather than the efficacy of the new button design.

Other potential issues appear to be handled. The sample sizes (50,200 vs. 50,100) are consistent with the planned even split, ruling out SRM. The analysis occurred only at the planned end date with no interim looks, excluding PEEK. The sample size exceeded the power calculation requirement, addressing POWER. The metric denominator matches the randomized unit (user), avoiding DENOM. Finally, the lift remained stable over 28 days, suggesting the effect is not a fleeting NOVELTY.

Because the pooled result reverses the direction of the effect seen in every segment, the current recommendation to roll out is unsupported by the segment-level data.

ERRORS: SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
