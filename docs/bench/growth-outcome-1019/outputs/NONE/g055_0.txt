This reversal of trends when data is aggregated versus segmented is the definition of **SIMPSON**'s paradox.

Although the readout mentions the sample size matched power calculations, the analysis was performed at the planned end date without early looks, and the metric denominator matches the randomization unit, these factors do not negate the fatal flaws identified above. The presence of SRM invalidates the statistical validity of the test entirely, and Simpson's paradox demonstrates that the observed positive lift is an artifact of traffic composition rather than a genuine improvement. Consequently, the recommendation to roll out cannot be accepted.
