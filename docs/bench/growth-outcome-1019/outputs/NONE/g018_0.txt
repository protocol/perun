The readout describes a critical flaw in the metric definition relative to the experimental design. Users were the unit randomized, yet the primary metric (booking conversion) is computed per page view (completed bookings divided by page views). This constitutes a denominator error, as the analysis unit does not match the randomization unit. If users generate different numbers of page views across arms, this can bias the results.

All other potential issues appear addressed: the arm sizes (50,080 vs 49,920) are consistent with a 50/50 split, ruling out SRM. The test ran to the planned end date without interim looks, excluding PEEK. The sample size exceeded the power calculation requirement, addressing POWER. The treatment lead persists across segments and the total, ruling out SIMPSON. The lift remained stable over 28 days, suggesting NOVELTY is not the primary driver.

However, the DENOM error invalidates the statistical validity of the claimed lift until the metric is recalculated using the user as the denominator. Therefore, the rollout cannot proceed based on this data.

ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
