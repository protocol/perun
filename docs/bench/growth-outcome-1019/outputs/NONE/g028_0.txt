The readout presents multiple critical statistical flaws that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, the assignment counts (60,400 vs. 50,100) deviate significantly from the planned even split, indicating a **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch). This suggests broken assignment or logging, making the groups incomparable.

Second, the readout explicitly states that while the overall treatment wins, it loses within both mobile and desktop segments. This is a classic case of **SIMPSON** (Simpson's Paradox), where aggregated data masks or reverses the true effect seen in subgroups. The overall "win" is likely an artifact of uneven traffic distribution between segments rather than a genuine treatment effect.

Third, the metric denominator is page views, whereas the unit of randomization was users (**DENOM**). Calculating conversion per page view instead of per user violates the experimental design assumptions and can skew results if users in different arms generate different numbers of page views.

The readout indicates that Power was handled (sample sizes matched calculations), PEEK did not occur (single analysis at end date), and NOVELTY is unlikely (stable lift over four weeks). However, the presence of SRM, Simpson's Paradox, and denominator errors makes the current results unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
