The readout indicates proper randomization with balanced arm sizes (50,200 vs 50,100) and confirms the sample size met power requirements, ruling out SRM and POWER issues. The analysis occurred only once at the planned end date, eliminating PEEK concerns. Metrics are calculated per randomized user, avoiding DENOM errors, and results are consistent across platforms, showing no SIMPSON paradox.

However, the readout explicitly states that the observed lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades thereafter, yet the team projects this day-one lift over a full year. This is a clear instance of treating a short-lived novelty effect as a permanent gain. Rolling out based on inflated long-term projections without accounting for the fade-out risks overestimating the feature's value. This matches the NOVELTY error category.
