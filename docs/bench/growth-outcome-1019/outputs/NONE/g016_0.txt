The readout presents multiple critical flaws preventing a safe rollout decision. First, the arm sizes (52,300 vs. 47,700) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) where assignment or logging is likely broken. Second, the results exhibit Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON): while the treatment leads in the pooled total, it actually trails within every individual platform segment. The aggregate lift is an artifact of the treatment arm receiving a disproportionate amount of high-converting desktop traffic, not a genuine improvement. Third, there is a severe contradiction regarding sample size; while the traffic numbers suggest a large test, the text explicitly states "only 120 users were enrolled per arm" and notes that "no power calculation was done." This indicates the study is Underpowered (POWER) to support the claimed effects, rendering the statistical significance unreliable. Although the test avoided early stopping (no PEEK) and used the correct denominator (no DENOM), the combination of SRM, SIMPSON, and POWER errors makes the reported 3.4% lift untrustworthy. The recommendation to roll out cannot be accepted until these fundamental data integrity and analysis issues are resolved.

DECISION: HOLD
