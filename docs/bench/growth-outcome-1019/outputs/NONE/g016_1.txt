The readout presents multiple critical flaws preventing a safe rollout decision. First, the arm sizes (52,300 vs. 47,700) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Second, the results exhibit Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON): while the treatment leads in the pooled total, it actually trails within each specific platform segment because the treatment arm disproportionately attracted high-converting desktop traffic. This aggregation masks the true negative performance across segments. Third, the test is severely underpowered (POWER); despite the large numbers cited earlier, the context reveals only 120 users were enrolled per arm with no power calculation performed, making the claimed 3.4% lift statistically unreliable.

Although the readout confirms the denominator matches the randomized unit (ruling out DENOM), notes a single analysis at the end date (ruling out PEEK), and shows sustained lift over 28 days (ruling out NOVELTY), the presence of SRM, SIMPSON, and POWER errors invalidates the conclusion. The recommendation to roll out is unsupported by the data provided.
