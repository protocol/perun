The aggregate lift is an artifact of the treatment group receiving a disproportionate amount of high-converting desktop traffic, not a genuine improvement in the layout.

The sample size exceeded the power requirement, and the denominator matches the randomization unit, so POWER and DENOM are not flagged. SRM is not flagged as the split is within chance expectations.

Because the aggregated result is misleading due to Simpson's paradox and the statistical validity is compromised by early stopping, the recommendation to roll out cannot be accepted.
