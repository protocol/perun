The readout presents two critical statistical errors. First, it describes **SIMPSON**'s paradox: while the overall treatment result is a win, the treatment loses within both the mobile and desktop segments. This indicates the pooled result is misleading and likely driven by an imbalance in segment sizes rather than a true positive effect.

Second, the readout explicitly states **POWER** issues. It notes that "nobody sized the test for the lift it reports" and reveals a sample size of only 120 users per arm. This is drastically insufficient to detect the reported 3% lift reliably, making the finding likely a false positive. Additionally, there is a numerical contradiction regarding sample sizes (50k visitors vs. 120 users), but the explicit admission of no sizing confirms the underpowered nature of the analysis.

Other categories are cleared by the text: the split (50,200 vs 50,100) is within chance limits (no SRM), there was a single analysis at the planned end (no PEEK), the denominator matches the randomized unit (no DENOM), and the lift was stable over 28 days (no NOVELTY). However, the presence of Simpson's paradox and the lack of power make the current recommendation invalid.

ERRORS: SIMPSON, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
