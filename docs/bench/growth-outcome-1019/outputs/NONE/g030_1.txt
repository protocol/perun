The readout explicitly states that while users were the unit of randomization, the primary metric (conversion) was computed per page view. This violates the requirement that the metric's denominator must match the randomized unit, creating a risk of bias if users in different arms generate different numbers of page views. This matches the DENOM error category.

Other potential issues appear to be addressed within the text: the arm sizes (50,200 vs 50,100) are consistent with an even split, ruling out SRM. The analysis occurred only at the planned end date, ruling out PEEK. The sample size matched the power calculation, ruling out POWER. The treatment won in both mobile and desktop segments, ruling out SIMPSON. Finally, the lift remained stable over four weeks without fading, ruling out NOVELTY.

Because the denominator mismatch (DENOM) is present, the statistical validity of the 7.4% lift is compromised. The team cannot confidently attribute the result to the treatment rather than artifacts of counting page views instead of users. Therefore, the rollout decision must be paused until the metric is recalculated using the correct denominator.

ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
