The readout indicates a minor deviation in arm sizes (50,080 vs 49,920), but this falls well within the range expected by chance for a 50/50 split, so SRM is not present. The test ran for the full 28-day window with a pre-set stopping rule and a single read at the end, ruling out PEEK. The sample size exceeded the pre-calculated requirement of 25,000 per arm, addressing POWER concerns. Results were consistent across platforms and over time, with no indication of reversing trends in segments (SIMPSON) or decaying lifts (NOVELTY).

However, the readout explicitly states: "Conversions are divided by sessions while assignment was by user." Since the unit of randomization was the user, but the metric denominator is sessions, this violates the principle that the analysis unit must match the randomization unit. Heavy users contributing multiple sessions can skew the variance and significance calculations. This matches the DENOM category.
