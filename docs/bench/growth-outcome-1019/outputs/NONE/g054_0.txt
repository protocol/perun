The readout presents a critical contradiction indicative of Simpson's Paradox. While the overall aggregated data shows an 8.4% lift for the treatment group, the text explicitly states that the treatment loses within both the mobile and desktop segments. Since these two segments likely comprise the entire user base, it is mathematically inconsistent for the treatment to lose in every subgroup yet win in the total pool unless there is a severe imbalance in segment sizes between arms that is skewing the aggregate result. This reversal suggests the pooled result is misleading and hides the negative performance in specific contexts.
