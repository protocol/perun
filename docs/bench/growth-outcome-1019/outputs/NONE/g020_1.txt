The readout presents multiple critical flaws preventing a safe rollout. First, the traffic allocation is severely skewed: Control received 60,400 visitors while Treatment received only 50,100, despite a planned even split. This indicates a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM), suggesting broken assignment or logging. Second, the team explicitly stopped the test the first day the p-value dropped below 0.05 after checking daily. This is classic Peeking (PEEK), which invalidates statistical significance by inflating false positive rates. Third, the readout admits that "nobody sized the test for the lift it reports" and notes small sample sizes in the context of power analysis, flagging a Power (POWER) issue where the sample may be insufficient to reliably detect the claimed effect, regardless of the p-value.

While the readout attempts to mitigate concerns by noting stability over four weeks (addressing Novelty) and consistency across platforms (addressing Simpson's paradox), the presence of SRM, PEEK, and POWER issues renders the results unreliable. The metric denominator matches the randomization unit, so DENOM is not an issue. However, the foundational statistical validity is compromised by the other three errors.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
