The readout explicitly describes a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The planned split was even, yet the observed sizes are 60,400 for control and 50,100 for treatment, a discrepancy indicating broken assignment or logging.

Additionally, the readout identifies Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON). It states that while the treatment leads in the pooled total, it actually trails within each platform segment. The overall positive result is an artifact of the treatment arm receiving a disproportionate amount of high-converting desktop traffic, masking the negative performance across specific groups.

The readout indicates that other potential errors were handled: the sample size matched the power calculation (not POWER), the denominator matched the randomized unit (not DENOM), there were no early looks (not PEEK), and the lift was stable over 28 days (not NOVELTY). However, the presence of SRM and Simpson's paradox invalidates the headline result. The apparent 3.4% lift is misleading because the treatment performs worse in every segment, and the traffic allocation was flawed.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
