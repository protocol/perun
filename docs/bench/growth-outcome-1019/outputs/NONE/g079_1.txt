The readout contains several critical flaws preventing a safe rollout decision. First, while visitor counts look balanced, the text explicitly states "only 120 users were enrolled per arm," creating a massive discrepancy between enrolled users and reported visitors, which indicates a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) or logging failure. Second, the sample size of 120 per arm is extremely small for conversion metrics, and the admission that "no power calculation was done" confirms the test is underpowered (POWER). Third, the analysis projects a short-term lift observed in the first three days over a full year, despite the effect fading daily; this is a clear Novelty (NOVELTY) error where a transient effect is mistaken for a permanent gain.

Although the readout correctly notes that conversion is computed per randomized user (avoiding DENOM), the test was read once at the planned end date (avoiding PEEK), and results are consistent across segments (avoiding SIMPSON), the presence of SRM, POWER, and NOVELTY errors invalidates the conclusion. The discrepancy in user counts suggests broken assignment or logging, the small sample makes the result unreliable, and the projection method is fundamentally flawed.

ERRORS: SRM, POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
