The readout presents multiple critical flaws preventing a safe rollout. First, while the raw counts (50,200 vs 50,100) seem close, the text explicitly states only 120 users were enrolled per arm, indicating a massive discrepancy between assigned and analyzed units or a logging failure, which constitutes **SRM**. This tiny sample size of 120 per arm, combined with the admission that no power calculation was performed, clearly flags **POWER** issues; the test is severely underpowered to detect reliable effects.

Furthermore, the readout describes a classic case of **SIMPSON**'s paradox: the treatment leads in the pooled total solely because it attracted more high-converting desktop traffic, yet it actually trails the control within every specific platform segment. Aggregating these results masks the true negative performance across all individual groups.

Finally, the analysis admits the lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades subsequently, yet the projection assumes the day-one lift persists for a full year. This is a definitive **NOVELTY** error, mistaking a temporary user reaction for a permanent behavioral change. The **DENOM** issue is explicitly ruled out as handled, and **PEEK** is avoided due to the fixed stopping rule. However, the presence of SRM, Power, Simpson, and Novelty errors makes the current recommendation invalid.

ERRORS: SRM, POWER, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
