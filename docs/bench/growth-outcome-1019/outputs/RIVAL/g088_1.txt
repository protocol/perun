The readout explicitly describes two critical statistical errors that invalidate the headline result.

First, **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present. The planned even split should have yielded roughly equal group sizes, yet Control received 60,400 visitors while Treatment received only 50,100. This significant deviation suggests broken assignment or logging logic.

Second, **SIMPSON** (Simpson's Paradox) is confirmed. Although the pooled total shows a +6.2% lift for Treatment, the readout states Treatment actually trails within *each* platform segment. The aggregate gain is an artifact of Treatment drawing a disproportionate amount of high-converting desktop traffic, not because the feature performs better.

Additionally, **DENOM** (Denominator Error) is flagged. The metric calculates conversion per "page view," but the unit of randomization was "users." This mismatch can skew results if users generate different numbers of page views across variants.

The readout indicates that Power (sample size met), Peeking (fixed stopping rule), and Novelty (stable lift) were handled correctly, so those are not flagged. However, the presence of SRM, Simpson's paradox, and denominator errors makes the current data unreliable for a rollout decision.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
