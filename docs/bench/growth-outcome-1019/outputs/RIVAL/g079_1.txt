The readout contains multiple critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, there is a severe **POWER** issue. Only 120 users were enrolled per arm with no power calculation. This sample size is vastly insufficient to detect a reliable 9.4% lift in conversion rates, making the result highly susceptible to random noise rather than a true effect.

Second, the **NOVELTY** error is explicit. The lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades daily, yet the readout incorrectly projects the day-one lift across a full year. This ignores the decay of user attention to new features, leading to grossly inflated revenue projections.

While the readout correctly notes that conversion was computed per randomized user (avoiding DENOM), the split was even (avoiding SRM), and results were consistent across segments (avoiding SIMPSON), the fundamental lack of statistical validity and the erroneous long-term projection make a rollout decision impossible based on this data. The test must be re-run with a proper sample size determined by a power calculation, and projections must account for the observed novelty decay.

ERRORS: POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
