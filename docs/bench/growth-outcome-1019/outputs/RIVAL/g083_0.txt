The readout contains three critical analytical errors that invalidate the recommendation to roll out immediately.

First, **POWER**: The readout explicitly states "nobody sized the test for the lift it reports," despite having only 120 users per arm. This indicates the test is underpowered to reliably detect the claimed effect, making the result statistically fragile.

Second, **NOVELTY**: The lift is "concentrated in the first three days and fades," yet the team projects this day-one performance over a full year. This ignores the decay pattern, treating a temporary novelty effect as a permanent gain.

Third, while **SRM** is mentioned regarding traffic mix, the counts (50,200 vs 50,100) are nearly identical, suggesting assignment worked. However, the discrepancy between "visitors" and the "120 users" used for analysis suggests potential data loss or filtering issues, though the primary flagged errors are Power and Novelty. The denominator matches the randomization unit, so **DENOM** is not an issue. The stopping rule was followed, so **PEEK** is not an issue. Segments align with the total, so **SIMPSON** is not an issue.

Because the test lacks statistical power and relies on a fading novelty effect for its long-term projection, the decision cannot be made on this data alone.

ERRORS: POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
