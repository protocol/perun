The readout describes a clear case of Simpson's Paradox. While the pooled total shows a +3.4% lift for the treatment, the text explicitly states that the treatment "trails within each platform." The aggregate win is an artifact of the treatment group receiving a disproportionate share of high-converting desktop traffic, not because the variant is actually better. Since the variant performs worse than the control on both mobile and desktop individually, rolling it out to everyone would likely decrease overall conversion rates.

Additionally, there is a severe Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The planned split was even, yet the results show 60,400 visitors in control versus 50,100 in treatment. This ~55/45 split is statistically impossible to occur by chance in a properly functioning randomized experiment of this size, indicating broken assignment or logging logic. Although the text confusingly claims "Each arm got 25,000 users" for power purposes, the actual conversion metric relies on the full visitor counts provided, rendering the analysis invalid.

Because the variant loses in every segment and the data integrity is compromised by SRM, the recommendation to ship is unsupported.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
