Detecting a modest 3% lift with statistical reliability typically requires tens of thousands of users (as seen in standard sample size tables). With only 120 users, the test is severely underpowered, and any declared "win" is statistically unreliable noise.

Regardless of the total traffic, if the analysis relies on only 120 converted users or 120 total users per arm, the result is invalid due to lack of power.

The consistency across segments (mobile/desktop) and time (steady 3% lift) cannot rescue a test that was both peeked at and fundamentally underpowered. The statistical foundation for the decision is broken.
