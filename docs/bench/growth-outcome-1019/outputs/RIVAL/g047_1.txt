The readout reveals three critical errors preventing a rollout decision.

Second, **NOVELTY** is evident: the lift collapsed from 12% on day one to 1% by day fourteen, yet the forecast relies entirely on the initial 12% spike. This projects a short-lived curiosity effect as a sustained gain, which is unsupported by the trailing data.

Third, while **SRM** is not an issue (50,200 vs. 50,100 is within chance variation for an even split) and **PEEK** is avoided (single analysis at the end), the combination of the denominator mismatch and the novelty projection renders the reported impact invalid. The pooled result appearing consistent across platforms does not resolve the fundamental metric definition error.
