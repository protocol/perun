The readout contains multiple critical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, there is a severe **POWER** issue. The text explicitly states only 120 users were enrolled per arm with no power calculation. Detecting a reliable 9.4% lift on typical e-commerce conversion rates requires thousands of users, not hundreds. The sample is vastly too small to support the conclusion.

Second, the **NOVELTY** error is glaring. The lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades thereafter, yet the readout projects the day-one lift across a full year. This assumes a temporary spike in engagement due to newness is a permanent behavioral change, which is a fundamental forecasting error.

While the readout claims to handle **PEEK** (fixed stopping rule) and **SRM** (split is ~50/50), and explicitly avoids **SIMPSON** (treatment wins in both segments) and **DENOM** (conversion per randomized user), the power and novelty errors are fatal. You cannot make a rollout decision based on an underpowered test that misattributes a fading novelty effect as sustainable growth.

ERRORS: POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
