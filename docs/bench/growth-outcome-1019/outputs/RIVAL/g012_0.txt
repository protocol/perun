This readout contains multiple critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, **SRM** is present: the planned even split resulted in 60,400 control vs. 50,100 treatment visitors, a deviation indicating broken assignment or logging.

Second, **SIMPSON**'s paradox is explicitly described: while the pooled total shows a lift, the treatment actually trails within every platform segment. The aggregate "win" is an artifact of the treatment group receiving disproportionately more high-converting desktop traffic.

Third, **DENOM** error exists: the metric divides conversions by sessions, but randomization occurred at the user level. Since heavy users generate many sessions, this inflates the sample size artificially and violates the independence assumption required for standard significance tests.

While the readout correctly notes that **PEEK** was avoided (fixed stopping rule) and **POWER** was sufficient (sample size met), the structural flaws in data collection (SRM), aggregation (Simpson), and metric definition (Denom) make the 6.8% lift figure unreliable. The fact that the treatment loses in every segment when analyzed correctly suggests the change may actually be harmful or neutral, and the observed lift is purely compositional bias.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
