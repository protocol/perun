This readout contains multiple critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, there is a severe Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With 110,500 total visitors, an even split should yield roughly 55,250 per arm. The observed split (60,400 vs 50,100) deviates massively from expectation, indicating broken assignment or logging logic. Results from an SRM test are unreliable regardless of p-values.

Second, the team engaged in Peeking (PEEK). By checking daily and stopping the exact moment p < 0.05, they inflated the false positive rate. Fixed-horizon tests require pre-determined sample sizes, not optional stopping based on interim significance.

Third, the test is Underpowered (POWER). The readout explicitly states "nobody sized the test" and reveals a catastrophic data error: claiming tens of thousands of visitors while stating "Each arm has 120 users." Even if 120 is the typo, the admission that no power calculation occurred means the reported 12.4% lift could easily be noise. The sample size is insufficient to validate the claim.

While the readout attempts to rule out Simpson's Paradox (consistent results across platforms) and Novelty effects (stable over four weeks), the fundamental integrity of the experiment is compromised by the assignment mismatch, optional stopping, and lack of power planning.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
