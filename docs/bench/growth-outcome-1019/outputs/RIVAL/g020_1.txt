The readout contains multiple critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present. The planned split was even, yet Control received 60,400 visitors while Treatment received only 50,100. This ~17% deviation indicates a broken assignment or logging mechanism, making the groups incomparable.

Second, **PEEK** (Peeking) occurred. The team monitored results daily and stopped immediately upon reaching p < 0.05. This "optional stopping" invalidates the p-value and drastically inflates the false positive rate.

Third, **POWER** is insufficient. The readout admits "nobody sized the test" and reports only 120 users per arm. This sample size is far too small to detect realistic effects reliably, rendering the reported 12.4% lift statistically untrustworthy.

While the readout attempts to rule out **SIMPSON** (consistent results across platforms) and **NOVELTY** (stable lift over four weeks), the fundamental flaws in data collection (SRM), analysis method (PEEK), and sample size (POWER) make the results unusable for decision-making.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
