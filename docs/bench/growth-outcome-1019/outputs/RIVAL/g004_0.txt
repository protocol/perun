The readout contains multiple critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, **DENOM** is present: users were randomized, but conversion is computed per page view. This denominator mismatch violates the unit of randomization, biasing the results.

Second, **SIMPSON** paradox is explicitly described: while the overall treatment appears to win, it loses within both the mobile and desktop segments. This indicates the aggregate result is misleading and likely driven by unequal segment weighting rather than a true treatment effect.

Third, **POWER** is a clear issue. The readout states each arm has only 120 users (contradicting the earlier mention of ~50k users, suggesting a severe data inconsistency or that the analysis was done on a tiny, non-representative subset) and explicitly notes "nobody sized the test for the lift it reports." The sample is insufficient to support the claimed 3.1% lift.

While the test avoided **PEEK** by adhering to a pre-set stopping rule and **SRM** appears absent (50,080 vs 49,920 is within chance variation), the presence of denominator mismatch, Simpson's paradox, and severe underpowering makes the result unreliable. The contradiction between the total user count and the "120 users" analysis sample further suggests data integrity issues.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
