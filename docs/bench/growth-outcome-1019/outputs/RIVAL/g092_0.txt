The readout contains multiple critical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, **SRM** is present: the split is 52,300 vs 47,700 against a planned 50/50. This 4,600-user deviation suggests broken assignment or logging, making the groups non-comparable.

Second, **DENOM** is explicitly admitted: users were randomized, but the metric divides conversions by sessions. Since heavy users generate more sessions, this biases the result toward users with high activity rather than measuring the true effect per user.

Third, **NOVELTY** is evident: the lift collapsed from 12% on day one to 1% by day fourteen, yet the forecast relies entirely on the initial spike. This indicates a temporary curiosity effect rather than sustainable value.

While the test avoided **PEEK** (single analysis) and achieved **POWER** (sample size met), and **SIMPSON**'s paradox was avoided (treatment won in both segments), the remaining errors are fatal. The metric definition is fundamentally flawed, the traffic split is suspicious, and the projected impact relies on a decaying novelty effect.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
