The readout contains multiple critical errors that invalidate the recommendation to roll out immediately.

First, **SRM** is present: the planned even split resulted in 60,400 control users versus 50,100 treatment users. This significant deviation suggests broken assignment or logging, making the groups incomparable.

Second, **SIMPSON**'s paradox is explicitly described. While the pooled total shows a lift, the treatment actually trails within every platform segment. The aggregate "win" is an artifact of the treatment group receiving a disproportionate amount of high-converting desktop traffic, not a genuine improvement.

Third, **NOVELTY** is evident. The lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades thereafter, yet the readout incorrectly projects the day-one lift across a full year. This ignores the likelihood that users are simply clicking due to the change's newness rather than lasting value.

While the test avoided **PEEK** (single analysis) and met **POWER** requirements, the fundamental validity of the results is compromised by the sampling error, the reversed segment trends, and the unrealistic projection of temporary effects.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
