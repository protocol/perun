This readout contains multiple critical analytical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, the visitor counts (60,400 vs. 50,100) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split. This indicates **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch), suggesting broken assignment or logging that biases the results.

Second, while the overall result shows a win, the treatment loses in both mobile and desktop segments. This is a classic case of **SIMPSON** (Simpson's Paradox), where the aggregate result contradicts the stratified data, often caused by the aforementioned traffic imbalance.

Third, the lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades, yet the projection assumes day-one performance for a full year. This ignores **NOVELTY** effects, overstating long-term value.

While the test avoided peeking (stopped at planned date) and met power requirements with the correct denominator, the presence of SRM and Simpson's paradox means the observed "win" is likely an artifact of data quality issues rather than a true treatment effect. You cannot trust the direction of the effect when the segments disagree and traffic is unbalanced.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
