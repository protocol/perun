The readout contains two critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation.

First, **DENOM** (Denominator Error) is present. The unit of randomization was the *user*, yet the primary metric is calculated per *page view*. Since treatment users interact with the inline toggle during vehicle selection, they likely generate different page view counts than control users who see the post-booking upsell. This mismatch biases the conversion rate; the metric must be calculated per randomized user (e.g., add-on purchases per user) to be valid.

Second, **POWER** (Underpowered) is a major issue. With only 120 users per arm, the sample size is extremely small for a conversion metric. Without a pre-test power calculation, the "directionally strong" result is likely noise or a false positive. Small samples are highly susceptible to variance, making the observed 4.8% lift unreliable for a business decision.

While the readout correctly notes that PEEK is avoided (fixed horizon) and SRM is absent (balanced split), the fundamental flaws in metric definition and sample size render the results inconclusive.

ERRORS: DENOM, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
