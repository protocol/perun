The readout contains two critical errors that invalidate the rollout recommendation.

First, there is a clear **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch). The test planned an even split, yet the treatment arm received 50,100 visitors while control received 60,400. This ~55/45 split is too large to be random chance, indicating broken assignment or logging logic that biases the results.

Second, the forecast relies on **NOVELTY**. The lift dropped from 12% on day one to 1% by day fourteen, suggesting users initially engaged due to curiosity rather than lasting value. Projecting the day-one spike as the permanent effect ignores this decay.

While the readout correctly notes a single analysis (avoiding PEEK), sufficient sample size per arm (addressing POWER), consistent segment wins (avoiding SIMPSON), and correct denominator usage (avoiding DENOM), the presence of SRM and NOVELTY makes the current data unreliable for a full rollout.
