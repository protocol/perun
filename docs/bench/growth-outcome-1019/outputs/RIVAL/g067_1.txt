The readout contains two critical analytical errors that invalidate the rollout recommendation.

First, there is a clear **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch). The test planned an even split, yet the treatment arm received 50,100 visitors compared to 60,400 in control. This roughly 45/55 split deviates significantly from the intended 50/50 distribution, suggesting broken assignment logic or data logging issues that bias the results.

Second, the forecast exhibits a **NOVELTY** error. The readout explicitly states the lift was 12% on day one but decayed to 1% by day fourteen. Despite this sharp drop-off indicating a short-lived novelty effect where users initially engage with the new feature out of curiosity, the recommendation forecasts using the inflated 12% figure rather than the stabilized 1%.

While the analysis correctly avoided peeking (single analysis at end date), maintained proper denominators, and showed consistent directional wins across segments (avoiding Simpson's paradox), the presence of SRM and the inappropriate handling of the novelty effect make the current results unreliable for a full rollout decision.

ERRORS: SRM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
