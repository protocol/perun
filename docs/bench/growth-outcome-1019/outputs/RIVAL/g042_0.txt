The readout demonstrates sound experimental design in several areas: the arm sizes align with the planned split (no SRM), the analysis occurred only at the pre-determined end date (no PEEK), and the sample size matches the power calculation (no POWER). The unit of analysis correctly matches the randomized user (no DENOM), and the directional consistency across platforms and the pooled total rules out Simpson's paradox (no SIMPSON).

However, a critical error exists regarding the durability of the results. The data explicitly shows the lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades daily, indicating a novelty effect where users temporarily engage with the new interface simply because it is different. Despite this clear decay pattern, the readout projects the day-one lift across a full year. This is a classic NOVELTY error; projecting transient engagement gains as permanent behavioral changes will lead to overestimating the long-term value of the rollout. The recommendation to roll out based on inflated, unsustainable projections is premature.

ERRORS: NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
