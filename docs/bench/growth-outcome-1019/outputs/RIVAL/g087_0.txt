The readout contains multiple critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation.

First, **SIMPSON**'s paradox is explicitly present: the overall treatment wins, yet the treatment loses within *both* the mobile and desktop segments. This mathematical impossibility in a two-segment split indicates severe data aggregation errors or hidden confounding variables, making the top-line result untrustworthy.

Second, **POWER** is flagged. The readout admits "nobody sized the test" and reveals a sample size of only 120 users per arm. For a booking conversion metric, this sample is far too small to detect meaningful effects reliably, rendering any claimed significance spurious.

Third, **DENOM** is a concern. While the text states conversion is "computed per randomized user," the extremely low effective sample (120 users) versus the visitor count (50k+) suggests the denominator used for the final calculation may not actually reflect the full randomized pool, or there is a massive drop-off issue unaddressed. If the 120 users are the only ones who reached a specific step, dividing by the total randomized users without accounting for this funnel stage correctly could skew results, or vice versa.

Given the reversed segment trends and lack of power, the data cannot support a rollout.

ERRORS: SIMPSON, POWER, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
