The readout describes a classic case of Simpson's Paradox. Although the treatment shows a +3.4% lift in the pooled total, it actually trails the control within each platform segment (desktop and mobile). The aggregate lift is an artifact of the treatment group receiving a disproportionately higher volume of high-converting desktop traffic compared to the control, not because the variant itself performed better.

Additionally, there is a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The experiment planned an even split, yet the control received 60,400 visitors while the treatment received only 50,100. This significant deviation suggests a broken assignment mechanism or logging error, invalidating the comparison. While the text mentions "25,000 users" regarding power, the actual exposure numbers contradict this, further highlighting data integrity issues.

Because the overall positive result disappears when examining segments and the traffic allocation was fundamentally flawed, the reported lift is unreliable. Rolling this out based on the current data would likely result in a neutral or negative impact across the board.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
