The readout describes a clear case of Simpson's Paradox. While the pooled total shows a 3.1% lift for the treatment, the text explicitly states that the treatment "trails within each platform." The apparent winner is an artifact of the treatment group receiving a disproportionate share of high-converting desktop traffic, not because the button change itself is effective. Since the variant performs worse than the control in every individual segment (platform), rolling it out would likely decrease overall conversion rates once the traffic mix normalizes.

The readout indicates other areas are handled correctly: the sample sizes match the planned split (no SRM), the study ran to the planned end date without early looks (no PEEK), the sample size met the power calculation requirements (no POWER), the metric denominator matches the randomization unit (no DENOM), and the lift was consistent over 28 days (no NOVELTY). However, the segmentation failure is fatal to the recommendation.

ERRORS: SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
