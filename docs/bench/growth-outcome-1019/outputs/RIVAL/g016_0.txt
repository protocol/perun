The readout contains multiple critical statistical errors that invalidate the recommendation to roll out.

First, **SRM** is present: the split is 52,300 vs 47,700 against a planned 50/50. This ~9% deviation suggests broken assignment or logging, making the groups incomparable.

Second, **SIMPSON**'s paradox is explicitly described: the treatment wins in the pooled total but loses within every platform segment. This occurs because the treatment arm accidentally received far more high-converting desktop traffic. The aggregate lift is an artifact of skewed traffic composition, not the banner's effectiveness.

Third, **POWER** is a major issue. The text states "only 120 users were enrolled per arm" (contradicting the earlier traffic numbers, further indicating data integrity issues). A sample of 120 is vastly underpowered to detect a reliable 3.4% lift on purchase rates, rendering the result statistically meaningless.

While the readout correctly notes no early looks (avoiding PEEK), stable daily lifts (mitigating NOVELTY concerns), and correct denominators (avoiding DENOM), the presence of SRM, Simpson's Paradox, and severe underpowering makes the data unusable for decision-making. The observed "lift" is likely entirely due to traffic mix bias.
