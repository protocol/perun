The readout contains multiple critical errors that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, **SRM** is present: the planned even split should yield roughly equal arms, yet Control has 60,400 visitors versus Treatment's 50,100. This ~17% deviation indicates broken assignment or logging, making the groups incomparable.

Second, **POWER** is flagged. The readout explicitly states "nobody sized the test for the lift it reports" and notes only "120 users" per arm. This sample size is grossly insufficient to detect the reported effects reliably, rendering the statistical claims meaningless.

Third, **NOVELTY** is evident. The lift collapsed from 12% on day one to 1% by day fourteen, yet the forecast relies entirely on the initial 12% spike. This is a classic novelty effect where users interact with new elements briefly before ignoring them; projecting the day-one lift as permanent is analytically unsound.

While the readout correctly notes that the stopping rule was followed (avoiding PEEK), conversion was computed per randomized user (avoiding DENOM), and segments align with the overall result (avoiding SIMPSON), the presence of SRM, POWER, and NOVELTY errors makes the data unreliable for decision-making.
