The readout describes a critical denominator error. Users were the unit of randomization, yet the primary metric (booking conversion) is computed per page view rather than per user. This violates the requirement that the metric's denominator must match the randomized unit, as multiple page views from a single user are not independent observations and can bias variance estimates and significance. This corresponds to the DENOM error category.

While the sample sizes align with the planned split (ruling out SRM), the test stopped at the pre-committed date (ruling out PEEK), power requirements were met (ruling out POWER), results are consistent across segments (ruling out SIMPSON), and the lift remained stable over 28 days (ruling out NOVELTY), the fundamental construction of the metric invalidates the statistical claim. The +3.1% lift cannot be trusted without recalculating the metric using the user as the denominator.

ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
