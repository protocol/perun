The readout contains three critical flaws preventing a rollout decision. First, **DENOM**: the metric divides conversions by sessions while randomization occurred at the user level. Heavy users generate more sessions, biasing the result; the denominator must be unique users. Second, **NOVELTY**: the forecast relies on the day-one 12% lift despite a drop to 1% by day fourteen, incorrectly treating a transient novelty spike as a sustained gain. Third, **PEEK**: although the analysis occurred at the planned end date, selecting the day-one metric for the forecast after observing the decay constitutes data dredging/peeking at interim values to maximize apparent lift.

The sample ratio is acceptable (50,200 vs 50,100), power requirements were met, and Simpson's paradox is explicitly ruled out by consistent segment performance. However, the denominator error invalidates the statistical test, and the novelty bias renders the business case false.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
