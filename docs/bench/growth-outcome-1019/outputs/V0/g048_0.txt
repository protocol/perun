The readout contains multiple critical flaws that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, the arm sizes (60,400 vs 50,100) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split despite randomization, indicating **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch). This suggests broken assignment or logging, making causal inference unreliable.

Second, while the pooled result shows an +8.2% lift, the treatment actually trails within every platform segment. The aggregate win is driven entirely by an imbalance in traffic mix (more high-converting desktop users in treatment), which is a classic case of **SIMPSON** (Simpson's Paradox). The global lift is an artifact of composition, not genuine performance.

Third, the lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades thereafter, yet the readout projects this initial spike as a sustained annual gain. This ignores the **NOVELTY** effect, overestimating long-term value.

Although the test avoided early stopping (no PEEK) and met sample size requirements (no POWER), the presence of SRM, Simpson's Paradox, and unadjusted novelty effects means the reported win is illusory. The decision to roll out cannot be made on these figures.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
