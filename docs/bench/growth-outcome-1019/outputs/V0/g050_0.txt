The readout describes a 52,300 vs 47,700 split against a planned 50/50 allocation, yet explicitly claims "Each arm got 25,000 users." This internal contradiction indicates a broken assignment or logging mechanism where the actual exposure does not match the reported counts or the planned randomization. This is a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). When arm sizes deviate significantly from the planned split (here, ~52% vs ~48%), the validity of the causal inference is compromised regardless of the reported lift. The claim that the denominator matches the randomized unit is undermined by the conflicting population counts provided in the same text. Because the fundamental integrity of the experiment's allocation is in question, the result cannot be trusted for a rollout decision.

ERRORS: SRM
DECISION: HOLD
