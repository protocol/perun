The readout contains two critical errors. First, the planned even split should have yielded roughly equal arm sizes, yet Control received 60,400 visitors while Treatment received only 50,100. This significant deviation indicates a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM), suggesting broken assignment or logging that invalidates the comparison. Second, the forecast relies on the Day 1 lift of 12% while ignoring the drop to 1% by Day 14. Projecting the initial spike as lasting performance is a Novelty (NOVELTY) error; the effect clearly decayed, and using the peak number overstates long-term value.

Although the readout correctly notes a single analysis at the planned end (avoiding peeking), adequate power, consistent segment results (no Simpson's paradox), and the correct denominator, the presence of SRM and NOVELTY makes the current recommendation unsafe. The traffic imbalance must be investigated to ensure data integrity, and the forecast must be revised to reflect the sustained Day 14 lift rather than the transient Day 1 spike.
