Although the readout explicitly rules out peeking (single analysis at end), Simpson's paradox (consistent across platforms), and denominator errors (metric matches randomization unit), the SRM is fatal. You cannot trust the +3.1% lift when the randomization mechanism clearly failed to distribute users as intended. The consistency of the lift across days and platforms does not cure a broken experiment design; it may simply reflect a systemic bias in how users were routed.

Before any rollout decision, the engineering team must investigate why the traffic split skewed so heavily against the treatment arm. Was there a deployment error, a caching issue, or a filter dropping treatment events?
