The readout contains multiple critical flaws preventing a safe rollout decision.

First, **SRM** is present: the arms received 52,300 and 47,700 users against a planned 50/50 split. This significant deviation suggests broken assignment or logging, invalidating the comparison.

Second, **DENOM** is explicitly admitted: conversion was computed per page view despite users being the randomized unit. This mismatch biases the results.

Third, **PEEK** occurred: the team checked the dashboard daily and stopped immediately upon hitting p < 0.05. This "peeking" inflates the false-positive rate, meaning the reported significance is likely noise rather than a real effect.

While the readout claims sufficient power and consistent lift across segments (ruling out POWER and SIMPSON for now), the combination of sample ratio mismatch, denominator error, and sequential testing without correction makes the +3.1% lift unreliable. The stability over 28 days might argue against NOVELTY, but the fundamental statistical validity is compromised by the other errors.

You cannot ship based on these numbers. The experiment must be re-run with correct randomization, proper user-level denominators, and a pre-committed stopping rule (fixed horizon or valid sequential testing).
