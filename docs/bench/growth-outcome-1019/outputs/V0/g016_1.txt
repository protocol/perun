The readout contains multiple critical flaws preventing a rollout decision. First, the arm sizes (52,300 vs 47,700) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch), which suggests broken assignment or logging. Second, while the total shows a 3.4% lift, the treatment trails within every platform segment because it disproportionately attracted high-converting desktop traffic; this is a classic case of **SIMPSON** (Simpson's Paradox), meaning the pooled result is misleading. Third, the statement that "only 120 users were enrolled per arm" contradicts the traffic counts and, if true regarding the analysis set, indicates severe **POWER** issues (underpowered test) especially since no power calculation was performed. Although the test ran to the planned date (no **PEEK**) and used the correct denominator (no **DENOM**), the combination of assignment bias, paradoxical aggregation, and potential sample size insufficiency invalidates the "win." The claim that daily lift held steady does not resolve the structural bias introduced by the traffic mix and split error.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
