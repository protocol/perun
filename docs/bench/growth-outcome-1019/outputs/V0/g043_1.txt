The readout contains two critical flaws preventing a rollout decision.

First, **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present. The planned split was 50/50, yet the actual assignment was 52,300 vs 47,700 users. This ~9% deviation suggests broken assignment logic or logging bias, invalidating the comparison regardless of the observed lift.

Second, **DENOM** (Denominator Error) is explicitly admitted. Users were the unit of randomization, but conversion was computed "per page view." This mismatch allows users with multiple page views to skew the metric, violating the statistical assumptions of the test. The correct denominator must be the randomized user.

While the readout correctly notes that power was sufficient, stopping rules were pre-committed (avoiding PEEK), and stability was checked (mitigating NOVELTY), the fundamental integrity of the experiment is compromised by the assignment imbalance and the incorrect metric definition. The pooled lift and segment consistency cannot be trusted until the data is re-aggregated using the correct user-level denominator and the cause of the traffic skew is resolved.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
