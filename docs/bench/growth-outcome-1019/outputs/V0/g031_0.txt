The readout contains two critical flaws preventing a rollout decision. First, it exhibits **NOVELTY**: the lift collapsed from 12% on day one to 1% by day fourteen, yet the forecast relies on the initial spike. This indicates a short-lived curiosity effect rather than sustained value. Second, it suffers from **DENOM**: the metric divides conversions by sessions, while randomization occurred at the user level. Heavy users generate multiple sessions, violating the independence assumption and biasing the results.

Although the test avoided early peeking (single analysis at end date), had correct sample sizes (POWER ok), and showed consistent direction across platforms (no SIMPSON), the denominator error invalidates the statistical significance, and the novelty decay invalidates the business case.
