The readout contains multiple critical flaws preventing a rollout decision. First, **DENOM**: users were randomized, but conversion is computed per page view, violating the unit of analysis. Second, **SIMPSON**: while the pooled total favors treatment, it trails within every platform segment; the aggregate win is driven solely by a traffic mix shift toward high-converting desktop users in the treatment arm. Third, **NOVELTY**: the lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades thereafter, yet the readout incorrectly projects this transient spike as a permanent annual gain.

Although the test avoided early stopping (not PEEK) and achieved the planned sample size (not POWER), and arm sizes appear balanced (not SRM), the structural errors in measurement, aggregation, and projection invalidate the claimed +8.3% lift. The result is likely an artifact of traffic composition and short-term curiosity rather than genuine value.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
