The readout contains three critical flaws. First, **PEEK**: the team stopped the test the first day p < 0.05 after checking daily. This "peeking" inflates the false-positive rate; a fixed-horizon test must be read once at the pre-committed end, or a valid sequential rule used. Second, **DENOM**: the metric divides conversions by sessions while randomization was by user. Heavy users generate multiple sessions, violating independence assumptions and biasing the result. Third, **NOVELTY**: while the lift held for 28 days, the recommendation projects this as lasting without evidence that the two-step composer isn't simply driving short-term engagement through novelty rather than sustained value.

Because the statistical validity is compromised by early stopping and the wrong denominator, the reported win is unverifiable. You cannot ship based on a flawed test design.
