The readout contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: the planned even split should yield roughly equal arm sizes, yet Control has 60,400 users while Treatment has only 50,100. This ~17% deviation indicates broken assignment or logging, invalidating the comparison. Second, **SIMPSON**: while the aggregated result shows a +3.1% lift for Treatment, the readout explicitly states Treatment loses within *both* mobile and desktop segments. This reversal confirms Simpson's Paradox, meaning the overall "win" is a statistical artifact of uneven segment mixing, not a real effect.

Although the test avoided early peeking (single analysis at end date) and met power requirements, the presence of SRM and Simpson's Paradox makes the reported lift unreliable. You cannot ship a change based on an aggregate win that disappears (and reverses) in every underlying segment, especially when the traffic split itself is corrupted.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
