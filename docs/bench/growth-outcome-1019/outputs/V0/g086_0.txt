The readout explicitly states that each arm received 25,000 users, satisfying the power calculation. However, it simultaneously reports that Control got 60,400 visitors and Treatment got 50,100. This massive discrepancy between the planned/equalized sample size used for analysis and the actual observed traffic indicates a broken assignment or logging mechanism where the arms are not balanced as intended. This is a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). When arm sizes differ this significantly from the planned split, the randomization assumption is violated, making any comparison of conversion rates invalid regardless of the calculated lift. The claim that "traffic mix" was the same does not resolve the fundamental imbalance in total volume versus the analyzed subset. Because the integrity of the experiment's randomization is compromised, the result cannot be trusted for a rollout decision.

ERRORS: SRM
DECISION: HOLD
