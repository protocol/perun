The readout contains multiple critical flaws that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, the team "stopped the test the first day p fell below 0.05" after checking daily. This is **PEEK**ing; repeated looks inflate the false-positive rate, making the significance claim unreliable. Second, the test was not sized for the reported lift and relies on a small subset ("each arm has 120 users" used for the decision context vs the total assigned), indicating a **POWER** issue where the sample is insufficient to trust the effect size. Third, the forecast projects the "12%" lift observed on day one, ignoring the drop to "1% by day fourteen." This treats a transient spike as a permanent gain, a clear **NOVELTY** error.

While the arm sizes (50,080 vs 49,920) appear balanced and the denominator matches the randomized unit (ruling out SRM and DENOM), and the effect direction is consistent across platforms (ruling out SIMPSON), the combination of optional stopping, underpowering, and forecasting a novelty spike renders the result `UNVERIFIED`. You cannot ship based on a p-value obtained via peeking, nor can you forecast revenue based on day-one novelty.

ERRORS: PEEK, POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
