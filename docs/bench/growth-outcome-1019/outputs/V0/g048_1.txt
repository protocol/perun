The readout contains multiple critical flaws that invalidate the recommendation to ship.

First, the arm sizes (60,400 vs 50,100) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split despite randomization, indicating **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch). This suggests broken assignment or logging, making causal inference unreliable.

Second, while the pooled result shows a lift, the treatment trails within every platform segment. The aggregate win is driven solely by an over-representation of high-converting desktop traffic in the treatment group. This is a classic case of **SIMPSON** (Simpson's Paradox); the change likely hurts users on all specific platforms.

Third, the lift is concentrated in the first three days and fades, yet the projection assumes this day-one lift persists for a year. This ignores the **NOVELTY** effect, overstating long-term value.

Although the test avoided early stopping (no PEEK) and met sample size requirements (no POWER), the combination of broken randomization, reversed segment trends, and inflated projections makes the result unsafe for rollout.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
