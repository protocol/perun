The readout contains multiple critical flaws preventing a rollout decision.

First, **PEEK**: The team checked the dashboard daily and stopped the moment $p < 0.05$. This "peeking" inflates the false-positive rate, making the significance claim invalid. A fixed-horizon test must be read once at the end, or a valid sequential design used.

Second, **POWER**: The readout admits "nobody sized the test" and notes only 120 users per arm. This sample is likely too small to detect the reported lift reliably, rendering the result underpowered.

Third, **NOVELTY**: The lift collapsed from 12% on day one to 1% by day fourteen, yet the forecast relies on the initial 12%. This indicates a short-lived novelty effect rather than sustained value.

While SRM (split is ~50/50), SIMPSON (trend holds across segments), and DENOM (denominator matches randomization unit) appear handled, the combination of peeking, lack of power, and novelty projection makes the current "win" unverifiable.
