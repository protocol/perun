## Recommendation: sunset Field Notes, but only if three checks pass by about 30 November

Don't take "sunset" to Dana on 31 October as a settled call. Take it as a recommendation with a pre-agreed gate. The deadline that matters is the announcement, not the 31st. Announcing by mid-December still gives six months' notice, so you have time to replace your two unknowns with evidence.

**Steelman:** a seven-year-old product losing $380k a year (about $7.90 per user) ties up 6 of 90 people (6.7% of headcount) on a line the company no longer wants. Those people could strengthen the core.

**Stress test of your leaning**
1. **The one-third migration figure is a guess, and it may not matter.** Roughly 16,000 users would move. The real question is what a migrated user pays and whether that beats the cost of running the migration. Neither number is in your brief.
2. **The loss is not the true cost.** $380k is net of the team's cost. The real comparison is the loss plus what the 6 people would produce elsewhere, against the exit costs: migration tooling, support surge, refunds, severance, and any churn in the core product.
3. **Goodwill is partly knowable now.** Compare core renewal rates for customers who also use Field Notes against comparable customers who don't. That uses data you already hold.
4. **Cheaper alternatives are untested:** a sale or handoff to a buyer or community steward, and maintenance mode with about 2 people. Either could beat a full shutdown at lower reputational risk, and a sale would keep the community alive.
5. **Pre-mortem:** a loyal community reacts publicly, the core product's renewals dip, and the freed people are not actually redeployed, so the saving never materialises.

**The three checks (all doable before 30 November)**
- Run the renewal comparison from point 3.
- Send a short survey or email test to a sample of Field Notes users asking how likely they are to move, to replace the one-third assumption.
- Total the exit cost, and confirm the 6 roles have named destinations.

**Decision:** sunset, with a 60-day sale/handoff attempt running in parallel. Announce by 15 December at the latest. Make the exit gentle: a migration path, data export and a clear timeline.

**Reverse the decision (shift to maintenance mode or a sale) if any of these hold:**
- Field Notes overlap users renew at a materially higher rate in core, and the gap is worth more than the savings. Set the threshold with Finance before you look at the result.
- Exit cost exceeds about 12 months of the current loss (~$380k) with no buyer in sight.
- Survey migration intent is so low that expected core revenue from migrants is negligible. In that case you are choosing a clean exit over a managed one, so confirm Dana accepts that.

**Leading metrics:** survey migration intent, the renewal gap, and sale interest. Review on 30 November, then again 30 days after the announcement for core churn and sentiment.

*Note:* I haven't seen your pricing, renewal data or cost breakdown, so the thresholds above need your Finance team's numbers.
