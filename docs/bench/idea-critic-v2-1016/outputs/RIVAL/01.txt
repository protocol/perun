# Recommendation: go with the agency, but gate it, and open one senior hire now

**Why hiring alone can't work.** If you start recruiting on 23 Oct, hires land between late January and late February. Adding 6 weeks of ramp, they become productive between mid-March and mid-April. That gives you roughly 0 to 4 weeks of useful work against a 9 engineer-month scope. Hiring doesn't meet the deadline, whatever the cost.

**So the real question is whether Brightline can deliver.** I'd make that a go/no-go by Friday rather than a preference.

## Stress test of your leaning

**What you may be underweighting:**
1. **Payments experience is the central risk, not speed.** Faster to start is worthless if the people are generalists learning billing on your dime. Require named individuals and two references from billing or payments projects.
2. **Team size vs. scope.** At $145k/month, you're likely buying several people. Between a mid-November start and 15 April there are about 21 weeks. Two engineers give ~9.7 engineer-months with no slack. You need at least 3 for ~14, a ~1.5x buffer. Confirm the team size and whether $145k is sized sensibly for this.
3. **Your own bandwidth.** Agencies need a product owner and a senior internal reviewer. Budget real hours from your best engineer.
4. **Scope creep.** Your whole plan assumes scope holds. Freeze it in writing with Jane and the customer's requirements.
5. **The cliff after handoff.** A billing system built by outsiders and then left unowned is a liability. This is the strongest argument for the hybrid below.
6. **Slack.** A six-month contract starting in November ends in May. A slipped start eats your buffer directly.

**What you may be overweighting:** the cost gap. The agency is about $870k against about $215k per year per hire, but the cheaper option misses the deadline, so the comparison is moot. Compare the agency against the cost of losing your largest contract.

## What to do by Friday 23 Oct

- **Ask Brightline for:** named engineers with payments or billing experience, a start date no later than mid-November, and the team size behind the $145k.
- **Contract terms:** milestone-based acceptance, a cap on spend, a code and IP ownership clause, a documented handoff, and a 2 to 4 week overlap with your team.
- **Open one senior hire now.** This person isn't there to hit the deadline. They receive the handoff and own the system afterward, and they'd be a useful reviewer in Q1 if hired early.
- **Defer the second hire** until you can show a post-April roadmap (billing extensions, invoicing, integrations) that justifies it.

## If Brightline fails the check

Brightline can't show real payments people, or can't start by mid-November. In that case, try a second agency, or a specialist billing contractor, or renegotiate scope or date with the customer. Do not fall back to hiring-only.

"Hiring can't land people in time; an agency is the only path to 15 April. I'm validating Brightline's payments depth this week, then committing about $870k at most, with milestones, and hiring one senior engineer now to own the system afterward."
