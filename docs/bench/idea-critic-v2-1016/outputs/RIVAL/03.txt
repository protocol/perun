# Recommendation for Maya (by 7 Nov)

**Stay self-hosted for now. Don't sign the 3-year term, and don't commit to the migration until the compliance answer is in.** Treat the 1-year renewal as a bridge to a real decision, not as the decision.

**Why**
- The managed service costs $80k/yr more in run-rate ($290k vs $210k), plus 2-3 months of migration effort and scheduled downtime.
- It only pays off if the reassigned engineer's time is worth more than that gap. That is plausible, but it is an assumption you haven't validated.
- If the data-residency answer is no, the question is moot. If it is yes, you have a real comparison to make.
- The answer is due around 30 Oct, before your 7 Nov deadline, so you can tell Maya the decision rule now and the result a week later.

**Stress test of your leaning**
1. **The 1-year option may not exist, or may cost more.** Vendors often price short terms at a premium. Get the quote in writing now. If it is much above $210k, a 3-year term with a termination right after month 12 may be cheaper than the 1-year.
2. **A 1-year renewal defers the problem.** The migration takes 2-3 months plus planning, so you would need to start by about mid-2027. Put that date on the calendar or you will renew again by default.
3. **The headcount saving may not materialise.** "About one engineer's time" is only worth money if the work is genuinely reallocated to something valuable. With three engineers, you may get a slice of each person's time rather than one whole person, which is much harder to capture. Also check what the engineers' time currently costs you in on-call burden, since that never shows up in the $210k.
4. **The cost comparison isn't like-for-like.** $210k covers hosting and support add-ons. It excludes the three engineers. Compare total cost of ownership: $210k plus the share of engineer time spent on the database, against $290k plus whatever residual operations work remains.
5. **Risks you may be underweighting on the self-hosted side:** key-person dependence on three people, and recovery and upgrade burden. On the managed side: the cutover downtime and one-off migration cost. For a health company, the downtime window needs sign-off from whoever owns customer commitments.
6. **Your 3-year objection may be right but incomplete.** The real risk of 3 years is paying $630k for something you might leave after year 1. The risk of 1 year is price and renegotiation exposure. Quantify both rather than defaulting to flexibility.

**Decision rule to give Maya**
- **Managed fails the compliance requirement:** stay self-hosted. Negotiate the best term the vendor will give, and prefer 3 years only with an exit clause.
- **Managed passes:** compute the five-year cost. If the freed engineer time is worth more than $80k/yr plus amortised migration cost, plan the migration for 2027 on a 1-year bridge. If not, stay.

**Do before 7 Nov:** get the written 1-year quote, a loaded engineer cost figure from finance, an estimate of how many engineer-weeks the database consumes today, and the managed vendor's compliance answer.

Note: I've assumed the engineers' cost is separate from the $210k, and I have no figures for engineer cost or migration effort, so the break-even above needs your numbers.
