**Recommendation: Sunset Field Notes, but as a managed wind-down with a go/no-go gate, not an announcement-first decision. Bring Dana a "yes, conditionally" by 31 October.**

**Why sunset holds up**
- $380k/yr loss plus 6 of 90 people (about 6.7% of headcount) is a real drag on a focus-the-company mandate.
- Seven years of running at a loss suggests the economics are structural, not a growth problem you can fix in a quarter.
- Deadline math: 6 months' notice before a mid-2027 shutdown means an announcement by about end of December. You have roughly 10 weeks, so decide now but don't rush the execution.

**Stress test of your leaning**
1. **The one-third migration figure is a guess, and it drives the case.** If it is 33%, that is about 16,000 users. If it is 5%, about 2,400. Do not put either number in front of Dana as fact. Present a range (5% / 15% / 33%) and show that the decision to sunset holds even at the low end. If it only works at 33%, the case is weak.
2. **Brand goodwill is the biggest unknown.** A loyal community of 48,000 may include your best advocates for the core product. A badly handled shutdown could hit core renewals, and you have no data on how large that effect is. Even a small renewal drop on a large core base could rival the $380k saved. You can estimate break-even: what core renewal loss equals $380k? If it is tiny, goodwill risk matters a lot.
3. **Are the savings real?** $380k is the net loss. Check whether it includes allocated overhead that remains after shutdown. Also count wind-down costs: severance or redeployment for 6 people, refunds, hosting during the notice period, support load.
4. **Alternatives you should rule out explicitly:** (a) sell or spin out to a buyer or community maintainers; (b) shrink to a 1-2 person maintenance mode; (c) raise prices (do the 48,000 users pay anything near cost?); (d) open-source it. Option (a) or (d) may preserve goodwill at near-zero cost and is what I would test first.
5. **Why does it lose money?** If it is a pricing issue rather than demand, a fix might beat a shutdown. If users are mostly free, the "loyal community" may be a cost, not an asset.

**What to do before 31 October (about two weeks)**
- Pull core-product data: how many Field Notes users already hold a core account, and how Field Notes users compare on renewal and referral.
- Run a quick survey or email test of a sample of Field Notes users asking about a migration offer, to replace the guess with a range.
- Build the break-even calculation for goodwill.
- Sound out one or two possible acquirers or maintainers informally.

**Framing for Dana:** Recommend sunset, conditional on three checks: the savings are real, the goodwill break-even is plausible, and no buyer or spin-out is available. Include a trigger: if migration looks below your low case, or buyer interest appears, revisit before the year-end announcement.
