# Recommendation

**Sunset Field Notes, but as a gated decision, not a flat yes.** Take "sunset, subject to two checks and an exit-by-sale attempt" to Dana on 31 October, and lock the final call by early December. The Dec 31 announcement deadline leaves room for this.

**Why sunset is still the leaning:** a $380k annual loss and 6 of 90 people (about 7% of headcount) is a real drag on a company that wants to focus. The case holds even if no one migrates, so your unsupported one-third figure is upside, not a pillar. Don't put it in the headline.

## Stress test

1. **The risk is on the other side.** Shutting it down is a one-way door. The danger is not the $380k you save but what it costs you in core renewals if goodwill from a loyal community erodes. You don't know that number, so the decision rests on an unmeasured loss.
2. **Break-even:** sunset loses money if (core users who also use or love Field Notes) × (extra churn after shutdown) × (core revenue per user) exceeds $380k plus the value of the redeployed people. Each input can be pulled from data you already have.
3. **The $380k is probably understated as a saving.** Running through a 6-month notice period costs roughly another $190k at the current loss rate. Add refunds for prepaid annual plans, data export tooling, and support load. Also, the 6 people are only a saving if you redeploy or cut them, and the cost of that wasn't in your figures.
4. **"48,000 active users" is ambiguous.** If many are free, the loss per paying user is far higher, which strengthens the case for sunset. If most pay, a price rise might narrow the loss. Check which.
5. **The one-third migration figure** is an assumption, and a notebook-to-core move is a different product choice, so treat it as unproven. Even a smaller number is fine for the case; just don't promise it.

## Do before 31 October (15 days)

- **Overlap:** what share of Field Notes users are also core customers, and what share of core customers use Field Notes?
- **Cohort comparison:** compare core renewal rates for overlapping vs non-overlapping users. It's correlation, not causation, but it bounds the goodwill risk.
- **Quick demand test:** a short survey or "interested in a migration offer?" prompt to a sample of Field Notes users. That replaces the one-third guess with a number.
- **Alternatives:** sound out a buyer, or a community or spin-out handoff. This preserves goodwill at lower cost than a shutdown.
- **Cheaper middle path:** maintenance mode with 1–2 people, or a price change. Both are reversible.

## Reverse the recommendation if

- the overlap and cohort data suggest core renewal exposure above roughly $380k a year, or
- a credible buyer or community steward emerges at acceptable terms, or
- maintenance mode cuts the loss to a level Dana accepts.

## What to tell Dana

"Sunset is my recommendation, with announcement by Dec 31. I'm confirming overlap and renewal exposure by [date], and exploring a sale or handoff in parallel. If exposure exceeds the savings, I'll reverse."
