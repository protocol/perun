## Recommendation

Stay self-hosted and secure a 1-year renewal (or a 3-year term with a termination-for-convenience clause after 12 months). Don't commit to a 3-year lock-in or start a migration until the managed vendor's residency answer is in hand.

The strongest reason is timing. Migration takes 2-3 months, so you cannot be off the current contract by 30 November under any scenario. You need a bridge term either way, and a 1-year term is the cheapest way to keep the option open.

## Stress test of your leaning

**1. Your price is unverified.** You don't yet know that a 1-year term is offered, or at what premium. If it costs, say, $250k, the cost gap to managed shrinks. Ask the vendor for the 1-year price in writing now.

**2. The cost comparison is closer than the sticker suggests.**
- Over 3 years: $630k self-hosted vs about $870k managed, a $240k gap before migration.
- Managed wins only if the freed engineer capacity plus avoided incident and on-call cost exceeds about $80k/yr plus migration cost.
- The engineer-reassignment assumption is your weakest input. With three engineers, losing one may erode on-call coverage and database expertise. Test it: would you actually reduce headcount or reallocate, and to what specific work with what value?
- Migration cost is unpriced. Two to three months of how many engineers, plus the downtime cost, is missing from your numbers.

**3. A 1-year renewal can defer the decision rather than make it.** You'll face the same cliff next November. Use the year to get the compliance answer in writing, price a pilot, and set a decision date at month 8.

**4. You may be anchored on the status quo.** Test it: if you were starting today with no cluster, would three engineers running a database be your choice for a 120-person company? If not, that's the case for managed, and the compliance answer is the gate.

**5. The compliance answer decides much of this.** If managed can't meet data residency, the comparison ends. If it can, the cost gap, not risk, becomes the real question. You'll have the answer around 30 October, before your 7 November deadline, so don't present a final position to Maya until then.

## What to do before 7 November

- **Now:** ask the vendor for 1-year pricing and any early-termination terms.
- **Now:** ask the managed vendor for a written residency and audit-evidence answer, and ask your auditor what documentation they will accept.
- **By ~30 October:** estimate engineer-months for migration and the real value of the reassigned engineer.
- **For Maya:** present the decision as a recommendation to renew for 1 year, with stated switch criteria. Suggested criteria: residency confirmed in writing, managed total cost within about 15% of self-hosted after counting the freed engineer, and a downtime window the business accepts.
