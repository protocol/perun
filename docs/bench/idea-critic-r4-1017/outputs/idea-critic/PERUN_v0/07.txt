2.  **Revenue Math Error:** The $66k/month lift assumes captured capacity. If clinics cannot fill the recovered slots (the 30% no-show reduction) with paying patients, the revenue gain vanishes despite better attendance.

*   Implementation costs exceed **$50,000** (approx. 11% of dev budget) without a clear path to the July 1 launch.
