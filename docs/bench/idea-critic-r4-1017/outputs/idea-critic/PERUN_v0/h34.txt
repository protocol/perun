**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional launch.**
Do not commit to the full $305,000 budget yet. Launch a minimal viable tier in Q4 targeting only 1,000 members (0.4% conversion) with a capped marketing spend of $20,000. Proceed to the full 5,000-member scale only if the pilot proves a churn rate below 6% after month three and a customer acquisition cost under $40.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Churn Miscalculation:** Your revenue model assumes stable membership, but you lack data beyond three months. If your churn matches the competitor's 8% monthly rate, you will lose nearly 75% of your cohort annually, making the $600k revenue target impossible without unsustainable marketing spend to replace lost users.
2.  **Conversion Overestimation:** You are banking on a 2% conversion from a free listener base that differs significantly from the competitor's 1.2M audience. If conversion lands at 1% (2,500 members) instead of 2%, revenue drops to ~$300k, which barely covers the $225k in known operating costs, leaving no margin for the $150k integration sunk cost or unexpected expenses.
3.  **Hidden Cost Explosion:** The budget excludes fully factored P&L impacts. If bandwidth costs scale non-linearly with paid content consumption or if community management requires full-time attention due to higher payer expectations, the $75k buffer for bandwidth and staffing will vanish, turning the project net-negative even at target membership.

**Trigger to Stop or Pivot:**
 Halt the full rollout immediately if, by the end of Month 3, the **net retention rate** (revenue retained from the original cohort) falls below 85% or if the **cost per acquired paying member** exceeds $50. These metrics directly validate whether the unit economics can support the $150k integration investment before scaling further.
