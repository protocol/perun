**Recommendation:** Proceed with the pilot on 1 Oct 2025, but strictly as a "go-if" experiment where continuation beyond the initial three months is conditional on validating unit economics, not just top-line revenue.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Cannibalization & Margin Erosion:** The 30% conversion assumption is risky; if high-value private students ($30) downgrade to group seats ($15) without bringing in new volume, net revenue per tutor drops while operational complexity rises.
2.  **Quality Dilution:** Scaling from 1:1 to 1:15 without proven pedagogy or platform tools for group management could degrade learning outcomes, driving churn for both group and private tiers.
3.  **Break-Even Miscalculation:** Your fixed cost buffer is thin. If the $120k budget includes hidden platform engineering hours or marketing CAC that exceeds estimates, the 800-seat break-even point shifts right, potentially making the pilot insolvent before validation occurs.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers:**
*   **Stop Condition:** Halt expansion immediately if **net revenue per active tutor** decreases by more than 5% compared to the pre-pilot baseline, indicating cannibalization outweighs new growth.
*   **Pivot Condition:** If total seat sales exceed 800/month but **retention after session 4 is below 40%**, pivot from a subscription model to a drop-in workshop model before Q2 2026.
*   **Success Metric:** Validate that at least 20% of group participants are *new* customers (not existing private students) and that their Lifetime Value (LTV) projection exceeds the $15 acquisition cost within 6 months.
