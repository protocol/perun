**Recommendation: MODIFY (Proceed with a Phased Pilot, Not Full Rollout)**

Do not commit the full $1.2M or hire all 12 officers immediately. Instead, launch a 90-day pilot in **two** high-potential locations with **four** loan officers and a capped technology spend of $400k. This approach validates the "instant-approve" adoption rate and underwriting capacity assumptions before exposing the bank to the full risk of a 30% overhead increase in a flat-rate environment. The current revenue projection is flawed; a simple 15% volume increase does not equal 15% revenue growth if net interest margins compress or if the new product attracts lower-quality borrowers.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Margin Erosion & Revenue Miscalculation:** The $2M revenue gain assumes linear growth, ignoring that rapid expansion often requires pricing concessions to win market share from the fintech competitor. If rates stay flat but you lower effective rates to compete, or if the "instant-approve" product carries higher default risks, the actual revenue could fall significantly short of the $2M target while the $1.2M fixed cost remains.
2.  **Regulatory & Compliance Bottleneck:** The assumption that current underwriting capacity can handle a 15% book increase without additional compliance staff is a critical vulnerability. A 30% staff increase in loan officers without proportional compliance support risks regulatory breaches, audit failures, and delayed closings, which would negate the speed advantage of the new branches.
3.  **Adoption Misjudgment:** If small businesses do not switch to your branches as anticipated due to loyalty to the fintech's "instant-approve" ecosystem, the new branches will generate insufficient volume to cover their $200k tech upgrade and staffing costs, turning them into immediate loss centers.

**Pre-Agreed Abort Trigger:**

If **net new loan originations** across the pilot locations do not reach **$450,000** (approx. 25% of the quarterly target required for the full 15% annual goal) by **September 30, 2025**, immediately halt further branch openings and freeze the remaining hiring plan.
