**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" condition.**
Move forward immediately with design and pilot preparation, but **condition the full $120k spend and Oct 1 launch** on receiving written confirmation from the state health board regarding remote prescription certification by **15 June 2025**. Without this regulatory clarity, the core value proposition (remote prescription) is unproven, making the projected revenue and clinic-visit reductions speculative.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Blockade:** The state health board delays certification beyond Q3 2025 or denies remote prescription capabilities entirely, rendering the app a simple logbook rather than a clinical tool, destroying the $30/month value justification.
2.  **Adherence-Revenue Mismatch:** The assumed 70% adherence rate is overly optimistic for unsupervised home exercises; if actual adherence drops below 40%, churn will likely exceed 5%, preventing the cohort from reaching the 500-patient target or sustaining the $15k monthly revenue.
3.  **Clinic Cannibalization Resistance:** Physiotherapists may resist an app that explicitly aims to cut their in-clinic visits by 10%, leading to poor internal referral rates and failure to onboard the initial patient cohort.

**Pre-Agreed Kill-Switch Condition:**
If, after the first three months of the pilot (by 1 Jan 2026), **monthly active users paying the full $30 fee drop below 150** (30% of the 500 target) OR if the **state health board has not issued a provisional certification decision by 15 June 2025**, immediately halt further development spending and pivot to a non-prescription wellness model or terminate the project.

**Hidden Cost/Risk Flag:**
The plan overlooks the **liability insurance premium increase** required for prescribing exercises remotely versus in-clinic; this operational cost is absent from the $120k budget and could erode margins if claims arise from home-exercise injuries. Additionally, the "10% reduction in visits" metric may conflict with clinic revenue models, creating internal political friction that stalls adoption regardless of patient demand.

*Note: The $15k monthly revenue projection assumes 100% collection from 500 users; verify if the $30 is patient-paid or insurer-reimbursed, as collection cycles differ vastly. The May 1 start date leaves only 45 days for the critical regulatory check before major design lock-in.*
