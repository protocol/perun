**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional approval.**

Approve the $1.8M development budget and nine-month timeline only if the project team delivers a verified regulatory compliance map for US and EU AI scheduling by October 2025 (end of Q3). Without this, the regulatory unknown creates an unacceptable risk of post-launch shutdown or costly re-engineering. The unit economics are strong ($840k ARR potential vs. $1.8M cost implies a ~2.1 year payback on development alone, excluding maintenance), and the 30% admin reduction is a compelling value prop for enterprise clients. However, the regulatory gap is a binary blocker that must be resolved before full-scale build-out.

**Main Failure Modes & Hidden Risks:**
1.  **Regulatory Whiplash:** The EU AI Act or emerging US state laws could classify automated scheduling as "high-risk," requiring expensive audits, data localization, or human-in-the-loop mandates that destroy the 30% efficiency gain.
2.  **Adoption Friction:** The 15% adoption assumption may be optimistic if HR departments fear bias lawsuits from AI-driven time slot suggestions, leading to near-zero uptake despite the cost savings.
3.  **Integration Debt:** The $1.8M budget likely underestimates the cost of integrating with the diverse, legacy ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) used by 3,500 enterprise clients, potentially blowing the timeline.
4.  **Liability Exposure:** If the AI inadvertently discriminates in scheduling (e.g., offering fewer slots to certain demographics based on historical data), the company faces direct legal liability, not just reputational damage.

**Pre-agreed Kill/Pivot Condition:**
**Kill the project** if, by October 2025, the compliance map reveals that mandatory human review is required for >20% of scheduling decisions in either the US or EU, as this invalidates the core 30% efficiency value proposition. **Pivot to a "Human-Assist" model** (lower price point, lower efficiency gain) if regulations require full audit trails but not human review.

*Note: The $500k client savings calculation assumes 100% of the 30% time reduction is monetizable or retainable; verify if clients view this as headcount reduction (savings) or capacity expansion (revenue). The $2/seat price point must be stress-tested against competitors who may bundle similar features for free.*
