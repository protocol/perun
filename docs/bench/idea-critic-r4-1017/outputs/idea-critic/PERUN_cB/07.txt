**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the $650,000 investment and July 1 pilot only if you pre-commit to a hard kill date of October 15, 2024, triggered if the pilot clinics do not demonstrate a verified >15% reduction in no-shows (half your target) by day 75. Do not commit to the full 18-month rollout based on current assumptions.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Adoption Math Mismatch:** Your revenue projection assumes 70% adoption of the *existing* 120 customers within six months. Converting 84 established clients to a new workflow in half a year is historically anomalous for dental practices; if adoption hits a realistic 30% (36 clinics), your revenue timeline extends from 18 months to over 4 years, destroying the ROI case.
2.  **Pilot Selection Bias:** Testing only with "top clinics" (high volume, likely sophisticated staff) creates a false positive. These sites have the bandwidth to manage new tools; average customers may lack the operational slack to implement SmartSchedule, leading to high churn post-pilot.
3.  **Revenue Attribution Error:** You attribute a $66,000/month lift purely to no-show reduction. However, if the system fills slots with lower-margin procedures or disrupts provider flow causing longer appointment times, the net revenue gain could be negligible despite improved attendance.

**Kill Criteria (Pre-agreed Triggers)**
*   **Adoption Trigger:** If fewer than 12 existing customers (10% of base) have fully activated the module by September 1, 2024, halt marketing spend immediately.
*   **Efficacy Trigger:** If the pilot clinics show <15% no-show reduction by October 15, 2024, terminate development of further features and sunset the pilot.
*   **Cost Trigger:** If development costs exceed $500,000 (11% overrun) before the July 1 launch, pause for a board review.

**Hidden Risks**
*   **Cannibalization:** Existing scheduling staff may view this as a threat, leading to passive resistance or data entry errors that skew pilot results.
*   **Integration Debt:** The $450k development budget likely underestimates the cost of integrating with the varied, often legacy, Practice Management Systems (PMS) used by your 120 disparate customers.
*   **Market Pull Void:** You explicitly noted zero data on new customer acquisition. Betting the 18-month return on "unknown" new signups is speculative; the financial model should exclude new customer revenue entirely until proven.
