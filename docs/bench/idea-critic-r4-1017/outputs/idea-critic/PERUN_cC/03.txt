**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks**

1.  **The Licensing Trap:** The unpriced "Chronicles of Alder" series could consume the entire $15,000 cushion. If these titles are essential for the projected 30% adoption, failing to secure them at a predictable cost renders the value proposition void.
2.  **Adoption Overestimation:** Assuming 30% adoption (3,600 patrons) in year one is aggressive. If actual adoption tracks closer to 10–15%, the cost-per-checkout will spike, making the $85,000 annual run-rate difficult to justify against other digital needs.
3.  **Cannibalization vs. Lift:** The projected 15% lift in *overall* digital checkouts may not materialize if patrons simply shift existing activity to the new platform rather than borrowing more. Without net-new usage, the investment yields no strategic growth.
4.  **Timeline Rigidity:** A hard stop on September 1 for results assumes a clean launch on June 1. Any delay in vendor onboarding or content ingestion compresses the data collection window, leaving the Director with inconclusive pilot data.
