A single resignation in the pilot wipes out the entire year's projected software ROI, making the "human factor" your largest financial risk, not mileage.

If the algorithm optimizes for distance but ignores real-time traffic patterns or delivery window constraints, drivers may burn more fuel idling or miss slots, negating the $1,800 fuel saving and increasing customer complaints.
