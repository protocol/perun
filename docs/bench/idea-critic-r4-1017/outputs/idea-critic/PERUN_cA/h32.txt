**Three Biggest Failure Risks:**
1.  **Margin Collapse from Hidden Fees:** The model assumes a 15% commission but leaves the national carrier data feed cost as a wide range ($10k–$25k) and completely excludes competitor subscription benchmarks. If the data feed requires a per-load fee rather than a flat annual rate, or if the "off-the-shelf" comparison reveals hidden operational costs you missed, your $30,000/month ops budget will be insufficient, destroying the projected 12% IRR.
2.  **Integration Feasibility & Delay:** You assume $85,000 covers integration with three carrier APIs, but without technical due diligence, this is a guess. Carrier APIs often have poor documentation, rate limits, or require expensive certification processes. If one key carrier blocks access or demands a six-month certification timeline, your 18-month break-even point slips indefinitely while fixed costs burn.
3.  **Volume Sensitivity:** The model hinges on handling 3,200 loads/month immediately. There is no ramp-up curve modeled. If adoption takes 12 months instead of six due to carrier resistance or shipper inertia, the $535,000 initial capital will be exhausted before reaching the $800,000 monthly gross revenue target, forcing a cash crunch.

**Pre-agreed Kill Trigger:**
Terminate the project immediately if, by the end of the six-week discovery tranche, you cannot secure a fixed-price contract for the national carrier data feed under $15,000/year OR if any of the three target carriers confirm that API integration will take longer than four months or cost more than $40,000 per carrier.
