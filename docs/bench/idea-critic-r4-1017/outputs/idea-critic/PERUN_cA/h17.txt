**Recommendation:** Do not launch the full 350-customer program yet. Instead, execute a **3-month pilot capped at 50 customers** to validate default rates and unit economics before committing capital. Your current revenue projection contains a critical error: you calculated $462,000 as *monthly* revenue, but that figure represents the **total contract value per month of new signings**, not recurring monthly cash flow. Actual initial monthly cash flow is roughly $350 × ($150 + $100) = $87,500, growing only as the cohort expands, while refurbishment costs ($28,000 for 350 units) and ad spend ($20,000) hit immediately. Without verified default data, the assumption of 95% on-time payments is a dangerous guess that could turn the projected $10,000 profit into a significant loss.

**Strongest Objection:** The unit economics collapse if the default rate exceeds 8% (breaking your 95% success assumption). Since you have no demographic data, a single wave of defaults could drain cash reserves before the 18-month break-even point, leaving you with depreciated inventory and unpaid refurbishment costs. Additionally, unpriced regulatory risks in CA, TX, and NY could impose legal fees or contract voids that erase margins entirely.

**Better Alternative:** Pursue a **partnership model** with an existing instrument rental platform or local music schools. Instead of bearing the capital cost of inventory and refurbishment, offer your customer acquisition channel in exchange for a revenue share. This shifts the default and inventory risk to the partner while allowing you to test customer demand for rent-to-own offers with near-zero capital exposure.

**Kill Criterion:** Terminate the pilot immediately if, after 60 days, the **net loss per active customer exceeds $15** (accounting for defaults, refurbishment, and ad spend) or if **legal counsel identifies a blocking regulatory constraint** in any of the three target states. Do not proceed to the full 350-customer launch unless the pilot demonstrates a verified net profit of at least $25 per customer per month.
