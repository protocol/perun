**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **conditional pilot** of one locker (not three) for 90 days, explicitly excluding public property placement until the permit cost is confirmed in writing.

**Why this fails:**
1.  **The Permit Trap:** If the city classifies the warehouse sidewalk as "public property," the $3,000 fee destroys your six-month break-even target, turning a projected profit into an immediate loss.
2.  **Labor Leakage:** You have not costed the peak-hour staffing required to manage locker jams or failed scans. Even two extra hours of labor weekly at standard rates could erase the margin on the "free" pickup advantage.
3.  **False Equivalency:** Comparing your $0 locker fee to a competitor's $5 two-day delivery is flawed; customers paying for speed likely value convenience over price. Your true competitor is free in-store pickup, not paid delivery.

**Kill Switch:**
Halt the rollout immediately if the **city permit fee exceeds $500** OR if **weekly utilization drops below 60% (120 pickups)** after the first 30 days. These thresholds protect your capital from regulatory surprise and validate the demand assumption before scaling to three units.
