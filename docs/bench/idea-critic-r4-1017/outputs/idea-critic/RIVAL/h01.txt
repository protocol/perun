**Recommendation: Modify before proceeding.**

The proposal is financially viable but contains a critical arithmetic error and an unrealistic operational assumption that must be addressed before presenting to the board.

**The Arithmetic Slip**
Your annualized revenue projection is incorrect. You calculated $1,180,800, but the actual figure is **$972,000** (30 slots × $45 × 2 trips × 365 days). You likely double-counted the trips or miscalculated the daily total ($2,700/day, not $3,240). This overstates first-year revenue by ~$208k. While the project likely remains profitable over time given the $2.75M upfront cost, presenting inflated numbers to a board chair damages credibility immediately.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Delay:** "A few weeks" is not a date. If approval slips to Q1 2025, you incur holding costs on the software (maintenance, server fees) with zero revenue, blowing up your ROI timeline.
2.  **Operational Friction:** Assuming "no effect on turnaround time" is risky. Reserved slots require verification. If boarding takes 2 minutes longer per vehicle to check reservations, you lose 40 minutes per trip. On a tight ferry schedule, this causes cascading delays, missed tides, and customer complaints that outweigh premium revenue.
3.  **Cannibalization:** If you simply re-label existing general admission slots as "premium" without new demand, you net zero revenue. The 30 slots must come from *new* demand or successful upselling of existing traffic, not just re-seating current passengers.

**Trigger to Stop or Pivot**
Establish a **90-day review clause**. If, after full launch, the **net occupancy (paid slots) does not exceed the pre-launch baseline by at least 15 vehicles per day** OR if **average turnaround time increases by more than 5 minutes**, immediately suspend the reserved system and revert to first-come-first-served while renegotiating the software vendor's integration scope.

**Hidden Cost Flag**
You omitted **ongoing transaction fees and system maintenance**. A reservation platform typically charges 2–3% per transaction plus a monthly SaaS fee. On $972k revenue, that's another ~$25k–$30k/year in direct costs, plus IT staff time for troubleshooting. Add a line item for "Annual OpEx: $50k" to be safe.
