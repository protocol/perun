If the GDPR content delays launch by even 8 weeks, development costs could balloon to $650k+, wiping out pilot profitability entirely before Year 1 ends.
2.  **The "80% Cross-Sell" Fallacy:** Assuming 80% of 120 customers ($9.6M opportunity) will buy a *new* add-on is unrealistic. Industry standard for security add-ons is typically 15–25%. Banking your Year 2 strategy on an 80% attach rate sets you up to miss targets by 3–4x, which will look like a sales failure rather than a planning error.
3.  **Adoption vs. Churn Contradiction:** You target 30% user adoption within pilot accounts but forecast 15% account churn. In security training, low user adoption (<30%) is usually the *leading indicator* of account churn. If users don't engage, the security posture doesn't improve, and the client cancels. These two metrics are correlated, not independent; achieving 30% adoption is the only way to prevent the 15% churn.
