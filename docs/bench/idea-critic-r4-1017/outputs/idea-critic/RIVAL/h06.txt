**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with the current proposal. The financial model contains a critical error in listener-to-impression conversion that inflates projected revenue by 95%, turning a projected profit into a significant loss.

**Primary Failure Points:**
1.  **Revenue Collapse:** You assume 5% of listeners convert to ad impressions. In radio/podcasting, an "impression" is typically counted per download/stream, not per listener converting. If you literally mean only 5% of the 250,000 listeners hear the ad, your effective audience is 12,500. At a $25 CPM, revenue per show is $312.50, not the implied $6,250. Across 12 shows, monthly revenue per advertiser drops from ~$75,000 to ~$3,750. Against $48,000 in monthly production costs ($4k x 12), you lose $44,250 per advertiser, not make $11,000.
2.  **Platform Fee Risk:** Assuming a 0% platform fee is dangerous. Standard distribution fees range from 15-30%. If a 20% fee applies to the corrected (lower) revenue, your losses deepen.
3.  **Sales Velocity:** Securing three advertisers at $15,000 each in a shrinking market within Q3 is aggressive if the value proposition (actual reach) is misunderstood by buyers during due diligence.

**Kill/Pivot Trigger:**
Terminate or restructure the bundle immediately if **audit of the last quarter's raw download data confirms that average completed listens per episode are below 200,000**, or if **pre-commitments from advertisers total fewer than two signed LOIs by August 15, 2025**. The August 15 date is moved up from September 30 because the margin for error is nonexistent; you need cash flow earlier to cover the fixed production costs that accrue daily.
