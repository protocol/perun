**Recommendation: Proceed with a Conditional Pilot, Not Full Build**

Do not commit the full $1.8M budget yet. Instead, allocate $250k for a 90-day "Regulatory & Feasibility Sprint" to build a non-AI concierge prototype and secure legal clearance in the US and EU. If clearance is granted and pilot users confirm the 30% admin time reduction, greenlight the remaining $1.55M for full AI development. If not, kill the project immediately. This protects the company from building an illegal or unusable product while validating the core value proposition.

**Main Failure Modes & Hidden Risks**

1.  **Regulatory Blockade (Critical):** The EU AI Act and emerging US state laws may classify autonomous scheduling as "high-risk," requiring expensive audits, data residency controls, or explicit user consent flows that break the UX. *Hidden Cost:* Compliance could add $400k+ and 4 months to the timeline.
2.  **Adoption Overestimation:** The 15% adoption assumption (525 clients) is aggressive for an add-on. Enterprise sales cycles for add-ons are slow; if only 5% adopt in Year 1, ARR drops to $280k, making the $1.8M investment unrecoverable for 6+ years.
3.  **Liability Exposure:** If the AI double-books a candidate with a final-round interview or violates labor laws (e.g., scheduling outside legal hours in California/France), your firm faces lawsuits. *Hidden Risk:* Your current liability insurance likely excludes AI-driven operational errors.
4.  **Integration Debt:** Connecting to 3,500 clients' disparate ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) and calendar providers (Outlook, Google, Exchange) is a engineering nightmare. *Hidden Cost:* Custom connectors could consume 40% of the dev budget, delaying launch.

**Pre-Agreed Kill/Pivot Condition**

**Kill Switch:** If, by October 2025 (end of Sprint), Legal cannot provide a written "Low-Risk" opinion for deployment in both US and EU, OR if the concierge pilot fails to demonstrate a statistically significant reduction (>20%) in recruiter admin time across 20 pilot clients.

**Pivot Trigger:** If regulatory clearance is granted only for "Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL) modes, pivot immediately to a "Copilot" model where the AI suggests slots but a human confirms them. This reduces liability but also reduces the efficiency gain; recalculate ROI based on a 15% time savings before proceeding.
