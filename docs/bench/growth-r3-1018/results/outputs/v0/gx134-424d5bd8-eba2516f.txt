### Growth Measurement Framework: Healthcare Graduate Tier (RefiLondon)

**1. North Star Metric: "Verified Active Repayment"**
Reject cumulative sign-ups as the primary success metric; they are vanity figures that rise regardless of loan health. Instead, adopt **Verified Active Repayment** as the North Star. This counts a customer only after they have completed three consecutive on-time payments post-grace period. This metric rises only when the user derives value (successful refinancing) and the business realizes low-risk revenue. It inherently balances acquisition speed with risk management by filtering out borrowers who default early or fail to engage.

**2. Funnel Diagnosis: Read Bottom-Up (Retention First)**
Do not optimize for the top of the funnel (applications) until the bottom is secure. Pouring marketing spend into a leaky retention bucket wastes capital. Diagnose in this order:
*   **Retention (Risk Health):** Are borrowers making on-time payments in months 1–3? If this leaks, acquisition is irrelevant.
*   **Activation:** Did the user complete the full verification and first disbursement?
*   **Acquisition:** Only after retention and activation are stable, measure application volume.

**3. Event Taxonomy: Instrument Only Named Questions**
Do not instrument "everything." Define events strictly to answer specific hypotheses regarding the healthcare demographic's decision drivers:
*   *Hypothesis:* "Repayment flexibility drives conversion more than rate."
    *   **Event:** `calculator_scenario_saved` (Property: `scenario_type` = "income_contingent" vs "fixed").
    *   **Question:** Do users who model flexible scenarios convert at a higher rate than those who only view the lowest rate?
*   *Hypothesis:* "Customer service availability is a blocker."
    *   **Event:** `support_channel_selected` (Property: `channel` = "chat" vs "email" vs "phone").
    *   **Question:** Does access to live chat correlate with higher completion rates for complex healthcare income profiles?
*   *Risk Indicator:* `document_upload_abandonment` (Property: `document_type`).
    *   **Question:** Where do high-debt users drop off during verification?

**4. Leading Indicators vs. Lagging Volume**
To predict long-term loan health rather than just Q1 volume, track these leading indicators:
*   **Documentation Completion Rate:** The % of applicants who fully upload income/degree proof within 48 hours of starting. Low completion suggests friction or buyer remorse before the loan books.
*   **Scenario Engagement Depth:** The number of repayment scenarios modeled per user. High engagement with "flexibility" tools predicts higher satisfaction and lower default risk for this demographic.
*   **Early Payment Promptness:** Time between first due date and actual payment. Delays here are a stronger predictor of future default than credit score at origination.

**5. Experiment Rigor & Compliance**
*   **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** Do not compare conversion rates to industry averages or other sectors. Compare the Healthcare Tier's performance solely against RefiLondon's own historical baseline for other segments.
*   **UK Regulatory Constraints:** Ensure all user identifiers used for cohort analysis are pseudonymous. Route any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) handling and consent logic to the privacy compliance protocol; do not store raw NHS numbers or full names in analytics event logs.
*   **A/B Testing:** If testing messaging (e.g., "Low Rate" vs. "Flexible Terms"), pre-commit to a sample size and read results once. Do not stop early because a metric looks significant; this inflates false positives.
