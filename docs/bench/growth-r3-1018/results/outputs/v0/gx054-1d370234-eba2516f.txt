**Recommendation: North Star Metric**
**"Weekly Active Recruiters who send ≥1 Interview Request within 48 hours of viewing a profile with video."**

Do not optimize for raw speed (time spent) or final offers yet. Optimize for **activated efficiency**: recruiters moving fast *and* taking the core action that proves interest.

**Trade-off Analysis**

1.  **Reduction in time spent per profile (Reject as North Star):**
    *   *Risk:* This is a vanity efficiency metric. Recruiters could spend less time because they are confused, the video failed to load, or they are skipping critical qualification steps. Faster rejection is good; faster *decision* is the goal. If you optimize only for speed, you may incentivize superficial screening that lowers match quality later.
    *   *Verdict:* Use as a **guardrail** only. If time drops but interview requests do not rise, the change failed.

2.  **Final offer acceptance rate (Reject as North Star):**
    *   *Risk:* This is too distant and noisy for a single test cycle. The path from "profile view" to "offer accepted" involves candidate availability, salary negotiation, and external factors unrelated to the profile page design. With only one major test cycle, you will not gather enough statistical power to detect a signal here.
    *   *Verdict:* Too lagging. It measures business outcome, not product behavior change.

3.  **Increase in interview requests (Select as Core Component):**
    *   *Benefit:* This is the direct "value moment" for the recruiter. If video intros work, recruiters should feel confident enough to request an interview sooner.
    *   *Refinement:* Measuring *raw* interview requests risks encouraging spammy, low-quality outreach. You must pair volume with a **recency constraint** (48 hours) to capture the "speed" benefit without losing the "quality" signal. If a recruiter watches a video and immediately requests an interview, the video did its job.

**Why This Aligns with Sustainable Growth**
For HireRemote, sustainable growth means reducing the 45-day time-to-hire *without* increasing churn due to bad matches. By measuring recruiters who act quickly (efficiency) and decisively (quality signal), you validate that video intros reduce friction while maintaining confidence. If this metric rises, it proves the feature accelerates the funnel's top without breaking the match quality downstream.

**Experiment Design Note for Mateo**
*   **Primary Metric:** % of Weekly Active Recruiters sending ≥1 interview request within 48h of viewing a video-enabled profile.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Average time-to-reject (ensure we aren't just rejecting faster) and downstream "interview-to-offer" conversion rate (ensure match quality holds).
*   **Decision Rule:** Ship if Primary Metric lifts by ≥5% with no statistically significant drop in the guardrail. If the primary lifts but the guardrail drops, the speed gain is an illusion caused by lower quality screening.
