### Referral Program Success Framework: NeoVida

**North Star Metric: Referred User Activation Rate**
Define success not by invitations sent or bonuses claimed, but by the percentage of referred users who complete the **Core Value Action** within 14 days of signup. For NeoVida freelancers, this is likely "receiving first inbound payment" or "completing KYC + making first outbound transfer."
*   **Why:** Cash incentives drive signups (vanity); only product value drives retention. If a referred user claims the €10 but never uses the account for banking, they are a cost center, not growth. This metric filters out fraud rings and low-intent users immediately.

**Primary Metrics (The Growth Engine)**
1.  **Qualified Referral Conversion Rate:** The % of invited emails/links that result in a user hitting the Core Value Action.
    *   *Target:* Establish a baseline in Week 1; optimize for trend, not an external benchmark.
2.  **Net Viral Coefficient (k) of Activated Users:** Calculate $k$ using only *activated* referrers, not all signups.
    *   *Formula:* (Active Referrers) × (Avg Invites per Active Referrer) × (Activation Rate of Invitees).
    *   *Goal:* Ensure $k > 0$ for *value-generating* users. Your current projection of 0.8 invites is risky if the activation rate of those invitees is low.
3.  **Time-to-Value Delta:** Compare the days-to-activation for referred users vs. organic users.
    *   *Hypothesis Check:* If referred users take significantly longer to activate, the cash bonus may be attracting users who don't need the product, only the €10.

**Secondary Metrics (Guardrails & Quality)**
1.  **Fraud Signal Ratio:** The % of referred pairs where both accounts exhibit "bonus-only" behavior (e.g., KYC passed → Bonus claimed → No further login for 30 days).
    *   *Action:* If this exceeds 5% of total referrals, pause the campaign. This is your tripwire.
2.  **Referrer Retention Lift:** Do users who successfully refer a friend show higher *own* retention than those who don't?
    *   *Reasoning:* A successful referral reinforces the referrer's commitment to the platform. If referrer retention drops post-bonus, the incentive is cannibalizing loyalty.
3.  **Cost Per Activated User (CPAU):** Total bonus spend divided by *activated* referred users, not total signups.
    *   *Constraint:* Must remain below your Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) threshold.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)**
Do not track "link clicks" as a primary success metric. Instrument these specific events to answer named questions:
*   `referral_link_sent` (Who is inviting?)
*   `signup_completed` (Did they join?)
*   `core_action_completed` (Did they get value? **This is the gate for the bonus payout.**)
*   `bonus_claimed` (Track timing relative to core action).

**Critical Implementation Rules**
*   **Payout Logic:** Decouple the bonus from simple signup. Pay the €10 only after the referee completes the **Core Value Action**. This single change eliminates most fraud rings and aligns with your "immediate liquidity" hypothesis by rewarding *usage*, not just registration.
*   **Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous user ID to link referrer/referee pairs for cohort analysis. Do not use raw PII for analytics queries; route identity mapping to your privacy compliance layer.
*   **Baseline Comparison:** Do not compare your activation rates to industry averages. Compare Week 2 performance against Week 1. If activation drops while signups rise, you are buying low-quality users.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  Define the exact "Core Value Action" SQL query with engineering today; without this definition, the program cannot launch.
2.  Set up a dashboard showing the **Fraud Signal Ratio** daily; if it spikes above 5%, the automatic trigger is to pause payouts pending review.
3.  Run a dry-run calculation: If 1,000 users invite 0.8 people each, and only 20% activate, your effective CPA is €50 (€10 bonus / 0.2), not €12.50. Validate this math against your LTV before October.

*Note: The assumption that cash drives "higher quality" than fee waivers is unverified until the Time-to-Value Delta is measured; be prepared to pivot if fee waivers yield faster activation.*
