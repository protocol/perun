### Proposed Conversion Funnel & Event Taxonomy

To pinpoint friction for Mehmet's sprint planning, reject "photo upload" as a single step. It is a black box that hides whether users fail at permission, capture, or confirmation. Define the funnel by **named questions** and instrument only those events.

**1. The Core Funnel Stages (Activation Focus)**
Since you are in `mvp` stage (beta launched March 5), ignore acquisition vanity (4,000 downloads) and focus entirely on the leak between "Install" and "Value Delivered" (Completed Estimate).

*   **Step 0: App Open** (Baseline)
*   **Step 1: Estimate Started** (User enters flow)
*   **Step 2: Camera Permission Requested** (System prompt shown)
    *   *Question:* Do users deny access immediately?
*   **Step 3: Photo Captured** (Image taken/uploaded)
    *   *Question:* Is the drop-off in the act of taking the photo or the prior permission?
*   **Step 4: Photo Confirmed** (User accepts the specific image)
    *   *Question:* Do users retake endlessly or abandon after seeing the preview?
*   **Step 5: Estimate Completed** (North Star Input: Value Delivered)

**2. Handling Drafts & Return Users (The Identifier Seam)**
You noted 15% save drafts. To track this without fabricating data, you must link sessions to a **stable, pseudonymous user ID** (not raw PII).
*   **Event:** `estimate_saved_draft` (Property: `step_reached`, `timestamp`)
*   **Event:** `estimate_resumed` (Property: `time_since_save`, `original_step`)
*   **Analysis:** Create a **Retention Cohort** specifically for "Users who saved a draft." Compare their `estimate_resumed` → `estimate_completed` rate against users who finished in one session. If the resume rate is low, the friction isn't the upload complexity; it's the re-engagement mechanism.

**3. Immediate Actions for Mehmet (Starting April 10)**
Do not wait for "more data." Instrument these exact events now to validate the hypothesis:
1.  **Instrument the Split:** Break the current "photo_upload" event into `permission_requested`, `photo_captured`, and `photo_confirmed`.
2.  **Define the Tripwire:** If `photo_captured` / `permission_requested` < 80%, the friction is **camera access/UX**. If `photo_confirmed` / `photo_captured` < 90%, the friction is **image quality/preview**.
3.  **Query for Drafts:** Run a cohort query: "Of users who triggered `estimate_saved_draft` in Week 1, how many triggered `estimate_resumed` within 48 hours?"

**Verdict on Hypothesis:** Your 25% lift hypothesis is `UNVERIFIED` until the split events above are live. If the data shows 95% pass permission but only 40% confirm the photo, simplifying "access" will yield 0% lift; you must simplify the "preview/confirm" UI instead.
