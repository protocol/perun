### Analysis: The Click-Completion Divergence

Your data reveals a classic **vanity metric trap**: the badge system successfully optimized for the wrong behavior. The 35% rise in button clicks is a measure of *curiosity* or *reward-seeking*, not *value delivery*. The 12% drop in completed modules indicates that the new incentives are either distracting users from the core task (math problems) or attracting a lower-intent cohort that clicks but quits when faced with actual work.

**Diagnosis (Bottom-Up Funnel)**
Do not pour more acquisition or engagement features into this leak. The failure is at **Retention of the Core Action** (completing a module), not Activation (clicking start).
1.  **Distraction Hypothesis:** Users click, get overwhelmed by the leaderboard/badge UI, and abandon the lesson before starting.
2.  **Mismatch Hypothesis:** The "gamified" users are different from your core learners; they want quick wins, find the math too hard relative to the reward, and drop off.
3.  **Instrumentation Gap:** You are measuring the *entry* (click) but not the *quality* of the session between entry and completion.

### What to Measure Next (Event Taxonomy)

Stop tracking "button clicks" as a success metric. Instrument only these three events to answer specific questions before the June 15th design sprint:

| Event Name | Question It Answers | Properties to Capture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `lesson_start_to_first_problem` | **Are users distracted immediately?** Measures time/drop-off between clicking "Start" and seeing the first math question. | `time_to_first_problem`, `exit_point` (did they close on the badge screen?) |
| `problem_attempt_abandonment` | **Is the difficulty mismatched?** Tracks if users quit *during* the module after the gamification update. | `problems_attempted`, `time_spent`, `difficulty_level` |
| `reward_view_without_completion` | **Are users farming clicks?** Counts views of the badge/leaderboard screen by users who did *not* complete a module that day. | `user_cohort` (new vs. returning), `session_count` |

**The Identifier Seam:** Ensure these events link to a **stable, pseudonymous user ID** to track individual retention cohorts (e.g., "Users who saw badges on May 20 vs. those who didn't"). Do not use raw PII; route identity mapping to your privacy compliance protocol.

### Immediate Action Plan (This Week)

1.  **Segment the Drop-off:** Run a cohort analysis comparing completion rates for users who viewed the leaderboard vs. those who ignored it. If viewers have lower completion, the UI is the distraction.
2.  **Define the "Core Action" North Star:** Reset your dashboard. Your North Star is **Weekly Active Completers** (users finishing ≥3 modules/week), not "Clicks" or "Badges Earned."
3.  **A/B Test Design (Pre-committed):** Before June 15, design a test where Group A sees the current badges, and Group B sees a simplified version (badges hidden until *after* completion).
    *   **Primary Metric:** Module Completion Rate.
    *   **Guardrail Metric:** Daily Active Users (ensure hiding badges doesn't kill return visits).
    *   **Rule:** Pre-commit to a 14-day run or a fixed sample size (e.g., 2,000 users per variant). Do not peek at day 2; a early "win" in clicks is likely false.

**Verdict:** The badges are currently cannibalizing your core value. Hide the rewards until *after* the work is done to realign incentives with completion.
