### Pilot Measurement Plan: RoboCare Riyadh Deployment

**1. The North Star: Clinical Rounds Completed per Shift**
Reject "nurse travel time saved" as your primary metric; it is an input, not a value outcome. If the robot saves time but doctors refuse to use it due to latency, the business fails. Your North Star must be **Completed Remote Rounds per Shift**. This rises only when the technology delivers real clinical value. Pair this with two input metrics: **Average Video Latency (ms)** and **Session Completion Rate** (rounds started vs. finished without technical abort).

**2. Event Taxonomy: Instrument Only What Answers a Question**
Do not log every sensor reading. Instrument exactly three events to answer specific pilot questions:
*   `round_started`: Answers "Are staff attempting to use the unit?" (Properties: hospital_id, shift_time).
*   `video_quality_degraded`: Answers "Is Wi-Fi infrastructure causing friction?" (Properties: latency_ms, packet_loss, duration_seconds). Trigger only when latency exceeds 200ms for >5 seconds.
*   `round_aborted_technical`: Answers "Did infrastructure failure kill the workflow?" (Properties: reason_code, time_elapsed).

**3. Reading the Funnel Bottom-Up (Retention First)**
Do not measure adoption by counting logins (vanity). Read the data in this order:
*   **Retention/Quality First:** Look at `video_quality_degraded` frequency. If >15% of rounds experience degradation, acquisition is wasted. The Wi-Fi constraint is your bottleneck, not staff willingness.
*   **Activation Second:** Check `round_aborted_technical`. If doctors start rounds but abort due to lag, the "20-minute savings" hypothesis is invalid because the workflow is broken.
*   **Acquisition Last:** Only analyze usage volume once quality and completion are stable.

**4. The Decision Framework: Proceed, Pause, or Pivot**
You requested a decision rule for the COO and procurement directors based on the first two weeks. Use this strict logic:
*   **PROCEED to Full Rollout:** If `Session Completion Rate` > 90% AND average `video_quality_degraded` incidents are < 2 per shift per hospital. This proves the infrastructure holds and the workflow delivers value.
*   **PAUSE for Infrastructure Upgrade:** If `round_aborted_technical` > 10% of attempts AND correlates directly with `video_quality_degraded` spikes. Do not blame the product; the Wi-Fi is the blocker. Fix the network before March 15.
*   **PIVOT Workflow:** If completion is high but `Completed Remote Rounds` remains low despite staff availability. The issue is not technical but procedural; the "20-minute savings" assumption may not align with actual hospital routines.

**5. Critical Warning on Your Hypothesis**
Your assumption that "reducing travel time correlates with adoption" is **UNVERIFIED** until the pilot runs. It is possible that saving 20 minutes is irrelevant if the video quality makes diagnosis feel unsafe. Do not optimize for time savings if the `round_aborted_technical` rate is high. Safety and reliability trump efficiency in a hospital context.
