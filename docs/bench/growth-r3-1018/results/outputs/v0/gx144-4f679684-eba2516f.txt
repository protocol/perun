### Proposal: Privacy-First Aggregate Measurement for HealthConnect

To evaluate user experience and infer friction points without storing Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or using device fingerprinting, we propose an instrumentation strategy based on **stateless, aggregated event counts** and **cohort-level metadata**. This approach satisfies strict privacy mandates while providing the data needed to diagnose the 20% completion rate.

**1. The North Star Metric**
*   **Metric:** Weekly Completed Appointments (Aggregate Count).
*   **Definition:** The total number of users who successfully reach the "Confirmation" state per week.
*   **Why:** This measures actual citizen value delivered, distinct from vanity metrics like pageviews or session starts.

**2. Funnel Instrumentation (Stateless Aggregation)**
Instead of tracking individual user paths, we will instrument discrete, anonymous counters at each critical step of the booking flow. We do not link these events to a user ID; we only count occurrences.
*   **Event Taxonomy:**
    *   `booking_started`: Count of sessions entering the flow.
    *   `eligibility_viewed`: Count of sessions reaching the criteria page.
    *   `eligibility_error_triggered`: Count of sessions where the "Not Eligible" logic fires (validates the confusion hypothesis).
    *   `booking_abandoned`: Count of sessions exiting after `booking_started` but before `booking_completed`.
    *   `booking_completed`: Count of successful transactions.
*   **Analysis Method:** Calculate conversion rates between steps (e.g., `eligibility_viewed` / `booking_started`). A sharp drop between "Started" and "Eligibility Viewed" indicates navigation confusion; a high count of `eligibility_error_triggered` confirms the hypothesis that criteria clarity is the blocker.

**3. Inferring Device Friction Without Fingerprinting**
Since unique device IDs are restricted, we will rely on **User-Agent string aggregation** processed at the edge or within the secure enclave before analytics ingestion.
*   **Method:** Map User-Agent strings to broad categories (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop) immediately upon request, discard the raw string, and increment a counter for that category.
*   **Metric:** `completion_rate_by_device_category`.
*   **Insight:** If `completion_rate` for the "Mobile" aggregate is significantly lower than "Desktop," we infer a responsive design or mobile-UX friction point without ever identifying a specific device or user.

**4. Temporal Cohort Analysis**
To measure retention and return visits without PII:
*   **Method:** Group events by **Date of First Visit** (anonymized). Compare the volume of `booking_completed` events in Week 1 vs. Week 2 for the cohort that started in Week 1.
*   **Constraint:** Do not attempt to link specific users returning; measure the *flow volume* of the cohort over time.

**5. Compliance & Governance Guardrails**
*   **Data Minimization:** No event properties shall contain names, health card numbers, IP addresses, or precise timestamps (round to the nearest hour).
*   **Threshold Suppression:** Apply a minimum threshold (e.g., k=10) to all reported aggregates to prevent statistical deduction of individuals in low-traffic segments.
*   **Verification:** All instrumentation code must undergo a privacy impact assessment (PIA) to ensure no transient IDs are logged to disk.

**Immediate Next Steps**
1.  Define the exact JSON schema for the five aggregate events above, ensuring zero PII fields.
2.  Deploy a pilot to the staging environment to verify that `eligibility_error_triggered` counts align with known test cases.
3.  Present the "Device Category" aggregation logic to legal counsel for sign-off on the User-Agent parsing method.

This framework shifts the focus from "who is failing" to "where is the flow breaking," providing actionable engineering insights while maintaining absolute citizen anonymity.

*Note: This proposal assumes the backend can categorize User-Agents before logging; if not, this logic must be implemented in a privacy-preserving middleware layer.*
*All metrics derived are `UNVERIFIED` until the proposed instrumentation is deployed and data begins flowing.*
