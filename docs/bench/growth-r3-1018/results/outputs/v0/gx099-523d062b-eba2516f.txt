### Diagnostic Plan: DesignFlow Trial Conversion Leak

**1. Quantitative Cohort Analysis (Pinpointing the "Where")**
Stop analyzing the funnel as a single aggregate. Slice the August data (2,000 starts, 120 converts) into these specific behavioral cohorts to locate the drop-off:
*   **The "Core Action" Cohorts:** Segment users by whether they completed the specific "advanced export" action within Days 1–3. Compare the conversion rate of those who did vs. those who didn't. If the hypothesis is correct, the conversion gap between these two groups should be massive.
*   **Time-to-Value Cohorts:** Group users by the day they first performed *any* export. Plot retention curves for "Day 1 exporters" vs. "Day 3+ exporters." A steep cliff for late exporters confirms the urgency of the feature discovery window.
*   **Feature Depth Segments:** Create cohorts based on the number of unique tools used in the first 48 hours. Determine if non-converters are "one-trick ponies" (using only basic tools) versus those exploring deeply but failing to find the specific export path.
*   **Traffic Source vs. Intent:** Segment by acquisition channel. If paid ads bring users expecting enterprise features while organic search brings hobbyists, the "leak" may be a mismatch in expectation, not product utility.

**2. Qualitative Validation (Confirming the "Why")**
Quantitative data shows *where* they leave; qualitative data reveals *why*.
*   **Session Replay Sampling:** Randomly select 20 recordings of users who started a trial, attempted an export (or navigated near the export menu), and then churned. Look specifically for "rage clicks," repeated navigation loops, or abrupt exits after hitting the export menu.
*   **The "Almost-Converted" Interview:** Recruit 5–7 users who reached Day 10–12 of the trial but did not convert. Conduct 30-minute interviews focusing on their mental model of the pricing page and their confidence in finding necessary features. Ask: "At what point did you doubt this tool would work for your professional workflow?"
*   **Pricing Perception Survey:** Embed a one-question micro-survey on the cancellation screen or Day 13 popup: "What is the primary reason you aren't upgrading?" with options distinguishing between "Missing Feature X," "Too Expensive," and "Didn't have time to evaluate."

**3. Immediate Instrumentation & Next Steps**
*   **Verify the Event Taxonomy:** Ensure your analytics tool is explicitly tracking `export_attempted`, `export_failed`, and `export_menu_viewed`. If these events are not currently logged, you cannot validate the hypothesis; instrument them immediately for new trials.
*   **Define the "Aha" Moment:** Calculate the exact correlation between "advanced export completed" and "paid conversion." If users who export advanced files convert at 40% while others convert at 2%, your North Star for the trial becomes "Advanced Export Completed within 72 Hours."

**Deliverable for Nov 1st:** A report presenting the conversion delta between "Early Exporters" and "Non-Exporters," supported by 3 specific UX friction points identified in session replays, and a clear recommendation to either redesign the onboarding flow to force-export or adjust pricing tiers based on feature access.

*Note: Do not fabricate industry benchmark conversion rates; compare all findings strictly against DesignFlow's own historical baselines. Ensure all user identifiers in replays are pseudonymized before sharing with leadership.*
