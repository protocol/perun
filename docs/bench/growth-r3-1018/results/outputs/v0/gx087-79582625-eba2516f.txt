**North Star Metric: Weekly Active Residents Informed (WARI)**
Define "Informed" as a unique resident completing a **Core Value Action**: reading an article relevant to their specific neighborhood or interest tag, OR clicking a link in the SMS digest. Do not use "Time Spent" or "Total Pageviews." A user reading one critical school board update for 45 seconds is more valuable than a user scrolling aimlessly for 10 minutes. WARI counts unique users (app + SMS linked) who complete this action at least once per week. This shifts the goal from addiction to utility.

**Input Metrics (The Levers)**
1.  **Breaking News Activation Rate:** The percentage of users who open the app or SMS within 2 hours of a "Breaking" tag being published. This measures your speed and relevance during spikes (football games, meetings).
2.  **Community Share Velocity:** The count of articles shared to local social groups (via native share sheet events) per 1,000 active users. This proves you are the conversation starter, not just a passive feed.
3.  **Digest-to-App Bridge:** The percentage of SMS digest recipients who click through to the app for "read more" details. This unifies your channels.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)**
*   `content_consumed`: Fires when an article is opened. *Properties:* `topic_tag` (e.g., "School Board"), `source` (App/SMS), `user_neighborhood`. **Question answered:** Did we deliver relevant local info?
*   `content_shared`: Fires on the native share button click. *Properties:* `destination_app` (e.g., Facebook, WhatsApp), `article_id`. **Question answered:** Is this content compelling enough to spread locally?
*   `breaking_alert_opened`: Fires when a push/SMS notification is tapped. **Question answered:** Do residents trust us for urgent updates?

**Measurement Strategy for the Editorial Board**
*   **Unify Channels:** You cannot measure success if SMS and App are siloed. Implement a pseudonymous stable user ID. When a user clicks an SMS link, pass a token that identifies them as the same entity as the app user (or creates a unified profile). Without this, your "reach" is artificially fragmented.
*   **Cohort Analysis over Averages:** Do not report "Average Daily Users." Report "Retention of Users Acquired During Football Season" vs. "Retention of Users Acquired During School Board Controversy." This tells advertisers which content types build habitual readers.
*   **Advertiser Proof Point:** Create a weekly dashboard showing **WARI by Neighborhood**. Advertisers care about density. Showing "800 informed residents in Oak Creek North" is more sellable than "3,200 total downloads."

*Note: "Time spent" is a vanity metric here; a quick, successful lookup is better than a long, confused session. If you cannot link SMS to a user ID yet, report SMS metrics as a separate, UNVERIFIED channel until the instrumentation is complete.*
