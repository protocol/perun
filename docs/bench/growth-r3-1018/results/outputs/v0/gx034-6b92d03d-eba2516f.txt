### PawsProtect Beta Measurement Plan (UK)

**1. The North Star Metric**
**Weekly Active Users Completing a Valid Quote Request.**
Reject "Total Pageviews" or "Cumulative Signups" as vanity metrics; they rise regardless of value. A "Valid Quote" must include completed data entry (pet age, breed, postcode) to filter low-intent traffic. This metric rises only when a user receives actual value (a price comparison) and signals business potential.

**2. The Funnel: Read Bottom-Up (Retention & Quality First)**
Do not optimize acquisition until the bottom of the funnel holds. Diagnose in this order:
*   **Retention/Quality (The Leak):** % of Quote Requests that result in a confirmed partner lead (click-through) *and* subsequent feedback loop (see "Offline Gap" below). If users request quotes but never click partner links, the quote output is irrelevant or confusing.
*   **Activation:** % of visitors who complete a Valid Quote.
*   **Acquisition:** Total visitors.
*   *Warning:* Pouring traffic into a funnel where users drop off before clicking partner links wastes your £5,000 visitor budget.

**3. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not track "everything." Instrument these specific events:
*   `quote_started`: *Question:* Where do users abandon the form? (Properties: step_number, drop_reason).
*   `quote_completed_valid`: *Question:* Are we generating usable leads? (Properties: pet_type, postcode_region).
*   `partner_link_clicked`: *Question:* Is the comparison result relevant? (Properties: partner_id, premium_range).
*   `offline_conversion_signal`: *Question:* How do we close the loop? (See below).

**4. Solving the "Offline Purchase" Gap**
You cannot track offline purchases directly without integration. Do not fabricate a conversion rate.
*   **Action:** Implement a "Post-Quote Survey" triggered 7 days after a quote: *"Did you purchase a policy? If yes, which provider?"*
*   **Action:** Negotiate a weekly CSV data share with top 3 partners matching on anonymized email hashes or quote IDs to reconcile clicks vs. sales.
*   **Status:** Until this data exists, the "Quote-to-Purchase" metric is **UNVERIFIED**. Report it as such to investors; do not estimate.

**5. Leading vs. Lagging Indicators**
*   **Daily (Leading):** Valid Quote Completion Rate; Partner Link Click-Through Rate (CTR) per quote; Form Abandonment Rate by step. These tell you if the product works *today*.
*   **Monthly (Lagging):** Estimated Policy Conversion Rate (via survey/partner data); Cost Per Acquired Lead; Cohort Retention (do users return for renewal checks?).

**6. Handling the April Seasonality Spike**
Do not compare April raw numbers to March.
*   **Method:** Track **Rates**, not absolutes. If traffic doubles but Quote Completion Rate stays flat, the product is stable. If traffic doubles and Completion Rate drops, the new "adoption" traffic is lower intent or the UX is breaking under load.
*   **Cohorting:** Tag all April users with `cohort: adoption_spike_2024`. Compare their retention solely against other high-volume cohorts, not low-volume beta months.

**7. This Week's Steps**
1.  **Define "Valid Quote":** Engineering to enforce form completion rules (no empty breeds/postcodes) before logging `quote_completed_valid`.
2.  **Build the Loop:** Draft the 7-day post-quote email survey to capture offline purchases; launch with the beta.
3.  **Baseline the Drop-off:** Run a query on form abandonment by step for the first 500 test users to identify the primary friction point before scaling to 5,000 visitors.

*Note: No industry benchmarks for "good" pet insurance conversion are asserted here; success is defined by your own baseline trend. Ensure user IDs used for cohorting are pseudonymous and routed to your privacy compliance protocol.*
