### North Star Metric Evaluation for CookConnect

**Recommendation:** Adopt **"Weekly Active Engagers"** defined as users who perform at least two high-value community actions (comments, recipe uploads, or thread replies) within a 7-day window.

While "Total Recipes Saved" is a strong indicator of intent, your beta data proves it is a vanity metric for retention; saving is passive. "Daily Active Users" measures traffic, not value. The metric that correlates with your 45-day retention lift is *active discussion*. Therefore, your North Star must measure the frequency of that specific behavior, not the accumulation of static content.

#### Comparison of Candidates

| Metric Candidate | Definition | Alignment with Vision | Primary Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **1. Total Recipes Saved** (Current Assumption) | Cumulative count of "save" actions across the platform. | **Low.** Measures library size, not kitchen usage. A user can save 100 recipes and never cook one. | **False Growth.** This number only goes up. It will look impressive on a chart while retention flatlines, misleading investors about product-market fit. It ignores your own data showing savers churn faster than discussers. |
| **2. Daily Active Users (DAU)** | Count of unique users opening the app per day. | **Medium.** Shows reach, but not depth. An 8-minute session could be mindless scrolling, not "kitchen companion" utility. | **Vanity & Noise.** DAU spikes with marketing spend but doesn't guarantee community health. It fails to distinguish between a user finding a recipe in 30 seconds (high value) and a user scrolling aimlessly for 10 minutes (low value). |
| **3. Weekly Active Engagers** (Recommended) | Users completing ≥2 deep interactions (comments, posts, replies) per week. | **High.** Directly ties to the 45-day retention lift observed in beta. Measures the *exchange* of value, which defines a community. | **Scaling Friction.** As you grow from 5k to 50k users, maintaining high discussion density is harder. If new users don't find active threads, this metric could stall. This is a product challenge, not a measurement error, and highlights exactly where you must invest (onboarding into conversations). |

#### Strategic Rationale for Investors
Maria, presenting "Weekly Active Engagers" signals to investors that CookConnect understands its own leverage point. You are not optimizing for screen time or database size; you are optimizing for the specific behavior (discussion) that your data proves creates long-term users.

The risk of scaling discussion is a known operational hurdle, whereas the risk of "Saved Recipes" is a fundamental flaw in the business model assumption. Investors prefer a harder metric that proves unit economics (retention) over an easy metric that hides leakage.

#### Immediate Next Steps
1.  **Instrumentation:** Verify that your event taxonomy explicitly tags "comment," "reply," and "upload" as distinct from "save" or "view" to calculate this metric accurately.
2.  **Baseline Establishment:** Calculate the current percentage of your 5,000 users who are "Weekly Active Engagers" to set the baseline for the Series A deck.
3.  **Cohort Analysis:** Run a retention cohort analysis specifically on users who hit this threshold in their first week versus those who do not, to quantify the lift for the pitch.
