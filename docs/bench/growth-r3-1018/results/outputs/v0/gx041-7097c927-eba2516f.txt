2.  **Invoice-to-Login Latency:** Measure the time delta between invoice generation (showing the variable rate) and the next user login or support contact. If the drop in engagement happens *before* the invoice arrives, the issue is broader market perception or competitor activity.
3.  **Feature Utilization vs. Price Sensitivity:** Correlate the specific peak hours where prices spiked with usage volume for the affected accounts. If usage dropped precisely during high-price windows, it is a direct price elasticity reaction. If usage remained flat but renewal intent dropped, the issue is psychological (trust/fairness) rather than economic (cost).
