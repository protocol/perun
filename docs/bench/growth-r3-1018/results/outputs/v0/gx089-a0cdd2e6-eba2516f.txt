For LearnLagos, stop tracking "quiz completion rates" immediately. This is a vanity metric that rises even if students are guessing, masking the fact that they may not be learning. Your North Star must be **Weekly Active Learners Completing Core Actions with Mastery**, defined specifically as: *students who return within 7 days and correctly answer a question they previously missed, without hints.*

Do not try to measure everything. With zero budget and spreadsheet exports, focus only on these three manual calculations to validate learning and guide Tunde's next build:

1.  **The "Guessing" Ratio (Retry Rate per Question)**
    *   **The Question:** Are students learning or just clicking until it works?
    *   **The Metric:** Count the average number of attempts before a correct answer for each specific question ID.
    *   **How to Calculate:** Export your logs. Filter for `question_id`. Count total attempts vs. unique users who eventually solved it.
    *   **The Signal:** If the average attempts > 3 and time-to-solve is < 10 seconds, students are guessing.
    *   **Action for Tunde:** If this ratio is high on specific questions, do not build new content. Fix those questions or add a "cool-down" timer that forces a 5-minute wait after two failed attempts before allowing a retry. This kills random guessing.

2.  **Retention of the Core Action (Weekly Return Frequency)**
    *   **The Question:** Do students find enough value to come back next week?
    *   **The Metric:** Cohort Retention. Of the students who completed a lesson in Week 1, what percentage completed *any* lesson in Week 2?
    *   **How to Calculate:** Create a simple spreadsheet. Row 1: User IDs active in Week 1. Column B: Did they log an activity in Week 2? (Yes/No). Average the "Yes" column.
    *   **The Signal:** If this number is below 20%, your content isn't sticking, regardless of how many signups you have. Acquisition is wasted money if this leaks.
    *   **Action for Tunde:** If retention is low, stop building new levels. Build a "nudge" system (simple email or SMS) that triggers 3 days after a user's last visit, referencing the specific topic they left off on.

3.  **Mastery Verification (Time-Weighted Accuracy)**
    *   **The Question:** Is speed masking a lack of understanding?
    *   **The Metric:** Average time spent on correct answers vs. incorrect answers.
    *   **How to Calculate:** Sum `time_spent` for all `correct=true` events and divide by count. Do the same for `correct=false`.
    *   **The Signal:** If `time_spent` for correct answers is suspiciously low (e.g., under 5 seconds for a complex math problem), it confirms guessing. Real learning takes cognitive load and time.
    *   **Action for Tunde:** Implement a "confidence score" feature where students must self-rate how sure they are before submitting. Correlate this with actual results in your spreadsheet to identify over-confident guessers.
