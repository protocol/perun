**Success Criteria for Digital Mortgage Approval Pilot**

**1. Risk Guardrail: Non-Inferiority of Default Rates**
To satisfy the Chief Risk Officer, the pilot will not be deemed a failure based on speed alone, but strictly on risk performance. The 30-day early-delinquency rate (payments missed within the first month) for the 200-user pilot group must not exceed the baseline early-delinquency rate of traditional mortgage approvals by more than 0.5 percentage points. If the pilot default rate exceeds this threshold, the feature triggers an automatic pause for review, regardless of satisfaction scores. This ensures that speed does not come at the cost of underwriting quality or applicant reconsideration time.

**2. Growth Metric: Activation Conversion Rate**
To satisfy the Head of Retail Banking, we measure actual commitment rather than sentiment. The primary success metric is the percentage of pre-approved applicants who formally accept the mortgage offer and complete the initial documentation within 7 days of receiving the decision. We hypothesize that reducing time-to-decision from 48 hours to under 4 hours will increase this conversion rate by at least 15% compared to the historical average of the waitlist cohort. This moves beyond "satisfaction scores" (which are lagging and subjective) to a concrete behavioral indicator of product-market fit and funnel efficiency.

**3. Operational Efficiency: Median Time-to-Funded**
To validate the core value proposition without relying on arbitrary A/B denial, we track the end-to-end median time from application submission to funds disbursement for the pilot group. Success is defined as achieving a median time of under 24 hours (accounting for the <4 hour decision window plus subsequent processing). This metric serves as the North Star for the operational team, ensuring the backend processes support the frontend speed promise. If the decision is fast but funding remains slow, the customer value is not realized, and the process requires optimization before full scaling.

These criteria prioritize risk containment (Criterion 1) while measuring tangible growth (Criterion 2) and operational reality (Criterion 3), providing a balanced scorecard for launch approval.

*Note: As no A/B test with a control group is permitted, these metrics will be evaluated against historical baselines from Sterling Bank's traditional mortgage pipeline; any significant deviation in macroeconomic conditions during the pilot window must be noted as a confounder.*
*Ensure all user data in this pilot is pseudonymized for analysis, with PII handling strictly routed to the privacy compliance team per bank policy.*
