**Recommendation: Define "Conversion" as Final Fund Disbursement**

For PrestamoRápido, the North Star metric must be **completed loan disbursements**, not offer acceptances. In peer-to-peer lending, value for the lender is not created when a borrower clicks "accept"; value is created only when funds are deployed and the repayment clock starts. An accepted but unverified loan generates zero yield and carries administrative cost. Counting acceptances as conversion is a vanity metric that masks your critical 40% leakage in identity verification.

**Analysis of Definitions**

*   **Option A: Offer Acceptance (Rejected)**
    *   *Pros:* High volume immediately; useful for measuring marketing reach and initial interest.
    *   *Cons:* Decouples the metric from actual business value. It incentivizes optimizing for clicks rather than funded loans. With a 40% drop-off post-acceptance, this metric will rise while your actual revenue remains flat, misleading the steering committee about product-market fit.
*   **Option B: Fund Disbursement (Recommended)**
    *   *Pros:* Directly correlates to revenue and lender ROI. It forces the organization to fix the identity verification bottleneck before scaling acquisition.
    *   *Cons:* Lower volume (~150/day initially). However, low volume on a truthful metric is superior to high volume on a misleading one. It accurately reflects the current constraint (verification) rather than hiding it.

**Proposed Metric Structure**

Do not rely on a single number. Structure your dashboard to diagnose the leak while keeping the North Star honest.

1.  **North Star:** Daily Completed Disbursements (Target: ~150/day initially).
2.  **Primary Input Metric (The Bottleneck):** Identity Verification Completion Rate.
    *   *Calculation:* (Users completing verification) / (Users accepting offer).
    *   *Current Baseline:* 60%.
    *   *Goal:* Increase to >85% before scaling ad spend.
3.  **Secondary Funnel Metrics:**
    *   Offer Acceptance Rate (Marketing/Product fit).
    *   Time-to-Verify (Operational friction).
4.  **Guardrail Metric:** Average Time from Acceptance to Disbursement.
    *   Ensure that pushing for verification does not create excessive friction that causes users to abandon the process entirely due to frustration.
