**Recommended North Star Metric:** **Weekly Active Learners Completing Core Milestones**

Reject "Total Sign-ups" (15,000) as your North Star; it is a vanity metric that rises regardless of whether students learn or drop out. It aligns with donor reach but fails your mission of impact. Conversely, "Job Placements" is too narrow and lagging, excluding students seeking personal enrichment and delaying feedback for curriculum teams.

Instead, adopt **Weekly Active Learners Completing Core Milestones**. This metric counts unique users who, within a given week, complete a defined "value moment" (e.g., finishing a module, passing a project review, or earning a micro-certificate).

**Why this balances scale and impact:**
1.  **Mission Alignment:** It measures *learning*, not just registration. If a student completes a milestone, they derived value, whether for a job or personal growth.
2.  **Stakeholder Bridge:** Donors see active engagement (proof of life) rather than hollow accumulation. Curriculum designers see exactly where the 88% drop-off occurs.
3.  **Actionable:** Unlike job placements (which happen months later), this provides weekly signals to fix retention leaks immediately.

**Implementation Framework:**

1.  **Define "Core Milestone" by Intent:**
    *   Segment users at sign-up: "Career Changer" vs. "Personal Enrichment."
    *   *Career Track Milestone:* Completion of a capstone project or technical interview prep module.
    *   *Enrichment Track Milestone:* Completion of any full course module or interactive exercise.
    *   *The Metric:* Sum of unique users hitting their respective track's milestone per week.

2.  **Measure Bottom-Up (Retention First):**
    *   Do not pour resources into acquiring more sign-ups until the completion rate rises above 12%.
    *   Instrument events specifically answering: "Did the user return in Week 2 to hit their next milestone?"
    *   Ignore pageviews or time-on-site; they are noise.

3.  **Regional Consistency:**
    *   Use a stable, pseudonymous User ID to track learners across devices and regions, ensuring you measure people, not sessions.
    *   Report the North Star as an absolute number and a percentage of the active cohort to normalize for regional population differences.

**Immediate Next Steps (This Week):**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Define and tag the specific event IDs for "Module Complete" and "Project Submit" in your analytics tool for both tracks. Stop reporting total sign-ups in executive dashboards.
2.  **Baseline Query:** Run a cohort analysis for the last 3 months to calculate the current "Weekly Active Learners" count. This is your baseline to present to the Board, replacing the 15,000 sign-up figure.
3.  **Donor Narrative:** Frame the shift to the Executive Director as moving from "Potential Reach" (sign-ups) to "Verified Impact" (completed milestones), directly addressing the risk of funding a leaky bucket.

*Note: Do not invent a "good" completion rate benchmark; compare your future performance strictly against your own current 12% baseline. Ensure all user data remains pseudonymous to comply with privacy standards.*
