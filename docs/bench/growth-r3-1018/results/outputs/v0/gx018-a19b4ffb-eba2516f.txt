### Post-Fix Evaluation Rubric: GlobeChat Q2 Strategy

**Decision Framework:** Do not assume stability drives growth linearly. The data shows a **decoupling**: crash rates improved 53% (4.5% → 2.1%), but Daily Active Users (DAU) rose only 2%. This indicates that crashes were likely a *hygiene factor* (their absence is expected) rather than the primary *growth lever*, or that trust erosion has created a lagging effect that stability alone cannot fix.

Use this rubric to decide between **Further Stability Investment** vs. **Re-engagement Pivot**.

#### 1. The North Star Definition (Value vs. Vanity)
*   **Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing the **Core Action** (e.g., sending a message, joining a channel), *not* raw DAU or session duration.
*   **Why:** Session duration is flat, suggesting users who return aren't finding more value, just the same amount. Raw DAU includes low-intent traffic.
*   **Success Threshold:** A 5% week-over-week increase in *Core Action* completion among users who experienced pre-fix crashes. If this remains flat while crashes are low, the bottleneck is **activation/retention**, not stability.

#### 2. The Diagnostic Funnel (Bottom-Up Read)
Analyze retention *before* acquisition. Pouring marketing spend (re-engagement) into a leaky bucket is waste; over-engineering stability when users aren't activating is over-build.
*   **Step A: Segmented Retention (The Trust Test)**
    *   **Query:** Cohort users who crashed ≥2 times in January. Compare their Week-2 retention rate (post-Feb 20) against users who never crashed.
    *   **Signal:** If the "crashed" cohort retention is still >15% lower than the "stable" cohort, **trust erosion** is real.
    *   **Action:** If true, stability investment is done; the need is **re-engagement** specifically targeting this cohort with a "we fixed it" narrative.
*   **Step B: The Remaining 2.1% (The High-Value Leak)**
    *   **Query:** Correlate the remaining 2.1% crash rate with user Lifetime Value (LTV) or subscription tier.
    *   **Signal:** If >40% of remaining crashes hit top-tier users, the problem is **targeted instability**.
    *   **Action:** If true, authorize **further stability investment** specifically for those devices/flows. If crashes are random across low-value users, stop investing here.

#### 3. Experiment Rigor for Q2
Do not launch a broad re-engagement campaign without a controlled test.
*   **Design:** A/B Test on the "Crashed Cohort" only.
    *   **Control:** No contact.
    *   **Treatment:** Push notification/Email: "GlobeChat is now 50% more stable. Come back."
    *   **Primary Metric:** Core Action completion (not just app open).
    *   **Guardrail:** Unsubscribe rate / Negative feedback.
    *   **Rule:** Pre-commit to a 14-day read. Do not peek at day 2. If the treatment group does not show a statistically significant lift in *Core Actions* (not just opens) by day 14, the hypothesis that "trust is the blocker" is **UNVERIFIED** or false.

#### Final Decision Matrix for VP Eng & Head of Growth

| Scenario | Data Signal | Q2 Strategy Call |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trust Erosion** | Crashed cohort retention remains significantly lower than stable cohort despite fix. | **Pivot to Re-engagement.** Run targeted campaigns acknowledging the fix. Stop stability spend. |
| **High-Value Leak** | Remaining 2.1% crashes disproportionately affect top 10% LTV users. | **Targeted Stability.** Fix specific high-value flows only. No broad re-engagement yet. |
| **Value Gap** | Retention is equal across cohorts, but Core Action completion is flat globally. | **Product Discovery.** Stability isn't the issue; the product isn't delivering value. Pause both stability and re-engagement; investigate feature fit. |
