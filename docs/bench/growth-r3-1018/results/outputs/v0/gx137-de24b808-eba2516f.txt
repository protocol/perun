### Critical Interaction Points for User Testing (Dec 2–6)

To balance data quality against acquisition friction without a live A/B test yet, your design team must observe specific behavioral breakpoints during next week's user testing sessions. Do not track "time on page" or "clicks" generally; instrument only these named questions:

**1. The Friction Threshold (Drop-off Point)**
*   **Question:** At exactly which question does the user hesitate, express frustration, or abandon the flow?
*   **Event to Observe:** `onboarding_step_abandonment`
*   **Properties to Record:** `step_number` (1–5), `question_topic` (e.g., "allergies", "goals"), `hesitation_duration` (observed >5s), `verbal_frustration_flag` (yes/no).
*   **Why:** This identifies the specific "tipping point" where friction outweighs perceived value. If 20% drop-off is concentrated at Question 4, that question is the culprit, not the whole flow.

**2. The Value Perception (Activation Signal)**
*   **Question:** Does the user believe the extra effort yields a better result immediately?
*   **Event to Observe:** `recommendation_engagement_post_onboarding`
*   **Properties to Record:** `time_to_first_click_on_recommendation`, `did_user_modify_recommendation` (yes/no), `verbal_confidence_score` (user says "this looks right" vs. "meh").
*   **Why:** Retention is driven by the "aha" moment. If users complete all 5 questions but ignore the resulting plan, the data collection was vanity, not value.

**3. The Trade-off Decision (Qualitative Pivot)**
*   **Question:** Would the user have preferred a shorter flow even if the result was slightly less accurate?
*   **Event to Observe:** `preference_trade_off_choice` (Simulated during testing)
*   **Method:** After the session, present two options: "Fast setup (2 questions, generic plan)" vs. "Detailed setup (5 questions, personalized plan)." Record their choice.
*   **Why:** This validates your 10% retention hypothesis. If users choose "Fast" despite claiming they want personalization, your assumption about "quality" is flawed.

**Testing Protocol for Next Week**
Run 5–8 sessions. Do not calculate statistical significance yet; look for patterns.
*   **Stop Rule:** If >3 users abandon at the same step, that step is broken. Redesign it before the Dec 5th code freeze.
*   **Success Signal:** Users complete all 5 questions *and* immediately interact with the recommendation without expressing doubt.

**Note:** Do not estimate conversion rates or retention lifts from these sessions; those are `UNVERIFIED` until the Dec 5th live test with pre-committed sample sizes.
Ensure any user IDs recorded for follow-up are pseudonymous; route PII handling to your privacy compliance guide.
