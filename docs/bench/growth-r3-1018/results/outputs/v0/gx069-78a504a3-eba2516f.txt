Do not adopt ticket sales volume as your North Star yet; it is a vanity metric for your current model because it ignores free events and cash-only doors, creating a false negative for venue value. Instead, define your North Star as **Weekly Active Users completing a "Discovery Action"** (defined as viewing an event detail page + clicking "Get Directions" or "Save"). This measures real user intent regardless of payment method.

For your 3-month pilot with 20 venues and ~500 active users, reject external benchmarks. Success is defined by your own baseline trends in these three specific areas:

**1. The Core Value Loop (User Side)**
*   **Metric:** Activation Rate (Users who perform a Discovery Action within 3 days of install).
*   **Threshold:** Do not aim for an industry average. Aim for **>40% of your 500 monthly actives** to complete this action. If 1,200 installs yielded only 500 actives, your immediate leak is activation, not retention.
*   **Verification:** If you cannot instrument the "Click" event now, this metric is `UNVERIFIED`. Instrument it immediately; do not estimate.

**2. Venue Marketing Value (B2B Side)**
*   **Metric:** **Attributed Footfall Proxy**. Since you cannot track cash sales, measure the ratio of "Direction Clicks" or "Save to Calendar" events per venue listing.
*   **Threshold:** A healthy pilot shows a **positive week-over-week trend** in this ratio for at least 12 of the 20 venues. Absolute numbers matter less than the trajectory. If a venue gets 5 direction clicks in Week 1 and 15 in Week 4, that is success. If they get 50 in Week 1 and 50 in Week 4, that is stagnation.
*   **Artifact:** Provide each venue a simple weekly report: "X users expressed high intent to visit via the app."

**3. Retention (The Bottom-Up Read)**
*   **Metric:** Week-2 Retention of users who performed a Discovery Action in Week 1.
*   **Threshold:** Compare Week 2 retention to Week 1. If it drops by >20%, stop all acquisition marketing. Pouring users into a leaky bucket wastes your pilot budget. Fix the core action retention before worrying about the 1,200 waitlist users.

**Experiment & Measurement Rules**
*   **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument only three events: `event_viewed`, `action_clicked` (directions/save), and `venue_profile_viewed`. Do not track "pageviews" or "time on site"; they are noise.
*   **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** If a partner asks "Is 10 clicks good?", answer: "We do not know yet. We will compare your Week 4 clicks to your Week 1 baseline. An upward trend proves value."
*   **Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous User ID to track cohorts across sessions. Do not use raw emails or device IDs in analytics logs; route PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument:** Add tracking for `action_clicked` immediately. Without this, your North Star is `UNVERIFIED`.
2.  **Baseline:** Calculate the current % of your 500 active users who performed a Discovery Action last week. This is your Day 1 baseline.
3.  **Report Design:** Draft the one-page venue report showing only "High Intent Actions" (clicks) and their week-over-week change. Remove all other data.

*Note: Do not set fixed percentage thresholds for "success" (e.g., "5% conversion") without your own historical data; doing so creates false expectations for your venue partners.*
*Focus entirely on the trend line over the 12 weeks; a rising curve with small absolute numbers is a stronger sales tool than a flat line with high numbers.*
