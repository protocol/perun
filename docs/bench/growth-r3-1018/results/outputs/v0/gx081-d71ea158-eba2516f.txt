### Immediate Measurement Plan for CodeBridge Board Proposal

**1. Define the North Star: "Weekly Resolved Interactions"**
Reject "total posts" and "raw registrations" as vanity metrics; they rise regardless of value. Your North Star must be **Weekly Resolved Interactions**: the count of unique threads per week where a question receives an answer marked as "Accepted" or where the original poster replies confirming resolution. This measures *value delivered*, not just noise generated. If this number stalls while registrations rise, your community is leaking value.

**2. Diagnose the Funnel Bottom-Up (Retention First)**
Do not focus on Daily Active Users (DAU) yet; for a niche developer hub, **Weekly Returning Users** performing the *core action* is the true health signal. Diagnose in this order:
*   **Retention:** Of users who posted a question in Week 1, what % returned in Week 2? If this is low, pouring money into moderators or ads is wasted.
*   **Activation:** Of users who registered, what % actually posted a question or answered one within 7 days? High logins with zero contribution indicates a "lurker trap."
*   **Acquisition:** Only analyze registration sources *after* fixing retention.

**3. Instrument Specific Events (Answer Named Questions)**
Stop tracking "unique logins." Implement these three specific events immediately, each tied to a board-level question:
*   `question_resolved`: (Property: `time_to_first_answer`, `is_mentor_verified`).
    *   *Question:* "Are developers finding solutions quickly?"
*   `mentorship_handshake`: (Property: `user_role`, `thread_id`). Triggered when a designated mentor replies to a novice-tagged thread.
    *   *Question:* "Is our moderator budget driving high-value guidance?"
*   `solution_accepted`: (Property: `answerer_id`, `asker_id`).
    *   *Question:* "What is our true solution acceptance rate?" (Currently `UNVERIFIED`).

**4. The Identifier Seam & Privacy**
To track retention cohorts (e.g., "January registrants vs. February registrants"), you must link sessions to a **stable, pseudonymous user ID**. Do not use raw emails or IPs in analytics tools. Ensure this ID generation complies with GDPR (relevant for Brno/Czech EU context) by routing PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.

**5. Experiment Rigor for Moderator Budget**
When testing the impact of increased moderation, do not just watch total posts rise. Pre-commit to a metric: "Increase `question_resolved` rate by 15% over 4 weeks." Do not peek at day 3 and declare victory; read the result once at the pre-committed end date to avoid false positives from natural variance.
