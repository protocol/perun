### Measurement Plan: MetroStyle Rebrand Impact

**1. North Star Metric (NSM)**
Adopt **Weekly Active Purchasing Customers (WAPC)** as your single North Star. Unlike raw traffic or cumulative signups, this metric rises only when users derive real value (purchasing) and directly correlates to revenue. Reject "total site visits" or "brand impression count" as primary success criteria; these are vanity metrics that can increase while the business stagnates.

**2. Critical Input Metrics & Leading Indicators**
To detect early signals before full sales data matures, track these input metrics weekly:
*   **Direct Traffic Retention Cohort:** Instead of total direct traffic, measure the percentage of users who visited directly in Week N and returned to purchase in Week N+1. This isolates brand loyalty from seasonal acquisition spikes.
*   **Returning Visitor Conversion Rate:** Your stated hypothesis metric. Segment this strictly by "pre-launch baseline users" vs. "new post-launch users" to prevent mix-shift errors.
*   **Core Action Completion:** Define the "aha moment" for the rebrand (e.g., viewing the new brand story page + adding to cart). Track the completion rate of this specific flow.

**3. Baseline & Seasonality Control**
You cannot assert a "good" benchmark without your own data. Establish your baseline using the **same calendar window from the previous year (YoY)** adjusted for any known structural changes, rather than the immediate pre-launch weeks which may be distorted by holiday noise.
*   **Control Group Strategy:** If possible, roll out the rebrand creative to a small, geographically isolated segment (e.g., specific ZIP codes) or a holdout group in paid social 2 weeks prior. Compare their trend against the national average to isolate the rebrand effect from general holiday seasonality.
*   **Attribution Rule:** Any metric labeled "UNVERIFIED" due to missing tracking tags must be instrumented immediately. Do not estimate values.

**4. Reporting Structure & Experiment Rigor**
Structure weekly reports to read the funnel **bottom-up**:
1.  **Retention:** Did returning customers buy again? (If this leaks, do not increase ad spend).
2.  **Activation:** Did new visitors complete the core brand interaction?
3.  **Acquisition:** Did direct traffic volume rise?

**Warning on "Early Wins":** If you see a 20% spike in direct traffic in the first 3 days post-launch, treat this as **UNVERIFIED** regarding long-term success. Short-term novelty effects often regress. Do not declare victory based on peeking at early data. Pre-commit to evaluating the primary lift (20% direct traffic, 5% conversion) only after a fixed duration (e.g., 4 weeks post-launch) to avoid false positives caused by holiday volatility.

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Define the Event Taxonomy:** Document exactly three events to instrument: `direct_session_start`, `core_brand_interaction_view`, and `purchase_complete`. Ensure each has a stable, pseudonymous user ID to track cohorts across sessions.
2.  **Lock the Baseline Query:** Run the YoY retention and conversion query for the target window now. Record these numbers as the immutable baseline for the executive dashboard.
3.  **Set the Guardrail:** Establish a "brand sentiment" guardrail (e.g., customer support ticket volume mentioning "confusion" or "old site"). If this spikes, the rebrand rollout pauses regardless of traffic gains.

*Note: Ensure all user identifiers used for cohorting are pseudonymized and routed to your privacy compliance protocol; do not use raw PII for analytics. This plan assumes your analytics platform can segment by traffic source and user history; if not, instrumentation is required before launch.*
