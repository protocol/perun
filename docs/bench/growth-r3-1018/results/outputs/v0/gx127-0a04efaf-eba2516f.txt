**Recommended North Star Metric: Weekly Active Transactors (Value-Adjusted)**

Reject "Daily Active Users" (DAU) as your North Star. In your context, DAU is a vanity metric that measures network reliability rather than product value. A 40% drop due to infrastructure outages reflects telco performance, not user churn or lack of product-market fit. Similarly, "Total Value Locked" (TVL) is a lagging financial aggregate; it proves capital exists but not that small business owners are deriving recurring utility from it.

Your North Star must be **Weekly Active Transactors**, defined as the count of unique business owners who successfully complete at least one value-moving transaction (send, receive, swap, or pay) within a 7-day window, regardless of the interface used (App, USSD, or Agent).

**Why this metric works for SafiWallet:**
1.  **Captures True Utility:** It measures the core job-to-be-done (moving money) rather than the method of access. A user transacting via USSD during an outage derives the same economic value as an app user.
2.  **Smooths Volatility:** A weekly cadence absorbs daily connectivity shocks, providing investors a stable view of retention and habit formation.
3.  **Aligns with Business Health:** For small businesses, frequency of successful transactions correlates directly with their operational viability and your revenue potential.

**Input Metrics to Drive the North Star:**
*   **USSD Success Rate:** Percentage of initiated USSD sessions that result in a confirmed transaction.
*   **Agent Attribution Rate:** Percentage of offline/agent transactions successfully linked to a digital user profile.
*   **Core Action Retention:** The percentage of users who transact in Week 1 and return to transact again in Week 2.

**Addressing the Attribution Gap:**
You noted it is an "open question" how to attribute offline/agent transactions. Until this is solved, any metric claiming to track "unique users" across channels is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not fabricate an estimate for your pitch deck. Instead, explicitly state in your data strategy slide that "Cross-channel identity resolution" is the primary instrumentation milestone for the next quarter. Your immediate action is to define the event taxonomy for agent-led transactions, ensuring every agent submission captures a stable, pseudonymous user identifier (e.g., phone number hash) to bridge the offline-online seam.

**Investor Narrative:**
Frame the shift from DAU to Weekly Active Transactors as a strategic maturity move. It demonstrates you distinguish between *access* (infrastructure) and *value* (transactions). Show the trend of TVL alongside this new metric to prove that while capital is growing, the *velocity* of that capital (active usage) is the true engine of your Series A thesis.
