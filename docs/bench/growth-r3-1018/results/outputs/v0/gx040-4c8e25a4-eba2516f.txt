### Balanced Scorecard for ReWear Tokyo Holiday Campaign

**North Star Metric:** **30-Day Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) per Active Seller.**
This metric rises only when items sell (velocity) at sustainable prices (value), directly aligning with your goal of optimizing sell-through without eroding seller earnings. It rejects vanity metrics like "total listings" or "price drop count," which can increase while the business stagnates.

**Input Metrics (The Levers):**
1.  **Median Time-to-First-Price-Drop:** Tracks your specific hypothesis on velocity.
2.  **Final Sale Price vs. Initial List Price Ratio:** Monitors the depth of discounting to ensure velocity isn't bought solely through excessive markdowns.
3.  **Seller Retention Rate (Cohort-based):** The percentage of sellers who list again within 60 days after a sale. This is your primary proxy for seller trust and long-term health.

**Diagnosing the Trade-off (Velocity vs. Liquidity/Trust):**
Do not look at aggregate averages. Read the data bottom-up via **retention cohorts**.
*   **The Warning Signal:** If *Time-to-Sale* decreases but *Seller Retention* for that specific cohort drops compared to your baseline, you are conditioning sellers to undervalue items and churn.
*   **The Liquidity Trap:** If *Listing Volume* rises but *Sell-Through Rate* flatlines or drops, you are accumulating inventory that buyers do not want at the offered prices, indicating a mismatch in pricing strategy rather than a speed issue.
*   **Verification Requirement:** Any claim that "fast sales equal happy sellers" is `UNVERIFIED` until you compare the 60-day return rate of sellers with <48-hour sales against those with 7–14 day sales using your own historical data. Do not assume a benchmark; use your internal baseline.

**Tracking Seller Lifetime Value (LTV):**
You cannot calculate LTV without instrumentation. Define two distinct events: `seller_fast_sale` (sale < 72 hours) and `seller_slow_sale` (sale > 7 days).
*   **Instrumentation Question:** "Does a fast sale increase the frequency of future listings or the average list price of subsequent items?"
*   **Calculation:** Track the cumulative GMV generated by the *cohort* of sellers experiencing fast sales over 6 months versus the slow-sale cohort. If the fast-sale cohort lists fewer items or at lower prices in Month 2, their LTV is lower despite the immediate win.

**Experiment Rigor for the Holiday Campaign:**
If you A/B test a "nudge to drop price" feature:
*   **Pre-commit:** Define the sample size and duration before launching. Do not stop the test early because "significance" appears on day 2; this is peeking and inflates false positives.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Set a hard floor on *Average Seller Earnings per Listing*. If the test increases velocity but breaches this guardrail, the result is a failure regardless of sell-through gains.
*   **Decision Rule:** Ship only if the treatment group shows higher 30-day GMV per seller *and* meets the earnings guardrail.
