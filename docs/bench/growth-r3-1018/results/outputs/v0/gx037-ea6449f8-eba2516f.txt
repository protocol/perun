**FundLatam Q1 Measurement Plan: Brazil & Mexico Launch**

**1. North Star Metric (NSM)**
Reject "Gross Volume" as your primary weekly diagnostic; it is a lagging outcome. Adopt **Weekly Active Backers Completing a Pledge** as your NSM. This measures realized customer value. If this number does not rise, volume targets are vanity. Gross Volume remains the board-level goal, but the NSM drives weekly operational decisions.

**2. Diagnostic Funnel (Read Bottom-Up)**
Do not optimize acquisition until retention and activation are stable. Diagnose in this order:
*   **Retention (The Leak Check):** % of users who return within 7 days of landing. If this is low, acquisition spend is wasted.
*   **Activation (The "Aha" Moment):** % of visitors who view a project detail page *and* initiate the checkout flow. This isolates interest from payment friction.
*   **Conversion (The Friction Point):** % of checkout initiations that result in a successful payment. Segment this strictly by payment method (Credit Card vs. Boleto Bancário vs. Local Alternatives).

**3. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not track "everything." Instrument these specific events to answer named questions:
*   `payment_method_selected`: *Does Boleto usage correlate with higher drop-off than credit cards?*
*   `checkout_abandonment_stage`: *At which specific field (CPF entry, barcode generation) do Brazilian users quit?*
*   `campaign_view_to_pledge_initiated`: *Is the issue project relevance (market fit) or transaction failure (friction)?*
*   `repeat_backer_flag`: *Are early adopters returning, or is every transaction a one-off?*

**4. Distinguishing Friction vs. Market Fit**
Use the **Activation-to-Conversion Ratio** to diagnose low performance:
*   **Scenario A (High Activation, Low Conversion):** Users view projects and start checkout but fail to pay. This is **Platform Friction**.
    *   *Action:* Investigate Boleto expiration windows, CPF validation errors, or local gateway timeouts.
*   **Scenario B (Low Activation, Low Conversion):** Users land but do not view projects or start checkout. This is **Market Fit/Acquisition Mismatch**.
    *   *Action:* Your ad creative or project selection does not resonate with local cultural norms. Do not tweak the checkout; tweak the offer or audience.

**5. Experiment Rigor & Board Reporting**
*   **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** Do not compare your conversion rates to US data or industry averages. Compare Week 2 to Week 1. If Boleto conversion is 40% lower than Credit Card, that is your baseline truth; do not assume it "should" be equal.
*   **The 30-Day Verdict:** For the board meeting, report the **Cohort Retention Curve** of users acquired in Week 1. If they do not return or pledge again by Day 30, you have a retention problem, regardless of the $2M volume push.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Monitor **Cost Per Acquired Backer (CPAB)**. If ad impressions (100k) drive traffic but CPAB spikes due to low conversion, pause spend immediately.
