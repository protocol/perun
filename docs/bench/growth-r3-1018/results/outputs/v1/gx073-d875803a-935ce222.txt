**Interpretation of Anomalies**
The stability of the treatment group versus the sharp dip in the control group during the June 8–9 outages suggests a **mix shift** or **selection bias**, not necessarily feature superiority. If the treatment group inadvertently contained a lower proportion of users reliant on the affected southern banks, their performance would appear artificially stable compared to a control group heavily exposed to the outage. Alternatively, if the one-click flow bypasses the specific bank gateway that failed, the result measures **resilience to a specific bank failure**, not a general improvement in checkout usability. Without quantifying the overlap between "users on affected banks" and "treatment/control assignment," the 40% lift cannot be attributed to the feature design itself.
