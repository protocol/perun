**Subject:** Diagnostic Framework for Referral Coefficient Drop (ShopBuddy)

**Decision Context:** Determine by May 20th whether to kill the referral program or iterate rewards based on distinguishing natural saturation from technical failure.

**Diagnostic Data Slices**
To isolate the root cause of the viral coefficient drop from 1.2 to 0.6, execute the following three data cuts immediately. Each slice dictates a specific action.

**1. The "Link Health" Slice (Technical vs. Behavioral)**
*   **Query:** Compare the *click-through rate (CTR)* and *landing page load success rate* of referral links generated before Day 3 maintenance versus those generated after. Segment by "Day of Generation."
*   **Interpretation & Action:**
    *   **If Post-Day-3 CTR/Success drops significantly:** The issue is technical (broken links or latency). **Action:** Halt all promotion spend immediately. Engage engineering to fix the link generation service. Do not touch rewards.
    *   **If CTR/Success remains stable:** The user experience is intact; the drop is behavioral. Proceed to Slice 2.

**2. The "Sender Saturation" Slice (Power User Exhaustion)**
*   **Query:** Calculate the average number of successful referrals *per unique sender* for Day 1–3 senders vs. Day 4+ senders. Specifically, check the distribution curve: did the top 10% of senders stop referring entirely on Day 4, or did their individual conversion rates drop?
*   **Interpretation & Action:**
    *   **If top senders stopped sending entirely:** This confirms your hypothesis of immediate social circle exhaustion (natural saturation). **Action:** The current "broad" reward is inefficient for deeper networks. Iterate rewards to incentivize *new* sender acquisition (e.g., tiered rewards for reaching 2nd-degree connections) rather than killing the program.
    *   **If senders are still sending but conversions failed:** The audience is seeing the link but not converting. Proceed to Slice 3.

**3. The "Receiver Conversion" Slice (Reward Fatigue)**
*   **Query:** Compare the *signup-to-activation rate* of users who arrived via referral links pre-Day 3 vs. post-Day 3.
*   **Interpretation & Action:**
    *   **If activation rate dropped post-Day 3:** The reward mechanism likely lost appeal or the offer is unclear to the broader, less-engaged audience reached after power users exhausted their close circles. **Action:** Iterate on the reward value or messaging by May 20th.
    *   **If activation rate is flat:** The funnel is healthy; the volume drop is purely due to fewer links being sent (return to Slice 2 findings).

**Required Instrumentation Check**
Ensure your analytics platform tracks a stable, pseudonymous user ID across the referral click and subsequent signup to accurately attribute these cohorts. If you cannot link the referrer to the referee reliably, these slices are `UNVERIFIED`.

**This-Week Steps**
1.  **Run the "Link Health" query today.** If technical failure is found, the decision is made: fix before iterating.
2.  **If technical health is clear, run the "Sender Saturation" cut.** If power users exhausted their networks, draft a proposal for tiered rewards to reach wider circles by May 18th.
3.  **Define the stop-tripwire:** If the viral coefficient remains <0.8 after fixing any technical issues and testing one reward iteration by May 20th, kill the program to reallocate budget.
