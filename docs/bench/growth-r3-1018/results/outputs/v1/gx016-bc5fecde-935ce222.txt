**Analysis Brief: Italy Price Increase Impact Assessment**
**To:** Regional Director of Sales
**Date:** January 31
**Subject:** Drivers of Churn Spike and Recommended Action

**Executive Summary**
Following the 12% price increase on January 10, cancellation requests rose to 7% (vs. <3% assumption) and new sign-ups dropped 20%. While price sensitivity is a primary suspect, a competitor promotion launched January 15 introduces a confounding variable. We cannot definitively separate these effects without a controlled comparison, as the current data represents a single treated group with multiple simultaneous changes.

**Diagnostic Method: Isolating Price vs. Market Factors**
To distinguish between price sensitivity and competitor influence, we must analyze the **timing of the inflection point** and **segment behavior**:

1.  **Inflection Point Analysis:** Plot daily cancellation requests from Jan 1–Jan 31.
    *   If the spike began immediately Jan 10–14, the driver is likely **price sensitivity**.
    *   If the rate accelerated sharply after Jan 15, the **competitor promotion** is a significant contributor.
2.  **Cohort Comparison (The "Control" Proxy):** Compare churn rates of the **Italy Small Business** segment against:
    *   *Italy Enterprise/Other Tiers:* Did they see a similar spike? If yes, the cause is likely market-wide (competitor). If no, the cause is specific to the price-hiked segment.
    *   *Similar Markets (e.g., Spain/France):* Did regions without a price hike experience a sign-up drop? If yes, external market factors are dominant. If Italy is an outlier, the price hike is the primary driver.
3.  **New Sign-up Quality:** Analyze if the 20% drop in sign-ups correlates with a change in lead source or industry vertical that the competitor targets.

**Decision Framework & Recommendations**
We must act before month-end to stabilize the base. Do not roll back pricing globally yet, as this signals weakness and trains users to wait for reversals. Instead, execute a targeted intervention based on the diagnostic above.

**Option A: If data shows the spike started Jan 10–14 (Price Dominant)**
*   **Action:** Launch a "Legacy Rate" retention offer immediately for existing subscribers who have not yet cancelled. Offer a 6% discount (splitting the difference) locked for 12 months if they renew now.
*   **Rationale:** Acknowledges price sensitivity without fully reversing the strategy.

**Option B: If data shows acceleration post-Jan 15 (Competitor Dominant)**
*   **Action:** Deploy a value-based campaign highlighting the "recent analytics features" specifically against competitor gaps. Do not discount yet; fight on value.
*   **Rationale:** Price is not the sole driver; matching the competitor on price erodes margin unnecessarily if the value prop is strong.
