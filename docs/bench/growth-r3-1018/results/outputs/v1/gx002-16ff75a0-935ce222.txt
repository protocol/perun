### Launch Success Criteria: Digital Mortgage Pilot (200 Applicants)

**1. Primary Growth Metric: Activation Retention at Day 30**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of pilot users who not only accept the mortgage offer within 4 hours but remain in good standing (no missed payments) 30 days post-disbursement.
*   **Rationale:** This replaces "customer satisfaction" (a vanity metric in this context) with a value-based North Star. It confirms that speed drives *completed, viable* loans, not just signed contracts.
*   **Target Logic:** Compare against the historical baseline of the waitlist cohort. If the rate matches or exceeds the baseline while reducing time-to-decision, the growth hypothesis holds.

**2. Primary Risk Guardrail: Early-Delinquency Rate (EDR) Delta**
*   **Definition:** The incidence of payments missed within the first 60 days, specifically monitoring for a statistically significant increase compared to the historical control group (standard 48-hour process).
*   **Rationale:** This directly addresses the risk of "insufficient reconsideration time." Since we cannot A/B test denial, we treat the historical 48-hour cohort as the control.
*   **Stop/Tripwire Rule:** If the pilot's EDR exceeds the historical baseline by >0.5% (or a threshold defined by your capital reserves), the feature triggers an automatic pause for review. This satisfies the Chief Risk Officer's requirement for immediate containment of default risk.

**3. Process Integrity Metric: "Cooling-Off" Engagement Rate**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users who access the "review your commitment" summary page or contact support *after* receiving the instant decision but *before* final disbursement.
*   **Rationale:** This acts as a proxy for the "reconsideration" variable. If speed removes necessary friction, we expect this number to drop near zero. If it remains stable, users are still self-regulating despite the faster timeline.
*   **Actionable Insight:** If this rate drops significantly while EDR rises, it confirms the hypothesis that speed is bypassing critical user reflection, necessitating a mandatory "wait period" insertion before funding.
