**To:** Chief Risk Officer, RefiLondon
**Subject:** Early Performance Metrics Framework – Healthcare Graduate Tier (Launch Jan 15)

**1. The North Star: "Verified Value Retention"**
Reject cumulative sign-ups as the primary success metric; they are vanity figures that rise regardless of loan health. Instead, adopt **Week-4 Active Repayment Commitment** as the North Star. This measures the percentage of approved healthcare graduates who not only sign but complete the first verified payment setup and remain active 30 days post-disbursement. This metric only rises when the user derives actual value (successful refinancing) and the business secures a performing asset, balancing acquisition speed with immediate risk validation.

**2. Input Metrics & Event Taxonomy (Answering Named Questions)**
Instrument only events that answer specific hypotheses regarding the healthcare demographic's decision drivers. Do not track "everything."

*   **Hypothesis A (Rate Sensitivity):** *Do lower rates drive completion more than flexibility?*
    *   **Event:** `rate_calculator_completed` → `application_submitted`
    *   **Question:** What is the conversion delta for users who view the "estimated monthly saving" vs. those who view "flexible pause options"?
*   **Hypothesis B (Flexibility/Service):** *Is repayment flexibility the primary converter?*
    *   **Event:** `flexibility_terms_viewed` → `document_uploaded`
    *   **Question:** Does engagement with "income-contingent repayment" terms correlate higher with final signing than interest rate pages?
*   **Risk Guardrail (Early Delinquency Signal):**
    *   **Event:** `first_payment_failed` OR `contact_support_post_disbursement`
    *   **Question:** Are healthcare graduates missing the first payment due to administrative delays (common in NHS payroll) or genuine inability to pay?

**3. Funnel Diagnosis: Retention Before Acquisition**
Do not pour marketing spend into acquisition until the **Activation → Retention** link is proven.
*   **Step 1:** Verify that users who activate (sign + fund) are retaining (making month 1 payment). If this leaks, acquisition is wasted capital.
*   **Step 2:** Once retention is stable, diagnose Activation. Are they dropping at the document upload stage?
*   **Step 3:** Only then optimize Acquisition volume against the 200-customer goal.

**4. Experiment Rigor & Risk Governance**
*   **No Peeked Results:** If we A/B test messaging (e.g., "Low Rate" vs. "NHS-Friendly Flexibility"), we must pre-commit to a sample size or duration. A result looking significant on Day 3 of a 14-day test is `UNVERIFIED` noise and cannot be presented to the board as a win.
*   **Guardrails:** Any experiment optimizing for speed must have a **hard guardrail** on `application_rejection_rate` or `early_fraud_flag`. If speed increases but rejection rates rise by >2%, the test fails regardless of sign-up volume.
*   **Compliance Note:** All user tracking must use a stable, pseudonymous identifier for cohort analysis. Personally Identifiable Information (PII) regarding health sector employment must be routed strictly to our privacy compliance protocols, not stored in raw analytics logs.

**5. This Week's Actions**
1.  **Define the "Active Repayment" Query:** Engineering to build the dashboard for the North Star (funded loans with scheduled first payment) by Friday; do not wait for launch data to build the view.
2.  **Pre-commit the Messaging Test:** Finalize the A/B test design comparing "Rate" vs. "Flexibility" headlines with a fixed 2-week duration and a declared guardrail on rejection rates before January 15th.
3.  **Baseline the "Drop-off":** Measure the current application drop-off rate for non-healthcare users to establish a internal baseline; without this, we cannot judge if the new tier is underperforming.

*Note: All benchmark comparisons will be made against RefiLondon's internal historical baselines, not external market averages, to ensure regulatory accuracy. Any metric lacking an instrumentation plan is marked `UNVERIFIED` and will not be reported.*
