### Prioritized Event Trackers for Retention Dashboard

To isolate true user commitment from market speculation within your three-event budget, instrument the following. These metrics prioritize behavioral persistence over financial volatility.

**1. Event: `core_loop_completion`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when a user completes the primary non-monetary gameplay action (e.g., finishing a quest, winning a match, or crafting an item) regardless of token reward status.
*   **Why this over transaction volume:** Transaction volume is a lagging indicator of token price, not user intent. A user may trade heavily during a pump and vanish during a dip, yet still play daily. This event proves the user derives value from the *game mechanics*, not just the asset price.
*   **Trade-off:** Requires clear definition of the "core loop" with studio partners. If the game is purely speculative with no gameplay loop, this metric cannot exist, and your retention hypothesis is invalid.

**2. Event: `session_depth_no_tx`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when a session exceeds a time threshold (e.g., >5 minutes) or page depth *without* triggering a wallet transaction.
*   **Why this over wallet connection frequency:** Your assumption that wallet connections are stable is risky; users often disconnect wallets to save gas or due to security fears during volatility, yet remain engaged. This event captures the "watching and strategizing" cohort you fear missing. It validates engagement even when the wallet is idle.
*   **Trade-off:** You must define a meaningful time threshold to avoid counting idle tabs as engagement. This metric may inflate DAU slightly compared to strict "action-based" counts, but it better reflects community health during bear markets.

**3. Event: `returning_user_core_action`**
*   **Definition:** Fired only when a specific user ID completes `core_loop_completion` on Day N, having also completed it on at least one day in the previous 7 days.
*   **Why this over standard DAU:** Standard DAU swung from 8k to 25k because it counts *presence*. This metric counts *habit*. It filters out the one-day speculators who joined for the season launch and left, giving you a stable baseline of committed users unaffected by the 25k spike.
*   **Trade-off:** This is a derived metric requiring stateful tracking or complex querying. It delays real-time visibility but provides the only accurate view of retention stability.

### Why We Ignore Obvious Metrics
We exclude **Transaction Volume** and **Wallet Connection Frequency** as primary retention indicators because they correlate too highly with token price. In your recent swing, these metrics would have shown a 200% spike and subsequent crash, falsely suggesting you lost 60% of your user base when, in reality, you likely only lost the speculative fringe. By focusing on the three events above, you measure *product stickiness* independent of *asset performance*.

**Note:** Ensure the `user_id` used for these events is a persistent, pseudonymous internal ID, not the public wallet address, to maintain tracking continuity if users rotate wallets. Present this to your CTO as a shift from "financial activity monitoring" to "product engagement verification."
