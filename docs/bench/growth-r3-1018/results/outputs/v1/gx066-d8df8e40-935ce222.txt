**Why Bayesian methods do not solve this immediately:**
While Bayesian analysis can incorporate prior beliefs, it cannot manufacture information that does not exist. With only a handful of downtime events across 14 facilities over six weeks, the data is too sparse to overwhelm even a strong prior. The resulting posterior distribution would remain too wide to make a confident "ship/no-ship" decision without relying heavily on subjective assumptions rather than observed evidence.
