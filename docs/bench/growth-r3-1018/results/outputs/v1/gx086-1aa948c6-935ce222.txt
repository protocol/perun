**Recommendation: Reject "File Upload Volume" as the North Star**
File uploads are a vanity metric in this context; they rise regardless of whether real scientific value is created (as seen in your pilot where critical discussion happens without uploads). Measuring them will mislead resource allocation.

**1. The North Star: "Weekly Active Experiments with Multi-User Engagement"**
Define a single metric that rises only when real value occurs: *The count of distinct experiment threads where at least two physicists perform a "value action" within a 48-hour window.*
*   **Why:** This captures coordination (the core value of a consortium) rather than just storage usage.
*   **Input Metrics:**
    *   *Activation:* % of invited physicists who perform one value action in their first session.
    *   *Retention:* % of active experiments that see a second value action in Week 2.

**2. The Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not log every click. Instrument only these three events to answer specific questions about the September 12 readiness:

| Event Name | Question It Answers | Properties to Log |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `hypothesis_discussed` | Are teams aligning on theory before data generation? | `thread_id`, `participant_count`, `has_link_to_dataset` (boolean) |
| `dataset_version_checked` | Is the team verifying data integrity without re-uploading? | `dataset_id`, `action_type` (view/download/compare), `user_role` |
| `sync_session_joined` | Is synchronous coordination happening effectively? | `meeting_id`, `duration_bucket`, `attendee_count` |

*Note:* Ignore raw comment counts or meeting scheduling links unless they result in the actions above. A scheduled meeting with no attendees is noise.

**3. Infrastructure & Privacy Seam**
*   **Stable Identifier:** Use a pseudonymous `researcher_id` (hashed) to track users across sessions for cohort analysis. Do not use raw emails or names in the analytics stream.
*   **PII Routing:** Any metadata containing personal identifiers or specific experiment secrets must be routed to your secure compliance layer (`privacy-compliance.md`); the analytics engine receives only the hashed ID and event properties.
*   **Cost Control:** By filtering out "chat only" events that lack a `has_link_to_dataset` or `version_check` signal, you reduce log volume by an estimated 40-60% compared to full instrumentation, preserving processing power for the September run.
