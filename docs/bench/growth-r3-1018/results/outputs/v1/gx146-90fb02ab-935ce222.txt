# CreatorHub Growth Measurement Strategy: Scaling from 10k to 1M

## Executive Summary for Venture Partners
To validate unit economics during hyper-growth, CreatorHub must shift from vanity metrics (raw volume) to value-based metrics (sustainable engagement). Our current 40% Daily Active Users (DAU) is a baseline, not a target; as we onboard the mass market, this percentage will naturally dilute. Success is not maintaining 40% DAU, but maintaining the *value density* of those users. We will measure **Weekly Active Creators Publishing to a Community Thread** as our North Star Metric. This rises only when a user derives real value (creation + connection), rejecting raw logins or passive consumption which inflate during growth but do not predict revenue or retention.

## The North Star and Input Metrics
**North Star:** Weekly Active Creators Publishing to a Community Thread.
*Why:* It captures the core loop (create → share → interact) rather than passive presence. If this metric grows, the business grows.

**Input Metrics (Drivers):**
1.  **Activation Rate:** % of new signups who publish their first piece and receive one comment within 48 hours.
2.  **Retention Cohort Strength:** % of users who publish in Week 1 and return to publish or comment in Week 4.
3.  **Community Depth:** Average comments per published piece (quality of interaction vs. noise).

## Detecting Shifts: Quality vs. Quantity
To distinguish early adopter behavior from mass market habits, we will stop looking at aggregate averages and start reading **cohort retention curves**.
*   **The Signal:** Plot retention curves by signup week. If the curve for the "mass market" cohort drops faster than the "early adopter" cohort, user quality is diluting.
*   **The Diagnosis:** If retention drops, we do not pour money into acquisition. We fix the activation leak first. We will segment these cohorts by behavior: "Creators who interact" vs. "Creators who only consume." If the "interact" cohort retains 3x better, our product roadmap must prioritize community features over content discovery tools.

## Event Taxonomy: Instrumenting for Answers
We will instrument only events that answer specific strategic questions, avoiding "track everything" noise.
1.  **Event:** `content_published` (Properties: user_id, content_type, time_to_publish). *Question:* Are users reaching the "aha" moment faster?
2.  **Event:** `community_interaction` (Properties: user_id, target_user_id, interaction_type). *Question:* Does receiving feedback drive the next publishing event?
3.  **Event:** `session_start` (Properties: user_id, device). *Question:* Is traffic growth driving actual usage or just idle loads?

*Identifier Seam:* To track users across sessions for cohort analysis, we will implement a stable, pseudonymous user ID. All Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and consent management will be routed to our privacy compliance framework; analytics will only see the pseudonymous token.

## Roadmap for Hyper-Growth Health
1.  **Immediate (Month 1-3):** Deploy the North Star dashboard. Establish the baseline retention curve for the current 10k users. Define the "break-even" retention rate where unit economics hold.
2.  **Scaling (Month 4-12):** Monitor the gap between early adopter and mass market cohorts. If the gap widens, trigger a product intervention on the onboarding flow to force early community interaction.
3.  **Maturity (Month 12-18):** Introduce guardrail metrics (e.g., server cost per active creator) to ensure growth remains profitable. Retire any events that no longer inform a decision.
