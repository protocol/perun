### Board Brief: Measuring CodeBridge Community Health

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Resolvers (users who post a question and receive an accepted solution or a "helpful" marked reply within 7 days).
*Why:* Total posts and logins are vanity metrics; they rise even if the community is broken. This metric only increases when a user receives tangible value.

**The Funnel (Read Bottom-Up):**
Do not focus on Daily Active Users (DAU) yet. For a niche developer hub, **Monthly Return Rate** of contributors is the leading indicator of health. If experts do not return, acquisition is wasted. Diagnose in this order:
1.  **Retention:** Of users who posted in Week 1, how many returned to answer a question in Week 4?
2.  **Activation:** Of new registrants, what percentage posted their first question or answer within 48 hours?
3.  **Acquisition:** Only after retention is stable, measure new signups.

**Immediate Event Taxonomy (Instrument These Questions Only):**
Stop tracking "total posts." Implement these three specific events to answer named questions for the board:

| Event Name | Question It Answers | Properties to Capture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `solution_accepted` | Are users finding working answers? | `time_to_solution_minutes`, `is_mentor_verified` (bool) |
| `mentor_interaction` | Is the mentorship program functioning? | `mentor_id` (pseudonymous), `thread_depth`, `sentiment_flag` |
| `return_contribution` | Do experts come back? | `days_since_last_login`, `user_cohort_month` |

**Mentorship & Quality Specifics:**
To track mentorship without inventing benchmarks, measure the **Mentor Response Ratio**: the count of threads with a reply from a verified mentor divided by total unanswered threads after 24 hours. For solution quality, track the **Acceptance Rate**: the percentage of threads where the original poster marks a reply as the solution. If these numbers are low, high post volume is merely noise, not health.

**Decision Framework for the Board:**
*   **If** `solution_accepted` is low but `mentor_interaction` is high: The bottleneck is technical complexity; justify budget for *senior* moderators to close loops.
*   **If** `return_contribution` is low: The community feels unrewarding; justify budget for gamification or recognition programs before spending on acquisition.
*   **If** both are rising: The current moderator budget is working; request an increase proportional to the growth in `Weekly Active Resolvers`.

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Define "Resolved":** Agree with moderators on the exact button click or tag that constitutes a solved thread and ensure it is logged as `solution_accepted`.
2.  **Tag Mentors:** Add a boolean property `is_mentor` to user profiles (stored pseudonymously) to filter the `mentor_interaction` event.
3.  **Baseline Query:** Run a one-time query on existing data to count how many January registrants have posted in the last 14 days; use this as your `UNVERIFIED` baseline until new events flow.

*Note on Data Privacy: Ensure user IDs used for cohort tracking are pseudonymous hashes; route any PII handling to your privacy compliance review. No external benchmarks were used; all success criteria are based on CodeBridge's own trend lines.*
