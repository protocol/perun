### PawsProtect Beta Measurement Plan (UK)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing a **Valid Quote Request**.
*Definition:* A user who completes the full comparison flow and triggers a partner link click.
*Why:* This captures the core value exchange (user gets price, partner gets lead) without relying on untrackable offline purchases during beta. "Click-through rate" alone is vanity; it must be tied to a completed quote form to filter accidental clicks.

**Input Metrics (Daily Monitoring):**
1.  **Activation Rate:** % of visitors who start the quote wizard and reach step 3 (pet details). *Diagnoses friction in the funnel start.*
2.  **Quote Completion Rate:** % of starters who finish the wizard. *Diagnoses form fatigue or trust issues.*
3.  **Partner Link Click-Through (Post-Quote):** % of quote completers who actually click a partner offer. *Validates if the quote results are relevant.*

**Lagging Metrics (Monthly Review):**
1.  **Lead Quality Score:** % of generated quotes that partners flag as "valid" vs. "spam/incomplete" (requires manual feedback loop with partners).
2.  **Estimated Conversion Proxy:** Since offline purchase is `UNVERIFIED` without partner API integration, track the **repeat visit rate** of users who requested a quote. High return visits within 7 days correlate with high purchase intent.

**Seasonality Adjustment (April Spike):**
Do not adjust the target numbers artificially. Instead, **segment cohorts by acquisition date**. Compare April users against March users *only* after 7 and 30 days. If April acquisition spikes but Day-7 retention drops, the seasonal traffic is low-intent "browsers," not buyers. The metric to watch is **Retention of the April Cohort**, not total volume.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These):**
*   `quote_wizard_started` (Question: Is the entry point clear?)
*   `quote_wizard_abandoned` + `step_number` (Question: Where do we lose them?)
*   `quote_completed` (Question: Did we deliver value?)
*   `partner_link_clicked` + `partner_id` (Question: Which partners are preferred?)
*   `user_returned_7d` (Question: Are they considering purchase?)

**Investor Reporting Narrative:**
"We are optimizing for **Valid Quote Requests**, not raw traffic. While we expect 5,000 visitors, our success metric is the conversion from Visitor → Quote → Partner Click. We are currently treating 'Purchase' as `UNVERIFIED` until we establish a data share with partners; until then, 'Repeat Visit within 7 Days' serves as our proxy for high-intent leads."

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  Implement the 5 events above with a **pseudonymous user ID** to track returns across sessions (do not use raw emails/PII in analytics; route identity mapping to privacy compliance).
2.  Establish a weekly manual check with 2 key insurance partners to classify the last 50 leads as "High/Low Quality" to build your internal baseline.
3.  Set a dashboard alert if `quote_wizard_abandoned` at step 1 exceeds 40% of starters, indicating a broken entry promise.

*Note: Do not invent industry benchmarks for "good" conversion rates; compare all metrics against your own Week 1 baseline. Any claim of "purchase conversion" without partner data integration is `UNVERIFIED`.*
