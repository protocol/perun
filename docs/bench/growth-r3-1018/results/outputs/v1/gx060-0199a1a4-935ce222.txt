**Subject:** Q3 Referral Program Strategy: Social vs. Email Allocation

**Executive Summary**
The 30-day test (ending May 5) yielded an overall viral coefficient of 0.8, missing the 1.2 target. However, aggregate data masks a critical divergence: Social Media cohorts achieved a coefficient of 1.5 (exceeding targets), while Email cohorts stalled at 0.4. This variance indicates the incentive structure is not perceived uniformly; it resonates socially but fails in direct communication contexts.

**Strategic Recommendation**
Do not attempt to "fix" the email flow immediately. The gap between 1.5 and 0.4 suggests a fundamental mismatch in user intent or offer framing for email recipients, not merely a tactical leak. Attempting to force email performance to match social without understanding the root cause risks inflating Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) beyond our $15 limit.

**Action Plan for Q3:**
1.  **Reallocate Budget to Social:** Shift 80% of the remaining referral budget to scale social acquisition channels where product-market fit for the referral offer is already proven (k=1.5). This maximizes volume within the $15 CPA constraint.
2.  **Pause Broad Email Blasts:** Halt generic email referral campaigns. Retain email only for high-intent triggers (e.g., post-purchase) if data supports it, but stop broad spending here until the disconnect is resolved.
3.  **Diagnostic Sprint (2 Weeks):** Before reinvesting in email, investigate three specific factors:
    *   **Offer Framing:** Does the email copy feel transactional compared to the social "status" signal?
    *   **Friction Points:** Is the referral link click-to-share path significantly longer in email clients vs. social apps?
    *   **Audience Intent:** Are email recipients primarily existing users being asked to refer, whereas social captures new users referring peers?

**Decision Criteria**
We will revisit email investment only if the diagnostic sprint identifies a fixable friction point that projects a lift to k>0.9 without exceeding the $15 CPA. Until then, scaling the winning channel (Social) is the only mathematically sound path to hitting Q3 growth targets.

**Next Steps**
*   **May 8:** Finalize reallocation of Q3 budget to social channels.
*   **May 20:** Present findings from the diagnostic sprint on email friction/framing.
*   **June 1:** Launch optimized social-only scale campaign.
