**Recommendation for Growth Lead Priya: Activation Definition for WriteMate**

Define "Activated" strictly as: **Generate an outline AND apply at least one edit.**

Do not lower the bar to "Sign-in" or "Generate only," and do not raise it to "Export."

**Rationale & Trade-off Analysis**
Your internal data indicates a 60% week-one retention rate for users who edit. This is your "Aha!" moment signal. Users who only generate without editing are exhibiting "casual curiosity"; they consume compute resources but rarely realize value or convert. Lowering the definition to "Sign-in" or "Generate only" would inflate your activation metric with vanity volume while masking the fact that these users churn. Since 30% of sign-ups already drop off at profile setup, adding an edit step does not create meaningful *new* friction for qualified leads; it merely filters out low-intent traffic earlier.

Conversely, raising the bar to "Export" is too high for a 14-day conversion window. Exporting is a "Revenue" or "Referral" stage action in the AARRR funnel, not "Activation." If you wait for export to count a user as activated, you will under-report the health of your onboarding flow and delay necessary interventions for users who are engaged but haven't yet reached the final output stage.

**Implementation Plan for April 5th Launch**
1.  **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument two specific events: `outline_generated` and `outline_edited`. The "Activated" cohort is the intersection of users completing both within their first session or 24-hour window.
2.  **Welcome Email Trigger:** Configure the April 5th sequence to trigger the first educational email only upon `outline_edited`. Users who only generate should receive a different, lower-frequency nudge focused on *how* to refine results, rather than the standard "getting started" flow.
3.  **Friction Mitigation:** If the edit rate is unexpectedly low post-launch, do not change the metric definition. Instead, A/B test the UI to make the "Edit" button more prominent or offer one-click "Improve Tone" suggestions to reduce the effort required to perform that first edit.
