### Pilot Success Framework for FreshBox Board

**North Star Metric: 4-Week Rolling Retention of Core Action**
Reject weekly retention as your primary success indicator; in a population of 12,000, single-week data is too volatile to distinguish signal from noise (e.g., local festivals). Instead, measure the percentage of subscribers who complete the **core action** (receiving and keeping a box) at least once within any rolling 4-week window. This smooths out single-event anomalies while proving users derive recurring value.

**Supporting Input Metrics (The Viability Dashboard)**
Present these three metrics to the board to demonstrate stability despite local volatility:
1.  **Cohort-Based Retention Curve:** Group users by their start week (e.g., "Jan 15 Cohort," "Feb 1 Cohort"). Plot the % of each cohort active in Week 1, Week 2, Week 3, and Week 4. Success is defined not by a flat line, but by the curve flattening (stabilizing) after Week 4. If the "Jan 15" curve looks similar to the "Feb 1" curve despite different local events, the model is robust.
2.  **Order Frequency per Active Subscriber:** Calculate the average number of boxes ordered per *active* user per month. This isolates engagement depth from acquisition spikes. If this number holds steady (e.g., 3.5 boxes/month) even when total subscriber count dips, the core value proposition is intact.
3.  **Qualitative Churn Reason Tagging:** Since quantitative data is noisy, instrument a mandatory "reason for skipping/canceling" field. Success requires that <20% of churn is attributed to "product fit" issues. If 80% of churn is tagged "away for festival" or "travel," the drop in retention is external, not a product failure.

**Event Taxonomy & Instrumentation Rules**
Do not instrument every click. Track only these events to answer specific board questions:
*   `subscription_renewed`: Answers "Do they see long-term value?" (Property: `cohort_id`, `skip_reason`).
*   `box_skipped`: Answers "Is this a product issue or life event?" (Property: `reason_code`).
*   `referral_code_used`: Answers "Is word-of-mouth working?" (Property: `source_event`).

**Handling the "Small Town" Volatility**
Explicitly state to the board: "We do not compare week-over-week totals. We compare **cohort behavior** over 4-week cycles." A drop in Week 3 for the January cohort due to a festival is irrelevant if the February cohort shows the same Week 3 dip. Parallel cohort behavior proves the external factor; divergent behavior proves a product issue.
