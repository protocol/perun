### Core Metric Definitions for BuildFlow Safety Compliance Beta

**1. North Star Metric: Weighted Safety Compliance Rate (WSCR)**
*Definition:* The percentage of required safety protocols completed, normalized by project stage and active workforce, representing the true adoption of safety culture regardless of firm size or project phase.
*Formula:* `(Sum of Completed Protocols across all active projects) / (Sum of Required Protocols based on Project Stage & Headcount)`
*Why this works:* This rejects raw counts (vanity) which favor large firms. It answers: "Are users actually performing the safety work required for their specific context?"

**2. Input Metric A: Stage-Normalized Activation Rate**
*Definition:* The percentage of beta firms that complete their first full cycle of stage-appropriate safety checks within 7 days of onboarding.
*Logic:*
- **Planning Phase Projects:** Required cycle = Submission of Risk Assessment + Site Setup Checklist (2 items).
- **Active Construction Projects:** Required cycle = Daily Hazard Log + Equipment Check + Personnel Briefing (3 items).
*Formula:* `(Firms completing stage-specific cycle within 7 days) / (Total Firms onboarded)`
*Context for CS Team:* Explain to small operators that "activation" means finishing their 2-item planning list, while large firms must finish their 3-item active list. Both count equally as "activated."

**3. Input Metric B: Retention of Core Action (Weekly Cohort)**
*Definition:* The percentage of firms that maintain a WSCR > 80% for at least 4 out of 5 days in a given week, measured by cohort (week of signup).
*Logic:* Filters out "one-and-done" compliance. True value is consistent behavior.
*Formula:* `(Firms with ≥4 days of WSCR > 80% in Week X) / (Total Active Firms in Week X)`
*Context for CS Team:* Use this to identify struggling firms. If a single-person operator drops off, it's a usability issue. If a 50-person firm drops off, it's likely a workflow integration issue.

**4. Event Taxonomy (Instrumentation Rules)**
Only instrument events that answer the specific questions above. Do not track every click.
- `safety_protocol_viewed`: Answers "Did they see the requirement?" (Property: `project_stage`, `firm_size_bucket`)
- `safety_protocol_completed`: Answers "Did they do the work?" (Property: `protocol_type`, `worker_count_at_time`)
- `project_stage_updated`: Answers "Did the requirements change?" (Triggers recalculation of denominator for WSCR)

**Normalization Logic for Project Stages**
To solve the variance between planning and active construction, the system must dynamically assign a "Required Protocol Count" per project per day:
- **If Stage = Planning:** Denominator = 2 (Risk Assessment, Site Setup).
- **If Stage = Active:** Denominator = 3 + (0.1 × Active Workers on Site). *Note: The worker multiplier caps at 5 to prevent large sites from skewing the average disproportionately.*
- **If Stage = Paused:** Denominator = 0 (Project excluded from WSCR calculation for that day).

**Customer Success Talking Points**
- **For Single-Person Operators:** "Success is hitting 100% on your 2-item planning list. You aren't compared to the big guys."
- **For Large Firms:** "Success is maintaining an 80% rate even with 50 workers. We measure your consistency, not just your volume."
- **For Mixed Portfolios:** "Your dashboard shows a weighted average. A paused project doesn't hurt your score; it just drops out of the math until work resumes."
