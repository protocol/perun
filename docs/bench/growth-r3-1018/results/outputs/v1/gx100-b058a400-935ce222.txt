# MetroStyle Rebrand Measurement Plan

**Audience:** External Agency Partners & Internal Executive Team
**Objective:** Isolate rebrand impact from seasonal noise; validate leading indicators before sales data matures.

### 1. The North Star & Input Metrics
Reject vanity metrics (total pageviews, raw impression counts). Focus on value realization.
*   **North Star:** **Weekly Active Returning Customers completing a Purchase.** This rises only when the rebrand successfully resonates with existing loyalty, decoupling brand health from one-off holiday spikes.
*   **Input Metrics:**
    *   Direct Traffic Volume (segmented by "New" vs. "Returning").
    *   Conversion Rate (Returning Visitors only).
    *   Brand Search Volume (branded keywords only).

### 2. Baseline & Seasonal Control (The "Before" State)
Do not use industry benchmarks. Establish MetroStyle's own baseline using **January 2023 vs. January 2024** data to normalize for post-holiday seasonality.
*   **Baseline Window:** Measure weekly trends from Oct 1 – Dec 31 for the last two years.
*   **Control Group Strategy:** If possible, hold back a small percentage (5-10%) of email subscribers or retargeting audiences from seeing the new creative until Feb 1. Compare their behavior against the exposed group to isolate the "rebrand effect" from general market uplift.
*   **Attribution Rule:** Any lift <10% in January is `UNVERIFIED` until it exceeds the historical variance of your own post-holiday drop-off.

### 3. Leading Indicators (Early Signals)
Monitor these weekly starting Jan 15 to predict sales before the month closes:
*   **Direct Navigation Rate:** % of sessions arriving via direct URL entry or branded search. A rise here signals improved brand recall before conversion happens.
*   **Return Visitor Depth:** Average pages per session for returning users. Increased engagement with new brand assets often precedes conversion.
*   **Cart Initiation Rate (Returning):** Are returning users adding items to carts at a higher rate? This is a faster signal than completed purchase.

### 4. Reporting Structure & Isolation Logic
Structure the weekly report to answer one question: *Did the rebrand move the needle beyond normal seasonal decay?*

| Metric | Comparison Logic | Success Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Traffic** | YoY (Jan '25 vs Jan '24) adjusted by 2023-2024 decay rate | >20% lift over adjusted baseline |
| **Returning CVR** | Week-over-Week (Jan 15-21 vs Jan 8-14) | Sustained 5% lift for 2 consecutive weeks |
| **Brand Search** | MoM (Jan vs Dec) | Flat or rising (contrary to typical post-holiday drop) |

**Guardrail Metric:** Monitor **New Visitor Bounce Rate**. If the rebrand confuses new users acquired via paid channels, this will spike. A win on returning users paired with a spike in new user bounce indicates a brand disconnect.

### 5. This-Week Steps
1.  **Extract Historical Data:** Pull weekly direct traffic and returning CVR for Oct–Jan for 2023 and 2024 to calculate the exact "post-holiday decay" baseline by EOD tomorrow.
2.  **Define Cohorts:** Configure analytics to tag users exposed to rebrand assets (Jan 15+) vs. unexposed control (if feasible) or pre-launch baseline.
3.  **Set Alert Thresholds:** Program alerts for "Direct Traffic" and "Returning CVR" to trigger only if movement exceeds ±2 standard deviations of your historical January variance, preventing false alarms from normal noise.

*Note: All metrics require a stable, pseudonymous user ID to track returning visitors accurately across sessions; ensure PII handling complies with privacy standards before implementation.*
*Do not declare victory on Day 2; wait for the first full week (Jan 15-21) to close before assessing significance.*
