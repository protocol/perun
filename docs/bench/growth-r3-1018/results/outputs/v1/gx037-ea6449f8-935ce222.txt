### FundLatam Launch Measurement Plan (Brazil & Mexico)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Gross Volume Completed (funds successfully cleared via local methods like *boleto* or PIX, not just pledged).
*Why:* "Pledged" is vanity in these markets if payment completion fails. Only cleared volume validates the business model and moves toward the $2M quarterly target.

**Input Metrics (Weekly):**
1.  **Payment Method Completion Rate:** Split by *boleto bancario*, PIX (Brazil), and OXXO/SPEI (Mexico). This isolates whether low conversion is friction (payment failure) or fit (lack of intent).
2.  **Activation Rate:** Percentage of visitors who view a campaign detail page AND initiate a checkout flow.
3.  **Retention Cohort (Backers):** Percentage of users who make a second contribution within 30 days.

**Diagnostic Signals to Distinguish Friction vs. Fit:**
*   **Signal A (Friction):** High "Initiate Checkout" but low "Payment Completed" specifically on *boleto*/cash vouchers.
    *   *Diagnosis:* The market wants the product, but the payment UX is failing. Users abandon because generating/printing the voucher is too hard or instructions are unclear.
    *   *Action:* Optimize the payment instruction UI; do not change the campaign offering.
*   **Signal B (Fit):** Low "Initiate Checkout" despite high ad impressions and campaign page views.
    *   *Diagnosis:* The offer does not resonate with local cultural or economic contexts; pricing or reward structures may be misaligned.
    *   *Action:* Pivot campaign messaging or reward tiers; do not optimize the checkout flow yet.

**30-Day Board Justification Framework:**
Do not report raw conversion rates against US benchmarks. Instead, present a **Trend-vs-Baseline** analysis:
*   **Week 1 Baseline:** Establish the local completion rate for *boleto*/cash methods.
*   **Week 2-4 Trend:** Show the week-over-week delta. If the trend is positive (even if absolute numbers are below US averages), investment is justified to optimize the funnel. If the trend is flat or negative despite UX fixes, the hypothesis of market fit is `UNVERIFIED` and requires a strategic pivot.

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Instrument:** Tag the `payment_initiated` and `payment_completed` events with a `payment_method` property immediately. Without this split, you cannot distinguish friction from fit.
2.  **Query:** Run a cohort analysis on Day 7 comparing completion rates of users who selected *boleto*/cash vs. credit card.
3.  **Decision Rule:** If cash-method completion is <40% of initiation by Day 14, pause acquisition spend and fix the payment flow before buying more of the 100k impressions.

*Note: All metrics must be tracked using a stable, pseudonymous user ID to link multi-session behaviors (e.g., generating a boleto today, paying it tomorrow) without violating privacy compliance.*
*Do not assert a "good" conversion rate; compare your Week 2 performance strictly against your Week 1 baseline.*
