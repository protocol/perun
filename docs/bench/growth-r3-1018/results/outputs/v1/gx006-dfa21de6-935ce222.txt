### Referral Program Success Framework: NeoVida

**Primary Metric (North Star): Referred User Activation Rate**
*Definition:* The percentage of referred users who complete the core value action (e.g., first successful deposit, card activation, or first bill payment) within 14 days of signup.
*Rationale:* Acquisition counts are vanity; this metric isolates "quality" sign-ups from fraud rings. Fraudsters rarely complete the friction of a genuine financial transaction required to activate an account. This directly validates your hypothesis that cash incentives drive liquidity-seeking behavior rather than just account creation.

**Secondary Metrics**
1.  **Referrer Retention Delta:** The difference in 30-day retention between users who successfully referred an *activated* friend versus those who did not.
    *   *Decision Rule:* If referrers who invite activated users show higher retention, the program builds community value. If retention is flat or negative, the incentive is attracting low-quality referrers gaming the system.
2.  **Cost Per Activated Referral (CPAR):** Total bonus spend divided by the number of referred users who hit the Activation Metric.
    *   *Decision Rule:* Compare CPAR against your standard Cost Per Acquisition (CAC). If CPAR > 1.2x CAC, the cash bonus is inefficient despite high volume.
3.  **Fraud Velocity Flag:** The rate of referrals originating from identical IP addresses, device fingerprints, or funding sources within a 24-hour window.
    *   *Decision Rule:* Any spike >2% of total referrals triggers an immediate pause on bonus payouts for manual review.

**Guardrail Metric: Net Revenue Impact (30-Day)**
*Definition:* (Revenue from referred cohort) minus (Bonus payouts + Operational costs).
*Purpose:* Prevents the "win" of high acquisition volume from masking a net loss. The program fails if this number is negative after 30 days, regardless of user count.

**This-Week Steps**
1.  **Instrument the "Activation" Event:** Define and tag the specific transaction (e.g., `first_deposit_completed`) in your analytics pipeline immediately; without this, the Primary Metric is `UNVERIFIED`.
2.  **Pre-commit the Fraud Threshold:** Set the specific `Fraud Velocity Flag` percentage (e.g., 2%) and the automatic payout freeze rule in your operations playbook before the October launch.

**Note:** Do not compare these rates to industry benchmarks; measure them against NeoVida's organic baseline to determine true lift. Ensure user identifiers used for cohort tracking are pseudonymized and routed to your privacy compliance protocol.
