### Balanced Scorecard: Holiday Velocity vs. Seller Health

**North Star Metric:** **30-Day Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) per Active Seller.**
This rises only when items sell (velocity) at sustainable prices (health), rejecting vanity metrics like "total listings" or "price drop count."

#### 1. Buyer Conversion & Velocity (The Accelerator)
*   **Time-to-First-Sale (Median):** Track the median hours from listing to sale, segmented by category.
*   **Price Drop Efficiency:** The percentage of items selling *within 48 hours* of a price drop. If this drops while drop frequency rises, the tactic is failing.
*   **Conversion Rate Post-Drop:** Compare conversion rates of items that dropped price vs. those that didn't, controlling for item age.

#### 2. Seller Health & Liquidity (The Guardrails)
*   **Realized Yield Ratio:** (Final Sale Price) / (Initial Listing Price). A declining trend here indicates sellers are conditioning to underprice immediately.
*   **Seller Churn Rate (30-day):** Specifically for sellers whose items sold via rapid price drops vs. organic sales.
*   **Relisting Frequency:** The rate at which sellers relist the *same* item after a failed rapid-drop sale. An increase suggests inventory instability and seller frustration.

#### 3. Signals of Optimization Failure
Watch for these specific divergences indicating velocity is killing trust or liquidity:
*   **The "Race to the Bottom" Signal:** Velocity (sales/day) increases, but **Realized Yield Ratio** decreases by >5% week-over-week. This confirms sellers are earning less to move stock.
*   **The "Ghost Inventory" Signal:** **Relisting Frequency** spikes. Sellers are gaming the algorithm with fake drops, then relisting at original prices, corrupting data and buyer trust.
*   **The "Support Burden" Signal:** A rise in support tickets tagged "pricing confusion" or "unexpected low sale" from the cohort exposed to aggressive drop prompts.

#### 4. Tracking Seller Lifetime Value (LTV)
Do not estimate LTV; instrument the comparison directly using a **stable, pseudonymous Seller ID** (routing PII to privacy compliance).
*   **Cohort Definition:** Split sellers into two groups based on December behavior:
    *   *Cohort A (Fast Sale):* >50% of items sold within 48 hours of a price drop.
    *   *Cohort B (Organic/Slow):* Items sold without price drops or after >7 days.
*   **The Metric:** **Net Revenue Per Seller (NRPS) at Day 90.**
    *   Calculate: (Total GMV generated by seller) − (Platform Fees) − (Support Costs attributed to seller).
    *   Compare the Day 90 NRPS of Cohort A vs. Cohort B. If Cohort A has higher velocity in December but lower NRPS at Day 90 (due to churn or lower average order value in Jan/Feb), the strategy destroys long-term value.
