### Recommended Event Taxonomy and Success Score for LutongKusina Video

To define "engagement" without fabricating benchmarks, instrument only the events that answer specific questions about creator value and repeat behavior. Reject "total watch time" as a standalone North Star; it rewards passive length over actual utility. Instead, track the following four events, each tied to a named question:

1.  **`video_completion_rate`**
    *   **Question:** Did the user see the critical cooking steps?
    *   **Definition:** Count of sessions where watch time ≥ 80% of video duration / Total video starts.
    *   **Why:** A 30-second view on a 2-minute recipe is likely a failure (user missed the method), whereas a 30-second view on a 40-second tip is success. Normalizing by duration answers this.

2.  **`active_interaction_rate`**
    *   **Question:** Did the content spark community action (the signal your managers observed)?
    *   **Definition:** (Count of `comment_added` + `share_initiated` + `recipe_saved`) / Total video starts.
    *   **Why:** Your hypothesis is that active signals drive repeat uploads. This isolates those specific behaviors from passive scrolling.

3.  **`creator_return_interval`**
    *   **Question:** Does this engagement actually lead to more supply?
    *   **Definition:** Time delta (in days) between a creator's `video_upload` event and their *next* `video_upload`, correlated against the `active_interaction_rate` of the previous video.
    *   **Why:** This validates your community managers' observation. If high comments/shares correlate with shorter return intervals, that is your leading indicator.

4.  **`quality_flag_rate`**
    *   **Question:** Are we incentivizing the *right* kind of content?
    *   **Definition:** Count of `content_reported` or `low_quality_flagged` / Total uploads.
    *   **Why:** Prevents gaming the system with spammy short clips just to boost completion rates.

#### The "Creator Health" Score Formula
Do not present raw numbers alone. Combine these into a single weighted index for the dashboard:

$$ \text{Health Score} = (0.4 \times \text{Completion Rate}) + (0.4 \times \text{Active Interaction Rate}) + (0.2 \times \text{Normalized Return Lift}) $$

*Note: "Normalized Return Lift" is a derived metric comparing a creator's return interval against their own historical baseline (not a global benchmark).*

**Implementation Constraints:**
*   **Identifier Seam:** To calculate `creator_return_interval`, you must link events via a stable, pseudonymous `creator_id`. Do not use raw emails or device IDs directly in this analytics layer; route PII mapping to your privacy compliance protocol.
*   **Baseline Rule:** The dashboard must display trends against the creator's *own* prior performance (e.g., "Your engagement is up 10% vs. your last 3 videos"). Do not display "Industry Average" thresholds; we have no source for Filipino recipe app benchmarks, and inventing one would be misleading.
