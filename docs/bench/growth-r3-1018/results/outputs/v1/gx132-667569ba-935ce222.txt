### Success Definition: PayBridge Instant Conversion Launch (Vietnam & Thailand)

**North Star Metric**
**Weekly Active Transacting Merchants (WATM)** completing an instant conversion.
*Why:* Raw transaction volume ($500k target) is a vanity metric that can be spiked by one-off large transfers. WATM proves that small businesses are deriving recurring value from the speed upgrade, directly correlating to long-term viability.

**Primary Input Metrics (The Levers)**
1.  **Activation Rate:** % of eligible merchants who complete their first instant conversion within 7 days of feature access.
    *   *Question answered:* Is the "under 10 minutes" promise clear and accessible enough to trigger the first try?
2.  **Day-14 Retention Cohort:** % of activators who perform a second conversion within 14 days.
    *   *Question answered:* Does speed drive habitual use, or was the first transaction a one-time test? This validates the "speed vs. fee" hypothesis better than surveys.
3.  **Fee Sensitivity Ratio:** Average transaction size segmented by users who opted for "Instant" vs. "Standard" (if both are offered).
    *   *Question answered:* Are merchants using instant conversion only for small, urgent cash flows while saving large batches for cheaper, slower rails?

**Secondary Viability Metrics**
*   **Repeat Usage Frequency:** Average conversions per active merchant per week.
*   **Failed Transaction Rate (User-Initiated):** % of users who abandon the flow after seeing the final exchange rate/fee but before confirming.
    *   *Note:* Distinguish this from technical failures; this measures price/shock resistance.

**Cultural & Regional Data Interpretation Notes**
*   **Trust via Community:** In both Vietnam and Thailand, small business decisions are heavily influenced by local peer networks. A spike in activation clustered by geographic district or trade association may indicate organic word-of-mouth rather than product-market fit alone. Do not attribute clustered growth solely to marketing spend.
*   **Cash-Flow Urgency vs. Cost:** Merchants in these markets often operate on thin margins with daily liquidity needs. If retention is high but average transaction size remains small, it confirms they prioritize speed for operational survival, not necessarily for large settlements. Do not interpret small ticket sizes as lack of confidence; interpret them as fitting the use case.
*   **Mobile-First Behavior:** Expect 90%+ of interactions on mobile. If activation drops on mobile compared to desktop, the friction is likely UI-related, not value-related.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument the "Quote-to-Confirm" Funnel:** Define events for `conversion_quote_viewed`, `fee_displayed`, and `conversion_confirmed` to calculate the specific drop-off where fee shock occurs.
2.  **Pre-commit the Retention Cohort Query:** Define the Day-14 retention query now using the `merchant_id` (pseudonymized) to ensure consistent tracking from Day 1; do not wait for data to accumulate to define the cohort.
3.  **Establish the Baseline:** Run a query on current standard-transaction repeat rates for Vietnam/Thailand to serve as the control group for the new feature's retention comparison.

*Note: All metric values (e.g., "good" retention %) must be derived from PayBridge's own historical baseline once live; no external benchmarks are applied. Per-user identifiers must be pseudonymized and routed to privacy compliance protocols before analysis.*
