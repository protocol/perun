**Recommendation for the Executive Director: The North Star Metric**

**Proposed North Star:** **Weekly Active Learners Completing Core Milestones.**

This metric replaces "Total Sign-ups" as our primary measure of success. It counts the number of unique students who complete a defined, value-delivering action (a "Core Milestone") within a given week.

**Why This Metric Balances Scale and Impact:**
1.  **Rejects Vanity:** Total sign-ups (15,000) measure intent, not value. A user who signs up but never codes generates cost without impact. This metric ignores them.
2.  **Aligns Donors and Mission:** Donors fund *change*, not clicks. By tracking active completion, we prove we are delivering education, not just hosting it. It directly addresses the 12% completion leak by making retention visible weekly.
3.  **Accommodates Diverse Goals:** We define "Core Milestone" regionally or by track:
    *   *Career Track:* Completing a capstone project or applying to 3 jobs.
    *   *Enrichment Track:* Completing a module or building a personal script.
    *   *Result:* Both groups count toward the North Star if they achieve their specific "aha" moment, solving your quantification uncertainty without diluting the metric.

**Input Metrics (The Levers):**
To move the North Star, we will monitor these three inputs:
*   **Activation Rate:** % of sign-ups who complete their *first* Core Milestone within 7 days.
*   **Retention Cohort:** % of students returning to complete a second Milestone in Week 2, 3, and 4.
*   **Milestone Diversity:** The ratio of Career vs. Enrichment completions (to ensure we aren't neglecting one group).

**Measurement Framework for Multi-Region Accuracy:**
*   **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument only one event per track: `milestone_completed` with properties `{track_type: "career" | "enrichment", region: "LATAM_X"}`. Do not instrument every click; only what proves value.
*   **Identity Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous Student ID to track progress across sessions and devices. Do not use raw PII (names/emails) in analytics logs; route those to our secure CRM for compliance.
*   **Baseline Comparison:** We will not compare against external "industry benchmarks" (which do not exist for this specific demographic). Success is defined by **month-over-month growth in our own active learners** and **improvement in our own retention cohorts**.

**Decision for the Upcoming Year:**
Shift resources from "Acquisition Campaigns" (driving sign-ups) to "Activation & Retention" (ensuring the first milestone is reached). Pouring traffic into a funnel with 12% completion is waste. Fixing the leak ensures that every new sign-up actually contributes to the North Star.
