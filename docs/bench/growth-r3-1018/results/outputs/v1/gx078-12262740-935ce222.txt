**Recommended North Star Metric:** **Compliant Rides per Available Bike per Day**

This single metric balances your business need for asset utilization with the city's requirement for order. It is calculated as:
*(Total Rides Ending in Designated Zones) ÷ (Total Deployed Bikes)*

**Why this metric works for both sides:**
1.  **Business Health:** Unlike raw "ride counts," this normalizes for your fleet size (5,000 bikes). If rides go up but compliance goes down, this metric drops, signaling that growth is unsustainable due to redistribution costs or regulatory risk. It directly targets your 20% non-compliance leak.
2.  **Regulatory Satisfaction:** By counting only rides that end correctly, you align your success definition with the mayors' goal of reducing sidewalk clutter. A rising metric proves to officials that increased usage is not coming at the cost of public space.
3.  **Operational Focus:** It forces the expansion team to prioritize redistribution and geofencing accuracy over blind acquisition. If bikes are stuck in non-compliant zones, they cannot generate "compliant rides," immediately highlighting operational bottlenecks.

**Input Metrics to Track (The Levers):**
To move the North Star, track these three specific inputs:
*   **Compliance Rate:** Percentage of rides ending in designated zones (Current baseline: ~80%). Goal: >95%.
*   **Asset Utilization:** Average rides per bike per day (only for bikes available in valid zones).
*   **Redistribution Latency:** Time taken to move a bike from a non-compliant zone back to a valid hub.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These):**
Do not instrument "everything." Implement these three events to answer specific questions:
1.  `ride_end_location_verified`: Answers "Did this user park correctly?" (Properties: `zone_type`, `is_designated`).
2.  `bike_availability_status`: Answers "Is this bike rentable?" (Properties: `battery_level`, `last_known_zone_status`).
3.  `redistribution_task_completed`: Answers "How fast do we fix clutter?" (Properties: `time_to_resolve`, `origin_zone`, `destination_zone`).
