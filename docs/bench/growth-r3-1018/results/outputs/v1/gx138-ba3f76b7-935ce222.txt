**To:** Head of Trust and Safety, LuxVerify
**Subject:** Onboarding Pilot Funnel Definition (Velocity vs. Integrity)
**Date:** Pre-Pilot Review for Jan 10 Launch

To validate the hypothesis that instant ID scanning reduces time-to-list from 4 days to 24 hours without elevating fraud above the 0.5% baseline, we will monitor the following four funnel stages. Each stage pairs a velocity metric with a specific integrity guardrail to ensure security is not compromised for speed in the luxury market context.

**1. Identity Verification Completion**
*   **Velocity Metric:** Time elapsed from "Sign-Up Start" to "ID Scan Success."
    *   *Target:* < 5 minutes (down from manual upload delays).
*   **Integrity Guardrail:** "ID Scan Rejection Rate" and "Manual Override Rate."
    *   *Risk Check:* A spike in manual overrides indicates the automated scan is failing to catch sophisticated forgeries common in luxury goods, shifting burden to ops or letting fraud through.
*   **Decision Rule:** If rejection rates drop significantly compared to the manual baseline, pause the pilot; lower friction may mean lower security.

**2. Account Activation to First Listing Draft**
*   **Velocity Metric:** Percentage of verified sellers who initiate a listing draft within 1 hour of verification.
    *   *Target:* Increase in immediate activation signals reduced friction.
*   **Integrity Guardrail:** "Incomplete/Anomalous Draft Rate."
    *   *Risk Check:* Monitor for listings with missing provenance fields or suspiciously low prices entered rapidly. Fraudsters often rush listings; genuine luxury sellers take time to detail authenticity.
*   **Decision Rule:** If draft completion speed increases but anomaly flags rise, the speed gain is likely driven by bad actors.

**3. First Listing Submission to Live Status**
*   **Velocity Metric:** Time from "Draft Complete" to "Listing Live."
    *   *Target:* Contribute to the overall 24-hour goal.
*   **Integrity Guardrail:** "Post-Listing Takedown Rate (First 48 Hours)."
    *   *Risk Check:* This is the critical lagging indicator. Since the prompt specifies not ignoring post-listing outcomes, we must track items flagged or removed shortly after going live due to authenticity failures.
*   **Decision Rule:** If the 48-hour takedown rate exceeds the historical 0.5% fraud baseline, the instant scan is insufficient, regardless of speed gains.

**4. First Successful Transaction (The True North Star)**
*   **Velocity Metric:** Time from "Sign-Up" to "First Sale."
    *   *Target:* Reduction toward 24 hours.
*   **Integrity Guardrail:** "Chargeback/Dispute Rate due to Authenticity."
    *   *Risk Check:* The ultimate cost of fraud in luxury markets is not just a fake listing, but a disputed transaction damaging marketplace reputation.
*   **Decision Rule:** Any increase in authenticity-related disputes triggers an immediate revert to manual document review.

**Pilot Governance Note:**
We will pre-commit to a sample size of 500 new sellers for the pilot. We will not declare a "win" on speed until the 48-hour post-listing window has closed for the cohort to ensure the fraud guardrails are valid. The primary metric is "Time to First Listing"; the non-negotiable guardrail is "Authenticity Dispute Rate ≤ 0.5%."

**Next Step:** Implement event tracking for "ID Scan Rejection" and "48-Hour Takedown" by January 8th to ensure data readiness for the launch.
