**Recommended North Star Metric: Weekly Settled Transaction Value per Active Business**

For SafiWallet, "Daily Active Users" is a vanity metric that measures network reliability, not product value. In a market with intermittent connectivity and heavy USSD usage, counting app opens penalizes you for infrastructure outside your control. Instead, your North Star must be **Weekly Settled Transaction Value per Active Business**.

This metric captures true economic utility because:
1.  **It is channel-agnostic:** It counts value whether the user transacts via the mobile app, USSD, or through a partner agent, provided the transaction settles on the protocol.
2.  **It aligns with customer value:** A small business owner uses SafiWallet to move money, not to open an app. If they successfully transact once a week via USSD during an outage, they have received full value.
3.  **It filters noise:** It ignores users who open the app but fail to transact due to connection errors, focusing only on completed economic activity.

**Supporting Input Metrics**
To move this North Star, track these specific inputs:
*   **Settlement Success Rate:** The percentage of initiated transactions (app or USSD) that successfully settle. This isolates infrastructure friction from user intent.
*   **USSD vs. App Mix:** The ratio of transactions by channel. A shift toward USSD during outages should not drop the North Star if total value remains stable.
*   **Agent Attribution Rate:** The percentage of total value successfully linked to a specific business ID (addressing your open question on offline attribution).

**Investor Narrative Strategy**
For your Series A deck, frame the 40% drop in "active users" not as a product failure, but as a data artifact of measuring the wrong thing. Present a revised chart showing "Weekly Settled Value" remaining stable or growing during outages, proving that demand is inelastic even when access methods shift. This demonstrates product-market fit despite infrastructure constraints. Total Value Locked (TVL) of $2M is a strong lagging indicator of trust, but Weekly Settled Value is the leading indicator of ongoing utility.

**Addressing the Attribution Gap**
You noted it is an open question how to attribute offline/agent transactions to individual profiles. Until this is solved, any metric claiming "per-user" precision for those specific transactions is **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Immediate Action:** Implement a stable, pseudonymous User ID that is captured at the point of initiation, whether via USSD string parsing or agent keypad entry, before the transaction hits the blockchain.
*   **Compliance Note:** Ensure this identifier remains pseudonymous; route any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) collection and consent management to your legal team's privacy compliance framework, separate from this product analytics stream.
