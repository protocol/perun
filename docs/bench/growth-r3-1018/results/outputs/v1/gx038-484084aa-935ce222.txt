**Proposal: Incremental Touchpoint Valuation for Q3 Budget Allocation**

**Objective**
Resolve the attribution conflict between Marketing (early-stage influence) and Sales (closing interaction) by shifting from "credit assignment" to "incremental lift estimation." This approach prevents double-counting revenue while quantifying the specific value added by whitepapers, webinars, and demos without requiring a true holdout test on enterprise clients.

**Methodology: The Counterfactual Baseline Approach**
Instead of splitting the $4.5M closed ARR arbitrarily, we will estimate what would have happened *without* specific touchpoints using historical internal baselines.

1.  **Establish a "Natural Close" Baseline**
    Identify the subset of won deals from the last four quarters that closed *without* any marketing touchpoints (whitepapers/webinars), relying solely on direct sales outreach or inbound demo requests. Calculate the average sales cycle length and win rate for this group. This represents the revenue Sales would likely capture without marketing support.

2.  **Calculate Incremental Lift per Touchpoint Type**
    For deals that *did* include specific touches, compare their performance against the baseline:
    *   **Whitepaper Lift:** Compare the win rate and cycle speed of opportunities that downloaded a whitepaper in the awareness stage versus the baseline. The revenue difference attributed to this group is the "Whitepaper Lift."
    *   **Webinar Lift:** Isolate opportunities where a decision-maker attended a webinar. Measure the increase in progression speed from "Awareness" to "Demo Requested" compared to the baseline.
    *   **Demo Lift:** Measure the conversion delta between "Demo Requested" and "Closed Won" for leads that attended a webinar versus those that did not.

3.  **Apply a Decay Weighting Logic**
    To avoid double-counting the same $12M pipeline value, apply a diminishing return logic based on proximity to the close, but adjusted for observed lift:
    *   If data shows whitepapers accelerate the cycle by 15%, assign 15% of the *incremental* value to that touch, not 15% of the total contract value.
    *   Assign the remaining value to the final interaction (Sales) only if the earlier touches did not statistically alter the probability of closing.
    *   **Rule:** The sum of attributed values for any single deal must not exceed 100% of the actual revenue. If the calculated lifts sum to >100%, normalize them proportionally.

**Execution Plan for the $12M Pipeline**
*   **Tagging Audit:** Immediately tag all 300 active opportunities with a binary flag for each touchpoint type (Whitepaper: Yes/No, Webinar: Yes/No).
*   **Cohort Analysis:** Segment the pipeline into four groups: (1) Sales Only, (2) Sales + Whitepaper, (3) Sales + Webinar, (4) Full Journey.
*   **Projection:** Apply the historical win-rate lift observed in past closed deals to these current cohorts. If "Full Journey" deals historically close 20% faster and at 10% higher rates than "Sales Only," attribute that specific probability gain to the marketing touches.

**Decision Framework for the CRO**
*   **If Whitepaper Lift > 0:** Allocate budget to content creation proportional to the observed acceleration in cycle time.
*   **If Webinar Lift is negligible on win-rate but high on velocity:** Fund webinars as a sales-efficiency tool, not a primary acquisition channel.
*   **If Sales-only close rates match Full Journey:** Reallocate marketing budget from awareness tactics to direct sales enablement or demo optimization.

**Immediate Next Steps (This Week)**
1.  Run the historical cohort analysis on the $4.5M closed ARR to establish the "Natural Close" baseline and specific lift percentages by touchpoint.
2.  Apply these lift factors to the current $12M pipeline to generate a projected revenue attribution report for the Q3 budget meeting.
3.  Present the findings as "Incremental Value Generated" rather than "Credit Split," satisfying Sales' need for closing ownership and Marketing's need for influence recognition.

*Note: This method relies entirely on EnterpriseCloud's historical win/loss data; no external benchmarks are used. If specific touchpoint data is missing for past deals, those instances will be marked UNVERIFIED and excluded from the lift calculation.*
