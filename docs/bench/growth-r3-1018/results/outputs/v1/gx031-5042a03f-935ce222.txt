### Data Points to Isolate the Cause

To distinguish between latency (SMS delay) and usability (UI confusion), pull the following event properties for the `sms_verification_screen` cohort from March 12 onward, segmented by device screen width:

1.  **Time-to-Code-Received:** Timestamp of `sms_sent` minus timestamp of `code_received_event` (triggered when the user pastes or enters the first digit). This isolates network/carrier latency.
2.  **Time-to-Submit:** Timestamp of `code_received_event` minus timestamp of `submit_clicked`. This isolates input friction.
3.  **Resend Frequency:** Count of `resend_clicked` events per session. High resends coupled with low `code_received_event` firing indicates delivery failure; high resends with immediate `code_received_event` suggests user error or confusion.
4.  **Input Error Rate:** Count of `submit_failed` (invalid code) events. A spike here on small screens specifically points to UI/keyboard overlap or copy-paste failures, not latency.
5.  **Drop-off Timestamp:** Exact time elapsed on screen before `session_ended` without submission. If users leave immediately (<5s), it is likely a UI rendering issue. If they leave after 45s+, it is likely latency frustration.

**Interpretation Logic:**
*   **Latency Hypothesis Confirmed:** High `Time-to-Code-Received` correlates strongly with `session_ended`. `Resend Frequency` is high, but `Input Error Rate` is stable.
*   **UI Hypothesis Confirmed:** `Time-to-Code-Received` is normal, but `Time-to-Submit` is elevated on small screens. `Input Error Rate` spikes on devices <375px width. Drop-off occurs almost immediately upon screen load.
