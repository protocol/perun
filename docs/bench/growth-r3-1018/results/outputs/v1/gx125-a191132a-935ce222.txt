### Experiment Readout Framework: Referral Credit Test

**1. Recompute the Primary Claim (Verification Layer)**
Before analyzing quality, validate the "15% increase" against the test design.
*   **Check Pre-commitment:** Confirm the sample size (50k treatment) and duration (30 days) were fixed *before* August 1st. If the team peeked at data daily and stopped early because significance was hit, label the result `UNVERIFIED` due to inflated false-positive risk.
*   **Recalculate Lift:** Derive the absolute lift from raw counts (Treatment Sign-ups vs. Control Sign-ups). Ensure the 15% figure isn't driven by a baseline anomaly in the control group during that specific month.

**2. Define the "Quality" North Star (Beyond Sign-ups)**
Reject "Total New Sign-ups" as the success metric; it is a vanity metric in this context. Replace it with a value-based North Star for this experiment: **"Week-2 Active Referral Pairs."**
*   *Definition:* A dyad where both the referrer and referee have completed the core networking action (e.g., sending a message or connecting) within 14 days of the referee's sign-up.
*   *Rationale:* This measures network density, not just account count.

**3. The Diagnostic Funnel (Bottom-Up Analysis)**
Analyze the data in this specific order to detect "credit farming":

*   **Step A: Activation Rate (The "Empty Profile" Check)**
    *   Compare the % of referred users who completed their profile *and* performed one core action within 48 hours (Treatment vs. Control).
    *   *Signal:* If sign-ups are up 15% but activation is flat or down, you are acquiring low-intent users.
*   **Step B: Retention Cohorts (The "Churn" Check)**
    *   Plot Week-1 and Week-2 retention curves for the *referred* users only.
    *   *Signal:* A steep drop-off after the credit is claimed indicates transactional behavior. Compare this cohort's retention to organic sign-ups from the same period.
*   **Step C: Referrer Behavior (The "Gaming" Check)**
    *   Segment referrers by volume. Identify users with abnormally high referral counts (>5 in a month).
    *   *Signal:* If a small % of referrers drive the bulk of the lift, investigate if they are self-referring or farming credits.

**4. Guardrail Metrics (Risk Assessment)**
Present these alongside the lift to show due diligence:
*   **Cost Per Active User (CPAU):** Did the cost of credits divide by *active* users increase?
*   **Spam/Abuse Rate:** Monitor reports of spammy connection requests or profile flags from the treatment cohort.
*   **Cannibalization:** Did organic (non-incentivized) referrals drop during the test period?

**5. Decision Matrix (Pre-defined Actions)**
Do not wait for the meeting to decide what the data means. Map the outcomes now:
*   **Ship:** If Week-2 Active Pairs increase ≥10% AND spam rates remain stable.
*   **Iterate:** If sign-ups are up but activation is low → Restrict credit payout until the *second* core action (not just profile completion).
*   **Stop:** If retention of referred users is <50% of the organic baseline OR if abuse flags spike.
