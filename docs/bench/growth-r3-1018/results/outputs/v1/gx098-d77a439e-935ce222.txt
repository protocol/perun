### Framework: Activation-to-Retention Correlation Analysis

**Objective:** Identify the single early user action that most strongly predicts six-month retention to serve as the foundation for SafeChain Wallet's North Star Metric.

**1. Define the Candidate Activation Events**
Instrument and isolate three distinct cohorts based on your hypotheses. Do not track "everything"; measure only these specific triggers:
*   **Event A (Current Hypothesis):** Completed first outgoing transfer.
*   **Event B (Alternative):** Completed first token swap.
*   **Event C (Alternative):** Connected to a decentralized application (dApp).

**2. Establish the Retention Baseline (The "Truth" Metric)**
Define long-term value strictly as **Six-Month Active Retention**: A user who performs *any* value-generating transaction (transfer, swap, or dApp interaction) in Month 6.
*   *Note:* Do not use "logged in" or "wallet opened" as retention; these are vanity metrics in blockchain contexts where users may check balances without transacting.

**3. The Analysis Method: Cohort Correlation Matrix**
For users onboarded in November (N=5,000 projected), calculate the following for each candidate event once six months of data exist (or use historical data if available for early users):
*   **Activation Rate:** % of total signups who completed Event A, B, and C respectively within the first 7 days.
*   **Conditional Retention Rate:** Of the users who completed Event X, what % retained at Month 6?
*   **The Predictor Score:** Multiply *Activation Rate* × *Conditional Retention Rate*.

**Decision Logic for the Board:**
*   **Select the North Star** based on the event with the highest **Predictor Score**, not just the highest retention rate.
    *   *Scenario 1:* If "First Swap" has 80% retention but only 2% of users do it, it is a poor North Star (too rare to drive growth).
    *   *Scenario 2:* If "First Transfer" has 30% retention (as observed) and 40% adoption, it may still be the best driver if "dApp Connect" has 50% retention but only 5% adoption.
*   **Reject Vanity:** If cumulative signups rise but the chosen North Star flatlines, the business is not growing, regardless of marketing spend.

**4. Immediate Instrumentation Requirements**
To execute this by December 10th using early trends:
*   Ensure every event includes a **stable, pseudonymous user ID** to track individuals across sessions without exposing PII (route PII handling to privacy compliance protocols).
*   Tag each event with `campaign_source` to isolate the November Singapore cohort from organic traffic.
*   **Do not fabricate benchmarks.** Compare the November cohort only against your own pre-November baseline. If no prior data exists, label the retention correlation as `UNVERIFIED` until Month 1 data matures.

**5. Recommended Board Presentation Structure**
*   **The North Star Proposal:** "We will optimize for [Selected Event] because it balances frequency and long-term value better than transfers or swaps."
*   **The Risk:** Explicitly state the 30% churn rate on transfers as a known leak to be plugged post-activation.
*   **The Ask:** Approval to instrument these three specific events and pause acquisition spend if the "Activation Rate" for the chosen metric drops below the November baseline.
