### North Star Metric Evaluation for CookConnect

**Recommendation:** Adopt **"Weekly Active Engagers"** defined as users who perform at least two high-value interactions (comments or detailed saves with notes) within a 7-day window. This shifts focus from passive accumulation to active community participation, directly aligning with your beta data showing that commenting drives 45+ days of additional retention.

#### Candidate Comparison

| Metric Candidate | Definition | Why It Fits | Primary Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **1. Total Recipes Saved** (Current Assumption) | Cumulative count of "save" actions across all users. | Easy to track; correlates with content library growth. | **Vanity Trap:** As noted in your beta, saving is passive. Users can hoard recipes without ever cooking or returning. This metric can rise while retention flatlines, misleading investors about true product-market fit. |
| **2. Weekly Active Engagers** (Recommended) | Users completing ≥2 meaningful interactions (comments, notes) per week. | Directly ties to your strongest retention signal (commenting). It measures *value realization* (connecting cooks) rather than just consumption. It scales with community health, not just database size. | **Scaling Friction:** As you grow from 5k to 50k users, maintaining high comment density per user may become harder. If the feed becomes too noisy, interaction quality could drop, artificially suppressing the metric despite healthy usage. |
| **3. Average Session Duration** | Mean time spent in-app per session (currently 8 mins). | Captures depth of immersion; suggests users are reading discussions. | **Efficiency Paradox:** Longer sessions aren't always better. Users might be stuck navigating poor UX or searching for content. Investors increasingly view time-spent as a vanity metric unless tied to a specific conversion outcome. |

#### Strategic Rationale for Investors
For the November 5th pitch, **Weekly Active Engagers** tells a superior story. It proves CookConnect is not just a static recipe box but a living community where value is created through interaction. While "Total Saved" measures inventory, "Active Engagers" measures the *engine* of retention. Your beta data provides the causal link: interaction drives longevity. By committing to this metric, you signal to investors that you prioritize sustainable community dynamics over hollow growth numbers.
