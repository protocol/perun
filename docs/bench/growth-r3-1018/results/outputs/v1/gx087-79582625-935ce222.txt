**Deliverable: Success Metrics for Hometown Herald (Editorial Board Brief)**

**1. The North Star: "Weekly Informed Residents"**
Reject "Time Spent" and raw "Downloads." Time spent rewards confusion or addiction; downloads are a one-time vanity metric. Instead, measure **Weekly Informed Residents**: the count of unique users (App + SMS) who complete a **Core Value Action** at least once per week.
*   **Definition of Core Value Action:** Consuming a full article (scroll to bottom or 30s dwell) OR clicking a link in the SMS digest.
*   **Why:** This captures the "read one and leave" behavior as a success. If a resident reads the school board summary and leaves, they are informed. The metric rises only when real community value is delivered.

**2. The Input Metrics (What Moves the North Star)**
Track these three specific inputs to diagnose health:
*   **Breaking News Return Rate:** The % of users who return within 24 hours of a "Breaking News" push/SMS. This validates your hypothesis about event-driven usage without demanding daily habit formation for non-news days.
*   **Local Amplification:** The count of articles shared to external local groups (Facebook, Nextdoor) *per published article*. This proves essentiality to the social fabric better than internal clicks.
*   **Cross-Channel Reach:** The ratio of SMS-only users to App-only users. Do not force SMS users into the app funnel; count an SMS click as equal value to an App read for the North Star.

**3. The Funnel Diagnosis (Retention First)**
Do not pour money into acquiring more downloads (Acquisition) until you verify retention.
*   **Check:** Of the 3,200 downloaders, how many performed the Core Value Action in Week 2?
*   **Action:** If Week 2 retention is low, the content mix or push timing is broken. Fix this before buying ads for the fall season. High downloads with low retention is a leaky bucket.

**4. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Stop tracking generic "pageviews." Implement only these events:
*   `content_consumed`: Fires when an article is read to completion. *Question: Did we inform the user?*
*   `digest_link_clicked`: Fires on SMS link taps. *Question: Is the SMS channel driving value?*
*   `external_share`: Fires on click of "Share to Social." *Question: Are we sparking community conversation?*
*   `breaking_news_return`: Fires if a user opens the app/SMS within 24h of a breaking alert. *Question: Are we the go-to source for urgent info?*

**5. Experiment Rigor for Fall Season**
If you test a new "Fall Sports" section:
*   **Pre-commit:** Define the test duration (e.g., 2 weeks) and the primary metric (Weekly Informed Residents) before launching.
*   **Guardrail:** Ensure "Unsubscribe Rate" (SMS) or "App Uninstalls" does not spike.
*   **Rule:** Do not stop the test early because numbers look good on Day 2. Peeking creates false wins. Read the result once at the end.
