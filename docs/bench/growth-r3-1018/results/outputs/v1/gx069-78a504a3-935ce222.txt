### Pilot Success Framework: EventFindr Lodz

**North Star Metric: Weekly Active Engagements (WAE)**
Reject "ticket sales volume" as your primary metric; it ignores free events and marketing value, creating a blind spot for 20 of your venues. Instead, track **Weekly Active Engagements**, defined as a user completing a high-intent action: viewing an event detail page, saving an event, sharing an event, or purchasing a ticket. This rises only when users find real value, regardless of payment method.

**Input Metrics (The Drivers)**
1.  **Venue Activation Rate:** % of the 20 pilot venues posting at least one event update per week.
2.  **Core Action Retention:** % of users who perform a WAE in Week 2 after installing.
3.  **Discovery Depth:** Average number of unique event pages viewed per active user.

**AARRR Diagnosis (Bottom-Up)**
Do not focus on your 1,200 pre-launch installs (Acquisition) yet. If users do not return to view events in Week 2 (Retention), pouring marketing spend into Lodz is wasted.
*   **First, verify Retention:** Are the first 500 users coming back?
*   **Second, verify Activation:** Are they finding the "aha" moment (saving/sharing an event)?
*   **Third, verify Venue Supply:** Are venues keeping content fresh enough to drive that return?

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not track "everything." Instrument these specific events to answer named questions:
*   `event_detail_view`: *Do users care about this specific event?* (Properties: event_id, venue_id, is_free).
*   `event_save`: *Is the user intending to attend?* (Properties: event_id, time_to_event).
*   `event_share`: *Is the app providing marketing value to the venue?* (Properties: channel, event_id).
*   `ticket_click_out`: *Did we drive a transaction (even if cash/external)?* (Properties: venue_id, ticket_provider).

**Success Thresholds & Benchmarks**
You asked for specific thresholds. **I cannot fabricate benchmarks** for "healthy" engagement in Lodz or for event apps generally; asserting a number like "10% retention is good" would be an invented statistic.
*   **The Rule:** Compare your pilot data strictly against your **own baseline**.
*   **The Method:** Set your success threshold as a **positive trend over your first two weeks**. If Week 3 WAE is higher than Week 2, you have traction. If it drops, you have a leak.
*   **For Venues:** Define success qualitatively combined with quantitative deltas. A venue sees value if their `event_detail_view` count from the app exceeds their foot traffic estimate from other sources, or if `event_share` counts correlate with their own social media spikes.
*   **Status:** Any specific target number (e.g., "500 shares") is currently **UNVERIFIED**. You must establish this by running the pilot for 14 days, recording the actual numbers, and setting the "success" bar at maintaining or beating that initial baseline.
