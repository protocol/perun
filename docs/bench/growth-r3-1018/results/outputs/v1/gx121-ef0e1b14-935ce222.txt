**To:** VP of Product, FreshCart

**Subject:** North Star Definition and Decision Framework for Checkout Redesign

**1. Primary North Star: Revenue Per Active User (RPAU)**
Do not choose between Order Frequency and Basket Size in isolation. Selecting either alone invites the exact failure mode you identified: optimizing for basket size while cannibalizing frequency, or vice versa. The single metric that rises only when the business wins is **Revenue Per Active User** over a fixed observation window (e.g., 30 days post-exposure).

*   **Formula:** (Total Revenue from Cohort) / (Total Active Users in Cohort).
*   **Why:** This mathematically captures the net effect of your hypothesis. If basket size increases by 10% but frequency drops by 15%, RPAU will fall, correctly signaling a failed experiment despite the "win" in basket size.

**2. Input Metrics & Guardrails**
While RPAU is the verdict, track these inputs to diagnose *how* the result occurred:
*   **Primary Input:** Average Basket Size (per transaction).
*   **Primary Input:** Inter-Order Interval (days between orders).
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Checkout Abandonment Rate. (Ensure simplification does not introduce friction that kills conversion entirely).

**3. Interpreting Conflicting Results**
If Basket Size rises while Order Frequency falls (or inter-order interval lengthens), apply the following decision logic based on the net impact on RPAU:

*   **Scenario A: RPAU Increases.**
    *   *Interpretation:* The value of the larger immediate order outweighs the delay in the next purchase. Users are likely consolidating trips but spending more overall.
    *   *Action:* **Ship.** Monitor long-term churn to ensure this isn't a one-time stock-up followed by cancellation.
*   **Scenario B: RPAU Decreases.**
    *   *Interpretation:* The delay in frequency is costing more revenue than the impulse buys generate. The "simplification" may be removing necessary engagement triggers or the impulse items are low-margin distractions.
    *   *Action:* **Stop.** Do not ship. The hypothesis that impulse buys drive net growth is invalid for this flow.
*   **Scenario C: RPAU is Flat (within confidence interval).**
    *   *Interpretation:* The trade-off is neutral financially.
    *   *Action:* **Defer to Margin.** Calculate the gross margin impact. If impulse buys have lower margins than staple goods, **Stop**. If margins are neutral/higher, consider qualitative user feedback before deciding.

**4. Experimental Rigor Requirements**
To ensure this read is valid and not noise:
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define the sample size and duration (minimum 2 full purchase cycles, approx. 3–4 weeks) *before* launch. Do not peek at day 3 and stop if p < 0.05; this inflates false positives.
*   **Unit of Randomization:** Randomize by **User ID**, not session, to accurately measure frequency changes across multiple orders.
*   **Data Source:** All figures must be pulled from FreshCart's production analytics. No external benchmarks will be used.
