**Recommendation for Executive Steering Committee: Q2 Primary Goal Metric**

**Selected North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users (WAU) completing a **Verified Career Action**.

**Definition:** A user counts toward this metric only when they complete a core learning module *and* execute a tangible career step within the platform ecosystem (e.g., downloading a verifiable certificate, adding a badge to a LinkedIn profile, or completing a capstone project submission).

**Rationale for Selection**
Current metrics present a false dichotomy. "Course Completions" (45%) risks incentivizing content length over utility, penalizing users who efficiently skim for specific answers—a valid behavior for mid-career professionals. Conversely, "Weekly Active Minutes" is a vanity metric; time spent does not equal value gained and can be inflated by passive playback.

The proposed metric resolves this by shifting focus from *consumption* to *outcome*. It acknowledges that value is delivered not when a video ends, but when the learner applies the knowledge. This aligns with the hypothesis that certificate sharing (a specific Verified Career Action) correlates stronger with long-term retention than mere completion. By counting the *action* rather than the *time*, we capture both the deep learners (who finish modules) and the efficient skimmers (who extract value and apply it immediately), provided both groups trigger a career outcome.

**Input Metrics to Monitor**
To move the North Star, the team will optimize these specific inputs:
1.  **Activation Rate:** Percentage of new enrollees who complete their first Verified Career Action within 7 days.
2.  **Action Conversion:** The ratio of module completions that result in a certificate download or social share.
3.  **Retention Cohort:** Percentage of users performing a Verified Career Action in Week 2 relative to Week 1.

**Addressing the Strategic Risks**
*   **Skimming vs. Completing:** This metric validates skimming if it leads to an action. If a user finds an answer in 5 minutes and downloads a checklist, they contribute to the North Star. If they watch 10 hours but take no action, they do not.
*   **Social Sharing Correlation:** We will treat "Social Share" as a primary variant of the Verified Career Action. If data shows users who share retain at 2x the rate of those who only download, the optimization focus shifts explicitly to friction-reduction in the sharing flow.

**This-Week Steps**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Define and tag the specific events constituting a "Verified Career Action" (certificate download, LinkedIn API click, project submit) in the analytics pipeline; until tagged, any analysis of this metric is `UNVERIFIED`.
2.  **Baseline Query:** Run a historical cohort analysis comparing 4-week retention rates between users who previously shared certificates vs. those who only completed courses to validate the correlation hypothesis before April 5th.
3.  **Guardrail Setup:** Establish "Refund Rate" and "Support Ticket Volume" as guardrails to ensure that optimizing for career actions does not degrade content quality or increase confusion.

*Note: All benchmarks must be derived from SkillUp Pro's historical baseline; no external industry averages will be used to define success thresholds.*
*PII Handling: User identifiers for tracking these actions must be pseudonymous; raw PII routing adheres to existing privacy compliance protocols.*
