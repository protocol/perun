**Recommended North Star Metric: Verified Learning Outcomes (VLO)**

**Definition:** The number of students per month who complete a course module *and* achieve a verified outcome aligned with their personal intent.

**Formula:**
$$VLO = (\text{Career Track Completers} \times \text{Placement Rate}) + (\text{Enrichment Track Completers} \times \text{Skill Application Rate})$$

This metric replaces "Total Sign-ups" to align your board's need for scale with your mission of actual impact. It treats a sign-up as potential, not success, directly addressing the 12% completion leakage.

**Strategic Alignment for Donors**
Presenting VLO to donors shifts the narrative from "cost per sign-up" to "cost per life changed." While total sign-ups (15,000) demonstrate reach, VLO demonstrates efficacy. If you maintain 15,000 sign-ups but improve completion and outcome verification from 12% to 25%, your VLO doubles without increasing marketing spend. This proves to donors that additional funding will be leveraged for efficiency and depth, not just vanity metrics. It satisfies the board's scale requirement by keeping the top-of-funnel visible while forcing operational focus on the bottom-of-funnel where the mission is actually delivered.

**Measurement Framework**
To measure this accurately across Latin America with limited resources, implement a lightweight, intent-based tracking system:

1.  **Intent Segmentation (Onboarding):** At sign-up, require a single selection: "I want a new job" or "I want to upskill for my current role/personal growth." This splits the population into two measurable cohorts without complex profiling.
2.  **Career Track Verification:** For job seekers, the "outcome" is a verified job placement or freelance contract. Measure this via a simple automated email survey at 3 and 6 months post-completion, incentivized with access to advanced alumni networks.
3.  **Enrichment Track Verification:** For personal learners, "success" is subjective. Define it as "Skill Application." Measure this by asking one question post-course: "Did you apply this skill to a project, work task, or community problem?" A "Yes" counts as a verified outcome. This quantifies personal enrichment without needing employment data.
4.  **Regional Normalization:** Report VLO as both a raw number (for total impact) and a rate (VLO / Total Completers) per region. This highlights whether low numbers in specific areas are due to low enrollment or systemic barriers in course delivery, allowing targeted resource allocation.

**Addressing the "Personal Enrichment" Dilemma**
You noted uncertainty on quantifying non-career success. By explicitly counting "Skill Application" as a verified outcome, you validate the 60-70% of users who may not seek jobs but still derive mission-critical value. Excluding them would artificially shrink your impact; including them without verification would return you to vanity metrics. The "Application Rate" provides the necessary rigor to claim these users truly benefited.

**Implementation for January Launch**
Do not attempt to track historical data immediately. Start fresh in January. Update the sign-up flow to capture intent. Delay the outcome survey until the first cohort finishes (approx. 3 months in). For the gala presentation, project the VLO based on current completion rates to show the "hidden" impact you are currently ignoring, then set a target to increase the verification rate by 10% year-over-year.

This approach unifies stakeholders: donors see a sophisticated, outcome-based metric; curriculum designers get clear feedback on which courses actually lead to jobs or application; and you secure the mission integrity of LearnBridge.
