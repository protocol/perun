**Pilot Launch Success Criteria: Digital Mortgage Approval Feature**

**1. Customer Experience Threshold (Growth Goal)**
Achieve a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of +45 or higher among the 200 pilot participants, with at least 85% of users rating "Speed of Decision" as 4 or 5 out of 5.
*Rationale:* Validates the hypothesis that reducing time-to-decision from 48 hours to under 4 hours significantly improves satisfaction without compromising the user's perceived control over the process.

**2. Risk Stability Guardrail (Risk Management Goal)**
Maintain a 30-day early-delinquency rate (payments missed within the first 30 days of funding) for the pilot group that does not exceed the historical baseline of the standard 48-hour approval cohort by more than 0.5 percentage points.
*Rationale:* Directly addresses the concern that faster decisions may lead to hasty commitments and higher defaults. This strict cap ensures that speed does not come at the cost of asset quality.

**3. Process Integrity Metric (Compliance & Operational Goal)**
Ensure 100% of pilot approvals include a verified "Cooling-Off Confirmation" event, where the user explicitly acknowledges the loan terms via a secondary digital signature after the initial fast decision but before funds are disbursed.
*Rationale:* Mitigates regulatory risk by inserting a mandatory reconsideration step. This satisfies compliance requirements for informed consent, ensuring the "fast" decision is not the final binding moment without user re-verification.

***

*Note: These criteria must be measured daily during the pilot; if Criterion 2 is breached, the feature must pause immediately for review.*
*Data sources: Core banking ledger for delinquency, in-app survey for NPS, and audit logs for confirmation events.*
