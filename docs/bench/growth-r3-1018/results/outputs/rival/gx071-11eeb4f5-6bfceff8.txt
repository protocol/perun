# RoboCare Riyadh Pilot: Measurement & Decision Plan

## Executive Summary
This plan validates two critical hypotheses for the March 15 rollout: (1) The unit reduces nurse travel time by ≥20 minutes/shift, and (2) Hospital Wi-Fi supports HD video with <200ms latency to ensure clinical usability.

## Phase 1: Infrastructure Validation (Days 1–3)
*Before measuring efficiency, confirm technical viability.*

| Metric | Target Threshold | Data Source | Action if Failed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Video Latency** | < 200ms (avg), < 500ms (p95) | Robot telemetry logs | **Pause Deployment.** Escalate to hospital IT for bandwidth allocation or QoS prioritization. |
| **Packet Loss** | < 1% during rounds | Network monitoring | **Pause Deployment.** Requires network infrastructure audit before proceeding. |
| **Connection Drops** | 0 per 4-hour shift | System uptime logs | **Pause Deployment.** Investigate Wi-Fi dead zones in specific wards. |

**Interpretation:** If any metric fails the threshold, do not proceed to efficiency tracking. The rollout cannot succeed without stable connectivity.

## Phase 2: Operational Efficiency & Adoption (Days 4–14)
*Measure the correlation between time savings and staff usage.*

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Travel Time Saved** | (Baseline manual rounds time) – (Telepresence rounds time) per nurse/shift. | ≥ 20 mins | Time-motion study: Sample 5 nurses/day, tracking start/stop times for rounds. |
| **Active Session Duration** | Total minutes of active video connection per robot/day. | ≥ 180 mins | Automated system logs. |
| **Adoption Rate** | % of scheduled rounds completed via robot vs. in-person. | ≥ 60% by Week 2 | Schedule logs vs. actual usage logs. |
| **Interruption Frequency** | Number of times a session is aborted due to technical/user frustration. | < 2 per day | User feedback button + support tickets. |

### Data Interpretation Matrix
Use this matrix to decide on the March 15 rollout based on Week 2 data:

| Scenario | Travel Time Saved | Latency/Stability | Adoption Rate | **Decision** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Green Light** | ≥ 20 mins | Stable (<200ms) | ≥ 60% | **Proceed.** Efficiency gain is proven; staff are adopting. |
| **Yellow Light A** | ≥ 20 mins | Stable | < 40% | **Pause & Investigate.** Tech works and time is saved, but staff refuse to use it. Conduct qualitative interviews to identify workflow friction before rollout. |
| **Yellow Light B** | < 15 mins | Stable | Any | **Pause & Reconfigure.** The robot is not saving enough time to justify the cost. Re-evaluate routing logic or use cases. |
| **Red Light** | Any | Unstable (>500ms drops) | Any | **Stop Rollout.** Infrastructure cannot support the unit. Require IT remediation contract before resuming pilot. |

## Reporting Cadence for Stakeholders
*   **Daily (08:00):** Automated alert to COO if Latency > 500ms or Connection Drops > 0.
*   **Weekly (Friday):** Summary report to Procurement Directors showing average Time Saved vs. Adoption Rate trend.
*   **Final (Day 14):** Go/No-Go recommendation based on the Decision Matrix above.

**Note:** Ensure baseline manual round times are recorded *before* robot introduction to ensure accurate delta calculation.
**Note:** All latency data must be timestamped to correlate with specific hospital peak usage hours.
