# Hometown Herald: Community Informedness Metrics Framework

To demonstrate value to advertisers and align with your mission of informing Oak Creek, shift your primary success definition from **Time Spent** (addiction) to **Information Reach & Action** (utility). For a hyperlocal audience of 12,000, a resident reading one critical article and leaving is a successful interaction, not a failure.

### 1. Primary Metric: Daily Informed Reach (DIR)
Replace "Daily Active Users" with **Daily Informed Reach**. This counts unique residents who consumed at least one piece of content deemed "essential" (e.g., school board outcomes, safety alerts, game scores) across all channels.
*   **Why:** It validates your core promise: "Did Oak Creek know what happened today?"
*   **Advertiser Value:** Represents the true size of your daily audience, regardless of how long they stayed.

### 2. Secondary Metric: The "Essential Return" Rate
Instead of generic return visits, track **Breaking News Return Rate**: The percentage of users who open the app or click an SMS link within 4 hours of a designated "breaking news" push.
*   **Why:** This proves habit formation specifically around *your* utility, not mindless scrolling. High spikes here during school board meetings confirm you are the town square.

### 3. Cross-Channel Attribution (Solving the SMS Gap)
You cannot ignore the SMS audience. To account for them without app installs:
*   **Unique Link Clicks:** Use distinct UTM parameters for every SMS blast. Count a "session" when an SMS link is clicked, even if no app opens.
*   **The "Informed" Flag:** If a user clicks an SMS link, mark them as "Reached" in your daily totals, equal to an app user.
*   **Metric:** **Total Cross-Channel Reach** = (App Unique Users) + (SMS Unique Clickers) - (Estimated Overlap).

### 4. Community Amplification Score
Track **Local Share Velocity**: The number of times an article is shared to local social groups (Nextdoor, Facebook Community Groups) within the first 6 hours of publication.
*   **Implementation:** Use "Click-to-Tweet" style buttons or specific "Share to Nextdoor" deep links to track this explicitly.
*   **Why:** In a town of 12,000, word-of-mouth is your distribution engine. High share velocity proves you are setting the local agenda.

### Implementation Plan for the Editorial Board
Create a weekly "Community Pulse" dashboard for advertisers containing only these four numbers:
1.  **% of Oak Creek Reached:** (Total Informed Reach / 12,000 population). Aim to show growth toward 20-30% daily penetration.
2.  **Breaking News Response Time:** Average time from publish to 50% of daily readership.
3.  **Cross-Channel Mix:** % of reach coming from App vs. SMS (shows you meet users where they are).
4.  **Amplification Rate:** Shares per 1,000 readers.

**Note:** Stop reporting "Average Session Duration" to advertisers; it incentivizes clickbait over essential news.
**Note:** Ensure your SMS provider passes click data into your main analytics view to avoid double-counting users who both click SMS and open the app.
