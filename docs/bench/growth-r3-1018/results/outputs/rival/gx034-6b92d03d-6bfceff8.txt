# PawsProtect Beta Measurement Plan (UK)

## Metric Hierarchy

### Tier 1: North Star & Business Value (Monthly/Investor Reporting)
These metrics determine if the business model works.
*   **Qualified Lead Volume:** Total quote requests where the user completes ≥80% of form fields (filters out low-intent bounces).
*   **Partner Conversion Rate (Proxy):** Since offline purchases are invisible initially, track **"Quote-to-Partner-Click" rate**. This measures how many quote requesters actually click through to a partner offer.
    *   *Why:* A user requesting a quote but not clicking a partner link indicates a mismatch in price or coverage presented.
*   **Estimated Revenue Per Visitor (RPV):** (Total Partner Clicks × Average Commission Per Click) ÷ Total Visitors. Use this as a placeholder until offline conversion data is integrated.

### Tier 2: Funnel Health & Intent Validation (Weekly Reviews)
These metrics validate your assumption that "quote request = high intent."
*   **Quote Initiation Rate:** % of visitors who start the quote wizard.
*   **Quote Completion Rate:** % of starters who finish.
    *   *Benchmark:* If completion is <40%, the form is too long or asks for sensitive data too early.
*   **Drop-off by Step:** Identify exactly where users abandon the wizard (e.g., "Pet Age" vs. "Postcode").
*   **Partner Click-Through Rate (CTR):** % of quote completers who click at least one partner offer.
    *   *Critical Insight:* If Quote Completion is high but Partner CTR is low, your leads are "low quality" for partners, risking future partnerships.

### Tier 3: Engagement & Diagnostics (Daily Monitoring)
Operational metrics to ensure the beta is functioning.
*   **Daily Active Users (DAU) vs. Target:** Track against the 5,000 monthly visitor goal (~167/day).
*   **Error Rate:** % of quote submissions failing technically.
*   **Device Split:** Mobile vs. Desktop conversion rates (insurance comparisons often shift heavily by device).

## Leading vs. Lagging Indicators

| Frequency | Leading Indicators (Predictive) | Lagging Indicators (Outcome) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily** | • Traffic volume vs. target<br>• Quote initiation rate<br>• Form error rates | • None (too noisy daily) |
| **Weekly** | • Quote completion rate trend<br>• Partner CTR trend | • Total qualified leads generated |
| **Monthly** | • Cohort retention (do users return?) | • Total estimated revenue<br>• Overall conversion funnel % |

## Accounting for Seasonality (April Pet Adoption Spike)

Do not compare April data directly to March. The influx of new pet owners changes user behavior (new owners research differently than existing owners shopping for better rates).

1.  **Segment by User Type:** Add a form field: "Is this a new pet?" (Yes/No). Analyze conversion rates for "New Pet Owners" separately from "Existing Owners."
2.  **Establish a Seasonal Baseline:** In April, expect higher traffic but potentially lower immediate conversion (new owners are often browsing, not buying instantly). Adjust success targets:
    *   *March Goal:* High conversion rate, lower volume.
    *   *April Goal:* High volume, acceptable dip in conversion rate, higher total lead volume.
3.  **Cohort Analysis:** Tag all April users. When reporting in May/June, track if April's "browsers" returned to purchase later. This validates the "longer consideration window" hypothesis for new pet owners.

## Immediate Next Steps for Data Collection
*   **Tagging:** Ensure every "Quote Request" and "Partner Click" event pushes `user_type` (new/existing) and `pet_type` (dog/cat) to your analytics.
*   **Offline Reconciliation:** Immediately negotiate a weekly data share with at least one pilot partner to match your quote IDs against their actual policies sold. This is the only way to validate the "quote-to-purchase" assumption.

*Note: With 5,000 visitors, avoid splitting traffic for A/B tests unless the expected lift is massive (>20%), as statistical significance will be hard to reach.*
*Focus first on fixing funnel leaks before optimizing copy or design.*
