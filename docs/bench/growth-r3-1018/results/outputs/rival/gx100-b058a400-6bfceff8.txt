# MetroStyle Rebrand Measurement Plan

## Executive Summary
This plan isolates the rebrand's impact (launching Jan 15) from seasonal holiday noise by establishing a clean baseline in early January and focusing on leading indicators of brand perception before sales data matures.

## 1. Baseline & Observation Windows
To separate rebrand effects from holiday seasonality, we will **exclude December data** from the primary baseline comparison.
*   **Baseline Period:** January 2 – January 14 (Post-holiday normalization, pre-launch).
*   **Launch Window:** January 15 – February 15.
*   **Comparison Logic:** Week-over-week (WoW) growth from the Jan 2–14 baseline, rather than Year-over-Year (YoY), to avoid comparing against last year's holiday spike.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Primary Success Metrics (Lagging)
*These confirm your stated goals but require time to mature.*
1.  **Direct Traffic Volume:** Target +20% vs. Baseline.
    *   *Definition:* Sessions with `source/medium` = `(direct)/(none)` or branded organic search.
2.  **Returning Visitor Conversion Rate (CVR):** Target +5% vs. Baseline.
    *   *Definition:* Purchases by users with `user_type` = `returning` / Total returning sessions.

### Leading Indicators (Early Signals)
*Monitor these weekly starting Jan 15 to predict success before sales data stabilizes.*
1.  **Brand Search Lift:**
    *   *Metric:* Volume of organic searches for "MetroStyle" + new brand terms.
    *   *Signal:* Immediate increase indicates successful awareness transfer.
2.  **Direct Navigation Rate:**
    *   *Metric:* % of total traffic that is Direct.
    *   *Signal:* If this % rises before total volume rises, brand recall is improving even if total reach is flat.
3.  **Engagement Quality (Returning Users):**
    *   *Metric:* Engagement Rate and Pages Per Session for `user_type` = `returning`.
    *   *Signal:* Higher engagement precedes higher conversion. If returning users linger longer immediately post-launch, the new positioning is resonating.
4.  **New Email Signup Velocity:**
    *   *Metric:* Signups per 1,000 sessions.
    *   *Signal:* Indicates renewed interest/value proposition clarity.

## 3. Reporting Structure & Isolation Strategy

### Weekly Dashboard Format
Report data in a strict table format to prevent mental math errors. Compute all rates via script/spreadsheet.

| Week | Period | Direct Sessions | Returning CVR | Brand Search Vol. | Returning Engagement Rate | Notes/External Factors |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Baseline** | Jan 2-14 | [Value] | [Value] | [Value] | [Value] | Post-holiday normalization |
| **Week 1** | Jan 15-21 | [Value] | [Value] | [Value] | [Value] | Launch week |
| **Week 2** | Jan 22-28 | [Value] | [Value] | [Value] | [Value] | |
| **Delta %** | vs Base | `((W1-Base)/Base)` | `((W1-Base)/Base)` | ... | ... | |

### Isolation Tactics
1.  **Segmentation is Mandatory:** Never report aggregate totals. Always split reports by `user_type` (New vs. Returning) and `session_source` (Direct vs. Paid vs. Organic). The rebrand hypothesis specifically targets *Returning* users and *Direct* traffic; aggregating them with cold paid traffic will dilute the signal.
2.  **Cohort Observation:** For conversion rates, only analyze cohorts where the observation window has closed. Do not divide January spend by partial January revenue.
3.  **Control Group (If feasible):** If running any paid social during launch, keep one audience segment on the old creative/message briefly to compare lift against the new brand assets.

## 4. Implementation Requirements
*   **Tagging:** Ensure `user_type` (new/returning) is passed as a user property to GA4/Mixpanel.
*   **Filters:** Create a permanent view/filter excluding internal IP and bot traffic to protect data quality.
*   **Alerts:** Set automated alerts for drops in Returning CVR > 2% (guardrail metric) to catch potential UX breaks in the new site design immediately.

**Note:** This plan assumes GA4 or equivalent is already implemented with enhanced measurement enabled.
**Note:** Re-evaluate the baseline if a major external event (e.g., weather, news) disrupts the Jan 2–14 window.
