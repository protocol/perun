**Weighing Engagement vs. Revenue**
In travel commerce, immediate engagement metrics (time on site, search clicks) are leading indicators, but booking completion is the lagging indicator of actual value. Optimizing solely for the initial click risks creating a "leaky funnel" where users feel productive but fail to convert. The data suggests Variant A may be encouraging "window shopping" behavior—users clicking the search bar out of curiosity rather than intent—whereas Variant B filters for higher-intent users who move faster toward purchase, evidenced by the reduced engagement time (-30s) but higher conversion (+8%).

Prioritizing Variant B protects Customer Lifetime Value (CLV). Users who complete bookings are the only cohort that generates revenue to fund future retention efforts. A friction-heavy experience that boosts clicks but kills conversions erodes trust and wastes marketing spend acquiring users who never transact.
