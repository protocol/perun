# FundLatam Launch Measurement Plan (Brazil & Mexico)

## Core Success Metrics (Weekly Dashboard)

**Primary North Star:** Gross Funding Volume (GFV) committed per week.
*Target Trajectory:* ~$167k/week average to hit $2M in Q1.

**Conversion Funnel Rates:**
1.  **Landing Page → Project View:** Benchmark >25%. Low rates indicate messaging/channel mismatch.
2.  **Project View → Payment Initiation:** Benchmark >15%. Low rates suggest trust issues or unclear value prop.
3.  **Payment Initiation → Completed Pledge:** Benchmark >60% (US norm). *Critical Diagnostic:* If <40%, suspect payment friction (Boleto/Pix complexity) rather than market fit.

**Unit Economics:**
*   **Average Pledge Value (APV):** Track separately by country. Do not assume US parity.
*   **Cost Per Completed Pledge (CPCP):** Ad spend / Completed Pledges.
*   **Completion Rate by Payment Method:** Split metrics for Credit Card vs. Boleto (Brazil) vs. OXXO/Transfer (Mexico).

## Diagnostic Signals: Friction vs. Market Fit

To distinguish between platform friction and lack of market fit when conversion is low, monitor these specific signals:

**Signal A: The "Drop-off Point" Test**
*   **Friction Indicator:** High traffic to project pages and high "Payment Initiation" clicks, but low completion. Specifically, if users select "Boleto" but do not return to confirm payment within 24 hours, this is a payment workflow friction issue, not a lack of desire to fund.
*   **Market Fit Indicator:** Low click-through from ads to landing page, or high bounce rates on project pages before any payment action is attempted. This signals the offer or campaign stories do not resonate locally.

**Signal B: The "Return-to-Pay" Lag**
*   In markets using Boleto, users often generate the ticket and pay later at an ATM or via banking app.
*   **Metric:** Time-to-Completion median.
*   **Diagnosis:** If US completion is immediate (minutes) and LatAm is 24–48 hours, do not flag this as failure. Adjust your attribution window to 72 hours for "Completed Pledge" events in these regions. If completion remains low after 72 hours, investigate friction.

**Signal C: Support Ticket Ratio**
*   **Metric:** Support tickets per 100 pledges.
*   **Diagnosis:** A spike in "how to pay" or "payment failed" tickets confirms friction. A spike in "is this real?" or "who are you?" confirms trust/market fit issues.

## Weekly Reporting Cadence for the Board

**Week 1-4 Focus:** Validate the funnel, not just the revenue.
1.  **Volume:** Total GFV vs. Target ($167k/week run rate).
2.  **Efficiency:** CPCP trend line.
3.  **Friction Audit:** Completion rate by payment method (Credit Card vs. Local Cash methods).
4.  **Fit Signal:** Project view-to-initiation rate.

**Decision Rule for Day 30:**
*   **Continue Investment If:** Payment initiation rates match US benchmarks (>15%) even if final conversion lags (fixable via payment UX/reminders), OR if GFV is >50% of target with a clear path to optimize CPCP.
*   **Pause/Pivot If:** Project view-to-initiation rates are <5% despite qualified traffic, indicating the core value proposition does not translate culturally.

*Note: Ensure your analytics tool captures "Payment Initiated" as a distinct event from "Payment Completed" to isolate the Boleto latency factor.*
*Do not compare Day 1-7 conversion rates directly to US historicals; allow a 72-hour attribution window for local payment methods before assessing performance.*
