# North Star Metric Evaluation for CookConnect

**Recommendation:** Adopt **Weekly Active Engagers (WAE)** defined as users who perform at least two distinct social actions (comments, recipe uploads, or direct shares) within a 7-day window.

While "Recipes Saved" correlates with utility, your beta data proves that *interaction* drives retention. Users commenting twice weekly stay 45 days longer. WAE captures this behavioral loop without the vanity of simple logins. It shifts the focus from passive consumption (hoarding recipes) to active community participation (cooking together), directly aligning with your mission to be a global kitchen companion.

### Candidate Comparison

| Metric Candidate | Definition | Strategic Fit | Primary Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **1. Weekly Active Engagers (WAE)** | Users with ≥2 social actions/week (comment, upload, share). | **High.** Directly measures the "community" aspect of your mission. Correlates with the 45-day retention lift observed in beta. Signals to investors that you are building a network, not just a database. | **Scaling Friction.** As you grow from 5k to 50k users, maintaining high conversation density is difficult. If new users don't find immediate replies, engagement may drop, artificially depressing the metric despite growth. |
| **2. Recipes Saved per Active User** | Total saves divided by DAU/MAU. | **Medium.** Validates utility and content value. Easy to scale mechanically; users can save content even if no one else is active. Safe for short-term growth charts. | **Vanity & Passivity.** Encourages "digital hoarding" without actual cooking or connection. Your data shows this does *not* predict long-term retention as well as commenting. Investors may view this as a bookmarking tool, not a community platform. |
| **3. Weekly Returning Cooks** | Users who log in and view a recipe on ≥3 distinct days/week. | **Low-Medium.** Captures habit formation and session frequency (leveraging your 8-min avg duration). Shows stickiness better than simple DAU. | **Consumption Bias.** Measures presence, not contribution. A user can view recipes daily without ever cooking or connecting. It fails to distinguish between a loyal community member and a passive browser, weakening the "community-driven" pitch. |

### Implementation Note for Series A Pitch
To address the scaling risk of WAE, present a "Community Density" leading indicator alongside it: the ratio of comments received per new recipe posted within the first 24 hours. This demonstrates to investors that you have a plan to manage the quality of interactions as the user base expands tenfold.

**Decision Deadline:** Finalize definition by October 25th to allow 10 days of data collection before the November 5th pitch.
