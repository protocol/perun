**Recommended North Star Metric: Compliant Rides per Available Bike per Day**

For your October 15th presentation to city mayors, do not report raw ride counts or simple utilization rates. Instead, adopt **Compliant Rides per Available Bike per Day** as your primary North Star Metric.

This metric directly balances your operational efficiency goals with the city officials' regulatory concerns by mathematically forcing a trade-off between volume and compliance. It is calculated as:

*(Total Rides Ending in Designated Zones) ÷ (Total Fleet Size × Days in Period)*

**Why This Metric Works for Both Stakeholders:**

1.  **For VeloChina (Business Goals):** It retains the efficiency focus of "rides per bike," ensuring you are maximizing asset utilization and revenue potential. If bikes sit idle, the metric drops, signaling a need for better redistribution or demand generation.
2.  **For City Officials (Regulatory Satisfaction):** It explicitly penalizes non-compliant behavior. With 20% of your current rides ending in non-designated zones, those rides contribute *zero* value to this metric. This aligns your team's incentives directly with the city's goal of reducing sidewalk clutter. To improve the score, your team must either increase total compliant rides or reduce the percentage of bad parking endings (or both).

**Current Situation Analysis:**
You deployed 5,000 bikes on September 1st. If your current data shows high ride counts but a 20% non-compliance rate, your "Compliant Rides" metric is artificially suppressed by one-fifth. By shifting focus to this composite metric, you demonstrate to the mayors that VeloChina values *orderly* mobility over chaotic volume. It proves you are not ignoring the 20% risk factor but are making it central to your performance definition.

**Implementation for the October 15th Review:**
Present the trend line of this metric from September 1st to October 15th. Break it down into two supporting components in your appendix:
*   **Utilization Rate:** (Total Rides / Fleet Size) – Shows demand.
*   **Compliance Rate:** (Compliant Rides / Total Rides) – Shows operational discipline.

If the North Star metric is rising, it proves you are growing sustainably. If it is flat or falling despite high ride counts, it honestly signals that your growth is currently threatened by compliance issues, allowing you to proactively propose redistribution solutions before the city imposes penalties.

This approach transforms the conversation from "you have too many messy bikes" to "we are jointly optimizing for efficient, compliant transport."

*Note: Ensure your tracking system distinguishes between ride start and end locations to accurately calculate the "compliant" numerator.*
*Do not use this metric alone for driver incentives without a secondary guardrail for bike availability to prevent over-restriction of supply.*
