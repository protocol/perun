# Evaluation Framework: Search Ranking Freshness Test

**Recommendation:** Do not declare this a win yet. While the 9% CTR lift is statistically notable, the shift toward low-quality, unverified content suggests a "hollow victory" that risks long-term retention. **Pause the rollout** and execute the following quality audit before making a final revert/tune decision.

### 1. Define Quality Metrics (Beyond CTR)
Replace "clicks" as the primary success metric with a **Quality-Adjusted Engagement Score**. Track these specific signals for the treatment vs. control groups:

*   **Dwell Time Threshold:** Percentage of clicks resulting in >45 seconds on page (filters out "bounce" clicks on sensational headlines).
*   **Scroll Depth:** Average percentage of article read (target: >60% for news).
*   **Verification Signal:** Ratio of clicks going to "Verified/Premium" sources vs. "Unverified/User-Generated" sources.
*   **Return Visit Rate (7-Day):** Percentage of users who return to the platform within 7 days of the initial search session. This is your leading indicator for the 5% monthly churn risk.

### 2. Cohort Analysis Plan
Segment the 2 million users into three cohorts to isolate the impact:
*   **Heavy Users:** >5 sessions/week. (Most sensitive to quality degradation).
*   **Casual Users:** 1-4 sessions/week.
*   **New Users:** <1 month tenure. (May tolerate lower quality for novelty).

*Hypothesis:* Heavy users in the treatment group will show a statistically significant drop in 7-day return rates compared to control, even if their immediate CTR is higher.

### 3. Decision Matrix
Use this table to guide the Editorial/Engineering decision:

| Scenario | CTR | Dwell Time | 7-Day Return | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **True Win** | ↑ | ↔ or ↑ | ↔ or ↑ | **Roll Out** (Tune slightly if verification ratio dropped) |
| **Hollow Victory** | ↑ | ↓ | ↓ | **Revert Immediately** (Prioritizing recency is destroying trust) |
| **Mixed Signal** | ↑ | ↔ | ↓ | **Tune Parameter** (Reduce freshness weight; add authority boost) |
| **False Positive** | ↑ | ↓ | ↔ | **Monitor Closely** (Run for 7 more days to confirm churn lag) |

### 4. Immediate Next Steps
1.  **Engineering:** Query logs for the test period to calculate *Dwell Time* and *Source Verification Ratio* for both variants.
2.  **Editorial:** Manually review the top 50 most-clicked articles in the treatment group. Tag them as "High Quality," "Sensational," or "Unverified."
3.  **Analytics:** Calculate the 7-day return rate for users exposed to the treatment vs. control. If the treatment group's return rate is >2% lower than control, the long-term LTV loss outweighs the short-term CTR gain.

**Note:** A 9% CTR increase driven by unverified content often correlates with a 10-15% increase in churn within 30 days for news platforms. Prioritize the 7-day return metric over immediate engagement.
