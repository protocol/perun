# Healthcare Graduate Tier: Early Performance Metric Framework

## Executive Summary
To achieve the Q1 target of 200 new customers while ensuring long-term loan health, RefiLondon must shift from vanity metrics (raw sign-ups) to **quality-adjusted acquisition metrics**. Given the hypothesis that healthcare graduates prioritize repayment flexibility over pure rate sensitivity, the following framework balances acquisition speed with risk management under UK regulatory constraints.

## 1. Leading Indicators (Predictive of Loan Health)
These metrics serve as early warnings for portfolio performance before the first repayment is made.

*   **Flexibility Utilization Rate:** Percentage of applicants who actively engage with or select flexible repayment options (e.g., payment holidays, income-contingent plans) during the application flow.
    *   *Why:* Validates the hypothesis that flexibility drives conversion. High utilization without default suggests product-market fit; high utilization coupled with early delinquency signals adverse selection.
*   **Application Completion Delta:** The drop-off rate between starting an application and final submission, segmented by whether "flexibility features" were highlighted vs. "lowest rate."
    *   *Why:* Identifies if the value proposition resonates. A higher completion rate when flexibility is foregrounded confirms the demographic's priority.
*   **Debt-to-Income (DTI) Verification Speed:** Time taken for applicants to provide verified income documentation (crucial for NHS/healthcare staff with complex pay structures).
    *   *Why:* Friction here often predicts future payment administrative failures. Faster verification correlates with higher financial organization and lower early-stage default risk.
*   **Offer Acceptance by Rate Tier:** Distribution of accepted offers across different rate/flexibility combinations.
    *   *Why:* Determines true price elasticity. If customers accept slightly higher rates for better flexibility terms, rate sensitivity is secondary.

## 2. Balanced Acquisition Metrics (Speed vs. Risk)
Replace "Total Sign-ups" with these composite metrics to satisfy both growth and risk objectives.

*   **Quality-Adjusted Acquisition Cost (QAAC):**
    *   *Formula:* Total Marketing Spend / (New Customers × Risk Quality Score)
    *   *Risk Quality Score:* A weighted index (0–1) based on initial DTI, employment verification status, and selection of auto-pay.
    *   *Goal:* Prevents buying low-quality volume just to hit the 200-customer target.
*   **Commitment-to-Funding Ratio:** Percentage of approved offers that result in funded loans within 14 days.
    *   *Why:* High sign-ups with low funding indicate friction in the final stage or buyer's remorse, predicting early churn or cancellation.
*   **Segmented Conversion Velocity:** Time from landing page to funded loan, specifically for healthcare job titles vs. general graduates.
    *   *Why:* Ensures the specific targeting isn't creating unnecessary friction that slows down the Q1 goal.

## 3. Risk Guardrails (UK Regulatory Compliance)
Per FCA Consumer Duty principles, these metrics ensure fair outcomes and prevent predatory lending.

*   **Affordability Stress Test Pass Rate:** Percentage of applicants passing internal stress tests at varying interest rate hikes (e.g., +2%, +4%).
    *   *Constraint:* Do not lower thresholds to meet volume targets.
*   **Early Delinquency Signal (30+ Days):** While full default takes time, track any payment arrangement requests or missed payments within the first 60 days of funding.
*   **Complaint & Inquiry Ratio:** Volume of pre-funding inquiries regarding terms clarity divided by total applications.
    *   *Why:* High confusion indicates a breach of Consumer Duty "clear communication" requirements.

## Implementation Plan for Board Review
1.  **Baseline Immediately:** Capture current conversion and DTI data for non-healthcare graduates to establish a control group.
2.  **Tagging Strategy:** Ensure all Q1 traffic for this tier is tagged with `segment=healthcare` and `campaign=flexibility-focus` vs. `campaign=rate-focus` to test the core hypothesis.
3.  **Weekly Review Cadence:** Monitor *Flexibility Utilization* and *Offer Acceptance* weekly. If flexibility selection rises but *QAAC* deteriorates, pivot messaging immediately.

This framework ensures the 200-customer goal is met with borrowers who are not only acquired quickly but are structurally positioned for long-term repayment success.

*Note: All metrics avoid reliance on credit score changes, focusing instead on behavioral and structural data points available at origination.*
*Data collection must commence on Day 1 of the launch to ensure sufficient sample size for Q1 analysis.*
