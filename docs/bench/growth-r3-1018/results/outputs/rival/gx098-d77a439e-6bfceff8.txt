### Framework for Selecting the SafeChain Wallet Activation Metric

To select the activation event that best predicts long-term retention for your December 10th board meeting, execute the following four-step analysis using your existing user data. This approach moves beyond assumptions to identify the specific "Aha!" moment that correlates with six-month retention.

**1. Define the Cohort and Candidate Events**
Isolate a historical cohort of users who signed up at least six months ago (e.g., users from May or earlier) to ensure you have complete retention data. For each user in this cohort, record the timestamp of their signup and whether they performed the following actions within their first 7 days:
*   **First Outgoing Transfer** (Current assumption)
*   **First Token Swap**
*   **First DApp Connection**
*   **First Incoming Transfer** (Receive action)

Create a binary flag for each user indicating whether they performed each action (1 = Yes, 0 = No).

**2. Calculate Six-Month Retention**
Define "retained" as a user who initiated *any* on-chain transaction (send, swap, or DApp interaction) in month 6 post-signup. Calculate the overall six-month retention rate for the entire cohort to establish your baseline.

**3. Compute Correlation and Lift for Each Event**
For each candidate event, calculate two metrics:
*   **Conditional Retention Rate:** The percentage of users who performed the specific action in week 1 *and* were retained at month 6.
    *   *Formula:* (Users who did Action X AND retained) / (Total users who did Action X)
*   **Retention Lift:** How much higher the retention rate is for users who did the action compared to those who did not.
    *   *Formula:* (Conditional Retention Rate) – (Retention Rate of users who did NOT do Action X)

*Example Output Table:*
| Event | % of New Users Doing Action | 6-Mo Retention (Did Action) | 6-Mo Retention (Did Not) | Retention Lift |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Outgoing Transfer | 40% | 15% | 8% | +7% |
| Token Swap | 12% | 45% | 9% | +36% |
| DApp Connection | 8% | 52% | 9% | +43% |

**4. Select the North Star Based on Predictive Power and Influence**
Choose the event with the highest **Retention Lift**, provided it meets two criteria:
*   **Predictive Strength:** The lift must be statistically significant (not driven by a tiny sample size of power users).
*   **Product Influence:** The team must be able to drive this behavior through onboarding flows. If "DApp Connection" has the highest lift but is hard to influence, consider "Token Swap" if its lift is comparable and more actionable.

**Recommendation Strategy for the Board:**
Present the selected event as the new "Activation" metric. Explain that while 30% of users doing outgoing transfers churn, users who [Selected Event] are X times more likely to stay. Commit to optimizing the November onboarding flow specifically to drive this action among the 5,000 new users, projecting a Y% increase in six-month retention based on the historical lift.

*Note: Ensure you exclude users who performed multiple actions in the same week from overlapping counts if analyzing mutually exclusive paths, though the lift method above handles overlaps naturally.*
*Do not confuse correlation with causation; validate the winner with an A/B test in November that forces one group toward the new activation event.*
