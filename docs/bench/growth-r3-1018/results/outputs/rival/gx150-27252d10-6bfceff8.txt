# Experiment Plan: Recency vs. Popularity Ranking

**Hypothesis**
Because users currently prioritize depth (18 min/day), we believe shifting the ranking algorithm to prioritize recency over popularity will increase visit frequency but may decrease session duration. We predict a net neutral or slight positive impact on total daily read time, provided content quality guardrails are maintained. We will know this is true if the treatment group shows a statistically significant increase in total daily read time without a >10% drop in average session duration or a spike in negative feedback.

**Test Design: Segmented A/B Test**
To prevent severe fragmentation and address category-specific concerns, we will not run a global 50/50 split. Instead, we will use a **stratified rollout**:
1.  **Audience Split:** 10% of users randomly assigned to the "Recency-First" variant (Treatment); 90% remain on "Popularity-First" (Control). This limits risk while gathering sufficient data.
2.  **Category Stratification:** The algorithm change will be applied differently per content vertical to monitor reaction:
    *   *High-Velocity (Politics, Breaking News):* Full recency weighting.
    *   *Evergreen (Sports Analysis, Features):* Hybrid weighting (70% popularity, 30% recency) to preserve depth.
3.  **Duration:** 21 days to capture full weekly cycles and news events.

**Key Metrics to Monitor**

*Primary Metric (Success Criteria)*
*   **Total Daily Read Time per User:** Target lift from 18m to >18.5m (interim step toward 20m goal).

*Secondary Metrics (Behavioral Context)*
*   **Session Frequency:** Average visits per user/day (Expected to rise).
*   **Average Session Duration:** Minutes per session (Expected to fall; monitor closely).
*   **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Articles clicked per impression.
*   **Return Rate:** Percentage of users returning within 24 hours.

*Guardrail Metrics (Trust & Quality)*
*   **Negative Feedback Rate:** "Hide story" or "Not interested" clicks per 1,000 impressions.
*   **Source Diversity Index:** Ensure the algorithm doesn't collapse to only 5-10 high-frequency publishers.
*   **Category Balance:** Percentage of impressions by category (Politics vs. Sports) to ensure no vertical is starved.
*   **Churn Signal:** Unsubscribe rate or app deletion rate (lagging indicator, monitor weekly).

**Analysis & Decision Framework**
The Editorial Board will review results based on the following scenarios:
1.  **Win:** Total read time increases, and negative feedback remains stable. *Action:* Gradually expand treatment to 50%, then 100%.
2.  **Loss:** Total read time drops, or negative feedback spikes >15%. *Action:* Halt test, revert to control.
3.  **Mixed:** Read time holds, but session duration drops significantly while frequency rises. *Action:* Analyze by category. If "Evergreen" content suffered, adopt a hybrid model permanently where recency applies only to breaking news tags.

**Mitigation for User Experience**
To address the fear of fragmented experience, the UI will remain identical for both groups. The only change is the backend sorting logic. We will explicitly track "Source Diversity" to ensure the recency push does not inadvertently amplify low-quality, high-frequency spam sources, protecting the brand's reputation for comprehensive coverage.

*Note: Ensure tracking events capture `article_publish_time` relative to `user_view_time` to validate the recency signal strength.*
*Recommendation: Run a qualitative survey with 50 users from the treatment group post-test to assess perceived "news freshness" vs. "content depth."*
