**Recommendation: North Star Metric**

**Primary Metric: Weekly Active Plots (WAP)**
*Definition:* The number of unique garden plots with at least one recorded productive activity (plot allocation confirmed, tool exchange completed, or harvest logged) within a 7-day window.

**Rationale for the Board**
While tool exchanges indicate trust, they are a leading indicator of engagement, not the core value outcome. Conversely, harvest weight is a lagging indicator that fluctuates seasonally and ignores the maintenance effort required to grow food. **Weekly Active Plots** bridges this gap by measuring the fundamental unit of value: a plot being actively used.

This metric aligns both teams:
*   **Engineering:** Focuses on reducing friction in the three key flows (allocation, sharing, logging) to increase the count of active plots.
*   **Community:** Focuses on onboarding gardeners who will actually cultivate their space, rather than just registering.

By counting *any* productive action, we acknowledge that tool sharing is a means to the end (gardening), not the end itself. A plot where tools are shared but no gardening occurs is still "active," but if tool sharing becomes the *only* activity without eventual harvests, the secondary metrics will flag the disconnect. This metric scales linearly from our current 450 users to a city-wide launch, providing a consistent measure of platform health regardless of seasonal harvest cycles.

***

**Secondary Guardrail Metrics**

1.  **Harvest Conversion Rate:** The percentage of Weekly Active Plots that log a harvest event at least once per month.
    *   *Purpose:* Ensures that "activity" translates to actual food production. If WAP rises but this rate falls, we are optimizing for busywork rather than growing food.

2.  **Tool Exchange Success Rate:** The percentage of initiated tool requests that are completed within 48 hours.
    *   *Purpose:* Prevents the team from inflating collaboration metrics by making requests easy to start but hard to fulfill. It ensures the "trust" mechanism functions efficiently without becoming a bottleneck to the primary goal of gardening.

***

**Note:** This framework shifts the focus from "features used" to "value created," satisfying the board's requirement for clear value creation prior to capital release. Implementation requires ensuring the three trigger events are tracked with equal fidelity in the analytics pipeline.
