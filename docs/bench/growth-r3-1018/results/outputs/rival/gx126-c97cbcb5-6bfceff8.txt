## 3. Implementation Notes for Finance & Ops

**Data Segmentation Requirement:**
All metrics must be segmented by **Country (DE, FR, etc.)** and **Carrier**. Aggregating EU data will hide specific market failures (e.g., French customs delays vs. German last-mile issues). The dashboard must allow drilling down to the individual shipment level for any order exceeding the cost threshold.

**Handling European Volatility (Holidays & Disruptions):**
European logistics face distinct disruptions (e.g., August holidays in France/Germany, local bank holidays).
*   **Dynamic Baselines:** Do not compare EU performance against static US baselines during known disruption windows. Establish a "Disruption Flag" in the dashboard. When active (e.g., mid-August), adjust the OTD Alert Threshold temporarily by +10% to prevent false alarms.
*   **Customs Buffer:** The $5.85 cost threshold assumes a 30% increase. If the *Fully Landed Cost* exceeds $6.50 (45% increase) for two consecutive weeks, an immediate pricing or carrier review is triggered, as this indicates structural tax misclassification rather than temporary variance.

**Action Protocol:**
*   **Yellow Alert (Threshold Breach):** Automated email to Ops Manager; review within 24 hours.
*   **Red Alert (Threshold + 10%):** Immediate pause on ad spend in affected region; CFO/VP Ops emergency review within 4 hours.
