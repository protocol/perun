**Recommendation: Primary Goal Metric for Q2**

**Selected Metric:** **Certification Achievement Rate**
*Definition: The percentage of enrolled users who complete all required modules AND successfully pass the final certification assessment within 45 days of enrollment.*

**Executive Rationale**
For the April 5th certification track launch, "Certification Achievement Rate" is the superior primary metric over "Course Completion" or "Weekly Active Minutes." This metric directly aligns with the product's core value proposition for mid-career professionals: tangible career advancement via a verified credential, not just content consumption.

**Evaluation of Alternatives**

1.  **Course Completion (Current: 45%)**
    *   *Verdict:* **Reject as Primary.**
    *   *Reasoning:* As noted in your assessment, this metric penalizes "search-and-find" learners who derive value without finishing every module. Conversely, it can be gamed by users clicking through content without mastery. It measures activity, not outcome.

2.  **Weekly Active Minutes (Current: 120/user)**
    *   *Verdict:* **Reject as Primary.**
    *   *Reasoning:* This is a vanity metric in this context. Higher time-on-site could indicate deep learning, but it could equally signal confusion, poor UX, or inefficient content. It does not correlate linearly with career outcomes or willingness to pay.

3.  **Certificate Social Sharing**
    *   *Verdict:* **Reject as Primary (Use as Secondary).**
    *   *Reasoning:* While sharing correlates with satisfaction, it is a lagging indicator of *advocacy*, not *value delivery*. Many users achieve career value (e.g., a promotion) without posting on LinkedIn. Making this the primary goal risks optimizing for "shareability" rather than educational rigor.

**Strategic Implementation for Q2**

*   **Primary North Star:** **Certification Achievement Rate.**
    *   *Target:* Establish a baseline during the first 14 days post-launch; aim for a 20% lift by end of Q2 through iterative improvements in assessment clarity and module pacing.
    *   *Why:* It balances the need for comprehensive learning (modules) with proof of competency (assessment), filtering out both skimmers and dropouts.

*   **Secondary Guardrail Metrics:**
    *   **Time-to-Certification:** Ensure we aren't creating unnecessary friction. If achievement rises but time-to-cert doubles, we may be over-simplifying assessments.
    *   **Social Share Rate:** Track this to measure advocacy and organic reach, but do not let it drive product decisions.
    *   **Module Drop-off Points:** Use this diagnostically to improve content, not as a success criterion.

**Conclusion for Steering Committee**
Adopting **Certification Achievement Rate** shifts our focus from "keeping users busy" to "ensuring users succeed." This metric best captures the intersection of immediate engagement (completing work) and long-term career outcomes (earning a credential that holds market value). It provides a clear, binary signal of value delivery that justifies the platform's premium positioning for mid-career professionals.

*Note: Ensure tracking is implemented to distinguish between "module completion" and "assessment passing" before the April 5th launch.*
*Data review for baseline establishment should occur no earlier than April 20th to allow for initial user flow.*
