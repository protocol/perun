# EventFindr Pilot Success Framework (Lodz, 3-Month Trial)

Given your constraints (500 active users, mixed paid/free events, 20 venues), success must be defined by **engagement density** and **venue perceived value** rather than raw ticket revenue. The following composite framework balances user activity with venue satisfaction to prove marketing ROI.

### 1. User Engagement Metrics (The "Demand" Signal)
*Target Audience: 500 Monthly Active Users (MAU)*

| Metric | Definition | Success Threshold (Monthly) | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Discovery Rate** | % of MAU viewing ≥1 event page | **60%** (300 users) | Proves the app solves the "finding" problem. |
| **Intent Signal** | % of viewers clicking "Get Directions," "Save," or "Share" | **25%** of viewers | Captures value for free/cash events where no ticket is sold. |
| **Retention** | % of Month 1 users returning in Month 2 | **30%** | Indicates habit formation in a mid-sized city. |
| **Social Proof** | Total social shares or referral link clicks | **50/month** | Validates organic growth potential beyond the waitlist. |

### 2. Venue Value Metrics (The "Supply" Signal)
*Target Audience: 20 Pilot Venues*

Since direct sales are incomplete, measure **attribution** and **visibility**.

| Metric | Definition | Success Threshold (Per Venue/Month) | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Listing Visibility** | Unique event page views per venue | **150 views** | Demonstrates reach to ~22% of your user base per venue. |
| **Conversion Proxy** | Total "Intent Signals" (see above) per venue | **40 actions** | Tangible proof of interest driving foot traffic potential. |
| **Content Freshness** | % of venues updating events ≥2x/month | **70%** (14 venues) | High adoption indicates venues see operational value. |
| **Claimed Value** | Venues reporting "high/medium" satisfaction in survey | **80%** (16 venues) | Direct feedback on perceived marketing ROI. |

### 3. The Composite "Health Score"
To simplify reporting for partners, calculate a single **Pilot Health Score (0–100)** weekly:

$$ \text{Health Score} = (0.4 \times \text{Discovery Rate \%}) + (0.3 \times \text{Venue Satisfaction \%}) + (0.3 \times \text{Intent Conversion \%}) $$

*   **Green Light (>75):** Ready for paid conversion discussions.
*   **Yellow Light (50–74):** Extend trial 1 month; optimize onboarding.
*   **Red Light (<50):** Pivot value proposition before launch.

### Implementation Notes for Partners
*   **Tracking Free Events:** Require venues to tag events as "Free/Door Pay" in the CMS. Track the *click-to-directions* ratio for these specifically as your primary "foot traffic" proxy.
*   **Qualitative Validation:** Conduct 15-minute exit interviews with all 20 venue owners at Month 3. Ask: *"Did you see attendees mentioning the app at the door?"* This anecdotal data often converts skeptics faster than dashboards.

*Note: Thresholds assume a concentrated local market; adjust downward by 10% if Month 1 MAU falls below 400.*
*Ensure all "Intent Signals" are logged as distinct analytics events (e.g., `button_clicked_directions`) to validate these numbers.*
