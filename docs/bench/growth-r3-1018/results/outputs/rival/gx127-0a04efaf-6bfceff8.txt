**Recommended North Star Metric: Weekly Active Transacting Users (WATU) by Economic Value**

Replace "Daily Active Users" with **Weekly Active Transacting Users (WATU)**, defined as unique users who successfully complete at least one value-moving transaction (send, receive, swap, or pay) within a 7-day window, regardless of access channel (App, USSD, or Agent).

**Why this metric works for SafiWallet and your investors:**

1.  **Captures True Utility, Not Connectivity:** DAU penalizes you for Nairobi's infrastructure failures. A user trying to access the app during an outage but failing is counted as "inactive," distorting your health signal. WATU smooths over intermittent connectivity by measuring successful outcomes over a week, aligning with the actual rhythm of small business cash flows.
2.  **Channel Agnostic:** Whether a user transacts via the mobile app, USSD code, or through a partner agent, the economic outcome is identical. This metric unifies your fragmented user base into a single growth story, proving that your product solves the problem regardless of the interface.
3.  **Investor Alignment:** Investors care about retention and economic activity, not just logins. WATU directly correlates to revenue potential (transaction fees) and product stickiness. Pair this with a secondary metric of **Weekly Transaction Volume per Active User** to demonstrate that not only are users returning, but their economic activity is deepening (supporting your $2M TVL growth narrative).

**Implementation Strategy for Your Pitch Deck:**

*   **Reframe the Narrative:** Present the 40% DAU drop not as a product failure, but as evidence of market constraint that your new metric overcomes. Show the WATU trend line alongside TVL to demonstrate that while access fluctuates, economic utility is compounding.
