### CodeBridge Community Health Measurement Plan

To justify your moderator budget and shift focus from vanity metrics to value, implement the following four measurement pillars immediately. These metrics prioritize **solution density** and **retention** over raw post volume.

#### 1. Solution Effectiveness (The "Value" Metric)
Replace "Total Posts" with **Resolved Thread Rate**. High post volume often indicates confusion, not health.
*   **Metric:** `Resolved Threads / Total Threads Created` (Target: >40% within 48 hours).
*   **Implementation:** Add a "Mark as Solution" button to threads. If a thread starter marks a reply as the answer, tag the thread `status:resolved`.
*   **Why for the Board:** This proves the forum saves time for Czech developers. A high resolution rate directly correlates to reduced support costs and higher developer productivity.

#### 2. Mentorship & Engagement Quality
Track **Mentorship Interactions** to identify power users who deserve moderator support.
*   **Metric:** `Helpful Replies per Power User`.
*   **Implementation:** Add a "Helpful" (upvote) button specifically for replies. Define a "Power User" as anyone providing 5+ "Helpful" marked replies in a month.
*   **Specific Data Point:** **Mentorship Ratio** = `(Replies by Top 10% Users) / (Total Replies)`.
*   **Why for the Board:** If 20% of users provide 80% of the solutions, your budget request shifts from "general moderation" to "retaining key community leaders."

#### 3. Retention & Stickiness (DAU vs. MAU)
For a niche technical community, **Monthly Return Rate** is superior to Daily Active Users (DAU). Developers solve problems episodically, not daily.
*   **Metric:** **30-Day Return Rate** = `(Users who logged in this month AND last month) / (Users who logged in last month)`.
*   **Metric:** **Time-to-First-Value** = Average days between `signup_completed` and `first_solution_received`.
*   **Why for the Board:** DAU will look artificially low for a niche hub. A high 30-day return rate proves that when developers *need* help, CodeBridge is their primary destination.

#### 4. The "Lurker" Value Index
Acknowledge that 90% of users browse without posting.
*   **Metric:** **Search-to-Post Ratio**.
*   **Implementation:** Track `search_performed` and `thread_viewed` events.
*   **Calculation:** If a user searches, views a thread, and does *not* post, count this as a "Self-Served Solution."
*   **Why for the Board:** This captures the silent majority who find answers without engaging publicly, vastly inflating your reported "impact" numbers beyond just active posters.

### Immediate Implementation Steps (Before Tuesday)
Since you cannot wait for full analytics integration, use this manual proxy for the board meeting:
1.  **Sample Audit:** Manually review the last 50 threads. Tag them as "Resolved," "Unresolved," or "Discussion." Extrapolate this % to the total.
2.  **Leaderboard:** Identify the top 10 users by reply count. Calculate what % of total replies they own.
3.  **Survey Popup:** Add a one-question popup on exit: "Did you find a solution today? (Yes/No)." This gives you an immediate "Solution Success Rate" without complex engineering.

**Recommendation:** Present the "Resolved Thread Rate" and "30-Day Return Rate" as your primary KPIs. Argue that moderators are needed not to delete spam, but to accelerate the "Time-to-First-Value" by guiding new users to existing solutions and recognizing mentors.

*Note: Do not report "Total Registrations" (4,200) as a success metric; frame it as "Potential Reach" and pivot immediately to the activation rates above.*
*Ensure your "Mark as Solution" feature is live before the next board meeting to start gathering real data.*
