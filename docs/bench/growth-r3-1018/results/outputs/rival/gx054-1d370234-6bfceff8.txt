**Recommendation: Primary North Star Metric**

**Metric:** **Interview Request Rate per Profile View** (specifically, the percentage of profile views that result in an interview request within 48 hours).

**Why this metric?**
For HireRemote's goal of reducing time-to-hire without sacrificing quality, **Interview Request Rate** is the only metric that balances efficiency and quality while being statistically measurable in a single test cycle.

**Trade-off Analysis of Your Options:**

1.  **Reduction in Time Spent per Profile (Efficiency Only)**
    *   *Pros:* Directly measures speed; easy to track.
    *   *Cons:* Dangerous as a north star. Recruiters could spend less time because they are confused or because the profile lacks necessary info, leading to *fewer* qualified interviews. Optimizing for speed alone risks increasing false negatives (rejecting good candidates) or false positives (interviewing bad fits), ultimately lengthening the total time-to-hire due to bad matches later in the funnel.

2.  **Final Offer Acceptance Rate (Quality Only)**
    *   *Pros:* Ultimate business outcome; guarantees quality.
    *   *Cons:* Statistically impossible for this test. With only one test cycle and typical conversion rates, the sample size required to detect a significant change in *offer acceptance* (which happens weeks later and depends on salary negotiation, candidate choice, etc.) is far too large. You will finish the quarter with "inconclusive" data. It is also too distal; video intros influence the *screening* decision, not the final contract signing.

3.  **Interview Request Rate (The Balanced Choice)**
    *   *Pros:* This is the critical "handoff" moment. If video intros work, recruiters should feel confident enough to move a candidate to the next stage *faster* and *more often*. It captures efficiency (they decided quickly) and quality (they decided "yes").
    *   *Cons:* It doesn't measure the final hire, but it is the strongest leading indicator available within a single quarter.

**Implementation Strategy for Mateo:**

To address your concern about quality while using this metric, add two **Guardrail Metrics** that you monitor but do not optimize for:
*   **Guardrail 1:** *Interview-to-Offer Ratio.* If this drops significantly, it means you are generating more interviews but they are lower quality (false positives).
*   **Guardrail 2:** *Time from Interview Request to Decision.* This ensures the speed gain isn't lost in subsequent stages.

**Hypothesis for the Experiment:**
"Because video introductions provide richer context on communication skills and personality than text alone, we believe prominently displaying candidate video intros will increase the **Interview Request Rate per Profile View by 15%** within 48 hours, without decreasing the downstream **Interview-to-Offer Ratio**."

This approach allows you to validate the value of video intros quickly (within one cycle) while ensuring you aren't trading match quality for speed.

*Note: Ensure your tracking captures the timestamp of the profile view and the subsequent request to accurately measure the 48-hour window.*
*Do not stop the test early based on initial spikes; wait for the full sample size to account for weekly traffic fluctuations.*
