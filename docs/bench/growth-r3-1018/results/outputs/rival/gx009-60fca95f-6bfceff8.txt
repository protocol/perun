### Analysis Plan: Application Volume vs. Candidate Quality Trade-off

**Objective:** Quantify whether the 3-minute portfolio requirement reduces application volume disproportionately to the gain in candidate quality, providing a data-driven recommendation for the form structure.

#### 1. Define Metrics & Data Requirements

**Volume Metrics (Funnel Efficiency):**
- **Start Rate:** Applications started / Job views (Current: 12%)
- **Completion Rate:** Applications completed / Applications started (Current: 25%)
- **Overall Conversion:** Applications completed / Job views (Current: 3%)
- **Time-to-Complete:** Average duration per step (isolate the portfolio upload step)

**Quality Metrics (Post-Hire Performance):**
- **Screen-to-Interview Rate:** % of applicants reaching phone screen (by cohort: pre-update vs. post-update)
- **Interview-to-Offer Rate:** % of interviewed candidates receiving offers
- **Offer Acceptance Rate:** % of offers accepted
- **90-Day Retention:** % of hires still employed after 90 days
- **Hiring Manager Satisfaction:** Average rating (1-5) on candidate fit (survey data)
- **Time-to-Fill:** Days from job post to offer accepted

**Segmentation Variables:**
- Job category (engineering, design, marketing, etc.)
- Seniority level (junior, mid, senior, executive)
- Traffic source (organic, paid, referral)
- Device type (mobile vs. desktop)
- Enterprise vs. SMB client jobs

#### 2. Analytical Approach

**A. Funnel Drop-off Analysis (Pre vs. Post Update)**
Compare the last 30 days before the portfolio requirement with the last 30 days after. Calculate the absolute and relative change in completion rates. Isolate where users abandon: is it specifically at the portfolio upload step, or earlier?

**B. Cohort Quality Comparison**
Create two cohorts:
- **Cohort A:** Applicants from the 30 days pre-portfolio requirement
- **Cohort B:** Applicants from the 30 days post-portfolio requirement

Track each cohort through the hiring pipeline for 60-90 days. Compare quality metrics side-by-side. If Cohort B has a 40% lower application volume but a 50% higher interview-to-offer rate, the trade-off may be justified.

**C. Cost-Impact Model**
Build a simple model:
- **Cost of Lost Volume:** Estimate revenue loss from unfilled roles due to fewer applicants (especially for hard-to-fill roles)
- **Cost of Lower Quality:** Estimate cost of bad hires (recruiting time, onboarding, potential turnover) based on pre-update quality data
- **Net Impact:** (Quality gains × value per good hire) - (Volume loss × value per filled role)

**D. Segment-Specific Analysis**
Enterprise clients may value portfolio-reviewed candidates more than SMB clients. Analyze whether the quality lift is concentrated in enterprise job postings. If so, consider a segmented form: simple for SMB jobs, detailed for enterprise.

#### 3. Recommended Experiments

If data is inconclusive, run an A/B test:
- **Variant A (Control):** Current form with portfolio upload
- **Variant B (Test):** Simplified form (portfolio optional or removed)
- **Metric:** Track both completion rate AND downstream quality metrics for 4-6 weeks
- **Sample Size:** With 1,200 daily starts, you can detect a 10% difference in completion rates within 2 weeks

#### 4. Presentation Framework for Stakeholders

**For Head of Product (focus on user experience & volume):**
- Show drop-off rates by step
- Highlight mobile vs. desktop completion gaps (portfolio uploads are harder on mobile)
- Present A/B test results on user friction

**For VP of Sales (focus on client satisfaction & quality):**
- Show interview-to-offer and retention rates by cohort
- Present hiring manager satisfaction scores
- Calculate revenue impact of faster time-to-fill vs. better candidate fit

**Recommendation Options:**
1. **Keep current form** if quality lift > volume loss (enterprise-focused strategy)
2. **Simplify form** if volume loss outweighs quality gains (growth-focused strategy)
3. **Hybrid approach:** Make portfolio optional, or trigger it only for senior roles/enterprise clients

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1. Pull 60 days of funnel and hiring pipeline data
2. Segment by job type and client tier
3. Build the cost-impact model with Finance input
4. Present findings with clear recommendation within 10 business days

---
*Note: If historical quality data is limited, prioritize running the A/B test immediately while collecting baseline metrics. Consider adding a post-application survey to capture candidate feedback on friction points.*
