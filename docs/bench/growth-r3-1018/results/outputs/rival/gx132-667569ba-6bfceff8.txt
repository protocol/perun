# PayBridge Southeast Asia Launch: Success Metrics Framework

## Primary Success Indicator (North Star)
**30-Day Processed Volume:** $500,000 USD equivalent across Vietnam and Thailand combined.
*Definition:* Total settled value of transactions using the instant conversion feature within the first 30 days of launch. This is the binary pass/fail metric for the regional directors.

## Adoption Metrics (Immediate Viability)
These metrics determine if the "speed vs. fee" hypothesis holds true in the local context.

1.  **Feature Activation Rate**
    *   *Calculation:* (Merchants executing ≥1 instant conversion) / (Total eligible merchants in VN & TH).
    *   *Purpose:* Measures initial curiosity and onboarding friction.

2.  **Speed-to-First-Transaction**
    *   *Calculation:* Median time from account eligibility to first instant conversion.
    *   *Purpose:* Validates if the "instant" promise drives immediate action compared to the legacy 48-hour flow.

3.  **The "Speed Premium" Acceptance Rate**
    *   *Calculation:* % of transactions where the merchant accepts the instant rate despite a potentially higher spread/fee compared to the standard 48-hour rate.
    *   *Purpose:* Directly answers your core uncertainty. If this is high, speed is the primary driver. If low, price sensitivity dominates, and the feature may need repricing.

## Engagement & Retention Metrics (Long-term Viability)
These metrics indicate if the feature becomes a habitual part of the merchant's cash flow management.

4.  **Repeat Usage Rate (Day 7 & Day 30)**
    *   *Calculation:* % of activators who perform ≥2 additional conversions within 7 and 30 days.
    *   *Purpose:* Distinguishes between one-off testing and operational reliance.

5.  **Share of Wallet (Instant vs. Standard)**
    *   *Calculation:* (Volume via Instant Conversion) / (Total Merchant Volume).
    *   *Purpose:* Determines if this feature captures *new* volume or simply cannibalizes existing slower transactions.

6.  **Average Transaction Size (ATS) Delta**
    *   *Calculation:* Average value of Instant transactions vs. Standard 48-hour transactions.
    *   *Purpose:* In Southeast Asia, smaller businesses often transact frequently in smaller amounts. If ATS is significantly lower on instant, it confirms the feature is being used for urgent, small-scale liquidity rather than large settlements.

## Cultural & Regional Context for Data Interpretation

When presenting to Singapore regional directors, apply these lenses to the data:

*   **Vietnam (Cash-Flow Sensitivity):** Vietnamese SMEs often operate on thin margins with immediate liquidity needs. A high adoption rate with *smaller* average transaction sizes suggests the feature is solving a daily working capital gap. Do not interpret low ATS as lack of trust; interpret it as high utility for micro-liquidity.
*   **Thailand (Relationship & Trust):** Thai business culture places high value on established relationships and stability. A slower initial activation rate followed by a sharp spike in *Repeat Usage* is a stronger positive signal than a viral spike that fades. Look for "steady growth" over "hockey stick" curves in this market.
*   **The "Face" Factor:** In both markets, public failure is avoided. If merchants try the feature once and stop (high churn after first use), it may indicate a hidden friction point they are unwilling to report directly (e.g., confusing UI or perceived risk) rather than a dislike of the speed/fee trade-off. Qualitative follow-up is essential if Day-7 retention drops below 20%.
*   **Mobile-First Behavior:** Ensure data is segmented by device. If adoption is skewed heavily toward mobile, it confirms the feature fits the on-the-go management style prevalent in SEA markets, whereas desktop-heavy usage might indicate it is only being used by accountants for specific end-of-day reconciliations.

**Note:** Present the "Speed Premium Acceptance Rate" as the key learning metric; it validates whether your product strategy should double down on speed marketing or pivot to fee competitiveness.
**Note:** Ensure the $500k target is clearly split by country in the appendix, as aggregating them may hide a failure in one specific market.
