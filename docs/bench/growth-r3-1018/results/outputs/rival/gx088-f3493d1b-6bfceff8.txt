### Recommended Activation Definition for AutoBot SG

**Primary Activation Metric:** "Production Value Realized"
**Definition:** A customer account is considered "activated" when **three distinct bots** have successfully executed **at least 50 automated transactions each** in a live production environment within a 14-day window.

**Why this definition over your alternatives:**
*   **Vs. First Bot Built:** Building is easy; running in production is hard. Your support tickets confirm that "built but not running" is a churn risk, not a success signal.
*   **Vs. First Successful Transaction:** One transaction could be a test or a pilot. It doesn't prove the bot is embedded in a critical workflow.
*   **Vs. Training Completion:** Training is an input, not an outcome. Users can complete training and still fail to derive value.
*   **Vs. Deployment to Live Server:** Deployment is technical; *execution* is business value. A deployed bot that never runs is shelfware.

**Individual User vs. Department-Wide:**
Measure at the **Department/Workflow Level**, not just the individual user level. In enterprise banking, one "champion" user building bots does not guarantee renewal if the wider team isn't relying on the output. However, track individual "Power Users" as leading indicators. If no single user hits the activation threshold, the department likely won't either.

### Implementation Plan for Priya's Team

**1. The "At-Risk" Threshold (Leading Indicator)**
Flag an account as "At-Risk" if, 45 days post-sale:
*   Zero bots have reached the "50 successful transactions" mark.
*   OR: Bots are built but have <5 successful runs in the last 30 days.

**2. The "Renewal Safe" Threshold**
An account is statistically likely to renew if:
*   They have hit the Primary Activation Metric (3 bots x 50 runs).
*   AND: They have at least two distinct users running bots (reducing "bus factor" risk).

**3. Immediate Actions for Q3 Renewals**
*   **Segment Current Clients:** Divide your three banks into "Activated," "Partial," and "Stalled" based on the *Production Value Realized* metric.
*   **Intervention for "Stalled":** For banks where bots are built but not running, shift CS focus from "how to build" to "how to deploy and trust." The barrier is likely IT security approval or internal change management, not product usability.
*   **Expand for "Activated":** For banks hitting the metric, push for department-wide expansion. Ask: "Which other workflows can we automate with this same reliability?"

**Data Requirements:**
You must instrument your backend to track `bot_execution_status` (success/fail) and `environment_type` (test/prod). Do not rely on frontend clicks. If you cannot currently distinguish "test" vs. "production" runs technically, add a mandatory "Deploy to Production" toggle in the UI that logs this specific event.

**Note:** Validate this hypothesis by correlating these specific usage patterns with the renewal decisions of any past churned clients if data exists.
Prioritize identifying the "deployment blocker" (IT vs. User fear) for the "Stalled" accounts immediately.
