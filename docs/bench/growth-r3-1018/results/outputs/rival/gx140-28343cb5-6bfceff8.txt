# FreshBox Pricing Test: Weekly Leading Indicator Report

**Reporting Period:** November 1 – November 7 (Week 1)
**Test Variant:** 4-Box Discount vs. Control (1-Box Discount)

### 1. Conversion Health (Acquisition Quality)
*Tracks if the discount attracts buyers or just browsers.*

| Metric | Control (1-Box) | Test (4-Box) | Variance | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Trial Start Rate** | 25% (Baseline) | 34% | +9% | 🟢 On Target |
| **Payment Method Entry** | 92% | 88% | -4% | 🟡 Monitor |
| **Cost Per Trial Start** | $45.00 | $38.50 | -14% | 🟢 Improved |

*Insight:* The deeper discount is successfully driving volume (+36% lift in starts), exceeding the 35% conversion target early. However, a slight drop in payment method entry suggests some users are hesitant to commit even with the longer discount.

### 2. Early Engagement & Churn Signals (Weeks 1-2)
*Predicts retention before the 4-box period ends. High engagement in Week 1 usually correlates with long-term retention.*

| Metric | Control (1-Box) | Test (4-Box) | Variance | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1 Box Activation** | 96% | 91% | -5% | 🟡 Moderate |
| **Recipe Customization Rate** | 78% | 62% | -16% | 🔴 High Risk |
| **Support Tickets (Setup)** | 2% | 5% | +3% | 🟡 Moderate |
| **Early Cancel Request** | 1.5% | 4.2% | +2.7% | 🔴 High Risk |

*Insight:* **Critical Warning.** While acquisition is up, engagement quality is down. Test users are 16% less likely to customize recipes—a strong predictor of long-term churn. The spike in early cancel requests suggests some users are "gaming" the four-week deal with no intent to stay.

### 3. Unit Economics Preview (Cash Flow Impact)
*Simple view of cash burn per new customer during the discount window.*

| Metric | Control (1-Box) | Test (4-Box) | Variance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Avg. Revenue (First 4 Weeks)** | $110 | $95 | -14% |
| **COGS (First 4 Weeks)** | $60 | $210 | +250% |
| **Contribution Margin (4 Wks)** | +$50 | -$115 | **Negative** |
| **Break-even Point** | Month 2 | Month 5 (Proj.) | +3 Months |

*Insight:* The test variant burns $115 per customer in the first month compared to earning $50 in the control. To justify this, the Test group must stay subscribed **3 months longer** than the Control group on average. Given the low recipe customization rates, this break-even target is currently at risk.

### Recommendation for CFO
**Proceed with caution.** The strategy successfully lowers acquisition costs and boosts sign-ups, but early behavioral data indicates a "promotion-seeking" cohort that is less engaged and more likely to churn immediately after week 4.

**Immediate Actions:**
1.  **Add a "Commitment Gate":** Require credit card authorization at sign-up (already happening) but consider adding a "pause" limitation during the discount period to reduce gaming.
2.  **Engagement Intervention:** Trigger an automated email/SMS sequence for Test users who do *not* customize their first box by Day 3.
3.  **Monitor Week 3 Drop-off:** The true test will be cancellation spikes in Week 5. Prepare a retention offer specifically for this cohort to bridge them to Month 5.
