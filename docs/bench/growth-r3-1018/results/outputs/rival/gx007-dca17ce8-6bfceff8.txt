# HealthConnect SMS Reminder Pilot: KPI Framework

**Objective:** Evaluate the viability of the 24h + 2h SMS reminder frequency by balancing no-show reduction against patient communication health before scaling to 300 clinics.

### 1. Primary Outcome Metric (The "North Star")
*   **Net No-Show Reduction Rate:** The percentage point decrease in no-shows compared to the current 18% baseline, specifically within the 50 pilot clinics.
    *   *Target:* Statistically significant reduction below 18% without triggering negative secondary metrics.
    *   *Calculation:* `(Baseline No-Shows - Pilot No-Shows) / Total Pilot Appointments`

### 2. Communication Health & Risk Metrics (Guardrails)
These metrics determine if the frequency causes "spam" fatigue or financial friction for patients.

*   **Opt-Out Rate (Critical Stopper):** The percentage of patients replying "STOP" or unsubscribing after receiving the pilot messages.
    *   *Threshold:* If >1.5% of recipients opt out, the frequency is too aggressive for the market context.
*   **Delivery Failure Rate:** The percentage of messages failing to deliver due to network issues or invalid numbers.
    *   *Context:* High failure rates in rural areas may indicate connectivity barriers rather than content issues.
*   **Support Ticket Volume:** Number of incoming calls/messages to clinic front desks complaining about "too many texts" or asking how to stop them.
    *   *Why:* Opt-outs are passive; complaints are active friction that burdens clinic staff.

### 3. Engagement & Efficiency Metrics
*   **Confirmation Response Rate:** Percentage of patients who reply to the SMS (e.g., replying "Y" to confirm).
    *   *Insight:* High confirmation rates correlate strongly with attendance. If this is low, the 2h reminder may be redundant or ignored.
*   **Cost Per Saved Appointment:** Total SMS cost divided by the number of no-shows prevented.
    *   *Context:* Ensures the solution is economically viable given varying mobile data costs and clinic margins.
*   **Rural vs. Urban Delta:** Comparison of no-show reduction and opt-out rates between clinics in Metro Manila vs. rural provinces.
    *   *Why:* To detect if the 2h reminder is ineffective or costly for users with intermittent connectivity.

### 4. Operational Impact
*   **Clinic Staff Time Saved:** Estimated hours saved by front desk staff not having to manually call patients for reminders.
*   **Reschedule Rate:** Percentage of patients who reschedule via SMS link/reply rather than no-showing.
    *   *Value:* Converts a potential loss (no-show) into retained revenue.

### Decision Matrix for Steering Committee

| Scenario | No-Show Rate | Opt-Out Rate | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Green Light** | < 14% | < 1.0% | Roll out to all 300 clinics immediately. |
| **Yellow Light** | < 14% | 1.0% - 2.5% | Proceed with caution; test reduced frequency (24h only) for rural segments. |
| **Red Light** | > 16% OR | > 2.5% | Halt rollout. Re-evaluate frequency or channel strategy. |

**Data Collection Note:** Ensure all SMS logs tag the specific pilot group to isolate data from the 250 non-pilot clinics.
**Reporting Cadence:** Weekly dashboards for the first month, then bi-weekly until the pilot concludes.
