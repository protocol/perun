**Recommended North Star Metric: "Actionable Intervention Rate"**

**Definition:** The percentage of enrolled patients per week who receive a timely, clinically validated intervention triggered by the CarePulse system that prevents a potential adverse event.

**Formula:** (Number of patients receiving verified preventive interventions) / (Total active enrolled patients) × 100

### Evaluation of Current Candidates

| Metric | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily Device Usage** | Easy to track; indicates patient engagement. | **Vanity Metric:** High usage does not equal better health. Patients can wear devices without data leading to action. Encourages engineering to optimize connectivity over clinical utility. | **Reject** as North Star. Use as a guardrail metric only. |
| **Hospital Readmission Rates** | Directly measures ultimate health outcome; highly valued by hospitals. | **Too Lagging/Noisy:** Influenced by external factors (patient compliance, unrelated illnesses, hospital discharge policies). Takes months to show statistical significance. Hard for engineers to connect daily work to this number. | **Reject** as primary North Star. Keep as a long-term validation metric. |
| **Alert Response Times** | Measures operational efficiency. | **Gaming Risk:** Teams might close alerts quickly without resolving the underlying issue. Fast response to false alarms adds no value. | **Reject** as North Star. Use as an operational KPI. |

### Why "Actionable Intervention Rate" Works for CarePulse

1.  **Bridges the Gap:** It connects the *leading indicator* (device data) to the *lagging outcome* (readmissions). It proves the system isn't just collecting data, but actively improving care.
2.  **Aligns Teams:**
    *   **Engineering:** Must build accurate algorithms that reduce false positives and highlight true risks, ensuring alerts are worth acting upon.
    *   **Clinical Success:** Must verify alerts quickly and document the intervention, focusing on quality of care rather than just speed.
3.  **Short-Term Visibility:** Unlike readmissions, you can measure this weekly. If the rate drops, you know immediately that either the device isn't detecting issues or the clinical team is overwhelmed.
4.  **Hospital Value:** This metric demonstrates to partners that CarePulse is an active safety net, not just a data dashboard. It quantifies "prevented crises."

### Implementation Framework for Dr. Evans' Roadmap

To finalize the roadmap by October 1st, adopt this three-tier measurement strategy:

*   **North Star (Strategic):** **Actionable Intervention Rate.** Target: Increase from current baseline to X% by Q2. This drives the core product strategy.
*   **Input Metrics (Tactical):**
    *   *Data Quality Score:* % of days with valid readings (ensures device reliability).
    *   *Alert Precision:* % of alerts resulting in an intervention (ensures algorithm accuracy).
*   **Outcome Validation (Long-term):** **Readmission Rates.** Monitor quarterly to confirm that increased interventions correlate with reduced readmissions over time.

**Behavioral Guardrails:**
To prevent gaming, define "Actionable Intervention" strictly: it must be a logged clinical action (call, medication adjustment, visit) triggered by a specific alert, verified by a clinician within 2 hours. Random checks of intervention logs should be conducted monthly.

This approach shifts the focus from "how much data we collect" to "how many patients we actively help," providing a clear, defensible narrative for your hospital partners.

*Note: Ensure your clinical team agrees on the definition of "verified intervention" to maintain data integrity.*
