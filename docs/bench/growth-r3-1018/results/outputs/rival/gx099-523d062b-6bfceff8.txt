# Diagnostic Plan: DesignFlow Trial Conversion Leak

## Executive Summary
Current trial-to-paid conversion stands at 6% (120/2,000), missing the 12% target. This plan isolates whether the leak stems from feature discoverability (advanced exports) or pricing perception by correlating behavioral cohorts with qualitative feedback.

## 1. Quantitative Behavioral Cohorts
Analyze the August trial cohort (n=2,000) segmented by the following behaviors to pinpoint the drop-off location:

*   **The "Export-Active" vs. "Export-Inactive" Cohort:**
    *   *Segment A:* Users who attempted any export (basic or advanced) within Days 1–3.
    *   *Segment B:* Users who created projects but performed zero exports in Days 1–3.
    *   *Hypothesis Test:* If Segment A converts at >10% and Segment B at <2%, the issue is engagement/utility. If both convert equally poorly, the issue is likely pricing or overall value perception.

*   **The "Feature Discovery" Path:**
    *   *Segment C:* Users who clicked the "Advanced Export" menu item but did not complete an export (indicating confusion or paywall friction).
    *   *Segment D:* Users who successfully completed an advanced export.
    *   *Analysis:* Compare conversion rates of C vs. D. A high drop-off at C suggests UI/UX friction; low traffic to C suggests discoverability issues.

*   **Time-to-Value Velocity:**
    *   *Segment E:* Users who reached their first "Aha!" moment (e.g., first successful advanced export) within 24 hours vs. Days 2–3 vs. Days 4–14.
    *   *Goal:* Determine if the 3-day window is the critical threshold. If users converting after Day 4 have similar success rates, the onboarding urgency is the bottleneck.

*   **Plan Selection Abandonment:**
    *   *Segment F:* Users who visited the pricing page during the trial but did not convert.
    *   *Analysis:* High traffic here with low conversion indicates pricing friction rather than product utility.

## 2. Qualitative Validation Methods
Deploy these methods immediately to validate the quantitative findings before the November 1st deadline:

*   **Targeted Exit Surveys (Non-Converters):**
    *   Trigger a one-question survey on Day 13 for users who haven't converted.
    *   *Question:* "What is the primary reason you are not upgrading today?" (Options: Didn't find needed features, Price too high, Didn't have time to explore, Missing specific tool).
    *   *Sample:* All 1,880 non-converters.

*   **"Lost User" Interviews:**
    *   Recruit 10 users from Segment B (no exports) and 10 from Segment C (attempted but failed export).
    *   Conduct 15-minute retrospective calls asking them to walk through their first three days.
    *   *Goal:* Observe if they mention "looking for exports" or express confusion about the interface.

*   **Session Replay Analysis:**
    *   Review 50 random session recordings from non-converters who had high activity (many clicks) but no exports.
    *   *Look for:* "Rage clicks" near export menus, repeated navigation loops, or hesitation at pricing prompts.

## 3. Synthesis & Reporting Framework
For the November 1st presentation, map findings to this decision matrix:

| Finding Scenario | Primary Cause | Recommended Q4 Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High conversion among "Export-Active" users; Low traffic to export features.** | Discoverability | Revise Day 1 onboarding flow; add tooltips guiding users to exports. |
| **Low conversion even among "Export-Active" users.** | Product Utility/Value | Re-evaluate feature set or competitive differentiation; consider extending trial. |
| **High pricing page visits but low conversion; Export usage is high.** | Pricing Perception | A/B test pricing tiers, offer trial extension discounts, or clarify ROI. |
| **High drop-off at "Attempted Export" step.** | UX Friction | Fix the export UI/flow immediately; remove friction points. |

## Immediate Next Steps
1.  **Data Pull:** Extract August event logs for export-related actions and pricing page views by Day 3.
2.  **Survey Deployment:** Launch exit survey to current active trials to gather real-time data.
3.  **Interview Scheduling:** Reach out to 20 August non-converters for interviews.

*Note: Ensure all data analysis matches observation windows (e.g., only count conversions within the 14-day window for each specific user cohort) to avoid skewed rates.*
