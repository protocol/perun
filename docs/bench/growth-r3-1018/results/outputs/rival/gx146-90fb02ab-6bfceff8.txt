# CreatorHub Product Health & Measurement Strategy

## Executive Summary
To sustain hyper-growth from 10k to 1M users while satisfying unit economics scrutiny, CreatorHub must shift from measuring **aggregate volume** (Total DAU) to **cohort-based quality** (Retention by Behavior). The following framework isolates whether growth is driven by high-value community builders or passive consumers, directly addressing investor concerns about long-term viability.

## 1. North Star Metric: Weekly Active Community Contributors (WACC)
**Definition:** The number of unique users who perform at least one *community-generating action* (comment, share, collaboration request, or upload) within a 7-day window.

**Rationale:**
*   **Decouples Quality from Quantity:** Unlike DAU, which inflates with passive traffic, WACC only counts users creating network effects.
*   **Predictive of Retention:** If your hypothesis holds that community drives retention, WACC will correlate more strongly with Day-30 retention than total signups.
*   **Investor Alignment:** Demonstrates focus on the active user base that drives organic growth and lowers CAC, directly improving unit economics.

## 2. Supporting Metric Framework

### A. The Quality vs. Quantity Dashboard
To detect shifts in user quality during scaling, track these metrics side-by-side:

| Metric Category | Metric Name | Definition | Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quality (North Star)** | **WACC Rate** | WACC / Total MAU | Declining % indicates dilution by passive users. |
| **Quantity (Growth)** | **New Signups** | Total new accounts per week | Raw volume growth. |
| **Efficiency** | **Activation Ratio** | % of new users hitting "Aha!" (first community action) within 24h | Measures onboarding efficacy for mass market. |
| **Retention** | **Cohort Retention Curves** | % of users returning in Week 2, 4, 8 by *acquisition source* | Identifies if new channels bring lower-quality users. |

### B. Segmentation Strategy for Scale
Stop analyzing "all users." Implement these three mandatory cuts for every report:
1.  **Behavioral Segment:** *Creators* (upload/collab) vs. *Consumers* (view/like) vs. *Community* (comment/share). Track retention curves for each separately.
2.  **Cohort Segment:** Group users by signup week. Compare Week 1 (Early Adopters) vs. Week 50 (Mass Market) retention. Divergence signals a product-market fit shift.
3.  **Channel Segment:** Organic vs. Paid vs. Viral. If Paid cohorts show 40% lower WACC rates than Organic, your CAC payback period is at risk.

## 3. Roadmap for Hyper-Growth Measurement

### Phase 1: Baseline & Instrumentation (Months 1-2)
*   **Action:** Redefine data pipelines to tag every event with `user_type` and `cohort_week`.
*   **Goal:** Establish the current correlation between "Community Actions" and "Day-30 Retention." If the correlation is weak, pivot the North Star immediately before scaling spend.

### Phase 2: The "Canary" Cohort System (Months 3-6)
*   **Action:** As you launch new acquisition channels, treat the first 1,000 users as a "Canary Cohort."
*   **Rule:** Do not scale ad spend on a channel until the Canary Cohort's WACC Rate matches within 10% of the organic baseline.
*   **Outcome:** Prevents scaling low-quality traffic that ruins unit economics.

### Phase 3: Predictive Modeling (Months 6-18)
*   **Action:** Build a simple regression model: *Input = First Week Actions; Output = Month 3 Retention.*
*   **Goal:** Identify the specific "magic number" of community interactions (e.g., "3 comments in week 1") that predicts long-term survival. Optimize onboarding to hit this threshold.

## 4. Addressing Investor Concerns
*   **Unit Economics:** Report LTV based on *behavioral cohorts*, not averages. Show that "High Community" users have an LTV 3x higher than "Passive" users. This justifies higher CAC for targeted acquisition.
*   **Mass Market Shift:** Explicitly report the "Dilution Factor"—the gap between early adopter retention and mass market retention. A widening gap triggers a product pivot, not a marketing push.

**Note:** This framework requires event-level tracking of specific community actions, not just pageviews.
**Note:** Review cohort curves bi-weekly; aggregate monthly averages will hide early warning signs of degradation.
