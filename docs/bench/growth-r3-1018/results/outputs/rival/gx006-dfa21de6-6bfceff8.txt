# NeoVida Referral Program: Success Definition & Metrics Framework

## Primary Success Metric: Activated Referral Retention Rate (ARRR)
**Definition:** The percentage of referred users who complete three specific actions within 14 days of signup: (1) identity verification (KYC), (2) an initial deposit of ≥€50, and (3) at least one transaction (payment, transfer, or card use).

**Target:** ≥65% of referred users must reach "Activated" status to count toward the 50,000-user goal. Users who signup but fail to activate within 14 days are excluded from acquisition targets, preventing inflation by fraud rings.

**Rationale:** Cash incentives attract both genuine freelancers needing liquidity and fraud operators. The 14-day activation window with a €50 deposit threshold filters out bonus hunters who won't engage with the product. Freelancers and gig workers typically need immediate account utility for income/receipts, making this a strong behavioral signal of intent.

## Secondary Metrics

### Quality Indicators
| Metric | Target | Purpose |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **Day-30 Retention (Activated Cohort)** | ≥45% | Confirms activated users remain engaged beyond the bonus period |
| **Average Revenue Per Activated Referral (ARPAR)** | ≥€3.50/month by Month 3 | Validates economic viability against €20 total bonus cost (€10 + €10) |
| **Time-to-First-Transaction** | Median <48 hours | Faster activation indicates genuine need vs. fabricated accounts |

### Fraud Detection Guards
| Metric | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Referrer Concentration Ratio** | >5 successful referrals from single user in 7 days | Flag for manual review |
| **Device/IP Overlap Rate** | >3 referred accounts sharing device fingerprint or IP | Auto-hold bonuses, trigger investigation |
| **KYC Failure Rate (Referred vs. Organic)** | Referred >2× organic rate | Pause campaign, audit traffic source |
| **Deposit Reversal Rate** | >5% of referred deposits reversed within 48 hours | Suspicious pattern; withhold bonuses pending clearance |

### Program Efficiency
| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Cost Per Activated User (CPAU)** | Total bonuses paid ÷ Activated referrals | ≤€25 (accounts for ~20% fraud/abuse buffer) |
| **Referral Conversion Funnel** | Invites sent → Clicks → Signups → Activated | Identify drop-off points; optimize messaging |
| **Organic Lift Ratio** | (Total new users - Attributed referrals) ÷ Pre-campaign organic baseline | ≥1.0 (ensures referral program doesn't cannibalize organic growth) |

## Approval Criteria for Budget Release

The €20 per-referral budget (€10 referrer + €10 referee) is approved for initial rollout to 5,000 users if:

1. **Pilot Phase (First 2 weeks):** ARRR ≥60% and fraud guard metrics remain below thresholds
2. **Economic Validation:** Projected LTV of activated referrals exceeds €75 (3.75× CPAU target)
3. **Fraud Capacity:** Fraud team can review flagged accounts within 24 hours to prevent bonus payout delays for legitimate users

## Reporting Cadence

- **Daily:** Fraud guard metrics (auto-alerts if thresholds breached)
- **Weekly:** ARRR, activation funnel, CPAU trends
- **Monthly:** Day-30 retention, ARPAR, organic lift analysis

---
*Note: This framework assumes KYC verification is mandatory before bonus payout. If bonuses are paid pre-KYC, fraud thresholds must be tightened by 50%.*
*Recommendation: Run a 500-user pilot in early October to validate the €50 deposit threshold before full December rollout.*
