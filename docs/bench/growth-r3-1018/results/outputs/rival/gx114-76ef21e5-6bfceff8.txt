# Recommendation: Proceed with Desktop-Only Rollout

**Decision:** Authorize the immediate rollout of the new dashboard layout to **desktop users only**, while maintaining the current legacy layout for all mobile traffic.

**Rationale:** The experiment reveals a statistically significant divergence in user behavior that invalidates the assumption of consistent responsive performance.
*   **Desktop Opportunity:** The 18% increase in engagement time and +2.5 pages per session indicates a successful enhancement of the investment research workflow. Delaying this rollout incurs a clear opportunity cost by withholding a proven value driver from our primary power-user segment.
*   **Mobile Risk:** The 10% drop in engagement and significant bounce rate increase signals a critical usability failure on mobile. Rolling this out universally would degrade the experience for a substantial portion of our user base, potentially impacting retention and trust.

**Strategic Narrative for Stakeholders:**
Frame this not as a "partial fix" but as a **device-optimized deployment strategy**. The data confirms that desktop and mobile users interact with complex financial data differently; a "one-size-fits-all" responsive approach has failed in this instance. By decoupling the release, we capture immediate value on desktop while protecting mobile users from a broken experience. This approach allows the product team to treat the mobile failure as a distinct design challenge requiring specific diagnostics (touch targets, load performance, chart complexity) without holding the desktop win hostage.

**Next Steps & Mitigation:**
1.  **Immediate Action:** Configure feature flags to enable the new layout for desktop devices only.
2.  **Mobile Diagnostic Sprint:** Initiate a targeted investigation into mobile-specific friction points (loading speed on cellular, touch interaction heatmaps, chart rendering) with a goal to present a revised mobile hypothesis within two weeks.
3.  **Monitoring:** Establish guardrail metrics for the desktop rollout to ensure the engagement lift translates to long-term retention, and monitor mobile traffic to the legacy layout to ensure no spill-over effects occur.

This strategy maximizes upside capture while strictly containing downside risk, aligning with our goal of data-driven, iterative improvement.

---
*Note: Ensure engineering validates that the feature flag logic correctly identifies device types to prevent leakage. The mobile diagnostic sprint should prioritize session replay analysis to pinpoint exact drop-off moments.*
