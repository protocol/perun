**Executive Summary: Biometric Verification Pilot Results (Sept 15 – Oct 15)**

**Recommendation:** Do not mandate biometrics for all users at this time. Instead, implement a **hybrid default strategy**: serve biometric authentication as the primary flow for compatible devices, but automatically route users with older operating systems or failed biometric attempts to the legacy SMS flow. Make biometrics an optional "preferred method" in settings rather than a mandatory requirement.

**Performance Trade-offs**

*   **User Experience (Gain):** The pilot achieved a **7% reduction in overall drop-off** across 50,000 applicants. For users with modern devices, removing the SMS wait time successfully reduced friction and accelerated account activation.
*   **Support Costs (Risk):** The spike in "verification failed" tickets among older phone models indicates a critical segmentation failure. If rolled out universally, this technical incompatibility will likely cause a net increase in support volume, eroding the operational savings gained from higher completion rates.
*   **Security (Neutral/Positive):** Biometrics generally offer superior security to SMS (which is vulnerable to SIM swapping). However, forcing a failing biometric prompt creates a "soft lockout" where users cannot proceed to the secure SMS fallback, effectively denying service rather than enhancing security.

**Decision Framework**

| Scenario | Impact on Drop-off | Impact on Support | Operational Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mandate Biometrics** | Likely worsens for 15-20% of user base (older devices) | **High Spike** (Bug/Usability fix required first) | **Reject:** High risk of excluding valid customers. |
| **Keep Status Quo** | Baseline (Higher friction for majority) | Baseline | **Suboptimal:** Leaves 7% conversion gain on the table. |
| **Hybrid Default (Recommended)** | Captures ~90% of the 7% gain | **Managed:** Only handles genuine edge cases | **Approve:** Balances speed with inclusivity. |

**Immediate Next Steps**
1.  **Engineering:** Isolate whether the "verification failed" error is a hard crash on specific OS versions (bug) or a user confusion issue (usability) by analyzing device logs from the pilot.
2.  **Product:** Configure the rule engine to detect device compatibility *before* presenting the biometric prompt.
3.  **Operations:** Prepare support scripts for the hybrid transition, noting that ticket volume may temporarily rise as the new routing stabilizes.

This approach secures the majority of the conversion uplift while mitigating the risk of alienating users with legacy hardware.

*Note: Final device compatibility thresholds must be defined by Engineering before the Tuesday approval. Support cost projections should be updated once the root cause of the "failed" errors is confirmed.*
