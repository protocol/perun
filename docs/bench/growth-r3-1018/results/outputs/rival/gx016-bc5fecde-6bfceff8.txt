# Analysis Brief: Italy Price Increase Impact Assessment

**To:** Regional Director of Sales
**From:** Analytics Lead
**Date:** February 2, 2024
**Subject:** Urgent Analysis Plan to Isolate Price Sensitivity vs. Competitor Impact in Italy

## Executive Summary
Following the 12% price increase on January 10, Italy small business churn rose to 7% (vs. <3% projected) and new sign-ups dropped 20%. With a competitor promotion launching January 15, we must distinguish between price elasticity and competitive displacement to determine whether to roll back pricing or deploy targeted discounts by month-end.

## Key Questions to Answer
1. **Price Effect:** What portion of the 7% churn is attributable solely to the 12% price increase?
2. **Competitor Effect:** Did the January 15 competitor promotion accelerate cancellations beyond price sensitivity?
3. **New Business Impact:** Is the 20% sign-up decline driven by price barriers or competitor acquisition offers?
4. **Segment Variation:** Are specific sub-segments (by company size, tenure, or feature usage) disproportionately affected?

## Proposed Analysis Method

### 1. Cohort Segmentation by Exposure Window
Create three distinct cohorts to isolate timing effects:
- **Pre-Competitor Cohort:** Customers who received price increase Jan 10–14 (5 days, price effect only)
- **Post-Competitor Cohort:** Customers who received price increase Jan 15–31 (price + competitor effect)
- **Control Cohort:** Similar small business customers in Spain or France (no price change, exposed to same competitor)

### 2. Difference-in-Differences Calculation
Compare churn rates across cohorts to separate effects:
- **Pure Price Effect** = Churn rate (Pre-Competitor Cohort) − Baseline churn (historical Italy average)
- **Combined Effect** = Churn rate (Post-Competitor Cohort) − Baseline churn
- **Competitor Effect** = Combined Effect − Pure Price Effect

Apply the same logic to new sign-up conversion rates using daily traffic data.

### 3. Cancellation Reason Analysis
Segment the 7% churn by stated cancellation reason:
- "Too expensive" or "pricing" → Price sensitivity indicator
- "Switching to [competitor]" or "better offer elsewhere" → Competitive displacement
- "No longer need service" → Unrelated churn (exclude from analysis)

Cross-reference cancellation dates with the January 15 competitor launch to identify acceleration patterns.

### 4. Feature Usage Correlation
Analyze whether cancelling customers actively used the new analytics features:
- **High usage + churn:** Price sensitivity despite value recognition (critical signal)
- **Low usage + churn:** Value proposition mismatch (different problem)

### 5. Geographic Control Comparison
Compare Italy metrics against Spain/France (similar market, no price change):
- If Spain/France show stable churn and sign-ups → Italy impact is local (price + competitor)
- If Spain/France show similar sign-up declines → Broader market factor affecting new business

## Data Requirements
- Daily churn and sign-up data for Italy (Dec 1–Jan 31)
- Daily churn and sign-up data for Spain/France controls (Dec 1–Jan 31)
- Cancellation reason codes with timestamps
- Feature usage logs for cancelling accounts (last 30 days before cancellation)
- Competitor promotion details (start date, offer type, target segment)

## Decision Framework

| Scenario | Evidence | Recommended Action |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| **Price-Dominant** | Pre-competitor cohort shows >4% churn; cancellation reasons cite pricing; control regions stable | Roll back price for Italy or introduce permanent localized pricing |
| **Competitor-Dominant** | Pre-competitor cohort shows <3% churn; sharp spike after Jan 15; reasons cite competitor | Launch targeted win-back discount (15–20%) for at-risk segment; accelerate value communication |
| **Combined Effect** | Both cohorts elevated; mixed cancellation reasons | Hybrid approach: temporary 10% discount for 3 months + enhanced onboarding for analytics features |
| **Value Mismatch** | High churn among low feature users regardless of timing | Revise onboarding and value demonstration; pricing secondary issue |
