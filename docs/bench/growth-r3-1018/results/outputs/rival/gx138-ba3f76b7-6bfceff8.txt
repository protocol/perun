# LuxVerify Seller Onboarding Funnel: Velocity & Integrity Metrics

To balance the goal of reducing time-to-list from 4 days to 24 hours against the risk of increasing fraud beyond the 0.5% baseline, the following funnel stages must be monitored. These stages track both the speed of progression and the quality of verification at each step, specifically tailored for high-value luxury goods.

### Core Onboarding Velocity Stages
*Measure time deltas between these steps to track the 24-hour goal.*

1.  **Account Initiation:** User completes email/phone verification and creates credentials.
2.  **Identity Verification Start:** User initiates the new instant ID scan flow (replacing manual upload).
3.  **Identity Verification Pass:** System automatically validates ID and liveness check.
    *   *Integrity Check:* Track "False Acceptance Rate" here by sampling passed IDs against manual review for the first 30 days.
4.  **Business Profile Completion:** User submits tax info, payout details, and specialty categories (e.g., watches, handbags).
5.  **First Listing Drafted:** User uploads item photos, description, and asking price.
6.  **First Listing Submitted:** User formally submits the item for marketplace review.
7.  **First Listing Live:** Item passes automated checks and is visible to buyers.

### Post-Listing Integrity & Fraud Detection Stages
*Measure conversion rates and flag rates at these steps to ensure security is not compromised.*

8.  **Post-Listing Automated Flag:** Percentage of first listings flagged by AI for counterfeit indicators (e.g., poor photo quality, pricing anomalies, brand-specific risk patterns).
9.  **Manual Trust Review Triggered:** Cases escalated to human agents due to high-risk signals or random sampling.
    *   *Critical Metric:* Compare manual review trigger rates between the "Instant ID" cohort vs. historical "Manual Upload" cohort.
10. **Confirmed Fraud Detection:** Instances where a listed item is confirmed as inauthentic post-submission or post-sale.
    *   *Target:* Maintain ≤ 0.5% of total first listings.
11. **Chargeback/Return Due to Authenticity:** Disputes filed by buyers claiming inauthenticity within the guarantee window.
12. **Seller Account Suspension:** Accounts banned within 30 days of onboarding due to fraud verification failures.

### Essential Derived Metrics for the Head of Trust & Safety

*   **Velocity-to-Fraud Ratio:** The correlation between time-to-list (e.g., <24 hrs vs. >24 hrs) and subsequent fraud detection rates. This determines if speed directly predicts risk.
*   **ID Scan Bypass Rate:** The percentage of users who abandon the funnel specifically at the instant ID scan step. A high drop-off may indicate friction or users attempting to evade detection.
*   **False Negative Rate (Security Leak):** The percentage of sellers who pass instant ID and list an item, but are later flagged for fraud. This is the primary risk indicator for the new system.
*   **Category-Specific Risk Delta:** Breakdown of fraud rates by luxury category (e.g., Rolex vs. Louis Vuitton) to identify if the instant ID scan is less effective for specific high-risk verticals.

**Note:** Ensure the "Instant ID Pass" event logs the confidence score from the vendor API to allow for retrospective tuning if fraud spikes.
**Note:** Define a "cooling off" period where first listings from the new flow undergo enhanced automated scrutiny before going live, even if ID verification passes.
