# DataPrecise Pricing Test Evaluation Plan (March 1 – April 30)

## 1. Primary Success Metrics
Track these weekly to determine if the price increase is sustainable:
*   **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** Did the 50% price hike offset any volume loss from churn?
*   **Segmented Churn Rate:** Compare churn between "Small Business" (<50 employees) and "Enterprise" (>200 employees). Your hypothesis relies on Enterprise stability; verify this immediately.
*   **Upgrade Conversion Rate:** Percentage of eligible existing customers who accepted the new tier vs. those who downgraded or requested exemptions.

## 2. Leading Indicators of Dissatisfaction (Early Warning Signals)
Do not wait for cancellations. Monitor these behavioral signals weekly to predict churn before it happens:
*   **Support Ticket Sentiment:** Tag and count tickets containing "price," "cost," "budget," or "expensive." A spike in volume or negative sentiment in the first 14 days is a critical red flag.
*   **Login Frequency Drop:** Compare March login rates against the January/February baseline. A >15% drop in active users within a specific account often precedes cancellation by 30 days.
*   **Feature Adoption Gap:** Specifically track usage of the **new reporting module**. If customers on the €600 tier are not logging into this module at least twice a week, they do not perceive the value justification.
*   **Payment Friction:** Monitor failed payment retries. An increase in declined cards may indicate customers are instructing finance to block charges rather than formally cancelling.

## 3. Data Collection Cadence
*   **Daily:** Automated alert on cancellation requests and payment failures.
*   **Weekly:** Cohort analysis of churn by company size and industry sector.
*   **Bi-Weekly:** Qualitative review of 5–10 lost customer exit interviews or support threads.

## 4. Decision Framework for May Board Meeting
Use the following thresholds to recommend action to the CFO:

| Scenario | Condition | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Green Light** | Total churn remains ≤4% AND NRR >100% | **Double Down.** The market accepts the value. Increase sales targets. |
| **Yellow Light** | Small Business churn >8% BUT Enterprise churn ≤3.5% | **Segment Strategy.** Maintain €600 for Enterprise; revert or discount for Small Business. |
| **Red Light** | Total churn >6% OR NRR <95% | **Roll Back.** The price elasticity was underestimated. Revert to €400 to prevent further revenue erosion. |

## 5. Value Perception Validation
To answer if the reporting module justifies the cost, correlate **module usage frequency** with **retention status** at Day 45.
*   *Hypothesis:* Customers who use the reporting module ≥4 times/month will have churn rates similar to the historical 3% baseline.
*   *Action:* If high usage correlates with retention but overall churn is still high, the issue is awareness, not value. If high usage correlates with high churn, the module itself is not worth €200 to them.

**Note:** Ensure all data is segmented by company size immediately; aggregating data will hide the specific risk to your small business cohort.
**Note:** Begin collecting qualitative feedback on "value perception" now via account managers; quantitative data alone will lag behind customer sentiment.
