# GlobeChat Post-Fix Evaluation Rubric

**Objective:** Determine Q2 strategy allocation between Stability Investment vs. Re-engagement Campaigns based on data beyond aggregate crash rates.
**Decision Deadline:** End of Week
**Stakeholders:** VP of Engineering, Head of Growth

### 1. Segment-Specific Stability Impact
*Goal: Identify if remaining crashes disproportionately affect high-value users.*

| Metric | Data Source | Success Threshold | Failure Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crash Rate by User Tier** | Analytics (Free vs. Paid) | Paid user crash rate ≤ Global avg (2.1%) | Paid user crash rate > 3.5% |
| **Crash Frequency per Session** | Product Analytics | < 1 crash per 50 sessions for retained users | > 1 crash per 20 sessions for any segment |
| **Feature-Specific Crashes** | Error Logging | No crashes in core flow (e.g., messaging) | > 0.5% crash rate in top 3 features |

### 2. Trust & Re-engagement Correlation
*Goal: Determine if flat DAU is due to lingering trust issues or lack of awareness.*

| Metric | Data Source | Success Threshold | Failure Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Re-install Rate (Churned Users)** | Attribution Tool | > 15% of previously churned users re-install | < 5% re-install rate despite stability |
| **Session Initiation Success** | Client Logs | > 98% of app opens result in successful load | < 95% success rate on first open of the day |
| **Negative Sentiment Ratio** | App Store/Support Tickets | < 10% of new reviews mention "crash" | > 25% of new reviews still cite stability |

### 3. Engagement Quality (Beyond Duration)
*Goal: Assess if users who stay are deriving value, even if session length is flat.*

| Metric | Data Source | Success Threshold | Failure Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Action Completion Rate** | Event Tracking | ≥ Pre-crash baseline (e.g., messages sent) | Significant drop vs. pre-Feb 20 baseline |
| **Day-7 Retention (Cohort)** | Cohort Analysis | Stabilizing or trending up for post-fix cohorts | Continuing decline for post-fix cohorts |
| **Critical Error Exposure** | Session Replay/Logs | < 1% of active users hit a non-fatal error | > 5% of users experience non-fatal errors |

### Decision Matrix: Q2 Strategy Recommendation

| Scenario | Data Pattern | Recommended Strategy | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A: Targeted Instability** | High crash rates in Paid/Power segments; Core actions dropping. | **Double Down on Stability** | Trust is broken where it matters most; re-engagement will fail if the product still breaks for whales. |
| **B: Perception Lag** | Low crash rates across all segments; Low re-install; High negative sentiment. | **Aggressive Re-engagement** | The product is stable, but the market doesn't know. Invest in "We Fixed It" campaigns and win-back offers. |
| **C: Value Mismatch** | Low crashes; Flat retention; Low core action completion. | **Product/Feature Pivot** | Stability is solved, but the app isn't delivering value. Shift resources to feature development, not more stability or just marketing. |
| **D: Mixed Signals** | Moderate crashes in low-value segments; Flat growth. | **Balanced Approach** | Allocate 70% to re-engagement, 30% to monitoring/fixing specific low-priority crashes. |

### Immediate Next Steps (Before Friday)
1.  **Engineering:** Slice crash data by user subscription tier and top 3 features.
2.  **Growth:** Analyze re-install rates and sentiment trends for users who churned between Jan 1–Feb 20.
3.  **Joint Review:** Map findings to the Decision Matrix to lock Q2 budget allocation.

*Note: Ensure "success" is defined by retention and revenue impact, not just technical stability metrics. If Scenario B is confirmed, craft messaging that explicitly addresses the past instability to rebuild trust.*
