**Subject:** Recommendation on Fundraising Email A/B Test (Sept 1–30)

**Recommendation: Roll out Version B campus-wide, contingent on final video costs remaining under $1,500.**

Version B generated superior net revenue despite a slightly lower average gift, driven by a higher conversion rate. The math confirms the uplift:
*   **Version A:** 4,752 recipients × 2.3% = 109 donors × $120 = **$13,080 total**.
*   **Version B:** 4,800 recipients × 2.7% = 130 donors × $115 = **$14,950 total**.
*   **Gross Uplift:** $1,870 (not $2,500; the finance dashboard appears to have overestimated by ~$630).

Even with the maximum estimated production cost of $1,500, Version B yields a **net gain of $370**. If we can negotiate the video cost toward the $1,200 estimate, the net gain rises to $670. While the margin is slim, Version B demonstrates that the creative approach resonates more effectively with our alumni base, converting 19% more donors than Version A.

**Primary Risks to This Decision:**
1.  **Cost Overruns:** If final video editing or licensing fees exceed $1,870, the campaign becomes net-negative compared to Version A.
2.  **Donor Fatigue:** The higher conversion rate in the test sample may not scale; rolling out to the full list could trigger diminishing returns if the "early adopter" effect was skewed.
3.  **Mis-attributed Uplift:** If external factors (e.g., a specific news event during the test window) drove the lift rather than the creative, the campus-wide rollout may not replicate the 2.7% conversion rate.

**Stop/Change Course Trigger:**
I advise halting the rollout or reverting to Version A if the **final net gain falls below $1,000** after all costs are accounted for. At that threshold, the operational risk and effort of deploying the custom video asset no longer justify the marginal financial return over the static Version A. Additionally, if the campus-wide conversion rate for Version B drops below 2.4% in the first 48 hours of launch, we should pause and re-evaluate the creative fatigue.

*Note: The finance team should reconcile the $630 discrepancy in their incremental revenue model before finalizing Q3 reports. We must secure the final invoice for the video production before scheduling the campus-wide send.*
