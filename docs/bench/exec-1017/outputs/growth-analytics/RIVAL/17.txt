**Recommendation: Launch Globally**

Proceed with the global rollout of "Spend Alerts" to all free-tier users. The A/B test demonstrates a statistically significant and economically viable opportunity. With an observed uplift from 3.2% to 4.1%, the variant generated approximately 473 paid conversions versus 384 in the control, yielding ~89 incremental users. At $9 average first-month revenue, this generates ~$801 in incremental monthly recurring revenue (MRR). While the $10,000 development cost requires ~12.5 months to recoup based solely on first-month revenue from this cohort, the feature drives ongoing value through retained users (assuming 5% monthly churn) and potential long-term LTV growth. The 28% relative uplift is substantial enough to justify scaling, provided we monitor the unmeasured referral impact.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Referral Cannibalization:** If the alert annoys users enough to suppress organic referrals, the net acquisition cost could rise, negating the conversion gain.
2.  **Churn Spike:** If the feature increases perceived pressure, causing free-tier churn to exceed the historic 5% baseline before conversion, the funnel volume collapses.
3.  **Uplift Decay:** The 28% lift may diminish at scale if the test audience was unrepresentative or if the "novelty effect" wears off, dropping the conversion rate closer to control levels.

**Stop/Change Course Triggers:**
*   **Immediate Pause:** If post-launch monitoring shows free-tier churn increases by >2 percentage points (e.g., rising from 5% to 7%) within the first 30 days.
*   **Re-evaluation:** If the realized conversion uplift drops below 10% relative to control (approx. 3.52% absolute rate) after 60 days, making the payback period exceed 18 months.
*   **Feature Rollback:** If net incremental revenue (conversion gains minus churn losses) turns negative for two consecutive months.

*Note: Implement immediate tracking on referral sign-up rates for users exposed to the alert to validate the unknown variable.*
*Ensure customer support is briefed on the feature to handle potential confusion tickets that could impact retention.*
