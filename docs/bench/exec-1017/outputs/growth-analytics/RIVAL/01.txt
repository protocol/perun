**Subject:** Strategic Direction for Onboarding Initiative

**1. Recommended North-Star Metric**
Adopt **"Day-30 Retention of Activated Users"** as our single North-Star metric.
While "activation rate" measures initial success, it ignores the Q2 churn reality (9% vs. 5% target). Focusing solely on activation risks optimizing for users who start but don't stay. Day-30 retention directly correlates activation quality with long-term value, ensuring the "quick-start" tutorial drives sustainable usage rather than just initial configuration.

**2. Three Biggest Failure Modes**
*   **The Attribution Trap:** We are assuming onboarding completion is the *only* driver of churn. If churn is actually driven by data integration failures or lack of manager adoption post-onboarding, optimizing the tutorial will yield diminishing returns while the real leak persists.
*   **Statistical Prematurity:** The A/B test showed a 2-point absolute gain (12% → 14%), not the 30% relative lift cited. With only ~350 converted users total, the confidence interval is wide. Scaling based on this preliminary data risks implementing a change that offers negligible ROI once support costs are factored in.
*   **The Efficiency Paradox:** The "quick-start" may reduce time-to-value but increase support complexity later. If users configure the system quickly but incorrectly, we may see a spike in downstream support tickets, eroding the margin gains from reduced churn.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
We will immediately halt or pivot the initiative if either of the following occurs in the next 30 days:
*   **Conversion Decay:** The variant's conversion rate drops below **12.5%** (losing half the observed lift), indicating the initial test result was noise or context-dependent.
*   **Support Cost Breach:** Average support cost per activated employee exceeds **$45** (or 20% above current baseline), signaling that speed of setup is compromising configuration quality.

***

*Note: We must validate the "onboarding-only churn" assumption via exit interviews before Q3 forecasting. The 30% lift figure should be corrected to 16.7% relative lift (2/12) in all future executive communications to maintain credibility.*
