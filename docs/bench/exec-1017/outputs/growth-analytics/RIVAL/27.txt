**Recommendation: Launch Nationally with a 30-Day Cost Guardrail**

Approve the national rollout of "Protein Plus" immediately. The test demonstrated a statistically robust 4.8% lift in Average Order Value ($84.12 → $88.03) and a meaningful 2.1% increase in 14-day repeat purchase rates (18.0% → 20.1%). The test generated $23,460 in incremental revenue against a $12,000 test spend, yielding a positive return even before accounting for the long-term value of higher retention. While the exact incremental shipping cost remains unquantified, the $3.91 per-order revenue uplift provides sufficient margin to absorb the known $0.45 packaging increase and the projected $0.30 fulfillment fee. The dual lift in immediate revenue and early retention signals a strong product-market fit that justifies scaling despite the minor cost uncertainties.

**Three Biggest Risks to Rollout Success**

1.  **Shipping Cost Erosion:** The "unknown" incremental shipping cost is the critical variable. If the added weight or dimensional changes of the "Protein Plus" packaging push shipping costs up by more than $3.16 per unit, the entire $3.91 revenue lift is consumed, turning a profitable gain into a margin-negative operation.
2.  **Creative Efficiency Decay:** The test relied on a specific $12,000 spend. Scaling to a $45,000 monthly creative budget assumes consistent CPMs and conversion rates. If ad fatigue sets in quickly or the broader national audience responds less positively than the test cohort, the customer acquisition cost could rise enough to negate the AOV lift.
3.  **Operational Friction:** Introducing a new SKU variant often creates fulfillment bottlenecks. If the new packaging slows down pick-and-pack times or increases error rates (e.g., wrong items shipped), the resulting customer service costs and delayed deliveries could degrade the very retention metrics (the 2.1% lift) that make this offer valuable.

**Halt or Pivot Trigger**

**Monitor the "Net Contribution Margin per Unit" daily for the first 30 days.** Specifically, halt or pivot the program if the **actualized shipping cost exceeds $3.15 per unit**. At this threshold, the combined costs of packaging ($0.45), fulfillment ($0.30), and shipping ($3.15) total $3.90, effectively erasing the $3.91 revenue uplift. If this metric breaches the limit, pause the rollout to renegotiate carrier rates or redesign the packaging for weight reduction before proceeding.
