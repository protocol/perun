2.  **Churn Acceleration:** If the new flow attracts lower-intent users who complete a document but fail to find ongoing value, the 5% churn assumption will worsen, destroying LTV.
3.  **Traffic Quality Shift:** If the new landing page copy inadvertently attracts non-solo lawyers (e.g., enterprise legal teams unsuited for the $49 tier), conversion may stay high while downstream revenue collapses.

**Stop/Change Triggers**

*   **Revenue Flatline:** 30-day net revenue per acquired user shows **< 1% improvement** over the Variant B baseline (indicating the conversion lift isn't translating to paid retention).
