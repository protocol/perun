**Recommendation:** Proceed with a phased fleet-wide rollout limited to 40% of the fleet over the next six months, contingent on installing dedicated high-speed charging infrastructure before deployment.

While the pilot demonstrates strong unit economics ($9,500 monthly savings vs. $15,000 upfront premium yields a payback period under two months), the four instances of significant downtime reveal a critical infrastructure bottleneck that will scale linearly with fleet size. A full immediate rollout risks replicating these charging failures across hundreds of vehicles, potentially causing revenue loss that outweighs maintenance and fuel savings. The phased approach allows you to capture immediate savings on the majority of diesel units while stress-testing charging solutions for the electric segment without exposing the entire client operation to systemic downtime risk.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Infrastructure Saturation:** As electric van density increases, current charging capacity will cause cascading downtime, dropping fleet uptime below the 85% pilot level and directly impacting delivery revenue.
2.  **Battery Degradation Cliff:** If battery capacity drops significantly after month 12, the projected $9,500 monthly savings (which assume flat electricity prices and consistent range) could evaporate due to increased charging frequency and eventual replacement costs.

**Stop/Change Triggers:**
*   **Stop Immediately:** If fleet-wide uptime drops below 80% for two consecutive weeks post-rollout, indicating charging infrastructure cannot support operational density.
*   **Change Course (Pause & Re-evaluate):** If battery degradation exceeds 15% capacity loss by month 10, or if total cost of ownership (including unexpected charging infrastructure upgrades) exceeds $55,000 per vehicle.
*   **Revert to Diesel:** If driver satisfaction scores drop below the baseline of 65, suggesting operational friction outweighs vehicle benefits.

The pilot proves the vehicle economics work; the rollout success now depends entirely on solving the charging equation before scaling.
