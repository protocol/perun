**To:** Ravi Singh, COO
**From:** Product Lead, Metro Line Digital
**Date:** October 24, 2024
**Subject:** Recommendation on "Live-Crowd" Push Notification Rollout

**Recommendation: Do Not Roll Out Agency-Wide Yet.**

While the treatment group showed a statistically observable lift in click-through rate (CTR) from 1.8% to 2.2%, the data is currently insufficient to justify a full launch due to confounding variables and negative revenue signals. The concurrent city-wide advertising campaign launched on September 10 introduced ~5,000 new users whose behavior is mixed into the DAU growth (58k to 62k), making it impossible to isolate the feature's true impact on retention versus simple acquisition influx. Furthermore, the 11% decline in revenue per click ($0.45 to $0.40) suggests the feature may be driving lower-value engagement. We must run a corrected analysis excluding the ad-campaign traffic and validate that the net revenue impact is neutral or positive before scaling.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Revenue Dilution:** Scaling a feature that increases engagement volume but decreases value per interaction could lower total monetization despite higher traffic.
2.  **Attribution Error:** Rolling out based on inflated DAU numbers caused by the ad campaign may result in the feature failing to sustain engagement once the ad spend normalizes.
3.  **Notification Fatigue:** If the "Live-Crowd" data is not highly accurate, increased push frequency could lead to higher opt-out rates, damaging our primary communication channel.

**Stop/Change Course Triggers:**
We will immediately halt or rollback the rollout if post-launch monitoring reveals:
*   **CTR Degradation:** A sustained drop in click-through rate below **1.5%** across the broader population (indicating the test group was an anomaly).
*   **Negative Net Revenue:** Any measurable decrease in total daily revenue attributable to the feature, even if engagement metrics remain high.
*   **Unsubscribe Spike:** An increase in push notification opt-outs exceeding **0.5%** of the daily active user base within the first 14 days.

***

*Note: A revised test excluding the September 10–28 ad-driven cohort is required to validate the true lift. We need ridership correlation data before linking this feature to long-term satisfaction goals.*
