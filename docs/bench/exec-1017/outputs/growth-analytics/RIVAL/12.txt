**Recommendation for Maya Patel:** Shift the majority of the remaining budget to the text channel immediately, but do not pause the email channel entirely.

While email generated higher total revenue, text messaging is the superior efficiency driver. Your data shows text acquired donors at $8 CPA compared to $12.50 for email, and delivered a higher initial recurring intent (12% vs. 5%). Even with a conservative estimate of processing fees (~3.2%), the text channel's lower acquisition cost provides a larger safety margin against churn. If the six-month churn rate for text donors is even slightly higher than email, the lower CPA still makes text more profitable long-term. Maintain a small email baseline to capture the segment of donors who do not engage via text, but reallocate 70-80% of future spend to scale the text program.

**How This Plan Could Fail:**
1.  **Churn Reversal:** The pilot's 12% recurring rate was an anomaly, and text donors actually churn faster than email donors after month three, erasing the CPA advantage.
2.  **Compliance/Carrier Blocking:** Scaling text volume triggers carrier spam filters or regulatory fines (TCPA), suddenly inflating costs or halting delivery.

**Stop/Change Course Metric:**
Stop the shift if the **Day-90 retention rate** for text donors drops below **50% of the email retention rate**. At that point, the lifetime value (LTV) advantage disappears, and you should revert to a balanced split.
