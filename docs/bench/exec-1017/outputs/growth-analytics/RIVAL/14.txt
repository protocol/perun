**Recommendation: Roll out the variant company-wide, contingent on immediate retention monitoring.**

The variant delivered a statistically significant 25% relative lift in "first payroll run" conversion (12% vs. 15%) and generated $1,300 in incremental gross revenue ($9,800 - $8,500). Even after deducting the $2,000 incremental email cost, the short-term net position is -$700. However, the 81 additional converted users represent approximately $9,720 in annualized subscription value ($120/month × 12 months × 81 users). If these users retain beyond month two, the variant becomes highly profitable within 60 days. The risk of maintaining the inferior control flow (losing 81 potential customers per 4,500 sign-ups) outweighs the temporary cash flow dip caused by one-time setup costs.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Retention Collapse:** The aggressive email strategy drives initial action but annoys users, causing month-2 or month-3 churn to spike above baseline, negating the lifetime value of the 81 new converts.
2.  **Support Overload:** The 25% increase in active users may overwhelm current support capacity if the new users require more hand-holding, degrading service for existing customers.
3.  **Seasonality Distortion:** If Q1 2024 behavior was anomalously high due to post-holiday business formation trends (unlike Q4 2022), the 15% conversion rate may not sustain in other months.

**Stop/Change Course Triggers:**
*   **Churn Threshold:** Halt or revert if month-2 retention for the variant cohort drops more than 5 percentage points below the control group's historical month-2 retention.
*   **Support Volume:** Revert if support tickets per active user increase by more than 20% compared to the control baseline.
*   **Revenue Payback:** If the cumulative net revenue (revenue minus email costs) for the variant cohort does not turn positive by the end of Month 2, pause the rollout to re-evaluate the email frequency or content.

*Note: The Q4 2022 comparison should be disregarded for decision-making until seasonality factors are quantified.*
*Ensure the email cost is a one-time acquisition expense; if it recurs monthly, the recommendation changes to "Do Not Roll Out."*
