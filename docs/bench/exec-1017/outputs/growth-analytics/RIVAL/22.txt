**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **staged rollout to 25% of the customer base** over four weeks, rather than an immediate full launch or maintaining limited beta.

The A/B test demonstrates a statistically significant lift in engagement (10.8% vs. 8.5%), validating the feature's appeal. However, the financial case relies on unproven upsell assumptions. With a known hard cost of $15 per completion, rolling out to 100% of users immediately exposes Redoak Secure to ~$180,000 in monthly incremental costs (based on current volume) without confirmed revenue offset. A staged approach caps this exposure at ~$45,000/month initially, allowing us to measure actual upsell conversion and revenue per user before committing full budget. The comparison to the prior 9% pilot is irrelevant; the concurrent A/B test provides the valid baseline.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Negative Unit Economics:** The 5% upsell lift fails to materialize, or the revenue per upsell is too low to cover the $15 variable cost, turning engagement into a loss leader.
2.  **Support Overload:** "Live" simulations generate complex technical tickets or false-positive panic among users, spiking support costs beyond the modeled $15/completion.
3.  **Churn Correlation:** High engagement masks dissatisfaction; users who complete the simulation churn at a rate higher than the 6% baseline due to fatigue or perceived difficulty.

**Stop/Change Triggers:**
*   **Conversion Rate:** If the completion rate in the 25% cohort drops below **9.0%** (eroding the margin of safety over the control).
*   **Cost Efficiency:** If the fully loaded cost per completion (licensing + support labor) exceeds **$20**.
*   **Upsell Validation:** If the attach rate for upsells among completers is less than **3%** after the first 30 days of the staged rollout.
*   **Churn Spike:** If monthly churn for participants exceeds **7.5%** (a 25% increase over baseline).

Do not expand beyond the 25% cohort until the incremental revenue from upsells in that group covers at least 80% of the incurred simulation costs.
