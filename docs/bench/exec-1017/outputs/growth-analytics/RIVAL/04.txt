**Recommendation: Conditional Go (Pilot Expansion Only)**

Roll out the "Second Seat Premium" tier to 25% of national traffic immediately, but **do not** launch nationwide yet. While the experiment generated a clear short-term profit uplift (~$22.60 net margin gain per converted order), the 90-day sustainability assumption and churn impact are unverified. A full nationwide launch exposes the company to irreversible brand damage and recurring revenue loss if the "Fast-Track" perception erodes long-term loyalty. Use the 25% pilot to validate retention metrics over the next 45 days before committing to 100% distribution.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Cannibalization:** Existing high-value customers downgrade to the standard tier or delay repeat purchases, expecting perpetual subsidies.
2.  **Margin Erosion:** Operational shipping costs exceed the $5 subsidy assumption during peak volume, flipping the unit economics negative.
3.  **Brand Perception:** Customers view the standard tier as "negligent" rather than "standard," creating friction for non-premium users.

**Stop-Loss Trigger:**
Halt expansion immediately if **weekly net revenue from repeat customers drops >5% for two consecutive weeks** compared to the control group, or if the **conversion rate on the premium tier falls below 10%** (indicating the initial 14.9% lift was merely novelty-driven).
