**Recommendation: NO-GO on full rollout.**

Do not launch "Freight-Flex" broadly. While the test delivered a significant volume lift (+18.7%), it failed the primary business constraint: net revenue. The treatment group generated **$89,400 less** in revenue despite higher activity, driven by a 11.3% drop in average revenue per shipment ($115 → $102). Scaling this model would systematically erode top-line revenue. Furthermore, the 58% surge in support tickets (12 → 19/day) indicates operational friction that is currently unpriced; if these tickets require senior agent time, the actual profit loss is significantly deeper than the $89k revenue gap suggests. Before reconsidering, you must restructure the pricing logic to protect margin per shipment and resolve the support bottleneck.

**Three Biggest Failure Risks:**

1.  **Profitability Collapse:** The current volume lift does not compensate for the margin compression. If the discount cannibalizes existing high-margin contracts as users downgrade, the net revenue loss will accelerate beyond the observed $89k, turning volume growth into value destruction.
2.  **Operational Overload:** Support ticket volume rose 58% with only a partial rollout. A full launch could push daily tickets well over 50, overwhelming the support team, degrading service levels for premium clients, and introducing hidden labor costs that wipe out any remaining gross profit.
3.  **Q4 Elasticity Mismatch:** The test ran in a stable period. Q4 seasonal spikes often change price sensitivity; brokers may prioritize reliability over discounts during peak capacity crunches. If the "Freight-Flex" tier attracts low-quality volume that clogs capacity during peak weeks, you risk displacing lucrative spot-market shipments.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers:**

*   **Net Revenue Decline > 2%:** Even a small negative impact on total net revenue is unacceptable given the operational strain; do not tolerate *any* revenue loss for volume gains unless lifetime value (LTV) modeling proves otherwise.
*   **Support Tickets > 30/day:** If a scaled rollout (approx. 2x current exposure) pushes tickets above 30 daily, the unit economics break due to support costs alone.
*   **Cannibalization Rate > 10%:** If more than 10% of users on "Freight-Flex" are downgraded from existing higher-margin contracts rather than being incremental volume, pause immediately to re-segment the offer.

*Note: The 18.7% lift is statistically significant given the sample size, but statistical significance does not validate a negative business outcome. Do not let the "win" on volume obscure the loss on value.*
