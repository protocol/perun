**Subject:** Peoplegrid Onboarding Strategy & Risk Assessment

**1. Recommended North Star Metric**
Adopt **"Weekly Active Users Completing Core Configuration"** rather than raw activation or signups.
*Reasoning:* Current activation (68%) measures a one-time event, but churn (9%) indicates users aren't deriving sustained value. A North Star based on recurring completion of the core action (configuration) directly ties user behavior to retention and revenue, filtering out vanity metrics like total active users who never configure their grid.

**2. Three Biggest Failure Modes**
*   **Peeking Bias in Experiment Results:** The A/B test (June 5–19) showed a 14% vs. 12% conversion. If this result was monitored daily and stopped early because it looked significant, the "win" is likely statistical noise (false positive). Without a pre-committed sample size or sequential testing rule, we cannot trust the 2-point lift is real.
*   **Single-Cause Attribution Error:** Assuming onboarding completion is the *only* driver of churn ignores post-onboarding friction (e.g., data integration failures, lack of manager adoption). Fixing onboarding while ignoring downstream blockers will fail to move the 9% churn rate toward the 5% target.
*   **Undefined Unit Economics:** Proceeding without the average support cost per employee makes ROI calculations impossible. If the "quick-start" tutorial reduces churn but spikes support tickets due to confusion, the initiative could be profitable in retention but loss-making overall.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Conversion Decay:** If the variant's conversion rate drops below **11%** (below the original control baseline) over a full 14-day cycle, pivot the tutorial design immediately.
*   **Support Cost Threshold:** If the average support cost per employee exceeds **$X** (insert current margin tolerance), halt the rollout until the cost driver is isolated.
*   **Retention Disconnect:** If activation rises above 75% but **Week-4 Retention** does not improve by at least 2 percentage points within 60 days, stop investing in onboarding tweaks and shift focus to post-activation value delivery.
