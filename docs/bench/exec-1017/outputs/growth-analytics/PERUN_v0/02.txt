**Recommendation to CEO Maya Patel:** Do not globally roll out Variant B yet. While the test shows a clear revenue lift (+$6,700) and higher CTR (3.2% vs 2.8%), the decision rests on unverified assumptions regarding subscriber churn and incremental server costs. Rolling out globally based solely on top-line revenue risks eroding the North Star metric (long-term listener retention) and violating our profit margins if the $7k cost estimate is inaccurate.

**Immediate Action:** Launch a targeted "churn watch" cohort analysis for the test group over the next 30 days to measure cancellation rates against the control, and finalize the exact incremental infrastructure cost per impression. Only proceed to global rollout if subscriber churn remains statistically flat (within ±0.2% of baseline) and the verified cost per impression stays below $0.06.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Retention Leak:** Higher ad load drives premium subscribers to cancel, negating the $6,700 gain within weeks.
2.  **Cost Overrun:** Serving extra mid-roll ads increases bandwidth/compute costs beyond the rough $7k estimate, shrinking net margin.
3.  **Brand Safety/Quality:** Increased ad frequency degrades user experience, leading to negative reviews or reduced session duration (a leading indicator of churn).

**Stop/Pivot Triggers:**
*   **Churn:** If subscriber cancellation rates in the variant cohort exceed the control group by **>1.5%** over the next 30 days.
*   **Cost:** If the finalized incremental cost per impression exceeds **$0.08** (eroding the margin gained from the CTR lift).
*   **Engagement:** If average session duration drops by **>5%** compared to the control, indicating user fatigue.

*Note: This recommendation adheres to experiment rigor by refusing to declare a "win" while critical guardrail metrics (churn, exact costs) remain unverified.*
