**Positioning Recommendation for ClassPulse**

**Hypothesis:** For **mid-sized K‑12 districts (2,000–5,000 teachers) facing urgent teacher burnout**, ClassPulse is a **core LMS module** that **maximizes daily teacher engagement**; unlike **EduTrack's lower-cost but lower-adoption model**, it **delivers proven workflow stickiness (1.8 daily logins vs. industry churn)**.

**Headline Message:** "Stop paying for licenses teachers don't use. ClassPulse drives the daily habits that make LMS investments stick."

**Why this segment:** Your 28 wins suggest traction, but the Riverbend pilot data (1.8 logins) is your only hard evidence of value. Competing on price against EduTrack or features against BrightPath without AI is a loss. Competing on *actual usage* leverages your observed strength: teachers are logging in. The "zero-implementation" claim by ScholarSphere is an unverified risk; do not position against it until you have pricing data.

**How This Could Fail (Risks & Assumptions):**
*   **The "Engagement Trap" Assumption:** We assume 1.8 logins equals *value*. If teachers are logging in only to complain or navigate poor UX, high frequency is a negative signal, not a differentiator. *Hidden Cost:* High support volume masking as engagement.
*   **Budget Variance Blindness:** The $150k budget figure is a single data point. If the variance is high and your price exceeds the median for mid-sized districts, the "lower license fee" competitor (EduTrack) wins regardless of your engagement metrics.
*   **Feature Gap:** Without AI analytics, you cannot compete with BrightPath on "insights." If the market shifts to demand predictive analytics within 12 months, this positioning becomes obsolete.

**Kill Metric (Falsification Test):**
**Stop or pivot if:** After a 3-month controlled rollout in a new mid-sized district, **active daily login rates fall below 1.2 per teacher** (a ~33% drop from Riverbend) OR **renewal intent scores drop below 6/10** despite hitting login targets. This proves that frequency does not correlate with perceived value or retention.
