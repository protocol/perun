**Positioning Recommendation (Hypothesis to Validate)**

*   **Target Segment:** High-intent individual donors triggered by immediate hunger metrics, specifically those who have previously donated based on tangible meal counts rather than organizational overhead.
*   **Value Proposition:** Greater Valley Food Bank converts every $10 directly into one family fed for a month, delivering a 22% higher meal output than baseline operations without the friction of third-party platform fees.
*   **Category:** Direct-impact donor engagement program.
*   **Current Alternative:** Third-party platforms like "MealMatch" (high conversion but 12% fee erosion) or "FoodShare" (no fee but low 5% conversion), or the donor's current habit of giving to general funds with opaque impact.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike platforms that sacrifice donation value to fees or suffer from low conversion, our pilot proves a superior net-impact model: $527k in commitments generated 3,210 meals/week internally, retaining 100% of the donation value for food rather than platform costs.
*   **Headline Message:** "Your $10 Feeds a Family for a Month: 22% More Meals, Zero Platform Fees."

**Falsification Test**
Recruit 500 new prospects matching the target segment and expose them to this headline. If the conversion rate drops below **50%** (our current win-rate baseline) or if the cost-per-acquired-dollar exceeds the 12% fee equivalent of "MealMatch" within the next quarter, the hypothesis that "donors prioritize meal count over platform convenience" is false, and we must pivot to a partnership model or overhead-transparency message.

**Primary Failure Modes & Pivot Triggers**
1.  **Donor Churn Unknown:** If the average annual churn rate of new donors after six months exceeds **40%** (industry high-risk threshold), the "volume" strategy fails due to acquisition costs; pivot to retention-focused messaging.
2.  **Hidden Staff Costs:** If the internal labor cost to process these direct donations exceeds the 12% fee saved by avoiding "MealMatch," the net benefit vanishes; pivot to automation or platform adoption.
3.  **Competitor Claim Verification:** If "MealMatch's" claimed 30% higher conversion is verified in an A/B test against our direct pitch, our "higher net impact" argument loses to their "higher total volume" reality; pivot to hybrid usage.

*Note: This positioning is a hypothesis based on your pilot data and must be validated against the unknown churn rate before full scaling. Trademark clearance for "MealMatch" and "FoodShare" comparisons should be reviewed by legal counsel before public use.*
