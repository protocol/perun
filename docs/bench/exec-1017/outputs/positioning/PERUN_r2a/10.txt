**Positioning Brief: Dispatch-Automation Module**

**Recommendation:** Target mid-tier freight brokers processing >1,000 shipments/month who are currently losing margin to manual paperwork.
**Headline:** "Cut dispatch paperwork by 30% in two weeks, or stay stuck in spreadsheets."
**Value Proposition:** For brokers drowning in administrative overhead, our module automates dispatch documentation to recover 30% of lost processing time. Unlike VersaTrack, CargoFlow, or LoadLogic, which require lengthy deployments, we deliver measurable paperwork reduction within a 14-day implementation window.
**Differentiation:**
*   **Vs. VersaTrack/CargoFlow:** We target higher-volume operators (1,200 vs. 950/1,050 shipments/month) where manual friction costs more.
*   **Speed to Value:** 2-week implementation vs. industry-standard months (based on 15 pilot signals).
*   **Proof Point:** 42 of 87 interviewed brokers explicitly committed to switching for a 30% paperwork drop.

**How This Could Fail:**
1.  **Integration Friction:** The assumed 2-week implementation hits hidden technical debt in broker legacy systems, stretching timelines and killing the "speed" differentiator.
2.  **Volume Mismatch:** The "mid-tier" definition is too broad; brokers under 1,000 shipments may not feel enough pain to justify switching costs despite the promise.
3.  **Competitor Response:** VersaTrack or CargoFlow launches a "lite" rapid-deployment feature, neutralizing our time-to-value advantage before we scale.

**Kill Metric:**
If **<20% of pilot users** achieve the 30% paperwork reduction within 60 days of go-live, or if **conversion from trial to paid is <15%** after 6 months, stop the current positioning and pivot to a service-led model or different segment.
