**Positioning Hypothesis for "HIIT Fusion"**

**Target Segment:** Time-constrained corporate professionals seeking efficient, high-impact workouts who currently struggle to fit standard 60-minute classes into packed schedules.

**Headline Message:** "HIIT Fusion: The 30-minute corporate wellness solution that delivers maximum metabolic impact without clearing your calendar."

**Differentiation:** Unlike FlexFit and CorePulse, which prioritize broad class variety and traditional hour-long formats, HIIT Fusion offers a specialized, time-compressed protocol designed specifically for the corporate lunch-break window, backed by our studio's proven 3.2 visits/week member engagement rate.

**Top Three Failure Risks:**
1.  **Price Sensitivity Mismatch:** The assumption that corporate clients value flexibility over price may be incorrect; if the $25/class equivalent exceeds their perceived value for a shorter session, adoption will stall.
2.  **Competitor Reaction:** FlexFit's unreleased "FlexPass" could undercut our pricing or offer superior scheduling flexibility, neutralizing our primary differentiator before launch.
3.  **Engagement Drop-off:** Members accustomed to longer sessions may perceive the 30-minute format as insufficient value, leading to lower retention despite the convenience.

**Kill Metric:** Halt or pivot the program if corporate sign-ups are fewer than 5 in the first quarter OR if member churn for this specific class exceeds 15% within the first 60 days.
