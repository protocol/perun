**Positioning Recommendation for ReadNow**

**Headline Message:** "ReadNow delivers the books you want, instantly, without leaving the library system you already trust."

**Target Patron Segment:** Active adult library users (ages 25–54) who currently abandon digital borrowing due to friction in regional systems or pay for commercial convenience.

**Value Proposition:** For patrons who need immediate access to popular titles, ReadNow is the library's native on-demand platform that integrates seamlessly with existing accounts; unlike LibConnect's delayed inter-library loans or BookStream's separate subscription fees, ReadNow offers instant fulfillment at no extra cost using your current library card.

**Differentiation:**
*   **Vs. LibConnect:** LibConnect requires waiting for transfers between counties. ReadNow provides instant access because it is hosted directly within Alder County's infrastructure. *Proof:* Pilot data shows 14 of 20 trial users switched from LibConnect specifically to avoid wait times.
*   **Vs. BookStream:** BookStream charges monthly fees and requires a separate login. ReadNow is free and uses existing credentials. *Proof:* While 6 pilot users stayed with BookStream (likely for specific title exclusivity), the 5% month-over-month growth in general digital checkouts suggests high sensitivity to cost and friction among the broader base.

**How This Could Fail:**
1.  **Content Gap:** If the "instant" catalog lacks the specific bestsellers that drive the 6 BookStream holdouts, the convenience benefit vanishes.
2.  **Discovery Friction:** If patrons cannot find the ReadNow integration within the existing catalog easily, adoption will stall despite the value prop.
3.  **Misidentified Barrier:** If the 6 BookStream users represent a high-value segment whose needs (e.g., specific audiobooks) ReadNow cannot meet, focusing solely on "convenience" may ignore a critical feature deficit.

**Kill Metric (Stop/Pivot Trigger):**
If fewer than **800 unique patrons** (approx. 25% of the current 3,200 monthly digital checkout users) activate ReadNow within the first quarter, pause the $150,000 campaign. This threshold indicates that "instant access" is not the primary barrier for your specific audience, requiring a pivot to content acquisition or a different value angle before further spend.
