**Positioning Recommendation (Hypothesis to Validate)**

*   **Target Segment:** Maintenance directors at mid-size metal fabricators currently managing unplanned downtime via reactive repairs or legacy on-prem hardware.
*   **Value Proposition:** A subscription-based vibration monitoring service that delivers enterprise-grade predictive insights without the heavy upfront capital expenditure of legacy systems.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike SteelGuard's $6,500 upfront hardware trap or TitanPulse's premium-priced AI bundle, Ferrous Sensing offers a low-barrier, pure-play monitoring subscription at $500/month, aligning cost directly with operational uptime rather than asset ownership.
*   **Headline:** "Stop buying hardware to predict failure: Enterprise vibration monitoring for fabricators, scaled by subscription."

**Three Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** The assumption that every customer buys exactly one sensor proves false; if multi-sensor deployments drive the average price down or support costs up, the path to $1.2M ARR requires significantly more than 250 customers.
2.  **Feature-Price Mismatch:** Pilots convert poorly because the $500 tier lacks the "predictive AI" capabilities included in TitanPulse's $800 offer, causing buyers to perceive the lower price as lower value rather than better efficiency.
3.  **Legacy Inertia:** Fabricators prefer the known CAPEX model of SteelGuard (owning the asset) over a recurring OPEX commitment, viewing the subscription as a perpetual cost with no residual asset value.

**Abandon/Pivot Metric**
If the pilot-to-paid conversion rate is **below 40%** within 90 days of the pricing announcement (requiring >625 total pilots to hit the 250-customer target given the 12-month timeline), abandon the current pricing/segmentation and pivot to a tiered model or direct sales approach.
