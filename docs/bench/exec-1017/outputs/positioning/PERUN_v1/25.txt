**Headline Positioning Statement**
"For mid-size automotive and food processing plant managers struggling to justify labor costs to CFOs, Peoplegrid is the HR analytics platform that delivers verified ROI within 12 months; unlike generic dashboards like Factora or LaborPulse, it connects workforce data directly to production outcomes to secure budget approval faster."

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1. **The "Generic Dashboard" Trap:** If Factora or InsightForge releases a specific "production-outcome" module before we scale, our differentiator evaporates, reducing us to a commodity where price becomes the only lever.
2. **The CFO Skepticism Gap:** If our "verified ROI" claims cannot be substantiated with case studies from the 5 pilot factories that match the CFO's specific 12-month payback requirement, the sales cycle will stall regardless of plant manager enthusiasm.
3. **Price Elasticity Shock:** Since price sensitivity is unquantified, a standard enterprise price increase could disproportionately crush conversion in the mid-market segment, revealing that our current win rate is artificially inflated by underpricing rather than product fit.

**Abandon/Revise Metric**
Revise or abandon this positioning if **new logo churn exceeds 15% within the first 9 months** or if **trial-to-paid conversion drops below 25%** during a controlled 10% price increase test, indicating the perceived value does not support the required ROI timeline or price point.

*Note: The 62% segment coverage claim based on 5 pilots requires validation against total market size before presenting as fact. Ensure the "production outcomes" claim is backed by specific data points from the 1,200 active users.*
