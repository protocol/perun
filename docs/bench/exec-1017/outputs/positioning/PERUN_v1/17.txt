**To:** Ravi Kaur
**Subject:** Positioning Recommendation for "Ethereal Echo"

**Recommendation:**
Position "Ethereal Echo" as the **narrative-first alternative** for players who have exhausted the visual spectacle of "Skyward Quest" but crave deeper emotional stakes. Unlike BrightStar's benchmark, which prioritizes graphical fidelity, "Ethereal Echo" delivers a complex, branching story engine where player choices permanently alter the world state, leveraging our proven "Moth Lantern" audience's desire for depth over polish. We will validate this by targeting the 34 narrative-focused Discord users interviewed, offering early access in exchange for feedback on story branching rather than graphics.

**Key Failure Modes:**
1.  **Misreading the Competitor:** Assuming "Skyward Quest" lacks story depth when it may simply market graphics better; if their narrative is actually superior, our differentiator collapses.
2.  **Underestimating Engine Costs:** The hypothesis relies on repurposing 60% of art assets, but if the new physics engine licensing fee exceeds our remaining cash runway (given the 45% churn impact on revenue), development halts.
3.  **Over-relying on Churn Data:** Applying "Moth Lantern's" 45% churn rate to a new genre without accounting for the different retention curves of narrative-heavy games could lead to inaccurate revenue projections.

**Kill/Pivot Metric:**
Scrap this specific "narrative-depth" positioning if **beta sign-ups from the narrative Discord segment remain under 2,500 by June 2025**. This threshold indicates that even our most story-hungry segment does not perceive the value proposition as distinct enough from the market leader to switch.

*Note: The physics engine licensing fee must be secured before full production; this plan assumes the cost is viable but treats it as the primary financial risk. All competitor claims regarding "Skyward Quest" must be verified through direct gameplay analysis, not second-hand reports.*
