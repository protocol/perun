*   **Headline:** "Cut patient wait times by 15% and boost same-day fill rates without replacing your Epic core."

1.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** The vendor's "under $20k" Epic integration estimate is inaccurate, eroding the 10% discount margin and extending payback beyond the CFO's threshold.
2.  **Workflow Friction:** Clinicians reject the new coordination layer if it adds clicks to their existing Epic workflow, causing adoption to stall despite administrative benefits.
3.  **Competitor Price War:** MedFlow or HealthQueue undercuts our effective price point by bundling scheduling modules into broader contract renewals, making our standalone value proposition financially inferior.

**Kill/Pivot Metrics**
*   **Stop/Pivot Condition A (Cost):** If the final signed quote for Epic integration exceeds **$28,000** (40% above the vendor's high-end estimate), halt deployment to re-evaluate the technical architecture.
