**Positioning Recommendation: "The Executive Efficiency Block"**

*   **Target Segment:** Corporate professionals and HR decision-makers who need guaranteed, time-boxed wellness solutions that fit strictly within lunch breaks or pre-work windows, rather than open-gym access.
*   **Headline Message:** "HIIT Fusion: The 30-minute high-intensity protocol designed for corporate schedules—maximizing health ROI without disrupting your workday."
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike FlexFit's likely broad "FlexPass" model (assumed to prioritize volume and open access) and CorePulse's general studio approach, Tempo Studios offers a **curated, schedule-guaranteed cohort experience**. We sell predictable outcomes and time efficiency, not just class access. Our differentiation is the "finished-by-30-minutes" promise backed by our existing 3.2 visits/week engagement data, positioning us as the productivity tool for wellness, not just a gym.

**Three Biggest Failure Risks**
1.  **Price Sensitivity Mismatch:** The assumption that corporate clients value flexibility over price may be false; if FlexFit's "FlexPass" undercuts us significantly while offering "good enough" scheduling, our premium positioning will fail to convert the 20 members who preferred bundles.
2.  **Capacity vs. Demand:** Limiting classes to strict 30-minute windows to guarantee the "efficiency" promise may cap total revenue if the studio cannot turnover rooms fast enough to match the 120,000 visit volume needed to sustain the $1.8M baseline.
3.  **Corporate Sales Cycle Lag:** Relying on corporate deals (where you won 8/14 in Q3) may be too slow for a launch in two weeks; if B2B sign-ups stall, the B2C price point ($25/class) may be too high for the remaining undecided prospects to fill the gap.

**Kill Metrics (Stop or Pivot Conditions)**
*   **Corporate Sign-ups:** Fewer than **5 new corporate contracts** signed by December 31 (end of Q4).
*   **Retention/Churn:** Class attendance drop-off greater than **20%** after the first month (indicating the "efficiency" hook isn't retaining users).
*   **Bundle Conversion:** Less than **30%** of new individual sign-ups converting to the monthly bundle within 45 days, proving the single-class price barrier is too high.
