**Positioning Recommendation for Greenfurrow Leasing**
**Target Segment:** Mid-scale row-crop farmers in the Midwest facing seasonal cash-flow gaps during equipment upgrades.
**Value Proposition:** Flexible, short-term equipment leasing that aligns payments with harvest cycles, preserving operating capital without long-term debt.
**Differentiation:** Unlike CropCycle Lease and AgriFlex, which push standard 12-month terms, Greenfurrow offers sub-6-month contracts tailored to specific growing seasons.
**Headline:** "Keep Your Capital in the Field: Equipment Leasing That Matches Your Harvest Cycle."

**Three Main Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Block:** The Iowa regulatory cap effective 1 Jan 2026 limits contracts to 3 months, rendering the current 4.3-month average model non-compliant and unprofitable for many crops.
2.  **Invalid Core Assumption:** Farmers prioritize outright ownership or long-term stability over cash-flow flexibility, making the short-term value prop irrelevant despite the financial logic.
3.  **Competitor Response:** CropCycle Lease or AgriFlex rapidly introduce comparable short-term products, eroding the 68% and 54% win rates before Greenfurrow can secure market dominance.

**Pivot Metric**
Stop or pivot if new lease sign-ups in Iowa drop below 40% of the projected run-rate within 30 days of the January 2026 regulation taking effect, or if win rates against competitors fall below 50% in two consecutive months post-regulation.

*Note: The 12% YoY growth figure was used after correcting the spreadsheet error; the 120% figure was discarded as a data artifact. This brief treats the "cash-flow priority" as a hypothesis requiring validation against the regulatory change.*
