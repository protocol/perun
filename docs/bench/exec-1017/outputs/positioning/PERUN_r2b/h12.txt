*   **Target Segment:** Elite CrossFit and triathlon athletes currently using PowerPlate or FitFuel who are failing to hit specific macro targets due to generic portioning.
*   **Headline Message:** "The only meal kit engineered for macro-precision, not just convenience—guaranteed to hit your exact protein/carb/fat targets within 2 grams."

**Top Three Failure Risks & Pivot Metrics**

1.  **Price Elasticity Barrier:** Athletes reject the value proposition if the cost exceeds their perceived ROI for precision.
    *   *Pivot Metric:* If conversion drops below 15% for kits priced above $15/serving during the May launch window.
2.  **Convenience Trade-off:** The operational complexity of custom macro packing slows delivery or increases errors, negating the "kit" benefit.
    *   *Pivot Metric:* If order fulfillment error rates exceed 5% or delivery windows slip beyond 48 hours in the first 30 days.
3.  **Retention Gap:** The "precision" novelty wears off if athletes do not perceive a correlating performance increase.
    *   *Pivot Metric:* If the repeat purchase rate falls below 30% in Month 1 (currently 38% in pilot, trailing EnduroEats' 45%).
