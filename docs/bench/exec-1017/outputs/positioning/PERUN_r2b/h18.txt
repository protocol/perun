**Positioning Brief for Maya Patel, CPO**

**Headline Positioning (Hypothesis to Validate):**
"For freelancers currently paying per-signature or high monthly fees on tools like SignFlow, QuickSign, or DocuSeal, Penstroke is the flat-rate e-signature platform that guarantees predictable costs under $5/month; unlike current alternatives, it eliminates per-signature penalties entirely, validated by 45% of interviewed freelancers expressing intent to switch."

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Price-Sensitivity Misread:** The assumption that price is the primary driver is `[ASSUMED]`; freelancers may prioritize specific integrations (e.g., with invoicing software) or legal enforceability over saving a few dollars, rendering the low-price hook ineffective.
2.  **Hidden Switching Costs:** While 54 freelancers expressed interest, the actual friction of migrating existing templates and client workflows from entrenched competitors may be higher than anticipated, causing the 45% expressed interest to collapse during actual onboarding.
3.  **Competitor Reaction:** Incumbents (SignFlow, QuickSign, DocuSeal) could introduce temporary "freelancer tiers" or match the $5 price point, neutralizing Penstroke's sole differentiator before we establish brand loyalty.

**Kill Metric:**
Scrap or pivot this specific "under $5 flat-rate" positioning if **conversion from trial to paid falls below 25% within the first 90 days** post-launch, despite achieving the projected traffic volume. This threshold accounts for the 45% expressed interest while allowing for natural attrition; falling below it indicates the value proposition does not overcome the status quo.
