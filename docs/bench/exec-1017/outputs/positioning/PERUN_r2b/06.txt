**Positioning Recommendation (Hypothesis to Validate)**

*   **Target Segment:** Retention-focused individual donors ($50–$250/mo) triggered by seeing a direct, fee-free link between their gift and a specific family's monthly food security.
*   **Value Proposition:** Greater Valley Food Bank converts every $10 directly into one family fed for a month, with zero platform fees diverting funds from the mission.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike "MealMatch" (which charges a 12% fee) or "FoodShare" (which yields low conversion), our direct model maximizes both the dollar impact per gift and the donor's emotional return on investment.
*   **Headline Message:** "Your $10 feeds a family for a month—100% of your gift goes to food, never to fees."

**Falsification Test**
Launch an A/B test in Q2 2024 comparing the "Meals" headline against an "Impact Transparency" headline (focusing on overhead efficiency) across 5,000 lapsed donors. **Kill Metric:** If the "Meals" variant yields a conversion rate below 50% of the control group's baseline or if donor churn exceeds 15% within the first six months, the hypothesis that "donors care primarily about meal count" is false, and we must pivot to an efficiency-centric message.

**Primary Failure Modes & Pivot Triggers**
1.  **Mis-estimated Donor Churn:** If the unknown annual churn rate for new donors exceeds 40% after six months, the acquisition cost becomes unsustainable regardless of conversion rates. *Pivot:* Shift messaging to long-term community stability rather than immediate meal counts.
2.  **Hidden Staff Costs:** If the administrative burden of manually tracking "family/month" units increases operational costs by more than 8%, the "zero fee" claim erodes net impact. *Pivot:* Introduce a transparent optional "ops support" add-on or revise the value prop to include necessary infrastructure.
3.  **Competitor Fee Structure Changes:** If MealMatch lowers fees to <5% or FoodShare improves conversion to >15%, our differentiation narrows. *Pivot:* Emphasize local control and specific regional outcomes over generic conversion metrics.

**Note:** This positioning treats your pilot data as a strong signal but remains a hypothesis until validated against the unknown churn rate. The "100% to food" claim must be audited internally to ensure no indirect costs violate this promise before public use.
