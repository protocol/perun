**Positioning Recommendation**

**Hypothesis:** For independent restaurant owners (10–30 seats) who lose critical operational time to manual payroll administration, Acme Payroll is a dedicated compliance and automation platform that reduces payroll processing time by 50%; unlike generalist HR suites or spreadsheet routines, it delivers restaurant-specific tax filings and tip reporting out-of-the-box.

**Recommended Tagline Strategy:** Do **not** launch with "Profit-First Payroll." Your evidence indicates 68% of buyers prioritize "time saved" over financial optimization. A "Profit-First" message targets a secondary gain (42% accuracy/cash-flow concern) rather than the primary pain (time). Position instead on **"Reclaim Your Floor Time"** or **"Payroll Done in Minutes, Not Hours."** Use the profit angle only as a supporting pillar once the time-saving promise is validated.

**Three Ways This Could Fail**
1.  **Misaligned Value Prop:** If the product does not tangibly reduce admin time by the claimed margin, the core hypothesis collapses because the primary buyer motivation (time) remains unaddressed.
2.  **Segment Mismatch:** If owners with <10 seats prove too price-sensitive for the estimated $2,500 integration support cost, the unit economics for the target segment will fail, requiring a pivot to larger venues or a self-serve model.
3.  **Competitive Displacement:** If BistroPay or similar competitors match your specific restaurant features while leveraging their larger installed base to undercut pricing, your differentiation becomes negligible.

**Kill Metric / Pivot Signal**
Maya should stop or pivot this approach if, in a controlled message test of 50 qualified leads, fewer than **20%** respond to the "Time-Saving" headline with a request for a demo, OR if discovery calls reveal that less than **50%** of prospects identify "time spent on payroll" as their top-3 operational headaches. If the data shows "cash flow" rising above "time" as the primary trigger in future interviews, pivot the messaging to "Profit-First."

*Note: The 10% budget allocation and $2,500 integration cost are marked as [ASSUMED] and must be validated against actual willingness-to-pay data before finalizing pricing.*
