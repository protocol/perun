**Positioning Recommendation for CEO Maya Patel**

**Hypothesis:** For independent dental practices (≤5 chairs) struggling with high upfront capital and maintenance downtime, Brightside Portable Sterilizer 2.0 is a sterilization solution that delivers enterprise-level throughput via a predictable per-use cost model. Unlike ApexClean, PrismSterile, and NovaGuard, which require 12–15% higher capital investment and ongoing maintenance, Brightside eliminates upfront risk with a "zero-maintenance, pay-per-cycle" approach.

*Rationale:* This commits to the "predictable per-use cost" differentiator, directly attacking the capital sensitivity of small practices identified in your pilot (68 "must-have" responses). It frames competitors' speed claims as secondary to their cost/maintenance burden. Note: The revenue calculation provided ($2.4M from 150 cycles/day at $0.02) conflicts with the $1,800 unit price; this brief assumes the *value hypothesis* relies on the per-use pricing model being the primary disruptor, pending financial reconciliation.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Competitor Price War:** If ApexClean's Q1 2025 next-gen model drops its upfront cost by >15% or introduces a similar subscription model, our primary differentiator evaporates, forcing a pivot to service speed or reliability metrics we haven't proven superior.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** If the actual cost to service/maintain a unit under the "zero-maintenance" promise exceeds the $0.02/cycle revenue (given the 150 cycles/day volume), the model becomes unsustainable, turning high adoption into high loss.
3.  **Regulatory/Trust Barrier:** If "per-use billing" triggers complex insurance billing friction or regulatory scrutiny regarding sterilization validation that competitors avoid with standard upfront sales, adoption will stall regardless of cost benefits.

**Kill/Pivot Metric**
**Stop or Pivot if:** The conversion rate of pilot "must-have" respondents (68 clinics) to paid long-term contracts falls below **40%** within 90 days of full launch, OR if the average cost-per-cycle (including maintenance/logistics) exceeds **$0.015** for two consecutive months.

*Note:* The 10% market switch assumption is a hypothesis to validate; do not bake this into revenue forecasts until the pilot conversion data confirms it. The conflicting pricing data ($0.02 vs $1,800) must be resolved before finalizing the financial model for the board.
