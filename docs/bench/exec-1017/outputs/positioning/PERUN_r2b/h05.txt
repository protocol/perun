**Positioning Recommendation for Jenna**

**Target Segment:** Office managers at 100–200 seat companies who are currently manually reconciling inconsistent coffee bills and vendor contracts.
**Value Proposition:** Bluebird Roasters provides predictable, premium office coffee with transparent all-in pricing, eliminating hidden maintenance fees and administrative friction.
**Differentiation:** Unlike Crestline Brew ($0.28/cup) and GreenBean Supply ($0.32/cup), which layer quarterly maintenance fees ($1,200) on top of usage costs, Bluebird offers a single bundled rate that guarantees cost certainty and reduces total cost of ownership by ~15% for average usage patterns.
**Headline Message:** "One price per cup. Zero hidden fees. Finally, coffee billing that makes sense."

*Note: This positioning treats "cost certainty" as the primary driver based on your revenue/maintenance data, explicitly marking the "70% sustainability" assumption as unverified due to the small sample size (n=12).*

**Three Biggest Risks of Failure**
1.  **The Sustainability Miscalculation:** If the untested assumption holds true and 70% of decision-makers actually prioritize sustainability over cost transparency, our price-focused message will fail to resonate with the majority of the market, rendering our primary differentiator irrelevant.
2.  **Price Elasticity Misjudgment:** If office managers are highly sensitive to the base per-cup price (before fees) rather than the total cost of ownership, the bundled rate required to cover maintenance may appear sticker-shock high compared to competitors' lower advertised per-cup rates, causing immediate disqualification in early conversations.
3.  **Competitor Reaction:** If Crestline or GreenBean quickly match our model by absorbing their maintenance fees into a slightly higher per-cup rate, our differentiation evaporates, forcing a competition purely on brand or taste where we have not yet established superiority.

**Pivot or Stop Metric**
Stop or pivot this strategy if, in the next 20 sales conversations, **fewer than 30% of prospects** identify "billing transparency" or "hidden fees" as a top-3 pain point when asked about their current vendor. This threshold indicates that cost certainty is not a strong enough lever to drive switching behavior, requiring a return to discovery to validate the true primary driver (e.g., sustainability or taste).
