**Positioning Recommendation for Evermore Planner**

**Hypothesis to Validate:** For independent wedding planners managing 5+ clients monthly who struggle with fragmented vendor contracts and timeline updates, Evermore Planner is the all-in-one operations hub that centralizes communication and documentation; unlike DiamondDay's "Premium Suite" (pricing unknown) and CelebrationSync, it offers native, no-cost integration with vendor-match CRMs to eliminate double-entry.

**Target Segment:** Independent wedding planners actively managing five or more client projects per month.
**Value Proposition:** Reduce administrative overhead by unifying timelines, contracts, and client communications in a single workflow, leveraging free API integrations to sync vendor data without extra cost.
**Differentiation:**
*   **Vs. DiamondDay:** While DiamondDay's 2025 pricing is currently unknown, Evermore commits to a transparent cost structure with zero-fee essential integrations, avoiding hidden tier gates for CRM connectivity.
*   **Vs. CelebrationSync:** Evermore specifically targets the "5+ projects" power user with deep contract management, whereas competitors often focus on lighter lead generation or simple checklists.
**Headline Message:** "Stop juggling tabs for every wedding. The only planner built to sync your vendors, contracts, and timelines at no extra integration cost."

**Failure Modes:**
1.  **Integration Fragility:** The assumption that the vendor-match CRM's free API tier remains free and stable is unverified; if costs rise or access is restricted, the core differentiation collapses.
2.  **Competitor Pricing Shock:** If DiamondDay's "Premium Suite" launches with aggressive bundling that includes similar integrations at a lower total cost of ownership, our value prop weakens.
3.  **Segment Misalignment:** If planners managing 5+ clients prefer best-of-breed specialized tools over an all-in-one suite, adoption will stall despite the convenience claim.

**Pivot Trigger:**
Stop or pivot if, within the first month of launch, **new sign-ups from the target segment (5+ projects) are fewer than 100** OR if **churn exceeds 5%** among users who attempt the vendor-CRM integration. These metrics indicate either a lack of product-market fit for the specific workflow or a technical failure in the promised integration value.

*Note: The market adoption calculation has been corrected internally to reflect that 30% of 2,500 planners equals 750 customers, not 800. DiamondDay's pricing must be confirmed via direct sales inquiry before finalizing competitive battle cards.*
