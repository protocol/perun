**Positioning Recommendation (Hypothesis to Validate)**

*   **Target Segment:** Small landlords (1–20 units) currently managing ~12 units via spreadsheets or manual routines, specifically those triggered by scaling pains or upcoming compliance changes.
*   **Value Proposition:** Rowan Rentals automates the administrative burden of small-scale property management, prioritizing time recovery over cost minimization.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike generic tools or heavy-duty property management suites, Rowan offers a simplified, flat-fee model ($14.99/mo) that eliminates per-unit transaction friction, tailored specifically for the owner-operator who is not a professional property manager.
*   **Headline:** "Reclaim your weekends: The flat-fee rental manager built for owners, not agencies."

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Price Sensitivity Misread:** The assumption that landlords prioritize time over cost may be false; if the $14.99 flat fee exceeds their perceived value compared to free spreadsheets or lower-cost per-unit models, adoption will stall despite time savings.
2.  **Regulatory Whiplash:** The July 2025 state law change could either render the product mandatory (boosting demand) or impose compliance costs that the current financial model (omitting the $3/unit fee) cannot absorb, forcing a pricing restructuring that alienates early adopters.
3.  **Engagement Gap:** With a pilot DAU of 2.4, if users do not integrate Rowan into daily workflows enough to feel the "time saved" benefit, churn will increase regardless of the pricing model.

**Kill Metric / Pivot Signal**
Stop or pivot if, in a 60-day post-launch window, the **daily active user rate per landlord drops below 1.5** or if **churn exceeds 8% monthly** among the pilot cohort. These thresholds indicate the product is failing to become a habitual time-saver, invalidating the core value hypothesis before the regulatory deadline influences the market.

*Note: This positioning treats the "time over cost" preference and the impact of the 2025 law as assumptions requiring validation through the proposed kill metrics. The DAU comparison to LandLordPro is noted as directional only due to differing user bases.*
