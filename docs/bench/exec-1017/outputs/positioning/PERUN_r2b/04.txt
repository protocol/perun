**Positioning Recommendation for Anchor Mutual Brokers**

*   **Target Segment:** Small-business owners currently abandoning quotes due to wait times (the 67% prioritizing speed), specifically those lost to Competitor A.
*   **Value Proposition:** Instant, transparent insurance quotes without agent bottlenecks, converting interest to coverage in under three minutes.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike Competitor A's speed-only approach or Competitor B's fee-focused model, we combine sub-3-minute delivery with full price transparency, addressing the top two purchase drivers simultaneously.
*   **Headline Message:** "Get your bound quote in under 3 minutes—price transparent, no agent wait."

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**

1.  **Cannibalization Churn:** The assumed 30% adoption by existing brokered clients triggers high churn if those clients value human advice over speed, eroding the $540k projected gain.
2.  **Speed vs. Accuracy Trade-off:** Delivering quotes in under 3 minutes leads to pricing errors or coverage gaps, damaging trust and increasing loss ratios despite high conversion.
3.  **Misaligned Incentives:** Internal brokers sabotage or ignore the tool to protect commissions, preventing the 15% lead conversion target from being realized.

**Kill Metric (Pivot Trigger)**

Stop or pivot if, after 90 days, the **net revenue impact** (new self-serve premiums minus lost traditional brokered premiums) is negative, or if the churn rate among existing clients who switch to self-serve exceeds 10% within the first six months.

*Note: This position treats the 15% conversion and 30% adoption rates as hypotheses to validate against real Q2 data, not guaranteed outcomes. The $120k build cost is sunk; focus the board discussion on the marginal cost of the $15k/month hosting versus the net new premium captured.*
