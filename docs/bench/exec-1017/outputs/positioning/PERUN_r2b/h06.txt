**Positioning Recommendation**

*   **Target Segment:** HR Heads in mid-market enterprises (500–2,000 employees) facing mandatory compliance training deadlines within 6 months.
*   **Value Proposition:** Guarantee measurable language proficiency ROI within six months or extend licensing at no cost, turning language training from a cost center into a verified compliance asset.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike LinguaPro and PolyGlot, which sell "engagement hours," Lumen Learning sells "verified proficiency outcomes" backed by our 70% win rate in head-to-head evaluations where buyers prioritized guaranteed results over feature breadth.
*   **Headline Message:** "Stop paying for hours logged. Start paying for proficiency proven."

**Three Ways This Positioning Could Fail**

1.  **Proof Gap:** If we cannot operationally define and measure "proficiency" to the specific satisfaction of a skeptical CFO within the first 90 days, the guarantee becomes a liability rather than a differentiator.
2.  **Budget Misalignment:** If the corporate training budget shifts from "compliance/risk" to "employee wellness/perk" spending, our rigid ROI focus may lose to competitors offering broader, softer lifestyle language benefits.
3.  **Competitor Response:** If LinguaPro or PolyGlot immediately launch their own outcome-based guarantees or undercut pricing specifically for the compliance niche, our primary differentiator dissolves without a secondary moat.

**Pivot Trigger (Kill Metric)**

Scrap this approach if, in the next 45 days, fewer than **3 out of 10** qualified prospects in the target segment cite "guaranteed outcome" or "risk reversal" as a top-3 decision factor during discovery calls, indicating the market prefers feature richness or brand safety over our proposed ROI guarantee.
