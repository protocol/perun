**Positioning Recommendation: "The Corporate-Ready HIIT Protocol"**

*   **Target Segment:** Mid-market corporate wellness buyers and time-constrained professionals who need guaranteed schedule adherence, not just open gym access.
*   **Headline Message:** "HIIT Fusion: The only high-intensity program engineered for corporate schedules, delivering consistent energy peaks in 45 minutes or less."
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike **FlexFit** (whose broad "FlexPass" model risks diluting class availability) and **CorePulse** (which focuses on general fitness volume), Tempo Studios guarantees **schedule reliability** and **outcome consistency**. We trade infinite flexibility for guaranteed slots and measured intensity, solving the corporate buyer's fear of low utilization due to scheduling conflicts.

**Three Biggest Failure Risks**
1.  **Price Sensitivity Mismatch:** Your assumption that corporates value flexibility over price may be false; if FlexFit undercuts significantly, the "guaranteed slot" premium may not justify the cost.
2.  **Capacity Bottlenecks:** With 3.2 visits/member/week already, adding a rigid corporate block could alienate existing high-frequency members if studio capacity isn't expanded, causing churn in the base business.
3.  **Value Perception Gap:** If the "energy peak" outcome isn't immediately felt within the first three classes, the $25/class perceived value will collapse against cheaper, self-guided alternatives.

**Kill Metric (Stop/Change Course)**
Stop or pivot this positioning if **corporate sign-ups are fewer than 5 in the first quarter** OR if **existing member churn exceeds 8%** (not 15%, which is too late given your current volume) within 60 days of launch.

***

*Note: Your revenue math ($1.8M / 120k visits) implies an average yield of $15/visit, contradicting the $25 class price; verify if the $25 is a drop-in rate while most revenue comes from lower-cost bundles. Do not finalize pricing strategy until FlexFit's "FlexPass" details are public.*
