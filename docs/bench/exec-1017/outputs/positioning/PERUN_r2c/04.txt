**Positioning Recommendation**

*   **Target Segment:** Small-business owners currently abandoning quotes due to wait times (the 67% prioritizing speed), specifically those lost to Competitor A in Q1.
*   **Value Proposition:** Immediate, transparent insurance quotes without agent bottlenecks, converting interest to coverage in under three minutes.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike Competitor A (fast but opaque) or traditional broker models (transparent but slow), Anchor Mutual delivers both speed and fee transparency in a single self-serve flow.
*   **Headline Message:** "Get your bound quote in under 3 minutes—no hidden fees, no agent wait."

**Top Three Failure Modes**

1.  **Cannibalization without Net Gain:** The assumed 30% adoption by existing brokered clients shifts revenue to a lower-margin self-serve channel without acquiring new business, eroding overall profitability given the $135k first-year cost base.
2.  **Speed vs. Accuracy Trade-off:** Achieving the "under 3-minute" promise requires simplifying underwriting logic, leading to higher bind errors or post-quote rejections that damage trust more than slow quotes did.
3.  **Incomplete Transparency:** Marketing "no agent fees" fails if users discover other administrative costs during checkout, causing the 48% seeking transparency to churn at a higher rate than the current manual process.

**Kill Metric (Stop/Pivot Trigger)**

Stop or pivot if, after 90 days, the **net new premium from non-existing clients** is less than **$45,000 per quarter** (representing <10% of the projected $540k annualized run-rate), or if **churn among migrating existing clients** exceeds **5%** due to service dissatisfaction. This threshold ensures the tool covers its monthly hosting costs and contributes meaningfully to the build ROI before scaling adoption.

*Note: Validate the "no agent fees" claim legally before public messaging to avoid regulatory risk. The 15% conversion assumption must be A/B tested against current baseline immediately upon launch.*
