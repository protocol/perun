**Positioning Brief: Dispatch-Automation Module**

**Target Segment:** Mid-tier freight brokers processing 1,000+ monthly shipments who are currently losing margin to manual paperwork inefficiencies.

**Headline:** Cut dispatch paperwork by 30% in two weeks, or don't switch.

**Value Proposition:** For high-volume brokers drowning in administrative overhead, Brightline's dispatch module is the only automation solution guaranteed to reduce paperwork volume by 30% within a 14-day implementation. Unlike VersaTrack, CargoFlow, or LoadLogic, which require lengthy onboarding and offer incremental efficiency gains, we deliver immediate, measurable labor savings backed by a deployment speed that matches your operational urgency.

**Differentiation Strategy:**
*   **VersaTrack:** They handle higher volume (950 vs. our 1,200 avg user baseline) but lack our speed-to-value. We win on implementation time (2 weeks vs. their assumed months).
*   **CargoFlow:** They sit in the middle (1,050 shipments). We differentiate on the specific outcome promise (30% reduction) rather than general capacity.
*   **LoadLogic:** Positioned as the legacy "do nothing" or manual spreadsheet alternative. We win by quantifying the cost of their current manual routine.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Integration Friction:** The unknown "true integration cost" exceeds the 2-week promise, causing churn before value is realized.
2.  **Outcome Gap:** Real-world paperwork reduction falls below the 30% threshold for the average user, invalidating the core promise.
3.  **Market Size Misalignment:** The assumption of 2,500 addressable firms is unverified; if the segment of brokers needing *both* high volume and fast switching is smaller, CAC will spike.

**Kill Metric:**
If **<15% of pilot users achieve the 30% paperwork reduction within 45 days** of go-live, halt general rollout. This threshold accounts for implementation variance while protecting the core value hypothesis. Do not wait 6 months; the 2-week implementation promise means validation must occur within the first quarter of deployment.
