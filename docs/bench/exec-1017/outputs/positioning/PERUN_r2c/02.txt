**Positioning Hypothesis for Quickbuild**
*For engineering leaders in FinTech, HealthTech, and SaaS who lose productivity to pipeline latency, Quickbuild is a CI accelerator that guarantees 30% faster build times; unlike TurboCI and BuildFast, it delivers this speed without requiring a daily 15-minute maintenance window, provided our internal benchmarks hold true in production environments.*

**Three Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Maintenance Overhead:** The "auto-scale" feature's required 15-minute daily maintenance window negates the time saved by faster builds, rendering the net value proposition zero or negative for high-frequency users.
2.  **Inaccurate Speed Claims:** Real-world performance fails to match the internal benchmark of being 30% faster than TurboCI and 20% faster than BuildFast once diverse customer infrastructure variables are introduced.
3.  **Price-to-Value Mismatch:** At $45,000 per seat/year, the cost exceeds the perceived ROI if the actual churn rate indicates customers do not view the speed gain as critical enough to justify the expense compared to cheaper alternatives.

**Pivot or Halt Triggers**
*   **Churn Threshold:** If verified churn exceeds **12%** within the first six months post-launch.
*   **Market Loss:** If we lose more than **5%** of the initial 150 target enterprises to BuildFast specifically due to price or maintenance complaints during the sales cycle.

**Falsification Test (Run Now)**
Conduct a blinded "time-to-value" pilot with 10 target enterprises comparing Quickbuild against their current tool over a 14-day period. Measure **net developer hours saved** (build time reduction minus maintenance overhead). If the net time saved is less than 15% or if pilot participants cite the maintenance window as a blocker in >30% of feedback sessions, the hypothesis is falsified, and the launch should be halted to re-architect the auto-scale feature.

*Note: The $4.7M ARR projection relies on an unverified 70% adoption assumption and should be treated as a separate financial model, not a positioning fact. Trademark clearance for "Quickbuild" relative to competitors must be confirmed by legal counsel before public use.*
