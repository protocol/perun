**Positioning Recommendation: The "Meals-First" Commitment**

*   **Target Segment:** High-intent individual donors triggered by immediate hunger metrics, specifically those who have previously responded to outcome-based appeals rather than organizational storytelling.
*   **Value Proposition:** A donor-engagement program that guarantees maximum meal conversion per dollar by eliminating platform fees and administrative friction, directly translating contributions into weekly food distribution.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike "MealMatch" (which sacrifices 12% of donor impact to platform fees) or "FoodShare" (which suffers from low 5% conversion efficiency), our pilot proves a superior balance: high conversion (68% win rate) with zero fee erosion, resulting in a verified 22% increase in meals delivered.
*   **Headline Message:** "Every $10 feeds one family for a month—no fees, no friction, just meals."

**Falsification Test**
Launch a controlled A/B test in Q2 targeting 500 lapsed donors: if the "Meals-First" message yields a conversion rate **below 50%** or fails to retain **>75%** of new donors after six months (once the unknown churn data is established), the hypothesis that "donors prioritize meal count over overhead transparency" is false, and we must pivot to an overhead-transparency narrative.

**Failure Modes & Pivot Triggers**
1.  **Mis-estimated Donor Churn:** If the unknown annual churn rate for new donors exceeds **40%** within the first year, the acquisition cost becomes unsustainable regardless of win rate. *Pivot:* Shift focus to donor retention education rather than acquisition volume.
2.  **Hidden Staff Costs:** If the operational cost to manage the "no fee" model rises such that the cost-per-meal delivered exceeds the competitor's net impact (after their fees), the efficiency claim collapses. *Pivot:* Reintroduce a transparent, capped administrative line item.
3.  **Competitor Fee Structure Changes:** If "MealMatch" lowers fees below 5% or "FoodShare" doubles conversion via new tech, our differentiation erodes. *Pivot:* Emphasize local control and direct impact verification over pure math.
4.  **Donor Sentiment Shift:** If qualitative feedback indicates donors suspect "no fee" means "low accountability," trust drops. *Pivot:* Integrate third-party audit badges into the core message.

*Note: This positioning relies on your pilot data; the unknown churn rate must be calculated by month 6 to validate long-term viability. Trademark clearance for specific campaign names should be verified by legal counsel before public rollout.*
