**Positioning Recommendation for Anita Patel**

*   **Target Segment:** Solo practitioners currently drafting contracts manually or via generic word processors, specifically those creating 3+ templates weekly.
*   **Value Proposition:** Reclaim 10+ hours weekly by automating contract drafting through a specialized, low-friction workflow designed exclusively for solo legal practices.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike LexDraft and DocuForm, which burden users with complex enterprise features and steep learning curves, Clause & Co delivers a "fewer clicks" experience that reduces template creation time by 60%, validated by our pilot users' average of 3.2 templates/week.
*   **Headline Message:** "The only contract automation built for the solo lawyer: Draft faster, click less, and bill more."

**Three Main Failure Modes**
1.  **Feature Creep vs. Simplicity:** Attempting to match LexDraft's feature breadth to win enterprise-adjacent deals, thereby destroying the "fewer clicks" advantage that drives our 12/5 win ratio.
2.  **Pricing Misalignment:** Assuming the $199/month price point is sustainable without accounting for the incremental cost of a dedicated support specialist, potentially eroding margins if high-touch onboarding becomes necessary.
3.  **Category Confusion:** Positioning as a general "legal tech platform" rather than a specialized "drafting assistant," causing solo practitioners to evaluate us against full-practice management suites instead of direct drafting alternatives.

**Pivot/Stop Metric**
Anita should trigger a stop or pivot if the **Q3 2024 launch tier churn rate exceeds 8% within the first 90 days**, indicating that the "fewer clicks" value proposition fails to retain users once the novelty of the pilot wears off or if support costs force a price increase that violates the "no extra support cost" assumption.
