**Positioning Recommendation for CareSync Scheduler**

*   **Target Segment:** Mid-sized outpatient clinics (50–200 providers) currently using Epic who struggle with same-day access metrics but lack the budget for enterprise-grade workforce management suites.
*   **Headline:** "Cut patient wait times by 15% and boost same-day fill rates without the enterprise price tag or complex implementation."
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike MedFlow and HealthQueue, which position as broad enterprise suites requiring heavy customization, CareSync is a specialized, rapid-deployment layer specifically for Epic-based outpatient scheduling that guarantees measurable access improvements (wait time/fill rate) within 90 days at a price point ~10% below competitors.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** The "under $20k" Epic integration estimate proves inaccurate, eroding the margin advantage needed to undercut MedFlow/HealthQueue.
2.  **Pilot-to-Scale Disconnect:** The 15% wait-time reduction achieved in a controlled pilot fails to replicate in diverse, high-volume clinic environments due to workflow resistance.
3.  **Feature Gap Loss:** Prospects reject the "specialized" pitch, demanding the broader HR/Staffing modules offered by MedFlow, rendering CareSync "too narrow."

**Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Cost Trigger:** Stop sales efforts if the finalized Epic integration quote exceeds **$35,000**, as this eliminates the pricing advantage required to win against HealthQueue.
*   **Adoption Trigger:** Pivot the product strategy if post-launch data shows **same-day fill rate improvements below 4%** (less than half the pilot result) across the first five live sites, indicating the value proposition does not scale.
