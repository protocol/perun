**Positioning Recommendation for Greater Valley Food Bank**

**Target Segment:** High-intent individual donors motivated by tangible, immediate impact rather than institutional prestige.

**Value Proposition:** A donor-engagement program that maximizes food delivery efficiency by directing 100% of contributions to meals, proven to increase weekly meal output by 22% over baseline through a transparent "$10 feeds one family" model.

**Differentiation:** Unlike MealMatch, which sacrifices 12% of donor funds to platform fees, and FoodShare, which suffers from low conversion efficiency (5%), our program delivers superior volume per dollar donated by eliminating intermediary fees and leveraging a high-conversion (68%) narrative focused on family sustenance.

**Headline Message:** "Your $10 feeds a family for a month—no fees, no friction, just 22% more meals on the table."

**Falsification Test:** Launch a controlled A/B test in Q2 targeting 500 lapsed donors; if the cohort exposed to the "family feeding" message does not achieve a conversion rate of at least 55% (a 13% drop from the pilot's 68%) or if the cost per acquired dollar rises above $0.15, the positioning is invalid.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Donor Churn:** The assumption that donors care only about meal count may be wrong; if annual churn exceeds 40% after six months due to lack of administrative storytelling, lifetime value collapses.
2.  **Hidden Operational Costs:** Scaling meal volume by 22% may incur unstaffed logistics costs (storage, distribution) that erode the "no fee" advantage.
3.  **Competitor Response:** MealMatch could lower fees or FoodShare could optimize conversion, narrowing our differentiation gap.

**Pivot Trigger:** Stop or pivot the program immediately if donor conversion drops below 50% in the next quarter or if the average annual churn rate of new donors exceeds 35% after the first six months.

*Note: The missing average annual churn rate is critical; until known, treat the 22% volume increase as a short-term pilot success rather than a sustainable long-term metric.*
*Board Chair Patel should approve the Q2 test but withhold full-scale budget expansion until churn data is validated.*
