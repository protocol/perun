# Meridian Trials Positioning Brief
**Target:** Mid-size biotech firms launching Phase II studies where timeline delays threaten funding rounds or competitive first-to-market advantage.

**Value Proposition:** Meridian Trials guarantees the fastest patient enrollment in the industry, securing your study timelines even when budget flexibility is required. We convert sponsor anxiety over delays into certainty of speed.

**Differentiation:** Unlike Celerity Recruit (market scale) and TrialFlow (low-cost leader), Meridian specializes exclusively in rapid Phase II activation. While competitors optimize for cost-per-patient, we optimize for days-to-first-patient, accepting a premium price ($320 vs. $260) to deliver enrollment speeds that protect your critical path.

**Headline Message:** "Don't let a cheap recruit cost you your launch window. Meridian Trials: The fastest path to Phase II completion."

### Three Biggest Failure Modes
1.  **Speed Promise Breach:** If operational reality cannot consistently beat competitor timelines by a significant margin (e.g., >20% faster), the premium price becomes indefensible, and churn to TrialFlow will spike.
2.  **Misidentified Priority:** If "speed" is a stated preference but not a *budgeted* priority, procurement teams at target biotechs will override scientific leads and force a switch to lower-cost alternatives during contract negotiation.
3.  **Market Compression:** If Celerity Recruit leverages their 15% market share to bundle services or guarantee timelines at a lower effective rate, our niche differentiation collapses under their scale.

### Pivot Trigger
**Stop or pivot if:** The average time from contract signing to first patient enrolled exceeds **45 days** for three consecutive Phase II studies, or if win rates against TrialFlow drop below **40%** specifically due to price objections (indicating the market does not value our speed premium).

*Note: Validate the assumed price elasticity immediately by offering a tiered pilot to three target accounts before finalizing Q3 pricing strategy.*
