**Positioning Recommendation for Ferrous Sensing**

*   **Target Segment:** Mid-size metal fabricators (50–200 employees) operating continuous stamping or cutting lines where unplanned downtime exceeds 4 hours/month.
*   **Value Proposition:** A subscription-based vibration monitoring service that prevents catastrophic motor failure and reduces unplanned downtime by 40% within the first quarter, without requiring capital expenditure or specialized data science staff.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike SteelGuard's $6,500 upfront hardware burden or TitanPulse's complex $800/month AI suite, Ferrous Sensing delivers immediate, actionable anomaly detection at $500/month with zero installation downtime and a 30-day "pay-only-if-it-saves-you-money" guarantee.
*   **Headline:** "Stop Guessing When Your Press Will Break: Enterprise-Grade Vibration Monitoring for Mid-Market Fabricators at Half the Cost."

**Three Primary Failure Modes**

1.  **Pilot-to-Paid Conversion Cliff:** The 12 pilot customers remain on legacy terms or refuse the $500/month recurring fee after experiencing value, proving the perceived ROI does not match the subscription price point.
2.  **Feature-Price Mismatch:** Prospects reject the "basic" anomaly detection in favor of TitanPulse's "predictive AI," viewing Ferrous as a commodity sensor rather than a strategic solution, forcing a price war we cannot win.
3.  **Hardware Reliability at Scale:** As deployment scales from 12 to 250 units, sensor durability or connectivity issues in harsh fabricator environments spike support costs, eroding margins and damaging the brand before ARR targets are met.

**Abandon/Pivot Metric**

If the pilot-to-paid conversion rate is **below 40% (fewer than 5 of the 12 pilots convert)** within 60 days of the pricing announcement, abandon the current $500/month standalone model and pivot to a tiered pricing strategy or a hardware-subsidized contract.
