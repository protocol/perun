**Positioning Recommendation for Rowan Rentals**

*   **Target Segment:** Small-scale landlords (1–20 units) who personally manage their properties and prioritize operational simplicity over enterprise-grade features.
*   **Value Proposition:** A flat-fee digital management tool that eliminates per-unit accounting friction, saving landlords ~10 hours monthly on rent collection and compliance without variable costs.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike LandLordPro, which targets property managers with complex, usage-based pricing, Rowan Rentals offers a predictable $14.99/month flat rate specifically designed for the economics of small portfolios.
*   **Headline:** "Simple, flat-fee rent management for landlords who own the building, not the bureaucracy."

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Whiplash:** The July 2025 state law mandates specific digital receipt features we currently lack, forcing expensive engineering sprints that break our lean cost structure.
2.  **Pricing Misalignment:** Our assumption that landlords value time savings over transaction fees proves false; if competitors undercut us with a "$0 base + $1/unit" model, our $14.99 flat fee becomes a barrier for owners with fewer than 5 units.
3.  **Engagement Ceiling:** The 2.4 DAU indicates the product is merely a utility (used only for rent day); if we cannot increase stickiness through maintenance or communication features, churn will spike once the initial novelty fades.

**Pivot Trigger Metric**
Stop or pivot if **net new logo acquisition drops below 5% month-over-month for two consecutive months post-launch**, or if **churn exceeds 8% in the first 90 days** specifically citing "pricing" or "missing compliance features" as the reason. These signals would indicate either the value proposition (time savings vs. cost) is invalid or the regulatory risk has materialized into a product gap we cannot quickly fill.

*Note: Validate the "time saved" assumption with a follow-up survey to the 18 pilot customers before finalizing the Q3 roadmap.*
