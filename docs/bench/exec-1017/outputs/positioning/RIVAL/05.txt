**Positioning Brief: HIIT Fusion Launch**

**Target Segment:** Time-constrained corporate professionals seeking efficient, high-impact wellness solutions that fit unpredictable schedules.

**Headline Message:** "Maximum intensity, minimum commitment: The HIIT class designed for your calendar, not the other way around."

**Differentiation Strategy:**
While FlexFit competes on broad access and CorePulse on niche community, Tempo Studios wins on **corporate-grade flexibility**. Leverage our Q3 success (8/14 deals won) by positioning HIIT Fusion as the only program with dynamic scheduling that adapts to shifting work demands. Unlike rivals requiring rigid monthly bundles, offer a "Corporate Flex-Bundle" allowing employees to bank credits across any time slot, directly addressing the primary value driver for your 8 successful corporate partners.

**Three Biggest Risks of Failure:**
1.  **Price Sensitivity Mismatch:** If the market prioritizes low-cost bundles over flexibility, the $25/class anchor may suppress individual sign-ups despite corporate interest.
2.  **Competitive Reaction:** If FlexFit's unrevealed "FlexPass" undercuts our flexibility promise with lower pricing, our primary differentiator becomes commoditized.
3.  **Capacity Dilution:** With an existing load of 3.2 visits/member/week, adding a popular new class without expanding hours may degrade the experience for current members, increasing churn.

**Stop/Change Triggers:**
*   **Corporate Sign-ups:** Fewer than 5 new corporate contracts signed in Q4.
*   **Utilization Rate:** Average class occupancy below 60% after the first 30 days.
*   **Cannibalization:** Existing member visit frequency drops below 2.5 visits/week due to schedule crowding.

*Note: Your revenue calculation contains a discrepancy; 120,000 visits at $25 implies $3M, not $1.8M, suggesting significant discounting or bundle revenue that must be factored into pricing strategy.*
