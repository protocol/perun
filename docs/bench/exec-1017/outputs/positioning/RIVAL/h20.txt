**Positioning Recommendation for Atlas Arts Pass**

*   **Target Segment:** Frequent art donors and local cultural enthusiasts (approx. 8–10% of foot traffic) who currently visit multiple institutions and seek status and convenience over the lowest price.
*   **Value Proposition:** A premium all-access membership that eliminates transaction friction and unlocks exclusive donor-level experiences across the city's top museums for a single annual fee.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike CulturePass ($79) and ArtAccess ($85), which compete on basic entry price, Atlas Arts Pass integrates a white-glove onboarding service (justifying the $20 implementation fee) and bundles exclusive behind-the-scenes access that competitors cannot match without raising their base prices.
*   **Headline:** "One Pass, Unlimited Art, Exclusive Access: The Membership for True Patrons."

**Three Main Ways This Could Fail**
1.  **Price Sensitivity Misread:** The target segment may view the $20 implementation fee as a hidden cost rather than a service value, causing them to default to the cheaper CulturePass option.
2.  **Corporate Bulk Stagnation:** If corporate partners do not purchase bulk passes as anticipated, the reliance on individual high-value subscribers may not reach the $1.5M revenue projection given the 250,000 foot traffic cap.
3.  **Feature Dilution:** If museums fail to deliver the promised "exclusive experiences," the premium pricing becomes indefensible against lower-cost competitors offering identical basic entry.

**Stop/Change Metric**
If the conversion rate from trial (or inquiry) to paid membership drops below **3.5%** within the first 60 days of the Q4 launch, pause the premium positioning and re-evaluate the necessity of the implementation fee.

*Note: The current revenue math implies 12,000 passes at $99 equals $1.188M; reaching the $1.5M projection requires either selling ~15,152 passes or capturing the missing revenue through the implementation fees and corporate bulk sales not yet quantified.*
*Ensure the $20 implementation fee is clearly communicated as a one-time setup cost for family accounts or corporate integrations to avoid sticker shock at checkout.*
