**Positioning Brief: Stride Kitchen "Peak Performance"**

**Target Segment:** Elite endurance and functional fitness athletes (CrossFit Games competitors, Ironman triathletes) currently manually tracking macros or using generic "healthy" meal services that lack precision.

**Headline Message:** "Fuel your PRs, not just your hunger: The only meal kit engineered with macro-precision for elite performance."

**Key Differentiator:** Unlike PowerPlate and FitFuel, which prioritize convenience and general wellness, and EnduroEats, which targets broad endurance, Peak Performance delivers clinically validated macro-ratios tailored to high-intensity training cycles, turning meal prep from a guessing game into a competitive advantage.

**Top 3 Failure Modes:**
1.  **Price Sensitivity:** The assumption that elites prioritize precision over cost proves false if the >$15/serving price point causes acquisition costs to skyrocket without corresponding lifetime value.
2.  **Convenience Trade-off:** Athletes may find the "precision" requires too much customization effort, causing them to revert to the simpler (albeit less precise) interfaces of PowerPlate or FitFuel.
3.  **Retention Gap:** The 38% repeat rate lags behind EnduroEats (45%) because the product solves a "nice-to-have" optimization problem rather than a daily "must-have" hunger problem, leading to churn after the initial novelty.

**Pivot/Scrap Metric:** If the repeat purchase rate drops below **30%** within the first 60 days of the full launch, or if customer acquisition cost (CAC) exceeds **$120** due to price resistance, we must pivot to a lower-price "Pro Lite" tier or scrap the elite-only focus.

*Note: This brief assumes the 70% win rate against PowerPlate is driven by macro-accuracy; validate this specific driver in the next user testing round.*
