**Headline Positioning Statement**
"For plant managers at mid-size automotive and food processing factories struggling to link labor costs to output efficiency, Peoplegrid is the specialized HR analytics platform that proves ROI within 12 months by reducing overtime waste and absenteeism, unlike generic workforce tools like Factora, LaborPulse, or InsightForge which offer broad data without manufacturing-specific profitability models."

**Three Biggest Risks of Failure**
1. **Category Confusion:** If CFOs view Peoplegrid as an "HR tool" rather than an "operations efficiency" asset, they will budget it from limited HR funds instead of operational savings, stalling deals over $45k.
2. **Integration Friction:** The 3.2-hour weekly usage suggests deep workflow integration; if implementation takes longer than 30 days in legacy factory environments, the promised 12-month ROI window will collapse before value is realized.
3. **Competitor Mimicry:** If Factora or LaborPulse release a "manufacturing module" with basic overtime tracking, our differentiation evaporates unless we can prove superior predictive accuracy regarding labor-to-output ratios.

**Abandon/Revise Metric**
Revise positioning immediately if **net revenue retention (NRR) drops below 90%** after the first year or if **sales cycles extend beyond 9 months**, indicating the market does not perceive the urgent 12-month ROI promise as credible or achievable.

*Note: The 45% win rate is strong, but validate price elasticity before scaling sales headcount to ensure the $67.5k ACV holds under pressure.*
