**Positioning Brief: SolarFlex (Texas Launch)**

**Target Segment:** Texas suburban homeowners (2,500+ sq. ft.) facing rising summer AC costs who are cash-flow sensitive but credit-qualified, specifically those deterred by high upfront capital requirements.

**Value Proposition:** SolarFlex eliminates the barrier of high upfront solar costs through a flexible financing model that guarantees immediate cash-flow positivity, delivering an average 14% reduction in monthly energy bills within six months without depleting savings.

**Differentiation:**
*   **Vs. BrightVolt:** While BrightVolt competes on long-term security (10-year warranty), SolarFlex competes on immediate affordability and cash-flow relief, solving the "financing fatigue" that stalls purchases today.
*   **Vs. HelioHome:** Unlike HelioHome's bundled battery lease (which increases monthly obligations), SolarFlex offers a modular, lower-entry solar-only package that optimizes for bill reduction rather than backup power, lowering the monthly payment threshold.

**Headline Tagline:** "SolarFlex: Cut Your Texas Bill Day One, Pay Over Time."

**Top Three Failure Risks:**
1.  **Permitting Fee Shock:** The unmodeled $1,200 municipal permitting fee erodes the "immediate savings" promise, turning a positive cash-flow sale into a net-neutral or negative first-year experience.
2.  **Financing Fatigue Miscalculation:** If the primary barrier is actually distrust of solar ROI rather than upfront cost, flexible financing will not convert the hesitant leads identified in the 12 loss analyses.
3.  **Competitor Price War:** BrightVolt or HelioHome could introduce short-term promotional financing that neutralizes SolarFlex's core differentiator before we establish market share.

**Abort/Pivot Metric:** If the "Cost Per Acquired Customer" exceeds $2,500 or if the churn/cancellation rate during the permitting phase exceeds 15% due to fee disclosures within the first 90 days.
