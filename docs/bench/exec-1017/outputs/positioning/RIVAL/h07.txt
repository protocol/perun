**Positioning Recommendation for Sunrow Leads**

*   **Target Segment:** Growth-focused solar installers (5–20 crews) struggling with inconsistent lead flow and high upfront marketing costs.
*   **Value Proposition:** A performance-based lead generation platform that delivers verified, ready-to-close homeowner leads, reducing customer acquisition costs by 40% and guaranteeing a 5-month payback period.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike SolarPros, HelioConnect, and BrightPath, which charge flat fees regardless of lead quality, Sunrow Leads utilizes a dynamic verification engine that only bills upon confirmed homeowner interest, ensuring a faster ROI and eliminating wasted ad spend on unqualified prospects.
*   **Headline:** "Stop Paying for Clicks. Start Paying for Contracts: The Only Solar Lead Platform with a 5-Month Payback Guarantee."

**Three Primary Ways This Could Fail**
1.  **Lead Quality Erosion:** If the "dynamic verification" fails to filter low-intent homeowners, the promised 5-month payback will stretch to match competitors' 6–7 months, destroying our core differentiation.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** Our current CAC calculation is flawed (ignoring 6 extra monthly leads); if the true cost per acquired installer rises above $1,500 due to these hidden volumes, the business model becomes unsustainable at the current pricing.
3.  **Trust Deficit:** Since we lack data on referral conversion after year one, long-term churn could spike if installers realize lead volume drops off after the initial contract, making the "performance-based" claim feel like a short-term trap.

**Pivot or Stop Metric**
Stop or pivot the messaging immediately if the **actual blended payback period for new contracts exceeds 6.5 months for two consecutive months**, as this invalidates our primary advantage over SolarPros, HelioConnect, and BrightPath.
