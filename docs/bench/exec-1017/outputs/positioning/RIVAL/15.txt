**Positioning Recommendation for ReadNow**

**Headline Message:** "ReadNow: Your Library's Instant Bookshelf—No Wait, No Extra App, No Cost."

**Target Patron Segment:** Active adult patrons (ages 25–54) who currently use commercial apps or neighboring library systems but value local integration and zero-cost access.

**Value Proposition:** For active library patrons who need immediate access to popular titles without navigating multiple accounts or paywalls, ReadNow is a fully integrated on-demand e-book platform that delivers instant checkouts directly through your existing library card, eliminating waitlists and extra subscriptions.

**Differentiation:**
*   **Unlike LibConnect**, which requires separate regional registration and often imposes inter-library loan delays, ReadNow provides instant, seamless access unified with your home library account.
*   **Unlike BookStream**, which charges per-title fees or requires a separate commercial subscription, ReadNow offers unlimited borrowing at no additional cost to the patron, funded entirely by the library.

**Potential Failure Modes:**
1.  **Catalog Fragmentation:** If the integration fails to show real-time availability, patrons will revert to the reliability of BookStream or LibConnect, eroding trust in the library's digital capability.
2.  **Content Gap:** If the initial collection lacks the specific bestsellers driving the 6 BookStream holdouts, the "instant" value proposition collapses, and adoption will stall despite the marketing spend.

**Stop/Pivot Metric:**
If fewer than **800 unique patrons** (25% of the active digital base) adopt ReadNow within the first quarter, or if the month-over-month growth rate drops below **2%** after the initial launch spike, halt the broader campaign and pivot to a targeted content-acquisition strategy focused on the specific titles demanded by the BookStream users.

*Note: The $150k budget should prioritize content licensing for high-demand titles over broad brand awareness if early adoption lags.*
*Success depends on resolving the specific title gaps that kept 30% of pilot users on BookStream.*
