**Positioning Recommendation for ClassPulse**

*   **Target Segment:** Mid-to-large K‑12 districts (1,000+ teachers) prioritizing teacher adoption and workflow stability over bleeding-edge AI features.
*   **Value Proposition:** The only LMS module engineered for sustained daily teacher usage, reducing onboarding friction to drive consistent engagement without requiring district-wide retraining.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike EduTrack (lower cost but lower engagement) and BrightPath (complex AI analytics), ClassPulse delivers proven, high-frequency utility out-of-the-box, avoiding the "shelf-ware" risk of feature-heavy competitors.
*   **Headline Message:** "ClassPulse: The LMS teachers actually use every day."

**Primary Risks & Failure Modes**
1.  **The "Good Enough" Trap:** Our 1.8 vs. 2.1 daily login gap against EduTrack suggests we are not yet the engagement leader; if Riverbend's usage drops below 1.5 logins/teacher by Q3, our core differentiation collapses.
2.  **Hidden Implementation Costs:** ScholarSphere's "zero-implementation" claim may force us into unbudgeted professional services to match their perceived ease of entry, eroding margins on the assumed $150k district budget.
3.  **Feature Parity Pressure:** Without AI analytics, we risk losing deals where administrators equate "AI" with "modern," forcing a reactive roadmap that dilutes our focus on usability.

**Stop/Change Trigger**
If ClassPulse fails to achieve **>60% active weekly teacher adoption** within the Riverbend pilot by Month 6, or if we lose **3 consecutive deals** primarily due to missing AI capabilities, we must pause general launch to either integrate predictive analytics or pivot to a niche "usability-first" micro-segment.

*Note: The 70% win rate is strong but based on a small sample (42 interviews); validate if these wins are against ScholarSphere specifically, as their pricing model remains an unknown variable.*
