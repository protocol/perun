# Evermore Planner Positioning Brief
**To:** Maya Patel, CEO
**Date:** October 23, 2026

### 1. Core Positioning Recommendation
*   **Target Segment:** High-volume independent wedding planners managing 5+ concurrent client projects (representing 78% of our active user base).
*   **Value Proposition:** Centralize timelines, contracts, and communications into a single workflow to eliminate the friction of switching between disjointed tools, enabling planners to scale beyond 10 events/year without hiring admin support.
*   **Differentiation:**
    *   *Vs. DiamondDay:* While DiamondDay's "Premium Suite" focuses on static planning templates, Evermore provides dynamic, real-time vendor coordination and contract tracking.
    *   *Vs. CelebrationSync:* Unlike CelebrationSync's broad event management approach, Evermore is purpose-built specifically for the complex, multi-vendor lifecycle of weddings.
*   **Headline Message:** "The only workspace built for independent planners scaling past 5 concurrent weddings."

### 2. Primary Failure Modes
*   **Integration Dependency:** The assumption that the vendor-match CRM integration remains free is a single point of failure; if they introduce fees or restrict API access, our value proposition erodes immediately.
*   **Market Saturation:** With only ~2,500 total planners in the U.S., capturing 30% (750 customers) is an unrealistic "home run" scenario. Failure to penetrate niche sub-segments will result in hitting a revenue ceiling quickly.
*   **Feature Parity:** If DiamondDay releases a coordination module matching our dynamic tracking before we secure strong lock-in, our differentiation collapses.

### 3. Pivot Triggers
We will stop or pivot the current strategy if either of the following occurs within six months of launch:
1.  **Adoption Failure:** Fewer than 100 new sign-ups from the target segment (planners with 5+ projects) in the first month.
2.  **Retention Breach:** Monthly churn exceeds 5% among users who have onboarded at least three client projects, indicating the workflow does not stick.
