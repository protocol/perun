**Positioning Brief: Metro Line Digital Rider App**
**To:** Alana Torres, Chief Product Officer
**From:** Samir Patel

**Recommendation (Hypothesis to Validate)**
*   **Target Segment:** Daily commuters who currently cross-check multiple sources due to unreliable schedule displays.
*   **Value Proposition:** The only rider app guaranteed to prioritize real-time arrival accuracy over fare incentives, reducing wait-time uncertainty.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike TransitPro (broad coverage) or CityPulse (fare discounts), we focus exclusively on prediction precision for high-frequency riders.
*   **Headline:** "Don't guess when it's coming. Know exactly."

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Data Dependency:** The value proposition collapses if the city's new open data API integration proves too costly or technically unstable to deliver superior accuracy compared to competitors.
2.  **Misread Priority:** Win/loss interviews may reflect stated preferences rather than actual behavior; riders might claim to want accuracy but churn if a competitor offers even a small fare discount.
3.  **Competitor Response:** TransitPro or CityPulse could improve their own accuracy algorithms, neutralizing our sole differentiator without sacrificing their broader feature sets.

**Pivot/Stop Metrics**
Alana should stop or pivot this approach if:
*   **Integration Cost:** The engineering estimate to integrate the new API exceeds $45k (30% of the quarterly budget), threatening the core value delivery.
*   **Retention Signal:** After rollout, the cohort of users opening the app 3+ times daily drops below 35%, indicating that "accuracy" alone is insufficient to drive habitual use.
*   **Acquisition Cost:** If the cost to acquire a new active user rises above $12.50 (based on the $150k budget and growth targets) within the first 6 weeks.

***

*Note: This brief treats market claims (e.g., competitor market share) as unverified external data points requiring independent confirmation before final investment. The positioning above is a hypothesis to be tested via landing page A/B testing or concierge trials before full build-out.*
