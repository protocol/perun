*   **Value Proposition:** A $30/month subscription that caps routine and preventive care costs, replacing variable pay-per-visit fees with a fixed monthly budget.

**Three Main Failure Modes**
1.  **Adverse Selection:** Only pets with chronic or immediate high-cost needs enroll, causing the medical loss ratio to exceed the $30 premium once the $180 tech labor cost per visit (15 mins × $12) is factored in.
2.  **Price Sensitivity Mismatch:** Owners prioritize low immediate cash flow over long-term predictability, churning to cheaper competitors or reverting to pay-per-visit when facing the recurring $30 charge.
3.  **Operational Drag:** The mandatory 15-minute tech overhead per visit erodes clinic throughput, increasing wait times for non-subscription clients and damaging overall brand reputation.
