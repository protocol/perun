**Positioning Recommendation: ReadNow**

**Headline Message:** "ReadNow delivers the books you want, the moment you want them—without the waitlists or extra fees of other services."

**Target Patron Segment:** Active adult patrons (ages 25–54) who currently abandon digital borrowing due to long hold times or fragmented app experiences.

**Value Proposition:** For Alder County residents who need immediate access to popular titles, ReadNow is the library's integrated on-demand platform that guarantees instant availability; unlike regional consortiums or commercial apps, it leverages our local collection to provide zero-wait access included in your existing membership.

**Differentiation:**
*   **Vs. LibConnect (Regional Service):** LibConnect relies on shared consortium pools, resulting in unpredictable waitlists for high-demand titles. ReadNow differentiates by prioritizing local instant-access licenses, eliminating the "hold and wait" friction identified by 14 of 20 pilot users who switched away from LibConnect.
*   **Vs. BookStream (Commercial App):** BookStream requires separate subscriptions, payment details, and often excludes backlist titles. ReadNow differentiates by being free at the point of use, fully integrated with your existing library card, and curated specifically for community interests rather than profit-driven bestsellers.

**How This Could Fail:**
1.  **Inventory Gap:** If the local budget cannot sustain enough instant-access licenses to meet demand, the "no wait" promise breaks, causing users to revert to LibConnect.
2.  **Discovery Friction:** If patrons cannot easily find ReadNow titles within the existing catalog interface, adoption will stall regardless of availability.
3.  **Habit Inertia:** The 6 pilot users who stayed with BookStream may value its specific user interface or recommendation engine over cost, making them resistant to switching despite the financial benefit.

**Stop/Pivot Metric:**
If fewer than **800 unique patrons** (25% of the 3,200 monthly digital checkout base) activate ReadNow within the first quarter, or if the month-over-month growth rate falls below **2%** for two consecutive months after launch, pause the campaign. This indicates the value proposition is not resonating or the inventory strategy is insufficient, requiring a pivot to either expand the instant-access license budget or re-evaluate the user experience integration.

*Note: The $150k campaign budget and catalog integration costs are treated as fixed constraints based on your input; actual license availability per title must be verified before promising "instant" access universally.*
