**Positioning Brief: HIIT Fusion**

**Target Segment:** Time-constrained corporate professionals seeking efficient, high-impact wellness solutions that fit unpredictable schedules.

**Headline Message:** "HIIT Fusion: The 30-minute corporate wellness reset that adapts to your calendar, not the other way around."

**Differentiation:**
*   **Vs. FlexFit:** While FlexFit focuses on variety through their upcoming "FlexPass," HIIT Fusion guarantees *schedule certainty* with dedicated corporate time-slots that never conflict with meeting blocks.
*   **Vs. CorePulse:** Unlike CorePulse's generalized intensity, HIIT Fusion integrates specific stress-recovery metrics tailored for high-performance workdays, turning fitness into immediate cognitive recovery.

**Top 3 Failure Risks:**
1.  **Schedule Misalignment:** The assumed priority of "flexibility" is incorrect; if corporate clients actually prioritize lowest cost per class over time-slot guarantees, the premium pricing model will fail.
2.  **Competitor Reaction:** If FlexFit's unannounced "FlexPass" undercuts our $25/class equivalent with superior scheduling tools, our primary differentiator becomes obsolete.
3.  **Retention Drop-off:** The "high-intensity" promise leads to burnout or injury if the 30-minute format compromises form, causing churn among the very demographic we targeted for consistency.

**Stop/Change Triggers:**
*   **Corporate Sign-ups:** Fewer than 5 new corporate contracts signed in the first quarter post-launch.
*   **Churn Rate:** Member churn exceeds 15% within the first 60 days of the program.
*   **Utilization:** Average visits per corporate member drop below 1.5 per week after the first month.

*Note: Your revenue calculation ($1.8M from 120k visits at $25) implies an average yield of $15/visit, suggesting significant discounting or bundle usage; verify this margin before finalizing the $25 single-class anchor. The assumption that corporate clients value flexibility over price must be validated against the 20 prospects who preferred bundles.*
