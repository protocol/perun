**Headline Positioning Recommendation:**
Position Cornerstone Bids as the "Profit-Proven Bid Platform for Mid-Size General Contractors ($50M+ revenue)" that guarantees labor cost recovery within six months, distinguishing it from lower-sticker competitors like BuildMate and PlanPro by explicitly framing the $5k onboarding fee as an investment with a verified $480k ROI rather than a barrier to entry.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Price Sensitivity & Implementation Cost:** The $5k upfront fee combined with a potential 10% price increase may stall deals if prospects focus on initial cash outflow rather than the six-month payback period, especially if the "80% win rate" claim is challenged during procurement due to the earlier arithmetic error (120 wins / 150 total = 80%, not 75%).
2.  **Win-Rate Miscalculation:** If the calculated 80% win rate includes pilots that are not yet fully commercialized or if the definition of a "win" is inconsistent, positioning on this metric creates immediate credibility loss when prospects audit the data.
3.  **Retention Unknowns:** With no churn data beyond 12 months and an assumed 24-month contract length, a 10% price hike could trigger mass non-renewal at the first anniversary, invalidating the lifetime value assumptions used to justify the sales model.

**Pivot/Stop Metric:**
Maya should halt or pivot the strategy if the **net renewal rate at 13 months drops below 85%** following any price increase, or if **three consecutive enterprise pilots fail to document at least $20k in verified labor savings** within the first 90 days of deployment.
