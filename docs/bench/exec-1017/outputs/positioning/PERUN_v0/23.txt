**Positioning Recommendation for CareSync Scheduler**

*   **Target Segment:** Mid-sized outpatient clinics (50–200 providers) currently using manual spreadsheets or legacy modules within their EHR to manage high-volume specialty scheduling, specifically those facing >30-minute patient wait times.
*   **Headline:** "Cut patient wait times by 15% and boost same-day fill rates without replacing your Epic core."
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike MedFlow and HealthQueue, which require full suite replacements or costly custom builds, CareSync acts as a lightweight coordination layer proven to reduce wait times (32m → 27.2m) and increase fill rates (42% → 45.9%) with a predicted integration cost under $20k and a 10% price advantage over current market losers.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** The vendor's "under $20k" estimate is inaccurate, eroding the 10% price advantage and making the TCO higher than MedFlow.
2.  **Workflow Friction:** Clinicians reject the new coordination layer because it adds clicks to their existing Epic workflow, negating the efficiency gains seen in the pilot.
3.  **Scalability Limits:** The 15% wait-time reduction observed in the pilot does not replicate at higher volumes or across different specialty departments.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Cost:** Final integrated cost exceeds **$28,000** (eroding the margin buffer below the 10% discount threshold).
*   **Adoption:** Active daily usage by scheduling staff drops below **60%** within 45 days of go-live.
*   **Outcome:** Same-day fill rate improvement falls below **2%** (vs. the pilot's 3.9%) after 60 days of operation.

*Note: This positioning is a hypothesis based on your pilot data and must be validated against the final Epic integration quote before final budget approval. Do not present the $20k figure as a guaranteed fact to the CFO.*
