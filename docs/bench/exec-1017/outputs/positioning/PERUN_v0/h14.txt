2.  **Segment Mismatch:** The assumption that indies value analytics over inventory may be incorrect; if indies simply need *any* monetization and mid-size networks are locked into long-term WaveCast contracts, the target segment shrinks below viability.
3.  **Competitor Response:** WaveCast's Q4-2025 pricing update could undercut your mid-market pricing or bundle analytics for free, nullifying your primary differentiator.
