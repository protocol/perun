**Positioning Recommendation for Anita Patel**

*   **Target Segment:** Solo practitioners currently drafting contracts manually or via generic word processors, triggered by the inability to scale billable hours due to administrative overhead.
*   **Value Proposition:** Reclaim 10+ hours weekly by automating contract drafting without the complexity of enterprise legal tech.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike LexDraft and DocuForm, which prioritize feature breadth, Clause & Co reduces the workflow to the absolute minimum clicks required to generate a court-ready document.
*   **Headline Message:** "Stop drafting. Start closing. The only contract automation built for the solo lawyer who bills by the hour, not by the click."

**Three Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **The "Good Enough" Trap:** The alternative isn't competing SaaS tools, but entrenched manual routines; if the switching cost (learning new UI) outweighs the time saved, adoption will stall despite the "fewer clicks" claim.
2.  **Support Cost Erosion:** The assumption of "no extra support cost" is fragile; if solo lawyers require hand-holding to build their first template, the required dedicated specialist will destroy the $199/month unit economics.
3.  **Differentiation Dilution:** "Fewer clicks" is a feature, not a moat; if LexDraft or DocuForm releases a simplified "Solo Mode," our positioning collapses without deeper workflow integration or proprietary data advantages.

**Pivot/Stop Metric**
Anita should halt or pivot the Q3 launch if the **new tier churn rate exceeds 8% in the first 60 days** or if **template creation averages below 1.5 per user per week**, indicating the product is failing to become a habitual part of the drafting workflow.
