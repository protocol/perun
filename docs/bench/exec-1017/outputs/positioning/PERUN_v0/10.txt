**Positioning Brief: Dispatch-Automation Module (Hypothesis to Validate)**

**Target Segment:** Mid-tier freight brokers processing 1,000+ shipments/month who are currently losing margin to manual paperwork inefficiencies.
**Headline:** Cut dispatch paperwork by 30% without the 2-week implementation drag.
**Value Proposition:** For brokers drowning in administrative overhead, our module automates dispatch documentation to recover 30% of processing time. Unlike VersaTrack, CargoFlow, or LoadLogic, which require complex, multi-week integrations, we deliver immediate workflow relief with a streamlined setup designed for high-volume operations.
**Differentiation:**
*   **Vs. VersaTrack/CargoFlow:** We target the specific pain of *implementation friction*. While competitors offer similar end-states, our hypothesis is that buyers will choose us to avoid the 2-week downtime they fear.
*   **Proof Point Required:** Your data shows users process 1,200 shipments/month vs. competitor averages of 950–1,050. We must validate if this volume correlates directly to a lower tolerance for implementation time.

**How This Positioning Could Fail:**
1.  **The "30%" Assumption:** If the 42 interested brokers actually require a 50% reduction to switch, the value prop misses the mark.
2.  **Implementation Reality:** If the "streamlined setup" actually takes 2 weeks for complex mid-tier brokers, the core differentiator collapses.
3.  **Competitor Response:** If VersaTrack or CargoFlow launches a "fast-track" onboarding campaign before we do, our unique angle evaporates.
4.  **Integration Cost Unknown:** Since you lack data on true integration costs, the total cost of ownership might still favor incumbents despite our speed claim.

**Kill/Pivot Metric:**
If fewer than **15% of pilot users** achieve the promised 30% paperwork reduction within the first 60 days, or if **implementation time exceeds 10 days** for more than half of new deployments, stop the current rollout and pivot to a services-led integration model.
