### Positioning Hypothesis for Evermore Planner

**Target Segment:** Independent wedding planners managing 5+ concurrent client projects who are currently losing billable hours to manual contract tracking and fragmented communication.

**Value Proposition:** Evermore Planner centralizes timelines, vendor contracts, and client communications into a single workflow, reducing administrative overhead by consolidating tools.

**Differentiation:**
*   **Vs. DiamondDay:** Unlike DiamondDay's "Premium Suite" (pricing and specific feature set unverified), Evermore offers immediate, transparent integration with vendor-match CRMs at no extra API cost. *Note: DiamondDay's actual pricing must be validated before launch claims are finalized.*
*   **Vs. CelebrationSync:** Unlike CelebrationSync, which focuses primarily on visual timelines, Evermore prioritizes the legal and operational backbone (contracts + comms) required to scale beyond 5 clients.

**Headline Message:** "Stop juggling contracts and chats. The all-in-one workflow for independent planners scaling past five weddings."

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Integration Fragility:** The assumption that the vendor-match CRM's free API tier remains free and stable is unverified; if costs rise or access is restricted, the core value prop collapses.
2.  **Feature Misalignment:** If "managing contracts" is not the primary bottleneck for planners (vs. marketing or design), the product solves a low-priority pain point.
3.  **Competitor Response:** If DiamondDay lowers prices or bundles similar features aggressively, our differentiation on "cost-effective integration" may become irrelevant.

**Pivot/Stop Triggers:**
*   **Churn > 5%** within the first 90 days post-launch.
*   **Fewer than 100 sign-ups** in the first month despite targeted outreach to the 2,500-planner market.
*   **Vendor-match API costs** exceed $0, invalidating the unit economics for the target segment.
