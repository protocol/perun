**Positioning Brief: Peak Performance**

**Recommendation**
*   **Target Segment:** Elite CrossFit and triathlon athletes currently relying on manual meal prep or generic "healthy" kits, who view macro-inaccuracy as a performance bottleneck.
*   **Headline Message:** "Stop guessing your gains. Peak Performance delivers lab-grade macro precision for elite training, so you fuel exactly like you train."
*   **Key Differentiator:** Unlike PowerPlate, FitFuel, and EnduroEats, which prioritize variety or convenience, Peak Performance guarantees gram-level macro accuracy verified by batch testing, directly addressing the athlete's need for metabolic consistency over ease of use.

**Top 3 Failure Modes**
1.  **Convenience Trumps Precision:** Athletes abandon the kit because the rigidity required for macro precision makes the meals unpalatable or too time-consuming to consume during peak training blocks.
2.  **Price Elasticity Breach:** The target segment proves unwilling to pay the premium required for batch testing, causing volume to collapse before unit economics stabilize.
3.  **Trust Deficit:** Athletes do not believe the "lab-grade" claim without third-party certification, viewing it as marketing fluff compared to their own tracking methods.

**Pivot/Scrap Metric**
Scrap or pivot this specific positioning if the **repeat purchase rate drops below 30% within the first 60 days** of the general launch, indicating that the value of precision does not outweigh the cost or friction for the broader segment.
