**Positioning Recommendation for Greenfurrow Leasing**

*   **Target Segment:** Mid-size row-crop farmers in the Midwest facing seasonal cash-flow gaps and equipment upgrade cycles, specifically those currently relying on short-term rentals or aging owned machinery.
*   **Value Proposition:** "Unlock immediate operational capacity without capital strain by leasing premium equipment for exactly the duration you need, preserving cash reserves for harvest volatility."
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike rigid annual contracts from competitors like CropCycle Lease or AgriFlex, Greenfurrow offers modular lease terms (averaging 4.3 months) that align strictly with crop cycles, validated by our 68% win rate against rigid annual providers.
*   **Headline:** "Farm on Your Schedule: Flexible Equipment Leasing That Matches Your Harvest, Not Your Bank's Fiscal Year."

1.  **Regulatory Obsolescence:** The Iowa regulatory cap limiting leases to 3 months (effective Jan 1, 2026) renders our core 4.3-month average product illegal in a key market, forcing a costly product rebuild.

3.  **Competitor Adaptation:** CropCycle Lease or AgriFlex rapidly copy our modular term structure, neutralizing our primary differentiator while leveraging their larger balance sheets to undercut pricing.

**Pivot or Stop Signal**
Stop or pivot immediately if **lease renewal rates drop below 85%** or if **new customer acquisition cost (CAC) rises by >20%** following the January 2026 regulatory change, indicating the market cannot sustain the product under the new 3-month constraint. Additionally, if farmer interviews shift from praising "flexibility" to citing "total cost of ownership" as the primary blocker, the value hypothesis requires fundamental retesting.
