**Headline Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate)**
"For mid-size automotive and food processing plant managers struggling to justify labor costs to CFOs, Peoplegrid is an HR analytics platform that delivers verified ROI within 12 months; unlike generic dashboards like Factora or LaborPulse, it connects workforce data directly to production outcomes to secure budget approval faster."

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1. **The "Generic Dashboard" Trap:** If Factora or InsightForge can easily replicate the specific production-outcome connectors we claim as unique, our differentiator collapses, rendering the positioning indistinguishable from competitors.
2. **The CFO Misalignment:** If the CFO's primary constraint is cash flow rather than ROI justification (e.g., they need immediate cost cuts, not 12-month projections), our core value proposition fails to address the actual buying trigger.
3. **Price Elasticity Blind Spot:** Since price sensitivity is unquantified, a 10% price increase could crater conversion rates below our 45% pilot win rate, proving that our "ROI" argument does not outweigh cost concerns in this specific segment.

**Abandon/Revise Metric**
Revise or abandon this positioning if **trial-to-paid conversion drops below 25%** or if **churn exceeds 15% within the first 6 months**, indicating the promised ROI is not being realized or valued by the buyer.
