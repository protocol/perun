**Positioning Recommendation: "The Zero-Fee Impact Engine"**

*   **Target Segment:** High-intent individual donors ($50–$500/month) who have previously donated to food security causes but feel disconnected from tangible outcomes.
*   **Value Proposition:** A direct-giving program where 100% of contributions fund meals, validated by a pilot that increased weekly meal output by 22% without increasing administrative overhead.
*   **Differentiation:** Unlike **MealMatch** (which claims higher conversion but deducts a 12% fee) or **FoodShare** (no fee but low 5% conversion), our model combines a **0% fee structure** with a proven **68% win rate** on the "every $10 feeds one family" message. We prioritize net impact over gross conversion metrics.
*   **Headline Message:** "Your entire donation feeds families. No platform fees, no hidden overhead—just 22% more meals per week than our baseline."

**Falsification Test**
Run an A/B test in Q2 targeting 500 lapsed donors: Group A receives the "Zero-Fee/Net Impact" message; Group B receives a "High Conversion/Platform Ease" message (mimicking MealMatch's angle). **The hypothesis is proven wrong if** Group B's net revenue (after simulated fees) exceeds Group A's by more than 10%, indicating donors prioritize the *act* of giving over the *efficiency* of the dollar.

**Failure Modes & Pivot Triggers**
1.  **Mis-estimated Donor Churn:** If the unknown **6-month churn rate exceeds 45%**, the acquisition cost outweighs the lifetime value, rendering the high win rate irrelevant. *Pivot:* Shift focus from acquisition messaging to retention mechanics before scaling.
2.  **Hidden Staff Costs:** If the manual effort required to verify "meals per week" data drives administrative costs up by **>8% of revenue**, the "zero fee" claim becomes financially unsustainable. *Pivot:* Introduce a voluntary "keep the lights on" add-on or automate reporting.
3.  **Competitor Fee Structure Shift:** If MealMatch drops their fee to **<5%** while maintaining 30% higher conversion, our differentiation evaporates. *Pivot:* Re-position on local community transparency rather than fee efficiency.

**Stop/Pivot Metric:** If the conversion rate for the "Zero-Fee" message drops below **50%** in the next quarter (a 18-point drop from the pilot's 68%), immediately halt the broad rollout. This signals that the "fee" argument is not the primary driver for this segment, and the core assumption that donors care primarily about meal counts over platform ease is incorrect.

*Note: The 6-month churn rate must be calculated from the Jan–Oct 2023 cohort before finalizing the long-term budget. All competitor claims (MealMatch/FoodShare stats) should be re-verified via current public pricing pages prior to board presentation.*
