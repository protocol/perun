**Recommendation:** Do not implement the blanket price increase to $95 on July 1 yet; instead, run a limited A/B test on 10% of traffic. While the arithmetic suggests a price hike improves unit economics, the unknown regulatory cost variable (FDA guidance) and the lack of price elasticity data create a high risk of margin erosion if volume drops even slightly.

**The Arithmetic (Lane A):**
*   **Current State:** Price $85, Gross Margin 18% ($15.30/unit). LTV:CAC is 7.5x ($210/$28), which is healthy.
*   **Proposed State ($95):** If costs remain static, the new margin per unit is $25.30 (assuming the $69.70 cost base holds). This is a 65% increase in contribution margin per unit.
*   **The Break-Even Sensitivity:** To maintain the same total gross profit dollars, you can afford a volume drop of roughly **39%** ($15.30 / $25.30). However, if the FDA guidance increases your cost of goods sold (COGS) by just $5/unit, your new margin is $20.30, and you can only afford a **24%** volume drop.
*   **The Risk:** You assumed conversion stays flat. In reality, an 11.8% price increase often triggers churn or conversion decay. If conversion drops by more than 24-39% (depending on the unseen FDA costs), you lose total profit despite the higher sticker price.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Margin Erosion via Regulatory Surprise:** If the September FDA guidance mandates expensive packaging changes, your COGS rises. If you have already locked in a higher price that suppressed volume, you face a "double squeeze" of lower sales and higher costs.
2.  **Conversion Decay:** The assumption that conversion stays flat at 2% is unverified. If price sensitivity is higher than anticipated, the 1.5M annual volume could contract faster than the margin gain compensates.
3.  **LTV Compression:** If the price hike increases early churn (customers refusing to reorder refills), the $210 LTV will collapse, invalidating the current CAC payback model.

**Pivot/Stop Thresholds:**
*   **Immediate Stop:** If the A/B test shows a **>15% drop in conversion rate** on the $95 tier compared to the $85 control.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If monthly repeat orders drop by **>5%** within 45 days of full implementation.
*   **Regulatory Halt:** If the FDA guidance released in September indicates a COGS increase of **>$3 per unit**, pause any price strategy until new unit economics are modeled.
