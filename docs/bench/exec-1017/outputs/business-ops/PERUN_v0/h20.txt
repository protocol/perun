**Recommendation for Maya Patel (CFO):** Do not implement a blanket price increase to $7 on 15 Oct 2026. Instead, run a controlled A/B test on 10–20% of new seller traffic for 30 days to measure actual price elasticity before committing the full user base.

**Rationale & Failure Modes:**
Your finance team's projection ($151,200) assumes zero volume loss, which contradicts your stated elasticity assumption of –0.8. Applying that elasticity mathematically: a 40% price hike ($5 → $7) should theoretically reduce quantity demanded by 32% (0.8 × 40%). If volume drops 32%, your new revenue would be roughly $103,000 (8,160 users × 1.8 listings × $7), a **21% revenue decrease**, not a 15% gain. Even if elasticity is half as sensitive (–0.4), a 16% volume drop still yields only ~$127,000 in revenue, failing to cover the additional $5,000 support cost and risking net loss against the current $131,400 baseline.

The primary ways this fails are:
1.  **Overestimated Willingness-to-Pay:** If true elasticity is closer to –0.8 than –0.4, the price hike destroys more value than it creates, shrinking top-line revenue and increasing churn beyond the 5% baseline.
2.  **Churn Amplification:** The transition marketing budget ($30k) implies anticipated friction. If price-sensitive users churn immediately, the CAC ($22) required to replace them erodes the LTV ($190) calculation, especially if the new ARPU does not materialize due to volume loss.
3.  **Support Cost Overrun:** If the price change triggers a spike in support tickets beyond the modeled $5,000, contribution margin narrows further.

**Pivot/Stop Thresholds:**
Establish these decision rules before testing:
*   **Stop Immediately:** If the A/B test shows a net revenue decline (price effect < volume loss effect) or if churn spikes above 8% in the test cohort.
*   **Pivot Strategy:** If net revenue growth is under 5% after two quarters, revert to $5 for the mass market and restrict the $7 tier to "featured" placements only (changing the value metric rather than the base price).
*   **Validation Requirement:** Do not scale until you have empirical data replacing the assumed –0.8 elasticity. The current model relies on an unverified input; treating it as fact is a financial risk.

*Note: Ensure your terms of service allow for price changes with the notice period you intend to give. The $30k retention budget should be tracked against actual saved LTV to verify its efficiency.*
