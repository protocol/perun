**Decision Aid & Thresholds**
The math works only if churn remains suppressed. The "flip input" here is churn elasticity.
*   **Break-even Churn Rate:** To maintain the current LTV of $240 at a $15 price point, churn can rise to $15 \div 240 = 6.25\%$.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If LTV:CAC drops below 3.0x, the model is at risk. At $15 ARPU and $30 CAC, this implies a maximum sustainable churn of $15 \div (30 \times 3) = 16.6\%$ (theoretical floor), but practical SaaS health usually demands <3-4% for this stage.

**Recommendation**
Proceed with a **controlled rollout** (e.g., new users only or 10% of base) rather than a blanket hike on Oct 1. The competitive pressure from SignFlow ($11) creates high elasticity risk.

**Failure Modes**
1.  **Churn Spike:** If churn exceeds **6.5%** immediately, the LTV gain from higher pricing is erased, and total revenue may drop due to volume loss.
2.  **Upgrade Friction:** If Enterprise adoption is <2%, the projected $10k lift fails to materialize.
3.  **Fundraising Timing:** Relying on organic revenue lift to influence a Series A closing in 90 days is risky; investors weigh growth rate and retention more than marginal ARPU increases if churn spikes.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger**
If **monthly churn exceeds 6.5%** in the first 30 days post-hike, or if **net revenue retention (NRR) drops below 95%**, pause the price increase for the existing base and revert to a segmented pricing test.
