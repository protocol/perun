**Recommendation to CFO Laura Greene:**
Proceed with a **phased pilot launch** limited to 50% of daily slots for 90 days, contingent on validating the patient acceptance rate. While the unit economics show a strong theoretical upside—specifically a **$275,000 annual labor saving** (5 staff × $55k) and recovered revenue from reduced no-shows—the projection relies entirely on the unverified assumption that a 15% throughput increase translates directly to billable visits without physician bottlenecks. A full rollout risks locking in $24,000/year in fixed service fees plus variable reminder costs against revenue that may not materialize if provider capacity is maxed out or patient adoption falls below the estimated 70%.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Capacity Bottleneck:** The 15% throughput gain generates appointments that physicians cannot physically see, converting potential revenue into scheduling chaos and patient dissatisfaction.
2.  **Adoption Shortfall:** If digital check-in acceptance is <50% (vs. the 70% estimate), the reduction in administrative workload will not materialize, making the planned reduction of 5 FTEs operationally impossible and damaging morale.
3.  **Margin Erosion:** The "reminder math" fails if the cost per acquired/retained visit exceeds the $80 contribution margin; at $0.10 per reminder, this is unlikely unless message volume spikes exponentially without conversion.

**Stop / Re-evaluate Triggers:**
*   **No-Show Impact:** If the no-show rate does not drop by at least **2.5 percentage points** (from 12% to ≤9.5%) after 60 days of full reminder usage.
*   **Cost Threshold:** If total monthly platform costs (fixed $2,000 + variable reminders) exceed **$4,500** while net new revenue remains flat for two consecutive months.
*   **Adoption Rate:** If actual patient usage of digital check-in remains below **50%** after the first 1,000 eligible appointments.

*Note: The corrected gross margin of ~53% ($80 contribution) should be used for all break-even calculations, not the initial 85% figure. Ensure physician capacity is modeled before scaling beyond the pilot.*
