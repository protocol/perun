**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Measure-First" Launch (Do not re-price yet; do not delay entirely).**
The unit economics support a launch only if the "cannibalization" risk is contained. Your model assumes 100% retention of studio memberships among app users. If even 10% of app subscribers (168 people) drop their $120 studio membership to keep only the $15 app subscription, you lose $20,160 in monthly revenue while gaining only $2,520, resulting in a net loss of $17,640/month. Launch immediately to instrument this specific behavior, but cap marketing spend until real-world churn data validates the assumption.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Adoption:** Conversion falls below 20% (fewer than 1,680 users), extending the payback period beyond your 12-month target.
*   **Churn (Critical):** App users cancel studio memberships ("downgrading"), turning the app into a revenue-replacement tool rather than an expansion tool.
*   **Technical:** App instability drives early churn before value is proven, skewing initial data.
*   **Regulatory:** Handling health data triggers compliance regimes (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, or HIPAA depending on data type/jurisdiction) requiring unbudgeted legal/engineering spend.

**Stop/Change Triggers (Decision Thresholds)**
*   **Stop/Pivot:** If >5% of app subscribers cancel their studio membership within the first 60 days.
*   **Stop/Pivot:** If total net new monthly revenue (App Revenue minus Lost Studio Revenue) is below $10,000 after Month 3.
*   **Re-price:** If conversion is <10% but user feedback indicates high value, consider testing a higher price point or tiered model before scaling.

**Math & Assumptions Applied**
*   *Projected Monthly Revenue:* 1,680 users × $15 = $25,200.
*   *Break-even Analysis:* $250,000 development cost ÷ $25,200 monthly revenue = ~9.9 months to recover development costs (ignoring ongoing maintenance/compliance costs). This aligns with your 12-month target only if ongoing costs are <~$4,200/month.
*   *Runway Impact:* With 10 months of cash remaining, you can fund the launch and roughly 9 months of operations without the additional $500k raise, provided the break-even holds.
