**Recommendation:** Do not implement a blanket price increase to $5,500 on July 1, 2027. Instead, adopt a "Good-Better-Best" tiering strategy where the current feature set remains at $4,500 (anchoring against the rumored $4,800 competitor) and a new premium tier launches at $5,500+ with expanded services.

**Rationale:** Your current LTV calculation ($30,000) assumes a static 8% churn on a 5-year life, which overstates value if the price hike triggers the projected 12% churn. At 12% churn, the effective lifetime drops to ~8.3 years theoretically, but in practice, early-year churn destroys LTV faster. More critically, losing 10 sign-ups per quarter (40/year) at a CAC of $1,200 represents a $48,000 annual efficiency loss, plus the forgone LTV of those missing accounts. With a Series A target of $1.5M ARR by end-2028, risking net contraction or stagnation in late 2027 jeopardizes the round. The rumored competitor price ($4,800) makes your proposed $5,500 price point an outlier without proven differentiated value, increasing the likelihood of negotiation or rejection.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Churn Spiral:** If churn exceeds 10% immediately post-hike, the loss of recurring revenue from existing schools outweighs the margin gain from the price increase, causing actual ARR to drop below the current $540k baseline.
2.  **Sales Stall:** The 10-school-per-quarter reduction in sign-ups compounds; if the sales cycle lengthens due to price objections, you miss the Q4 2027 momentum needed to pitch the Series A, leaving you with flat growth rather than the required path to $1.5M.
3.  **Competitive Displacement:** If "SecureClass" launches at $4,800 with comparable features, your $5,500 price creates an easy justification for prospects and renewing customers to switch, turning a pricing experiment into a market share loss.

**Pivot/Stop Metric:** Halt the price increase implementation if **net revenue retention (NRR) falls below 95%** in the first 90 days post-launch (indicating churn + downgrades > 5%) OR if **new logo acquisition drops by more than 20%** compared to the prior quarter average.
