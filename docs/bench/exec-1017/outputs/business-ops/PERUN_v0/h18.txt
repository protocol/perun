**Recommendation:** Do not finalize the proposal for CFO review until the EU data-residency cost impact is quantified; proceed immediately with a limited A/B price test on new signups only while awaiting legal's cost estimate.

**Analysis of the Plan:**
Your arithmetic on the upside is correct *if* assumptions hold:
*   **Base Uplift:** 1,200 users × $5 increase = $6,000 MRR.
*   **Add-on Revenue:** (1,200 × 4%) × $15 = $720 MRR.
*   **Total New MRR:** $6,720 (projecting ~$3.1M ARR).
*   **Unit Economics Check:** At $25 price and $45 CAC, assuming your 7% monthly churn persists, the simplified Lifetime Value (LTV) drops significantly if churn increases. Current LTV approximation ($20 / 0.07) ≈ $285. New LTV at $25 (same churn) ≈ $357. However, if churn rises to 10%, LTV falls to $250, eroding the margin gain from the price hike.

**How This Plan Could Fail:**
1.  **Churn Sensitivity:** Your model assumes upgrade conversion (4%) does not affect churn. In practice, price hikes often trigger involuntary churn or downgrades. If overall churn exceeds **8.5%**, the net revenue gain from the price hike may be neutralized by lost subscribers within 6 months.
2.  **Regulatory Cost Cliff:** You identified the EU data-residency regulation (effective 1 Jun 2024) as an unknown. If compliance requires dedicated EU infrastructure, hosting costs could rise by 20–40%. Without this number, your projected margin expansion is unverified. This is a **Lane R** (regulatory/compliance) variable that dictates financial viability; you cannot finalize the P&L impact without it.
3.  **Add-on Cannibalization:** If the "Premium Events" feature was previously expected or included, users may perceive the $25 + $15 structure as a double dip, increasing resistance to the base price hike.

**Stop/Pivot Thresholds:**
Define these triggers before launch:
*   **Churn Trigger:** If monthly churn exceeds **9%** for two consecutive months post-implementation, revert pricing for the affected cohort.
*   **Revenue Trigger:** If net ARR (after churn and refunds) falls below **$2.6M** (not $2.8M, allowing for a small dip) after 90 days, pause the add-on rollout.
*   **Cost Trigger:** If legal confirms EU hosting costs will increase operational expenses by more than **$5,000/month**, the price hike must be recalculated to cover this specific liability before proceeding.

**Note:** The EU data-residency question involves legal interpretation of applicable regimes; route the specific compliance cost estimation to your legal counsel or a qualified privacy professional.
