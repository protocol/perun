2.  **Hidden Fee Impact:** The $3,000 platform fee reduces margins immediately. While it doesn't change the *gross* revenue comparison, it tightens the runway and makes the loss in volume even more painful to net income.

The pricing change makes you more vulnerable to volume shocks because you are relying on higher value per unit rather than volume stability.
