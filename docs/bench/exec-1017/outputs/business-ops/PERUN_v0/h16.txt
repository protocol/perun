**Subject:** Recommendation on Lumen Learning Corporate Seat Price Increase

**Recommendation**
Proceed with the price increase to $229 effective July 1, 2026, **conditional** on validating that the 30% YoY growth rate holds post-announcement. The arithmetic supports the move: raising the price by 15% ($30/seat) on 250 seats generates an incremental $90,000 annually per cohort if churn remains static. Over a 12-month contract cycle, this aligns with your projected ~$720k ARR lift as new sales and renewals cycle into the new rate. With a current CAC of $150k per account and high seat density (avg. 83 seats/account), the contribution margin expansion from the price hike significantly improves LTV:CAC ratios, assuming the "no churn impact" assumption holds.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Churn Sensitivity:** The model assumes 10% annual churn. If the price hike triggers even a 5% *incremental* churn spike among the three legacy accounts (TechNova, GreenWave, Apex), the lost recurring revenue could offset ~40% of the gained revenue immediately.
2.  **Pipeline Contraction:** The unknown variable is Polyglot Corp. If they do *not* raise prices in August, our relative price position worsens. A 15% pipeline loss due to price sensitivity would extend the payback period on the $150k CAC, potentially pushing it beyond the 12-month contract length, rendering new customer acquisition cash-flow negative.
3.  **Runway Misallocation:** While $3M runway is healthy, relying on the $720k uplift to fund operations is risky if the uptake is slower than the 12-month projection. If sales velocity drops due to price friction, the effective runway shortens despite higher per-unit revenue.

**Stop/Reverse Trigger**
Implement a "clawback" clause: If **net new seat growth** (new sales minus churned seats) turns negative for two consecutive months following the July 1 implementation, or if **any** of the three legacy accounts threaten non-renewal specifically citing price, immediately revert to $199 for those specific negotiations and pause the general increase.

**Note:** This analysis applies your provided inputs; the decision to expose the company to potential churn volatility rests with leadership. Verify Polyglot Corp's pricing strategy via market intelligence before finalizing the July date.
