**1. Financial Impact Analysis (Lane A: Arithmetic)**

*   **Proposed State (Sept Projection):** 720 leases × ($20,000 × 0.85) = $12.24M Revenue.

*   **Break-Even Logic:** To maintain current total gross profit dollars with a 15% price cut, volume must increase by roughly **29%**, not the projected 12-16%. The math suggests the volume uplift is insufficient to offset the margin loss.
