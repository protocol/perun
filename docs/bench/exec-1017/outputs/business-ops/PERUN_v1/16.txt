**Recommendation:** Proceed with a controlled pilot only; do not full rollout until the Westhaven compliance cost is quantified. While the unit economics on the subscription model appear strong (LTV:CAC of 9:1), launching without a confirmed regulatory cost figure violates the "no fabricated inputs" rule and risks turning a profitable product into a loss-leader if the fee is substantial.

**Arithmetic Check (Lane A):**
*   **Current State:** 70 leads/month × $120 CAC = $8,400 spend (close to your $9k budget).
*   **Subscription Projection:**
    *   Users converting: 3,500 MAU × 8% = 280 paid subscribers.
    *   Monthly Revenue: 280 × $25 = $7,000.
    *   Current Equivalent Revenue (implied): $7,000 / 1.15 (15% lift) ≈ $6,087.
    *   **Risk:** Your projected revenue ($7k) is lower than your current monthly ad spend ($9k). Unless the "converted policy" LTV ($1,080) is *also* generated by these subscribers separately from the $25 fee, the subscription alone does not cover acquisition costs.
    *   **LTV:CAC:** If the $25/mo is the *only* revenue, LTV depends entirely on churn (which you assume is static). If churn is 5%/mo, LTV ≈ $500. If CAC remains $120, LTV:CAC ≈ 4.1:1 (healthy). If the Westhaven fee adds $20/user/mo, margins collapse.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Cost Shock:** The "low" Westhaven certification fee turns out to be a fixed high cost or a per-user levy that exceeds the $25 price point margin.
2.  **Conversion Overestimation:** The 8% conversion rate drops significantly when moving from a free demo to a paid wall, especially with a 32% price increase ($19 to $25), causing revenue to miss the break-even point against the $9k ad spend.
3.  **Value Metric Mismatch:** Users paying $25/month expect ongoing value (e.g., continuous updates); if the tool is a one-time quote generator, churn will spike despite your assumption, destroying the LTV calculation.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
Stop the rollout immediately if the Westhaven compliance fee exceeds **$8 per user per month** (eroding >30% of the new price) or if the pilot conversion rate falls below **5%** in the first 30 days.

**Note:** The assumption that churn will not increase after a 32% price hike is highly optimistic; sensitivity testing on churn is required before final approval. The compliance question regarding Westhaven regulations is a Lane R issue; confirm the exact fee structure with legal counsel before committing capital.
