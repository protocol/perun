2.  **Churn Acceleration:** If the price hike signals dissatisfaction and pushes annual churn from 6% to >10% across the remaining base, the LTV compression will devalue the book long-term, even if short-term ARR holds.
3.  **Sales Friction:** A 16.7% price hike may lengthen sales cycles for new logos, delaying the recognition of the higher ACV and straining Q1 cash flow.
