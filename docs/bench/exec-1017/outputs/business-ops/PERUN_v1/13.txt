Based on your current numbers, the price hike alone (keeping subscribers constant) adds ~$1,850/month, but the projected volume growth (31%→38% of a growing user base) is the primary value driver, potentially adding ~$14,000/month in gross revenue by year-end. **Main Failure Modes & Arithmetic Oversights:**
1.  **Churn Sensitivity:** You assume churn stays at 4% despite a 25% price hike. If churn rises to just 5.5%, the net gain from the price increase is neutralized.
2.  **Linear Growth Fallacy:** Projecting 15% month-over-month growth linearly through Q4 is aggressive; a drop to 10% growth reduces your total addressable premium users by ~8,000, cutting projected revenue by ~$6,000/month.
3.  **Compliance Cost Variance:** The $8k–$12k quarterly range is material. At $12k, your fixed costs rise by $4,000/month. If ad revenue ($0.45/user) or storage costs ($0.03/user) fluctuate, this fixed regulatory burden could push the premium tier into negative contribution margin during low-growth months.
4.  **Revenue Double-Counting Risk:** Ensure the $53,100 ad revenue includes both free and premium users. If premium users are ad-free, raising the paywall shrinks the ad-supported pool, creating a hidden revenue cannibalization not reflected in your current top-line figures.

**Stop/Pivot Metric:** **Net New Contribution Margin.**
Set a hard tripwire: If the **actual** net new revenue (Price Uplift + Volume Lift − Increased Churn Loss − Compliance Fee) falls below **$2,500/month** for two consecutive months post-implementation, pivot immediately. This threshold ensures the initiative covers the worst-case compliance cost ($4k/mo) and leaves a minimal buffer for operational variance; failing to clear it means the regulatory risk outweighs the pricing gain.
