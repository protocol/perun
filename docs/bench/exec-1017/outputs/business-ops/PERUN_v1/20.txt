**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Churn Acceleration:** Small restaurants operate on thin margins. A 25% price hike ($12 to $15) may exceed their willingness-to-pay, causing churn to spike above the 3% baseline.
2.  **Regulatory Cost Under-estimation:** You noted the California tax change impact is unknown. If the compliance cost exceeds **$0.50 per employee per month**, your projected 70% gross margin assumption becomes invalid, directly reducing the net benefit of the hike.
3.  **Regulatory Pushback:** While pricing is generally discretionary, sudden changes tied to tax events can invite scrutiny if customers perceive the pass-through as inaccurate.

**Stop/Change Tripwires**
Execute the price increase only if the following conditions are met; otherwise, halt or revert:
*   **Churn Threshold:** If observed churn exceeds **6% in either of the first two quarters** post-implementation, freeze the price for new cohorts or revert existing ones.
