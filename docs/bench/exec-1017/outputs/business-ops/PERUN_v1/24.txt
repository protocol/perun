**Recommendation:** Do not launch the full subscription model globally until the EU regulatory clearance path is defined; instead, initiate a limited pilot in non-EU markets immediately to validate unit economics while legal counsel assesses the firmware compliance regime.

**Analysis of the Proposal:**
Your arithmetic shows a strong theoretical case but relies on unverified inputs.
*   **Revenue Target:** 10,000 units × $500 = $5M ARR in Year 1. To reach $25M ARR by FY2027, you need ~50,000 cumulative units assuming low churn, requiring aggressive scaling.
*   **Unit Economics:**
    *   **Contribution Margin:** $500 (Price) - $150 (COGS) = $350 per unit/year.
    *   **Payback Period:** $2,000 (CAC) ÷ $350 (Margin) = **5.7 years**.
    *   **LTV Estimate:** At 8% churn, average life is ~12.5 years. Gross LTV ≈ $4,375. LTV:CAC ≈ 2.2:1.
    *   **Sales Cost:** 8 reps × $120k = $960k fixed cost. At 10,000 units, this is $96/unit, reducing margin further if not already in CAC.

**The Fatal Flaw:** A 5.7-year payback period exceeds your 3-year minimum contract commitment. If a customer churns at month 36, you lose money on that account unless the $2,000 CAC includes multi-year amortization (which is unlikely for upfront sales costs). Additionally, assuming 8% SaaS churn for hardware-dependent subscriptions is risky; hardware failure or obsolescence often drives higher churn.

**Three Biggest Ways This Could Fail:**
1.  **Negative Unit Economics on Early Churn:** With a payback period (5.7 years) longer than the minimum contract (3 years), any customer leaving before year 6 results in a net loss, destroying the projected $3M profit.
2.  **Regulatory Blockade:** If the EU (a major market for industrial sensing) classifies the telemetry firmware as a regulated component requiring 18 months of clearance, you lose access to a significant portion of your TAM during the critical scaling phase, missing the 2027 target.
3.  **Channel Conflict & Sales Incentive Misalignment:** Moving from a $12M one-time sale model to recurring revenue may depress short-term cash flow. If sales reps are not compensated correctly for multi-year deals (e.g., paid only on booking vs. collected revenue), they may resist selling the new model.

**Stop/Pivot Metric:**
**Trigger:** If the validated CAC remains above $1,500 *or* if early pilot churn exceeds 15% annually within the first 6 months.
**Action:** Halt global rollout, revert to a hybrid "device + service" one-time fee model, and re-engineer the cost structure before re-attempting pure subscription.

**Note:** The EU regulatory timeline is a Lane R (legal/compliance) question; you must route the specific firmware classification to counsel immediately. The 8% churn assumption is unverified for hardware; instrument a pilot to get real data before finalizing the P&L.
