**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with a blanket price increase to $15/user in Q3 2024. Instead, execute a targeted price test on 10% of the cohort immediately to validate price sensitivity before committing the full base.

**The Math & The Gap:**
Your headline projection assumes zero volume loss, but your own elasticity assumption (-0.3) contradicts this. A -0.3 elasticity implies that a 25% price hike ($12 → $15) should cause roughly a 7.5% drop in quantity (users or seats).
*   **Current Net Revenue per User:** $12.00 - $0.50 (support) = $11.50.
*   **Proposed Net Revenue per User:** $15.00 - $0.50 (support) = $14.50.
*   **Break-even Churn Threshold:** You can afford to lose up to ~20.7% of your volume ($11.50 / $14.50) just to maintain current net revenue, ignoring CAC payback.
*   **The Risk:** If elasticity is actually -1.0 (common in mid-market without unique lock-in), you lose 25% of users. Result: 250 clients × 40 users × 0.75 retention × $14.50 = $3.26M ARR. You risk $3M in operational disruption for only ~$60k in theoretical gain, while likely spiking actual churn beyond your 6% baseline due to "sticker shock" separate from pure elasticity.

**How This Fails:**
1.  **Elasticity Underestimation:** If elasticity is -0.5 or worse, you destroy net ARR.
2.  **Support Cost Variance:** If the $0.50/user cost scales non-linearly with price-pushback (more tickets, more negotiations), margins compress further.
3.  **Competitive Anchor:** Competitors currently anchored at $12 become the default alternative, accelerating churn beyond mathematical elasticity.

**Halt & Fallback Triggers:**
*   **Halt Trigger:** If pilot churn exceeds **8% monthly** (vs. 6% baseline) in the first 45 days post-announcement, or if net revenue per account drops below **$450/month** (current $460), freeze the rollout.
*   **Fallback Trigger:** If churn stays between 6-8%, pivot to a "Good-Better-Best" tiering model where the $15 price includes premium support/features, isolating the price hike to value-add rather than a base tax.
