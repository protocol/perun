2.  **Adoption Friction:** If the actual patient acceptance rate falls significantly below the estimated 70% (e.g., <50%), the workflow splits into parallel tracks (digital vs. manual), increasing rather than decreasing admin workload.
3.  **Margin Erosion:** The $0.10 per reminder cost scales linearly with volume; if the "no-show" reduction is less than predicted while reminder volume stays high, the unit economics of the reminder system turn negative.

*   **Stop Immediately:** If total monthly platform costs (fees + reminders) exceed **$6,000** (roughly double the baseline expectation due to unexpected volume or fee changes).
