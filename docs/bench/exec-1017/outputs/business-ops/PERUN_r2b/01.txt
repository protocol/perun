**Recommendation:** Proceed with the price increase to $119, but implement a "churn circuit breaker" effective immediately upon launch. The arithmetic supports the move: your current LTV ($4,320) vs. CAC ($1,200) yields a healthy 3.6:1 ratio. Even if the $20 increase causes a temporary 1% spike in churn (to 5%), the contribution margin from the remaining 95% of customers likely outweighs the lost revenue, provided your gross margins on the base service are above 60%. The projected $12.5M ARR lift assumes near-zero attrition due to price; while optimistic, the upside justifies the test given your strong unit economics.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Churn Spike >6%:** If monthly churn exceeds 6% for two consecutive months post-launch, the LTV collapses below $2,400 (at 6% churn, average life drops to ~16 months), destroying the 3:1 LTV:CAC safety margin and making the $1,200 acquisition cost unsustainable.
2.  **Competitor Undercutting:** If ClearDental launches a $10 discount program in Q4, your effective price advantage shrinks to only $10. If their feature set is comparable, price-sensitive segments may defect, accelerating churn beyond the 6% threshold.
3.  **Hidden Support Costs:** The $5 surcharge may not cover the marginal cost of support if the price hike triggers a flood of tier-downgrade requests or cancellation calls, increasing operational burn.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
If verified monthly churn exceeds **6%** in either October or November 2024, immediately pause the rollout for new customers and offer a "grandfathered" rate to existing at-risk practices to stabilize the base. Do not wait for Q1 data; the velocity of churn in the first 60 days is the leading indicator of long-term cohort failure.

**Note:** The $12.5M ARR projection requires ~52,000 retained/upgraded seat-months net of churn; validate this volume against your actual seat-count growth, not just practice count. ClearDental's potential reaction is an unknown variable; treat it as a high-probability risk in your sensitivity analysis, not a certainty.
