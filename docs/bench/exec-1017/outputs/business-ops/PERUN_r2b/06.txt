Your estimate of $28.8M assumes a 12-month term, but the contract is 24 months (corrected revenue: **$57.6M**). **Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Capital Insolvency:** The $3M budget covers only ~70 vehicles (2.5% of the fleet). Financing 1,930 vehicles at 6% APR on negative-cash-flow assets will spike interest expenses and breach covenants before the first lease matures.
2.  **Tax Credit Dependency:** If the 30% federal credit ($13,500/vehicle) is delayed, disallowed, or cannot be monetized immediately, your effective hardware cost rises, widening the loss per unit. Lane R (Tax/Securities): Confirm credit transferability and timing with a CPA; do not bank on it for liquidity.
3.  **Take-Up Rate Variance:** If driver adoption is <60% post-pilot, fixed inventory costs remain while revenue drops, accelerating burn.

**Halt Trigger:** Stop or renegotiate if **net cash burn exceeds $4.5M within the first 90 days** (indicating <15% cost recovery via credits/financing) OR if the **pilot take-up rate is <70% by September 2025**.
