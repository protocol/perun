**Recommendation:** Do not approve the $49 rollout yet; the math shows a likely revenue contraction unless the new tier reduces churn below 3% or drives conversion above 9%.

**The Arithmetic (Lane A)**
Your projected new Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) from the tier is **$58,800** (1,200 users × $49).
Your current MRR is **$10,000** ($120k ARR ÷ 12).
While the headline MRR grows, you flagged a double-count in cross-sell. The $12k expansion ($132k - $120k) is annual, not monthly. If you treated it as immediate monthly lift, that is an error. Corrected annualized projection: $120k (base) + $12k (expansion) = $132k ARR.
However, dropping price from $79 to $49 requires a **61% volume increase** just to break even on per-unit revenue. You are projecting 1,200 sign-ups from 20,000 trials (6%). If your current paid base is small, this volume might offset the price cut, but only if the 1,200 are *net new* and do not cannibalize existing $79 users.
**Critical Unknown:** You have not priced the California privacy compliance cost. If this requires legal counsel or engineering changes (Lane R), it will reduce your $2.1M cash runway immediately.

**How This Fails**
1.  **Cannibalization:** Existing $79 customers downgrade to $49, shrinking total revenue despite higher user count.
2.  **Compliance Shock:** Unpriced California privacy obligations (CCPA/CPRA) consume cash reserves, shortening the 12-month runway before the new revenue materializes.
3.  **Conversion Overestimate:** If the 6% conversion (1,200 users) drops to 4% (800 users), monthly revenue falls to $39,200, potentially failing to cover the incremental cost of the AI add-on.

**Halt & Pivot Triggers**
*   **Halt Trigger:** If legal counsel confirms the California privacy regime requires specific engineering changes costing >$50k upfront, pause rollout until funded.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If Day-30 conversion from trial to paid is <4% (fewer than 800 users) or if downgrade requests from existing customers exceed 5% of the current base, revert to the $79 price point or restrict the AI feature to a higher tier.
