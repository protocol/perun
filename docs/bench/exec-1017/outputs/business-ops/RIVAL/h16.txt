**Decision Memo**
**To:** Maya Patel, CFO
**From:** Pricing Strategy Lead
**Date:** October 24, 2025
**Subject:** Recommendation on Lumen Learning Corporate Seat Price Increase

**Recommendation: Proceed with the price increase to $229/seat effective July 1, 2026.**

The proposed 15% price hike is financially prudent given our strong growth trajectory (30% YoY) and low projected churn (10%). Even if the price increase causes churn to double to 20%—a severe stress test—the net revenue impact remains positive due to the immediate uplift on the retained base and new sales. With $3M in runway and a high CAC ($150k), maximizing Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) is critical to extending our runway without dilutive fundraising. The risk of Polyglot Corp matching our hike in August actually de-risks the move; if they follow, price sensitivity in the market will likely normalize, mitigating the feared 15% pipeline loss.

**Primary Failure Modes**

1.  **Churn Spike Beyond Model:** If annual churn exceeds 18% post-implementation, the revenue gain from the higher price point will be erased by seat contraction, negatively impacting cash flow.
2.  **Pipeline Contraction:** If Polyglot Corp *does not* raise prices in August and we lose more than 20% of the qualified pipeline due to price objections, our Q3/Q4 2026 growth targets will be missed, accelerating cash burn.
3.  **Renewal Friction:** Existing contracts at TechNova, GreenWave, and Apex may face friction upon renewal if the increase is applied indiscriminately rather than grandfathered or phased, potentially triggering early termination clauses or negotiation stalemates.

**Stop/Reverse Triggers**

We will halt the rollout or revert to the $199 price point if either of the following occurs within 90 days of implementation:
*   **Metric:** Monthly churn rate exceeds 2.5% (annualized >30%) for two consecutive months among affected accounts.
*   **Event:** Loss of any one of the three current enterprise accounts (TechNova, GreenWave, or Apex) directly attributed to the price increase during their renewal window.

**Note:** Ensure the increase applies only to new bookings and renewals after July 1, 2026, to honor existing contract terms.
**Note:** Monitor Polyglot Corp's August announcement closely; if they hold prices, prepare a value-add bundle to justify the premium.
