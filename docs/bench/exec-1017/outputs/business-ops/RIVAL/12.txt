The actual additional revenue would be approximately $3.5 million/year ($80 × 3,690 users × 12 months), not $24 million.

Given that you are raising prices by 67% for existing customers and targeting a higher price point for new ones, a static 6% churn assumption is unrealistic. A slight increase in churn to 8-9% would collapse the LTV, making the $150 CAC unsustainable.
