**Recommendation: Do not implement the blanket price increase to $5,500 on July 1, 2027.**

Proceeding with this hike jeopardizes your Series A readiness. While the model projects a 15% ARR lift, the risk profile is too high given the competitive landscape and your growth targets. With "SecureClass" rumored at $4,800, raising your price to $5,500 creates a $700 gap (14.5% premium) without confirmed differentiated value to justify it. If churn rises to 12% and new sign-ups drop by 40 schools annually (10 per quarter), your net new ARR turns negative. You would start 2028 with a smaller base, making the $1.5M ARR goal mathematically improbable without unrealistic acquisition spikes. Instead, maintain the $4,500 price for existing customers (grandfathering) and test the $5,500 price point *only* on new logos starting Q3 2027 to validate willingness to pay against SecureClass before touching your installed base.

**Three Biggest Ways This Change Could Fail:**

1.  **Competitive Displacement:** If SecureClass launches at $4,800 with comparable features, your $5,500 price makes you the expensive outlier. Schools facing budget constraints will switch during renewal, spiking churn beyond the projected 12% and causing immediate revenue contraction.
2.  **Acquisition Freeze:** A 10-school per quarter reduction in new sign-ups compounds rapidly. Over four quarters, you lose 40 new customers ($180k–$220k potential ARR). Combined with higher churn, this creates a "leaky bucket" scenario where you cannot grow fast enough to hit the $1.5M target for investors.
3.  **Valuation Destruction via Churn:** Investors scrutinize net revenue retention (NRR) heavily for Series A. If churn hits 12%, your NRR likely dips below 100%. Even if total ARR momentarily rises due to the hike, a declining retention rate signals a flawed product-market fit or pricing strategy, potentially lowering your valuation or stalling the round entirely.

**Stop/Pivot Metric:**

**If renewal churn exceeds 10% in any single month post-implementation or if new logo acquisition drops by more than 20% compared to the previous quarter's run rate.** Hitting either threshold indicates the market is rejecting the price relative to value/competition, requiring an immediate rollback to $4,500 for renewals and a strategic review of packaging.

*Note: Avoid grandfathering forever; use it only as a 12-month transition to migrate legacy users to new value tiers.*
