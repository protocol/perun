**Recommendation:** Proceed with the $99 to $119 price increase on October 1, 2024, but **immediately suspend the assumption** that churn will remain flat at 4%. Your current 4% monthly churn is already high for a mature SaaS product; assuming zero elasticity on a 20% price hike is a critical modeling error that risks overstating your $12.5M ARR projection. Instead of betting on stability, execute a **staggered rollout**: apply the new price only to *new* customers in October to validate willingness-to-pay, then migrate 10% of the existing base in November. This contains the blast radius if ClearDental launches a discount program or if churn spikes.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Churn Spike:** If monthly churn jumps from 4% to 6%, you will lose ~312 practices per month instead of 208. At an LTV of ~$4,300, this accelerates revenue erosion, potentially negating the gain from the $20 hike within 6–9 months.
2.  **Competitor Reaction:** If ClearDental undercuts you by $10 in Q4 while you raise prices, the perceived value gap widens to $30/month. Without a corresponding feature announcement or value justification, this creates a prime switching opportunity for price-sensitive SMB practices.
3.  **Support Cost Hidden Drag:** Your model includes a $5 surcharge, but price hikes often trigger increased support ticket volume ("bill shock" inquiries). If this spikes operational costs or requires manual retention saves, your net margin improvement will shrink.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
As you noted, if **monthly churn exceeds 6% for two consecutive months** post-implementation (November and December data), halt the rollout to the remaining 90% of the base immediately. Do not wait for year-end. At >6% churn, your customer lifetime drops from 36 months to roughly 20 months, destroying the unit economics required to justify the $1,200 CAC. In this scenario, pivot to a "value-add" strategy: revert the base price to $99 but introduce a mandatory "Premium Support" tier at $20 for new features, effectively decoupling the hike from the core subscription to reduce friction.

*Note: Ensure your communication emphasizes specific value shipped in the last 12 months to justify the increase, rather than presenting it as an inflation adjustment.*
