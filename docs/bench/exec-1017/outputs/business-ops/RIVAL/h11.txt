**Recommendation: Delay Launch and Re-price**

Do not launch next quarter. The current model relies on a fragile assumption: that 20% of members will pay an extra $15/month without any evidence of willingness to pay, while ignoring the risk of "cannibalization" (users dropping studio memberships to keep only the cheaper app). With only 10 months of cash runway, spending more on a launch that might accelerate churn is an existential risk.

**Action Plan:**
1.  **Run a Willingness-to-Pay Test:** Before writing more code, survey 500 members using a Van Westendorp price sensitivity meter. Test if $15 is acceptable or if it triggers cancellation of the core $120 membership.
2.  **Pilot with a Cap:** Launch a beta to only 5% of members (420 users) with a hard cap. Measure if app usage correlates with *retention* or *cancellation* of studio access.
3.  **Re-evaluate Pricing:** If the app is a standalone product, $15 may be too low to cover health data compliance costs. If it is a retention tool, it should perhaps be included in the $120 fee to prevent churn, rather than sold as an add-on.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
*   **Adoption Failure:** Less than 5% of members subscribe, failing to cover even basic server costs, let alone the $250k sunk cost.
*   **Cannibalization Churn:** Users cancel their $120 studio membership to keep only the $15 app, causing a net revenue loss of $105 per converted user.
*   **Regulatory/Technical:** Unexpected compliance costs for health data (HIPAA/GDPR) exceed the $15/user margin, turning every subscriber into a liability.
*   **Distraction Risk:** The launch team diverts focus from core studio operations during a cash-constrained period, impacting the 8,400 existing members.

**Stop/Change Triggers:**
*   **Churn Threshold:** If more than **2%** of app subscribers cancel their core studio membership within the first 60 days, halt the rollout immediately.
*   **Revenue Threshold:** If net new revenue (App Revenue minus Lost Studio Revenue) is below **$5,000/month** after 3 months, pivot the app to a free retention feature or kill the project.
*   **Compliance Cost:** If legal/compliance estimates exceed **$50,000** annually, do not proceed without raising the $500k capital first.

*Note: Do not raise the $500k at 10% equity ($5M valuation) until you have validated that the app prevents churn rather than causing it; raising now dilutes heavily on an unproven bet.*
