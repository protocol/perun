**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased pilot launch** limited to 40% of daily appointments for 90 days, rather than a full rollout. While the projected annual net benefit of approximately $418,000 (driven by $275,000 in labor savings and ~$263,000 in recovered no-show revenue, less $24,000 in software fees and $105,000 in reminder costs) is compelling, the assumption that a 15% throughput increase translates directly to revenue is fragile due to potential physician bottlenecks. A phased approach validates the 70% patient acceptance rate and operational capacity without risking systemic disruption.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Physician Bottleneck:** The 15% increase in checked-in patients exceeds provider capacity, causing wait times to spike and patient satisfaction to plummet, nullifying revenue gains.
2.  **Adoption Friction:** Patient acceptance falls significantly below the 70% estimate, resulting in high support volumes that erode the projected $275,000 labor savings.
3.  **Margin Erosion:** The cost of reminders and service fees outweighs recovered revenue if the no-show reduction fails to meet the 4% target.

**Stop/Re-evaluate Triggers:**
*   **No-Show Improvement:** Less than a **2.5% absolute drop** in no-show rates (e.g., moving from 12% to only 9.5%) after 60 days.
*   **Cost Threshold:** Total monthly operational costs (fees + reminders + incremental support labor) exceed **$8,500** in any single month.
*   **Throughput Realization:** Less than **5% actual increase** in completed visits despite higher check-in volumes, indicating a provider bottleneck.
