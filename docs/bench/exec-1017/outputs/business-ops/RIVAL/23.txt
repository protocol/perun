**Recommendation: Do not proceed with the price increase yet; proceed only with the feature launch and measure conversion lift first.**

Your proposal conflates two distinct variables: price elasticity and feature value. By raising the price to $12.99 simultaneously with launching the "investment tracker," you will be unable to determine if a dip in conversion is due to price resistance or a failure of the new feature to resonate. Furthermore, your arithmetic contains a critical oversight regarding churn impact.

**How This Could Fail:**

1.  **The Churn Multiplier:** You currently have ~176,000 paid users (8% of 2.2M). At $9.99, you generate ~$1.76M/month. If you raise the price by 30% ($3.00), you only need a **~23% increase in churn** (from 5% to ~6.15%) to wipe out all incremental revenue gains, assuming conversion stays flat. If conversion drops even slightly due to price friction, you become cash-flow negative on the existing base immediately.
2.  **The Compliance Cliff:** You have $1.2M in runway. A "few hundred thousand" in unexpected CCPA compliance costs could consume 20–30% of your remaining capital in a single quarter. Increasing prices on a consumer budgeting app during a privacy regulatory crackdown may trigger reputational backlash, accelerating churn exactly when you need stability.
3.  **CAC vs. LTV Distortion:** Your current LTV ($119.90) vs. CAC ($30) is healthy (~4:1). However, if the price hike increases churn to 7%, your LTV drops to ~$86. If acquisition costs rise because the higher price point lowers free-to-paid conversion efficiency, your unit economics could flip from profitable to unsustainable within two quarters.

**Stop/Change Course Metric:**

Implement a **A/B test on 10% of new signups only** at the $12.99 price point with the new feature.
*   **STOP immediately** if the paid churn rate on the test group exceeds **6.5%** in the first 30 days or if the free-to-paid conversion rate does not increase by at least **0.2%** (partial validation of your 0.5% hypothesis).
*   **DO NOT roll out to existing users** until you have 90 days of data proving the new feature alone drives the conversion lift, independent of price pressure.

*Note: Isolate the variable (feature) before changing the constraint (price). Protect your runway by validating compliance costs before altering revenue models.*
