**Decision Memo: Municipal Parking Permit Price Increase**

**Recommendation:** Proceed with the price increase to $18/permit/month, effective immediately for all renewals on or after January 1, 2025. Do not apply retroactively to active contracts before their renewal date to avoid breach of contract risks. The projected gross revenue uplift of $270,000 ($1.62M vs $1.35M) provides a necessary buffer against the looming $200k–$6M regulatory compliance risk, provided churn remains below the modeled 2%. However, you must explicitly exclude the $1.5M historical implementation fee from this specific decision model; those are sunk costs and irrelevant to the marginal profitability of the price hike.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Regulatory Cost Explosion:** If the Jan 1, 2025 state data-privacy regulation mandates a full API redesign, the cost could reach $200k per city ($6M total). At the current volume, even the increased revenue ($1.62M) would fail to cover these one-time compliance costs, potentially causing a net loss of ~$4.4M for the year unless fees are passed directly to cities.
2.  **Churn Sensitivity Underestimation:** The model assumes only 2% churn (less than 1 city). If the price hike triggers 4% churn (1–2 cities leaving), you lose ~$54k–$108k in annual recurring revenue. While this seems small, losing key reference cities could damage sales momentum for new municipal contracts, compounding the revenue miss.
3.  **Budget Cycle Misalignment:** The model assumes a 5% municipal budget increase for tech. If inflation or political pressure freezes city budgets at 0% growth, your 20% price hike ($15 to $18) may be rejected outright by city councils, forcing a last-minute rollback that undermines pricing authority and delays revenue recognition.

**Abort/Pivot Trigger:**
Initiate an immediate pivot (rollback to $15 or offer a capped "compliance surcharge" instead of a base hike) if **churn exceeds 4% (more than 1 city)** during the Q1 2025 renewal cycle OR if the state regulator issues guidance before Dec 31, 2024, confirming that the API redesign is mandatory and cannot be billed as a separate pass-through line item.

*Note: Ensure legal reviews all 30 contracts for "price adjustment" clauses before sending renewal notices.*
*Do not present the $1.5M implementation fee as a variable cost in the board deck; it distorts the marginal analysis.*
