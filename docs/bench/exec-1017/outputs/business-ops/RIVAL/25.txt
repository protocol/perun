1.  **Churn Explosion:** If price sensitivity is higher than the A/B test indicated, monthly churn could jump from 6% to over 10%, eroding the student base faster than the higher ARPU can compensate, leading to negative net revenue retention.
2.  **Marketplace Liquidity Crisis:** Since this is a two-sided market, a 10% drop in student bookings directly reduces tutor earnings. If tutors see their income fall significantly, they may leave the platform, reducing supply quality and causing a secondary wave of student churn.
3.  **Margin Erosion via Fixed Costs:** The $2 platform fee helps, but if the booking volume drops sharper than 10% due to network effects, your total contribution margin may shrink rather than grow, failing to offset the impending EU tax liability.

**Stop/Rethink Trigger:**
Halt the rollout immediately if **monthly revenue growth turns negative for two consecutive months** post-implementation, or if **monthly churn exceeds 8.5%** among the affected cohort. These thresholds indicate that the loss in volume and retention is outpacing the value captured by the price hike, threatening your five-month runway.
