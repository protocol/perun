**Recommendation: Do not launch the full subscription model yet.**

Instead, initiate a "Pre-Subscription Pilot" immediately. Launch the $500/year pricing and three-year contract structure only to a controlled group of 50–100 strategic customers who explicitly agree to beta-test the firmware. Secure the commitments and cash upfront if possible, but frame the telemetry features as "pending regulatory finalization." This allows you to validate willingness to pay and lock in early ARR without exposing the entire base to a 6–18 month regulatory delay that could breach contracts or damage trust. Wait for EU clearance before scaling sales to the full 10,000-unit target.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Regulatory Breach & Contract Default:** If you sell full subscriptions now and EU clearance takes 18 months, you will be collecting revenue for a service you legally cannot deliver. This creates immediate refund liabilities, potential fines, and catastrophic reputational damage with industrial clients who rely on compliance.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse from Churn Mismatch:** You are modeling 8% churn based on "industry SaaS averages," but hardware-enabled subscriptions often face different dynamics. If the firmware creates friction or if customers perceive the $500 recurring fee as a "tax" on owned hardware, churn could spike to 20%+. At $2,000 CAC and $350 gross profit per year ($500 price - $150 COGS), high churn destroys LTV before you recover acquisition costs.
3.  **Sales Incentive Misalignment:** Your eight reps earn $120k salaries. If the shift to subscription reduces their immediate commissions (compared to booking large device sales) or complicates the sale with regulatory caveats, they will deprioritize the new model. Without a revised comp plan, the 10,000-unit target is mathematically impossible regardless of market demand.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:**

**Metric:** If pilot customer churn exceeds **3% in the first 90 days** post-firmware activation, or if **regulatory clearance is not granted within 12 months**.
**Action:** If churn is high, pivot to a "Hybrid License" model (one-time fee + optional support) immediately. If regulation stalls past 12 months, pause all new subscription sales and revert to pure device sales with a paid support add-on to avoid contract breaches.

*Note: The $3M net profit target assumes flawless execution; given the regulatory variance, treat this as an upside case, not a baseline.*
*Ensure your legal team reviews the "pending firmware" contract language before the pilot begins.*
