**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with a blanket price increase to $25/month at this time. Instead, implement the $15 "Premium Events" add-on immediately for all existing users as an opt-in, and test the base price increase ($20 → $25) only on *new* customers starting June 1st.

Your current monthly churn of 7% is already critically high (indicating a potential retention crisis rather than a pricing opportunity). In SaaS, healthy churn is typically 2–4% for SMBs; at 7%, you are losing nearly half your base annually. Raising prices on a cohort that is already leaving at this rate will almost certainly spike churn beyond your break-even point, destroying the MRR gains you anticipate. Furthermore, introducing a cost uncertainty (EU data residency) without a defined number makes a simultaneous price hike financially reckless; if hosting costs rise significantly, you could face a "double squeeze" of higher expenses and lower revenue.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Churn Explosion:** A price hike on a 7% churn base could push monthly attrition to 12–15%. If churn exceeds 10%, the loss of recurring revenue from cancellations will outweigh the $5 uplift from remaining users within 45 days.
2.  **Add-on Cannibalization:** If the "Premium Events" features are perceived as essential rather than optional, forcing users to pay extra may be viewed as a de facto price hike, triggering resentment and cancellation even among those who don't upgrade.
3.  **Margin Compression:** If the EU data-residency compliance costs exceed $6,000/month (the projected gain from the base hike), the strategy results in higher operational costs with no net revenue benefit, especially if churn reduces the total seat count.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers:**
Initiate an immediate rollback to the $20 price point for any affected customers or halt the new pricing trial if, after 60 days:
*   **Monthly churn rises above 9%** (a ~30% relative increase from baseline).
*   **Net MRR growth is negative** for two consecutive months (meaning lost revenue from churn > gains from price/add-ons).
*   **Legal confirms EU compliance costs will exceed $8,000/month**, necessitating a revised unit economics model before any price changes.
