**Recommendation:** Do not launch "Rapid Credit Line" next quarter. The proposed structure destroys margin without a validated volume offset, and the unpriced regulatory risk creates an unacceptable exposure. Specifically, reducing the rate by 100 basis points while eliminating the 1.2% origination fee removes roughly $2,400 in immediate revenue per loan, which the $300 processing saving cannot recover. With defaults rising to 2% and no increased risk reserve, the product likely operates at a net loss on a risk-adjusted basis before accounting for the new $15,000 quarterly technology overhead.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Halt:** The state regulator rejects the reduced spread as unsustainable or predatory under usury interpretations, forcing an immediate product suspension and reputational damage.
2.  **Adverse Selection Spiral:** The streamlined approval and lower rate attract higher-risk borrowers who were previously rejected or priced out, pushing actual default rates significantly above the projected 2% ceiling.
3.  **Portfolio Cannibalization:** The projected 20% migration of existing customers occurs, but the volume of *new* loans falls short of 150/quarter, resulting in a net reduction of total portfolio yield and revenue.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers:**
*   **Default Rate:** Actual defaults exceed **2.2%** in any single month post-launch.
*   **Regulatory Action:** Receipt of any formal inquiry or objection from the state regulator regarding the rate structure or reserve adequacy.
*   **Volume Shortfall:** New loan origination falls below **90 loans** (60% of target) by the end of Q3 2024.

*Note: A revised proposal must include a specific risk-reserve adjustment and a formal pre-launch consultation with the state regulator.*
