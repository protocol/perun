**Recommendation:** Do not launch the "Premium Ad-Insertion" tier as a broad rollout now. Instead, initiate a controlled beta with 50–100 hand-picked customers to validate the 20% upgrade assumption and quantify the hidden operational costs before committing to a full release.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Cannibalization & Margin Erosion:** If the historical 3% migration rate holds, you will lose ~$1.3M in existing ARR ($350 × 12,450 × 0.03) while simultaneously absorbing $180k in annual support costs and variable CPM fees, potentially resulting in net negative revenue growth despite the projected $1.2M uplift.
2.  **Regulatory Obsolescence:** With 22% of advertisers based in Europe, the October 2026 privacy regulations could render the core value proposition (targeted ad insertion) illegal or severely restricted for a significant portion of your revenue base, turning this new tier into a stranded asset requiring expensive re-engineering.
3.  **Support Capacity Bottleneck:** The projected $15,000/month support increase assumes linear scaling; if "higher-touch" service triggers complex integration tickets, costs could spike exponentially, destroying the unit economics of the $500 price point.

**Pivot/Stop Metric:**
Halt or pivot the initiative if **Net ARR Growth** (New Tier Revenue minus Cannibalized Standard Revenue minus Direct Support/CPM Costs) is **under 5%** after 12 months, or if **Churn** among Premium adopters exceeds **6% per quarter**.
