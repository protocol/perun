Proceed with the price increase to $14,000/seat effective January 1, 2025, but implement a **"Staggered Rollout with Grace Period"** rather than a universal immediate hike. Apply the new pricing immediately to all *new* contracts signed after December 1, 2024. If the average deal slippage pushes closings from Q1 into Q2 or Q3, the anticipated cash infusion to fund the module development will be delayed, potentially causing a liquidity crunch despite the higher theoretical ARR.

 Halt the rollout for existing customers immediately if **two or more** current clients formally notify you of non-renewal or cancellation specifically citing price as the primary driver before January 15, 2025. Given the high contract value, losing more than one client (2% of the base) exceeds your historical annual churn rate in a single month, signaling that the price elasticity is significantly worse than modeled and threatening the core revenue base required to fund operations.
