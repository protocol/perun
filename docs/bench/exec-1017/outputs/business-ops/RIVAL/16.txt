**Recommendation:** Do not launch the $25/month subscription model yet. While the unit economics appear viable on paper ($25 price vs. $120 CAC is attractive if retention holds), the unquantified "low" compliance certification fee for Westhaven regulations creates an unacceptable risk to your margin structure. Furthermore, raising the price from $19 to $25 while assuming zero impact on churn or conversion contradicts basic pricing elasticity principles; you are betting on a 15% revenue lift while ignoring the high probability that a 32% price hike will depress your 8% conversion rate. Validate the exact compliance cost and run a limited A/B test on the price increase before a full rollout.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1. **Regulatory Margin Erosion:** The "low" compliance fee turns out to be a fixed annual cost that, when amortized across a slower-than-expected subscriber base, eliminates the profit margin per user entirely.
2. **Conversion Collapse:** The price increase from $19 to $25 causes the free-to-paid conversion rate to drop below 5%, negating the projected 15% revenue lift and reducing total monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
3. **Channel Cannibalization:** The self-serve tool distracts the 70 monthly high-value leads generated by your $9,000 ad spend, causing them to self-serve at $25/month rather than converting to the $1,200 policy value through your standard sales motion.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
 Halt the rollout immediately if the pilot shows a conversion rate below 6% or if the final compliance certification fee exceeds $1,500 per month (equivalent to the revenue of ~60 subscribers).

*Note: Your assumption that churn will not increase after a significant price hike is the single weakest link in this forecast and requires validation.*
