Defer Pulse and Atlas until Vantage's subscription mechanics are stable.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Financial:** The unfunded $300k training gap causes delayed adoption or support failures, eroding the projected 20% YoY growth.
2.  **Market:** Unknown price elasticity triggers a signup collapse if the "Premium" tier is priced >10% above current expectations; the 12% churn assumption proves optimistic if value isn't immediately perceived.
3.  **Technical:** The 60k man-hours allocated to the API layer are insufficient to support concurrent data streams from all three future products, requiring costly re-architecture during Phase 2.

**Halt & Fallback Triggers**
*   **Halt Trigger:** If Vantage's **month-over-month churn exceeds 5%** for two consecutive months post-launch, or if **signup conversion drops >15%** upon revealing the Premium price.
    *   *Action:* Freeze Pulse/Atlas development; re-engineer value proposition or pricing model.
*   **Fallback Trigger:** If the **$300k training budget is not secured by 30 days pre-launch**.
    *   *Action:* Scale back launch to "Basic" tier only, delaying Premium features until funding is resolved.

**Re-based Assumption**
The 20% YoY adoption growth is re-based to **10%** for planning purposes due to the unverified price elasticity. The $3M–$5M ARR targets should be treated as upside cases, not baseline commitments, until the Premium tier price is validated via a small-scale A/B test before full rollout.

**Decision:** Proceed with Vantage only, conditional on securing the $300k training budget and running a 2-week price sensitivity test on the Premium tier.
