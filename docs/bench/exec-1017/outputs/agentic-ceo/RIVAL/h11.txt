**SecureClass Rollout Plan: Executive Summary**
**To:** Maya Patel | **Date:** 24 Oct 2024 | **Budget:** $160,000

**1. Recommended Order of Work**
1.  **Compliance Audit (Immediate):** Engage legal counsel to define FERPA requirements. This is the critical path; development cannot finalize data handling protocols without it.
2.  **Development ($120k):** Build the interactive phishing module parallel to audit findings. Prioritize data isolation features required for school districts.
3.  **Curriculum Creation ($30k):** Develop content once core module functionality is stable to ensure alignment.
4.  **Pilot Execution (15 May 2025):** Deploy to 5 districts (2,400 students). Validate the 60-day adoption assumption.
5.  **Pricing & Communications ($10k):** Finalize the 30% discount structure and launch marketing only after successful pilot validation.

**2. Top Three Failure Modes**
*   **Compliance Blocker:** FERPA audit reveals architecture changes needed that exceed the remaining budget or delay the Oct 1 launch beyond the school year start.
*   **Adoption Friction:** Districts fail to adopt within 60 days despite the discount, indicating the sales cycle is longer than anticipated or the discount is insufficient.
*   **Technical Scalability:** The module fails under concurrent load during the pilot, eroding trust before the full launch.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **STOP:** FERPA audit cost exceeds **$8,000** (leaving insufficient buffer for remediation).
*   **PIVOT:** Pilot enrollment drops below **1,200 students** (50% of target) by 15 June 2025, requiring a shift from B2B district sales to direct-to-parent or charter school models.
*   **STOP:** Critical security vulnerability found during pilot that cannot be patched within 14 days.

**4. Mandatory Human Sign-Offs**
*   **Legal Sign-Off:** Required on the FERPA compliance report before any student data is ingested during the pilot.
*   **CEO Sign-Off:** Required on the final pricing model and discount structure before the marketing push begins.
*   **CTO Sign-Off:** Required on security penetration test results before the October 1 public launch.

***

*Note: The $160k budget assumes the FERPA audit stays under the $8k trigger; if it approaches $5k, a contingency review is advised. All dates are hard stops aligned with the academic calendar.*
