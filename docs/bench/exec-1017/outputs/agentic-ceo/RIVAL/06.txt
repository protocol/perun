**Recommendation: Launch Vantage First**

Launch the **Vantage sensor** immediately as the sole Phase 1 product. Despite having the lowest total ARR potential ($3M) among the three, it offers the fastest path to revenue validation with the lowest complexity risk. The Pulse analyzer and Atlas hub should follow in sequence only after Vantage stabilizes.

**Strategic Rationale:**
*   **Revenue Velocity:** Vantage captures $3M ARR by June 2025, providing immediate cash flow to fund the heavier integration costs of the subsequent products.
*   **Risk Containment:** Launching all three simultaneously spreads the 150k engineering hours too thin, risking delays across the board. Vantage allows the team to perfect the billing integration (45k hours) and API layer (60k hours) on a single product line before scaling to the more complex Pulse and Atlas systems.
*   **Financial Clarity:** The current budget shows a $300k gap for training. Launching sequentially allows you to fund Phase 2 (Pulse) training costs using Phase 1 (Vantage) revenue, rather than seeking immediate additional capital.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Technical:** The 60k-hour API layer estimate is insufficient to support the data throughput of all three devices simultaneously, causing system-wide latency that triggers churn before the Atlas launch.
2.  **Financial:** The unaccounted $300k training budget creates a cash crunch mid-integration, forcing a pause in development when the billing module is only partially complete.
3.  **Market:** The unknown price elasticity for the "Premium" tier results in a >15% drop in signup rates upon the 10% price test, rendering the projected ARR mathematically impossible across all three lines.

**Kill/Re-order Metric:**
**Stop or Re-order if:** Vantage's **net new subscription growth is negative for two consecutive months** post-launch, OR if **churn exceeds 1.5% monthly** (annualized >18%) during the first quarter.

This specific threshold indicates that either the price point is fundamentally broken or the value proposition does not support the recurring cost model, invalidating the financial assumptions for Pulse and Atlas.
