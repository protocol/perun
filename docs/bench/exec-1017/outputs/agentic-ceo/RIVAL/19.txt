## Critical Corrections & Risks
1.  **Math Error:** Your pilot adoption calculation is incorrect. 30% of 200 sites is **60 sites**, not 80. This overestimates early traction by 33%.
2.  **Budget Risk:** If the API license hits $15k/month, annual operational cost increases by $84k. This must be validated before committing engineering resources.
3.  **Timeline Risk:** The 45-day build estimate assumes zero friction. With a hard October 15 deadline, any slip in the API contract or security review jeopardizes the beta.

## Failure Modes
*   **The "Leaky Bucket":** We launch the beta, but the API costs force a pricing model customers reject, resulting in high churn despite the product working.
*   **The "Security Brake":** Security review discovers architectural flaws in the data ingestion pipeline late in the cycle, pushing the beta past October 15.
*   **The "Vapor Value":** The module functions correctly, but the predicted labor savings do not materialize for customers, invalidating the 5% churn reduction hypothesis.

## Kill Switch Criteria
**Stop or pivot the project immediately if:**
1.  **API Cost > $12k/month:** If the fixed license exceeds $12k/month, the unit economics likely fail for mid-size manufacturers.
2.  **Pilot Adoption < 40 sites:** If fewer than 40 of the 200 pilot sites activate the beta within the first 30 days, the product-market fit is weak.
3.  **Forecast Accuracy < 85%:** If the engine cannot predict labor needs with at least 85% accuracy against historical data during internal testing, the core value prop is broken.
