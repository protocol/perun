**Glowspire "Nightfall" DLC: Q4 2024 Execution Plan**

**Recommended Sequence**
1.  **Secure Audio Licensing (Week 1):** Immediately negotiate the third-party audio fee. This is the critical path blocker; without a fixed cost, the $250k budget cannot be validated.
2.  **Parallel Build & GTM Strategy (Weeks 2–10):** Engineering and Art begin "Nightfall" production while Marketing finalizes pricing models based on the confirmed audio cost. Do not wait for beta to define pricing; use historical data adjusted for the smaller user base.
3.  **Closed Beta & Integration (Weeks 11–13):** Deploy to 5,000 players for the 12-day window. Reserve the final 3 days strictly for critical bug fixes and feedback integration.

**Financial Reality Check**
*   **Known Costs:** $80k (Eng) + $45k (Art) + $30k (Beta/QA) = **$155,000**.
*   **Remaining for Audio:** $95,000. If the license exceeds this, the plan breaks.
*   **Revenue Projection:** 15% of 600k users @ $14.99 = 90,000 units × $14.99 ≈ **$1.35M**.
*   **Risk:** The 15% conversion assumption is aggressive given the user base dropped from 40M to 0.6M. If engagement has decayed proportionally, real conversion could be <5%, yielding only ~$450k.

**Three Main Failure Modes**
1.  **Budget Breach:** The audio licensing fee exceeds $95,000, forcing a scope cut or additional capital raise mid-Q4.
2.  **Conversion Collapse:** The active user base is less monetizable than the legacy 40M cohort; achieving <5% conversion fails to cover Q4 operational burn.
3.  **Beta Churn:** The 12-day window reveals game-breaking bugs or negative sentiment, requiring a delay that pushes launch into Q1 2025.

**Abort/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Audio Cost > $100,000:** Pivot to a simplified audio solution or defer DLC.
*   **Beta Net Negative Sentiment:** If >10% of beta users request refunds or report game-breaking bugs, halt launch for a full remediation sprint.
*   **Pre-order Conversion < 3%:** If early signaling (wishlists/pre-orders) tracks below 3% of the active base two weeks pre-launch, pivot marketing spend to retention campaigns for the core game instead of DLC acquisition.
