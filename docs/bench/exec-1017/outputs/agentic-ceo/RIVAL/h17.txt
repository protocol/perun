**Cobalt Cycles Rental Fleet Launch Plan**

**1. Order of Work (Build First vs. Defer)**
*   **Build First (Critical Path):** Secure Riverton regulatory fees and insurance premiums immediately; these are the only unknowns threatening the $750k budget. Deploy a manual "white-glove" operations model in Metroville only for the first 30 days to validate the 70% utilization assumption before spending on software automation or expanding to Oakridge/Riverton.
*   **Defer:** Full three-city simultaneous launch, custom mobile app features beyond basic unlocking, and marketing spend in Oakridge/Riverton. Do not purchase the full 135 bikes until the Metroville pilot confirms maintenance costs are under $15/bike/month.

**2. Required Human Sign-offs**
*   **Finance:** CFO approval required once actual insurance/regulatory costs are known if they exceed 10% of the allocated budget.
*   **Legal:** Sign-off on liability waivers specifically for the 12-minute average ride duration before any bike hits the street.
*   **Operations:** Country Manager must certify the maintenance workflow can handle 5% daily vandalism/breakage rate before scaling past 20 bikes.

**3. Top Three Failure Modes**
*   **Regulatory Blockade:** Riverton permits take >60 days (vs. 30-day assumption), burning cash on idle inventory and staff while delaying revenue.
*   **Utilization Collapse:** Actual utilization drops below 50% due to seasonality or competition, extending break-even from 6 months to 18+ months, causing a cash flow crisis.
*   **Maintenance Bleed:** Vandalism and repair costs exceed $40/bike/month (unpriced in current model), turning profitable units into loss leaders within 90 days.

**4. Pivot/Stop Trigger**
*   **Halt Condition:** If average daily utilization per bike remains below **45% for two consecutive months** OR if **monthly maintenance/vandalism costs exceed $35 per bike**.
*   **Action:** Immediately freeze deployment in Oakridge and Riverton. Shift strategy from "scale fast" to "unit economics repair," focusing solely on increasing ride frequency or reducing loss rates in Metroville before spending further capital.

***

**Note:** This plan prioritizes validating the unpriced risks (maintenance/insurance) over speed. Do not commit the full $750k until the Metroville manual pilot proves the unit economics hold.
