### PowerPack Q4 Rollout Plan

**Executive Summary**
*   **Launch Date:** 15 November 2024
*   **Budget Status:** **$58,000 Remaining** (Total Costs: $242k vs. $300k Budget). The previous estimate of $85k remaining was incorrect; it failed to account for $30k packaging and $12k legal fees.
*   **Primary Goal:** 5,000 kits sold in Month 1 ($240k Revenue).

**1. Recommended Work Stream Order**
Prioritize based on the Critical Path to the November 15 launch:
1.  **Supplier Negotiations (Weeks 1–2):** *Critical Path.* You cannot finalize recipes or calculate accurate unit economics without locked protein costs and supply guarantees. The 5% COGS increase must be validated immediately.
2.  **Recipe Finalization & Lab Certification (Weeks 1–4):** *Longest Lead Time.* Start immediately alongside negotiations. Lab certification is a hard gate for launch; any failure here delays the date.
3.  **E-commerce & Subscription Integration (Weeks 3–5):** Can begin once recipe specs are stable. Ensure the platform handles the new pricing tier before marketing drives traffic.
4.  **Packaging & Legal (Parallel):** Execute concurrently with tech build to ensure assets are ready for the site launch.

**2. Main Failure Modes**
*   **Certification Delay:** If the lab misses the 4-week window, the launch date slips regardless of tech readiness.
*   **Margin Erosion:** The combination of a 5% COGS increase and potential churn from the $48 price point could render the unit economics negative if conversion does not hit the +10% target.
*   **Budget Sufficiency:** With only $58k remaining for marketing, achieving 5,000 sales requires a highly efficient CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) of ≤$11.60. If CAC exceeds $12, you will miss the volume target or burn budget.

**3. Halt / Re-prioritization Metric**
**Metric:** **Week-One Sales Volume < 2,000 kits.**
*   **Action:** If sales are under 2,000 in the first 7 days, **halt all paid marketing spend immediately.**
*   **Reasoning:** At this pace, you will not hit 5,000 units without exceeding the remaining $58k budget. Use the pause to A/B test pricing or messaging before deploying remaining funds.

**Secondary Watch Metric:** **Churn Rate > 8% in Month 1.** If early subscribers cancel immediately due to the price point, the long-term LTV model is broken, requiring a pricing strategy review.

***

*Note: The 15% prep time reduction is a strong marketing hook but should be treated as a conversion booster, not a revenue guarantee. Validate this claim with user testing before finalizing ad copy.*
