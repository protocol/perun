**Recommendation for Board Presentation (Oct 24)**

**Strategic Stance:** Proceed with a **Staggered "Downtown-First" Rollout** rather than a simultaneous three-store launch. Prioritize the Downtown location to validate the "Pulse" concept and hardware integration before committing full capital to Riverside and Hillcrest.

**Proposed Order of Work:**
1.  **Renovation (Immediate):** Secure Downtown first. Pause Riverside finalization until the $300k permitting fee is explicitly confirmed or denied in writing; treat this as a critical path risk.
2.  **Hardware Licensing (Parallel):** Negotiate the pilot licensing cost immediately. Do not sign a chain-wide deal until the per-unit cost is verified against the Downtown pilot data.
3.  **Hiring (T-Minus 8 Weeks):** Hire the core lead instructors for Downtown first. Delay the remaining hires until the Downtown opening date is fixed to avoid burn rate on idle staff.
4.  **Marketing (T-Minus 4 Weeks):** Launch marketing sequentially per location to manage trial volume, ensuring the 20% conversion assumption is stress-tested against actual operational capacity.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Capital Overrun:** The unconfirmed $300k Riverside permitting fee is real, creating an immediate $300k liquidity gap that stalls construction or forces a reduction in hardware quality.
2.  **Conversion Collapse:** Actual trial-to-membership conversion falls below 12% (vs. the 20% model), rendering the $1.2M revenue projection unachievable and extending the break-even point beyond 18 months.
3.  **Hardware Integration Delay:** Licensing negotiations or technical embedding of heart-rate monitors delay the "Pulse" launch, causing the marketing campaign to promote a feature not ready at opening, damaging brand credibility.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
If the Downtown location achieves **<10% conversion** from free trial to paid membership after the first 30 days of operation, immediately **pause construction on Hillcrest** and **renegotiate the Riverside scope** to a lower-cost model. Do not proceed with the full chain rollout until the unit economics are proven at the pilot site.

***

*Note: The $300k permitting uncertainty must be resolved before any further funds are released for Riverside. The hardware licensing cost remains the single largest variable in the margin model.*
