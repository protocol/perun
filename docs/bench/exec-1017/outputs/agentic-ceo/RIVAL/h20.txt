**Subject:** Executive Plan: Tiered Membership Launch (Target: June 30, 2024)

### **1. Strategic Recommendation: Order of Work & Deferrals**
**Recommendation:** Adopt a "Compliance-First, Feature-Lite" sequence. We must front-load the legal data-handling sign-off and the privacy law contingency before any UI redesign begins. The $120k UI budget should be restricted to the membership selection flow only; advanced dashboard features are deferred to post-launch to protect the June 30 date.

**Execution Sequence:**
1.  **Immediate (Now – April 15):** Legal sign-off on data handling and development of the "contingency consent flow" for the July 1 privacy law. *Human sign-off mandatory here before code commit.*
2.  **Core Build (April 16 – May 30):** Backend tier logic and API integration (syncing starts May 15 per partner readiness).
3.  **UI & Beta (June 1 – June 20):** Restricted UI sprint followed immediately by beta testing with 4,500 DAUs.
4.  **Measurement & Launch (June 21 – June 30):** A/B test validation and full rollout.

**Deferred:** Advanced user analytics dashboards and secondary marketing campaigns (preserving ~$25k of the marketing budget for post-launch optimization).

### **2. Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Whiplash:** The new state privacy law requires a consent architecture fundamentally incompatible with our current database schema, forcing a rebuild of the onboarding stack in June.
2.  **API Integration Latency:** The partner museum's API (due May 15) lacks documented rate limits or authentication standards, causing sync failures that invalidate the beta test data.
3.  **Cannibalization without Conversion:** The A/B test reveals that while tiered options increase engagement, they confuse existing users, causing the core conversion rate to drop below the 3.2% baseline without sufficient upsell revenue to compensate.

### **3. Stop/Pivot Trigger**
**The Metric:** During the beta window (June 1–20), if the **net renewal rate** for the pilot group drops by **>5%** compared to the control group, or if the **legal team cannot certify** the data flow by May 1.

**Action:** Immediate pivot to a "soft launch" (existing users only) or full pause. We will not hard-launch on June 30 if the legal certification is pending, regardless of marketing spend.

### **4. Mandatory Human Sign-Off Point**
**Gate:** **Legal & Data Privacy Certification.**
**Timing:** Before any backend code touches production user data (Target: April 15).
**Requirement:** Written approval from Legal confirming the data model satisfies both current obligations and the July 1 state law changes. No code ships to the beta group without this signature.
