1.  **Infrastructure Collapse:**

2.  **Performance Miss:**

    *   *Risk:* Changes to the build process frustrate users, increasing churn despite speed gains.
    *   *Pivot Metric:* If Beta customer churn exceeds **8%** or NPS drops by >10 points from baseline, **HALT** general availability for UX remediation.
