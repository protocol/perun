# CEO Action Plan: Smart Subcontractor Matcher Launch

**Recommendation: Parallelize Compliance & Integration; Defer Full Marketing Until Pilot Validation**

**1. Order of Work (Critical Path)**
*   **Weeks 1–2 (Immediate):** Initiate **Compliance Sign-off** and **API Licensing Negotiation** simultaneously. Legal cannot sign off without knowing the data flow, and Engineering cannot finalize the budget without the licensing fee.
*   **Weeks 3–10:** Execute **XYZ ERP Integration**. This is the longest technical lead time (6 weeks) and blocks all other value creation.
*   **Weeks 9–12:** Develop **Marketing Creative Assets**. Start this 2 weeks before integration completes so assets are ready for the pilot announcement.
*   **Weeks 13–15:** **Pilot Setup & Execution**. Onboard the 5 customers immediately following integration testing.
*   **Week 16+:** Finalize **Tiered Pricing Model** based on actual pilot usage data rather than projections.

**2. Deferrals & Budget Adjustments**
*   **Defer:** Full-scale marketing launch. Limit spend to pilot-specific communication until revenue assumptions are validated.
*   **Adjust:** The pilot budget is currently over-allocated ($500k allocated vs. $120k estimated cost + integration). Reallocate the surplus $380k to cover the uncertain API licensing fee ($30k max) and the uncalculated **staff training costs**. Do not proceed without a specific line item for training.

**3. Mandatory Human Sign-offs**
*   **Legal/Compliance:** Required before any customer data touches the XYZ ERP system.
*   **CFO/Finance:** Required to approve the final API licensing fee and the revised budget allocation including training.
*   **CEO (You):** Required to approve the "Go/No-Go" decision after the pilot, before committing to the tiered pricing model.

**4. Main Failure Modes**
*   **Budget Breach:** The API licensing fee hits $30k AND training costs exceed $50k, eroding the margin needed for the pilot.
*   **Integration Delay:** If the XYZ ERP integration slips beyond 8 weeks, the Feb 1st go-live is missed, pushing revenue into Q2.
*   **Adoption Failure:** Pilot customers refuse to adopt the new workflow due to lack of training, invalidating the $600k revenue projection.

**5. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **STOP:** If the API licensing fee exceeds $30k. The unit economics will no longer support the projected 12% uplift.
*   **PIVOT:** If pilot customers report <15% time savings (vs. the projected 30%). The value proposition is insufficient to justify the price increase; revert to a service-assist model rather than a full platform upsell.

***

*Note: The current revenue projection assumes 100% pilot success; validate this assumption weekly during the 3-week setup. Ensure the training budget is defined before Week 3 to prevent workflow friction.*
