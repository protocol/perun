**Bluebird Roasters: Wholesale Office Coffee Program Rollout Plan**

**Phase 1: Discovery & Definition (Weeks 1–4)**
*   **Action:** Immediately commission a competitor pricing audit for the office segment ($2k budget) before finalizing packaging costs.
*   **Deliverable:** Finalized pricing strategy and confirmed packaging vendor quote.
*   **Sign-off:** CEO approval on final unit economics and margin targets.

**Phase 2: Product & Pilot Setup (Weeks 5–10)**
*   **Action:** Execute packaging redesign (12-oz biodegradable) and secure inventory for 1,800 lbs (3 pilots × 200 lbs/mo × 3 months buffer).
*   **Deliverable:** Landed cost verified; pilot contracts signed with TechCo, GreenSpace, and Apex including measurement clauses.
*   **Sign-off:** VP BizDev confirms contracts; Ops Director confirms capacity schedule (1,560 lb/wk limit).

**Phase 3: Execution & Measurement (Weeks 11–24)**
*   **Action:** Launch deliveries. Track weekly consumption vs. projected 50 lbs/week/client.
*   **Deliverable:** Monthly performance reviews against the Pilot-Measurement Framework (retention rate, brew quality scores, margin realization).
*   **Sign-off:** Go/No-Go decision for Q4 full launch based on Month 2 data.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Capacity Bottleneck:** Overtime fails to sustain the 30% capacity boost (1,560 lb/wk), causing stockouts during peak office hours and breaching service level agreements.
2.  **Margin Erosion:** Unanticipated competitor pricing forces a discount >10% to win pilots, or packaging costs exceed $8k, destroying the projected 60% budget recovery.
3.  **Consumption Mismatch:** Actual office consumption falls below 200 lb/month/client due to waste or preference mismatches, leaving inventory stranded and revenue targets unmet.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger**
**Pivot immediately if:** Net revenue from all three pilots combined is **< $5,000 after 60 days** of active delivery. This indicates either a fundamental pricing rejection or a consumption volume failure that cannot be solved by operational tweaks.

***

*Note: The $8,000 packaging estimate is a critical variance risk; do not commit to pilot volumes until the vendor contract is signed. Ensure the competitor audit is completed before Phase 2 begins to prevent pricing strategy misalignment.*
