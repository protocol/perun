*   **Compliance Bottleneck:** The audit reveals a structural flaw requiring a refactor. If this happens post-build, you burn the Q3 budget with zero revenue to show for it.

*   **API Cost > $25,000:** If the quoted usage fees for projected volume exceed $25k/year, the margin thesis is broken.
