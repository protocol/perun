**Subject:** Prioritized Rollout Plan & Risk Assessment for Alumni Donations Initiative

**Executive Recommendation**
Proceed immediately with a **"Integration-First" Critical Path**. Do not start portal development until the legacy CRM integration is scoped and prototyped. The current plan assumes a 25% adoption rate based on undergraduate behavior, which is a dangerous extrapolation for alumni. We must validate this assumption before committing the full $45k development budget.

**Prioritized Rollout Plan (Target: Live by 15 Oct 2024)**

1.  **Phase 1: Technical De-risking & Scope Lock (Weeks 1–3 | Est. Cost: $5k)**
    *   **Action:** Assign senior engineer to execute a "spike" (time-boxed research) on the legacy CRM integration. Produce a concrete hour estimate and API schema.
    *   **Action:** Finalize "Impact Tier" pricing model based on historical alumni giving data, not undergraduate benchmarks.
    *   **Gate:** If integration complexity exceeds 4 weeks or $20k, pause development and re-evaluate architecture.

2.  **Phase 2: MVP Development & Dashboard Definition (Weeks 4–9 | Est. Cost: $45k)**
    *   **Action:** Build the digital pledge portal *only* after Phase 1 gate clearance.
    *   **Action:** Define KPI dashboard specs (Pledge Conversion, Avg Gift, Churn) alongside dev work to ensure data capture is built-in, not retrofitted.
    *   **Deliverable:** Functional beta environment ready for internal testing.

3.  **Phase 3: Pilot Validation (Weeks 10–12 | Est. Cost: $12k)**
    *   **Action:** Launch pilot to 200 targeted alumni segments.
    *   **Action:** Distribute incentives and monitor real-time adoption against the 25% benchmark.
    *   **Gate:** Review pilot data. If metrics hold, proceed to full launch. If not, pivot messaging or tier structure.

4.  **Phase 4: Senior Sign-off & Public Launch (Weeks 13–14 | Est. Cost: $5k buffer)**
    *   **Action:** Present pilot results and integration stability report to the Board.
    *   **Action:** Full public release by 15 Oct 2024.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Integration Quagmire:** The legacy CRM requires custom middleware not accounted for in the 2–4 week estimate, causing a slip past the October deadline.
2.  **Demographic Mismatch:** Alumni ignore the portal because the UX/incentives are tailored to undergraduates, resulting in <10% adoption (vs. the assumed 25%).
3.  **Revenue Illusion:** The "10% uplift" relies on existing donors upgrading tiers rather than new acquisition; if current donors are price-sensitive, total revenue may flatline despite higher average gifts.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger**
**Immediate Pivot Required if:** Pilot participation is **less than 12%** (half of the conservative expectation) OR if the CRM integration spike reveals a timeline exceeding **5 weeks**. Do not proceed to full build if either condition is met; reassess the platform strategy.

*Note: Secure the integration spike budget immediately to validate the critical path. The $150k total budget leaves a ~$83k contingency, which is healthy but should be reserved for the integration unknown.*
