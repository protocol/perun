**GreenLogistics Fleet-Electrification Rollout Plan**

**1. Recommended Activity Sequence & Mandatory Sign-Offs**
Execute in this strict order to protect the critical path:
1.  **Design & Site Audit:** Finalize station layouts and grid capacity checks.
2.  **Procurement:** Secure chargers and battery packs (longest lead time).
3.  **Site Work:** Civil construction and grid interconnection.
4.  **Software Integration:** Connect fleet management to charging infrastructure.
5.  **Pilot Testing:** Deploy 10 trucks to validate operations.
6.  **Full Deployment:** Roll out remaining 110 trucks.

**Mandatory Human Sign-Off:** Required twice. First, **before Procurement** to lock capital expenditure against the final design. Second, **before Full Deployment**, strictly contingent on the Pilot Testing phase confirming vehicle range and charging uptime meet SLA requirements. No trucks hit the road for commercial routes without this second approval.

**2. Top Three Failure Modes**
*   **Grid Interconnection Delays:** Utility upgrades often exceed 6 months. If site power isn't ready by Q3 2025, the entire rollout stalls, incurring storage costs for idle hardware.
*   **Grant Dependency Risk:** The budget relies on the $1.2M federal grant (14.6% of total capex). If denied, the project faces an immediate $1.2M shortfall with no identified contingency, forcing a scope reduction or funding pause.
*   **Energy Price Volatility:** The ROI model assumes flat $0.11/kWh electricity. A 20% rate increase erodes the projected $1.5M annual savings, extending the payback period beyond the client's investment horizon.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
Immediately halt or pivot the project if any of the following occur:
*   **Grant Denial:** Formal notification that the $1.2M federal grant is rejected.
*   **Cost Overrun >10%:** Total projected costs exceed $9.02M ($8.2M base + 10%), indicating the budget cannot absorb unforeseen infrastructure complexities.
*   **Installation Delay >3 Months:** Charging station commissioning slips past the original schedule by more than one quarter, jeopardizing the Sep 2026 go-live window and invalidating the fuel savings timeline.
