**Course‑Bundle Rollout Plan: Quill Exchange**

**1. Order of Work & Sign‑offs**
*   **Phase 1 (Now – Oct 15): Core Engine & Data Foundation.**
    *   *Build:* Recommendation algorithm logic, bundle pricing database schema, and backend API endpoints.
    *   *Defer:* UI polish, seller dashboard analytics, and automated marketing triggers.
    *   *Sign‑off Required:* COO approval on the final bundle pricing logic before code merge to ensure margin safety.
*   **Phase 2 (Oct 16 – Nov 15): UX & Seller Onboarding.**
    *   *Build:* Frontend bundle display, seller opt‑in flow, and manual outreach tools for top 500 sellers.
    *   *Defer:* Advanced A/B testing framework (use simple split traffic initially).
    *   *Sign‑off Required:* Head of Sales approval on the seller outreach script and incentive structure.
*   **Phase 3 (Nov 16+): Scale & Optimize.**
    *   *Build:* Automated marketing spend allocation and detailed performance dashboards.

**2. Top Three Failure Modes**
*   **Seller Adoption Stall:** Fewer than 20% of top 500 sellers opt in by Week 4, starving the engine of inventory and keeping conversion <5%.
*   **Margin Erosion:** The recommendation engine prioritizes high‑conversion bundles that are low‑margin, causing gross profit to drop despite revenue hitting the $540k target.
*   **Technical Debt Bottleneck:** The 3‑developer team hardcodes logic to meet the Oct 15 deadline, making the Nov 15 UI integration require a rewrite rather than an extension.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Stop:** If seller adoption is <10% after 30 days of active outreach (indicates product‑market mismatch for bundles).
*   **Pivot:** If marketing CPA exceeds $15 (25% over the implied target) for two consecutive weeks, shift budget from acquisition to seller incentives.
*   **Stop:** If engineering velocity drops by >40% in Phase 2, indicating the Phase 1 foundation is unstable; freeze features and refactor.

***

*Note: This plan assumes the $540k revenue baseline is accurate; verify this figure with Finance before Phase 1 kickoff. The 15% conversion lift assumption should be treated as a stretch goal, not a baseline for budgeting.*
