**Recommendation:** Launch a sequential, city-staggered pilot starting with Metroville only on July 15, deferring Oakridge and Riverton until permit and insurance baselines are confirmed.

**Order of Work:**
1.  **Immediate (Weeks 1–2):** Secure binding insurance quotes and file permits in Metroville only. Do not purchase bikes yet.
2.  **Procurement (Weeks 3–4):** Upon permit approval and finalized insurance costs, purchase 45 bikes for Metroville.
3.  **Deployment (Weeks 5–8):** Install docking/geo-fencing and launch Metroville.
4.  **Review Gate:** After 30 days of Metroville data, validate unit economics. Only then procure for Oakridge and Riverton.

**Required Human Sign-offs:**
*   **CFO Approval:** Required before any bike purchase to confirm final unit cost including the unknown insurance premium.
*   **Legal Counsel:** Required before deployment in each city to verify permit compliance and liability coverage limits.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Delay:** Permits take >60 days, burning budget on idle inventory and missing the Q3 window.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** Actual utilization falls below 50% or maintenance/vandalism costs exceed 25% of revenue, extending break-even beyond 12 months.
3.  **Insurance Shock:** Premiums exceed $15/bike/month, invalidating the projected margin and requiring a price increase that suppresses demand.

**Pivot/Halt Trigger:**
Stop expansion and reassess the model if **utilization averages below 45% for two consecutive months** OR if **total operational cost per bike exceeds $90/month** (including maintenance, insurance, and fees).

**Deferred Items:**
*   Full 135-bike procurement (split into tranches).
*   Marketing spend in Oakridge and Riverton.
*   Custom app features beyond basic unlock/lock functionality.

**Note:** The $750k budget covers hardware ($337.5k) with ~$412.5k remaining for ops; this buffer is tight if vandalism exceeds 5% monthly. The 70% utilization assumption is aggressive for a new market; stress-test the financial model at 40% before committing capital.
