Postpone the "dynamic pricing" module entirely for this phase; deferring this 25% budget item saves $2M and removes critical path risk without impacting the initial go-live requirements.

1.  **Weeks 1–2:** Complete CityGrid integration and stress-test against unknown throttling limits. Deploy "real-time violation alert" to a staging environment mirroring Greenfield.

3.  **November 1:** Greenfield Go-Live (Core features + Alerts only).

1.  **API Throttling Bottleneck:** CityGrid limits are stricter than assumed, causing the "real-time alert" feature to fail under load, invalidating the $1.5M revenue projection.
2.  **Staffing Insufficiency:** Two developers cannot simultaneously finalize integration, build the alert feature, and fix bugs before the November 1 deadline, forcing a delayed launch.
3.  **Revenue Dependency Risk:** The business case relies entirely on the alert feature; if it is delayed or defective, the rollout launches with high costs but no immediate incremental revenue to offset them.

Halt the rollout immediately if load testing reveals that CityGrid API throttling limits restrict transaction throughput by more than 10% below projected peak volume, or if the "real-time violation alert" feature is not fully operational in the Greenfield staging environment by October 20.
