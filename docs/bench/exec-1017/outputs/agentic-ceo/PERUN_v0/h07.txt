### Sunrow Leads Growth Push: Executive Plan for Ravi Kapoor

**1. Recommended Order of Work & Mandatory Sign-offs**
Execute streams in this parallel sequence to de-risk revenue before full spend:
*   **Weeks 1–2 (Foundation):** Finalize tiered pricing logic and draft A/B test variants. **Mandatory Sign-off:** CEO must approve final price tiers and discount boundaries before engineering begins (prevents revenue leakage).
*   **Weeks 3–6 (Build & Validate):** Develop "Instant Quote" widget while running a small-scale pricing sensitivity test on 5% of current traffic. **Mandatory Sign-off:** CMO/CEO must greenlight full pricing rollout only if the 5% test shows no >8% drop in conversion volume.
*   **Weeks 7–12 (Scale & Optimize):** Launch widget globally, activate full tiered pricing, and run the full email nurture A/B test on the incoming 3,500 leads.

**2. Three Most Likely Failure Modes**
*   **Pricing Elasticity Miscalculation:** The assumed 10% ARPL uplift triggers a >15% drop in lead volume, resulting in net revenue loss despite higher per-unit value.
*   **Competitor Displacement:** The rival AI scoring tool lowers their CPA significantly, forcing Sunrow to overspend on acquisition to maintain the 3,500 lead target within the $250k budget.
*   **Integration Latency:** The "Instant Quote" widget delays beyond Week 6, compressing the testing window for the email nurture sequence and making the 15% conversion target statistically unprovable by Sept 30.

**3. Tripwire Metric for Re-prioritization**
**Weekly Blended CAC > $85.**
If the cost to acquire a *converted* lead exceeds $85 for two consecutive weeks after the pricing rollout, pause the tiered pricing experiment immediately. This threshold ensures that even with the projected $127 ARPL, the margin remains sufficient to cover operational overhead; exceeding it invalidates the unit economics of the entire growth push.
