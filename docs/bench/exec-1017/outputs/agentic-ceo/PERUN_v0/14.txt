**Recommendation for Maya Patel: Phased Rollout Plan & Go/No-Go Criteria**

**Prioritized Order of Work**
1.  **Integration Discovery & Sign-off (Weeks 1–3):** Immediately allocate resources to resolve the 2–4 week CRM integration unknown. Do not start portal coding until a concrete technical spec and hour estimate are signed off by the CTO. Secure senior sign-off on this revised technical scope before proceeding.
2.  **Pricing Validation (Weeks 2–4):** Run a rapid survey with 50 alumni to test the "impact tiers" assumption. Do not rely on the theoretical 10% uplift model; validate willingness to pay before building the logic into the portal.
3.  **Portal Development (Weeks 5–10):** Build the digital pledge portal only after integration specs and pricing models are locked.
4.  **Pilot Launch (Weeks 11–13):** Deploy to the 200 targeted alumni.
5.  **KPI Dashboard & Full Launch (Week 14+):** Finalize the dashboard based on actual pilot data, then launch broadly if go criteria are met.

**Three Main Failure Modes**
*   **Integration Blowout:** The legacy CRM requires custom middleware not accounted for in the 4-week estimate, causing the Oct 15 deadline to be missed and budget to overrun the $150k cap.
*   **Demographic Mismatch:** The 25% adoption assumption fails because alumni engagement mechanics differ significantly from undergraduate crowdfunding, resulting in a pilot participation rate below 10%.
*   **Pricing Resistance:** The "impact tiers" are perceived as confusing or too expensive, yielding no revenue uplift over the current donation method, invalidating the $30k revenue projection.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Technical:** Integration discovery exceeds 3 weeks or costs >$20k.
*   **Adoption:** Pilot participation is <15% (30 alumni) after two reminder cycles.
*   **Revenue:** Average gift size in the pilot does not exceed the current baseline by at least 5%.

**Note:** The current timeline is at high risk due to the undefined CRM integration; the Oct 15 deadline is likely unachievable without descoping the integration depth. The budget has a ~$93k buffer after known costs, which should be reserved entirely for the integration contingency.
