**Subject:** SecureClass Rollout Plan & Recommendation

**Executive Summary**
Proceed with the $160k investment to launch SecureClass by 1 Oct 2025, contingent on resolving FERPA compliance costs before full development completion. The pilot (5 districts, 2,400 students) begins 15 May 2025.

**Recommended Order of Work**
1.  **Audit (Immediate):** Commission FERPA compliance review to quantify unknown costs before locking the development scope.
2.  **Development (May–Aug):** Build the interactive phishing module ($120k budget) parallel to curriculum creation ($30k).
3.  **Pilot Execution (15 May – July):** Deploy to 5 districts; validate the 30% discount hypothesis ($11.25/student) against adoption speed.
4.  **Communications (Aug–Sep):** Execute the $10k marketing push targeting broader district adoption based on pilot data.
5.  **Full Launch (1 Oct):** General availability pending audit clearance and pilot success metrics.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Compliance Blocker:** FERPA audit costs exceed $8,000 or reveal architectural changes that delay the Oct 1 launch.
2.  **Adoption Friction:** Pilot enrollment falls below 1,200 students (50% of target) despite the 30% discount, indicating price is not the primary barrier.
3.  **Content/Dev Mismatch:** The interactive module fails to integrate smoothly with the curriculum, reducing perceived value and renewal likelihood.

**Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **STOP:** If the FERPA audit cost exceeds **$8,000** or requires >2 weeks of re-engineering.
*   **PIVOT:** If pilot active usage is **<50%** of enrolled students by 15 July, shift focus from new sales to engagement fixes before the full launch.

**Mandatory Human Sign-Offs**
*   **Legal/Compliance:** Written sign-off on the FERPA audit report is required before any student data is ingested during the pilot.
*   **CEO Approval:** Final go/no-go for the 1 Oct public release requires your signature after reviewing pilot retention data and final compliance status.

***

*Note: This plan assumes the 30% discount is sufficient to drive the 60-day adoption window; if pilot data suggests otherwise, pricing strategy must be revisited prior to the marketing push.*
*All financial figures reflect the corrected total budget of $160,000.*
