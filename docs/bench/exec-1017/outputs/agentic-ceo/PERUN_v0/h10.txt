**Recommendation: Phased Rollout Plan for Quill Exchange "Course-Bundle"**

**1. Order of Work & Human Gates**
*   **Phase 1 (Immediate – Oct 15): Core Engine & Data Foundation.** Deploy the recommendation engine logic and backend bundle pricing structures using the 3 existing developers.
    *   *Human Gate:* Require COO sign-off on the final pricing algorithm logic before code merge to ensure margin safety.
*   **Phase 2 (Oct 16 – Nov 15): UI Integration & Seller Onboarding.** Integrate the UI/UX designer to build the frontend bundle display and create the seller adoption portal.
    *   *Human Gate:* Require Head of Sales sign-off on the seller onboarding flow before inviting the top 500 sellers.
*   **Phase 3 (Defer until Phase 2 metrics validate): Advanced Marketing Automation.** Hold complex automated marketing sequences until initial conversion data confirms the 15% lift hypothesis. Use the $30k marketing budget strictly for A/B testing manual campaigns in Phase 2.

**2. Top Three Failure Modes**
*   **Seller Adoption Stall:** Fewer than 20% of the top 500 sellers opt into the bundle model within 30 days, starving the engine of inventory and rendering the recommendation logic useless.
*   **Margin Erosion:** The 15% conversion lift is achieved only by deep discounting that reduces average order value below $10, negating revenue gains despite higher volume.
*   **Engineering Bottleneck:** The 3-developer team fails to deliver the backend logic by Oct 15, delaying the UI designer's start and causing the $30k contingency to be consumed by extended labor costs rather than risk mitigation.

**3. Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Stop:** If conversion lift on bundles is <5% after two weeks of live traffic with full seller participation.
*   **Pivot:** If total initiative spend exceeds $135k (90% of budget) before achieving a statistically significant validation of the 15% conversion hypothesis.
*   **Stop:** If seller adoption remains below 10% after direct outreach to the top 50 accounts.

**Note:** This plan assumes the $540k revenue baseline is accurate; verify this figure with Finance before finalizing the ROI model. The unknown seller adoption rate is the critical path risk and requires a manual outreach campaign alongside the technical build.
