**1. Recommended Sequence & Mandatory Gates**
Execute in three locked phases. No code ships to production without the Phase 2 gate.

*   **Phase 1: Compliance & Definition (Days 1–20).**
    *   **Action:** Complete legal review of anonymized data sharing.
    *   **Mandatory Gate:** Legal must certify regulatory risk before marketing spend begins. *Do not proceed if unquantified regulatory costs exceed $45k (remaining contingency).*
*   **Phase 2: Build & Internal Beta (Days 21–70).**
    *   **Action:** Develop feature ($120k budget). Run internal dogfooding.
    *   **Mandatory Gate:** CFO sign-off required here to release the $80k marketing budget. Trigger depends on Phase 1 legal clearance and confirmed unit economics.
*   **Phase 3: Staged Rollout (Days 71–90).**
    *   **Action:** Release to 5% of free users. Measure conversion.
    *   **Decision:** Full launch only if conversion meets threshold (see below).

**2. Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Halt:** Legal determines anonymized data sharing violates new privacy statutes post-review, rendering the core value proposition illegal and sinking the $120k dev spend.
2.  **Conversion Miss:** Free-to-Pro conversion lands <6% (vs. 10% target), extending payback period beyond 18 months and violating our capital efficiency mandate.
3.  **Budget Overrun:** Unforeseen compliance requirements force a scope reduction or timeline slip, pushing total cost >$275k and eroding projected Year-1 ROI.

**3. Kill/Pivot Triggers**
Immediately halt or pivot the project if either of the following occurs during the Phase 3 staged rollout:
*   **Beta Churn > 12%:** Indicates the feature confuses users or degrades core app stability.
*   **Marketing ROI < 1.8x:** If the cost to acquire a paying Pro user via this feature exceeds $55 (based on $80k spend / projected conversions), the channel is inefficient.
