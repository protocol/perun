**GreenLogistics Fleet-Electrification Rollout Plan**

**1. Recommended Activity Sequence & Mandatory Gate**
Execute in this strict order: (1) **Design & Site Assessment** (validate grid capacity for 45 stations); (2) **Procurement** (lock truck/charger contracts); (3) **Site Work** (civil/electrical installation); (4) **Software Integration** (fleet management/charging orchestration); (5) **Pilot Testing** (10 trucks, 30 days); (6) **Full Deployment** (remaining 110 trucks).
**Mandatory Human Sign-off:** Required after **Pilot Testing** and before **Full Deployment**. No trucks enter full service until the CEO signs off on pilot energy consumption data vs. the $0.11/kWh assumption and verified uptime.

**2. Top Three Failure Modes**
*   **Grid Capacity Shortfall:** Site assessments reveal existing infrastructure cannot support 45 DC fast chargers without $2M+ in utility upgrades, blowing the $8.2M budget.
*   **Grant Dependency Risk:** The $1.2M federal grant is denied or delayed, creating an immediate 15% funding gap that halts procurement.
*   **Operational Cost Variance:** Real-world electricity rates exceed $0.11/kWh or charging efficiency losses reduce the projected $1.5M annual fuel savings by >20%, breaking the ROI model.

**3. Specific Stop/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Grant Denial:** Formal notification that the $1.2M federal grant will not be awarded.
*   **Cost Overrun >10%:** Total projected costs (including unforeseen grid upgrades) exceed $9.02M.
*   **Installation Delay >3 Months:** Site work or equipment delivery pushes the first operational date beyond 1 June 2025, missing the critical shipping season window.
