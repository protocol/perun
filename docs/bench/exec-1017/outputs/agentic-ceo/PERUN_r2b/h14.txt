**To:** Maya Patel, CEO
**From:** Strategic Planning
**Subject:** Decision Memo: Q4 Growth Push Plan

**Decision:** Proceed with a sequenced rollout starting with the Sponsorship Marketplace API, followed by the Analytics Dashboard, and deferring the Dynamic Ad-Insertion Engine to a post-Q4 stabilization window. This recommendation rests on a single corrected financial reality: the project is currently **$200,000 over budget** ($2.7M required vs. $2.5M allocated) once engineering overtime is included, creating an immediate funding gap that threatens all three deliverables.

**Financial Reconciliation:**
Your drafted costs total $2.5M, but adding the estimated $0.2M for engineering overtime brings the true requirement to **$2.7M**. Furthermore, the unknown AAC+ codec licensing fee represents an unquantified liability. We must re-baseline the budget to $2.7M plus the codec fee before full commitment. The current plan fails because it assumes zero contingency for known overtime needs and unknown licensing costs.

**Sequencing & Rationale:**
1.  **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4): Sponsorship Marketplace API.** This offers the fastest path to incremental revenue with the lowest technical risk to core infrastructure. It directly addresses the $4.2M revenue target without stressing the ad server.
2.  **Phase 2 (Weeks 5–8): Redesigned Analytics Dashboard.** This supports retention goals by empowering users but does not generate direct revenue. It serves as a necessary stabilization step before high-load changes.
3.  **Phase 3 (Deferred): Dynamic Ad-Insertion Engine.** We defer this because your assumption that the ad server can double inventory without increasing latency is unverified and high-risk. Attempting this within the 12-week window alongside the other two initiatives guarantees the overtime budget will be exceeded and jeopardizes the churn reduction target due to potential performance degradation.

**Failure Modes:**
*   **Latency Breach:** If ad server latency exceeds **150 ms** during Phase 1 load testing, the assumption of doubled inventory is false, and the Ad-Insertion Engine must be cancelled entirely to protect core user experience.
*   **Budget Breach:** If engineering overtime costs exceed **$300,000** (a 50% variance from estimate) by Week 6, all development halts to reassess scope.
*   **Licensing Block:** If the AAC+ codec fee exceeds **$50,000**, the project pauses until legal secures alternative funding or a codec substitute.

**Halt Trigger:** Immediate suspension of all work if latency hits 150 ms or overtime spend reaches $300k.
**Fallback Trigger:** If the Sponsorship API does not show integration readiness by Week 4, we pivot resources solely to the Analytics Dashboard to secure at least one delivery milestone.
