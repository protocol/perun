**Corrected Math Note:** Your 12% ROI projection is likely inflated. If you spend $150k to generate $250k ARR, your first-year return is ($250k - $150k) / $150k = 66% gross, but this ignores churn. With 30% YoY churn, you will lose $75k of that ARR in year one unless you replace it. Net new value is far lower than $250k. **Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **API Cost Explosion:** The "tentative" $30k is insufficient because usage scales non-linearly with adoption. If cost-per-request is not fixed, the 80% adoption scenario could burn the entire budget in month two.
2.  **Adoption Delusion:** The 80% adoption rate is based on a sample of five attorneys (n=5). This is statistically insignificant. If real-world adoption is 40%, revenue halves while fixed dev costs remain, destroying the ROI model.
3.  **Compliance Block:** The audit occurs too late. If the audit (Weeks 5–6) finds that the AI generates non-compliant clauses requiring architectural rework, the launch slips, and the $120k budget is exhausted before revenue starts.
