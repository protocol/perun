**Recommendation to CEO Maya Patel**

**Decision:** Proceed with a **staggered parallel launch**, prioritizing the Insertion Engine and Analytics Dashboard immediately while gating the "Premium CPM" pricing model on EU regulatory clarity.

**Sequence of Work:**
1.  **Weeks 1–3 (Critical Path):** Deploy the two engineers exclusively to the **Insertion Engine**. Simultaneously, the UI/UX designer and data scientist finalize the **Analytics Dashboard** specs and data pipelines. This ensures the core value proposition (dynamic ads) and the measurement tool (dashboard) are ready for the June 15 target.
2.  **Weeks 4–6 (Conditional):** Activate the **Premium CPM** pricing model *only if* EU data-privacy regulations confirm current targeting granularity is permissible. If regulations restrict data, pivot the pricing model to a flat "Sponsored Slot" fee using non-personalized data, utilizing the remaining budget to adjust the logic rather than launching a broken tier.
3.  **Resource Allocation:** The $120k budget supports exactly two engineers for three months ($90k/yr ÷ 4 quarters × 3 months × 2 engineers = $45k each; total $90k). The remaining $30k covers the data scientist and designer's partial allocation. The product marketer begins adoption outreach to the top 100 podcasters immediately to secure the required 80% commitment before code freeze.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
*   **Regulatory Blocker:** EU rules invalidate the data granularity required for "Premium" targeting, rendering the new pricing tier unsellable and the engine less valuable to high-paying advertisers.
*   **Adoption Friction:** Top podcasters reject the feature due to integration complexity or revenue share disputes, missing the 80% threshold and capping revenue well below the $350k projection.
*   **Timeline Slip:** The insertion engine encounters latency issues during real-time bidding, causing audio glitches that damage listener experience and trigger podcaster churn.

**Kill Tripwire:**
Stop or pivot the initiative if **adoptions by top 100 podcasters are <40% after 30 days** post-launch, or if the **EU regulation explicitly bans listener-level targeting** before the pricing model launch. In either case, halt further engine optimization and re-allocate resources to a contextual (non-behavioral) ad solution.
