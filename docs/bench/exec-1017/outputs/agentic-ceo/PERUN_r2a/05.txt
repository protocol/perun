**Decision:** Proceed with the Smart Savings Goal launch under a gated three-phase sequence, contingent on immediate legal clarification regarding data sharing.

**1. Execution Sequence & Sign-offs**
*   **Phase 1 (Days 1–30): Compliance & Architecture.** Immediate priority is resolving the unquantified regulatory risk of third-party data sharing. **Mandatory Sign-off:** General Counsel must certify the data-sharing model before any engineering sprint begins. If legal cannot clear this by Day 30, the feature scope must be reduced to internal-only savings tracking.
*   **Phase 2 (Days 31–75): Build & Internal Beta.** Execute the $120k development budget. Deploy to 5% of staff/internal users first. **Mandatory Sign-off:** CTO approves security audit results before external beta.
*   **Phase 3 (Days 76–90): Limited Market Rollout.** Activate the $80k marketing spend targeting only 50,000 users (not the full 1.2M base) to validate the 10% conversion assumption. **Mandatory Sign-off:** CFO (Maya Patel) releases marketing funds only after Phase 2 unit economics are verified.
*   **Deferred:** Full-scale marketing blitz and expansion to the entire user base until conversion rates are proven in Phase 3.

**2. Top Three Failure Modes**
*   **Regulatory Halt:** Legal determines that anonymized data sharing violates emerging privacy statutes, rendering the core value proposition illegal and wasting the $30k legal spend plus opportunity cost.
*   **Conversion Miss:** Free-to-Pro conversion lands at <4% (instead of the assumed 10%), resulting in an annualized revenue gain of ~$1.3M instead of the projected ~$4.3M, extending payback period beyond 18 months.
*   **Budget Overrun on Compliance:** The unknown regulatory costs exceed $50,000, forcing a cut to the marketing budget that cripples user acquisition velocity.

**3. Halt & Pivot Triggers**
*   **Halt Trigger:** If Phase 3 beta churn exceeds **15%** within the first 14 days of limited rollout, stop all marketing spend immediately. This indicates the feature confuses or frustrates users rather than engaging them.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If the ROI on the initial $80k marketing spend is **<1.5x** (not 2x) after 45 days, pivot from a "Pro conversion" model to a "data-partnership" revenue model (if legally cleared) or shelve the feature.

**Financial Reconciliation:**
Total allocated: $250,000.
Explicit costs: $120k (Dev) + $80k (Mkt) + $30k (Legal) = $230,000.
**Contingency Buffer:** $20,000 (8% of budget) remains unassigned to absorb the unknown regulatory costs. If legal estimates exceed this buffer, the project pauses.

**Note:** This plan assumes the $3 ARPU remains stable; any dilution requires a revised ROI calculation. Legal clearance is the critical path item; without it, the $230k spend is at risk.
