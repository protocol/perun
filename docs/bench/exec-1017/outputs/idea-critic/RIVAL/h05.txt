**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Competitor Price War:** If TextTrade cuts fees to 8% while you maintain 12%, price-sensitive users will migrate regardless of your matching speed. Your value prop (speed) may not outweigh their value prop (cost).
2.  **Liquidity Mismatch:** The algorithm needs balanced supply and demand. If you attract 5,000 buyers but only 2,000 sellers (or vice versa), "instant matches" will fail, leading to user churn despite the marketing spend.
3.  **Infrastructure Latency:** The $200k server upgrade assumes linear scaling. If the "instant" matching creates database locks under load, latency could spike, making the service slower than the current version during peak hours.

**Kill Trigger:**
Terminate the project if **net new active users average below 2,800 per month for three consecutive months** post-launch.
*Reasoning:* Your break-even analysis implies you need roughly 3,300 users/month to cover the $800k cost given the conversion lift. Setting the trigger at 2,800 (approx. 85% of target) gives you an early warning signal before you burn through the entire budget, allowing you to pivot or shut down with capital remaining. Waiting until month 4 to check against 3,500 risks overspending on a failing trajectory.
