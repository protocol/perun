**Three Most Likely Failure Points**
1.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** If the $2.70 estimate excludes packaging waste disposal, your margin evaporates. High-protein ingredients and specialized packaging for freshness often carry hidden disposal fees that can push costs over $3.50/meal, turning a profit into a loss.
2.  **Lead Quality Overestimation:** Relying on gyms for 1,500 qualified leads (500 each) is risky. Gym members often resist upsells, and "qualified" leads for endurance nutrition are narrower than general gym goers. If conversion drops below 3% due to poor lead quality, revenue falls to ~$65k, barely covering costs.
3.  **Subscription Retention Cliff:** Endurance athletes are notoriously brand-loyal to specific nutritional timings and flavors. If the pilot meals don't perfectly match their training cycles, churn could spike immediately after the 8-week trial, preventing the projected 6-month subscription value from materializing.

*   **Cost Trigger:** Stop immediately if verified all-in cost per meal exceeds **$3.15**. (At this price, even with 5% conversion, your net margin drops below 15%, making the venture capital-inefficient).
