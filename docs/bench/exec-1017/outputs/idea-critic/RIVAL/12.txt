**Recommendation: Do Not Pitch Yet (Condition: Revise Financial Model)**
We cannot currently recommend proceeding with the pitch. The financial model contains a critical mileage underestimation (1.5M vs. actual 1.8M miles) and excludes mandatory battery module replacements after year two. Presenting now exposes Harbor to significant variance risk. We must recalibrate the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) against the corrected mileage and explicit maintenance caps before seeking approval.

**Three Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **TCO Inversion via Fuel Volatility:** Our model assumes static diesel costs ($3.80/gal). If fuel drops to the forecasted $2.90/gal by Q3 2025 while electricity rates rise, the operational savings vanish, potentially making the EV fleet more expensive to run than the legacy diesel fleet over the 24-month term.
2.  **Unfunded Capital Liability:** The $1.2M outlay excludes battery module replacement costs. With 1.8M miles driven, degradation will likely exceed the 5% annual assumption, triggering unplanned capital calls in Year 2 that destroy the projected ROI.
3.  **Rebate Dependency Risk:** The model relies on a rumored $200k state rebate with no confirmed schedule. If this incentive is delayed, reduced, or denied, the upfront capital requirement spikes, breaching our internal hurdle rates for green initiatives.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Condition**
Abort the transition if the **revised 24-month Total Cost of Ownership (including confirmed battery replacement reserves and zero rebate credit) exceeds the diesel baseline scenario by more than 8%.**
