2.  **Adoption Shortfall:** If app adoption tracks closer to industry standards (30–40%) rather than your 70% hope, you will miss the 4,800 boarding target entirely, leaving expensive electric assets underutilized while fixed costs remain high.
3.  **Flawed Benchmarking:** By ignoring the competitor's mileage subsidy, you are underestimating their true cost basis. If your corrected cost-per-ride exceeds theirs, the pilot fails its primary efficiency mandate regardless of ridership gains.

Terminate or pivot the pilot if, after 9 months, the **net ridership lift is <2%** or the **corrected cost-per-ride exceeds the competitor's subsidized rate by more than 15%**.
