**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the 90-day pilot only if you secure a written commitment from the three clinics to provide weekly data access and define "adoption" as active usage (not just installation). Do not commit to the full $650,000 spend or the 18-month revenue projection until the pilot validates the no-show reduction mechanism.

**Main Failure Modes**
1.  **Revenue Math Collapse:** The $66k/month lift assumes recovered no-shows convert immediately to billable visits. If your clinics are already capacity-constrained, filling a no-show slot simply shifts revenue from one patient to another, yielding $0 incremental gain.
2.  **Adoption Delusion:** The 70% adoption target relies on existing customers wanting this. If "SmartSchedule" requires workflow changes (e.g., earlier confirmations) that staff resist, installation will remain low regardless of efficacy.
3.  **Pilot Bias:** Selecting "top clinics" creates a survivorship bias. Their disciplined staff may mask the very no-show problems the tool solves, leading to a successful pilot that fails in average clinics.

**Kill Trigger**
Terminate the project if, by Day 45 of the pilot, the **net reduction in no-shows is <15%** (half the target) OR if the **cost to support the three clinics exceeds $25,000**. Do not wait for the full 90 days to see these signals.

**Hidden Risks**
*   **Cannibalization:** Aggressive scheduling might push patients into less profitable time slots or overload specific providers, causing burnout rather than revenue growth.
*   **The "New Sign-up" Void:** Your projection ignores new market pull. If the $200k marketing spend relies on this feature to close *new* deals, and the feature doesn't drive new logos (only retains old ones), your CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) will skyrocket, breaking the 18-month ROI model.
*   **Implementation Drag:** The $450k dev cost assumes a clean launch. If integration with legacy practice management systems takes 2x longer, your July 1 start date slips, pushing revenue recognition into Q4 or next year.

**Note:** Recalculate the $1.2M projection assuming only 50% of recovered appointments are *new* revenue, not shifted.
