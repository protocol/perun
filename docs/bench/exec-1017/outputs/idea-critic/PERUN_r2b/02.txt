**Recommendation: MODIFY (Hold Branch Expansion; Pilot "Instant-Approve" Digitally First)**

Do not commit to the $1.2M physical expansion yet. The proposal's core revenue math is flawed: a 15% growth on a $12M book yields $1.8M in *total new loan volume*, not $2M in *revenue*. Assuming a net interest margin of ~6%, the actual gross revenue gain is only ~$108,000 annually, which fails to cover the $360,000+ estimated increase in staff overhead (30% hike) and the $1M technology outlay. The plan burns cash immediately based on a category error between "loan volume" and "income."

Instead, redirect the $200k technology budget to a digital-only pilot of the "instant-approve" product in the existing Metro footprint. This tests the competitor threat and adoption rates without the fixed cost of five new branches or 12 new officers.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Margin Collapse:** The "instant-approve" product forces a rate war; if competitors lower rates to gain share, your 6% margin could compress to 3%, turning the projected $108k gain into a loss even if volume targets are met.
2.  **Regulatory & Capacity Breach:** The assumption that current underwriting can handle 30% more volume without extra compliance staff is dangerous. A 15% volume spike often triggers heightened regulatory scrutiny or audit failures if staffing doesn't scale linearly with risk exposure, leading to fines that exceed growth profits.
3.  **Fixed-Cost Trap:** If adoption lags due to the competitor's head-start, you are left with five leased branches and 12 salaried officers generating insufficient volume to cover their fixed costs, creating a multi-year drag on the balance sheet.

**Abort Trigger:**
If the digital pilot does not generate **$450,000 in net new loan originations** (not revenue) by **September 30, 2025**, immediately halt all branch leasing and hiring. This volume is the minimum required to justify the fixed overhead of even a single physical branch; failing to hit it digitally proves the physical expansion model is insolvent.

*Note: The $2M revenue figure must be corrected to ~$108k gross income in your presentation to maintain credibility with the CLO. The "flat interest rate" assumption also requires a sensitivity analysis, as a 1% rate drop would erase the entire projected profit.*
