**Recommendation: Proceed with the pilot, but revise the Year‑2 revenue forecast and secure the GDPR content scope before committing marketing funds.**

The pilot's unit economics are sound ($750k revenue vs. $620k direct cost), offering a viable path to validate product-market fit. However, the strategic plan contains a critical arithmetic error and an unquantified regulatory risk that must be addressed before presentation.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes:**

1.  **Flawed Cross-Sell Assumption (Arithmetic Slip):** The plan assumes 80% adoption across 120 customers for a $9.6M opportunity. This implies 96 customers buying the add-on. However, the base math suggests the add-on price is $150k (based on the pilot). $150k × 96 customers = $14.4M, not $9.6M. Conversely, if the $9.6M figure is correct, the implied price per customer is only $100k, contradicting the pilot pricing. This inconsistency undermines the Year‑2 credibility. Additionally, assuming 80% uptake for a new, unproven add-on is historically aggressive; a more conservative 30–40% rate would halve the projected opportunity.
2.  **Unbounded GDPR Compliance Cost:** The "unknown" effort for EU‑GDPR content is a hidden schedule and budget killer. If this content is mandatory for the 5 pilot clients (any of whom could be EU-based or have EU employees), development costs could exceed the $500k cap, destroying the pilot's margin. If it is not ready, you risk non-compliance or delivering an incomplete product to enterprise clients, triggering the 15% churn forecast immediately.
3.  **Adoption vs. Churn Contradiction:** The plan targets 30% user adoption within pilot accounts but forecasts 15% account churn. In enterprise security, low user adoption (70% of users ignoring the training) is often the leading indicator of account churn. If the "culture" change fails at the user level, the $150k contract renewal is at risk, making the 15% churn estimate potentially optimistic if the 30% adoption target is missed.

*   **Stop/Pivot Condition A:** Pilot Net‑Promoter Score (NPS) falls below **30** at the 3-month mark.
*   **Stop/Pivot Condition B:** Calculated Return on Investment (ROI) is under **12%** after six months.
