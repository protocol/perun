**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with development only if a binding pilot with the three venue APIs confirms <2s latency and 99.5% uptime before the 2024-09-01 launch; otherwise, defer.

**Correction on Financials:** Your revenue projection contains a calculation error. Charging a $20 premium with 10% uptake on 12,000 users yields $28,800 annually ($20 × 1,200 users × 12 months), not $1.2 million. At this rate, the $150,000 development cost alone requires over five years to break even, excluding maintenance.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Financial Viability:** The unit economics are negative based on the stated price and uptake; the project will burn cash indefinitely unless the price is increased to ~$100/month or uptake exceeds 50%.
2.  **Technical Dependency:** Reliance on third-party API stability creates a single point of failure; if the calendar service degrades, real-time sync breaks, causing booking errors that increase rather than decrease churn.
3.  **Market Adoption:** Planners may reject a premium fee for a feature they expect to be standard, resulting in near-zero uptake and no offset for the development spend.

**Kill Criterion:**
Terminate the project immediately if the pilot phase (pre-launch) demonstrates an uptake rate of less than 2% among a test group of 500 users, or if the third-party API latency consistently exceeds 3 seconds during peak load testing.
