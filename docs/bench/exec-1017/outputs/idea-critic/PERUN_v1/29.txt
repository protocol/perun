**Recommendation: GO-IF** – Proceed only if the payment gateway integration cost is confirmed under $100k and a pilot achieves ≥1.5% conversion within six months. Do not approve the full $400k development spend yet; authorize only a minimal viable payment integration and a limited beta to validate the conversion rate before committing to the full roadmap.

**Key Failure Modes:**
1.  **Revenue Arithmetic & Churn:** The model incorrectly sums $1.2M (ads) + $800k (subscriptions) to reach $2.0M. This ignores that converting 3% of users (15,600 users) removes them from the ad pool. If those users were high-engagement, ad revenue could drop by ~$36k–$60k, not to zero, but the net gain is lower than projected. More critically, if churn for paid users matches the free tier (30%), the steady-state ARR drops from $800k to ~$560k, missing the target entirely.
2.  **Hidden Integration Cost:** The plan explicitly excludes the payment gateway cost. If this requires a custom enterprise integration rather than a standard SDK, costs could easily exceed $150k, eroding the first year's profit margin.
3.  **Support Capacity:** Assuming zero additional headcount for 15,600 new paying customers is risky. Paid users demand higher SLAs; even a 5% contact rate generates 780 extra tickets/month, likely overwhelming the current team and degrading service for free users.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate or pivot the initiative if, after six months of the beta launch:
*   **Conversion Rate:** Net new paid subscriptions are <1.5% of the active free base (approx. 7,800 users).
*   **Cost Overrun:** The total estimated cost for payment integration and initial compliance exceeds $250k.
*   **Cannibalization:** Ad revenue from the remaining free tier drops by more than 10% compared to the prior year baseline, indicating the premium tier is stripping out the most valuable ad-viewing activity without sufficient replacement revenue.
