**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the migration only if the vendor provides a binding cap on cut-over downtime costs and a pilot contract securing at least $400k of the projected $1.2M revenue before the January 15 start date. Without these, the risk of unquantified downtime and unverified adoption assumptions makes the plan financially unsafe.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Technical:** Unquantified cut-over downtime exceeds the budget buffer, causing immediate cash flow distress and SLA penalties that erase the projected $141k annual savings.
*   **Adoption:** The assumption that 80% of 12,000 schools will adopt within 12 months proves false; if adoption lags at 40%, revenue falls short by ~$720k, turning the projected profit into a net loss against migration costs.
*   **Contractual:** Legacy licensing terms include hidden exit fees or data-migration penalties that exceed the $350k annual saving, locking the company into double-running costs during the 9-month transition.

**Kill Switch Criteria**
Trigger an immediate pivot or stop if, by Month 4 of the migration:
1.  **Revenue Shortfall:** Secured pilot revenue is less than 50% of the pro-rated $1.2M target (i.e., under $600k committed).
2.  **Cost Overrun:** Actual migration costs (including downtime penalties) exceed the original budget by more than 20%.
