**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional approval.**
Approve the centralized hub and cloud platform only if the triage nurse hiring is phased. Do not commit to two full-time nurses immediately. Instead, hire one nurse on a three-month trial linked directly to no-show reduction. The $250,000 software cost is justified only if the booking speed gain is realized within 30 days of go-live; otherwise, revert to the legacy system during the transition. This approach caps immediate exposure while testing the core assumption that staffing drives attendance.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Revenue Shortfall from Overestimated Savings:** The plan assumes a 20% reduction in no-shows ($9,000/month savings) driven solely by new nurses. If the actual reduction is less than 12%, the monthly savings will not cover the amortized cost of the new hires and software, turning the project into a net loss within six months.
2.  **Operational Disruption During Transition:** The assumption of "zero integration downtime" is high-risk. If the cloud migration causes even 48 hours of scheduling blindness or data loss, the resulting patient backlog and reputation damage could outweigh the efficiency gains of the first year.
3.  **Patient Satisfaction Erosion:** Centralizing three desks into one location may create a single point of failure for phone lines and physical access. If patients experience longer hold times or confusion regarding the new single entry point, satisfaction scores could drop, leading to a secondary increase in no-shows that negates the nurse intervention.

**Kill Criteria for End of Q1:**
Terminate the project and revert to the legacy workflow if, by January 15, 2025:
*   The no-show rate has not decreased by at least 10% (verified against the baseline 15%).
*   OR the average appointment booking time has not improved by 15% compared to pre-implementation metrics.
*   OR patient complaints related to "scheduling access" or "phone wait times" increase by more than 20% over the previous quarter's average.
