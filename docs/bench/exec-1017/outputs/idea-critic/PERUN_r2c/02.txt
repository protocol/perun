**Recommendation: MODIFY (Go-If)**
Proceed with the expansion only if the plan is revised to cap initial outlay at $600k (two branches, four officers) and explicitly budgets for two additional compliance staff. The current proposal relies on a flawed revenue model and an unsafe assumption regarding regulatory capacity.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Revenue Miscalculation:** The projected $2M gain assumes new loans yield the same margin as the existing book instantly. In reality, new branches have a 6–9 month ramp-up period where origination costs exceed interest income, likely turning the projected profit into a net loss for 2025.
2.  **Regulatory Bottleneck:** Assuming current underwriting capacity can absorb a 30% staff increase and 15% volume growth without added compliance headcount is a critical risk. Regulatory scrutiny on small-business lending is high; a single compliance backlog could freeze all new originations, rendering the new hires idle while fixed costs burn.
3.  **Competitive Displacement:** The "unknown" adoption rate of the competitor's instant-approve product is treated as a variable rather than a threat. If even 20% of your target demographic switches to the faster fintech solution, the new branches will miss volume targets entirely, failing to cover the $1.2M fixed investment.

**Abort Trigger**
Abort the full rollout if **net new loan originations** from the pilot locations do not reach **$450,000 by September 30, 2025**. This figure represents the minimum velocity required to annualize to the 15% growth target; falling short indicates the market uptake or operational ramp-up is too slow to justify the remaining capital deployment.

**Notes**
*   The $2M revenue projection is almost certainly overstated as it ignores the time-value of money for loans originated late in the year and potential charge-offs from rapid scaling.
*   Immediate verification of state-specific licensing timelines for the five new Metro locations is required before any lease signing, as this often delays openings by 3–4 months.
