**Recommendation: Proceed with Pilot, But Revise Year‑2 Forecast and Compliance Buffer**

Approve the $1.12M investment for the 12-month pilot to validate product-market fit, but **reject** the $9.6M Year‑2 opportunity figure as a planning baseline. The pilot revenue covers only 67% of total upfront costs ($750k vs. $620k), meaning the pilot itself operates at a net loss of $130k before accounting for overhead. Treat the pilot as a paid learning experiment, not a profit center. Crucially, do not commit resources to the full cross-sell strategy until the EU-GDPR content timeline is quantified; this unknown represents a critical path risk that could delay the entire Year‑2 rollout.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes:**

1.  **Compliance Bottleneck:** The "unknown" duration for EU-GDPR content creation creates a hidden cost. If legal review or specialized content production takes >3 months, it delays the pilot start, burns cash without revenue, and potentially disqualifies the 5 pilot clients if they require immediate compliance certification.
2.  **Adoption Math Mismatch:** The plan targets 30% adoption among pilot users but assumes 80% cross-sell across the entire base for Year‑2. If the pilot confirms only 30% adoption, the Year‑2 revenue drops from $9.6M to $3.6M (30% of 120 clients × $100k avg), destroying the business case for the marketing push.
3.  **Churn Amplification:** A 15% churn rate on the core platform triggered by a poorly received add-on (or distraction from core service) would cost ~$2.16M annually (15% of 120 clients × $120k avg core value), vastly outweighing the maximum theoretical add-on revenue.

**Pre‑Agreed Stop/Pivot Triggers:**

*   **Stop:** If Pilot Net‑Promoter Score (NPS) < 30 after 4 months.
*   **Pivot:** If ROI after 6 months is < 12% **OR** if the EU-GDPR content effort exceeds $75,000 (15% of dev budget).

**Arithmetic & Logic Corrections:**

*   **Revenue vs. Cost:** Pilot revenue ($750k) does **not** cover total pilot costs ($500k Dev + $120k Marketing = $620k). You have a $130k gap, not a profit.
*   **Year‑2 Flaw:** The $9.6M figure assumes 100% of the 80% cross-sell target pays the pilot price ($150k). If the pilot price is a premium introductory rate, scaling to 96 customers at $150k is unrealistic; if it's the standard rate, the 30% adoption pilot result contradicts the 80% Year‑2 assumption.
*   **Churn Impact:** You listed 15% churn as a forecast, but did not calculate its cost against the core business. At Redoak's scale, losing 18 customers (15% of 120) is a catastrophic revenue event compared to the add-on gain.
