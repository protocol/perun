**Recommendation:** Proceed with the 3-month pilot starting 1 Oct 2024, but **revise the financial model** to reflect a conservative 30% tutor adoption rate (not 70%) and explicitly budget for a 15% revenue cannibalization from existing one-on-one sessions. Do not commit to a full rollout until the pilot proves a net incremental revenue lift after these adjustments.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Cannibalization Overload:** Students switch from higher-margin or existing one-on-one sessions to the cheaper group option, resulting in net revenue loss despite high group sign-ups.
2.  **Tutor Adoption Cliff:** Tutors reject the format due to increased preparation complexity or lower effective hourly earnings compared to private tutoring, missing the 70% uptake target entirely.
3.  **Quality & Retention Drop:** The 8:1 student ratio degrades the learning experience relative to 1:1 sessions, leading to higher churn rates that offset the acquisition of new low-cost users.

**Halt Trigger:** Stop the pilot immediately if, by the end of Month 2, the **net incremental revenue** (Group Revenue minus Estimated Cannibalized Revenue minus Variable Costs) is less than **$15,000 per month** across the entire pilot cohort.

**Hidden Risks & Costs Missed:**
*   **Scheduling Friction:** Coordinating 8 students and 1 tutor for a specific 90-minute window creates significantly higher operational friction than 1:1 booking, likely requiring new software logic or manual support hours not currently costed.
*   **Liability & Moderation:** Group dynamics introduce risks of inappropriate student behavior or cultural clashes requiring active tutor moderation skills that current staff may lack, necessitating unpaid training time.
*   **Platform Stability:** A sudden spike in concurrent video connections (8 students × 400 tutors potential) could strain current infrastructure, requiring unforeseen server upgrades.

*Note: The original $320k projection assumes perfect substitution and zero churn impact; the revised conservative model reduces expected monthly lift to approximately $110k. Ensure the pilot tracks "net new revenue" specifically, not just gross group sales.*
