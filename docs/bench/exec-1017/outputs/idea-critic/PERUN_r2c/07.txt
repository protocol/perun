**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the 90-day pilot only if you secure a written commitment from the three clinics to integrate their legacy scheduling data by June 15, 2024. Do not commit the full $650,000 budget or the 18-month revenue forecast until this integration is verified in a sandbox environment.

**Main Failure Modes**
1.  **Integration Friction:** The 30% no-show reduction assumes seamless data flow. If legacy systems resist API integration, the pilot stalls, and the projected $66,000/month lift per clinic never materializes.
2.  **Adoption Overestimation:** Betting on 70% adoption from existing customers within six months is aggressive without a dedicated change-management budget. If adoption lags at 40%, the $1.2M revenue projection collapses.
3.  **Market Pull Void:** You have zero data on new sign-ups. If the feature attracts no new practices, the $200,000 marketing spend yields no net-new revenue, extending the payback period indefinitely.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Trigger**
Terminate the project immediately if, by Day 45 of the pilot, the aggregate no-show rate across the three clinics has not dropped by at least 15% (half the target) OR if total implementation costs exceed $550,000.

**Hidden Risks**
*   **Revenue Leakage:** A 30% drop in no-shows increases clinical load. If your current staff cannot handle the recovered volume, you will face overtime costs or patient dissatisfaction that offsets the revenue gain.
*   **Cannibalization:** Existing customers might delay other paid upgrades to wait for this module, causing a temporary dip in overall account revenue during the rollout.
