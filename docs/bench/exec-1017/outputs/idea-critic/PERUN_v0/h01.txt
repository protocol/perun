**Critical Failure Points & Arithmetic Corrections**
1.  **Revenue Shortfall:** Your projection assumes 15% uptake of *all* 20,000 users (3,000 seats). However, you only have 20,000 users total, not necessarily 20,000 *seats* available for upsell if some accounts are single-user or already maxed. More critically, $12/seat × 3,000 seats = $36,000/month, which is $432,000 annually, not $360,000. Conversely, if the 15% uptake applies to *accounts* rather than users, and average account size varies, the revenue could be significantly lower. The math needs strict validation against current seat inventory.
2.  **Margin Erosion:** At $0.018/minute and 250 minutes/user, the cost is $4.50/user/month. If the add-on is $12, the gross margin is $7.50 before overhead. If the "corporate discount tier" applies to the $12 price point, you risk dropping below the $4.50 variable cost, losing money on every active user.
