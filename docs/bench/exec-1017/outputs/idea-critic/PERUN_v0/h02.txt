**Recommendation: Do not approve the full rollout yet.** Proceed only with a **30-day, capped-budget pilot** starting July 15 to validate the $15 cost-per-lead and 8% conversion rate before committing to the 12,000-lead scale.

**Main Failure Points:**
1.  **Scaling Fallacy:** Assuming an 8% conversion rate will hold while scaling volume 30% higher than a competitor's 6-month pilot is statistically unsafe; conversion rates typically drop as ad relevance dilutes during rapid expansion.
2.  **Unpriced Compliance Risk:** Launching without a fixed cost for federal tax credit reporting creates an undefined liability that could erase margins on the projected $2.5M revenue.
3.  **Timeline Compression:** Attempting in 3 months what took a benchmark competitor 6 months increases the risk of operational bottlenecks and data inaccuracies.
4.  **Arithmetic Gap:** The projection ignores churn after twelve months; if long-term retention is low, the lifetime value of these leads may not support the acquisition cost.

**Kill/Pivot Trigger:**
Terminate or pivot the initiative immediately if the pilot yields a cost-per-lead exceeding **$18** or a conversion rate below **6%** for two consecutive weeks.
