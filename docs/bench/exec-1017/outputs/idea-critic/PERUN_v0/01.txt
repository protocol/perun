**Recommendation:** Proceed with the 3-month pilot starting 1 Oct 2024, but strictly condition full rollout on achieving a **net revenue contribution of at least $150 per tutor-week** (after variable costs) and maintaining **one-on-one session retention above 90%**. Do not commit to the $80,000 marketing spend until Week 2 data confirms organic tutor adoption exceeds 40%.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Cannibalization over Expansion:** Students switch from higher-margin one-on-one sessions ($40+/hr typical) to the $25/hr group option, reducing total revenue per student despite higher volume. Your model assumes pure增量 (additive) growth, which is rare without strict segmentation.
2.  **Tutor Adoption Cliff:** The 70% adoption assumption is likely optimistic for a new format. If only 30% of tutors engage, fixed development costs ($120k) remain unchanged while variable marketing costs become inefficient, pushing the break-even point beyond the pilot window.
3.  **Quality & Churn Risk:** Group dynamics are harder to manage than 1:1. A few bad sessions due to mismatched student levels or technical audio issues could damage brand reputation, causing churn in the core high-margin business.

**Halt Trigger:** Stop the rollout immediately if, by the end of Month 2, the **net profit margin per group session falls below 45%** (accounting for tutor payout, platform fees, and support) OR if **one-on-one booking volume drops by more than 8%** compared to the pre-pilot baseline.

**Hidden Risks/Costs Missed:**
*   **Scheduling Complexity:** Coordinating 8 students and 1 tutor across time zones adds significant friction and support overhead not captured in the $120k dev estimate.
*   **Tutor Payout Structure:** You calculated revenue but not the tutor's cut. If tutors earn less per hour in groups than 1:1, they will silently sabotage adoption by not promoting it.
*   **Refund Liability:** Group classes have higher refund risk if one student dominates or technical issues occur; a clear refund policy is needed before launch.

*Note: The $800/week/tutor figure assumes 100% seat fill rate consistently; model sensitivity for 50% fill rates immediately. Verify tutor payout economics before finalizing the $25 price point.*
