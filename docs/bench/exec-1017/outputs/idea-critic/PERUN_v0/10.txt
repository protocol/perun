**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the launch only if you secure a binding commitment from Support leadership confirming capacity for a 50% ticket increase without new hires, and if you revise the revenue model to reflect a conservative 10% conversion rate ($150k ARR) rather than the optimistic 30% ($675k ARR) for initial budgeting.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Support Collapse:** The assumption that current staff can absorb a 50% increase in tickets without additional hiring is unverified; if false, response times will degrade immediately, causing churn among high-value premium users before the product proves its value.
2.  **Revenue Shortfall:** The 30% conversion target from free to premium within six months is exceptionally aggressive for a $15k/year product; failing to hit even half this target (15%) leaves you with $337k ARR against $195k in upfront costs, jeopardizing ROI before the second year begins.
3.  **Churn Cliff:** With no data beyond a 3-month trial, there is a significant risk that usage drops sharply after the first year if the "3 hours per month" utility assumption proves incorrect for solo practitioners, turning high-acquisition costs into a net loss.

**Pre-agreed Kill Condition**
Terminate the project if Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) does not reach **$25,000** (equivalent to ~17 subscribers) by **31 March 2025**. This threshold represents the minimum viable traction required to justify continued API licensing costs if the 30% conversion goal is missed by a wide margin.
