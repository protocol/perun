**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the pilot only if the Q1 2025 pilot confirms a processing cost ≤$0.12/reading and a committed intent-to-buy from ≥45 OEMs (30% of the 150-site pilot). Do not commit to the full $4M build or the $8M ARR target until these two variables are verified.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes**
1.  **Margin Collapse:** The $0.02/unit cost underestimation scales to a $2M annual shortfall at 100M readings, turning the projected gross margin negative before volume efficiencies kick in.
2.  **Conversion Friction:** Achieving a 30% conversion rate on a *new* software service from an installed hardware base is historically anomalous; if conversion lands at 15%, ARR drops to $4M, failing to cover the $4M build cost in Year 1.
3.  **Cannibalization Risk:** Assuming hardware renewals stay flat is unverified; if the subscription model delays hardware upgrades or shifts bargaining power, the core $12M hardware revenue could erode, offsetting subscription gains.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Trigger**
*   **Kill Switch:** Halt the full rollout if the pilot reveals a sustained processing cost >$0.14/reading or if <20% of pilot customers sign a Letter of Intent at the target price point by March 2025.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If conversion is strong (>25%) but hardware renewals dip >5%, immediately pivot the strategy to a bundled "Hardware + Service" renewal contract rather than selling the subscription standalone.

**Note:** The $2M cost variance must be stress-tested against the $4M build budget immediately, as it represents 50% of your initial capital outlay.
