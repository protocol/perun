**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" condition.**
Launch the Scale-Up Donor Drive in Q3 2025, but explicitly condition full-year funding continuation on achieving a **6-month donor retention rate of at least 45%**. Without this guardrail, the plan risks acquiring expensive, one-time donors that fail to cover the $120,000 fixed cost of the new coordinators.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Churn Risk:** If new donors give once and leave (churn >55%), the projected $250,000 revenue collapses to ~$112,500 (assuming only first gifts), resulting in a net loss of $7,500 against the budget, excluding event costs.

3.  **Fixed Cost Inflexibility:** Hiring two full-time coordinators ($110,000 total) creates high fixed costs before revenue is proven; a slower ramp-up in Q3 burns cash that cannot be recovered by scaling back hours.

Conduct a formal review on **March 31, 2026** (6 months post-launch).
