**Recommendation: Go-if**
Proceed with a **single-hub pilot in Cedar Creek only** for Q3 2025, conditional on verifying warehouse handling capacity within 30 days. Do not commit to Oakridge or Pine Hollow until the pilot confirms the 30% conversion rate and proves no degradation to peak weekend delivery networks.

**Principal Failure Modes**
1.  **Network Congestion:** Diverting inventory to hubs without modeled peak-capacity logic causes system-wide shipping delays, eroding the brand promise for remaining online-only customers.
2.  **Conversion Shortfall:** If pickup adoption stays below 18% (vs. the required 30%), the $1.2M fixed cost creates a net loss rather than margin expansion, extending break-even beyond 24 months.
3.  **Hidden Handling Costs:** The assumption that existing warehouse space absorbs new flow without extra fees proves false; if third-party handling fees rise by >$0.50/unit due to complexity, the $2M savings target collapses.

**Kill Criteria (Tripwires)**
*   **Operational Degradation:** A sustained **>15% increase in weekend delivery delays** for non-pickup orders in the pilot region lasting more than 3 weeks.
*   **Financial Breach:** A **cumulative cost overrun of $250k** against the $1.2M budget by Month 6, or a gross margin lift of <2 percentage points after 5 months.
*   **Adoption Stall:** Pickup volume growth falling below **4% month-over-month** for two consecutive months post-launch.

*Note: The 8% month-over-month growth assumption is aggressive for a new behavioral channel; stress-test this against a 3% baseline before finalizing the budget.*
*Note: Explicitly model the "peak weekend" constraint before signing any lease, as this is the only unmodeled variable capable of cascading failure to the core business.*
