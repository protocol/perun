**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Revenue Arithmetic Collapse:** Your net profit projection ($400k) vanishes if the competitor TextTrade cuts fees to 8%. To maintain volume against an 8% fee, you may be forced to match pricing, slashing your margin per transaction below the cost of acquisition, turning the projected $1.2M revenue into a net loss.
2.  **User Acquisition Fantasy:** The model requires 5,000 new active users monthly. If the actual cost per acquired user exceeds your implicit assumption (derived from the $150k marketing budget), you will burn through the marketing fund in under three months, leaving the new algorithm with no traffic to convert.
3.  **Infrastructure Bottleneck:** The $200k server upgrade is treated as a fixed cost. If "instant" pairing creates latency spikes during peak trading hours due to unanticipated database locking on course codes, conversion rates could drop below the current 12% baseline due to user frustration, negating the core value proposition.

**Kill Criterion**
Terminate the project immediately if, by Month 4, the rolling average of new active users is below 3,500 *or* if the realized conversion rate fails to exceed 14% (a 2% absolute lift) after accounting for the learning period.

If that omission exists in the master budget, your approved runway is already overstated by nearly 20%.
