**Deliverable: Ethical Testing Framework & Prevention Guidelines for ConnectNow**

**1. Harm Inventory (Map)**
Before testing, explicitly record these specific risks for your dating generator:
*   **Deceptive Patterns:** Generated backstories that fabricate life events, careers, or emotions to manufacture false intimacy.
*   **Emotional Manipulation:** Personas designed to exploit vulnerability (e.g., "tragic backstory" tropes) to lower user guardrails.
*   **Over-reliance:** Users presenting generated fiction as absolute truth without disclosure, leading to trust collapse upon discovery.
*   **Bias:** Stereotypical gender roles or cultural caricatures in generated interests.

**2. Measurement Plan (Measure)**
Do not assert safety; run these specific evaluations before launch. Create one test row per harm above:
*   **Probe Set:** Input edge cases requesting "sad stories to gain sympathy," "wealthy personas to attract gold-diggers," or "perfect matches for lonely widows." Include known-bad positive controls (prompts explicitly asking for scams) and known-good negative controls (benign bio requests).
*   **Metric:** Rate of outputs containing unverifiable factual claims, emotional manipulation tactics, or stereotypical tropes.
*   **Threshold:** Zero tolerance for outputs facilitating financial scams or explicit emotional exploitation. For other deceptive elements, define a maximum acceptable percentage (owner-set).
*   **Stop Condition:** If any probe generates a persona encouraging financial transfer or severe emotional deception, block launch immediately.
*   **Review:** Assign one engineer to manually review all flagged outputs; do not rely solely on automated scoring.

**3. Risk Management & Guidelines (Manage)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Since generating a complete identity is a high-stakes action that can lead to irreversible emotional harm, **do not auto-publish** generated profiles. Require explicit user confirmation and editing before a generated bio goes live. The system must not act fully autonomously here.
*   **Disclosure & Uncertainty:** The interface must clearly label content as "AI-Generated Draft." Do not present generated backstories as verified facts. Encourage users to edit details to match their reality.
*   **Prevention Guidelines:**
    *   **No Financial Triggers:** Hard-code filters to block generation of content mentioning crypto, investments, emergency funds, or financial hardship.
    *   **No "Perfect Match" Logic:** Prevent the AI from claiming to be the "soulmate" or mirroring user trauma too perfectly.
    *   **Edit-First Workflow:** Force the user to modify at least 20% of the generated text before saving, ensuring human agency and reducing copy-paste deception.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a lightweight "Model Card" for internal use documenting intended use (conversation starters), limitations (not for creating fake identities), and the results of your red-team tests. Mark any unmeasured metrics as `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Governance**
Name one owner responsible for reviewing these harms weekly. If the model or prompt logic changes, re-run the evaluation.

**Notes:**
*   Legal requirements regarding automated decision-making or specific disclosures in dating apps vary by jurisdiction; route this specific determination to counsel immediately.
*   Do not invent a "0% deception" metric; report the actual failure rate from your tests and manage the residual risk via the human gate.
