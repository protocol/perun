**Rapid Verification Protocol for High-Risk Medical Translations**

Given the 48-hour deadline and the potential for fatal overdose, you cannot "confidently deploy" a fully automated patch without a human safety gate. The following protocol prioritizes stopping harm over completing full coverage.

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory & Triage (Hours 0–4)**
*   **Define the "Kill List":** Do not test all terms. Immediately extract the top 50 highest-risk concepts specific to infusion pumps (e.g., "bolus," "mcg/kg/min," "dilute," "hold," specific drug names like "insulin" or "heparin," and units of measure).
*   **Worst-Case Definition:** For each term, define the fatal error (e.g., translating "hold" as "administer," or swapping "milligrams" for "micrograms").
*   **Scope Limitation:** Explicitly mark all non-critical UI text (menus, help screens) as **UNVERIFIED** and out of scope for this 48-hour window. Focus 100% of resources on the Kill List.

**2. Adversarial "Red Team" Evaluation (Hours 4–24)**
*   **Construct Probe Sets:** Create three specific test rows for each of the 50 high-risk terms across all supported languages:
    *   *Positive Control:* A known dangerous mistranslation (e.g., unit swap) to ensure your test catches it.
    *   *Negative Control:* A known correct translation to ensure you aren't flagging safe outputs.
    *   *Edge Case:* Ambiguous medical phrasing likely to confuse the model.
*   **Execution:** Run these probes immediately. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy scores. A single failure in the "Positive Control" or "Edge Case" for a high-risk term is a **STOP CONDITION** for that specific language/term pair.
*   **Metric:** Pass/Fail only. Do not calculate a percentage accuracy rate; an unmeasured rate is **UNVERIFIED**. If a high-risk term fails, the automated output for that term is blocked.

**3. The Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory Floor)**
*   **Irreversible Action Control:** Because an incorrect dosage instruction can cause irreversible physical harm or death, **fully automated deployment of these specific translations is prohibited.**
*   **Implementation:**
