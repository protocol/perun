### Safety Evaluation Plan: FitPulse Chest Pain Advisor

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, explicitly record the primary risk: **False Negative Medical Advice**.
*   **Harm:** The device advises "muscle fatigue" when the user is experiencing a cardiac event.
*   **Affected Group:** Users outside the training demographic (women, older adults, non-athletes, individuals with pre-existing conditions).
*   **Worst Case:** User ignores a heart attack, delays seeking care, and suffers severe injury or death.
*   **Secondary Harm:** **Over-reliance**. Users treat the device as a diagnostic tool rather than a fitness tracker.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert accuracy. Because your data lacks diversity, any aggregate accuracy metric is **UNVERIFIED** for underrepresented groups. Structure your evaluation as follows:

*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Construct a synthetic test suite of edge cases representing demographics missing from your training data (e.g., female heart attack symptom patterns, atypical pain presentations in older adults).
*   **Control Groups:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs known to be cardiac events (must never be classified as "fatigue").
    *   *Negative Control:* Inputs known to be muscle fatigue.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Define a **Zero-Tolerance Threshold** for false negatives on cardiac events. If the model classifies *any* known cardiac event in the probe set as "muscle fatigue," the feature fails the test.
*   **Stop Condition:** Launch is blocked if the false negative rate on the adversarial probe set is >0%.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** stating clearly: "Intended for fitness context only; not a medical device. Training data limited to healthy young males; performance unverified for other demographics."

**3. Risk Management & Guardrails (MANAGE)**
Given the high stakes and data gaps, you must implement strict behavioral constraints:

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (The Floor):** The device must **never** autonomously dismiss chest pain as "muscle fatigue" with high confidence.
    *   *Rule:* If biometric signals indicate chest pain, the output must default to: "Chest pain detected. This could be serious. Please stop activity and consult a medical professional immediately."
    *   *Constraint:* Remove the binary "Fatigue vs. Serious" classification for the user. Replace it with a **triage advisory** that always escalates uncertainty to human medical review.
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not show users a percentage (e.g., "87% likely fatigue"). This implies false precision. Use coarse, defined tiers:
    *   *Confirmed Exertion:* (Only for clear, non-pain exertion signals).
    *   *Unverified/Alert:* (For any ambiguous or pain-related signal).
*   **Disclosure:** Prominently display: "AI-generated advice based on limited data. Not a diagnosis. Always seek professional care for chest pain."

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the Safety Owner responsible for reviewing any failure in the probe set.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the model or addition of new data types requires re-running the adversarial probe set.
*   **Legal Routing:** Because this feature touches health outcomes, route the question of "regulated medical device status" to legal counsel immediately. Do not assume beta status exempts you from health regulations.

**Summary of Immediate Actions:**
1.  Halt any logic that allows the device to say "It's just fatigue" for chest pain.
2.  Build the adversarial probe set for missing demographics.
3.  Publish the Model Card with the "UNVERIFIED" status for non-male demographics.

*Note: This plan identifies residual risk; it does not certify the feature as safe. The decision to ship rests with your leadership.*
*Legal determination regarding medical device regulation must be made by counsel, not this technical review.*
