### Hard-Coded Refusal Triggers and Response Templates for MindfulMate

To ensure the safety of users discussing medication, implement the following logic layer **before** any generative model processes the input. This acts as a deterministic gate that overrides the AI's conversational flow.

#### 1. Trigger Keyword & Pattern List
Configure the input parser to flag a message if it contains **any** of the following patterns (case-insensitive). Do not rely on semantic understanding alone; use exact string matching or simple regex for these high-risk terms.

**Medication Actions (High Risk):**
*   "stop taking"
*   "quit my meds"
*   "change dosage"
*   "skip my pill"
*   "alter prescription"
*   "discontinue medication"
*   "come off [drug name]"
*   "reduce dose"

**Medication Entities (Contextual):**
*   "antidepressant"
*   "SSRI" / "SNRI"
*   "prescription"
*   "medication" / "meds"
*   "psychiatrist" (when paired with action verbs above)
*   Common drug suffixes: "-pram", "-zine", "-pam", "-ine" (use with caution to avoid false positives, best paired with action verbs).

**Logic Rule:**
`IF (Input contains [Medication Action]) OR (Input contains [Medication Entity] AND [Action Verb like "stop", "change", "bad"])`
`THEN Trigger Safety Protocol`

#### 2. Mandatory Response Templates
When a trigger is activated, the system must **bypass** the generative model entirely and return one of the following fixed strings. Do not allow the AI to elaborate, apologize excessively, or offer medical reasoning.

**Template A (Direct Medication Alteration Request):**
> "I cannot advise on changing, stopping, or adjusting your medication. Only a qualified healthcare provider can make those decisions safely. Please contact your doctor or pharmacist immediately to discuss your concerns."

**Template B (Frustration with Side Effects):**
> "I hear that you are struggling with side effects, and that is very difficult. However, I cannot suggest changes to your prescription. Please reach out to your prescribing physician right away; they can help you manage these effects safely."

**Template C (General Medication Mention in Crisis Context):**
> "It is important that you speak with a medical professional before making any changes to your treatment plan. I am an AI companion and cannot provide medical advice. Please contact your healthcare provider."

#### 3. Implementation Constraints
*   **No Context Dependency:** These triggers must fire based on the **current input only**. Do not wait for conversation history or long-term patterns, adhering to your 24-hour privacy constraint.
*   **Hard Stop:** Once a trigger fires, the session branch for that turn ends. The generative model receives no prompt to process.
*   **Resource Link:** Append a static, non-dynamic link to a crisis resource (e.g., local emergency services or a suicide prevention hotline) to every safety response.

#### 4. Verification Step
Before launch, run a "red team" test using only the specific phrases listed in the Trigger List. If the bot generates *any* text other than the templates above for these inputs, the safety gate is failing.

***

**Note:** Because this involves high-stakes health outcomes, these hard-coded rules should be reviewed by legal counsel and a clinical advisor to ensure they meet regulatory duties for your specific region.
**Note:** Even with these guards, clearly disclose to users in the app onboarding that the bot is not a medical device and cannot replace professional care.
