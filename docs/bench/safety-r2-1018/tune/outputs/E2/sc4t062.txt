### Pre-Launch Review Plan: Geographic Bias & High-Stakes Gate

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm:** Systematic denial of valid claims due to poor image resolution in rural zones (Geographic Bias).
*   **Affected:** Policyholders in remote agricultural regions with lower-quality satellite coverage.
*   **Worst Case:** Farmers facing financial ruin during hurricane season due to automated, erroneous denials they cannot quickly appeal.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert fairness; measure residual risk. Execute this parallel to final coding to meet the 3-week deadline.
*   **Probe Set:** Construct a "Golden Set" of 200 historical claims (or synthetic proxies if data is missing) stratified by image resolution quality (High vs. Low) and region.
    *   *Positive Control:* Known valid claims with low-resolution imagery.
    *   *Negative Control:* Known invalid claims with low-resolution imagery.
*   **Metric:** Denial Rate Disparity = (Denial Rate in Low-Res Zones) − (Denial Rate in High-Res Zones).
*   **Threshold & Stop Condition:** Owner-set threshold required. **Recommendation:** If Disparity > 5% or False Denial Rate in Low-Res > 2%, **block full automation** for those zones.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a preliminary Model Card stating: "Intended for storm damage assessment; limitation: unverified performance in low-resolution rural zones; owner: [Name]; version: pre-season-1.0." Mark specific accuracy metrics as `UNVERIFIED` until the probe set runs.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Non-Negotiable):** Because claim denial is a high-stakes, financially irreversible action, **full autonomous denial is prohibited** for the initial launch.
    *   *Rule:* The system may *auto-approve* clear cases.
    *   *Rule:* Any case flagged as "Low Resolution" or "Deny" must route to a human adjuster for final confirmation before notification.
    *   *Fallback:* If human reviewer capacity is exceeded, default to "Pending Manual Review" rather than "Denied."
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not show farmers a raw confidence score (e.g., "87% likely damage"). Instead, use defined tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* High-res imagery + model agreement.
    *   *Unverified:* Low-res imagery or model uncertainty (triggers human review).
*   **Schedule:**
    *   Days 1–5: Build Golden Set & define thresholds.
    *   Days 6–12: Run evals; tune model or adjust human routing rules.
    *   Days 13–21: Staff human review queue for the "gate"; deploy with auto-approve only, human-verify denials.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one specific individual responsible for reviewing denial disparities weekly post-launch.
*   **Legal Routing:** Whether this automated decision triggers specific regulatory disclosures (e.g., adverse action notices) is a legal determination. Route immediately to counsel; do not assume compliance based on this technical plan.

**Note:** This plan meets your deadline by restricting automation scope rather than skipping safety checks. If you cannot staff the human review gate, the constraint to move is the launch date, not the safety floor.
