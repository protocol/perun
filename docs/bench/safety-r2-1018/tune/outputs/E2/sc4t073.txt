**Immediate Vendor Vetting Protocol (48-Hour Launch)**

**1. Halt Irreversible Automation (Human-in-the-Loop Gate)**
Do not allow the bot to automatically finalize bookings or process payments for any vendor until this vetting is complete. High-stakes actions (spending user money, binding contracts) must require explicit human confirmation or a secondary verification step by your team. Fully autonomous execution of financial transactions to unverified vendors is the primary failure mode to prevent.

**2. Rapid Harm Inventory & Data Sweep**
Before launch, manually review your existing internal data for the following specific risks. Record findings for every vendor currently in the database:
*   **Fraud Indicators:** Search internal logs, support tickets, or notes for keywords like "scam," "no-show," "refund dispute," or "fake."
*   **Data Provenance:** Verify the source of each vendor's listing. If a vendor was added via an unverified bulk upload or a single untrusted source without prior human review, flag them as **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Worst-Case Scenario:** For any flagged vendor, document the specific harm to a couple (e.g., "Loss of $2,000 deposit," "No food at wedding").

**3. Immediate Mitigation Actions**
*   **Quarantine Unverified Vendors:** Remove any vendor lacking a clear, trusted provenance or those with any negative internal flags from the bot's active selection pool immediately. Do not wait for a perfect score; if the data is unclear, exclude the vendor.
*   **Define Confidence Tiers:** Do not display a numerical "trust score" (e.g., "95% safe") to users, as this implies a precision your rapid review cannot support. Instead, use coarse, defined tiers for internal tracking:
    *   *Confirmed:* Manually verified by a team member + no negative history.
    *   *Unverified:* Lacks manual verification or has ambiguous data (Exclude from bot).
    *   *Flagged:* Known complaints (Permanently exclude).
*   **Disclosure:** Ensure couples know they are interacting with an automated system. If the bot recommends a vendor, include a clear statement that the selection is algorithmic and that users should perform their own due diligence.

**4. Measurement Plan (Post-Launch)**
Since you cannot run extensive adversarial tests in 48 hours, establish a "stop condition" for post-launch:
*   **Probe:** Monitor the first 50 bookings closely.
*   **Metric:** Zero confirmed fraud complaints.
*   **Stop Condition:** If a single user reports a vendor issue linked to a bot recommendation, immediately pause automated booking for that vendor category and revert to human-only selection until re-vetted.

**5. Ownership**
Assign one specific team member as the owner of this vetting protocol. They must sign off on the "Confirmed" list before the bot goes live. An empty inventory is not a pass; it is an unfilled requirement.

**Note:** This protocol manages residual risk; it does not certify your system as "safe" or "unbiased."
**Note:** If legal regulations regarding automated consumer decisions apply to your region, route that determination to legal counsel immediately; do not assume compliance based on this checklist.
