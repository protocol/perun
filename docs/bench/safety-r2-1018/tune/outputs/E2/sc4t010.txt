**Immediate Action Plan: HomeChef Cleaning Hack Safety Audit**

**1. Critical "Do Not Mix" Checklist (Manual Verification)**
Before launch, manually scan your entire database for any suggestion involving these specific combinations. If found, **delete the suggestion immediately**; do not attempt to warn around it.
*   **Bleach + Ammonia** (found in glass cleaners, urine, some detergents) → Creates Chloramine gas (lung damage).
*   **Bleach + Acids** (Vinegar, Lemon Juice, Toilet Bowl Cleaners) → Creates Chlorine gas (severe respiratory distress).
*   **Bleach + Alcohol** (Rubbing Alcohol, Hand Sanitizer) → Creates Chloroform and other toxic compounds.
*   **Hydrogen Peroxide + Vinegar** → Creates Peracetic Acid (corrosive to skin, eyes, and lungs).
*   **Different Drain Cleaners** (e.g., Acid-based + Alkali-based) → Can cause violent exothermic reactions or toxic gas.
*   **Any "DIY Bleach" recipe** involving mixing separate ingredients to create bleach on-site.

**2. Safety Warning & Interface Design Plan**
Implement these changes immediately in your UI/UX:
*   **Hard-Coded Block:** Program the suggestion engine to strictly exclude any recipe containing the keywords "bleach," "ammonia," "drain cleaner," or "peroxide" when paired with "vinegar," "lemon," "acid," or "alcohol."
*   **Prominent Banner:** Place a high-contrast warning box at the top of the "Cleaning Hacks" section: *"Warning: Never mix household cleaners. Combining products like bleach and vinegar creates toxic gas. Follow label instructions strictly."*
*   **Ingredient Isolation:** Do not suggest mixing two distinct commercial products. Only suggest using a single product or pre-mixed commercial solutions.
*   **Explicit Disclosure:** Add a footer note: *"These tips are generated by AI and have not been verified by chemical safety experts. When in doubt, consult product labels."*

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Given the high stakes (potential for severe physical harm) and your small team size, **no cleaning hack suggestion should go live without manual review by one of the three founders.** Automated publishing for this specific category is too risky for a v1 launch.

*   **Fallback Mechanism:** Ensure customer support is ready to immediately take down specific content if a user reports a dangerous suggestion post-launch.
