### Safety Evaluation Framework for LinguaLeap Conversational AI

**1. Harm Inventory & Scope**
Before building filters, explicitly define the risks: **Bias/Unfair Outcomes** (stereotypes disguised as idioms), **Hallucination as Fact** (fabricated phrases presented as authentic), and **Unsafe Automation** (teaching offensive language without warning). Record that the worst-case scenario involves a user inadvertently offending someone in a real-world interaction due to AI-generated content.

**2. Measurement Plan (Automated & Adversarial)**
Do not rely solely on standard code tests; test the *outputs*.
*   **Probe Sets:** Create specific test rows for each harm. Include "known-bad" inputs (prompts asking for slang in sensitive contexts) and "known-good" controls.
*   **Cultural Slicing:** Run probes across diverse cultural segments, not just aggregate English. Test for idioms that may be harmless in one culture but hateful in another.
*   **Injection Testing:** Specifically probe for prompt injection where user interests might manipulate the AI into generating toxic content.
*   **Metrics & Thresholds:** Define a pass threshold (e.g., zero instances of hate speech in 1,000 generated dialogues). If the threshold is breached, the launch stops.
*   **Status:** Any metric not yet measured must be labeled **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "99% safe" claim.

**3. Management Controls (Human-in-the-Loop)**
Given the budget constraint preventing review of *every* phrase:
*   **High-Stakes Gate:** Implement a human review gate for any content flagged by automated filters as "low confidence" or "potentially sensitive." Fully autonomous delivery of unverified idioms is the failure mode to avoid.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not present AI-generated idioms with false precision. Use defined coarse tiers for confidence (e.g., "Verified by Native Speaker" vs. "AI-Generated Draft – Verify Context"). This prevents over-reliance.
*   **Feedback Loop:** Build an immediate "Report Inappropriate Content" button in the UI. This acts as a post-deployment human sensor.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model Card)**
Create a durable **Model Card** for this feature. It must state:
*   **Intended Use:** Conversational practice for adults.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly note that not all idioms are native-verified due to budget constraints.
*   **Performance:** Report results by cultural subgroup, not just overall accuracy.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing flagged content.

**5. Governance**
Assign an owner to re-review the harm inventory whenever the underlying model or data sources change. If legal questions arise regarding regulated content or specific regional liabilities, route those immediately to counsel; do not assert legal compliance internally.

***

**Note:** This framework measures residual risk; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased."
**Note:** Budget limits require strict triage: automate the obvious, but human-review the ambiguous.
