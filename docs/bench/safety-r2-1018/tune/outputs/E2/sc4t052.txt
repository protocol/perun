**Immediate Deployment Plan & Testing Protocol**

Given the tight budget and immediate timeline, do not attempt to "verify" the algorithm is unbiased or perfect; no test can certify this. Instead, implement a **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) gate** for all final distribution decisions. The bot must function as a recommendation engine only, requiring explicit coordinator confirmation before resources are dispatched. This prevents irreversible harm from sending aid to saturated zones while ignoring emerging crises.

**Harm Inventory & Worst Cases**
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Historical density data systematically excludes new, undocumented settlements. *Worst Case:* Starvation or lack of medical aid in emerging crisis zones due to algorithmic invisibility.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Outdated open-source maps present closed roads or destroyed bridges as passable. *Worst Case:* Supply trucks stranded or diverted, delaying aid to critical areas.
*   **Over-reliance:** Coordinators trust the bot's prioritization over local intelligence. *Worst Case:* Systemic misallocation of life-saving resources.

**Key Variables to Simulate**
To verify adaptation to dynamic conditions, your simulations must vary:
1.  **Data Latency:** Introduce time-delays between ground reality changes (e.g., a bridge collapsing) and map updates.
2.  **Population Flux:** Rapidly increase population density in previously empty coordinates to test if the bot overrides historical baselines.
3.  **Conflict Signals:** Input reports of access denial or safety risks in high-density areas to ensure the bot deprioritizes unreachable locations.

**Transparency & Governance**
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Do not display raw probability scores (e.g., "87% chance of need"). Display defined text tiers: **Confirmed** (multiple sources), **Corroborated** (one source + historical match), or **Unverified** (new area, no history). Require human review for any "Unverified" high-priority suggestion.
*   **Model Card:** Publish a one-page artifact stating: *Intended Use:* Triage support only. *Limitations:* Relies on outdated open-source maps; fails to detect new settlements without human input. *Owner:* [Name of Team Lead].
*   **Legal Routing:** Whether this automated decision falls under specific humanitarian regulations or requires formal AI disclosure to affected populations is a legal determination. Route this immediately to counsel; do not assert compliance here.

**Residual Risk:** Even with testing, the reliance on outdated maps creates an **UNVERIFIED** risk of missing emerging needs. The HITL gate is the only control preventing this from becoming a fatal failure.
