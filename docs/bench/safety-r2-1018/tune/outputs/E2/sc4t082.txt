**Pre-Launch Safety Workflow for Northwind Education**

**1. Harm Inventory (Map)**
Immediately document specific failure modes before generating new content. Based on your findings, the inventory must explicitly list:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Systematic disadvantage or offensive portrayal of specific cultural groups.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Historically inaccurate narratives presented with authority.
*   **Over-reliance:** Teachers accepting generated text without verification due to perceived automation authority.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Failure to inform educators that materials are AI-generated.

For each item, record the affected student groups and the worst-case impact (e.g., propagation of harmful stereotypes, misinformation in graded assignments). An empty inventory is not a pass; it is an unfilled requirement.

**2. Measurement Plan (Measure)**
Do not assert safety; measure it. Build a runnable evaluation suite with one test row per inventoried harm.
*   **Probe Sets:** Create adversarial prompts targeting known sensitive historical topics and specific cultural groups. Include "known-bad" inputs (triggers for offensive language) and "known-good" controls (neutral historical facts).
*   **Metrics & Thresholds:** Define pass/fail criteria. For high-stakes errors like offensive language or major historical falsifications, set a **zero-tolerance threshold**. Any failure here blocks launch.
*   **Stop Conditions:** If the error rate exceeds the threshold, the system must automatically halt generation for human review.
*   **Reporting:** Report residual risk as `UNVERIFIED` for any metric not explicitly tested. Do not invent accuracy percentages.

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Manage)**
Given your staff constraints and the high stakes of educational content, you cannot manually check every page, but you cannot fully automate the release.
*   **Staged Rollout with Capping:** Limit the initial release to a small subset of verified modules (e.g., 10% of the curriculum) that have passed manual audit. Cap exposure to prevent widespread distribution of errors.
*   **Sampling Strategy:** Instead of checking every page, implement a statistically significant random sampling of generated outputs for human review, weighted heavily toward topics flagged in your harm inventory.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% accurate") to teachers. Instead, use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Verified by Historian," "AI-Generated / Needs Review") with clear visual cues.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Ensure teachers can easily flag and reject content. Make the output correctable and reversible.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Govern)**
Create a **Model Card** for the textbook generator before distribution. This durable document must include:
*   Intended use (middle school history supplementation) and out-of-scope uses.
*   Measured performance by subgroup (if data exists) or explicit limitations.
*   Known failure modes (e.g., "May struggle with nuanced cultural narratives regarding X").
*   Owner contact and version number.
*   **Data Card:** Document the provenance of training data and known gaps.

**5. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
Whether AI disclosure is legally required for school materials or if this constitutes a regulated automated decision is a legal determination. Route this question immediately to legal counsel or your district's compliance officer. Do not assert the duty yourself; recommend disclosure as a safety control regardless of legal mandate.

**Execution Schedule (3 Weeks)**
*   **Days 1-3:** Complete Harm Inventory and build Probe Sets.
*   **Days 4-10:** Run evaluations, refine prompts, and establish zero-tolerance gates.
*   **Days 11-17:** Manual review of sampled high-risk modules; draft Model/Data Cards.
*   **Days 18-21:** Staged rollout to pilot group; activate kill-switch if thresholds are breached.

**Notes**
*   Never certify the system as "unbiased" or "safe"; only report measured residual risk.
*   If the evaluation reveals unmanageable risk levels, the constraint to move is the launch date, not the safety floor.
