# Pre-Launch Impact Assessment Plan: Wildlife Conservation Drone System

## 1. Harm Inventory & Scope
**Primary Risk:** Automation bias leading to ecosystem disruption. The system's aggressive path optimization may prioritize detection efficiency over animal welfare, causing displacement from breeding grounds.
**Affected Parties:** Endangered species (specifically during breeding seasons), local ecosystem balance, and conservation credibility.
**Worst Realistic Case:** Chronic stress or permanent migration of target species due to drone noise/patterns, rendering the conservation effort counterproductive.

## 2. Simulation & Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)
Do not assert safety; measure behavioral thresholds before full deployment.

*   **Simulation Scenarios:**
    *   **Variables:** Test flight altitudes (50m, 100m, 150m+) and speeds against species-specific tolerance levels.
    *   **Acoustic Modeling:** Simulate acoustic signatures at ground level for each scenario. Compare against known disturbance thresholds for target species.
    *   **Control Groups:** Establish "no-fly" zones as negative controls and standard patrol patterns as positive controls for comparison.
*   **Evaluation Metrics:**
    *   **Displacement Rate:** Percentage of animals leaving a defined radius upon drone approach.
    *   **Return Time:** Duration required for animals to resume natural behavior post-exposure.
    *   **Stress Indicators:** Observable agitation or abandonment of nests/burrows.
*   **Stop Conditions:** Immediate suspension of any flight pattern causing >10% displacement or failure to return within 1 hour.

## 3. Mitigation Guidelines (MANAGE)
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** High-stakes actions (entering known breeding zones during critical seasons) require explicit human authorization. Fully autonomous entry into these sensitive areas is prohibited until safety is verified.
*   **Uncertainty & Overrides:** Display confidence tiers for animal detection (e.g., "Confirmed," "Probable," "Unverified") rather than precise percentages. Operators must have an immediate "Abort & Retreat" override.
*   **Acoustic Minimization:**
    *   Mandate flight paths that utilize natural wind noise or terrain masking.
    *   Restrict maximum rotor RPM in sensitive zones, even if it reduces battery efficiency.
    *   Implement "glide modes" where motors idle during passive observation phases.

## 4. Monitoring & Transparency (GOVERN)
*   **Post-Deployment Tracking:**
    *   Deploy passive acoustic monitors and camera traps in patrol zones to track animal movement independent of drone telemetry.
    *   Correlate drone flight logs with animal activity data weekly.
*   **Transparency Artifact (Model/Data Card):**
    *   Publish a versioned document detailing: intended use (poaching detection), out-of-scope uses (breeding season intrusion), measured performance by species/subgroup, and known limitations (battery-driven path aggression).
    *   Mark any unmeasured impact metrics as **UNVERIFIED**; do not fabricate safety rates.
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific team member as the "Ecological Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing data and halting operations if thresholds are breached.

## 5. Legal & Regulatory Routing
*   **Regulatory Duty:** Whether this automated monitoring triggers specific environmental protection regulations or requires public disclosure of AI usage is a legal determination. Route this question to legal counsel immediately; do not assume compliance based on internal testing.
*   **Standards Alignment:** Align governance with frameworks like the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Map, Measure, Manage) and ISO/IEC 42001, but verify specific binding requirements with counsel.

**Definition of Done:**
1.  Harm inventory completed with worst-case scenarios documented.
2.  Simulation evals run with clear pass/fail thresholds for animal displacement.
3.  Human gate implemented for breeding zone access.
4.  Model/Data card published with UNVERIFIED labels where data is missing.
5.  Legal counsel consulted on regulatory disclosure duties.

*Note: This plan identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased." The final launch decision rests with the project owner based on residual risk acceptance.*
*Battery constraints must not override the human-in-the-loop gate for high-stakes ecological interactions.*
