**Prioritized Safety Changes for Fitness Challenge App Beta**

**1. Algorithmic Incentives (Critical – Implement Before Launch)**
*   **Cap Daily Weight Loss Recognition:** Modify the scoring algorithm to ignore or cap weight loss entries exceeding 1-2 lbs (0.5-1 kg) per day. Rapid loss beyond this physiological norm often indicates dehydration or unsafe restriction. Do not award points or leaderboard status for exceeding this threshold.
*   **Decouple Water Intake from "Winning":** Remove water volume as a direct competitive metric for the leaderboard. High water intake alone does not equal health and can encourage water intoxication (hyponatremia) if users drink excessively to climb ranks. Instead, treat water logging as a binary "completed daily goal" badge that unlocks participation, rather than a score multiplier.
*   **Introduce "Consistency" Over "Intensity":** Reweight the algorithm to reward streaks of moderate, sustainable activity over single-day spikes. This shifts the incentive from dangerous bursts to long-term habit formation.

**2. User Interface & Notification Copy (High Priority)**
*   **Remove Urgency Language:** Scan all copy for words like "crush," "drastic," "rapid," "detox," or "fastest." Replace with "sustainable," "consistent," "healthy," and "steady."
    *   *Change:* "Drop pounds fast to lead the pack!" → "Build steady habits to climb the ranks."
    *   *Change:* "Chug water to boost your score!" → "Stay hydrated to keep competing."
*   **Add Explicit Health Disclaimers:** Place a visible, non-dismissible notice on the logging screen: "Rapid weight loss can be dangerous. This app rewards sustainable habits, not extreme measures. Consult a doctor before starting any weight loss program."
*   **Uncouple Notifications from Extreme Metrics:** Ensure push notifications never congratulate users on extreme single-day results (e.g., "Wow, you lost 5lbs today!"). Instead, notify only on streak milestones or consistent weekly averages.

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Guardrails (Operational Control)**
*   **Manual Review Flag:** Since you cannot hire auditors, build a simple internal dashboard flag. If a user logs weight loss >2lbs/day or water intake >1 gallon/day, flag the account for manual review by your team before their score updates the public leaderboard. This acts as your immediate human gate against unsafe automation.
*   **Easy Opt-Out/Reset:** Ensure users can easily hide their stats or reset their streak without penalty if they feel pressured. Do not make these options hard to find.

**Note:** These changes preserve competition by shifting the focus from "who suffers most" to "who is most consistent."
**Note:** Because this involves health outcomes, you must route a final review of your disclaimer language to legal counsel to ensure it meets local liability standards before launch.
