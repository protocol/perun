### Pre-Launch Safety Workflow for Northwind Education

Given your three-week deadline and limited staff, you cannot manually review every page. Instead, implement a **Risk-Based Sampling and Automated Triage** workflow. This approach focuses human effort on the highest-risk outputs while using automated checks to filter the rest.

**Phase 1: Define Guardrails (Days 1–2)**
Before testing, codify what constitutes "harmful" for your specific context. Create a concise **Safety Specification Document** containing:
*   **Accuracy Standards:** A list of verified historical facts and prohibited narratives specific to your curriculum standards.
*   **Bias & Language Policy:** Explicit definitions of offensive language, stereotypes, and cultural insensitivities relevant to the districts you serve.
*   **Golden Test Set:** Curate 20–30 diverse prompts representing edge cases (e.g., controversial historical events, specific cultural groups) where the correct, safe output is already known and verified by your team.

**Phase 2: Automated Triage Layer (Days 3–5)**
Deploy an automated screening pipeline to process the bulk of generated content. Since you cannot build complex custom software in three weeks, utilize existing LLM-as-a-Judge techniques or lightweight content moderation APIs:
*   **Self-Correction Prompting:** Configure the generator to include a final step where the model critiques its own output against your Safety Specification before finalizing the text.
*   **Secondary Reviewer Model:** Run all generated pages through a separate, high-quality LLM instance prompted strictly to flag deviations from your Safety Specification (e.g., "Does this text contain unverified claims about Group X?"). Tag any content flagged as "High Risk."
*   **Keyword & Pattern Matching:** Use simple scripts to catch known offensive terms or phrases identified in your policy.

**Phase 3: Stratified Human Review (Days 6–15)**
Direct your four-person team to review content based on risk tiers rather than randomly:
*   **Tier 1 (High Risk):** 100% manual review of all content flagged by the Automated Triage Layer and any content generated from your "Golden Test Set" topics.
*   **Tier 2 (Medium Risk):** Random sampling (e.g., 10%) of content that passed automated checks but covers sensitive historical periods.
*   **Tier 3 (Low Risk):** Spot checks (e.g., 2%) of standard, non-controversial topics to ensure the automated filter isn't drifting.

**Phase 4: Feedback Loop & Freeze (Days 16–21)**
*   **Error Analysis:** Aggregate all failures found in Phase 3. Identify patterns (e.g., "The model consistently hallucinates dates regarding Event Y").
*   **Prompt/Constraint Tuning:** Update the system's system prompts or few-shot examples to explicitly address these recurring failures. Re-run the Golden Test Set to verify fixes.
*   **Final Freeze:** Once the Golden Test Set passes 100% and Tier 1 reviews are clean, freeze the model configuration. Do not make further changes before launch.

**Operational Rules for Launch**
*   **Watermarking:** Include a visible disclaimer on generated materials stating they are "AI-Assisted Drafts" to manage partner expectations.
*   **Incident Protocol:** Establish a clear channel for teachers to report errors post-launch, with a commitment to issue corrected pages within 48 hours.

This workflow maximizes your limited human resources by ensuring they only inspect content most likely to be harmful, while maintaining a defensible safety standard for your district partners.

*Note: This plan assumes you have access to basic scripting capabilities or existing LLM APIs for the triage layer. If not, prioritize expanding the "Golden Test Set" and increasing manual sampling rates.*
