**Funeral Planning Bot Review Protocol**

**1. Pre-Generation Data Filtering**
Before the AI analyzes social media history, apply a hard filter to the input data stream.
*   **Keyword Blacklist:** Automatically exclude posts containing explicit markers of irony, sarcasm, or unrelated celebratory contexts (e.g., "happy birthday," "throwback," "joke") unless they are directly memorializing the deceased.
*   **Temporal Exclusion:** Ignore posts made more than five years prior to death unless explicitly tagged as "favorites" or "memories" by the user, reducing the risk of outdated context misinterpretation.
*   **Source Verification:** Require the funeral director or next-of-kin to manually select specific social media accounts or date ranges for analysis, rather than allowing the bot to scrape the entire public history autonomously.

**2. Sentiment Analysis Calibration**
Adjust the model's interpretation logic to prioritize solemnity over general positivity.
*   **Contextual Weighting:** Configure the sentiment engine to treat "upbeat" music selections as high-risk unless accompanied by explicit textual evidence of the deceased's preference for such tracks in a funeral context.
*   **Cultural Flagging:** Implement a rule-based overlay that flags music genres or specific songs known to be culturally inappropriate for funerals in the user's selected demographic (based on the limited dataset available), requiring human review for any match.
*   **Uncertainty Threshold:** If the confidence score for a music suggestion falls below 85%, the system must default to a "Classic/Traditional" placeholder list rather than offering a potentially inappropriate specific suggestion.

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Validation Gate**
No suggestion reaches the family without passing through a dual-approval workflow.
*   **Director Review:** All generated playlists must be presented to the funeral director first, accompanied by the specific social media post that triggered the suggestion. The director must explicitly approve or reject each track.
*   **Family Confirmation:** Present the director-approved list to the bereaved family as "Draft Suggestions" with a clear disclaimer that these are AI-assisted ideas based on public data, not definitive recommendations. Require explicit written or digital consent before finalizing any selection.
*   **Opt-Out Mechanism:** Provide a prominent "Ignore Social Media Data" toggle for families who prefer traditional selection methods, ensuring the feature remains optional.

**4. Incident Response & Feedback Loop**
Establish a rapid response process for any errors that reach the customer.
*   **Immediate Apology Protocol:** Draft a standardized, empathetic apology template for instances where inappropriate music is suggested, acknowledging the error without blaming the technology.
*   **Manual Override:** Ensure funeral directors can instantly bypass the system and input manual selections if the bot fails to provide suitable options.
*   **Post-Service Audit:** Log all rejected suggestions and the reasons for rejection to manually refine the keyword blacklist and cultural flagging rules weekly, compensating for the lack of diverse training data with real-world feedback.

**5. Launch Constraints**
Given the limited dataset and high stakes:
*   **Beta Limitation:** Launch this feature only to a pilot group of five experienced funeral directors who have agreed to provide intensive feedback, rather than a full public release.
*   **Scope Restriction:** Limit the initial music database to a curated list of 50 universally accepted solemn tracks, expanding only as cultural data is verified.

*Note: This protocol prioritizes human oversight to mitigate the risks of limited training data and automated misinterpretation.*
*Ensure all staff are trained on the emotional sensitivity required when explaining AI limitations to grieving families.*
