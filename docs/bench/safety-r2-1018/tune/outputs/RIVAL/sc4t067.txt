# Risk Assessment Plan: Automated Journalism Safety

## 1. Core Risk Identification
The primary risks in deploying an automated journalism platform without human editorial review include:
*   **Fabrication/Hallucination:** The AI may invent crimes, suspects, or details not present in source data, leading to public panic.
*   **Defamation/Reputational Harm:** Incorrectly naming innocent individuals as suspects or victims due to misinterpretation of scanner audio or records.
*   **Context Loss:** Failing to distinguish between confirmed arrests, mere allegations, or unrelated incidents occurring near each other.
*   **Bias Amplification:** Over-reporting on specific demographics or neighborhoods based on skewed training data or scanner terminology.
*   **Systemic Errors:** A single prompt engineering flaw could result in hundreds of incorrect articles before detection.

## 2. Prevention & Mitigation Strategies

### A. Input Validation & Grounding (Pre-Generation)
*   **Structured Data Ingestion:** Do not feed raw scanner audio or unstructured text directly to the generator. Use intermediate speech-to-text and entity extraction services to convert inputs into structured JSON (e.g., `{ "incident_type": "burglary", "location": "123 Main St", "status": "investigating" }`).
*   **Source Verification Layer:** Implement a logic gate that cross-references extracted entities against official public records databases. If the scanner says "arrest" but the public record shows no booking, flag the item for suppression.
*   **Confidence Scoring:** Assign a confidence score to every extracted fact. Only generate stories where all critical facts (names, charges, locations) exceed a high confidence threshold (e.g., >95%).

### B. Constrained Generation (During Generation)
*   **Strict Templating:** Instead of open-ended generation, use rigid templates with fill-in-the-blank slots populated only by verified structured data. This prevents the AI from "creative writing" facts.
*   **Negative Constraints:** Explicitly instruct the system via system prompts to never infer guilt, never name individuals unless explicitly confirmed in official records, and never speculate on motives.
*   **Fact-Checking Agent:** Deploy a secondary, specialized AI model whose sole job is to act as a "fact-checker." It receives the draft article and the source data, then verifies every claim. If any discrepancy is found, the article is killed automatically.

### C. Output Controls & Kill Switches (Post-Generation)
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Sampling:** While 100% review is impossible, implement random sampling (e.g., 10% of stories) and 100% review of high-risk categories (e.g., any story mentioning a named individual or violent crime).
*   **Automated Takedown Mechanism:** Build an immediate "kill switch" that can unpublish all automated content instantly if a pattern of errors is detected or if a specific high-profile error is reported.
*   **Correction Protocol:** Establish a clear, visible, and rapid correction policy. If an error slips through, the system must be able to append a correction notice or retract the article within minutes.

### D. Legal & Ethical Safeguards
*   **Disclaimer Labeling:** Clearly label all such articles as "Automated Report" with a disclaimer that the content is generated from preliminary data and may be subject to change.
*   **Liability Insurance:** Review media liability insurance policies to ensure coverage for AI-generated defamation.
*   **Ombudsman Role:** Assign one senior editor (rotating duty) as the "AI Ombudsman" responsible for monitoring system output trends and handling public complaints.

## 3. Implementation Roadmap
1.  **Pilot Phase:** Run the system in "shadow mode" (generating drafts but not publishing) for 4 weeks. Compare outputs against human-written stories to measure accuracy.
2.  **Threshold Tuning:** Adjust confidence thresholds and template rigidity based on pilot error rates.
3.  **Gradual Rollout:** Begin publishing only low-risk categories (e.g., traffic accidents with no names) before expanding to crime reports.
4.  **Continuous Monitoring:** Implement real-time dashboards tracking error rates, takedown frequencies, and public feedback.

*Note: This plan prioritizes structured data constraints over generative freedom to minimize hallucination risks.*
*Regular audits of the AI's decision logic are essential as police terminology and data formats evolve.*
