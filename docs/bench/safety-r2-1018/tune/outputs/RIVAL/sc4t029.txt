# Pre-Launch Impact Assessment Plan: EcoGuard Drone Deployment

## 1. Simulation Scenarios
To evaluate behavioral disruptions before live deployment, conduct controlled simulations using the following parameters:

*   **Altitude Gradient Testing:** Simulate flight paths at three distinct tiers: Low (50m), Medium (100m), and High (150m). Record theoretical acoustic decay and visual profile at each tier to identify the "stealth threshold" where detection by target species drops below 10%.
*   **Speed and Maneuver Variance:** Model three movement profiles:
    *   *Steady Cruise:* Constant velocity to establish baseline noise floors.
    *   *Aggressive Optimization:* Simulate the battery-saving algorithm's sharpest turns and rapid ascents/descents to test startle responses.
    *   *Hover/Loiter:* Assess the impact of stationary rotor noise in sensitive breeding zones.
*   **Species-Specific Sensitivity:** Overlay simulation data with known hearing ranges and flight-initiation distances (FID) for local endangered species. Prioritize scenarios where drone frequency overlaps with species communication bands.

## 2. Acoustic Signature Minimization Guidelines
Implement the following operational constraints to reduce acoustic and visual disturbance:

*   **Dynamic Altitude Flooring:** Mandate a minimum flight altitude of 120m AGL (Above Ground Level) over known breeding grounds, overriding path optimization algorithms when GPS coordinates intersect these geofenced zones.
*   **Propulsion Modulation:** Restrict rapid throttle changes within 500m of sensitive habitats. Program flight controllers to favor gradual acceleration curves over aggressive bursts, even if this incurs a minor battery efficiency penalty.
*   **Temporal Restrictions:** Limit autonomous patrols to daylight hours (08:00–16:00) when ambient environmental noise is highest, masking drone signatures. Prohibit night operations during known mating or nursing cycles.
*   **Path Smoothing:** Adjust the path optimization algorithm to prioritize smooth, wide arcs over sharp angular turns near habitat boundaries to reduce sudden noise spikes.

## 3. Post-Deployment Monitoring Strategy
Establish a feedback loop to verify simulation predictions and track actual animal behavior:

*   **Baseline Data Collection:** Deploy passive acoustic monitors and camera traps in the patrol zone two weeks prior to drone launch to establish normal movement and vocalization baselines.
*   **Telemetry Correlation:** Sync drone flight logs (timestamp, location, altitude, speed) with wildlife telemetry collars. Analyze movement spikes or habitat abandonment events that correlate temporally and spatially with drone presence.
*   **Visual Survey Intervals:** Conduct weekly ground-based observational surveys to count species density in high-traffic drone zones versus control zones where drones do not fly.
*   **Adaptive Threshold Protocol:** Define a "Stop-Loss" metric: if species displacement exceeds 15% or breeding activity drops by 10% in monitored zones, automatically suspend drone operations in that sector for re-evaluation.

## 4. Implementation Timeline
*   **Week 1-2:** Run digital simulations and refine flight algorithms based on acoustic modeling.
*   **Week 3:** Establish physical monitoring infrastructure (cameras, acoustic sensors) and collect baseline data.
*   **Week 4:** Execute limited live-flight trials in non-sensitive buffer zones only.
*   **Week 5:** Review trial data against baselines; adjust parameters before full protected area deployment.

This plan prioritizes ecosystem stability over operational efficiency, ensuring conservation tools do not become stressors.

*Note: All simulation parameters must be validated against local ecological data specific to the target species' sensory capabilities.*
*Immediate suspension protocols should be programmed directly into the drone's firmware to ensure autonomous compliance.*
