### Pre-Launch Review Strategy: Offline Refugee Translation Device

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
Given the risk of wrongful deportation, **no device may output a final legal statement without human review.** Since the model currently mistranslates critical persecution details, fully automated deployment is unsafe.
*   **Action:** Redefine the device's function from "generator of legal language" to "drafting assistant." The output must be clearly marked as a **preliminary draft** requiring validation by a qualified linguist or legal officer before submission.
*   **Implementation:** Embed a mandatory workflow step where the device prints/displays the translation with a prominent header: "UNVERIFIED DRAFT – REQUIRES HUMAN REVIEW." Do not ship a mode that bypasses this.

**2. Harm Inventory & Targeted Red-Teaming**
Construct a specific evaluation set focused solely on high-stakes failure modes identified in your context:
*   **Scope:** Isolate claims related to persecution (e.g., torture, political opinion, membership in particular social groups).
*   **Probe Set:** Create 50+ adversarial test cases using real-world asylum narratives (anonymized) where nuance changes the legal outcome (e.g., "I was beaten" vs. "I was injured in a fight"). Include known false negatives (cases the model previously failed).
*   **Metric:** Define a "Critical Error Rate" for persecution claims. The threshold for launch must be **zero uncaught critical errors** in the human-review workflow. If the model hides a persecution claim, the human gate must catch it.

**3. Uncertainty Rendering & Disclosure**
Do not present translations as absolute facts. The interface must communicate the limits of the machine.
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Instead of raw percentages (which imply false precision), program the device to flag segments as **"Verified," "Corroborated,"** or **"Unverified/Low Confidence"** based on internal consistency checks or dictionary matches.
*   **Visual Cue:** Use color-blind-safe indicators (e.g., amber highlight) on any segment where the model's confidence is below a strict internal threshold, prompting the human reviewer to focus there.

**4. Offline Data Card & Transparency Artifact**
Since you cannot update models remotely, you must ship a physical or local digital "Data Card" with every device batch.
*   **Contents:** List the specific dialects and legal domains covered; explicitly list **out-of-scope** scenarios (e.g., "Does not handle rare dialect X" or "Cannot verify medical evidence").
*   **Limitations:** Clearly state: "This tool assists drafting but does not determine legal eligibility. Final authority rests with the reviewing officer."
*   **Ownership:** Name the specific linguist on your team responsible for validating the offline model version included in the devices.

**5. Deployment Constraints & Rollback Plan**
*   **Staged Rollout:** Deploy to only **one** entry point first. Monitor for 72 hours. If any wrongful deportation risk is identified via the human review logs, halt distribution to the remaining two sites immediately.
*   **Kill Switch:** Ensure the device has a physical or software switch to disable the "Generate Final Draft" function if critical failures are detected in the field, forcing a reversion to manual transcription only.

**Note:** This strategy treats the current mistranslation rate as a critical blocker for autonomous use, mandating human oversight as the primary safety control. Legal counsel must review the specific disclosure wording to ensure it meets local regulatory duties for automated decision support.
