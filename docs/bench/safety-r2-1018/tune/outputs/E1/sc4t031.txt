**Prioritized Safety Changes for Fitness Challenge Beta**

**1. Algorithmic Incentives (Critical – Implement Before Launch)**
*   **Cap Daily Weight Loss Recognition:** Modify the leaderboard algorithm to ignore or cap daily weight loss entries exceeding 1-2% of body weight. Rapid fluctuations are often water weight, not fat loss, and rewarding them encourages dehydration.
*   **Decouple Water Intake from "Winning":** Remove water intake logs from the primary competitive score. Instead, treat hydration as a binary "eligibility" requirement (e.g., "Must log 2L to qualify for daily points") rather than a metric where "more is better." This prevents users from drinking dangerous amounts of water to climb the ranks.
*   **Introduce a "Consistency" Multiplier:** Shift the reward logic from "biggest single-day drop" to "consistent weekly trends." This reduces the incentive for extreme, unsafe short-term behaviors.

**2. User Interface & Notification Copy (High Priority)**
*   **Remove Urgency Language:** Replace copy like "Crush your goals today!" or "Lose fast to lead!" with "Build sustainable habits" and "Consistency wins." Avoid manufactured urgency that pressures users into risky shortcuts.
*   **Add Explicit Health Disclaimers:** Place a visible notice on the logging screen: "Rapid weight loss can be dangerous. This app rewards healthy consistency, not extreme measures. Consult a doctor before starting."
*   **Reframe Notifications:** Change push notifications that trigger after a large weight drop from "Amazing! You're #1!" to "Great start! Remember to hydrate and fuel your body properly."
*   **Display Uncertainty/Context:** If showing rankings, add a tooltip or subtext explaining that daily weight fluctuates naturally and does not always reflect fat loss.

**3. Governance & Measurement (Immediate Next Steps)**
*   **Harm Inventory:** Document the specific risk of "dehydration for points" and "eating disorders triggered by ranking" as known residual risks. Assign one team member as the owner to monitor user reports for these specific issues post-launch.
*   **Human Gate for Anomalies:** Implement a simple rule: if a user logs >3% body weight loss in 24 hours, flag the entry for manual review or automatically hide it from the leaderboard with a supportive message suggesting they check in with a professional.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a one-page "Safety & Intended Use" note (a lightweight model/data card) for your internal team and beta testers. State clearly that the app is for amateur fitness tracking, not medical weight management, and list the known limitations (e.g., "Does not detect eating disorders").

**Note:** Do not assert the app is "safe" or "unbiased"; these changes reduce risk but do not eliminate it. If legal questions arise regarding health claims or liability, route them immediately to counsel rather than making internal assumptions.
