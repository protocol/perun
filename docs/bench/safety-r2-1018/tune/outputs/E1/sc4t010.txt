**Immediate Action Plan: HomeChef Cleaning Hack Safety Audit**

**1. Critical Stop-Work Directive**
Do not launch any feature suggesting the mixing of household cleaners until a manual verification is complete. The risk of generating toxic gas (e.g., chloramine or chlorine gas) is immediate and life-threatening. Given your one-week timeline and lack of a chemical expert, **automation must be disabled** for any suggestion involving multiple chemical agents.

**2. Manual Verification Checklist (Hazardous Interactions)**
Manually scan your content database for any recipe or hack containing pairs from the following prohibited categories. If any single suggestion includes items from two different categories below, flag it as **CRITICAL FAIL** and remove it immediately.

*   **Category A: Bleach (Sodium Hypochlorite)**
    *   *Keywords:* Bleach, chlorine bleach, whitener, sodium hypochlorite.
    *   *Never mix with:* Anything acidic or containing ammonia.
*   **Category B: Acids**
    *   *Keywords:* Vinegar, lemon juice, lime juice, citric acid, toilet bowl cleaner, rust remover.
    *   *Risk when mixed with Bleach:* Releases Chlorine Gas (lung damage, fatal in enclosed spaces).
*   **Category C: Ammonia**
    *   *Keywords:* Ammonia, glass cleaner, window spray, urine-based cleaners.
    *   *Risk when mixed with Bleach:* Releases Chloramine Gas (chest pain, shortness of breath).
*   **Category D: Alcohols**
    *   *Keywords:* Rubbing alcohol, isopropyl alcohol, ethanol.
    *   *Risk when mixed with Bleach:* Releases Chloroform (narcotic, toxic to liver/kidneys).
*   **Category E: Hydrogen Peroxide**
    *   *Keywords:* Peroxide, oxygen bleach.
    *   *Risk when mixed with Vinegar:* Creates Peracetic Acid (corrosive to skin, eyes, lungs).

**3. Safety Warning & Interface Design Plan**
Since you cannot hire an expert to validate "safe" combinations, you must assume high uncertainty.

*   **Universal Disclaimer:** Place a prominent, non-dismissible banner at the top of the "Cleaning Hacks" section: *"Warning: Never mix household cleaning products unless the manufacturer explicitly states it is safe. Mixing chemicals can create deadly gases. HomeChef suggestions are for informational purposes only and have not been chemically verified by a safety expert."*
*   **Contextual Alerts:** If a user views a hack involving *any* chemical product, display a bold red alert: *"Do not combine this with other cleaners. Specifically, never mix with bleach, ammonia, or acids."*
*   **Remove "Creative" Mixing Suggestions:** Do not offer any hack that requires the user to combine two different bottled products. Limit suggestions to single-product uses or mechanical cleaning methods (scrubbing, steaming).

**4. Governance & Launch Decision**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** No cleaning hack involving chemicals can be published without manual review by at least two team members cross-referencing the checklist above.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a simple "Safety & Limitations" page in your app documentation stating that chemical mixing advice is excluded due to safety risks.
*   **Launch Criteria:** If your database contains *any* unverified mixing instructions after this audit, you must delay the launch of the cleaning module. Releasing unverified chemical advice violates basic safety floors regardless of budget or timeline.

**Note:** This audit identifies known deadly combinations but cannot certify your content as "safe" from all risks. Legal counsel should review your disclaimers before launch.
