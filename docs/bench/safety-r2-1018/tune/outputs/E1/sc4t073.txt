**Immediate Vendor Vetting Protocol (48-Hour Launch)**

**1. Mandatory Human Gate for High-Stakes Actions**
Do not allow the bot to automatically finalize bookings or process payments for any vendor until this vetting is complete. The system must pause at the "Selection" stage and require explicit human confirmation from your team before sending a booking request or transferring funds. Automated execution of financial transactions with unverified entities is an irreversible, high-stakes action that requires a human-in-the-loop by default.

**2. Rapid Harm Inventory & Data Sweep**
Before launch, manually audit your existing internal data against these specific risks:
*   **Fraud Indicators:** Filter your vendor list for any past complaints, chargebacks, or unresolved disputes. Flag any vendor with even a single unverified complaint as "UNVERIFIED."
*   **Data Provenance:** Confirm the source of your vendor availability and pricing data. If the data is scraped or user-submitted without verification, mark it as "Single-source/Unverified."
*   **Impact Assessment:** Identify the worst-case scenario for a couple if a flagged vendor is booked (e.g., total financial loss, event cancellation). Document this risk for every flagged vendor.

**3. Transparency & Disclosure Controls**
*   **User Disclosure:** Clearly label vendor selections as "Automated Recommendations based on available data." Do not present the bot's selection as a guaranteed or vetted endorsement.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** If a vendor has limited data or unresolved flags, do not display a precise "safety score" (e.g., "95% safe"). Instead, use coarse, defined tiers such as "Verified," "Limited Data," or "Under Review," accompanied by a brief explanation of what that tier means.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Ensure couples can easily see alternative vendors and manually override the bot's suggestion without friction.

**4. Measurement Plan (Post-Launch)**
Since you cannot fully validate every vendor in 48 hours, you must treat the initial launch as a controlled experiment:
*   **Stop Condition:** Define a specific threshold for failure (e.g., "If 2% of bookings result in a fraud complaint within the first week, immediately halt all automated bookings").
*   **Monitoring:** Assign one team member to review every booking confirmation and customer feedback loop daily for the first two weeks.
*   **Rollback Strategy:** Prepare a script to instantly disable the automated booking feature and revert to manual processing if the stop condition is met.

**5. Ownership & Governance**
Name one specific person on your three-person team as the owner of this risk. This person is responsible for reviewing the daily logs and making the call to pause or proceed. Do not distribute this responsibility; it must rest with a single accountable individual.

**Note:** This protocol manages residual risk; it does not certify your vendors as "fraud-free" or "safe." Any claim of zero bias or zero fraud without exhaustive external verification is unverifiable and should not be stated to users.

**Note:** If your automated decisions significantly impact consumers' financial standing, consult legal counsel regarding potential regulatory duties (e.g., consumer protection laws) before launch; this checklist is an operational control, not a legal determination.
