**Pre-Launch Equity Assessment & Mitigation Plan**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm:** Systematic exclusion of eligible students from specific neighborhoods due to biased zip-code boundary definitions.
*   **Affected Group:** High school students in excluded zip codes; disproportionately impacts low-income or marginalized communities often mapped to these boundaries.
*   **Worst Case:** Qualified students receive no matching opportunities, miss the application cycle entirely, and are denied access to higher education funding based solely on geographic algorithmic bias.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
*   **Eval Design:** Construct a "boundary stress test" dataset.
    *   *Positive Control:* Profiles of known eligible students currently excluded by the filter.
    *   *Negative Control:* Profiles of ineligible students correctly excluded.
    *   *Probe Set:* Synthetic profiles varying only by zip code across boundary edges to map the exact exclusion zone.
*   **Metric:** Exclusion Rate Disparity (difference in match rates between adjacent zip codes with similar demographic profiles).
*   **Threshold:** Owner-defined zero tolerance for known eligible students being excluded by the filter logic.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the stress test confirms exclusion of known eligible profiles, the current geographic filter must not run autonomously for those segments.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** section explicitly stating: "Current geographic filtering relies on zip-code boundaries which may exclude eligible candidates in transition zones. Intended use is initial screening only; final eligibility requires human review." Mark any unmeasured bias metrics as **UNVERIFIED**.

**3. Risk Management & Mitigation (MANAGE)**
*   **Immediate Control (Human-in-the-Loop Gate):** Given the 10-day deadline and constraint against modifying the core engine, implement a mandatory **human review gate** for all applicants flagged as "No Match" or "Low Match" due to geographic filtering.
    *   *Action:* Route all "No Match" results from the identified at-risk zip codes to a manual review queue immediately.
    *   *Owner:* Assign one team member to oversee this queue daily until the deadline.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Update the user interface for students in affected zones to state: "Matching results are preliminary. If you believe you are eligible but received no matches, please contact support for manual review." Do not present the algorithmic "No Match" as a final decision.
*   **Fallback Rule:** If manual review capacity is exceeded, default to "Refer for Manual Review" rather than "Reject" for any application near a boundary zip code.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Designate a specific team lead responsible for the equity outcome of this cycle.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** This logic must be re-evaluated before the next cycle; the current fix is a procedural gate, not a code fix.

**Note:** This plan treats the algorithmic output as unverified for boundary cases and inserts a human gate to prevent irreversible harm (missed funding). Legal counsel should review the disclosure language to ensure it meets regulatory requirements for automated decision-making in education/finance.
