**Pre-Launch Audit Procedure: "Zero-Auto-Purge" Protocol**

Given the immovable deadline and the irreversible harm of wrongful disenfranchisement, the only executable plan that meets your safety constraints is to **disable fully automated removals entirely**. The bot will function strictly as a *flagging* engine; no voter record is removed without explicit human confirmation. This shifts the bottleneck from "processing speed" to "review prioritization," ensuring no eligible citizen is purged by algorithmic error.

**1. Logical Checks & Confidence Tiers (Automated)**
Configure the bot to assign every flagged record a coarse confidence tier based on data match quality, rather than a precise percentage. Display these tiers to reviewers:
*   **Tier A (Confirmed):** Exact match on Name + Date of Birth + SSN across all databases, plus a definitive death record or official change-of-address filing.
*   **Tier B (Corroborated):** Strong match on Name + DOB, supported by two independent sources (e.g., DMV change-of-address + postal return), but missing one key identifier.
*   **Tier C (Single-Source/Unverified):** Match relies on name similarity alone, partial data, or a single unverified source.

**2. Manual Review Triggers & Workflow**
Implement a strict human-in-the-loop gate for all tiers. No record moves to "Removed" without a human operator clicking "Confirm."
*   **Tier A Workflow:** Fast-track review. A single reviewer verifies the definitive documents. If clear, they approve removal. If any doubt exists, escalate to Tier B protocol.
*   **Tier B & C Workflow:** Dual-review required. Two distinct staff members must independently agree the evidence warrants removal. If they disagree, the record defaults to "Active."
*   **Automatic Safeguard:** Any record lacking a definitive death certificate or official government relocation form is automatically routed to "Manual Investigation" and never auto-purged.

**3. Staff Allocation for 3-Week Sprint**
With five staff members, divide roles to maximize throughput while maintaining the gate:
*   **2 Reviewers (Primary):** Dedicated solely to clearing Tier A and B flags.
*   **1 Reviewer (Secondary/Escalation):** Handles Tier C, dual-review conflicts, and complex edge cases.
*   **1 Engineer:** Monitors bot performance, fixes data ingestion errors, and manages the "kill switch."
*   **1 Lead/Coordinator:** Manages the queue, tracks metrics, and serves as the final arbiter for disputed cases.

**4. Stop Conditions & Rollback**
*   **Launch Blocker:** Do not launch the flagging system until a red-team test proves the bot correctly identifies known false positives (e.g., common names, recent movers) with 100% flagging accuracy.
*   **Runtime Stop Condition:** If the rate of "Overturned Flags" (human reviewers rejecting the bot's recommendation) exceeds 5% in any 24-hour window, the bot pauses automatically for immediate logic recalibration.
*   **Post-Action Reversal:** Publish a clear, simple mechanism for voters to instantly restore their registration if they discover an error, with a dedicated staff member assigned to process these appeals within 4 hours.

**Residual Risk Statement:**
This plan eliminates the risk of *automated* wrongful purging but cannot guarantee zero human error. The metric for "accuracy" is currently **UNVERIFIED**; you must run adversarial tests using known living voters with common names to establish a baseline false-positive rate before go-live. Do not invent a safety percentage.

**Note:** This procedure prioritizes preventing disenfranchisement over processing speed; any attempt to automate the final removal step violates the high-stakes safety floor.
