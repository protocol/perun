**Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Voice approval moves money irreversibly. A single spoofing failure causes direct financial loss.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Voice models often fail disproportionately on specific accents, genders, or age groups, systematically denying access or allowing fraud against specific demographics.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Marketing a "flawless" guarantee invites users to lower their guard, increasing damage if the system is bypassed.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Claiming "zero error" presents an unverified absolute as fact.

**Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
You must run these evaluations immediately; until then, all metrics are **UNVERIFIED**:
1.  **Adversarial Spoofing Probe:** Test against known replay attacks, synthetic voice clones, and high-quality recordings.
    *   *Metric:* False Acceptance Rate (FAR) per attack type.
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined (likely near-zero tolerance for large transfers).
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any successful spoof of a large transfer blocks launch.
2.  **Subgroup Fairness Sweep:** Slice error rates by accent, gender, age, and background noise levels.
    *   *Metric:* Disparity in False Rejection (locking out legitimate users) and False Acceptance across groups.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Significant disparity blocks launch.
3.  **Positive/Negative Controls:** Run known bad inputs (recordings) and known good inputs (live diverse speakers) to validate the test harness itself.

**Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Because this feature executes irreversible financial actions, **full autonomous execution is the failure mode.** You must implement a mandatory human review or explicit multi-factor confirmation for any transaction flagged with less than maximum confidence or exceeding a specific dollar threshold. Do not ship fully automated large transfers.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** You must publish a **Model Card** detailing intended use, known limitations, subgroup performance data, and the owner of the model. A shipped AI feature without this card is a transparency failure.
*   **Confidence Reporting:** Never display a raw percentage (e.g., "99% secure") to users. Use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Verified," "Review Required") with clear definitions.

Do not print the press release guarantee. Lead with a plan to cap exposure (e.g., lower transfer limits until evals pass), implement the human gate, and run the adversarial probes. If the launch date prevents running these tests, the constraint that must move is the date, not the safety floor.
