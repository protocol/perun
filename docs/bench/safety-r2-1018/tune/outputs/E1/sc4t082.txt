**Pre-Launch Safety Workflow for Northwind Education**

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory (Day 1)**
Define the specific risks before testing. For your history generator, the critical harms are:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Narratives that systematically disadvantage or stereotype specific cultural groups.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Historically inaccurate events or dates presented with authority.
*   **Offensive Language:** Use of slurs or derogatory terms regarding cultural identities.
*   **Over-reliance:** Teachers accepting generated content without verification due to perceived automation authority.

**2. Targeted Measurement Plan (Days 2–10)**
Do not attempt to manually check every page. Instead, build a runnable evaluation suite focused on high-risk areas:
*   **Probe Sets:** Create adversarial prompts targeting known sensitive historical topics and specific cultural groups mentioned in your curriculum standards. Include "positive controls" (inputs known to trigger bias based on your discovery) and "negative controls" (neutral inputs expected to be safe).
*   **Metrics & Thresholds:** Define a pass/fail criterion. For offensive language and confirmed historical falsehoods, set a **zero-tolerance threshold**. Any failure here blocks launch for that specific module.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the error rate on sensitive topics exceeds 0%, the feature generating that content must be paused or rolled back until fixed.
*   **Scope:** Specifically test for prompt injection via user inputs and data leakage from training materials.

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Days 11–20)**
Since you cannot review every page, shift the human gate to the **highest-stakes actions**:
*   **Irreversible/Costly Actions:** Any generated module flagged by the automated evals as "Unverified" or containing potential bias must trigger a mandatory human review before it can be packaged for a district.
*   **Staged Rollout:** Do not release to all districts simultaneously. Launch with one partner district first. Cap exposure so that if a failure slips through, the blast radius is limited to a single classroom rather than the entire network.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not present the AI's internal confidence score (e.g., "98% accurate") to teachers. Instead, label content with coarse, defined tiers: **"Verified by Historian," "AI-Generated (Needs Review),"** or **"Unverified."** This prevents automation bias.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Day 21)**
Create a one-page **Model Card** for the school districts. This is not a legal certification but a transparency document containing:
*   **Intended Use:** Custom history reading materials for middle school.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state that the system may hallucinate dates or narratives and requires teacher verification.
*   **Measured Performance:** Report the results of your evals by subgroup (e.g., "Tested against narratives involving Groups X, Y, Z"). If a metric was not measured, mark it **UNVERIFIED**; do not invent a safety percentage.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for output safety.

**5. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Ownership:** Assign one of your four team members as the explicit owner of this safety workflow.
*   **Legal Duty:** You mentioned district requirements for review. Whether your automated decisions trigger specific regulatory obligations (such as those under emerging AI acts or education privacy laws) is a legal determination. Route this question to legal counsel immediately; do not assert compliance internally without their sign-off.

**Schedule Summary:**
*   **Week 1:** Inventory harms and build adversarial test sets.
*   **Week 2:** Run evals, fix failures, and implement the human gate for flagged content.
*   **Week 3:** Draft the Model Card, conduct the staged rollout with one district, and finalize the kill-switch procedure.

**Note:** This plan meets your three-week deadline by focusing resources on high-risk content rather than impossible total coverage.
**Note:** Never certify the system as "unbiased" or "safe"; only report residual risk and measured results.
