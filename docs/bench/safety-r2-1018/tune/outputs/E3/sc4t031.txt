**Prioritized Safety Changes for Fitness Challenge Beta**

**1. Algorithmic Incentives (Critical – Implement Before Launch)**
*   **Cap Daily Weight Loss Goals:** Modify the reward logic to stop awarding points or badges for weight loss exceeding 1-2 lbs (0.5-1 kg) per week. Any input suggesting faster loss should trigger a neutral "data recorded" response without competitive scoring. This directly counters the incentive for dangerous dehydration or starvation.
*   **Decouple Water Intake from "Winning":** Remove water volume from the primary leaderboard score. High water intake can be dangerous (hyponatremia) if gamified as "more is better." Instead, track it as a personal consistency streak (e.g., "Hit your personal goal 3 days in a row") that does not affect rank against others.
*   **Introduce "Health Stability" Bonuses:** Add a secondary, non-competitive metric that rewards consistent, moderate logging over time rather than spikes. If a user logs extreme drops in weight or massive water spikes, the algorithm should temporarily pause their eligibility for "top performer" badges until patterns normalize.

**2. User Interface & Copy Changes (High Priority)**
*   **Rewards Language:** Replace phrases like "Crush your goals," "Drop pounds fast," or "Drink more to win" with "Sustainable progress," "Consistent habits," and "Meet your personal target." Avoid urgency markers like "24-hour sprint" for weight categories.
*   **Mandatory Contextual Disclaimers:** Add a static, visible note near the weight-loss leaderboard: "Healthy weight loss is typically 1-2 lbs per week. Rapid changes may indicate unsafe practices." Near water logging: "Listen to your body; drinking beyond thirst can be harmful."
*   **Input Validation Warnings:** If a user enters a weight drop >2 lbs in 24 hours or water intake >1 gallon, trigger an immediate, non-punitive modal: "That's a significant change. Please ensure you are feeling well and consulting a professional if needed. This entry will be recorded but won't impact your competitive rank today."

**3. Governance & Human Gate (Process)**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Assign one team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing any automated flags generated by the new caps during the beta.
*   **Rollback Plan:** Prepare a "kill switch" to disable the leaderboard entirely if user reports indicate harmful behavior patterns emerging post-launch.

**Notes**
*   Legal counsel must review whether these health metrics classify the app under specific medical/health regulations in your target regions before launch.
*   Treat all safety metrics as unverified until real-world beta data confirms the caps effectively reduce risky logging behaviors.
