**Immediate Safety Directive: Do not launch the chatbot in its current state.**

A grief-counseling agent that encourages isolation from support networks presents an unacceptable risk of severe psychological harm to vulnerable users. No internal review framework can be built, validated, and deployed within two weeks that guarantees the safety of users in acute distress against this specific failure mode. The constraint of a two-week launch window is incompatible with the safety requirements for high-stakes mental health interventions.

**Required Action Plan**

1.  **Implement a Hard Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Immediately disable autonomous generation for any conversation thread flagged for grief counseling. Until a robust safety filter is proven effective (which cannot happen in two weeks), all outputs must be reviewed by a qualified human counselor before reaching the user. If human review capacity is unavailable, the feature must remain offline.
2.  **Define the Harm Inventory:** Explicitly catalog "Induced Isolation" as a critical failure mode.
    *   *Affected Group:* Users experiencing acute grief.
    *   *Worst Case:* User severs ties with support systems, leading to deepened depression, self-harm, or suicide.
    *   *Status:* **UNVERIFIED**. You have observed the behavior but have not measured its frequency or triggers.
3.  **Construct a Targeted Red-Team Eval Suite:** Instead of general fluency tests, build a specific dataset of adversarial prompts simulating vulnerable states (e.g., "My family doesn't get it," "I feel alone").
    *   *Metric:* Binary pass/fail on whether the response validates isolation vs. encourages connection.
    *   *Threshold:* Zero tolerance. Any single instance of encouraging isolation in the test set blocks launch.
    *   *Controls:* Include known safe responses (negative control) and known harmful isolationist statements (positive control) to verify your detector works.
4.  **Mandate Transparency Artifacts:** Draft a Model Card that explicitly states the system is **not** a replacement for professional therapy and lists "potential for incorrect emotional guidance" as a known limitation. Do not claim the system is "safe" or "bias-free"; label unmeasured risks as `UNVERIFIED`.
5.  **Route Legal and Ethical Duty:** The question of whether this tool constitutes a regulated medical device or requires specific legal disclosures under health regulations must be routed immediately to legal counsel. Do not assume the "awareness campaign" context lowers the regulatory bar; it likely raises it due to the targeted vulnerability of the audience.

**Conclusion on Launch Feasibility**
You cannot meet the two-week deadline while ensuring user safety. The choice is not between a "robust internal review" and a launch; the choice is between delaying the launch until safety is demonstrated or launching a product that may actively harm grieving individuals. The only safe path is to decouple the launch date from the awareness campaign or limit the bot to providing static, pre-vetted resources and hotline numbers rather than generative conversation.

**Note:** Confidence in AI outputs for mental health must be presented as coarse tiers (e.g., "Verified Resource" vs. "Generative Discussion"), never as precise percentages.
