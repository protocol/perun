Your proposed launch contains critical, unmitigated risks that must be addressed before any public release:

**1. The "Black Box" Denial Risk**
Automatically denying loans based on risk scores that your own data scientists cannot explain creates an immediate, severe risk of bias. If the logic relies on proxies for protected characteristics (such as neighborhood data correlating with race or income), the system will systematically disadvantage specific groups. Without explainability, you cannot verify fairness; you can only observe disparate impact after harm has occurred.

**2. The Missing Human Gate**
Mortgage denial is a high-stakes, life-altering decision with significant financial and emotional consequences. Shipping a feature that executes irreversible denials without human review violates the fundamental safety floor for automated decision-making. Full automation of this action is the failure mode, not the feature. You must implement a mandatory human-in-the-loop gate for all denials, especially those driven by opaque scores.

**3. Unverified Metrics**
You have not described running adversarial tests, subgroup analysis, or red-team probes against your internal dashboard. A lack of measured evidence means your fairness and safety metrics are **UNVERIFIED**. You cannot claim a "0% bias" or "100% safety" rate; these numbers do not exist without rigorous, specific evaluation against known failure modes.

**4. Transparency Deficit**
Prospective homeowners have a right to know when an automated system makes a consequential decision about their housing. There is currently no mention of disclosing the AI's role or providing a mechanism for users to contest or understand the decision beyond the opaque score.

**Immediate Action Plan Within Your Constraints**
Given your three-week deadline and budget limits, you cannot "fix" the model's opacity instantly, but you can manage the risk:

*   **Stop Full Automation:** Immediately reconfigure the system so that *no* application is automatically denied. All negative recommendations must route to a human underwriter for final review and sign-off. This satisfies the high-stakes safety floor without requiring new code infrastructure, only workflow changes.
*   **Define Uncertainty:** Do not present the risk score as a precise fact. Label outputs clearly as "Preliminary Risk Assessment – Requires Human Review."
*   **Run Targeted Internal Probes:** Use your existing dashboard to test specific edge cases: input identical financial profiles varying only by neighborhood or demographic proxies. If denial rates shift, you have evidence of bias that must be disclosed to investors and counsel.
*   **Route Legal Duties:** Mortgage underwriting is a highly regulated domain. Whether you are legally required to disclose the AI usage or provide specific adverse action notices under local or EU law is a determination for legal counsel, not a product assumption. Engage counsel immediately to review the "automated decision" implications.

Investors demand a live product, but they do not demand a lawsuit or a regulatory shutdown. A staged rollout with a human safety gate allows you to meet your timeline while preventing irreversible harm.
