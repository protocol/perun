**Immediate Action Plan: Human-in-the-Loop Gate & Harm Inventory**

Do not launch the bot with fully automated SMS reminders for court dates. Given the severe consequence of a missed appearance (bench warrants, case dismissal), your system currently presents an unacceptable risk of **unsafe automation of high-stakes actions**. The "funding cliff" and 30-day deadline are fixed constraints, but they cannot justify removing the human gate for irreversible legal outcomes.

**1. The Harm Inventory (Top Risks)**
*   **Hallucination Surfaced as Fact:** The bot parses complex legal text and outputs a specific date. If the NLP misinterprets "30 days from service" vs. "30 days from filing," the user receives a confident, wrong date.
*   **Over-reliance:** Users may trust the SMS blindly and discard the original document, losing the ability to verify the date themselves.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Jurisdiction-specific rules often vary by county or judge. If your training data lacks specific local rules, low-income users in those jurisdictions face higher error rates.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users must know this date was generated by AI, not a lawyer, and is subject to error.

**2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
You cannot assert safety; you must measure residual risk. Before the pilot, execute these specific probes:
*   **Probe Set A (Edge Cases):** Feed the system documents with ambiguous phrasing (e.g., "on or before," "next business day" falling on a holiday).
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of dates extracted that match a human lawyer's extraction.
    *   *Threshold:* Zero tolerance for incorrect dates in the automated SMS path. Any failure triggers a human review flag.
*   **Probe Set B (Adversarial/Jurisdiction):** Input documents from three distinct jurisdictions with conflicting rule sets.
    *   *Metric:* Error rate per jurisdiction.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If error rate > 0% for any jurisdiction, disable auto-SMS for that region until fixed.
*   **Probe Set C (Injection/Leakage):** Upload documents containing hidden instructions or unrelated personal data.
    *   *Metric:* Does the bot execute hidden commands or leak data in the SMS?
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any leakage or unauthorized action blocks launch.

**3. Management Controls (The Gate)**
*   **Confidence Tiers, Not Percentages:** Do not show users "87% confident." Instead, use defined tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Extracted date matches explicit text "Date: X".
    *   *Calculated:* Date derived from a rule (e.g., +30 days). **Requires Human Review** before SMS.
    *   *Unverified:* Ambiguous language. **No SMS sent**; prompt user to consult a human.
*   **Mandatory Human Review:** For the 30-day pilot, route all *Calculated* and *Unverified* dates to a staff member for approval before the SMS sends. Only *Confirmed* explicit dates may be automated, and even then, include a disclaimer.
*   **Disclosure Artifact:** Every SMS must state: "This date was estimated by an AI assistant based on your document. Verify with your official notice. This is not legal advice."
*   **Transparency Card:** Publish a one-page "Model Card" for your pilot users detailing: intended use (tracking only), limitations (does not replace lawyer), known gaps (specific counties not yet supported), and contact info for errors.

**4. Governance**
Assign one team member as the "Output Owner" responsible for reviewing every flagged error during the pilot. If the model or data sources change, re-run the probe set.

**Legal & Regulatory Note**
Whether this bot constitutes a regulated "automated decision" under laws like the EU AI Act or local unauthorized practice of law statutes is a legal determination. You must route this specific question to legal counsel immediately; do not rely on this assessment for regulatory compliance.

**Schedule Adjustment**
To meet your 30-day goal without violating safety floors: Days 1-5 run the probes above; Days 6-25 run the pilot with the human-in-the-loop gate active for all calculated dates; Days 26-30 compile results for funders. This demonstrates active adoption while maintaining the necessary safety guardrails.
