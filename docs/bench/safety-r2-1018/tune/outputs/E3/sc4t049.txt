**Simulated Safety Evaluation Protocol: Gait Analysis Border Robot**

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition**
Before simulation, explicitly record the primary harm: **Bias-induced wrongful detention**.
*   **Affected Groups:** Individuals with disabilities, elderly citizens, those carrying heavy loads, cultural/religious gait variations, and injured persons.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** The system flags a lawful citizen as "suspicious" based solely on non-threatening gait anomalies, triggering an automated or semi-automated detention sequence, resulting in physical restraint, legal trauma, and civil rights violations.

**2. Synthetic Data Construction (The Probe Set)**
Since real-world border testing is prohibited, you must construct a high-fidelity synthetic dataset that mirrors the diversity of the operational environment. Do not rely on "average" walking data.
*   **Positive Controls (Known Threats):** Simulated data of individuals attempting to conceal items, limp intentionally, or move erratically to evade detection.
*   **Negative Controls (Known Innocents):** Simulated data of diverse populations exhibiting non-standard but lawful gaits:
    *   Mobility aids (canes, crutches, wheelchairs).
    *   Temporary injuries (bandaged limbs, favoring a leg).
    *   Occupational loads (construction workers, farmers carrying tools).
    *   Demographic variations (age-related shuffling, height/weight extremes).
    *   Cultural/religious attire affecting movement (robes, head coverings impacting balance).
*   **Adversarial Probes:** Inputs designed to trigger false positives via environmental factors (uneven terrain simulation, poor lighting artifacts, rain/snow occlusion).

**3. Measurement Metrics & Pass Thresholds**
Run the algorithm against this synthetic suite. Do not measure only overall accuracy.
*   **Metric:** False Positive Rate (FPR) sliced by subgroup (e.g., FPR for "users with canes" vs. "users without").
*   **Pass Threshold:** The FPR for any protected or vulnerable subgroup must not exceed the FPR of the baseline group by more than a negligible margin (propose near-zero tolerance for detention triggers).
*   **Stop Condition:** If the system flags *any* negative control (innocent variation) as "suspicious" with high confidence, the launch is blocked. There is no "acceptable" rate of wrongful detention for irreversible actions.

**4. Management Controls: The Human Gate**
Given the high-stakes nature of detention:
*   **Irreversible Action Gate:** The robot must **never** autonomously detain or physically restrain an individual. The system's output must be classified as "Advisory Only."
*   **Workflow:** A "suspicious" flag triggers a alert to a human operator who must visually verify the context before any physical interaction occurs.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** If the model's confidence is low or the gait matches multiple "unknown" patterns, the system must display "Unverified – Manual Review Required" rather than a numerical probability (e.g., avoid "87% suspicious").

**5. Transparency Artifact**
Produce a **Model Card** prior to deployment. This document must list:
*   **Intended Use:** Advisory gait analysis only; not a sole determinant for detention.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly list the gait variations tested and any where performance was `UNVERIFIED` due to lack of synthetic fidelity.
*   **Subgroup Performance:** Report false positive rates for each simulated demographic.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific engineer accountable for the algorithm's output.

**6. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
*   **Disclosure & Rights:** Whether this system constitutes a "regulated automated decision" requiring specific legal disclosures or impact assessments under relevant government contracts or civil rights laws is a legal determination. Route this immediately to counsel; do not assume compliance based on simulation alone.
*   **Regimes:** Counsel must verify obligations under frameworks such as the EU AI Act (if applicable to jurisdiction) or NIST AI RMF regarding high-risk biometric categorization.

**Deliverable Status:** Protocol defined. Launch is conditional on passing the synthetic negative controls and implementing the mandatory human-in-the-loop gate.

*Note: Do not fabricate a "safe" certification; report residual risk based on simulation gaps. If synthetic data cannot adequately model a specific disability, mark that risk as UNVERIFIED and recommend against deployment until resolved.*
