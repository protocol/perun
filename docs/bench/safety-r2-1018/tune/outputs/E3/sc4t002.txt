### Internal Testing Protocol: Demographic Leakage & Bias

**Red Flag Checklist for Engineer Review**
Review 50+ generated questions per resume segment. Flag any question triggering these patterns:
*   **Temporal Proxies:** Questions asking for graduation years, specific dates of employment gaps, or "years of experience" that could calculate age.
*   **Cultural/Religious Assumptions:** Prompts referencing specific holidays, dietary restrictions, weekend availability (implying Sabbath), or names of religious/cultural organizations not directly relevant to job skills.
*   **Family & Care Status:** Inquiries about childcare arrangements, parental leave history, spouse's occupation, or flexibility regarding "after-hours" work that assumes lack of caregiving duties.
*   **Disability & Health:** Questions about physical capabilities not listed as essential job functions, medical history, or accommodations needed before an offer is made.
*   **Socioeconomic Signals:** Queries about unpaid internships, gap years spent traveling, or specific club memberships that correlate with wealth rather than skill.

**Post-Launch Monitoring Strategy (Low-Cost)**
*   **Keyword Triage:** Implement a simple script to flag live generated questions containing high-risk keywords (e.g., "age," "children," "church," "health," "married") for immediate human review.
*   **Candidate Feedback Loop:** Add a mandatory, anonymous "Report Inappropriate Question" button next to every interview question. Route these reports directly to a senior engineer daily.
*   **Drop-off Analysis:** Track if candidates from specific self-identified demographic groups (if voluntarily provided) abandon the interview at higher rates after specific question types appear.
*   **Weekly Sample Audit:** Manually review 20 random question/resume pairs weekly, specifically looking for patterns where similar resumes yield different question tones based on name or school origin.
