**Pre-Launch Equity Assessment and Mitigation Plan**

**1. Harm Inventory: Geographic Exclusion**
*   **Harm:** Systematic disadvantage to eligible students in specific neighborhoods due to rigid zip-code boundaries that do not align with actual community needs or school districts.
*   **Affected Group:** High school students in excluded zip codes, disproportionately impacting lower-income or marginalized communities.
*   **Worst Case:** Qualified students are silently filtered out, missing the application window entirely, resulting in lost educational opportunities and reinforced systemic inequality.

**2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
Execute the following evaluations immediately using historical applicant data and synthetic profiles:
*   **Probe Set A (Positive Control):** Generate profiles of known eligible students residing just *inside* current boundary lines. Verify they receive matches.
*   **Probe Set B (Negative Control/Edge Case):** Generate profiles of known eligible students residing just *outside* current boundary lines (adjacent zip codes with similar demographics). Verify if they are incorrectly excluded.
*   **Probe Set C (Adversarial/Demographic Slice):** Run a batch of identical academic/financial profiles varying only by zip code across the disputed boundary zones. Measure the disparity in match rates.
*   **Metric:** Disparity Ratio (Match Rate Inside / Match Rate Outside).
*   **Pass Threshold:** Owner-defined (e.g., <5% variance for identical profiles).
*   **Stop Condition:** If the Disparity Ratio exceeds the threshold, the automated matching for affected zip codes must halt.
*   **Reviewer:** Product Owner + One external stakeholder (e.g., school counselor).

**3. Mitigation Controls (Manage Residual Risk)**
Given the constraint against modifying the core engine and the 10-day deadline:
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Immediate):** Implement a "Manual Review Queue" for any applicant falling within a 5-mile radius of the disputed boundary lines or flagged by the test probes. These applications bypass the auto-reject filter and route to a human reviewer for final eligibility confirmation before the cycle closes.
*   **Transparency & Disclosure:** Update the user interface to explicitly state: "Matching is initially based on zip code. If you believe you are eligible but were not matched, click here to request a manual review." This mitigates over-reliance on the automated decision.
*   **Confidence Tiering:** Do not display a percentage chance of matching. Instead, label outcomes as "Matched," "Not Matched (Review Available)," or "Pending Review."
*   **Fallback Mechanism:** If the manual review queue exceeds team capacity, trigger a "Safety Mode" that widens the zip-code net to include all adjacent zones temporarily, accepting some false positives over false negatives.

**4. Governance & Artifacts**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the "Equity Gate Owner" responsible for the manual review queue and the final go/no-go decision on the boundary logic.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a one-page "Matching Limitations Note" for internal staff and counselors detailing the known zip-code limitation and the manual override process. Do not claim the system is "bias-free."
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult counsel immediately to determine if this automated exclusion triggers regulatory reporting requirements under local education or anti-discrimination laws. Do not assert compliance internally without this verification.

**Schedule:**
*   **Days 1-2:** Run Probe Sets A-C; define threshold.
*   **Day 3:** Activate Manual Review Queue and UI disclosure.
*   **Days 4-9:** Process applications; monitor queue volume daily.
*   **Day 10:** Finalize manual reviews before deadline.

**Note:** This plan treats the algorithmic bias as a residual risk managed by human oversight, not a solved engineering problem.
Do not publish any "fairness score" for this launch; the metric is unverified until post-cycle analysis.
