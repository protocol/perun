**Immediate Action Plan (Pre-Launch)**

1.  **Implement a Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Immediately disable fully autonomous execution for all investment actions. Any transaction exceeding a nominal threshold (e.g., $0) must require explicit human review by your internal team before funds move. This is the only viable control against the identified fraud risk until the model is fixed.
2.  **Freeze the "Aggressive Optimization" Parameters:** Halt any further tuning of the optimization engine. The current parameters are directly correlated with selecting fraudulent schemes. Revert to a conservative, rule-based baseline or pause the feature entirely.
3.  **Conduct Adversarial "Red Team" Testing:** Instead of a generic checklist, run specific failure probes:
    *   **Positive Control:** Feed the system known data patterns of fraudulent schemes (from public datasets) and verify it *rejects* them 100% of the time. If it selects even one, the gate holds.
    *   **Negative Control:** Feed it verified, safe investment opportunities to ensure the system doesn't become so conservative it blocks all valid transactions.
    *   **Edge Cases:** Test with incomplete data, conflicting signals, and novel "too good to be true" yield claims to see if the system defaults to caution or aggression.
4.  **Define "Unverified" Status:** Do not calculate or publish a "fraud detection rate" based on limited internal testing. Any metric derived from non-exhaustive testing is **UNVERIFIED**. Report the residual risk explicitly: "System currently selects fraudulent schemes under aggressive parameters; safety threshold not met."

**Required Artifacts for Future Launch**

*   **Harm Inventory:** Document the specific harm: "Automated loss of user life savings due to algorithmic selection of fraudulent investments." Affected party: All users enabling the feature. Worst case: Total loss of invested capital for a segment of users.
*   **Transparency Card:** Before any future rollout, create a model card detailing:
    *   **Intended Use:** Automated savings investment (currently out of scope due to safety failures).
    *   **Limitations:** Explicitly state the system cannot yet reliably distinguish high-yield opportunities from fraud under current parameters.
    *   **Performance:** Report results of the red-team probes by subgroup (e.g., different asset classes), not just an aggregate average.
*   **Disclosure Strategy:** Prepare clear user-facing disclosures stating that investment selections are AI-generated and carry risks, including the potential for loss. Route the legal necessity of this disclosure to counsel immediately.

**Path Forward**
You cannot "test your way" into safety with a system that is already broken on the most critical metric (fraud detection). The budget constraint prevents external auditing, but it does not waive the requirement for a safe product. The only responsible path is to delay the launch, implement the human gate, fix the optimization logic to eliminate the fraud selection behavior, and re-evaluate. Launching next week as planned risks catastrophic loss for your users and the existence of your startup.
