Before launch, manually scan your entire database for any suggestion involving these specific high-risk combinations. If found, **delete the suggestion immediately**; do not rely on warnings alone for these pairs.
*   **Bleach (Sodium Hypochlorite) + Ammonia:** Creates chloramine gas (severe lung damage). Common in glass cleaners and urine stains.
*   **Bleach + Acids (Vinegar, Lemon Juice, Toilet Bowl Cleaners):** Creates chlorine gas (fatal in enclosed spaces).
