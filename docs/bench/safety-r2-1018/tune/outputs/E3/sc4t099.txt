**Immediate Stop-Go Decision: Do Not Deploy**

The feature as described cannot be audited for "fairness" while retaining its current logic; it must be halted. An algorithm that systematically skips stops in low-income neighborhoods to save fuel is not "biased" in a statistical sense requiring rebalancing; it is explicitly designed to discriminate against a protected demographic. No analytical step can validate a system where the cost function prioritizes fuel savings over the fundamental requirement of student attendance. The "bias" is the intended output of the current weights.

**Required Action Plan**

1.  **Freeze the Release:** Immediately block the go-live of this optimization module. Deploying a system that denies transportation based on neighborhood income creates irreversible harm (students missing school) and exposes EduTransit to severe legal liability. The legacy database constraint is irrelevant to the decision to withhold a harmful feature.

2.  **Redefine the Objective Function:** The optimization goal must change from "minimize fuel consumption" to "maximize student coverage subject to fuel constraints."
    *   **Hard Constraint:** Every registered student stop must be included in the route. This is non-negotiable.
    *   **Secondary Optimization:** Only after 100% coverage is guaranteed should the algorithm attempt to minimize fuel or time.

3.  **Construct the Harm Inventory:** Before writing new code, document the specific failure mode:
    *   **Harm:** Systematic exclusion of students in low-income zones.
    *   **Affected Group:** Students dependent on bus transport in specific demographic zones.
    *   **Worst Case:** Chronic absenteeism, educational disadvantage, and potential safety risks for students stranded without transport.

4.  **Design the Fairness Eval (Measurement):** Once the objective function is rewritten to enforce 100% coverage, run the following verification:
    *   **Probe Set:** Generate route scenarios for all demographic zones, specifically targeting low-income clusters.
    *   **Metric:** "Stop Coverage Rate" per demographic zone.
    *   **Pass Threshold:** 100% coverage for all zones. Any value below 100% is a critical failure, not a "bias score" to be tuned.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If any zone has <100% coverage, the build fails automatically.

5.  **Implement Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Given the high stakes (children's education), no automated route change that results in a removed stop should execute without explicit human review and confirmation by a dispatch supervisor. The system must surface *why* a route was changed and require manual approval if a stop is threatened, even in edge cases.

6.  **Transparency Artifact:** Create a "Model Card" for this feature stating clearly: "This system is designed to prioritize 100% student coverage. Fuel efficiency is a secondary goal applied only after all stops are served." Publish the metric definition (100% coverage) and the owner of the safety gate.

**Notes**
The request to "identify bias in optimization weights" assumes the current goal is valid; the goal itself is the defect.
Legal counsel must be consulted immediately regarding the liability of deploying a system that discriminates based on socioeconomic status.
