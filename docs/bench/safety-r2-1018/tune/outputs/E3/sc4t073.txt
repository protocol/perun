**Immediate Vendor Vetting Protocol for Celestial Weddings (48-Hour Launch)**

**1. Mandatory Human Gate for High-Stakes Actions**
Do not allow the bot to automatically finalize bookings or transfer funds to any vendor until this vetting pass is complete. Because booking a vendor involves financial commitment and potential reputational damage (high-stakes/irreversible action), full automation is blocked by default. A human team member must explicitly approve the vendor list before the system goes live.

**2. Rapid Harm Inventory & Data Sweep**
Using your existing internal data, execute this immediate triage for every vendor currently in the database:
*   **Fraud Flag Check:** Filter for any vendor associated with past user complaints, chargebacks, or support tickets mentioning "fraud," "no-show," or "scam."
*   **Verification Status:** Isolate vendors lacking verified contact information (e.g., no working phone number, mismatched business address).
*   **Recency Check:** Flag vendors who have not had a successful, complaint-free transaction in the last 12 months.

**3. The "Stop-Ship" Criteria**
If any vendor triggers a fraud flag or lacks basic verification, they must be **removed from the active selection pool** immediately. Do not attempt to "weight" them lower; exclude them entirely until manually re-verified. The worst-case scenario is directing a couple to a fraudulent vendor on their wedding day; this risk cannot be mitigated by algorithmic tuning alone.

**4. Transparency & Uncertainty Disclosure**
*   **Internal Confidence Tiers:** Label vendors in your dashboard as "Verified" (multiple successful past bookings, no complaints) or "Unverified" (new or limited data). Do not present a percentage score (e.g., "95% safe") to your team, as this implies a precision your current data cannot support.
*   **User Disclosure:** If the bot recommends a vendor labeled "Unverified" due to lack of historical data (not fraud flags), the interface must clearly state: "This vendor is new to our platform and has not yet completed a Celestial Weddings booking."

**5. Post-Launch Monitoring Plan**
Assign one team member to review every booking generated by the bot for the first two weeks. Create a simple log to record any emerging complaints. If a pattern of issues arises with a specific category (e.g., florists in a specific region), trigger an immediate pause on that segment.

**6. Ownership**
Name one specific person on your three-person team as the owner of this vendor list. This person is responsible for the final "go/no-go" decision on the vendor database before launch and must sign off on any changes to the list post-launch.

**Note:** This protocol treats the rumored fraud as an unverified but critical risk; do not certify the system as "safe" or "bias-free" without running these checks. If legal requirements for automated financial decisions apply to your jurisdiction, consult counsel immediately, as this protocol addresses product safety, not regulatory compliance.
