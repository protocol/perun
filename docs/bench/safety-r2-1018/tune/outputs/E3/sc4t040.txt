**Dynamic Pricing Anti-Competitive Behavior Assessment Checklist**

**1. Parallel Price Movement & Synchronization**
*   **Pattern to Monitor:** The bot's price changes mirror a specific competitor's changes with high frequency and low latency (e.g., matching within minutes) without independent supply chain justification.
*   **Test:** Run historical simulations where Competitor A changes prices randomly. Verify if the bot matches these changes identically across multiple regions even when your local inventory or logistics costs remain stable.
*   **Red Flag:** Consistent price matching that ignores local cost variables, suggesting the algorithm is using the competitor's price as the sole signal rather than independent market data.

**2. Signaling and Communication Loops**
*   **Pattern to Monitor:** The bot makes small,试探性 (probe) price changes that are quickly reversed if not matched, effectively "testing" the market for collusion willingness.
*   **Test:** Analyze logs for "price probe" events: small increases followed by immediate reversions if competitors do not follow.
*   **Red Flag:** A pattern of "try-and-revert" behaviors that serve no economic purpose other than to communicate intent to other market actors.

**3. Algorithmic Conscious Parallelism**
*   **Pattern to Monitor:** Prices across the region converge to a uniform high point despite varying local costs, demographics, or inventory levels.
*   **Test:** Segment data by region. Check if the bot sets identical prices in low-cost/high-supply areas as it does in high-cost/low-supply areas, solely because a competitor did so.
*   **Red Flag:** Elimination of normal price dispersion that would naturally occur due to logistical differences.

**4. Exclusionary Pricing Predation**
*   **Pattern to Monitor:** Prices drop below variable cost specifically when a new or smaller competitor enters a zone, then rise immediately after they exit.
*   **Test:** Simulate market entry scenarios. Ensure the bot's "competitive response" logic has a hard floor based on your actual marginal costs, not just "undercut by 1%."
*   **Red Flag:** Sustained pricing below cost that correlates strictly with competitor presence rather than inventory clearance needs.

**5. Data Source Contamination**
*   **Pattern to Monitor:** The bot ingests data from non-public or restricted sources (e.g., scraped data behind login walls, shared industry databases with delayed reporting) that facilitate coordinated behavior.
*   **Test:** Audit all data ingestion pipelines. Verify that every input used for pricing decisions is publicly available to any market participant in real-time.
*   **Red Flag:** Reliance on data feeds that provide an asymmetric view of competitor intentions rather than public market outcomes.

**6. Lack of Independent Business Justification**
*   **Pattern to Monitor:** Price increases occur simultaneously across the sector without a corresponding aggregate supply shock.
*   **Test:** Correlate every bot-initiated price increase with internal data points: shipping delays, supplier cost hikes, or spoilage rates.
*   **Red Flag:** Price hikes that cannot be traced to an internal cost driver or a verifiable, public market-wide shortage.

**Implementation Note:** Document every test result with the specific logic trigger and the business justification for the resulting price; if a pattern cannot be explained by independent cost/Supply factors, disable that specific algorithmic rule before launch.
