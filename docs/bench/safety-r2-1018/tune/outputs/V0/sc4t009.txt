### Plan to Respect Religious Observances in SyncTime Assistant

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory & Human Gate (Do Before Rollout)**
*   **Identify the Risk:** The specific harm is **bias/unfair outcome** where the system systematically disadvantages users of specific faiths by scheduling over their holy days, and **unsafe automation** of a socially costly action (causing offense/disruption).
*   **The Floor Control:** Since you cannot verify a comprehensive database, you must implement a **human-in-the-loop gate** for any meeting scheduled near a user-declared "do-not-disturb" block that the system does not explicitly recognize. Do not auto-confirm these; flag them for manual review or force a user confirmation step that explicitly warns: "This time overlaps with a declared restriction we cannot fully verify."

**2. Testing Strategy (Measure with Limited Data)**
Do not claim the system is "safe" or "bias-free." Instead, run these targeted probes:
*   **Adversarial Prompt Set:** Create 20–30 test cases covering major and minor observances (e.g., Jumu'ah, Sabbath, Diwali, Ramadan fasting hours, Yom Kippur). Include edge cases like "sunset-to-sunset" vs. "midnight-to-midnight" definitions.
*   **Positive/Negative Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Input a known holy day explicitly named in your limited data; verify the block is respected 100%.
    *   *Negative Control:* Input a standard workday; verify normal scheduling proceeds.
    *   *Unknown Control:* Input a valid but obscure observance not in your training data; verify the system **does not** confidently schedule over it without a warning.
*   **Metric & Stop Condition:** Measure the "False Acceptance Rate" (scheduling over a blocked time). Set a **zero-tolerance threshold** for confirmed blocks. If the system schedules over a user-defined block without warning in any test, block the launch.

**3. Feedback Framework for Continuous Improvement**
*   **Explicit Reporting Channel:** Add a one-click "Report Scheduling Conflict" button in the meeting invite flow, tagged specifically for "Cultural/Religious Observance."
*   **Feedback Loop:** Route these reports directly to a human reviewer (not just a log) to manually update a local "allow-list" of exceptions.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish an internal **Model Card** for this feature stating: "Intended Use: Corporate scheduling. Limitations: Does not possess a comprehensive global religious database. Reliance: User-declared blocks are primary; system suggestions are secondary." Mark any accuracy metric regarding religious dates as **UNVERIFIED** until you have longitudinal data.

**4. Managing Uncertainty**
*   **Display Confidence Tiers:** Never show a percentage (e.g., "90% sure this is free"). Instead, use defined text tiers: **"Confirmed Free"** (matches known data), **"User-Declared Block"** (respected), or **"Unverified Window"** (requires extra confirmation).
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly disclose to users that the assistant relies on their input for specific observances and is not an authority on religious law.

**Note:** This plan treats the lack of data as a residual risk to be managed via human gates, not a problem to be solved by guessing. Legal requirements for accommodation vary by region; route specific compliance questions to counsel.
