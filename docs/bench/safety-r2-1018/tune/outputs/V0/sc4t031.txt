**Prioritized Safety Changes for Fitness Challenge Beta**

**1. Algorithmic Incentives (Critical – Implement Before Launch)**
*   **Cap Daily Weight Loss Recognition:** Modify the leaderboard algorithm to ignore or cap daily weight loss entries exceeding 1-2% of body weight. Rapid fluctuations are often water weight, not fat loss, and rewarding them encourages dehydration.
*   **Decouple Water Intake from "Winning":** Remove water intake logs from the primary competitive score. Instead, treat hydration as a binary "eligibility" gate (e.g., users must log minimum safe hydration to appear on the board) rather than a metric where "more is better." Excessive water intake can lead to hyponatremia.
*   **Introduce a "Consistency" Multiplier:** Shift the reward logic from "biggest single-day drop" to "consistent weekly trends." This reduces the incentive for dangerous crash behaviors right before a leaderboard reset.

**2. User Interface & Notification Copy (High Priority)**
*   **Remove Urgency Language:** Replace copy such as "Drop pounds fast," "Crush your goals today," or "Rapid results" with "Sustainable progress," "Consistent habits," and "Long-term health."
*   **Add Explicit Health Disclaimers:** Place a visible disclaimer on the logging screen: "Rapid weight loss can be dangerous. This app rewards consistent habits, not extreme measures. Consult a doctor before starting any weight loss program."
*   **Reframe Notifications:** Change push notifications from "You're falling behind! Log more water now!" to "Stay consistent! Remember to hydrate safely throughout the day." Avoid manufacturing urgency that pressures users to log unsafe amounts instantly.
*   **Hide Extreme Outliers:** Do not highlight users with statistically anomalous daily results (e.g., losing 5lbs in 24 hours) as "Top Movers," as this signals to others that such behavior is the path to victory.

**3. Governance & Human Gate (Mandatory Floor)**
*   **Hard Stop on Automated Coaching:** Ensure the app never automatically generates specific medical advice (e.g., "Drink 4 liters now" or "Fast for 24 hours"). Any algorithmic suggestion must be generic and pre-approved.
*   **Owner Sign-off:** As a six-person team, designate one founder as the explicit owner of these safety constraints. Document that the "rapid result" mechanic was intentionally dampened to prevent harm.

**Notes**
*   **Legal Routing:** Whether these changes satisfy specific consumer protection laws or health regulations in your target regions is a legal determination; route this to counsel immediately, even post-beta.
*   **Measurement:** You cannot certify the app is "safe" without testing; plan to monitor user logs post-launch for spikes in extreme entries and be prepared to rollback features if dangerous patterns emerge.
