**Immediate Action Plan: HomeChef Cleaning Hack Safety Audit**

**1. Critical "Do Not Mix" Verification Checklist**
Manually scan your entire content database for any suggestion involving the following combinations. If found, **delete the suggestion immediately**; do not attempt to warn around it.
*   **Bleach (Sodium Hypochlorite) + Ammonia** (found in glass cleaners, urine, some fertilizers) → Creates Chloramine gas (lung damage).
*   **Bleach + Acids** (Vinegar, Lemon Juice, Toilet Bowl Cleaners, Rust Removers) → Creates Chlorine gas (severe respiratory injury).
*   **Bleach + Alcohol** (Rubbing Alcohol, Hand Sanitizer) → Creates Chloroform and other hazardous compounds.
*   **Hydrogen Peroxide + Vinegar** → Creates Peracetic Acid (corrosive to skin, eyes, and lungs).
*   **Different Drain Cleaners** (e.g., Acid-based + Alkali-based) → Can cause violent exothermic reactions or toxic gas.
*   **Any "DIY Bleach" recipe** involving mixing ingredients to create bleach-like substances.

**2. Mandatory Safety Warning Implementation**
Implement these warnings before launch. Given the one-week timeline, use hard-coded text overlays rather than dynamic AI generation for these specific alerts.
*   **Global Header:** Place a permanent, high-contrast banner on the "Cleaning Hacks" section: *"Warning: Never mix household cleaners. Combinations can create deadly gases. Follow recipes exactly and never combine products not explicitly listed together in a single step."*
*   **Ingredient-Level Alert:** If a recipe contains Bleach, Ammonia, Acid (Vinegar/Lemon), or Alcohol, insert a bold, colored box immediately preceding the instructions: *"DANGER: This recipe contains [Chemical]. Do not use this product within 24 hours of using [Counter-Chemical]. Ensure the area is well-ventilated."*
*   **User Confirmation Gate:** Require a one-time checkbox click before viewing any cleaning hack: *"I understand I must never mix cleaning products unless explicitly instructed in this specific recipe."*

**3. Process Control & Human Gate**
*   **Freeze Automated Generation:** Disable any AI feature that dynamically generates *new* cleaning combinations post-launch until a chemical safety expert reviews the logic. Ship only the pre-vetted, manually audited list.
*   **The "One-Week" Constraint:** With no budget for an expert, your only safe path is **reduction, not verification**. If a recipe's safety cannot be 100% confirmed via the checklist above, **remove it**. It is safer to launch with 5 verified hacks than 50 unverified ones.
*   **Owner Assignment:** Assign one of the three founders as the sole "Safety Gatekeeper." No cleaning hack goes live without their signature on the checklist.

**Note:** This audit relies on general chemical safety knowledge; it does not replace a formal review by a certified industrial hygienist or toxicologist.
**Note:** If your app allows users to input their own cleaner combinations, this feature must be disabled or strictly keyword-blocked for the terms listed above before launch.
