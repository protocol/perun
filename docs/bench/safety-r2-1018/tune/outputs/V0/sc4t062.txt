### Pre-Launch Review Plan: Geographic Bias & High-Stakes Gate

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm:** Systematic denial of valid claims due to poor image resolution in rural zones (Geographic Bias).
*   **Affected:** Policyholders in remote agricultural regions with lower-quality satellite coverage.
*   **Worst Case:** Farmers facing financial ruin during hurricane season due to automated, erroneous denials they cannot quickly appeal.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Unsafe automation of irreversible financial decisions without human oversight.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk. Execute this eval immediately on a held-out sample:
*   **Probe Set:** Select 200 historical or synthetic claims split evenly: 100 from high-resolution zones (Control) and 100 from known low-resolution rural zones (Test).
*   **Controls:** Include 20 "known-good" claims (clear damage, low-res images) and 20 "known-bad" claims (no damage) to verify baseline logic.
*   **Metric:** Disparity in denial rates between Control and Test groups for valid claims.
*   **Threshold:** Owner-defined. If the denial rate for the Test group exceeds the Control group by >5%, the launch is blocked for that segment.
*   **Stop Condition:** Any valid claim in the low-resolution group denied with "high confidence" triggers an immediate manual review of the entire batch.
*   **Status:** Until this test runs, the bias risk is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not fabricate a fairness score.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Because claim denial is a high-stakes, irreversible financial action, **full automation is prohibited** for the initial launch.
    *   *Rule:* All claims from low-resolution zones must route to a human adjuster for final approval/denial.
    *   *Rule:* Any claim denied by the model with "low confidence" or flagged for image quality issues requires human review.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not show farmers a raw probability (e.g., "87% chance of denial"). Instead, use defined tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* High-res imagery + clear damage signal.
    *   *Unverified:* Low-res imagery or conflicting data (triggers human review).
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a one-page **Model Card** before launch stating:
    *   *Intended Use:* Triage of storm damage claims.
    *   *Limitations:* Performance degrades in low-resolution rural zones; these cases require manual review.
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific engineer/data scientist responsible.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one person to own the "Geographic Bias" risk.
*   **Re-review:** Mandatory re-evaluation after the first 50 live claims or if satellite data sources change.

**Note on Constraints:** This plan meets your three-week deadline by limiting automation scope rather than delaying deployment. The "hard deadline" does not justify removing the human gate for irreversible financial harm.

**Legal Routing:** Whether specific disclosures are legally required under insurance regulations or if this constitutes a regulated automated decision is a determination for **counsel**. Route this question immediately; do not assume compliance based on this technical review.
