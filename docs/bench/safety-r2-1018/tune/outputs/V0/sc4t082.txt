**Pre-Launch Safety Workflow for Northwind Education**

Given your three-week deadline and limited staff, you cannot manually verify every page. Instead, implement a **risk-based gating system** that combines automated filtering with targeted human review. This approach prioritizes safety without halting your launch.

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory (Days 1–2)**
Define the specific failures you must catch before any testing begins. For your history generator, these are:
*   **Historical Hallucination:** Fabricated events or dates presented as fact.
*   **Cultural Bias/Offense:** Stereotypes, derogatory language, or exclusion of specific groups.
*   **Curriculum Misalignment:** Content that contradicts the specific district standards you promised to meet.
*   **Impact:** Identify that the affected users are middle school students; the worst case is the dissemination of harmful misinformation or offensive material in a classroom setting.

**2. Automated Triage & "Confidence Tiering" (Days 3–10)**
Do not rely on the model's raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% accurate"), as these are often uncalibrated and misleading. Instead, configure your pipeline to tag every generated section with a **defined coarse tier** based on source verification:
*   **Confirmed:** Matches multiple approved curriculum sources.
*   **Single-Source:** Matches only one reference; requires spot checks.
*   **Unverified/Novel:** No direct match in approved texts; **automatic hold**.

Run an adversarial evaluation suite immediately. Feed the generator known "trap" prompts regarding sensitive cultural topics and historical controversies. Measure the rate at which it produces offensive language or fabrications. If the failure rate on these specific probes exceeds zero, the system is not ready for autonomous generation.

**3. The Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Days 11–20)**
You lack the staff to read everything, so you must only read what matters. Implement a strict rule: **Any content tagged "Unverified" or containing flagged keywords regarding culture/race must trigger a human review before inclusion in the final PDF.**
*   **Workflow:** The system generates the draft → Auto-filter flags high-risk sections → Human reviewer approves/rejects *only* the flagged sections → Final assembly.
*   **Fallback:** If the system cannot generate a safe version of a specific topic after two attempts, insert a placeholder note: "Content pending review," rather than risking hallucinated or biased text. This ensures the book ships on time without compromising safety.

**4. Transparency & Disclosure (Days 21–23)**
Include a clear notice in the teacher's guide and the student intro stating that materials are AI-assisted and have undergone automated safety filtering, but that teachers should report any inaccuracies. Create a simple "Model Card" document for your district partners listing:
*   **Intended Use:** Middle school history supplementation.
*   **Known Limitations:** Potential for cultural nuance errors; reliance on teacher oversight for complex social topics.
*   **Data Sources:** List the specific curriculum standards and textbooks used for training/retrieval.

**5. Launch & Monitor (Day 24+)**
Ship with a "Kill Switch" mechanism. If a district reports a severe bias incident, you must be able to remotely disable or patch the specific generated module immediately. Assign one team member as the owner of "Output Safety" to review incident reports weekly.

**Note:** Do not claim the system is "unbiased" or "safe" to your partners; state that risks are managed via the above gates. Legal requirements for AI disclosure in education vary by region; consult counsel to confirm if your notice meets statutory obligations.
