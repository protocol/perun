**Protocol: Human-Gated Funeral Music Recommendation Review**

**1. Harm Inventory (Map)**
Before launch, explicitly record these specific risks for this feature:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Systematic disadvantage or offense to specific cultural, religious, or demographic groups due to limited training data.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Misinterpreting sarcasm, irony, or context in social media posts as literal music preferences.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Suggesting inappropriate (e.g., upbeat/comedic) tracks for solemn occasions, causing emotional distress to grieving families.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Users unaware that suggestions are machine-generated interpretations of public data, not verified wishes.

**2. Measurement Plan (Measure)**
Do not assert safety. Run these specific evaluations before go-live:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Test the model with known edge cases: ironic posts ("I'd want 'Happy Birthday' played at my funeral"), culturally specific mourning traditions, and ambiguous sentiment.
*   **Controls:** Include "known-bad" inputs (posts clearly indicating opposite preferences) and "known-good" inputs (explicit solemn requests).
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Define a "Solemnity Violation Rate." Given the high emotional stakes, propose **zero tolerance** for inappropriate suggestions in the final gate. Any failure triggers an immediate rollback.
*   **Data Gap Acknowledgement:** Explicitly mark cultural sensitivity metrics as **UNVERIFIED** where diverse datasets were unavailable. Do not invent a "99% culturally appropriate" figure.

**3. Risk Management Controls (Manage)**
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** This is the non-negotiable floor. **No music suggestion reaches a customer without explicit review and approval by a funeral director.** Fully automated delivery of music recommendations is prohibited due to the irreversible emotional harm of errors.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If the model's confidence in a suggestion is low or the source data is ambiguous, the system must flag the output as "Unverified/Needs Review" rather than presenting it as a definitive recommendation. Do not show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% match") to users; use defined tiers like "Corroborated" or "Single-Source Estimate."
*   **AI Disclosure:** Clearly label all suggestions as "AI-generated based on public profiles; please verify with family." Route the specific legal requirement for this disclosure to counsel immediately.
*   **Fallback Mechanism:** If the system cannot generate a confident, culturally neutral suggestion, it must default to a standard, safe list of traditional solemn tracks or prompt the human operator to select manually.

**4. Governance & Artifacts**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific individual responsible for reviewing failure logs and re-evaluating the model if social media parsing logic changes.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a **Model Card** for this feature documenting: intended use (funeral planning), known limitations (limited cultural data, risk of misinterpreting irony), and the UNVERIFIED status of cross-cultural performance. This must be available to your funeral director users.

**5. Immediate Next Steps for Q4 Launch**
*   Implement the HITL gate in the workflow immediately; do not wait for perfect model accuracy.
*   Draft the Model Card with current known limitations.
*   Consult legal counsel regarding disclosure obligations for automated decisions involving bereaved individuals.

**Note:** This protocol prioritizes human oversight over automation speed to prevent emotional harm.
**Note:** Revenue targets cannot justify bypassing the human gate for high-stakes emotional interactions.
