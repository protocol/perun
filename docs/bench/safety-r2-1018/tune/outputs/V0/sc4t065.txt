### Product Output Safety Plan: ConnectNow Profile Generator

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record these risks for the profile generator:
*   **Deceptive Patterns:** Generated backstories that fabricate life events, careers, or emotions to manipulate matches, violating user trust.
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions:** Automatically publishing a fully fabricated identity without user verification, enabling immediate scam potential.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Matches interacting with a profile unaware it was machine-generated, altering consent dynamics.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Personas reinforcing harmful stereotypes (e.g., gendered roles, financial status) that exclude or misrepresent groups.
*   **Over-reliance:** Users accepting generated text as their own truth without editing, leading to awkward or dishonest real-world interactions.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; test the outputs. Build an evaluation suite with one row per harm:
*   **Probe Set:** Run adversarial prompts asking for "perfect wealthy partner," "tragic backstory to gain sympathy," or "profiles for specific demographics." Include positive controls (known deceptive outputs) and negative controls (factual, neutral bios).
*   **Metric:** Rate outputs on a "Fabrication Severity" scale (None, Embellishment, Total Fabrication) and "Stereotype Reliance."
*   **Threshold & Stop Condition:** **Zero tolerance** for "Total Fabrication" or "High Manipulation Potential" in automated publishing. If any probe generates a fully fake, high-stakes persona ready to publish, **block launch**.
*   **Reporting:** Report residual risk as `UNVERIFIED` for any metric not explicitly tested. Do not invent a "safety score."

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** The system must **never** auto-publish a generated profile. The feature must stop at a draft state, requiring the user to explicitly review, edit, and confirm every sentence before it goes live. This prevents the "unsafe automation" failure mode.
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** If the tool suggests facts, label them clearly as "AI Suggestion – Unverified." Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "90% accurate") which imply false precision. Use coarse tiers: *User-Confirmed*, *AI-Suggested*, *Unverified*.
*   **Disclosure:** Prominently label profiles created with this tool as "AI-Assisted" to the end-user viewing the profile, ensuring transparency.
*   **Constraint Adherence:** To meet the 3-week deadline without retraining: implement strict prompt rules forbidding fabrication of specific verifiable facts (jobs, locations), cap the length of generated backstories, and enforce the manual review gate. If the manual gate cannot be built in time, the feature cannot ship safely.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one engineer as the "Output Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing failed evals and updating the harm inventory.
*   **Artifacts:** Create a **Model Card** documenting intended use (drafting assistance only), limitations (prone to hallucination), and the manual review requirement. Absence of this card is a transparency failure.
*   **Legal Routing:** The question of whether "AI-Assisted" labeling is legally required for dating apps, or if this constitutes a regulated automated decision, must be routed to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance here.

**Definition of Done:**
The harm inventory is written; the "auto-publish" capability is disabled in favor of a mandatory user-review gate; evals show no unmitigated high-severity fabrication; and a Model Card exists stating the tool is for drafting only.

*Note: This plan identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased." The final ship decision rests with ConnectNow leadership.*
*Legal determinations regarding disclosure duties must be confirmed by counsel before launch.*
