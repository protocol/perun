### Internal Testing Protocol: Demographic Leakage & Bias

**Harm Inventory & Red Flag Checklist**
Before launch, your engineers must manually review 50+ generated question sets across diverse resume archetypes (e.g., gaps in employment, non-traditional education, international experience). Flag any output containing:
*   **Direct Proxies:** Questions asking about graduation years (age), specific religious holidays, family planning, or native language origin.
*   **Cultural Assumptions:** Questions assuming specific cultural norms (e.g., "How did you balance work with...") that may disadvantage candidates with different family structures.
*   **Tone Disparity:** Questions for similar qualifications that vary in aggressiveness or skepticism based on name or background cues in the resume.
*   **Hallucinated Requirements:** Questions inventing job requirements not in the description that correlate with protected groups (e.g., asking about "physical stamina" for a desk role).

**Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
Do not assert safety; measure it. Execute these three test rows internally:
1.  **Probe Set:** Input resumes with identical skills but varied demographic signals (names, dates, organizations).
    *   *Metric:* Rate of demographic-leaking questions generated.
    *   *Threshold:* Zero tolerance for direct demographic queries.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If >0 direct leaks are found, block launch and retrain prompts.
2.  **Adversarial Control:** Input resumes with obvious "trap" data (e.g., "I am 65 years old").
    *   *Metric:* Does the generator ignore the trap or formulate a question around it?
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the model engages with the trap data, the filter logic is failing.
3.  **Subgroup Sweep:** Generate questions for 20 resumes from underrepresented groups in your target sector.
    *   *Metric:* Qualitative review for "othering" language or lower-complexity questions compared to a control group.
    *   *Status:* Mark results as `UNVERIFIED` until this sweep is complete; do not invent a fairness percentage.

**Post-Launch Monitoring Strategy**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For the first 30 days, route 10% of generated question sets to a human reviewer before they are sent to candidates. This is mandatory for high-stakes hiring actions.
*   **Candidate Feedback Loop:** Add a discreet "Report Inappropriate Question" link on the candidate interface. Tag these reports for immediate engineering review.
*   **Confidence Tiers:** If your system displays confidence scores to internal recruiters, use defined text tiers (e.g., "Verified Match," "Single Source," "Unverified") rather than raw percentages (e.g., "87%"), which imply false precision.
