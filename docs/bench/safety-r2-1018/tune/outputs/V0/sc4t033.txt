### Immediate Risk Assessment & Mitigation Plan

**Critical Failure Modes Identified**
Based on your description, the highest risks are not in date arithmetic but in **semantic interpretation** and **jurisdictional scope**:
1.  **Hallucination as Fact:** The bot may confidently extract an incorrect date from a poorly scanned document or complex clause, presenting it as a verified court deadline.
2.  **Scope Creep:** Users may upload documents outside the bot's trained jurisdiction (e.g., federal vs. state, different counties), leading to silent failures where no deadline is extracted or a wrong rule is applied.
3.  **Automation Bias:** Users may trust the SMS reminder as absolute truth, ignoring contradictory physical mail or failing to verify ambiguous outputs.

**Required Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Safeguards**
Given the severe consequence of missed appearances (loss of housing, custody, or liberty), **fully automated extraction and notification is unsafe for launch.** Implement these gates immediately:

*   **Confidence Tiering with Defined Thresholds:** Do not show raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure"). Instead, classify outputs into three defined tiers:
    *   *Verified:* High-confidence match on standard forms within known jurisdictions. (Auto-send SMS).
    *   *Unverified:* Low-confidence scan, non-standard document, or out-of-jurisdiction file. (Hold SMS; trigger human review).
    *   *Unknown:* File type not supported. (Return immediate error: "We cannot read this file type; please contact staff.")
*   **Mandatory Human Review for "Unverified" Tier:** Any document falling into the *Unverified* tier must be routed to a staff member for manual date extraction before any user notification is sent. Given your team of five, cap the pilot intake volume to match your manual review capacity rather than risking automated errors.
*   **Explicit Disclosure & Disclaimer:** Every SMS must state: "This date was extracted by an AI assistant from your uploaded document. It is not legal advice. Please verify this date against your official court notice."
*   **Reversibility:** Provide a clear, one-step mechanism for users to report a wrong date via SMS reply, which immediately flags the case for human intervention.

**Measurement Plan (Pre-Launch)**
Do not claim the system is "accurate" without evidence. Before the 30-day deadline:
1.  **Adversarial Testing:** Run a "red team" exercise using 50 known-bad inputs (blurry scans, conflicting dates, wrong jurisdictions) and 50 known-good inputs.
2.  **Stop Condition:** If the system misclassifies *any* high-stakes deadline in the test set as "Verified" when it is wrong, you must lower the automation threshold or expand the human review queue.
3.  **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a one-page "System Card" for internal use listing: intended jurisdictions, known failure modes (e.g., handwritten notes), and the specific human review workflow.

**Constraint Note:** You cannot certify this system as "safe" or "unbiased" in 30 days; you can only demonstrate that high-risk cases are caught by humans. If your funding cliff requires a claim of full automation safety, you must route that legal liability question to counsel immediately, as this assessment identifies residual risk that requires human management.
