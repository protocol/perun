**Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Bias Mitigation for Northwind Education Beta**

**1. Map the Harm Inventory (Immediate)**
Before testing, explicitly document the specific risk: **Bias/Unfair Outcomes**.
*   **Affected Group:** Users from or learning about specific regions currently triggering stereotypical corrections.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** The app reinforces harmful cultural stereotypes to 5,000 students, causing reputational damage and educational harm.
*   **Action:** Create a one-page document listing these specific regions and the types of stereotypes observed (e.g., gender roles, economic status). Do not proceed without this written inventory.

**2. Measure with Internal Red-Teaming (No External Audit Needed)**
Since external auditing is unavailable, your two engineers must execute a targeted "adversarial" evaluation using internal resources.
*   **Construct Probe Sets:** Create a list of 50–100 specific input sentences designed to trigger the known stereotypes. Include:
    *   *Positive Controls:* Inputs known to produce biased outputs (based on your observations).
    *   *Negative Controls:* Neutral inputs that should not trigger regional stereotyping.
    *   *Segmented Inputs:* Variations targeting the specific regions identified in step 1.
*   **Define the Metric & Threshold:** The metric is the **count of stereotypical outputs**. Given the high stakes of educational bias, propose a **zero-tolerance threshold** for confirmed stereotypical outputs in the probe set. Any failure here is a stop condition.
*   **Execution:** Run these probes manually or via simple scripts. Record every failure. Do not invent a "bias rate" (e.g., "95% accurate"); if a specific harm was not tested, mark it **UNVERIFIED**.

**3. Manage Residual Risk (Human-in-the-Loop & Transparency)**
You cannot guarantee the elimination of all bias before launch, so you must manage the exposure.
*   **Human Gate for High-Stakes Contexts:** If the app provides cultural context (not just grammar), consider disabling fully automated cultural explanations for the identified high-risk regions until fixes are verified. Replace them with a disclaimer: *"Cultural context is AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies."*
*   **Show Uncertainty:** Do not present corrections as absolute facts. Use phrasing like *"Suggested correction"* rather than *"Corrected."*
*   **Feedback Loop:** Implement a prominent "Report Bias" button in the beta app. This turns your 5,000 users into a distributed monitoring team, allowing you to catch issues post-launch that internal testing missed.

**4. Governance & Transparency Artifact**
*   **Owner:** Assign one engineer as the explicit owner of this bias risk.
*   **Model Card (Lite):** Draft a simple internal/external note stating:
    *   *Intended Use:* Grammar correction and cultural context for language learners.
    *   *Limitations:* Known potential for regional stereotyping; currently under active internal review.
    *   *Measured Performance:* Results from your internal probe set (e.g., "0/50 stereotypical outputs in tested scenarios" or "X failures identified and patched").
    *   *Unverified Areas:* Explicitly state which regions or topics were not fully tested due to resource constraints.

**Critical Constraints:**
*   **Do Not Certify:** Do not claim the system is "unbiased" or "safe." State clearly that residual risk remains and is being monitored.
*   **Legal Routing:** If questions arise regarding legal obligations for AI disclosure or regulated decisions, route these immediately to legal counsel; do not assert legal duties internally.
*   **Deadline Adherence:** This plan uses existing engineering capacity for targeted testing rather than broad auditing, fitting your timeline. If the zero-tolerance threshold is breached on critical stereotypes, the only safe path is to disable the specific cultural context feature for those regions, not to ship the bias.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes targeted internal verification over comprehensive auditing to meet your deadline.
**Note:** Never fabricate safety metrics; report only what was actually tested.
