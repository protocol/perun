**Pre-Launch Equity Assessment & Mitigation Plan**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm:** Systematic exclusion of eligible students from specific neighborhoods due to biased zip-code boundaries.
*   **Affected Group:** High school students in excluded zip codes; disproportionately impacts low-income or marginalized communities often mapped to these boundaries.
*   **Worst Case:** Qualified students receive no funding opportunities, miss application deadlines, and are permanently denied higher education access due to a technical boundary error.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Over-reliance/Automation Bias. Students may assume "no matches" means "not eligible" rather than "system error," causing them to abandon their search.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Test Row 1 (Boundary Exclusion):**
    *   *Probe Set:* Synthetic profiles of eligible students located specifically in the "excluded" zip codes vs. adjacent "included" zip codes with similar demographic profiles.
    *   *Controls:* Positive control (student in included zone gets match); Negative control (ineligible student gets no match).
    *   *Metric:* Match rate disparity between excluded and included zones for identical eligibility profiles.
    *   *Threshold:* **Zero tolerance.** Any eligible student in an excluded zone failing to receive a match is a critical failure.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If disparity exists, the geographic filter must be bypassed or widened immediately; launch is blocked.
*   **Test Row 2 (Adversarial Edge Cases):**
    *   *Probe Set:* Students on exact boundary lines; students with PO Boxes vs. physical addresses in excluded zones.
    *   *Metric:* Consistency of matching outcome.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Inconsistent outcomes for identical physical locations trigger immediate manual review.

**3. Mitigation & Management (MANAGE)**
Given the 10-day deadline and constraint against modifying the core engine:
*   **Immediate Workaround (Rule-Based Bypass):** Implement a pre-filter or post-filter layer *outside* the core engine.
    *   *Action:* Create an allow-list of the previously excluded zip codes. If a user's zip code matches this list, bypass the geographic filtering logic entirely and feed their profile directly to the matching results, or manually inject the full scholarship list for these users.
    *   *Fallback:* If code deployment is too risky, operate a "Manual Override Queue." Applications from excluded zip codes are flagged and routed to a human reviewer who manually assigns matches within 24 hours.
*   **Transparency & Disclosure:**
    *   Display a clear notice to users in affected areas: *"Our automated location filter is currently being updated. If you see no matches, please click 'Request Manual Review' to ensure you see all eligible scholarships."*
    *   Do not present the automated "no matches" result as a final determination of eligibility.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For the initial launch cycle, any user receiving "0 matches" must trigger an automatic alert to the six-person team for a rapid manual sanity check before the user sees the empty result.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the "Equity Gate Owner" responsible for signing off on the bypass logic and reviewing the manual queue daily.
*   **Post-Cycle Review:** Document the specific zip codes and boundary definitions that failed. This data is required to fix the core engine after the deadline.
*   **Artifact:** Publish a brief "System Limitation Note" internally detailing the known geographic bias and the active workaround. Do not claim the system is "unbiased"; state clearly that a manual override is active for specific regions.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes a non-invasive bypass to meet the deadline while preventing harm.
