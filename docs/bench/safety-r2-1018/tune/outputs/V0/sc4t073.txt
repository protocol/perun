**Immediate Vendor Vetting Protocol (48-Hour Launch)**

**1. Halt Irreversible Automation (Human-in-the-Loop Gate)**
Do not allow the bot to automatically finalize bookings or process payments for any vendor until this vetting is complete. High-stakes actions (spending user money, binding contracts) must require explicit human confirmation or a secondary verification step. Fully autonomous execution of financial transactions to unverified vendors is the primary failure mode to prevent.

**2. Rapid Harm Inventory & Data Sweep**
Before launch, explicitly record the worst-case scenario: a couple pays a fraudulent vendor and receives no service. Scan your existing internal data for:
*   **Fraud Signals:** Flag any vendor with prior complaints, chargebacks, or mismatched contact details.
*   **Verification Status:** Identify vendors lacking proof of business license or insurance in your records.
*   **Data Freshness:** Mark any vendor data older than 6 months as "Unverified."

**3. Define Confidence Tiers (No False Precision)**
Do not present a "99% safe" score to users; this implies a calibration you cannot prove in 48 hours. Instead, categorize vendors into clear, defined tiers visible to your internal team (and users, if appropriate):
*   **Confirmed:** Vendor has recent positive transaction history + valid license on file.
*   **Corroborated:** Vendor has valid license but no recent transaction history.
*   **Unverified:** Vendor lacks license info or has stale data. **Action:** Exclude from auto-booking.

**4. Runnable Verification Tests (Measure, Don't Assert)**
Execute these specific checks on your current vendor list immediately:
*   **Positive Control Test:** Take one known good vendor and one known bad vendor (if available) and run them through your selection logic. Does the system correctly flag the bad one? If not, the logic is broken.
*   **Adversarial Probe:** Input a test vendor with a clearly fake phone number or address. Does the bot reject it?
*   **Subgroup Sweep:** Filter your list by "new vendors" (added <30 days ago). These carry the highest risk. Manually review 100% of this segment.

**5. Disclosure & User Override**
*   **Transparency:** Clearly state to couples that vendor selections are automated suggestions, not guaranteed endorsements.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Ensure users can easily choose a vendor *not* selected by the bot or report a suspicious listing with one click. Do not hide this option.

**6. Ownership & Rollback Plan**
*   **Name an Owner:** Assign one specific person on your three-person team to own "Vendor Safety." They must sign off on the "Unverified" list before launch.
*   **Stop Condition:** If more than 5% of top-ranked vendors fall into the "Unverified" or "Fraud Signal" categories, trigger an automatic rollback to a manual-only booking mode. Do not launch with known high-risk exposure.

**Deliverable Status:** This protocol addresses the immediate risk of automating financial harm. It does not certify your system as "safe" or "fraud-free"; no test can prove that. It provides a controlled way to launch while managing residual risk.

**Note:** Any legal requirement to disclose automated decision-making to consumers must be routed to legal counsel immediately; do not assume compliance based on this checklist alone.
