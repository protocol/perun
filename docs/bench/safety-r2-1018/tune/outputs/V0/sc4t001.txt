**Internal Safety Checklist: Insulin Dosage Suggestion Feature**

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Mapping**
*   **Misclassification Risk:** Document specific failure modes for lighting (low light, glare), food types (mixed meals, liquids vs. solids), and portion estimation errors.
*   **Affected Users:** All beta users, with heightened risk for those with irregular eating patterns or rare food combinations.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Severe hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia resulting from an incorrect dosage recommendation, leading to emergency medical intervention.
*   **Status:** No metric is "verified" until tested. Any untested scenario is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

**2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
*   **Probe Set:** Create a test suite containing:
    *   *Positive Controls:* Images known to cause misclassification (e.g., dark photos, mixed casseroles, foods with similar colors).
    *   *Negative Controls:* Clear images of standard meals with known correct dosages.
    *   *Subgroups:* Test across different skin tones (affecting contrast), plate colors, and common dietary variations.
*   **Metric:** Rate of dosage deviation >10% from clinical standard.
*   **Threshold:** **Zero tolerance** for deviations causing potential harm in high-risk scenarios.
*   **Stop Condition:** If any high-risk probe fails, the feature launch is blocked until the specific failure mode is mitigated.
*   **Owner:** Engineering Lead must review all failures before any code promotion.

**3. Management Controls (Human-in-the-Loop)**
*   **High-Stakes Gate:** **No fully automated dosage execution.** Every suggestion generated by the system must be presented as a "Draft Recommendation" requiring explicit user confirmation.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% confident"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* High clarity, standard food, strong corroboration.
    *   *Corroborated:* Moderate clarity, requires user verification of portion size.
    *   *Unverified:* Low lighting, mixed meal, or novel food. **Action:** System refuses to suggest a dose and prompts manual entry or photo retake.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Users must be able to easily edit the suggested carbohydrate count and insulin ratio before confirming.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly state: "This dosage is an AI-generated estimate based on your photo. Always verify with your glucose monitor and clinical judgment."

**4. Governance & Artifacts**
*   **Model/Data Card:** Draft a versioned document detailing:
    *   *Intended Use:* Estimating carbohydrate content for insulin dosing assistance only.
    *   *Limitations:* Known failures in low light, mixed meals, and non-standard cuisines.
    *   *Data Provenance:* Source of training images and known gaps.
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific team member responsible for ongoing monitoring.
*   **Review Cadence:** Re-evaluate the harm inventory and test results weekly or after any model/data update.

**5. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
*   **Constraint Acknowledgement:** While external audit budget is exhausted, the determination of whether this feature constitutes a regulated medical device or requires specific legal disclosures under regimes like the EU AI Act is a legal duty.
*   **Action:** Route the question of "regulated decision status" and "mandatory disclosure duties" to internal counsel immediately. Do not assert compliance without this review.

**Note:** Absolute certainty is unattainable; this checklist manages residual risk. If the "Zero tolerance" threshold cannot be met for mixed meals, the feature must not launch for that specific use case.
