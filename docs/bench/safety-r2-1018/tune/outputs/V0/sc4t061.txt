**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Facial recognition historically exhibits higher false-positive rates for specific demographics. If the system misidentifies a resident or guest as an intruder due to bias, it could trigger a traumatic police response against innocent people.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Automatically contacting law enforcement is an irreversible, high-stakes action. A false positive wastes critical police resources and creates immediate physical danger for your users (e.g., armed police response to a family member).
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system may present a low-confidence match as a definitive "Intruder Detected" fact, leading to over-reliance by users or authorities.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
You must run output-harm evaluations immediately; do not rely on aggregate accuracy claims.
*   **Probe Set:** Test against edge cases (poor lighting at night, angles, residents with changed appearances) and sliced subgroups (age, skin tone, gender).
*   **Controls:** Include known bad positives (residents in unusual clothes) and known good negatives (delivery drivers, neighbors).
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure the False Positive Rate (FPR) specifically for the "Call Police" trigger.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If FPR > 0% for the police-trigger action in testing, the current autonomous configuration must not ship.
*   **Reporting:** Any unmeasured subgroup performance must be labeled **UNVERIFIED** in your documentation. Do not invent a "negligible" number for marketing.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
Given the two-week deadline and the high stakes:
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** You cannot ship fully autonomous police dispatch. The system must detect an unknown face, alert the *user* via app/notification, and require explicit user confirmation before contacting law enforcement. Full automation of this action is the failure mode this review prevents.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If the system confidence is low, it must display a defined tier (e.g., "Unverified Motion") rather than a precise percentage or a definitive "Intruder" label.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly disclose to users that this is an automated assistance tool, not a guaranteed security guarantee.

*   **Legal Routing:** Whether automatic police dispatch triggers specific legal liabilities or regulatory requirements (e.g., EU AI Act, local false alarm ordinances) is a legal determination. Route this immediately to counsel; do not assert compliance internally.
