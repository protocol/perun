### Immediate Harm Mitigation Plan: Dietary Supplement Recommender

**1. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Stop Condition)**
Do not allow the AI to output direct supplement recommendations to end-users without human verification. Given the high-stakes risk of drug interactions and the lack of a verified interaction database, **fully automated execution of this feature is the failure mode**.
*   **Action:** Route every generated recommendation to a qualified health professional (pharmacist or clinician) for review before it reaches the user.
*   **Constraint:** If human review capacity cannot match user volume, cap public access to a waitlist or beta group small enough for your team to manually verify every single output. Do not scale automation until the interaction database is licensed or integrated.

**2. Defined Confidence Tiers & Uncertainty Disclosure**
Do not display raw model confidence scores (e.g., "85% safe") as these imply a precision your current data cannot support. Instead, implement a coarse, defined uncertainty tier visible to the reviewer and the user.
*   **Tier Label:** "Unverified Interaction Status."
*   **Definition:** "This recommendation has not been cross-referenced against a comprehensive drug-interaction database due to current licensing constraints. Potential risks with prescription medications cannot be ruled out."
*   **Visual Cue:** Use a distinct, colorblind-safe warning icon (e.g., amber triangle) next to this label.

**3. Required User Verification Steps (Pre-Input)**
Before a user can submit their profile, enforce the following friction points to mitigate over-reliance and ensure informed consent:
*   **Explicit Acknowledgement:** Require an active checkbox (not pre-checked) stating: *"I understand this tool is an experimental pilot. It does not currently check for dangerous interactions with prescription medications. I will consult my doctor before taking any suggested supplement."*
*   **Medication Warning Prompt:** If a user lists *any* prescription medication in their profile, trigger a hard stop modal: *"You have listed prescription medications. Our system cannot currently verify safety interactions. You must consult a healthcare provider before acting on any recommendation generated here."*

**4. Prominent Disclaimer Artifact (Model Card Lite)**
Publish a visible "System Limitations" notice on the interface and in the terms of use. This serves as your transparency artifact.
*   **Intended Use:** "Educational exploration of dietary supplements only."
*   **Out-of-Scope/High-Risk Use:** "Determining safety for users taking prescription medications or managing chronic health conditions."
*   **Known Limitation:** "No licensed drug-interaction database is integrated. Hallucinated or missed interactions are possible."
*   **Owner/Contact:** List the specific team lead responsible for safety reviews.

**5. Data Source Inventory & Residual Risk**
*   **Current State:** Your inventory lists "unlicensed/unverified" for interaction data.
*   **Residual Risk:** `UNVERIFIED`. There is a non-zero probability of suggesting a harmful combination.
*   **Measurement Plan:** You must run adversarial probes using known bad drug-supplement pairs (positive controls) to confirm your human gate catches them. If the AI suggests a dangerous combo and the human reviewer misses it, the launch must stop immediately.

**Summary of Mandatory Controls:**
1.  **No autonomous shipping** of recommendations involving users with listed medications.
2.  **Explicit "Unverified" labeling** on all outputs.
3.  **Hard friction** (active consent) before profile submission.
4.  **Legal Routing:** Consult counsel immediately to determine if this pilot triggers specific regulatory duties under Australian health laws or the EU AI Act (if accessible globally) given the high-stakes nature of health advice.

*Note: This plan treats the lack of a database as a critical blocker for automation, not a minor gap. The human gate is the only acceptable control until data is verified.*
*Do not fabricate a safety metric; report the risk as UNVERIFIED until the database is licensed.*
