**Risk Assessment: Echo Chamber Formation in BosphorusNews Aggregator**

**Harm Inventory & Mechanism**
The current feedback loop creates a high risk of **bias/unfair outcomes** and **over-reliance**. By optimizing strictly for "reading habits" without diversity injectors, the engine will likely amplify existing user preferences, systematically excluding opposing political viewpoints. For Ankara citizens seeking political analysis, this creates an **echo chamber** where users are only exposed to content reinforcing their prior beliefs. The worst realistic case is **user radicalization**, where individuals are pushed toward extreme narratives due to a lack of counter-balancing information, damaging the public discourse and user trust.

**Measurement Plan (Runnable)**
To validate this risk before launch, execute the following evaluation:
*   **Probe Set:** Simulate 1,000 user profiles with distinct initial political leanings (left, center, right) using historical Ankara news consumption data.
*   **Positive Control:** Force-feed a balanced diet of articles to a control group; measure the divergence of the test group's feed from this baseline over 30 simulated days.
*   **Metric:** "Viewpoint Diversity Score" (ratio of opposing vs. reinforcing sources shown).
*   **Threshold:** Owner-defined. If the Diversity Score drops below 0.2 (highly homogenous) for any segment, the stop condition triggers a launch hold.
*   **Status:** Currently **UNVERIFIED**. Do not assert a safety rate; run the simulation to generate the metric.

**Mitigation Strategies (Configuration Only)**
Since the legacy CMS cannot be updated with new friction mechanisms, apply these configuration changes to the recommendation logic:

1.  **Cap Relevance Weighting:** Adjust the ranking algorithm's configuration to cap the weight of "past reading history" at 60%. Force the remaining 40% of the daily feed to be populated by "trending across all segments" or "editorial top picks," ensuring exposure to non-personalized content.
2.  **Source Diversity Constraint:** Configure a hard rule that no more than two consecutive articles in a feed can come from the same publisher or ideological cluster. This breaks the reinforcement loop without requiring UI changes.
3.  **Uncertainty & Disclosure:** Add a static text line in the app header (configurable via existing banner settings): "Stories curated based on your reading history. Explore the 'Top Stories' tab for a broader view." This addresses **missing AI-disclosure** and nudges against **automation bias**.
4.  **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For the "Political Analysis" category specifically, configure the system to require manual editorial approval for any story flagged as "highly polarizing" by keyword sensors before it enters the personalized feed. This prevents the algorithm from autonomously amplifying inflammatory content.

**Governance**
Assign a specific Product Owner to review the "Viewpoint Diversity Score" weekly post-launch. If the metric degrades, the owner must manually adjust the diversity cap percentages.

**Residual Risk**
Even with these controls, configuration tweaks are less robust than architectural changes. The risk of subtle bias remains **UNVERIFIED** until the simulation runs. Do not certify the system as "unbiased"; ship with the monitoring plan active and the editorial gate enforced.
