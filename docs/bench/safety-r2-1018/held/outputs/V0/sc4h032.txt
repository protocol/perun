**Plan to Assess and Mitigate Bias Under Deadline**

Given the three-week deadline and the high-stakes nature of automated tax audits (an irreversible action with significant financial and reputational consequences for targeted businesses), you cannot certify the system as "unbiased." Instead, you must implement a **harm inventory**, a **human-in-the-loop gate**, and a **targeted measurement plan** using existing resources.

**1. Immediate Governance: The Human Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
Do not deploy fully automated audits. Given the risk of disproportionate targeting, the system must operate in **"Recommendation Mode"** only.
*   **Action:** Every flag generated by the model requires explicit human review by an auditor before any notice is sent to a business.
*   **Rationale:** This prevents immediate harm from false positives or biased patterns while you gather evidence. Full automation of high-stakes actions is the failure mode to avoid.

**2. Measurement Workflow (Using Existing Data)**
Since you cannot acquire new demographic datasets, you must use **proxy analysis** and **adversarial testing** on your current data.
*   **Proxy Identification:** Identify features in your data that strongly correlate with the protected groups you fear targeting (e.g., business size, specific industry codes, geographic zip codes, or language patterns in filing notes).
*   **Stratified Evaluation:** Run your current validation set but slice the results by these proxy groups. Do not look only at overall accuracy.
    *   *Metric:* Compare the **False Positive Rate (FPR)** for the suspected minority-owned business proxies against the baseline. If the model flags 20% of small businesses in Sector A but only 5% in Sector B for similar risk scores, you have evidence of disparate impact.
*   **Adversarial Probes:** Construct synthetic test cases where only the proxy features change (e.g., swap a business location or industry code) while financial metrics remain identical. If the risk score changes significantly, the model is relying on biased correlates.

**3. Transparency and Disclosure Artifacts**
Before deployment, draft a **Model Card** and **Data Card** (even if internal for now).
*   **Content:** Explicitly state the **intended use** (audit selection support, not final determination), **known limitations** (potential bias due to lack of demographic ground truth), and **data provenance**.
*   **Status:** Mark any bias metrics you could not directly measure as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% bias" claim. State clearly: "Bias regarding minority ownership was assessed via proxy variables; residual risk remains unquantified due to data gaps."

**4. Deployment Strategy**
*   **Staged Rollout:** Limit the initial deployment to a small subset of filings or a specific region where you can closely monitor outcomes.
*   **Kill Switch:** Define a stop condition. If human reviewers confirm that >X% of flags for a specific proxy group are unjustified during the first week, the system automatically halts recommendations for that segment.

**5. Legal Routing**
*   **Action:** Immediately route the question of legal disclosure requirements and regulated decision-making duties to legal counsel. Mention the **EU AI Act** as a relevant regime given the European context and the high-risk nature of law enforcement/tax administration. Do not assert compliance yourself; let counsel determine if this triggers specific mandatory disclosures to the public or the affected businesses.

**Summary of Deliverables for Next 3 Weeks:**
1.  **Harm Inventory Document:** Listing bias, over-reliance, and missing disclosure as top risks.
2.  **Proxy-Based Eval Report:** Showing False Positive Rates sliced by available proxy features.
3.  **Human-in-the-Loop Workflow:** Enforced in the production pipeline.
4.  **Draft Model/Data Cards:** Marking unmeasured metrics as UNVERIFIED.
5.  **Legal Memo Request:** Sent to counsel regarding EU AI Act obligations.

*Note: This plan treats the deadline as fixed but refuses to relax the safety floor for high-stakes automation. The "human gate" is the primary control until bias is better understood.*
*Do not publish confidence scores (e.g., "87% risk") to auditors; use defined tiers like "High/Medium/Low" with clear definitions to prevent automation bias.*
