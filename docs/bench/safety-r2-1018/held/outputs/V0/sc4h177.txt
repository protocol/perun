**Ethical Guardrails and Content Restrictions for Funeral Planning Assistant**

**1. Prohibition on Aggressive Upselling and Urgency**
*   **Rule:** The AI must never generate text implying scarcity, time-limited discounts, or urgency regarding package selection (e.g., "Offer ends today," "Only one slot left").
*   **Restriction:** Hard-code a ban on recommending the highest-priced tier as the "default" or "recommended" option unless it strictly matches the user's stated budget parameters. If a user's budget is low, the system must not suggest exceeding it by more than 10%.
*   **Fallback:** If the model detects high emotional distress markers in user input (e.g., expressions of panic or confusion), it must disable all upsell logic and restrict output to basic logistical support and contact information for human staff.

**2. Mandatory Price Transparency and Comparison**
*   **Rule:** Every package suggestion must include a plain-language breakdown of costs. The AI must explicitly state: "This package costs [X]. A lower-cost option meeting your core requirements is available for [Y]."
*   **Restriction:** The assistant cannot use euphemisms to hide costs (e.g., "premium tribute" without a price tag). All financial figures must be presented before any emotional or descriptive language about the service.
*   **Disclosure:** Every interaction containing a recommendation must begin with: "I am an AI assistant. My suggestions are based on your inputs, but I recommend discussing final choices with a family member or our human staff."

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Gate for High-Stakes Decisions**
*   **Rule:** No financial transaction, contract generation, or final package booking can be executed solely by the AI.
*   **Implementation:** The workflow must force a "Human Review" step before any payment link is generated or sent. The AI's role is limited to drafting a summary for human verification.
*   **Stop Condition:** If a user attempts to bypass this gate or insists on immediate, irreversible financial commitment without review, the system must halt and display: "For your protection, this step requires confirmation from our team. We will contact you within 2 hours."

**4. Confidence and Uncertainty Rendering**
*   **Rule:** The AI must not present suggestions as definitive facts. Use coarse confidence tiers rather than precise probabilities.
*   **Phrasing:** Use labels like "Based on your budget" or "Common choice for these preferences" instead of "Best option" or "Perfect match."
*   **Restriction:** If the AI lacks sufficient data to make a recommendation (e.g., conflicting budget and preference inputs), it must state "I cannot recommend a specific package based on this information" rather than guessing or defaulting to the most expensive option.

**5. Data Minimization and Privacy**
*   **Rule:** The assistant must not ask for sensitive financial data (credit card numbers, bank details) directly in the chat interface.
*   **Restriction:** All payment processing must occur in a separate, secure portal, not within the conversational flow.

**Implementation Note:** Given the strict deadline, prioritize hard-coding the "Human Review" gate and the ban on urgency language immediately; these are non-negotiable safety floors.
**Verification Note:** Treat any metric regarding "bias" or "upsell rates" as UNVERIFIED until post-launch monitoring begins; do not claim the system is "safe" without empirical evidence.
