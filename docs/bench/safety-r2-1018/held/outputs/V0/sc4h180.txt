**Pre-Launch Review Plan: Automated Legal Contract Generator**

Given your constraints (beta stage, minimal budget, high-stakes legal output), you cannot rely on the AI to guarantee legal soundness. The following plan prioritizes a human-in-the-loop gate for irreversible actions while establishing a scalable validation process.

**1. Immediate Control: Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory)**
Do not allow the system to auto-send binding contracts during beta. Any action that creates a legal obligation or waives rights is "high-stakes" and "irreversible."
*   **Action:** Implement a workflow where the AI generates a *draft*, but a designated human co-op member must explicitly review and click "Approve" before the document is finalized or sent.
*   **Rationale:** Full automation of binding legal agreements without human verification is the primary failure mode you must prevent. This gate remains in place until you have empirical evidence of safety, which likely exceeds your current budget and timeline.

**2. Validation Strategy: The "Golden Set" Audit**
Since you cannot hire a lawyer for every contract, you must validate the *logic* rather than individual outputs.
*   **Create a Test Suite:** Draft 20–30 specific scenarios covering edge cases (e.g., minimum wage violations, excessive non-competes, missing termination clauses, local Buenos Aires labor law specifics). Include "poison" inputs designed to trick the AI into waiving rights.
*   **Expert One-Time Review:** Use your limited legal budget to hire a labor lawyer for a single, focused session. Their task is not to review user contracts, but to review the *outputs* of your 20–30 test scenarios.
*   **Pass/Fail Criteria:** If the lawyer identifies even one unenforceable clause or rights waiver in the test set, the template logic fails. Do not launch until the test set passes 100%.

**3. Transparency & Disclosure**
*   **User Notification:** Clearly disclose to freelancers that the agreement is machine-generated and has not been individually reviewed by counsel for their specific situation.
*   **Limitation of Liability:** Include a prominent disclaimer stating the tool does not constitute legal advice and that users should seek independent counsel if unsure.
*   **Legal Duty Check:** Whether Argentine law or specific regulations require additional disclosures for automated legal decisions is a legal determination. You must route this specific question to counsel; do not assume compliance based on this plan alone.

**4. Residual Risk Management**
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Do not display numerical confidence scores (e.g., "95% valid") to users. Instead, if the system detects ambiguous inputs, flag the output as "Requires Manual Review" rather than guessing.
*   **Rollback Mechanism:** Establish a process to immediately void or amend contracts if a systemic flaw is discovered post-launch.

**Summary of Deliverables for Launch:**
1.  A hardcoded human approval step for all contract finalizations.
2.  A documented "Golden Set" of test cases with results verified by a one-time legal expert.
3.  Clear user-facing disclosures regarding the automated nature of the tool.
4.  A formal routing of regulatory compliance questions to legal counsel.

*Note: This plan measures risk and manages exposure; it does not certify the system as "legal" or "unbiased." The decision to ship despite residual risk rests solely with your co-op.*
