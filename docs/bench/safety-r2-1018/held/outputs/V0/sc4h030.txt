# Contingency Plan: Manual Identification and Service of Unmapped Locations

**Objective:** Ensure equitable supply distribution to families in remote, unmapped Haitian villages when the AI routing system fails to include them due to outdated map data.

**Phase 1: Local Intelligence Gathering (Pre-Deployment)**
*   **Establish Community Liaison Points:** Before trucks depart, station team members at known central hubs (markets, churches, schools) in the nearest mapped towns. Equip them with paper maps and radios.
*   **Activate Trusted Networks:** Partner immediately with local community leaders, religious figures, and existing grassroots organizations. These individuals possess mental maps of unrecorded settlements. Ask them to identify specific landmarks (e.g., "the big mango tree near the river bend") that define unmapped village entrances.
*   **Collect Verbal Coordinates:** Record directions using relative terms familiar to locals (time to walk, distinct physical features) rather than relying solely on GPS coordinates which may be inaccurate for these specific spots.

**Phase 2: Real-Time Detection and Verification**
*   **Driver Spotter Protocol:** Instruct drivers to treat the AI route as a "primary suggestion," not a final mandate. If a driver sees footpaths, smoke from cooking fires, or makeshift shelters diverging from the optimized route, they must halt and report via radio.
*   **The "Two-Source" Rule:** To prevent resource diversion or fraud, a location is verified for manual addition only if confirmed by both a driver's visual observation AND a separate report from a community liaison or local leader.
*   **Rapid Triage:** The central coordinator (human-in-the-loop) maintains a physical whiteboard or simple spreadsheet. When a new location is verified, they manually assign the nearest available truck based on fuel levels and current load, overriding the AI.

**Phase 3: Manual Execution and Delivery**
*   **Guided Escort:** For the first delivery to a newly identified village, a local guide identified in Phase 1 must physically ride with the driver to navigate the unrecorded path safely.
*   **Standardized Drop-Off:** Upon arrival, distribute supplies using the same fairness criteria as the AI (e.g., per household or per vulnerable individual) to maintain equity.
*   **Data Marking:** The driver must mark the location physically on their paper map and log the approximate GPS reading (even if imprecise) and descriptive landmarks in the central log. This creates an immediate, low-tech dataset for future runs.

**Phase 4: Feedback Loop**
*   **End-of-Day Reconciliation:** Every evening, the operations team compares the AI's planned route against the actual deliveries made.
*   **Route Adjustment:** Manually update the next day's starting parameters. If the AI allows manual waypoint injection, add the new coordinates. If not, print revised paper manifests for drivers that include the new stops in logical order.
*   **Community Validation:** Return to the liaison points to confirm no other villages were missed and that the new locations received aid without incident.

**Critical Constraints & Safety**
*   **Human Authority:** The AI has no veto power over a verified human report of an unmapped village. If a local leader says a family is there, the system assumes they are until proven otherwise.
*   **Safety First:** Drivers must not attempt off-road navigation in unstable post-storm terrain without a local guide. If a path is too dangerous, supplies are to be staged at the nearest safe access point for community runners to transport.
*   **Transparency:** Clearly communicate to recipients that this is an emergency manual override to ensure they know the system is adapting to help them, not ignoring them.

This plan prioritizes human local knowledge over incomplete automated data, ensuring no community is excluded due to map errors.

*Note: This plan assumes functional radio communication between drivers and base; if radios fail, pre-arranged check-in times at specific landmarks are required.*
*Ensure all local guides are compensated or provided supplies immediately to maintain trust and safety.*
