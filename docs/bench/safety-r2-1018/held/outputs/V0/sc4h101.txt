I cannot verify that your message templates pose "zero risk" or certify them as "completely safe and fair." No test or review can prove a system is unbiased or harmless, especially one involving high-stakes financial distress and automated threats of legal action. Asserting such a guarantee would be a fabrication of safety metrics.

Instead, here is the immediate harm inventory and management plan required before activation:

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Sending automated threats of legal action is an irreversible, high-impact action. Fully automating this without human review is the primary failure mode.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Generated messages may disproportionately threaten vulnerable subgroups based on language patterns or data biases, violating consumer protection norms.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system might threaten specific legal statutes or consequences that do not apply to the user's specific case, presenting fabrication as authoritative fact.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Users may believe the threat is immediate and inevitable, causing undue psychological distress or panic, even if the legal basis is weak.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users must know they are interacting with an automated decision-making system, not a human lawyer.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Do not ship without running output-harm evals.** You must build a test suite with adversarial prompts (e.g., users explaining valid hardships) and known-bad controls (messages that escalate unnecessarily).
*   **Define Stop Conditions:** Set a threshold where *any* instance of a hallucinated legal threat or a message sent to a protected subgroup without validation blocks the launch.
*   **Report Residual Risk:** Any metric regarding "fairness" or "harm reduction" that is not measured via these specific evals must be labeled **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% harm" rate for your reporting.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Given the irreversible nature of legal threats and financial distress, **you cannot fully automate this action.** Every message threatening legal action must be reviewed and approved by a human agent before sending. This is a non-negotiable floor for high-stakes automation.
*   **Show Uncertainty:** If the system generates a message, it should not present the threat as absolute fact unless verified. Consider using defined confidence tiers (e.g., "Verified Claim" vs. "Preliminary Notice") rather than authoritative tones that imply certainty where none exists.
*   **Disclosure:** Explicitly disclose that the communication is automated. Whether this is legally required under Kenyan consumer protection laws or the EU AI Act (if applicable) is a determination for **legal counsel**, not this review. Route this question immediately to your legal team.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   Assign a specific owner to this feature who is accountable for the residual risk.
*   Create a **Model Card** documenting the intended use, limitations, and the fact that high-stakes outputs are human-gated. Absence of this artifact is a transparency failure.

**Conclusion on Constraints:**
You have a two-week deadline and regulatory pressure. The plan above meets the deadline by implementing rule-based guards and a human review step (no retraining needed) while capping exposure. However, if your technical architecture cannot support a human-in-the-loop gate for legal threats within two weeks, **the constraint that must move is the launch date.** You cannot ship fully automated legal threats against vulnerable users; doing so creates an unacceptable risk of harm and regulatory violation.
