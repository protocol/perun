### Risk Mitigation Plan: Smart Waste Bin Deployment

**1. Manual Inspection Schedule (Interim Control)**
Given the sensor's known failure to detect hazardous buildup and intermittent connectivity, automated monitoring cannot be the primary safety layer until the inspection passes.
*   **Frequency:** Increase manual physical inspections to **daily** for all bins located in or near underground parking areas and high-foot-traffic zones. Standard above-ground bins in low-risk areas will be inspected **every 48 hours**.
*   **Scope:** Inspectors must visually verify bin fullness and specifically check for signs of hazardous material buildup (unusual odors, discoloration, heat) that sensors miss.
*   **Timeline:** This schedule begins immediately and continues until the municipal inspection is successfully completed and sensor reliability is proven under legacy network constraints.

**2. Signal Loss & Alert Protocol**
To prevent overflow during connectivity blackouts, the system will operate on a "fail-safe" assumption rather than waiting for a digital trigger.
*   **Trigger Condition:** If a bin fails to transmit a status update for **6 consecutive hours** (exceeding the typical intermittent gap), it is automatically flagged as "Signal Lost – Assume Risk."
*   **Alert Mechanism:** Since real-time digital alerts to crews are unreliable underground, the central dashboard will generate a **daily consolidated "Red List"** at 06:00 AM. This list includes all bins with "Signal Lost" status or those approaching capacity based on the last known data point.
*   **Action:** Sanitation crews must physically visit every bin on the "Red List" before noon. No bin remains on the list without a physical verification stamp from the crew lead.
*   **Overflow Prevention:** If a bin is found >80% full during any manual check, it is emptied immediately, regardless of the sensor reading.

**3. Implementation Timeline (3-Week Sprint)**
*   **Week 1:** Deploy daily manual inspection roster; train sanitation crews on the "Red List" protocol and hazardous material visual identification.
*   **Week 2:** Run parallel operations (digital sensors + manual checks). Log all instances where manual checks caught issues sensors missed or where signal loss occurred.
*   **Week 3:** Refine the "Red List" thresholds based on Week 2 data. Conduct a mock municipal inspection using the manual logs as proof of safety controls.

**Note:** This plan treats the sensor system as advisory only; human verification is the mandatory gate for safety until connectivity and sensor accuracy are resolved.
**Note:** All manual inspection logs must be preserved digitally and physically to demonstrate due diligence during the municipal inspection.
