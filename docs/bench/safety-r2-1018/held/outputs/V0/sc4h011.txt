**Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Extreme Heat Wave Lockout Feature**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record these specific risks for the regulator:
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The feature executes an irreversible action (locking out manual control) that could cause physical harm or death to vulnerable users (elderly, medical equipment dependence) during a heat wave.
*   **Over-Reliance/Automation Bias:** The system assumes grid safety outweighs individual survival needs without verifying the user's specific context.
*   **Missing Override Mechanism:** A total lockout prevents users from acting on local knowledge (e.g., "I have a medical exemption" or "My AC is broken and this is my only cooling").

**2. Immediate Mitigation & Human Gate (MANAGE)**
*   **Implement a Hard Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** You cannot ship fully autonomous lockouts for high-stakes physical safety scenarios. Modify the feature immediately:
    *   **Soft Lock Default:** The system *recommends* locking manual adjustments but requires a secondary confirmation or allows an immediate "Emergency Override" PIN code sent via SMS/email to the account holder.
    *   **Exemption Registry:** Create a simple, manual flag in your database for users who self-identify as vulnerable (medical equipment, elderly). The lockout must never trigger for these accounts.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not present the lockout as a guaranteed safety measure. The UI must state: "Grid stress detected. Manual adjustments are restricted to prevent outage. Click here for Emergency Override if you have medical needs."

**3. Measurement Plan with Zero Budget (MEASURE)**
Since you lack real-world data and hardware, you must simulate human failure modes using your existing team and software:
*   **Adversarial "Red Team" Exercise:** Dedicate 2 days where half your team acts as vulnerable users (e.g., "confused elderly person," "panicked parent with sick child"). Attempt to bypass the lockout using only the provided UI. Record every failure to override.
*   **Paper Prototype Review:** Before coding changes, walk through the "Emergency Override" flow with 5 non-engineers (friends/family). If they cannot find the override within 30 seconds, the design fails.
*   **Metric & Stop Condition:**
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of test participants who successfully execute an emergency override within 60 seconds.
    *   *Threshold:* 100% success rate required for launch.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If any tester cannot bypass the lockout, the feature is blocked from launch regardless of the regulatory deadline.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model/Data Card)**
Prepare a one-page "Safety & Limitations Statement" for the regulators:
*   **Intended Use:** Grid load balancing during declared emergencies.
*   **Known Limitations:** "System cannot verify individual medical status automatically. Relies on user-initiated exemption flags."
*   **Residual Risk:** "Risk of heat exposure remains if user cannot access override. Mitigated by mandatory emergency bypass channel."
*   **Status of Metrics:** "Real-world impact on vulnerable populations is **UNVERIFIED** due to lack of field data. Launch proceeds with strict human-override safeguards."

**5. Governance**
*   **Owner:** Name one specific engineer responsible for monitoring override usage post-launch.
*   **Review Trigger:** Any use of the "Emergency Override" function must trigger an immediate manual review of that account's status.

**Note:** Do not claim the system is "safe" or "unbiased" to regulators; present the *controls* (the override) and the *residual risk* honestly.
**Note:** If legal counsel is available, route the question of "mandatory disclosure language" to them immediately; do not assert legal compliance yourself.
