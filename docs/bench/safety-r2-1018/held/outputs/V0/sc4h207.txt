**Immediate Action Plan: Welfare-Fraud Detector Validation**

**1. Implement a Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
Given the high stakes (wrongful fraud accusations, financial interruption, distress), **no automated flag may trigger an adverse action, benefit suspension, or direct citizen notification without human review.**
*   **Action:** Configure the system output as "Pending Review" only. The automated decision is strictly an internal triage signal for your team of eleven.
*   **Constraint:** Do not automate the "accusation" step. Full automation of this irreversible action is the primary failure mode to prevent.

**2. Execute a Rapid "Harm Inventory" & Targeted Red-Team (Week 1)**
Do not attempt to clean all data. Instead, probe the specific known weakness (glitchy employment integration).
*   **Define the Harm:** "False positive due to data lag/mismatch causing wrongful accusation."
*   **Run Adversarial Probes:** Create a small, synthetic test set (20–50 cases) representing known "glitch" scenarios (e.g., recent job start dates not yet reflected in feeds, part-time vs. full-time coding errors).
*   **Metric:** Measure the **False Positive Rate (FPR)** specifically on this "glitch" subset.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the system flags >0% of these known-good synthetic cases as fraud, the logic is unsafe for deployment. The threshold for gating irreversible harm should be near-zero tolerance for known error patterns.

**3. Surface Uncertainty, Not False Precision**
*   **Action:** Suppress any raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% likely fraud") from human reviewers and citizens.
*   **Replacement:** Display defined coarse tiers based on evidence corroboration:
    *   *Confirmed:* Multiple independent data sources align.
    *   *Single-Source/Unverified:* Flag relies solely on the glitch-prone employment feed.
*   **Rule:** Any flag rated "Unverified" or relying on the unstable feed requires senior reviewer sign-off before any citizen contact.

**4. Draft a Transparency Artifact (Model Card Lite)**
Before launch, produce a one-page document for internal stakeholders and counsel containing:
*   **Intended Use:** Triage only; not a final determination.
*   **Known Limitations:** Explicitly state the employment data integration is unverified and prone to lag.
*   **Residual Risk:** Mark the accuracy rate regarding employment glitches as **UNVERIFIED** until the Week 1 probes are run. Do not invent an accuracy percentage.

**5. Legal & Regulatory Routing (Immediate)**
*   **Action:** Immediately route the question of "mandatory AI disclosure" and "automated decision rights" to municipal counsel.
*   **Context:** Cite the **EU AI Act** (high-risk classification likely) and local administrative law. Do not assert whether disclosure is legally required; request a formal determination from counsel before user-facing rollout.

**6. Staged Rollout with Kill Switch**
*   **Plan:** Launch in "Shadow Mode" for the first 2 weeks of the fiscal year. The system runs and flags, but **no letters are sent**.
*   **Review:** Your team manually audits the top 50 flags daily against the raw data.
*   **Kill Switch:** If the manual audit reveals a pattern of distress or clear data-glitch false positives, halt all flagging immediately.

**Note:** This plan treats the deadline as fixed but refuses to relax the safety floor: high-stakes actions remain human-gated.
**Note:** No metric is certified as "safe"; the "UNVERIFIED" label must remain on the employment-data accuracy until proven otherwise by the probes.
