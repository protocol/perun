**BLUF: Do not proceed with the pilot deployment next week.** The proposed feature—inferring mental health states from social media to target political ads—constitutes a high-stakes, irreversible automated decision with severe potential for harm. Under current constraints (no third-party audit, internal-only testing), you cannot satisfy the safety floor required to ship. The following plan documents the ethical blockers and the mandatory controls you must implement before any launch can be considered.

### 1. Harm Inventory (MAP)
Record these specific risks immediately. An empty inventory is not a pass.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Mental health inference models historically exhibit high false-positive rates for marginalized groups. Systematic disadvantage in political messaging is a near-certain outcome.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Inferring a clinical state from social media activity is probabilistic guesswork, not diagnosis. Presenting these inferences as valid targeting criteria treats fabrication as fact.
*   **Deceptive Patterns & Missing Disclosure:** Users cannot know they are being segmented by inferred mental health status. This violates the principle of informed consent.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Influencing voter behavior based on psychological vulnerability is a high-stakes action. Fully automating this without human review is the primary failure mode.

### 2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)
You cannot certify safety with internal staff alone, but you must attempt to measure residual risk.
*   **Adversarial Probes:** Construct a test set where known mental health indicators are absent but demographic markers (age, gender, ethnicity) are present. If the model infers mental health issues based on demographics, the model is biased.
*   **Positive/Negative Controls:** Run inputs with explicit self-harm language (positive control) and neutral language (negative control). If the model fails to distinguish or over-flagging neutral text from specific groups, halt.
*   **Metric Reporting:** Do not invent a "bias rate." If you cannot test against a representative external population, mark all bias metrics as **UNVERIFIED**. Report this status explicitly to the ethics board.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** stating: "Intended Use: Political targeting (High Risk). Limitations: Inferences are unverified guesses, not diagnoses. Subgroup Performance: UNVERIFIED due to lack of diverse testing data."

### 3. Risk Management (MANAGE)
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** You must implement a hard gate. No ad targeting a user based on an inferred mental health state may run without explicit human review and justification. Fully automated execution of this logic is prohibited.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** If you proceed, you must surface uncertainty. Do not show "87% likely depressed." Use coarse, defined tiers (e.g., "Signal Detected - Unverified") and provide an easy override/opt-out for users.
*   **Legal Routing:** Inferring mental health for political advertising likely triggers strict regulations (e.g., EU AI Act, GDPR special category data). **Route immediately to legal counsel.** Do not assert compliance internally; this is a legal determination, not an engineering one.

### 4. Governance (GOVERN)
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name a single individual responsible for the harm of "psychological manipulation via inferred health data."
*   **Stop Condition:** Define a clear rollback trigger. If *any* ad is served to a user based on a false positive mental health inference during the pilot, the system must automatically disable targeting for that segment.

**Note:** Operating under a "tight budget" does not lower the safety floor for high-stakes psychological profiling; it simply means you lack the evidence to ship safely.
