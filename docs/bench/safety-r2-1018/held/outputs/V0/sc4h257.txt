**Pre-Launch Safety Checklist: Historical Accuracy & Cultural Appropriateness**

**1. Harm Inventory (Map)**
*   **Risk:** Hallucination surfaced as fact (fabricated dates, events, or figures presented as truth).
*   **Risk:** Cultural bias (narratives that marginalize local perspectives or reinforce colonial stereotypes).
*   **Affected:** Middle school students in developing nations; worst case is the internalization of false history or cultural erasure.
*   **Status:** Do not proceed to deployment until this inventory is signed off by the five-person team.

**2. Measurement Plan (Measure)**
*   **Action:** Execute a "Red-Team" probe set immediately using your limited connectivity.
    *   *Positive Control:* Input known historical falsehoods; verify the system rejects or corrects them.
    *   *Negative Control:* Input verified local historical facts; verify the system reproduces them without alteration.
    *   *Adversarial Probe:* Test prompts designed to trigger biased or stereotypical outputs regarding local cultures.
*   **Metric:** Zero tolerance for unverified claims in high-stakes narrative blocks.
*   **Reporting:** Any metric not explicitly tested via the probes above must be labeled **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent an "accuracy rate" (e.g., "99% accurate") without this data.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a one-page **Model Card** for internal use listing: intended use (education), known limitations (potential gaps in specific local eras), and the owner of the content review. Shipping without this is a transparency failure.

**3. Risk Management (Manage)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Because correcting historical misinformation in students is hard to reverse, **no content goes live without human approval.**
    *   *Constraint Adaptation:* With only five staff and a 30-day deadline, you cannot review everything. Implement a **staged rollout**: deploy only the top 20% of highest-priority curriculum modules first.
    *   *Process:* The five educators must manually approve every generated narrative in this initial batch before it reaches students. Full automation is prohibited for this launch.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If the system generates content where sources are thin, it must surface a **defined coarse tier** of confidence (e.g., "Corroborated by multiple sources" vs. "Single-source account") rather than a raw percentage. Ensure students/teachers see this distinction.
*   **Fallback Rule:** If the system cannot verify a fact against its retrieved context, it must default to "I cannot confirm this detail" rather than guessing.

**4. Governance (Govern)**
*   **Owner:** Name one specific person from your five-person team as the "Content Safety Owner" responsible for the final sign-off.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the underlying model or the historical dataset requires a re-run of the Red-Team probes before re-deployment.

**5. Legal & Ethical Routing**
*   **Disclosure:** While this tool adapts curricula, determining if an "AI-generated" disclosure is legally required for minors in your target nations is a legal determination. Route this question to counsel immediately; do not assume it is optional.
*   **Regulatory Regimes:** Be aware that educational AI targeting minors may fall under scrutiny from frameworks like the **EU AI Act** (if operating globally) or local child safety laws. Name these regimes in your risk log but rely on counsel to determine binding applicability.

**Definition of Done for Launch:**
1.  Harm inventory completed and signed.
2.  Red-team probes run on the top 20% of content; results logged.
3.  Human-in-the-loop gate active for all initial content.
4.  Model Card drafted and attached to the release.
5.  Legal question regarding minor protection routed to counsel.

*Note: This checklist identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased." The final deployment decision rests with your named Content Safety Owner.*
*If external fact-checking is impossible due to connectivity, strictly limit the launch scope to content your five educators can personally verify.*
