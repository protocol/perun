**Immediate Guardrails and Escalation Strategy**

**1. Hard Stop Trigger & Content Suppression**
Implement a deterministic keyword and sentiment filter on all user inputs. If the system detects indicators of severe distress (e.g., self-harm, hopelessness, crisis), it must immediately **suppress all career advice generation**. The model is strictly forbidden from outputting resume tips, interview strategies, or motivational platitudes in these instances. The system state must switch from "Career Coach" to "Safety Mode," halting any further conversational flow regarding employment until the safety protocol is resolved.

**2. Static Resource Injection (No Model Generation)**
In "Safety Mode," the app must bypass the AI entirely and display a static, pre-approved message containing verified local emergency contacts. Since you cannot hire experts, rely on established Australian infrastructure. The response must be immediate and visible:
*   **Lifeline Australia:** 13 11 14 (24/7 crisis support)
*   **Beyond Blue:** 1300 22 4636
*   **Emergency:** 000
*   **Text Support:** Specific SMS shortcodes for immediate text-based counseling.
Do not allow the AI to summarize, paraphrase, or "explain" these numbers. Display them as hard-coded text with clickable call/SMS links.

**3. Explicit AI Disclosure & Limitation Statement**
Every interaction, especially during distress detection, must carry a clear, non-negotiable disclosure: *"I am an AI career assistant, not a mental health professional. I cannot provide crisis counseling."* This prevents over-reliance and automation bias where users might mistake the bot's empathy for clinical competence. This disclosure must appear before any resource list is shown.

**4. Human-in-the-Loop Escalation (Lean Team Adaptation)**
Since you cannot staff mental health experts, implement a "Human Review Queue" for flagged sessions.
*   **Action:** When a distress trigger fires, the session is logged and flagged for immediate review by any available founding team member.
*   **Protocol:** The team member's sole role is to verify the automated safety message was delivered and, if the user remains engaged, manually send a follow-up reinforcing the referral to professional help.
*   **Constraint:** Do not attempt to counsel. The human gate exists only to ensure the bridge to professional help was successfully built, not to walk the user across it.

**5. Data Privacy & Minimal Retention**
For users in crisis, minimize data retention. Do not store the specific details of their distress in long-term logs used for model training. Mark these sessions as "Sensitive" and exclude them from any future fine-tuning datasets to prevent the model from learning to mimic or trivialize crisis scenarios.

**6. Launch Gate Condition**
The feature cannot launch until the "Safety Mode" override is tested against adversarial prompts designed to trick the bot into giving career advice despite expressed despair. If the bot offers a resume tip to a user stating they want to give up, the launch is blocked.

**Notes**
*   Legal counsel must review the specific wording of your crisis disclaimers to ensure compliance with Australian consumer law and duty-of-care standards before release.
*   This strategy treats the detection of distress as a high-stakes irreversible risk; therefore, full automation of advice is disabled by default in these scenarios.
