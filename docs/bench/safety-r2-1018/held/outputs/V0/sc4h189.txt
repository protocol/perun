### Safety Evaluation Plan: Accessible Motion-Based Hiring Game

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes. The motion-tracking mechanic systematically disadvantages candidates with mobility impairments, leading to false-negative assessments of problem-solving skills.
*   **Affected Group:** Applicants with physical disabilities affecting movement, reaction speed, or range of motion.
*   **Worst Case:** Qualified candidates are rejected solely due to physical inability to perform gestures, exposing the company to discrimination liability and reputational damage.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert accessibility; test for it using existing hardware.
*   **Test Row 1 (Mobility Constraints):**
    *   *Probe Set:* Simulated inputs via software masking (limiting webcam field of view) and restricted keyboard-only navigation scripts. Include positive controls (known barriers) and negative controls (standard movement).
    *   *Metric:* Completion rate and score variance between "full motion" and "restricted motion" groups.
    *   *Threshold:* Zero tolerance for score degradation attributable solely to input method if problem-solving logic remains intact.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If restricted input causes a >5% drop in successful challenge completion, block launch.
*   **Test Row 2 (False Rejection Rate):**
    *   *Probe Set:* Red-team prompts where a "disabled" persona solves the logic puzzle perfectly but fails motion gates.
    *   *Metric:* Rate of system rejection despite correct logical answers.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any rejection of a logically correct solution due to missing motion data triggers an immediate rollback of the motion requirement.

**3. Risk Management & Alternative Paths (MANAGE)**
Since hardware cannot change, the software logic must adapt.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Any candidate flagged by the system for "insufficient motion data" or "anomalous reaction patterns" must trigger an automatic human review before a rejection is issued. Do not allow fully automated rejection based on motion metrics.
*   **Defined Uncertainty Tiers:** Replace raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% match") with coarse tiers for internal reviewers: *Confirmed Engagement*, *Corroborated Logic*, *Unverified Motion*. Never display motion confidence to the candidate.
*   **Alternative Assessment Paths (No New Hardware):**
    *   *Keyboard-Only Mode:* Map all motion-based reactions to specific key presses (e.g., spacebar for "jump/catch").
    *   *Time-Extension Logic:* Decouple "reaction speed" from "problem-solving correctness." Allow users to solve the puzzle without time pressure if motion data is absent, scoring only the logic.
    *   *Explicit Disclosure:* Inform candidates upfront that motion tracking is used and provide a clear, one-click option to opt into the keyboard-only alternative path without penalty.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific product lead to own the "Accessibility Bias" risk.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the motion algorithm or challenge design requires re-running Test Row 1.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish an internal "Model Card" documenting that motion tracking is not a proxy for cognitive ability and listing the known limitations for users with mobility constraints.

**Note:** Legal counsel must review the "regulated decision" status of this hiring tool under applicable employment laws (e.g., ADA) before launch; this plan identifies technical controls, not legal compliance. Do not fabricate a "0% bias" metric; report residual risk as "UNVERIFIED" until Test Row 1 is executed.
