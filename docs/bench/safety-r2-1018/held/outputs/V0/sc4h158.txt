### Safety Preparation Guide: Automated Social Media Moderator (Beta)

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Assessment**
Before expanding the beta, record these specific risks for the Manila forum context:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The outdated keyword library may flag emerging activist slang or coded political speech as hate speech, silencing legitimate community voices.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Fully automating the deletion of posts without human review risks irreversible loss of critical community discourse and erodes trust.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Users may not realize their posts are being filtered by an AI, leading to confusion when content disappears without explanation.
*   **Excess Agency:** The system currently lacks the ability to detect coordinated bot attacks using new slang, allowing harmful campaigns to slip through while legitimate users are blocked.

**2. Measurement Plan (Do Not Assert Safety)**
Do not claim the model is "fair" or "accurate" without evidence. Instead, run these specific evaluations:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Compile a list of known activist slogans, local slang, and coded hate speech. Test the model against these inputs.
    *   *Positive Control:* Known hate speech (must be flagged).
    *   *Negative Control:* Known activist speech (must NOT be flagged).
*   **Bot Simulation:** Simulate a coordinated attack using variations of emerging slang to verify if the system misses the pattern.
*   **Metric:** Report the "False Positive Rate on Activist Speech" and "False Negative Rate on Bot Attacks." If these are not measured, label them **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a percentage.

**3. Managed Workflow: Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate**
Given the high stakes of silencing activists and the tooling constraints, full automation is unsafe. Implement this manual review workflow immediately:

*   **Tier 1: High-Confidence Hate Speech (Auto-Hide with Appeal):** Only auto-hide content matching clear, non-ambiguous hate speech definitions. Provide an instant, one-click "Appeal to Human" button for users.
*   **Tier 2: Low-Confidence & Slang Matches (Quarantine):** Any post flagged due to emerging slang, low confidence scores, or potential activist coding must be **quarantined**, not deleted.
    *   *Action:* Route these to the team of 11 specialists for daily review.
    *   *SLA:* Review within 4 hours to prevent discussion stagnation.
*   **Tier 3: Bot Pattern Detection (Manual Triage):** Since the library cannot detect new bot patterns automatically, create a daily "Suspicious Activity" report showing spikes in volume from new accounts or repetitive phrasing. Specialists must manually investigate these clusters.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If a post is held for review, show the user a clear message: "This post is under human review for policy compliance. It is not deleted."

**4. Transparency Artifact**
Create a simple **Model Card** for the internal team and a public **Transparency Note** for users.
*   *Internal:* Document the known limitations of the keyword library, the specific slang terms currently missed, and the owners responsible for weekly updates.
*   *Public:* Disclose that an automated system assists in moderation but that all contested decisions receive human review.

**5. Governance**
Assign one content specialist as the "Safety Owner" for this beta. They must re-evaluate the harm inventory weekly as new slang emerges. If the false positive rate on activist speech rises, the threshold for auto-hiding must be tightened immediately.

***

*Note: Legal requirements for AI disclosure in the Philippines or specific regional regulations must be verified by counsel; this guide recommends disclosure as a safety control, not a legal verdict.*
*Do not proceed to full automation until the False Positive Rate on activist speech is measured and deemed acceptable by the Safety Owner.*
