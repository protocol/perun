**Internal Review Plan: Synthetic Voice Deployment (Nairobi Call Center)**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before integration, document these specific risks for your four-person team:
*   **Deceptive Patterns / Impersonation:** The core risk is the system being confused for a real family member or trusted human agent due to high-fidelity voice replication. This violates user trust and constitutes a deceptive pattern.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Users cannot distinguish machine-generated speech from human speech without explicit, audible disclosure.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** If the voice agent handles sensitive data (account changes, payments) without verifying the user knows it is an AI, it creates an over-reliance trap.
*   **Bias:** Ensure the synthetic voices do not systematically exclude or misrepresent specific Kenyan dialects or accents, leading to unfair outcomes.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Since third-party auditing is not feasible, execute these internal "red-team" tests immediately:
*   **The "Family Confusion" Probe:** Create a test set where callers (internal staff acting as users) are subjected to the synthetic voice without prior warning.
    *   *Positive Control:* A script where the AI attempts to build rapport using personal-sounding language.
    *   *Negative Control:* A script where the AI immediately discloses its nature.
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of testers who explicitly ask, "Are you a real person?" or express confusion about the speaker's identity after the call.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If >5% of testers cannot confirm the speaker is AI within the first 30 seconds, the feature fails the safety gate.
*   **Injection & Leakage Test:** Feed the system prompts containing other users' names or private data to ensure it does not hallucinate or leak information while trying to sound "personal."
*   **Dialect Sweep:** Test routine inquiries across major local language variants to ensure comprehension and tone appropriateness.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Mandatory Audible Disclosure (Human-in-the-Loop Logic):** The system must state "I am an AI assistant" within the first two sentences of every interaction. This is non-negotiable for preventing impersonation.
*   **Confidence Tiers, Not False Precision:** Do not display internal confidence scores to users. If the system is unsure of an intent, it must escalate to a human agent rather than guessing and sounding authoritative.
*   **Irreversible Action Gate:** The synthetic voice must **never** execute irreversible actions (e.g., closing accounts, transferring funds, changing passwords) autonomously. These require explicit human agent confirmation or a secondary verification channel (SMS/Email).
*   **Uncertainty Signaling:** If the AI detects emotional distress or complex queries, it must immediately switch to a "handover" protocol rather than attempting to mimic empathy, which can be perceived as manipulative.

**4. Documentation Artifacts (GOVERN)**
Produce these two internal documents before the two-week deadline:
*   **Model Card (Internal Version):** List intended use (routine inquiries only), out-of-scope uses (emotional support, financial authorization), known limitations (dialect gaps, potential for confusion), and the owner (lead data scientist).
*   **Data Card:** Document the source of the voice patterns. Confirm consent was obtained for any specific voice replication. If the data source is unclear, mark the provenance as `UNVERIFIED` and halt deployment of those specific voices.

**Ownership:** Assign one data scientist as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing any incidents where users reported confusion. Re-review this inventory if the voice models or scripts change.

**Note:** Legal requirements regarding AI disclosure in Kenya and potential liabilities for impersonation must be routed to legal counsel; this plan recommends disclosure as a safety control, not a legal verdict.
**Note:** Never fabricate a "0% confusion" metric; report actual test results from the probes above.
