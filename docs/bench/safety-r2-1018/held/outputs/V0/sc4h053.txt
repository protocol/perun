**Product Output Safety Review Plan: Rental-Price Optimizer**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly document the following specific harms for your feature:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The algorithm systematically suggests higher increases for units occupied by long-term residents or specific demographics, accelerating displacement in gentrifying areas.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Property managers blindly accept recommendations that effectively evict tenants, an irreversible social and financial harm.
*   **Over-reliance:** The interface presents prices as "optimized market facts" without showing uncertainty or alternative ethical scenarios, inviting blind trust.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Tenants and landlords are unaware that the pricing strategy is machine-generated and potentially aggressive.

*Action:* Create a one-page document listing these harms, who is affected (long-term tenants), and the worst-case scenario (mass displacement in specific neighborhoods). An empty list is not a pass.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert the tool is "fair." You must test the outputs. Since you cannot hire counsel, you must run these internal evaluations:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Construct a test dataset of identical apartments differing *only* in simulated tenant tenure (e.g., 2 years vs. 10 years) or neighborhood gentrification status.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure the percentage price difference suggested for long-term vs. short-term tenants.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the algorithm suggests a >5% higher increase solely based on long-term tenure (proxy for displacement risk), the launch is blocked.
*   **Red-Team Prompting:** Feed the system edge cases where "maximizing revenue" conflicts directly with "tenant retention." Does it default to maximum extraction?
*   **Reporting:** Report the residual risk. If you cannot measure a specific bias metric, label it **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% bias" claim for your marketing.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
Given your fixed deadline and lack of legal counsel, you must engineer constraints into the product behavior:
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Critical):** Do not allow the tool to auto-apply price hikes. The system must require explicit property manager confirmation for any increase above a standard inflation cap (e.g., >3%). Frame the output as a "suggestion range" with a warning: "High increases may trigger displacement risks."
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not show raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% optimal"). Instead, use coarse, defined tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Supported by multiple recent comparable leases.
    *   *Unverified:* Based on sparse data or aggressive trend extrapolation (flag this clearly).
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a "Model Card" for your users (property managers). It must state: "Intended Use: Market analysis only. Limitations: May suggest aggressive hikes in gentrifying zones. Owner: [Your Startup Name]." Shipping without this is a transparency failure.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one founder as the explicit owner of "Output Harm." They must re-review the logic if the underlying market data or algorithm changes.
*   **Legal Boundary:** While you cannot afford external counsel, you must acknowledge internally that automated decisions affecting housing may fall under the **EU AI Act** or local tenancy laws. Do not assert you are compliant; instead, add a disclaimer in your terms that users are responsible for final legal compliance of price settings.

**Summary of Immediate Next Steps:**
1.  Build the "tenure-based" adversarial test set immediately.
2.  Hard-code a human confirmation gate for any recommendation flagged as "high impact."
3.  Draft the Model Card disclosing the risk of displacement.

*Note: This plan identifies risks and controls but does not certify your system as safe or legally compliant. The decision to ship rests with your team.*
*If testing reveals systematic displacement drivers, the only responsible control is to disable the aggressive pricing logic entirely.*
