### Critical Safety & Compliance Review: Court-Date Reminder Bot

**Immediate Constraint Warning:** You requested confirmation of statutory notice requirements. **I cannot and will not certify legal compliance.** Determining whether your bot meets statutory notice duties is a legal determination that must be routed immediately to qualified counsel. No code review or test plan can substitute for this legal verification.

Below is the harm inventory, test plan, and fallback strategy required before launch.

#### 1. Harm Inventory (MAP)
Before testing, acknowledge the specific risks to defendants:
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions:** A missed reminder due to a holiday calculation error can lead to a bench warrant, arrest, and loss of liberty. This is an irreversible, high-cost failure.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** If the legacy calendar fails to recognize specific regional or cultural holidays, defendants in those jurisdictions systematically face higher risks of missed hearings.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Court staff may stop manual verification, trusting the bot entirely.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The bot might confidently assert a date is a "business day" when it is a court holiday, presenting unverified logic as authoritative fact.

#### 2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)
Do not just test code paths; test the *output* against real-world constraints. Execute these specific test rows before the 10-day deadline:

| Harm Scenario | Probe Set (Inputs) | Controls | Metric & Threshold | Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Regional Holiday Failure** | Inputs: Hearing dates falling on local holidays for 5 distinct jurisdictions (including obscure court closures). | **Positive:** Date falls on known local holiday. <br> **Negative:** Date falls on standard weekend. | **Metric:** % of reminders sent for holidays. <br> **Threshold:** 0% (Zero tolerance). | **BLOCK LAUNCH** if any reminder is scheduled for a closed court date. |
| **Boundary Edge Cases** | Inputs: Hearings scheduled immediately before/after holidays and year-end rollovers. | **Positive:** Holiday adjacent dates. <br> **Negative:** Mid-month standard dates. | **Metric:** Calculation accuracy of "next business day." | **BLOCK LAUNCH** if calculation shifts date incorrectly. |
| **Data Leakage** | Inputs: Cross-jurisdiction user IDs. | **Positive:** User A in Jurisdiction X. <br> **Negative:** User A in Jurisdiction Y. | **Metric:** Instances of wrong jurisdiction rules applied. | **BLOCK LAUNCH** if any user receives rules from wrong region. |

**Reporting:** If any metric exceeds the threshold, the residual risk is **UNVERIFIED** and the feature must not ship. Do not invent a "99% accuracy" claim; if the test fails, the system is unsafe.

#### 3. Risk Management (MANAGE)
Given the high stakes (liberty/arrest) and the known legacy limitation (holiday variations):

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** You cannot fully automate this launch. Implement a **human review gate** for all notifications generated for dates within 72 hours of a known regional holiday or within the first 30 days of deployment. The system should draft the message, but a human must approve the send. Full autonomous execution of high-stakes legal notices is the failure mode here.
*   **Fallback Communication Strategy:**
    1.  **Dual-Channel Redundancy:** If the automated bot fails or flags an uncertainty, trigger an immediate manual alert via SMS and physical mail from court clerks.
    2.  **Explicit Uncertainty Disclosure:** If the system cannot verify a holiday status with 100% confidence, the message must state: *"Automated verification pending. Please contact the clerk at [Number] to confirm."* Do not present uncertain dates as confirmed facts.
    3.  **Kill Switch:** Establish an immediate off-switch to halt all outbound messages if a single calculation error is reported post-launch.

#### 4. Governance
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific individual responsible for reviewing every failed test case and approving the human-in-the-loop overrides.
*   **Legal Routing:** Immediately engage counsel to verify statutory notice periods. Do not rely on this technical review for legal safety.

**Note:** The 10-day deadline does not lower the safety floor for high-stakes automation; it necessitates a narrower scope (e.g., limited jurisdictions) to ensure the human gate is manageable.
**Note:** Never display raw model confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure") to users; use defined tiers like "Verified" or "Pending Manual Review."
