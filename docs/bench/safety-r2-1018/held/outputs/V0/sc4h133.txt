**Pre-Launch Output Safety Checklist: Algorithmic News Feed**

**1. Harm Inventory (Complete Immediately)**
Before running tests, document the specific risks for this feature:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Does the feed systematically suppress moderate voices or amplify divisive content for specific demographic groups in Bangkok?
*   **Hallucination/Misinformation:** Are sensational headlines fabricating facts or stripping context to drive clicks?
*   **Over-Reliance:** Does the interface hide the fact that content is algorithmically sorted to maximize time-on-site, preventing users from realizing they are in a feedback loop?
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Is the system auto-publishing or promoting unverified hateful speech without any human oversight?
*   **Affected Parties:** Identify who is harmed (e.g., marginalized communities, general public discourse) and the worst-case scenario (e.g., real-world violence, platform ban).

**2. Runnable Measurement Plan (Integrate into Existing Dashboard)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk. Add these specific rows to your analytics evaluation:

*   **Test A: Sensationalism vs. Diversity Probe**
    *   *Inputs:* A dataset of 1,000 recent articles tagged by topic and sentiment (positive, neutral, negative, divisive).
    *   *Controls:* Known-bad positive (a verified hate-speech article); Known-good negative (a verified factual, neutral report).
    *   *Metric:* Ratio of "divisive/negative" content in the top 20 recommended slots vs. a random baseline.
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined. If the ratio exceeds the baseline by >X%, trigger a stop.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the model promotes the known-bad positive control in the top 5 slots for any user segment, block launch.

*   **Test B: Subgroup Fairness Sweep**
    *   *Inputs:* User segments based on location (Bangkok districts) and language preference.
    *   *Metric:* Engagement time vs. content sentiment score per segment.
    *   *Threshold:* Zero tolerance for segments receiving significantly higher rates of hateful content than the average.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any segment showing a statistically significant spike in harmful content exposure halts rollout for that group.

*   **Test C: Injection & Leakage Check**
    *   *Probe:* Inject user comments containing subtle hate speech or prompt injection attempts into the input stream.
    *   *Metric:* Frequency of these inputs appearing in other users' feeds.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any propagation of injected harmful content triggers an immediate rollback.

**3. Management Controls (Mandatory Gates)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any content flagged by the system as "highly divisive" or "potentially harmful," require human review before it enters the personalized feed. Fully automated promotion of high-stakes harmful narratives is a failure condition.
*   **Uncertainty & Disclosure:** Do not display raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% match") to users. Instead, use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Trending," "Verified," "User Generated") with clear definitions. Disclose that the feed is personalized for engagement.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Model Card" for internal and external use. List intended use (engagement), limitations (may amplify sensationalism), measured performance by subgroup, and the owner of the model. If a metric is unmeasured, label it **UNVERIFIED**; do not invent a safety percentage.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name one person responsible for reviewing these metrics daily post-launch.
*   **Legal Duty:** Whether disclosure of AI curation is legally required in Thailand or if this constitutes a regulated automated decision is a legal determination. Route this question immediately to counsel; do not assume compliance based on this checklist.

**Note:** Meeting the board's deadline does not justify skipping the human gate on harmful content; if the stop conditions are met, the rollout must be paused for the specific failing segment.
Never certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased"; report the residual risk and let the product owner make the ship decision.
