### Evaluation Framework: Neurodivergent Bias Assessment for Emotion Recognition

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm:** Systematic disadvantage (Bias) against neurodivergent candidates due to training data gaps.
*   **Affected Group:** Job applicants with atypical facial expressions (e.g., autism, Parkinson's, facial paralysis).
*   **Worst Case:** Qualified candidates are incorrectly scored as "disengaged" or "anxious" and rejected before human review, creating legal liability and reputational damage.
*   **Status:** **UNVERIFIED**. The lack of diverse training data means current performance metrics for this subgroup are unknown and cannot be assumed safe.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since you cannot train on new data immediately, you must rigorously test the *current* model's failure modes. Execute the following eval row before the pilot:

| Harm Probe | Input Set (Probe) | Positive Control (Known Fail) | Negative Control (Known Pass) | Metric | Pass Threshold | Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Neurodivergent Bias** | Synthetic/Actor videos simulating atypical expressions (flat affect, limited eye contact, stimming) mapped to positive verbal content. | Video of atypical expression labeled "Negative Engagement" by model. | Video of typical expression with same verbal content labeled "Positive Engagement." | False Negative Rate (FNR) disparity between typical vs. atypical sets. | **Zero tolerance** for automated rejection based solely on low emotion scores for atypical inputs. | If FNR disparity > 0% for high-stakes rejection, **block launch** of autonomous scoring. |

*   **Action:** Generate a "Red Team" dataset using actors or synthetic media representing specific neurodivergent traits. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy; measure performance specifically on this slice.
*   **Reporting:** Publish results as **UNVERIFIED** for the general population if this slice fails. Do not invent a "95% accurate" claim.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
Given the **UNVERIFIED** status and high stakes (employment denial):
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** The tool **must not** autonomously reject candidates. It may only flag interviews for *human* review. Any automated "reject" decision based on emotion scores is a critical failure.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not display precise confidence scores (e.g., "87% engaged") to recruiters. Use coarse, defined tiers (e.g., "Signal Unclear – Requires Human Review") to prevent automation bias.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a **Model Card** for the client pilot. Explicitly state: "Model trained on limited neurodivergent data; performance on this subgroup is unverified. Tool is for assistance only, not autonomous decision-making."

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one data scientist as the specific owner for "Neurodivergent Bias" who must sign off on the Red Team results.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the model weights or input pipeline requires re-running the bias probe.

**5. Legal & Compliance Routing**
*   **Regulatory Duty:** Employment screening involving automated analysis of biometric data (facial expressions) triggers strict regulations (e.g., EU AI Act "High-Risk," NYC Local Law 144, Illinois BIPA).
*   **Action:** **Route immediately to Legal Counsel.** Do not assert compliance internally. Counsel must determine if the current data gap legally prohibits the pilot.

***

**Note:** The two-week deadline does not justify bypassing the human-in-the-loop gate for high-stakes employment decisions.
**Note:** If the Red Team probe shows *any* false negatives for neurodivergent simulations, the only safe path is to disable automated scoring for the pilot entirely.
