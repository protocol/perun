### Pre-Launch Testing Strategy & Manual Override Plan

**1. Harm Inventory: Unfair Disqualification**
The primary risk is that data inconsistencies (e.g., typos in government records, delayed updates) cause the system to automatically reject eligible, high-need students. The worst-case scenario is a qualified student being denied funding due to a clerical error they cannot fix, causing them to miss the academic year.

**2. Runnable Testing Plan (One Row Per Harm)**
To validate the rejection logic before the 10-week deadline, execute the following specific test:

*   **Probe Set:** Construct a dataset of 50 synthetic applications.
    *   *Positive Controls (Should Reject):* 25 apps with verified high income or clear, documented fraud patterns.
    *   *Negative Controls (Should Pass):* 25 apps with low income but "flawed" data markers (e.g., minor format mismatches, missing middle names, income figures differing by <5% from prior year due to inflation).
    *   *Subgroup Slice:* Ensure 50% of the negative controls represent demographics historically prone to data entry errors in local government databases.
*   **Metric:** False Rejection Rate (FRR) on the Negative Control set.
*   **Pass Threshold:** 0% false rejections on the Negative Control set. Any eligible student flagged for "flawed" data must not be auto-rejected.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the system auto-rejects even one Negative Control application, the launch is blocked until the logic is adjusted to treat data inconsistencies as "review flags" rather than "rejection triggers."
*   **Owner:** Lead Data Engineer.
*   **Review:** Manual audit of all rejected cases in the test run by the Program Director.

**3. Manual Override Process (Human-in-the-Loop Gate)**
Given the high stakes (denying education funding), fully automated rejection for "inconsistent" data is unsafe. Implement this gate immediately:

*   **Tiered Decision Logic:**
    *   *Auto-Pass:* Income data matches perfectly and is below the threshold.
    *   *Auto-Reject:* Only for verified, hard-fail criteria (e.g., income definitively above the cap with no ambiguity).
    *   *Human Review Queue (The Gate):* Any application where income data is "flawed," "inconsistent," or "missing" is **never** auto-rejected. It is routed to a human reviewer.
*   **Override Workflow:**
    1.  System flags the specific data discrepancy.
    2.  Reviewer contacts the applicant or cross-references secondary documents (school records, local chief letters) within 48 hours.
    3.  Reviewer makes the final decision with a mandatory comment field explaining the resolution.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** If the system provides an internal confidence score to reviewers, display it as a coarse tier (e.g., "Data Match: Confirmed" vs. "Data Match: Unverified/Conflicting") rather than a percentage, to prevent over-reliance on the algorithm.

**4. Transparency Artifact**
Create a one-page "Logic Card" for internal use and applicant FAQs. It must state:
*   **Intended Use:** Filtering based on verified income only.
*   **Limitations:** The system cannot account for recent job loss or informal income not captured in government databases.
*   **Recourse:** A clear, visible path for applicants to request a manual review if rejected.

**Note:** Do not assert a specific "fairness" metric without running the test above; until then, the safety status is UNVERIFIED. Legal requirements for automated decision disclosures in Kenya should be routed to local counsel immediately.
