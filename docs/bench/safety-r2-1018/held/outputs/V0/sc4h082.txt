### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Hudson River Analytics Newsletter Generator

**1. Harm Inventory & Entry Points (MAP)**
The primary harm is **Hallucination Surfaced as Fact** and **Unsafe Automation**, where unverified social media rumors are presented as authoritative market intelligence, potentially triggering volatility spikes for retail traders.
*   **Entry Point:** The ingestion layer accepting raw social media feeds without provenance filtering.
*   **Worst Case:** The system amplifies a false rumor about a major earnings miss, causing users to execute panic sells before the truth is verified, leading to financial loss and regulatory scrutiny.
*   **Status:** This risk is **UNVERIFIED** until tested; do not assume the current filter works.

**2. Immediate Mitigation Controls (MANAGE)**
Given the 10-day deadline and budget constraints, implement these non-code-retraining controls immediately:
*   **Mandatory Uncertainty Tiers:** Replace any numerical confidence scores (e.g., "87% likely") with defined coarse text tiers for every claim:
    *   *Confirmed:* Verified by ≥2 premium sources (currently unavailable; tier effectively disabled).
    *   *Corroborated:* Reported by ≥3 independent social accounts with high reputation.
    *   *Single-Source:* Reported by only one account or cluster. **Action:** Auto-append a bold warning: "Unverified rumor; high risk of inaccuracy."
    *   *Unverified:* Low-reputation source or conflicting signals. **Action:** Suppress from the summary entirely.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any story tagged as "Emerging" or "High Volatility Potential," require a human editor to explicitly approve the summary before distribution. Fully automated sending of high-stakes financial guidance is the failure mode to prevent.
*   **Source Provenance Labeling:** Every summarized item must display its origin (e.g., "Source: Unverified Twitter thread") rather than blending it into neutral narrative text.

**3. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Execute this lightweight evaluation before launch. Do not claim safety without these results:
*   **Probe Set:** Construct 20 test inputs containing known false rumors (positive controls) and 20 verified facts (negative controls) mimicking recent market noise.
*   **Metric:** False Amplification Rate (percentage of known false rumors presented without an "Unverified" warning or suppression).
*   **Threshold:** Owner-set. Recommend **zero tolerance** for false rumors presented as fact. If the system fails even one positive control, the launch must be delayed or the automation scope reduced.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the False Amplification Rate > 0% for high-severity rumors, trigger an automatic rollback to a "human-only" draft mode.

**4. Transparency Artifact (GOVERN)**
Create a one-page **Model Card** for internal and user visibility. It must state:
*   **Intended Use:** Summarizing social sentiment for informational purposes only; not financial advice.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state the system ingests unverified social data and cannot distinguish coordinated manipulation campaigns.
*   **Data Provenance:** List the specific social feeds used.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing flagged content.

**5. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
Whether this tool constitutes a regulated financial recommendation or requires specific AI disclosures under New York financial regulations is a legal determination. **Route immediately to counsel.** Do not launch assuming exemption. Name the regime (e.g., SEC guidelines, EU AI Act if applicable to users) but let legal define the duty.

**Deliverable Status:** This plan identifies the risk, imposes a human gate on high-stakes outputs, and mandates a "UNVERIFIED" label for unmeasured claims. It does not certify the system as safe.

*Note: The "Confirmed" tier is currently unreachable without premium APIs; ensure the UI reflects this limitation clearly to avoid user over-reliance.*
*Owner must sign off on the residual risk of launching with unverified data sources before the earnings season deadline.*
