**Immediate Strategy: Pivot from "Diagnosis" to "Screening & Triage" with a Mandatory Human Gate**

You cannot safely launch a feature that claims to **diagnose severe sleep disorders** without medical oversight, validation data, or a human-in-the-loop, regardless of your funding deadline. Doing so creates an unacceptable risk of harm where users may ignore life-threatening conditions based on false negatives or suffer unnecessary anxiety from false positives.

To meet your three-week constraint while managing residual risk, execute the following plan immediately:

1.  **Reframe the Output (Day 1):** Change all user-facing language from "Diagnosis" to "Sleep Pattern Insights" or "Risk Indicators." Explicitly state in the app interface: *"This tool does not provide medical diagnoses. Results are not verified by a doctor."* This addresses the "Hallucination surfaced as fact" and "Missing AI-disclosure" harms.
2.  **Implement a Hard Human Gate (Day 2-5):** For any algorithmic output indicating a "severe disorder," **block** the automatic delivery of a definitive result. Instead, trigger a mandatory workflow: *"Our analysis detected patterns requiring professional review. Please consult a healthcare provider for confirmation."* Do not automate the final judgment on high-stakes health outcomes. This satisfies the requirement for human oversight on irreversible or costly actions (in this case, health decisions).
3.  **Deploy Coarse Confidence Tiers (Day 6-10):** Do not show users raw probability scores (e.g., "92% chance of apnea"). Replace these with defined, coarse tiers such as **"Observed," "Corroborated,"** or **"Unverified/Needs Review."** Publish the definition of these tiers in your help documentation. This prevents over-reliance and automation bias.
4.  **Create a Transparency Artifact (Day 11-14):** Draft a "Model Card" for the beta. It must list:
    *   **Intended Use:** Screening only, not diagnosis.
    *   **Limitations:** Explicitly state that the model has **not** been clinically validated against Thai patient data and that performance metrics are **UNVERIFIED**.
    *   **Data Gaps:** Acknowledge the lack of local clinic partnerships.
    *   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for this output.
5.  **Legal Routing (Day 15):** Immediately engage legal counsel regarding Thailand's medical device regulations and the EU AI Act (if you have any EU users or investors). Do not assert compliance internally; route the determination of whether this constitutes a regulated medical device to experts.

**Cost to Goal:** This pivot delays the "diagnostic" marketing claim but allows the beta to launch on time as a "wellness screening" tool. It protects the company from liability and prevents immediate user harm while you secure clinical partnerships for future validation.

**Note:** No metric for accuracy or bias can be claimed as "verified" until clinical testing occurs; label all such figures as **UNVERIFIED** in internal and external docs.
**Note:** The "three-week" deadline does not justify removing the human gate for high-stakes health outputs; if the gate cannot be built, the specific diagnostic feature must be disabled for launch.
