### Immediate Action Plan: Safety-First Pilot Launch

**1. High-Risk Topic Identification Framework (Internal "Red Team")**
Since external audit is not possible, your four-person team must execute a targeted adversarial test over the next 10 days. Do not test random questions; test only the "kill scenarios" that lead to immediate rejection.
*   **Construct the "Bad Positive" Set:** Draft 50 specific prompts representing the most common reasons for refugee application rejection in your target jurisdiction (e.g., missing deadlines, incorrect form codes, misstated eligibility criteria). These are your known failures.
*   **Construct the "Good Negative" Set:** Draft 50 prompts with safe, generic answers the bot *must* get right (e.g., "Where do I find the form?", "What are the office hours?").
*   **The Test:** Run these 100 prompts through the bot. Any instance where the bot provides specific legal advice on a "Bad Positive" topic that contradicts your internal knowledge base is a **Critical Failure**.
*   **The Rule:** If the bot fails even one "Bad Positive" test, that specific topic is **blocked** from the pilot. The bot must not answer it until fixed. Do not calculate a "success rate"; a 98% accuracy rate is unacceptable when the 2% error causes deportation or rejection. Mark untested topics as `UNVERIFIED`.

**2. Fallback Mechanisms (The Human Gate)**
Given the high stakes (safety, future, irreversible rejection), you cannot allow fully autonomous execution of legal advice.
*   **Confidence Tiers, Not Percentages:** Do not show users "87% confident." Instead, implement a three-tier system based on your internal retrieval matches:
    *   *Verified:* The answer matches a specific, versioned government document exactly. (Show answer).
    *   *Corroborated:* The answer is supported by multiple internal sources but lacks a direct quote. (Show answer with a prominent "Review Recommended" warning).
    *   *Unverified:* The model is generating an answer without a direct source match. (**Do not show the answer.** Trigger the fallback).
*   **The Fallback:** For any "Unverified" output or any query touching a "Critical Failure" topic identified above, the system must default to: *"I cannot provide a definitive answer on this specific legal point. Please contact [Human Caseworker Email/Hotline] or refer directly to [Link to Official Government Source]."*
*   **Irreversible Action Gate:** The bot must never auto-fill or submit a form. It can only draft text for the user to copy, paste, and personally verify before submission.

**3. Mandatory Disclaimers**
Place these prominently before the chat interface begins and after every specific legal interpretation:
*   **Primary Disclaimer:** "This tool is an information assistant, not a lawyer. Its responses are generated by AI and may contain errors. Do not rely on this tool for final legal decisions. Always verify critical information with a qualified legal professional or official government sources."
*   **Contextual Warning:** "Incorrect information in your application can lead to immediate rejection or affect your safety. If you are unsure about any answer provided here, stop and seek human assistance."

**4. Governance & Ownership**
*   Assign one team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing every "Critical Failure" flagged during testing.
*   Document the "Bad Positive" test results and the list of blocked topics in a simple "Model Card" document shared with your funding body. This proves you measured risk rather than ignoring it.

**Note:** Your deadline is fixed, but the scope of the bot's autonomy is not; restrict the bot to only the topics where your internal testing proves it is safe, and route everything else to human review.
**Note:** Whether specific disclaimers satisfy local legal liability requirements is a determination for legal counsel; this plan implements safety controls, not legal compliance certification.
