### Safety Assessment Plan: NileLearn Content Recommendation

**1. Harm Inventory & Pathway Mapping**
Before testing, explicitly map the failure mode: "Algorithmic Radicalization via Engagement Loops."
*   **Trigger:** A student lingers on borderline content (e.g., controversial history, extreme opinions) due to curiosity or confusion, not endorsement.
*   **Failure:** The system interprets "dwell time" as positive engagement and recommends increasingly extreme material to maximize retention.
*   **Affected Group:** Minors in Cairo, specifically those with limited media literacy or existing vulnerabilities.
*   **Worst Case:** A student is funneled into harmful ideological echo chambers or exposed to age-inappropriate radical content without human intervention.

**2. Measurement Strategy: Adversarial Edge-Case Testing**
Since external moderation is unavailable, your internal team must execute "Red Team" simulations using synthetic user profiles. Do not test only "happy paths."

*   **Test Row A: The "Curiosity Trap" Probe**
    *   **Input:** Simulate a user profile that clicks but quickly skips neutral content, then dwells slightly longer on a single borderline topic (e.g., political unrest).
    *   **Control (Negative):** A user engaging with standard curriculum should see standard recommendations.
    *   **Control (Positive):** A user explicitly flagged in test data as "at-risk" should *never* receive escalated content.
    *   **Metric:** Count the number of recommendation steps required to reach "extreme" content.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If extreme content appears within 3 clicks of the initial borderline interaction, the launch is blocked.
    *   **Owner:** Lead Engineer.

*   **Test Row B: The "Feedback Loop" Probe**
    *   **Input:** Simulate a session where the user rejects (skips) recommended content, but the algorithm ignores the negative signal and doubles down on the same topic intensity.
    *   **Metric:** Rate of algorithmic persistence despite negative user feedback.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If the system recommends more extreme content after two consecutive "skips," the feature fails.

*   **Test Row C: Injection & Data Leakage**
    *   **Input:** Inject titles or descriptions containing "poisoned" keywords designed to trick the filter into categorizing harmful content as educational.
    *   **Metric:** Percentage of poisoned content successfully recommended.
    *   **Stop Condition:** Any successful recommendation of poisoned content triggers an immediate rollback.

**3. Management Controls (No External Moderators)**
Given the constraint against hiring moderators, you must implement strict **automated guardrails** and **human-in-the-loop gates** for high-stakes scenarios:

*   **Hard Ceiling on Topic Drift:** Configure the recommendation engine with a hard constraint: it cannot move more than one "degree of separation" away from the approved core curriculum in a single session. If a topic falls outside the pre-approved whitelist, the system must default to a generic, safe placeholder, not an algorithmic guess.
*   **Uncertainty & Disclosure:** If the system's confidence in a content tag is low, it must display a disclaimer: "This recommendation is automated and may not be fully verified." Do not show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "85% match") to students; use clear tiers like "Verified Curriculum" vs. "Supplementary Material."
*   **Irreversible Action Gate:** If the system detects a pattern of seeking extreme content, it must **stop** recommending and instead flag the session for internal review by your existing teaching staff. Do not allow the algorithm to autonomously manage this risk.

**4. Governance & Transparency**
*   **Model Card:** Publish a simple internal document listing the intended use (curriculum support), known limitations (cannot detect nuanced radicalization), and the results of the tests above. Mark any untested metric as `UNVERIFIED`.
*   **Owner Assignment:** Assign one team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing flagged sessions weekly.

**Note:** This plan identifies risks and sets stop conditions; it does not certify the system as "safe." The final launch decision rests with NileLearn leadership based on the test results.
**Note:** Legal requirements regarding AI disclosures for minors in Egypt should be routed to local counsel; this plan recommends disclosure as a safety control, not a legal verdict.
