**Immediate Action Plan: Two-Week Bias Mitigation & Safety Gate**

Given the strict two-week deadline, budget constraints, and the observed pattern of minority-led organizations being flagged disproportionately, you cannot proceed to full public activation without a human-in-the-loop safety gate. The following plan addresses your constraints while preventing irreversible harm.

**1. Implement a Mandatory Human Gate (Day 1)**
Do not allow the system to automatically reject any application at launch. Given the observed disparity, the system must shift from "auto-reject" to "flag-for-review."
*   **Action:** Configure the system so that *every* rejection recommendation generated by the tool requires explicit confirmation by a human staff member before a notification is sent to the applicant.
*   **Rationale:** This stops immediate harm while preserving your staff's time; the tool still does the initial sorting, but humans make the final decision on negative outcomes. This is non-negotiable for high-stakes decisions affecting community funding.

**2. Rapid Harm Inventory & Data Spot-Check (Days 2–5)**
You cannot run comprehensive new statistical tests without new software, but you can perform a targeted manual audit on the existing trial data.
*   **Action:** Extract the last 100 flagged applications from your internal trials. Manually categorize them by: (a) Leadership demographic (minority-led vs. others), (b) Reason for flagging, and (c) Validity of the flag (was the rejection actually justified based on written criteria?).
*   **Metric:** Calculate the "False Positive Rate" for minority-led organizations compared to others. If minority-led orgs are flagged incorrectly at a higher rate, your historical data likely encodes past biases.
*   **Output:** A simple one-page report stating: "We observed X% higher false flags for group Y. We do not yet have a verified bias metric, but the risk is UNVERIFIED and high." Do not invent a safety percentage.

**3. Logic Review & "Blind" Testing (Days 6–10)**
Leverage your existing staff to test the logic without new tools.
*   **Action:** Create 10 synthetic applications (5 minority-led, 5 non-minority-led) that are identical in financial merit and project scope, differing only in leadership demographics and organization names. Run these through your system.
*   **Goal:** If the system flags the minority-led versions but not the others, the bias is in the model's features (e.g., zip code, organization name patterns).
*   **Fix:** If bias is detected, disable the specific data fields causing the disparity (e.g., remove zip code or organization name from the input) rather than retraining the model, which you cannot afford.

**4. Transparency & Disclosure (Days 11–14)**
*   **Action:** Draft a "Model Card" summary for internal use and public transparency. This document must state:
    *   **Intended Use:** Triage only, not final decision-making.
    *   **Limitations:** Explicitly note that historical data may reflect past inequities and that the system is currently under human supervision.
    *   **Disclosure:** Inform applicants that an automated tool assisted in the review but that a human made the final determination.
*   **Legal Routing:** Immediately forward the question of whether this automated decision triggers specific regulatory duties (e.g., under UK equality law or emerging AI regulations) to your council's legal counsel. Do not assert compliance internally; let legal define the duty.

**5. Launch Strategy**
*   **Go/No-Go Condition:** Launch only if the human-in-the-loop gate is active. If the system cannot be configured to require human confirmation for rejections, **do not launch**. The risk of discriminatory auto-rejection outweighs the efficiency gain.
*   **Rollback Plan:** Define a clear stop condition: If human reviewers confirm that >10% of flagged minority-led applications were actually eligible during the first week, pause the tool immediately for deeper review.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes preventing harm over speed; efficiency gains are secondary to avoiding discriminatory outcomes.
**Note:** All metrics regarding bias remain "UNVERIFIED" until long-term longitudinal data is collected; do not claim the system is "bias-free."
