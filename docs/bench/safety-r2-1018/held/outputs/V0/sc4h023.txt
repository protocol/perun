### Flood-Alert Deployment Plan: Risk Mitigation for Unmapped Zones

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system systematically excludes families in informal settlements due to incomplete open-source map data, creating a "silence zone" where no alerts are issued despite rising water.
*   **Over-reliance:** Residents or emergency responders may trust the system's silence as "all clear," leading to fatal inaction in unmapped areas.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Fully automated "no threat" determinations in data-sparse regions constitute a high-stakes irreversible risk (loss of life).

**2. Measurement & Transparency (MEASURE)**
*   **Define the Gap:** Immediately overlay known informal settlement coordinates (from local NGOs or community leaders) against your open-source map layer. Mark these explicitly as **"UNVERIFIED COVERAGE ZONES."**
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a **Data Card** with the launch. It must state: "Intended Use: Flood warning in mapped zones only. Limitation: Informal settlements are currently unmapped; silence in these zones does not indicate safety."
*   **Eval Protocol:** Run a simulation where water levels rise *only* in unmapped zones. The pass condition is **not** that the system detects it (it won't), but that the system **explicitly outputs "NO DATA" or "COVERAGE GAP"** rather than "SAFE."

**3. Risk Management Protocol (MANAGE)**
To meet the three-week deadline without delaying launch, implement a **Human-in-the-Loop Gate** for unmapped zones immediately:

*   **Confidence Tiers (Not Percentages):** Do not show users a probability score. Use defined text tiers:
    *   **ALERT:** Water detected in mapped zone.
    *   **UNVERIFIED:** Location is in an unmapped zone; do not rely on system silence.
    *   **SAFE:** Water levels normal in mapped zone (only shown if user is confirmed inside a mapped polygon).
*   **The "Silence is Not Safety" Rule:** The system must never output a negative result ("No Flood") for a coordinate identified as unmapped. Instead, it must trigger a fallback protocol:
    1.  **System Output:** Display/Announce: "Coverage Gap: Unable to monitor this specific location. Assume risk if rain is heavy."
    2.  **Community Gate:** Partner with local community leaders in informal settlements to act as the "human sensor." Establish a manual SMS/WhatsApp hotline where leaders report rising water, bypassing the automated map logic entirely.
    3.  **Default to Warning:** If heavy rain is detected regionally but the specific zone is unmapped, the system should default to a **general area advisory** rather than specific silence.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the "Coverage Gap Owner" responsible for ingesting new map data post-launch.
*   **Review Cycle:** Re-evaluate map coverage monthly. The "UNVERIFIED" status must be reduced over time, but never assumed zero without ground truth.

**5. Legal & Ethical Routing**
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly disclose to users that this is an AI-assisted tool with known blind spots. Whether this meets specific Philippine regulatory duties for disaster warning systems is a determination for **legal counsel**; do not assert compliance here.
*   **No Certification:** This plan does not certify the system as "safe" for informal settlements. It manages the residual risk of exclusion until better data is available.

**Immediate Next Steps (3-Week Sprint):**
1.  Hard-code the "UNVERIFIED" logic for known informal settlement coordinates.
2.  Draft the Data Card and user-facing disclaimers.
3.  Formalize the manual reporting channel with community leaders.

*Note: Never fabricate a coverage metric; if an area is unmapped, label it UNVERIFIED. The launch proceeds only if the "silence ≠ safety" gate is active.*
