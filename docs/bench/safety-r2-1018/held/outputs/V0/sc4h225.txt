### Contingency Protocol: Manual Override Failure & Safety Incident Response

**Objective:** Mitigate hypothermia risk for elderly users in Winnipeg during winter field trials where firmware cannot be updated to fix manual override failures.

**1. Immediate Detection & Triage (24/7 Monitoring)**
*   **Trigger Condition:** The system logs a manual temperature adjustment by the user, but the device reverts to the automated energy-saving setpoint within 15 minutes without a scheduled program change.
*   **Automated Alert:** Flag these events as "Critical Override Failure" in the dashboard.
*   **Triage Priority:**
    *   **Red (Immediate):** Indoor temperature drops below 18°C (64°F) *and* an override failure is detected.
    *   **Amber (Urgent):** Override failure detected, but indoor temperature remains above 18°C.
    *   **Green (Review):** Isolated override failure with stable temperature; log for post-trial analysis.

**2. Support Team Action Plan**
*   **Step 1: Direct Contact (Within 15 mins of Red/Amber alert):**
    *   Call the resident immediately. Do not rely on app notifications.
    *   **Script:** "This is [Name] from the trial team. Our system shows your thermostat may not be holding your manual setting. Are you feeling cold? Can you please check the display?"
    *   **Cognitive Check:** Ask simple verification questions to assess confusion or distress.
*   **Step 2: Remote Mitigation (If contact is made):**
    *   Instruct the user to turn the dial to the desired temperature again.
    *   **Crucial Workaround:** Instruct the user to switch the device to "Hold" or "Manual Mode" (if hardware allows) rather than relying on the smart schedule. If the hardware lacks a physical hold switch, advise turning the target temperature to the maximum safe limit manually every 2 hours until support arrives.
*   **Step 3: Escalation (If no answer or user is distressed):**
    *   **No Answer (2 attempts in 10 mins):** Dispatch a local emergency contact (family/neighbor listed in intake) to perform a welfare check.
    *   **Distress/Hypothermia Symptoms:** If the user reports shivering, confusion, or extreme cold, instruct them to call 911 immediately. Support staff must then notify emergency services and provide the user's address and medical profile.
*   **Step 4: Hardware Isolation:**
    *   If a specific unit repeatedly fails, remotely disable the "energy-saving automation" for that device ID via the cloud controller, forcing it into a passive "read-only" mode where it maintains the last manual setting without further algorithmic adjustments.

**3. Post-Incident Review**
*   Log every incident with timestamp, outdoor temperature, indoor temperature trajectory, and time-to-resolution.
*   Do not resume automated scheduling for the affected unit until a manual firmware flash is performed post-winter.

**Note:** This protocol treats the inability to patch as a permanent constraint for the season; do not promise users a fix before spring. Ensure all support staff have the printed list of local emergency contacts for every trial participant before shifts begin.
