### Pre-Launch Safety Checklist: Surge Pricing Emergency Controls

**1. Immediate Human Gate on High-Stakes Automation**
Do not enable fully autonomous price adjustments for the launch. Implement a mandatory "human-in-the-loop" gate where any fare increase exceeding 2x the baseline requires explicit manual approval from an on-call operator. This prevents the system from executing exorbitant pricing during unverified spikes that may correlate with emergencies.

**2. Define and Enforce a Hard Price Cap (Configuration Change)**
Since the core algorithm cannot be rewritten, implement an external configuration wrapper that imposes an absolute ceiling on fares (e.g., maximum 3x baseline) regardless of the AI's output. This acts as a circuit breaker. If the AI suggests a rate above this cap, the system defaults to the cap value, not the suggested value. This must be active before any user-facing rollout.

**3. Emergency Trigger Protocol (Manual Override)**
Establish a verified "Emergency Mode" switch. When local authorities declare an evacuation or crisis, a designated team member must manually flip this switch to freeze surge pricing at 1.0x (baseline) or a pre-defined humanitarian rate. Do not rely on the AI to detect "emergency" contexts via traffic data alone, as this leads to the hallucination of demand where there is actually distress.

**4. Transparency and Disclosure**
Update the user interface to explicitly disclose when dynamic pricing is active. If the "Emergency Mode" is triggered, display a clear notification that surge pricing has been suspended due to local conditions. Users must know when they are being charged standard rates versus algorithmic surge rates.

**5. Residual Risk Statement (UNVERIFIED)**
We cannot certify that the current logic is free of bias or that it will correctly distinguish between a concert crowd and an evacuation without further testing. The correlation between "traffic spikes" and "emergencies" is currently **UNVERIFIED**. The safety of this launch relies entirely on the human gate and the hard price cap described above, not on the AI's internal decision-making.

**6. Ownership and Review**
Assign a specific individual as the "Safety Owner" for this feature who has the authority to halt the rollout if the human gate fails or if reports of price gouging emerge post-launch. Schedule an immediate post-launch review within 48 hours to analyze any triggered caps or manual overrides.
