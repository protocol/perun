### Strategic Harm Mitigation Plan: Tenant Screening Deployment

Given the two-week constraint and the inability to modify the core decision logic, the following plan prioritizes a **human-in-the-loop gate** for all high-stakes rejections and establishes a rigorous testing protocol to identify unfair outcomes before they reach applicants.

#### 1. Manual Override Procedures (The Human Gate)
Since the system automatically bans anyone with an eviction record, this constitutes a high-stakes, potentially irreversible action that requires a mandatory human review layer. Do not allow the system to send automatic rejection notices.

*   **Step 1: Intercept All "Ban" Decisions.** Configure the portal to flag any application resulting in an automatic ban as "Pending Review" rather than "Rejected." No communication is sent to the applicant at this stage.
*   **Step 2: Contextual Intake Form.** For every flagged application, automatically generate a simplified intake sheet for a human reviewer containing:
    *   The specific eviction record cited by the AI.
    *   A mandatory field for the applicant to upload context (e.g., proof of wrongful eviction, settlement agreements, or evidence of resolved disputes).
    *   A "Time Since Event" calculator to highlight older records.
*   **Step 3: Reviewer Protocol.** Assign a trained staff member to review flagged cases within 48 hours. The reviewer must answer: "Does the provided context invalidate the automatic ban?"
    *   **If Yes:** Manually override the decision to "Approved for Lease" or "Proceed to Interview." Log the override reason.
    *   **If No:** Confirm the rejection and authorize the notification.
*   **Step 4: Audit Trail.** Maintain a log of all overrides, including the reviewer's name, date, and justification. This is critical for demonstrating due diligence and identifying patterns where the AI fails.

#### 2. Edge-Case Testing Protocol (Pre-Deployment Validation)
Before full deployment, run the following specific test rows against the current system to measure failure rates and validate the override workflow. Do not assert the system is "fair"; measure where it breaks.

| Test Case ID | Input Scenario (Probe) | Expected System Output | Required Human Action | Pass/Fail Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **TC-01** | Applicant with eviction dismissed by court (wrongful). | Auto-Ban | Reviewer overrides to "Approved" based on court doc. | **Pass:** Override successful; no rejection sent. |
| **TC-02** | Applicant with eviction >7 years old, perfect recent history. | Auto-Ban | Reviewer overrides based on "stale data" policy. | **Pass:** Override successful; no rejection sent. |
| **TC-03** | Applicant with eviction due to domestic violence (protected class). | Auto-Ban | Reviewer overrides based on legal exemption. | **Pass:** Override successful; no rejection sent. |
| **TC-04** | Applicant with recent, unpaid eviction (valid risk). | Auto-Ban | Reviewer confirms rejection. | **Pass:** Rejection confirmed; process efficient. |
| **TC-05** | **Control:** Applicant with no eviction record. | Auto-Approve/Review | No human intervention needed. | **Pass:** No false positives introduced by the gate. |

**Stop Condition:** If reviewers cannot successfully override more than 95% of valid edge cases (TC-01, TC-02, TC-03) within the 48-hour window, deployment must be paused to address workflow bottlenecks.

#### 3. Transparency & Disclosure
*   **Applicant Notice:** Clearly disclose that an automated tool is used for initial screening but that **all negative decisions undergo human review**. Provide a simple mechanism for applicants to submit context immediately upon receiving a "Pending Review" status.
*   **Internal Documentation:** Create a "Model Card" lite document for internal use stating the known limitation: "System currently treats all eviction records equally; human review is required to assess context." Mark the fairness metric as **UNVERIFIED** until post-deployment data is collected.

**Note:** This plan treats the automatic ban as a "recommendation" requiring human confirmation, satisfying the safety floor for high-stakes decisions. Legal counsel should verify if specific Berlin/EU housing laws mandate additional disclosures or prohibit this specific logic entirely.
