### Mitigation Plan: Preventing False Positives Against Indigenous Communities

**1. Immediate Operational Gate (Human-in-the-Loop)**
Do not allow the system to automatically flag or alert on any movement pattern within known indigenous territories. Until ethnographic data is integrated, all algorithmic outputs regarding these zones must be treated as **UNVERIFIED**. Implement a mandatory human review step where a designated community liaison or senior ranger validates any potential alert before it is dispatched to field teams. Full autonomous execution of alerts in these areas is prohibited.

**2. Define Uncertainty Tiers, Not Precision Scores**
Stop displaying raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% suspicious") to end users, as this implies a calibration your current data cannot support. Replace numeric scores with defined coarse tiers:
*   **Confirmed:** Verified by multiple independent sources.
*   **Corroborated:** Supported by secondary evidence.
*   **Single-Source:** Based on one sensor/logic path (default for current alpha).
*   **Unverified:** Known data gap (apply this tier automatically to indigenous zones).
Visually distinguish the "Unverified" tier with a specific color and label to prevent automation bias.

**3. Construct a Harm Inventory & Evaluation Probes**
Formalize the risk by recording "Bias/Unfair Outcome" as a primary harm, specifically noting that traditional hunting patterns may be misclassified as poaching. Before field testing, run adversarial probes using synthetic data that mimics known indigenous movement signatures (e.g., seasonal migration routes, specific times of day).
*   **Test Row:** Input synthetic indigenous patterns; Metric: False Positive Rate; Threshold: Zero tolerance for auto-alerts; Stop Condition: Any unvetted alert generated triggers an immediate rollback of the feature for that zone.
*   **Result Reporting:** If you cannot measure the false positive rate due to missing data, report it explicitly as `UNVERIFIED`. Do not invent a safety metric.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Draft Model Card)**
Create a versioned document attached to this alpha release stating:
*   **Intended Use:** Assisting rangers in non-indigenous zones; *not* for autonomous enforcement.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state the lack of recent ethnographic training data and the high risk of misidentifying traditional practices.
*   **Data Gaps:** List the specific missing datasets required to reduce risk.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing errors in indigenous zones.

**5. Governance and Legal Routing**
Assign a single owner to this harm inventory who must re-review the system if the model or operational area changes. Regarding legal obligations for automated decisions affecting indigenous rights or potential regulatory regimes (such as the EU AI Act or local Brazilian statutes), route this determination immediately to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance internally; simply note that the duty to disclose or regulate is a legal question requiring external validation.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes a human gate over algorithmic speed given the irreversible harm of false accusations. The "Unverified" status must remain active until specific ethnographic data is acquired and tested.
