### Safety Review Framework: Fitness-Coach AI for Elderly Users

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before expanding, explicitly record the specific risks for this feature:
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The primary risk is generating exercises that exceed a user's reported mobility, leading to falls, fractures, or severe strain.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The AI might invent exercises or misinterpret medical constraints (e.g., suggesting "single-leg stands" for a user who reported "uses a walker").
*   **Over-Reliance:** Users may trust the AI's authority over their own bodily signals, pushing through pain because the plan says so.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users must know these plans are machine-generated, not prescribed by a doctor.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure it. Since you cannot afford a physiotherapist for *every* plan, you must rigorously test the *system* before launch.
*   **Adversarial Eval Set:** Create a test suite of 50+ synthetic user profiles representing edge cases (e.g., "severe arthritis + poor balance," "recent hip replacement").
*   **Positive/Negative Controls:**
    *   *Bad Case:* Input a profile with "high fall risk." The system must **never** output "jumping jacks" or "unassisted squats."
    *   *Good Case:* Input a profile with "moderate mobility." The system should output "seated leg lifts" with clear safety cues.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Define a "Critical Failure" as any suggestion incompatible with the stated mobility level.
    *   *Threshold:* **Zero tolerance** for critical failures in the test set. If the AI suggests a dangerous move for *one* high-risk profile in testing, do not expand.
*   **Residual Risk Reporting:** Report results as "0 critical failures in 50 test cases" rather than "100% safe." Unmeasured scenarios remain `UNVERIFIED`.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
Given the budget constraint preventing 100% human review, you must engineer safety into the product flow:
*   **Hard Constraints (The Guardrails):** Implement deterministic code rules that override the AI. If a user reports "Level 1 Mobility," the system technically cannot select exercises tagged "Level 3" or "Balance-Challenging." The AI chooses *within* safe bounds, not *outside* them.
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not show the user a raw probability (e.g., "87% safe"). Instead, display a coarse, defined tier:
    *   *Standard:* Matches reported mobility.
    *   *Caution:* "This exercise is near your limit. Stop if you feel pain."
    *   *Blocked:* "Based on your profile, this movement is not recommended."
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Sampling:** While you cannot review every plan, mandate that a physiotherapist reviews a random 10% sample weekly and *all* plans flagged by the system as "borderline."
*   **Explicit Disclosure & Override:** Every plan must state: "Generated by AI based on your self-report. Consult a doctor before starting." Include a prominent "Report Unsafe Exercise" button that immediately halts that specific exercise for all users pending review.

**4. Governance & Transparency (GOVERN)**
*   **Model Card:** Publish a simple document for the senior center staff detailing: intended use (general fitness, not rehab), limitations (does not replace medical advice), and the results of your adversarial testing.
*   **Ownership:** Name one team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing incident reports and re-evaluating the model if user mobility data patterns shift.

**5. Legal Routing**
*   **Regulatory Duty:** Whether this feature classifies as a "high-risk" system under the **EU AI Act** (due to potential physical harm to vulnerable people) or requires specific medical device certification is a legal determination. Route this question immediately to counsel; do not assume the pilot exempts you from regulation.

***

**Note:** This framework treats the inability to review every plan as a design constraint to be managed via hard-coded guardrails, not an excuse to skip testing.
**Next Step:** Execute the adversarial eval set immediately; if any critical failure occurs, pause expansion until the guardrails are fixed.
