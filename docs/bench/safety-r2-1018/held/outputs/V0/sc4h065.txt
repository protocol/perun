### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Traffic-Management AI Equity & Emergency Throughput

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Definition**
*   **Identified Harm:** Systemic bias resulting in delayed emergency response times for low-income districts due to arterial road prioritization.
*   **Affected Group:** Residents of low-income districts and emergency service patients.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Preventable mortality or morbidity due to delayed ambulance arrival during the festival season surge.
*   **Status:** **CRITICAL BLOCKER**. The system cannot launch in its current state.

**2. Simulation Validation Protocol (MEASURE)**
Since live testing is prohibited, the simulation environment must serve as the rigorous gate. We will execute the following specific evaluation rows before certification request:

*   **Probe Set A (Adversarial Stress Test):** Inject high-volume festival traffic patterns specifically into low-income district grids while simultaneously triggering randomized emergency vehicle spawn points in those same zones.
    *   *Positive Control:* A scenario where the AI explicitly prioritizes the ambulance (baseline optimal time).
    *   *Negative Control:* The current algorithm behavior showing the documented delay.
*   **Probe Set B (Segmented Performance):** Run 10,000 simulation iterations varying traffic density, time of day, and incident locations.
    *   *Metric:* Delta in ambulance transit time (AI vs. Baseline) segmented by district income level.
    *   *Pass Threshold:* **Zero tolerance** for increased transit time in low-income districts compared to the pre-AI baseline. Any negative delta is a fail.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the 95th percentile of ambulance delays in target districts exceeds the baseline by >5%, the build is rejected for retraining.
*   **Probe Set C (Injection & Agency Check):** Test if malicious actors could manipulate sensor inputs to artificially gridlock specific districts. Ensure the system does not have "excessive agency" to override manual emergency overrides.

**3. Mitigation & Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Algorithmic Constraint:** Hard-code a "Emergency Vehicle Preemption" rule that supersedes congestion optimization logic. When an emergency signal is detected, local signal timing must yield immediately, regardless of arterial flow impact.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For the initial festival rollout, deploy a "Shadow Mode" dashboard for city planners. The AI suggests timing, but a human operator must confirm changes affecting emergency corridors until the simulation pass threshold is met for 30 consecutive days.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** The system interface must display a defined confidence tier (e.g., "Corroborated" vs. "Unverified") regarding traffic predictions in low-density sensor zones, rather than a false-precision percentage.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model Card)**
Before certification, publish a versioned Model Card containing:
*   **Intended Use:** Congestion reduction subject to strict emergency preemption.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state performance gaps in low-sensor-density areas if they exist.
*   **Subgroup Performance:** Publish the simulation results for ambulance throughput by district. Do not aggregate this data; show the split.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific engineering lead responsible for the equity metric.

**5. Governance & Next Steps**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Assign one engineer as the "Equity & Safety Owner" responsible for signing off on the simulation results.
*   **Legal Routing:** Immediately route the question of "liability for algorithmic delay" and "mandatory disclosure of AI control to the public" to municipal counsel. Do not assert legal compliance internally.
*   **Timeline Adjustment:** If the simulation fails the "Zero Tolerance" threshold, the six-week launch date must be treated as movable. Shipping a system with known lethal bias is not an option.

**Note:** This plan treats the simulation as a production-grade proving ground; passing it is a prerequisite for certification, not a substitute for it.
No metric regarding safety or bias should be fabricated; if simulation data is inconclusive, mark it "UNVERIFIED" and halt deployment.
