### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Manual Verification & Disclosure

**1. Manual Verification Process (Human-in-the-Loop Gate)**
Given the strict constraint preventing algorithmic changes and the high-stakes nature of financial quotes for grieving families, full automation of price generation is unsafe until the audit completes. Implement the following immediate workflow:

*   **Stop Condition:** No quote exceeding the median market rate for Chicago funeral services (or a predefined internal threshold based on historical data) may be sent to a family automatically.
*   **Mandatory Review:** Every generated quote must be reviewed by a licensed funeral director or senior pricing specialist before presentation.
*   **Verification Steps:**
    1.  **Input Validation:** The reviewer confirms the selected service package matches the family's explicit requests.
    2.  **Cross-Check:** The reviewer manually calculates the total using the static, pre-audit price list (spreadsheet or printed catalog) to verify the algorithm's output.
    3.  **Discrepancy Protocol:** If the algorithmic quote differs from the manual calculation by more than 5%, the algorithmic quote is discarded. The manual calculation becomes the binding offer.
    4.  **Logging:** Record every instance where the algorithm differed from the manual check, noting the service package and the variance amount. This log serves as the primary data for the upcoming 30-day audit.
*   **Ownership:** The Branch Manager at each funeral home location is the named owner responsible for ensuring no unverified quote leaves the office.

**2. User Disclosure Statement**
Transparency is required to manage expectations and mitigate reliance on the automated tool during this soft-launch phase. Include the following statement clearly on the quote document and the digital interface:

> "Important Notice Regarding Your Price Estimate: This quote was generated with the assistance of an automated estimation tool currently undergoing a mandatory compliance audit. To ensure absolute accuracy and fairness, every estimate is personally verified by a licensed funeral director before being presented to you. The final price you pay will be the manually verified amount confirmed by our staff, not solely the initial automated figure. If you notice any discrepancy between your selected services and this quote, please ask to speak with a manager immediately for a manual review."

**3. Risk Management Notes**
*   **Confidence Tiering:** Do not present the initial automated number as "final." Label it internally as "Unverified – Pending Human Review" until the staff member signs off.
*   **Audit Preparation:** The log of discrepancies collected during this manual gate is not just a safety measure; it is the required evidence set for your compliance team's 30-day audit. Without this data, the audit cannot validate the fix.

This approach treats the algorithm as a draft generator rather than a decision-maker, satisfying the need for speed while enforcing a human gate on high-stakes financial outcomes.
