**Pre-Launch Bias Mitigation Plan for AI Recruitment Screener**

Given the three-week deadline and constraints on new data acquisition, we cannot certify the system as "unbiased" or "fair." No test proves the absence of discrimination. Instead, we must map specific harms, measure residual risk using your existing assets, and manage the danger of automated exclusion through strict human gates.

**1. Map the Harm Inventory**
Before testing, explicitly record the specific risk: **Systematic exclusion of qualified candidates** with non-traditional backgrounds (bias/unfair outcomes). Define the worst-case scenario: high-potential candidates from underrepresented groups are auto-rejected without human review, leading to legal liability and reputational damage. Acknowledge that without diverse training data, this risk is elevated, not eliminated.

**2. Measure: Internal Adversarial Testing (The "Synthetic" Audit)**
Since you cannot buy new data, you must stress-test the current model against your internal data using adversarial probes. Execute one test row per identified harm:
*   **Probe Set Construction:** Take 50 known-high-quality resumes from your internal database that were previously hired successfully. Create "synthetic variants" by manually altering indicators of non-traditional paths (e.g., removing university names, adding gap years, changing job titles to equivalent but non-standard terms, translating names) while keeping core skills identical.
*   **Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* The original, unmodified high-quality resume (must pass).
    *   *Negative Control:* A clearly unqualified resume (must fail).
    *   *Test Input:* The modified "non-traditional" variants.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure the "Drop-off Rate" (percentage of qualified candidates rejected after modification).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the drop-off rate exceeds 0% for irreversible auto-rejections, the feature cannot launch in fully autonomous mode.
*   **Execution:** Your team of eight can manually create these variants and run them through the screener. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy; slice results by the specific modifications made.

**3. Manage: The Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
Because the risk of excluding qualified talent is high and the action (rejection) is costly to the candidate:
*   **No Fully Autonomous Rejections:** Do not allow the AI to auto-reject any candidate in this initial launch. Configure the system to "Recommend for Review" rather than "Auto-Reject."
*   **Uncertainty Tiers:** If the model outputs a confidence score, do not display raw percentages (e.g., "87% match") to recruiters, as this implies false precision. Instead, map scores to defined tiers: *Confirmed Match*, *Potential Match (Review Required)*, and *Low Match (Review Required)*.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Ensure recruiters can easily override the AI's ranking and see *why* a candidate was flagged, specifically checking if non-traditional keywords triggered a downgrade.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model Card)**
Draft a one-page internal Model Card before launch. It must state:
*   **Intended Use:** Screening only; final decisions require human review.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly note that the model was not trained on diverse external datasets and may underperform on non-traditional career paths.
*   **Measured Results:** Report the results of your internal adversarial test above. If a metric was not tested, label it **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a fairness score.

**5. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner:** Assign one person responsible for reviewing rejected candidates daily.
*   **Legal Duty:** Automated hiring decisions often trigger strict regulations (e.g., EU AI Act, local employment laws). Whether this tool constitutes a "high-risk" regulated system requiring specific disclosures or audits is a legal determination. **Route this immediately to counsel.** Do not assume the internal test satisfies legal compliance.

**Summary of Deliverable:** A restricted launch where the AI acts only as a sorting assistant, not a decision-maker, backed by an internal adversarial audit using synthetic resume variants.

*Note: This plan manages risk but does not eliminate it; the lack of diverse training data remains a structural limitation.*
*Legal counsel must verify regulatory obligations before any candidate data is processed.*
