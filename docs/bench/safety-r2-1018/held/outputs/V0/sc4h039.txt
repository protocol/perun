### Evaluation Framework and Immediate Adjustments for Hiring-Game Scorer

**1. Harm Inventory & Measurement Plan (Runnable in 2 Weeks)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Systematic disadvantage (bias) against candidates with motor impairments, leading to discriminatory hiring outcomes.
*   **Test Design:** Execute a "Red Team" evaluation using two distinct input sets:
    *   **Positive Control:** Simulated inputs representing standard motor function (baseline).
    *   **Negative Control:** Simulated inputs representing diverse motor impairments (e.g., reduced click speed, tremor simulation, single-switch navigation).
*   **Metric:** Calculate the "Performance Delta" between the Positive and Negative controls for identical in-game problem-solving scenarios.
*   **Pass Threshold:** Owner-defined. If the Delta exceeds the threshold where a qualified candidate would be rejected solely due to input speed/precision, the current weighting fails.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the Delta indicates high risk of exclusion, halt automated screening for affected roles and trigger mandatory human review of those specific applications.
*   **Reporting:** Report the residual risk as **UNVERIFIED** until these specific probes are run. Do not assert fairness without this data.

**2. Immediate Adjustments (No Mechanic Changes Required)**
Given the constraint of using the current game build without code changes to mechanics:
*   **Decouple Input Speed from Problem Solving:** Immediately adjust the scoring weights in the post-processing layer (not the game engine). Reduce the weight of "time-to-complete" or "actions-per-minute" metrics. Increase the weight of "decision accuracy," "path efficiency," or "puzzle resolution," which are less dependent on fine motor speed.
*   **Alternative Assessment Path:** Implement a "Human-in-the-Loop" gate for any candidate whose score falls within a borderline range or who self-identifies a disability. For these cases, bypass the automated reject/accept decision. A human reviewer must assess the *outcome* of the gameplay (did they solve the puzzle?) rather than the *mechanics* of how they achieved it.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a preliminary **Model Card** for internal use. Document the intended use (screening), the known limitation (potential motor bias in current build), and the owner of the risk. Explicitly state that the "fairness" metric is currently **UNVERIFIED** pending the red-team results above.

**3. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific owner to this harm inventory and the re-review process post-deadline.
*   **Legal Duty:** Whether this automated decision constitutes a regulated high-stakes decision requiring specific legal disclosures or accommodations under employment law is a determination for **counsel**. Route this question immediately to your legal team; do not assert compliance here.
*   **Confidence Display:** If sharing scores with candidates or internal stakeholders, do not present raw percentages implying false precision. Use defined tiers (e.g., "Strong Match," "Review Needed") based on the adjusted weights.

**Notes**
*   This plan treats your deadline and build constraints as fixed, offering a management layer solution rather than a code refactor.
*   Never certify the system as "unbiased"; only report the measured residual risk after running the proposed probes.
