### Pre-Launch Evaluation Strategy for Surge Pricing

Given your constraints of no new simulations, a tight deadline, and a team of ten, focus on **deterministic stress testing** and **logic-based guardrails** using your existing historical data and current model code.

#### 1. Deterministic "Golden Set" Stress Test
Instead of running thousands of random simulations, manually curate a small, high-fidelity dataset of 50–100 known historical emergency scenarios (e.g., specific timestamps/locations near major trauma centers during past accidents or festivals).
*   **Action:** Run your new algorithm *only* against this fixed "Golden Set."
*   **Metric:** Calculate the maximum price multiplier for these specific coordinates. If any scenario exceeds a predefined ethical threshold (e.g., 3x base fare), flag it immediately.
*   **Benefit:** This requires negligible compute power but provides concrete evidence of failure modes in critical zones.

#### 2. Logic-Based Guardrail Implementation
Since you cannot retrain the model, implement hard-coded "circuit breakers" in the deployment pipeline that override the algorithm's output before it reaches the user.
*   **Geofenced Caps:** Define hard coordinates around major hospitals. Force the multiplier to a maximum of 1.5x within a 1km radius, regardless of demand signals.
*   **Absolute Price Ceilings:** Set a hard currency cap (e.g., ₹500) for rides originating from verified emergency zones during declared crisis windows.
*   **Rate-of-Change Limits:** Prevent the price from jumping more than 20% within a 5-minute window to avoid shock spikes during rapidly developing situations.

#### 3. Manual "Tabletop" Walkthroughs
Leverage your team of ten for a rapid, low-tech review.
*   **Action:** Divide the team into pairs. Assign each pair 5 scenarios from your "Golden Set." Have them manually trace the algorithm's logic step-by-step on a whiteboard or shared document to identify where the demand signal overrides safety logic.
*   **Outcome:** This often reveals logic errors (e.g., double-counting demand signals) that code reviews miss, without needing execution time.

#### 4. Mitigation Tactics for Immediate Deployment
If high-risk spikes are identified and cannot be fixed in the core logic before launch:
*   **Voucher Override:** Pre-generate a pool of "Emergency Relief" codes that customer support can instantly apply to refund the difference if a user complains about gouging.
*   **Driver Incentive Decoupling:** Ensure drivers still receive high pay during these capped periods by subsidizing the difference from a marketing budget, rather than passing the full cost to the patient. This maintains driver availability while protecting the rider.
*   **Transparency Labeling:** Update the app UI to explicitly state "Emergency Zone Cap Applied" when these guardrails trigger, building trust rather than hiding the mechanism.

This approach shifts the burden from computationally expensive probability modeling to deterministic verification and hard-coded safety limits, ensuring patient protection without delaying the launch.

*Note: Ensure your legal team reviews the specific currency caps and geofence radii to comply with local consumer protection regulations.*
*Prioritize the "Golden Set" creation immediately, as the quality of your evaluation depends entirely on the realism of these selected scenarios.*
