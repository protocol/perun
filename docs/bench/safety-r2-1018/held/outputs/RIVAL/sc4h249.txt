# Comprehensive Risk Assessment Plan: Drone Impact on Wildlife

## 1. Pre-Deployment Baseline & Simulation (Weeks 1-2)
Since physical access is restricted, establish a robust baseline using historical data and simulation before any flight occurs.
*   **Historical Behavioral Analysis:** Collaborate with conservation biologists to aggregate existing data on nesting sites, breeding timelines, and known sensitivity thresholds for target species in the pilot zones. Define "disturbance metrics" (e.g., flush distance, abandonment rates, altered feeding patterns) based on literature and expert consensus.
*   **Acoustic & Visual Modeling:** Use your engineering team to model drone noise signatures and visual profiles against the specific reserve topography. Simulate flight paths digitally to predict sound propagation and line-of-sight exposure without entering the reserve.
*   **Controlled Proxy Testing:** Conduct flight tests in non-sensitive areas with similar acoustic properties using decoy nests or non-sensitive animal surrogates to validate sensor accuracy and noise measurement tools.

## 2. Phased Micro-Pilot Deployment (Weeks 3-4)
Execute a highly constrained, evidence-gathering phase within the limited access window, prioritizing data quality over coverage.
*   **Stratified Sampling:** Select three distinct micro-zones representing varying sensitivity levels (low, medium, high risk) rather than attempting broad coverage.
*   **Step-Wise Exposure Protocol:**
    *   *Phase A (Static Hover):* Deploy drones at maximum altitude with rotors spinning but no forward movement to isolate noise impact.
    *   *Phase B (Low-Speed Transect):* Execute slow, predictable flight paths away from known nests.
    *   *Phase C (Operational Pattern):* Run standard AI tracking patterns only if Phases A and B show zero adverse reactions.
*   **Multi-Modal Monitoring:** Equip rangers with high-zoom optical scopes and directional microphones positioned at safe distances to record bird behavior continuously before, during, and after flights. Do not rely solely on the drone's onboard AI, which may miss subtle stress indicators.
*   **Immediate Abort Criteria:** Establish hard limits (e.g., any bird leaving the nest for >2 minutes) that trigger immediate mission termination.

## 3. Data Synthesis & Safety Case Construction (Week 5)
Rapidly analyze collected data to build the evidence required for grant reporting and expansion approval.
*   **Comparative Impact Analysis:** Statistically compare behavioral metrics during flight windows against baseline historical data and control periods (no-flight times).
*   **Threshold Definition:** Define specific operational envelopes (maximum decibel levels, minimum approach distances, acceptable flight angles) where no significant disturbance was observed.
*   **Risk Mitigation Framework:** Draft standard operating procedures (SOPs) mandating these envelopes for future deployments, including "no-fly" buffers around active nests identified during the pilot.
*   **Grant Reporting Package:** Compile a report highlighting: (1) the rigorous methodology used despite access constraints, (2) quantitative evidence of safety within defined parameters, and (3) a clear roadmap for scaling that ties expansion directly to adherence to the new SOPs.

## Strategic Recommendations for Expansion
*   **Adaptive AI Training:** Feed the behavioral response data back into your AI models to automatically adjust flight paths in real-time, avoiding areas where distress signals are detected.
*   **Stakeholder Validation:** Have the conservation biologists formally sign off on the "Safety Envelope" definitions to ensure the technical data aligns with biological protection goals before presenting to grantors.

**Note:** This plan maximizes limited access by shifting heavy lifting to simulation and precise, small-scale physical validation.
**Note:** Ensure all field staff are briefed on abort protocols prior to entering the reserve to prevent data loss from uncontrolled incidents.
