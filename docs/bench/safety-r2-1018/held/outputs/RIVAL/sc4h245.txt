# AudioSafe Noise Cancellation Safety Testing Strategy & Mitigation Plan

## Simulation-Based Testing Strategy (2-Week Timeline)

**Phase 1: Acoustic Scenario Modeling (Days 1-3)**
Create digital twin simulations of high-risk urban environments using existing acoustic datasets. Model specific emergency scenarios:
- Ambulance/fire truck sirens at varying distances (10m–200m)
- Police sirens with Doppler effect at intersection approaches
- Construction warning alarms and backup beepers
- Pedestrian crossing signals and shouted warnings

**Phase 2: Algorithm Stress Testing (Days 4-10)**
Run Monte Carlo simulations with 10,000+ iterations varying:
- Ambient noise levels (60–95 dB)
- Siren frequencies (500 Hz–2.5 kHz range)
- User head orientation and movement patterns
- Simultaneous noise sources (traffic + construction + sirens)

Measure detection latency, attenuation rates, and false-negative rates for each scenario. Establish baseline metrics for "safe cancellation thresholds."

**Phase 3: Edge Case Identification (Days 11-14)**
Focus on worst-case combinations:
- Maximum cancellation + weakest siren signal + user distraction
- Rapid environmental transitions (quiet sidewalk to busy intersection)
- Battery-low performance degradation scenarios
- Firmware update rollback situations

Document failure modes with specific acoustic signatures that trigger unsafe cancellation.

## Firmware Mitigation Tactics

**1. Emergency Frequency Passthrough**
Implement a hardware-level filter that preserves frequencies between 500 Hz–2.5 kHz regardless of cancellation intensity. This range covers 95% of emergency sirens while allowing aggressive cancellation of lower-frequency traffic rumble.

**2. Dynamic Threshold Adaptation**
Deploy machine learning models trained on emergency siren datasets to detect siren-like patterns. When detected, automatically reduce cancellation by 40–60% within 50 milliseconds, creating an "awareness mode" without disabling the feature entirely.

**3. Ambient Sound Transparency Mode**
Add a mandatory "urban safety mode" that users must acknowledge during setup. This mode maintains 20% ambient sound passthrough specifically for transient high-priority sounds, activated automatically when GPS detects pedestrian-speed movement in dense urban areas.

**4. Multi-Sensor Fusion**
Integrate accelerometer data to detect sudden user stops or head turns (potential reaction to unheard sirens). Trigger immediate transparency mode for 3 seconds when these patterns occur in high-traffic zones.

**5. Graduated Cancellation Profiles**
Replace binary on/off cancellation with five discrete levels. Level 1 (maximum safety) preserves all sounds above 45 dB; Level 5 (maximum cancellation) applies full algorithm. Default to Level 2 for first-time users with clear warnings about higher levels.

**6. Real-Time Acoustic Monitoring**
Implement a background process that continuously measures the signal-to-noise ratio of preserved frequencies. If the ratio indicates potential masking of critical sounds, automatically dial back cancellation and notify the user via subtle haptic feedback.

**7. Regulatory Compliance Buffer**
Build in a 5 dB safety margin below local regulatory limits for ambient sound preservation. This ensures compliance even with component variance or firmware drift over the product lifecycle.

## Validation Metrics

Before shipping, confirm through simulation that:
- Emergency siren detection rate exceeds 99.5% at 50 meters in all modeled scenarios
- Time-to-transparency mode is under 100 milliseconds for detected threats
- False-positive transparency activations remain below 2% to maintain user trust
- Battery consumption impact of safety features stays under 8%

These measures balance innovative noise cancellation with fundamental pedestrian safety, creating defensible positioning for regulatory review and consumer confidence.

*Note: Prioritize frequency passthrough and dynamic thresholds as immediate firmware updates. Consider third-party acoustic safety certification before mass market release.*
