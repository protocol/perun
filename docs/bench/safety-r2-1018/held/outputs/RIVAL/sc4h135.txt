# Risk Assessment Plan: Automated Stock Tip Manipulation

**1. Scope and Objective**
Assess the risk that coordinated AI-generated stock tips on small-cap companies, derived solely from public social media sentiment, could artificially inflate prices or create a "pump-and-dump" appearance. The assessment must be completed within a 48-hour window to meet the launch deadline next Friday.

**2. Identified Manipulation Vectors**
Given the constraint of using only social media sentiment and the focus on small-cap stocks (which have lower liquidity and are more susceptible to volatility), the following specific risks are prioritized:
*   **Sentiment Feedback Loops:** The AI may identify rising sentiment, issue a "buy" tip, which drives user buying, further increasing social media buzz, causing the AI to reinforce the recommendation. This creates an artificial price spiral disconnected from fundamentals.
*   **Herd Behavior Amplification:** If the five quant models share similar training data or logic, they may issue near-identical recommendations simultaneously. This lack of diversity mimics coordinated manipulation, even if unintentional.
*   **Susceptibility to Poisoning:** Bad actors could inject fake positive sentiment into social media to trigger the AI into issuing tips that inflate prices for the bad actors' benefit.

**3. Rapid Assessment Methodology (48-Hour Sprint)**
Due to the lack of historical trade data for the new service, the assessment will rely on theoretical modeling and historical simulation using public data.

*   **Day 1: Vulnerability Mapping & Logic Review**
    *   **Input Stress Testing:** Manually simulate extreme social media sentiment spikes (e.g., bot-driven campaigns) against the current AI logic to observe output sensitivity. Determine the threshold at which the AI shifts from "neutral" to "strong buy."
    *   **Concentration Analysis:** Review the list of target small-cap companies. Flag any where the daily trading volume is low enough that a coordinated response from the startup's user base could move the price by >5% within one hour.
    *   **Model Diversity Check:** Verify if the five quant models produce independent signals. If correlation is >90%, the risk of perceived coordination is critical.

*   **Day 2: Impact Simulation & Mitigation Design**
    *   **Liquidity Impact Modeling:** Estimate the potential price impact if 10%, 25%, and 50% of the projected user base acts on a single tip simultaneously for the flagged small-cap stocks.
    *   **Regulatory Alignment Check:** Map findings against Singapore's Securities and Futures Act (SFA) provisions on market manipulation, specifically focusing on "creating a false or misleading appearance of active trading."
    *   **Drafting Guardrails:** Define immediate operational constraints, such as:
        *   Excluding stocks with average daily volume below a specific threshold.
        *   Implementing a "cool-down" period where the AI cannot re-issue a tip on the same stock within X hours.
        *   Adding explicit disclaimers regarding the limitations of social media data.

**4. Deliverables**
*   **Risk Matrix:** A heatmap categorizing target small-cap stocks by liquidity risk and manipulation susceptibility.
*   **Logic Audit Report:** Documentation of AI behavior under simulated sentiment attacks.
*   **Launch Go/No-Go Criteria:** Specific thresholds (e.g., maximum recommended position size, minimum stock liquidity) that must be met to proceed with Friday's launch.
*   **Emergency Stop Protocol:** A defined process to immediately halt recommendations if unusual price volatility is detected post-launch.

**5. Immediate Next Steps**
*   Convene the five quants immediately to run the Input Stress Testing.
*   Legal counsel to review the draft guardrails against MAS (Monetary Authority of Singapore) guidelines before finalizing the plan.

*Note: This plan assumes no access to proprietary trading data; all simulations must use public historical records. Immediate legal review is essential before launch.*
