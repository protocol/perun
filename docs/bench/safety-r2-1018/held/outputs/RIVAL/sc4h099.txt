### Pre-Launch Review Process: Vintage-Car Valuation AI

**1. Triage and Flagging Protocol**
Implement an automated threshold trigger within the alpha interface. Any valuation exceeding a specific monetary limit (e.g., £50,000) or involving a "rare model" tag defined in your historical dataset must be flagged as "Pending Expert Review." These items are withheld from immediate public display and routed to a dedicated review queue accessible only to your team of four and invited external specialists.

**2. The Human-in-the-Loop Validation Step**
For every flagged vehicle, assign a human expert (either an internal team member or a contracted classic car specialist) to perform a manual comparative market analysis (CMA). Since the AI lacks recent private sale data, the expert must:
*   Consult private networks, recent auction house private ledgers, and dealer-to-dealer transaction logs.
*   Compare the AI's generated estimate against these off-market realities.
*   Assign a "Confidence Score" and a "Market Adjustment Factor" to the listing.

**3. The Correction Feedback Loop**
Create a structured data entry form for the experts to record the *reason* for any deviation between the AI's estimate and the expert's verified value. Categories should include:
*   "Recent Private Sale Missing"
*   "Unique Restoration/Provenance Not Weighted"
*   "Regional Demand Spike"
*   "Data Outlier/Error"

Store these corrections in a separate "Golden Dataset." Before the public launch, retrain or fine-tune the valuation model using this Golden Dataset. This teaches the AI to recognize patterns where historical auction data underestimates value, effectively allowing the model to learn the "private market premium" without needing direct access to the private records themselves.

**4. Staged Rollout with Continuous Monitoring**
Do not open to the general public immediately. Begin with a "Closed Beta" for a select group of trusted collectors. Continue the human review process for high-value items during this phase. Monitor the ratio of AI-to-Expert deviations; once the deviation rate drops below a pre-defined acceptable margin (e.g., 5%) for three consecutive weeks, gradually automate the approvals for lower-tier vehicles while maintaining human oversight for the top 1% of valuations.

**5. Transparency Disclaimer**
Until the feedback loop has processed sufficient data to stabilize valuations, display a clear disclaimer on high-value estimates: *"Valuation based on historical auction data; pending final verification against current private market conditions."* This manages seller expectations and protects the platform's reputation while the data gaps are being actively filled.

***

*Note: Ensure all expert adjustments are anonymized before being fed back into the model to protect sensitive private sale information.*
*This process turns your current data limitation into a structured data-collection opportunity prior to full-scale automation.*
