### Pre-Launch Review Workflow: Balancing Safety and Education

**Phase 1: Triage and Categorization (Automated + Human)
**
Before human review, implement a lightweight automated pre-filter that flags content based on confidence scores rather than binary blocks.
*   **High Confidence Harm:** Auto-block (e.g., explicit violence, known prohibited keywords).
*   **High Confidence Educational:** Auto-allow (e.g., content from verified educational partners, titles containing standard curriculum keywords like "matemática" or "história").
*   **Ambiguous Zone (The Review Queue):** Route only these items to your team of seven. This reduces volume and focuses human effort where dialect nuance matters most.

**Phase 2: Context-Rich Human Review Protocol**
For items in the Ambiguous Zone, moderators must follow a structured checklist to evaluate context over isolated keywords:
1.  **Source Verification:** Is the uploader a recognized educational institution or creator?
2.  **Intent Analysis:** Does the surrounding audio/visual context explain the potentially flagged term? (e.g., a documentary explaining a dangerous concept vs. promoting it).
3.  **Dialect Check:** If a term is unfamiliar, consult a shared, living "Regional Dialect Glossary" maintained by the team. Do not rely solely on standard Portuguese dictionaries.
4.  **Decision Matrix:**
    *   *Educational Context Confirmed:* Approve and tag as "Verified Educational" to train the filter against future false positives.
    *   *Harmful Intent Confirmed:* Block and tag with specific violation reason.
    *   *Uncertain:* Escalate to a senior engineer for a second opinion; default to "Allow with Monitoring" rather than blocking if the risk is low.

**Phase 3: Feedback Loop and Filter Tuning**
To address the lack of specialized linguistic tools, use your human decisions as training data:
*   **False Positive Log:** Every time a moderator overrides a block, record the specific phrase, regional dialect, and context.
*   **Weekly Rule Updates:** Engineers should review the top 10 false positives weekly to adjust keyword weightings or add exception rules for specific educational contexts.
*   **Community Sourcing:** If possible, create a simple internal form where moderators can submit new regional slang or educational terms they encounter, building your own proprietary dataset over time.

**Phase 4: Final Pre-Launch Audit**
Before going live:
*   Run a "Red Team" simulation where two team members intentionally try to bypass the filter with educational content phrased ambiguously.
*   Verify that the "Appeal Process" is visible and easy for users if a mistake slips through.
*   Confirm that the "Verified Educational" tag effectively bypasses strict filtering for trusted sources.

**Roles Allocation (Team of 7):**
*   **4 Moderators:** Focus on the Ambiguous Queue and maintaining the Dialect Glossary.
*   **2 Engineers:** Handle filter tuning based on moderator logs and manage the "Verified Educational" partner list.
*   **1 Lead:** Oversees the workflow, manages escalations, and coordinates the weekly rule update meetings.

This workflow prioritizes human judgment for nuance while using automation to handle clear-cut cases, ensuring educational content remains accessible without compromising child safety.

*Note: Success depends on consistently logging every override to gradually improve automated accuracy.*
