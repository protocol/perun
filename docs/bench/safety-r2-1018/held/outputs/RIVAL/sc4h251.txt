### Pre-Launch Safety Review Plan: Automated Insulin Dosing

**1. Algorithmic Robustness & Edge Case Handling**
*   **Rapid Physiological Change Scenarios:** Document test results for sudden glucose spikes/drops (e.g., post-prandial, exercise-induced, illness) where historical data may not predict immediate trends.
*   **Data Gap Resilience:** Evidence of system behavior during sensor signal loss, calibration delays, or corrupted historical data inputs.
*   **Boundary Conditions:** Verification of dosing limits when predicted glucose approaches hypoglycemic or hyperglycemic thresholds.
*   **Cold Start Performance:** Analysis of algorithm behavior for new users with minimal historical data.

**2. Fail-Safes & Human Oversight**
*   **Hard Limits:** Confirmation of absolute maximum/minimum dosage caps that cannot be overridden by algorithmic predictions.
*   **Alert Triggers:** Defined criteria for when the system must defer to user input or alert clinical support rather than auto-adjust.
*   **Manual Override Latency:** Measurement of time required for a patient or clinician to interrupt an automated dosing sequence.
*   **Degraded Mode Operation:** Protocol for safe operation if predictive features are disabled or malfunction.

**3. Data Integrity & Privacy**
*   **Input Validation:** Checks ensuring only plausible physiological values are accepted from sensors or user logs.
*   **Data Provenance:** Audit trail showing the source and quality of historical data used for training and real-time prediction.
*   **GDPR Compliance:** Documentation of data anonymization, storage security, and patient consent mechanisms specific to London/UK regulations.
*   **Bias Assessment:** Review of training data demographics to ensure performance across diverse age groups, BMI ranges, and diabetes types.

**4. Usability & Human Factors**
*   **Alarm Fatigue Analysis:** Evidence that alert frequencies do not lead to user desensitization or ignored warnings.
*   **Interface Clarity:** Verification that dosage changes and rationale are clearly displayed to the patient before/during administration.
*   **Misuse Scenarios:** Testing for potential user errors (e.g., incorrect carb entry) and the system's response to prevent harmful dosing.

**5. Regulatory & Clinical Alignment**
*   **Requirement Traceability:** Matrix linking each safety requirement to specific test cases and outcomes.
*   **Clinical Logic Review:** Sign-off from clinical team members confirming algorithmic decisions align with current medical guidelines.
*   **Incident Response Plan:** Defined workflow for reporting, investigating, and mitigating adverse events during trials.

**Internal Evidence Checklist for Due Diligence**
*   [ ] Test logs covering identified edge cases with pass/fail criteria met.
*   [ ] Code review records focusing on decision logic and limit enforcement.
*   [ ] Simulation reports showing system response to sensor failures and data anomalies.
*   [ ] Usability study summaries highlighting user comprehension of automated actions.
*   [ ] Data management policy documents verifying GDPR adherence.
*   [ ] Clinical validation minutes documenting expert review of dosing rules.
*   [ ] Risk register updated with mitigation status for all identified high-severity issues.
*   [ ] Version control history demonstrating reproducible builds for the submitted dossier.

*Note: This plan structures internal review efforts; it does not constitute formal certification or guarantee clinical outcomes.*
*Final safety determinations require statutory regulatory approval and independent clinical trial data.*
