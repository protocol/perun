### Pre-Launch Safety Review: Metrics & Mitigation Checklist

**Daily Post-Launch Metrics for Early Harm Detection**
To detect unsustainable debt among low-income users, track these specific indicators daily, segmented by "new cohort" (users <30 days since limit increase):

1.  **Essential Spend Displacement Ratio:** The percentage of a user's remaining balance spent on non-essential categories (e.g., entertainment, dining) immediately after a limit increase, versus essential categories (groceries, utilities, transport). A spike suggests the extra credit is displacing basic needs rather than smoothing cash flow.
2.  **Minimum Payment Dependency:** The percentage of the new cohort paying only the minimum due for two consecutive cycles. This is a leading indicator of debt traps.
3.  **Overdraft Frequency vs. Limit Utilization:** Correlate users who utilize >80% of their new limit within 48 hours with subsequent overdraft fees. Rapid max-out behavior indicates the limit exceeds repayment capacity.
4.  **Social Graph Homogeneity in Delinquency:** Track if delinquency clusters within specific social connectivity groups. If one user misses a payment, monitor their direct connections for similar behavior within 7 days, indicating potential systemic risk in specific communities.
5.  **Rolling 7-Day Missed Payment Rate:** Instead of monthly, track missed payments on a rolling 7-day window for the new cohort to enable faster reaction times.
6.  **Credit Line Cycling:** Count users who pay down the balance and immediately re-max the limit within the same billing cycle, indicating reliance on credit for daily survival.

**Immediate Mitigation Strategies for Payment Spikes**
If the missed payment rate for the newest cohort rises by >15% week-over-week, execute the following immediately:

1.  **Dynamic Limit Freeze:** Automatically pause further automatic limit increases for the affected cohort. Do not revoke existing limits immediately to avoid destabilizing current cash flow, but halt any upward adjustments.
2.  **Hard Cap on Non-Essential Categories:** Temporarily restrict the use of the *increased* portion of the credit limit to essential merchant categories (groceries, utilities, healthcare) only. This prevents the extra credit from being used for discretionary spending while allowing basic needs coverage.
3.  **Proactive Restructuring Outreach:** Trigger an automated, empathetic in-app notification offering an immediate payment plan extension or split-payment option before the payment is officially marked "late." Frame this as a support feature, not a penalty.
4.  **Model Input Dampening:** Temporarily reduce the weight of "social connectivity" in the decision engine for new approvals. If social data is driving correlated risk, rely solely on individual transaction history until the spike stabilizes.
5.  **Grace Period Extension:** Automatically extend the payment due date by 5 days for the affected cohort to accommodate irregular income cycles common in low-income demographics.

**Note:** These measures prioritize preventing deep debt traps over short-term revenue from interest or fees.
Ensure all communications regarding restrictions are transparent and framed as protective measures to maintain user trust.
