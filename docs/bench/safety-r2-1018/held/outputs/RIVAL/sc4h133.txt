### Pre-Launch Content Safety & Engagement Checklist

**1. Sentiment Polarization Stress Test**
*   **Action:** Filter the last 30 days of recommended content by sentiment score (highly negative vs. highly positive).
*   **Metric:** Calculate the ratio of negative-to-positive content in the top 10% of most-viewed posts.
*   **Pass Criteria:** Negative content should not exceed 40% of top-performing recommendations unless tied to verified breaking news events. If sensationalism drives >60% of engagement, adjust the ranking weight for sentiment.

**2. Topic Diversity Entropy Check**
*   **Action:** Group recommended feeds by primary topic (e.g., Politics, Entertainment, Sports, Local Community).
*   **Metric:** Compute the Shannon Entropy of topic distribution per user session.
*   **Pass Criteria:** Ensure no single user segment receives >70% of content from a single controversial topic cluster (e.g., politics) over a 24-hour window. Low entropy indicates an echo chamber forming.

**3. "Rage-Bait" Velocity Analysis**
*   **Action:** Identify posts with high engagement velocity (likes/shares per minute) but low dwell time (users click and leave quickly).
*   **Metric:** Correlate high-velocity/low-dwell posts with keyword triggers associated with divisive rhetoric.
*   **Pass Criteria:** Flag any content cluster where engagement velocity spikes >200% above baseline while average dwell time drops below 15 seconds. These are candidates for manual review or down-ranking.

**4. Cross-Segment Exposure Audit**
*   **Action:** Simulate user profiles representing distinct demographic segments within your analytics sandbox.
*   **Metric:** Measure the overlap percentage of recommended stories between opposing or distinct user clusters.
*   **Pass Criteria:** Maintain a minimum 15% "bridge content" rate (neutral or universally appealing stories) appearing in feeds across all segments to prevent total fragmentation.

**5. Engagement-Quality Trade-off Curve**
*   **Action:** Plot "Average Session Duration" against "Percentage of High-Sentiment-Negative Content" for the past week.
*   **Metric:** Identify the inflection point where adding more sensational content yields diminishing returns on total time spent.
*   **Pass Criteria:** Set the recommendation threshold at the inflection point, not the absolute maximum engagement peak. This proves to the board that reducing harmful content does not linearly crash retention.

**6. Keyword Co-occurrence Heatmap**
*   **Action:** Generate a heatmap of frequently co-occurring keywords in the top 5% of recommended articles.
*   **Metric:** Highlight pairs linking protected groups (ethnicity, religion, location) with negative predicates (threat, crime, scandal).
*   **Pass Criteria:** Zero tolerance for unverified negative associations involving protected groups in the top recommendation tier. Any detected pair triggers an immediate filter rule.

**7. A/B Shadow Mode Validation**
*   **Action:** Run a "shadow" algorithm alongside the current model for 48 hours that applies a diversity penalty to sensational content.
*   **Metric:** Compare the projected retention delta between the current model and the safety-adjusted model.
*   **Pass Criteria:** If the projected retention drop is <5%, proceed with the safer model. If >5%, iterate on the penalty weight rather than abandoning safety constraints.

**Implementation Note:** Execute these queries directly on your existing event logs; no new data collection is required.
**Board Reporting:** Present the "Engagement-Quality Trade-off Curve" as the primary evidence that safety and retention are balanced, not mutually exclusive.
