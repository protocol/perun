### Evaluation Framework for Neurodivergent Bias in Emotion Recognition

Given the constraint of lacking diverse training data, your evaluation framework must rely on **synthetic stress-testing**, **adversarial simulation**, and **proxy metrics** rather than historical validation. The following four-phase approach is designed for immediate implementation by your data science team.

#### 1. Synthetic Variance Injection (The "Digital Twin" Approach)
Since real neurodivergent data is unavailable, you must algorithmically generate it to test model robustness.
*   **Action:** Create a synthetic test suite by applying geometric and temporal perturbations to your existing "neurotypical" validation set.
*   **Specific Perturbations:**
    *   **Reduced Expressivity:** Dampen facial action unit intensities (simulating flat affect common in some autism spectra or depression).
    *   **Atypical Timing:** Introduce delays between stimulus and response, or irregular blink rates.
    *   **Incongruence:** Deliberately mismatch facial cues with context (e.g., a smile while discussing a negative topic, common in some neurodivergent communication styles).
    *   **Stimming Artifacts:** Overlay repetitive motion patterns on video feeds to simulate self-regulatory movements.
*   **Success Metric:** The model's confidence scores should drop (indicating uncertainty) rather than outputting a confident but incorrect classification (e.g., labeling "flat affect" as "disinterest" or "deception").

#### 2. Adversarial "Red Team" Simulation
Leverage your team to act as adversarial users before external pilots.
*   **Action:** Conduct structured "blind" interviews where team members intentionally adopt neurodivergent communication styles based on clinical literature and lived-experience accounts.
*   **Protocol:**
    *   Recruit internal volunteers (or external consultants if possible) to role-play specific profiles (e.g., ADHD hyperfocus, autistic monotone speech, Tourette's tics).
    *   Run these sessions through the full pipeline without revealing the intent to the scoring algorithm.
*   **Analysis:** Compare the AI's scoring against human evaluator notes. A significant divergence where the AI penalizes the candidate while humans rate them positively indicates bias.

#### 3. Proxy Group Fairness Metrics
Without protected attribute labels, use proxy analysis to detect disparate impact.
*   **Action:** Cluster your synthetic and role-played data by "expression style" rather than demographic labels.
*   **Metric:** Calculate the **False Negative Rate (FNR)** disparity between the "Standard Expression Cluster" and the "Atypical Expression Cluster."
*   **Threshold:** If the FNR for the Atypical Cluster exceeds the Standard Cluster by more than 5%, the model is failing the fairness threshold for deployment. The system must flag these candidates for human review rather than auto-rejection.

#### 4. The "Uncertainty Gate" Mechanism
Implement a safety valve for out-of-distribution inputs.
*   **Action:** Calibrate the model's entropy threshold. If the model encounters facial dynamics that deviate significantly from its training distribution (high entropy), it must trigger a "Human Review Required" flag instead of generating a low score.
*   **Validation:** Verify that your synthetic neurodivergent cases consistently trigger this gate. If the model confidently scores them, the uncertainty threshold is too loose.

### Immediate Next Steps for the 2-Week Sprint
1.  **Day 1-3:** Build the synthetic perturbation pipeline and generate 500+ variant test cases.
2.  **Day 4-7:** Execute internal Red Team role-plays and record baseline discrepancies.
3.  **Day 8-10:** Tune the "Uncertainty Gate" thresholds to ensure atypical expressions are routed to human review.
4.  **Day 11-14:** Finalize the bias report documenting the FNR disparities and the mitigation logic implemented.

**Note:** This framework shifts the goal from "accurate classification of neurodivergent traits" (which is currently impossible without data) to "safe refusal to classify," preventing automated rejection.
**Critical:** Document these limitations explicitly in your client pilot agreement to manage liability regarding false negatives.
