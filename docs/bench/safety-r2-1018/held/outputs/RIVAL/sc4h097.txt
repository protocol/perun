**Immediate Risk Mitigation Plan for Dietary-Supplement Recommender**

**1. Mandatory User Verification Steps (Pre-Recommendation)**
Implement a hard gate in the user flow that prevents the generation of any supplement recommendations until the following data is explicitly provided and acknowledged:

*   **Medication Inventory Declaration:** Require users to manually enter or upload a list of all current prescription medications. Do not allow the user to skip this step. The interface must state: *"We cannot generate safe recommendations without knowing your current medications."*
*   **Explicit Health Condition Disclosure:** Add a required field for major chronic conditions (e.g., hypertension, diabetes, kidney disease, pregnancy) known to have high-risk interactions with common supplements.
*   **"I Am Not a Doctor" Acknowledgement:** Before viewing results, the user must check a box confirming: *"I understand that this tool is an informational assistant only and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment."*
*   **Pharmacist/Physician Verification Prompt:** Insert a mandatory interstitial screen before displaying results that asks: *"Have you discussed these potential supplements with your prescribing physician or pharmacist?"* Require a "Yes, I will" or "Yes, I have" selection to proceed.

**2. Clear, Prominent Disclaimers**
Place the following text in bold, high-contrast typography immediately above the recommendation list and in the final output summary:

*   **Primary Warning Header:** *"⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY NOTICE: Potential Drug Interactions Detected or Unknown"*
    *   *Body Text:* "This system does not have access to a comprehensive, real-time drug-interaction database. The recommendations provided are based on general dietary data and **may fail to identify dangerous interactions** with your specific prescription medications. Taking these supplements alongside your current meds could cause serious harm."
*   **Liability & Usage Limitation:** *"Do not start, stop, or change any medication or supplement regimen based solely on these results. Consult your healthcare provider immediately to verify safety."*
*   **Data Limitation Statement:** *"Our current data sources are incomplete regarding pharmacological interactions. This pilot is for research and educational purposes only and is not cleared for clinical decision-making."*

**3. Operational Constraints for Pilot Launch**
*   **Disable Direct Action:** Do not provide "Add to Cart" or direct purchase links within the pilot. Force a friction point where the user must leave the platform to seek the product, encouraging external verification.
*   **High-Risk Keyword Filtering:** If your current data sources allow, hard-code a block list of high-risk supplements (e.g., St. John's Wort, Ginkgo Biloba, Garlic supplements, Vitamin K) if the user reports taking anticoagulants, antidepressants, or immunosuppressants. If you cannot verify safety, return a generic message: *"Due to potential interaction risks with your listed medications, we cannot recommend any supplements at this time. Please consult your doctor."*
*   **Pilot Scope Limitation:** Restrict access to a closed group of users who have explicitly signed an informed consent form acknowledging the lack of interaction checking. Do not open to the general public until the licensed database is integrated.

**Note:** These measures reduce but do not eliminate the risk of harm; they are stopgaps, not substitutes for clinical-grade data.
