# Pre-Launch Safety Report: Equitable Routing Analysis

## 1. Impact Analysis: Service Denial and Food Access
The current fuel-optimization algorithm creates a **digital redlining** effect by systematically excluding low-income neighborhoods characterized by narrower street grids. This exclusion impacts marginalized residents in three critical ways:

*   **Exacerbation of Food Deserts:** Residents in excluded zones lose access to fresh produce delivered via the platform, forcing reliance on local convenience stores with limited healthy options and higher prices.
*   **Mobility Penalty:** The algorithm penalizes residents for infrastructure characteristics (narrow streets) outside their control, effectively treating their neighborhood as "high cost" solely due to historical urban planning disparities.
*   **Contractual & Reputational Risk:** Launching with known exclusionary logic violates the spirit of "city-wide" coverage obligations and exposes SeineFresh to regulatory scrutiny regarding discriminatory algorithmic practices in essential services.

## 2. Immediate Algorithmic Adjustments
To ensure equitable coverage while maintaining delivery time guarantees before the month-end launch, implement the following **hybrid constraint adjustments**:

### A. Mandatory Coverage Zones (Hard Constraint)
Override the fuel-efficiency objective function with a **geofenced mandatory service layer**.
*   **Action:** Define polygon boundaries for all identified low-income neighborhoods.
*   **Logic:** The router must accept and assign orders within these zones regardless of the calculated fuel penalty.
*   **Implementation:** Add a binary constraint `is_mandatory_zone = true` that bypasses the "skip" logic triggered by narrow street classifications during peak hours.

### B. Weighted Objective Function Recalibration
Adjust the optimization score to de-prioritize fuel savings in favor of coverage equity during specific windows.
*   **Current Logic:** `Minimize (Fuel Cost + Time Penalty)`
*   **Proposed Logic:** `Minimize (0.4 * Fuel Cost + 0.6 * Time Penalty + Equity Bonus)`
*   **Equity Bonus:** Apply a negative cost (bonus) to routes entering mandatory zones, counterbalancing the fuel inefficiency of narrower streets. This ensures the solver views these routes as viable without breaking time guarantees.

### C. Dynamic Fleet Allocation
Instead of optimizing individual routes in isolation, manage capacity at the zone level.
*   **Action:** Reserve a fixed percentage (e.g., 15%) of the fleet specifically for "High-Friction Zones" during peak hours.
*   **Validation:** These vehicles are assigned exclusively to the narrower street grid, accepting slightly higher per-drop fuel costs as a distributed operational expense rather than an optimization failure.

## 3. Rapid Validation Plan
Given the seven-person team and tight deadline, execute this validation loop within 48 hours:

1.  **Historical Replay (Day 1):** Run the modified algorithm against last month's order data. Compare the new route acceptance rate in target neighborhoods against the baseline. Verify that average delivery times remain within contractual SLAs.
2.  **Shadow Mode Simulation (Day 2):** Deploy the updated logic in a read-only "shadow mode" alongside the live system. Compare proposed routes without affecting actual deliveries.
3.  **Canary Launch (Day 3):** Enable the new logic for 10% of traffic in the affected zones. Monitor driver feedback on navigability and actual fuel consumption vs. projections.

## 4. Reporting Commitment
The safety report must explicitly state:
*   **Acknowledgment:** Recognition that the initial model inadvertently discriminated based on neighborhood infrastructure.
*   **Mitigation:** Documentation of the hard constraints and weighted adjustments implemented.
*   **Monitoring:** A commitment to track delivery success rates by zip code weekly post-launch, with a trigger to manually dispatch if automated success rates drop below 95% in any zone.

This approach satisfies the contractual obligation for city-wide launch while ethically correcting the algorithmic bias before it impacts customers.
