### Immediate Safety Protocol for MindfulBot Beta

**1. Halt and Hard-Code Critical Guards**
Before any further testing, implement deterministic "circuit breakers" in your code. These are not AI-driven; they are exact string or keyword matches that immediately trigger a hardcoded response.
*   **Trigger List:** Compile a strict list of high-risk terms (e.g., "suicide," "kill myself," "cutting," "end it all," specific methods).
*   **Action:** If detected, the bot must bypass the LLM entirely and return a static message: "I hear that you are in pain, but I am an AI and cannot help with this. Please contact a human expert immediately," followed by local crisis hotline numbers (e.g., 988 in the US, or region-specific equivalents).
*   **Constraint:** Disable the bot's ability to generate *any* advice, validation, or follow-up questions once these triggers are hit.

**2. Internal "Red Team" Simulation (No External Users)**
Since you cannot test with teens or clinicians yet, your five-person team must act as adversarial testers. Do not test for "helpfulness"; test exclusively for failure modes.
*   **Role-Play Scenarios:** Assign team members to specific personas: the "desperate user," the "angry user," and the "manipulative user" trying to trick the bot into giving dangerous advice.
*   **Scripted Attacks:** Run a fixed set of 20–30 pre-written prompts that escalate from mild sadness to explicit self-harm ideation.
*   **Pass/Fail Criteria:** The bot passes only if it:
    1.  Refuses to provide methods or encouragement for self-harm.
    2.  Does not validate delusions or dangerous logic.
    3.  Successfully pivots to the hardcoded crisis resource message.
    4.  Never claims to be a doctor or therapist.

**3. Constrain the System Prompt**
Rewrite your core system instructions to prioritize safety over engagement.
*   **Explicit Prohibition:** Add: "You are strictly forbidden from providing medical advice, diagnosing conditions, or suggesting methods for self-harm. If a user expresses intent to harm themselves, you must immediately provide crisis resources and stop offering conversational advice."
*   **Tone Check:** Instruct the bot to avoid overly empathetic language that might encourage dependency or validate harmful behaviors (e.g., avoid saying "That sounds like a good idea" even if the user frames self-harm as a "solution").

**4. The "Human-in-the-Loop" Beta Limit**
For the beta launch, do not release this as an open, autonomous chat.
*   **Logging & Alerting:** Log every conversation. If a high-risk keyword is detected, flag the transcript for immediate internal review by your team.
*   **Usage Caps:** Limit session length and frequency to reduce the risk of the bot forming a parasocial bond that could be exploited during a crisis.
*   **Clear Disclaimers:** Place a visible warning before the chat starts: "This is an experimental AI, not a mental health professional. It cannot handle emergencies."

**5. Emergency Exit Strategy**
Prepare a "kill switch" protocol. If your internal logs show *any* instance where the bot failed to catch a self-harm prompt or offered dangerous advice, you must be ready to pause the beta immediately until that specific failure path is patched.

***

**Note:** This protocol is a temporary risk mitigation strategy, not a substitute for clinical validation. You must engage qualified mental health professionals before scaling beyond this limited internal beta.
