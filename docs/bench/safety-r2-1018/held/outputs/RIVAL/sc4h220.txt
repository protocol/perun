### Safety Review Plan: Positive Adherence & Stigma Elimination

**Phase 1: Immediate Harm Cessation (Days 1–3)**
*   **Disable Public Shaming:** Immediately deactivate the community feed feature that broadcasts missed doses. Replace all public "missed dose" notifications with a system-wide hold.
*   **Data Audit:** Identify all historical posts containing missed-dose data. Queue these for permanent deletion or anonymization before the revised launch.
*   **Crisis Protocol:** Draft a temporary support response for any user who contacts the team regarding distress caused by previous public shaming features.

**Phase 2: Redefine Engagement Mechanics (Days 4–10)**
*   **Shift to Private-First:** Redesign the core loop so all adherence data (successes and misses) remains strictly private by default.
*   **Positive Reinforcement Only:** Develop new social interactions based solely on *voluntary* sharing of successes (e.g., "I hit my 7-day streak!") rather than automatic system broadcasts.
*   **Neutral Nudging:** Rewrite notification copy to be supportive and clinical (e.g., "Time for your medication") rather than judgmental or urgent. Remove any language implying failure or letting others down.
*   **Opt-In Social:** Create a granular privacy setting where users must explicitly opt-in to share *any* activity with the community. Default this to "Off."

**Phase 3: Rapid Risk & Usability Evaluation (Days 11–20)**
*   **Heuristic Safety Review:** Conduct an internal walkthrough of the new flows using a "Stigma Checklist." Verify that no screen, push notification, or email could be interpreted as shaming if seen by a third party.
*   **Micro-Usability Testing:** With limited bandwidth, run 5–7 rapid remote sessions with target users. Focus strictly on emotional response to the new notifications and the clarity of privacy controls. Ask: "How does this message make you feel?" and "Who can see this?"
*   **Edge Case Mapping:** Map scenarios where a user might accidentally expose their status (e.g., screen sharing, shared devices) and ensure the UI mitigates these risks (e.g., blurring sensitive details by default).

**Phase 4: Validation & Launch Prep (Days 21–30)**
*   **Go/No-Go Criteria:** Establish a hard rule: If any test participant reports feeling anxious, judged, or embarrassed by the new system, the feature does not launch.
*   **Final Content Sweep:** Review all microcopy, emails, and push templates one last time to ensure tone is empathetic and neutral.
*   **Launch Monitoring:** Prepare a post-launch feedback channel specifically tagged for "Safety & Comfort" to catch any unforeseen negative reactions immediately.

**Deliverable:** A revised product specification removing public shaming, a set of private-first engagement mechanics, and a pass/fail criteria list based on user emotional safety.

*Note: Given the hard deadline, prioritize disabling the harmful feature over perfecting new engagement models; a boring but safe app is preferable to a harmful one.*
*Ensure legal counsel reviews the data deletion plan for the historical shaming posts to comply with health data regulations.*
