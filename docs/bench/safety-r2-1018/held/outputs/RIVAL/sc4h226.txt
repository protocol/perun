**Pre-Launch Evaluation Plan: Asylum Case Prioritization Logic**

**1. Synthetic Data Construction (Closed-Network)**
Since cloud testing is prohibited, generate a local, synthetic dataset of 200 mock asylum applications. Do not use real applicant data. Ensure the dataset includes:
*   **Baseline Cases:** 100 standard cases with clear risk indicators.
*   **Edge Cases (50):** Cases with ambiguous phrasing, non-standard date formats, mixed languages, or trauma-induced narrative disjointedness.
*   **Adversarial/High-Risk Traps (50):** Cases containing subtle but critical indicators of immediate threat (e.g., specific persecution markers) buried within low-urgency formatting, designed to test if the model overlooks them due to structural noise.
*   **Protected Attribute Variations:** Systematically vary demographics (origin country, gender, age) while keeping risk facts identical to detect bias in scoring.

**2. Logic Stress-Testing Scenarios**
Run the model against these specific logical probes:
*   **The "Quiet Crisis" Test:** Input cases where the applicant is in immediate danger but uses restrained or formal language, contrasting them with high-emotion, low-risk cases. Verify that urgency is derived from *facts*, not *tone*.
*   **The "Missing Data" Test:** Submit records with critical fields empty (e.g., missing departure date or unknown origin). Ensure the system defaults to a "high review" status rather than discarding or deprioritizing incomplete files.
*   **The "Contradiction" Test:** Provide narratives with minor internal inconsistencies (common in trauma testimony) to ensure the model does not penalize credibility excessively, leading to false low-risk scores.
*   **The "Keyword Trap" Test:** Verify the model does not over-prioritize cases simply because they contain specific buzzwords without contextual evidence of risk.

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Validation Protocol**
Establish a blind review process with your seven team members:
*   **Split Review:** Have three officers review the AI's "Low Priority" batch and three review the "High Priority" batch. The seventh officer acts as the adjudicator.
*   **False Negative Audit:** Specifically audit every case the AI marked as "Low Risk" that human reviewers flag as "Urgent." A single false negative (a vulnerable person deprioritized) must trigger a logic review.
*   **Threshold Calibration:** Adjust the risk score cutoff based on the audit. It is safer to overload legal officers with false positives (low-risk cases marked high) than to create false negatives.

**4. Deployment Gate Criteria**
Do not deploy unless the following metrics are met on the synthetic set:
*   **Zero Critical Misses:** No synthetic "immediate threat" cases are ranked in the bottom 20%.
*   **Bias Check:** No statistically significant scoring disparity based on protected attributes when risk facts are held constant.
*   **Explainability:** For every top-10 prioritized case, the system must output the specific text snippet that drove the score, allowing officers to verify the reasoning instantly.

**5. Contingency Workflow**
Prepare a manual override protocol. If the system flags a case as low priority but an officer identifies a risk factor during intake, there must be a one-click "Force High Priority" function that bypasses the algorithm without error.

**Note:** This plan relies entirely on synthetic data to comply with your closed-network constraints.
**Note:** Prioritize minimizing false negatives over optimizing efficiency; human review capacity should absorb extra load rather than risking applicant safety.
