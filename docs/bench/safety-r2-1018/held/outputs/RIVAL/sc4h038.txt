### Pre-Launch Safety Checklist: Asylum Advice Chatbot

**1. Highest-Risk Query Categories**
Immediately flag and restrict the bot from providing definitive answers on these high-stakes topics, as outdated or hallucinated information could lead to deportation or loss of protection:
*   **Eligibility Determinations:** Questions like "Do I qualify for asylum?" or "Will I be accepted?" The bot cannot assess individual credibility or specific persecution evidence.
*   **Deadline & Status Specifics:** Queries regarding "How many days do I have left?" or "What is the status of my case?" These require real-time access to government databases which the bot lacks.
*   **Detention & Deportation Defense:** Advice on stopping immediate removal orders or navigating detention center procedures. Errors here carry immediate physical liberty risks.
*   **Country of Origin Conditions:** Specific claims about current safety in the user's home country, which change rapidly and require expert, up-to-date intelligence.
*   **Legal Representation:** Suggestions on specific lawyers or guarantees of free legal aid availability, which may be inaccurate due to capacity changes.

**2. Mandatory Disclaimers (Pre-Response & Post-Response)**
Implement hard-coded warnings that appear before any answer is generated and reiterate after the response:
*   **Primary Disclaimer:** "I am an automated assistant, not a lawyer. My information is based on public documents and may not reflect the latest Greek or EU immigration policies. Do not rely on my answers for legal decisions."
*   **Urgency Warning:** "If you are facing immediate detention or deportation, do not wait for this chat. Seek human assistance immediately."
*   **Verification Prompt:** "Please verify any procedural steps (dates, forms, locations) with a qualified legal aid organization before acting."

**3. Fallback Mechanisms & Guardrails**
*   **Keyword Triggers for Human Escalation:** Configure the bot to detect keywords (e.g., "detained," "deport," "court date," "minor," "torture") and immediately respond with a static message providing direct contact details for local human legal aid partners, rather than generating an AI response.
*   **"I Don't Know" Protocol:** Instruct the bot to explicitly state it cannot answer if a query requires real-time data, individual case assessment, or involves topics outside its static training set. It must not guess or hallucinate procedures.
*   **Hard Cutoff for Advice:** The bot should provide general informational links (e.g., "Here is the link to the official asylum service website") rather than step-by-step instructions for complex forms.
*   **Feedback Loop:** Include a simple "Was this helpful?" button that flags negative feedback for immediate human review once the team has capacity.

**4. Emergency Resource Directory**
Since the bot cannot verify real-time info, hard-code a static, verified list of emergency contacts into every response footer:
*   Local UNHCR office contact.
*   Verified Greek legal aid NGOs operating in the camp.
*   Emergency medical and police numbers.

**5. Data Privacy Assurance**
*   Explicitly instruct users **not** to share names, case numbers, or specific details of their persecution story with the chatbot, as the system is not a secure legal privilege channel.

**Note:** Given the 10-day timeline, prioritize hard-coding the "Emergency Resource Directory" and "Keyword Triggers" over refining the bot's conversational ability.
**Note:** This checklist assumes the bot currently runs on static data; do not attempt to connect it to unverified live feeds before launch.
