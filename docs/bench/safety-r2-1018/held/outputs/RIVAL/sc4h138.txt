**1. Data Audit Strategy (Representation Bias)**
Since you are restricted to existing historical databases, you must treat the data as a record of *police activity*, not necessarily *criminal activity*.
*   **Normalize by Population:** Do not use raw crime counts. Calculate crime rates per capita for each neighborhood to prevent densely populated areas from automatically appearing as high-risk.
*   **Correlate with Reporting Mechanisms:** Map crime data against the locations of police stations and historical patrol routes. High crime counts in specific zones may simply indicate higher surveillance intensity (the "over-policing" feedback loop) rather than higher criminal intent.
*   **Identify Data Voids:** Actively look for neighborhoods with low reported crime but high population density or low socioeconomic status. In biased datasets, these areas often represent under-reporting or lack of police engagement, not safety. Flag these zones as "data uncertain" rather than "low risk."

**2. Adjusting Allocation Logic**
To break the cycle of reinforcing historical bias, the allocation algorithm must be modified before deployment:
*   **Decouple from Pure Historical Volume:** Reduce the weight of historical crime counts in the predictive model. Instead of predicting where crime *was*, attempt to model risk based on static environmental factors (e.g., lighting, infrastructure) that are independent of past police presence, if such data exists within your current constraints.
*   **Implement a "Bias Cap":** Program a hard limit on how much police resources can increase in any single neighborhood week-over-week, regardless of what the algorithm suggests. This prevents sudden surges in presence that drive community tension.
*   **Randomized Patrol Elements:** Introduce a non-deterministic component to resource allocation. Dedicate a percentage of resources to be deployed randomly or to areas identified as "data uncertain" during the audit, rather than solely to historical hotspots. This helps gather unbiased data on actual crime distribution.
