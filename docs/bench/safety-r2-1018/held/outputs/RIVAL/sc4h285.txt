# Vision Recognition Module Verification Checklist (Synthetic Data)

**1. Synthetic Dataset Construction**
*   **Shape/Color Matrix:** Generate high-resolution images covering all target pill shapes (round, oval, capsule) and colors, explicitly including "near-miss" pairs (e.g., 10mg vs. 20mg of the same drug differing only by shade or imprint).
*   **Imprint Variability:** Create synthetic variations of drug imprints (text/codes) accounting for font wear, rotation angles (0–360°), and partial occlusion to simulate real-world handling.
*   **Lighting & Environment:** Render images under varying lighting conditions (glare, shadow, low contrast) and background textures found in the dispensing bin to test robustness.
*   **Defect Injection:** Introduce synthetic defects such as chipped edges, cracks, or discoloration to ensure the system rejects damaged medication rather than misidentifying it.

**2. Algorithmic Logic Verification**
*   **Strength Differentiation Test:** Run the recognition engine against paired synthetic inputs where only the strength indicator differs. Verify 100% rejection of mismatched strength labels.
*   **Confidence Threshold Calibration:** Feed ambiguous synthetic images (e.g., blurred imprints) to confirm the system defaults to "reject/unknown" rather than guessing when confidence falls below the safety threshold.
*   **Cross-Contamination Check:** Simulate scenarios where multiple pill types are present in the field of view to ensure the system isolates and identifies the target pill without confusing adjacent objects.
*   **False Positive/Negative Analysis:** Measure the rate of incorrectly accepting wrong pills (false positive) versus rejecting correct pills (false negative). Prioritize minimizing false positives to zero, even at the cost of higher false negatives.

**3. System Integration & Fail-Safes**
*   **Reject Mechanism Trigger:** Verify that every synthetic "unidentified" or "mismatch" result triggers the physical reject bin logic in the simulation.
*   **Audit Trail Logging:** Confirm that every synthetic test case generates a detailed log entry including the image hash, decision rationale, confidence score, and final action (dispense vs. reject).
*   **Emergency Stop Simulation:** Inject a sequence of rapid consecutive failures to ensure the system halts operation and flags for human review rather than continuing with reduced accuracy.

**4. Edge Case Stress Testing**
*   **Orientation Extremes:** Test pills positioned vertically, horizontally, and stacked slightly overlapping to ensure the vision module correctly segments individual units.
*   **Batch Consistency:** Run large-volume synthetic batches (e.g., 10,000 images) to detect memory leaks or performance degradation that could lead to timeout errors and skipped checks.
*   **New Drug Onboarding:** Simulate the introduction of a new synthetic pill profile to verify the update process does not degrade recognition accuracy for existing medications.

**5. Documentation & Sign-off**
*   **Traceability Matrix:** Map every synthetic test case to a specific safety requirement (e.g., "Test Case #402 verifies Requirement: No dispensing of incorrect strength").
*   **Discrepancy Review Protocol:** Define a mandatory human review step for any synthetic test where the system's confidence score is borderline, regardless of the final pass/fail status.

*Note: This checklist relies entirely on synthetic data; physical validation with actual pill samples from generic partners is mandatory before live clinic trials.*
*Ensure all synthetic data generation scripts and results are version-controlled for regulatory audit purposes.*
