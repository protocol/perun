### Strategic Mitigation Plan for Housing Secure Tenant Screening

Given the constraint that the core decision logic cannot be modified before the two-week deadline, the following plan establishes a human-in-the-loop safety net to prevent unfair rejections based on rigid eviction data.

#### 1. Manual Override Procedures
Implement a "Pending Review" workflow for any application flagged by the AI. No automated rejection letter should be sent immediately upon a positive eviction match.

*   **Trigger Condition:** Any application where the AI output is "Reject" solely due to an eviction record.
*   **Escalation Path:** Route these applications to a dedicated "Fair Housing Review Queue" accessible to your team of thirteen.
*   **Evidence Collection Protocol:** Upon escalation, the system must automatically prompt the applicant (via a secure portal link) to upload specific mitigating documentation within 48 hours:
    *   Court documents proving the eviction was dismissed or wrongful.
    *   Proof of settled debts or payment plans completed post-eviction.
    *   Character references from previous landlords or housing counselors.
*   **Review Criteria:** Reviewers will manually assess the uploaded context. If the eviction is deemed resolved, wrongful, or outdated (e.g., >5 years with clean history since), the reviewer executes a "Manual Approve" override.
*   **Audit Trail:** Every override must be logged with the reviewer's ID, the specific reason for the override, and the timestamp. This creates a defensible record of due diligence for property managers and regulators.

#### 2. Edge-Case Test Suite
Before full deployment, run the following manual test scenarios against the integrated portal to verify the override workflow functions correctly under pressure. These tests simulate real-world complexities the AI ignores.

*   **Test Case A: The Wrongful Eviction:**
    *   *Input:* Application with a recorded eviction that was legally dismissed due to landlord retaliation.
    *   *Expected AI Action:* Flag for rejection.
    *   *Expected System Action:* Trigger "Pending Review," allow document upload, and enable reviewer to override to "Approved" after viewing the dismissal order.
*   **Test Case B: The Resolved Dispute:**
    *   *Input:* Application with an eviction from 4 years ago due to job loss, followed by 48 months of perfect rental history and a paid settlement.
    *   *Expected AI Action:* Flag for rejection.
    *   *Expected System Action:* Trigger "Pending Review," allow upload of settlement proof and recent references, and enable reviewer to override to "Approved."
*   **Test Case C: The Identity Error:**
    *   *Input:* Application where the eviction record belongs to a different individual with a similar name (common in large databases).
    *   *Expected AI Action:* Flag for rejection.
    *   *Expected System Action:* Trigger "Pending Review," allow applicant to submit ID and proof of non-involvement, and enable reviewer to override to "Approved."
*   **Test Case D: High-Volume Stress Test:**
    *   *Input:* Simulate 50 simultaneous applications triggering the eviction flag.
    *   *Expected System Action:* Verify the "Fair Housing Review Queue" does not crash, notifications are sent to reviewers, and no automated rejection emails are dispatched before human review.

#### 3. Deployment Guardrails
*   **Sunset Clause:** Include a contractual addendum with property managers stating that for the first 90 days, all AI rejections are subject to mandatory human review.
*   **Daily Triage:** Hold a 15-minute daily stand-up with the review team to clear the queue and identify recurring false-positive patterns for future logic updates.

This approach satisfies the contractual deadline by deploying the tool as an initial filter rather than a final judge, ensuring compliance with fair housing principles through immediate human oversight.

*Note: Ensure all reviewers receive brief training on local fair housing laws regarding eviction records before handling the queue.*
*Note: Document all override reasons weekly to build a dataset for future logic refinement.*
