### Rapid Transparency & Fairness Audit Workflow

Given your two-week constraint and lack of manual review capacity, you cannot audit every decision. Instead, implement a **"Shadow Mode" Risk-Based Sampling** workflow. This validates the system against your existing case studies without altering the live deployment path.

**Phase 1: The "Golden Set" Reconstruction (Days 1–3)**
Select 50–100 historical case studies from your existing data that represent the full spectrum of outcomes: clear approvals, clear denials, and borderline cases. Manually label these with the *correct* decision and the *primary medical justification* based on your underwriting guidelines. This creates your "Golden Set." Do not use new data; rely strictly on these verified historical records to establish a baseline for fairness and accuracy.

**Phase 2: Automated Explanation Extraction (Days 4–7)**
Run the Golden Set through your automated underwriting system in a isolated test environment (shadow mode). For every decision generated, force the system to output a structured "Decision Packet" containing:
1.  The final verdict (Approve/Deny).
2.  The top three data factors weighted most heavily in the decision.
3.  A plain-text justification sentence generated by the model explaining *why* those factors led to the verdict.

Compare the automated verdicts against your manual labels. Any mismatch is an immediate failure. For matching verdicts, proceed to the transparency check.

**Phase 3: The "Opaque Correlation" Stress Test (Days 8–10)**
Focus specifically on the denied cases in your Golden Set. Apply a simple "Counterfactual Test" to the input data:
*   Identify the top negative factor cited (e.g., "History of Condition X").
*   Programmatically create a duplicate input where *only* that factor is removed or normalized.
*   Re-run the system.
*   **Pass Criteria:** If the decision flips from "Deny" to "Approve," the logic is transparent and causal.
*   **Fail Criteria:** If the decision remains "Deny" or flips based on an unrelated factor (e.g., zip code or age), you have detected an opaque correlation or bias. Flag these specific logic paths immediately.

**Phase 4: The "Human-in-the-Loop" Escape Hatch (Days 11–14)**
Since you cannot fix all logic errors before launch, implement a mandatory **Appeal Trigger** for the live system. Configure the system to automatically flag any decision where:
1.  The confidence score is below a specific threshold (e.g., <85%).
2.  The decision is a "Deny" driven by complex, multi-factor correlations rather than a single clear guideline violation.
3.  The input data matches patterns identified as "risky" in Phase 3.

These flagged cases are routed to a human underwriter for final review before the applicant is notified. This ensures that while 90% of cases may be instant, the high-risk, opaque decisions are caught manually, satisfying investor requirements for speed while protecting against unfair denials.

**Deliverable:** A validated Golden Set report, a list of flagged opaque logic paths, and a configured live-system filter that routes high-risk denials to human review.

*Note: This workflow isolates high-risk decisions for human review rather than attempting to fix the entire model pre-launch. Ensure all test data used is anonymized to comply with health data regulations.*
