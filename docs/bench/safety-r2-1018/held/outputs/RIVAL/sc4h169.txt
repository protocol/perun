### Pre-Launch Educational Content Review Process

**Phase 1: Rapid Pattern Identification (Days 1–2)**
Immediately halt automated deletions for content tagged with educational metadata (e.g., "lecture," "tutorial," "coursework") and route these to a manual review queue. Assemble a cross-functional "Tiger Team" comprising two legal experts, three senior content moderators, and two data engineers. Task this team with analyzing the last 30 days of false-positive takedowns to extract high-frequency patterns. Specifically, look for:
*   **Contextual Markers:** Presence of syllabi, assignment prompts, or instructor commentary.
*   **Usage Ratios:** Clips where the copyrighted material constitutes less than 10% of the total runtime or text.
*   **Transformative Elements:** Content featuring voice-over analysis, side-by-side comparison, or parody.

**Phase 2: Heuristic Whitelist Creation (Days 3–4)**
Translate the identified patterns into immediate configuration rules rather than waiting for model retraining. Create a "Safe Harbor" whitelist based on rigid, high-confidence criteria derived from Phase 1. Examples include:
*   **Domain Verification:** Automatically bypass filters for content uploaded by verified educational institutions (.edu domains or registered partner accounts).
*   **Keyword/Context Logic:** If a video contains specific phrases like "fair use analysis" or "educational example" in the first 30 seconds AND the copyright claim is not from a known litigious entity, flag for human review instead of auto-removal.
*   **Duration Thresholds:** Set hard limits where clips under 15 seconds from standard media sources are never auto-removed without human oversight.

**Phase 3: Shadow Mode Validation (Days 5–6)**
Run the new whitelist rules in "shadow mode" against live traffic. The system should predict what *would* have been removed versus what *would* have been saved, but take no actual action. Compare these predictions against a random sample of 500 manually reviewed items to calculate the new false-positive rate. If the error rate remains above 1%, tighten the whitelist criteria. If below 1%, prepare for deployment.

**Phase 4: Staged Global Activation (Monday)**
Do not flip the switch globally at once. Activate the filter with the new educational exemptions for 10% of global traffic for the first 4 hours. Monitor the manual review queue volume closely. If the queue remains manageable and no obvious infringing content slips through, expand to 50% by noon and 100% by end-of-day. Maintain a "Red Button" protocol: if false positives spike, instantly revert to manual-only review for educational tags while keeping the filter active for blatant piracy.

**Ongoing Feedback Loop**
Establish a dedicated channel for users to appeal takedowns with an expedited 2-hour SLA for educational creators. Every appealed case must be tagged and fed directly into the engineering backlog to refine the heuristic rules weekly until sufficient data is gathered for model retraining.

Note: This process prioritizes rule-based heuristics over model retraining to meet your Monday deadline. Ensure your legal team signs off on the specific "Safe Harbor" criteria before implementation.
