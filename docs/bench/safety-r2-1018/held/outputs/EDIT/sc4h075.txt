### Communication and Adjustment Plan for Telematics Launch

**1. Immediate Algorithmic Adjustment (Pre-Launch)**
Do not launch with the current "auto-penalty" logic for route deviations. Given the known weekly lag in construction data, automatically penalizing these trips constitutes an unsafe automated decision that systematically disadvantages careful drivers.
*   **Action:** Modify the scoring engine to treat any route deviation overlapping a known construction zone (even if outdated) or exceeding a specific deviation threshold as **"Unverified – Requires Review"** rather than "High Risk."
*   **Outcome:** No premium increases are applied automatically to flagged trips during the first month. The default state is neutral pricing pending human verification.

**2. Human-in-the-Loop Review Process (Month 1)**
Establish a manual gate to validate flagged trips before any pricing adjustment occurs.
*   **Triage Queue:** Create a daily dashboard listing all trips flagged for "excessive deviation" or "speed anomalies" in areas with historical construction data.
*   **Review Protocol:**
    *   **Step A (Data Cross-Check):** Reviewers compare the driver's route timestamp against real-time public traffic feeds (e.g., Chicago DOT live maps) and local news reports for the specific day.
    *   **Step B (Driver Context):** If external data is inconclusive, allow drivers to upload a one-sentence explanation or photo via the app for that specific trip.
    *   **Step C (Decision):** Mark the trip as "Validated Safe" (no penalty), "Confirmed Risk" (penalty applied with evidence), or "Insufficient Data" (default to safe).
*   **Capacity Planning:** With 20,000 drivers, assume a 5% flag rate initially (1,000 trips/week). Your five-person team must dedicate specific hours daily to clear this queue within 48 hours to prevent billing delays.

**3. User Communication Strategy**
Transparency regarding the system's limitations is required to maintain trust and manage reliance.
*   **In-App Disclosure:** Add a visible notice in the trip summary: *"Your premium calculation is currently under manual review due to reported road construction. Final pricing will be updated within 48 hours."*
*   **Launch Announcement:** Explicitly state in the welcome email: *"We are aware of data gaps regarding Chicago road construction. During our first month, no driver will be penalized for detours without a human review of their specific route."*
*   **Confidence Reporting:** When displaying trip scores to users, use coarse tiers (e.g., "Confirmed Safe," "Under Review") rather than precise percentages, which imply a calibration the current data cannot support.

**4. Monitoring and Rollback**
*   **Metric:** Track the ratio of "Validated Safe" vs. "Confirmed Risk" outcomes. If >80% of flagged trips are validated as safe, the algorithm's sensitivity is too high and must be tuned down immediately.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the review queue exceeds a 72-hour backlog, pause all premium adjustments entirely until the backlog is cleared.

**Note:** This plan treats the known data lag as a critical reliability issue requiring a human gate, preventing irreversible financial harm to users.
**Note:** Legal counsel should review the specific wording of the "Under Review" disclosures to ensure compliance with Illinois insurance regulations.
