### Immediate Action Plan: Surge Pricing Safety Gate

**1. Implement a Hard "Danger Zone" Override (Do this before launch)**
Since your pipeline cannot ingest real-time crime stats, you must hard-code a static geofence list of known high-risk areas based on historical data or local authority advisories.
*   **Rule:** If a pickup or drop-off location falls within a designated "high-risk" zone, the surge multiplier is capped at 1.0x (normal price) or subsidized, regardless of demand.
*   **Goal:** Prevent price spikes from discouraging drivers from entering or leaving these zones, eliminating the "trapping" mechanism immediately.

**2. Establish a Human-in-the-Loop Kill Switch**
Do not rely on the algorithm to self-correct during the festival.
*   **Action:** Designate one engineer as the "Safety Operator" for the festival duration.
*   **Capability:** They must have a dashboard showing active surge multipliers by zone and a single-button override to flatten pricing globally or in specific districts if user complaints spike or driver supply drops critically in any area.

**3. Deploy a "Stuck User" Detection Alert**
Create a real-time monitor for abnormal session behavior.
*   **Metric:** Flag any user session where the app is open for >5 minutes with no ride booked while located in a high-surge zone.
*   **Response:** Trigger an automated push notification offering a fixed-price ride or a safety resource link, and alert the Safety Operator to investigate potential trapping.

**4. Mandatory Transparency Disclosure**
*   **Action:** Update the app interface to explicitly state: "Prices are elevated due to high demand. In specific areas, prices may be manually adjusted for safety."
*   **Reasoning:** Users must know the pricing is dynamic and that safety overrides exist; hiding this creates deceptive patterns.

**5. Post-Launch Data Review Protocol**
*   **Action:** Schedule a review 2 hours after launch and again at the end of the festival.
*   **Focus:** Analyze trips where the surge multiplier was >2.0x in known low-supply areas. If any users were priced out of leaving a dangerous area, document this as a critical failure for the next iteration.

***

**Note:** This plan treats the inability to ingest real-time crime data as a known limitation; do not claim the system is "safe" or "unbiased" without testing these overrides. The hard-coded geofence is a temporary control, not a permanent solution.
