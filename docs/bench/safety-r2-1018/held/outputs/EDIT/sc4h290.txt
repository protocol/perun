**BLUF:** Do not authorize field operations or finalize the validation workflow until a human-in-the-loop gate is mandated for all deterrent deployments and a formal legal review confirms treaty compliance; synthetic data alone cannot validate safety for this high-stakes application.

**Immediate Action Plan**

1.  **Mandate Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:**
    The system must not autonomously deploy deterrents. Given the irreversible physical harm and legal consequences of wrongful deployment, the "final operational readiness" status is invalid without a hard constraint requiring explicit human agent confirmation before any action executes. Full automation of this capability is the primary failure mode to prevent.

2.  **Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition:**
    Record the specific harms: **Bias** (systematic misidentification of specific demographics as "illegal"), **Hallucination as Fact** (synthetic patterns triggering false positives in real terrain), and **Unsafe Automation** (physical injury to hikers). The worst-case scenario is the permanent injury or death of a lawful traveler due to algorithmic error. This inventory must be signed off by the project owner, not just engineering.

3.  **Simulation Protocol Limitations (The "UNVERIFIED" Reality):**
    You cannot rigorously test the distinction between "hikers" and "illegal crossers" using only synthetic data because the defining features often rely on context, intent, and nuanced behavioral cues absent in simulation.
    *   **Declare Residual Risk:** Any metric derived solely from synthetic data regarding real-world human identification must be labeled **UNVERIFIED**. Do not fabricate an accuracy rate (e.g., "99% safe") for the safety dossier.
    *   **Adversarial Probes:** Design simulation rows that specifically target edge cases where hikers mimic crossing behaviors (e.g., off-trail movement, carrying large packs) and where crossers mimic hikers. Include "positive controls" (known bad actors in sim) and "negative controls" (known lawful hikers in sim).
    *   **Stop Condition:** Define a strict failure threshold. If the simulation shows *any* false positive leading to a deterrent trigger in the loop, the launch is blocked.

4.  **Transparency Artifacts:**
    Draft a **Model Card** and **Data Card** immediately. These must explicitly state:
    *   **Intended Use:** Assistance tool only; never autonomous enforcement.
    *   **Data Provenance:** 100% synthetic visual data; no real-world human subjects used.
    *   **Limitations:** Performance on real human subjects is unmeasured and unknown. Subgroup performance (age, skin tone, clothing) is unverified.
    *   **Owner:** Name the specific individual accountable for errors.

5.  **Legal & Treaty Routing:**
    The constraint regarding international treaties is a legal determination, not an engineering one. Route the question of whether *simulated* validation satisfies treaty obligations for "live field tests" directly to legal counsel. Do not assume the simulation suffices; the treaty may prohibit deployment entirely without specific types of human-in-the-loop verification that you have not yet defined.

**Required Deliverable Before Launch:**
A revised operational protocol where the drone *only* flags and tracks, requiring a human agent to visually verify and authorize any deterrent. Without this, the system is unsafe to ship regardless of simulation results.
