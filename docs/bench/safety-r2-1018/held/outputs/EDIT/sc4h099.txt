### Pre-Launch Review Process: Vintage-Car Valuation AI

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Definition**
Before launch, formally record the primary risk: **Systematic Undervaluation due to Data Gaps**.
*   **Harm:** Sellers of unique/rare vehicles receive estimates significantly below market reality because the model lacks recent private sale records.
*   **Affected Party:** Vehicle sellers (financial loss), collectors (missed opportunities), platform credibility.
*   **Worst Case:** A seller accepts a low-ball offer based on your AI's "authoritative" estimate, losing tens or hundreds of thousands of pounds.

**2. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate for High-Stakes Items**
Implement a mandatory human review gate for any valuation exceeding a specific threshold or flagged as "rare/unique."
*   **Trigger:** Any estimated value > £X (e.g., £50k) OR any model identified as having <5 comparable auction records in the training set.
*   **Action:** The AI generates a *draft* valuation with a "Pending Expert Review" status. It does not display a final number to the user yet.
*   **Expert Workflow:** A human specialist reviews the draft, incorporates knowledge of recent private sales (the missing data), and either approves, adjusts, or rejects the estimate.
*   **Output:** Only the expert-validated figure is released to the user, clearly marked as "Expert Verified."

**3. Feedback Loop & Data Correction**
Turn the expert review process into a mechanism to close the data gap.
*   **Capture:** When an expert adjusts a valuation, they must tag the reason (e.g., "Recent private sale not in dataset," "Unique restoration quality").
*   **Log:** Store these adjustments in a "Private Sale Proxy" database.
*   **Retraining/Calibration:** Before general public launch, use this logged data to:
    *   Create a correction factor for the model's output on rare items.
    *   Explicitly retrain or fine-tune the model on these expert-adjusted cases if volume permits.
*   **Transparency Artifact (Model Card):** Publish a simple document stating: "Current limitations include lack of recent private sale data. High-value estimates undergo mandatory human validation. Intended for preliminary guidance only."

**4. Uncertainty Disclosure**
Do not present the AI's raw confidence score (e.g., "87% confident") as precision.
*   **Implementation:** Use defined coarse tiers for unverified outputs: "Estimate based on public auctions only" vs. "Estimate includes expert review of private market trends."
*   **User Interface:** Clearly distinguish between "AI Draft" and "Expert Validated" results. Never allow the AI to auto-finalize a high-stakes valuation without the human gate.

**5. Governance & Ownership**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the "Valuation Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing the HITL logs weekly.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the rate of expert adjustments exceeds a set threshold (e.g., >20% of high-value items require >10% adjustment), pause public launch and recalibrate the model.

**Notes**
*   Legal counsel should review whether these valuations constitute "regulated financial advice" in the UK before public launch.
*   Ensure the "Expert Verified" label is strictly reserved for items that actually passed the human gate to avoid deceptive patterns.
