**Pre-Launch Safety Review Plan: Auto-Trading Bot for Minors**

**1. Harm Inventory & Failure Modes**
Before launch, explicitly document the following high-stakes risks where the bot could exceed regulatory daily loss limits:
*   **Latency-Induced Overshoot:** Market volatility spikes faster than the decision loop executes, causing trades to settle beyond the hard-coded cap before the kill-switch triggers.
*   **Simulation-to-Reality Gap:** Historical simulation data lacks "black swan" micro-second spikes common in crypto/volatile equities, leading to false confidence in the cap's effectiveness.
*   **Compound Exposure:** The bot treats multiple micro-investments as isolated events, allowing aggregate losses across a user's portfolio to exceed the single-user daily limit.
*   **Data Feed Manipulation:** Spoofed or delayed market data feeds trick the bot into executing trades that appear safe locally but violate limits in reality.
*   **Minor-Specific Vulnerability:** Algorithms optimized for adult risk tolerances inadvertently pushing higher-volatility assets to teenage users, violating fiduciary duties for minors.

**2. Measurement Strategy: Runnable Test Rows**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk using these specific probes. Each row requires a pass/fail threshold set by your compliance owner.

| Harm Scenario | Probe Set (Inputs) | Positive Control (Known Bad) | Negative Control (Known Good) | Metric & Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Latency Overshoot** | Inject synthetic market spikes with <10ms duration into the decision loop. | Spike magnitude exceeds cap by 5%; system must reject or halt. | Stable market data; system executes normal micro-trades. | **Max Loss Deviation.** Stop launch if *any* trade exceeds cap by >0.01%. |
| **Aggregate Exposure** | Simulate 50 concurrent micro-trades per user during a crash. | Total portfolio loss > Daily Cap. | Single trade execution within cap. | **Aggregate Loss Ratio.** Stop launch if total loss > 99% of cap. |
| **Data Integrity** | Feed delayed/stale price data during high volatility. | Bot trades on stale price resulting in loss > Cap. | Bot halts trading on data latency warning. | **Stale-Data Execution Count.** Stop launch if >0 trades execute on data >2s old. |
| **Minor Suitability** | Run bot against profiles flagged as "under 18" with low risk tolerance. | Bot recommends/executes high-volatility assets unsuitable for minors. | Bot restricts to low-volatility savings vehicles. | **Unsuitable Asset Rate.** Stop launch if >0% of recommendations violate minor safeguards. |

**3. Management Controls & Human Gates**
*   **Hard-Code Verification:** The regulatory cap must be enforced at the *execution layer*, not just the *decision layer*. If the decision engine says "buy" but the execution wrapper detects a potential breach, the trade must hard-fail.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For the beta launch, **disable full autonomy** for any account approaching 80% of their daily loss limit. Require explicit human approval (or guardian consent) for the final 20% of allowable loss or any trade exceeding a specific volatility threshold. Full automation of irreversible financial loss for minors is the primary failure mode to prevent.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not display precise confidence percentages (e.g., "95% safe") to teenage users. Use defined coarse tiers: **"Verified Limit," "Approaching Limit," "Trading Halted."**
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a **Model Card** detailing: intended use (micro-savings for minors), known limitations (cannot guarantee zero loss during extreme flash crashes), and subgroup performance (specifically for under-18 profiles). Absence of this card is a critical transparency gap.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name a specific Product Owner accountable for "Daily Limit Breaches."
*   **Legal Determination:** You asked about regulatory caps; however, whether this feature constitutes a "regulated automated decision" under Singapore's MAS guidelines or requires specific AI disclosures for minors is a **legal determination**. Route this immediately to counsel. Do not assume the hard-code satisfies all fiduciary duties for minors.
*   **Status of Metrics:** Any safety metric not generated by the tests above is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% breach rate" for marketing materials.

**Note:** Two weeks is insufficient to prove safety against all market conditions; prioritize the "Latency Overshoot" and "Minor Suitability" tests first.
**Note:** If the "Human-in-the-Loop" gate cannot be implemented before launch, the feature should not ship to minors.
