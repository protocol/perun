### Pre-Launch Safety Review Plan: Automated Insulin Dosing

**1. Harm Inventory: Key Risk Categories**
Before submission, explicitly document the following potential harms specific to automated dosing for vulnerable users. Do not leave any category blank; if a risk is deemed low, record the justification.
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions:** The system executes irreversible physiological changes (insulin delivery) without human confirmation. The worst case is severe hypoglycemia during rapid physiological shifts (e.g., exercise, illness) where the algorithm lags or over-corrects.
*   **Hallucination Surfaced as Fact:** The algorithm presents a predicted glucose trend or recommended dose with high confidence despite insufficient or conflicting sensor data, leading the user to trust a fabricated certainty.
*   **Bias and Unfair Outcomes:** The model systematically under-performs for specific subgroups (e.g., pediatric patients, specific BMI ranges, or skin tones affecting sensor accuracy) due to gaps in the historical training data.
*   **Over-Reliance and Automation Bias:** The interface design encourages patients or clinicians to accept the automated dose without independent verification, specifically when the system fails to display uncertainty.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Users are unaware that the dosage recommendation is machine-generated and probabilistic, potentially mistaking it for a clinically verified absolute.

**2. Evidence Checklist for Due Diligence**
Gather the following artifacts to demonstrate rigorous internal review. These items show *process* and *residual risk awareness* without certifying safety or fabricating error rates.

*   **Runnable Evaluation Plan (One Row Per Harm):**
    *   Define specific probe sets for each risk above (e.g., "Rapid GlDrop Simulation" for high-stakes automation).
    *   Include known-bad positive controls (inputs that should trigger a safe fail) and known-good negative controls.
    *   Specify metrics and **owner-set stop conditions** (e.g., "If >0 instances of unmitigated overdose in simulation, launch blocks").
    *   *Note:* Mark any unmeasured metric as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% error rate."

*   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate Protocol:**
    *   Document the specific mechanism requiring explicit human confirmation for any dose adjustment exceeding a defined safety threshold or occurring during detected sensor instability.
    *   Evidence must show that full autonomous execution of high-risk actions is disabled by default until this gate is passed.

*   **Uncertainty Communication Artifact:**
    *   Draft the user-facing display logic that renders confidence as **defined coarse tiers** (e.g., "Confirmed," "Corroborated," "Unverified") rather than raw percentages (e.g., "87% confident").
    *   Include the explicit definition for each tier to ensure consistent interpretation by patients and clinicians.

*   **Transparency Card (Model/Data Card):**
    *   Create a versioned document listing: intended use, out-of-scope scenarios, data provenance (historical patient data sources), known limitations (e.g., "performance unverified during extreme exertion"), and the specific owner of the model.
    *   Ensure no unmeasured performance claims appear in this card.

*   **Legal and Regulatory Routing Memo:**
    *   A formal record stating that questions regarding mandatory AI disclosure or regulated decision status under the **EU AI Act** or local UK health regulations have been routed to legal counsel.
    *   This memo must name the regimes considered but explicitly state that no binding determination is made by the engineering team.

**3. Governance Assignment**
*   Assign a named owner responsible for re-reviewing these harms if the model, data, or clinical protocol changes prior to or during the trial.

***

**Note:** This plan structures your due diligence by mapping risks and defining measurement protocols; it does not certify the system as safe.
**Note:** Any legal determination regarding mandatory disclosures must be finalized by counsel before dossier submission.
