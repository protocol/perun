### Risk Assessment Plan: Drone Impact on Wildlife Behavior

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Primary Harm:** Ecological disruption causing nesting abandonment or population decline in sensitive bird species due to drone noise and flight patterns.
*   **Affected Parties:** Protected bird populations, ecosystem integrity, and long-term conservation goals.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Irreversible local population collapse in a protected zone, leading to grant revocation, legal liability, and permanent loss of access to reserves.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Over-reliance by rangers on automated tracking data that misses stress behaviors not visible in standard telemetry.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Given the restricted access during breeding seasons, you cannot rely solely on live field trials for the initial safety gate. Execute the following runnable test rows:

*   **Test Row A: Simulated Disturbance (Proxy Testing)**
    *   *Probe:* Use archival audio/visual data of bird stress responses combined with recorded drone signatures at varying distances/altitudes.
    *   *Controls:* Positive (known disturbing noise), Negative (ambient wind/nature sounds).
    *   *Metric:* Predicted stress index vs. established biological thresholds.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If simulated stress exceeds 10% above baseline for any sensitive species, halt field deployment in that zone.
*   **Test Row B: Staged Micro-Pilot (Limited Field Access)**
    *   *Probe:* Deploy drones at maximum permitted distance from *non-critical* zones first, gradually reducing distance only if no behavioral change is observed.
    *   *Metric:* Direct observation of flush rates (birds taking flight) and nesting continuity.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any observed nesting abandonment triggers immediate rollback to the previous safe distance.
*   **Test Row C: Algorithmic Bias Check**
    *   *Probe:* Verify AI tracking does not systematically miss distressed behaviors (e.g., erratic flight) compared to calm movement.
    *   *Metric:* False negative rate on stress indicators.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Failure to detect known stress markers in test footage blocks autonomous mode.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Do not enable fully autonomous flight paths near known nesting sites. Require explicit ranger confirmation for any flight plan entering a "sensitive" geo-fenced zone. The drone must not auto-correct its path closer to a nest without human approval.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** The system must display confidence tiers (e.g., "Confirmed Sighting" vs. "Probable Movement") rather than precise percentages. If the AI is unsure if a bird is nesting, it must default to "Nesting Assumed" and enforce wider separation distances.
*   **Kill Switch & Reversibility:** Implement an immediate "Hover and Land" command accessible to rangers if unexpected agitation occurs. All flight logs must be immutable for post-incident review.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a "Mission Card" for each deployment zone documenting the specific species present, the tested safe distances, and the known limitations of the AI in those specific weather/lighting conditions.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific Conservation Biologist as the "Safety Owner" who must sign off on each new zone expansion.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any change in drone firmware, noise profile, or target species list requires a fresh risk assessment before the next flight.

**Note:** Since legal duties regarding protected species vary by jurisdiction, route the question of regulatory compliance for "automated disturbance" to legal counsel immediately; do not assume the pilot grant exempts you from wildlife protection laws. Do not fabricate a "0% disturbance" rate; report residual risk as "Unverified" until Field Test Row B is successfully completed.
