### Immediate Action Plan: Suitability Verification Roadmap

**1. Establish a Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Effective Immediately)**
Do not allow the bot to execute trades or finalize high-volatility recommendations for users aged 50+ without explicit human review or a secondary confirmation step. Given the irreversible nature of financial loss, fully automated execution of high-risk strategies for this demographic is unsafe. Implement a rule where any recommendation exceeding a defined volatility threshold for users within 10 years of retirement triggers a "Pending Review" status requiring manual approval by your internal team before being shown to the user.

**2. Construct the Harm Inventory & Stress Tests**
Create a specific test suite targeting the failure mode: "High-risk assets recommended to near-retirement users." Run these internal probes before any public release:

*   **Positive Control Probe:** Input profiles for users aged 55, 60, and 65 with "Low" or "Moderate" risk tolerance. Verify the system *never* outputs high-volatility assets (e.g., leveraged ETFs, crypto, speculative small-caps). If it does, this is a critical failure.
*   **Negative Control Probe:** Input profiles for users aged 25 with "High" risk tolerance. Verify the system *does* allow higher volatility assets here to ensure you haven't over-constrained the model for everyone.
*   **Adversarial Edge Cases:** Test users aged 58–62 who select "High" risk tolerance due to UI confusion or misunderstanding. The system should flag this mismatch (age vs. selected risk) and present a warning or require an explicit "I understand the risks" acknowledgment, rather than silently complying.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Define a "Suitability Violation Rate." For the 50+ low/moderate risk segment, the pass threshold must be **zero violations**. Any single instance of an inappropriate high-risk recommendation is a stop condition for launch.

**3. Define Uncertainty and Disclosure**
Do not present recommendations as absolute facts. If the model's confidence in a specific asset's suitability is not mathematically proven, label the output with a coarse tier such as "Unverified" or "Requires Review" rather than a false-precision percentage. Include a clear disclosure stating that the advice is machine-generated and has not been vetted by a certified financial planner, especially given the pre-launch status.

**4. Documentation Artifact (Model Card)**
Draft a one-page "System Card" for your internal team and beta testers. It must list:
*   **Intended Use:** Preliminary retirement planning for users 50+.
*   **Known Limitations:** The model may hallucinate asset performance or misalign risk profiles; it is not a fiduciary.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing flagged recommendations.
*   **Status:** Mark all suitability metrics as **UNVERIFIED** until the stress tests above are run and passed. Do not invent a safety percentage.

**5. Legal and Compliance Routing**
While budget constraints prevent hiring external consultants, you must internally acknowledge that providing personalized investment advice likely triggers regulatory obligations (e.g., fiduciary duty, suitability rules). This roadmap mitigates technical risk but does not resolve legal liability. You must route the question of "Is this launch legally permissible without external compliance sign-off?" to legal counsel immediately. If counsel cannot be engaged, the safest control is to delay the public release or restrict the bot to "educational scenarios" only, explicitly disabling real-money execution.

**Note:** This plan addresses product safety controls; it does not certify the system as compliant or unbiased.
The "zero violation" threshold for high-risk recommendations to older users is a non-negotiable floor for launch readiness.
