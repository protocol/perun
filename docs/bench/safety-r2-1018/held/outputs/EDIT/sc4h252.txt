**Immediate Halt and Risk Declaration**
Do not launch the application in its current state. Deploying a contact tracing app that collects precise location data from millions of users without advanced encryption and with integrated third-party advertising SDKs creates an unacceptable risk of mass surveillance and data leakage. The combination of sensitive health-adjacent movement data, missing encryption, and ad-network integration violates fundamental safety principles for high-stakes automated systems. No amount of pipeline auditing can safely mitigate the risk of data exfiltration through an advertising SDK when the underlying transport and storage lack advanced encryption.

**Required Remediation Plan (Pre-Launch)**
1.  **Remove Third-Party Advertising SDKs Immediately:** Advertising networks have no authorized purpose in a public health contact tracing tool. Their presence introduces an uncontrollable data egress point. You must strip these SDKs entirely before any further testing. There is no configuration setting that safely prevents an ad network from harvesting location data if the SDK is present.
2.  **Implement Encryption Before Data Collection:** You cannot audit a pipeline to ensure safety if the data is unencrypted in transit or at rest. Licensing delays do not justify bypassing encryption for sensitive location data. You must utilize open-source, standard encryption libraries (e.g., Signal Protocol, libsodium) that do not require paid licensing to secure the data pipeline immediately.
3.  **Establish a Human-in-the-Loop Gate for Data Access:** Automated access to sensitive movement patterns by "health officials" must not be unconditional. Implement a strict access control layer where data retrieval requires explicit, logged human authorization and justification, rather than automated API access.
4.  **Conduct a Red-Team Data Flow Audit:** Once encryption is active and ad SDKs are removed, perform an adversarial audit. Attempt to leak data using standard exfiltration techniques. Verify that no data leaves the device or server except through the encrypted, authorized channel.
5.  **Publish a Transparency Artifact:** Create a data card detailing exactly what data is collected, why it is needed, who has access, and the specific encryption standards used. Explicitly state that no data is shared with advertisers.

**Constraint Reality Check**
Your ten-day government mandate cannot override the physical reality that unencrypted sensitive data shared with ad networks will be compromised. If you cannot implement encryption and remove the SDKs within the remaining time, the only safe path is to delay the launch or drastically reduce the data scope (e.g., using on-device Bluetooth matching only, with no central location collection). Launching as described invites catastrophic harm to user privacy and public trust.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes harm prevention over schedule; launching without these fixes constitutes a failure of duty to the users. Legal counsel must review the removal of ad SDKs and encryption standards to ensure compliance with health data regulations.
