**Immediate Safety Plan: Dietary Supplement Recommender Pilot**

Given the high-stakes risk of dangerous drug-supplement interactions and the confirmed absence of a verified interaction database, **you cannot safely launch this feature to the public, even with disclaimers.** A disclaimer does not mitigate the harm of an AI confidently suggesting a lethal combination; it only shifts legal liability after the damage occurs. The following controls are the mandatory floor to prevent immediate physical harm while operating under your budget constraints.

**1. Implement a Hard Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
Because the system cannot verify safety autonomously, **fully automated recommendations must be disabled.**
*   **Action:** The system must generate a "Draft Recommendation" that is **never** shown directly to the user.
*   **Process:** Every draft must be reviewed and explicitly approved by a qualified human (pharmacist or clinician) before being released to the user.
*   **Constraint:** If you cannot staff a human reviewer for every pilot query, you must pause the pilot. There is no safe middle ground for irreversible health actions without verification.

**2. Replace Confidence Scores with Defined Uncertainty Tiers**
Do not display percentages (e.g., "90% match") as this implies a precision you do not possess. Instead, surface the limitation explicitly using coarse, defined tiers:
*   **"Unverified – Requires Review":** Used for all outputs when medication lists are present.
*   **"General Wellness Only":** Used only if the user explicitly confirms they are taking *no* prescription medications.
*   **Visual Cue:** Pair these text tiers with a distinct, colorblind-safe icon (e.g., an amber caution symbol) to signal that the output is not a finalized medical directive.

**3. Mandatory User Verification & Disclosure Steps**
Before a user can submit a profile, enforce the following friction points:
*   **Explicit Medication Declaration:** Require a mandatory checkbox: *"I confirm I have listed ALL prescription medications I am currently taking. I understand this system cannot check for drug interactions without a licensed database."*
*   **Prominent AI Disclosure:** Place a visible banner at the point of decision: *"This is an AI-generated suggestion based on limited data. It is NOT medical advice and has NOT been checked for dangerous drug interactions by a computer system. A human review is required."*
*   **Opt-In for High-Risk Profiles:** If a user lists any condition or medication keyword, the system must block immediate results and state: *"Due to potential safety risks, your profile requires manual review. Results will be delayed."*

**4. Harm Inventory & Measurement Plan**
You must document the specific risk: **Hallucination surfaced as fact** leading to **unsafe automation of high-stakes actions**.
*   **Metric:** Do not claim a "0% error rate." Mark the interaction safety metric as **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Test:** Run adversarial probes using known dangerous pairs (e.g., St. John's Wort + SSRIs, Vitamin K + Warfarin). If the system suggests the supplement without flagging the risk for human review, the gate has failed.
*   **Stop Condition:** Any instance where a dangerous interaction is not caught by the human gate triggers an immediate rollback of public access.

**5. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner:** Name a specific individual responsible for reviewing every pilot output.
*   **Legal Duty:** Whether this automated decision falls under specific health regulations (e.g., TGA in Australia) or requires specific statutory disclosures is a legal determination. **Route this immediately to counsel.** Do not rely on internal assumptions about "pilot" status exempting you from health safety laws.

**Summary of Required Artifacts Before Launch:**
1.  A workflow diagram showing the **mandatory human review step** blocking all user-facing outputs.
2.  A **Model Card** stating the intended use is "Drafting assistance only," listing "Drug Interaction Checking" as a known limitation, and marking safety performance as **UNVERIFIED**.
3.  Signed confirmation from legal counsel regarding regulatory obligations for health-related AI in your jurisdiction.

**Note:** This plan treats the lack of a database as a critical failure mode that requires a process control (human review), not a text control (disclaimer).
**Note:** Never certify the system as "safe"; report the residual risk that a human reviewer might also miss an interaction.
