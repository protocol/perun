### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Manual Verification & Disclosure

**1. Manual Verification Process (Human-in-the-Loop Gate)**
Given the strict prohibition on algorithmic changes and the high-stakes nature of financial quotes for grieving families, full automation of price generation is unsafe until the audit completes. Implement the following immediate workflow:

*   **Stop Condition:** No quote exceeding the median market rate for Chicago funeral services (or a predefined internal threshold based on historical data) may be sent to a family automatically.
*   **Mandatory Human Review:** Every generated quote must be reviewed by a licensed funeral director or senior pricing specialist before presentation.
    *   **Step A:** The system flags any quote that deviates >10% from the package baseline or triggers known bug patterns (e.g., specific service combinations).
    *   **Step B:** The reviewer manually validates the line items against the official price list.
    *   **Step C:** The reviewer signs off (digitally or via log entry) confirming the total is accurate before the quote is emailed or printed for the family.
*   **Fallback Protocol:** If the system generates a clearly erroneous figure (e.g., double the expected cost), the staff must bypass the tool entirely and calculate the quote using the static price list until the bug is resolved post-audit.

**2. User Disclosure Statement**
Transparency is required to manage expectations and maintain trust during this soft-launch phase. Place the following notice clearly on the estimation interface and any preliminary documents:

> "Important Notice Regarding Your Estimate:
> Evergreen Memorials is currently soft-launching a new pricing estimation tool to better serve families in Chicago. While we strive for accuracy, this automated system is currently undergoing a mandatory 30-day compliance audit.
>
> **Your Protection:** To ensure financial fairness and prevent errors, **no final price quote is binding until it has been personally verified and confirmed by a licensed Evergreen Memorial director.** Any estimate you receive initially is provisional. We will contact you within [X] hours to confirm the final, audited pricing before any services are secured or payments are requested. If you see a price that seems incorrect, please call us immediately at [Phone Number]."

**3. Risk Inventory & Next Steps**
*   **Identified Harm:** Financial harm via inflated quotes (Bias/Unfair Outcome); Emotional distress due to errors during bereavement.
*   **Mitigation:** The human gate prevents irreversible financial commitment based on buggy data. The disclosure manages reliance on the initial output.
*   **Action:** Log every instance where the manual review corrects the automated quote. This data serves as the "harm inventory" required for your post-audit report to demonstrate the bug's impact and verify the fix.

**Note:** This plan treats the pricing engine as "UNVERIFIED" for high-stakes decisions until the audit passes, adhering to safety floors that cannot be relaxed by deadline pressure. Legal counsel should review the specific wording of the disclosure to ensure it meets Illinois consumer protection standards.
