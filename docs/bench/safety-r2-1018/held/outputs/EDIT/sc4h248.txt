### Stress-Test Protocol: Power Instability & Fail-Safe Verification

**Objective:** Verify that power fluctuations during storm conditions trigger a "Fail-Secure" state (lock remains engaged) and never a "Fail-Open" state (unauthorized entry), within the two-week pre-storm window.

**Phase 1: Lab Simulation (Days 1–7)**
*   **Setup:** Connect lock units to a programmable AC power supply capable of simulating brownouts (voltage drops), blackouts (total loss), and surges. Isolate units from the cloud to test local logic first, then re-enable for cloud-dependency tests.
*   **Test Scenarios:**
    1.  **Sudden Cut:** Immediate power loss during an "unlock" command execution.
    2.  **Brownout Ramp:** Gradual voltage reduction from 100% to 0% over 5 seconds while idle and during active verification.
    3.  **Surge & Flicker:** Rapid on/off cycling (simulating lightning or grid instability) during the handshake process.
    4.  **Battery Backup Transition:** If applicable, simulate main power failure to verify seamless switch to backup without unlocking.
*   **Success Criteria:** In 100% of trials, the physical bolt must remain extended or immediately retract to a locked position upon power anomaly. Any instance of the bolt retracting without a valid, completed authentication sequence is a critical failure.

**Phase 2: Controlled Field Pilot (Days 8–12)**
*   **Selection:** Deploy 10 units in a controlled indoor environment that mimics outdoor exposure (temperature/humidity) but remains safe from actual storm hazards.
*   **Execution:** Induce power faults using portable generators and interruption devices while attempting authorized and unauthorized entry attempts simultaneously.
*   **Cloud Latency Injection:** Simulate cloud verification delays or timeouts caused by network instability typical of storms. Ensure the local device defaults to "Deny Access" when cloud confirmation cannot be received within the timeout window.

**Phase 3: Data Review & Go/No-Go Decision (Days 13–14)**
*   **Analysis:** Aggregate logs focusing on "state transitions" during power events.
*   **Decision Gate:**
    *   **Go:** Zero instances of unauthorized entry during power faults.
    *   **No-Go:** Any single instance of the lock opening without valid verification. This requires an immediate hardware or firmware revision before any deployment.

**Safety Constraint:** Do not conduct live field testing on occupied homes during actual storm conditions until the lab and controlled pilot phases are passed with zero failures.

**Note:** This protocol prioritizes physical safety over feature completeness; if the fail-safe mechanism cannot be guaranteed under simulated stress, the product must not ship until resolved.
**Note:** Ensure all test data is logged immutably to serve as a safety audit trail for future liability assessments.
