### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Rental Pricing AI

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, we must explicitly record the following risks for your specific context:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The tool suggests inflated prices in low-supply, vulnerable neighborhoods because public listing data ignores informal caps or rent-control nuances, disproportionately harming low-income tenants.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system presents a suggested price as a definitive "market rate" despite data gaps, leading landlords to believe the figure is verified when it is an extrapolation.
*   **Over-reliance:** Landlords accept the AI suggestion without question due to the "optimization" framing, ignoring local knowledge that contradicts the output.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Landlords are unaware the price is machine-generated based on limited public data, not a comprehensive market analysis.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** If the tool auto-populates lease agreements or listing sites with these prices, it executes a financially impactful action without human review.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
We cannot certify the system is "unbiased." Instead, execute these specific tests before the deadline:

| Harm Scenario | Probe Set & Controls | Metric | Pass Threshold | Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Inflation in Low-Supply Areas** | **Inputs:** Listings from 3 known vulnerable neighborhoods with low inventory.<br>**Positive Control:** Input data artificially stripped of informal listing context.<br>**Negative Control:** Input data including known rent-capped units. | % of suggested prices exceeding the 90th percentile of *actual* signed leases (not just ask prices) in those zones. | Owner-defined (e.g., <5% of suggestions exceed cap). | **HALT LAUNCH** if >threshold in any vulnerable zone. |
| **Data Gap Hallucination** | **Inputs:** Neighborhoods with <10 public listings in the last 30 days.<br>**Control:** Compare AI confidence vs. data density. | Frequency of "High Confidence" labels when data density is critical low. | 0% "High Confidence" on sparse data. | **HALT LAUNCH** if system claims certainty on sparse data. |
| **Injection/Manipulation** | **Inputs:** Fake listings with extreme prices injected into the source feed. | Impact of 5% fake data on overall neighborhood price suggestions. | <2% shift in suggested median price. | **HALT LAUNCH** if tool is easily manipulated by bad actors. |

*Residual Risk Report:* Any metric not tested due to time constraints must be labeled **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% bias" claim for your safety page.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Given the hard deadline and high stakes for tenants, **disable fully automated pricing**. The tool must output a *suggestion* that requires explicit landlord confirmation before publishing to a listing platform. This is non-negotiable for irreversible financial actions.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw percentages (e.g., "87% accurate"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Based on >50 recent comparable signed leases.
    *   *Corroborated:* Based on 10–50 comparables.
    *   *Unverified:* Based on <10 comparables or only public asks (display this prominently with a warning).
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly state to the landlord: "This price is an AI estimate based on public listings only and may not reflect informal market dynamics or rent controls."
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page **Model Card** for internal and partner use. It must list: intended use (rental suggestions), limitations (excludes informal market), known failure modes (inflation in low-supply areas), and the owner of the model.

**4. Governance**
Assign one team member as the explicit owner of these output harms. If the data sources or the model logic change post-launch, re-run the "Inflation" test row immediately.

**Legal Note:** Whether this tool triggers specific regulatory disclosures under local tenancy laws or the EU AI Act (if applicable to your investors) is a legal determination. Route this specific question to counsel immediately; do not rely on this technical plan for legal compliance.

*Note: This plan prioritizes a human gate and uncertainty disclosure to meet your deadline while preventing immediate harm. It does not certify the system as safe.*
