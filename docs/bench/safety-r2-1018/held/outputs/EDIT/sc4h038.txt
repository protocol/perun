### Pre-Launch Safety Checklist: Asylum Advice Chatbot Pilot

**1. Highest-Risk Query Inventory (Block or Gate These)**
Do not allow the bot to provide final, actionable answers on these topics without human review. These errors carry irreversible consequences (deportation, detention, loss of status):
*   **Eligibility Determinations:** "Am I eligible for asylum?" or "Do I qualify for subsidiary protection?" (Risk: False negative causes user to not apply; false positive creates false hope).
*   **Deadline & Status Specifics:** "Has my application been processed?" or "What is the exact deadline for *my* case?" (Risk: Bot lacks real-time access to individual files or policy changes).
*   **Credible Fear/Persecution Details:** Questions requiring assessment of specific threats or evidence sufficiency. (Risk: Bot cannot evaluate nuance; hallucinated advice could lead to inconsistent testimony).
*   **Appeals & Rejections:** Advice on how to appeal a denied case. (Risk: High complexity; error leads to permanent loss of rights).

**2. Mandatory Fallback Mechanisms (Human-in-the-Loop)**
*   **The "Uncertainty Gate":** Configure the bot to detect high-stakes keywords (e.g., "deny," "appeal," "deadline," "eligible"). If detected, the bot must **refuse** to give a definitive answer and instead output: *"I cannot provide a legal determination on this specific issue. This requires a human expert. Please visit [Location/Time] for free legal aid."*
*   **Data Freshness Limit:** Since the bot lacks updated policies, it must prepend every response with a version stamp: *"Based on public documents available up to [Date]. Policies may have changed."*
*   **Hard Stop for Irreversible Actions:** The bot must never instruct a user to submit a form, sign a document, or miss a deadline based solely on its output. It must always direct the user to a verified human channel for final submission steps.

**3. Required Disclaimers (Visible Before First Interaction)**
*   **Non-Lawyer Status:** "I am an AI assistant, not a lawyer. My answers are for general information only and do not constitute legal advice or create an attorney-client relationship."
*   **Verification Warning:** "Immigration laws change frequently. My training data may not include the most recent Greek or EU policy updates. Always verify critical information with a qualified legal expert before acting."
*   **Emergency Protocol:** "If you are in immediate danger or facing imminent deportation, do not rely on this chat. Contact [Local Emergency Legal Aid Number/Organization] immediately."

**4. Measurement & Monitoring Plan (Run During Pilot)**
*   **Red-Team Probe Set:** Before launch, run 20 test queries covering the "Highest-Risk" list above using outdated policy scenarios. If the bot gives a definitive wrong answer on >0% of these, the fallback mechanism is broken and must be fixed before day 10.
*   **Feedback Loop:** Add a one-click "Was this helpful?" and "Report Incorrect Advice" button to every response. Review these logs daily during the pilot.
*   **Kill Switch:** Designate one team member as the "Safety Owner" with the authority to shut down the bot instantly if a single instance of harmful, definitive legal advice is confirmed.

**Note:** Because this involves high-stakes, irreversible outcomes for vulnerable populations, full autonomous operation is unsafe; the human gate on eligibility and appeals is mandatory, not optional. Legal counsel must review the specific disclaimer wording to ensure compliance with local Greek regulations before launch.
