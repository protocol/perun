**Pre-Launch Bias Mitigation Plan (3-Week Sprint)**

**1. Harm Inventory & Scope (Days 1–2)**
Define the specific risk: **Systematic exclusion of qualified candidates** with non-traditional backgrounds (bias/unfair outcomes).
*   **Affected Group:** Applicants without standard degrees or linear career paths.
*   **Worst Case:** Qualified diverse talent is silently rejected; legal liability under EU equality directives; reputational damage.
*   **Constraint Acknowledgement:** We cannot certify the system is "unbiased" or "fair" without new diverse data. We can only measure residual risk using current assets and implement strict human gates.

**2. Measurement Strategy: Internal Red-Teaming (Days 3–10)**
Since external data is unavailable, construct a **Synthetic Control Set** from your existing internal data to test for differential treatment.
*   **Probe Set Construction:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Take 20 resumes previously hired/promoted successfully. Create "twins" by altering only the education section (e.g., replace "University X" with "Bootcamp Y" or "Self-Taught") while keeping skills/experience identical.
    *   *Negative Control:* Take 20 resumes previously rejected for lack of skill. Create "twins" by adding prestigious degrees but keeping skills identical.
    *   *Segmentation:* Run these pairs through the screener.
*   **Metric:** **Disparate Impact Ratio.** Compare the pass rate of the "Non-Traditional Twin" vs. the "Traditional Twin."
*   **Threshold & Stop Condition:** If the non-traditional twin passes **<90%** as often as the traditional twin, the feature fails the launch gate. Do not launch until the gap is reduced or the process is gated.
*   **Adversarial Probe:** Inject "decoy" resumes with impossible credentials or keyword stuffing to ensure the model isn't just matching buzzwords but evaluating substance.

**3. Management Controls: The Human Gate (Immediate Implementation)**
Given the high stakes (employment decisions) and unverified bias metrics, full automation is unsafe.
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:** The AI must **never** auto-reject a candidate. It should only output a "Recommendation Tier" (e.g., *High Match*, *Potential Match*, *Review Needed*).
    *   *Rule:* Any candidate flagged as "Potential Match" or falling below a high-confidence threshold must be reviewed by a human recruiter before rejection.
    *   *Rule:* Any candidate with a non-traditional background identified by the system triggers a mandatory secondary human review.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not show recruiters a precise score (e.g., "87% match"). Use coarse, defined tiers: *Confirmed Fit*, *Corroborated Skills*, *Unverified Path*. This prevents automation bias where recruiters blindly trust a specific number.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a one-page **Model Card** for internal use. List:
    *   *Intended Use:* Initial sorting only, not final decision making.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly state "Not validated on non-traditional education paths; high risk of false negatives for this group."
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific product lead responsible.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing (Days 11–14)**
*   **Legal Determination:** Automated employment decisions are highly regulated (e.g., EU AI Act, local equality laws). **Route immediately to legal counsel** to determine if a specific AI disclosure to applicants is legally required. Do not assume compliance; get a written determination.
*   **Rollback Plan:** Define the kill switch. If post-launch monitoring shows a spike in complaints from non-traditional candidates, the tool reverts to keyword-only filtering or manual review within 24 hours.

**5. Deployment Schedule**
*   **Week 1:** Build synthetic twins, run baseline evals, draft Model Card.
*   **Week 2:** Implement human-gate logic in the UI, train recruiters on "Uncertainty Tiers," finalize legal review.
*   **Week 3:** Soft launch to 10% of traffic with full human oversight on all "Reject" recommendations. Monitor daily.

**Note:** This plan measures risk but does not eliminate it; the "UNVERIFIED" status of bias on external populations remains until real-world data is collected.
**Note:** Full autonomous rejection of applicants is prohibited under this plan due to the irreversible harm of lost employment opportunity.
