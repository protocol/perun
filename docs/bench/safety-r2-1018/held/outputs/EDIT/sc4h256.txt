### Evaluation Framework: Neurodivergent Bias Assessment for Emotion Recognition

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm Identified:** Systematic disadvantage (Bias) against neurodivergent candidates due to training data gaps.
*   **Affected Group:** Neurodivergent job applicants (e.g., Autism, ADHD) with atypical facial expressions.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Qualified candidates are falsely scored as "disengaged," "anxious," or "dishonest," leading to automatic rejection before human review. This creates legal liability and reputational damage.
*   **Status:** **UNVERIFIED**. The lack of diverse training data means current performance metrics for this subgroup are unknown and cannot be assumed safe.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since you cannot train on new data immediately, you must rigorously test the *current* model's failure modes. Execute one test row per harm:

*   **Probe Set Construction:**
    *   **Synthetic/Proxy Data:** Generate or source video clips of neurodivergent expressions using public datasets (e.g., AffectNet subsets, research papers) or synthetic avatars configured with atypical muscle movements. Do not rely solely on "neurotypical" test sets.
    *   **Positive Control:** Inputs known to trigger high confidence in *incorrect* emotion labels for neurodivergent patterns (e.g., flat affect labeled as "bored").
    *   **Negative Control:** Neurotypical expressions that should pass standard thresholds.
*   **Metric:** Disparity in "hire-recommendation" scores between neurotypical and neurodivergent proxy groups.
*   **Pass Threshold:** **Owner-defined.** Given the high stakes (employment), propose **zero tolerance** for automated rejection based solely on emotion scores for this subgroup until parity is proven.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the model assigns negative sentiment scores to neutral/atypical neurodivergent expressions at a rate higher than the defined threshold, **block the pilot**.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** immediately. It must explicitly state: "Model performance on neurodivergent expressions is UNVERIFIED due to data absence. Intended use excludes sole-source automated rejection for this demographic."

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** You cannot automate the final decision for this pilot.
    *   **Control:** The tool must function as an *assistive* draft only. No candidate can be rejected based exclusively on the AI's emotion score. All low-score outputs for the pilot must trigger a mandatory human review by a trained recruiter aware of neurodiversity.
    *   **Rationale:** Full autonomous execution of hiring decisions with unverified bias is the specific failure this framework prevents.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% anxious") to recruiters, as this invites over-reliance. Instead, use coarse, defined tiers (e.g., "Signal Unclear – Requires Human Review") when input patterns deviate from the training distribution.
*   **Disclosure:** Recommend clear disclosure to candidates that AI is analyzing facial expressions, allowing them to opt-out or request accommodation.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific Data Scientist as the "Bias Owner" for this feature. They are responsible for re-evaluating the model if the client pilot reveals new edge cases.
*   **Legal Routing:** **Immediate escalation to counsel required.** Employment screening involving automated decision-making is heavily regulated (e.g., NYC Local Law 144, EU AI Act). Counsel must determine if a bias audit is legally mandated before the pilot starts. Do not proceed without this determination.

**Definition of Done for Pilot Launch:**
1.  Harm inventory documented with "UNVERIFIED" status for neurodivergent accuracy.
2.  Hard code gate implemented: AI cannot auto-reject; human review is mandatory for all pilot outcomes.
3.  Model Card published internally stating data limitations.
4.  Legal counsel has reviewed the pilot scope for regulatory compliance.

**Note:** Do not fabricate a "bias score" or claim fairness; without the data, any such metric is false. The safety of this feature relies entirely on the human gate, not the model's current accuracy.
