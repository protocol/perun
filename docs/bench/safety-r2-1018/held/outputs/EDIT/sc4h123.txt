### Editorial Review Framework for Political Ad Copy

**1. Pre-Generation Harm Inventory**
Before drafting, the editor must define the specific risk for each candidate's output:
*   **Target:** Which voter demographic could be misled?
*   **Worst Case:** What is the most damaging false claim the tool could generate based on the provided policy documents?
*   **Constraint:** Acknowledge that without external APIs, the system cannot verify facts autonomously; human review is the sole gate.

**2. The "Source-Anchor" Workflow**
Implement a mandatory three-step editorial loop for every generated draft:

*   **Step A: Claim Extraction & Segmentation**
    Break the AI-generated copy into individual factual assertions (e.g., "Candidate X voted against Bill Y"). Ignore persuasive adjectives; isolate only verifiable claims.

*   **Step B: Document Traceability (The "Zero-External" Rule)**
    For every extracted claim, the editor must locate the exact sentence in the uploaded policy documents that supports it.
    *   *Pass:* The claim is a direct paraphrase or quote of the provided text.
    *   *Fail (Hallucination):* The claim adds external context, statistics, or outcomes not present in the source text. **Action:** Delete immediately.
    *   *Fail (Over-Reach):* The source text is ambiguous, but the copy presents it as definitive. **Action:** Rewrite to reflect uncertainty or remove.

*   **Step C: Deceptive Pattern Scan**
    Review the phrasing for manufactured urgency or hidden defaults. Ensure the copy does not imply a guarantee of outcome that the policy document does not support.

**3. Confidence Tiering & Disclosure**
Do not present AI output as final truth. Apply a coarse confidence tier to the internal draft before it reaches the client:
*   **Confirmed:** Every claim is directly anchored to a specific page/section of the source document.
*   **Unverified:** Claims rely on inference or general knowledge not in the source. **Status:** Blocked from publication until manually researched by senior staff.

**4. Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
No ad copy proceeds to the client or publication without a signed "Source Anchor Checklist" confirming that every factual assertion maps to the provided documents. If a claim cannot be anchored, the default action is removal, not external verification.

**5. Residual Risk Reporting**
Maintain a log of "Unverified" claims that were removed. This documents the tool's limitation and protects the firm from liability regarding invented statistics or outcomes. Do not invent an accuracy rate; report the volume of claims blocked by the human gate.

***

**Note:** This framework treats the inability to use third-party APIs as a hard constraint, shifting the burden entirely to document traceability.
**Note:** Legal counsel must determine if specific political advertising regulations require additional disclosures beyond this internal workflow.
