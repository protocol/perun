**Pre-Launch Safety Checklist: Emergency Price Cap Protocol**

**1. Immediate Configuration Overrides (No Core Rewrite Required)**
*   **Hard Cap Rule:** Implement a deterministic "ceiling" rule in the configuration layer that overrides any AI output exceeding 1.5x the base fare. This acts as a hard stop regardless of the model's internal logic.
*   **Emergency Trigger List:** Manually curate a static list of keywords and geofences associated with recent Nairobi emergencies (e.g., specific flood zones, protest areas, fire incidents). Configure the system to flag any request originating from or destined for these coordinates.
*   **Freeze Mechanism:** When an emergency trigger is detected, the system must default to "Standard Base Fare" or a pre-defined "Crisis Rate" rather than attempting to calculate dynamic surge.

**2. Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory for High-Stakes Actions)**
*   **Irreversible Action Block:** Since overcharging during an evacuation is financially damaging and ethically irreversible, fully autonomous execution of surge pricing >2x is prohibited.
*   **Manual Approval Workflow:** Any surge event triggered by the AI that exceeds the 1.5x cap must route to a dashboard for manual approval by an on-call operator before being shown to the user. If no operator confirms within 30 seconds, the fare defaults to the Crisis Rate.
*   **On-Call Rotation:** Assign one of the six team members to monitor this queue continuously during the launch week.

**3. Measurement and Validation Plan**
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Before launch, run a simulation using historical data from known Nairobi emergency events. Input these scenarios to verify the Hard Cap Rule activates every time.
*   **Pass/Fail Criteria:** The test passes only if zero simulated emergency rides exceed the Crisis Rate. Any failure blocks the launch of the dynamic module.
*   **Residual Risk Reporting:** Document that while the hard cap prevents exorbitant pricing, the system may still under-supply drivers in crisis zones due to low fares. Mark this risk as `UNVERIFIED` regarding long-term supply impact until post-launch data is collected. Do not claim the system is "fair" or "safe"; state only that the specific harm of price-gouging is mitigated by the cap.

**4. Transparency and Disclosure**
*   **User Notification:** When the Crisis Rate is active, display a clear message: "Fares are capped due to current emergency conditions." Do not hide this logic.
*   **Internal Documentation:** Create a one-page "Model Card" for this feature listing the intended use (dynamic pricing), the specific limitation (fails to account for supply scarcity during caps), and the owner of the emergency trigger list.

**5. Governance and Ownership**
*   **Named Owner:** Assign a specific individual from the six-person team as the owner of the "Emergency Trigger List" and the "Hard Cap" configuration. This person is responsible for updating geofences as new crises emerge.
*   **Rollback Switch:** Prepare a single toggle to disable the AI surge bot entirely and revert to static pricing if the human-in-the-loop queue becomes overwhelmed.
