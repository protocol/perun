**Internal Review Framework: Parole Risk Pilot (Philadelphia)**

**1. Harm Inventory & Scope**
*   **Primary Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes. The model may systematically overestimate risk for minority offenders due to historical policing data skew, leading to unjust denial of parole.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Over-reliance. Judges may treat the output as definitive fact rather than an advisory input.
*   **Affected Population:** Incarcerated individuals from minority groups; public trust in the justice system.
*   **Worst Case:** Systematic extension of incarceration for protected groups based on flawed algorithmic inference.

**2. Measurement Plan (Using Anonymized Proxies)**
Since real case files cannot be shared, construct a **Synthetic Proxy Test Suite**:
*   **Data Construction:** Generate statistical proxies that mirror the demographic distribution of the Philadelphia jail population without using PII. Create paired profiles: identical criminal history and behavior metrics, varying only the protected attribute proxies (e.g., neighborhood zip code as a proxy for race, if correlated in training data).
*   **Adversarial Probes:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs designed to trigger high-risk scores based on known bias vectors (e.g., specific zip codes + minor infractions).
    *   *Negative Control:* Inputs designed to yield low-risk scores across all demographics.
*   **Metric:** Disparate Impact Ratio. Compare the rate of "High Risk" classifications between the proxy groups.
*   **Threshold & Stop Condition:** Owner-defined tolerance (e.g., <80% ratio). **Stop Condition:** If the disparate impact ratio falls below the threshold during internal stress tests, the pilot **must not launch** until the model is retrained or the feature is gated.
*   **Injection/Leakage Check:** Verify that anonymized proxies cannot be reverse-engineered to reveal real identities.

**3. Management Controls (The Human Gate)**
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:** The AI output is **advisory only**. No parole decision can be executed or finalized by the system. The judge must explicitly affirm the decision, documenting whether they accepted or rejected the AI's risk assessment.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% risk"). Display defined coarse tiers: **Confirmed** (multiple corroborating factors), **Corroborated** (single strong factor), or **Unverified** (reliant on skewed historical proxies). Include a visible disclaimer: "This assessment relies on historical data known to contain policing biases."
*   **Override Mechanism:** Judges must have a one-click mechanism to flag an assessment as "Perceived Unfair Bias" for immediate human review by the consortium.

**4. Governance & Transparency**
*   **Artifacts:** Produce a **Model Card** before day one. It must list: intended use (judicial aid only), limitations (known historical bias), measured performance by proxy subgroup, and the owner (Consortium Lead).
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific researcher as the "Bias Owner" responsible for reviewing flagged overrides weekly.
*   **Legal Routing:** **Crucial:** Whether this automated decision support triggers specific regulatory duties under Pennsylvania law or federal civil rights statutes is a legal determination. Route this immediately to legal counsel; do not assert compliance internally.

**5. Definition of Done for Launch**
*   Harm inventory signed off.
*   Synthetic proxy tests run with results recorded (if unmeasured, mark "UNVERIFIED" and do not launch).
*   Human-in-the-loop workflow hardcoded (no auto-decisions).
*   Model Card drafted and available to the judicial users.
*   Legal counsel consulted on disclosure duties.

**Note:** No metric can certify this system as "unbiased"; only residual risk can be measured and managed.
If the synthetic proxy tests show significant disparity, the only safe mitigation is to withhold the tool's output until the model is improved.
