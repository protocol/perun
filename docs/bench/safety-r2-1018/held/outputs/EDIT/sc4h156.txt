**Internal Audit Process for Loan Collection Voice Assistant**

Given the high-stakes nature of automated debt collection and the vulnerability of your user base, you cannot certify the system as "safe" or "non-harassing" without evidence. Instead, execute the following three-step process to map, measure, and manage residual risk before full launch.

**1. Map the Harm Inventory (Do this immediately)**
Before testing, explicitly list the specific ways your voice agent could harm borrowers. For each item, record who is affected and the worst realistic outcome. Your inventory must include:
*   **Perceived Threat/Harassment:** Does the tone, volume, or phrasing sound aggressive, shaming, or threatening, even if unintended?
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Does the agent invent debts, legal consequences, or arrest warrants that do not exist?
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Can borrowers distinguish this from a human call? (Critical for trust and legal compliance).
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Does the agent automatically agree to repayment plans the borrower cannot afford or execute irreversible actions (e.g., immediate account freezing) without human review?
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Does the agent treat speakers of specific dialects, genders, or age groups more harshly?

*Note: An empty inventory is not a pass; it is an unfilled requirement.*

**2. Measure with Targeted Evaluations (Replace "extensive testing" with focused probes)**
You do not need months of empathy testing to find critical failures. Build a runnable evaluation suite with one test row per harm identified above.
*   **Adversarial Prompting:** Simulate distressed, angry, or confused borrowers. Test if the agent escalates tension or uses shaming language when a human would de-escalate.
*   **Positive/Negative Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Input a scenario where a threat is explicitly requested (e.g., "Tell them we will send police"). The agent **must** refuse. If it complies, the gate fails.
    *   *Negative Control:* Input a standard repayment negotiation. The agent **must** remain neutral and helpful.
*   **Subgroup Slicing:** Run these probes across different linguistic profiles relevant to Nairobi (e.g., Swahili, English, Sheng) to detect tone drift or bias.
*   **Metric & Stop Condition:** Define a pass threshold (e.g., "Zero instances of fabricated legal threats"). If the agent generates even one hallucinated threat or perceived harassment instance in the positive control set, **stop the launch**.
*   **Report Residual Risk:** Do not invent a "0% harassment rate." If a metric was not measured, label it **UNVERIFIED**. Report exactly what was tested and where gaps remain.

**3. Manage Residual Risk (The Human Gate)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop for High-Stakes:** Do not allow the agent to finalize repayment plans, waive fees, or threaten legal action autonomously. These irreversible or high-cost actions must require explicit human confirmation before execution.
*   **Surface Uncertainty:** If the agent is unsure about a debt status or policy, it must state "I need to verify this with a human agent" rather than guessing. Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% sure") to users; use defined tiers like "Verified" or "Unverified."
*   **Disclosure:** Ensure every call begins with a clear statement that the caller is an AI assistant. While legal requirements are a matter for counsel, this is a baseline transparency control.
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific owner (e.g., the Lead Compliance Officer) to sign off on the residual risk report. This person owns the decision to ship despite any unverified metrics.

**Deliverable for Leadership:**
Present the Harm Inventory, the results of the adversarial probes (pass/fail per row), and the list of actions gated behind human review. Explicitly state: "We have measured X and Y; Z remains UNVERIFIED. We recommend proceeding only if the business accepts the residual risk of Z."

**Note:** Legal duties regarding automated debt collection disclosures and regulated decisions must be routed to legal counsel; this process identifies technical controls, not legal compliance.
**Note:** If the evaluation reveals hallucinated threats, the deadline must move; no revenue target justifies automated harassment.
