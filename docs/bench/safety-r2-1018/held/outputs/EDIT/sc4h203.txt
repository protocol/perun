### Targeted Evaluation Protocol for Sensitive Community Topics

**1. Harm Inventory & Scope**
*   **Identified Harm:** Bias/unfair outcomes systematically disadvantaging LGBTQ+ users by misclassifying support discussions as harmful speech.
*   **Affected Group:** LGBTQ+ community members and allies in the MENA region.
*   **Worst Case:** Silencing vulnerable users, eroding trust, and causing reputational damage during regional expansion.
*   **Constraint Acknowledgement:** No full model retraining; solution relies on threshold adjustment, rule-based fallbacks, and human-in-the-loop (HITL) gating.

*   **Reporting:** Report results as `UNVERIFIED` until this eval runs. Do not invent a "bias rate."

**3. Management Controls (No Retraining Required)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Any post flagged as "harmful" containing keywords associated with LGBTQ+ support (e.g., "pride," "transition," "queer") must bypass auto-deletion and enter a **mandatory human review queue**. Auto-deletion of these topics is prohibited until bias metrics improve.
*   **Threshold Adjustment Strategy:**
    *   **Tiered Confidence:** Do not use raw model scores (e.g., "87% harmful"). Map scores to coarse tiers: *Confirmed Harm* (auto-remove), *Corroborated* (human review), *Unverified* (allow).
    *   **Asymmetric Thresholds:** Set a higher bar for removal on sensitive topics. It is safer to let some harmful speech slip (false negative) than to silence support groups (false positive).
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** section for this feature stating:
    *   *Intended Use:* Filtering explicit hate speech.
    *   *Limitations:* Known gaps in cultural context for LGBTQ+ topics; high risk of false positives in this domain.
    *   *Measured Performance:* Insert FPR results from the eval above (by subgroup if possible).
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific team member responsible.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly indicate to users when content is held for review vs. automatically removed.

**4. Governance**
*   **Owner:** Assign one person to own the "LGBTQ+ Bias" risk.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any change to the keyword list, threshold, or model version requires re-running the eval.
*   **Legal Route:** Consult counsel regarding local regulations on LGBTQ+ content and automated decision-making in the expansion regions; do not assume compliance based on this plan.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes preventing harm to vulnerable groups over perfect automation efficiency. If human review capacity is exceeded, the expansion scope must be reduced, not the safety gate relaxed.
