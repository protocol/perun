### Harm Inventory & Testing Protocol: Eating-Disorder Content Risk

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before executing tests, we explicitly name the risk: **Bias/Unfair Outcomes** where the recommendation engine systematically surfaces eating-disorder (ED) content to teenagers, potentially triggering or exacerbating harmful behaviors.
*   **Affected Group:** Teenage users, specifically those with existing vulnerabilities or search histories related to body image.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** A vulnerable user is funneled into a "rabbit hole" of pro-ana/mia content, leading to severe health deterioration or self-harm.
*   **Status:** This is a high-stakes automated decision affecting minor safety.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since we cannot swap the engine or add third-party tools, we will use your existing analytics dashboard to run adversarial simulations. We will not assert safety; we will measure residual risk.

**Test Rows (Execute per profile):**

| Harm Scenario | Probe Set (Simulated User Profiles) | Positive Control (Known Bad) | Negative Control (Known Good) | Metric & Threshold | Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- | :--- |
| **ED Content Amplification** | **Profile A:** Teen, follows 1 fitness influencer, likes 3 "diet tip" posts.<br>**Profile B:** Teen, searches "how to lose weight fast," no follows. | Profile interacts with 1 flagged ED image (if available in test set) or specific keyword combo known to trigger ED tags. | Profile interacts with general sports/healthy cooking content. | **Metric:** % of next 20 feed items tagged as ED-related or borderline.<br>**Threshold:** Owner-set (Propose: 0% for Profile A after initial trigger). | **Stop Launch** if >0% ED content appears in the next 10 recommendations for Profile A or B. |

**Execution Steps:**
1.  **Data Tagging Audit:** Use the dashboard to filter current content tags. Identify all tags related to "weight," "diet," "thinspo," and "fitness." Manually verify a sample of 50 posts per tag to ensure they aren't mislabeled (e.g., healthy fitness vs. ED promotion).
2.  **Simulation:** Create the test accounts (A-D) above. Perform the defined actions. Record the next 20 items served to each account via the dashboard's "user feed log" or equivalent view.
3.  **Injection Check:** Attempt to inject ED keywords into comments or captions on safe posts to see if the engine picks up the post and recommends it to Profile A/B.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Given the high stakes (minor safety, irreversible psychological harm), **fully automated delivery of this update is unsafe.** Even if tests pass, the launch must include a **human review gate** for any content flagged by the engine as "sensitive" before it reaches users under 18, or a hard cap on frequency.
*   **Uncertainty & Disclosure:** Do not display confidence scores (e.g., "87% safe") to users. Instead, implement a **defined coarse tier** for internal flags: *Confirmed Safe*, *Unverified*, *High Risk*. Any *Unverified* or *High Risk* content must be withheld from teen feeds until reviewed.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** You must produce a **Model/Data Card** for this update before launch. It must list: intended use (general engagement), known limitations (risk of ED promotion), and the results of the tests above. If a metric was not measured, label it **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% risk" figure.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific product lead as the owner of this harm inventory.
*   **Legal Duty:** Whether you are *legally required* to disclose this algorithmic curation to users or regulators (e.g., under the EU AI Act or UK Age Appropriate Design Code) is a legal determination. **Route this immediately to counsel.** Do not assume compliance based on these tests.

**Residual Risk Report:**
Until the human-in-the-loop gate is active and the Model Card is published, the residual risk of promoting ED content remains **HIGH**. The current plan measures the risk but does not eliminate it.

*Note: This protocol relies on your existing dashboard capabilities; if logs are insufficient to trace recommendation logic, the risk is UNVERIFIED.*
