**Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Acme Weddings Bot**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Primary Risk:** Unsafe high-stakes automation. The bot recommends venues where receptions may be legally prohibited due to noise ordinances, leading to immediate shutdowns, financial loss, and reputational damage for couples.
*   **Secondary Risk:** Hallucination surfaced as fact. Presenting a venue as "available" or "suitable" without verifying local legal constraints implies a guarantee the data does not support.
*   **Affected Parties:** Couples (financial/emotional harm), Venue Owners (reputational harm), Acme Weddings (liability).
*   **Worst Case:** A couple books a historic venue based on bot advice, pays a deposit, and is shut down by police on the wedding day due to unverified noise restrictions.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Action:** Do not assert compliance. Since external audits are impossible, you must treat all noise ordinance data as `UNVERIFIED`.
*   **Eval Strategy:** Build a "Red Team" dataset of 20 known high-risk scenarios (e.g., specific historic towns with strict curfew laws).
    *   *Positive Control:* Input a request for a venue in a town known for strict noise bans. The bot must **not** recommend it as "safe" without a warning.
    *   *Negative Control:* Input a request for a venue in a zone with no known restrictions.
*   **Metric:** Zero tolerance for unqualified recommendations in the Positive Control set. If the bot suggests a restricted venue without a disclaimer, the launch is blocked.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Data Card" for the bot. Explicitly state: "Venue suggestions are based on public lists and do not include real-time verification of municipal noise ordinances. Users must confirm local regulations with authorities before booking."

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** You cannot automate the final "all-clear" for a venue.
    *   *Implementation:* Modify the bot's output to always append a prominent, non-dismissible warning: **"Critical Notice: This suggestion does not verify local noise ordinances. Receptions in historic towns often face strict curfews. You must confirm legal compliance with the local municipality before signing any contract."**
    *   *Constraint:* Do not remove this warning to improve "user experience." The risk of shutdown outweighs friction.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Never present a venue match as "Confirmed Safe." Use coarse tiers: "Listed in Public Database" vs. "Legally Verified." Since you cannot verify, all outputs must remain in the "Listed" tier.
*   **Fallback:** If a user asks specifically about noise rules, the bot must reply: "I cannot access real-time legal codes. Please contact the town hall directly."

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing any user reports of shutdowns or complaints.
*   **Review Cycle:** Re-evaluate the warning text and data sources immediately if a single incident occurs.

**Notes:**
*   This plan relies on disclosure and user verification rather than automated compliance, which is the only viable path given your zero-budget constraint.
*   While this addresses product safety, the legal duty to disclose limitations varies by jurisdiction; this plan assumes a conservative stance to mitigate liability.
