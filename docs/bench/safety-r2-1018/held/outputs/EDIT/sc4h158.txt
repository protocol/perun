### Safety Preparation Guide: Automated Social Media Moderator (Beta)

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Assessment**
Before expanding the beta, record these specific risks for the Manila forum context:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes (Silencing Activists):** The outdated keyword library may flag emerging slang used by activists as hate speech, systematically disadvantaging legitimate community voices. *Worst Case:* Critical discourse is erased, chilling free speech and damaging community trust.
*   **Unsafe Automation (Missing Bot Attacks):** The model fails to recognize coded hate speech or coordinated bot patterns. *Worst Case:* Harassment campaigns escalate unchecked, causing real-world harm to users.
*   **Over-reliance:** Content specialists may trust the "filtered" queue as safe, missing nuanced violations that slipped through.

**2. Measurement Plan (Do Not Assert Safety)**
Do not claim the model is "fair" or "accurate" without testing. Execute these specific evaluations:
*   **Activist Voice Probe:** Curate a dataset of known legitimate activist posts using current local slang. Run them through the filter.
    *   *Metric:* False Positive Rate (legitimate posts blocked).
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined (e.g., zero tolerance for blocking verified activist leaders).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If false positives exceed the threshold, halt auto-deletion; revert to 100% human review for affected categories.
*   **Bot Attack Probe:** Simulate coordinated bot attacks using coded language identified in recent regional incidents.
    *   *Metric:* False Negative Rate (harmful posts allowed).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If coordinated attacks bypass the filter undetected in simulation, disable auto-approval for high-volume users.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** listing the outdated keyword library as a known limitation, specifying that performance on emerging slang is **UNVERIFIED** until the above tests are run.

**3. Managed Workflow: The Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
Given the high stakes of silencing speech or allowing harassment, full automation is unsafe. Implement this manual review workflow immediately:

*   **The "Grey Zone" Queue:** Do not auto-delete posts flagged solely by the outdated keyword library. Route them to a "Pending Review" queue visible to your 11 specialists.
*   **Activist Whitelist & Escalation:** Create a rapid escalation path for flagged content from known community leaders. If a post is flagged, a specialist must verify it within 15 minutes before it is hidden.
*   **Bot Swarm Triage:** Instead of auto-approving "clean" posts during traffic spikes, sample 10% of approved content for human audit to catch missed coded hate speech.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If the system confidence is low or relies on outdated keywords, surface a "Needs Context" flag to the reviewer rather than a binary "Safe/Unsafe" label.

**4. Governance**
Assign one content specialist as the owner for "Activist Silencing" risks and one developer for "Bot Evasion" risks. Re-review this workflow weekly as slang evolves.

**Notes**
*   Legal requirements for content moderation disclosure in the Philippines must be routed to counsel; this guide recommends disclosure as a safety control, not a legal verdict.
*   Never fabricate a "safety score"; report residual risk as "UNVERIFIED" until the probe tests above are completed.
