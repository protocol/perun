# Contingency Plan: Manual Identification and Service of Unmapped Locations

**Objective:** Ensure equitable supply distribution to families in remote, unmapped Haitian villages when the AI routing system fails to include them due to outdated map data.

**Phase 1: Rapid Community Intelligence Gathering (Pre-Deployment)**
*   **Leverage Local Liaisons:** Immediately deploy your twenty team members to established community hubs (churches, schools, market squares) in mapped perimeter zones. Task them with interviewing local leaders, motorcycle taxi drivers ("moto-taxi"), and relief volunteers to compile a handwritten list of known unmapped settlements, including landmarks (e.g., "near the blue bridge," "past the mango grove") and estimated household counts.
*   **Radio Net Establishment:** Set up a dedicated VHF/UHF radio channel for field coordinators. Since cellular networks may be spotty, radios will serve as the primary real-time link between truck drivers, base camp, and community scouts.

**Phase 2: The "Scout-and-Verify" Protocol (During Operations)**
*   **Dedicated Scout Teams:** Assign two team members not to drive trucks but to operate on motorcycles or foot. Their sole mandate is to validate the AI's route and physically search for deviations indicated by community intelligence.
*   **Trigger for Manual Override:** If a scout confirms an unmapped village or if a truck driver reports visible need off-route:
    1.  **Stop:** The driver halts at the nearest safe, mapped location.
    2.  **Report:** Coordinates (if available) or descriptive location are radioed to Base Camp.
    3.  **Authorize:** The Incident Commander (one of the 20) authorizes a manual deviation based on remaining cargo volume and fuel status.
    4.  **Escort:** A scout physically guides the truck to the unmapped location to prevent getting stranded.

**Phase 3: Distribution Execution & Data Capture**
*   **Manual Manifesting:** Do not rely on the AI for these stops. Use paper manifests to record:
    *   Village name (local spelling).
    *   Number of households served.
    *   Specific supplies dropped.
    *   GPS waypoint (marked manually on a handheld device or noted via landmark description).
*   **Equity Check:** At the end of each day, compare the paper manifests against the AI's digital log. Calculate the percentage of total supplies delivered manually. If this exceeds 15%, trigger a strategy review to adjust the next day's starting depots closer to unmapped clusters.

**Phase 4: Feedback Loop for Future Runs**
*   **Map Patching:** Each evening, transcribe the handwritten data into a simple shared spreadsheet or update a free, open-source map layer (like OpenStreetMap) if internet connectivity allows.
*   **Route Adjustment:** For Day 2+, manually inject the confirmed coordinates of these villages into the AI system as "mandatory waypoints" to force inclusion in the optimized route, even if the underlying map imagery remains outdated.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
*   **Incident Commander (1 person):** Holds final authority on route deviations and resource reallocation.
*   **Scout Team (2 people):** Responsible for physical verification and guiding trucks.
*   **Community Liaisons (5 people):** Focused on gathering intelligence from locals before trucks roll.
*   **Truck Drivers (Remaining staff):** Execute routes, maintain radio silence unless reporting issues, and manage paper manifests.

**Risk Mitigation**
*   **Security:** Never send a truck to an unmapped location without a scout verification or local guide due to potential road hazards or security risks.
*   **Resource Depletion:** Manual detours consume more fuel and time. Strictly cap manual deviations to 20% of the daily route budget unless a life-threatening emergency is verified.

This plan prioritizes human judgment and local knowledge over automated efficiency to ensure no community is invisible to the relief effort.

*Note: This plan assumes basic radio communication and motorcycle availability; if these are missing, rely on pre-positioned supply caches at known community gathering points.*
*Update all paper records digitally within 12 hours of collection to prevent data loss.*
