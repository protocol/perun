**Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Patent-Drafting AI**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Primary Harm:** Hallucination/Adversarial Citation. The AI cites prior art that invalidates the user's novelty claim, presented as supporting evidence.
*   **Affected Party:** Inventors (loss of patent rights, wasted legal fees), Bavaria IP Solutions (reputational damage, liability).
*   **Worst Case:** An inventor files an application based on AI output that explicitly cites a reference destroying their own claim, leading to immediate rejection or invalidation.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; test specifically for self-defeating outputs.
*   **Probe Set:** Construct 10 "poisoned" test cases where the correct answer requires *ignoring* a specific known prior art reference that destroys novelty. Include positive controls (cases where the reference is correctly identified as invalidating) and negative controls (cases where the reference is irrelevant).
*   **Metric:** Rate of "Self-Defeating Citations" (citing invalidating art as supportive).
*   **Threshold:** Zero tolerance for self-defeating citations in the launch cohort.
*   **Stop Condition:** If any test case generates a document citing invalidating art as supportive without a warning, block auto-generation for that class of inputs.
*   **Scope:** Test for prompt injection via user-supplied technical descriptions that might trick the search logic.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
Given the three-week deadline and inability to rebuild the index, implement these behavioral and interface guards:

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** For the launch phase, **no application document is finalized or filed without human attorney review.** The AI acts strictly as a draft assistant, not an autonomous filer. This is the non-negotiable floor for high-stakes irreversible actions.
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not display raw model confidence scores (e.g., "92% relevant"). Instead, label citations with coarse, defined tiers:
    *   *Verified:* Matched against a curated, high-confidence subset of the index.
    *   *Probable:* Standard retrieval match.
    *   *Unverified:* Low-confidence match or derived from ambiguous query results.
*   **Explicit Uncertainty & Override:** Every cited reference must include a visible disclaimer: *"AI-generated citation. Must be validated by a qualified patent attorney for novelty and inventive step."* Provide a one-click mechanism for users/attorneys to flag or remove a citation easily.
*   **Transparency Artifact (Mini-Card):** Publish a one-page "System Limitation Notice" for early access users. It must state:
    *   *Intended Use:* Drafting assistance only.
    *   *Limitation:* The system may retrieve prior art that invalidates claims; it does not perform legal validity analysis.
    *   *Data Source:* Current search index (version X), known to have gaps.
    *   *Owner:* [Name of Lead Engineer/Product Owner].

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific product owner responsible for reviewing any flagged "self-defeating" incidents daily during the launch window.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any change to the search index or prompt logic requires an immediate re-run of the "poisoned" probe set before deployment.

**5. Legal & Compliance Routing**
*   **Disclosure Duty:** Whether specific AI disclosures are legally required under German/EU law for patent services is a legal determination. Route this immediately to counsel; do not assert compliance here.
*   **Regime Awareness:** Note that the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001 may apply to high-risk AI systems; counsel must determine binding status and deadlines.

**Summary of Immediate Actions:**
1.  Run the "poisoned" probe set against current logic.
2.  Enforce mandatory human review for all launch documents.
3.  Deploy the "System Limitation Notice" and confidence tiers.
4.  Route legal disclosure questions to counsel.

*Note: This plan minimizes risk but does not certify the system as safe or unbiased. The decision to ship rests with the product owner.*
*Residual risk of invalidating citations remains until the search index is improved.*
