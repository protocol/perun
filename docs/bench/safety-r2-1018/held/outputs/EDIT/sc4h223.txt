**Bluf:** Do not launch the bot with full autonomous blocking before the monsoon; the risk of suppressing life-saving information is too high to validate in two weeks without large-scale simulation. Instead, deploy a **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate** for all blocked content and run a targeted, high-intensity "red team" evaluation on critical failure modes only.

**1. Immediate Control: The Human Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
Because this is a high-stakes, irreversible action (blocking emergency info), you must disable fully automated deletion immediately.
*   **Quarantine, Don't Delete:** Configure the bot to move suspected spam/misinformation to a "Review Queue" rather than deleting it.
*   **Rapid Human Review:** Assign your team of ten to rotate shifts reviewing this queue. During peak crisis hours, prioritize speed; false positives (blocking real help) are worse than false negatives (letting some spam through).
*   **Override Mechanism:** Ensure any human reviewer can instantly release a message to the public channel with one click.

**2. Targeted Testing Strategy (The "Two-Week" Plan)**
You cannot test everything. Focus exclusively on the worst-case scenario: **blocking critical relief updates.**
*   **Define the "Kill Set":** Create a small, curated dataset (50–100 examples) of *actual* critical messages that must never be blocked (e.g., "Flood rising in Zone A," "Medical supplies needed at School B," "Evacuation route changed"). Include variations in language (Bahasa Indonesia, local dialects), brevity, and urgency.
*   **Adversarial Probes (Red Teaming):** Have team members act as distressed citizens trying to bypass the filter with urgent news. Try to trick the bot into blocking these vital messages using typical spam patterns (all caps, links, repeated text).
*   **Metric & Stop Condition:**
    *   **Metric:** False Positive Rate on the "Kill Set."
    *   **Threshold:** **Zero tolerance.** If the bot blocks *even one* message from your "Kill Set" during testing, it fails the gate for autonomous action.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If the bot blocks a critical message in the live "Quarantine" phase, pause the feature, retrain/tune, and re-test that specific failure mode before continuing.

**3. Transparency & Disclosure**
*   **User Notification:** When a message is quarantined, display a system notice: "Message held for safety review. If this is an emergency, contact [Hotline] or repost with 'URGENT' tag."
*   **Documentation:** Create a simple "Model Card" document listing:
    *   **Intended Use:** Filtering obvious spam in non-crisis modes; quarantine-only during crises.
    *   **Known Limitations:** "Cannot guarantee 100% accuracy on urgent, unstructured emergency reports; human review required."
    *   **Owner:** Name the specific team lead responsible for the review queue.

**4. Governance**
*   **Owner:** Assign one person as the "Safety Owner" for the monsoon season. They have the authority to kill the switch if the queue backs up or errors spike.
*   **Legal Routing:** Since this involves government emergency comms, route the question of "legal liability for blocked messages" to your legal counsel immediately. Do not assert compliance yourself.

**Summary of Deployment Plan:**
1.  **Day 1-3:** Build "Kill Set" and configure "Quarantine Mode" (no auto-delete).
2.  **Day 4-10:** Run red-team probes against the "Kill Set." Tune until zero critical blocks occur in testing.
3.  **Day 11-14:** Train reviewers on the queue interface. Draft the user disclosure notice.
4.  **Launch:** Go live with **Quarantine Mode** only. Monitor the queue continuously.
5.  **Post-Monsoon:** Only consider limited auto-deletion for obvious, non-urgent spam patterns after collecting real-world data and verifying no harm occurred.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes preventing harm (blocking life-saving info) over efficiency (removing spam). The "Zero Tolerance" threshold for critical messages is the only safe standard for emergency systems.
