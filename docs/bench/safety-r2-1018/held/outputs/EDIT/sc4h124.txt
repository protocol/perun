**BLUF:** You cannot launch an automated health underwriting system with "no room for manual review" within two weeks without violating fundamental safety floors. High-stakes, irreversible decisions (denying health coverage) based on opaque correlations require a human-in-the-loop gate by default. Full automation of this action is the failure we must prevent, not the feature to ship.

**Immediate Action Plan (Meeting the 2-Week Constraint):**

1.  **Implement a Mandatory Human Gate (Days 1-3):**
    *   **Stop Condition:** Any application flagged as "Deny" or falling into high-risk medical categories must route to a human underwriter for explicit confirmation before a decision is sent.
    *   **Constraint Trade-off:** This slows "instant" approval for denied cases but prevents irreversible harm from opaque logic. You cannot automate the "No."
    *   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the interim review lead.

2.  **Construct the Harm Inventory & Eval Plan (Days 2-5):**
    *   **Inventory:** Document specific risks: Bias against specific demographics, hallucination of medical facts, and over-reliance on opaque correlations. Record the worst-case scenario for each (e.g., "Denial of critical care due to spurious data link").
    *   **Runnable Tests:** Create one test row per harm. Use your existing case studies as the "known-good" and "known-bad" controls.
        *   *Probe:* Run historical cases where humans approved but the model might deny based on opaque logic.
        *   *Metric:* Disagreement rate between model and historical human decision on denied cases.
        *   *Threshold:* Set owner-defined tolerance (recommend zero tolerance for unexplained denials).
        *   *Stop Condition:* If the model denies coverage where historical data shows approval without a clear, explainable reason, the launch is blocked.

3.  **Deploy Transparency Artifacts (Days 4-10):**
    *   **Model Card:** Draft a versioned document stating: Intended use (initial triage only), limitations (opaque correlations identified), and subgroup performance (if known, otherwise mark "UNVERIFIED").

    *   **Cap Exposure:** Launch to only 5% of traffic or a specific low-risk segment.
