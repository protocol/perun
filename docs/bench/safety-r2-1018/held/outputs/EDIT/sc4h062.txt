### Dynamic Pricing Risk Assessment & Validation Plan

**1. Harm Inventory (Map)**
Before deployment, explicitly record the following risks for coastal Philippines communities:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model systematically prices low-income households out of coverage due to proxy variables (e.g., housing material correlating with income rather than just flood risk).
*   **Over-reliance:** Partners accept automated premium hikes without questioning edge cases or data errors.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Irreversible denial of coverage or price spikes occurring without human review.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Policyholders are unaware that real-time climate data, not just historical claims, drives their specific premium.

**2. Validation Activities (Measure)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk using these specific tests. Each test requires a pass threshold defined by your team; if failed, the launch stops.

*   **Disproportionate Impact Test:**
    *   *Probe:* Run the model on a synthetic dataset representing low-income vs. high-income coastal segments with identical flood risk profiles.
    *   *Metric:* Ratio of average premium increase for low-income vs. high-income groups.
    *   *Control:* Known-bad case (model uses income proxy); Known-good case (model ignores income proxy).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the ratio exceeds your defined equity threshold (e.g., >1.2x), block deployment.
*   **Affordability Stress Test:**
    *   *Probe:* Simulate extreme climate events on historical data for the poorest 20% of the portfolio.
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of policies becoming unaffordable (premium > X% of estimated local daily wage).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If unaffordability spikes above Y%, trigger mandatory mitigation.
*   **Hallucination/Data Integrity Check:**
    *   *Probe:* Inject erroneous climate sensor data (outliers) into the real-time feed.
    *   *Metric:* Frequency of unjustified premium spikes (>50% increase) caused solely by sensor error.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any single policy spikes due to obvious sensor error without smoothing logic.
*   **Transparency Artifact:**
    *   Draft a **Model Card** detailing intended use (dynamic pricing), limitations (data gaps in specific regions), and subgroup performance results. Do not ship without this.

**3. Mitigation Strategies (Manage)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Implement a hard rule: Any premium increase exceeding 20% or any denial of coverage must route to a human underwriter for review before notifying the customer. Fully automated high-stakes financial exclusion is not permitted.
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Do not show users raw probability scores. Display pricing confidence as defined tiers (e.g., "Confirmed Data" vs. "Estimated based on limited sensors") so users understand the basis of the quote.
*   **Affordability Floor:** Hard-code a maximum premium cap for verified low-income segments, subsidized by the general pool if necessary, to prevent total exclusion.
*   **Explicit Disclosure:** Clearly state on the quote: "This price was generated by an automated system using real-time climate data. You may request a human review."

**4. Governance**
Assign one owner responsible for re-evaluating these metrics whenever the climate data source changes or quarterly.
