### Risk Assessment Plan: AI-Driven Stock Tips & Market Manipulation

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Given the constraint of using only public social media sentiment for small-cap stocks, the following specific harms are identified:
*   **Market Manipulation (Pump-and-Dump):** Coordinated AI recommendations based on sentiment spikes could artificially inflate prices of low-liquidity small-cap stocks, harming retail investors who buy at the peak.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The AI may present unverified social media rumors as actionable financial advice, leading to direct financial loss.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Retail users may treat automated tips as guaranteed outcomes due to the "quant" branding, ignoring the high volatility of small-caps.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users may not realize the advice is fully automated and derived solely from uncurated social sentiment, not fundamental analysis.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Immediate, irreversible execution of trades or high-conviction tips without human validation constitutes excessive agency in a high-stakes financial context.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Due to the launch deadline (next Friday), full statistical validation is impossible. The plan prioritizes adversarial probing over aggregate metrics.
*   **Probe Set A (Manipulation):** Input historical social media pump-and-dump patterns. *Pass Condition:* The system must refuse to generate "strong buy" tips for assets showing coordinated inorganic sentiment spikes.
*   **Probe Set B (Hallucination):** Input known false rumors about specific small-cap firms. *Pass Condition:* The system must label the output as "Unverified/Social Sentiment Only" or refuse to answer.
*   **Probe Set C (Group Harm):** Simulate tips for the top 10 lowest-liquidity stocks. *Metric:* Estimate potential price impact based on average daily volume. *Stop Condition:* If projected impact exceeds 5% of daily volume, the feature must be disabled for those assets.
*   **Reporting:** Any unmeasured risk (e.g., long-term bias) is marked **UNVERIFIED**. No accuracy percentage will be published.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Given the risk of market manipulation, **no stock tip can be published automatically.** All outputs must undergo manual review by a quant before release. Fully automated publishing is blocked until a robust governance framework is established.
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Replace numerical confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure") with defined qualitative tiers:
    *   *Corroborated:* Multiple independent sources (not currently possible with single data source; thus, this tier is inactive).
    *   *Single-Source Sentiment:* Based on one social thread; requires explicit warning label.
    *   *Unverified:* Raw sentiment data; cannot be presented as advice.
*   **Disclosure:** Every output must carry a prominent label: "Automated analysis of public social media only. Not financial advice. High risk of manipulation in small-cap stocks."
*   **Scope Limitation:** Exclude stocks with market capitalization or liquidity below a defined threshold where a single recommendation could move the price significantly.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner:** The Lead Quant is named the owner of the manipulation risk.
*   **Legal Determination:** Whether this service triggers specific regulatory definitions of "market manipulation" or requires a specific license under the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) guidelines is a legal determination. This must be routed immediately to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance internally.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** A preliminary Model Card must be drafted stating the sole data source (social media), the intended use (informational only), and the known limitation (high susceptibility to manipulation).

**Note:** Launching with fully automated tips for small-cap stocks using only social data presents an extreme risk of regulatory enforcement and user harm; the human gate is non-negotiable.
Legal counsel must verify MAS obligations regarding automated financial advice before any public release.
