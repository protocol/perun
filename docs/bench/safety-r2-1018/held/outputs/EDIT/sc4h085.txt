**Immediate Risk Assessment: Do Not Launch Fully Automated Payouts**

Your proposed workflow contains a critical failure mode that cannot be solved by "stress-testing" or "thresholds" alone within a two-week window. Releasing funds based on AI verification of uploaded files and client signals constitutes a **high-stakes, irreversible action**. If the AI hallucinates completion or is fooled by adversarial inputs (e.g., fake screenshots, manipulated metadata), the financial loss is immediate and often unrecoverable.

No amount of pre-launch testing can guarantee a 0% false-positive rate for fraud in an open system. Therefore, establishing a "threshold for automated approval" that allows full autonomy for fund release is unsafe. The gap in your workflow is the assumption that software confidence can replace human judgment for money movement.

**Required Mitigation Plan (Compatible with 2-Week Deadline)**

To meet your launch date without exposing clients to unchecked fraud or requiring new engineering headcount for a full manual queue, you must implement a **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate** for specific risk tiers. This is not a delay; it is a control change.

1.  **Segment Transactions by Risk:**
    *   **Low Risk:** Small amounts (<$50), repeat clients/workers with long history, and high-confidence AI signals. *Action:* Allow automated release.
    *   **High Risk:** First-time pairings, large amounts, mismatched file metadata, or AI confidence below a strict "Confirmed" tier. *Action:* **Hold funds.** Trigger a manual review.

2.  **Implement the "Manual Review" Without New Hires:**
    *   You stated you cannot build a queue for *every* transaction. You do not need to. Route only the **High Risk** segment (likely <20% of volume initially) to your existing team of seven.
    *   **Operational Shift:** Assign one team member per day as the "Escrow Officer." Their sole duty during launch is to review flagged transactions. This requires no new code, only a change in operational procedure and a simple "Approve/Reject" button in your admin panel (which likely already exists for support).

3.  **Stress-Test Plan (Run Immediately):**
    *   **Adversarial Probes:** Have team members upload blank files, renamed non-work files, and doctored screenshots to see if the AI approves them.
    *   **Signal Spoofing:** Simulate client signals (e.g., network interrupts, rapid clicking) to test if the AI interprets noise as approval.
    *   **Metric:** If the AI approves *any* clearly fraudulent test case in the "High Risk" category, the automated threshold is too loose. Tighten it until only obvious, trivial cases pass automatically.

4.  **Transparency & Disclosure:**
    *   Update your terms to explicitly state that "AI-assisted verification is subject to manual review for high-value or first-time transactions."
    *   Display a status to users: "Verification in Progress" rather than "Verified" until the human gate clears high-risk items.

**Conclusion:**
You cannot certify the system as "safe" from premature release. The only safe path is to assume the AI *will* make mistakes and ensure those mistakes do not result in automatic money movement for high-stakes cases. Launch with the HITL gate for high-risk transactions; this protects your partners and clients while adhering to your timeline.
