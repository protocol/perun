**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Sending automated threats of legal action is an irreversible, high-impact action. Fully automating this without human review is the primary failure mode.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** If the system generates specific legal claims, dates, or consequences not verified against actual case files, it presents fabrication as authority.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Generated tone may disproportionately escalate situations for specific demographic groups or dialects common in Nairobi, violating consumer protection norms.
*   **Deceptive Patterns/Over-reliance:** Users may believe a human lawyer has reviewed their specific case when only an algorithm has, creating false urgency and distress.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Do not ship based on assertion.** You must run adversarial evaluations (red-teaming) specifically probing for:
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs that should trigger a standard reminder but incorrectly trigger a legal threat.
    *   *Negative Control:* Inputs describing valid payment disputes that must *not* result in a threat.
    *   *Subgroup Testing:* Probe outputs across diverse Kenyan languages and socio-economic contexts to detect tonal escalation bias.
*   **Report Residual Risk:** Any metric regarding "fairness" or "harm rate" that is not measured via these specific evals must be labeled **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% harm" statistic for your reporting.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Given the irreversible nature of legal threats and financial distress, **fully autonomous execution of these messages is unsafe.** You must implement a gate where a human agent reviews and explicitly approves any message threatening legal action before it is sent. This is a non-negotiable floor for high-stakes automation.
*   **Defined Uncertainty:** If the system displays any confidence scores to internal agents, use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Verified Case," "Unverified Claim") rather than raw percentages (e.g., "87% likely"), which imply false precision.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly indicate to the user that the communication is automated. Whether specific local laws *require* additional disclosures is a legal determination.
