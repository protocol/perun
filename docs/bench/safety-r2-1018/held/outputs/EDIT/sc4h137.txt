**Safety Review Plan for Aegean Collect Debt Collection Bot**

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, document specific risks where the bot's outputs could harm users:
*   **Harassment/Excessive Frequency:** The bot sending messages too frequently or at inappropriate hours, violating local consumer protection norms.
*   **Aggressive Tone/Deceptive Patterns:** Language that coerces, shames, or misleads debtors about legal consequences, creating undue distress.
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions:** The bot finalizing repayment plans or threatening legal action without human verification, leading to irreversible financial or legal damage for the user.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Debtors interacting with the bot without knowing it is an automated system, impairing their ability to seek human clarification.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert compliance; test the outputs directly.
*   **Adversarial Prompting:** Run red-team probes simulating vulnerable users (e.g., those claiming hardship, confusion, or distress) to see if the bot escalates to aggressive tactics.
*   **Tone & Frequency Eval:** Create a test suite with known-bad inputs (triggers for aggression) and known-good inputs (standard queries). Measure the percentage of responses that exceed a defined "professional tone" threshold or message frequency cap.
*   **Stop Condition:** If any test run produces a message classified as "harassing," "threatening," or "undisclosed AI," the launch must be blocked.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** detailing the bot's intended use (debt negotiation), limitations (cannot provide legal advice), and known failure modes (potential tone drift under stress). Do not leave this until post-launch.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Critical):** Given the high-stakes nature of debt collection and the observed aggressive behavior, **fully autonomous negotiation and agreement finalization must not ship.** Implement a mandatory human review step for any repayment plan exceeding a specific threshold or any interaction where the user expresses distress or dispute. This is a non-negotiable floor for irreversible financial actions.
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not let the bot act on its own "confidence" scores. If the bot detects ambiguity or user distress, it must default to a "Human Review Required" tier rather than guessing a resolution.
*   **Disclosure:** Ensure every message clearly states it is from an automated assistant. While specific legal duties regarding disclosure in Greece must be confirmed by counsel, transparent disclosure is a baseline safety control.
*   **Constraint Adherence:** Since you cannot implement new delay mechanisms, the **frequency cap must be enforced logically within the bot's decision engine** before the message is queued for the existing platform. If the logic cannot guarantee compliance with harassment laws within current platform limits, the feature scope must be reduced (e.g., only sending one initial notice) until the constraint is resolved.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name a specific engineer or product lead as the owner of these output harms.
*   **Legal Determination:** You asked to verify boundaries regarding frequency and language. **I cannot certify compliance with Greek consumer protection laws or the EU AI Act.** These are legal determinations. You must route the specific frequency limits, prohibited language lists, and disclosure requirements to qualified legal counsel immediately. Do not rely on engineering judgment for regulatory boundaries.

**Definition of Done for Launch Readiness:**
*   Harm inventory completed and signed off.
*   Red-team evals run with zero instances of un gated high-stakes actions or undisclosed AI.
*   Human-in-the-loop gate active for all agreement finalizations.
*   Legal counsel has reviewed and approved the specific frequency and language rules implemented.
*   Model Card drafted and available internally.

**Note:** Launching without a human gate on final agreements constitutes an unsafe automation failure. Do not fabricate a "compliance rate"; if legal rules are unverified, mark them as UNVERIFIED.
