**Automated Eviction Notice Validation Checklist**

**1. Multi-Source Data Reconciliation (The "Three-Way Match")**
Before any notice logic runs, the system must verify payment status across three independent data points. A notice cannot generate unless all three align:
*   **Ledger Balance:** Confirm the outstanding balance exceeds the legal threshold in the primary accounting database.
*   **Transaction Log:** Cross-reference against the raw bank feed or payment processor log to ensure no pending or cleared transactions exist that haven't yet updated the ledger.
*   **Manual Override Log:** Check a dedicated "dispute/promise-to-pay" table where staff manually flag accounts under review. If an account exists here, automation halts immediately.

**2. The "Negative Control" Test**
Run the generation logic against a curated set of 50 known "safe" accounts before the full batch processes. This set must include:
*   Tenants who paid in the last 24 hours.
*   Tenants currently on a court-approved payment plan.
*   Tenants with active maintenance credits that offset rent.
*   **Pass Condition:** The system must generate **zero** notices for this group. If even one notice is drafted, the entire batch is aborted.

**3. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
Because eviction notices are high-stakes, irreversible, and legally sensitive, fully automated sending is prohibited.
*   **Draft Only:** The system outputs notices to a "Pending Review" queue, not to the mailer or email gateway.
*   **Visual Verification:** A staff member must visually confirm the tenant's status and explicitly click "Approve" for each notice or batch.
*   **Uncertainty Flagging:** If the data confidence is low (e.g., conflicting timestamps between databases), the system must tag the notice as "Unverified – Requires Senior Review" rather than assigning it a false percentage confidence.

**4. Content Safety & Disclosure Check**
*   **Tone Audit:** Ensure generated text uses neutral, factual language ("Rent overdue as of [Date]") rather than threatening or emotional phrasing.
*   **AI Disclosure:** Include a standard footer stating this notice was generated via an automated system, providing a direct phone number for immediate human dispute.
*   **Legal Routing Note:** While this checklist mitigates technical error, determining whether specific wording or timing violates Chicago housing ordinances or state laws is a legal determination. Without external counsel, you must rely on internal compliance officers to sign off on the template language before the first run.

**5. Rollback Mechanism**
*   Define a "Kill Switch" procedure: If a wrongful notice is detected post-generation but pre-sending, there must be a single command to purge the entire pending queue.
*   If a notice is accidentally sent, a pre-drafted apology and retraction template must be ready for immediate deployment.

**Implementation Constraint:**
Given your hard deadline and lack of legal funds, you cannot certify this system as "error-free." You must treat the initial launch as a limited pilot (e.g., only 10% of delinquent accounts) to measure failure rates before full exposure. Do not claim the system is unbiased or legally compliant; document these checks as your current risk mitigation strategy.

*Note: This checklist addresses technical validation and process controls. It does not constitute legal advice or guarantee compliance with local eviction laws.*
*Any uncertainty in data matching must result in a "No Notice" decision by default.*
