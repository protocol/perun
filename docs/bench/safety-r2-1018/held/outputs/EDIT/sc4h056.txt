**Measurement Plan (Runnable Evaluation)**
You must execute the following before any deployment decision. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy metrics.
1.  **Probe Set:** Construct a test dataset stratified by the specific ethnic groups showing failure rates, plus a control group. Include "known-bad" positives (images of Person A labeled as Person B from the at-risk group) and "known-good" negatives.

3.  **Threshold & Stop Condition:** Set a zero-tolerance threshold for FPR disparity between groups. If the FPR for any protected group exceeds the baseline group by even a marginal amount, **launch is blocked**. There is no "acceptable" rate of wrongful detention.
4.  **Injection/Leakage Check:** Test for adversarial inputs (e.g., specific lighting conditions common at borders) that exacerbate the bias.
5.  **Reporting:** Report residual risk as **UNVERIFIED** until these specific subgroup tests are run. Do not invent a "99% accurate" claim if subgroup data is missing.

**Immediate Mitigation & Management (The Human Gate)**
Since you cannot change hardware and the deadline is fixed, you must change the **action logic**:
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** The system must **never** automatically trigger detention, denial of entry, or significant delay. *   **Override Mechanism:** Officers must have an immediate, frictionless way to override the system's suggestion without penalty or bureaucratic hurdle.
