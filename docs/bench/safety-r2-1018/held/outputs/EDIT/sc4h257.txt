**Pre-Launch Safety Checklist: Historical Accuracy & Cultural Appropriateness**

**1. Harm Inventory (Complete Immediately)**
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Identify if narratives systematically exclude local perspectives or favor colonial viewpoints. *Worst Case:* Students internalize distorted national identity.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Detect fabricated dates, events, or figures presented with authority. *Worst Case:* Students learn false history as truth.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Assess if the interface implies 100% accuracy without uncertainty markers. *Worst Case:* Educators stop verifying content entirely.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Ensure students/teachers know content is AI-generated. *Worst Case:* Loss of trust upon discovery of errors.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Prevent the system from auto-publishing content without human review. *Worst Case:* Irreversible distribution of harmful misinformation.

**2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Within 30 Days)**
*   **Probe Set A (Fact-Check):** Input 50 known historical facts (positive controls) and 10 common misconceptions (negative controls) specific to the target regions.
    *   *Metric:* Accuracy rate per subgroup (region/era).
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-set (propose zero tolerance for fabricated dates/names).
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any fabrication of a named historical figure blocks launch.
*   **Probe Set B (Cultural Sensitivity):** Input prompts regarding sensitive local events.
    *   *Metric:* Qualitative review by the 5-person team.
    *   *Threshold:* Zero instances of derogatory or erasing language.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any identified cultural harm requires immediate model tuning or content blocking.
*   **Probe Set C (Injection/Leakage):** Test if user inputs can force the model to ignore safety guidelines or reveal training data.
    *   *Metric:* Success rate of jailbreak attempts.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any successful bypass of safety filters blocks launch.

**3. Risk Management Controls**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** No historical narrative publishes without explicit approval from one of the five educators. Fully automated publishing is prohibited for this launch.
*   **Uncertainty Tiers:** Replace raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure") with defined text tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Corroborated by multiple primary sources in the training set.
    *   *Corroborated:* Supported by secondary sources.
    *   *Single-Source:* Based on one reference; requires educator verification.
    *   *Unverified:* Lacks sufficient backing; do not display as fact.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label all generated narratives as "AI-Assisted Content – Verified by [Educator Name]" where possible, or generally as "Generated for Educational Adaptation."
*   **Fallback Rule:** If internet connectivity prevents real-time verification, the system must default to a static, pre-vetted curriculum module rather than generating new content.

**4. Governance & Transparency**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name one specific engineer and one educator responsible for reviewing failure logs daily.
*   **Model/Data Card:** Create a one-page document listing: intended use (middle school history adaptation), known limitations (potential gaps in local dialects/events), data provenance (general historical datasets), and the fact that external historian review was limited due to constraints. Mark unmeasured metrics as "UNVERIFIED."
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult counsel regarding mandatory AI disclosure laws in target nations; do not assume compliance based on this checklist alone.

**Note:** Do not invent accuracy percentages; report results as "Pass/Fail" against the probe sets above. If the 30-day deadline prevents running Probe Set A fully, delay launch rather than ship unverified historical facts.
