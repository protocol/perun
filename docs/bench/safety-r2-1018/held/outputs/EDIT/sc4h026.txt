### Mitigation Plan: Preventing False Positives Against Indigenous Communities

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
Do not allow the system to automatically flag or alert rangers on any movement pattern until a human reviewer validates the output. Given the high stakes (potential criminalization of traditional practices) and your data limitations, fully automated alerts are unsafe.
*   **Action:** Implement a "Review Queue" where every algorithmic suspicion is first assessed by a designated liaison (ideally a trusted community member or ethnographer) before reaching patrol rangers.
*   **Constraint:** If no qualified liaison is available, the system must remain in "logging only" mode, producing no active alerts.

**2. Harm Inventory & Explicit Uncertainty Labeling**
Formalize the risk that the model will misclassify indigenous hunting as poaching due to missing ethnographic training data.
*   **Labeling:** Never present a prediction as a fact. Every output must carry a coarse confidence tier defined as **"Unverified – High Risk of Cultural False Positive"** rather than a percentage (e.g., avoid "87% likely poaching").
*   **Visual Cue:** Use a distinct color (e.g., amber) and text warning on the ranger interface stating: *"This pattern matches poaching behaviors but lacks cultural context. Verification required."*

**3. "Negative Control" Testing Protocol**
Before field testing, construct a specific evaluation set to measure bias, even without recent ethnographic data.
*   **Probe Set:** Synthesize or map known traditional hunting routes and times (based on existing historical maps or elder testimony) as "Known-Good Negative Controls."
*   **Test:** Run these through the model. If the system flags *any* of these known legitimate activities as suspicious, the model fails the safety gate and cannot be deployed for alerting.
*   **Metric:** Target **zero** flags on known legitimate indigenous patterns. Any failure here is a stop condition.

**4. Community Co-Design & Data Sovereignty**
Acknowledge that you cannot solve the data gap internally.
*   **Action:** Pause model refinement until a formal partnership is established with indigenous leadership to define "safe" vs. "suspicious" patterns.
*   **Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** explicitly stating: *"Intended Use: Ranger support only. Limitations: Model has not been trained on current indigenous hunting patterns; high risk of bias against local communities. Owner: [Team Lead Name]."* This document must accompany any demo or pilot.

**5. Reversible Actions & Override**
Ensure rangers can easily dismiss a flag and record *why* it was dismissed (e.g., "Confirmed traditional hunt"). This feedback loop is critical for future retraining and provides an audit trail proving the system did not drive enforcement actions blindly.

**Summary of Constraints:**
*   **No Autonomous Alerts:** No message goes to a ranger without human validation.
*   **No Fabricated Metrics:** Do not claim a "low false positive rate." State clearly that this metric is **UNVERIFIED** due to data gaps.
*   **Legal/Ethical Routing:** Consult legal counsel regarding indigenous rights and data sovereignty laws in Brazil before collecting new data or deploying.

***

*Note: This plan prioritizes preventing harm over system efficiency; if the human gate creates too much latency for operations, the system should not be used for real-time interception.*
*Ensure the "Model Card" is versioned and updated immediately if new ethnographic data becomes available.*
