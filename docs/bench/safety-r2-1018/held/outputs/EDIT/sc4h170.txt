### Pre-Deployment Contingency Plan & Checklist

**Immediate Action Plan (Next 96 Hours)**
Given the firmware lock and the critical risk of water waste due to calibration errors, full autonomy is unsafe. The plan prioritizes a "Human-in-the-Loop" gate for the initial planting window.

1.  **Disable Autonomous Actuation:** Deploy devices with the automatic valve-control feature disabled by default. The system will operate in "Monitor Only" mode, logging soil moisture data and sending alerts to farmers without executing water flow changes.
2.  **Manual Verification Protocol:** Establish a 14-day manual gate. Farmers receive daily SMS/App alerts recommending irrigation based on sensor data. A human must physically verify soil dryness (via spot-check or secondary manual probe) and explicitly confirm the command before water is released.
3.  **Staged Rollout:** Limit initial deployment to 10% of fields (low-risk zones). This caps the maximum potential water waste while validating sensor behavior in situ.
4.  **Fallback Mechanism:** Provide a physical "bypass key" or local override switch on the controller hardware to force immediate manual mode if the system behaves erratically, independent of software.

**Pre-Deployment Verification Checklist**
Execute these steps before devices leave the warehouse. Do not ship any unit that fails a single item.

*   **[ ] Sensor Calibration Stress Test:** Run a "known-bad" positive control test. Expose sensors to verified dry soil and verified saturated soil.
    *   *Pass Condition:* System correctly identifies dry soil as dry in 100% of trials.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any instance where dry soil is read as "saturated" triggers an immediate halt to shipping for that batch.
*   **[ ] Data Card Creation:** Generate a one-page "Sensor Limitations Card" for every device box.
    *   Must state: "Intended for loamy/clay soils; unverified on sandy substrates."
    *   Must state: "Autonomous mode disabled until 14-day manual verification complete."
    *   Must list: Owner contact and firmware version.
*   **[] Injection & Edge Case Probe:** Test sensor inputs with extreme values (e.g., sensor disconnect, electrical noise) to ensure the system defaults to "Safe/No-Flow" rather than guessing.
    *   *Pass Condition:* System reports "Error/Check Sensor" and keeps valves closed.
*   **[ ] Uncertainty Disclosure:** Verify the user interface displays confidence as a defined tier (e.g., "Reading Verified" vs. "Single Source/Unverified") rather than a precise percentage (e.g., "87% moist"), preventing over-reliance on potentially faulty data.
*   **[ ] Owner Assignment:** Name a specific engineer responsible for reviewing the first 48 hours of field data. No "team" ownership; one named individual must sign off on the transition from Manual to Autonomous mode.

**Residual Risk Statement**
The risk of sensor misclassification remains **UNVERIFIED** for real-world field conditions until the 14-day manual gate produces data. No claim of "zero waste" or "calibrated accuracy" can be made until the manual verification phase completes. If the planting season start date forces a launch before this data is collected, the system must remain in "Monitor Only" mode indefinitely until the owner signs off.

**Note:** This plan treats the firmware limit as a fixed constraint; do not attempt OTA fixes post-deployment.
