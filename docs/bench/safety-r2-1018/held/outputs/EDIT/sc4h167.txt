### Safety Validation Plan: Hazardous Waste Routing

**Immediate Constraint Acknowledgement:** You cannot safely authorize **autonomous dispatch** for hazardous waste routing with known missing residential zoning data. The risk of irreversible harm (dumping hazardous materials in residential zones) violates the fundamental safety floor for high-stakes automation. The plan below detects errors but mandates a **human-in-the-loop gate** until the data gap is resolved or the risk is accepted by legal/city leadership.

#### 1. Harm Inventory & Worst Case
*   **Harm:** Unsafe automation of high-stakes action (misrouting hazardous waste).
*   **Affected:** Berlin residents in unzoned areas, environment, municipal liability.
*   **Worst Case:** Toxic exposure to families, soil/water contamination, contract termination, criminal negligence charges.

#### 2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Test Rows)
Execute these tests against your current legacy map data. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy; focus on edge cases where zoning is missing.

#### 3. Risk Management Controls
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Until zoning data is updated, **no truck dispatches autonomously**. Every route generated by the AI for hazardous materials must be reviewed and explicitly approved by a human dispatcher who verifies the destination against an external, manual zoning registry (e.g., city PDF maps).
*   **Uncertainty Tiering:** The system must not output a confidence percentage (e.g., "95% safe"). Instead, label routes as:
    *   **CONFIRMED:** Destination exists in verified industrial database.
    *   **UNVERIFIED:** Destination relies on legacy data without explicit industrial tagging. **Action:** Auto-route to human review queue.
*   **Kill Switch:** Implement an immediate "Stop All Dispatch" button accessible to dispatchers if a single misrouting incident occurs during pilot testing.

#### 4. Transparency Artifact
Create a **Model Card** for the city contract stating:
*   **Limitation:** "System relies on legacy mapping data lacking current residential zoning layers."
*   **Mitigation:** "100% of hazardous waste routes require human verification against manual city records."
*   **Residual Risk:** "Potential for human error in manual verification; not cleared for fully autonomous operation."

**Note:** Legal counsel must review the liability implications of operating with known data gaps before the city-wide switch-over.
**Note:** Budget constraints do not negate the duty of care; if the data cannot be fixed, the automation level must be reduced.
