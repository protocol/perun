### 1. Harm Inventory (MAP)

*   **Affected Group:** Mobile-home residents in Florida hurricane zones.
*   **Worst Case:** Families receive insufficient funds to rebuild, leading to homelessness or financial ruin immediately post-disaster.

### 2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)
Since you cannot integrate new external data, you must rigorously test against what you have and simulate failure modes. Run these specific internal tests:

*   **Adversarial "Mobile-Home" Probe Set:** Isolate every satellite image in your existing training/validation set that contains *any* mobile home. Manually label a small subset (even 50–100 images) as a "Golden Set" with expert adjusters. Compare model outputs against this manual baseline.
    *   *Metric:* Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) specifically for the mobile-home subset vs. standard housing.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If error on mobile homes exceeds error on standard housing by >10%, block automated payout.
*   **Synthetic Stress Testing:** Generate synthetic satellite inputs representing known mobile-home failure modes (e.g., roof tie-down failures, skirting detachment, total overturning) which often look different from stick-built roof loss. Feed these to the model.
    *   *Goal:* Verify the model recognizes these specific visual signatures as "total loss" rather than "minor damage."
*   **Subgroup Slice Analysis:** Do not report aggregate accuracy. Report performance strictly segmented by "Mobile Home" vs. "Other." An aggregate "95% accurate" claim is misleading if the mobile-home slice is 40% accurate.

**Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** immediately. It must state: "Intended Use: Preliminary damage estimation. Limitations: Performance on mobile-home structures is UNVERIFIED due to lack of ground truth. Automated payouts for this segment are disabled."

### 3. Risk Management (MANAGE)
You cannot certify safety, so you must manage the residual risk through process controls:

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Configure the system to **auto-deny** full automation for any claim identified as a mobile home. These claims must route to a human adjuster for final review and sign-off before payment. This is the only way to prevent irreversible financial harm while meeting your launch timeline.
*   **Confidence Tiers, Not Percentages:** Do not display "87% confidence" to users or adjusters. Use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* High correlation with known damage patterns.
    *   *Unverified:* Mobile-home structure detected; requires human review.
*   **Explicit Disclosure:** Inform claimants that the initial assessment is AI-generated and subject to human review, especially for mobile homes.
