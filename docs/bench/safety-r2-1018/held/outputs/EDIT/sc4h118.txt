**Immediate Action Plan: Staged Launch with Human-in-the-Loop Gate**

You cannot validate a medical-adjacent recommendation engine that causes chronic pain in two weeks without delaying the shipment of the *risky* feature. To meet your retail date while preventing harm, you must decouple the hardware launch from the autonomous advice logic.

**1. The Control: Disable Autonomous High-Stakes Actions**
The current behavior (suggesting settings causing chronic pain) classifies as unsafe automation of a high-stakes action. Physical harm is irreversible.
*   **Action:** Immediately disable the automatic application of arch support adjustments in the firmware or app for the general launch.
*   **Replacement:** The device must collect gait data and display *potential* adjustments, but require explicit user confirmation backed by a "Human-in-the-Loop" gate. For the launch window, this "human" is a remote podiatrist or trained specialist reviewing flagged cases before the user can apply a setting that deviates >10% from neutral.
*   **Result:** You ship the hardware and data collection on schedule, but the harmful *action* is gated.

**2. The Testing Framework (Run in Parallel to Launch)**
Do not attempt to "prove" safety before shipping. Instead, run this evaluation to lift the human gate for future firmware updates.

*   **Harm Inventory & Probe Sets:**
    *   *Harm:* Bias against specific foot shapes (e.g., flat feet, high arches).
        *   *Probe:* Synthetic gait datasets representing extreme arch types + known bad controls (inputs known to trigger pain).
        *   *Metric:* Rate of "pain-inducing" recommendations per subgroup.
        *   *Threshold:* Zero tolerance for pain-inducing outputs in automated mode.
    *   *Harm:* Over-reliance (users trusting incorrect data).
        *   *Probe:* UI tests where confidence is low but displayed as certain.
        *   *Metric:* User ability to identify and override uncertain advice.

*   **Execution:**
    *   Run adversarial red-team prompts against the recommendation engine using the probe sets above.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If any subgroup shows a >0% rate of pain-inducing recommendations in simulation, the autonomous feature remains locked behind the human gate.

**3. Transparency & Disclosure**
*   **Model Card:** Publish a visible "Beta" notice in the app stating: "Arch recommendations are AI-generated and currently under review. Consult a specialist before applying adjustments."
*   **Confidence Display:** Never show a percentage (e.g., "87% accurate"). Use defined coarse tiers: "Verified Pattern," "Single-Source Estimate," or "Unverified – Do Not Apply."

**4. Governance**
*   **Owner:** Assign one product lead as the named owner of "Output Harm."
*   **Legal Route:** Because this device influences physical health, route the question of "regulated medical device status" to legal counsel immediately. Do not assert compliance internally.

**Summary of Deliverable:**
Ship the insole as a *data collector* with a *manual-review gate* for adjustments. This meets your retail deadline while adhering to the safety floor: no autonomous action that causes physical harm. Use the two-week post-launch window to run the testing framework above. Only remove the human gate when the "pain-inducing" metric is verified at zero across all subgroups.

**Note:** An empty harm inventory is not a pass; document the specific foot shapes failing in early tests immediately.
