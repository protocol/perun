Method (PREREGISTERED.md; hashes in SHA256-frozen.txt and ledger.jsonl)
- Arms: V0 shipped skill (prompts/V0.txt, v1.2.4), RIVAL humanizer v3.1.0 (the text vendored by ../comms-r5-1017/prompts/RIVAL.txt), NONE (a one-line neutral prompt), E1..E5 skill edits.
- A scenario is fictional raw notes plus gold for five required items: decision, action, owner, deadline, key number. Generator Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (3 samples at temperature 0.7); readers GLM-5.1 and gpt-oss_120b see only the generated message and five fixed questions; grader GLM-5.2, blind to the arm. Accuracy = true marks over 3 samples x 2 readers x 5 questions, per scenario; paired percentile bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 1. Guardrails are deterministic: a literal source fact dropped, a numeric token or weekday absent from the notes (invented), word count.
- Tune n=40 (scored before the held-out set existed): V0 0.712, NONE 0.742, RIVAL 0.713 (below NONE, so the edit trigger fired). Five edits tried on tune only: E1 +0.021, E2 +0.052, E3 +0.011, E4 +0.070, E5 +0.071 over V0; E1, E2 and E5 were barred by a higher tune invented rate; E4 selected by the preregistered rule, hashed, then 30 fresh held-out scenarios were written and every arm generated once.
- Tune per-question accuracy for the shipped skill: decision 0.71, action 0.50, owner 0.92, deadline 0.97, number 0.45; the weak spot is the key number, which the skill cuts or buries while the plain model states more figures.

Amendments (all written before any held-out call; AMENDMENT*.md): A1 a scenario audit (gold figures must occur in the notes; 4 of 40 tune scenarios replaced); A2 the invented-figure extractor stripped numbered-list markers after whitespace collapsed, and compared Thursday with Thu as different days (fixed.py patches bench._numbers; bench.py stays frozen); A3 12 of 40 tune gold actions were the drafting task itself and were replaced (tune scores had been seen; tune never yields a verdict). Held-out went through the same audit. The tune file scenarios_tune.json was rewritten in place by A3 and its frozen line replaced; the ledger records the superseded hash.

Known limits
- The grader marks some paraphrases false; it is the same for every arm but lowers all action scores. The "number" gold is the writer's pick of the deciding figure, so a message that headlines a different true figure is marked wrong.
- Scenario kinds are unbalanced after the audit (held: incident 9, pr 7, exec 6, decision 6, status 2), because status-note scenarios mostly produced a drafting gold action.
- One generator, two reader families, a grader that shares a vendor with one reader; scenarios written by a model; the skill is written for Claude and run here on another model. The invented check is regex-level and counts a correctly derived figure as invented in every arm alike.
- A failed call is retried by re-running; the first run of E3 and E4 on tune lost 80 and 180 grades to transport errors (HTTPError at concurrency 10) and a second run at concurrency 4 filled them before those arms were scored in full. Held-out had no missing record (missing_grades=0 for every arm). Outputs, reads and grades of the 12 tune scenarios dropped by A3 stay on disk and are not scored.
- scripts/rival_eval.py (chat client, bootstrap, GEN_TASK) is shared and not snapshotted here.

Reproduce: bash/zsh with PCSS_KEY in the environment; `bench.py scen|run SPLIT ARM..`, then `fixed.py report SPLIT ARM..` (BASE=V0). Results: report_tune.txt (V0 NONE RIVAL E1..E5), report_held.txt (V0 NONE RIVAL E4), per_scenario_*.json.
