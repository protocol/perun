**Sam, please approve Option A (guest checkout with email capture) or Option B (keep account but add social login) and the $180k rollout budget by Nov 30 to avoid vendor lock-in and address the 34% mobile checkout abandonment rate.**

Key figure: 34% mobile web checkout abandonment in Q3 (vs 22% in Q2).

**The Situation**
Mobile web checkout abandonment jumped from 22% in Q2 to 34% in Q3. Average queue times on the membership renewal path also increased from 3.1 minutes to 4.2 minutes. The audit identifies forced account creation for timed-entry tickets as the primary cause.

**The Risks**
Beyond revenue loss from abandonment, the report flags a legal risk: the payment button fails WCAG contrast ratio requirements. Additionally, the vendor contract renewal is due Nov 30; a decision after this date locks us into the current terms.

**The Options**
*   **Option A:** Implement guest checkout with email capture only.
*   **Option B:** Keep mandatory account creation but add social login.

**The Ask**
*   **Decision:** Pick Option A or Option B.
*   **Budget:** Approve $180k for rollout.
*   **Deadline:** Nov 30.
