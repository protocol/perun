**PR Description: Catalogue Migration Strategy Selection (Horizon to FOLIO)**

**Objective**
Requesting approval to select the migration approach for transferring 1.2M bibliographic records from the legacy Horizon system to the new FOLIO instance. A decision is required to proceed with technical preparation ahead of upcoming stakeholder milestones.

**Proposed Approaches**
Two strategies are under consideration based on pilot data:

*   **Approach A: Big-Bang Cutover**
    *   **Method:** Single migration event executed over a weekend.
    *   **Performance:** 92% success rate observed in pilot testing.
    *   **Impact:** Requires 6 hours of total system downtime.
    *   **Risk:** Higher potential for extended service interruption if issues arise during the window.

*   **Approach B: Phased Migration**
    *   **Method:** Segmented migration across three phases by collection type.
    *   **Performance:** 88% success rate observed in pilot testing.
    *   **Impact:** Zero downtime per phase; services remain available throughout.
    *   **Risk:** Slightly lower initial success rate requiring potentially more iterative data correction, but eliminates hard downtime.

**Context & Constraints**
*   **Scope:** 1.2M bibliographic records.
*   **User Impact:** 3,000 active users on the old system (training plan managed separately).
*   **Upcoming Milestones:**
    *   Vendor Demo: October 16.
    *   Library Board Status Update: October 20.
*   **Budget:** $150K allocated (not part of this technical approval).

**Decision Required**
Please review the trade-offs between the higher success rate with downtime (Approach A) versus the slightly lower success rate with continuous availability (Approach B). Approval of one approach is needed by **Friday, October 13**, to ensure alignment before the vendor demo and board update.
