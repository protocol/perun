**Summary**
This PR introduces a new pharmacy demand forecasting model to replace the current baseline. It also includes minor updates to stock alert thresholds and expands the test coverage. We are seeking your sign-off on the selected modeling approach to finalize the scope for the next sprint.

**Proposed Modeling Approaches**
The diff presents two distinct strategies for the new demand model:

*   **Approach A (Moving Average):** Utilizes a simple moving-average baseline.
    *   *Pros:* Low computational overhead; no external data dependencies.
    *   *Cons:* Higher forecast error.
*   **Approach B (Regression with Weather Covariates):** Implements a regression model incorporating weather data.
    *   *Pros:* Significantly improves accuracy, reducing Mean Absolute Error (MAE) from **12.4% to 9.1%** based on the last six months of data.
    *   *Cons:* Requires approximately **2.5x more compute runtime**. Additionally, the weather data pipeline has a **3-day lag**, which may impact real-time forecasting capabilities.

**Recommendation**
The team recommends proceeding with **Approach B**. Despite the increased compute cost and the 3-day data latency, the reduction in forecast error (3.3 percentage points) offers substantial value for inventory optimization. We believe the accuracy gains outweigh the operational costs, provided the latency constraint is documented for real-time use cases.

**Additional Changes**
*   **Stock Alert UI:** The threshold for triggering stock alerts has been adjusted from **5 days to 7 days** to align with the new forecasting horizon.
*   **Test Suite:** Coverage has been expanded to include **214 test cases** (up from 180), ensuring robust validation of both modeling approaches and UI changes.

**Action Required**
Please review the attached diff and confirm approval for **Approach B** by **Friday, Feb 16**. Your decision is critical to locking in the requirements for the upcoming sprint.
