**SUBJECT:** Urgent Decision Required: Parking Sensor Pilot Remediation

**Executive Summary**
The parking sensor pilot is currently experiencing significant performance degradation and budget variance. Occupancy accuracy has dropped to 71% last month, a sharp decline from 89% in March. Concurrently, project costs have reached $212K, exceeding the $180K budget by $32K. Immediate action is required to restore system reliability before the upcoming Board meeting on June 24.

**Root Cause Analysis**
Technical investigations confirm that signal interference from newly installed 5G towers is causing sensors in the Elm Street garage and the Riverside lot to misreport availability. This interference is the primary driver of the reduced accuracy metrics. While the vendor has indicated a potential firmware patch may be available by June 10, its efficacy is not guaranteed, posing an unacceptable risk for the Board presentation.

**Remediation Options**
Two viable paths forward have been identified to resolve the interference issues:

*   **Option A: Full Recalibration**
    *   **Scope:** Recalibrate all 1,400 sensors across the network.
    *   **Cost:** $48,000.
    *   **Timeline:** Can be completed prior to the July 4 long weekend.

*   **Option B: Targeted Replacement**
    *   **Scope:** Replace only the 320 affected units in the interference zones.
    *   **Cost:** $96,000.
    *   **Timeline:** Longer lead time due to hardware procurement and installation logistics.

**Recommendation**
We strongly recommend **Option A (Full Recalibration)**.

Despite addressing the entire fleet rather than just the affected units, Option A is both faster and significantly more cost-effective, saving $48,000 compared to Option B. Furthermore, a network-wide recalibration ensures uniform data integrity, eliminating the risk of residual interference affecting other zones as 5G coverage expands. This approach guarantees system stability in time for the June 24 Board meeting.

**Decision Request & Timeline**
To initiate work before the July 4 holiday and ensure results are ready for the Board, we require your approval by **Friday, June 14**.

**Next Steps upon Approval:**
1.  Authorize the $48K expenditure for recalibration.
2.  Schedule vendor deployment immediately.
3.  Validate accuracy metrics prior to June 24.
