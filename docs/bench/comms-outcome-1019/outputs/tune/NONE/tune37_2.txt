**Subject:** Decision Required: Parking Sensor Pilot Remediation

**Executive Summary**
The parking sensor pilot is currently experiencing significant performance degradation and cost overruns that require immediate executive intervention. Occupancy accuracy has declined from 89% in March to 71% last month. Concurrently, project costs have risen to $212K, exceeding the approved budget of $180K by $32K.

**Root Cause Analysis**
Technical investigations confirm that signal interference from newly installed 5G towers is causing sensors in the Elm Street garage and the Riverside lot to misreport space availability. This interference is the primary driver of the drop in system-wide accuracy.

**Remediation Options**
We have evaluated two distinct paths to restore system integrity:

*   **Option A: Full Recalibration**
    *   **Scope:** Recalibrate all 1,400 sensors across the network.
    *   **Cost:** $48,000.
    *   **Timeline:** Immediate deployment possible; completion expected before the July 4 holiday.

*   **Option B: Targeted Replacement**
    *   **Scope:** Replace only the 320 affected units in the interference zones.
    *   **Cost:** $96,000.
    *   **Timeline:** Procurement and installation delays likely to push completion past the July 4 holiday.

**Recommendation**
We strongly recommend **Option A (Full Recalibration)**.

Despite addressing a localized interference issue, recalibrating the entire network is both faster and significantly more cost-effective than replacing individual units. Option A saves $48,000 compared to Option B and ensures the system is fully operational before the upcoming board meeting on June 24. This approach mitigates the risk of presenting degraded metrics to the board and resolves the issue within the current fiscal constraints.

**Risk Note regarding Vendor Patch**
The vendor has indicated a potential firmware patch may be available by June 10. However, efficacy is not guaranteed, and reliance on this unproven solution poses a high risk of missing the board presentation deadline. We advise proceeding with the physical recalibration (Option A) as the primary strategy rather than waiting for uncertain software updates.
