**PR Description: Catalogue Migration Strategy Approval**

**Objective**
Requesting approval to select the migration approach for transferring 1.2M bibliographic records from the legacy Horizon system to the new FOLIO instance. A decision is required to proceed with the technical implementation plan.

**Proposed Approaches**
Two strategies are under consideration based on pilot data:

*   **Approach A: Big-Bang Cutover**
    *   **Method:** Single migration event executed over a weekend.
    *   **Performance:** 92% success rate observed in pilot testing.
    *   **Impact:** Requires 6 hours of system downtime.
    *   **Risk:** Higher potential for service interruption during the window; lower success rate compared to pilot expectations if issues arise mid-migration.

*   **Approach B: Phased Migration**
    *   **Method:** Segmented migration across three phases by collection type.
    *   **Performance:** 88% success rate observed in pilot testing.
    *   **Impact:** Zero downtime per phase; services remain available throughout the process.
    *   **Risk:** Slightly lower initial success rate per batch, but allows for iterative correction without total system blackout.

**Context & Constraints**
*   **Scope:** 1.2M bibliographic records.
*   **User Impact:** 3,000 active users on the old system (training plans are being managed separately and are not part of this technical decision).
*   **Upcoming Milestones:**
    *   Vendor Demo: October 16.
    *   Library Board Status Update: October 20.
*   **Budget:** $150K allocated (noted for context, not part of this approval request).

**Decision Required**
Please review the trade-offs between the higher success rate with downtime (Approach A) versus the zero-downtime model with a marginally lower success rate (Approach B). Your approval on one of these approaches is necessary to finalize the migration schedule.

**Deadline**
Approval is required by **Friday, October 13**, to ensure alignment before the vendor demo and board update.

***

Note: Training for the 3,000 active users is being handled separately and is excluded from this technical scope.
Note: The $150K budget is acknowledged but does not require action within this PR.
