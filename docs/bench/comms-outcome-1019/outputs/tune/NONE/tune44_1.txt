**Performance Analysis**
Our primary constraint is the 40ms response limit during peak loads for semesters with 5,000 courses.
*   **Approach A (Current Solver + Caching):** While this adds a caching layer, profiling indicates it will still hit the 40ms limit on 5k-course semesters. Furthermore, on smaller 2k-course runs, the caching improvement yields only a 9% gain, which is insufficient for our scalability goals.
*   **Approach B (Kotlin Rewrite):** The new algorithm in the spike successfully reduced peak conflict detection from **38ms down to 12ms**. This 12ms figure provides the necessary headroom for future growth and strictly adheres to our latency SLAs.

**Recommendation**
Based on the data, I recommend **Approach B**. The reduction from 38ms to 12ms is critical for stability under load, whereas Approach A fails to resolve the core bottleneck.
