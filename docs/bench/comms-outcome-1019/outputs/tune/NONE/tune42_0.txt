**PR Description: Catalogue Migration Strategy Selection (Horizon to FOLIO)**

**Summary**
This PR requests approval to proceed with the technical strategy for migrating 1.2M bibliographic records from the legacy Horizon system to the new FOLIO instance. We must select between two defined approaches to meet the upcoming vendor demo and Library Board reporting deadlines.

**Context & Scope**
*   **Data Volume:** 1.2M bibliographic records.
*   **Target System:** FOLIO.
*   **Source System:** Horizon.
*   **Stakeholder Impact:** 3,000 active users (training plan managed separately).
*   **Budget:** $150K (noted for context, excluded from this technical approval).

**Proposed Approaches**
We have evaluated two migration strategies based on pilot data and operational constraints:

*   **Approach A: Big-Bang Cutover**
    *   **Method:** Single migration event executed over a weekend.
    *   **Success Rate:** 92% (based on pilot).
    *   **Operational Impact:** Requires 6 hours of total system downtime.
    *   **Risk:** High visibility of failure if the single window is compromised; significant service interruption.

*   **Approach B: Phased Migration**
    *   **Method:** Migration executed in three phases segmented by collection.
    *   **Success Rate:** 88% (based on pilot).
    *   **Operational Impact:** Zero downtime per phase; services remain available throughout.
    *   **Risk:** Slightly lower aggregate success rate; increased complexity in coordination across three windows.

**Critical Deadlines**
Selection of the approach is required immediately to align with the following fixed dates:
*   **Approval Deadline:** Friday, Oct 13.
*   **Vendor Demo:** Oct 16 (Strategy must be locked to demonstrate path forward).
*   **Library Board Update:** Oct 20 (Status report must include confirmed methodology).

**Recommendation & Request**
While Approach A offers a higher statistical success rate (92% vs 88%), Approach B eliminates service downtime, preserving access for our 3,000 active users during the transition. Given the critical nature of continuous access for the Library Board update and the vendor demo, minimizing downtime is prioritized over the marginal difference in pilot success metrics.

**Action Required**
Priya, please review the data model implications of the phased segmentation in Approach B versus the single-load structure of Approach A. Your approval on the preferred technical approach is required by Friday, Oct 13, to ensure we can present a finalized plan to the vendors and the Board.
