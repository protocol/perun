**Subject: Decision Required by Nov 15: Evening Shift Pilot to Recover Perishables**

Hi Dana,

Following our recent ops review and the internal logistics audit completed on October 28, we need your direction on a critical scheduling issue affecting our perishable donations.

**Current Situation**
While shelf-stable donations dropped 12% in Q3 due to new sorting rules at three partner grocers, a more immediate operational inefficiency has been identified. Currently, 38% of our refrigerated trailers sit idle during peak donation hours (4:00 PM – 7:00 PM, weekdays) because our drivers are scheduled exclusively for morning pickups. This mismatch prevents us from capturing available food, leading to potential spoilage.

**Proposed Solution: Evening Shift Pilot**
Marcus in Logistics proposes shifting two drivers to an evening shift (2:00 PM – 9:00 PM). This adjustment is projected to recover approximately 1,500 lbs of perishables per week.

Implementing this pilot requires two specific operational changes:
1.  **Sanitation Schedule:** The warehouse cleaning window must move from its current time to 9:00 PM – 11:00 PM to avoid overlapping with the new driver shift.
2.  **Volunteer Staffing:** The volunteer coordinator noted the evening crew is currently short-staffed. We would need to recruit and provide a 2-hour training session for five additional volunteers. The estimated cost for this training is $800.

**Decision Options**
The board requires a final decision by **November 15**. Please choose one of the following paths:

*   **Option A: Adopt the Evening Shift Pilot.**
    *   *Action:* Approve the shift change, sanitation schedule adjustment, and $800 training budget.
    *   *Outcome:* Recover ~1,500 lbs of perishables weekly; mitigate spoilage risk.
*   **Option B: Maintain Current Schedules.**
    *   *Action:* Keep driver shifts in the morning and sanitation/cleaning in the evening.
    *   *Outcome:* No immediate costs, but we continue to leave 38% of refrigerated capacity unused during peak hours, risking the loss of donated perishables to spoilage.

Marcus is ready to execute either option immediately upon your approval. Please let us know your decision by the November 15 deadline so we can adjust the roster and training plans accordingly.
