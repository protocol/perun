**Current Situation**

More urgently, the audit revealed that 38% of our refrigerated trailers sit idle during peak donation hours (4:00 PM – 7:00 PM weekdays) because our drivers are currently scheduled only for morning pickups. This mismatch is resulting in significant spoilage of perishable items.

**Proposed Solution: Evening Shift Pilot**
Marcus in Logistics proposes shifting two drivers to an evening shift (2:00 PM – 9:00 PM). This change is projected to recover approximately 1,500 lbs of perishables per week.

Implementing this pilot requires two operational adjustments:
1.  **Sanitation Schedule:** The warehouse cleaning window must shift from its current time to 9:00 PM – 11:00 PM to avoid overlapping with the new driver schedule.
2.  **Volunteer Staffing:** The volunteer coordinator reports the evening crew is short-staffed. We will need to recruit and provide a 2-hour training session for five additional volunteers. The estimated cost for this training is $800.

**Decision Required**
The Board requires a final decision by **November 15**. We must choose between the following options:

*   **Option A: Adopt the Evening Shift Pilot.**
    *   *Action:* Approve the schedule changes, sanitation shift, and $800 training budget.
    *   *Outcome:* Recover ~1,500 lbs of perishables weekly; mitigate spoilage risks.
*   **Option B: Maintain Current Schedules.**
    *   *Action:* No changes to driver shifts or sanitation windows.
    *   *Outcome:* Continue losing perishable donations to spoilage during peak hours; no additional training costs incurred.
