**Migrate 1.2M bib records from Horizon to FOLIO via big-bang cutover (92% success, 6h downtime) or phased segments (88% success, zero downtime per phase); Priya, please approve one approach by Friday, Oct 13.**

This PR moves the full catalogue of 1.2M bibliographic records. We have validated two strategies:

*   **Option A: Big-bang cutover.** Executes the full migration over a single weekend. Pilot data shows a 92% success rate. The trade-off is a hard 6-hour system downtime window.
*   **Option B: Phased migration.** Splits the 1.2M records into three segments by collection. Pilot data shows an 88% success rate per phase. This approach eliminates downtime per phase but extends the overall migration timeline.

The decision rests on balancing the higher single-event success rate of Option A against the zero-downtime requirement of Option B. User training for the 3,000 active users and the $150K budget are handled outside this technical implementation PR.

Selecting an approach now is critical to meet the vendor demo on Oct 16 and the library board status update on Oct 20.

Training and budget items are tracked separately and excluded from this review.
No other technical dependencies block this decision.
