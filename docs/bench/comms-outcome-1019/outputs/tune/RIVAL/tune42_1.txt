**PR: Catalogue Migration Strategy – Horizon to FOLIO**

**Summary**
This PR requests approval for the migration strategy to move 1.2M bibliographic records from the legacy Horizon system to the new FOLIO instance. We must select an approach by Friday, Oct 13, to maintain the schedule for the vendor demo (Oct 16) and the Library Board status update (Oct 20).

**Options Analysis**
We are evaluating two migration paths based on pilot data:

*   **Option A: Big-Bang Cutover**
    *   **Method:** Migrate all 1.2M records in a single weekend event.
    *   **Success Rate:** 92% (pilot).
    *   **Impact:** Requires 6 hours of system downtime.
    *   **Risk:** Service interruption affects all 3,000 active users simultaneously.

*   **Option B: Phased Migration**
    *   **Method:** Migrate by collection segment across three phases.
    *   **Success Rate:** 88% (pilot).
    *   **Impact:** Zero downtime per phase; services remain available during transitions.
    *   **Risk:** Slightly lower overall success rate in pilot testing; extended migration window.

**Recommendation**
Approve **Option B (Phased Migration)**. While Option A offers a higher pilot success rate, the 6-hour downtime poses a significant disruption to 3,000 active users. Option B ensures continuous service availability, which aligns better with operational priorities. The 4% difference in success rate is acceptable given the benefit of zero downtime, and any data discrepancies can be addressed during the phased validation steps.

**Next Steps**
1.  **Approval Needed:** Please approve Option B by EOD Friday, Oct 13.
2.  **Training:** A separate plan will address training for the 3,000 active users; this is out of scope for this PR.
3.  **Budget:** The $150K migration budget is tracked separately and does not require action in this thread.

Once approved, we will finalize the phase segmentation and prepare the execution timeline for the vendor demo.
