Subject: Decision needed: 3.1.2 crash regression (94.2% crash-free)

Hi Maya,

The 3.1.2 rollout on Feb 12 triggered a crash regression. Crash-free sessions dropped to 94.2%, our lowest since 3.0.1 (97.8%).

Root cause is a memory leak in the new sync engine affecting ~8% of users on Android 12+. Consequently, support tickets for sync errors rose from 420 to 1,340 this week.

The build team has a patch ready and needs a go/no-go by Friday, Feb 20, to make the release train. We have two paths:

*   **Option A (Recommended):** Ship hotfix 3.1.3 with the leak patch. Beta testing on this fix shows 99.1% crash-free sessions over 48 hours with no reported crashes. This requires a minor update to Play Store notes.
*   **Option B:** Rollback to 3.1.1 and delay the sync feature by two weeks.

I recommend Option A given the stable beta results. Please let me know your decision or if you'd like to discuss. Could we hop on a brief call by EOD Thursday to finalize?

Thanks,
Nadia
