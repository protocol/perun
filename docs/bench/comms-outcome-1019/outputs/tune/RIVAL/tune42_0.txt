**PR: Catalogue Migration Approach Selection (Horizon to FOLIO)**

**Summary**
This PR requests approval to select the migration strategy for moving 1.2M bibliographic records from Horizon to FOLIO. We need a decision by Friday, Oct 13, to stay on track for the vendor demo (Oct 16) and library board update (Oct 20).

**The Choice**
We are evaluating two approaches:

*   **Option A: Big-Bang Cutover.** Execute the full migration over a single weekend.
    *   *Pros:* Higher pilot success rate (92%).
    *   *Cons:* Requires 6 hours of system downtime.
*   **Option B: Phased Migration.** Move data in three segments by collection.
    *   *Pros:* Zero downtime per phase; service remains available.
    *   *Cons:* Slightly lower pilot success rate (88%).

**Context**
The scope covers the full 1.2M record set. While Option A offers a marginal gain in success probability, Option B eliminates the service interruption that affects our 3,000 active users. (Note: User training is being handled separately and is not part of this technical decision. The $150K budget is confirmed but excluded from this technical review.)

**Action Required**
Priya, please review the trade-off between the 4% success rate difference and the 6-hour downtime impact. Approve either Option A or Option B in this thread by EOD Friday, Oct 13.
