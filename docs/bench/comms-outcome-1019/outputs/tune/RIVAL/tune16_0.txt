**Title:** Pharmacy demand model: regression with weather covariates (Approach B)

**Summary**
This PR introduces the new pharmacy demand forecasting model. We are requesting approval to merge **Approach B** (regression with weather covariates) over Approach A (moving-average baseline).

*   **Forecast Accuracy:** Approach B reduces mean absolute error from 12.4% to 9.1% based on the last six months of data.
*   **Performance Trade-off:** The improved accuracy comes with a 2.5x increase in runtime.

*   **UI Update:** Stock alert thresholds have been adjusted from 5 days to 7 days.
*   **Test Coverage:** The test suite has been expanded from 180 to 214 cases.

**Recommendation**
We recommend proceeding with Approach B despite the higher compute cost and data latency, given the significant reduction in forecast error.
