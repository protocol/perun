**PR: Pharmacy demand model – regression with weather covariates (Approach B)**

This PR updates the stock-forecast benchmark with two candidate models for pharmacy demand:

- **Approach A:** Moving-average baseline.
- **Approach B:** Regression model incorporating weather covariates.

**Recommendation:** Adopt **Approach B**.

**Key results (last 6 months of data):**
- Forecast error (MAE) drops from **12.4%** (A) to **9.1%** (B).
- Test coverage increases to **214 cases** (up from 180).

**Trade-offs and constraints:**
- **Compute cost:** Approach B requires approximately **2.5x** more runtime than A.
- **Data latency:** The weather data pipeline has a **3-day lag**, which may impact real-time forecasting accuracy.

**Additional changes:**
