**Pharmacy demand model: choose regression (B) for 9.1% error vs. moving average (A) at 12.4%, accepting 2.5x runtime and 3-day weather data lag.**

We propose two approaches for the new pharmacy demand model:

*   **Approach A (Moving Average):** Uses a moving-average baseline. Mean absolute error (MAE) is 12.4%. Runtime is standard. No external data dependencies.
*   **Approach B (Regression):** Uses regression with weather covariates. MAE drops to 9.1% on the last 6 months of data. Runtime increases by ~2.5x. Relies on weather data that lags by 3 days, potentially impacting real-time accuracy.

The team is leaning toward **Approach B** for the significant error reduction, despite the higher compute cost and data latency risk.
