**Pitch Readiness Assessment: CocoaPulse Pilot**

**1. Strongest Objection: The Temperature & Margin Trap**
Your unit economics collapse if the "non-refrigerated pallet" assumption fails. Chocolate requires strict temperature control (18°C) to prevent bloom, especially during Belgian winters (heated transport) or unexpected warm spells. If you are forced to switch to temperature-controlled logistics, shipping costs will likely jump from €3 to €8+ per box.
*   **Current Math:** Revenue €25 - Costs (€4 prod + €2 pack + €3 ship) = **€16 Gross Margin**.
*   **Failure Math:** Revenue €25 - Costs (€4 prod + €2 pack + **€8 ship**) = **€11 Gross Margin**.
This 31% margin erosion destroys the profitability needed to subsidize the 30% upgrade acquisition cost. If the cold chain breaks, you are paying to acquire customers who immediately degrade your product quality, leading to the high churn you fear.

**2. Smarter Route: The "Neighborhood Cluster" Pilot**
Do not launch simultaneously in Brussels and Antwerp. The logistical complexity of maintaining two distinct cold chains doubles your operational risk for unproven demand.
*   **Recommendation:** Restrict the pilot to **one specific postal code cluster in Brussels** for the first 90 days.
*   **Why:** This allows you to use a single, localized distribution partner (or even a dedicated van run) to guarantee temperature control without expensive palletized freight. It validates the "upgrade" conversion rate in a dense market before expanding to Antwerp. If the model works in one dense cluster, scaling to a second city is an operational task, not a strategic gamble.

**3. Abandonment Metric (Kill Criterion)**
**Stop the pilot if: Month 4 Net Retention Rate < 85% (equivalent to >15% monthly churn) among non-upgraded users.**
*   **Reasoning:** You projected a 30% upgrade rate. If more than 15% of the remaining 70% leave every month after the initial novelty wears off, the Lifetime Value (LTV) of the "standard" subscriber becomes negative once marketing costs are included. Waiting until month 6 is too late; by then, you will have burned cash on inventory and logistics for a cohort that has already rejected the core value proposition.

**Notes:**
Your revenue projection of €562,500 assumes 100% retention or perfect replacement of churned users; ensure your cash flow plan accounts for the working capital needed to replace subscribers monthly.
Verify the specific thermal inertia of your packaging with a real-world test run before committing to the December 1st start date, as holiday traffic delays could expose boxes to freezing temperatures.
