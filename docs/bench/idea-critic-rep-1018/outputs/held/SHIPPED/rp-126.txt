**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the six-month pilot on the Nairobi–Eldoret and Nairobi–Mombasa corridors only, conditional on securing a written "no-objection" or interim guidance from the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority (KCAA) before the July 1, 2025 start date. Do not commit to the remaining ten routes or the full $250k fee structure until the pilot validates the revenue lift and regulatory standing.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Halt:** The KCAA deems the algorithm non-compliant with fare transparency or consumer protection rules, forcing an immediate suspension after the $250k sunk cost.
2.  **Elasticity Mismatch:** Your assumption that price sensitivity remains constant across peak/off-peak seasons proves false; if the model overcharges during off-peak lows or undercharges during peaks, actual revenue may drop below the baseline rather than lifting it by 15%.
3.  **Data Integrity:** The engine relies on historical booking data that does not reflect post-pandemic commuter behaviors, leading to systematic mispricing that alienates core riders on the two pilot corridors.

**Kill Criteria & Triggers**
*   **Regulatory Trigger:** If the KCAA issues a formal compliance notice or requests a review that delays launch beyond August 1, 2025, **CANCEL** immediately.
*   **Performance Trigger:** If the pilot generates less than a **5% revenue uplift** (vs. the projected 15%) by the end of Q3 2025 (first quarter of the pilot), **PAUSE** expansion and re-evaluate the elasticity model.
*   **Customer Sentiment Trigger:** If customer complaints regarding fare volatility increase by more than **20%** compared to the previous year's baseline on the pilot routes, **PAUSE** algorithmic adjustments and revert to static pricing pending review.
