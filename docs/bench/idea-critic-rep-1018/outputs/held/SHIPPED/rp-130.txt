**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, time-boxed pilot (Go-If), contingent on immediate membrane validation.**

The proposal survives a basic financial stress test: even if water tariffs remain flat (ignoring the 5% escalation), the annual gross saving from a 30% reduction in 15,000L of dye usage likely exceeds the $128,000 total annual cost (amortized capital + disposal), provided the dye cost per liter is above ~$2.85. However, the plan relies on a fragile assumption regarding membrane longevity under high-temperature cycles. Do not commit to the full three-year horizon or the full $350,000 spend yet. Instead, authorize a **$50,000 capped proof-of-concept** for July–September 2025 focused solely on validating membrane degradation rates. If the membrane shows >10% efficiency loss in the first 90 days, the unit economics collapse, and the project must pivot to alternative filtration or stop.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Membrane Degradation:** High-temperature cycles reduce membrane life to <18 months, doubling the effective capital cost and erasing net savings.
2.  **Volume/Price Mismatch:** If actual dye consumption is lower than 15,000L or market dye prices drop, the 30% reduction yields insufficient cash flow to cover the $8,000 disposal fee and capital amortization.
3.  **Operational Drag:** The pilot disrupts current production schedules, incurring hidden labor or downtime costs not modeled in the $120,000 net saving.

**Kill Criterion & Leading Indicator:**
*   **Kill Trigger:** Halt the project if membrane filtration efficiency drops by **>10%** within the first **90 days** of the pilot (by 30 September 2025).
*   **Leading Metric:** Track **liters of dye recovered per membrane hour** weekly. A downward trend line in this specific metric during the first month is the early warning signal that the three-year lifespan assumption is false.
