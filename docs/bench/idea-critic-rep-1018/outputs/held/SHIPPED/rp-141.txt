**Subject:** Nimbus Shelf Pilot – Recommended Path & Risk Controls  

**Recommended Course of Action**  
Approve a **capped, 6-month pilot** limited to Finnish and Swedish only, with a hard budget ceiling of **€120,000** (roughly half the proposed integration cost). Defer Arabic and Somali support until verified translation costs are secured. This approach tests the core recommendation engine's value without exposing the library to unquantified linguistic integration risks or overspending the €500,000 allocation on unproven assumptions.

**Principal Failure Points**  
1.  **Unverified Linguistic Costs:** The plan assumes zero extra training data for Arabic and Somali; if specialized translation services or data labeling are required, the €210,000 integration estimate will collapse.  
2.  **Baseline Mismatch:** The projected 15% lift extrapolates from a 3-month, 250,000-visit test of a simpler "language-filter" tool. A complex AI recommendation engine may behave differently over a 12-month horizon, and the previous baseline may not reflect current patron behavior.  
3.  **Adoption Uncertainty:** There is no evidence quantifying how many patrons will actually engage with AI recommendations versus traditional browsing, making the ROI calculation speculative.

**Stop-or-Pivot Condition**  
Abandon or pivot the project if, by the **3-month mark** of the pilot, the **weekly active usage rate** of the recommendation feature fails to reach **5% of total catalog searches**. This leading metric directly validates patron interest before further capital is committed. If usage remains below this threshold, the assumed 15% checkout lift is unreachable, and the project should halt to prevent sinking costs into a feature patrons ignore.

*Note: The original 12-month timeline and full four-language scope carry too much financial risk given the unknown translation variables. The proposed 6-month, bilingual cap isolates the technology risk from the linguistic risk.*
