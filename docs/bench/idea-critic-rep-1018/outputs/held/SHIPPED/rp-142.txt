**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, time-boxed pilot (Go-If), not full rollout.**
Approve the $4.2M investment only if tied to a 6-month validation phase targeting 40% capacity (48 panels/month) before scaling to full 120-unit output. This limits exposure to the unpriced logistics and tariff risks while validating the core cost assumption.

**Top Failure Modes:**
1.  **Logistics Erasure of Margin:** The unpriced transport from Jebel Ali to sites 45–80km away could easily consume the $1,400 per-panel savings. If heavy haulage and permit costs exceed $1,500 per panel, the modular advantage vanishes compared to on-site building.
2.  **Tariff & Inflation Shock:** Relying on a 2023 steel quote amidst unclear inflation creates a massive budget hole. A 15% increase in steel tariffs or material costs alone would push the unit cost above your $8,500 kill threshold immediately.
3.  **Labor Assumption Fragility:** The model assumes labor stays fixed at 12% of total cost. If specialized factory technicians command a premium over general site labor (common in modular shifts), this ratio breaks, inflating the baseline cost before logistics are even added.
