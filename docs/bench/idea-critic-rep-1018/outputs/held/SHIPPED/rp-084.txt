**Recommendation:** Approve the pilot only as a "go-if" contingent on a verified connectivity audit and a capped, time-boxed first tranche of $40,000 (10 headsets + content) rather than the full $125,000 commitment.

**Key Failure Points:**
1.  **Connectivity Collapse:** The assumption that NGOs will provide routers at no charge is unverified, and the known 2 Mbps peak bandwidth at one site is insufficient for 10 simultaneous VR streams, risking a total inability to deploy the curriculum.
2.  **Budgetary Illusion:** The $125,000 arithmetic excludes mandatory ongoing costs (headset maintenance, software licensing, and hardware replacement), creating a funding cliff after Year 1 that could strand the assets.
3.  **Logistical Friction:** Rolling out to three remote Amazon sites by 15 Oct 2024 leaves negligible margin for customs delays or on-site training disruptions, threatening the entire pilot timeline.

**Kill Criterion:** Halt expansion or pivot to offline-only content if the connectivity audit confirms sustained bandwidth below 5 Mbps per school or if the NGO router commitment is not formalized in writing by 1 Sept 2024.

*Note: The current budget underestimates total cost of ownership by excluding Year 2+ licensing and repair reserves. A single-site proof-of-concept is safer than a three-school rollout given the bandwidth uncertainty.*
