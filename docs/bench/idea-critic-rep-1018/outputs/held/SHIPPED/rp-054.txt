**Why this could flop:**
1.  **Certification Block:** If automotive OEMs require a new, lengthy certification process, your Q3 2025 revenue start date slips, extending the 14-month breakeven indefinitely while fixed costs burn.
2.  **Hidden Labor & Uptime:** Your model assumes 90% uptime and static $3 finishing costs. If real-world uptime drops to 80% or finishing complex printed parts requires skilled labor ($15+/hr) instead of current methods, your $75k monthly savings evaporates, turning the project cash-flow negative.

**Kill Criterion:**
Stop the project immediately if, **six months after launch**, the net cash flow (savings minus added labor, software, and material costs) is **under $30,000**. This threshold accounts for ramp-up friction; failing to hit it proves the unit economics cannot support the capital debt.
