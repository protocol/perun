**Principal Failure Points**
1.  **Margin Collapse:** The $8 kit cost explicitly excludes the $3 shipping fee you identified. This raises the variable cost per unit by 37.5%, slashing the contribution margin from $7 to $4 per subscriber. At 2,500 subscribers, monthly gross profit drops from $17,500 to $10,000, pushing breakeven from month 8 to month 14 and exhausting the $85k pilot budget by month 5.

To hit 2,500 subscribers, you need roughly 13 successful blasts at that exact rate or a viral coefficient not currently modeled. If the actual conversion is a more industry-standard 1–2%, you will run out of budget before acquiring 1,000 subscribers.
