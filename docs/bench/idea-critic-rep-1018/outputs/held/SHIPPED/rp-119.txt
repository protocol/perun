**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the pilot only if the vendor caps the monthly service fee at **€650** in writing before signing. At €800/month, the project burns cash immediately; at €650, it breaks even in month four assuming your volume targets hold. Do not sign without this cap, as the current "around €800" estimate destroys the business case.

**Principal Failure Modes**
1.  **Adoption Math Error:** Your 70% adoption rate (2,450 users) implies each of the 3,500 members visits the specific lobby frequently enough to use the kiosk. With only two units, physical congestion and location friction will likely cap actual adoption near 30–40%, slashing check-out volume and extending payback beyond the pilot window.
2.  **Negative Unit Economics:** If the service fee is €800, your annual cost is €57,000 (€47k hardware + €9.6k service). Your projected savings are only €9,000. Unless the 1,200 monthly check-outs generate new revenue or significant non-labor value not listed, you are losing ~€48,000 in year one. The savings do not cover the service fee, let alone the hardware.
3.  **Staff Displacement Illusion:** The €9,000 savings assumes you will reduce part-time hours immediately. If staff are retained despite the kiosk (common in unionized or fixed-contract environments), the "savings" are imaginary cash flow, leaving you with full labor costs plus the new kiosk fees.

**Kill Criterion (Pull the Plug)**
Halt the project if **actual check-outs average below 700 per month** by the end of Month 3. This is the threshold where the operational value fails to justify even a negotiated €650 service fee, indicating the adoption model is fundamentally broken regardless of price.
