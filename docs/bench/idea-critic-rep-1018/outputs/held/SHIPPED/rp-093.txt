**Verdict: REVISE**

**Single Red Flag:** The claim that 24,000 tonnes equals the emissions of your entire 28-aircraft short-haul fleet is almost certainly false. A single short-haul flight often emits 5–10 tonnes; if each of 28 aircraft flies just 4 times a day for a year, the fleet emits hundreds of thousands of tonnes, not 24,000. Presenting this equivalence to the COO would destroy the proposal's credibility immediately.

**Key Failure Routes:**
1.  **Mathematical Collapse:** As noted, the "fleet equivalence" marketing claim fails basic arithmetic, risking accusations of greenwashing.
2.  **Revenue vs. Cost Mismatch:** Projected revenue is ~$864k (1.2M passengers * 12% uptake * $5 * 12 months), yet the budget is $2.5M. Even ignoring offset costs, the program loses >$1.6M in year one. At $15/tonne, purchasing 24,000 tonnes costs $360k, leaving only ~$504k for marketing and integration against a $2.2M non-offset budget. The unit economics are negative.
3.  **Regulatory Blind Spot:** Assuming the subscription is not a "tax-deductible benefit" or subject to specific carbon-claim regulations is a dangerous assumption. If regulators require strict pass-through accounting or disallow the deduction, the financial model breaks.

**Recommended Alternative:**
Pivot from a "revenue-generating subscription" to a "break-even opt-in contribution."
*   **Remove the $5 fee:** Frame it as a direct donation where 100% of the passenger's contribution buys offsets, removing the profit motive that invites scrutiny.
*   **Correct the Claim:** Market it as "Offset your specific journey" rather than "Offsetting our whole fleet."
*   **Fix the Budget:** Slash the $2.5M budget. Integration should be a minor IT tweak, not a multi-million dollar project. Use existing verification partners rather than a $300k custom fee.
*   **Re-calculate:** If 12% of passengers participate, calculate the *actual* tonnes their specific flights emit and buy exactly that amount. Do not promise fleet-wide neutrality based on a subscription add-on.

**Note:** You must re-verify the fleet emission totals and the $2.5M budget line items before showing this to anyone. The current numbers suggest the program is designed to lose money while making an unverifiable environmental claim.
