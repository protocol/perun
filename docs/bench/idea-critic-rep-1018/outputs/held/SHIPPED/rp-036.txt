**Decision: GO-IF** the Ministry of Primary Industries (MPI) confirms a regulatory approval path within 8 weeks AND a pilot with three member farms validates transaction fees at ≤$0.01 per batch.

**Strongest Objection (The Deal-Breaker):**
The plan assumes a $350k net saving based on "reduced recalls" and "premium pricing," yet admits the regulatory timeline for blockchain traceability is unknown. If MPI approval takes >12 months, the $165k upfront cost ($120k license + $45k integration) yields zero operational value during that period, turning the projected 24-month ROI into a net loss. Furthermore, if transaction fees exceed $0.01 even slightly across 78 farms processing 1.2M litres/month each, the volume-based costs will erode the "premium pricing" margin entirely, making the system a cost center rather than a savings engine.

**Smarter Alternative:**
Before committing $165k, execute a **90-day "Paper Blockchain" pilot** with three representative farms. Manually simulate the data entry and traceability workflows using existing low-cost database tools or even secured spreadsheets to verify the operational burden on farmers and the actual data granularity required by MPI. This costs < $5k, validates the "user value vs. replay" risk (ensuring farmers can actually provide the data), and provides concrete evidence to negotiate the software license or pivot to a cheaper, non-blockchain centralized ledger if the regulatory body accepts equivalent proof of provenance.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers:**
1.  **Regulatory Stop:** If MPI does not provide a written preliminary assessment or approval pathway within **8 weeks** of submission, halt all integration spending. The risk of a 12+ month delay invalidates the 24-month payback window.
2.  **Cost Cap:** If the verified integration quote exceeds **$60k** (not the estimated $45k), terminate the vendor negotiation. The budget has no contingency for a >33% overrun on fixed costs.
3.  **Fee Threshold:** If the pilot demonstrates average transaction fees > **$0.012** per batch (allowing a 20% buffer over the $0.01 assumption), abandon the blockchain approach in favor of a traditional centralized database, as the volume (approx. 93.6 million litres/month across the co-op) makes fee sensitivity the primary failure mode.

**Leading Metric to Watch:**
MPI regulatory response time (weeks) and actual cost-per-batch during the 3-farm pilot. Review date: 15 Jan 2025.
