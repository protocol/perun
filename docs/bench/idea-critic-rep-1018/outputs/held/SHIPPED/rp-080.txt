**Executive Summary: Critical Gaps & Recommendations**

The current proposal contains fatal arithmetic and timeline errors that invalidate the budget and feasibility assessment.

1.  **Budget Mismatch:** 12 drones at $95,000 each equals **$1.14 million**, not $1.2 million. More critically, your maintenance math is incorrect: $18,000 × 12 drones = **$216,000/year**, not $80,000. The total Year 1 cost is actually ~$1.54M (hardware + training + correct maintenance), a 28% underestimate.
2.  **Impossible Timeline:** You cannot deploy operational hardware by **1 March 2025**. Today is 12 October 2026. The target date is 18 months in the past. If you meant 2027, procurement and training for 12 units typically require 6–9 months minimum, making a Q1 launch highly aggressive.
3.  **Flawed Efficacy Logic:** Extrapolating a 5% reduction from a single month's variance (30 vs. 28 incidents) is statistically invalid. This is normal noise, not a trend. A 2-unit drop does not prove causation or predict future performance at scale.
4.  **Unverified Assumption:** The belief that community groups will accept constant drone noise without protest is a high-risk assumption. Noise pollution often triggers local opposition, potentially halting operations regardless of technical success.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Financial:** The project runs out of operating cash in Month 4 due to the underestimated maintenance burden.
*   **Operational:** Poachers adapt by operating at night or in dense canopy where quad-copters are less effective, rendering the "presence" deterrent useless.
*   **Social:** Local protests ground the fleet due to noise complaints, creating a political crisis for the board.

**Recommended Alternative Approach**
Do not commit to 12 units immediately. Adopt a **phased hybrid model**:
*   **Phase 1:** Deploy 3 drones integrated with existing ground patrols (not replacing them) for a 90-day pilot.
*   **Integration:** Use satellite imagery for broad sweeps and drones only for verification, reducing flight hours and noise.
*   **Cost:** This lowers initial hardware risk and validates the community acceptance variable before full spend.

**Kill Criteria (Abort/Re-engineer Triggers)**
*   **Metric:** If illegal incidents do not decrease by at least **15%** (not 2%) after the first 90 days of *verified* operational uptime. A 2% threshold is too low to justify the capital expenditure; if you can't achieve 15%, the deterrent effect is negligible.
*   **Social:** If formal complaints from community leadership exceed **two** documented instances regarding noise or privacy.
*   **Financial:** If actual maintenance costs exceed the $18k/unit projection by more than 10% in the first quarter.

**Note:** Recalculate the ROI using the corrected $216k maintenance figure before presenting to the board. The current proposal will be rejected immediately upon financial review due to the math errors.
