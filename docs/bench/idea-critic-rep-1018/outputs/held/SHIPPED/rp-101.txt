**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, conditional pilot only (Go-If).**
Do not commit to the full 2,400-keg rollout. Authorize the €30k pilot strictly as a discovery phase to validate battery life and ERP integration costs. Cap total exposure at €50k (including a contingency for integration) until the pilot proves a net positive ROI after actual integration fees. The current proposal relies on unverified assumptions that could erase the projected margin.

**Principal Failure Modes:**
1.  **Battery Life Mismatch:** If sensors fail before 12 months, replacement labor and hardware costs will exceed the €300k annual savings projection, turning the project into a recurring operational loss.
2.  **Hidden Integration Costs:** The omitted €20–30k ERP fee reduces the first-year net savings from €270k to ~€240k, pushing the true ROI break-even point beyond the promised six months.
3.  **Inaccurate Loss Baseline:** The 18% loss reduction assumes your loss profile matches the rival brewery's. If your actual loss rate is lower or caused by factors sensors cannot track (e.g., internal theft vs. external loss), the €300k savings figure is inflated.

**Abandonment Trigger (Kill Criterion):**
Terminate the project immediately if the pilot demonstrates **less than 10% net savings** (after deducting actual integration and maintenance costs) over a 3-month period, or if **any sensor battery fails** before 9 months of operation.

*Note: The rival brewery's data (500 sensors) is too small to extrapolate linearly to 2,400 kegs without accounting for network congestion or management overhead.*
