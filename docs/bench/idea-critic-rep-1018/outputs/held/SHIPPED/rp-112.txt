**Why this plan could collapse:**
1.  **Margin Illusion:** The modeled 20% gross margin likely vanishes once the €0.45/litre packaging surcharge is applied. At 15,000 litres/day, this is a €6,750 daily cost impact (~€2.4M annually) that could turn the project unprofitable before the first unit sells.
2.  **Shelf-Life Bottleneck:** If the pea-protein base shelf-life under current refrigerated logistics is significantly shorter than dairy, distribution reach shrinks, making the 30% market share target physically impossible without new infrastructure.
3.  **Capacity Mismatch:** Allocating 20% of total plant capacity to a new category creates massive operational rigidity; if the 12% market growth assumption slows, you cannot easily repurpose the line, leaving €4.2M stranded.

**Abort Signal (Kill Criterion):**
Stop the project immediately if the feasibility phase confirms that the **net gross margin (after the €0.45 surcharge) falls below 12%** OR if the **verified shelf-life is under 14 days**. These thresholds ensure the unit economics survive the hidden cost and the product fits the existing logistics network; failing either means the core business model is broken.
