**Board Brief: Greenflare East Africa Rollout – Critical Risks & Decision Gates**

**1. The Strongest Objection: Unit Economics Collapse**
The projection that a 5% transaction fee covers all operating costs is the plan's fatal flaw. With $1.44M in gross revenue, the platform collects only **$72,000** in fees. This must cover the budgeted $620,000 in upfront costs (tech, compliance, marketing) plus ongoing operations, farmer support, and verification expenses. The model currently operates at a massive loss from day one, assuming the fee covers costs without a mathematical basis. Without a revised revenue model or significant subsidy, the marketplace is insolvent before launch.

**2. A Smarter Path: Partner, Don't Build**
Building a proprietary platform ($320k tech budget) is unnecessary and risky given the regulatory uncertainty (3–6 months with no concrete timeline). A smarter approach is to **partner with an existing certified exchange** already operating in East Africa. This leverages their established regulatory standing, reduces tech spend to near zero, and shifts the burden of compliance. This allows Greenflare to focus capital on farmer onboarding and verification rather than infrastructure, validating demand before committing to a custom build.

**3. Kill Criteria: When to Pivot or Stop**
Abandon or pivot the project if either of the following occurs:
*   **Adoption Failure:** Fewer than **400 farms** (33% of target) sign binding agreements within the first **six months** of operational launch.
*   **Regulatory Stall:** No formal regulatory approval or written pathway is secured from the Ministry of Environment within **9 months** (extending the worst-case 6-month estimate by 50%).

*Note: The current budget assumes the transaction fee covers ongoing ops, but the math shows a $548k deficit immediately. Recalculating the unit economics is a prerequisite for any board approval.*
