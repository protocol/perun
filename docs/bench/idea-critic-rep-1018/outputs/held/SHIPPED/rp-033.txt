**Decision: GO-IF (Capped Pilot)**
Do not recommend a full 4-unit go-ahead tomorrow. Instead, propose a **2-unit, 6-month pilot** capped at **$380,000** (hardware + integration), withholding the remaining budget until specific risks are retired. The current plan relies on unverified assumptions that could turn a capital investment into a stranded asset.

**Critical Holes & Triggers**
1.  **Revenue Illusion:** You project a $210,000 revenue boost from an 8% circulation lift. Libraries typically do not charge per checkout; this "revenue" is likely theoretical value or grant eligibility, not cash flow to pay the $45,000/year maintenance. If this is not actual new cash, your ROI is negative from Day 1.
    *   *Trigger:* If the Board cannot identify the **cash source** for the $45k annual fee within the existing operating budget (not capital), **STOP**. Do not sign the hardware contract.
2.  **Adoption Math:** The 70% adoption rate assumes the Eastside pilot (5,200 checkouts/2 weeks) scales linearly. However, if the June 2025 network upgrade slips, the kiosks become paperweights. You have no penalty clause or fallback for this dependency.
    *   *Trigger:* If the Network Team cannot provide a **written guarantee** that the backend will be ready by May 1, 2025 (one month before rollout), **DELAY** the hardware purchase.
3.  **The "To Be Determined" Trap:** Signing a $608k hardware deal with an undefined maintenance contract is a governance failure. The vendor holds all leverage once the units are installed.

**The Single Kill Metric**
**Adoption < 45% after 90 days of operation per unit.**
*   *Why:* Your model requires 70% to hit the projected lift. If you miss 45% (two-thirds of target) in the first quarter, the curve will not recover to 70%.
*   *Action:* If the metric fires, immediately halt the purchase of Units 3 and 4, renegotiate the maintenance fee based on actual usage, and revert to staffed checkout for low-volume hours.

**Note:** The $210k "revenue" figure must be rigorously defined as either real income or removed from the financial justification entirely.
