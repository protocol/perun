**Strongest Objection:** The plan assumes the 12% conversion rate from the pilot will hold at scale, but the pilot likely involved early adopters with higher digital literacy. If the broader patient base (including the unquantified portion of your 1,200 monthly patients lacking digital skills) cannot effectively use the bot, the "30% workload reduction" will fail to materialize. Instead of saving costs, you may incur the full 8 PHP/chat fee *plus* the existing call-center costs for users who fail the bot and call anyway, turning the projected savings into a net loss.

**Simpler Alternative:** Before committing 150,000 PHP, expand the nurse-led phone line with a structured triage script. This requires no new technology, faces no regulatory approval delays, and immediately serves patients regardless of digital literacy. It achieves the goal of standardized triage without the risk of low adoption or the "double-cost" failure mode of a unused chatbot.

**Stop Trigger (Kill Criterion):** Halt the rollout immediately if, after the first 60 days, the **bot-to-human escalation rate exceeds 40%** or if the **Health Authority approval is not received by 15 May 2024**. The escalation rate directly invalidates the workload reduction math; the date ensures you do not burn budget on a legally blocked launch.
