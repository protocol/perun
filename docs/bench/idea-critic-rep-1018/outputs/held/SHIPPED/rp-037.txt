**Evaluation of RailPulse Systems Pilot Plan**

**Single Most Plausible Objection:** The plan assumes a linear 15% quarterly reduction in failure rates, implying immediate crew proficiency with predictive alerts. The most likely failure point is not sensor accuracy, but **alert fatigue and workflow friction**. If field crews lack a streamlined interface to act on alerts, Mean-Time-To-Repair (MTTR) may initially *increase* due to false positives or diagnostic hesitation, negating the downtime savings in Q1 and Q2. The ROI model treats "predictive alerts" as an automatic efficiency gain rather than a change management challenge.

**More Efficient Route:** Replace the full 120-locomotive, $3.2M immediate commitment with a **staggered "Shadow Pilot."**
*   **Phase 1 (Jan–Mar):** Deploy sensors on only 20 locomotives. Do not integrate alerts into the active maintenance workflow yet; run the data in parallel ("shadow mode") to validate the 15% failure reduction hypothesis against historical baselines without risking operational disruption.
*   **Phase 2 (Apr–Jun):** If Phase 1 data confirms the failure reduction *and* a simulated workflow shows MTTR improvement, expand to the remaining 100 units.
*   **Benefit:** This caps initial exposure to ~$530k (1/6th of the budget) while validating the core "linear reduction" assumption before scaling licensing and installation costs.

**Defensible Halt Trigger:** Your proposed 8% net maintenance cost increase is valid but lagging. A more defensible, leading trigger is: **"Halt expansion if the ratio of 'Actionable Alerts' to 'Total Alerts' is below 40% after the first 90 days of Phase 1."**
*   *Reasoning:* If >60% of alerts do not result in confirmed preventative maintenance, the system is generating noise. This directly predicts future MTTR bloat and crew distrust before the full $900k installation cost is sunk.

**Arithmetic & Hidden Cost Check:**
*   **Math Verification:** The savings logic holds directionally ($2.1M annualized vs. $3.2M pilot cost implies a ~18-month payback post-pilot), but the "linear 15% reduction" is the fragile variable. If Q1 achieves only 5% reduction (due to the learning curve), the cumulative annual savings drop significantly, potentially extending payback beyond the asset life.
*   **Hidden Cost:** The budget lists "data-platform licensing" but omits **ongoing cellular/IoT connectivity fees** for 120 locomotives over 6 months and the **internal labor cost** for data analysts to interpret the shadow data. These could easily add $50k–$100k in unbudgeted operational expense.

**Note:** The plan conflates "installation complete" with "value realized"; the value is entirely dependent on the untested human response time to alerts.
Ensure the contract includes a clause allowing termination of the licensing portion if the Phase 1 alert-quality metric is not met.
