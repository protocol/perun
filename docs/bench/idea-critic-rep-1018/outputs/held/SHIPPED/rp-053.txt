**Core Failure Modes**
1.  **Infrastructure Capacity:** The plan assumes existing cell sites can absorb 30,000 new users (150 schools × 200 learners) without upgrades. If site congestion causes speeds below 128kbps, the educational value drops to zero, triggering mass abandonment.
2.  **Hidden Cost Blowout:** Excluding device-management software licenses and regulatory clearance timelines creates a "silent budget killer." If licensing exceeds ZAR 50 per student/year or regulatory delays push launch past Q3, the Q4 2025 break-even becomes impossible.
3.  **Arithmetic & Unit Error:** Your total student count is **30,000** (150 schools × 200 learners), not implied higher figures. At ZAR 2.4 million, the budget allows **ZAR 80 per student total** for the entire period until break-even. Given LTE data costs, this ZAR 80 cap likely covers only 2–3 months of data, let alone hardware, licenses, and ops for a multi-year rollout. The unit economics currently do not sustain the timeline.

**Kill Criteria (Pull the Plug)**
Terminate or pivot the full rollout if, after the 6-week pilot:
*   **Network Performance:** Average throughput per student falls below 200kbps during peak school hours (indicating site saturation).
*   **Adoption Rate:** Active usage is below 60% (vs. your 80% assumption) due to device incompatibility or lack of teacher integration.
*   **Cost Variance:** The fully loaded cost per student (including management software and pro-rated regulatory fees) exceeds ZAR 100 for the pilot period, projecting a break-even later than Q2 2026.
