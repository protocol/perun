Your pilot generated $42,000 in revenue but only $7,800 in profit, implying an 18.5% margin. However, your costs were heavily skewed by one-time or inefficient spend: $30,000 on a boutique photographer and $8,000 on ads for just 280 members.

Unless the photographer's work directly drives the projected 25% lift in average order value (AOV) immediately, you are burning cash on fixed costs while your variable CAC has exploded. You are effectively paying $150 to earn $150, then losing money on shipping and fulfillment.

**A Better Route: The "Regional Density" Strategy**
Instead of a costly national rollout, restrict expansion to three specific zip-code clusters where your current 280 members are concentrated.
1.  **Slash Shipping:** Consolidate deliveries to reduce the $12/case average.
2.  **Lower CAC:** Use referral programs for existing members rather than $180,000 in cold Facebook ads.
3.  **Validate Content:** Commission the photographer only for specific high-conversion assets after proving that imagery drives the 25% AOV lift in a small test, rather than committing to a full boutique fee upfront.
This approach lowers the capital requirement from ~$230,000 to under $50,000 and fixes the unit economics before scaling.

**The Abandonment Trigger (Kill Criterion)**
