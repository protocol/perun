**Pitch Critique for Maya Rivers**

**Strongest Objection:** The financial model collapses on the "cultural authenticity premium." Assuming a 15% price premium without validated market data is speculative, not strategic. If the market treats these NFTs as standard digital art, average sales drop to ~$1,130. At that price, first-quarter revenue falls to ~$395,500, wiping out the $30,000 surplus and leaving a $24,500 deficit before accounting for the unknown royalty spikes at 5,000 transactions. You are betting the entire surplus on an unproven psychological valuation.

**More Viable Route:** Pivot from a "premium pricing" strategy to a "volume and utility" model. Instead of relying on a 15% markup, secure pre-launch commitments for 40% of the 350 pieces at the base $1,300 rate to cover the $240,000 tech cost immediately. Use the remaining inventory for dynamic pricing or bundled experiences (e.g., physical gallery access) to drive volume rather than unit price. This removes the dependency on the authenticity premium for basic solvency.

**Kill Signal:** Do not wait for week 4. If pre-launch commitments (LOIs or deposits) do not cover 30% of the projected tech costs ($72,000) by **September 1, 2025**, halt the development spend. This leading indicator confirms demand failure before the irreversible build is complete.

2.  **Liquidity Freeze:** The 22 artists fail to drive their own collector bases to the platform, resulting in <50% sell-through, causing reputational damage and artist churn.
