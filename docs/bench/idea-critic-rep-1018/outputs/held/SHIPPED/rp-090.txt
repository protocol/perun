**Critical Failure Points & Arithmetic Corrections**
Your revenue math is flawed. A 5% lift on $24M online sales ($1.2M) does not automatically apply to in-store traffic without proof of cross-channel behavior. More critically, you ignored the cost basis: $3.05M upfront cost requires ~2.5 years of *full* $1.2M annual gains just to break even, ignoring the unpriced bandwidth, maintenance, and staff training costs. If the lift is only realized in-store (approx. $12M implied if online is half the business), the payback period extends beyond the hardware lifecycle.

The specific points where this falls apart:
1.  **Integration & Privacy:** Without a verified API path to your legacy POS and a completed PIPEDA (Canada) impact assessment, the project cannot pass legal or technical gatekeeping.
2.  **Hidden OpEx:** Bandwidth for 12 stores running real-time AR will likely exceed standard retail leases; without this line item, your ROI is imaginary.
3.  **Loyalty Churn:** If data harvesting for the fitting room triggers a >2% drop in loyalty retention, the lifetime value loss outweighs the conversion gain.

**Better Approach**
Reject the "big bang" 12-store launch. Execute a **3-store, 90-day pilot** capped at $800k. This isolates integration risk and validates the "in-store conversion" variable before exposing the full capital budget. An AR-only mobile overlay (using customer phones) should be modeled as a cheaper alternative to dedicated hardware kiosks.

**Kill Criteria & Leading Metric**
*   **Abort Trigger:** If the pilot stores show a conversion lift of **<1.5%** (not 1%, as your margin likely won't cover hardware depreciation at that level) after 90 days, or if loyalty churn exceeds **1%** among users engaging with the tool.
*   **Leading Metric:** "Fitting Room Engagement-to-Purchase Ratio" (weekly review). If <20% of users who try the tool buy something, the novelty is not driving revenue.

**Note:** The assumption that foot traffic (3,200/store/week) remains static is risky; if the hardware causes queue friction, traffic conversion could drop regardless of the tool's appeal.
