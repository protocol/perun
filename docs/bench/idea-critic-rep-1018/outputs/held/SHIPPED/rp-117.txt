**Key Failure Modes**
1.  **Hardware Mortality:** The model assumes zero replacement costs. In desert heat, if sensor lifespan drops below 18 months, the unmodeled capex of ~$144,000 (full replacement cycle) erodes 60% of Year 2 savings, destroying the ROI.
2.  **Labor Illusion:** The $2M savings assumes 100% redeployment efficiency. If technicians cannot be fully redeployed due to geographic mismatch or skill gaps, the "savings" remain on the books but do not materialize as cash flow.
3.  **Accuracy Cliff:** The business case breaks even at roughly 55% accuracy. If real-world desert dust or heat drives accuracy below this threshold, false alarms will increase labor hours rather than cut them, creating a net loss.

**Kill Criteria & Triggers**
*   **Stop/Pivot Trigger:** If the 3-month pilot shows **sensor failure >5%** or **effective labor reduction <15%**, halt expansion immediately. Do not pass Go; do not collect $2M.
*   **Board Re-evaluation Trigger:** If the pilot confirms hardware degradation rates requiring replacement cycles shorter than 24 months, return to the board with a revised OPEX-heavy model (leasing vs. buying) before committing the remaining $100k+ capex.
*   **Leading Metric to Watch:** *False Positive Rate per Depot/Week.* If this exceeds 2 incidents per week per depot during the pilot, the system creates more work than it saves. Review date: End of Month 2 of the pilot.
