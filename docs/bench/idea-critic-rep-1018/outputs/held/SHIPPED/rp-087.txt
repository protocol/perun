**1. Critical Oversights**

*   **Competitor Blind Spot:** Dynamic pricing algorithms often trigger rate wars if multiple hotels in a localized market (like Phuket) use similar logic to maximize occupancy, potentially depressing RevPAR for everyone.

*   **Cost:** Negotiate a reduced data-integration fee (e.g., $50k total) instead of the full license.
*   **Goal:** Compare the AI's *suggested* rates against actual manual outcomes and realized RevPAR without risking live revenue or paying the full annual fee. This validates the 12% lift claim before capital deployment.
