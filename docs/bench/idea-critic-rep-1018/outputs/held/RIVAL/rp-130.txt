1.  **Membrane Degradation:** The high-temperature cycles could reduce membrane life from the assumed three years to under 18 months, destroying the ROI model.
2.  **Tariff Volatility:** If water tariffs rise slower than the projected 5% annually, the financial urgency and total savings diminish significantly.
3.  **Hidden Operational Costs:** The brief excludes maintenance downtime and labor for the new loop, which could erode the net $120,000 saving.

**Specific Early Indicator to Halt:**
Monitor **filtration efficiency drop** during the first 90 days. If efficiency declines by more than 10% within the first quarter of the pilot, halt the expansion plan immediately. This is the leading proxy for membrane degradation under heat stress and will tell you if the three-year lifespan assumption is valid before you spend further capital.
