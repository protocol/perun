**Verdict: NO-GO (in current form)**

The financial thesis contains a critical arithmetic error and unquantified operational risks that make the $3.05M investment unjustifiable at this stage.

**Critical Failure Points:**

1.  **Revenue Miscalculation:** You projected $1.2M extra revenue from a 5% lift on $24M *online* sales. However, this rollout is for *physical* stores.
    *   Current Annual Store Revenue: 12 stores × 3,200 visitors × $85 basket × 52 weeks = ~$170M (assuming 100% conversion, which is impossible).
    *   Realistically, if only 20% convert today, store revenue is ~$34M. A 5% lift on *store* revenue is ~$1.7M, not $1.2M.
    *   **Risk:** If you meant a 5% lift in *conversion rate* (e.g., 20% → 25%), the revenue impact is massive (~$8.5M gain). If you meant 5% more *revenue*, the math needs to be explicit. The current pitch conflates online baselines with physical rollout economics.

2.  **Missing OpEx:** You have priced CapEx ($3.05M) but ignored ongoing costs. High-fidelity AR/VR fitting rooms require significant bandwidth, cloud rendering, and hardware maintenance. Without this, your ROI timeline is fictional.

3.  **Integration & Privacy:** Integrating real-time body scanning with legacy POS systems is a known failure point for retail IT. Furthermore, storing biometric data (body measurements) triggers strict Quebec Law 25 compliance requirements. One data breach here destroys brand trust entirely.

4.  **Loyalty Churn Unknown:** Rolling this out without knowing if it alienates non-tech-savvy loyalty members is reckless. If high-value older customers feel underserved, the net gain could be negative.

**Abort Metric:**
*   **Primary:** If the pilot (2 stores) shows <1.5% lift in *conversion rate* (not just revenue) after 90 days.
*   **Secondary:** If data-privacy legal review flags "high risk" for biometric storage under Quebec law.

**Better Approach:**
Do not roll out to all 12 stores. Execute a **Phased AR-Only Pilot**:
1.  **Scope:** Deploy to 2 stores only. Use iPad-based AR (lower hardware cost) instead of full immersive booths.
2.  **Goal:** Validate the *conversion lift* and *privacy compliance* with minimal CapEx (~$400k).
3.  **Decision:** Scale to the remaining 10 stores only if the pilot clears the 1.5% conversion threshold and legal sign-off.

Fix the revenue math, quantify the OpEx, and run the 2-store pilot before asking for $3M.
