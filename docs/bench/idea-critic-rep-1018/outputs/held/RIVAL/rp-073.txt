**Recommendation: Proceed with a Conditional Pilot, Not Full Deployment**

Do not authorize the full $500k rollout for March 2025. Instead, approve a capped $75k "Integration Proof" phase targeting only the data-audit module's connection to the legacy ERP. Full deployment should remain contingent on successful technical validation of this interface.

**Principal Failure Modes**

1.  **The Integration Illusion:** The vendor's claim of "compatible" is the plan's single point of failure. If the audit module cannot extract or write data to the legacy ERP without custom middleware or causing latency spikes, the governance framework becomes blind. You cannot govern what you cannot measure.
2.  **Regulatory Complacency:** Assuming a static 5% risk premium because regulators have signaled "no major changes" is dangerous. Ethics governance is reactive; if the AI products exhibit emergent bias post-launch, the lack of a functioning audit trail transforms a compliance issue into a fiduciary breach.
3.  **ROI Fragility:** The projected 15% ROI relies on the automation of audit workflows. If the integration requires manual intervention to bridge the ERP gap, operational costs will spike, eroding the margin below the risk premium threshold.

**The Trigger Condition to Pull the Plug**

Establish a hard technical gate by **2024-11-15**. If the vendor cannot produce this evidence by the deadline, or if the test reveals that custom code is required to maintain data integrity, **terminate the contract immediately.** Do not negotiate for extensions or patches. The cost of a failed integration after full deployment exceeds the cost of abandoning the vendor now.
