**Recommendation: Do not proceed in current form.** The financial model contains a critical arithmetic error that invalidates the ROI projection, and the omission of O&M costs and degradation creates a dangerous illusion of certainty.

**The Arithmetic Error**
Your capacity factor calculation is overstated. A 5 MW system running at a 15% capacity factor generates:
$5,000 \text{ kW} \times 8,760 \text{ hours/year} \times 0.15 = 6,570 \text{ MWh}$.
Your projection of 6,500 MWh is actually consistent with the 15% assumption, so the yield math is roughly correct. **However**, the financial conclusion is wrong because you excluded O&M. Typical solar O&M is €15–25/kW/year. For 5 MW, that is €75,000–€125,000 annually. Over 3.5 years, this alone adds ~€350k in unmodeled costs, pushing the ROI back by nearly 4–6 months. Furthermore, ignoring panel degradation (typically 0.5%/year) overstates year-5 revenue by ~2.5%.

**Three Biggest Ways This Could Unravel**
1.  **Post-Tariff Revenue Cliff:** The model assumes the €0.08/kWh tariff holds through 2027, but offers no scenario for spot prices afterward. If spot prices drop to €0.04/kWh (a common occurrence in saturated markets), your revenue halves while fixed costs remain, potentially turning a profit into a loss in years 4–5.
2.  **Battery Cycle Degradation:** You have paired a 2 MWh battery with 6,500 MWh generation. If the strategy involves daily arbitrage, the battery may face more cycles than anticipated. Without a replacement reserve in the capex, a battery failure in year 6 (common for LFP if cycled aggressively) could wipe out the remaining project value.
3.  **O&M Escalation:** With no O&M priced beyond year one, you are exposed to inflation and unexpected inverter failures. Inverters often need replacement once in a 5-year window; a €100k unplanned CAPEX event in year 3 destroys the 3.5-year ROI claim.

**Single Walk-Away Trigger**
**Walk away if the signed Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) or tariff guarantee does not extend beyond 2027 at a minimum of €0.06/kWh.** Without a floor price for years 4 and 5, the project is a speculative bet on spot markets rather than a yield-generating asset, misaligning with the "pilot" risk profile.

*Note: The 3.5-year ROI is aggressive for infrastructure; stress-test the model with 20% higher O&M and 20% lower post-2027 prices before presenting to the board.*
