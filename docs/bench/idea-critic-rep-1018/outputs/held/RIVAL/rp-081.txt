**Recommendation: NO-GO (Proceed with Caution / Pilot First)**

Do not commit to the full €150,000 investment yet. While the projected 125% net profit increase is compelling, the decision relies entirely on an unverified assumption: that customers will uniformly accept a 10% price premium without survey data. Investing €150,000 (75% of your current annual net profit) on an unproven price elasticity is an unjustifiable risk. Instead, commission a targeted market validation study in Germany, France, and the UK to confirm willingness-to-pay before spending on audits.

**Critical Failure Points**
1.  **Price Elasticity Rejection:** The core risk is that customers value "biodynamic" but refuse the extra 10% cost, resulting in volume loss rather than revenue gain.
2.  **Cash Flow Strain:** The €150,000 upfront cost represents a massive liquidity event relative to your €200,000 profit base; any delay in revenue realization could strain operations.
3.  **Timeline Slippage:** If the process exceeds six months, the cost of capital increases and the projected 12-month ROI window shrinks, degrading the return rate.

**Abort Triggers**
*   **Validation Failure:** If pilot surveys indicate less than 60% of key accounts are willing to absorb the 10% premium.
*   **Cost Overrun:** If total certification and audit costs exceed €180,000 (20% buffer).
*   **Revenue Lag:** If actual premium revenue falls below €100,000 in the first six months post-certification.

*Note: Secure a fixed-price contract for the audit to mitigate cost overrun risks before proceeding.*
