**Recommendation: Conditional Go**
Proceed with the pilot only if two conditions are met immediately: (1) Legal obtains a written "no-objection" or informal clearance from the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority (KCAA) regarding algorithmic fare adjustments, and (2) Finance secures a contractual clause with the vendor tying the remaining implementation fees to verified performance milestones. Without regulatory clarity, the risk of a forced shutdown mid-pilot outweighs the potential 15% revenue lift. The assumption of constant price sensitivity across seasons is also fragile; the pilot must explicitly test elasticity variance between peak and off-peak periods before any expansion decision.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Intervention:** The KCAA deems the dynamic algorithm non-compliant with consumer protection or fair pricing statutes, forcing an immediate suspension and reputational damage.
2.  **Data & Model Integrity:** Poor historical data quality leads to incorrect elasticity modeling, causing price points that suppress demand rather than optimize it, resulting in revenue flatlining or decline.
3.  **Public & Political Backlash:** Perceived price gouging on essential commuter routes (especially Nairobi–Mombasa) triggers negative media cycles or political pressure, compelling management to halt the program regardless of financial performance.

**Exact Pause/Cancel Triggers**
*   **PAUSE the pilot immediately if:** The KCAA issues any formal inquiry, compliance notice, or public statement questioning the legality of the fare-adjustment algorithm.
*   **CANCEL the project (no expansion) if:**
    *   Net revenue uplift across both pilot corridors averages **less than 8%** (adjusted for seasonality) over the first three months of the pilot.
    *   Customer complaint volume regarding pricing increases by **more than 25%** compared to the same period in the prior year.
    *   The vendor fails to deliver real-time reporting accuracy within **5%** of actual ticketing system data for two consecutive weeks.

*Note: The 8% threshold provides a safety margin below the promised 15% to account for implementation friction while still proving viability. Ensure the vendor contract defines "revenue uplift" as net of any discounts or operational costs they influence.*
