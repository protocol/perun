**Recommendation: Do not proceed with the GlowCycle plant in its current form.**

The proposal relies on a critical undefined variable: Operations & Maintenance (O&M) costs. Without a fixed O&M figure, the financial model is speculative. Furthermore, the vendor's energy yield claim of 2.5 MWh per tonne for packaging and off-cuts is technically suspect; typical waste-to-energy conversion for this feedstock yields significantly less (often 0.5–0.8 MWh/tonne). If the yield is even 40% lower than claimed, the project shifts from profitable to a net loss compared to your current hauling contract.

**Principal Failure Modes:**
1.  **Yield Overestimation:** The 2.5 MWh/tonne figure is likely optimistic for low-density packaging waste. Lower yield directly destroys revenue.
2.  **Hidden O&M Costs:** "Reasonable" often masks high consumable, labor, and downtime costs that can exceed the savings from the eliminated hauling fee.
3.  **Feedstock Variability:** If waste composition shifts (e.g., less combustible off-cuts), energy generation drops while intake obligations remain.
4.  **Financing Risk:** An 18-month construction delay at 8% interest adds ~ZAR 1.1 million in pure interest cost before operation begins.

**The Pull-the-Plug Condition:**
Reconsider or terminate the project immediately if the vendor cannot contractually guarantee a **minimum net energy yield of 1.8 MWh per tonne** and a **fixed maximum annual O&M cost of ZAR 450,000**.
