**Executive Brief: Dynamic Pricing Engine Pilot Review**

**Critical Oversights**
1.  **Revenue Double-Counting:** You correctly identified the $150k implementation cost error. Ensure the financial model treats this strictly as CAPEX/OPEX, not a revenue driver.
2.  **Competitor Blind Spot:** The model assumes a vacuum. If competitors use similar algorithms, a "race to the bottom" could erode margins despite higher occupancy.
3.  **Contract Rigidity:** The flat $2.5M fee lacks volume scaling. Expanding to 30 properties without a tiered pricing clause leaves significant value on the table.
4.  **Data Latency:** AI models require clean, real-time data. If our PMS (Property Management System) integration has lag, the pricing decisions will be reactive, not predictive.

**Recommended Alternative: The "Shadow Mode" Pilot**
Before committing $2.5M, run a 90-day **Shadow Mode** test on the three pilot hotels:
*   **Action:** Integrate the vendor's API to *receive* pricing recommendations but do not *publish* them to OTAs (Online Travel Agencies).
*   **Comparison:** Run your current manual/rule-based pricing live.
*   **Analysis:** Post-stay, compare the vendor's suggested rate against the actual booked rate and competitor sets.
*   **Benefit:** This validates the algorithm's logic against real market data for a fraction of the cost (integration fees only, no license fee) and reveals data compatibility issues before full deployment.

**Kill Signals (Stop-Loss Triggers)**
Advise pulling the plug immediately if any of the following occur during the pilot:
1.  **RevPAR Lift < 3%:** After six months, if the net RevPAR increase (after fees) is below 3%, the ROI does not justify the operational risk.
2.  **ADR Erosion > 2%:** If Average Daily Rate drops by more than 2% while occupancy rises, the engine is discounting too aggressively to fill rooms, devaluing the brand.
3.  **Channel Conflict:** If the engine causes rate parity violations across Expedia/Booking.com leading to delisting or penalties.
4.  **Staff Workaround > 20%:** If front desk or revenue managers manually override the system's prices more than 20% of the time, the tool lacks usability or trust.

**Note:** Ensure the contract includes a "Most Favored Nation" clause regarding the $2.5M fee should other Wavecrest regions adopt the tool later. The Shadow Mode approach shifts leverage to us before the full financial commitment.
