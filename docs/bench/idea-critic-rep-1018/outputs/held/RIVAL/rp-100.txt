**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with the full $120,000 rollout. Instead, execute a **$15,000 "Concierge MVP" pilot** running for 45 days. Use low-code tools to simulate the virtual gallery experience for a closed group of 500 existing donors before licensing the $15,000 software or spending $25,000 on marketing. This validates whether your patrons actually want a digital experience without risking the bulk of your capital.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Behavioral Mismatch:** Your 5,000 past visitors attended for the *social event* and physical presence, not just the art. If they don't log in, the projected 20% donor boost vanishes, leaving you with high fixed costs and no revenue lift.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** The assumption of $1,250/month for the license is unverified. If the true enterprise cost is higher, or if "miscellaneous fees" (hosting, bandwidth, security) spike with traffic, your burn rate will outpace donor growth immediately.
3.  **Adoption Friction:** Even if patrons are interested, if the login process or navigation isn't flawless, the 8,000 visitor target will miss by wide margins, making the marketing spend look wasted.

**Walk-Away Trigger:**
Terminate the project if the **pilot conversion rate is under 15%** (fewer than 75 of the 500 test donors engage) OR if the **verified all-in software cost exceeds $1,800 per month**. Do not build the full platform if you cannot prove engagement with a fraction of the budget first.

*Note: The current plan bets the organization's liquidity on an unproven behavioral shift; the pilot shifts that risk to a manageable experiment.*
