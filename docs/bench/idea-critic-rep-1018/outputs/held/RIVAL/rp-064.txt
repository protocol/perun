**Recommendation: Proceed with a Conditional Pilot, Not Full Rollout**

Do not commit to the full $420,000 rollout or the 70% adoption assumption yet. Instead, authorize a **90-day Phase 0 Pilot** capped at $85,000 involving only 3 friendly distributors and 15,000 units. This validates the blockchain throughput and real-world adoption friction before exposing the company to the full financial and regulatory risk of a 300,000-unit mandate.

**Core Failure Points**

1.  **Adoption Friction (Highest Risk):** Your model assumes 70% distributor adoption in six months. Distributors operate on thin margins; if the integration adds even 15 minutes of labor per shipment or requires costly hardware upgrades they must fund, adoption will stall below 30%, breaking the network effect required for provenance tracking.
2.  **Regulatory Whiplash:** Betting on "no firm quote" for EU compliance is dangerous. If the final guidance mandates specific data residency or private-chain architectures contrary to your current setup, you could face a six-figure retrofit cost mid-rollout.
3.  **Unit Economics Sensitivity:** The $75/unit licensing fee is steep. If the anticipated $1.2M premium pricing uplift relies on consumer awareness that doesn't materialize, distributors will refuse to absorb the cost, leaving Clara Vohl holding the bag for the entire licensing fee.

**Trigger Events to Pull the Plug or Pivot**

*   **The Adoption Trigger:** If **fewer than 2 of the 3 pilot distributors** actively scan/log 90% of their eligible inventory within the first 45 days, **pull the plug**. This indicates the workflow is too burdensome regardless of the technology's promise.
*   **The Cost Trigger:** If the EU regulator releases guidance requiring architecture changes that exceed **$150,000 in estimated retrofit costs**, **pivot immediately** to a regional (non-EU) rollout strategy until clarity emerges.
*   **The Value Trigger:** If post-pilot analysis shows the premium pricing uplift is **below 5%** (vs. the projected 15%), **pause expansion**. The ROI case collapses at that level, and the strategy must shift from "premium branding" to "loss prevention" only.

**Note:** Secure a contractual clause allowing exit if EU compliance costs exceed 20% of the pilot budget. Do not sign the full 12-month agreement until the Phase 0 data validates the 70% adoption hypothesis.
