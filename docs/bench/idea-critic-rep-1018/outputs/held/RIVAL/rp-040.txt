**Pressure-Check Briefing for Klaus Weber**

**1. The Single "Pull the Plug" Indicator**
**Data Integrity Failure Rate >15% during Pilot Phase 1.**
If more than 15% of the sensor data from the pilot sites requires manual cleansing or is rejected by the AI as "noise" within the first 60 days, halt immediately. The entire financial model collapses if the assumption that data is "good enough" proves false, as retroactively fixing data pipelines on live production lines is exponentially more expensive and disruptive than anticipated.

**2. Most Likely Failure Modes**
*   **The "Legacy Gap" Blind Spot:** The plan explicitly admits uncertainty regarding older CNC machines. If these machines constitute more than 20% of the fleet, the AI will create a two-tier maintenance system where the most failure-prone assets remain unpredictable, skewing the overall downtime metric and eroding operator trust.
*   **Integration Friction:** The budget allocates funds for integration but assumes smooth ingestion. The most probable delay will not be the AI itself, but the middleware required to force legacy PLCs to speak modern protocols, causing the timeline to slip and the "9-month payback" to stretch beyond 18 months.
*   **Alert Fatigue:** If the system generates false positives in the early months, maintenance teams will ignore the warnings, rendering the €2.5M investment a "shadow system" that nobody acts upon.

**3. Recommendation**
**Proceed, but with a "Data-First" Gate.**
Do not commit to the full phased rollout on July 1st based on the current assumptions. Instead, authorize the three pilots *strictly* as data-validation exercises. Release only 20% of the budget initially. Make the subsequent €2M tranche contingent on a verified "Data Readiness Score" achieved by month 3 of the pilot. The ROI is attractive (17% downtime reduction is significant), but the risk is entirely concentrated in the unverified data quality and legacy hardware compatibility.

**4. Stop/Pivot Trigger Condition**
**Trigger:** If the **actual** downtime reduction across the three pilot sites is **<8%** after six months (less than half the projected 17%).
**Action:** **Pivot to "Targeted Monitoring" only.** Abandon the predictive automation aspect. Use the sensors purely for dashboard visibility and manual analysis. This acknowledges that while the data might be visible, the noise floor on the legacy equipment is too high for reliable algorithmic prediction, preventing further waste on a broken predictive model.
