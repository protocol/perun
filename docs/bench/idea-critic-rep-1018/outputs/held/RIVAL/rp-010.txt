**Recommendation:** Do not commit to the full $1.7M rollout yet. Instead, propose a **$250,000, 6-month pilot** with 250 fishers (10% of the network) targeting a single export lane. Make the full deployment contingent on two verified milestones: (1) written provisional acceptance from Chilean customs authorities that the blockchain data satisfies certification requirements, and (2) demonstrated 85% scanner uptime and data fidelity in the pilot.

**How This Could Fall Apart:**
1.  **Regulatory Rejection (The Fatal Flaw):** If customs authorities do not formally recognize the blockchain stream as equivalent to paper certs by Q2 2026, the projected $3M revenue premium evaporates instantly. You are left with high costs and no market advantage.
2.  **Operational Fracture:** Fishers often resist new hardware. If adoption stalls below 60% due to device loss, damage, or refusal to use the scanners, the data chain breaks. Incomplete traceability renders the system useless for premium claims, turning the $500k hardware spend into stranded assets.
3.  **Cost Escalation:** The $0.02 per-record fee assumes steady volume. If transaction spikes occur during peak season or integration requires custom middleware not included in the license, the $1.2M software budget could breach $1.6M before realizing value.

**Pull-the-Plug / Renegotiation Triggers:**
*   **Regulatory Deadline:** If formal written acceptance from customs is not secured by **December 31, 2025**, halt expansion immediately.
*   **Adoption Failure:** If active scanner usage is **below 50%** of the pilot group by **March 2026**, renegotiate the hardware model or cancel.
*   **Budget Breach:** If total committed costs (hardware + software) exceed **$1.5 million** before any revenue premium is realized, freeze spending and demand a revised scope.
*   **Revenue Miss:** If the price premium on exported salmon is **less than 6%** (half the projection) after 12 months of full operation, terminate the contract at the next renewal window.

This approach limits your downside to $250k while validating the single biggest assumption: that the regulator will accept the tech.
