**Executive Brief: SavannaGuard Alliance Drone Network Proposal**

**Primary Risks & Oversights**
1.  **Timeline Impossibility:** The target operational date (1 March 2025) is in the past relative to the email date (Oct 2026). If this is a typo for 2027, the 12-month lead time for importing 12 specialized units, securing aviation permits, and training pilots in a remote reserve is still highly optimistic.
2.  **Flawed Efficacy Baseline:** Extrapolating a 5% system-wide reduction from a single-month fluctuation (30 vs. 28 incidents) is statistically invalid. Poaching is seasonal and opportunistic; a two-count difference is noise, not a trend. Basing a $1.2M investment on this risks funding a solution for a non-existent pattern.
3.  **Community Friction:** The plan assumes acceptance of constant drone noise. In wildlife tourism and indigenous lands, noise pollution often triggers immediate backlash, potentially turning local scouts—who are your best intelligence source—against the project.
4.  **Maintenance Underestimation:** $18,000/year per drone assumes minor repairs. In dusty, high-heat environments with heavy daily usage, battery degradation and motor failure will likely double this cost. There is no budget line for "downed" drone replacement during repair cycles, creating coverage gaps.

**More Robust Approach**
Do not rely on air-only surveillance. Adopt a **Layered Deterrence Model**:
*   **Integrate Ground Patrols:** Use drones as force multipliers for existing ranger teams, not replacements. Drones scout; rangers intercept. A drone without a rapid response team is merely an expensive camera.
*   **Satellite + Acoustic Sensors:** Supplement with low-cost acoustic gun-shot detection sensors and periodic satellite imagery for broad-area monitoring, reserving expensive drone flight hours for verified alerts.
*   **Community-Led Monitoring:** Allocate 15% of the hardware budget to community liaison programs and local employment. Social license is a stronger deterrent than rotor noise.

**Abort/Re-engineer Triggers**
Establish these specific go/no-go metrics for the first quarter of operation:
*   **Effectiveness Threshold:** If verified poaching incidents do not decrease by **≥15%** (not 5%) within the first **90 days** of full deployment, suspend expansion and re-evaluate the deterrence model. The current 5% target is too low to justify capital expenditure.
*   **Operational Readiness:** If **>2 drones** are non-operational at any time due to maintenance or weather, trigger an immediate review of the maintenance contract and environmental hardening specifications.
*   **Community Sentiment:** If **one formal complaint** is lodged by community leadership regarding noise or privacy, pause operations in that sector for mediation.

**Recommendation:** Reject the current proposal in its entirety. Request a revised plan that addresses the statistical baseline, includes a ground-response integration strategy, and extends the timeline to allow for proper regulatory and community engagement.

*Note: Verify the "2025" date immediately; if not a typo, the proposal contains a fundamental error in project scheduling.*
