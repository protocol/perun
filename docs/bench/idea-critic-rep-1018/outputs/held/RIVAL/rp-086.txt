**Recommendation: Proceed with a Phased Blend Pilot, Not Full Conversion**

Do not commit to 100% organic conversion immediately. Instead, launch a "Silkspire Organic Blend" line using 30% organic cotton for the July 2025 pilot. This reduces your immediate COGS exposure from $180,000 to $54,000 while allowing you to test the critical 15% price premium assumption with wholesale buyers. Use the remaining capital to secure a flexible supply contract rather than locking in full volume before validating demand.

**Main Failure Points**
1.  **The Price Premium Assumption:** Your entire $270,000 net gain relies on buyers accepting a 15% price hike. If the market only bears 8%, your revenue uplift drops to ~$240,000, leaving you with only $60,000 net profit against a $500k capex and significant operational risk. This is your single biggest sink point.
2.  **Hidden Certification & Segregation Costs:** The $75k certification fee is likely just the entry ticket. You have missed the ongoing costs of *segregation*. Running organic and conventional cotton through the same ginning facility requires rigorous cleaning cycles, separate storage silos, and third-party auditing to maintain certification. If your current facility cannot physically separate the flows without cross-contamination, you risk losing certification entirely, rendering the $500k capex worthless.
3.  **Supply Volatility:** Organic cotton yields are typically 20-30% lower than conventional and more weather-dependent. A bad harvest could spike the $180/bale cost further, squeezing your margin if you are locked into fixed-price contracts with buyers.

**Clear Walk-Away Condition**
Walk away if the pilot data (collected between July 2025 and January 2026) shows that wholesale buyers are unwilling to pay a **minimum 10% price premium** consistently across three consecutive orders.

At 10%, your revenue uplift is ~$300,000. Against the $180,000 increased COGS, this leaves a $120,000 gross buffer. Anything less than 10% fails to generate sufficient margin to service the $500k capex within a reasonable timeframe, making the ROI negative. Do not proceed to the March 2026 full rollout if this threshold is not met.
