**Recommendation: NO-GO (in current form)**

The plan contains a critical arithmetic error and two unpriced liabilities that invalidate the financial model.

**1. The Arithmetic Failure**
Your math underestimates the scale by a factor of 10.
*   **Your Plan:** 150 schools × 200 learners = **30,000 students**.
*   **Your Budget:** ZAR 2.4 million / 30,000 students = **ZAR 80 per student/year**.
*   **The Reality:** At 350 MB/month, each student needs 4.2 GB/year. Even at a wholesale rate of ZAR 50/GB, the data cost alone is **ZAR 210 per student/year**.
*   **The Gap:** You are short **ZAR 3.9 million** just for data, excluding hardware, software, and installation. The ZAR 2.4M budget covers only ~38% of the data costs, let alone the rollout.

**2. Core Failure Modes**
*   **The "Free Software" Fallacy:** Device-management licenses are missing from the model. Managing 30,000 SIMs and devices requires enterprise MDM (Mobile Device Management), likely costing ZAR 50–100 per device/year. This adds another **ZAR 1.5M–3M** in uncovered OpEx.
*   **Regulatory Paralysis:** Launching without confirmed regulatory clearance for bulk data resale or educational zero-rating is a compliance bomb. If clearance takes 6 months (common), the June 30 deadline is impossible, burning cash on idle inventory.
*   **Network Capacity Assumption:** Assuming existing sites absorb 12.6 TB/month (30k students × 4.2 GB/year) without upgrades is risky. Rural/Eastern Cape sites often lack backhaul capacity; congestion will cause immediate churn, negating the "churn reversal" revenue thesis.

**3. The Pivot/Plug Trigger**
**Trigger:** If the **Cost Per Active User (CPAU)** exceeds **ZAR 250/year** after the 3-month pilot (including data + MDM + amortized hardware).

*   **Action:** If CPAU > ZAR 250, **pivot immediately** to a "Bring Your Own Device" (BYOD) voucher model or reduce the data cap to 100 MB. Do not proceed to the full 150-school rollout. The current unit economics suggest you are buying churn at a loss.

**Immediate Next Step for Sipho:** Halt the June 30 commitment. Re-model based on a realistic ZAR 250+ per-student cost and secure the MDM vendor quote before seeking regulatory approval.
