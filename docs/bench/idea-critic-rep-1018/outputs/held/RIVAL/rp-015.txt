**Verdict: Proceed with a Staged Pilot, Not a Full Rollout**

Do not commit to the full €250,000 development spend or the February 2027 launch date yet. The financial model relies on a fragile assumption: that 30% of members will adopt a new, unproven AI service immediately upon launch. This adoption rate is exceptionally high for a new feature without a track record. If adoption is only 15%, you will operate at a significant monthly loss against the €45,000 hosting cost.

Instead, recommend a **3-month Pilot Phase** starting in November 2026 (aligning with the ERP upgrade completion). Limit the pilot to 500 members (approx. 5% of the base). This reduces initial development complexity, allows for real-world stress testing of the ERP integration, and provides actual data on adoption and churn impact before the full capital outlay is deployed.

**Key Failure Modes**

1.  **Integration Latency:** The 6-month integration window overlaps the ERP upgrade. If the ERP upgrade slips even slightly, the AI concierge launch is blocked, burning the €45,000/month hosting budget with zero revenue.
2.  **Adoption Shortfall:** The break-even model requires ~2,940 users (30%). If you secure only 1,500 users (15%), monthly revenue drops to ~€118,000. Against hosting and dev amortization, this delays break-even from 12 months to over 24 months, destroying the ROI case.
3.  **Privacy & Personalization Gap:** If the AI fails to deliver "personal" insights due to strict data privacy guards required for member data, the value proposition collapses. Members will not pay €79/month for a generic chatbot.

**The Halt Trigger**

**Metric:** **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) < 95% after Month 2 of the Pilot.**

Do not wait for a 5% churn rate in the general population. During the pilot, track the specific cohort using the Concierge. If their total spend (membership + services) does not increase by at least 5% compared to their pre-pilot baseline within 60 days, the "15% uplift in overall member spend" thesis is invalid.

**Action:** If NRR < 95% at Day 60, **halt full development immediately.** Pivot to a lower-cost, rules-based automation tool rather than a full AI concierge, or shelve the project entirely. Do not proceed to the full February 2027 launch without passing this metric.
