**Sanity Check & Strategic Angle**

Your base math holds ($150k gross → $112.5k net), but the model is dangerously static regarding secondary royalties. You are assuming the 4% secondary fee is pure profit, yet you haven't modeled the *velocity* of those trades. If liquidity is low (your primary concern), that revenue stream vanishes, making the $60k upfront burn ($40k + $12k + $8k) impossible to recoup without selling 1,600+ primary units just to break even on costs.

**The Missing Piece:** You are overlooking **artist opportunity cost and reputation risk**. If the secondary market freezes, artists see no long-term tail income, turning this from a "royalty pilot" into a one-off cash grab that could sour relationships with your top 5 acts.

**Suggested Angle:** Pivot the pitch from "Revenue Generation" to **"Fan Data & Loyalty Acquisition."** Frame the NFT not as an investment vehicle for fans, but as a dynamic access key. Even if secondary volume is zero, the *primary* sale gives you direct wallet-to-fan connectivity for future tours/merch. This lowers the success threshold: the pilot succeeds if you acquire 3,000 verified super-fans, regardless of secondary trading.

**Specific Kill Criteria (Pull the Plug If):**
1.  **Primary Pre-sales < 40%:** If you cannot secure commitments for 1,200 units (40% of 3,000) before smart contract deployment, cancel. This indicates insufficient artist buy-in or fan demand.
2.  **Secondary Volume < 150 units in Day 30:** If fewer than 5% of holders trade within the first month, liquidity is dead. Do not market further; fulfill obligations and wind down.
3.  **Gas Fees > $25/tx during launch window:** If network congestion spikes deployment or minting costs by >20% of budget, pause. Volatility kills mass adoption for $50 items.

**Note:** Your current budget leaves zero margin for error; add a 15% contingency ($9k) specifically for gas spikes.
