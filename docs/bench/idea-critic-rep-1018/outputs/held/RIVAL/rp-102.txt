**Board Brief: Greenflare East Africa Launch Risks & Alternatives**

**Strongest Objection: The Unit Economics Are Fatal**
The plan assumes a 5% transaction fee ($7.20 per credit) covers all operating costs. This is mathematically impossible for a physical-heavy rollout.
*   **The Math:** Your total fee revenue is $72,000 (5% of $1.44M). Your stated operating budget (Tech + Compliance + Marketing) is $620,000. You are raising $72k to spend $620k.
*   **The Reality:** Onboarding 1,200 smallholder farms requires intense physical verification, land surveying, and agent commissions. Industry standard verification costs alone often exceed $5–$10 per credit. At $7.20 total revenue per credit, you lose money on every single transaction before paying a single staff member. The model relies on volume to fix margins, but the cost of acquiring that volume destroys cash flow first.

**Smarter Alternative: The "Aggregator" Pivot**
Do not build a proprietary exchange or hire a large compliance team yet.
*   **Partner, Don't Build:** Integrate with an existing verified carbon standard (e.g., Verra or Gold Standard) and an established regional aggregator. Let them handle the regulatory heavy lifting and verification methodology.
*   **Role Shift:** Greenflare should act purely as the technology interface and data layer for the aggregators, charging a SaaS fee or a higher rev-share on the *verification* premium, rather than trying to survive on a thin transaction slice. This reduces your capex from $620k to roughly $150k and shifts regulatory risk to the partner.

**Specific Kill/Pivot Triggers**
1.  **Adoption Trigger (Pivot):** If fewer than **400 farms** are fully verified (not just signed up) within the first 6 months. Signed LOIs are worthless; only verified credits generate revenue. If you cannot verify 400 farms, your physical onboarding cost per unit is too high.
2.  **Regulatory Trigger (Kill):** If the Ministry of Environment does not provide a written provisional approval or a clear regulatory sandbox framework within **90 days**. A "3–6 month" vague timeline is a red flag; without a written path, assume a 18-month delay which burns your runway.
3.  **Cost Trigger (Kill):** If third-party verification costs exceed **$4.00 per credit**. At this level, your 5% fee model is structurally broken regardless of volume.

**Note:** The current budget implies you can onboard a farm for ~$260 ($320k+$210k / 1,200 farms); realistic field costs in East Africa often triple this.
**Recommendation:** Pause the "build" phase and commission a $20k pilot to verify 50 farms using existing partners before committing the full $620k.
