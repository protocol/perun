This reduces your initial build cost by ~40%, isolates regulatory risk to one jurisdiction, and validates the SwiftRiver cost-saving assumption before you scale capital expenditure.

**Top Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Delay:** If clearance slips past June 2025, your $300k marketing burn becomes dead weight while fixed costs accrue, turning the projected $1.35M profit into a ~$500k loss for Year 1.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** The model assumes a static $200/transaction margin. If SwiftRiver delivers only 15% savings instead of 30%, or if competitor pricing forces a fee cut, your break-even volume doubles, making the 12,000 tx/month target insufficient to cover ops.
3.  **Liquidity Trap:** Remittance requires pre-funding corridors. The model ignores the working capital needed to float funds during settlement windows; a 3-day delay at 12,000 tx/month could tie up millions in cash flow not accounted for in the $750k build budget.

**Kill-Switch Assessment:**
Your proposed threshold (**abort if Q1 net loss > $200k**) is **dangerously lagging**. By the time you realize a $200k loss, you have likely already committed the full $300k marketing budget and signed long-term vendor contracts.

**Better Kill-Switches (Leading Indicators):**
*   **Regulatory Gate:** If full clearance is not granted by **April 15, 2025**, pause all marketing spend immediately.
*   **Cost Validation:** If the pilot's first 500 transactions show a per-tx cost reduction of **<20%** (vs. the modeled 30%), halt scaling to renegotiate or pivot.
*   **Adoption Rate:** If pilot conversion is **<2%** of targeted leads after 4 weeks, abort the broader rollout.
