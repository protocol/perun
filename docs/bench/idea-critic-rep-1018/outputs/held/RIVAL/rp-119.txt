**Recommendation: NO-GO (Proceed with Caution / Renegotiate)**

Do not approve the pilot in its current form. The financial model collapses under the vague service fee, and the adoption math is physically impossible.

**The Arithmetic Failure**
Your savings assumption is flawed. Saving €9,000 at €30/hour requires 300 hours of labor reduction monthly. With only two kiosks expecting 1,200 total check-outs (600 per unit), each transaction would need to save 30 minutes of staff time to reach that figure. In reality, a kiosk transaction saves roughly 2–3 minutes. Your actual labor savings are likely closer to €1,000–€1,500/month, not €9,000.

Furthermore, the "around €800" service fee is a critical vulnerability.
*   **Best Case (€800/unit):** €1,600/month cost vs. ~€1,200 real savings = **€400/month net loss**.
*   **Worst Case (€1,200/unit):** €2,400/month cost vs. ~€1,200 real savings = **€1,200/month net loss**.
You are proposing to pay a premium to lose money every month for six months.

**The Adoption Reality Check**
Your 70% adoption target (2,450 members) contradicts the vendor's volume cap.
*   To get 2,450 members to borrow via kiosk even once a month, you need 2,450 transactions.
*   The vendor caps volume at 1,200 transactions.
*   **Result:** You can hit the volume cap *or* the adoption rate, never both. Given two units in a lobby, 1,200 check-outs implies ~40 transactions per day total. This is low-traffic usage, not a 70% member shift.

**Principal Failure Modes**
1.  **Cost Creep:** The supplier locks you into a €1,500+/month service contract after installation, making exit costly.
2.  **Bottlenecking:** Two units cannot handle peak hour rushes, causing members to abandon the kiosk and demand staff help anyway, negating savings.
3.  **Vendor Lock-in:** The "setup fee" covers proprietary integration that prevents you from swapping hardware later.

**The Kill Metric**
**Net Operational Cost per Transaction.**
Calculate: `(Monthly Service Fee + Amortized Hardware Cost) / Actual Check-outs`.
If this figure exceeds **€1.50 per transaction** (your current internal staff cost per checkout), **pull the plug immediately** at the end of Month 1. Do not wait for the six-month term.

**Immediate Action:** Demand a fixed-price service contract capped at €600/unit/month and a revised labor savings model based on 3-minute transaction times before presenting to the board.
