**Strongest Objection: The "False Economy" of Unverified Digital Exclusion**
Your plan assumes the 30% workload reduction applies to the total patient base, but you have not quantified the digital literacy gap among your 1,200 monthly patients. If 40–50% of your demographic (likely elderly or low-income) cannot effectively use a chatbot, your actual addressable volume drops drastically. Consequently, your cost-per-chat for the *eligible* population skyrockets from 8 PHP to potentially 15+ PHP, while the call center workload remains unchanged for the excluded half. You risk paying for a parallel system (bot + human agents) rather than a replacement, turning a projected 150,000 PHP investment into a net loss without achieving the 30% efficiency target.

**Simpler Alternative: Structured Nurse-Led Triage with Decision Support**
Before automating, expand the nurse-led phone line using a rigid scripting protocol (decision trees) rather than an AI bot.
*   **Why:** It requires zero patient digital literacy, handles complex empathy-driven triage better than current NLP models, and captures the same data for conversion analysis.
*   **Cost:** Likely lower than 150,000 PHP for six months (training + scripts vs. dev/licensing), with immediate deployment no matter the regulatory delay.
*   **Strategy:** Run this for 8 weeks. If the script adherence is high and conversion matches the pilot, *then* automate the script into a bot once the digital literacy baseline is proven.

**The "Stop" Trigger (Kill Criterion)**
Dr. Reyes should halt the rollout immediately if, after the first 30 days of operation:
1.  **Metric:** The **Call Deflection Rate** (calls successfully resolved by the bot without human handoff) is **<15%**.
    *   *Reasoning:* If the bot cannot deflect at least half of your projected 30% savings target within the first month, the adoption curve is too flat to ever justify the fixed costs.
2.  **Event:** The Health Authority approval is not secured by **May 15, 2024** (30 days pre-launch).
    *   *Reasoning:* Launching without explicit approval risks a cease-and-desist order, which would invalidate all pilot data and damage board credibility.

**What You Are Overlooking**
*   **Regulatory Slip Impact:** Your budget assumes a hard start date of June 15. If approval slips 60 days, you burn budget on standby licensing/development with zero ROI. You need a "pause clause" in vendor contracts.
*   **Conversion Quality:** A 12% conversion rate is good, but are these *necessary* visits? If the bot is too aggressive, you may be driving up costs by converting cases that a skilled nurse would have resolved remotely. Measure "Avoidable In-Person Visits" as a negative metric.

**Recommendation**
Delay the June 15 launch until the digital literacy audit is complete. Re-forecast the budget based on only the *verified* digitally literate segment. If that segment is <600 patients/month, the bot unit economics will fail; pivot to the enhanced nurse-line immediately.
