**Recommendation for Marie:**
Proceed with a **single-market pilot (Germany only)** for the first 45 days rather than a simultaneous three-country launch. Your current model assumes uniform shipping (€4) and ignores customs duties, which will likely erase your margin in the UK (post-Brexit) and Spain immediately. Germany offers the most predictable regulatory environment for dairy imports from your location. Validate the actual landed cost and certification requirements there before expanding. If you launch all three markets on October 15th based on current assumptions, you risk selling every unit at a loss due to unaccounted duties and variable logistics.

**Three Main Failure Points:**
1.  **Margin Erasure by Duties & Variable Shipping:** The €4 shipping estimate is likely too low for the UK and Spain, and customs duties (often 10–15% for dairy plus VAT handling fees) are completely unbudgeted. At €25 price point, these added costs could turn your projected €7.50 gross profit per wheel into a net loss.
2.  **Regulatory Stoppage:** Local dairy regulations may require specific health certifications or border inspections not yet identified. If a shipment is held at customs for lacking a specific certificate, you lose the product (spoilage) and the customer, plus face potential fines.
3.  **Demand Concentration Risk:** Assuming linear demand (100 orders/month per country) is speculative. If 90% of demand comes from one country with higher logistics costs, your blended margin calculation collapses, and the "average" €4 shipping cost becomes a fatal error.

**Cut-Off Point (Pivot Trigger):**
If **net profit falls below €2,000 per month** after 45 days of operation, or if **customs/duty costs exceed 8% of revenue** in any single market, immediately pause direct-to-consumer expansion in that region and pivot to a **wholesale distribution model**. Wholesale shifts the logistics and customs burden to the distributor, preserving brand presence while protecting cash flow. Do not wait for the full three-month pilot if these thresholds are breached in the first six weeks.
