**Primary Red Flag to Monitor:**  
**Daily churn rate among non-buyers.** If this spikes above 0.8% (a 60% increase from your baseline) during the pilot, it indicates the loot-box mechanics are degrading the core experience for the majority who don't engage, risking long-term player base erosion that $9.6M in revenue cannot fix.

**Concise Recommendation:**  
Proceed with the pilot but **immediately freeze the "8% conversion" revenue assumption** in your financial models. Treat the $120k as a sunk cost for data acquisition, not a profit generator. Do not allocate future budget based on the $9.6M projection until you have 30 days of live data validating both conversion and churn impact.

**How This Could Fall Apart:**  
1.  **Regulatory Whiplash:** The EU classifies your specific implementation as gambling mid-pilot. This forces an immediate geo-block in Europe (your largest market), invalidating the revenue model and leaving you with art/integration costs you cannot recoup.
2.  **The "Pay-to-Win" Backlash:** If the loot box contains gameplay advantages rather than cosmetics, social sentiment could turn toxic. A coordinated review-bombing campaign can tank organic acquisition, making the cost of new user acquisition exceed the lifetime value of loot-box buyers.
3.  **Infrastructure Collapse:** You haven't priced the server capacity for transaction spikes. A denial-of-service effect caused by legitimate player volume during launch could crash login services for *all* 1.2M users, causing mass churn unrelated to the loot boxes themselves.

**Abandonment Triggers:**  
*   **Regulatory:** Any formal statement from the European Commission or a key member state (e.g., Belgium, Netherlands) explicitly naming your game's mechanic as illegal gambling. **Action:** Immediate global disablement of the feature to prevent legal liability.
*   **Financial:** Server scaling costs exceed **$15,000/month** (25% of your total pilot budget burned in one month). This indicates your unit economics are broken; the margin per transaction is being eaten by infrastructure overhead.
*   **Engagement:** A sustained **7-day rolling average churn rate > 0.7%** for non-buying segments. This proves the feature is repelling your core audience faster than it is monetizing them.
