**2. The Kill Switches (When to Pull the Plug)**
Do not proceed if any of the following three conditions are met during the discovery phase (Months 1–2):

*   **Regulatory Hard-Stop:** If the CRTC explicitly states that a decentralized ledger does not satisfy "chain of title" legal requirements for Canadian broadcasters. *Mitigation:* Structure the blockchain as a verification layer anchored to a traditional legal database, rather than a full replacement.
*   **Gas Fee Spike:** If average transaction fees exceed **$0.05** consistently. At $0.01, fees are negligible. At $0.05, operational costs rise 5x, eroding the margin on low-value rights clearances. *Trigger:* Implement a fee cap; if exceeded, batch transactions or pause writes.
*   **Integration Drag:** If legacy system API integration requires more than **400 engineering hours**. If the current database is too rigid to sync with a distributed ledger, the 12-month timeline will slip to 18+, delaying the revenue lift.

**3. The Pivot Strategy**
If the CRTC stance remains ambiguous by Month 3, pivot to a **Hybrid Model**:
*   Keep the current SaaS for legal storage (paying the $45k).
*   Use blockchain *only* for immutable audit logs and timestamping (reducing dev cost to ~$100k).
*   This captures the "trust" benefit without risking non-compliance.
