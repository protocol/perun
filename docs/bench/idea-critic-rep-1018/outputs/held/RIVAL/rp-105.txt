**Recommendation:** Proceed with the rollout only if the 2026 spectrum licence renewal is confirmed at ≤€5.5M; otherwise, pause deployment after the Warsaw phase (40 nodes) to recalibrate the business case.

**Principal Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Cost Shock:** If the spectrum fee rises above €6.8M annually, the projected €18M revenue gain is erased by licensing and maintenance overhead, turning the rollout net-negative by Q2 2027.
2.  **Permit Bottleneck:** The 30-day permit assumption is aggressive for three major municipalities. If average approval stretches to 60 days, the 12-month schedule slips to 18 months, delaying revenue recognition by two full quarters and increasing capital carry costs by ~€4.2M.
3.  **Market Share Overreach:** Capturing 20% of the enterprise segment assumes competitors remain static. If a rival launches a competing edge mesh in these cities during our deployment window, share capture could stall at 8–10%, yielding only €9M in additional revenue—insufficient to cover the €92M outlay within the investment horizon.

**Abandonment Trigger:** Walk away if the pilot phase in Warsaw (first 40 nodes) fails to achieve a 12% ARPU lift within six months of activation. This metric is the leading indicator of enterprise adoption; anything below 12% implies the 20% market-share target is unattainable, making the remaining €61M investment in Kraków and Gdańsk unjustifiable.

**First Alarm:** The moment the 2026 spectrum licence renewal quote exceeds €5.5M.

**Why it matters:** Your current model treats the €4.3M fee as fixed, but spectrum auctions are volatile and often political. A €1.2M increase (to €5.5M) consumes 6.7% of your projected €18M upside. Anything beyond that—say, a €2.5M hike—eats more than 13% of the gain, pushing the internal rate of return below our cost of capital. Unlike deployment delays or slower adoption, licensing costs are non-negotiable fixed overhead that compound annually. If the regulator signals a higher fee before the May 2025 start date, you are committing to a structurally impaired asset before laying a single fiber. Secure the licence terms in writing before authorizing hardware procurement.
