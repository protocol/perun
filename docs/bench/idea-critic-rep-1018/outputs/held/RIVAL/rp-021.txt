**The Toughest Objection: The "Hidden Tax" on Margins**
Luca will immediately spot that your €180k savings calculation assumes *only* water acquisition costs vanish. In reality, membrane systems carry a heavy "energy and chemical tax." You are swapping a variable utility bill for a fixed capital debt plus new variable costs (electricity for high-pressure pumps, anti-foulant chemicals, and membrane replacement every 3–5 years). If the combined energy and chemical load exceeds €0.40 per cubic meter treated, your net savings drop by over 30%, pushing the payback period beyond 7 years—unacceptable for infrastructure with a 10-year technical lifespan.

**Stronger Alternative: The "Modular Pilot" Strategy**
If the economics slip due to high O&M or energy volatility, do not abandon the goal; abandon the *scale*. Propose a **€150k modular pilot unit** treating 50,000 L/day (one production line) instead of the full €950k plant.
*   **Why:** It reduces capital exposure by 84%. It allows you to measure *actual* energy/chemical consumption and membrane fouling rates on your specific wastewater stream before betting the company budget. It turns a "bet the farm" decision into a data-gathering operational expense.

**The Walk-Away Trigger**
Luca walks away if the **Blended Cost Per Cubic Meter** (Amortized CapEx + Energy + Chemicals + Labor) exceeds **€0.75**.
*   *Logic:* At €0.75, you are saving only €0.45 per cubic meter versus municipal water (€1.20). This thin margin offers no buffer for regulatory changes, energy spikes, or system downtime. If the internal cost to recycle is more than 62.5% of the external buy price, the resilience benefit is not worth the balance sheet risk.

**Three Main Ways This Flops**
1.  **Fouling & Downtime:** The membrane clogs faster than anticipated due to specific contaminants in your stream, forcing frequent shutdowns for cleaning. You lose production hours (revenue) while still paying for the system.
2.  **Energy Price Volatility:** Your model assumes static energy costs. If electricity prices rise 20%, the high-pressure pumps make the recycling process more expensive than buying municipal water, turning the asset into a liability.
3.  **Regulatory Moveable Goalposts:** The system is designed for current discharge standards. If environmental regulations tighten in 2026 requiring a secondary treatment stage, the CAPEX jumps another 40% instantly, breaking the business case.

**The Kill Metric**
**Net Savings < €120,000/year.**
If the verified O&M costs reduce your annual savings from €180k to below €120k, the project fails. At €120k savings, the simple payback stretches to nearly 8 years (€950k / €120k), which is too slow for a technology asset subject to rapid obsolescence and maintenance degradation.
