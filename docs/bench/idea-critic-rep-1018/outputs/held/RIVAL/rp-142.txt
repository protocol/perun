1.  **Logistics Eroding Margins:** The unpriced transport from Jebel Ali to sites 45–80km away could easily consume the $1,400 per-panel savings. If heavy-haulage costs exceed $400 per panel, the economic advantage over traditional building vanishes.

3.  **Labor Assumption Fragility:** The plan assumes labor stays fixed at 12% of total cost. In a high-growth prefabrication environment, skilled labor shortages often drive wages up faster than general inflation, disproportionately hitting the modular model which is more labor-intensive in the factory phase than on-site.

Your defined trigger is sound: **Exit or pivot if the actual all-in panel cost exceeds $8,500 for three consecutive months.** However, add a leading indicator: **Pause expansion immediately if the confirmed logistics cost per panel exceeds $500** during the pilot phase, as this mathematically guarantees the failure of the full-scale business case regardless of production efficiency.
