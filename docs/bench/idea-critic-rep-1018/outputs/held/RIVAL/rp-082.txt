3.  **Unverified Churn:** Assuming 5% churn without data is dangerous; if real churn is 10%, you will spend your entire marketing budget just replacing lost subscribers rather than growing.
4.  **Packaging Waste Fees:** If return rates on packaging are high, municipal waste fees could turn a profitable unit economics model into a loss leader.

**Pull-the-Plug Metric:**
Stop the program immediately if the **Churn-to-Acquisition Ratio exceeds 1:1** (i.e., for every new subscriber you buy for $12, you lose one existing subscriber within the same month), indicating you are funding a leaky bucket rather than building growth.

**Insights for Maya:**
Your current math suggests a $16k net surplus, but once you deduct the cost of goods sold (green coffee, packaging, shipping), night-shift labor premiums, and waste fees, you may actually be losing money on every box sold.
