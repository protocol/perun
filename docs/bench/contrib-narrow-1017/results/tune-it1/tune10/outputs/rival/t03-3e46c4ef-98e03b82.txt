Run these commands in your terminal to fix the author information for this specific commit without affecting your global configuration:

```bash

```

**Why this is the safest path:**
1.  **Legal Clarity:** Using a personal email explicitly signals that you are contributing as an individual, not as a representative of Globex Corporation. This avoids any implication that your employer endorses the project or claims ownership of your contribution.
2.  **Project Norms:** Since the project history shows mostly personal addresses, matching this pattern respects the maintainers' established culture and reduces friction during review.
3.  **No Side Effects:** The `--author` flag overrides your global config only for this specific commit, leaving your employer's settings intact for other work.

**Note:** Ensure you have already signed any required Contributor License Agreement (CLA) under your personal identity if the project mandates one. If you have already pushed the branch with the corporate email, the `--force-with-lease` command above will safely overwrite the remote history while protecting against concurrent changes.
