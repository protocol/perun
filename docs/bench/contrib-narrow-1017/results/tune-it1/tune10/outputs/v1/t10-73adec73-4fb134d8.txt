1.  **Generate Synthetic Data:** Create a new, entirely synthetic dataset that mimics the *structural complexity* of your workload without copying specific patterns. Focus on reproducing the statistical properties that trigger the lock contention (e.g., specific distribution of key collisions, transaction sizes, or write skew) rather than the exact query shapes. Use randomized values and invented table names (e.g., `table_a`, `col_x`) that bear no resemblance to OmniCorp's schema.

3.  **Share Aggregated Results Only:** Present only the aggregated performance results (e.g., "20% throughput improvement under high contention") and graphs derived from the synthetic run. Do not include raw logs, trace files, or any artifact that could be reverse-engineered to reveal the original workload characteristics.
