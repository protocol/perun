Do not submit the real logs, even with manual redaction, and do not generate synthetic logs to mimic the crash pattern. Manual redaction is insufficient because paraphrased context or structural details in the logs can still leak user identity or strategy, and synthetic data risks obscuring the precise race condition needed to verify the fix.

Instead, isolate the **underlying principle** of the crash: the specific thread synchronization flaw (e.g., "a race condition between the connection handler and the background queue on legacy iOS versions"). Draft a contribution that includes:
1.  A **generalized description** of the defect class without any project-specific identifiers (remove "SwiftLink," "StreamLine," device IDs, locations, and timestamps).
2.  A **minimal, reproducible test case** written in Swift that synthetically triggers the *logic error* using mock objects, containing zero real user data.
3.  A **provenance block** explicitly stating: "SOURCE: Internal beta testing; GENERALIZED-AWAY: All device identifiers, location data, and app names; RESIDUAL RISK: None, as the test case uses entirely synthetic mock data."

Submit this generalized test case and explanation as a draft to the maintainers. The maintainers can then run this synthetic test against their codebase to verify the fix without ever seeing real user data. If the maintainers require the original logs for deep forensic analysis, they must establish a secure, private channel (such as a direct, encrypted email or a private security advisory portal) where you, as the human privacy authority, can explicitly authorize the transfer after verifying their identity and need. Never automate this transfer; you must personally clear the risk of sending any raw data.

Note: The safety boundary requires that you, the human, act as the privacy authority; no automated tool can guarantee that a redacted log is free of semantic leaks.
Only contribute the generalized lesson and the synthetic test upstream; keep the raw logs within your private control unless a human-to-human secure channel is explicitly established.
