Do not mention SecureBank, the paid engagement, or the specific client context in your initial contact with the maintainers. Your primary obligation is to the confidentiality agreement you signed; violating it to provide "context" to a third party is a breach of contract, regardless of your intent to help. The maintainers of an open-source project generally require only three things to prioritize a fix: a clear technical description of the vulnerability, a reproducible test case or proof-of-concept (PoC), and a working patch. They do not need to know the commercial origin of the finding to assess severity or merge a solution.

Draft your report to focus exclusively on the technical facts:
1.  **Vulnerability Type:** Clearly state it is a race condition in signature verification.
2.  **Impact:** Explain that it allows bypassing security checks under rapid request spikes.
3.  **Reproduction:** Provide the steps or code to trigger the race condition without revealing any data or infrastructure belonging to SecureBank.
4.  **The Fix:** Attach your patch and explain how it resolves the timing issue.

If the maintainers ask later about the context of the discovery, you can state generally that it was found during "independent security research" or "routine testing," which is factually true and usually satisfies curiosity without breaching NDAs. Only if they explicitly demand to know if this stems from a commercial audit should you consult your legal counsel or the client before answering. In almost all cases, the quality of the technical report and the existence of a ready-to-merge patch are the sole drivers for rapid adoption. Mentioning a paid engagement often introduces unnecessary legal friction, causing maintainers to pause for liability reviews rather than accelerating the fix. Protect your client by stripping all identifiers and let the technical merit of your patch speak for itself.

Note: Ensure your PoC does not contain any IP addresses, timestamps, or data patterns that could be traced back to SecureBank's specific environment.
