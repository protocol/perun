**Recommended Strategy**

Submit your pull request directly against the `main` branch. Do not rebase on top of unreviewed, stale branches, as those changes are not part of the official codebase and may never be merged. Waiting indefinitely is inefficient for a critical fix like a financial discrepancy.

By targeting `main`, you place the responsibility of conflict resolution where it belongs: with the maintainers who control the merge queue. Your PR serves as a formal signal that this specific file is active and blocked by stale contributions. If a merge conflict arises later, you can then rebase onto the newly merged code. This approach respects the "avoid merge conflicts" guideline by not creating artificial conflicts now, while ensuring your fix enters the review pipeline immediately.
