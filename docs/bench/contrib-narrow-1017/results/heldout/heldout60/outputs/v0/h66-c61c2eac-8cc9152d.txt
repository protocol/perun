Do not open the pull request yet. Submitting code you are not legally authorized to contribute violates the open-source project's license terms and exposes both you and your employer to significant legal risk. The potential consequence of a license violation or a forced code retraction far outweighs the benefit of beating a release cycle or avoiding a "stale" label.

The correct strategy is to engage the community **without code** while your internal approval processes:

1.  **Open an Issue, Not a PR:** Immediately file a detailed issue on the project's tracker describing the touch-input latency bug, the affected devices, and the technical root cause you identified. Explicitly state that you have a working fix ready internally.
2.  **Disclose the Constraint:** In the issue description, clearly note: "A fix has been developed internally, but corporate legal approval for external contribution is currently in process (estimated 3–4 weeks). I will submit the Pull Request as soon as clearance is granted."
3.  **Request Guidance:** Ask the maintainers if this timeline aligns with their release schedule or if there are specific steps you should take in the interim to keep the ticket active.

This approach respects the project's momentum by alerting them to a known bug and a pending solution, preventing duplicate work by others. It also maintains professionalism by being transparent about your constraints rather than ghosting reviewers or risking a legal breach. Most maintainers will appreciate the heads-up and may even mark the issue as "pending contribution" to protect it from stale bots.

If the release cycle is critical and the legal delay is absolute, you might also ask your legal team if they can provide a provisional "letter of intent" to contribute, which you could share with the maintainers to further solidify trust, though this depends on your company's specific policies.

**Note:** Never contribute code without explicit authorization; the open-source community relies on the certainty that submitted code is legally clean.
**Note:** Engaging via an issue keeps the conversation alive and demonstrates good faith while you navigate internal bureaucracy.
