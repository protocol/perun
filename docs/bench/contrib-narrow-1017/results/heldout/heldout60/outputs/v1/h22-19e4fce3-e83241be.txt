**Addressing Your Uncertainty**
You do not need to prove the severity with real data; the *mechanism* of leakage is the severity. Showing that *any* string assigned to an environment variable appears unmasked in the log file is sufficient proof. Maintainers care that the pipe is broken, not what specific water flowed through it.
