**Direct Answer:** Yes, it is standard for Contributor License Agreements (CLAs) to request a physical address to establish legal jurisdiction and identity, but you can almost always use a P.O. Box or business address instead of your home address. True anonymity is rarely compatible with signed CLAs because the agreement is a legal contract requiring an identifiable party.

**Action Plan:**

1.  **Verify the CLA Provider:** Identify the third-party service (e.g., CLA Assistant, EasyCLA, Individual CLA). Check their privacy policy; reputable providers encrypt data and restrict access to project maintainers only for legal enforcement, not public display.
2.  **Attempt P.O. Box Entry:** Return to the form and enter your P.O. Box or business address in the "Street Address" field. Most automated validators only check for the presence of *an* address format, not its residential status.
3.  **Contact Maintainers (If Validation Fails):** If the form strictly rejects non-residential addresses, do not bypass it. Instead, open a public issue on the GeoMap repository titled "Question regarding CLA address requirement for privacy."
    *   *Draft Message:* "I am attempting to sign the CLA for a minor documentation fix. The form requires a physical home address. For privacy reasons, can I provide a P.O. Box, or is there an alternative process for small contributions? The README does not clarify this."
4.  **Alternative Contribution Path:** If the maintainers are unresponsive or insist on a home address for a trivial fix, consider whether the contribution is worth the risk. You may choose to fork the project and maintain your fix locally, or submit the documentation improvement as an unmerged issue description for a maintainer to apply themselves (though this transfers the copyright liability to them).

**Ready-to-Paste Issue Comment:**
> "Hi maintainers, I'm eager to contribute a small documentation fix. However, the automated CLA process requests my full physical home address. While I understand the need for legal jurisdiction, I am uncomfortable sharing my private residence details for a minor contribution. Is it permissible to use a P.O. Box or business address? If not, is there an exemption process for trivial changes, or should I submit the text here for a maintainer to commit directly? Thanks for clarifying."

**Fallback:** If the project insists on a home address without a compelling legal explanation and refuses a P.O. Box, you are within your rights to withdraw the contribution. No open-source project is entitled to your private data, and minor documentation fixes are rarely critical enough to warrant compromising personal security.

**Note:** Never fabricate an address on a legal document, as this invalidates the contract and could expose you to liability. Always communicate your concerns transparently with the project leads.
