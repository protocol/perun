# LogiTrack Pilot Success Metrics: Vietnam Electronics Manufacturing (April 5 – June 4)

## Executive Summary
This document defines the quantitative and qualitative indicators required to validate LogiTrack's value proposition during the 60-day pilot with five Ho Chi Minh City factories. The primary objective is to verify the correlation between reduced delay detection time and inventory cost savings, while establishing a baseline for trust and reliability.

## 1. Primary Quantitative Metrics (Value Verification)

These metrics directly test the core hypothesis: *Reducing detection time from 48h to <6h yields a 15% reduction in inventory holding costs.*

| Metric Name | Definition & Calculation | Target (60-Day Pilot) | Data Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)** | Average time elapsed between a shipment deviation event (e.g., port delay, truck breakdown) and the system alert generation. <br>*(Sum of detection latencies / Total incidents)* | **< 6 hours** | LogiTrack System Logs vs. Carrier Actuals |
| **Inventory Holding Cost Delta** | Percentage change in costs associated with safety stock and warehousing during the pilot window compared to the prior 60-day baseline. <br>*Note: Isolate costs for SKUs covered by LogiTrack.* | **≥ 15% Reduction** | Client ERP / Finance Records |
| **Alert Accuracy Rate** | Percentage of proactive alerts that corresponded to actual, impactful delays (excluding false positives). <br>*(Valid Alerts / Total Alerts Sent)* | **> 90%** | LogiTrack Logs + Manager Confirmation |
| **Proactive Resolution Rate** | Percentage of detected delays where mitigation actions (e.g., rerouting, expedited customs) were initiated *before* the scheduled arrival time was missed. | **> 75%** | Client Logistics Action Logs |

**Strategic Note on Speed vs. Frequency:**
Do not prioritize "frequency of alerts" as a standalone metric. High frequency with low accuracy erodes trust. Prioritize **Alert Accuracy Rate** and **MTTD**. The goal is *actionable* speed. If MTTD is <6 hours but accuracy drops below 80%, the system creates noise rather than value.

## 2. Qualitative Metrics (Trust & Reliability)

To measure the intangible value of "trust" mentioned by account managers, we will use a structured scoring system administered bi-weekly to the primary Logistics Manager at each of the five factories.

| Indicator | Measurement Method | Success Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Decision Confidence Score** | Bi-weekly survey (1-10 scale): *"How confident were you in making inventory adjustments based solely on LogiTrack data this week?"* | Average score **≥ 8/10** by Day 45 |
| **Communication Efficiency** | Qualitative interview question: *"How many hours per week did you save on status update calls/emails compared to your previous process?"* | Reported savings of **≥ 5 hours/week** per manager |
| **Reliability Perception** | Net Promoter Score (NPS) style question at Day 30 and Day 60: *"How likely are you to recommend LogiTrack to another factory owner in the VSIP industrial zones?"* | Net Positive Score (>0) by Day 60 |

## 3. Data Collection Infrastructure Requirements

To ensure these metrics are computable and auditable, the following data points must be captured automatically starting April 5th:

1.  **Event Timestamping:** Every shipment status update from carriers must be ingested with a precise UTC timestamp.
2.  **Alert Logging:** Every alert sent to a user must log: *Time Sent, Trigger Condition, User ID, and Acknowledgement Time.*
3.  **Baseline Capture:** Secure agreement from all five partners to share aggregated inventory holding cost data for the period *February 5 – April 4* to establish the pre-pilot baseline.
4.  **Feedback Loop:** Implement a simple "Thumbs Up/Down" mechanism on every alert within the UI to track real-time accuracy perception without waiting for bi-weekly surveys.

## 4. Reporting Cadence

*   **Weekly Internal Review:** Monitor MTTD and Alert Accuracy. If Accuracy < 85%, pause proactive alerts and tune rules immediately.
*   **Day 30 Check-in:** Present preliminary Decision Confidence Scores and Communication Efficiency estimates to country management.
*   **Day 60 Final Report:** Correlate MTTD improvements with Inventory Holding Cost Delta to validate the 15% savings hypothesis.

***

*Ensure all data collection complies with Vietnam's Cybersecurity Law regarding data localization if server infrastructure is hosted outside the country.*
*Baseline inventory cost data must be secured before April 5th to enable valid before/after comparison.*
