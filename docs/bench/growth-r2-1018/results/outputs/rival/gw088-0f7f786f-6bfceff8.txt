### Proxy Metrics and Success Criteria for ClassiRu Ranking Algorithm Launch

**Primary Success Metric (North Star)**
*   **First-Page Engagement Rate:** The percentage of search sessions where a user clicks at least one job listing on the first results page.
    *   *Rationale:* Directly measures the algorithm's ability to surface relevant content immediately, aligning with your hypothesis that better relevance drives clicks.
    *   *Target:* Statistically significant increase vs. control group (or pre-launch baseline) over the 14-day window.

**Secondary Proxy Metrics (Quality & Intent)**
Since "successful hire" is unobservable, use these behavioral proxies to ensure clicks represent genuine interest rather than accidental or low-quality traffic:

1.  **Deep Engagement Ratio:** Percentage of clicks resulting in >30 seconds on the job detail page or scrolling >50% of the listing.
    *   *Why:* Filters out "bounce" clicks; indicates the user found the listing promising enough to read details.
2.  **Action Propensity:** Percentage of job detail views triggering a "Show Contact Info," "Save Job," or "Share" event.
    *   *Why:* These are high-intent micro-conversions that strongly correlate with application initiation, even if the final application happens offline or in a separate flow.
3.  **Search Refinement Rate:** Percentage of sessions where the user modifies their search query or clicks "Next Page" within 10 seconds of landing on results.
    *   *Why:* A decrease suggests the first page successfully satisfied the user's intent, reducing the need to refine or dig deeper.

**Guardrail Metrics (Safety Checks)**
Monitor these to ensure the new algorithm does not degrade the ecosystem:
*   **Unique Listings Clicked per Session:** Ensure this does not drop significantly (prevents the algo from showing the same few popular jobs repeatedly).
*   **Geographic Relevance:** Monitor click-through rates on listings outside the user's stated location (should not increase).
*   **Spam/Low-Quality Click Share:** If you flag low-quality employers, ensure clicks to them do not rise.

**Success Criteria for Sign-Off (Two-Week Window)**
To approve the release, the following conditions must be met simultaneously:

1.  **Primary Metric Lift:** First-Page Engagement Rate increases by **≥2%** (relative lift) with 95% statistical confidence.
2.  **Quality Validation:** Deep Engagement Ratio and Action Propensity remain **statistically neutral or positive** (no significant decrease). This confirms the extra clicks are high-quality.
3.  **No Ecosystem Harm:** Guardrail metrics show no statistically significant negative deviation.

**Implementation Note:**
Split traffic 50/50 between the old and new algorithm starting September 30th. Do not evaluate based on raw totals; compare the *rates* between the two groups daily to account for traffic volatility. If the primary metric rises but secondary quality metrics fall, the algorithm is generating "clickbait" relevance, not value.

*Define "Deep Engagement" thresholds (e.g., 30s) based on your current 75th percentile dwell time data before launch to ensure the bar is realistic.*
