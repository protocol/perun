# CloudVault Free-Tier Adjustment: Customer Success Monitoring Brief

**Objective:** Monitor user reaction to the storage cap reduction (10GB → 5GB) to distinguish between successful conversion drivers and harmful friction leading to churn.

## Critical User Actions to Monitor

Track these specific behaviors daily. A spike in any category compared to the previous 30-day baseline indicates a reaction to the change.

### 1. The "Wall" Reaction (Immediate Friction)
*Actions occurring within 24 hours of hitting the 5GB limit.*
*   **Failed Upload Attempts:** Users trying to upload files after reaching the cap without clicking "Upgrade." High volume here suggests confusion or immediate frustration rather than purchase intent.
*   **Support Ticket Volume:** Specifically tickets tagged "Storage Limit," "Cannot Upload," or "Account Full."
*   **Delete Spree:** Users deleting >20% of their stored data within one session immediately after hitting the limit. This indicates rejection of the paid value prop.

### 2. Conversion Signals (Positive Indicators)
*Actions suggesting the constraint is working as hypothesized.*
*   **Pricing Page Visits:** Traffic to the upgrade page originating directly from the "Storage Full" error modal.
*   **Trial Initiation:** Clicking "Start Paid Trial" or "Upgrade Now" within 5 minutes of a failed upload.
*   **Feature Exploration:** Viewing "Premium Features" or "Business Plans" documentation after hitting the limit.

### 3. Churn & Dissatisfaction Signals (Negative Indicators)
*Actions suggesting users are abandoning the platform entirely.*
*   **Data Export Requests:** Users downloading their entire dataset via the "Export All" function. This is the strongest predictor of imminent cancellation.
*   **Account Cancellation:** Immediate closure of accounts by users who previously had <8GB usage (now suddenly capped).
*   **Negative Sentiment Keywords:** In support chats or NPS responses, flag mentions of "misleading," "bait and switch," "too small," or competitor names (e.g., "moving to Dropbox").
*   **Session Drop-off:** Users logging in, seeing the limit warning, and closing the browser within 10 seconds without further interaction.

## Interpreting Early Warning Signs

Use these thresholds to escalate issues to Product Strategy immediately.

| Signal Pattern | Interpretation | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Delete Spree + Low Upgrade Clicks** | Users feel the 5GB limit is unusable for their needs and are shrinking their footprint to stay free, not upgrading. | **Alert:** The value gap is too wide. Users don't see enough value in paid to justify the hassle. |
| **Spike in "Export All" Requests** | Mass exodus risk. Users are voting with their feet and leaving for competitors. | **Critical:** Pause communication campaigns; prepare retention offers or reconsider the limit severity. |
| **High Support Volume on "Confusion"** | Users don't understand *why* they can't upload or *how* to fix it. The UI copy is failing. | **Tactical:** Update error messaging to be clearer about the path to resolution (upgrade vs. delete). |
| **High Upgrade Clicks + Low Completion** | Users are interested but balk at the price or the payment process. | **Optimization:** Review the checkout flow; the friction is in the transaction, not the storage limit. |
| **Stable Baseline + 15% Upgrade Lift** | **Success.** The constraint is pushing conversions without triggering mass churn. | **Maintain:** Continue current monitoring; no intervention needed. |

## Feedback Categorization Protocol

When logging tickets or calls, Customer Success must tag interactions with one of the following specific labels to ensure accurate data aggregation:

1.  **`LIMIT_SHOCK`**: User expresses surprise or anger at the new 5GB cap specifically.
2.  **`VALUE_GAP`**: User states the paid plan is "too expensive" for the extra storage provided.
3.  **`TECH_BLOCK`**: User is genuinely confused by the error message or unable to navigate the upgrade path.
4.  **`COMPETITOR_MENTION`**: User explicitly names a competitor as their alternative.
5.  **`ACCEPTED_CONSTRAINT`**: User acknowledges the limit and asks about upgrade options without negativity.

**Note:** Do not offer manual storage extensions unless authorized by the Crisis Protocol. Every exception skews the experiment data.
**Escalation:** If `LIMIT_SHOCK` or `COMPETITOR_MENTION` tags exceed 10% of total daily interactions, notify the Product Strategy lead immediately.
