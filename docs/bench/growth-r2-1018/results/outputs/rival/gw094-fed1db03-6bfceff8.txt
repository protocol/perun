**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Head of Sustainability, GreenCorp Solutions
**FROM:** Product Strategy Team
**DATE:** October 26, 2023
**SUBJECT:** Strategic Framework for Demonstrating ROI Amid Data Variability

**Executive Summary**
To secure enterprise adoption by February, we must pivot our value proposition from "absolute precision" to "actionable intelligence." Clients prioritize Scope 3 visibility, yet supplier data gaps are inevitable. Our strategy will demonstrate ROI by measuring *improvement in data maturity* alongside *emission reduction trends*, positioning GreenCorp as the engine for supply chain decarbonization rather than just a calculator.

**Core Metrics Framework**
We will report on three distinct metric categories to balance precision with usability:

**1. Data Integrity & Maturity Indicators (The Trust Layer)**
Before clients trust the reduction numbers, they must trust the input quality. We will track:
*   **Data Completeness Rate:** Percentage of spend-covered suppliers providing actual primary data vs. estimated spend-based data. Target: >60% primary data coverage for Tier 1 suppliers within 6 months.
*   **Verification Score:** A weighted score (0-100) based on data source type (e.g., third-party verified = 1.0, self-reported with evidence = 0.7, estimated = 0.3). This quantifies the "confidence interval" of the total footprint.
*   **Gap Closure Velocity:** The month-over-month rate at which "estimated" emissions are replaced by "actual" supplier data. This proves the platform actively improves accuracy over time.

**2. Actionable Reduction Metrics (The Value Layer)**
To address the fear of skewed accuracy, we will focus on directional trends and high-confidence reductions:
*   **High-Confidence Reduction Rate:** Emission reductions calculated *only* from suppliers with a Verification Score >0.7. This provides a conservative, defensible baseline for regulatory reporting.
*   **Intensity Normalization:** Emissions per unit of revenue or per product unit, rather than absolute totals. This controls for business growth and highlights efficiency gains even if total scope 3 numbers fluctuate due to data updates.
*   **Hotspot Identification Count:** Number of specific supply chain nodes identified as top 10% emitters where intervention is possible. Value is derived from *visibility*, not just the final number.

**3. Engagement & Influence Metrics (The ROI Layer)**
Since Scope 3 relies on supplier behavior, our tool's ROI is linked to client influence:
*   **Supplier Engagement Rate:** Percentage of invited suppliers who actively log into the portal or submit data.
*   **Data Request Response Time:** Average time for suppliers to respond to data queries, measuring the friction reduction our platform provides.

**Strategic Narrative for Prospective Customers**
We will frame data variability not as a defect, but as the primary problem GreenCorp solves. Our pitch will emphasize that "you cannot manage what you cannot measure." The initial baseline will be presented as a "maturity snapshot," with the primary ROI being the rapid transition from estimates to actuals.

We will explicitly state that early reduction figures are directional. By separating "High-Confidence Reductions" from "Total Estimated Footprint," we protect credibility while showcasing progress. The true product value is the *systematic improvement* of the data itself, enabling accurate Scope 3 reporting in future years.

**Recommendation**
Proceed with the February launch using this dual-track reporting structure. Marketing materials must highlight "Data Maturity" as a key deliverable. We will offer a "Confidence Dashboard" to enterprise clients, visually displaying the ratio of verified vs. estimated data, turning transparency into a competitive advantage.
