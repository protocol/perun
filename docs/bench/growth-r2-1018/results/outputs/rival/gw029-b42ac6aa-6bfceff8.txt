To differentiate between a messaging mismatch and technical friction causing your 75% bounce rate, gather the following specific data points immediately:

**1. Technical Performance Data (Friction Diagnosis)**
*   **Mobile vs. Desktop Bounce Rate Split:** Segment your analytics by device. If mobile bounce rates are significantly higher (e.g., >85%) than desktop (<60%), this strongly indicates a performance or layout issue specific to mobile devices.
*   **Page Load Time by Connection Speed:** Use tools like Google PageSpeed Insights or Chrome User Experience Report (CrUX) to measure "Largest Contentful Paint" (LCP) specifically for mobile 4G/3G connections. If LCP exceeds 2.5 seconds, users are likely abandoning before the page renders.
*   **Scroll Depth vs. Load Time Correlation:** Analyze if users on slower connections have zero scroll depth compared to those on faster connections. If slow loaders never scroll but fast loaders do, the cause is technical friction.

**2. Messaging Alignment Data (Relevance Diagnosis)**
*   **Traffic Source Segmentation:** Compare bounce rates between your email campaign traffic and other sources (e.g., direct, social, organic). If email traffic has a 75% bounce rate while organic search traffic bounces at 40%, the issue is likely a disconnect between the email promise and the landing page headline.
*   **Time-on-Page Distribution:** Check the distribution of time spent on the page.
    *   **< 5 seconds:** Indicates immediate rejection (likely messaging mismatch or extreme load failure).
    *   **5–15 seconds:** Suggests the user read the headline, found it irrelevant, and left (messaging mismatch).
    *   **> 15 seconds with no scroll:** Rare, but could indicate confusion or hidden content issues.
*   **Heatmap/Session Recording Analysis:** If available, review session recordings for mobile users. Look for "rage clicks" (repeated tapping) near the header or immediate back-button usage. Immediate exits without mouse movement or touch interaction often signal a load failure; reading behavior followed by an exit signals a content mismatch.

**3. Diagnostic Test (The "Smoking Gun")**
*   **A/A Test or Headline Swap:** If possible, temporarily change the landing page headline to exactly match the email subject line for 50% of email traffic. If the bounce rate drops significantly for this group, the issue is confirmed as a messaging mismatch.
*   **Asset Optimization Test:** Compress all images and scripts on a staging version of the page, then test load times on a real mobile device on a 4G network. If the page loads in under 2 seconds and the bounce rate hypothesis was technical, this confirms the friction point.
