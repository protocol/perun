# SwiftWing Oakhaven Trial: Measurement Plan

**Objective:** Determine operational viability and community acceptance for scaling to the next city based on a four-week trial (April 5 – May 2).

## 1. Core Efficiency Metrics (Daily Collection)
*Source: Automated Flight Logs*

| Metric | Definition | Success Threshold | Failure Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Median Delivery Time** | Time from hub departure to package drop-off. | ≤ 10 minutes | > 15 minutes |
| **Flight Completion Rate** | % of initiated flights completing delivery. | ≥ 95% | < 85% |
| **Weather Abort Rate** | % of flights aborted specifically due to wind/precip. | ≤ 5% | > 15% |
| **Van Speed Delta** | (Avg Van Time) – (Avg Drone Time). | > 8 mins faster | < 4 mins faster |

*Note: Use median instead of average for delivery time to prevent outliers (e.g., a single 45-minute delay) from skewing the perception of typical performance.*

## 2. Community Impact Indicators (Observable Behaviors)
*Source: Operational Logs & Public Records (No Surveys)*

To quantify "community acceptance" without biasing results via surveys, track these passive, observable signals:

| Indicator | Data Source | Success Threshold | Failure Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Noise Complaint Volume** | Count of calls/logs to city council or SwiftWing support citing "drone noise." | ≤ 2 per week | > 5 per week |
| **Opt-Out Rate** | % of eligible addresses in Oakhaven requesting removal from the service map. | < 1% of total addresses | > 5% of total addresses |
| **Delivery Refusal Rate** | % of customers who cancel or refuse delivery upon drone arrival (via app log). | < 2% | > 10% |
| **Repeat Usage Rate** | % of customers ordering a second time within 14 days. | > 40% | < 20% |

*Rationale: High opt-outs or refusals indicate active rejection. Low repeat usage suggests dissatisfaction despite initial curiosity. Noise complaints are a direct proxy for neighborhood disruption.*

## 3. Daily Reporting Cadence
The operations team must compile a daily dashboard by 9:00 AM the following day containing:
1.  **Volume:** Total flights attempted vs. completed.
2.  **Efficiency:** Median delivery time and Weather Abort Rate.
3.  **Community:** Cumulative noise complaints and new opt-outs.
4.  **Incidents:** Any safety near-misses or forced landings.

## 4. Go/No-Go Decision Framework
At the conclusion of the four-week trial, apply the following criteria to decide on scaling:

**GO (Scale to Next City):**
*   Median delivery time is ≤ 10 minutes.
*   Flight completion rate is ≥ 95%.
*   Noise complaints average ≤ 2 per week.
*   Repeat usage rate is > 40%.

**NO-GO (Pause & Iterate):**
*   Weather abort rate exceeds 15% (indicates route/climate mismatch).
*   Noise complaints exceed 5 per week (indicates regulatory/reputation risk).
*   Opt-out rate exceeds 5% (indicates fundamental community rejection).

**CONDITIONAL GO (Scale with Restrictions):**
*   Metrics fall between Success and Failure thresholds.
*   *Action:* Scale only if specific constraints are applied (e.g., no flights during high-wind forecasts, restricted flight paths over residential zones).

This plan relies entirely on automated logs and public records, ensuring data integrity without influencing customer behavior through intrusive feedback requests.

*Ensure weather data is correlated timestamp-by-timestamp with flight logs to accurately attribute aborts to wind speeds rather than technical faults.*
