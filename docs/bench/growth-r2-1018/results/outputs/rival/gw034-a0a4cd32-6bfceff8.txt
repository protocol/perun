### DevFlow Enterprise Tier: Success Metrics & Dashboard Draft

**Primary Metric (North Star): Value-Adjusted Compute Units (VACU)**
To resolve the skew between high-frequency/low-volume and low-frequency/high-volume users, replace raw call counts with a weighted unit.
*   **Formula:** `(Successful API Calls × Base Weight) + (Data Volume Processed in GB × Volume Multiplier) + (Complexity Score × Job Weight)`
*   **Why:** This normalizes value. A client making 10 calls that migrate 5TB of data registers higher health than a client making 10,000 calls moving test data, aligning the metric with actual business utility rather than just technical activity.

**Secondary Metrics (The Balanced Scorecard)**

| Category | Metric | Definition & Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Business Outcome** | **Engineering Hours Saved** | Estimated time saved per migration job vs. manual scripting. *Goal: Demonstrate direct ROI to the VP of Sales.* |
| **Reliability** | **Critical Job Success Rate** | Percentage of jobs completing without error or rollback. *Goal: Track stability for high-stakes enterprise data.* |
| **Adoption** | **Active Team Depth** | Number of unique developers within an account calling endpoints weekly. *Goal: Prevent churn by ensuring broad internal adoption, not just single-user reliance.* |
| **Efficiency** | **Average Time-to-Migrate** | Time elapsed from job initiation to completion. *Goal: Prove speed advantage over manual processes.* |

**Dashboard Draft for Prospective Clients**
*   **Header:** "Your Estimated ROI with DevFlow"
*   **Main Visual:** Cumulative "Engineering Hours Saved" trend line (calculated via the VACU metric converted to time).
*   **Supporting Stats:**
    *   Total Data Processed (TB)
    *   Critical Job Success Rate (%)
    *   Average Time Saved per Migration (Hours)
*   **Footer:** "Based on pilot data from [Month]. Custom benchmarks available upon signup."

**Questions for Pilot Customers**
To refine the weights in your VACU formula and validate the "Hours Saved" claim, ask these three specific questions:
1.  "For your last major migration, how many engineering hours would this have consumed if done manually without DevFlow?" (Validates the baseline for ROI).
2.  "Do you consider a job 'successful' only upon 100% data integrity verification, or upon completion of the transfer?" (Defines the numerator for Critical Job Success Rate).
3.  "What specific data volume threshold triggers a 'high-risk' manual review process for your team?" (Helps set the Complexity Score multiplier).

**Implementation Note:** Do not report raw uptime percentage as a primary metric; 99.9% uptime means little if the 0.1% downtime deletes critical data. Focus on *job-level* success.
**Data Warning:** Ensure your "Engineering Hours Saved" calculation uses conservative estimates provided by the customer, not internal assumptions, to maintain credibility with enterprise prospects.
