# Experiment Summary: Free Shipping Threshold Test ($50 vs. $75)
**Prepared for:** VP of Operations & Regional Marketing Leads
**Date:** March 16, 2024
**Test Period:** March 1 – March 15

## Executive Recommendation
**Do not proceed with a standardized national rollout at this time.** The data indicates that a uniform $75 threshold would likely result in a net revenue loss in the Midwest while leaving money on the table in the Pacific Northwest (PNW). We recommend an immediate **regionalized strategy**: maintain the $50 threshold in the Midwest and adopt the $75 threshold in the PNW. The South requires further analysis before a decision can be made.

## Regional Performance Breakdown

The test revealed significant heterogeneity in customer price sensitivity and basket composition across regions, directly contradicting the baseline assumption of a uniform 12% AOV lift.

### 1. Midwest: Negative Impact
*   **Result:** Conversion Rate (CVR) ↓ 8% | Average Order Value (AOV) ↑ 4%
*   **Analysis:** The 8% drop in conversion significantly outweighs the marginal 4% gain in order value. Mathematically, this results in a net revenue decline. Customers in this region appear highly sensitive to shipping costs, and the $75 bar is too high relative to their typical basket size.
*   **Action:** **Reject $75 threshold.** Revert to $50 immediately to protect volume. Investigate if lower average income or higher competitor density is driving this sensitivity.

### 2. Pacific Northwest: Positive Impact
*   **Result:** Conversion Rate (CVR) Flat | Average Order Value (AOV) ↑ 18%
*   **Analysis:** This is an ideal outcome. Customers absorbed the higher threshold without friction, driving a substantial increase in basket size (exceeding the 12% target). This suggests either higher disposable income, a product mix that naturally bundles to higher values, or a brand perception that justifies the shipping cost.
*   **Action:** **Adopt $75 threshold.** Implement immediately to capture incremental margin.

### 3. The South: Data Gap
*   **Status:** Results not provided in the initial brief.
*   **Action:** Urgent analysis required before Tuesday's meeting. If data mirrors the Midwest, a regional approach is confirmed. If it mirrors the PNW, we may have two distinct "zones" rather than three unique strategies.

## Strategic Implications for National Rollout

The conflicting signals suggest that **customer economics vary more by region than by the shipping policy itself.** A "one-size-fits-all" threshold ignores local purchasing power and logistical realities.

*   **Financial Risk:** Standardizing at $75 risks an immediate revenue contraction in the Midwest.
*   **Opportunity Cost:** Standardizing at $50 leaves ~18% potential AOV growth untapped in the PNW.
*   **Operational Complexity:** While managing regional thresholds adds minor complexity to the marketing logic, the revenue protection and upside far outweigh the implementation cost.
