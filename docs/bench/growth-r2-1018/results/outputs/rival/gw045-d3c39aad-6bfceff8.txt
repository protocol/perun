**Recommended Primary Metric: The "Sustained Match Rate"**

For your annual report, the single most critical metric to demonstrate value is the **percentage of onboarded students who achieve a "Sustained Match" within 60 days of joining.**

Based on your definition, a "Sustained Match" occurs when a student and tutor complete three sessions together. You should calculate this as:
*(Number of students completing 3+ sessions with the same tutor) ÷ (Total students onboarded in the period)*.

**Why This Metric Tells the Right Story**

Donors funding educational initiatives do not care about "activity"; they care about "outcomes." Simple registration counts (300 students) are vanity metrics that prove interest but not impact. A student who signs up but never attends a session has generated zero educational value. Similarly, total hours taught can be skewed by a few power users while masking the fact that most students are disengaged.

The Sustained Match Rate is superior because it validates the core value proposition of your marketplace: **connection leading to continuity.**

1.  **It Proves Quality, Not Just Quantity:** Reaching three sessions implies that the initial introduction was successful, the scheduling worked, and—most importantly—the student found enough value to return twice. In educational contexts, retention is the strongest proxy for learning progress.
2.  **It Balances Both Sides:** This metric cannot be high unless both supply (tutors showing up) and demand (students attending) are functioning. If you have many tutors but low matches, the metric exposes a supply-side friction. If you have many students but low matches, it highlights a demand-side engagement issue.
3.  **It Predicts Long-Term Impact:** Educational research consistently shows that consistent tutoring relationships yield better results than sporadic ones. By defining success at the "three-session" mark, you are measuring the formation of a habit, which is a leading indicator of the academic improvement you promise donors.

**Feasible Implementation Plan (No Engineer Required)**

Since you lack sophisticated tracking tools, do not attempt to automate this. With only 300 students, a manual audit is more accurate and manageable.

1.  **Data Collection:** Export your session logs (or gather tutor submission forms) into a simple spreadsheet.
2.  **The Formula:** Create a column for each student counting their completed sessions per tutor. Use a basic formula to flag any student with a count ≥ 3.
3.  **The Narrative:** In your June report, present the data as a funnel:
    *   **Top of Funnel:** 300 Students Onboarded (Reach)
    *   **Middle of Funnel:** X Students Completed 1 Session (Initial Engagement)
    *   **Bottom of Funnel:** Y Students Achieved Sustained Match (Real Impact)

Highlight the conversion rate from Top to Bottom. If 300 students joined and 90 achieved a sustained match, your headline is: *"30% of enrolled students established consistent tutoring relationships, moving beyond access to actual educational continuity."*

This approach transforms your data from a list of names into evidence of a working ecosystem. It shows donors that your platform doesn't just introduce people; it facilitates lasting educational change.

*Note: For the June report, also include a brief qualitative quote from one "Sustained Match" pair to humanize the statistic.*
*Avoid tracking "returning for a second term" as your primary metric now, as your March 1st relaunch is too recent for most students to have completed a full term.*
