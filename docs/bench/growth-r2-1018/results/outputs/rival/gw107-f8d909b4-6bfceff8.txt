**Recommended Primary Metric: "Valid Express Bookings per Active User"**

Do not optimize for raw speed (Time-to-Book) or raw volume (Total Bookings). Your data proves that maximizing speed degrades quality, evidenced by the cancellation rate jumping from 5% to 12%. Conversely, optimizing solely for low cancellations might reintroduce friction that kills the "express" value proposition.

Your primary goal must be a **compound metric** that only counts a conversion when it is both fast *and* sticks. Define a "Valid Express Booking" as a booking completed via the express flow that is **not cancelled within 24 hours**.

**Why this metric works for your Regional Director:**
1.  **Aligns with User Intent:** It respects the 60% of users who want speed but filters out the "regret bookings" that occur when speed overrides decision quality.
2.  **Solves the Diagnosis Gap:** You currently do not know if cancellations are due to wrong time slots or feeling rushed. This metric forces the team to solve for *both* possibilities simultaneously. If users are picking wrong slots, the UI must clarify availability without adding clicks. If they feel rushed, the flow must provide reassurance (e.g., "Free cancellation within 1 hour") without adding friction.
3.  **Drives Sustainable Growth:** It prevents the team from "gaming" the OKR by making the button bigger (which increases cancellations) or adding unnecessary confirmation steps (which increases drop-off). It demands a flow that is genuinely efficient and accurate.

**Implementation Strategy for H2 OKRs:**
*   **Primary Objective:** Increase "Valid Express Bookings per Active User" by X%.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Maintain average Time-to-Book under Y seconds (ensure you don't solve cancellations by slowing the flow to a crawl).
*   **Diagnostic Secondary Metric:** Track "Cancellation Reason" (if available) or correlate cancellation rates with specific time-slot selections to answer your unknown variable about *why* users are cancelling.

By shifting the goalpost from "Booking Confirmed" to "Booking Confirmed & Retained," you align the engineering and product teams around the actual business outcome: successful service delivery, not just transactional velocity.

*Note: Ensure your analytics pipeline filters out cancellations in real-time dashboards so the team sees the "net" impact of changes immediately.*
