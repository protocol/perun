Based on the data provided, here is the actionable retention strategy for the FreshBox customer success team.

### **Trigger Timing: The "First Skip" Window**
Do not wait for the second skip. The critical intervention window opens **immediately after the first skip is confirmed**.
*   **Action:** Trigger the first retention email automatically 24 hours after a user skips a delivery week.
*   **Rationale:** Since two consecutive skips yield an 85% churn probability, the first skip is the earliest reliable warning sign available in your current logs. Intervening here targets the user before the habit of skipping becomes entrenched.

### **Content Strategy: Addressing the Unknowns**
Since your logs cannot yet distinguish between menu variety, delivery timing, or price sensitivity, your email sequence must use **diagnostic questioning** to identify the driver while offering immediate value. Do not guess the reason; ask the user to self-segment.

**Email 1 (Day 1 after skip): The "Check-In" & Diagnostic**
*   **Goal:** Re-engage and identify the friction point.
*   **Content:** Acknowledge the skip gently. Offer three distinct click-options (buttons) to uncover the root cause:
    1.  *"Not feeling this week's menu?"* (Tests Menu Variety)
    2.  *"Need a different delivery day?"* (Tests Delivery Timing)
    3.  *"Looking for a smaller plan?"* (Tests Price Sensitivity)
*   **Call to Action:** "Tell us what's up, and we'll send a personalized offer."

**Email 2 (Day 3 after skip): The Targeted Incentive**
*   **Goal:** Convert based on the diagnostic click from Email 1.
*   **Content:** Dynamic content based on their previous click.
    *   If they clicked **Menu**: Highlight upcoming popular recipes or a "chef's choice" preview.
    *   If they clicked **Delivery**: Offer a one-time free delivery reschedule or flexibility guarantee.
    *   If they clicked **Price**: Offer a 15% discount on the next box or a temporary pause option instead of cancellation.
*   **Fallback:** If they didn't click Email 1, send a general "We miss you" with a broad incentive (e.g., free dessert with next order).

**Email 3 (Day 6 after skip): The "Save" Offer**
*   **Goal:** Last resort before the second skip occurs.
*   **Content:** A high-value "win-back" offer contingent on un-skipping the current or next week.
*   **Message:** "Don't lose your spot. Reactivate now and get [High Value Offer] off your next two boxes."

### **Immediate Data Collection Recommendation**
To move beyond assumptions in future cycles, you must tag the click-through rates on the diagnostic buttons in Email 1.
*   **If "Menu" clicks are highest:** Prioritize menu variety interventions.
*   **If "Delivery" clicks are highest:** Investigate logistics constraints in Copenhagen.
*   **If "Price" clicks are highest:** Review pricing tiers against competitors.

This approach turns your current data limitation into a data-gathering opportunity while attempting to save the 50% of at-risk users you identified.
