**Recommendation: Adopt "Number of Completed Lesson Plans" as your primary success indicator.**

For your August 30th impact report to donors, "hours of connection time" is a vanity metric that fails to prove educational value. It measures internet availability and presence, not learning. A student can remain logged in for hours while distracted, doing homework unrelated to the session, or simply keeping the connection open to save data. There is no logical guarantee that passive time translates to skill acquisition.

In contrast, a "Completed Lesson Plan" represents a structured educational unit with a defined beginning, middle, and end. It implies that specific learning objectives were addressed, materials were utilized, and a pedagogical sequence was followed. This metric aligns directly with your goal of improving exam scores because it measures the *dosage* of actual instruction rather than the *duration* of connectivity.

This shows donors you are measuring what actually happens during the session, not just whether the internet worked.

This is the logical link donors need to see to justify funding renewal: you are measuring the input that directly causes the output, rather than a correlated side effect.
