# GreenLoop Q2 Metric Framework: The "Dual-North Star" Approach

To satisfy both the CFO's need for financial viability and the Sustainability Board's need for environmental proof without diluting focus, GreenLoop should adopt a **Primary North Star** supported by a **Guardrail Metric**. Do not split your primary focus between two equal metrics; this creates conflicting incentives for product and marketing teams.

### 1. Primary North Star: "Active Utilization Rate"
**Definition:** The percentage of active subscribers who rent ≥2 items in their first 30 days.
**Why this wins:**
*   **Financial Viability:** Your data explicitly links renting ≥2 items to 6-month retention. Retention is the primary driver of LTV (Lifetime Value) for a subscription model at 499 DKK/month. High utilization reduces churn, directly securing revenue.
*   **Environmental Impact:** You cannot save carbon if garments sit idle. A rental model's environmental benefit is mathematically dependent on *displacement* (one rented item replacing one purchased item). Higher utilization per user equals higher carbon displacement per user.
*   **Alignment:** This single metric forces the team to optimize for both revenue stability and mission fulfillment simultaneously. If this number goes up, both the CFO and the Board win.

### 2. Secondary Guardrail: "Carbon Efficiency Per Transaction"
**Definition:** Estimated CO2e saved per rental transaction (calculated as: *Avg. CO2e of new garment production* minus *CO2e of cleaning/logistics per rental*).
**Role:** This is not a growth target but a quality check.
**Why it matters:** If you drive "Active Utilization" too hard (e.g., by encouraging rapid, low-value rentals or inefficient logistics), you might increase churn or actually increase your carbon footprint per item due to excessive cleaning/transport. This metric ensures that as you scale volume, you do not degrade the environmental integrity of each transaction.

### 3. Reporting Strategy for March Investor Report
Do not present these as competing goals. Structure your report to show the causal link:

*   **Headline Metric:** "Active Utilization Rate" (e.g., "45% of new users rent 2+ items").
*   **The Bridge:** Explicitly state: "Our data shows that hitting this utilization target drives 6-month retention (Financial) and maximizes garment displacement (Environmental)."
*   **Supporting Evidence:** Present "Total Carbon Saved" as an *outcome* of the Primary Metric, not a separate goal. Show that as Utilization rises, Total Carbon Saved rises linearly.

### Implementation for Q2
*   **Product Team:** Optimize onboarding flows to encourage the second rental within days of signup.
*   **Marketing Team:** Target acquisition channels that yield users likely to rent frequently, not just sign up.
*   **Operations Team:** Focus on logistics efficiency to keep the "Carbon Efficiency" guardrail healthy while volume grows.

By anchoring on **Active Utilization**, you prove to investors that GreenLoop's business model (retention) and mission (sustainability) are not a trade-off, but the same engine.

*Note: Ensure your carbon calculation methodology is audited or based on industry standards (e.g., Higg Index) before presenting to the board to maintain credibility.*
*Recommendation: Begin tracking the "Active Utilization Rate" retrospectively on your Jan 2023–Present data to establish a baseline before setting Q2 targets.*
