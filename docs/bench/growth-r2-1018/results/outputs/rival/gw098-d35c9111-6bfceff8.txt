# StorMix Automation Efficiency & Cost Impact Framework

## Core Metrics Definition

**1. Automation Trust Rate (ATR)**
*   **Formula:** `(Total Automated Moves - Manual Overrides) / Total Automated Moves`
*   **Purpose:** Quantifies confidence in the algorithm. A low ATR indicates users are fighting the system.
*   **Target:** >85% within 90 days of launch.

**2. Override Cost Delta (OCD)**
*   **Formula:** `Actual Customer Cost - Hypothetical Cost (if 100% Automated)`
*   **Purpose:** Directly measures the financial impact of manual intervention.
    *   *Positive Delta:* Customer is paying more by overriding (validates churn risk).
    *   *Negative/Zero Delta:* Customer optimization is valid or neutral (validates "unpredictable patterns" hypothesis).
*   **Action Threshold:** If OCD > 15% for a segment, trigger proactive success outreach.

**3. Data Temperature Accuracy**
*   **Formula:** `1 - (Percentage of "Cold" Data Accessed within 24h of Auto-Archive)`
*   **Purpose:** Validates if the algorithm correctly identifies access frequency. High error rates here justify manual overrides.

**4. Churn Correlation Coefficient**
*   **Measurement:** Correlation between high OCD scores and subscription cancellation within the first 6 months.
*   **Goal:** Prove or disprove the hypothesis that overrides drive churn.

## Essential Segmentation

To distinguish between "lack of trust" and "unpredictable patterns," data must be sliced by:

*   **Industry Vertical:** Media companies (bursty access) vs. Financial Services (steady access). Unpredictability is likely vertical-specific.
*   **Override Direction:**
    *   *Force Hot:* Moving cold data to fast storage (Potential cost risk).
    *   *Force Cold:* Keeping hot data in cheap storage (Performance risk).
*   **Tenant Maturity:** New accounts (<30 days) vs. Established accounts. Early overrides often indicate onboarding confusion rather than algorithm failure.
*   **Data Volume Tier:** Small vs. Enterprise tenants. Larger tenants may have dedicated IT teams optimizing manually, whereas smaller tenants rely on automation.

**Slide 3: Segment-Specific Hypotheses**

*   **Headline:** How we will demonstrate value to early adopters in Mumbai/Pune.
*   **Strategy:** Monthly "Efficiency Reports" sent to customers showing:
    *   Money saved by automation.
    *   Cost incurred by their specific overrides.
    *   Recommendation: "Let StorMix handle X% more to save ₹Y."
*   **Visual:** Mockup of the customer-facing efficiency dashboard.

**Slide 5: Implementation Roadmap (Next 90 Days)**
*   **Phase 1 (Launch):** Instrument tracking for every rule change and data move.
