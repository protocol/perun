### Diagnostic Metrics Plan: Pro Plus Launch Impact

To isolate whether the drop from 4.2% to 2.8% is caused by **choice paralysis** (comparison stage) or **value/pricing friction** (payment stage), track these specific metrics segmented by user cohort (Pre-Oct 15 vs. Post-Oct 15) and device type.

#### 1. Funnel Drop-off Analysis (The "Where")
Break down the conversion funnel into three distinct steps to pinpoint the exact exit point:
*   **Step A: Pricing Page View → Plan Selection Click**
    *   *Hypothesis:* If drop-off here increased significantly post-launch, users are experiencing **choice paralysis**. The $5 difference is causing hesitation, leading to analysis paralysis where they select neither and abandon the session.
    *   *Metric to isolate:* **Plan Selection Rate**. Compare the % of pricing page visitors who click *any* "Upgrade" button. If this dropped from ~X% to ~Y%, the pricing table layout or tier confusion is the primary blocker.
*   **Step B: Plan Selection → Payment Info Entry**
    *   *Hypothesis:* If users select a plan but drop off before entering details, the friction is **specific to the selected tier's perceived value** or unexpected costs (e.g., tax calculation, lack of preferred payment method).
    *   *Metric to isolate:* **Checkout Initiation Rate**. Segment this by which plan was clicked (Pro vs. Pro Plus). If Pro Plus has a high selection rate but low checkout initiation, the features aren't resonating enough to justify the immediate action.
*   **Step C: Payment Info Entry → Successful Transaction**
    *   *Hypothesis:* If drop-off here increased, the issue is **transactional friction** (card declines, form errors) rather than pricing strategy.
    *   *Metric to isolate:* **Payment Completion Rate**. If this remains stable pre/post-launch, the problem is upstream (decision-making), not technical.

#### 2. Tier Distribution & Cannibalization (The "What")
Determine if the new tier is stealing share or expanding the market.
*   **Plan Mix Ratio:** Calculate the percentage of total upgrades going to "Pro" vs. "Pro Plus" post-launch.
    *   *Signal:* If "Pro" upgrades dropped by more than the total conversion rate decline (e.g., Pro dropped 2.0% while total only dropped 1.4%), **cannibalization** is confirmed. Users who would have bought Pro are now hesitating or leaving.
*   **"No Plan Selected" Exit Rate:** Track users who view the pricing page but leave without clicking a specific tier.
    *   *Signal:* A spike in this metric confirms the **$5 confusion hypothesis**. Users cannot justify the marginal cost difference and choose to defer the decision.

#### 3. Behavioral Context (The "Why")
*   **Time on Pricing Page:** Compare average duration.
    *   *Signal:* A significant increase suggests users are struggling to compare features (confusion). A decrease suggests immediate rejection (price shock or lack of interest).
*   **Feature Tooltip/Modal Interactions:** Track clicks on "What's included" or comparison tooltips.
    *   *Signal:* High interaction followed by drop-off indicates users *want* to understand the difference but aren't finding a compelling reason to choose the higher tier.

#### Recommended Immediate Actions for VP Decision
1.  **If Step A (Selection) is the bottleneck:** Simplify the pricing table. Highlight a "Recommended" tier to reduce cognitive load, or temporarily increase the price gap to clarify value differentiation.
2.  **If Step B (Checkout) is the bottleneck for Pro Plus:** The features are not selling the $5 premium. Consider bundling a high-value feature exclusively to Pro Plus or reducing the price gap to $3 to make the upsell a "no-brainer."
3.  **If Cannibalization is >80%:** The new tier is merely splitting existing revenue. Sunset the old tier and migrate users to a single "Pro" structure with add-ons, or reposition Pro Plus as the only option for new users.

**Note:** Ensure all metrics exclude internal traffic and test accounts. Do not aggregate "Pro" and "Pro Plus" data in the initial view; the divergence between them holds the answer.
