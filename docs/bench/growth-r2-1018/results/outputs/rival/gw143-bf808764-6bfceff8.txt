### Recommended Funnel Stages for SafeHome Quotes Onboarding

To diagnose the drop-off between address entry and coverage selection, you must split your current "Quote Start to Quote Complete" phase into three distinct intent-based stages. Defining these by user action rather than page URL will allow your UX research team to trigger session recordings precisely where friction occurs.

**1. Intent Verification (Address Entry)**
*   **Trigger:** User submits a valid address and the system confirms serviceability.
*   **User Intent:** "I am checking if you can insure *my* specific home."
*   **Why split here:** This validates your assumption that early address entry correlates with completion. If users drop off immediately after this, the issue is likely a lack of immediate value confirmation or a technical delay in generating the property report.
*   **Recording Trigger:** Fire on successful address validation API response.

**2. Risk Assessment & Education (The "Black Box" Phase)**
*   **Trigger:** User views the property summary/risk factors but has *not* yet interacted with coverage sliders or inputs.
*   **User Intent:** "I am evaluating the risk profile you found and deciding if I trust your data before committing to specific numbers."
*   **Why split here:** This is your likely friction point. Users often abandon here if the property data looks incorrect, the risk factors seem too high, or the interface feels overwhelming before they've had a chance to customize. Treating this as a separate step isolates whether the drop-off is due to *data accuracy* or *UI complexity*.
*   **Recording Trigger:** Fire when the property summary screen loads; stop tracking this specific segment once the first coverage input is touched.

**3. Commitment (Coverage Selection)**
*   **Trigger:** User adjusts at least one coverage limit or deductible slider/input.
*   **User Intent:** "I am actively building my policy and accepting financial responsibility."
*   **Why split here:** Once a user touches a slider, they have moved from evaluation to construction. Drop-offs after this point are usually related to price shock or form fatigue, not trust in the data.
*   **Recording Trigger:** Fire on the first `change` event on any coverage input field.

**Implementation Note for UX Research:**
With 20,000 monthly visitors, you have sufficient volume to analyze these micro-conversions statistically. Configure your session recording tool to capture 100% of sessions that trigger Stage 2 but fail to reach Stage 3 within 5 minutes. This specific cohort represents your "considered abandonments" and will yield the highest ROI for qualitative review.

**Next Step:**
Align your analytics event naming to match these three intent states (e.g., `address_verified`, `property_reviewed`, `coverage_adjusted`) to ensure your quantitative funnel matches your qualitative recording triggers.
