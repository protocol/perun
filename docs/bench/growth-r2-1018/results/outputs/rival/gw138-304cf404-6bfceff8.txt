# CapitalFlow Metric Framework: LatAm Launch (Mexico & Colombia)

**Audience:** Chief Risk Officer, Regional General Manager
**Context:** Pre-launch monitoring setup for July 15th soft launch
**Primary Goal:** Balance speed-to-funding (growth) with portfolio quality (risk) in data-scarce environments.

## 1. North Star Metric: Risk-Adjusted Funding Velocity
**Definition:** The total value of invoices funded within the target SLA (<4 hours) that remain current after 30 days.
**Rationale:** Prioritizing raw volume or average invoice size alone invites adverse selection. In markets with incomplete credit bureau data, speed without quality control leads to rapid default. This composite metric ensures we only count "good" fast funding toward our primary success indicator.

## 2. Metric Hierarchy

### Tier 1: Customer Experience & Growth (The "Speed" Assumption)
*Validates the core hypothesis that speed drives retention.*

| Metric | Definition | Target | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Median Time-to-Cash** | Time from invoice upload to funds disbursed. | < 4 hours | Product |
| **Repeat Funding Rate** | % of SMBs submitting a 2nd invoice within 45 days. | +50% vs. baseline | Growth |
| **Funnel Drop-off Rate** | % of users abandoning at "Document Upload" or "Verification." | < 15% | Product |

### Tier 2: Risk & Portfolio Quality (The "Prudence" Guardrails)
*Addresses incomplete credit data by relying on behavioral and transactional signals.*

| Metric | Definition | Target | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Delinquency Rate (EDR)** | % of funded invoices overdue by >5 days within the first 30 days. | < 2% | Risk |
| **Concentration Risk Ratio** | % of total exposure held by top 10 debtors or single industry vertical. | < 20% | Risk |
| **Manual Review Trigger Rate** | % of applications flagged for human review due to data gaps. | < 25% | Ops/Risk |

### Tier 3: Operational Efficiency
*Ensures the model is scalable in the region.*

| Metric | Definition | Target | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost Per Funded Invoice** | Total ops + tech cost / # of funded invoices. | Decreasing MoM | Finance |
| **Data Enrichment Success** | % of applications where alternative data (bank statements, tax filings) successfully filled credit bureau gaps. | > 80% | Data |

## 3. Regional Risk Adaptation Strategy
Given incomplete credit bureau data in Mexico and Colombia, the framework prioritizes **alternative data validation** over traditional scoring:

1.  **Bank Statement Analysis:** Weight real-time bank transaction data (via open banking APIs where available, or uploaded statements) higher than static credit scores for initial limits.
2.  **Debtor Verification:** Shift risk assessment focus from the SMB borrower to the creditworthiness of their debtor (the entity paying the invoice), which is often more reliable in LatAm B2B chains.
3.  **Dynamic Limits:** Start all new SMBs with a low "probationary" limit. Increase limits automatically only after two successful cycles of on-time repayment, rather than upfront underwriting.

## 4. Implementation Priority
Before July 15th, engineering must build real-time alerts for:
*   **Time-to-Cash breaches:** Any transaction exceeding 6 hours.
*   **EDR Spikes:** If 30-day delinquency exceeds 3% in any rolling 7-day window.
*   **Concentration Breaches:** Immediate halt to funding if single-debtor exposure exceeds 15%.

This framework rejects "volume at all costs" in favor of sustainable, verified velocity, ensuring CapitalFlow scales safely in complex LatAm credit environments.

*Note: All time-based metrics must be calculated using local business hours (Mexico City/Bogotá) to avoid skewing SLA data.*
*Recommendation: Review Tier 2 thresholds weekly for the first 90 days post-launch to calibrate for local market realities.*
