### Success Metrics Framework: FinSecure Compliance Module Pilot

**Recommendation:** Do not use Daily Active Users (DAU). For enterprise compliance workflows with weekly cadences, DAU is a vanity metric that will artificially signal failure. Instead, measure **Weekly Active Users (WAU)** and prioritize **Task Completion Efficiency** over simple frequency.

#### 1. Primary Success Metric: Time-to-Report (Efficiency)
Since speed is your hypothesized critical factor, you must measure the delta between the old manual process and your module.
*   **Metric:** Average Time-to-Generate Report.
*   **Action:** Immediately conduct qualitative baseline interviews with the three pilot clients to estimate current manual hours. Without this baseline, you cannot calculate ROI.
*   **Target:** Define success as a **>50% reduction** in time spent compared to the estimated manual baseline.
*   **Why:** German leadership requires concrete ROI. Saving 10 hours per week per compliance officer translates directly to cost savings, justifying the €1.2M pilot value.

#### 2. Adoption Metric: Weekly Task Completion Rate
Replace "frequency of exported reports" with a binary success indicator per user per week.
*   **Metric:** % of Licensed Users who successfully generate at least one compliant report per week.
*   **Target:** **>80%** of licensed seats active weekly by Month 3 of the pilot.
*   **Why:** In B2B banking, one user exporting one critical report is a success; five users logging in and failing is a failure. This accounts for the weekly workflow cadence without penalizing low daily frequency.

#### 3. Quality/Guardrail Metric: Export Error Rate
Speed is irrelevant if the report fails audit standards.
*   **Metric:** Percentage of generated reports requiring manual correction or re-export.
*   **Target:** **<2%** error rate.
*   **Why:** In the DACH regulatory environment, a fast but non-compliant report is a liability, not an asset.

#### Pilot Evaluation Timeline (4 Months)
*   **Month 1 (Baseline & Onboarding):** Establish manual time estimates. Measure "Time-to-First-Report" for each client.
*   **Month 2 (Stabilization):** Track Weekly Task Completion Rate. Target 60% adoption.
*   **Month 3 (Optimization):** Measure average Time-to-Report. Target >40% efficiency gain vs. baseline.
*   **Month 4 (Validation):** Finalize metrics. Success = >80% weekly adoption + >50% time savings + <2% error rate.

#### Presentation Strategy for Leadership
Present the data as **Efficiency Gains (Hours Saved)** rather than raw software usage.
*   *Bad:* "We had 150 logins last week."
*   *Good:* "The module saved 120 compliance hours across three banks this week, reducing report generation time from 4 hours to 45 minutes."

This approach aligns with the specific constraints of German enterprise banking: low frequency, high stakes, and a strict requirement for measurable operational efficiency.

*Note: You must interview the pilot clients immediately to get the manual baseline estimates; without them, the efficiency metric cannot be calculated.*
*Do not wait for automated data to populate; the sample size (3 banks) requires qualitative validation to support the quantitative claims.*
