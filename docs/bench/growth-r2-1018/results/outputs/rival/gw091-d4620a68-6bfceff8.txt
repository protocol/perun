### Offline Conversion Tracking Pilot: La Tavola

**Strategy: Dynamic Number Insertion (DNI) with Session Binding**

To attribute offline calls to online sources without intrusive questioning, implement a DNI solution (e.g., CallRail, Invoca, or Twilio with custom logic) that swaps the visible phone number on your website based on the visitor's traffic source.

1.  **Number Pool Allocation:** Purchase a pool of tracking numbers distinct from your main line. Assign specific numbers or logic rules for your three main advertising channels (e.g., Google Ads, Meta, Organic/Direct).
2.  **Session Binding:** When a user lands on your site, the script identifies their UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign). It dynamically displays a specific tracking number associated with that session.
3.  **Data Capture:** When the call occurs, the provider logs the incoming number used, the timestamp, call duration, and the associated session data (UTMs, landing page, device).
4.  **Integration:** Push this call data back into your analytics platform (GA4) and ad platforms as "offline conversions" using the `gclid` or `fbclid` captured during the session. This allows you to see calls alongside online bookings in your dashboard.

**Validation Plan (Before Nov 15):**
*   **Channel Isolation:** Run low-budget test campaigns with unique UTMs for each of the three channels. Verify that the number displayed changes correctly for each source.
*   **Mobile vs. Desktop:** Test specifically on mobile devices, as DNI can sometimes fail if the user copies the number and calls later from a different device. Acknowledge this attribution gap in your reporting.
*   **Latency Check:** Ensure data flows from the call provider to your dashboard within 24 hours to maintain reporting freshness.

**Q1 Metrics for Regional Operations Director**

Report these four specific metrics to replace the current incomplete conversion rate:

1.  **True Omni-Channel Conversion Rate:**
    *   *Formula:* (Online Confirmations + Attributed Calls) / Total Sessions.
    *   *Goal:* Reveal the actual performance (likely restoring the rate closer to 18% or higher).

2.  **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) by Channel (Blended):**
    *   *Formula:* Ad Spend / (Online Conversions + Attributed Calls from that channel).
    *   *Goal:* Correctly allocate budget. You may find "Organic" drives many calls, or that Paid Social has a lower CPA once calls are included.

3.  **Call-to-Booking Ratio:**
    *   *Formula:* Attributed Calls resulting in reservation / Total Attributed Calls.
    *   *Goal:* Measure the quality of traffic and the effectiveness of the phone booking team. If traffic is high but call conversions are low, the issue is operational, not marketing.

4.  **Attribution Gap Estimate:**
    *   *Formula:* Total Calls Received - Attributed Calls.
    *   *Goal:* Quantify the "unknown." This represents callers who visited previously (cookies expired), called from a different device, or dialed the static number directly. Report this as a range (e.g., "15-20% of calls remain unattributed due to cross-device behavior").

**Note:** Ensure your call tracking provider complies with EU privacy regulations (GDPR) regarding call recording and data storage. Do not count calls under 30 seconds as conversions to filter out wrong numbers or hang-ups.
