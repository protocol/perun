**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Brand Director, User Acquisition Lead
**FROM:** Growth Analytics
**DATE:** January 25
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation on App Icon A/B Test (Jan 10–24)

**Executive Summary**
Do not proceed with a full rebrand based on the current test results. The data indicates the test is **inconclusive** regarding the primary hypothesis, and the observed metrics do not justify the operational cost of a system-wide asset update at this time.

**Statistical Interpretation**
The 1.2% lift in click-through rate (CTR) falls within your stated margin of error. In experimentation terms, this is a "no significant difference" result. We cannot reject the null hypothesis; essentially, we cannot prove the orange icon performs better than the blue one. The 8% decrease in Cost Per Install (CPI) driven by organic visibility is a valuable secondary signal, but it is insufficient on its own to validate a full rebrand without a corresponding significant lift in user acquisition volume or quality.

Acting on a 1.2% marginal gain that is statistically indistinguishable from noise risks a "false positive" decision. A full rebrand involves significant downstream costs (updating ads, social assets, web presence, and PR). The current data does not support this investment.

**Strategic Recommendation**
1.  **Maintain Current Icon:** Retain the blue icon as the primary asset for Q2.
2.  **Classify as "Learn," Not "Win":** Document that while color distinctiveness did not drive immediate curiosity (CTR), it may influence algorithmic discoverability (organic visibility). This suggests the value of the orange icon lies in search ranking factors rather than human visual appeal in the browse view.
3.  **Next Steps for Q2 Creative Strategy:**
    *   **Do not** launch a full rebrand now.
    *   **Do** design a follow-up experiment that isolates "discoverability" variables. Instead of a simple color swap, test an icon that combines the high-visibility orange with a simplified graphic element to see if we can compound the organic lift with a stronger CTR driver.
    *   **Extend the Learning:** If the 8% CPI reduction is critical to Q2 goals, consider a soft rollout of the orange icon specifically for campaigns where organic search volume is the primary lever, while keeping the blue icon for direct response channels where CTR is paramount. However, this should be done cautiously as a channel-specific test, not a brand-wide mandate.

**Conclusion**
The hypothesis that "warmer color increases CTR by 5%" was not supported. The nuance regarding organic visibility is promising but requires a dedicated test to validate before altering the entire brand identity. Preserve resources for a higher-confidence test in Q2.

***

*Note: Ensure your sample size calculation for the next test accounts for the smaller effect size needed to detect organic visibility changes specifically.*
*Finalize the Q2 backlog by prioritizing hypotheses that address both visual appeal and store algorithm factors separately.*
