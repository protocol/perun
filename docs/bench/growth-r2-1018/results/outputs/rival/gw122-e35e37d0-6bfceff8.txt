### Beta Success Framework: Carbon Footprint Tracker (Nov 15 – Feb 15)

**Objective:** Validate the hypothesis that "detailed weekly reports drive deeper organizational action than real-time dashboards" and establish concrete proxies for sustainability behavior change without direct energy grid integration.

#### 1. Leading Indicators (Adoption & Engagement)
*Measure these weekly to predict long-term retention and value perception.*

**A. Adoption Depth vs. Breadth Matrix**
Since you cannot measure total carbon reduced yet, measure how deeply the tool penetrates the client's workflow.
*   **Breadth (Seat Expansion):** Ratio of *Active Users* to *Licensed Seats* per account.
    *   *Target:* >60% of invited stakeholders (not just admins) log in weekly.
    *   *Signal:* If only admins use it, the tool is a compliance checkbox, not an operational driver.
*   **Depth (Data Granularity):** Average number of *data sources connected* per active account.
    *   *Metric:* Count of uploaded utility bills, travel logs, or supply chain spreadsheets per company.
    *   *Target:* Median of 3+ distinct data sources per company by Month 2.
    *   *Signal:* High depth indicates the tool is becoming the "system of record" for their carbon data.

**B. The "Report vs. Dashboard" Hypothesis Test**
To validate your assumption about weekly reports:
*   **Metric:** *Report Interaction Rate* vs. *Dashboard Session Duration*.
*   **Method:** Tag users who primarily download/view the weekly PDF report versus those who only log into the live dashboard.
*   **Success Signal:** Accounts where >50% of stakeholders engage with the weekly report show 2x higher data upload frequency than "dashboard-only" accounts.
*   **Proxy for Value:** Count of "Report Shares" (emails sent from the platform) per week. If admins are manually forwarding reports, the format is working. If they are sharing dashboard links, the hypothesis may be wrong.

#### 2. Lagging Indicators (Behavior Change Proxies)
*Measure these monthly to demonstrate tangible value to future clients.*

**A. Operational Velocity (The "Time-to-Insight" Proxy)**
*   **Metric:** Average time from *Data Upload* to *First Actionable Alert Generated*.
*   **Target:** Reduce from >48 hours (manual spreadsheet work) to <4 hours.
*   **Value Prop:** "We reduced your carbon accounting labor by X hours/week." This is a concrete cost saving even before carbon is reduced.

**B. Decision Density**
*   **Metric:** Number of *Internal Comments/Tags* added to high-emission data points within the tool.
*   **Target:** >10 comments/tags per company per month.
*   **Signal:** Users are discussing specific emission sources inside the platform, indicating the tool is driving internal dialogue and accountability, not just storage.

**C. Data Completeness Score**
*   **Metric:** Percentage of required data fields populated vs. left blank over time.
*   **Target:** Increase from 40% (onboarding) to 85% (Month 3).
*   **Signal:** As confidence grows, companies trust the tool with more sensitive or complex data, increasing stickiness.

#### 3. Success Thresholds for Sales Negotiations
To satisfy the Head of Enterprise Sales, define "Beta Success" as achieving **3 out of 4** of the following by Feb 15th:
1.  **Retention:** 14/15 companies renew for Phase 2.
2.  **Depth:** Average of 3+ data sources connected per company.
3.  **Validation:** Weekly report users show 20% higher retention than dashboard-only users.
4.  **Efficiency:** Customer testimonial confirming >5 hours/week saved on carbon reporting labor.

**Note:** Implement event tracking immediately on "Report Download," "Data Source Connect," and "Internal Comment" actions to capture these metrics.
**Note:** Use the "Data Completeness Score" as your primary negotiation lever; it proves the tool solves the messy data aggregation problem, which is the biggest barrier for most enterprises.
