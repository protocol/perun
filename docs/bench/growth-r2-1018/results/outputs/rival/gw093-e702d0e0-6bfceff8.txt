**Executive Summary: Measurement Strategy for Low-Volume, Long-Cycle B2B**

Given IronLink's niche traffic (150 qualified leads/week) and 45-day decision cycle, standard A/B testing and short-window attribution are statistically invalid. We must shift from "statistical significance" to "directional confidence" using leading indicators and extended observation windows.

**1. Extend Attribution & Observation Windows**
Your current 14-day window captures only ~30% of conversions, artificially depressing ROI and obscuring the impact of Q3 content.
*   **Action:** Immediately extend GA4/CRM attribution lookbacks to 90 days.
*   **Metric:** Switch from "Weekly Conversions" to **"Cohort Conversion Rate."** Track groups of users by signup week and measure their conversion over 45, 60, and 90 days. This isolates whether Q3 leads are converting slower or just not at all, separating seasonal noise from strategy impact.

**2. Replace Lagging Revenue with Leading Micro-Conversions**
Waiting for $45k deals to close creates a 2-month blind spot. With low volume, we must validate engagement earlier in the funnel.
*   **Action:** Define and track high-intent micro-events that historically precede an RFQ.
*   **Metrics to Monitor:**
    *   **Depth of Engagement:** % of visitors viewing >3 technical spec sheets or downloading whitepapers.
    *   **Return Visitor Rate:** Decision-makers rarely convert on visit one. Track the % of leads returning within 14 days.
    *   **RFQ Initiation Rate:** Track users who start but don't finish an RFQ. A rise here validates content interest even if final submission lags.

**3. Qualitative Triangulation (The "N=1" Approach)**
In sparse data environments, one lost deal matters more than aggregate trends.
*   **Action:** Implement mandatory "Source/Trigger" fields for sales reps on every closed/won and closed/lost deal.
*   **Metric:** **Qualitative Attribution %.** Manually tag deals as "Content Influenced" vs. "Outbound Only." If 60% of closed deals mention specific Q3 content pieces during discovery, the strategy is working regardless of statistical p-values.

**4. Aggregation Strategy**
Do not aggregate across quarters if seasonality is suspected; this masks the very fluctuation you need to understand.
*   **Action:** Use a **Rolling 12-Week Average** for top-line metrics to smooth weekly volatility without losing trend visibility.
*   **Comparison:** Compare Q3 2024 only against Q3 2023 (Year-Over-Year), not Q2 2024. This controls for seasonal demand fluctuations inherent to the manufacturing sector.

**Decision Framework for the CEO**
We will make product decisions based on **triangulated signals** rather than single metric thresholds:
1.  **Green Light:** Cohort retention improves + Micro-conversions rise + Sales team reports content mentions. (Proceed even without statistical significance).
2.  **Yellow Light:** Traffic rises but micro-conversions flatline. (Content is attracting browsers, not buyers; refine targeting).
3.  **Red Light:** Micro-conversions drop or sales reports zero content influence after 60 days. (Pause strategy).

**Conclusion**
We cannot wait for "perfect" data. By extending our window to match the customer journey (90 days) and validating interest through micro-conversions and sales feedback, we can confidently assess the Q3 content strategy within 30 days, rather than waiting for quarterly revenue reconciliation.

*Note: Ensure CRM and analytics platforms are synced to allow for 90-day cohort reporting; standard dashboards often default to 30 days.*
*Implementation requires updating data retention settings in GA4 and adding custom fields to the sales pipeline immediately.*
