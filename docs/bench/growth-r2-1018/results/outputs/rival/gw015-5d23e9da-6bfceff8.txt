# Diagnostic Framework: VelvetThread Marketing Efficiency Breakdown

To diagnose the disconnect between the 35% spend increase and flat revenue, you must isolate whether the friction lies in **Traffic Quality**, **Site Conversion**, or **Customer Composition**. Request the following specific data cuts from your data engineering team immediately. Ensure all metrics compare the current quarter (post-spend increase) against the previous quarter (baseline), using consistent observation windows.

### 1. Traffic Quality & Intent Analysis
*Hypothesis: The new creatives are generating high-volume, low-intent traffic, inflating top-of-funnel metrics while diluting conversion rates.*

**Data Cuts Required:**
*   **Channel-Level CTR vs. CVR:** Plot Click-Through Rate (CTR) against Conversion Rate (CVR) for each paid channel. Look for channels where CTR increased significantly but CVR dropped proportionally.
*   **Bounce Rate & Time-on-Site by Campaign:** Segment new users by specific ad creative/campaign. Identify if the new creatives drive sessions with <10 seconds duration or single-page views.
*   **Add-to-Cart (ATC) Rate by Source:** Compare ATC rates for traffic from "New Creatives" vs. "Legacy Creatives." A drop here indicates a mismatch between ad promise and landing page reality.
*   **Cost Per Qualified Session:** Calculate cost per session that lasts >30 seconds and views >2 pages. This filters out bot traffic and accidental clicks.

### 2. Funnel Friction & Site Experience
*Hypothesis: Traffic intent is stable, but a technical issue, pricing friction, or UX bottleneck is preventing completion.*

**Data Cuts Required:**
*   **Funnel Drop-off Rates by Device:** Break down the funnel (Landing Page → Product Page → ATC → Checkout → Purchase) by Mobile vs. Desktop. Isolate if the mobile experience degraded coinciding with the spend increase.
*   **Checkout Abandonment by Shipping Tier:** Analyze abandonment rates at the shipping calculation step. Did the new traffic come from regions with prohibitive shipping costs not previously targeted?
*   **Page Load Latency vs. Conversion:** Correlate page load times for landing pages associated with new campaigns against conversion rates.
*   **Promo Code Usage Rate:** Compare the percentage of transactions using a discount code vs. full price. If the new audience only converts with discounts not offered in the creative, there is a value perception gap.

### 3. Customer Composition & Retention
*Hypothesis: The spend shift altered the New vs. Returning customer mix, masking a decline in high-value returning customer revenue.*

**Data Cuts Required:**
*   **Revenue Split: New vs. Returning Customers:** Compare the % of total revenue driven by returning customers in the current quarter vs. the baseline. A dip here confirms your suspicion about cohort performance.
*   **Returning Customer Purchase Frequency:** For users who purchased in the last 12 months, has the time-between-orders increased in the last 90 days?
*   **CAC by Customer Type:** Calculate Customer Acquisition Cost specifically for *new* customers vs. *reactivated* customers. Determine if the extra spend is inefficiently targeting cold audiences who have lower LTV.
*   **Cohort Retention Curve:** Generate a retention curve for customers acquired in Jan/Feb vs. Nov/Dec. Are the newer cohorts churning faster or purchasing less frequently?

### 4. Creative-to-Landing Page Alignment
*Hypothesis: The "Right Audience" assumption is flawed because the creative promises something the landing page does not deliver immediately.*

**Data Cuts Required:**
*   **Landing Page Exit Rate:** Identify the top 5 exit pages for traffic coming from the new ad sets.
*   **Internal Search Usage:** Measure the volume of internal searches immediately after landing. High search usage suggests users cannot find the product shown in the ad.
*   **Product Page View Depth:** Average number of product pages viewed per session. Low depth suggests the landing page assortment does not match the creative's appeal.

### Summary Table for CEO Presentation
Prepare a summary matrix with these columns to present on Thursday:
1.  **Metric** (e.g., Returning Customer Revenue %)
2.  **Baseline (Pre-Increase)**
3.  **Current (Post-Increase)**
4.  **% Delta**
5.  **Diagnosis** (Traffic Quality / Site Friction / Mix Shift)

***

*Note: Ensure the data engineering team uses closed observation windows for all conversion rates to avoid attributing partial-period spend to full-period results. Do not aggregate data across channels; the bottleneck is likely isolated to specific underperforming segments.*
