### Proposed Metrics Framework: Feature Impact on Revenue

To determine if the new filtering feature drives revenue or adds friction, you must shift from measuring **usage frequency** to measuring **outcome quality** and **monetization velocity**. The following metrics define the specific relationships between feature usage and billing events required for your investor update.

#### 1. The Core Correlation: Filter-Adjusted Expansion Rate
Instead of a global 5% expansion rate, segment this metric by usage behavior to isolate the feature's impact.
*   **Metric:** `Expansion Rate (Filter Users)` vs. `Expansion Rate (Non-Filter Users)`
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users in each group who upgraded their plan or purchased add-ons within 30 days of a key action.
*   **The Relationship:** If `Expansion Rate (Filter Users)` is not significantly higher than the non-user baseline, the feature is not driving perceived value sufficient to trigger spend.
*   **Investor Insight:** Demonstrates whether the feature is a revenue driver or a cost center.

#### 2. Outcome Quality: Lead-to-Meeting Conversion Delta
Your hypothesis is that filters yield "better leads." You must measure if "better leads" actually convert to meetings for the agency, as this is the value they pay for.
*   **Metric:** `Meeting Booked Rate per 100 Leads Generated`
*   **Definition:** Compare the rate at which leads generated *using filters* result in a booked meeting (tracked via user input or integration) versus leads generated *without filters*.
*   **The Relationship:** A positive delta confirms the feature improves lead quality. If usage is high (40%) but this delta is flat or negative, the filters are creating false positives or irrelevant noise.
*   **Investor Insight:** Validates the core value proposition: "Does this tool actually help agencies close deals?"

#### 3. Friction Analysis: Time-to-First-Value (TTFV) Spread
To address the concern that the tool has become too complex, analyze the time it takes to achieve the first successful outcome.
*   **Metric:** `Median TTFV (Filter Users)` vs. `Median TTFV (Non-Filter Users)`
*   **Definition:** Time from login to the first "Lead Exported" or "Lead Contacted" event.
*   **The Relationship:** If `TTFV (Filter Users)` is significantly higher, the feature is introducing friction that delays value realization, potentially stalling expansion decisions.
*   **Investor Insight:** Identifies if complexity is the bottleneck preventing revenue growth despite high adoption.

#### 4. Monetization Velocity: Upgrade Lag Time
Measure how quickly feature usage translates into a billing event.
*   **Metric:** `Days from First Filter Use to Upgrade`
*   **Definition:** The average duration between a user's first use of the advanced filters and their next expansion event (upgrade/add-on).
*   **The Relationship:** Compare this against the baseline `Days from Signup to Upgrade`. If filter users take *longer* to upgrade than the historical average, the feature may be satisfying a need without triggering a willingness to pay, or it may be overwhelming users before they see ROI.
*   **Investor Insight:** Highlights the efficiency of the feature in driving immediate revenue.

#### 5. Retention of High-Value Segments
*   **Metric:** `Churn Rate (High-Volume Filter Users)` vs. `Churn Rate (Low-Volume Filter Users)`
*   **Definition:** Compare churn among users who use filters >5 times/week versus those who use them <2 times/week.
*   **The Relationship:** If high-volume users churn at a higher rate, the feature is likely failing to deliver sustained value or is too difficult to maintain in a workflow.
*   **Investor Insight:** Proves long-term stickiness and LTV potential of the new functionality.

### Implementation Note
Ensure your analytics event tracking captures `filter_applied` with properties for `filter_type` and `results_count` linked to the subsequent `lead_exported` and `subscription_updated` events. Without this event chain, correlation cannot be proven.

### Strategic Recommendation
If `Lead-to-Meeting Conversion Delta` is positive but `Expansion Rate` remains flat, your pricing packaging is misaligned, not the feature. If `TTFV` is negatively impacted, prioritize UX simplification over new sales pitches.
