**North Star Recommendation: "Net Client Cash-Flow Improvement"**

Reject "volume of invoices financed" as your North Star. It is a vanity metric that rose 25% while masking a 15% drop in loan size and a spike in fee confusion. Volume measures *activity*, not *value*. Your new North Star must be **Net Client Cash-Flow Improvement**, defined as: *(Total Payouts to Client) − (Total Fees Paid) − (Any Penalty Costs)*, measured weekly per active client. This metric only rises if the client ends up with more usable cash after using your service, directly aligning with "genuine financial health."

**Supporting Input Metrics (The Diagnostic Funnel)**
To move the North Star without encouraging risky behavior, track these three inputs:
1.  **Retention of Core Action (Week 4):** Are clients who used instant payout in March still using it in April? If volume is up but retention is flat or down, the 25% growth is likely one-off testing or distress borrowing, not sustained value.
2.  **Fee Clarity Ratio:** Calculate *(Support Queries Regarding Fees) / (Total Instant Payout Transactions)*. Your current doubling of queries indicates a friction point where "value" is being destroyed by confusion. A successful launch requires this ratio to trend toward zero.
3.  **Loan Size Stability Cohort:** Segment users by their average loan size change. Compare the retention rate of the "shrinking loan" cohort vs. the "stable/growing loan" cohort. If the shrinking cohort churns faster, you are attracting distressed users who do not find long-term value.
