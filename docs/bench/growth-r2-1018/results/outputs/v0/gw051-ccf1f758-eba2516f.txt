### Immediate Analysis Plan for Friday's Stakeholder Meeting

**1. Validate the Baseline Comparison (Critical Context)**
Before diagnosing the drop-off, you must address the discrepancy between your current 20% completion rate (850/4,200) and the beta's 30%.
*   **Action:** Request a **cohort comparison** of "Beta Users" vs. "Post-Launch Users" specifically for the *old* flow (if any traffic remains) or a historical trend line of the completion rate over the last 90 days.
*   **Why:** If the beta group was pre-selected or highly motivated, the 30% figure is a **vanity benchmark** and not a valid baseline for the general population. Comparing current performance to an inflated beta number may create a false alarm. The only valid benchmark is your own recent trend prior to October 12th.

**2. Funnel Breakdown: Isolate the Identity Verification Step**
To confirm if the new step is the bottleneck, you need a granular funnel view, not just start-to-finish numbers.
*   **Request:** A funnel report with these specific steps:
    1.  Started Application
    2.  Reached Identity Verification Screen
    3.  **Exited at Identity Verification** (Drop-off point A)
    4.  Submitted Verification Documents
    5.  **Exited Post-Verification/Pre-Submission** (Drop-off point B)
    6.  Final Submission Completed
*   **Metric to Watch:** The **step-to-step conversion rate** between steps 2 and 3. If this specific drop-off is significantly higher than the average drop-off of other steps in the flow, your hypothesis is supported. If the drop-off is uniform across all steps, the issue may be overall flow complexity, not just the verification step.

**3. Slice by Device and Platform (The "Where")**
Friction often varies wildly by interface. Users without local bank accounts may rely more heavily on mobile devices or specific browsers.
*   **Request:** Break down the **Identity Verification Drop-off Rate** by:
    *   **Device Type:** Mobile Web, iOS App, Android App, Desktop Web.
    *   **Browser/OS:** Specifically look for lower completion rates on mobile browsers vs. native apps.
*   **Hypothesis Check:** If mobile drop-off is 60% while desktop is 20%, the verification UI is likely broken or too difficult on small screens, regardless of the user's bank account status.

**4. Slice by User Attribute (The "Who")**
Since you suspect the issue relates to users without local bank accounts, you must proxy this attribute if you don't have it directly labeled yet.
*   **Request:** Segment the drop-off by **Geographic Location** (if applicable to non-local users) or **Document Type Selected** (e.g., Passport vs. Local Driver's License vs. International ID).
*   **Metric to Watch:** Compare the completion rate of users selecting "International ID" or non-local document types against those selecting local options. A stark difference here validates the "local bank account/ID" friction hypothesis.

**5. Time-to-Complete Analysis**
*   **Request:** Average time spent on the Identity Verification screen for completers vs. drop-offs.
*   **Signal:** If drop-offs spend very little time (<10 seconds), they are bouncing immediately upon seeing the requirement. If they spend a long time (2+ minutes) and then drop, they are struggling to complete the task.

**Summary of Data Requests for Your Team:**
1.  **Funnel Visualization:** Step-by-step conversion from "Start" to "Submit," highlighting the % drop at the Identity Verification entry and exit.
2.  **Segmentation Matrix:** Drop-off rates at the verification step sliced by **Device Type** and **Document Type/Origin**.
3.  **Historical Context:** Completion rate trend for the 4 weeks prior to October 12th to establish a true baseline.

**Note:** Do not accept "average industry conversion rates" as a benchmark; rely solely on your pre-Oct 12th trend. If the data team cannot slice by "Document Type," request a raw event log sample of the verification step to manually inspect error codes or exit events.
