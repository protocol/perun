### Growth Measurement Plan: SecureCoin Soft Launch (Dec 5)

**1. North Star Metric**
**Weekly Active Funded Users (WAFU):** The count of unique users who have successfully funded their wallet and performed at least one on-chain transaction or swap within a 7-day window.
*Why:* Raw signups and bank links are vanity metrics if they do not lead to actual wallet usage. This metric rises only when real customer value (secure asset management) is delivered.

**2. Core Funnel & Event Taxonomy (Bottom-Up Diagnosis)**
Do not instrument "everything." Track only these specific events to answer named questions about friction. Diagnose retention leaks before optimizing acquisition.

| Stage | Event Name (Trigger) | Question Answered | Critical Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Activation** | `signup_completed` | Did they finish identity verification (KYC)? | `kyc_provider`, `duration_seconds`, `failure_reason` |
| **Activation** | `bank_link_initiated` | Did they attempt to link a funding source? | `bank_type`, `entry_point` |
| **Activation** | `bank_link_success` | **Hypothesis Check:** Did they link within 10 mins? | `time_to_link_seconds`, `is_under_10_mins` (bool) |
| **Retention** | `first_fund_success` | Did the money actually arrive? | `amount`, `currency`, `time_since_signup` |
| **Retention** | `core_action_performed` | Did they use the wallet (swap/transfer)? | `action_type`, `gas_fee_paid` |
| **Drop-off** | `flow_abandonment` | Where did they quit? | `step_name`, `error_code`, `session_duration` |

**3. The Identifier Seam & Compliance**
*   **Stable ID:** Assign a pseudonymous `user_cohort_id` at signup to track the user across sessions and devices for retention analysis.
*   **Privacy Boundary:** Do **not** store raw bank account numbers, KYC document images, or PII in your analytics event stream. Route all sensitive data handling to `privacy-compliance.md` protocols. The analytics engine receives only the `user_cohort_id` and boolean/status flags.

**4. Stage-Appropriate Instrumentation (MVP)**
Since you are in the `mvp` stage (soft launch), ignore referral and broad acquisition metrics for now. Focus entirely on the **Activation → Retention** seam.
*   **Primary Hypothesis to Validate:** Compare the 30-day retention of users with `is_under_10_mins: true` vs. `false`. If the delta is not statistically significant, the "10-minute rule" is a distraction; pivot to other friction points.
*   **Friction Detection:** Calculate the conversion rate between `bank_link_initiated` and `bank_link_success`. A drop here indicates UI confusion or API failures with specific banks.

**5. Experiment Rigor Warning**
If you A/B test changes to the onboarding flow during the soft launch:
*   **Pre-commit:** Define your sample size and primary metric (`first_fund_success`) before looking at data.
*   **No Peeking:** Do not stop the test early because "p < 0.05" on Day 2. This inflates false positives. Read the result only at the pre-determined end date or use a valid sequential testing method.
*   **Guardrail:** Monitor `kyc_failure_rate` as a guardrail to ensure speed improvements do not compromise compliance.

3.  **Decision Rule:** If users linking after 10 minutes show <5% lower retention than those linking within 10 minutes, deprioritize "speed" optimizations and focus on `kyc_failure_reason` analysis instead.
