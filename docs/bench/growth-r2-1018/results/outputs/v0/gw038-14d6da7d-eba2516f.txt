### Measurement Plan: Bill Split Pilot (SecureBank)

**1. Define the North Star & Current Baseline**
*   **North Star:** Weekly Active Splitters (users completing ≥1 split/week).
*   **Current Status:** 300 completions / 5,000 exposed = **6% conversion**.
*   **Verdict:** This is an `UNVERIFIED` success rate. Without a baseline for similar features or industry data (which we do not fabricate), we cannot judge "good" vs. "bad." We must compare this 6% against your own future cohorts or a control group if possible.

**2. Proxy Metrics for Untracked Friction (The "Black Box" Selection Step)**
Since you cannot instrument the selection step, infer friction using these downstream proxies:
*   **Confirmation Page Dwell Time:** Measure time between landing on the confirmation screen and clicking "Confirm."
    *   *Hypothesis:* High dwell time + completion suggests users are double-checking errors made during the untracked selection.
    *   *Hypothesis:* Low dwell time + abandonment suggests immediate rejection of the summary.
*   **The "Zombie" Session Rate:** Count users who land on the confirmation screen but do not convert within 24 hours, yet return to the transaction history later. This indicates a failed attempt followed by a retry or abandonment.
*   **Support Ticket Tagging:** Create a specific tag: `BillSplit_SelectionError`. Manually review the first 50 tickets to identify if "selection difficulty" is the primary driver. Do not wait for volume; qualitative patterns emerge quickly.

**3. Immediate Data Collection Actions (Before Next Wednesday)**
*   **Deploy Exit-Intent Survey (Compliance Safe):** Trigger a single-question modal *only* on the confirmation screen if the user moves to close the tab or navigate away.
    *   *Question:* "What stopped you from completing this split?" (Options: Wrong transactions, Too slow, Privacy concern, Other).
    *   *Compliance:* No PII collected in the survey; link responses only to a pseudonymous session ID.
*   **Session Recording Sampling:** Record 100% of sessions for a random 5% of the pilot group (250 users).
    *   *Action:* Watch only the first 30 seconds of non-converting sessions. Look specifically for "rage clicks" or rapid back-and-forth navigation before the confirmation screen loads.
*   **Cohort Retention Check:** Of the 300 who completed a split, how many returned to split again within 7 days?
    *   *Why:* If retention is near 0%, the 6% conversion was likely accidental or the UX is too painful to repeat. Retention is more important than acquisition here.

**4. Decision Framework for Risk Team**
Do not present a definitive "success" claim. Present a **Risk-Adjusted Recommendation**:
*   **Ship Wider IF:** Retention (7-day repeat) > 20% AND Support Ticket volume < 0.5% of attempts.
*   **Extend Pilot IF:** Retention is unknown or < 20%. Extend for 2 weeks to gather repeat usage data.
*   **Stop/Rework IF:** Support tickets indicate > 30% of users are confused by transaction selection.
