### Instrumentation Plan: GreenFlow Beta (Pre-Launch)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users with **Active Automated Schedules** (not just app opens or total signups). This measures delivered value (water saved automatically), not vanity activity.

**Event Taxonomy: The "Why" Behind Abandonment**
Do not instrument "everything." Implement only these specific events to distinguish technical failure from usability friction during the 48-hour activation window.

#### 1. `schedule_setup_started`
*   **Question:** Are users even attempting the core value action?
*   **Properties:**
    *   `user_segment`: (e.g., "technical", "non-technical" – based on onboarding survey if available).
    *   `entry_point`: (e.g., "home_banner", "onboarding_wizard", "settings_menu").
    *   `device_model`: (Critical for hardware-specific bugs).
    *   `app_version`.

#### 2. `device_connectivity_check` (Technical Seam)
*   **Question:** Is the drop-off caused by hardware handshake failures?
*   **Properties:**
    *   `check_result`: (`success`, `timeout`, `device_not_found`, `auth_failed`).
    *   `signal_strength`: (RSSI value at time of attempt).
    *   `retry_count`: (Number of automatic retries before failure/success).
    *   `error_code`: (Specific hardware SDK error).
*   **Analysis Logic:** High drop-off after `schedule_setup_started` with `check_result` != `success` indicates **technical friction**.

#### 3. `schedule_configuration_step_viewed`
*   **Question:** Where in the UI flow do users hesitate or leave?
*   **Properties:**
    *   `step_name`: (e.g., "zone_selection", "frequency_setting", "duration_input", "review").
    *   `time_spent_seconds`: (Time on this specific screen).
    *   `help_icon_clicked`: (Boolean – indicates confusion).
    *   `default_values_used`: (Boolean – did they accept defaults or customize?).
*   **Analysis Logic:** High `time_spent_seconds` or frequent `help_icon_clicked` on a specific step indicates **usability friction**.

#### 4. `schedule_setup_completed` (Activation Event)
*   **Question:** Did they successfully create the automation?
*   **Properties:**
    *   `total_duration_seconds`: (Time from start to finish).
    *   `zones_configured_count`: (Number of zones set).
    *   `schedule_type`: (`fixed`, `weather_adjusted`, `soil_moisture_based`).
    *   `is_automated`: (Boolean – must be true for North Star).

#### 5. `schedule_execution_triggered` (Retention Validator)
*   **Question:** Did the automation actually run without user intervention?
*   **Properties:**
    *   `trigger_source`: (`timer`, `weather_api`, `sensor_input`).
    *   `water_volume_estimated`: (Derived metric).
    *   `execution_status`: (`success`, `failed_no_water`, `failed_valve_error`).

---

### Implementation & Privacy Notes
*   **Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` for cohort analysis (linking sessions across devices), but ensure no PII (names, exact addresses) is sent to the analytics engine. Route PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.
*   **Stage Awareness:** As this is an MVP/Beta, do not track granular button clicks outside this flow. If an event does not answer one of the questions above, do not instrument it yet.
