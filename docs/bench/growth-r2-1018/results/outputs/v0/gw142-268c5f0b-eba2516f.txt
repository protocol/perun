**Decision: Do not roll out globally. Kill the current experiment as a definitive test of enterprise value, but preserve the small-business signal for a segmented rollout.**

The 12% lift in small business sign-ups is a valid, actionable win for that specific cohort. However, the enterprise segment results (4 conversions vs. 3) are statistically `UNVERIFIED`. With only seven total events, the sample size is too small to detect any realistic effect size, rendering the p-value meaningless. Your assumption that the sales cycle exceeds the 30-day window confirms a design flaw: the test duration was insufficient for the primary metric (enterprise conversion) to mature. Reading this as a "failure" of the pricing model is incorrect; it is a failure of the measurement window.

You cannot wait another quarter for data, but you also cannot claim a win or a loss on the current numbers. Instead, pivot your recommendation to the CRO as follows:

1.  **Segmented Action:** Immediately roll out the simplified three-tier model to the **small business segment only**. The 12% lift suggests reduced friction for this group, and the risk is low compared to the opportunity cost of delaying.
2.  **Enterprise Pivot:** Halt the global enterprise rollout. The current data provides no evidence to support a change. Do not extend the current A/B test; the "peeking" risk and the mismatched sales cycle make extension futile.
3.  **New Evaluation Method:** Replace the binary A/B conversion metric for enterprise with a **leading indicator** that fits the 30-day window. Measure "qualified demo requests," "pricing page time-on-site for enterprise tiers," or "sales-qualified lead (SQL) creation rate" instead of closed-won deals. These events occur earlier in the funnel and will generate enough volume in 30 days to show a signal.
4.  **Guardrail Check:** Before any rollout, verify that the simplified tiers did not decrease average revenue per user (ARPU) for the small businesses who signed up. If the 12% volume increase comes with a >12% drop in price per unit, the net revenue impact is negative.
