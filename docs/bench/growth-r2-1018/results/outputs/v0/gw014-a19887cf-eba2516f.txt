### SwiftWing Oakhaven Trial: Measurement Plan

**North Star Metric:** Percentage of scheduled deliveries completed within the target time window without triggering a community disturbance event. This measures realized value (speed) constrained by viability (community impact), rejecting vanity metrics like "total flights launched" which rise even when flights abort.

**Event Taxonomy & Data Collection**
Instrument only events that answer specific go/no-go questions for scaling. Do not log "everything."

1.  **Core Value Event:** `delivery_completed`
    *   *Question:* Are we faster than vans reliably?
    *   *Properties:* `scheduled_time`, `actual_completion_time`, `weather_condition` (wind speed, precipitation), `abort_reason` (if applicable).
    *   *Baseline:* Compare against the 12-minute pilot average and local van benchmarks provided by your logistics partner.

2.  **Viability Leak Event:** `flight_aborted`
    *   *Question:* Is weather making the model unscalable?
    *   *Properties:* `abort_trigger` (e.g., wind > threshold), `location_zone`, `time_of_day`.
    *   *Analysis:* Track the abort rate daily. If the 10% pilot abort rate spikes above 15% in specific wind conditions, the operational model fails in those conditions.

3.  **Community Acceptance Proxy:** `disturbance_signal` (Observable Behavior)
    *   *Question:* Is the community tolerating us without surveys?
    *   *Definition:* A composite event triggered by **observable logs**, not self-reported feelings.
    *   *Inputs:*
        *   `noise_complaint_call`: Timestamped match between flight path and incoming calls to the dedicated trial hotline or local non-emergency line (requires data share agreement).
        *   `app_uninstall_or_disable`: Spike in users disabling location permissions or uninstalling the SwiftWing app within 1 hour of a flight overhead in their zone.
        *   `delivery_refusal`: Customer explicitly marking "Do not leave package" or refusing handover upon arrival.
    *   *Threshold:* >2 `disturbance_signal` events per 100 successful deliveries indicates failing community acceptance.

**Funnel Diagnosis (Bottom-Up)**
Do not celebrate high launch volumes if retention/completion is leaking.
1.  **Retention/Viability:** Are flights completing without aborts or complaints? (If no, stop scaling).
2.  **Activation:** Are customers accepting the delivery method? (Refusal rates).
3.  **Acquisition:** Only analyze volume growth if the above two are stable.

**Success/Failure Thresholds (Based on Your Baseline)**
*   **Success (Scale to Next City):**
    *   Average delivery time ≤ 12 minutes (matching pilot) with <5% variance.
    *   Flight abort rate ≤ 10% (matching pilot) across varied weather.
    *   `disturbance_signal` rate < 2% of total flights.
*   **Failure (Stop/Pivot):**
    *   Abort rate > 15% in moderate wind (indicates weather fragility).
    *   `disturbance_signal` rate > 5% (indicates community rejection).
    *   Average delivery time > 18 minutes (fails value prop vs. vans).
