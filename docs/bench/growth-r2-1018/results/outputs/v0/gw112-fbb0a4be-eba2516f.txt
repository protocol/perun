**Key Performance Indicators for Welcome Email Delay Experiment**

**Primary Outcome Metric**
*   **Day-7 Core Action Retention:** Percentage of users who perform the core value action (e.g., reading a full article, saving a story, or customizing preferences) within 7 days of signup. This replaces click-through rate as the North Star, measuring actual value delivery rather than link curiosity.

**Secondary Engagement Metrics**
*   **Dashboard Exploration Depth:** Average number of unique dashboard sections visited within the first 24 hours. This validates the hypothesis that users utilize the "silence" to self-explore.
*   **Time-to-First-Value:** Median time elapsed between signup and the first core action. We expect this to decrease or remain stable if the delay prevents premature disengagement.
*   **Email Sequence Completion Rate:** Percentage of users who open/click the *second* email in the sequence. A higher rate here indicates the delay preserved attention span.

**Guardrail Metrics (Critical for Go/No-Go)**
*   **Day-1 Unsubscribe Rate:** Percentage of users unsubscribing within the first 24 hours. This is your specific risk variable; set a hard tripwire (e.g., if this rises >0.5% above baseline, stop the test).
*   **Spam Complaint Rate:** Monitor for spikes indicating users forgot they signed up due to the delay.
*   **Overall 7-Day Retention:** Cohort retention at Day 7 regardless of email interaction. If this drops, the delay is causing churn despite any engagement gains.

**Diagnostic/Segmentation Metrics**
*   **Activation by Source:** Break down all above metrics by acquisition channel (e.g., organic search vs. paid social). The delay might help organic users who are more intentional but hurt paid users who need immediate reinforcement.
*   **Device-Type Engagement:** Compare mobile vs. desktop behavior during the 24-hour window to see if "silent exploration" happens more on specific devices.

**Experiment Governance Requirements**
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define the minimum detectable effect (MDE) for the Primary Outcome before launch. Do not peek at results before the full 7-day cycle completes for the last user in the cohort.
*   **Analysis Window:** Analyze data only after the full 7-day window closes for the entire 10,000-user cohort to avoid time-zone and cycle biases.

Note: Ensure user identifiers are pseudonymous in the dashboard to comply with privacy standards while allowing cross-session tracking.
Do not optimize for open rates alone; a higher open rate on a delayed email is vanity if core action retention falls.
