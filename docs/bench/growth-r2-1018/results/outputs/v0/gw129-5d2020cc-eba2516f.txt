### Diagnostic Approach: Paywall Friction vs. Value Perception

The drop from 42% to 28% day-30 retention indicates a structural leak at the monetization seam, not necessarily a lack of product value. Your hypothesis that users would pay to maintain streaks is `UNVERIFIED` without causal data; the sharp exit suggests the friction cost exceeds the perceived immediate value for the majority. Do not revert to a soft paywall yet. Instead, instrument specific events to distinguish between **price sensitivity**, **value misalignment**, and **technical friction**.

**1. Event Taxonomy: Instrument Only What Answers a Named Question**
Stop guessing. Instrument these three specific events to isolate the barrier:
*   **`paywall_view_duration`**: Measures hesitation. If users linger >15 seconds but do not click "Subscribe," the barrier is likely **price sensitivity** or **value perception**. If they bounce in <2 seconds, the barrier is **unexpected friction** or **shock**.
*   **`pricing_scroll_depth`**: Answers "Do they see the value proposition?" If <50% of users scroll to the feature breakdown, the copy is failing to justify the cost before the price is revealed.
*   **`payment_flow_dropoff_step`**: Break down the checkout into steps (Click Subscribe → Enter Details → Confirm). A drop at "Enter Details" signals **friction**; a drop at "Confirm" signals **price rejection**.

**2. Segmentation: Identify Resilient Cohorts**
Analyze retention by these specific segments to find your "willing to pay" baseline:
*   **Usage Intensity**: Compare users with >10 sessions in the first 7 days vs. <5. High-intensity users dropping off confirms a **pricing** issue, not a habit issue.
*   **Streak Length at Paywall**: Do users with a 7-day streak convert better than those with a 3-day streak? If not, the "streak" incentive is not driving perceived value as assumed.
*   **Acquisition Channel**: Organic users often have different price elasticity than paid ad users. Isolate the channel where the 42% baseline was strongest.

**3. Testing Strategy: Pre-Committed A/B Design**
Do not run a "soft vs. hard" test without a pre-committed design. Peeking at daily conversion rates will inflate false positives.
*   **Primary Metric**: Day-30 Retention (paid + active free).
*   **Guardrail Metric**: Revenue per User (ARPU). Ensure you don't increase retention by giving everything away for free, destroying unit economics.
*   **Hypothesis**: "A soft paywall (3 free premium sessions post-trial) will increase Day-30 retention by allowing users to experience value *after* the trial, without reducing ARPU by more than 10%."
*   **Design**: Split traffic 50/50. Run for a fixed duration (e.g., 4 weeks) to capture full Day-30 cohorts. Do not stop early if p < 0.05 on Day 3.
*   **Variant B (Soft Paywall)**: Allow 3 additional premium sessions after the trial expires before blocking access.
