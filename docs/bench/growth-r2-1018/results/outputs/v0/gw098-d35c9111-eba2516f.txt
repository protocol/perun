### StorMix Growth Measurement Framework: Automation Efficiency & Trust

**1. North Star Metric: Verified Cost Savings per Active Account**
Reject "Total Data Stored" or "Raw Signups" as vanity metrics. The single metric that proves value is the **percentage of monthly storage costs saved by automation versus a manual baseline**. If this number does not rise, the product is not delivering its core promise, regardless of how much data is ingested.

**2. Event Taxonomy: Answering Named Questions**
Instrument only events that resolve the specific unknowns regarding trust versus unpredictability. Do not track "everything."
*   **Event:** `automation_rule_triggered`
    *   *Question:* Is the system correctly identifying cold data?
    *   *Properties:* `data_age`, `predicted_access_frequency`, `target_tier`.
*   **Event:** `manual_override_executed`
    *   *Question:* Are users rejecting automation due to cost fear or pattern mismatch?
    *   *Properties:* `override_direction` (hot-to-cold vs. cold-to-hot), `time_since_last_access`, `user_role`.
*   **Event:** `data_re_accessed_post_move`
    *   *Question:* Did the automation make a mistake (false positive)?
    *   *Properties:* `time_to_re_access`, `retrieval_latency_impact`.

**3. The Identifier Seam**
To analyze cohorts (e.g., "users who override >5 times/week"), you must link events to a **stable, pseudonymous user ID**. This allows tracking behavior across sessions without exposing PII. Route all raw IP addresses and account details to your privacy compliance layer; the analytics engine receives only the hashed ID.

**4. Diagnostic Funnel: Read Bottom-Up (Retention of Trust)**
Do not focus on acquisition in Mumbai/Pune yet. Diagnose the leak in trust first:
*   **Retention of Automation:** Of the files moved to cold storage automatically, what percentage remain there without manual override for 30 days?
*   **Activation:** Do new accounts configure at least one rule and let it run for 7 days without intervention?
*   **Acquisition:** Only analyze signups after the above two layers are stable. Pouring marketing spend into a product where users immediately disable the core feature is waste.

**5. Segmentation for the Regional Directors**
Your deck must segment users by **Override Intent**, not just geography:
*   **The "Unpredictable" Cohort:** Users who override because data *was* actually accessed shortly after moving to cold storage. *Action:* Improve prediction algorithms.
*   **The "Distrustful" Cohort:** Users who override despite no subsequent access (paying higher prices unnecessarily). *Action:* This is a trust/education failure. Show them a "Cost of Inaction" dashboard comparing their actual bill vs. the automated bill.

**6. Experiment Rigor: Proving Cost Savings**
To prove reliability to early adopters, run a controlled comparison, not a dashboard peek:
*   **Design:** Pre-commit to a 4-week observation window. Define the primary metric as "Net Cost Delta" and the guardrail as "Data Retrieval Latency."
*   **Analysis:** Compare the "Automated-Only" simulated cost against the "Actual Mixed" cost.
*   **Warning:** Do not claim a win if you check the data daily and stop when the p-value dips below 0.05. This "peeking" inflates false positives. Read the result once at the end of the pre-committed period.
