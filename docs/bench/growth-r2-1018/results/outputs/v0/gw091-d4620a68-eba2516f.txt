### Approach to Measure Offline Conversions

To attribute offline calls to marketing channels without intrusive user questioning, implement **Dynamic Number Insertion (DNI)** immediately. This system swaps the phone number displayed on your website based on the visitor's traffic source (e.g., Google Ads shows Number A, Facebook shows Number B, direct traffic shows Number C).

**Execution Plan for November 15th Pilot:**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Deploy a DNI solution that pools numbers for your three main advertising channels. Ensure the script captures the session data (source, medium, campaign, landing page) *before* the number is swapped.
2.  **The Identifier Seam:** When a call arrives, the DNI provider logs the matched session ID against the incoming call. This creates a pseudonymous link between the digital visit and the offline action. Do not store raw PII in your analytics; route call recordings and personal details to your secure telephony system, passing only the anonymized session ID and conversion timestamp to your dashboard.
3.  **Validation:** Since you cannot verify the "60% website visitor" assumption yet, treat it as `UNVERIFIED`. The pilot's primary goal is to establish a baseline of *confirmed* web-to-call attributions. Run a parallel manual log for one week where agents ask a non-intrusive verification question ("Did you see our website today?") to a small sample to validate the DNI match rate, then discontinue to avoid friction.

### Metrics for the Regional Operations Director

Stop reporting the blended "conversion rate" that drops when call volume rises; this is a vanity metric that penalizes successful offline acquisition. Report these three specific metrics next quarter:

1.  **Attributed Call Conversion Rate:** The percentage of website sessions that result in a phone booking, broken down by channel. This replaces the generic conversion rate and proves which ads drive offline value.
2.  **Total Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) – Blended:** Combine online booking costs and ad spend against *both* online and attributed offline bookings. This prevents the false conclusion that digital marketing is failing when it is actually driving phone calls.
3.  **Channel-Specific Call Volume:** The raw count of calls generated per advertising channel. This identifies which campaigns are driving the "older demographic" behavior you suspect.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
*   **This Week:** Select a DNI vendor and define the three specific campaign tags required for the pool; do not instrument "all traffic," only the paid channels in question.
*   **Decision Trigger:** If the DNI match rate (calls linked to sessions) is below 70% after two weeks, the script implementation is failing or users are copying/pasting numbers; pause spend on the lowest-performing channel until the tracking integrity is resolved.

*Note: The 60% assumption remains unverified until the pilot data arrives; do not base budget decisions on it. Ensure your DNI provider complies with local privacy regulations regarding call recording and data retention.*
