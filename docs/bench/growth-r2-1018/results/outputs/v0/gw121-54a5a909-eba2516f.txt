### This-Week Measurement Plan: Canada Activation Drop (Oct 4 Launch)

**1. Isolate the Cause: Confusion vs. Latency**
To distinguish between user confusion and technical performance, track these two specific metrics segmented by **Step 3 (Social Share Prompt)** for Canadian users only:
*   **Interaction Rate vs. Time-to-Interactive:** Measure the percentage of users who *click* the new social button versus those who abandon the step immediately.
    *   *Diagnosis:* If click-through is near zero but load times are normal, users are confused or rejecting the value prop. If load times exceed 2 seconds and abandonment spikes correlatively, it is a latency bug.
*   **Error Rate by Event:** Instrument a specific `setup_step_3_error` event capturing client-side timeouts or API failures for the sharing module.
    *   *Diagnosis:* A rise in this error count confirms a technical block; flat errors with high drop-offs confirm UX friction.

**2. Segmentation Strategy**
Slice all data by the following dimensions to rule out confounders:
*   **Device/OS:** Is the drop isolated to iOS or Android? (Indicates a client-side rendering bug).
*   **Network Type:** Compare Wi-Fi vs. Cellular activation. (If cellular drops disproportionately, suspect payload size or latency).
*   **Cohort:** Compare users exposed to the feature (Post-Oct 4) against a holdout group (if available) or pre-launch baseline (Sept 20–Oct 3) strictly within the Canada region.

**3. The Decision Tripwire (Rollback vs. Hotfix)**

*   **Rollback Immediately If:** The `setup_step_3_error` rate exceeds 5% OR if the median time-to-interactive on Step 3 is >3s for Canadian IPs. This confirms a broken experience that cannot be fixed by copy changes.
*   **Push Hotfix If:** Errors are <1% and latency is normal, but interaction rate is low. This indicates the *placement* or *copy* is confusing. The hotfix should be removing the mandatory nature of the step or clarifying the value proposition, not a code rollback.
