### Recommended Metrics & Narrative for FreightFlow Series A

Given your high-value, low-volume B2B context (12 deals/quarter), standard volume-based funnels are indeed misleading. Investors in Series A B2B logistics care less about "how many" and more about "how deep" and "how sticky." Shift your narrative from **conversion rates** to **engagement depth** and **commitment signals**.

**1. The North Star: "Active Integration Depth" (Not Just Uploads)**
Your assumption that document uploads predict success is likely correct, but "total uploads" is a vanity metric if one customer uploads 100 useless files. Refine this to **Core Action Completion Rate per Trial Account**.
*   **Definition:** The percentage of trial accounts that upload the *specific* critical documents required to move a shipment (e.g., Bill of Lading, Rate Confirmation) rather than just test files.
*   **Why it works:** It proves users are deriving actual workflow value, not just poking the UI. With only ~12-15 trials per quarter, you can manually verify every single instance.
*   **Presentation:** "8 of 12 closed customers hit 'Integration Depth' within week 2 of trial. Those who did closed 30% faster."

**2. Leading Indicator: "Time-to-First-Value" (TTFV)**
In long sales cycles, speed of adoption during the trial is the strongest proxy for retention.
*   **Definition:** The elapsed time from account creation to the first successful carrier match or document processing event.
*   **Why it works:** It demonstrates product intuitiveness and immediate utility. If TTFV is dropping quarter-over-quarter, your product is getting easier to sell, even if total deal count is flat due to market conditions.
*   **Presentation:** "Median TTFV decreased from 14 days to 6 days this quarter, indicating reduced friction for our enterprise onboarding."

**3. Qualitative Proxy: "Workflow Embedding Score"**
Since statistical significance is impossible with N=12, use **qualitative intensity** as a quantitative proxy.
*   **Definition:** A manual score (1-5) assigned to each trial account based on: number of unique users logged in from that company, frequency of logins, and diversity of features used (e.g., did they just upload, or did they also message a carrier?).
*   **Why it works:** It captures "stickiness" that raw counts miss. High scores on lost deals explain *why* they didn't close (e.g., "High interest, but legal blocker"), while high scores on won deals prove causality.
*   **Presentation:** "100% of closed deals had a Workflow Embedding Score of 4+, compared to 20% of lost deals."

**4. The "Commitment" Metric: Expansion Within Pilot**
*   **Definition:** Did the customer add more lanes, more users, or more volume *during* the trial/pilot phase before the contract was signed?
*   **Why it works:** Organic expansion during a trial is the ultimate signal of product-market fit. It shows the product sells itself internally within the client organization.

**How to Frame the Story**
Do not apologize for the low volume; reframe it as **high signal density**.
*   **The Narrative:** "We are optimizing for *quality of fit* over *speed of leak*. Our data shows that when a customer hits [Core Action] within [X] days, close rate is 100%. Our focus this quarter was reducing Time-to-First-Value to maximize the number of accounts hitting that threshold. We have validated the mechanism; now we are scaling the input."
*   **Visuals:** Use a **Cohort Heatmap** (rows = companies, columns = weeks, cells = activity intensity) instead of a line graph. This visually demonstrates deep engagement across your specific customer set without implying false statistical power.

**Critical Guardrail:** Explicitly state that these are **leading indicators based on observed behavioral patterns**, not statistically projected conversion rates. Label any small-sample percentage as "Observed Trend (N=12)" to maintain credibility.

*Note: Ensure your event taxonomy distinguishes between "test uploads" and "production documents" to keep the North Star clean. Route user identity handling to your privacy compliance protocol.*
