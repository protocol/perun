**Memo: Recommendation on App Icon A/B Test (Jan 10–24)**

**Decision: Do Not Rebrand. Treat Result as Inconclusive for Engagement; Investigate Discoverability Signal.**

The observed 1.2% lift in click-through rate (CTR) is **not statistically significant** and does not justify a full rebrand. Because the result falls within your stated margin of error, it is indistinguishable from random noise. Acting on this marginal gain risks a costly rollout based on a false positive. Furthermore, without confirmation of a pre-committed sample size or a sequential testing rule, any claim of significance before the test concludes is invalid ("peeking").

However, the 8% decrease in Cost Per Install (CPI) indicates a potential win in **organic discoverability**, not direct appeal. This suggests the orange icon may perform better in search listings or category browsability, even if it does not drive higher curiosity clicks from known impressions. This is a nuanced finding that separates "appeal" from "visibility."

**Recommended Actions for Q2 Strategy:**

1.  **Halt Rebrand Rollout:** Maintain the blue icon as the primary asset. The 1.2% CTR lift is `UNVERIFIED` as a win; deploying it across all marketing assets assumes a causal link the data does not support.
2.  **Isolate the Discoverability Hypothesis:** Launch a follow-up test specifically designed to measure **organic search conversion** vs. **direct impression CTR**. Do not mix these metrics. If the orange icon truly aids discoverability, a test isolating search traffic should show a significant lift in conversion from search impressions, even if direct CTR remains flat.
3.  **Pre-commit the Next Design:** Before testing a new element (e.g., shape, logo scale), define the **primary metric** (e.g., Store Conversion Rate) and a **guardrail** (e.g., Brand Sentiment or Retention Day-1). Calculate the required sample size to detect a meaningful effect (e.g., >3%) and run the test to completion without interim checks.

**Tripwire for Future Tests:**
If a future test shows a CPI reduction >5% but a CTR lift <1% after reaching pre-committed significance, proceed with a **search-only** asset update rather than a full brand overhaul.

Pull the Jan 10–24 data segmented by **traffic source** (Search vs. Browse vs. Direct) to verify if the CPI drop correlates strictly with organic search volume. If the split data is unavailable, label the CPI finding `UNVERIFIED` until instrumented.
