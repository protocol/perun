### Diagnostic Approach: Isolating Checkout Leakage

Do not assume price sensitivity yet; your low bounce rate (15%) confirms interest, but the <1% conversion indicates a catastrophic failure in the middle or end of the funnel. To isolate the leakage without surveys, instrument and analyze these four specific micro-conversions immediately:

1.  **Product Detail Page (PDP) → Cart Addition Rate**
    *   **The Question:** Are users engaging with the product but refusing to commit to the cart?
    *   **The Metric:** % of PDP viewers who click "Add to Cart."
    *   **Diagnosis:** If this drops below 10%, the barrier is likely **price visibility** or **stock anxiety** on the PDP. If users add to cart but don't proceed, the issue is downstream.

2.  **Cart View → Checkout Initiation Rate**
    *   **The Question:** Is the "sticker shock" happening when shipping/tax estimates appear?
    *   **The Metric:** % of users who view the cart page vs. those who click "Proceed to Checkout."
    *   **Diagnosis:** A sharp drop here strongly suggests **hidden costs** (shipping/taxes) revealed only at this stage. This is your primary suspect for "price sensitivity."

3.  **Checkout Step 1 (Info) → Step 2 (Payment) Completion**
    *   **The Question:** Is the form friction or account creation forcing abandonment?
    *   **The Metric:** % of users who enter shipping info vs. those who reach the payment selection screen.
    *   **Diagnosis:** High drop-off here indicates **process friction** (forced account creation, complex forms) rather than price.

4.  **Payment Selection → Transaction Success**
    *   **The Question:** Are payment methods failing or declining?
    *   **The Metric:** % of users who select a payment method vs. completed orders.
    *   **Diagnosis:** Drop-off here points to **technical failures** or lack of preferred payment options (e.g., no Buy Now, Pay Later for high-ticket home goods).

**Immediate Action Plan for Friday's Presentation:**
Pull cohort data for the last 30 days segmented by these four steps. Visualize the funnel as a "leaky bucket" chart. The step with the largest percentage point drop is your critical failure point. If the drop is largest at **Cart → Checkout**, your hypothesis regarding late-stage shipping costs is confirmed, and inventory pricing strategy is secondary to checkout UX fixes. If the drop is at **PDP → Cart**, then price or product presentation is the root cause.

**Note:** Do not rely on aggregate "conversion rate" alone; the specific step-wise drop-off rates are the only metrics that justify inventory or UX changes. Ensure your analytics tool tracks these events with a stable user ID to prevent double-counting across sessions.
