### Critical Funnel Stages for SafeHome Quotes Onboarding

To diagnose the drop-off between address entry and coverage selection, you must split your current "Quote Start to Quote Complete" phase into intent-based micro-steps. Page URLs are insufficient because a single URL often hosts multiple distinct user decisions. Define the following five stages to align with user intent and enable precise session recording triggers:

1.  **Intent Declaration (Address Entry Complete)**
    *   **User Intent:** "I am ready to see if I can get insured."
    *   **Trigger Event:** `address_submitted` (Success response from address validation API).
    *   **Why this matters:** This is your current funnel entry. It confirms the user has passed the initial friction point.

2.  **Risk Assessment Loading (The "Black Box" Gap)**
    *   **User Intent:** "I am waiting to see my options."
    *   **Trigger Event:** `risk_calculation_started` (Immediately after address submit) AND `risk_calculation_completed` (Data received, UI ready to render).
    *   **Why this matters:** This is likely where your "massive drop-off" hides. If users leave between `started` and `completed`, the issue is **performance or perceived wait time**, not UI friction. If they leave after `completed` but before clicking next, the issue is **price shock or confusing options**. You cannot fix the problem without separating these two states.

3.  **Option Consideration (Quote Displayed)**
    *   **User Intent:** "I am evaluating if this price/coverage fits my needs."
    *   **Trigger Event:** `quote_options_viewed` (Core coverage cards rendered in viewport).
    *   **Why this matters:** This validates that the user actually saw the offer. If `risk_calculation_completed` fires but `quote_options_viewed` does not, you have a rendering bug or a layout issue hiding the content.

4.  **Coverage Customization (The Decision Point)**
    *   **User Intent:** "I am adjusting this to fit my specific situation."
    *   **Trigger Event:** `coverage_limits_interacted` (Any slider move, dropdown change, or toggle click on limits).
    *   **Why this matters:** Your team disagrees on whether "selecting coverage" is one step or two. It is two. **Viewing** defaults is passive; **interacting** with limits is active intent. A drop-off between *Viewed* and *Interacted* means the default options are unacceptable or confusing. A drop-off after *Interacted* means the "Continue" button or the final price update is the blocker.

5.  **Commitment (Selection Finalized)**
    *   **User Intent:** "I accept these terms and want to proceed."
    *   **Trigger Event:** `coverage_selected` (User clicks "Continue" or "Select Plan" with a valid configuration).
    *   **Why this matters:** This is your new, accurate "Quote Complete" start point.

**Implementation Note for UX Research:**
Configure session recording triggers to start specifically at `risk_calculation_started` and stop at `coverage_selected`. This isolates the exact 20–40 second window where you are losing users, filtering out the 80% of sessions that drop off before address entry.

**Data Warning:**
Your assumption that "entering address early makes users 40% more likely to finish" is currently `UNVERIFIED`. Without the `risk_calculation_completed` timestamp, you cannot distinguish between users who quit because the system was slow versus those who quit because the price was too high. Do not optimize the form flow until you measure the duration between Step 2 start and end.
