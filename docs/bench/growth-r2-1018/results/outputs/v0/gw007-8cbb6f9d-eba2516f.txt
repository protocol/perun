**Briefing: Monitoring Free-Tier Limit Adjustment Trial**

**Objective**
Observe user reactions to the reduced 5GB free-tier cap to validate conversion lift while detecting early signs of dissatisfaction or churn. Do not assume success; watch for the "exodus" risk where users leave before experiencing premium value.

**1. Specific User Actions to Monitor**
Track these behaviors daily to distinguish between healthy conversion and negative friction:

*   **The "Wall" Reaction:** Monitor what users do immediately upon hitting the 5GB limit.
    *   *Positive Signal:* Clicks "Upgrade" or views pricing pages within 24 hours of hitting the limit.
    *   *Negative Signal:* Immediate cessation of uploads, manual file deletion to stay under the limit, or account inactivity for >3 days post-limit.
*   **Support & Feedback Volume:** Track inbound tickets and chat logs tagged with "storage limit," "too small," or "competitor name."
    *   *Action:* Flag any mention of "canceling" or "switching" within 48 hours of hitting the limit.
*   **Feature Adoption Pre-Limit:** Compare usage depth of users who hit 5GB vs. those who didn't.
    *   *Risk Indicator:* If users hitting the limit have *low* engagement with core features (e.g., file sharing, version history) prior to the cap, they are unlikely to convert and highly likely to churn. They haven't seen the value yet.
*   **Referral & Sharing Activity:** Monitor if existing free users stop sharing folders with new users (a leading indicator of reduced viral growth).

**2. Interpreting Early Warning Signs**
Categorize feedback and data into three buckets for the Customer Success team:

*   **Category A: Friction (Expected)**
    *   *Signs:* Users ask "Why did my limit change?" or "How do I free up space?" but continue logging in.
    *   *Interpretation:* Normal adjustment. Provide clear guides on managing storage. Do not intervene with discounts yet.
*   **Category B: Value Gap (Critical Risk)**
    *   *Signs:* Users hit the limit, delete files to stay free, and never visit the pricing page. Support tickets mention "not enough space for basic use."
    *   *Interpretation:* The 5GB cap is too low for the "aha" moment. These users will not convert; they are being pushed to competitors. **Action:** Escalate to product immediately; this indicates the hypothesis is failing for this segment.
*   **Category C: Exodus (Emergency)**
    *   *Signs:* Sudden spike in account deletion requests or mentions of specific competitors (e.g., "moving to Dropbox") in support chats within 72 hours of the limit notification.
    *   *Interpretation:* Brand damage is occurring. The constraint is viewed as punitive rather than a nudge. **Action:** Halt the trial for new users if this exceeds 5% of the affected cohort.

**3. Instructions for Customer Success**
*   **Tagging Protocol:** Every ticket related to storage limits must be tagged with the user's current usage % and days since signup. This distinguishes new users (high churn risk) from mature users (higher conversion potential).
*   **Response Script:** Do not apologize for the limit change. Instead, ask: "What specific file type or workflow were you trying to save when you hit the limit?" This reveals if the cap blocks core value.
*   **Escalation Trigger:** If you receive three reports of "switching to competitor" from users who signed up in the last 14 days, notify the Product Lead immediately.

**Note:** Baseline these metrics against the current 10GB cohort behavior before the trial starts; without this internal comparison, you cannot distinguish normal churn from trial-induced damage.
Do not estimate conversion rates; rely strictly on the observed ratio of "Limit Hit" to "Upgrade Clicked" versus "Limit Hit" to "Account Closed."
