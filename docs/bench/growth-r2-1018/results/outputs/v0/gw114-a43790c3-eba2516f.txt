**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Banks completing a compliant report export.
Do not measure Daily Active Users (DAU) or raw logins. In enterprise banking, users perform specific weekly compliance tasks; DAU will appear artificially low and mislead leadership into thinking adoption is failing. The value is delivered only when a bank successfully generates and exports a report, not when they log in. Track the count of unique banks performing this action per week.

**Input Metrics (The Funnel):**
1.  **Activation Rate:** Percentage of pilot banks that generate their first report within 14 days of go-live.
2.  **Retention Rate:** Percentage of pilot banks that generate a report in Week 4, Week 8, and Week 12. This is your primary health indicator; if they stop exporting, the product is failing regardless of speed.
3.  **Efficiency Gain (UNVERIFIED):** You stated speed is critical but have no baseline for manual reporting time. You cannot claim a "time saved" benchmark yet. Instrument the event `report_generation_duration` (timestamp start to export complete) immediately. Until you have this data, any claim of efficiency is `UNVERIFIED`.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument only these):**
*   `user_login`: Answers "Did they access the system?" (Context only, not a success metric).
*   `report_generation_started`: Answers "Did they attempt the core task?"
*   `report_export_completed`: Answers "Did they receive value?" (Primary event).
*   `report_generation_duration`: Answers "Is it faster than manual?" (Requires baseline collection).

**Pilot Success Criteria (Concrete Numbers for Leadership):**
Since you cannot use external benchmarks, define success against your own pilot baseline:
*   **Adoption:** 3/3 pilot banks achieve `report_export_completed` at least once every 7 days by Month 2.
*   **Stickiness:** 3/3 pilot banks retain the weekly export rhythm through Month 4 (end of implementation).
*   **Efficiency:** Establish the median `report_generation_duration` by Month 2. Success for the wider rollout is defined as maintaining or improving this median as volume scales to 50 banks.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Deploy tracking for `report_export_completed` and `report_generation_duration` this week. Do not track "page views" or "time on site."
2.  **Baseline Capture:** Add a single optional field in the pilot onboarding survey asking admins: "How many minutes did your last manual compliance report take?" Use this only to triangulate the initial `UNVERIFIED` efficiency gap until your system data matures.
3.  **Review Cadence:** Present a weekly retention cohort chart to leadership showing the 3 pilot banks. If any bank misses a weekly export, trigger a customer success intervention immediately.

**Note:** Do not fabricate a "good" retention percentage; compare the pilots only to their own previous week's activity.
