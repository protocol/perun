### Recommended Primary Conversion Funnel for DriveShare

For a peer-to-peer rental model where identity verification is a known friction point, your primary optimization funnel must isolate **committed intent** from **casual browsing**. Starting at "Landing Page" or "App Download" introduces noise from users who are not yet ready to rent, masking the specific drop-off caused by your verification step.

**Define your primary funnel as follows:**

*   **Start Point:** **First Search Completed** (User enters dates/location and views available cars).
    *   *Rationale:* This action signals explicit intent to rent. Users prior to this are browsing; users after this are attempting to transact. This isolates the verification drop-off from general acquisition noise.
*   **Step 2:** **Identity Verification Initiated** (User clicks "Verify ID" or uploads first document).
    *   *Measurement:* Track the % of searchers who attempt verification.
*   **Step 3:** **Identity Verification Completed** (System returns "Verified" status).
    *   *Measurement:* Track the % of initiators who pass. This is your critical leak (currently ~40% completion).
*   **Step 4:** **Booking Confirmed** (Payment successful and rental booked).
    *   *Measurement:* Track the % of verified users who complete a rental.
*   **End Point:** **Completed Rental** (Trip started).

**Why this scope works for your UX tests:**
By starting at "First Search," you exclude users who downloaded the app just to check prices. Your UX researchers can now focus usability tests specifically on the transition from *Search* → *Verify* → *Book*. If users drop off before searching, that is a discovery/pricing issue, not a funnel friction issue. If they drop off during verification, your hypothesis (simplifying the 4-minute process) is directly testable against this specific segment.

**North Star Metric Alignment:**
Do not optimize for "Accounts Created." Optimize for **Weekly Verified Users Who Complete a Booking**. Account creation without verification is vanity; verification without booking is waste.

**Immediate Next Steps for UX Team:**
1.  Instrument events for `search_completed`, `verification_started`, `verification_passed`, and `booking_confirmed` with a stable pseudonymous user ID.
2.  Run usability tests only on users who triggered `search_completed` but failed to trigger `verification_passed`.

*Note: Ensure the "stable user ID" used for cohorting is pseudonymous; route any PII collection details to your privacy compliance review. Do not estimate the 20% lift until the new funnel data is live; compare only against your own historical baseline.*
