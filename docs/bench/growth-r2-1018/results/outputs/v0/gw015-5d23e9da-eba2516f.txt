### Diagnostic Approach: Funnel Leakage & Cohort Integrity

Your flat revenue despite a 35% spend increase indicates a breakdown in funnel efficiency, not necessarily a total market failure. Per the growth analytics method, we must read the funnel **bottom-up**. Pouring more acquisition spend into a leaky retention or activation bucket is the primary waste scenario here. Do not assume the creatives are working; assume the downstream experience is failing to convert the new volume until proven otherwise.

**Core Hypotheses to Test**
1.  **The "Leaky Bucket" Hypothesis:** New traffic from expanded ad spend has lower intent or poorer fit, causing activation (first purchase) rates to drop proportionally to the spend increase, neutralizing revenue gains.
2.  **The Retention Dip Hypothesis:** Your returning customer rate has declined due to product satisfaction, inventory issues, or post-purchase experience, masking any gains from new users.
3.  **The Attribution Lag Hypothesis:** New creatives have longer consideration cycles, and the 35% spend hit too recently to show in monthly revenue (though less likely if spend started early in the quarter).

**Immediate Data Cuts for Engineering (Request These Today)**
Do not ask for "everything." Request these specific slices to answer named questions:

*   **Cut 1: Activation Rate by Traffic Source (Last 90 Days)**
    *   *Question:* Did the conversion rate from "Landing Page View" to "First Purchase" drop for the new campaigns compared to the baseline?
    *   *Metric:* `(First Purchases / Sessions)` segmented by `Campaign_ID` and `Channel`.
    *   *Look for:* A divergence where session volume rose 35% but conversion rate fell by ~26% (mathematically offsetting the gain).

*   **Cut 2: Cohort Retention Curve (Jan–Mar vs. Oct–Dec)**
    *   *Question:* Has the returning customer rate dipped for users acquired in Q1 compared to Q4?
    *   *Metric:* `% of users making a 2nd purchase within 30/60/90 days` grouped by `Acquisition_Month`.
    *   *Look for:* A steeper drop-off in the recent cohort curve, indicating a quality or product issue unrelated to ad spend.

*   **Cut 3: Revenue Composition (New vs. Returning)**
    *   *Question:* Is total revenue flat because new revenue grew but returning revenue collapsed?
    *   *Metric:* `Total Revenue` split by `User_Type` (New vs. Returning) month-over-month.
    *   *Look for:* Flat total line hiding a swap: rising new user revenue canceling out a sharp decline in loyalist spend.

*   **Cut 4: Cart Abandonment by Device/Source**
    *   *Question:* Is the new traffic hitting a technical friction point (e.g., mobile site speed, payment gateway errors)?
    *   *Metric:* `Checkout Start` to `Purchase Complete` drop-off rate, segmented by `Device` and `Campaign`.
    *   *Look for:* Spikes in abandonment specific to the new high-spend channels.

**This Week's Step**
Run these four queries immediately. If Cut 1 shows a proportional drop in conversion, the issue is **traffic quality/creative mismatch**. If Cut 2 or 3 shows a decline in returning users, the issue is **product/retention**, and the ad spend is irrelevant to the root cause. Present the specific drop-off percentage in Thursday's sync; do not present total revenue alone.
