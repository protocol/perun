**Recommendation: Prioritize the "Upload-to-View" transition for immediate engineering investment.**

While the "Share with Colleague" step is a strong signal of viral growth and team adoption, the "Upload-to-View" drop-off represents a fundamental failure of core value delivery. In a legal tech context, if a lawyer uploads a contract but does not view the analysis, the product has provided zero utility. No amount of collaboration features can compensate for a broken core promise.

**Rationale for Prioritization**

1.  **Retention Foundation:** You reported a 30% drop-off between upload and viewing results. This is a critical leak in the retention bucket. According to the principle of reading the funnel bottom-up, acquisition (200 firms onboarded) is wasted if the core action (receiving and viewing analysis) is not retained. If users do not trust or see the value in the initial analysis, they will never reach the stage where sharing becomes relevant. Fixing the "Share" step for users who never see the results is optimizing a feature for a non-existent audience.
2.  **Value Verification:** Your hypothesis states that conversion depends on receiving an analysis within two minutes. The 30% who drop off before viewing results are explicitly failing this hypothesis. Whether due to latency, UI confusion, or anxiety about the tool's accuracy, this group never validates your core value proposition. Until this flow is seamless, measuring "Share" as a predictor of revenue is premature; you cannot have long-term retention without successful short-term activation.
3.  **Revenue Impact:** Enterprise contract value relies on individual user reliance before team-wide rollout. A lawyer who consistently gets fast, accurate analyses (high "Upload-to-View" success) becomes an internal champion. Only *after* this individual success is guaranteed does the "Share" step drive the multi-seat expansion that increases contract value. Therefore, "Upload-to-View" is the primary driver of individual conversion, which is the prerequisite for enterprise expansion.

**Measurement Plan for the CTO**

To maximize impact next month, engineering resources should focus on instrumenting and optimizing the **Upload-to-View** journey. Define the North Star metric as **"Weekly Active Lawyers who View an Analysis."**

*   **Instrumentation:** Track the specific event `analysis_viewed` immediately following `document_uploaded`. Do not track generic page views; ensure the event fires only when the analysis content is rendered and visible to the user.
*   **Segmentation:** Cohort users by law firm size and document type to identify if the 30% drop-off is concentrated in specific segments (e.g., larger firms with stricter firewalls or complex contract types).
*   **Experiment Design:** Before deploying fixes, pre-commit to an A/B test design. Define a primary metric (increase in `analysis_viewed` rate) and a guardrail metric (no increase in average processing time). Do not peek at results daily; run the test for a pre-determined duration or sample size to ensure statistical validity.

The "Share" step should be measured as a secondary input metric for future growth phases, but it is not the bottleneck for current revenue success. Fix the core value delivery first.
