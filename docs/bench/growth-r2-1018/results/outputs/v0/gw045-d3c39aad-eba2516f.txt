**North Star Metric: Weekly Active Matches (WAM)**

Define a "Match" as a student-tutor pair that has completed **three sessions**. Your North Star is the count of these active matches engaging in at least one session per week.

**Why this matters more than registration counts:**
Registration numbers (300 students, 45 tutors) are vanity metrics; they rise regardless of whether learning occurs. A student can sign up and never attend a class, inflating your dashboard while your actual impact remains zero. "Total hours taught" is slightly better but can be gamed by a few power users while the majority drop out. "Percentage returning for a second term" is a lagging indicator—you won't know it until months later, too late to fix current leaks.

WAM measures **realized value**. It only increases when a student and tutor successfully coordinate, show up, and derive educational benefit repeatedly. For donors, this proves you are not just a directory of names, but a functioning engine for education. If WAM is flat while signups rise, you have an activation problem, not a marketing problem.

**Implementation Plan (Feasible for Non-Engineers):**
Do not build complex dashboards. Use your existing communication logs (WhatsApp groups, SMS records, or simple Google Sheets).
1.  **Instrumentation:** Create a shared sheet with columns: `Student ID`, `Tutor ID`, `Session Date`, `Session Count`.
2.  **Tracking:** Tutors or coordinators log a row after every session.
3.  **Calculation:** Weekly, filter for pairs where `Session Count` ≥ 3 and `Session Date` is within the last 7 days. Count the unique pairs.

**The Story for Your June Report:**
Instead of saying "We onboarded 300 students," say: "We activated **X Weekly Active Matches**, meaning X pairs of students and tutors are consistently meeting weekly to drive educational progress." This shifts the narrative from "potential" to "proof."

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  Audit your current logs to identify how many of the 300 students have actually completed three sessions.
2.  Establish this number as your baseline; if it is zero or low, your immediate focus is not acquiring more students, but ensuring existing signups complete their third session.
3.  Share this baseline with your team as the single number that defines success for the next quarter.

*Note: Since you lack automated tools, manual logging must be frictionless; integrate it into the tutor's existing workflow to ensure compliance.*
*Avoid tracking "hours" until "matches" are stable; duration is irrelevant if the relationship doesn't persist.*
