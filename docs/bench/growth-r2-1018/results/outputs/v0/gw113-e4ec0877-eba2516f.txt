**Primary Metric (North Star): Monthly Active Renters Completing ≥2 Items**

This is your single North Star. It directly correlates to your validated retention driver (users renting ≥2 items stay 6+ months) and represents realized customer value. Without this behavior, revenue churns and environmental impact is zero.

**Supporting Input Metrics:**
1.  **Financial Viability:** *Net Revenue Retention (NRR)*. Track if existing subscribers upgrade, maintain, or downgrade their rental volume over time. This proves unit economics better than raw subscriber counts.
2.  **Environmental Impact:** *Carbon Saved per Active Renter*. Calculate this as a derivative metric based on the North Star volume. Do not track "carbon saved per transaction" as a primary goal; optimizing for individual transactions can encourage single-item rentals, which your data shows leads to churn.

**Why "Carbon Saved Per Transaction" Must Not Be Primary:**
Prioritizing impact per transaction creates a perverse incentive to limit rental frequency or push single high-impact items, directly contradicting your retention data. Your business model only works if users rent repeatedly. Environmental impact is a *result* of high retention and frequency, not a substitute for it.

**Measurement Framework for Q2:**
*   **Retention First:** Diagnose your funnel bottom-up. Before acquiring new subscribers, verify that current users are hitting the "2 items" threshold in Month 1. If this leaks, acquisition spend is wasted.
*   **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument only events that answer specific questions:
    *   `rental_completed` (properties: user_id, item_count, timestamp) → Answers: "Did they hit the activation threshold?"
    *   `subscription_renewed` → Answers: "Did the activation lead to retention?"
    *   Do not instrument "page views" or "browse events" unless they directly predict the 2-item threshold.
*   **Reporting Structure:**
    *   **To the CFO:** Present the North Star trend alongside NRR. Show that driving the "2-item" behavior directly extends Lifetime Value (LTV).
    *   **To the Sustainability Board:** Present total carbon saved as an *output* of the North Star growth. "Because X% more users hit the 2-item threshold, total carbon saved increased by Y%."
