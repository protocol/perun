### Tracking Specification for NexusRealms Staging (Deadline: Aug 15)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users (WAU) completing **Value-Generating Actions** (e.g., crafting, quest completion, PVP matches), *not* raw token volume. Token velocity is a lagging indicator of speculation; genuine engagement drives long-term economy health.

**Event Taxonomy & Properties**
Instrument only events that answer specific questions about bot vs. human behavior and earner vs. buyer retention. Do not track "everything."

| Event Name | Question Answered | Required Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `session_start` | Is this a persistent human or a script? | `wallet_id` (pseudonymous), `client_fingerprint` (hash of UA/screen res), `time_since_last_action` |
| `core_action_complete` | Did the user derive value? (North Star input) | `wallet_id`, `action_type` (craft/quest/pvp), `duration_seconds`, `token_cost`, `token_reward` |
| `token_acquire` | What is the source of funds? | `wallet_id`, `source_type` (**enum**: `earned_gameplay`, `bought_exchange`, `bridged_external`), `amount`, `tx_hash` |
| `token_spend` | Is circulation speculative or functional? | `wallet_id`, `sink_type` (**enum**: `gameplay_fee`, `marketplace_buy`, `liquidity_pool`), `amount`, `counterparty_wallet_id` |
| `item_trade` | Is trading organic or wash-trading? | `wallet_id`, `item_id`, `price_token`, `counterparty_wallet_id`, `time_held_before_trade` |

**Differentiating Bots from Humans**
To detect speculation/bots without PII, engineer these derived metrics in your analytics layer:
1.  **Action Regularity:** Humans have variable intervals; bots often act at fixed intervals. Track `time_since_last_action` variance per wallet.
2.  **Session Depth:** Bots often perform one action (trade) and leave. Humans engage in sessions with multiple `core_action_complete` events.
3.  **Wash-Trade Detection:** Flag trades where `wallet_id` and `counterparty_wallet_id` share the same `client_fingerprint` or IP subnet (if available legally), or where `time_held_before_trade` is near zero repeatedly.

**Cohort Strategy: Earners vs. Buyers**
You cannot distinguish these groups without tagging the *source* of the token at the moment of acquisition.
*   **Cohort A (Earners):** Users whose first `token_acquire` event has `source_type: earned_gameplay`.
*   **Cohort B (Buyers):** Users whose first `token_acquire` event has `source_type: bought_exchange`.
*   **Retention Query:** Compare the % of Cohort A vs. Cohort B returning to perform a `core_action_complete` in Week 1, Week 2, and Week 4.

**Identifier Seam & Privacy**
*   **Stable ID:** Use the `wallet_id` as the primary key for cross-session tracking. This is stable but pseudonymous.
*   **PII Routing:** Do not store IP addresses or raw device fingerprints in the product analytics database. Hash them immediately upon ingestion. Route any linkage keys to your privacy compliance store (`privacy-compliance.md`), not the growth dashboard.
*   **Bot Logic:** Do not attempt to block bots in tracking; instrument the *signals* so the data team can filter them post-hoc.

2.  **Data:** Define the SQL/logic for "Wash Trade Flag" based on `counterparty_wallet_id` and `time_held_before_trade` to run on Day 1 data.
