### Recommended Metrics for MedInsight ROI Demonstration

To demonstrate value without accessing protected health information (PHI) or Electronic Health Records (EHR), you must shift from tracking **vanity metrics** (logins, clicks) to **actionable efficiency proxies**. These metrics measure the *reduction* of administrative friction, which a Chief Medical Officer (CMO) accepts as a leading indicator of improved patient care capacity.

Here are four instrumentable metrics that connect usage to hospital performance:

**1. Task Completion Velocity (Time-to-Insight)**
*   **The Question:** "How much faster can a nurse or administrator generate a required report or view a critical status compared to the manual baseline?"
*   **Instrumentation:** Measure the timestamp delta between `session_start` (or dashboard load) and `report_exported` / `critical_view_confirmed`.
*   **ROI Narrative:** If your dashboard reduces a 20-minute manual data pull to 2 minutes, you have quantified **18 minutes of nursing time returned to patient care** per event. Multiply by daily frequency to show hours saved per week. This is a direct proxy for operational efficiency.

**2. Alert Resolution Latency**
*   **The Question:** "Does seeing an anomaly in our dashboard lead to faster administrative action?"
*   **Instrumentation:** Track the time between `alert_viewed` and the subsequent `action_taken` event (e.g., `staff_reassigned`, `inventory_ordered`, or `ticket_created` within your tool or via a tracked integration).
*   **ROI Narrative:** Reduced latency in addressing operational bottlenecks (like staffing gaps or supply shortages) directly correlates to reduced patient wait times. You are measuring the **speed of operational response**, a key driver of patient flow.

**3. Cross-Departmental Data Unification Rate**
*   **The Question:** "Are we eliminating the need for manual data reconciliation between departments?"
*   **Instrumentation:** Count instances where a single user accesses data views spanning multiple previously siloed domains (e.g., viewing `ER_wait_times` and `ICU_bed_availability` in a single session without switching systems).
*   **ROI Narrative:** Hospital administrators often waste hours manually merging spreadsheets from different units. High unification rates prove you are **reducing administrative fragmentation**, allowing leadership to make holistic decisions faster, which improves overall hospital throughput.

**4. Proactive Intervention Frequency**
*   **The Question:** "Are users acting on trends before they become crises?"
*   **Instrumentation:** Track the usage of predictive or trend-based features (e.g., `forecast_viewed`) followed by `preventative_action_logged` within a defined window (e.g., 24 hours).
*   **ROI Narrative:** This demonstrates a shift from reactive firefighting to proactive management. For a CMO, this proxies **risk mitigation**—preventing the conditions that lead to readmissions or safety incidents by addressing operational root causes early.

### Implementation & Privacy Constraints
*   **Identifier Seam:** To track these flows across sessions, you need a **stable, pseudonymous user ID** per hospital staff member. Do not use names or MRNs. Route any mapping of these IDs to real identities strictly to your `privacy-compliance.md` protocols; the analytics engine must only see the pseudonymous token.
*   **Baseline Requirement:** You cannot claim "X minutes saved" without a baseline. Your first step in Q3 is to instrument these events and run them for 30 days to establish the **current median time**. Your sales conversation in Q4 will compare the *post-implementation* trend against this *customer-specific baseline*, not an industry average.
