### North Star Metric
**Weekly Successful Matches Completed.**
This metric rises only when real value is delivered to both sides. It rejects vanity metrics like "total job posts" or "registered users," which can increase even if the marketplace is broken.

### Defining Success & Balance
*   **Agency Success:** Time-to-Hire under 5 days with a clear scope.
*   **Freelancer Success:** Proposal-to-Offer conversion rate (are they winning work when they apply?).
*   **Marketplace Balance:** Do not measure total supply vs. total demand. Instead, measure **Fill Rate per Week** (percentage of jobs posted that are filled within 7 days). If this drops, the market is imbalanced regardless of total user counts.

### Diagnosing the Bottleneck (The Funnel)
Your assumption that "demand outpaces supply" may be incorrect. The delay could be friction, not scarcity. We must read the data bottom-up, starting with retention of the core action, before assuming we need more users.

Track these three specific events to isolate the leak:
1.  **Proposal Sent Rate:** (Proposals sent / Jobs posted).
    *   *If low:* Freelancers are ignoring posts. This confirms your fear of "unclear scopes" or low supply interest.
    *   *Action:* Audit job descriptions for clarity; do not recruit more freelancers yet.
2.  **Negotiation Conversion Rate:** (Offers made / Proposals sent).
    *   *If low:* Friction is in the negotiation phase. Agencies and freelancers are connecting but failing to agree on terms.
    *   *Action:* Investigate pricing disputes or contract complexity.
3.  **Time-to-Acceptance:** (Days from Offer to Acceptance).
    *   *If high:* The delay is purely administrative after the match is effectively made.

**Current Status:** Without your internal data on these three rates, the root cause is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not guess; instrument these three events immediately.
