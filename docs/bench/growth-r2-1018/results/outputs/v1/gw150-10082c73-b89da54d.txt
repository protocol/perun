### NeonQuest Analytics Funnel Design (Soft Launch)

**1. North Star Metric**
**Weekly Active Players completing the Core Loop.**
*Definition:* Unique users who complete at least three full game levels or matches within a 7-day window.
*Rejection of Vanity:* Do not use "Total Downloads" or "Raw Session Starts." These rise regardless of value and mislead on engagement.

**2. The Funnel (Bottom-Up Diagnosis)**
Diagnose retention before acquisition. If users do not return to the core loop, acquisition spend is wasted.
*   **Retention (The Foundation):** % of users returning to complete the Core Loop in Week 1 and Week 2.
*   **Activation:** % of new installs who complete the "Aha!" moment (e.g., first successful level + first social share attempt) within 24 hours.
*   **Acquisition:** Source breakdown (Organic Social vs. Ads) *only* for users who reached Activation.
*   **Revenue:** % of activated users making a first In-App Purchase (IAP).

**3. Event Taxonomy & The "Short Burst" Seam**
Instrument only events that answer specific questions. Do not track "everything."
*   **Event:** `level_complete` (Properties: `level_id`, `duration_ms`, `offline_flag`).
    *   *Question:* Are users progressing through content regardless of session length?
*   **Event:** `social_share_attempt` (Properties: `platform`, `success_bool`).
    *   *Question:* Is social sharing actually driving the hypothesized organic growth?
*   **Event:** `iap_initiated` → `iap_completed`.
    *   *Question:* Where is the drop-off before monetization?

**Handling Intermittent Connectivity & Short Bursts:**
Do not rely on formal "Session Start/End" events as your primary unit of analysis, as 2-minute bursts and offline play will break this model.
*   **Strategy:** Shift the atomic unit from "Session" to "Action." Aggregate engagement based on `level_complete` timestamps.
*   **Offline Logic:** Tag events with `offline_flag: true` when detected locally. Queue these events and transmit with a `delay_ms` property upon reconnection.
*   **Churn Definition:** A user is churned only if they fail to complete *any* core action for 14 days, not merely if they stop starting formal sessions. This prevents misinterpreting intermittent play as loss.

**4. Identifier Seam & Privacy**
*   **Stable ID:** You must generate a stable, pseudonymous `user_cohort_id` stored locally to link actions across offline/online states and device restarts.
*   **Compliance:** Do not store raw device IDs or PII in analytics events. Route all consent management and PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol (`privacy-compliance.md`). The analytics system receives only the pseudonymous token.

**5. Experiment Rigor (Pre-Launch Prep)**
When testing the "Social Sharing" hypothesis post-launch:
*   **Pre-commit:** Define the sample size and duration *before* looking at data.
*   **Guardrails:** If testing a new share incentive, the primary metric is `social_share_attempt`, but the guardrail must be `retention_day_7`. Do not ship a feature that increases shares but crashes retention.
*   **No Peeking:** A result observed on Day 2 of a 14-day test is `UNVERIFIED`. Do not treat it as a win.
