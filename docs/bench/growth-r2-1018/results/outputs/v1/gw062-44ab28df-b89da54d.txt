### Proposed Metrics for Enterprise Customer Health

To distinguish between feature adoption gaps and pricing sensitivity, replace "Total Signatures Sent" with these three metrics. Each directly correlates to renewal likelihood by measuring value realization rather than transactional volume.

**1. Workflow Integration Depth (WID)**
*   **Definition:** The average number of distinct internal systems (e.g., Salesforce, SAP, HRIS) or API endpoints connected per enterprise account that actively transmit documents within a 30-day window.
*   **Rationale:** High signature volume with low WID indicates users are treating SignFast as a standalone utility rather than an embedded workflow component. Utility users are highly price-sensitive and easily churned by competitors. Conversely, high WID creates switching costs; if signing is woven into their core ops, renewal becomes an operational necessity, not a pricing negotiation. A drop in WID preceding a churn event signals an adoption failure, not a price objection.

**2. Multi-Stakeholder Activation Rate**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of seats purchased on an enterprise contract that have completed at least one signature action in the last 14 days, specifically segmented by role (e.g., Legal vs. Sales vs. HR).
*   **Rationale:** In B2B, the buyer (CFO/VP) is rarely the sole user. If only 20% of licensed seats are active, the champion loses internal political capital to renew. Low activation suggests the tool hasn't permeated the client's culture, making the contract vulnerable to budget cuts. If activation is high (>80%) but churn occurs, the issue is likely pricing or specific feature gaps for power users. This metric isolates whether the problem is broad disuse (adoption) or specific dissatisfaction among heavy users.

**3. Critical Document Cycle Time Reduction**
*   **Definition:** The median time delta between "document sent" and "fully executed" for the customer's top 3 most frequent document templates, compared against their baseline from the first month of onboarding.
*   **Rationale:** Enterprise clients renew to solve speed and compliance bottlenecks. If cycle times have not improved (or have worsened) despite high signature counts, the tool is failing to deliver efficiency gains. This metric directly measures the "value" promise. If cycle times are improving but churn risk remains high, the friction is likely financial (pricing). If cycle times are stagnant, the friction is functional (workflow integration), guiding Customer Success to focus check-in calls on template optimization rather than discount discussions.

**Implementation Note:** These metrics require joining signature logs with account configuration data (for WID) and seat license tables (for Activation). Until instrumented, any claim about the root cause of the 88% renewal rate is `UNVERIFIED`.
