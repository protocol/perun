### Diagnosis & Immediate Action Plan for TeamSync

**Current Status: UNVERIFIED Root Cause**
The 18% rise in zero-active organizations and the 4-minute drop in session duration are critical signals, but they are **outcome metrics**, not root causes. Without instrumented event data, we cannot yet distinguish between a product failure, a pricing mismatch, or natural project completion. Do not attribute this to "market shift" or "competitors" without evidence; those are hypotheses, not facts.

**Step 1: Isolate the Leak (Retention First)**
Stop looking at acquisition or total signups. Focus entirely on the **retention of the core action** for existing enterprise cohorts.
*   **Action:** Segment the "zero active users" metric by **customer cohort start date** and **account size**.
*   **Question to Answer:** Is the churn concentrated in customers onboarded before March 1st (suggesting a change in value/pricing) or only in new March/April signups (suggesting a product regression or onboarding break)?
*   **Hypothesis Check:** If old customers are churning, check for a **guardrail metric** spike in error rates or latency (ops observability) around March 1st. If new customers are churning, the "core action" may no longer be activating.

**Step 2: Contextualize the Session Duration Drop**
A 4-minute drop is significant but ambiguous. It could mean users are frustrated and leaving early, or they are completing tasks faster due to a UI change.
*   **Action:** Correlate session duration with **completion rate of the core workflow** (e.g., "project created," "task assigned," "meeting scheduled").
*   **Decision Rule:**
    *   If duration ↓ AND completion rate ↓ → **Product Friction** (users can't finish).
    *   If duration ↓ AND completion rate → (stable) → **Efficiency Gain** (not a churn driver).
    *   If duration ↓ AND support tickets ↑ → **Usability Crisis**.

**Step 3: Rule Out External Factors (The "Project Ending" Theory)**
Before assuming a competitor win, validate the "natural lifecycle" hypothesis.
*   **Action:** Query the **ratio of "project archived/completed" events** vs. "account abandoned" events for the churned accounts.
*   **Data Needed:** You need an event `project_closed` with a reason property. If this event is not instrumented, you cannot distinguish between a successful project end and a churn. Mark this data gap as **UNVERIFIED** until tracked.

**Step 4: The Identifier Seam & Privacy**
To track these organizations across sessions, ensure you are using a **stable, pseudonymous organization ID**. Do not use raw user emails for this analysis. Route any PII handling required for direct customer outreach to your `privacy-compliance.md` protocol immediately.
