### Approach to Measure Offline Conversions

To attribute offline calls to marketing channels without intrusive user questions, implement **Dynamic Number Insertion (DNI)** immediately. This system swaps the phone number displayed on your website based on the visitor's traffic source (e.g., Google Ads shows Number A, Facebook shows Number B, Organic shows Number C). All calls route to your single existing line, but the backend logs which number was dialed, linking the call to the specific campaign.

**Execution Plan for November 15th Pilot:**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Deploy a DNI script on the booking landing pages. Map three unique tracking numbers to your three main advertising channels. Keep one static number for direct traffic/non-digital sources.
2.  **Data Integration:** Configure your telephony provider to export call logs (timestamp, dialed number, duration, outcome) daily. Merge this with your web analytics using the dialed number as the key.
3.  **Validation:** Run a parallel test for 48 hours where agents manually ask a random 5% of callers, "How did you hear about us?" solely to validate that the DNI mapping matches user self-reporting.

**Addressing Your Unknown (DNI Reliability):**
You cannot assume DNI works reliably across channels without a pre-commitment to a validation metric. Do not launch the full pilot assuming your 60% web-to-call hypothesis is true; that figure is currently **UNVERIFIED**. Instead, define success for the pilot as: "The sum of attributed calls via DNI matches ≥90% of total call volume within a ±5% margin of error compared to the manual validation sample." If the discrepancy exceeds this, the implementation has a technical gap (e.g., numbers not swapping correctly on mobile devices or specific ad platforms) rather than a user behavior issue.

### Metrics for the Regional Operations Director (Next Quarter)

Stop reporting the "12% conversion rate" in isolation, as it excludes the majority of your actual business volume. Report these three metrics instead:

1.  **True Omni-Channel Conversion Rate:**
    *   *Formula:* (Online Confirmations + Attributed Phone Confirmations) / Total Sessions.
    *   *Why:* This corrects the artificial drop from 18% to 12% by including the 4,200 monthly calls. It reflects the actual business health.

2.  **Channel-Specific Call Attribution Rate:**
    *   *Formula:* (Calls from Channel X / Total Calls from Channel X's Traffic) per channel.
    *   *Why:* This replaces your assumption. It tells you exactly which of the three advertising channels drives phone bookings versus online bookings, allowing you to reallocate budget based on total value, not just digital clicks.

3.  **Call-to-Booking Efficiency:**
    *   *Formula:* (Confirmed Reservations via Phone / Total Calls Received).
    *   *Why:* This isolates the performance of your phone line operations separate from website UX. If this is low, the issue is agent training or wait times, not marketing attribution.
