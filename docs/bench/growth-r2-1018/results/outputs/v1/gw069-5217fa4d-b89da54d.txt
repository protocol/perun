**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Contributors completing a **Validated Core Edit**.

This metric counts unique volunteers who, within a 7-day window, submit at least one map feature that passes your existing quality assurance (QA) checks (e.g., geometry validation, tag completeness, or peer review). It explicitly rejects "total signups" or "raw edit attempts" as vanity metrics, as your data shows these rise while real value (retention) flatlines.

**Why this metric:**
1.  **Filters Vanity:** It ignores the 900 volunteers from last year who signed up but never contributed value.
2.  **Enforces Quality:** By requiring the edit to be "validated," it prevents gaming the system with spam or low-quality data, satisfying NGO requirements for data accuracy.
3.  **Measures Retention:** Tracking this *weekly* forces a focus on whether users return, addressing your drop-off concern directly.

**Input Metrics (Drivers):**
To move the North Star, track these three specific inputs:
*   **Activation Rate:** % of new signups who complete their first *validated* edit within 48 hours. (Tests your "lowering barrier" hypothesis).
*   **Feedback Loop Latency:** Average time between a user's submission and their receipt of acceptance/rejection feedback. (Addresses your unknown regarding lack of feedback).
*   **Week-2 Retention:** % of users who submitted a validated edit in Week 1 and return to submit another in Week 2.
