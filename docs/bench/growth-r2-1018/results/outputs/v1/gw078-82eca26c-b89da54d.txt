**Decision: Prioritize Engagement (Session Length & 7-Day Streak Retention) for the Summer Update.**

**The Call:** Halt the increased ad frequency immediately. The 20% revenue lift is a `UNVERIFIED` gain because it lacks a long-term baseline; it is likely a one-time extraction of value that degrades the asset (the player base) required to generate future revenue. Do not optimize for immediate cash flow at the expense of the core loop until the retention hypothesis is proven with your own data.

**Risk/Benefit Evaluation**

**Option A: Prioritizing Revenue (Current Path)**
*   **Benefit:** Immediate 20% uplift in top-line revenue. This looks positive on a weekly P&L.
*   **Risk:** You have observed a 3-minute reduction in session length. In mobile gaming, session length is a leading indicator of retention. If players spend less time, they complete fewer core loops, earn fewer rewards, and form weaker habits.
*   **Long-term Consequence:** If your assumption holds (7-day streaks drive purchases), shortening sessions makes reaching that 7-day threshold harder. You risk creating a "leaky bucket": acquiring or monetizing users so aggressively that they churn before becoming high-LTV (Lifetime Value) whales. The 20% gain may vanish within 4–6 weeks as the cohort matures and churns faster than new users replace them.

**Option B: Prioritizing Engagement (Recommended Path)**
*   **Benefit:** Stabilizes the core loop. By restoring session length, you increase the probability of users hitting the 7-day streak, which you hypothesize drives the highest LTV.
*   **Risk:** Short-term revenue dip compared to the ad-heavy scenario. The board may question the "lost" 20% if not framed correctly.
*   **Long-term Consequence:** Builds a larger, stickier base of players who are statistically more likely to convert later. This maximizes the total addressable market for your premium items and battle passes.

**The Measurement Plan (No Benchmarks, Only Your Data)**

Do not guess the outcome; measure the trade-off using your own baseline.

1.  **North Star Metric:** Define this as **"Weekly Active Users completing the Core Loop"** (e.g., finishing a dungeon/quest), *not* raw logins or ad impressions. This measures value delivery, not vanity.
2.  **The Experiment (A/B Test):**
    *   **Design:** Split traffic 50/50. Group A sees current high ad frequency; Group B sees reduced frequency (restoring session length).
    *   **Pre-commitment:** Run this for **28 days** (4 full weeks) to capture at least one full monetization cycle. Do not peek at day 2 or day 7 to declare a winner; early reads inflate false positives.
    *   **Primary Metric:** Day-14 Retention Rate.
    *   **Guardrail Metric:** Revenue Per Daily Active User (RPD).
    *   **Decision Rule:** If Group B (Engagement) has equal or higher Day-14 retention *and* the RPD drop is less than the projected LTV gain from retained users, ship Group B. If RPD collapses without a retention lift, reconsider.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument the "7-Day Streak" Event:** Verify you can track exactly which users hit 7 consecutive logins and their subsequent purchase rate. If this data is missing, the 50% assumption is `UNVERIFIED`.
2.  **Launch the 28-Day A/B Test:** Compare "High Ad" vs. "Balanced Ad" cohorts.
3.  **Define the Tripwire:** If Day-7 retention in the High Ad group drops >5% relative to your May baseline, kill the test early to prevent permanent brand damage.

**Note:** The 3-minute session drop is a critical warning sign; in freemium models, time-in-app usually precedes monetization intent.
