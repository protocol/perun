### Core Metrics for Operational Health: Vietnam Expansion

To distinguish between supply-side inefficiencies (driver allocation) and demand-side bottlenecks (merchant preparation), you must instrument a **time-segmented event taxonomy** around the order lifecycle. Do not track aggregate "on-time" rates alone; they hide the specific leak. Track these four timestamped events for every order:

1.  **`order_confirmed`**: The moment the customer completes checkout.
2.  **`merchant_accepted`**: The moment the merchant acknowledges the order.
3.  **`order_ready_for_pickup`**: The moment the merchant marks the order as ready.
4.  **`driver_assigned`**: The moment your algorithm links a driver to the order.
5.  **`driver_arrived_at_merchant`**: The moment the driver checks in at the location.
6.  **`order_picked_up`**: The moment the driver confirms possession.
7.  **`order_delivered`**: The moment the customer receives the goods.

**Derived Metrics for Decision Making**

Calculate the following durations from the events above to isolate the root cause:

*   **Merchant Preparation Time** = (`order_ready_for_pickup` timestamp) minus (`order_accepted` timestamp).
    *   *Diagnostic:* If this median time exceeds your home market baseline by >20%, the bottleneck is **merchant onboarding/process**, not driver supply. Hiring more drivers will not fix this.
*   **Assignment Latency** = (`driver_assigned` timestamp) minus (`order_ready_for_pickup` timestamp).
    *   *Diagnostic:* If this is high, your **allocation algorithm** is failing to match supply to demand in real-time, or there is a genuine shortage of idle drivers nearby.
*   **Pickup Lag** = (`driver_arrived_at_merchant` timestamp) minus (`driver_assigned` timestamp).
    *   *Diagnostic:* High values here indicate **traffic congestion** or driver scarcity relative to merchant density.
*   **Transit Time** = (`order_delivered` timestamp) minus (`order_picked_up` timestamp).
    *   *Diagnostic:* Isolates pure last-mile travel performance independent of merchant delays.

**Operational Verdict Framework**

Present these metrics as cohort averages per hour-of-day and day-of-week to separate "rush hour" traffic effects from systemic process failures.

*   **Hire More Drivers If:** *Assignment Latency* and *Pickup Lag* are rising while *Merchant Preparation Time* remains stable. This proves drivers are unavailable when needed.
*   **Optimize Merchant Onboarding If:** *Merchant Preparation Time* is the dominant variable in the total delay, regardless of driver availability.
*   **Adjust Algorithm If:** *Assignment Latency* is high despite a visible surplus of idle drivers in the zone (indicating a matching logic failure).
