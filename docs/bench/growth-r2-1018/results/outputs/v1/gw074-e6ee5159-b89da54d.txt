**Recommendation: Prioritize the "Upload-to-View" transition for immediate engineering investment.**

To maximize enterprise contract value, you must treat the 30% drop-off between upload and viewing results as a critical retention leak that invalidates downstream metrics. Your hypothesis states that conversion depends on a lawyer receiving an analysis within two minutes. The 30% of users who drop off before viewing results never validate this core value promise; they are functionally zero-value users regardless of their potential to share. In a bottom-up funnel analysis, retention of the core action (receiving value) precedes activation (sharing). Pouring resources into optimizing the "share" step for the 70% who remain is inefficient if the upstream leak continues to waste 30% of acquisition spend.

The "share with colleague" step, while important for viral growth in a PLG motion, is a secondary multiplier. It only matters if the primary value unit (the analysis) is successfully consumed. A user who views results but does not share has still realized individual value and remains a candidate for individual subscription conversion. A user who uploads but never views has realized no value and will certainly not convert. Therefore, the "Upload-to-View" transition is the stronger predictor of *any* revenue success, while "Share" predicts *scale* only after the base is secure.
