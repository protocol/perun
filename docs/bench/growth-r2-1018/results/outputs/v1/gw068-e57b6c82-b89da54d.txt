### North Star & Input Metrics
**North Star:** Weekly Active Agencies generating a "Qualified Lead" (defined as a prospect contacted or saved) using Advanced Filters.
*Reject vanity metrics:* Total filter clicks or cumulative signups do not equal value.

**Input Metrics to Move the North Star:**
1.  **Filter-to-Conversion Rate:** % of sessions using filters that result in a Qualified Lead within 24 hours.
2.  **Retention Cohort by Feature Usage:** Week-4 retention of users who used filters >3 times in Week 1 vs. those who did not.
3.  **Expansion Trigger Rate:** % of accounts upgrading their plan within 30 days of a "high-value filter session" (e.g., filtering by high-intent criteria).

### Event Taxonomy (Answering Named Questions)
Instrument only these events to resolve your specific unknowns:

| Event Name | Question Answered | Properties Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `filter_session_start` | Is the tool too complex? (Time-to-Value) | `user_id` (pseudonymous), `time_to_first_result`, `filters_applied_count` |
| `lead_quality_signal` | Does the feature solve the right problem? | `user_id`, `lead_status` (contacted/saved/ignored), `filter_criteria_used` |
| `billing_expansion_event` | Is there a revenue correlation? | `account_id`, `previous_plan`, `new_plan`, `days_since_last_filter_session` |

**The Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `account_id` to link filter usage to billing events across sessions. Do not use raw PII; route consent checks to your privacy compliance protocol.

### Diagnostic Strategy: Read Bottom-Up
Do not pour more acquisition spend until retention is proven.
1.  **Retention First:** If users try the filter once and never return, the problem is complexity or lack of value, not awareness. Compare the retention curve of the 40% adopters vs. non-adopters.
2.  **Activation Second:** If they return but don't generate "Qualified Leads," the filter logic may be flawed (solving the wrong problem).
3.  **Revenue Last:** Only after confirming the feature drives qualified leads should you expect expansion revenue to move. Flat revenue despite 40% usage suggests the "Qualified Lead" step is broken.

### Experiment Rigor & Investor Narrative
**Warning:** Do not claim causality for the investor deck based on current correlations without controlling for confounders (e.g., larger agencies might both use filters more *and* spend more naturally).
*   **Hypothesis Test:** Run a targeted holdout test. Force a random 10% of active users to *not* see the new filter for 30 days. Compare their expansion rate to the control group.
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define the Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE) on expansion revenue before looking at the holdout data. Reading the data daily to stop when "significant" is peeking and invalidates the result.
