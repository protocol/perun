**North Star & Funnel Diagnosis**
Your current North Star candidate ("photo uploads") is a vanity metric in this context; it rises while business value (purchases) remains flat. Shift your North Star to **"Weekly Active Users completing a 'Save Design' action"** or **"Designs Viewed to Cart Added."** Uploading a photo is merely activation; value is only created when the AI output drives a tangible next step.

You are measuring the top of the funnel (acquisition/activation) while the leak is almost certainly in retention or revenue. Do not instrument "everything." Instrument only these four specific events to answer your named questions:

1.  **Event:** `design_suggestion_viewed` (Property: `time_to_first_view`)
    *   **Question:** Is the AI slow or broken? If users upload but never wait for the result, the value prop fails immediately.
2.  **Event:** `design_interaction` (Properties: `action_type` [save, share, remix], `item_count_visible`)
    *   **Question:** Are users engaging with the output or bouncing? High upload + low interaction = poor recommendation quality or irrelevant styles for the Brazilian market.
3.  **Event:** `product_detail_view_from_design` (Property: `source_item_id`)
    *   **Question:** Is the intent missing, or is the bridge broken? If users interact with designs but never click items, the issue is **intent** (inspiration only) or **catalog relevance** (items don't match the AI style).
4.  **Event:** `cart_add_from_design`
    *   **Question:** Is this a pricing/friction issue? If users click items but don't add to cart, the problem is price sensitivity or checkout friction, not the AI.
