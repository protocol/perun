**Recommended Primary Funnel Scope: "Search-to-Booking"**

For your UX researchers planning next week's tests, define the primary conversion funnel as follows:

*   **Start Point:** **First Search Completed** (User enters dates/location and sees available cars).
*   **End Point:** **Booking Confirmed** (Payment authorized and rental active).
*   **Critical Intermediate Step:** **Identity Verification Started → Completed**.

**Rationale for this Scope:**
Starting at "Landing Page" or "App Download" mixes marketing acquisition noise with product friction. Users downloading the app or visiting the landing page are often in a browsing or research phase; their drop-off does not necessarily indicate a flaw in the rental mechanics. By starting at **First Search**, you isolate users who have demonstrated explicit intent to rent. This aligns with your North Star of "completed rentals" rather than vanity metrics like "total signups."

Since your data indicates a 60% drop-off during identity verification, this step must be treated as a distinct stage within the funnel, not merely a sub-step of signup. The friction is likely not that the step exists, but that it interrupts the flow *after* intent is established but *before* value is delivered.

**Specific Steps for UX Validation:**
1.  **Search Initiated:** User defines needs.
2.  **Results Viewed:** User sees supply (validates market fit).
3.  **Car Selected:** User chooses a specific asset.
4.  **Verification Triggered:** User is asked to verify identity (Current Leak Point).
5.  **Verification Completed:** User passes check.
6.  **Payment & Confirm:** Final transaction.

**Instruction for Researchers:**
Focus usability tests on the transition between **Step 3 (Car Selected)** and **Step 5 (Verification Completed)**. Your hypothesis is that reducing verification time from four minutes to under two minutes will increase completed bookings by 20%. The test must validate whether the *duration* or the *context* of the verification is the true friction. Are users abandoning because it takes too long, or because they are asked to verify before seeing car details?

Do not optimize the "Landing Page to Signup" path yet. High drop-off there is expected for browsing users. Pouring acquisition spend into the top of the funnel while 60% leak at verification is inefficient. Fix the retention of intent (Search → Booking) first.

**Note:** Ensure the "stable user identifier" used to track this funnel across sessions is pseudonymous; route any raw ID or PII collection plans to your privacy compliance review before instrumentation.
**Note:** Do not fabricate a "good" conversion rate benchmark; measure the lift strictly against your current baseline of completed bookings per search.
