### Diagnostic Approach: Isolating the Checkout Leakage

Do not assume price sensitivity yet; your low bounce rate (15%) confirms interest, but the <1% conversion indicates a catastrophic failure in the **middle or end of the funnel**, not the top. To isolate the leak without surveys, instrument and analyze these four specific micro-conversions immediately. These events answer named questions about where value perception breaks down.

**1. The "Add-to-Cart" vs. "View-Detail" Ratio (Activation Check)**
*   **Question:** Do users intend to buy but fail to commit, or do they browse without intent?
*   **Action:** Calculate the % of Product Detail Page viewers who click "Add to Cart."
*   **Interpretation:** If this is low (<5%), the issue is **product-page friction** (missing specs, unclear availability, or confusing variants), not checkout. If this is healthy (>10-15%), the intent exists, and the leak is downstream.

**2. The "Cart-to-Checkout-Start" Drop (The Shipping Shock Test)**
*   **Question:** Is the shipping cost revealed too late?
*   **Action:** Measure the drop-off between "Add to Cart" and "Click Checkout/Begin Checkout."
*   **Interpretation:** A sharp drop here (>40-50%) strongly suggests **sticker shock** when shipping/taxes are first displayed or estimated. This validates your hypothesis about late shipping costs without needing user quotes.

**3. The "Checkout-Step" Funnel (Process Friction)**
*   **Question:** Is the technical flow broken or too complex?
*   **Action:** Break down the checkout into discrete steps: *Shipping Info Entered → Payment Info Entered → Place Order Clicked*.
*   **Interpretation:**
    *   Drop at *Shipping Info*: Forced account creation or complex address validation.
    *   Drop at *Payment Info*: Lack of preferred payment methods or security concerns.
    *   Drop at *Place Order*: Technical errors or final total recalculation surprises.

**4. The "Exit-Intent" on Checkout Pages**
*   **Question:** Are users abandoning specifically when costs are summarized?
*   **Action:** Track exit events on the page where the final total (Item + Shipping + Tax) is fully visible before payment entry.
*   **Interpretation:** High exits here confirm the **total landed cost** is the barrier, distinguishing between "shipping is too high" vs. "the item itself is too expensive relative to value."

**Immediate Next Steps for Friday's Presentation:**
1.  **Pull the Cohort Data:** Segment these four steps by device (mobile vs. desktop). Mobile checkout flows often have hidden UI bugs that crush conversion.
2.  **Define the Tripwire:** If the "Cart-to-Checkout-Start" drop is >50%, the recommendation is to **test early shipping estimates** on the product page. If the drop is flat until the final "Place Order" click, the recommendation is to **audit payment gateway errors**.

**Note:** Do not present "price sensitivity" as the root cause until you rule out the "Cart-to-Checkout" shock; fixing the display timing of costs is faster than changing inventory pricing.
**Note:** Ensure your analytics tool tracks these as distinct events with timestamps; if you only have pageview data, you cannot diagnose step-specific friction.
