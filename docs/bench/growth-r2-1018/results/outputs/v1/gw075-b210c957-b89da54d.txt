**Recommendation: Adopt "Number of Completed Lesson Plans" as your North Star Metric.**

Reject "Hours of Connection Time" immediately. It is a vanity metric that measures internet availability and platform uptime, not educational value. A student can leave a session open while idle, inflating hours without generating learning. This misaligns your incentives with your mission; optimizing for connection time encourages passive presence, whereas optimizing for completed lesson plans encourages structured pedagogical progress.

**The Measurement Strategy**

1.  **Define the Core Action:** Your North Star must be **Weekly Active Students completing ≥2 Lesson Plans**. This directly proxies your hypothesis that frequency drives improvement. Unlike raw hours, a "completed lesson plan" implies a defined start, structured interaction, and a tangible outcome.
2.  **Address the Data Gap (The 40% Unknown):** You cannot fabricate exam scores for the 40% with irregular data. Do not estimate them. Instead, instrument a **proxy activation event** within the lesson plan flow: "Post-Session Confidence Check" or "Micro-Quiz Completion."
    *   *Action:* Add a mandatory 3-question knowledge check or a self-rated confidence slider at the end of every digital lesson plan.
    *   *Logic:* While not a final exam score, a trend of increasing micro-quiz scores or confidence ratings across multiple completed plans provides a verified, internal baseline of progress for *all* users, bypassing the external school schedule bottleneck.
3.  **Funnel Diagnosis (Retention First):** Do not focus on acquiring more students until you verify retention of the core action.
    *   *Question to answer:* "Of the students who completed two lesson plans in Week 1, how many return to complete two more in Week 2?"
    *   If this retention rate is low, pouring resources into marketing or connection stability is wasted. The leak is in the educational value, not the connectivity.
4.  **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument only these specific events to answer named questions:
    *   `lesson_plan_started`: Are users initiating?
    *   `lesson_plan_completed`: Are users finishing the structured unit? (Primary Input Metric)
    *   `micro_assessment_passed`: Is there evidence of immediate learning? (Proxy for Exam Score)
    *   `session_duration`: (Guardrail only) Use this to flag anomalies (e.g., 4-hour sessions likely indicate technical issues, not deep learning), not as a success metric.

**Addressing the Donor Report (August 30th)**

For the stakeholder meeting, frame the narrative around **structured engagement** rather than raw connectivity.
*   **Claim:** "X% of our students completed the recommended dosage (2+ plans/week)."
*   **Evidence:** Correlate this completion rate with the *available* 60% of exam data. If those who completed plans show the expected 10% lift, you have a strong causal link.
*   **Handling the Missing 40%:** Be transparent. Report the "Micro-Assessment Trend" for this cohort as a leading indicator. State clearly: "While final exam data is pending due to school schedules, internal competency checks show a Y% improvement trend among active users." This is honest, verified data, not an invented benchmark.
