You cannot set specific numerical thresholds (e.g., "lag < 100ms") or satisfaction benchmarks yet because you have no baseline data for this net-new capability. Asserting a number now would be a fabricated benchmark, which violates the prime directive of growth analytics. Instead, your success criteria must be defined as **relative stability against your existing baseline** and **instrumentation readiness**.

Here are the measurable outcomes and go-live gates for your Engineering Director:

### 1. Technical Success: The "No Degradation" Gate
Since you cannot define an absolute "good" latency without data, define failure as **deviation from the legacy baseline**.
*   **Metric:** Concurrent user capacity and error rates of the *existing* video system.
*   **Threshold:** The system must sustain 5,000 concurrent users with screen-sharing enabled without increasing the legacy system's current error rate or connection drop rate by more than **5%**.
*   **Action:** If enabling screen-sharing causes the legacy drop rate to spike >5%, the feature is `UNVERIFIED` and must be rolled back. This protects the core business while you gather data on the new feature.

### 2. Experiential Success: Instrumentation Before Measurement
You asked how to measure satisfaction when "no news is good news." In analytics, "no news" often means "no instrumentation." You cannot measure what you do not track.
*   **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument exactly three events before launch. Do not track everything.
    1.  `screen_share_start_attempt`: (Property: `user_segment`, `client_version`). *Question: Can users initiate the feature?*
    2.  `screen_share_session_quality`: (Property: `avg_latency_ms`, `frame_rate`, `duration_seconds`). *Question: What is the actual performance distribution?*
    3.  `screen_share_early_terminate`: (Property: `reason_code`, `duration_before_drop`). *Question: Are users abandoning the feature due to poor quality?*
*   **Success Criteria:** 100% of sessions must emit these events. If data is missing, the rollout is a failure regardless of perceived stability.

### 3. User Satisfaction: The "Silent Signal" Proxy
Do not wait for surveys. Use behavioral retention as your satisfaction proxy.
*   **Metric:** **Week-1 Retention of the Core Action**.
*   **Definition:** Of the users who successfully start a screen share in Week 1, what percentage start a screen share again in Week 2?
*   **Gate:** Since you have no baseline, you cannot set a target % yet. The go-live condition is simply that the **data pipeline is live** to calculate this cohort immediately. If you cannot compute this number on December 2nd, the launch criteria are not met.
