**North Star & Input Metrics**
Reject "Daily Active Users" (vanity) as your primary success signal; a 5% rise paired with a 2-minute drop in session duration indicates a potential engagement leak, not value creation. Your North Star must be **Weekly Active Listeners completing a Core Action** (e.g., finishing a song or playlist), not just opening the app. Pair this with two input metrics: **Social Share-to-Return Rate** (percentage of users who share and return within 48 hours) and **Net Listening Time per Cohort** (total minutes listened by the test group vs. control, normalized per user). If sharing increases opens but decreases total listening time, the feature is cannibalizing core value.

**Retention Measurement Plan (3-Month Horizon)**
To distinguish novelty from loyalty for the executive board, instrument these specific time-based cohorts starting from the November 10th baseline:

1.  **Week-2 & Week-4 Retention Curves:** Do not look at aggregate averages. Plot the retention curve specifically for users who triggered the social feature vs. those who did not. If the "sharer" curve drops faster than the control after Week 2, the 5% DAU lift is a novelty effect.
2.  **Frequency of Core Action:** Measure the **average number of listening sessions per week** per user over 12 weeks. A sustainable feature increases frequency even if individual session duration drops. If frequency remains flat or declines while duration drops, the trade-off is unacceptable.
3.  **Cohort Stickiness (DAU/MAU Ratio):** Calculate this ratio weekly for the test cohort. A rising ratio indicates the feature is becoming a habit; a falling ratio confirms the "distraction" hypothesis.

**Experiment Rigor & Confounders**
Your current read is **UNVERIFIED** as a win. Observing a metric mid-test without a pre-committed stopping rule constitutes "peeking," which inflates false positives. The drop in session duration is a critical **guardrail metric** violation. You cannot claim success on DAU while failing on engagement depth without proving long-term LTV (Lifetime Value) impact.

**Confounding Factors:**
*   **Novelty Effect:** Early adopters of social features often spike then churn. Only Week-4+ data validates sustainability.
*   **Cannibalization:** Users may be swapping listening time for sharing time. The net effect on "Minutes Listened" is the true business health indicator, not raw user counts.
