**Memo: Q2 Creative Strategy Recommendation – App Icon A/B Test**

**Decision:** Do not rebrand. Treat the current test result as **inconclusive** regarding the primary hypothesis and **do not ship** the orange icon based on this data alone.

**Analysis of Significance**
The observed 1.2% lift in click-through rate (CTR) is **statistically unverified** as a win. Your pre-committed hypothesis required a minimum detectable effect (MDE) of 5%. A result of 1.2% falling "within the margin of error" confirms that we cannot reject the null hypothesis; the difference is indistinguishable from noise. Acting on this marginal gain as a causal win constitutes "peeking" or over-interpreting noise, which inflates the false-positive rate. The 8% decrease in Cost Per Install (CPI) is a positive guardrail signal, suggesting improved organic discoverability, but it does not validate the primary claim that the color drives user curiosity or engagement.

**Confounding Factors**
The disconnect between flat CTR and lower CPI suggests the variable (color) impacted **discoverability** (search ranking or visual pop in a grid) rather than **appeal** (the decision to click once seen). This invalidates the original assumption that "warmer color = higher curiosity." Without isolating these mechanisms, a full rebrand risks diluting brand equity for a benefit that may be limited to app store algorithms rather than user preference.

**Recommended Actions (This Week)**
1.  **Extend or Redesign:** Do not stop entirely if the CPI gain is financially material, but do not declare victory. Either extend the test duration to reach the pre-committed sample size required to detect a 1.2% lift (which will be substantially larger than the original plan) or, more efficiently, **stop this specific test** and design a new experiment isolating "discoverability" vs. "appeal" (e.g., testing icon distinctiveness against a neutral background vs. within the store grid).
2.  **Guardrail Check:** Before any further creative iteration, verify that the orange icon did not negatively impact **Day-1 Retention**. If the icon attracts clicks via "curiosity" but fails to deliver on the brand promise (blue), retention may drop. If retention data is not yet available for the orange cohort, the result remains `UNVERIFIED` for full-funnel impact.
3.  **Q2 Strategy Pivot:** Shift Q2 creative testing from "color temperature" to **conceptual distinctiveness**. The data indicates users respond to visibility, not just warmth. Test high-contrast illustrative elements rather than simple hue shifts.

**Tripwire for Re-evaluation**
Re-evaluate the orange icon only if a subsequent, properly powered test demonstrates a CTR lift **>3%** with a confirmed neutral or positive impact on Day-1 retention. Until then, maintain the blue icon to preserve brand consistency.

*Note: The 8% CPI improvement should be monitored as a secondary metric, but never used to override a failed primary metric without causal proof.*
*Ensure all user identifiers used for retention cohort analysis are pseudonymized per privacy standards.*
