### Event Specification: `ai_background_removed`

To determine what drives upgrades without historical data, instrument only properties that answer specific hypotheses about value delivery. Do not log "everything"; every property must map to a question you can name.

**Event Name:** `ai_background_removed`
**Trigger:** Fired immediately upon successful completion of the background removal action (image saved or applied).

**Required Properties (The "Who" and "When"):**
1.  `user_id` (String, Pseudonymous): Stable identifier to track the same user across sessions for retention and conversion cohorts. *Note: Do not send raw PII; ensure this aligns with your privacy compliance routing.*
2.  `session_id` (String): To distinguish multiple attempts within a single sitting.
3.  `timestamp` (ISO 8601): For time-series analysis and cohort grouping.
4.  `is_paid_user` (Boolean): Snapshot of subscription status at the moment of the event to separate new conversion drivers from existing payer behavior.

**Hypothesis-Driven Properties (The "What" and "How"):**
These properties directly test your assumption that high-resolution editing predicts conversion and identify friction points.
5.  `image_resolution_px` (Integer): Total pixel count (width × height). *Question answered:* "Does editing higher resolution images correlate with a higher upgrade rate?"
6.  `edit_duration_ms` (Integer): Time elapsed between tool open and successful removal. *Question answered:* "Does faster completion (ease of use) or longer engagement (careful refinement) predict conversion?"
7.  `export_format` (String): e.g., "PNG", "JPG". *Question answered:* "Do users needing transparent backgrounds (PNG) convert more often?"
8.  `retry_count` (Integer): Number of failed attempts before success. *Question answered:* "Does tool friction (multiple tries) kill conversion intent?"
9.  `source_context` (String): e.g., "home_screen", "editor_toolbar". *Question answered:* "Where in the app are high-intent users discovering this feature?"

**Implementation Constraints for Engineering:**
*   **Stage Appropriateness:** Since this is an MVP launch, do not instrument granular mouse movements or intermediate states. Only log the completed action and the variables listed above.
*   **Verification:** If any of these properties are missing from the payload post-launch, any analysis regarding that specific driver is `UNVERIFIED`.
*   **Baseline:** You currently have no benchmark for "good" conversion rates on this feature. Do not set thresholds yet. Establish a baseline in Week 1, then compare Week 2 against Week 1.
