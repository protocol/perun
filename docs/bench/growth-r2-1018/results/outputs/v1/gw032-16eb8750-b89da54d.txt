*   **The Test:** For every proposed event, write down the exact SQL query or dashboard chart it enables. If you cannot name the chart, do not instrument the event.

**3. Step-by-Step Taxonomy Validation (Before Scaling Acquisition)**
Do not scale acquisition until you confirm the funnel is not leaking due to measurement errors.
*   **Step A: Define the North Star.** Move away from "total matches" (vanity). Adopt "Weekly Active Users who initiate a conversation" as your primary value metric.
*   **Step B: Bottom-Up Funnel Audit.** Verify retention before acquisition. Check: Do users who match today return to chat tomorrow? If retention is broken, new users from scaled acquisition will churn immediately.
*   **Step C: The Identifier Seam.** Ensure your new 40-event taxonomy uses a stable, pseudonymous user ID that persists across app updates and sessions. This is critical for cohort analysis but must remain decoupled from PII (route PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol).
*   **Step D: Shadow Validation.** Run the new 40-event stream parallel to a small sample of raw logs for 48 hours. Verify that `swipe` + `match` counts reconcile within a 1% margin of error before trusting the data for executive reporting.
