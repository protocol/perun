### Proxy Metrics and Success Criteria for ClassiRu Search Algorithm Launch

**North Star Proxy: Qualified Click-Through Rate (qCTR)**
Since "successful hire" is currently uninstrumented, reject raw CTR as a vanity metric; it rises even if relevance drops (e.g., clickbait titles). Instead, define **qCTR** as the primary success metric. A "qualified click" is a user session where a search result is clicked *and* the user remains on the listing page for >45 seconds OR scrolls past the fold. This filters out accidental clicks and immediate bounces, proxying genuine interest without needing employer-side conversion data.

**Input Metrics (The Funnel)**
1.  **Activation Depth:** Percentage of sessions where a user clicks ≥2 distinct job listings within 10 minutes. This indicates the algorithm is presenting a viable consideration set, not just one attractive outlier.
2.  **Return Rate (Day 3):** Percentage of users who perform a new search and click a result within 72 hours of their initial session. This measures short-term retention of value, ensuring the new ranking doesn't satisfy a single query while breaking future utility.

**Guardrail Metrics (Do Not Degrade)**
1.  **Zero-Result Rate:** Must not increase by >2% relative to the pre-launch baseline. Higher relevance must not come at the cost of excluding valid inventory.
2.  **Listing Diversity:** Average number of unique employers shown in the top 10 results per session. Prevent the algorithm from collapsing into a "rich-get-richer" loop that hides long-tail suppliers.

**Success Criteria for the First Two Weeks**
The launch is considered a **win** only if:
*   **qCTR** increases by ≥3% compared to the two-week pre-launch baseline (Sept 16–29), with statistical significance calculated on a pre-committed sample size (do not peek daily).
*   **Activation Depth** remains flat or improves.
*   **Guardrails** hold (no significant degradation in Zero-Result Rate or Diversity).

If qCTR rises but Activation Depth falls, the algorithm is optimizing for clickbait, not value. If qCTR rises but Return Rate drops, you are burning user trust for short-term gains. In either case, the result is **UNVERIFIED** as a business win and requires a rollback or iteration.

**Immediate Next Steps (This Week)**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Define and deploy the `listing_view_duration` event immediately. Without this, qCTR cannot be calculated, and the launch assessment will remain `UNVERIFIED`.
2.  **Baseline Capture:** Freeze the current qCTR, Activation Depth, and Return Rate cohorts for the period Sept 16–29 to serve as the strict comparison baseline.
3.  **Test Design:** Pre-commit to a 14-day evaluation window ending Oct 14. Do not declare a winner before this date based on interim data, as early fluctuations inflate false-positive rates.

*Note: Since offline hire data is missing, schedule a manual validation study with 5–10 key employer accounts in October to correlate "Qualified Clicks" with actual interview requests, refining the proxy threshold for Q4.*
*Ensure all user identifiers used for cohorting are pseudonymized and routed to privacy compliance protocols before engineering implementation.*
