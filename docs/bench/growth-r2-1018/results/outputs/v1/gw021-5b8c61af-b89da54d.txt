### Diagnosis Plan: Isolating the 'Pro Plus' Friction Point

Your hypothesis that the $5 price gap causes hesitation is plausible, but the data must distinguish between **choice paralysis** (users seeing both plans and leaving) and **value mismatch** (users wanting 'Pro Plus' features but balking at the total price). Since sign-ups are up 15% while conversion dropped, the leak is almost certainly in the activation or decision layer, not acquisition quality.

To decide by next Friday, isolate these three specific data cuts. Do not look at aggregate conversion yet; it hides the specific breakage.

**1. The "Comparison Drop-off" Rate (Pricing Table Friction)**
*   **Event to Isolate:** `pricing_page_view` → `pricing_plan_selected` → `exit_pricing_page`.
*   **The Cut:** Compare the % of users who view the pricing table but select *neither* plan before exiting, segmented by:
    *   Pre-launch (before Oct 15) vs. Post-launch.
    *   Users who see 2 tiers (old) vs. Users who see 3 tiers (new).
*   **What this tells you:** If this exit rate spiked post-launch, your assumption about **confusion** or **paralysis** is correct. Users are overwhelmed by the choice or the small price delta makes the decision feel high-stakes.
*   **Decision Trigger:** If the "no selection" exit rate is >20% higher than baseline, the pricing table layout or the $5 delta is the blocker. **Action:** Simplify the table or increase the feature/price gap to force differentiation.

**2. The "Plan Selection" Split (Cannibalization vs. Abandonment)**
*   **Event to Isolate:** `pricing_plan_selected` (broken down by `plan_name`: 'Pro' vs. 'Pro Plus').
*   **The Cut:** Of the users who *do* select a plan, what is the split between 'Pro' and 'Pro Plus'?
*   **What this tells you:**
    *   If 80%+ select 'Pro Plus' but then drop at payment: The issue is **price sensitivity** to the higher tier, not confusion. The new tier is attracting interest but failing at the wallet.
    *   If the split is 50/50 or heavily skewed to 'Pro' despite marketing 'Pro Plus': The new tier is indeed **cannibalizing** without adding net value, or users don't understand the upgrade benefit.
*   **Decision Trigger:** If 'Pro Plus' selection is high but payment completion is low, the features resonate but the price is wrong. **Action:** Adjust 'Pro Plus' pricing or bundle. If selection is low, the features aren't compelling enough to justify the switch.

**3. The Payment Step Leakage (Final Friction)**
*   **Event to Isolate:** `checkout_initiated` → `payment_failed` vs. `checkout_abandoned`.
*   **The Cut:** Compare abandonment rates at the credit card entry step for 'Pro' vs. 'Pro Plus' post-launch.
*   **What this tells you:** If 'Pro Plus' has a significantly higher abandonment rate at the *payment* step (after selection) compared to 'Pro', the friction is purely **financial**. If abandonment happens *before* payment (at the review screen), the friction is **value perception**.
*   **Decision Trigger:** High payment-step abandonment on 'Pro Plus' confirms the $5 up-sell is too steep for the perceived value. **Action:** Sunset 'Pro Plus' or re-bundle features to justify the cost.
