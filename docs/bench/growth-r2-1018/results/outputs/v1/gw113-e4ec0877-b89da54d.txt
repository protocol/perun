### Recommended Measurement Framework for GreenLoop

**1. The North Star: "Monthly Active Renters Completing ≥2 Items"**
Reject "cumulative subscribers" or "total pageviews" as vanity metrics; they rise regardless of value delivery. Your data explicitly links renting **≥2 items in month one** to six-month retention. Therefore, your single North Star must be the count (or percentage) of active subscribers who hit this threshold each month. This metric rises *only* when users derive actual value (finding clothes they like) and directly predicts financial viability (retention/LTV).

**2. The Input Metrics (Drivers)**
To move the North Star, track these specific inputs:
*   **Activation Rate:** % of new signups who rent their second item within 30 days.
*   **Inventory Availability:** % of requests fulfilled without substitution (a likely friction point for the second item).
*   **Return Friction:** Average time from "return requested" to "credit available" (delays here stop the second rental).

**3. Handling Environmental Impact: The Guardrail, Not the North Star**
Do **not** make "carbon footprint saved" a primary success metric for Q2 tracking alongside retention. A dual North Star dilutes focus and creates conflicting incentives (e.g., pushing low-impact items to boost carbon stats while hurting rental frequency).
*   **Role:** Treat carbon saved per transaction as a **guardrail metric**. It must stay positive and ideally trend up, but it is not the lever you pull to drive growth.
*   **Reporting:** In the investor report, present carbon saved as a *outcome* of the North Star. Formula: `(Total Items Rented) × (Average Carbon Saved per Item)`. If your North Star grows, your environmental impact grows automatically. This proves environmental impact is a byproduct of product-market fit, not a separate operational burden.

**4. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not instrument "everything." Define these specific events:
*   `rental_initiated`: Answers "Are users starting the process?"
*   `second_item_added_to_cart`: Answers "Are users overcoming the hurdle to our retention cliff?"
*   `return_processed`: Answers "Is the loop closing fast enough to enable the next rental?"
*   *Note:* Ensure these events link to a **stable, pseudonymous user ID** to track cohorts across sessions, while routing raw PII to your privacy compliance protocols.
