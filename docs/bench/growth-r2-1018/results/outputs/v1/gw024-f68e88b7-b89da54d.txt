**North Star Metric:** Weekly Qualified Calls Booked (capped at 15).
Do not optimize for "Form Completions." Your constraint is sales capacity, not lead volume. A metric that rises while your team sits idle is vanity.

**Event Taxonomy & Definitions**
Instrument only these three events to answer your specific questions:
1.  `form_started`: User lands on the form and interacts with the first field.
2.  `contact_info_submitted`: User enters email/phone (your proposed "partial" conversion).
3.  `form_fully_submitted`: User completes all 12 fields + budget questions.

**The Framework: Bottom-Up Funnel Diagnosis**
Read your data in reverse order of the funnel to diagnose the drop from 18% to 9%:

*   **Step 1: Retention of Value (The Sales Handoff).** Calculate the conversion rate from `form_fully_submitted` to "Qualified Call." If the post-August 20th leads convert to calls at a significantly higher rate than pre-August leads, the drop in volume is acceptable noise for higher quality. If the qualification rate is flat, the new questions are pure friction killing viable leads.
*   **Step 2: Activation (The Drop-off Point).** Analyze where users exit. If 80% of drop-offs happen *after* the budget questions, the length is the barrier. If they leave before reaching the budget fields, the initial value proposition is unclear.
*   **Step 3: Acquisition (Traffic Quality).** Compare `form_started` rates. If traffic volume is stable but starts are down, the page load or headline changed. If starts are stable but completions plummet, the form length is the culprit.

**Decision Logic: Partial vs. Full Conversion**
Do not define "conversion" as `contact_info_submitted` unless you have an automated nurture stream. Your sales team can only handle 15 calls/week (~3 per day).
*   **Scenario A (High Quality):** If `form_fully_submitted` yields >3 qualified calls/day, keep the long form. The 9% completion rate is filtering effectively.
*   **Scenario B (Over-Filtering):** If `form_fully_submitted` yields <1 qualified call/day, the form is too aggressive. Test a two-step form: capture `contact_info_submitted` first, then reveal the budget questions.
*   **The Signal for Unqualified Traffic:** If you shorten the form and `contact_info_submitted` spikes, but the % of those users who are "Qualified" (by sales review) drops below your historical baseline, you have attracted unqualified traffic.
