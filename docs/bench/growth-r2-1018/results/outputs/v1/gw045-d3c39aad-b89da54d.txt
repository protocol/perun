**North Star Metric: Weekly Active Matches (WAM)**

Define a "Match" as a student-tutor pair that has completed **three sessions**. Your North Star is the count of unique Matches that complete **at least one session** in a given week.

**Why this matters more than registration counts:**
Registration numbers (300 students, 45 tutors) are vanity metrics; they rise regardless of whether learning occurs. A student can sign up and never learn; a tutor can join and never teach. These numbers inflate your dashboard while hiding the reality of a leaky bucket. "Total hours taught" is a output metric, not an outcome metric; it rewards long, inefficient sessions rather than consistent progress. "Percentage returning for a second term" is a lagging indicator; by the time you measure it, the student has already churned, and it is too late to intervene.

WAM measures **realized value**. It only increases when a student and tutor successfully navigate the friction of scheduling, showing up, and completing the threshold for a stable relationship (3 sessions) AND continue to engage weekly. If WAM is flat while signups rise, you have an activation or retention problem, not a marketing problem. For donors, this tells a story of *sustained educational relationships*, not just platform traffic.

**Feasible Measurement Plan (No Data Engineer Required):**
Since you lack sophisticated tools, do not attempt to track every click. Use your existing communication logs (WhatsApp groups, SMS logs, or simple Google Forms used for session reporting).

1.  **The Input Metric:** Track "Session Completion Reports." Require tutors to submit a simple form (Student Name, Tutor Name, Date, Session #) after every session.
2.  **The Calculation:** Once a week, filter your list for pairs who have reached "Session 3" status. Count how many of those active pairs had a session recorded in the last 7 days.
3.  **The Story for Donors:** "While 300 students registered, 45 formed stable learning partnerships. Of those, X were actively learning this week. Our focus is now on converting registrations into these active matches."

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Audit the Baseline:** Manually review your logs from March 1st to today. Identify exactly how many pairs have hit the "3-session" threshold. This is your current verified baseline. Do not estimate; if the data is missing, mark it as `UNVERIFIED` and note the gap to donors as a "data infrastructure priority."
2.  **Simplify Reporting:** Create a single, mandatory Google Form for tutors to log sessions. Make this the only way they get credited or paid (if applicable). This ensures the data stream for your North Star starts flowing immediately.
3.  **Cohort Check:** Group your 300 students by their signup week. Calculate what percentage of Week 1 signups achieved a "Match" versus Week 4 signups. If Week 4 is lower, your onboarding is degrading; fix that before asking for more signups.

*Note: Do not fabricate industry benchmarks for "good" retention rates; compare your current week only to your previous week to show trend direction to donors.*
*Note: Ensure student names in your logs are pseudonymized (e.g., Student ID codes) to protect privacy before sharing reports externally.*
