The claim is that a front-wheel-drive, compact-platform van will save a cash-poor carmaker by creating a new consumer category. Steelman: This vehicle uniquely combines car-like handling and garage fitment to capture families abandoned by truck-based vans, leveraging existing assets for a capital-efficient turnaround.

**Skeptic Attack:** The primary blocker is the "no such category exists" risk combined with "little cash." If buyers perceive it as a commercial van, sales will fail to cover retooling costs, causing immediate insolvency. However, the proposal explicitly targets "families" buying station wagons, not fleets. The compact-car platform constraint is actually a strategic advantage here: it ensures the "garage fitment" and "car-like drive" differentiators that truck-based rivals cannot match without a ground-up redesign. The lack of a category is not a market void but an uncontested蓝海 (blue ocean); rivals cannot copy quickly because their legacy truck platforms prevent them from matching the size/handling profile without massive CAPEX they cannot justify for an unproven segment.

**Better-Way Analysis:** "Do nothing" guarantees collapse given the current trajectory. Building a new platform is impossible due to cash constraints. Reusing the compact platform is the *only* viable path to speed and cost-efficiency. Converting an existing factory minimizes upfront investment compared to new construction. There is no cheaper, safer alternative that addresses the core need for a differentiated product.

**Kill Criteria & Pre-mortem:** Failure would occur if marketing fails to distinguish the vehicle from commercial work vans. The pre-mortem shows failure only if the product is positioned as a utility vehicle rather than a family hauler. Since the proposal specifies the target demographic (families) and use case (garage/home), the strategic fit is sound. The risks listed are inherent to innovation but do not constitute factual blockers given the asset constraints. The "commercial perception" risk is mitigated by the fundamental design difference (FWD/car platform vs. RWD/truck platform).

No factual contradiction exists between the company's constraints (low cash, existing compact platform) and the proposed solution. The plan turns limitations into unique selling propositions.
