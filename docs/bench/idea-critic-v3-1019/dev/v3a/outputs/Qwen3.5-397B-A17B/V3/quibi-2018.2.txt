**Steelman:** A premium, mobile-native studio leveraging exclusive A-list talent and adaptive technology to capture high-value subscribers underserved by free, user-generated short-form content.

**Attack:**
1.  **Demand Contradiction:** The proposal admits "no proven demand" for premium short-form while citing "subscription fatigue." Consumers currently satisfy short-form needs via free, abundant user-generated platforms (YouTube, TikTok precursors). Asking users to pay a premium for a format they currently get free is a fundamental value mismatch, not just a pricing challenge.
2.  **Feature Irrelevance:** "Technology that makes video look right" vertically or horizontally is a baseline expectation, not a differentiator. By 2018, major platforms already handle orientation seamlessly. This cannot justify a billion-dollar valuation or overcome content apathy.
3.  **Unit Economics:** High production budgets + A-list talent + $1B raise implies massive burn. Without proven willingness to pay, the path to profitability is nonexistent. The "short-form" constraint limits ad inventory density compared to long-form, making a hybrid model difficult.
4.  **Competition:** The proposal identifies "competition for phone attention" but ignores that the *format* itself (short video) is dominated by free algorithms designed for infinite retention. A curated, expensive walled garden cannot compete with free, algorithmic dopamine loops on the same device screen.

**Blocker:** The core assumption that users will *pay* for short-form professional video when free alternatives dominate is contradicted by the stated market reality ("abundant free," "subscription fatigue"). No evidence suggests this specific combination (short + pro + paid) solves a pain point users are willing to fund. The "adaptive technology" is a commodity, not a moat.

**Verdict:** The plan relies on creating a new consumer behavior (paying for short video) in a saturated free market without a compelling mechanism to force that shift. The billion-dollar ask amplifies the risk of a solution looking for a problem.

DECISION: NO-GO
