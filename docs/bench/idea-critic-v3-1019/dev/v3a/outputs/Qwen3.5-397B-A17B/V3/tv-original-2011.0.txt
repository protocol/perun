The claim is that a data-driven, all-at-once release of two seasons of a prestige drama, bypassing the pilot model, will succeed despite high costs and unproven demand for an online-only service.

**Steelman:** Leveraging proprietary viewing data to greenlight a full series eliminates pilot waste and captures first-mover advantage in streaming originals, locking in top talent with a bold distribution model that matches binge-watching behavior.

**Attack:**
1.  **Data Validity:** Viewing data reflects existing licensed content preferences, not demand for original prestige drama. The leap from "users watch movies" to "users will subscribe for *this* specific expensive show" is unproven.
2.  **Financial Exposure:** Committing to two seasons (26 episodes) without a pilot or single-season test concentrates massive financial risk. If the show fails, the sunk cost is catastrophic relative to the budget.
3.  **Distribution Gamble:** Releasing all episodes at once is untested for prestige drama. It may generate buzz but fails to sustain subscription retention over months like weekly releases.
4.  **Market Fit:** No online-only service has successfully produced prestige scripted content. The audience may not associate the brand with high-quality originals yet.

**Better Way:** Commission a single season or a high-budget pilot first. Test the "all-at-once" model with lower-cost unscripted content before betting the content budget on a flagship drama.

**Kill Criteria:** The proposal lacks a clear metric for success beyond "viewing data." Without a pilot, there is no early audience feedback loop. The cost structure assumes immediate subscriber growth to justify the spend, which is an assumption, not a fact.

**Verdict:** The risks (financial concentration, unproven data applicability, untested distribution) outweigh the potential upside given the lack of de-risking steps. The proposal relies on assumptions about audience behavior that the cited data cannot fully support. A staged approach (one season, or pilot) is a strictly superior alternative that preserves capital while testing the core hypotheses.

DECISION: NO-GO
