**Steelman:** Direct-to-consumer distribution captures full lifetime value and data, securing survival as licensing windows shrink, justifying short-term losses for long-term dominance.

**Skeptic:** The proposal treats "billions" in guaranteed licensing revenue as expendable without a bridge. The math fails: replacing high-margin, immediate licensing cash with low-margin subscription fees requires acquiring hundreds of millions of users just to break even on lost income, let alone fund technology build-out. The "low price" strategy exacerbates this, extending the time to profitability while burning cash. The company has no history of running a global tech platform; execution risk is extreme.

**Better-Way:** The "do nothing" or "hybrid" alternative (licensing non-core content while building a premium, higher-priced niche service) preserves cash flow. A joint venture with an existing tech giant reduces platform risk. The proposed "burn cash to buy subs" model assumes infinite capital markets patience, which is not a fact but a hope.

**Kill-Criteria:** If the company cannot demonstrate a path to positive free cash flow within 3 years without raising dilutive equity, the plan fails. If subscriber acquisition costs exceed the lifetime value of a subscriber at the proposed low price point, the unit economics are broken.

**Pre-mortem:** The service launches, burns $5B, hits 20M subs (below target), but licensing revenue drops by $4B. The stock collapses, the board forces a fire sale of the streaming unit, and the library is sold off to pay debts. The "low price" traps them in a value position they cannot afford.

**Blockers:** 
1. **Math Mismatch:** Losing billions in immediate, high-margin licensing revenue cannot be offset by a "low price" subscription model targeting only "tens of millions" of users in five years against a competitor with 100M+. The revenue gap is a factual blocker to the "fund with losses" strategy unless the loss capacity is unlimited (unproven).
2. **Capability Gap:** Building a global streaming tech stack from scratch while simultaneously marketing globally is a dual-execution failure mode not addressed by the plan.

**Decision:** The proposal relies on optimistic subscriber growth to plug a massive, immediate hole in guaranteed revenue, with no proven technical capability to deliver the product. The financial arithmetic does not close as stated.

DECISION: NO-GO
