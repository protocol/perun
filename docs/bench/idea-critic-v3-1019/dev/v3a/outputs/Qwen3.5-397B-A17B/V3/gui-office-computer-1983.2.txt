**Steelman:** Launching a high-end, mouse-driven computer at $10,000 targets a niche willing to pay for superior usability, potentially establishing a premium brand foothold before competitors.

**Attack:** The proposal fails the fundamental economic and strategic reality of the 1983 market.
1.  **Price Dislocation:** At $10,000, the unit costs several times more than established business PCs. Office workers (the target) do not control capital budgets of this magnitude for individual stations; this price point excludes the entire volume market required to sustain a new platform.
2.  **Performance Deficit:** Selling a "slow" machine at a "premium" price is a fatal contradiction. High cost must be justified by superior speed or capability, not just a novel interface.
3.  **Ecosystem Void:** Business adoption relies on software utility. With "little third-party software," the hardware is a solution without a problem, regardless of the interface quality.
4.  **Internal Cannibalization:** A cheaper, similar internal alternative exists. Launching the expensive, slower version first invites immediate internal competition that renders the premium product unsellable.

**Better Way:** The "do nothing" option regarding this specific configuration is superior. Resources should shift to the cheaper internal alternative or wait until software availability and performance metrics justify a premium. Alternatively, reposition as a specialized workstation for high-value verticals (e.g., graphic design) rather than general "office workers," but only if performance improves.

**Kill Criteria:** The proposal lacks a path to volume. If the price cannot drop below 2x the competition within 12 months, or if software titles do not triple by launch, the venture fails. The current facts show the price is too high, speed too low, and software too scarce for the stated target.

**Verdict:** The combination of exorbitant price, poor performance, lack of software, and internal competition creates a guaranteed market failure. The claim that office workers will adopt this specific configuration at this price is unsupported by the provided facts.

DECISION: NO-GO
