The claim is that a $180 tabletop stereoscopic 3D console with red monochrome displays will successfully bridge the gap between handheld and home console generations.

**Skeptic:** The proposal admits the device is neither portable nor comfortable, directly contradicting the market demand for convenience implied by a "handheld" bridge. Red-on-black visuals limit software appeal, and admitted health risks (eye strain, headaches) create a liability barrier that could damage the brand before the next home console arrives. With few launch games, the value proposition relies entirely on a gimmick that causes physical discomfort.

**Better-way:** The "do nothing" option preserves capital and brand reputation while waiting for the true next-generation home console due in two years. A cheaper alternative is to release a software emulation layer or accessory for existing hardware rather than manufacturing a dedicated, flawed unit. The cost of delay is minimal given the short two-year window to the next major release.

**Kill-criteria:** The pre-mortem is already written in the risks: users reject the device due to discomfort and poor visuals. The edge is nonexistent; being first with uncomfortable 3D is not an advantage over being second with comfortable, full-color 3D. The stated facts (discomfort, limited library, high price for low fidelity) constitute a blocker: the product fails to deliver a compelling user experience required for adoption.

**Decision:**
Blockers: The device causes physical discomfort (headaches/eye strain) and offers inferior visuals (red monochrome) at a premium price ($180), violating basic user experience requirements.
Risks: Brand damage, low software adoption, inventory obsolescence within two years.
Edge: NONE. Being first with a flawed implementation is not a competitive advantage.

This plan commits real money to a product with known, fatal usability flaws and no clear path to profitability before obsolescence. The risks are not hypothetical; they are stated features of the design.

DECISION: NO-GO
