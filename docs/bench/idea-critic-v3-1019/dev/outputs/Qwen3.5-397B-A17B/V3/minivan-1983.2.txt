**Steelman:** A cash-strapped carmaker can survive by creating a new "minivan" category using existing compact-car parts and a converted factory, offering car-like handling in a garage-fit package before rivals react.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The primary blocker is the claim that "no such vehicle category exists." Historically, the absence of a category often indicates unmet demand rather than lack of interest, especially when families are forced to choose between unwieldy full-size vans and impractical station wagons. The proposal directly addresses this gap.

The financial constraint ("little cash") is mitigated by the specific strategy of reusing the compact-car platform and converting an existing factory. This avoids the capital expenditure of new tooling or greenfield plants, making the project feasible despite the lack of liquidity. The government loan guarantee provides the necessary bridge to execute this low-capital pivot.

Regarding the risk of buyers perceiving it as a commercial van: positioning it as a front-wheel-drive vehicle that "drives like a car" and fits in a home garage explicitly targets the family demographic, differentiating it from rear-wheel-drive commercial workhorses.

Rival copycats are a valid long-term threat, but first-mover advantage in a newly defined category allows the company to establish brand dominance before competitors can retool. The risk of limited space due to the compact platform is a necessary trade-off for the "garage-fit" requirement; if it were larger, it would fail the core use case.

There are no factual contradictions in the proposal. The risks are real but are adequately addressed by the specific constraints and assets cited (government backing, existing platform, converted factory). The "edge" is the unique combination of immediate need (survival), specific asset availability, and a clear gap in the market for family-oriented transport that competitors have ignored.

**Kill Criteria:** If pilot testing shows families reject the vehicle due to perceived commercial utility despite marketing, or if the compact platform cannot physically accommodate three rows of seating while maintaining cargo space, the project must stop.

**Edge:** First definition of a new category using idle assets and guaranteed capital when competitors are complacent.

DECISION: GO
