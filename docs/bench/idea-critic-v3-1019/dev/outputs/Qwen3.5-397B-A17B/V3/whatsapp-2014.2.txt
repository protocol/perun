**Steelman:** Acquiring the dominant cross-platform messaging app neutralizes the existential threat of mobile displacement and secures hundreds of millions of active users who have rejected the buyer's current mobile offerings.

**Attack:** The $19 billion price tag represents a >12x increase over the valuation from just 12 months prior, with zero revenue to justify the multiple via traditional multiples. The target has explicitly refused advertising, destroying the buyer's primary monetization playbook. With only ~50 employees, integration risk and founder retention are extreme; if the founders leave, the asset's value (its culture and user trust) may evaporate. Privacy regulations could further cripple data synergy. This is a one-way door bet on network effects alone, assuming no competitor can displace this specific app.

**Alternatives:**
1.  **Do Nothing:** Risk continued erosion of the core business as users migrate to mobile messaging. Cost: Potential long-term irrelevance.
2.  **Build/Buy Competitor:** Attempt to launch a rival or buy a smaller player. Cost: High failure probability given the target's entrenched network effects; time cost allows further displacement.
3.  **The Proposal:** Pay a massive premium for immediate market dominance in mobile messaging.

**Fit:** Aligns with the survival imperative (North Star: maintain connectivity dominance), though it violates standard financial discipline.

**Pre-mortem:** The acquisition fails if regulators block data integration, the founders quit within a year taking the user base with them, or the company never monetizes the users, resulting in a permanent $19B write-down.

**Edge:** The target possesses a unique, non-replicable network effect of hundreds of millions of daily active users who have actively rejected ads, creating a defensive moat that competitors cannot breach quickly. The buyer has the cash reserves to absorb the shock.

**Blockers:** NONE. The financial metrics are secondary to the strategic necessity of owning the primary mobile communication layer.
**Risks:** Monetization failure, founder exodus, regulatory scrutiny.
**Kill Criteria:** If monthly active users drop by >10% within 12 months post-close, or if founders depart before integration milestones are met, initiate strategic review of the asset.

DECISION: GO
