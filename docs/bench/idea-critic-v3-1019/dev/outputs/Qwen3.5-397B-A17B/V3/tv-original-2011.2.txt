**Steelman:** Leveraging proprietary viewing data to bypass traditional pilot testing and greenlight a high-budget original series allows the service to secure exclusive content, differentiate from competitors facing rising licensing costs, and lock in top talent before the market shifts.

**Attack & Alternatives:** The primary blocker is the absence of a pilot. Industry standard dictates testing audience reception before committing to 26 episodes. However, the proposal claims unique data substitutes for a pilot. If this data is robust, the "do nothing" alternative (relying solely on licensed content) guarantees margin erosion as licensing costs rise. A cheaper alternative (a low-budget pilot) fails to attract the attached film star and director, negating the prestige value required to drive subscriptions. The "all-at-once" release is a high-risk differentiator but creates a unique market position that weekly broadcasting cannot match.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:** This fits a north star of becoming a primary destination rather than a content aggregator. Pre-mortem: If the data misinterprets niche interest as mass appeal, the service burns a massive portion of its budget on a flop with no mid-course correction possible after season one. Yet, waiting for perfect certainty allows competitors to secure the same talent or for licensing costs to become unsustainable.

**Evidence & Edge:** The edge is the proprietary viewing data, which competitors lack. If the data explicitly shows demand for this specific genre/creator combination, the risk of the pilot phase is redundant. The "no pilot" approach is not negligence but a strategic exploitation of an information asymmetry. The cost is high, but the cost of inaction (losing relevance and margin) is higher.

**Blockers:** None. The lack of a pilot is a feature of the strategy, not a flaw, provided the data supports it. The proposal explicitly relies on this data.
**Risks:** Total budget loss if data interpretation is flawed; inability to recoup costs without a proven audience.
**Edge:** Exclusive access to granular viewer preference data allowing confident bypass of traditional gatekeepers.

DECISION: GO
