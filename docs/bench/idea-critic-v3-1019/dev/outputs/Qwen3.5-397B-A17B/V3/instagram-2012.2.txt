**Claim:** Acquire the photo-sharing app for $1B to neutralize a mobile threat and capture its user base.

**Steelman:** The $1B price is not for current revenue but for the exclusive option to own the dominant mobile photo network before an IPO makes it unbuyable, instantly solving the buyer's mobile deficit and removing a existential competitor.

**Analysis:**
The "no revenue" objection is irrelevant; the asset is the network effect and user growth, which are non-linear and impossible to replicate internally given the buyer's own weak mobile track record. Waiting for a business model to emerge cedes the platform to a rival or the public markets, where the cost would likely exceed $1B or the opportunity would vanish entirely. The "culture risk" is manageable via operational independence, a standard tactic for such acquisitions. Regulatory review is a delay, not a blocker, given the market definition in 2012.

The "do nothing" alternative is catastrophic: it leaves the buyer with a failing mobile product facing a rapidly scaling monopoly on mobile attention. Building a competitor from scratch has failed thus far and ignores the speed of the threat. No cheaper alternative exists; the target is the unique leader.

**Pre-mortem:** The deal fails if integration destroys the product's core appeal or if regulators block it. However, the cost of delay (losing the mobile generation) vastly outweighs the financial loss of the acquisition price. The "edge" is the buyer's unique capacity to absorb the cost and provide infrastructure while leaving the product intact, something no other bidder can match at this scale.

**Blockers:** NONE. The valuation is high, but the strategic necessity of owning the mobile layer justifies the premium over building or losing.
**Risks:** Integration friction, regulatory delay. These are managed via kill criteria (user retention rates post-close) and legal prep, not reasons to stop.
**Edge:** Only this buyer can pay the premium and absorb the risk to secure the mobile future immediately.

DECISION: GO
