**Attack & Alternatives:**
The primary blocker hypothesis is that consumers and carriers will reject a high-priced device without a physical keyboard or third-party apps, especially from a company with zero phone experience. However, the "do nothing" alternative guarantees erosion of the core music player business by competing handsets. Licensing the software to multiple carriers (the standard model) sacrifices the user experience and margin control required to differentiate. A cheaper, keyboard-equipped variant would dilute the premium brand and fail to solve the interface obsolescence risk.

This aligns with the north star of integrating communication and media. Yet, the cost of delay is catastrophic: ceding the "internet in your pocket" segment to competitors who will inevitably integrate music deeply, rendering the standalone player obsolete.
