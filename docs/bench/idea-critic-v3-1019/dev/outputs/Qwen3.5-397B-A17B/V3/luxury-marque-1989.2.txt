**Steelman:** A dedicated luxury brand with a distinct dealer network and an all-new flagship sedan can overcome economy-car stigma, undercut European rivals on price while matching quality, and capture market share despite high development costs and a limited initial lineup.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The primary blocker is brand perception: history suggests economy buyers do not seamlessly transition to luxury, and luxury buyers often reject mass-market roots. However, the proposal explicitly mitigates this via a *separate* brand and dealer network, creating the necessary psychological distance. The "thin lineup" risk is real but manageable if the flagship is exceptional; luxury segments often launch with a halo car.

The economic argument holds: pricing "well below" European leaders while matching quality creates a compelling value wedge. The sunk cost of development is irrelevant to the forward-looking decision; the question is whether future returns exceed the *additional* capital required for the dealer network and launch. If the engineering already matches leaders, the marginal cost to launch is the primary variable.

**Pre-mortem:** The venture fails if the separate dealers cannot achieve sufficient volume to cover overhead before brand equity is established, or if the "value" pricing signals "cheap" rather than "smart" to luxury shoppers.

**Kill Criteria & Edge:**
*   **Blockers:** None identified in the text. The risks are known and addressed by the structural separation of the brand.
*   **Edge:** The ability to offer European-matching quality at a significantly lower price point due to existing engineering efficiencies and a leaner cost base than legacy luxury incumbents.
*   **Risks:** Brand rejection, dealer profitability, single-model vulnerability.
*   **Decision Logic:** The proposal attacks the right problem (stigma) with the right structural solution (separation). The value proposition is clear. The risks are executional, not fundamental flaws in the thesis.

**Dissent Ledger:** Critics argue the dealer network cost is prohibitive and the brand stain is permanent. Counter: Separation isolates the stain; value disrupts the incumbents' pricing power.

**Leading Metric:** Sales volume of the flagship vs. break-even target within 18 months of launch.

DECISION: GO
