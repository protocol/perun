**Steelman:** Secure a generational talent early to dominate basketball culture, using controversy over uniform rules as a catalyst for brand identity and sales, accepting high upfront cost for long-term market leadership.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The proposal fails the "edge" test on current facts. The player is unproven; relying on potential rather than performance is speculation, not strategy. The financial exposure is disproportionate: committing a large share of the marketing budget to a single unverified asset creates a single point of failure. If the player underperforms or suffers an injury, the brand loses its entire basketball launch capital with no recovery path.

The "better way" is clear: wait for professional performance data. Signing after even one season reduces risk significantly while likely costing less than the premium required to secure an unknown now. The proposed "edge"—breaking uniform rules—is a double-edged sword. While it generates attention, it relies on the league fining the player, which could alienate team partners and limit court availability, directly hurting the visibility needed to sell shoes.

Historical data cited in the risks indicates signature shoes rarely last beyond a season. This proposal bets against that trend without evidence that this specific player can reverse it. The "cultural icon" outcome is a hope, not a fact derived from the proposal's premises. A sound strategy requires a proven asset before committing irreversible, budget-dominating resources. The plan lacks a kill criterion or a staged approach (e.g., a shorter initial term with performance escalators) to mitigate the total loss scenario.

**Verdict:**
The plan commits excessive resources to an unproven variable with known historical failure rates for similar initiatives. The reliance on regulatory conflict as a primary marketing driver introduces unnecessary operational friction. Without a proven track record or a capped, reversible financial structure, the downside outweighs the speculative upside.

DECISION: NO-GO
