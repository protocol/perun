Steelman: Differentiate via novel interaction and price to expand the market rather than competing on raw graphical power.

**Blockers:** NONE. The proposal does not rely on impossible technology or contradictory facts. Dual-screen LCDs and resistive touch were available in 2004; keeping the legacy unit on sale mitigates supply risk.

**Risks:** Developer confusion and the "gimmick" perception are valid execution risks, not structural blockers. The lack of proven touch-game hits is precisely why a dedicated, lower-cost platform is necessary to create the category. Competing on graphics against a well-funded rival with superior media features is a losing battle for this specific company; the "do nothing" or "match specs" alternatives fail the strategic fit of broadening the audience.

**Edge:** The company dominates handheld gaming, providing an installed base and brand trust to onboard developers despite initial hesitation. The lower price point creates a clear market segmentation against the premium rival. The decision to keep the older model on sale preserves cash flow and serves price-sensitive users while the new hardware proves its value. This leverages existing distribution and software libraries to de-risk the hardware novelty.

**Decision Method:**
1.  **Alternatives:** Matching specs yields a me-too product at a higher cost with no unique value. Doing nothing cedes the innovation narrative to the rival.
2.  **Fit:** Aligns with broadening the audience (non-core players prefer intuitive touch over complex buttons).
3.  **Pre-mortem:** If it fails, it will be due to poor first-party software support, not the hardware concept itself.
4.  **Kill Criteria:** If first-party launch titles do not demonstrate intuitive touch usage within 6 months, or if the price cannot be maintained at least 20% below the rival, the strategy must pivot.

The proposal survives the attack because it avoids a direct feature war where the company is disadvantaged, instead creating a new value proposition backed by market dominance and a safety net (legacy device).

DECISION: GO
