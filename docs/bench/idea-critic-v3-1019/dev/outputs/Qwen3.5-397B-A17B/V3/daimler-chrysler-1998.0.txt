**Steelman:** A stock-for-stock merger creates a top-three global automaker, combining German engineering prestige with US mass-market profitability and truck dominance to achieve unbeatable scale and platform synergies.

**Attack:** The proposal fails the "merger of equals" test and ignores fatal cultural friction.
1.  **Cultural Incompatibility:** German hierarchical, consensus-driven engineering culture clashes fundamentally with US aggressive, quarterly-profit-focused management. Integration costs will dwarf platform savings.
2.  **Cyclical Trap:** Acquiring a partner dependent on a light-truck boom just before a likely economic cycle turn exposes the combined entity to massive downside risk without diversification.
3.  **Governance Illusion:** "Combined board" structures in cross-border mergers of this size typically result in power struggles, not synergy. One side will dominate, rendering the "equals" pitch false and demoralizing the acquired talent.
4.  **Brand Dilution:** Mass-market association risks eroding the German firm's premium pricing power, while the US firm gains little immediate tech transfer value.

**Better Way:** Pursue a limited joint venture for specific platform sharing or component sourcing. This captures cost benefits without the existential risk of full cultural integration or balance sheet entanglement.

**Kill Criteria:** If integration costs exceed 15% of projected synergies within 24 months, or if premium brand margins drop by >3% due to brand association, the merger logic collapses.

**Edge:** NONE. The proposed "scale" is a commodity advantage easily matched by competitors without the cultural baggage. Being first to merge across these specific cultures is a liability, not an asset. The "complementary" nature is superficial; the operational realities are contradictory.

**Decision:** The risks of cultural collision, cyclical exposure, and governance paralysis outweigh the theoretical benefits of scale. The "merger of equals" framing masks a likely hostile takeover dynamic that will destroy value.

DECISION: NO-GO
