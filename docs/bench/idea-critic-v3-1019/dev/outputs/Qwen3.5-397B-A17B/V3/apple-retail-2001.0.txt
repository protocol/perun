Steelman: Direct retail is the only viable channel to demonstrate the integrated hardware-software music strategy, as third-party retailers lack the incentive or expertise to sell a premium, loss-making niche product during a recession.

**Skeptic:** The claim assumes brand equity is strong enough to draw foot traffic despite a 3–5% market share and recent losses. It assumes the "digital music strategy" is sufficiently compelling to overcome the industry's poor retail track record. However, the proposal admits high fixed lease costs amidst a recession; if the music strategy fails to resonate immediately, the burn rate from prime mall locations could accelerate bankruptcy faster than the current wholesale losses.

**Better-Way:** Alternatives include: (1) Shop-in-shop kiosks within existing retailers to control demonstration without full lease liability; (2) Online direct sales with a generous return policy to mitigate tactile needs; (3) Waiting for economic recovery before committing to long-term real estate. These options offer lower fixed costs and reversibility. The proposal rejects the status quo (wholesale), but does not prove why a capital-intensive middle ground (kiosks) is insufficient for the "demonstration" requirement.

**Kill-Criteria:** This is a one-way door; once leases are signed and build-outs complete, exit costs are massive. The cost of delay is losing the "first mover" advantage in digital music integration, but the cost of failure is insolvency.

**Decision Analysis:**
*   **Blockers:** None explicitly proven false, but the "recession + low share + high fixed cost" triad creates a fragile financial model where any slip in adoption is fatal.
*   **Edge:** The only potential edge is total control over the customer experience to sell the "music strategy," which competitors cannot replicate in their crowded, price-focused environments. If the music strategy is the sole path to profitability, and wholesale channels actively undermine it, this radical shift is the only logical move despite the risks.
*   **Verdict:** The proposal identifies a specific, existential channel conflict that cheaper alternatives cannot solve. While risky, the "do nothing" approach guarantees continued erosion by competitors in third-party stores. The high risk is the price of the required differentiation.

DECISION: GO
