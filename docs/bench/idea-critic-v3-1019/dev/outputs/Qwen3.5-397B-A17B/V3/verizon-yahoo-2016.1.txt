**Steelman:** Combining two distressed media assets creates sufficient scale and data to challenge the duopoly, leveraging the carrier's distribution to reverse declining traffic.

**Attack:** The claim fails on momentum and fit. Both assets suffer from structural, not cyclical, decline; adding two falling lines does not create an upward trajectory. The carrier lacks core competencies in consumer media, creating a high probability of integration failure rather than synergy. The "scale" sought is in a shrinking market segment, while the dominant platforms grow via algorithmic superiority, not just inventory volume.

**Alternatives:** 
1. *Do Nothing:* Preserve $4.8B capital; avoid integrating two toxic cultures.
2. *Build/Buy Tech:* Invest in mobile-first technology or data infrastructure where the carrier has native strength.
3. *Partner:* License content rather than owning decaying portals.

**Pre-mortem:** One year post-close, ad revenue accelerates downward due to talent exodus and technical debt. The carrier attempts to force wireless subscriber data into legacy ad stacks, triggering privacy backlash and regulatory scrutiny. The $4.8B becomes a massive goodwill write-down.

**Evidence & Fit:** The proposal admits the target's revenue and traffic are falling for years and management is unstable. The buyer has "no history" in the sector. There is no stated edge—no unique technology, product advantage, or proven demand—only the hope that size alone defeats incumbents. Being large in a dying category is not an edge.

**Blockers:** 
- **No Edge:** The plan relies on "scale" in a declining market without a mechanism to reverse the decline or out-innovate incumbents.
- **Capability Gap:** The buyer explicitly lacks the required operational history.
- **Momentum:** Acquiring negative momentum rarely yields positive results without a fundamental product pivot, which is not proposed.

**Risks:** Massive integration friction, accelerated revenue decay, culture clash, capital misallocation.

**Decision:** The proposal lacks a credible mechanism to reverse the target's decline or a defined competitive edge against the duopoly. It bets on scale in a shrinking market with an unqualified operator.

DECISION: NO-GO
