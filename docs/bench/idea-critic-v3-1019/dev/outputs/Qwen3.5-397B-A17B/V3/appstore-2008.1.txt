The claim is that a closed, curated native app store will succeed despite prior executive skepticism of native apps and existing open rival ecosystems. The steelman: a single controlled store balances developer reach with product integrity, turning security and stability from risks into selling points while capturing revenue via a 30% cut.

**Skeptic:** The CEO's public stance against native apps contradicts this pivot; if web apps were truly sufficient, this introduces unnecessary complexity. However, the existence of rival app ecosystems suggests market demand has shifted, forcing a strategic reversal. The "polish" concern is valid but mitigated by the proposed review process.

**Better-way:** Alternatives include maintaining the status quo (web apps only) or adopting the rivals' open distribution model. Web apps fail to match native performance and user engagement seen on rivals' platforms. Open distribution exacerbates security and carrier concerns. The proposed hybrid (native but curated) is the only path that addresses carrier objections while meeting user demand for rich apps.

**Kill-criteria:** If the review process creates a bottleneck delaying key apps by >3 months, or if carriers block the device due to bypassed services, the model fails. The 30% cut must not deter top-tier developers compared to open rivals.

**Edge:** The "walled garden" approach offers a unique value proposition: guaranteed stability and security that open rivals cannot match, appealing to both cautious carriers and quality-conscious consumers. This turns the CEO's previous "polish" argument into the product's primary differentiator.

**Blockers:** NONE. The strategic pivot is justified by competitive pressure and market validation.
**Risks:** Review scalability and carrier relations are manageable operational challenges, not existential blockers.
**Edge:** Curated quality as a differentiator against fragmented open rivals.

The proposal survives the attack: the contradiction in executive stance is resolved by market reality, and the curated model offers a distinct advantage over both the status quo and open rivals.
