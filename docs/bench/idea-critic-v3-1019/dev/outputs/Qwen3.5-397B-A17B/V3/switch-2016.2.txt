Steelman: A hybrid form factor leverages existing IP to capture both home and portable markets, creating a unique value proposition that rivals cannot match, thereby resetting the brand after a failure.

**Blockers:** NONE. The proposal acknowledges weak specs and thin software but posits that uniqueness, not power, drives adoption. No internal fact contradicts this; the financial loss from the previous console necessitates a high-upside bet, making "do nothing" (continued erosion by phones) the greater risk.

**Risks:** Third-party abandonment due to weak silicon; consumer confusion persisting from the previous generation; supply chain inability to meet hybrid demand; price sensitivity given the spec gap. These are execution risks, not structural blockers. The "modest-power" choice is a deliberate trade-off for battery life and form factor, not an oversight.

**Edge:** The maker possesses a deep library of exclusive franchises that drive hardware sales regardless of raw teraflops. The hybrid concept creates a new category where the maker is the sole incumbent, neutralizing direct comparison with more powerful stationary rivals. The detachable controllers enable social play patterns competitors ignore.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the marketing message focuses on power rather than versatility, or if the launch window lacks at least one system-selling exclusive title. The "confusing positioning" risk is mitigated only if the hybrid nature is demonstrated instantly, not explained.

**Decision Logic:** The alternative ("do nothing") guarantees continued decline as phones erode the handheld line and the brand remains tainted by the previous failure. A cheaper, lower-spec handheld alone cedes the living room; a traditional home console repeats the previous failure mode. The hybrid approach is the only path that differentiates the product sufficiently to command the proposed price point despite lower specs. The risks are manageable through focused marketing on the form factor and leveraging first-party content.

DECISION: GO
