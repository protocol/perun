**Steelman:** Leverage the computer maker's brand and the acquired OS to enter the tablet market immediately, betting that heavy investment in developer outreach and advertising can overcome a small app catalog and unproven status.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Ecosystem Deficit:** The proposal admits the app catalog is a "small fraction" of the leader's. In 2011, tablets are media consumption devices; without apps, the hardware is non-competitive regardless of advertising spend.
2.  **Market Timing:** Entering a market where a single competitor dominates with a mature ecosystem, while the OS has "almost no market share," creates a massive adoption barrier.
3.  **Leadership Void:** Launching during a "leadership transition" removes the strategic stability required to sustain the proposed "heavy investment" against inevitable early losses.

**Risks:**
*   Shoppers perceive the product as unproven (confirmed fact, not hypothetical).
*   Heavy ad spend may yield low conversion if the core value proposition (apps) is missing.
*   Developer outreach takes months/years to bear fruit; the launch window requires immediate parity.

**Edge:** NONE. Brand recognition and marketing budget are not edges when the core product utility (software library) is critically deficient compared to the incumbent. Being "first" with this specific OS/hardware combo is irrelevant if the ecosystem is empty.

**Pre-mortem:** The tablet launches to fanfare but returns are high within 90 days because users cannot find essential apps. Developers ignore the outreach because the installed base is too small to justify porting costs, creating a death spiral. The leadership transition causes budget cuts to the ad campaign before traction is built.

**Decision Logic:** The proposal relies on marketing to solve a product gap (app scarcity). This is a fundamental mismatch. The "small app catalog" is a blocker, not a risk, because it directly negates the primary use case of the device. A "go-if" requiring a minimum viable app count is impossible to meet instantly. Launching now guarantees capital destruction.

DECISION: NO-GO
