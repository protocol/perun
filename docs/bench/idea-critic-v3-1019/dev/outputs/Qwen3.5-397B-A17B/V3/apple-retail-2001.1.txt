**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only viable channel to demonstrate the integrated hardware-software music strategy, as third-party staff actively suppress the brand to push higher-margin competitors.

**Attack & Options:**
The proposal fails the "do nothing" test only if the digital-music strategy is the sole path to profitability. However, the plan ignores fatal blockers:
1.  **Financial Mismatch:** A company with 3–5% share and recent losses cannot sustain the high fixed costs of prime mall leases during a recession. Low PC margins will not cover rent before volume scales.
2.  **Channel Suicide:** Opening stores directly competes with the only existing sales force (third-party retailers). These partners will immediately de-prioritize or drop the brand to avoid showing customers a cheaper direct option, collapsing the remaining 95% of revenue.
3.  **Execution Risk:** The industry has a proven poor track record for manufacturer-run retail. Attempting this while losing money violates the basic principle of preserving cash during a downturn.

**Better Way:**
The "cheaper alternative" is a shop-in-shop demonstration program within existing partner stores or a targeted online campaign. This achieves the "demonstration" goal without the lease liability or channel conflict.

**Kill Criteria & Edge:**
*   **Blocker:** The company lacks the cash reserves to survive the ramp-up period of physical retail given current losses. The plan relies on immediate success in a recessionary market with low margins.
*   **Edge:** NONE. Being first to market with a music strategy does not justify destroying the existing distribution channel. "Strategic necessity" is not an edge when the execution method bankrupts the firm.
*   **Risk:** High fixed costs + channel alienation = liquidity crisis.

The proposal attempts to solve a marketing problem (demonstration) with a capital-intensive solution (real estate) that destroys the current revenue base. The risks are immediate and existential; the benefits are speculative and achievable via lower-cost channels.

DECISION: NO-GO
