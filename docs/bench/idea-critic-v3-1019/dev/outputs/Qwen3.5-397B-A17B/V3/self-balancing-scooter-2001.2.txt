**Steelman:** A novel, self-balancing transporter offers a unique, efficient solution for short-distance personal mobility, leveraging first-mover advantage and strong branding to create a new market category despite high costs and regulatory ambiguity.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The proposal fails the "Edge" test. While the technology is novel, the plan relies on "being first" and "publicity" rather than a verified asset that guarantees adoption. The stated risks are not merely potential hurdles; they are active blockers under current facts:
1.  **Legal Status:** If the legal status on sidewalks and roads is "unclear," mass deployment invites immediate regulatory crackdowns or bans, rendering the product unusable where needed. This is a factual blocker, not a risk.
2.  **Market Fit:** With a $5,000 price point, unproven use cases beyond institutions, and high social friction ("conspicuous"), the unit economics for a 40,000-unit factory are unsupported. The proposal assumes demand exists without evidence that the target user values the utility enough to overcome cost and stigma.
3.  **Utility Limitation:** The inability to carry cargo severely limits its practical application compared to existing alternatives (walking, bicycles), weakening the value proposition for the high price.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Avoids massive capital expenditure on a factory before regulatory clarity and product-market fit are proven.
*   **Pilot Program:** Deploy a limited batch to institutional buyers only to validate utility and gather data on legal treatment before scaling manufacturing.
*   **Price/Feature Revision:** Lower the entry barrier or solve the cargo issue before mass production.

**Verdict:**
The plan commits to irreversible capacity (factory) based on assumptions contradicted by the stated risks. The "unclear" legal status is a definitive blocker for a mass-market launch; you cannot sell a vehicle that may be illegal to operate. The high price and unproven use cases further erode the likelihood of reaching the 40,000-unit target. There is no named edge that overcomes these factual deficits. The correct path is a constrained pilot, not a full launch.

DECISION: NO-GO
