**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only viable path to demonstrate the integrated hardware-software music strategy, as third-party staff actively undermine the premium value proposition, making current losses a symptom of channel misalignment rather than product failure.

**Attack & Options:**
The primary blocker is the fixed-cost structure (high leases) amid a recession and historical industry failures. However, "do nothing" guarantees continued margin erosion and brand dilution by competitors in existing channels. Alternative low-cost marketing cannot replicate the hands-on demonstration required for the new music strategy. The risk of channel conflict is real but secondary; resellers already prioritize cheaper units, so the company has little to lose in those relationships compared to the gain of controlling its own narrative.

**Fit & Evidence:**
The proposal aligns with a pivot from a struggling commodity hardware maker to a lifestyle brand. While the industry track record is poor, those failures involved selling boxes; this plan sells an ecosystem experience. The "recession" argument is a double-edged sword: prime mall leases may be more negotiable now, and competitors are retreating, leaving a vacuum.

**Pre-mortem & Door:**
If this fails, it will be due to operational incompetence in retail management, not the concept itself. This is a one-way door for capital expenditure but reversible regarding lease exits if structured correctly. The cost of delay is the continued inability to launch the music strategy effectively, ceding the digital future to incumbents.

**Blockers, Risks, Edge:**
*   **Blockers:** NONE. The financial risks are severe but calculable; no fact proves failure is inevitable given the unique product differentiation.
*   **Risks:** High fixed costs, inventory mismanagement, execution complexity.
*   **Edge:** The proprietary digital-music strategy creates a unique in-store experience that competitors cannot match and third-party retailers cannot explain. This specific product advantage overrides the general industry rule against manufacturer retail.

**Decision:**
The plan survives the attack because the unique requirement to demonstrate a new ecosystem outweighs the generic risks of retail. The edge is defensible.

DECISION: GO
