Steelman: Transforming from a pure retailer into a hybrid marketplace creates immediate inventory depth and revenue via fees without capital expenditure, turning competitors into contributors while the core business burns cash.

**Blockers:** NONE. The proposal explicitly accepts the risk of cannibalization and undercutting as the cost of entry. No factual contradiction exists in the text preventing execution. The fear of supplier objection is a risk, not a proven blocker, especially given the company's current lack of profitability which weakens supplier leverage.

**Risks:** 
1. **Cannibalization:** Used copies directly reduce high-margin new sales.
2. **Trust Erosion:** Third-party failures (shipping, quality) reflect on the platform, potentially destroying the brand before profitability is reached.
3. **Supplier Retaliation:** Publishers may withhold new titles or increase wholesale costs.
4. **Price Wars:** Competitors listing on the site may drive prices below sustainable levels for the host.

**Edge:** The platform already owns the customer traffic and the product page real estate. Allowing external sellers monetizes traffic that would otherwise bounce due to stockouts or high prices. It shifts the model from capital-intensive inventory holding to asset-light fee collection, directly addressing the "not yet profitable" constraint. Being first to aggregate new and used offers on a single page creates a "destination" effect for price-sensitive customers that pure retailers cannot match.

**Kill Criteria:** If third-party defect rates exceed 5% within six months, or if publisher delistings exceed 10% of top-tier titles, the program must be paused. Leading metric: Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) from third-party sellers vs. margin erosion on first-party sales.

**Decision:** The strategic necessity of reaching profitability outweighs the controlled risks. The "do nothing" alternative guarantees continued losses. The proposal turns existing traffic into immediate revenue while testing a scalable, low-capital growth engine. The risks are manageable operational challenges, not fundamental flaws.

DECISION: GO
