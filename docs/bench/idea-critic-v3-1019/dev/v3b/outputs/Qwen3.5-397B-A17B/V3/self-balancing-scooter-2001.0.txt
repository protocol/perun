**Steelman:** A novel, self-balancing electric transporter offers a unique, efficient solution for short-distance personal mobility, leveraging first-mover advantage and strong branding to create a new market category despite high initial costs and regulatory ambiguity.

**Blockers:** NONE. No factual contradiction exists within the proposal's own premises; the technology functions, capital is secured, and production capacity is defined.

**Risks:** High price ($5,000) limits mass adoption; unclear legal status on public paths threatens usability; unproven consumer demand beyond niche institutional buyers; social stigma ("conspicuous" riding); limited utility (cargo capacity). These are execution and market risks, not fundamental flaws proving the plan fails as proposed.

**Edge:** The combination of a well-known inventor, prominent investor backing, and heavy publicity provides a rare "launch window" to define a new category before competitors emerge. The proprietary self-balancing mechanism creates a defensible product advantage that rivals currently lack. Waiting for legal clarity or price reductions cedes this first-mover status.

**Analysis:** The objections raised (price, law, use-case) are typical for disruptive hardware entering a regulatory gray zone. They do not constitute blockers because the proposal does not claim immediate mass-market profitability or universal legal clearance. The "do nothing" alternative yields zero upside in a potentially transformative sector. A cheaper alternative does not exist yet; this *is* the baseline. The cost of delay is the loss of the brand narrative and market definition to future entrants.

**Decision:** The plan survives the attack because its risks are known variables to be managed post-launch, not pre-launch showstoppers. The edge (brand + tech monopoly) justifies the gamble.

**Kill Criteria:** If sales to non-institutional buyers remain below 5% of total volume after 12 months, or if major municipalities explicitly ban the device within 6 months of launch, the project must pivot to exclusively institutional/government contracts or cease production.

**Leading Metric:** Weekly unit sales to non-institutional customers, reviewed quarterly.

DECISION: GO
