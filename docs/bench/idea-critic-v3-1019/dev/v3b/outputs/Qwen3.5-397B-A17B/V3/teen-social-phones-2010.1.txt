The proposal fails the blocker test. The core claim—that young, price-sensitive users will adopt a device requiring a full smartphone data plan without apps or games—contradicts the stated facts of the target market's economics and preferences.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Economic Mismatch:** The target demographic is explicitly "price-sensitive," yet the requirement is a "full smartphone data plan" costing as much as the device itself. This creates a total cost of ownership that exceeds the segment's likely budget, especially when competitors offer cheaper alternatives.
2.  **Value Proposition Void:** The device offers "no app store and no games." For a "social-phone project for young people," this removes the primary utility drivers for the demographic. A social network alone cannot justify a smartphone-tier data cost without the ecosystem (apps/games) that typically justifies such plans.
3.  **Cannibalization & Timing:** Launching this niche product shortly before the company's main new phone platform creates internal competition and splits marketing focus. Given the "history of internal conflict" between teams, this increases the risk of organizational failure rather than market success.

**Risks:**
*   Carrier rejection due to low projected uptake.
*   Brand damage if the product is perceived as overpriced and under-featured.
*   Acquisition team demoralization if the second delay occurs due to poor strategic fit.

**Edge:**
No edge is identified. The proposal relies on "strategic necessity" or fear of missing the social trend, but offers no unique asset, cost advantage, or proven demand that rivals (or the company's own upcoming main platform) lack. In fact, the constraints (no apps, high data cost) actively disadvantage it against any standard smartphone.

**Kill Criteria:**
Since the plan fails on current facts, no future metric can save it in its current form. The premise that users will pay a premium for a restricted experience is unsupported.

**Decision:**
The plan cannot proceed as proposed because the fundamental unit economics and value proposition are broken for the stated target audience. It requires a complete revision of the pricing model, feature set (adding apps/games), or target demographic before it can be reconsidered.

DECISION: NO-GO
