**Verdict: NO-GO**

**Blockers:**
1. **Value Mismatch:** The proposal sets a flagship price for a device lacking flagship essentials (mainstream apps) while offering a niche differentiator (3D display) with historically low consumer demand. The "edge" (shopping features) does not justify the premium cost or the app gap for the mass market.
2. **Distribution Ceiling:** Exclusivity with one carrier at an unsubsidized flagship price severely limits the addressable market, making it impossible to achieve the volume required to sustain a forked OS ecosystem.
3. **Ecosystem Failure:** A smaller app store without leading apps is a fatal flaw in a two-platform dominated market; users will not adopt a primary device that cannot run standard software, regardless of shopping shortcuts.

**Risks:**
High inventory write-downs due to saturation and price resistance; brand dilution if the 3D feature is perceived as a gimmick; carrier partnership strain if sales targets are missed.

**Edge:**
The retailer's existing customer base and one-touch shopping convenience provide a minor utility advantage, but this is insufficient to overcome the structural deficits of the platform and pricing strategy. The "shopping phone" niche has repeatedly failed to command premium pricing when core utility (apps) is compromised.

**Analysis:**
The proposal fails the "cheaper/simpler" test: a lower-priced device or a software-only shopping integration would reach more users with less risk. The pre-mortem indicates failure due to the inability to attract third-party developers to a niche, carrier-locked hardware play. The cost of delay is low; waiting for app ecosystem maturity or hardware cost reductions is preferable to a premature flagship launch. The plan commits real money to a high-risk, low-reversibility hardware bet without a verified path to ecosystem viability.

**Kill Criteria:**
If a pilot with 1,000 users shows <20% daily active usage of the 3D/shopping features within 30 days, or if carrier pre-orders miss 50% of targets, the project must pivot to a lower-cost model or terminate.

DECISION: NO-GO
