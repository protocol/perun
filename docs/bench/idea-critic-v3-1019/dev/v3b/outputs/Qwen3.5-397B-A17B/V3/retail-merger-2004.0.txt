Steelman: Acquiring the distressed department chain provides immediate scale and real estate to pivot the discount chain's format faster than organic growth allows, leveraging restructuring momentum to secure a market position before big-box rivals dominate completely.

However, the claim fails the blocker test. The proposal relies on merging corporate functions and converting stores to a "combined format" to generate value. Yet, the facts state both chains have declining sales, underinvested/old stores, and distinct customer bases. There is no evidence of a viable "combined format" that appeals to either demographic; historically, merging two deteriorating value propositions creates a larger, unfocused entity rather than a synergistic one.

The cost savings from merging functions are explicitly labeled "uncertain," while the capital required to refurbish old stores and rebrand them is massive and immediate. With big-box rivals already growing, this acquisition diverts critical cash flow from fixing the core discount business to propping up a dying department store model. The "edge" is missing: the proposer offers no asset, cost advantage, or proven demand that rivals lack. Instead, they propose taking on the liabilities of a failing competitor during a period of weakness.

This is a classic "diworsification." The pre-mortem is clear: the integration drags down the emerging discount chain's liquidity, the store conversions fail to attract the department store's aging clientele while alienating discount shoppers, and the combined entity runs out of cash before realizing any theoretical synergies. The risk of declining sales is not mitigated; it is doubled. The cost of delay (waiting to see if organic restructuring works) is lower than the cost of this irreversible capital deployment.

Blockers: No viable combined format proven; uncertain savings cannot cover known refurbishment costs; distinct customer bases suggest cross-selling will fail.
Risks: Liquidity crisis, brand dilution, integration failure against growing rivals.
Edge: None identified in the proposal.

DECISION: NO-GO
