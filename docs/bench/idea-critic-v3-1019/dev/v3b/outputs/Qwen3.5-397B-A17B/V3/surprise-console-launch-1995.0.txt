**Steelman:** Launching in May 1995 at $399 secures a critical four-month head start to establish market presence and developer mindshare before the competitor arrives, betting that early availability outweighs limited software and higher cost.

**Attack:** The proposal fails the "edge" test. A console's value is its software library; launching with "few games" negates the hardware advantage. The $100 price premium (25% higher than the rival) is unjustifiable without a robust game lineup to demonstrate superior value. Furthermore, alienating major retailers by excluding them from the initial shipment creates a distribution blockade just as the competitor launches with full retail support. The "hard to program" constraint means the four-month window will not yield sufficient quality titles to convert early adopters, resulting in high return rates and damaged brand reputation before the main holiday season.

**Alternatives:**
1.  **Do Nothing (September Launch):** Match the competitor with full retail distribution, a complete game library, and a competitive price. Risk: Head-to-head marketing battle.
2.  **Staged Rollout:** Announce in May, but ship only demo units to press/developers, keeping the retail price competitive and launch date in September. Risk: Loses "first to market" headline, but preserves retailer relationships.

**Pre-mortem:** By December 1995, the early launch is viewed as a failure. Retailers excluded in May prioritized the competitor's shelves. Early buyers returned the console due to lack of games, calling it a "expensive paperweight." The developer community is frustrated by the rushed, unstable launch window, delaying future titles.

**Blockers:**
*   **Distribution:** Excluding key retailers guarantees they will block shelf space during the critical holiday season.
*   **Value Proposition:** No software library exists to justify a 25% price premium over the rival.
*   **Developer Readiness:** The hardware difficulty combined with the rushed timeline ensures a barren launch catalog.

**Edge:** None identified. Being first with an inferior product (high price, no games, angry retailers) is a strategic disadvantage, not an asset.

**Kill Criteria:** If the launch cannot secure top-tier retailer participation and at least 10 playable flagship titles by the ship date, the launch must be delayed to September.

DECISION: NO-GO
