**Steelman:** A portable, affordable cassette player using existing parts can create a new personal listening market for youth, bypassing dealer skepticism through volume pricing.

**Attack & Options:**
1.  **Dealer Doubt:** Irrelevant if direct demand exists. The "antisocial" risk is actually a feature: privacy for the user.
2.  **No Research:** Standard for category creation. Waiting for proof cedes the first-mover advantage.
3.  **Copycats:** Inevitable, but irrelevant to the *first* run. Speed to market and brand dominance matter more than patent protection initially.
4.  **Large Run Risk:** The only true blocker. If the price target isn't met, the value proposition collapses. However, the proposal explicitly ties the large run to the price aim.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
This fits a "north star" of democratizing high-fidelity audio. If it fails, it will be because the price point was missed or the cultural shift to "private listening" was slower than anticipated. The cost of delay is high: competitors will release similar devices once the concept is proven.

**Evidence & Edge:**
The proposal relies on assumed demand, but the edge is clear: no competitor is targeting the "mobile, personal" segment because they are fixated on recording functionality and home hi-fi. By stripping non-essential features (speaker, recorder), the cost structure allows a price point inaccessible to existing complex units. The "antisocial" concern is a generational mismatch; youth culture often values individual immersion.

**Blockers:** None identified in the text that invalidate the core logic, provided the large production run successfully achieves the targeted price.
**Risks:** Inventory overhang if the cultural shift lags; rapid imitation.
**Edge:** First-mover advantage in a created category; cost leadership via feature reduction.

**Decision:**
The proposal survives the skeptic test. The lack of market research is not a blocker but a characteristic of disruptive innovation. The risks are manageable compared to the opportunity of defining a new product category. The large production run is a calculated bet required to unlock the price sensitivity of the target demographic.

DECISION: GO
