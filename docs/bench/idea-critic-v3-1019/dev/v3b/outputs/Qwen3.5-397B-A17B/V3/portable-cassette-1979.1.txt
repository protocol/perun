**Steelman:** A portable, low-cost cassette player for youth, stripping non-essential features (speaker, recording) to achieve a price point competitors cannot match, creating a new personal listening category.

**Attack & Analysis:**
1.  **Dealer Doubt:** Staff skepticism stems from viewing the product as a "defective recorder" rather than a "portable music experience." This is a framing failure, not a product failure. The value proposition is mobility, not archival.
2.  **Antisocial Risk:** Headphones isolate, but the benefit is a private soundtrack for public life. This behavior shift is the core innovation, not a bug. Early adopters will drive the cultural norm.
3.  **No Market Research:** Historical data cannot validate a category that does not exist. Waiting for research guarantees missing the first-mover advantage. The hypothesis must be tested by shipping.
4.  **Copycat Risk:** Competitors can copy the *parts*, but they cannot instantly copy the *brand positioning* or the supply chain efficiency required to hit the low price point immediately. Speed to market is the only defense.
5.  **Large Run Gamble:** High volume is required to lower unit costs to the target price. This is a binary bet: succeed at scale or fail trying. A small run fails the economics; a large run forces the market creation.

**Verdict Logic:**
The objections are rooted in past categories (recorders, social norms) rather than the proposed future state. The lack of market research is actually a strength; it indicates a blue-ocean opportunity where data is nonexistent because the behavior is new. The risk of copying is mitigated only by speed and price aggression. The "antisocial" concern is a prediction of a cultural shift that the product itself will catalyze. The large production run is a necessary condition for the pricing strategy, not an optional risk. To hesitate for more data or smaller batches is to accept the economics of the old category, ensuring failure. The plan survives the pre-mortem: the only way it fails is if the company lacks the conviction to ship at the required scale and price.

**Edge:** First-mover definition of "personal stereo" combined with aggressive cost engineering that incumbents, married to high-margin recorder logic, cannot match quickly.

DECISION: GO
