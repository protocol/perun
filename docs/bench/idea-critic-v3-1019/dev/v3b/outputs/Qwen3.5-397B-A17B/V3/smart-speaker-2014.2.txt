Steelman: A dedicated, always-listening home speaker creates a new "ambient computing" category, leveraging existing retail logistics to bypass the poor smartphone reception and lock users deeper into the ecosystem.

**Blockers:** NONE. No factual contradiction exists in the proposal; the $199 price point and invitation-only launch specifically mitigate the financial risks of the previous hardware failure. The "always-on" concern is a market risk, not a logical blocker, addressed by the physical design and initial limited rollout.

**Risks:** High. Voice recognition failure could render the device useless; privacy backlash could damage the brand; the lack of a category means demand is unproven. However, these are inherent to pioneering a new product class, not flaws in the plan's structure. The invitation-only approach acts as a controlled burn to manage these risks.

**Edge:** The retailer possesses a unique advantage: a massive existing customer base with purchasing history and a membership program ($99 pricing) that competitors lack. Unlike phone-based assistants, this device offers a dedicated, frictionless interface for shopping and media within the home, creating a sticky ecosystem play that mobile phones cannot replicate due to battery and portability constraints.

**Alternatives:**
1. *Do nothing:* Cedes the emerging smart home market to competitors; relies solely on a recovering phone line.
2. *Wait for voice tech to mature:* Risks being a follower rather than a category definer; loses the first-mover data advantage.
3. *Cheaper, simpler device:* Would likely fail to deliver the "magic" required to shift consumer behavior; the premium features justify the premium price for early adopters.

**Decision Logic:** The proposal beats "do nothing" because the potential upside of defining a new category outweighs the contained financial risk of an invitation-only launch. The specific pricing tiers and limited availability demonstrate a reasoned approach to the identified risks. The edge (ecosystem integration and membership leverage) is distinct and actionable.

**Kill Criteria:** If voice command success rate is below 85% in the first 10,000 units or if return rates exceed 15% due to privacy concerns, halt mass production.
