**Steelman:** Leverage existing compact-car assets to create a new "garage-fit" family vehicle category, avoiding commercial van stigma while minimizing capital expenditure via factory conversion.

**Blockers:** NONE. The proposal explicitly addresses the cash constraint through asset reuse and factory conversion rather than greenfield construction. The "no category exists" objection is a market opportunity, not a factual blocker, provided the design successfully mimics car driving dynamics.

**Risks:** Brand perception (commercial vs. family), competitor replication, and spatial constraints inherent to the compact platform. These are execution risks, not fundamental flaws preventing initiation. The risk of rivals copying is mitigated by first-mover advantage in a newly defined segment.

**Edge:** The company possesses a unique, distressed-asset advantage: a government loan guarantee providing immediate liquidity, an existing compact platform ready for derivation, and a convertible factory. Competitors lacking this specific convergence of rescue capital and adaptable infrastructure cannot match the entry cost or speed. The "car-like" driving experience differentiates it fundamentally from truck-based rivals.

**Analysis:** The "do nothing" option leads to continued reliance on declining station wagons or inability to compete in the growing family hauler segment. The proposed path creates a defensible niche (minivan) with lower upfront CAPEX than developing a new chassis. The risks are manageable through marketing (positioning as family transport) and design optimization. The lack of a current category validates the innovation potential rather than invalidating the plan.

**Kill Criteria:** If prototype testing reveals the compact platform cannot accommodate three rows of seating or fails to deliver car-like handling, the project must pivot or stop. Success requires defining the vehicle strictly as a passenger carrier, not a cargo hauler, in all marketing materials.

**Decision:** The plan survives the pre-mortem. The financial constraints are addressed by the specific mechanism of asset reuse. The market gap is the primary opportunity. The edge is clear and fact-based.

DECISION: GO
