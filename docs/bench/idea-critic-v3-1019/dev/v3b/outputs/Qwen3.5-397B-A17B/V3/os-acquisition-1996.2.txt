**Steelman:** Acquiring a modern, object-oriented OS core from a small vendor is the only viable path to stop market share erosion after two failed internal replacements, leveraging the founder's expertise despite historical friction.

**Attack & Options:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Continued decline is guaranteed; internal teams have already failed twice.
*   **License Alternative:** Perpetual licensing fees erode margins long-term; integration control remains with a competitor; does not solve the strategic dependency.
*   **Build Again:** Proven failure mode; time-to-market exceeds runway.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:** The goal is survival and regaining relevance. Failure mode: Integration stalls due to cultural clash with the founder, or the acquired tech proves unscalable. However, the "few hundred million" price is a fraction of the company's market cap and cash reserves, making the financial risk contained compared to total business collapse. The low unit sales of the target are irrelevant; the asset is the architecture and talent, not current revenue.

**Evidence & Blockers:**
*   **Blockers:** NONE. The "high price vs. small revenue" objection ignores that the purchase is for technology and IP, not cash flow. The "difficult history" is mitigated by the "advisor" role rather than immediate operational control.
*   **Risks:** Migration complexity and cultural friction. These are execution risks, not deal-breakers, manageable via the advisor role and focused engineering sprints.
*   **Edge:** Only this specific acquisition provides a proven, modern, object-oriented foundation immediately available, bypassing the company's demonstrated inability to innovate internally.

**Decision:**
The proposal beats "do nothing" (death) and "license" (dependency). The cost is affordable; the alternative is extinction. The risks are real but manageable compared to the certainty of failure without action.

Blockers: NONE
Risks: Integration friction, migration cost.
Edge: Immediate access to proven modern architecture after internal failures.

DECISION: GO
