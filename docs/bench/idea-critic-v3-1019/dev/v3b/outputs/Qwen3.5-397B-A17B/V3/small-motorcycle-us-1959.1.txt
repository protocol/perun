**Steelman:** Leveraging domestic dominance in 50cc bikes to create a new US commuter segment via non-traditional retail, bypassing the entrenched "big bike" culture.

**Blockers:** NONE. The proposal explicitly acknowledges the risk of larger models failing at highway speeds but does not propose selling them as the primary entry; it focuses on the 50cc class where speed requirements are lower. The foreign currency constraint is a known cost, not a factual contradiction of feasibility.

**Risks:** Cultural rejection of small bikes; brand obscurity; mechanical failure of larger units if tested; capital strain. These are execution risks, not proof of impossibility. The "do nothing" alternative guarantees zero US presence and continued reliance on a saturated home market.

**Edge:** The company possesses a proven, reliable product for a specific use case (short-distance urban/suburban travel) that current US manufacturers ignore. By targeting sporting-goods stores, the plan avoids direct competition with established motorcycle dealerships and reframes the product as lifestyle equipment rather than heavy machinery.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the company insists on pushing larger bikes before validating the 50cc market or if they demand traditional motorcycle dealership networks that refuse small margins. The proposal mitigates this by specifying non-motorcycle shops and focusing on the 50cc unit.

**Decision Logic:** The plan beats "do nothing" because it targets an unserved demographic (students/commuters) with a low-cost entry point. It is reversible (subsidiary can be closed, inventory liquidated) compared to the irreversible loss of market opportunity. The risks are manageable hypotheses to be tested by the initial rollout, not static blockers. The specific channel strategy (sporting-goods stores) provides a unique wedge against incumbents.

**Kill Criteria:** If 50cc unit sales do not reach profitability within 18 months, or if warranty claims on any introduced larger models exceed 15% due to highway stress, the expansion halts.

DECISION: GO
