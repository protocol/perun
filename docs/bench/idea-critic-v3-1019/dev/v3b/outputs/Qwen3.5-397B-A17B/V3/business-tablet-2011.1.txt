**Steelman:** A 7-inch device on a new OS targets a niche where portability outweighs app breadth, betting that phone-pairing solves the email gap while the company transitions its ecosystem.

**Attack:**
1.  **Value Contradiction:** The company leads in business email, yet the device lacks native email, calendar, or contacts unless paired. For a standalone tablet buyer (the primary tablet use case), this renders the device useless for its core competency immediately upon unboxing.
2.  **Price/Value Mismatch:** Matching the price of a rival's larger tablet with a superior app store, while offering a smaller screen, an untested OS, and missing core apps, provides no rational incentive for adoption. The "portability" advantage does not offset the functional deficit at equal cost.
3.  **Ecosystem Trap:** Relying on phone-pairing restricts the total addressable market to existing phone owners, capping growth precisely when the goal is to stop rival momentum. Developers will not commit to an untested OS with a artificially limited user base.
4.  **Strategic Confusion:** Shipping a "business" device without built-in business tools undermines the brand's primary equity. It signals that the new OS is not ready for prime time, validating developer skepticism.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Preserve brand reputation; wait until the new OS has native business apps before entering the tablet market.
*   **Differentiate on Price:** If the device is smaller and feature-limited, it must be significantly cheaper than the rival's larger, full-featured tablet to justify the compromise.
*   **Delay Launch:** Postpone until native email/calendar are integrated, ensuring the device stands on its own merits.

**Verdict:**
The proposal fails the "beat do nothing" test. It launches a product that actively contradicts the company's strength (email) at a price point that offers no advantage over the incumbent. The dependency on phone-pairing is a blocker, not a feature, for a general tablet launch. The risks of brand damage and developer abandonment are immediate and high. There is no named edge that allows this specific configuration to win; "strategic necessity" to have *a* tablet does not justify launching *this* broken tablet.

DECISION: NO-GO
