The current plan bets the company's liquidity on an unverified asset at a peak valuation.

**Kill Criteria:** The lack of verified financial integrity combined with internal executive dissent constitutes a hard blocker. The risk of overpaying for fraudulent or inflated earnings is too high to ignore.
