**Steelman:** Removing the jack forces a transition to wireless, creating a high-margin accessory ecosystem that leverages proprietary silicon for seamless integration, offsetting customer friction with superior convenience.

**Analysis:**
The primary blocker is the $159 price point against free wired alternatives. However, the value proposition shifts from "audio output" to "untethered experience." The risk of ridicule regarding aesthetics is transient; functional freedom typically overrides initial visual skepticism once adopted. Loss risk is mitigated by the charging case, which also solves the 5-hour battery limitation by providing multiple recharges on the go.

The "anger" risk is real but strategic. Removing the jack is a one-way door that compels market adoption. If the phone maker waits for perfect conditions or lower prices, they cede the category definition to competitors. The proprietary chip ensures the "automatic pairing" creates a moat that third-party $159 options cannot match at launch.

**Alternatives:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Retain the jack. *Result:* Misses the opportunity to lead the industry shift, delays wireless ecosystem revenue, and maintains dependency on legacy ports.
2.  **Lower price ($99):** *Result:* Margins erode on new silicon; fails to signal premium status; leaves money on the table from early adopters willing to pay for convenience.
3.  **Delay launch:** *Result:* Competitors launch first; the phone maker looks like a follower rather than an innovator.

**Edge:** The integration of the proprietary chip for automatic pairing is the specific asset rivals lack. This reduces friction significantly compared to generic Bluetooth pairing, justifying the premium over free wired options.

**Kill Criteria:** If adoption of the wireless accessory is below 15% of new phone buyers within the first quarter, or if return rates due to "fit/loss" exceed 8%, the pricing or form factor must be revisited immediately.

**Verdict:** The plan beats "do nothing" because it forces a necessary ecosystem evolution while the company controls the hardware stack. The risks are manageable through the charging case and brand loyalty. The edge (proprietary pairing) is distinct and valuable.

DECISION: GO
