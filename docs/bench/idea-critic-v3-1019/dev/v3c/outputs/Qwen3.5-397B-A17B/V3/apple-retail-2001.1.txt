**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only viable channel to demonstrate the integrated hardware-software music strategy, as third-party staff actively suppress the brand to push higher-margin competitors, making current losses structural rather than cyclical.

**Attack & Options:**
The primary blocker is the contradiction between "recent losses" and "high lease costs" in a low-margin sector. However, the proposal does not rely on retail hardware margins; it relies on stopping brand erosion and capturing the full margin of the digital music ecosystem, which third-party channels cannot support.
*   *Do Nothing:* Continued reliance on hostile resellers guarantees further market share loss and commoditization.
*   *Cheaper Alternative:* Online stores or kiosks fail to provide the hands-on music demonstration required to sell the strategy.
*   *Fit:* Aligns with the pivot to a digital music ecosystem; hardware becomes a vessel for high-margin services.

**Pre-mortem & Evidence:**
If this fails, it will be because lease fixed-costs outlast the cash runway during a recession. The "poor industry track record" applies to competitors selling commodity boxes, not a differentiated ecosystem play. The risk of channel conflict is actually a feature: resellers are already effectively "conflicted" against this brand.

**Blockers, Risks, Edge:**
*   **Blockers:** NONE. The financial contradiction is resolved if the store's purpose is ecosystem acquisition, not unit margin.
*   **Risks:** Cash burn during recession; execution complexity of real estate management.
*   **Edge:** Total control of the user experience for a novel product category (digital music integration) that competitors cannot replicate in a multi-brand environment.

**Decision:**
The proposal survives the skeptic test because the "losses" are a symptom of the current channel strategy. The high cost of leases is the necessary price to secure the distribution of the new music strategy. Without this edge, the company commoditizes. The kill criterion is clear: if the stores do not drive a measurable uplift in the attached music ecosystem revenue within 18 months, the experiment fails. Given the strategic necessity to bypass hostile resellers, the potential upside outweighs the fixed-cost risk.

DECISION: GO
