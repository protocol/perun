Steelman: A hardware manufacturer can disrupt a duopoly by leveraging superior disc economics and developer tooling to bypass incumbent cartridge constraints, despite lacking platform experience.

**Blockers:** NONE. The proposal acknowledges the lack of experience and incumbent strength but posits a structural cost/tech advantage (CD vs. cartridge) that changes the economic model for third-party developers. No fact presented proves this advantage is insufficient to attract the necessary software library.

**Risks:** High. Failure to secure key third-party franchises could leave the hardware with no value proposition. Selling hardware at a loss requires deep pockets and precise volume forecasting. An incumbent launching a competing 3D system simultaneously creates a direct feature war against a brand with established loyalty. The "no experience" factor increases the probability of execution errors in supply chain or developer relations.

**Edge:** The proposer possesses specific manufacturing scale and cost structures distinct from the current gaming incumbents. The shift to CD media offers a tangible, verifiable cost and capacity advantage over cartridges that directly addresses a known pain point for publishers (manufacturing cost and lead time). This is a structural economic lever, not merely a marketing claim.

**Decision Logic:**
The proposal identifies a clear market inefficiency (cartridge cost/limits) and offers a viable technical solution (CD). While the risks of execution and competition are severe, they are characteristic of market entry, not fatal flaws. The "no experience" risk is mitigated by the tangible economic incentive offered to developers. The presence of a competing 3D launch validates the market direction rather than invalidating the entry. The hardware margin risk is standard for the industry and manageable if the software attach rate succeeds. There is no evidence provided that the structural advantage is illusory or that the market is closed to new entrants on principle. The potential upside of establishing a new platform ecosystem outweighs the verified risks, provided the execution on developer relations is flawless.

DECISION: GO
