**Steelman:** Launching a $700 pen-based PDA with heavy CEO publicity creates a new handheld category by betting that superior input methodology outweighs current technical limitations and high cost.

**Attack & Options:**
The proposal fails the blocker test on three verified facts provided:
1.  **Core Mechanism Failure:** Handwriting recognition is explicitly "unreliable in testing." For a device where this is the *main* input, this is not a risk; it is a functional defect that renders the primary value proposition unusable.
2.  **Form Factor Mismatch:** The device is "too big for a pocket," contradicting the essential utility of a "handheld" or "personal" digital assistant intended for mobile use.
3.  **Ecosystem Void:** "Little software" and an "unproven category" at a "$700" price point (a premium tier in 1993) suggests no immediate market pull to justify the hardware spend.

**Alternatives:**
*   *Do Nothing:* Avoids sinking capital into a defective core mechanism.
*   *Delay/Revise:* Wait until recognition is reliable and form factor is pocket-sized.
*   *Pivot:* Target a vertical market where size and price matter less, rather than a mass-market "personal" device.

**Pre-mortem:**
Assume failure: The device launches to heavy publicity. Users attempt to input data; the system misinterprets handwriting repeatedly. The device is too large to carry conveniently. Developers ignore the platform due to low install base. The result is a high-profile market flop that damages the brand's reputation for quality and drains resources.

**Edge Analysis:**
The proposal cites "heavy publicity by the chief executive" as a driver. Publicity is not an edge; it amplifies both success and failure. There is no stated asset (cost, product advantage, or demonstrated demand) that allows this specific plan to win despite the unreliable core mechanism. Fear of missing a category or "strategic necessity" are not edges.

**Blockers:**
1.  Unreliable core input mechanism (stated fact).
2.  Non-portable form factor for a handheld device (stated fact).
3.  Lack of software ecosystem at premium price (stated fact).

**Risks:** Market rejection, brand damage, capital loss.
**Edge:** NONE.

Without a reliable input method and portable form factor, the product cannot function as proposed.
