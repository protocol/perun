The claim is that a clear cola, successful in test markets, should launch nationwide immediately with a major campaign. The steelman: this extension captures novelty demand and repositions the brand as "purer" before competitors act, leveraging existing distribution to offset slowing core growth.

However, critical blockers exist in the stated facts. First, the primary risk—that shoppers psychologically link cola flavor with dark color—is a fundamental product-market fit issue, not an execution risk. If the sensory expectation fails, no marketing spend can fix the repeat rate. Second, the proposal admits test markets often overstate demand for novelty items; relying on them for a nationwide "major" launch ignores the specific failure mode of novelty decay. Third, cannibalization of the flagship is a near-certainty without a differentiated usage occasion, yet the proposal offers no strategy to mitigate this beyond "purer" positioning, which may not be a strong enough driver to switch loyalists permanently.

The "better way" is a staged rollout. A nationwide launch with a "large marketing campaign" is a one-way door with high sunk costs. A cheaper, safer alternative is a regional launch in diverse demographics with a capped marketing budget to verify repeat purchase rates over 6 months, specifically testing the color-flavor association. This addresses the novelty trap and cannibalization fears without betting the brand's annual budget on unproven psychology.

The pre-mortem: Six months post-launch, sales have collapsed because initial buyers found the taste "wrong" due to the clear color, and the flagship brand lost share to rivals who launched their own clear colas with better retention. The cost of delay is low; the cost of a failed national launch is catastrophic to brand equity and capital. The proposal lacks a specific kill criterion for repeat purchase rates distinct from initial trial, and fails to prove the "purer" angle overcomes the deep-seated color-flavor expectation. Without evidence that the color barrier is broken, the edge is undefined.

DECISION: NO-GO
