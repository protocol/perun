**Steelman:** Leveraging dominant search/mail distribution to force-feed a superior social layer (Circles, Hangouts) can overcome network effects where organic growth failed.

**Attack:**
1.  **Fit & History:** The company has three prior social failures. Integrating into core utilities (search, mail) risks degrading the primary value proposition (utility) for a secondary feature (social), potentially annoying the massive existing user base who did not ask for this.
2.  **Incentives:** Tying executive bonuses to *adoption* rather than *engagement* or *retention* incentivizes coercive integration and dark patterns over genuine product-market fit. This repeats the "build it and they will come" fallacy.
3.  **Better Way:** The incumbent's network effect is a moat that distribution alone rarely breaches without a fundamental behavior change. A "do nothing" or "acquire" stance preserves core utility focus. If social is vital, a standalone experiment with organic pull is safer than a forced company-wide pivot.
4.  **Pre-mortem:** The launch succeeds in sign-ups (due to force) but fails in daily active usage. Users resent the clutter, privacy concerns over "Circles" create backlash, and the core search product suffers performance or reputation hits. The company abandons it in 2 years, having damaged brand trust.

**Blockers:**
*   **Incentive Misalignment:** Bonus structure guarantees optimization for vanity metrics (sign-ups) over value (connection), ensuring a hollow network.
*   **Forced Integration:** No evidence suggests users want social layers in email/search; high risk of churn in core products.
*   **Historical Precedent:** Three previous failures suggest a systemic inability to execute social strategy, which a "forced" approach does not solve.

**Edge:** None stated beyond distribution, which has already failed to convert in three prior attempts. Distribution is not an edge if the product lacks pull.

**Verdict:** The proposal repeats historical errors, misaligns incentives, and risks the core business for a low-probability outcome. The "forced" nature is a specific blocker to genuine adoption.

DECISION: NO-GO
