**Steelman:** Direct-to-consumer (DTC) capture of full lifetime value and data ownership outweighs the immediate loss of high-margin licensing fees and the risk of execution failure, provided the company can sustain losses long enough to reach scale.

**Analysis:**
The proposal fails the "cheaper alternative" test. The current licensing model generates billions in high-margin, low-risk cash flow. Swapping this for a speculative DTC play requires building a massive technology infrastructure from scratch—a capability the conglomerate explicitly lacks—and engaging in a price war against an entrenched leader with 100M+ subscribers.

The "edge" cited is merely content ownership, which rivals also possess or can license elsewhere. Being a late entrant with a lower price point is not a defensible moat; it is a race to the bottom on margins without the volume to compensate. The pre-mortem is clear: the company burns cash on tech debt and marketing while licensing revenue evaporates, failing to hit subscriber targets before capital markets demand profitability.

The risks are not theoretical; they are structural. The proposal admits to no experience running a streaming platform. The "multi-year operating loss" is not a strategy; it is a dependency on infinite capital in a winner-take-most market. A superior alternative exists: a hybrid approach maintaining licensing while launching a premium, higher-priced niche service, or partnering with an existing tech giant rather than building a competing stack.

There is no named blocker that can be fixed with a minor revision; the fundamental unit economics and capability gap render the specific plan (low price, massive loss, self-built tech) unsound. The lack of a verified path to profitability or a technical foundation constitutes a fatal flaw in the current proposal.

**Blockers:** No existing tech platform; negative unit economics at launch; reliance on sustained losses without a clear path to margin expansion.
**Risks:** Permanent erosion of licensing revenue; failure to scale against incumbents; cable cannibalization outpacing DTC growth.
**Edge:** None identified beyond raw content library, which is currently monetized more safely via licensing.

DECISION: NO-GO
