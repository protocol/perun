The proposal claims a front-wheel-drive, compact-platform van will save a struggling carmaker by targeting families. Steelman: This vehicle creates a new "minivan" category, leveraging existing assets to offer car-like handling and garage fitment at a cost rivals cannot immediately match, directly addressing the cash constraint.

Alternatives:
1. **Do nothing:** Continue selling station wagons/vans; high risk of continued decline as no current product fits the emerging family need for utility without truck-like drawbacks.
2. **New large van:** Too expensive, violates cash constraints, fails garage test.
3. **Wait for recovery:** Ignores the immediate collapse risk; market share will erode.

Fit: Aligns with survival (north star). Reuses compact platform (asset) and converted factory (cost advantage).

Pre-mortem: Failure occurs if buyers reject the "car-based" image as "cheap commercial van." However, the "station wagon" buyer base already accepts car-based utility. The risk of rivals copying is mitigated by the company's immediate need for a lifeline; being first defines the category. The space limitation is a feature (garage fit), not a bug, for the target demographic.

Evidence: The company has a compact platform and a convertible factory. Cash is low, making this reuse strategy the only viable path. No existing category means zero direct competition, allowing definition of terms.

Blockers: NONE. The lack of a current category is an opportunity, not a blocker. Cash scarcity demands this low-capital approach.
Risks: Brand perception (commercial vs. family). Mitigation: Marketing must emphasize "family wagon" utility, not "work van."
Edge: First-mover advantage in a blue-ocean segment using sunk-cost assets (platform/factory) that competitors cannot replicate quickly without similar distress or foresight.

The plan beats "do nothing" by creating a market rather than fighting in a dying one. It respects the cash constraint by avoiding all-new development. The risks are manageable compared to the certainty of decline without innovation.

DECISION: GO
