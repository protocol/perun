**Steelman:** A simultaneous, unannounced global rebrand shocks the market into perceiving the retailer as immediately contemporary, bypassing the inertia of incremental change.

**Attack:** The proposal fails on three verified fronts. First, it ignores the "endowment effect": loyal customers value the existing logo specifically because of its history; removing it without warning feels like a betrayal, not an upgrade. Second, the "no advance announcement" strategy in the social media age is catastrophic; it cedes the narrative to critics, guaranteeing a backlash that frames the change as arrogant rather than fresh. Third, launching an untested design across *every* channel simultaneously removes all safety valves. If the new wordmark fails resonance tests (a high probability given the lack of testing), the brand suffers universal damage instantly with no ability to pivot or roll back specific channels.

**Better Way:** A phased rollout starting with digital channels allows for real-time sentiment analysis and adjustment. Co-creating the transition with loyalists (e.g., "evolving our heritage") mitigates alienation. Testing the new design with a focus group before commitment is a basic hygiene factor, not an optional delay.

**Fit:** While the goal (contemporary look) aligns with the vision, the method actively destroys brand equity—the very asset required to remain a retailer. A contemporary brand listens to its audience; this plan silences them.

**Pre-mortem:** Six months post-launch, sales dip 15% due to customer confusion and alienation. Social media is flooded with #BoycottBrand memes mocking the "generic" new look. The company is forced to quietly reintroduce elements of the old logo, admitting failure but having already spent millions on the full rollout.

**Blockers:** 
1. **Untested Design:** No evidence exists that the target demographic prefers the new look; assumption of superiority is unfounded.
2. **Universal Rollout:** Eliminates the ability to contain failure.
3. **No Communication:** Guarantees negative narrative control.

**Edge:** None. The proposal relies on shock, which is a tactic, not a strategic advantage. It offers no unique asset or insight that competitors lack.

**Kill Criteria:** If customer sentiment scores drop below baseline within 48 hours of a soft launch, halt immediately.

This plan trades decades of recognition for an unverified aesthetic guess with zero mitigation.

DECISION: NO-GO
