The specific design choice (raised cockpit) creates a unique dual-market asset (passenger/freight) that competitors lack, directly addressing the "supersonic threat" risk by ensuring utility even if passenger demand shifts. The "unproven engine" is a supplier problem, not a fatal flaw, leveraging external innovation.

1.  **Fit:** Aligns with growth and cost-reduction north stars.

4.  **Kill Criteria:** Proceed only if the launch customer contract covers a significant portion of development costs (implied by "asked by largest customer").

The proposal survives the attack because the risks are inherent to high-stakes innovation, not indicators of a broken plan. The specific design mitigations (freighter capability) directly neutralize the primary existential threat (supersonic replacement).
