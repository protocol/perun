**Steelman:** A cylindrical, always-listening speaker at $199 ($99 for members) creates a new home category, leveraging membership loyalty to overcome hardware skepticism despite a recent phone failure.

**Blockers:** NONE. The proposal explicitly addresses the primary risks (privacy, accuracy, category creation) through an invitation-only launch, which limits blast radius and allows iterative refinement before mass adoption. The pricing strategy creates a compelling value gap for members that existing phone assistants cannot match due to form factor and audio quality.

**Risks:** High potential for privacy backlash; voice recognition failure could render the core utility useless; lack of category means uncertain demand; recent hardware failure damages brand credibility in this segment. These are significant but manageable via the proposed "invitation at first" gate, which serves as a live pilot rather than a full commitment.

**Edge:** The retailer possesses a unique distribution channel (existing member base) and a specific use case (shopping lists/re-ordering) that phone-based assistants do not optimize for. The cylindrical form factor differentiates it from existing flat smart speakers, and the aggressive member pricing ($99) lowers the barrier to trial significantly compared to non-member competitors.

**Pre-mortem:** If this fails, it will be because the "always-on" feature triggered a privacy scandal that the invitation model could not contain, or the voice accuracy was too poor for the noisy home environment, causing users to abandon the device after the novelty wore off.

**Kill Criteria:** If fewer than 40% of invited users activate the device within 48 hours, or if return rates exceed 15% in the first month due to "privacy concerns" or "voice errors," the program halts.

**Decision:** The proposal includes its own safety valve (invitation-only) and a clear differentiator (member pricing + shopping integration). The risks are real but the mitigation strategy is built into the launch plan. The edge in distribution and specific utility justifies the bet despite the recent phone failure.
