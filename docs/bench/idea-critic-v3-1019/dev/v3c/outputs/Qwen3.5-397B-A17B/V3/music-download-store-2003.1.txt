**Steelman:** Leveraging a dominant hardware ecosystem to create the first legal, convenient, and fairly priced alternative to piracy, securing exclusive label partnerships to legitimize digital ownership.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The primary blocker hypothesis is that consumers will not pay for what they can steal for free. However, piracy offers poor user experience (search difficulty, malware, inconsistent quality). A 99-cent price point removes friction compared to album purchases while respecting the "single" culture of music. The risk of label withdrawal is mitigated by the manufacturer's market leverage; labels are desperate for revenue as physical sales collapse due to the very file-sharing cited as a risk.

The limitation to proprietary computers is not a bug but a feature in this context: it creates a "walled garden" where the hardware and software integration drives device sales, subsidizing the thin margins of the store. While rivals pursue subscriptions, the market in 2003 lacks the bandwidth and licensing frameworks for reliable streaming; ownership is the only viable immediate product. The "do nothing" option guarantees continued erosion of hardware value as the device becomes less useful without a legal music source.

**Pre-mortem:** If this fails, it will be because the copy protection is too restrictive, driving users back to piracy, or because labels demand unsustainable revenue shares. However, the proposal includes "light" protection and leverages the manufacturer's unique position to negotiate.

**Edge:** No other entity combines the hardware dominance, brand loyalty, and negotiation leverage required to convince major labels to license individually tracked songs. The integration of store and player creates a seamless loop competitors cannot match without building their own hardware.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks are manageable execution challenges, not fundamental flaws. The alternative (subscriptions) is premature for the technical landscape of 2003.
**Risks:** Label relations, margin pressure.
**Edge:** Hardware-software integration and brand trust.

DECISION: GO
