The primary objection is the monochrome screen against a color rival. However, color hardware drastically increases cost and power consumption. At $89.95, this device undercuts competitors significantly, removing the price barrier for impulse buys and parental gifts. The "modest" processor ensures weeks of battery life on standard AA batteries, a critical utility for travel that rivals cannot match without frequent recharging or expensive proprietary packs.

The risk of "single-game history" is mitigated by bundling a timeless puzzle title with infinite replayability, shifting the perception from a toy to a lifestyle accessory. Thin margins are acceptable if volume is massive; the low price point aims for market saturation, where profit derives from software attach rates and ecosystem lock-in rather than hardware unit sales.
