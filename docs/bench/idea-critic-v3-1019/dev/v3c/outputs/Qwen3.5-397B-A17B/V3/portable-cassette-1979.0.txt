**Steelman:** A portable, affordable cassette player for youth, leveraging existing parts to create a new personal listening category, accepting high initial volume to achieve price parity.

**Blockers:** NONE. The "no recording" constraint is a feature for portability and cost, not a functional failure. The "large run" requirement is a standard manufacturing constraint for target pricing, not an impossibility.

**Risks:** Dealer skepticism, perceived antisocial behavior, lack of prior market data, and easy copyability by competitors. These are execution and adoption risks, not proof of failure. The proposal acknowledges them; they do not contradict the core claim that a market exists.

**Edge:** First-mover advantage in defining a new "personal stereo" category before competitors recognize the shift from shared hi-fi to private mobility. Using existing parts accelerates time-to-market, allowing the brand to capture the youth segment before imitators react. The low price point (enabled by volume) creates a barrier to entry for higher-cost incumbents slow to adapt.

**Pre-mortem:** If this fails, it will be because dealers refused to stock it or the cultural shift to "antisocial" listening took too long. However, the low unit cost and high volume strategy mitigate financial exposure if the pivot is quick. The lack of market research is a risk, but the use of proven components reduces technical failure probability.

**Decision Logic:** The proposal beats "do nothing" by creating a new revenue stream in a stagnating hi-fi market. It beats "waiting for research" by seizing a cultural moment. The risks are manageable through marketing (reframing "antisocial" as "personal freedom") and aggressive distribution incentives. No factual contradiction exists in the plan; the doubts raised are opinions, not disqualifying facts.

**Kill Criteria:** If first-month sell-through to dealers is <60% of the initial run, halt production expansion. Leading metric: Units sold per capita in the 15–25 demographic within 90 days.

DECISION: GO
