**Steelman:** A premium, mobile-first short-form service leveraging A-list talent and proprietary orientation technology can carve a unique niche despite subscription fatigue, capitalizing on underserved demand for high-quality vertical/horizontal agnostic content.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The proposal fails the "cheaper alternative" and "evidence" tests.
1.  **Demand Contradiction:** The prompt explicitly states "no proven demand for premium short-form" while "free ad-supported short video is abundant." Asking users to pay a premium for a format they currently access for free, during a period of "subscription fatigue," lacks a validated value proposition. The "orientation technology" is a feature, not a content moat; it does not solve the core lack of demand for *paid* short narratives.
2.  **Better Way:** The "do nothing" or "wait" option is superior. Existing platforms (YouTube, social giants) are already experimenting with premium short-form. Entering now with a $1B+ burn rate requires proving users will switch *and* pay, which the proposal admits is unproven. A lower-cost pilot (e.g., a single show on an existing platform) would validate demand before raising capital for a standalone tech stack and studio slate.
3.  **Blocker:** The core claim relies on users paying for premium short-form. The stated fact ("no proven demand") directly contradicts the revenue model required to sustain "high production budgets" and "A-list talent." Without a pivot to an ad-supported model or proof of willingness to pay, the unit economics cannot work. The "orientation" feature does not mitigate the risk of zero subscription uptake.
4.  **Edge:** There is no named edge. "A-list talent" and "major studio investors" are inputs, not advantages, especially when those same studios are likely competing with their own content on existing platforms. Being first is not an edge if the market isn't there.

**Conclusion:** The plan commits massive capital to an unproven demand curve in a saturated free market. The stated lack of proven demand is a factual blocker that the proposed features do not resolve.

DECISION: NO-GO
