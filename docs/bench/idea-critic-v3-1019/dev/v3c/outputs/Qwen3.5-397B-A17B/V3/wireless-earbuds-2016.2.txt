**Steelman:** Removing the headphone jack forces a transition to wireless audio, creating a market for premium, seamlessly integrated earbuds that leverage proprietary silicon for superior user experience, despite short-term friction.

**Attack & Analysis:**
The primary blocker is customer anger over removing a functional port without a mature, affordable alternative. However, the "do nothing" option cedes the wireless future to competitors and maintains reliance on legacy tech. The proposed alternative (free wired earphones) is a sunk cost with zero margin and no strategic upside.

*   **Risks:** Ridicule of design and loss liability are valid but manageable through marketing (framing "odd" as "futuristic") and the charging case (mitigating loss/battery anxiety). The $159 price point is high but justifiable if the automatic pairing creates a "magic" experience wired buds cannot match.
*   **Edge:** Proprietary chip enables instant pairing and ecosystem lock-in, a capability generic Bluetooth buds lack. This turns a hardware removal into a services/accessory revenue stream.
*   **Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the battery life (5 hours) proves inadequate for daily commutes without the case, or if the price resistance is absolute. Yet, waiting allows rivals to define the standard. The cost of delay is losing the narrative control of the "wireless future."

**Verification of Objections:**
*   *Anger:* Inevitable but transient; solved by bundling an adapter (not mentioned but implied necessity) and marketing the benefit.
*   *Price:* High, but positioned as a premium upgrade, not a direct replacement for the free bud.
*   *Battery:* 5 hours is sufficient for most use cases when paired with a charging case.

**Decision Logic:**
No factual blocker exists. The risks are executional, not fundamental flaws. The strategic edge (ecosystem integration via custom chip) outweighs the temporary goodwill loss. The plan moves the North Star of a wireless ecosystem forward; doing nothing stalls it. The proposal includes the necessary hardware (case) to mitigate the top technical risks (battery/loss).

**Kill Criteria:** If initial pre-orders show >40% rejection due to price compared to historical accessory attach rates, pivot to a lower-cost SKU immediately.

DECISION: GO
