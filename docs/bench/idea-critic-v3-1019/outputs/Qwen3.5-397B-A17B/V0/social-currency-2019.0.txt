**Steelman:** A consortium-governed global currency, backed by safe assets and embedded in a ubiquitous messaging platform, could democratize finance and reduce transaction costs for billions, provided it navigates regulatory hurdles.

**Skeptic:** The proposal assumes regulators will tolerate a private entity issuing a global currency that rivals sovereign money. Given the company's active antitrust scrutiny and privacy scandals, trust is at an all-time low. The "association" governance model is untested and likely insufficient to satisfy central banks concerned with monetary sovereignty and financial stability. The risk of immediate, coordinated regulatory blockade in the US and EU is extreme, not theoretical.

**Better-Way:** The "do nothing" option avoids existential regulatory warfare. A superior alternative is launching limited, localized payment features using existing fiat rails and partner banks, which solves user needs without triggering a global sovereign debt crisis. This captures value while building trust incrementally.

**Kill-Criteria & Pre-mortem:** Assume failure: The project launches, and within six months, the US Treasury and ECB issue joint cease-and-desist orders citing systemic risk, forcing a fire sale of reserves and massive reputational damage. The one-way door here is the irreversible political capital burned and the precedent set for crushing private innovation. The cost of delay is low compared to the cost of a forced shutdown.

**Fit:** This does not move the north star of safe connection; it invites a war the company cannot win given its current standing. The basket of assets does not mitigate the sovereignty threat; it amplifies it.

**Decision:** The risks are asymmetric and catastrophic. The regulatory environment is hostile, the governance model is unproven under this specific pressure, and the company lacks the political capital to survive the inevitable backlash. Do not proceed with a global currency launch. Pursue localized, compliant fiat integrations instead.

DECISION: NO-GO
