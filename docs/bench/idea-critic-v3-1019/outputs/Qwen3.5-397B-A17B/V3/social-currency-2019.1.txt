**Steelman:** A consortium-backed global stablecoin integrated into a ubiquitous messaging platform could drastically reduce remittance costs and bank the unprovided, leveraging existing network effects for immediate scale.

**Attack:** The proposal fails the "monetary sovereignty" test. A currency used by two billion people, backed by major economies' securities but governed by a private association, inherently challenges central bank control over money supply and interest rates. History shows regulators (US Treasury, ECB) will not cede this power to a corporation with a documented history of privacy violations and antitrust scrutiny. The "association" structure offers no legal shield against sovereign intervention; it merely diffuses liability while concentrating political target size.

**Alternatives:**
1.  **Do Nothing:** Avoids immediate regulatory war but forfeits fintech leadership.
2.  **Domestic-First Pilot:** Launch in a single, friendly jurisdiction to prove compliance before global scaling.
3.  **Infrastructure-Only:** Provide the wallet technology to banks rather than issuing the currency itself.

**Fit:** While aligned with a vision of global connectivity, it contradicts the current strategic necessity of regulatory rehabilitation. The "edge" is distribution, but in finance, distribution without sovereign trust is a liability, not an asset. Being first here invites destruction, not dominance.

**Pre-mortem:** One year post-launch, the project is halted. Cause: The US and EU simultaneously declare the asset a systemic risk, freezing reserves and demanding a charter the association cannot obtain. Partners, fearing collateral reputational damage, exit the consortium.

**Blockers:**
*   **Sovereign Hostility:** The core value proposition (global scale) is the exact trigger for regulatory blocking. No amount of private governance overrides central bank mandates.
*   **Trust Deficit:** The sponsor's ongoing antitrust and privacy scandals make it the worst possible entity to lead a trust-sensitive financial utility.

**Edge:** NONE. Scale without regulatory license is a trap.

**Decision:** The plan relies on regulators accepting a private competitor to sovereign currency despite active hostility toward the sponsor. This is a one-way door with near-certain failure.

DECISION: NO-GO
