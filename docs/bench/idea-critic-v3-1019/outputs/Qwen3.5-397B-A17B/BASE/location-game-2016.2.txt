The proposal presents a high-reward opportunity but carries critical, unmitigated risks that threaten immediate failure. The core technical infrastructure is demonstrably insufficient; servers currently supporting a modest, devoted base cannot withstand the traffic surge expected from a famous global franchise. A collapse at launch would destroy user trust and brand reputation instantly, with no stated plan for rapid scaling or load testing.

Safety liabilities are severe. Encouraging users to walk while distracted by screens creates significant exposure to accidents and legal action. Without an integrated safety framework or warning system detailed in the plan, the company assumes unacceptable liability. Furthermore, the revenue model relies on a volatile conversion rate typical of free-to-play titles, yet the proposal admits location-based games have historically remained niche. Banking on a genre shift without proof of mass-market viability is speculative.

While the partnership offers immense potential, proceeding "as proposed, now" ignores the fundamental gaps in scalability, safety, and market validation. The current plan lacks the necessary contingencies for server overload, user safety protocols, and a robust monetization strategy beyond hopeful assumptions. Launching immediately invites operational disaster and legal exposure. The project requires a dedicated phase for infrastructure stress testing, safety feature integration, and a revised go-to-market strategy before any public release can be considered.

DECISION: NO-GO
