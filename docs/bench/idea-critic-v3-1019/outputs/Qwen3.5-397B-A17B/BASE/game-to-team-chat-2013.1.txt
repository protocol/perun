The proposal presents a classic pivot scenario where existing assets are repurposed to address a failing core business. The game is unprofitable with insufficient players, yet significant venture capital remains. This liquidity provides a runway to execute a strategic shift without immediate external funding. The internal chat tool, already built with searchable history and integrations, represents a tangible, functional asset that has solved coordination problems for the team itself. This validates product-market fit on a micro-scale.

The risks identified are substantial but manageable. While enterprise communication tools existed in 2013, the market was far from saturated, and incumbent solutions were often clunky or expensive. The lack of direct business-software experience is a hurdle, yet the team has successfully engineered a complex real-time system for gaming, demonstrating relevant technical competency. Transitioning from consumer gaming to B2B requires a shift in sales and support mindset, which is achievable with the remaining capital.

Regarding morale, layoffs are inherently difficult, but retaining a small, focused team to build a viable product is preferable to a slow, demoralizing decline of the game studio. Clear communication about the pivot's potential to save the company can align remaining staff. Investor concerns regarding the shift from games to business tools are valid; however, investors ultimately seek returns. Continuing to burn cash on an unprofitable game guarantees loss, whereas pivoting offers a path to profitability using developed technology. The alternative is total failure. The proposed action leverages sunk costs into a new opportunity, utilizing remaining funds to capture a growing market need for efficient team communication. The timing is opportune before the category becomes overcrowded.

DECISION: GO
