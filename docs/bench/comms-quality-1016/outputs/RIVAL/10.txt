**Subject: Oct 9 checkout outage: two decisions for Monday 10/20**

**Decisions needed**
1. **Assign owners and dates to the four open action items** (below).
2. **Whether to send a proactive note to affected customers.** Recommendation: yes, a short apology and status note now, with no credit commitments until legal finishes its review.

**What happened**
On Oct 9, from 14:12 to 15:58 UTC (1h46m), about 70% of checkout attempts failed. A typo in a config change cut the payment connection pool from 200 to 20. The pool ran out under afternoon load. There was no data loss and no security impact.

**Impact**
- About 41,000 failed checkouts, affecting about 18,500 customers.
- Estimated lost GMV is **$1.1M to $1.6M**. This is provisional: the range depends on how many customers retried later, and analytics expects the final figure Oct 24.
- Support received 2,340 tickets in 48 hours, against a typical 400.
- Six enterprise merchants contacted their account managers. One, Fabrikam Retail, has asked about SLA credits (contract maximum is 10% of the monthly fee). Legal is reviewing whether the incident qualifies.

**Why it lasted so long**
- Config values have no range checks, and config changes ship without a canary.
- Alert-to-acknowledgement took 12 minutes, with no escalation path when the primary on-call is already on another incident.
- Config deploys don't appear on the incident dashboard, so finding the cause took 32 minutes after SEV1 was declared.
- Rollback needed a second approver during business hours, which added 14 minutes.
