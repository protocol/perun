Application Security Review: Executive Summary

Decisions needed
1. Fund remediation as one dedicated project, or fold it into team roadmaps.
2. Name an owner for the unowned Critical finding (C2, below).

Overview
Halvard Security, an external firm, reviewed the customer web app, public API, mobile apps, admin console and CI/CD pipeline from Sept 8 to Sept 26, with our internal AppSec team. It found 31 issues: 2 Critical, 6 High, 14 Medium and 9 Low. Last year's review found 24. Of those, 19 are closed, and 5 Mediums remain open, two for over 12 months.

Have we been breached?
We have no evidence of it. C1 is a flaw that lets any support-role user view any tenant's billing details by changing an ID in a URL. Halvard exploited it in testing, and the last 90 days of logs show no abuse. However, logs are kept for only 90 days, so we cannot say anything about earlier activity. Halvard recommends extending retention to one year. The C1 fix is in progress (Admin team, target Oct 24).

C2 has no owner
Long-lived production deploy credentials sit in plain CI variables, readable by about 140 repo maintainers. Platform says this belongs to Security, and Security says it is Platform's pipeline. Fixing it means moving all 37 pipelines to short-lived credentials, an estimated 6 to 8 engineer-weeks. Halvard recommends assigning an owner within two weeks of the report.

This matters for compliance. Our SOC 2 Type II audit window opens Jan 15, and auditors will ask about C2 because control over production deploys is a tested control. Halvard could not say whether an open C2 with a remediation plan would be raised as an exception.

Other findings
High: the password reset rate-limiting gap was fixed Oct 3. The Android token storage fix is targeted for Nov 15. Four services have outdated dependencies with known vulnerabilities, with owners but no target dates.
Medium and Low: 23 findings, mostly headers, verbose errors and session timeouts. Halvard rates them low likelihood. Details are in Appendix A.

Cost and timing (AppSec estimate)
Total is about 28 engineer-weeks, roughly $210k at blended internal cost: Critical 9, High 7, Medium/Low 12.
As a dedicated project: complete by end of Q1.
Spread across team roadmaps: about Q3, based on past completion rates. AppSec calls this a rough guess.

Other Halvard recommendations: set remediation SLAs by severity.
