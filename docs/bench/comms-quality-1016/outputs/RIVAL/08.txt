**Subject:** WMS replacement: Stackline vs Corvane, for the Oct 29 review

Olivia,

Here is where the WMS vendor evaluation stands ahead of Oct 29. Ops Systems (Ravi Menon, Jo Alvarez) wrote the report with Finance and IT Security. It deliberately makes no recommendation.

**Why we're replacing it:** Our in-house WMS (built 2014) runs 4 DCs, and support for its database ends June 30 next year. Pick rate has been flat at about 92 lines/hr/picker for 3 years, while peers report 110 to 130.

**The choice:** Of 7 vendors sent the RFP, 5 responded and 2 were shortlisted after demos. The DC managers scored them independently.

Stackline
- Score 78/100: functional fit 31/35, cost 17/25.
- 5-year TCO about $3.63M.
- DC1 live in 7 months, all 4 DCs in 11.
- About 180 staff, Series C in March, SOC 2 Type II.
- Risks: one reference said go-live slipped 2 months, and peak-season support runs through a partner we haven't vetted.

Corvane
- Score 74/100: functional fit 26/35, cost 22/25.
- 5-year TCO about $2.92M, with more internal staff time because of custom ERP integration.
- DC1 live in 9 months, all 4 DCs in 14. That runs past the June 30 database deadline for 3 DCs.
- About 2,000 staff, stable, ISO 27001, SOC 2 report due Q1.
- Risks: the connector for our ERP version is in beta, and one reference on our ERP said integration took twice as long as planned.

**Common to both:** Neither has deployed with our cold-storage setup at DC3, which handles about 18% of volume. Both need a scanner refresh of about $240k that isn't in either quote.

**Timing:** Both need a signature by mid-November to start in December. Stackline's quote is valid until Nov 15. Corvane's states no expiry.

**Still unknown**
- Cost of extended database support (IT has not priced it, no date).
- Whether DC3 goes first or last (DC managers disagree).
- Vetting of Stackline's support partner (procurement, about 3 weeks).
- Legal review of both MSAs (not started).
