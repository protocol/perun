**Exec summary: Checkout outage, Oct 9 (postmortem draft)**

**What happened**
On Oct 9, from 14:12 to 15:58 UTC (1h46m), about 70% of Acme storefront checkout attempts failed. A config change deployed at 14:05 cut the payment connection limit from 200 to 20 because of a typo. Nothing in our process checked the value, and the change went out to everyone at once. No data loss and no security impact.

**Impact**
- About 41,000 failed checkouts across about 18,500 customers
- Estimated lost GMV: **$1.1M to $1.6M (preliminary)**. The range depends on how many customers retried later. Analytics will give a final figure on Oct 24.
- Support tickets: 2,340 in 48 hours, against a typical 400
- Six enterprise merchants contacted their account managers. Fabrikam Retail has asked about SLA credits (contract allows up to 10% of the monthly fee). Legal is reviewing whether the incident qualifies.

**Why it lasted so long**
Four things stretched a bad change into a 106-minute outage:
1. No automated check on config values
2. A 12-minute gap between alert and acknowledgement, because the on-call engineer was already on another incident and there was no escalation path
3. Config deploys don't appear on the incident dashboard, so finding the cause took 32 minutes after SEV1 was declared
4. The rollback approval rule added a 14-minute wait during the outage

Whether the original change received a proper review is still unconfirmed and is being checked.
