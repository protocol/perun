**Upgrade date library from 2.x to 3.1.0**

**Why:** 2.x reaches end of life on Dec 31. This moves us to 3.1.0 ahead of that date.

**What changed**
- 63 files changed (+412 / −389). Most are import path renames done with the codemod.
- Hand-edited files (please review these closely): `utils/dates.ts`, `checkout/DeliveryPicker.tsx`, `account/OrderHistory.tsx`, `i18n/config.ts`.

**Breaking change handled:** In 3.x, `format()` uses the locale's week start. Without a fix, "week of" labels in order history and the delivery date picker would have shifted by a day for US users. I pinned `weekStartsOn: 0` in our wrapper in `utils/dates.ts` and added tests for it.
