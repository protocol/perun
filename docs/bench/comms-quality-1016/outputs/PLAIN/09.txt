Northwind Annual Application Security Review: Executive Summary

Halvard Security (external), working with internal AppSec, reviewed the customer web app, public API, mobile apps, admin console and CI/CD pipeline from Sept 8 to 26 (report dated Oct 10). The data warehouse and corporate IT were out of scope.

Results: 31 findings (2 Critical, 6 High, 14 Medium, 9 Low), up from 24 last year. Of last year's findings, 19 are closed; 5 Mediums remain open, two for over 12 months.

Have we been breached? We have no evidence of it, but we cannot fully rule it out.
- C1 (Critical): a flaw in the admin console let any support user view any tenant's billing details. It was exploited in testing. Logs show no abuse in the last 90 days, but we keep only 90 days of logs, so we cannot speak to earlier periods. The fix is in progress (target Oct 24). Halvard recommends extending log retention to one year.
- C2 (Critical): production deploy credentials are stored as plain CI variables, readable by about 140 people. We have not assessed whether they were misused.

Critical and High items:
- C2 has no owner. Platform says it belongs to Security, and Security says it belongs to Platform. Fixing it means moving 37 pipelines to short-lived credentials (6 to 8 engineer-weeks).
- H1 (password reset rate limiting) was fixed Oct 3.
- H2 (Android token storage) is due Nov 15.
- H3 to H6 (outdated dependencies with known vulnerabilities in 4 services) have owners but no target dates.
- The 23 Medium and Low findings are mostly headers, verbose errors and session timeouts. Halvard rates them low likelihood. The full list is in Appendix A.

Compliance: our SOC 2 Type II audit window opens Jan 15. Auditors will ask about C2 because control over production deploys is a tested control. Halvard could not say whether an open C2 with a remediation plan would be raised as an exception.

Decisions needed:
1. Funding model. Total remediation is about 28 engineer-weeks (roughly $210k): Critical 9, High 7, Medium/Low 12.
 - Dedicated project: completion by end of Q1.
 - Team roadmaps: completion around Q3, which is a rough guess based on past rates.
 Recommendation: fund a dedicated project, at minimum for the Critical and High items. Roadmap-based delivery has left some Mediums open for over a year, and H3 to H6 have no dates.
2. Owner for C2. Halvard recommends assigning one within 2 weeks of the report (about Oct 24). Starting now would give the best chance of finishing C2 before the Jan 15 audit window. Even the dedicated-project timeline of end of Q1 falls after that date.
3. Supporting actions: extend log retention to one year and set remediation SLAs by severity.
