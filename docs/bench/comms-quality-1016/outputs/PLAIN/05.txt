**Title: Retry failed provider webhooks ourselves with exponential backoff**

**Problem**
When our handler threw on a provider webhook, we returned 500 and relied on the provider to retry. The provider retries only 3 times over ~1 hour. During the 9/18 DB blip we lost ~120 events.

**Change**
- On handler failure we now ack with 200, store the event, and retry it ourselves.
- Backoff is exponential, with a max of 6 attempts and a cap of 30 min between attempts.
- After the final attempt the event is dead-lettered and an alert fires.
- Idempotency on event id is unchanged.

**Files**
- `services/payments/webhooks/handler.py`: ack-and-store flow instead of returning 500 (+86 -31)
- `services/payments/webhooks/retry.py` (new): backoff and retry logic
- `services/payments/models/webhook_event.py`: adds `attempt_count` and `next_attempt_at`
- `migrations/0147_webhook_event_attempts.py`: migration for the two new columns
- `tests/payments/test_webhook_retry.py` (new): 24 tests

**Testing**
- 24 new tests pass. Full payments suite: 418 passed, 0 failed.
- Ran in staging with fault injection for 2 hours: 0 lost events.

**Not tested / needs your eyes, Ines**
1. **Duplicate delivery during our own retry.** If the provider re-sends an event while our retry for it is mid-flight, I expect the event-id idempotency key to cover it, but I couldn't reproduce this in staging, so it's unverified. I don't know whether we need a lock. Please look closely at the handler/retry interaction here.
2. **Migration lock time.** The migration adds columns to a table with ~40M rows. I haven't measured how long it locks the table. This needs checking before it runs in production (e.g. against a prod-sized copy), and we should decide whether it needs a different rollout approach.

**Merge/rollout**
I'd treat both items above as open until we've settled them. I'm not claiming this is safe to merge on the migration yet.
