**Subject: WMS replacement: Stackline vs Corvane, summary for Oct 29**

Olivia,

Ahead of the Oct 29 ops review, here is where the WMS vendor evaluation stands. Ops Systems (Ravi Menon, Jo Alvarez) prepared the full report with Finance and IT Security. It deliberately makes no recommendation, and neither does this summary.

**Why we're replacing it**
Support for our in-house WMS database ends June 30 next year. Our pick rate has been flat at about 92 lines/hr/picker for 3 years, while peers report 110 to 130. Of 7 RFP recipients, 5 responded and 2 were shortlisted: Stackline and Corvane.

**The choice**

| | Stackline | Corvane |
|---|---|---|
| Weighted score | 78/100 | 74/100 |
| Functional fit | 31/35 | 26/35 |
| Cost score | 17/25 | 22/25 |
| 5-year TCO | ~$3.63M | ~$2.92M |
| DC1 live / all 4 DCs | 7 / 11 months | 9 / 14 months |
| Company | ~180 staff, Series C in March | ~2,000 staff, stable |
| Security | SOC 2 Type II | ISO 27001, SOC 2 due Q1 |
| Quote validity | Expires Nov 15 | No expiry stated |

**Main risks**
- **Stackline:** One reference said go-live slipped 2 months. Peak-season support relies on a partner we haven't vetted.
- **Corvane:** Their connector for our ERP version is in beta, and one reference said integration took twice as long as planned. Its 14-month rollout runs past the June 30 end of support for 3 DCs.
- **Both:** Neither has deployed with our cold-storage configuration (DC3, about 18% of volume).

**Still unknown**
- Cost of extended database support, which matters most for Corvane's timeline. IT has not priced it and has no date.
- Whether DC3 goes first or last. The DC managers disagree.
- Stackline partner vetting, which procurement estimates at about 3 weeks.
- Legal review of both contracts, which hasn't started.

**Timing:** Both vendors need a signature by mid-November to start in December. Stackline's quote expires Nov 15. With legal review not started and vetting taking about 3 weeks, there is little slack after Oct 29.

**Also budget for:** A handheld scanner refresh of about $240k is needed under either vendor and is not in the TCO figures above.

Happy to walk through any of this before the review.
