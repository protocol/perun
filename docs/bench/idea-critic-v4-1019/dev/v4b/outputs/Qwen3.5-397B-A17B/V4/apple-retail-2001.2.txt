First call: NO-GO. A loss-making manufacturer with thin margins proposes entering a low-margin, high-fixed-cost business with a history of industry failure during a recession.

**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only viable channel to demonstrate the integrated music strategy and stop third-party staff from sabotaging sales, justifying the massive capital burn.

**Attack:**
1.  **Financial Suicide:** The company is already losing money. Adding high mall leases and inventory costs creates a fixed-cost burden that accelerates bankruptcy if sales do not spike immediately. In a recession, foot traffic and discretionary spending drop, making the "prime location" bet lethal.
2.  **Channel Warfare:** Alienating existing resellers who currently provide the bulk of volume will cause an immediate revenue cliff before new stores can mature. Resellers may drop the brand entirely in retaliation.
3.  **Execution Risk:** The proposal admits the industry has a "poor retail track record." Attempting to build a retail operation from scratch while fixing a core manufacturing business splits management focus.
4.  **Alternatives:** A "store-within-a-store" demo zone in existing retailers or a targeted online campaign achieves the "demonstration" goal without the lease liability.

**Blockers:**
*   **Capital Mismatch:** Current losses + high fixed leases = unsustainable cash burn. The math fails without immediate, guaranteed volume which a recession makes impossible.
*   **Strategic Contradiction:** You cannot fix a profitability crisis by entering a notoriously low-margin, high-risk sector.

**Risks:** Inventory obsolescence, reseller abandonment, brand damage from empty stores.

**Verdict:** The plan relies on optimistic foot traffic and immediate cultural adoption to offset structural financial weaknesses. It bets the company on a channel it does not understand, in an economic environment that punishes fixed costs, while threatening its current distribution lifeline. The "digital music strategy" does not justify risking insolvency. The cheaper alternative (enhanced demos in existing chains) must be exhausted first.

DECISION: NO-GO
