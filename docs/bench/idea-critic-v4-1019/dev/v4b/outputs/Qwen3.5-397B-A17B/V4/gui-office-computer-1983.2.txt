First call: NO-GO. The stated price is prohibitive, performance is inadequate, and internal cannibalization is imminent.

Steelman: This machine offers a revolutionary interface that could redefine office productivity if the market values intuition over raw specs.

Attack:
1. **Price:** At $10,000, the unit costs several times more than established competitors. Without unique, high-value software to justify the premium, office managers will reject it immediately. The proposal admits "little third-party software," destroying the value proposition.
2. **Performance:** "Runs slowly" is a fatal flaw for business tools where throughput equals money. A graphical interface that lags frustrates users rather than empowering them.
3. **Internal Conflict:** A cheaper, similar machine is already in development internally. Launching the expensive, slower version now guarantees it will be obsolete upon the sibling's release, wasting marketing spend and confusing sales channels.
4. **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves capital. Waiting for the cheaper internal model captures the same interface benefit at a viable price point. Accelerating the cheaper model is superior to launching this flawed unit.

Blockers:
- **Price/Value Mismatch:** The cost is multiples of competitors without corresponding software or speed advantages.
- **Internal Cannibalization:** A superior (cheaper) internal alternative exists, making this launch strategically redundant.
- **Performance Deficit:** Slow operation contradicts business efficiency requirements.

Risks:
- Market rejection due to price.
- Brand damage from releasing an underpowered, overpriced product.
- Sales channel conflict between the two internal models.

The proposal fails the basic test of beating "do nothing" or the internal alternative. It asks the company to sell an inferior product at a premium just before a better version arrives. There is no path to profitability or strategic gain as proposed. The existence of the cheaper internal project removes any urgency to launch this specific configuration.

DECISION: NO-GO
