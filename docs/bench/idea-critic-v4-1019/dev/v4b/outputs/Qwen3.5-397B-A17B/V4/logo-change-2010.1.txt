First call: NO-GO. The proposal mandates a high-risk, irreversible brand change with zero validation and a communication strategy guaranteed to inflame the very loyalty it seeks to preserve.

**Steelman:** A simultaneous, unannounced rollout creates a singular shockwave that forces immediate market adaptation, preventing a prolonged period of brand confusion.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" retains current equity. A "staged pilot" in select markets or channels offers the same contemporary signal with measurable feedback loops. The proposal beats neither; it ignores the cheaper, safer option of testing.
2.  **Fit:** While "contemporary" is a valid north star, alienating the core loyal base contradicts the fundamental goal of a retailer: selling to existing customers.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure. Why? Loyalists perceive the unannounced change as erasure of heritage, not evolution. Social media amplifies this outrage instantly. The "no announcement" strategy removes the company's ability to frame the narrative, ceding control to critics.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal admits the design was "not tested." This is not a risk; it is a factual gap that invalidates the core assumption that customers will accept the new look. The risks raised (backlash, attachment) are stated as possibilities but treated as ignorable.

**Blockers:**
*   **Untested Core Assumption:** The plan relies on customers accepting a new identity without prior validation. The stated fact "new design was not tested" directly contradicts the viability of a "roll out to every channel at once."
*   **Self-Defeating Communication:** The "no advance announcement" clause ensures the first customer interaction is confusion or anger, maximizing the probability of the raised social media backlash.

**Risks:** (Converted to Kill Criteria if revised)
*   Loss of loyal customer segment.
*   Viral negative sentiment.

**Decision Logic:**
A plan that explicitly refuses to test its primary variable (customer acceptance) and chooses the most volatile distribution method (unannounced, all-channel) fails the basic operator test. The absence of a pilot or metric is a blocker here because the proposal explicitly rejects the only safe way to proceed (testing).

DECISION: NO-GO
