First call: NO-GO. Paying $1B for a pre-revenue app with no proven business model violates basic capital discipline, regardless of strategic fear.

**Steelman:** The acquisition is a defensive necessity to neutralize the primary mobile threat before the IPO locks in the buyer's stagnation, buying immediate market leadership and talent that cannot be built internally fast enough.

**Options:**
1. **Buy:** Immediate threat removal, massive culture/regulatory risk, $1B cash burn.
2. **Do Nothing:** Risk losing mobile relevance; save $1B for organic build or smaller targets.
3. **Partner/License:** Lower cost, but fails to stop the competitor's momentum or secure exclusivity.

**Fit:** Aligns with mobile survival (assumed north star) but contradicts fiscal prudence given zero revenue.

**Pre-mortem:** Integration fails; engineers quit due to culture clash; regulators block the deal post-announcement causing stock drop; the "threat" was a bubble that bursts naturally within 18 months, making the $1B payment look like panic.

**Evidence:** All figures ($1B, 12 employees, $0 revenue) are stated facts. The valuation multiple is infinite. The "threat" is qualitative, not quantified in lost revenue.

**Blockers:**
1. **Financial Incoherence:** No revenue means no basis to value the asset at $1B. The proposal admits "no business model," making the price purely speculative fear, not investment.
2. **Execution Risk:** Acquiring a 12-person culture into a large corp historically destroys the very value (talent/innovation) being purchased.

**Risks:** Regulatory blocking, culture destruction, opportunity cost of $1B.

**Decision Logic:** While the strategic threat is real, paying $1B for *zero* revenue and *no* business model is not a sound operational decision; it is panic. A sound operator caps the loss or demands a earn-out based on future monetization, not a blank check today. The lack of a business model is a blocker because it prevents validating that the $1B buys anything other than temporary fear relief.

DECISION: NO-GO
