First call: NO-GO. The unit economics and market timing fundamentally contradict the capital structure.

**Steelman:** A premium, niche service for global travelers can generate sufficient early cash flow to service debt while ground networks remain incomplete in key regions.

**Attack:**
1.  **Market Shrinkage:** The target demographic (international business travelers) is rapidly being served by expanding ground cellular roaming. The "window of opportunity" is closing faster than the sales channel can open.
2.  **Price Elasticity:** At $3,000 per handset and several dollars per minute, the addressable market is too small to support the subscriber growth rate required to service $5 billion in debt. The math does not close; the volume needed at that price point likely does not exist.
3.  **Product Deficit:** Bulky hardware with poor indoor performance creates a negative user experience that contradicts the "premium" pricing strategy, increasing churn risk immediately upon launch.
4.  **Operational Readiness:** Launching without ready sales channels guarantees a slow ramp-up, directly conflicting with the immediate cash flow needs of a highly leveraged balance sheet.

**Alternatives:**
*   *Do Nothing:* Halt commercial launch, restructure debt, and wait for hardware costs to drop or pivot to government/industrial contracts where price sensitivity is lower.
*   *Cheaper Path:* Lease capacity to existing carriers rather than building a consumer brand and retail channel from scratch.

**Pre-mortem:** The company launches, misses subscriber targets by 40% in year one due to price and channel friction, defaults on interest payments within 18 months, and enters bankruptcy while ground cellular achieves near-total coverage.

**Blockers:**
*   The projected subscriber base required to service $5B debt at the proposed price point exceeds realistic market size estimates given the rapid expansion of cheaper ground alternatives.
*   Sales channels are not ready, making the required immediate revenue ramp impossible.

**Risks:** High churn due to indoor signal failure; faster-than-expected ground network saturation.

The proposal requires immediate cash flow to service massive debt but offers a product with barriers to adoption (price, size, signal) that will prevent that cash flow. The gap between required growth and probable adoption is fatal.

DECISION: NO-GO
