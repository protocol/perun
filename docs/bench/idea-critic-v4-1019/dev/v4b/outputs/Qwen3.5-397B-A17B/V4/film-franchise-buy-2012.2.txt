First call: NO-GO. The proposal relies on a creative lead exiting and recent mixed reception, making a $4B bet on unproven new entries a speculative gamble rather than a secured asset.

**Steelman:** Acquiring the franchise secures essential IP to feed parks and merchandise, with the trilogy serving as a catalyst to reactivate dormant fan spending despite the founder's departure.

**Attack:** The claim fails the "creative lead" test. The founder is the primary asset; their retirement removes the unique vision that defined the franchise. Recent mixed reviews indicate declining relevance, not dormant potential. Paying a premium ($4B) for a declining asset without its creator is buying a liability. The risk of fan rejection is high given the recent track record. Furthermore, existing merchandise licenses limit immediate upside, delaying ROI while the company services the acquisition cost. The "do nothing" or "license only" alternative offers exposure to upside without the massive capital risk of ownership during a transition period.

**Fit:** While the strategy aims to feed all business units, the core engine (films) is unproven without the founder. A north star of "franchise synergy" cannot be met if the content pipeline fails to resonate.

**Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 2015. Cause: The new trilogy alienates core fans due to lack of original vision, box office underperforms against the $4B basis, and park integration stalls because the brand heat never reignites. The company is stuck with high fixed costs and diluted equity.

**Evidence:** The proposal itself admits mixed recent reviews and the founder's exit. These are facts, not risks. No evidence suggests a replacement creative team exists or that the market demands new entries without the founder.

**Blockers:**
1. **Creative Void:** The founder's retirement is a stated fact that removes the primary value driver, contradicting the claim of a "ready-to-exploit" asset.
2. **Valuation Mismatch:** The $4B price is based on past glory, not current trajectory (mixed reviews), creating an immediate value gap.

**Risks:** Fan rejection, license limitations, high capital cost.

Without a named successor creative lead or a mechanism to guarantee fan acceptance, the math does not work. The plan assumes success where the stated facts suggest high probability of failure.

DECISION: NO-GO
