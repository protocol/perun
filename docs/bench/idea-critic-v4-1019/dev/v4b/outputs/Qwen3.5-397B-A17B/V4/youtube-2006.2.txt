First call: NO-GO. The price is astronomical for an asset with zero revenue, unproven monetization, and existential legal threats that could bankrupt the combined entity.

**Steelman:** Acquiring the dominant cultural platform immediately secures the future of video search and user-generated content, neutralizing a competitor before they solve monetization.

**Attack:**
1.  **Financial Suicide:** $1.65 billion for a company with "no meaningful revenue" implies betting entirely on a hypothetical ad model. If the model fails, the stock destruction is catastrophic.
2.  **Legal Time Bomb:** The core inventory consists of copyrighted material owned by litigious media companies. A single adverse ruling could force the removal of all popular content, rendering the platform worthless overnight. The buyer inherits this liability immediately.
3.  **Operational Fragility:** "High bandwidth bills" with a "very small staff" suggests the infrastructure is unsustainable at current scale, let alone with growth. The technical debt may be unpayable.
4.  **Better Way:** Continue building the existing smaller service organically or wait for the target to collapse under legal/financial pressure, then acquire assets for pennies.

**Pre-mortem:** Six months post-acquisition, major studios win an injunction. All premium content is removed. User traffic drops 90%. The stock price halves, wiping out more than the purchase price. Bandwidth costs continue to bleed cash with no ad revenue to offset them.

**Evidence Check:** The proposal admits zero revenue and active legal threats. These are not risks; they are current facts contradicting the valuation. No calculation supports a $1.65B price tag without guaranteed monetization and legal immunity, neither of which exists.

**Blockers:**
*   **Valuation Mismatch:** Price relies on non-existent revenue and unproven models.
*   **Existential Liability:** Copyright lawsuits threaten the entire business model, not just margins.
*   **Unit Economics:** High fixed costs (bandwidth) with no income creates immediate cash burn.

**Risks:** Ad model failure, integration complexity, staff retention. These are secondary to the blockers.

**Kill Criteria:** Do not proceed unless legal counsel provides a written opinion that copyright liability is capped below $500M and a signed LOI from three major studios guarantees licensing within 90 days. Neither is present.

The proposal asks to trade liquid stock for a lawsuit wrapped in a bandwidth bill.

DECISION: NO-GO
