First call: GO. The proposal leverages existing assets (compact platform, converted factory) to address a clear market gap (garage-friendly family hauler) at a time when the brand is vulnerable but recently stabilized by government backing.

**Steelman:** A front-wheel-drive minivan on a compact platform offers car-like handling and garage fitment, creating a new category for families currently forced into oversized vans or wagons, utilizing idle capacity to minimize capital expenditure.

**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Continue selling wagons/full-size vans; risk irrelevance as fuel efficiency demands grow and competitors eventually fill the gap.
2.  **New large platform:** Prohibitively expensive given cash constraints; high risk of failure.
3.  **Proposed approach:** Reuses proven mechanics, lowers tooling costs via factory conversion, and targets an unserved demographic. It beats "do nothing" by creating a unique value proposition with manageable financial exposure.

**Fit:** Aligns with survival and efficient capital use. Assumes families prioritize convenience and efficiency over traditional "truck-like" van image.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if marketing fails to distinguish the vehicle from commercial work vans, or if the compact platform compromises cargo utility too severely. However, the "garage fit" is a strong differentiator. This is a two-way door; if it fails, production can cease with limited sunk cost due to shared parts. Delay costs first-mover advantage in a nascent segment.

**Evidence:** Based on stated facts: government guarantee provides breathing room; existing compact platform reduces R&D; converted factory lowers CapEx. No contradictory data provided.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks cited (no existing category, cash limits, copycats) are actually arguments *for* the plan: no category means blue-ocean potential; cash limits dictate the proposed low-capex approach; copycats validate the opportunity but cannot move as fast on a converted line.

**Risks:** Brand perception (commercial vs. family), platform space constraints. These are mitigated by design focus on "car-like" driving and marketing.

**Decision:** The plan turns constraints (cash, factory) into advantages. It creates a market rather than fighting for share.

DECISION: GO
