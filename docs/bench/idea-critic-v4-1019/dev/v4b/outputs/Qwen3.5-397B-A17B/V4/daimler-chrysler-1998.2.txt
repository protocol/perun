First call: NO-GO. The proposal relies on a cyclical peak and ignores fatal cultural asymmetry.

**Steelman:** Combining German engineering prestige with US mass-market profitability creates a diversified global giant that survives regional downturns through platform sharing and complementary brand portfolios.

**Attack:**
1.  **Cyclical Trap:** The US partner's profitability is tethered to a light-truck boom. Buying at the 1998 peak means acquiring maximum earnings just before a likely cyclical correction. The "merger of equals" math collapses when the US cash cow shrinks, turning the German partner into a rescuer rather than a peer.
2.  **Cultural Incompatibility:** "Merger of equals" is a fiction between a hierarchical, consensus-driven German engineering culture and a decentralized, financially aggressive US sales culture. Without a single dominant decision-maker, strategic paralysis will occur during the inevitable downturn. History shows such dual-headquarters, dual-board structures fracture under stress.
3.  **Brand Dilution:** Platform sharing between a premium luxury brand and a mass-market volume producer risks eroding the exclusivity that justifies the German firm's price premiums. The cost savings do not offset the long-term brand damage.
4.  **Takeover Dynamics:** The structural concerns regarding "true equality" suggest this is a disguised takeover. If the German firm dominates, the US talent flees; if the US firm dominates, the premium brand loses its soul.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Preserve the German firm's margins and brand equity; wait for the US cycle to turn before considering a distressed asset purchase.
*   **Strategic Alliance:** Form a joint venture for specific platform sharing without merging balance sheets or boards, limiting exposure to cultural clash and cyclical risk.

**Pre-mortem:** Two years post-merger, the truck market crashes. The combined entity misses earnings. The boards deadlock on restructuring costs. Key US executives exit, and the German brand suffers quality perception issues due to rushed cost-cutting. The stock trades below the sum of its parts.

**Blockers:**
*   The "merger of equals" structure cannot survive the asymmetric shock of a US truck market correction.
*   No mechanism exists to resolve dual-culture deadlock before a crisis hits.

**Risks:** Brand dilution, integration failure, cyclical timing error.

DECISION: NO-GO
