First call: GO. The hybrid form factor directly addresses the eroding handheld market and differentiates from failed home-only strategies, leveraging existing IP despite hardware deficits.

**Steelman:** A differentiated hybrid device leverages strong first-party software to overcome modest hardware specs, capturing both home and portable markets at a competitive price point.

**Options:**
1.  **Proposal:** Hybrid launch. High differentiation, moderate risk, reversible via price cuts.
2.  **Do Nothing:** Continue erosion by phones; inevitable decline.
3.  **Power-First Home Console:** Direct competition with rivals; high cost, high risk of repeating previous failure.
The proposal beats "do nothing" by offering a unique value proposition unavailable to phone users or rival console owners.

**Fit:** Aligns with the need to stop handheld erosion and recover from home console losses. Assumes the brand's IP remains strong enough to drive hardware sales.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if third-party support collapses entirely, leaving only a thin first-year lineup that fails to justify the price. The "confusing positioning" risk is mitigated only if marketing clearly demonstrates the "play anywhere" utility immediately.

**Evidence:** Facts provided indicate financial loss from the previous home console and erosion of handhelds. The proposal uses a modest chip to maintain price parity with powerful rivals, accepting a specs disadvantage to enable the hybrid form factor.

**Blockers:** NONE. No fact provided proves the plan fails as proposed. The "thin game line-up" is a stated constraint, not a contradiction; the plan relies on the maker's own studios, which is feasible.

**Risks:** Weak specs may limit long-term third-party interest; supply constraints could dampen launch momentum; positioning confusion could persist if marketing fails. These are execution risks, not blockers.

**Kill Criteria:** If sell-through does not exceed the previous home console's launch window sales within 6 months, pivot pricing or bundle strategy immediately. Leading metric: Weekly attach rate of first-party titles.

DECISION: GO
