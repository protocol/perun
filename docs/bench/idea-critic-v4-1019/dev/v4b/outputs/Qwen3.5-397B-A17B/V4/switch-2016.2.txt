First call: GO. The hybrid form factor directly addresses the eroding handheld market while leveraging existing IP, offering a unique value proposition distinct from both failing home consoles and phones.

**Steelman:** A hybrid device creates a new category where portability outweighs raw power, capturing users who value flexibility over fidelity, thereby revitalizing the brand without competing directly on specs.

**Alternatives:** 
1. *Do nothing:* Continued erosion of handheld line and brand irrelevance; high risk of exit.
2. *Power-focused home console:* Repeats previous failure against established rivals; ignores handheld decay.
3. *Mobile-only software:* Cannibalizes hardware margins and loses ecosystem control.
The hybrid approach beats "do nothing" by creating a defensible niche.

**Fit:** Aligns with the need to stop financial bleeding and reclaim market relevance. It assumes the brand's first-party studios can drive early adoption despite a thin lineup.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if third-party support remains non-existent due to weak specs, or if the "confusing positioning" narrative persists, leading to poor consumer understanding. The one-way door is the massive hardware investment; delay costs another year of handheld market share loss.

**Evidence:** Facts state the previous home console failed and handhelds are eroding. The proposal offers a novel solution (hybrid) rather than repeating the failed home-only strategy. No internal data contradicts the potential for a new form factor to reset consumer perception.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks (weak specs, confusion, supply) are valid but do not constitute factual proof of failure as proposed; they are execution challenges inherent to innovation. The pricing parity with powerful rivals is a risk, but justified if the hybrid utility is perceived as superior.

**Risks:** Third-party abandonment, consumer confusion, supply chain bottlenecks. These become kill criteria: monitor third-party attachment rates and sell-through velocity in Q1.

**Decision:** The proposal represents the only viable path to reverse current trends without repeating past mistakes. The potential upside of defining a new category outweighs the known risks of the status quo.

DECISION: GO
