First call: GO. The company faces existential decay with no viable internal path; acquiring a modern core is the only mechanism to reset the trajectory, despite high friction.

**Steelman:** Acquiring the external modern OS is the fastest, highest-leverage method to replace a failed internal stack, leveraging the founder's unique vision to unify hardware and software, outweighing migration costs and historical conflict.

**Alternatives:**
1.  *Do nothing:* Continued market share erosion leads to irrelevance or liquidation.
2.  *License another vendor:* Cedes control of the user experience and roadmap to a competitor, reducing the hardware to a commodity shell.
3.  *Third internal attempt:* Probabilistic failure based on two prior attempts; time-to-market is too slow.

**Fit:** Aligns with the urgent need for a differentiated, modern platform to stop the bleeding of market share.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the technical migration takes too long, burning cash before launch, or if the founder's return triggers internal mutiny among existing engineering leadership. This is a one-way door; delay costs the remaining brand equity.

**Evidence & Math:** The "few hundred million" price tag is high relative to the target's current revenue but negligible compared to the acquirer's potential loss of total market value. The target's low sales are irrelevant; the asset value lies in the architecture and the founder, not current units sold.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks cited (low sales, migration difficulty, founder history, price) are execution challenges, not factual contradictions. Low sales do not negate technical superiority. Migration risk exists in any OS replacement. The founder's history is the very asset needed to disrupt the status quo. No alternative reaches the same upside (full stack control + modern core) faster.

**Risks:** Integration friction, cultural clash, timeline slippage. These are managed via kill criteria: a working prototype within 12 months and developer adoption metrics at launch.

**Decision:** The proposal solves the core constraint (no modern OS) where all other paths have failed or compromise the business model.

DECISION: GO
