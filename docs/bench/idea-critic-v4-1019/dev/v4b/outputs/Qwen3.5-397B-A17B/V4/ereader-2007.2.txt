First call: GO. The proposal solves the core friction of early e-reading (need for a PC) and aligns with the company's catalog strength, despite hardware inexperience.

**Steelman:** A dedicated device with cellular connectivity removes the primary adoption barrier (complexity), creating a closed ecosystem that reassures publishers on piracy while driving high-margin digital sales.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
1.  **Do Nothing:** Cedes the digital future to competitors; print sales will eventually decline regardless.
2.  **Software Only:** Fails due to fragmented hardware and lack of publisher trust in 2007; piracy fears remain high.
3.  **Hardware Risk:** Valid, but outsourcing manufacturing mitigates the lack of internal experience. The "cannibalization" fear is a false dichotomy; digital expands the total addressable market to impulse buyers and travelers who cannot carry physical books.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the device is too expensive, the screen is poor, or publishers withhold content. The "free cellular" model is a sharp differentiator but creates a unit-economics trap if book margins are too thin.

**Evidence & Fit:** The proposal assumes a "paper-like" screen exists and is affordable (E-Ink). If this technology is not mature, the value proposition collapses. The single low price point must cover the connectivity subsidy; if the math doesn't hold per unit, the model bleeds cash.

**Blockers:** None found in the text. The risks (hardware inexperience, margin squeeze) are execution challenges, not fundamental flaws. The "cannibalization" argument is a strategic necessity, not a blocker. The proposal correctly identifies that owning the reader controls the bookstore experience.

**Kill Criteria:** If the Bill of Materials (BOM) cost prevents a consumer-friendly price point while maintaining the free connectivity promise, or if major publishers refuse content licensing due to price constraints, the project must pivot or stop.

**Decision:** The strategic fit is perfect for a bookseller. The hardware risk is manageable via partners. The "free cellular" hook is the killer app required to jumpstart the market.

DECISION: GO
