**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Continue losing share; safe but guarantees irrelevance in basketball.
2.  **Sign established star:** High cost, low differentiation, limited upside as the player's brand is fixed.
3.  **Proposed path:** Highest financial risk, but only option offering category redefinition and long-term asset creation. It beats "do nothing" because the status quo is failure.

The cost of delay is ceding the cultural zeitgeist to competitors who may sign the next big talent.

**Risks:** Career-ending injury (mitigated by insurance or accepted as gamble); consumer rejection of the style. These are risks, not blockers.
