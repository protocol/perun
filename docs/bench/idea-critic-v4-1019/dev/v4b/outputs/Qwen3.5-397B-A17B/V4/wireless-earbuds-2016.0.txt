First call: NO-GO. The proposal removes a universal, reliable standard (the headphone jack) and replaces it with a proprietary, expensive ($159), fragile, and easily lost accessory with inferior battery life (5 hours), creating immediate customer friction without a proven compensatory upside.

**Steelman:** Removing the jack forces an industry shift to wireless, and launching a seamless, proprietary ecosystem lock-in ($159 earbuds) captures high-margin recurring revenue while defining the future form factor.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" retains the jack and sells optional wireless earbuds separately, avoiding customer anger. A cheaper, universal Bluetooth option avoids the "proprietary chip" lock-in risk. The proposal fails to beat "do nothing" on immediate customer satisfaction.
2.  **Fit:** While potentially aligning with a "wireless future" vision, the execution ignores the current reality: users value reliability and cost. Angering the base to chase a future vision is a strategic mismatch at this stage.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** The product is ridiculed for its appearance ("odd"), lost frequently due to small size, and rejected due to the $159 price point compared to free wired alternatives. The battery life (5 hours) is insufficient for daily heavy use, leading to churn.
4.  **Evidence:** The risks are stated as facts in the proposal itself: "may be ridiculed," "easy to lose," "$159 is high," "battery life is about five hours," "removing the jack may anger customers." These are not hypotheticals; they are acknowledged defects with no mitigation plan.

**Blockers:**
*   **Value Mismatch:** The replacement product ($159, 5hr battery, easy to lose) is objectively inferior in utility and cost to the removed feature (free, unlimited battery, universal, hard to lose).
*   **Customer Alienation:** The plan explicitly admits it "may anger customers" by removing a standard without a comparable replacement, violating the core principle of not downgrading the user experience.

**Risks:** Market ridicule, low adoption due to price, high loss rate leading to negative sentiment.

**Kill Criteria:** If pre-orders do not exceed 50% of the previous year's wired accessory attach rate within 30 days, or if return rates exceed 15% due to fit/loss, the product line must be paused and the jack reinstated in the next revision.

DECISION: NO-GO
