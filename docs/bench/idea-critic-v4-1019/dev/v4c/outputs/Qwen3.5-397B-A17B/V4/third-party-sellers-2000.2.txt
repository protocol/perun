First call: GO. The proposal trades short-term margin protection for long-term liquidity and selection, which is the only path to profitability for a non-profitable retailer facing inventory limits.

**Steelman:** Transform the store from a single merchant into a liquid marketplace where third-party supply fills catalog gaps and absorbs used-demand, increasing total transaction volume despite internal cannibalization.

**Attack & Options:**
1.  **Cannibalization:** Used copies will undercut new sales. *Rebuttal:* If the retailer cannot profitably sell the new unit at a price competitive with the used market, that margin is illusory. Capturing a fee on the used sale is superior to zero revenue on the new one.
2.  **Trust/Quality:** Bad actors harm the brand. *Rebuttal:* This is an execution risk, not a strategic blocker. Mitigation requires strict seller vetting and guarantee policies, not rejecting the model.
3.  **Supplier Conflict:** Publishers may object. *Rebuttal:* Their leverage diminishes as the platform becomes the primary customer destination; they need the reach more than they fear the secondary market.
4.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves margin perception but caps growth and selection. "Exclusive inventory" is impossible given the "growing range" ambition and lack of profitability.

**Fit:** Aligns with the north star of becoming the primary destination for all goods. Inventory ownership is the bottleneck; this removes it.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the fee structure is too high (driving sellers elsewhere) or if quality control is absent (driving buyers away). The proposal acknowledges these risks, implying awareness.

**Blockers:** NONE. The facts state the company is not yet profitable; the status quo is already a failure mode. The risks listed are manageable operational challenges, not factual contradictions. The "fee per sale" model aligns incentives immediately without upfront inventory cost.

**Risks:** Supplier retaliation, temporary brand dilution, complex logistics integration. These become kill criteria: if supplier loss exceeds 10% of top SKUs or customer complaint rates triple, pivot.

**Decision:** The opportunity cost of waiting for perfect conditions exceeds the risk of execution errors. The model shifts the business from capital-intensive retail to asset-light platform economics, the only viable route to scale given the current financial state.

DECISION: GO
