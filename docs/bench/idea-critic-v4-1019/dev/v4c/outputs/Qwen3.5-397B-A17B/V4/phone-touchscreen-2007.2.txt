First call: GO. The proposal trades initial volume for unprecedented control over the user experience and a new revenue stream from service sharing, directly countering the erosion of the music player business.

**Steelman:** Launching a premium, keyboard-less touchscreen phone via an exclusive carrier partnership secures design sovereignty and service revenue, creating a defensible high-end wedge against converging competitors.

**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Cede the mobile market to rivals bundling music players, inevitably cannibalizing the core music business.
2.  **License software:** Follow the industry norm; carriers dictate design, margins collapse, and the user experience fractures.
3.  **Proposed path:** High risk due to inexperience and price, but the only option preserving brand integrity and capturing service value.

**Fit:** Aligns with the vision of integrating communication and media; assumes the market values a superior web and music experience over physical keyboards.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the single carrier cannot market a premium device effectively or if enterprise users reject the lack of a keyboard and apps. However, these are execution risks, not fundamental flaws. The "no apps" constraint is a two-way door; the platform can open later once the hardware base is established.

**Evidence:** The threat to music player sales is the primary driver; waiting for perfect market conditions guarantees loss of relevance. The carrier revenue share offsets the risk of exclusivity.

**Blocker Test:**
*   **Blockers:** NONE. No fact presented proves the plan fails as proposed. The lack of experience is a hurdle, not a contradiction of the plan's logic.
*   **Risks:** Premium pricing acceptance, keyboard-less adoption, limited initial reach. These are managed by the exclusivity deal's revenue share and the strategic necessity of controlling the interface.

The absence of third-party apps and a physical keyboard are deliberate constraints to ensure a polished initial launch, not fatal omissions. The exclusive deal provides the capital and control necessary to redefine the category.

DECISION: GO
