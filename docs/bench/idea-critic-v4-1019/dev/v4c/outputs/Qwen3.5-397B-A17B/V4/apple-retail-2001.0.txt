First call: NO-GO. A loss-making manufacturer with 3–5% share entering a low-margin, high-fixed-cost business during a recession, against industry history, fails the basic operator test.

**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only way to demonstrate the integrated music strategy and stop third-party staff from pushing cheaper rivals, justifying high leases through brand control.

**Attack:**
1.  **Financial Suicide:** The company is already losing money. Adding prime-mall leases (high fixed costs) in a recession creates immediate cash burn. Low hardware margins cannot cover retail overheads without massive volume, which the current market share does not support.
2.  **Channel War:** Alienating existing resellers who currently provide the bulk of sales volume, while building a tiny proprietary network, risks a total sales collapse before new stores mature.
3.  **Historical Precedent:** The proposal admits a "poor retail track record in the industry." Ignoring sector-wide failure patterns while lacking internal retail expertise is speculation, not strategy.
4.  **Better Way:** Create "shop-in-shop" demos within existing retailer floors or launch a direct-to-consumer web store. Both avoid long-term leases and channel conflict while enabling product demonstration.

**Blockers:**
*   **Capital Mismatch:** Current losses + recession + high fixed leases = insolvency risk. The math does not work without unlimited capital, which is not stated.
*   **Contradictory Evidence:** The proposal cites industry-wide retail failures as a risk but proceeds anyway without a unique mechanism to overcome them.

**Risks:** Inventory obsolescence, reseller retaliation, brand damage from empty stores.

**Kill Criteria:** If a pilot store cannot achieve positive unit economics within 6 months, or if reseller sales drop >10% post-launch, the program must terminate.

**Verdict:** The plan bets the company on a high-cost channel with negative historical data during an economic downturn. The "digital music strategy" does not justify the balance sheet risk. The cheaper, reversible alternative (web store or dedicated demo zones) must be exhausted first.

DECISION: NO-GO
