First call: NO-GO. Launching early with no games, alienated retailers, and a higher price against a rival destroys the platform before it starts.

**Steelman:** An early May launch at $399 secures mindshare and early adopters before the competitor's September arrival, accepting limited inventory as a trade-off for being first.

**Attack:**
1.  **Content Void:** A console without games is a brick. "Few games ready" guarantees negative word-of-mouth. Early adopters will play the few available titles, find the library empty, and turn to the competitor in September with money already spent or loyalty shifted. The hardware's difficulty for 3D development exacerbates this; developers cannot rush high-quality titles for a hard-to-program machine in four months.
2.  **Channel Suicide:** Shipping to "a few retailers" while excluding others invites immediate retaliation. Excluded partners will prioritize the competitor's September launch, giving them better shelf space and promotion. In 1995, retail relationships are the primary distribution channel; burning them for a partial launch is strategic malpractice.
3.  **Price Disadvantage:** At $100 above the rival, the value proposition relies entirely on software superiority or exclusivity. With "few games," the price is unjustifiable. Consumers will wait for the cheaper, better-supported alternative.
4.  **Developer Trust:** Surprising developers with an early deadline they didn't plan for fractures partner trust. If the hardware is already "hard to program," rushing them yields broken, unoptimized games that reinforce the "hard to use" reputation.

**Blockers:**
*   **Fact:** The proposal admits "few games ready" and "developers did not plan." This guarantees a failed user experience at launch.
*   **Fact:** Excluding retailers creates active enemies who will block future distribution.
*   **Fact:** $399 vs $299 (implied) with no content advantage ensures zero mass-market traction.

**Risks:** (None need listing as blockers already decide the call).

This plan trades long-term ecosystem health for a hollow headline. It ensures that when the competitor arrives, they face a weakened market, angry retailers, and a consumer base disappointed by the early entrant's lack of software.

DECISION: NO-GO
