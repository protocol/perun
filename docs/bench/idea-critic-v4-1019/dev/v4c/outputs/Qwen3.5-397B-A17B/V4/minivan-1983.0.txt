First call: GO. The proposal leverages existing assets (compact platform, converted factory) to address a clear market gap (garage-friendly family van) created by a competitor's collapse and shifting family needs.

**Steelman:** Reusing the compact-car platform and a converted factory minimizes capital expenditure, allowing a cash-strapped manufacturer to launch a unique, car-like van for families before rivals can react, effectively creating a new category.

**Attack & Analysis:**
1.  **Category Risk:** The claim that "no such category exists" is the opportunity, not a blocker. Families currently compromise with wagons (too small) or trucks (too bulky). A garage-fit vehicle fills this void.
2.  **Financial Constraint:** With little cash, the "converted factory" and "existing platform" strategy is the only viable path. Building anew is impossible; this is the cheapest alternative to "do nothing" (which leads to stagnation).
3.  **Brand Perception:** The risk of being seen as a "commercial van" is mitigated by the explicit design goal: "drive like a car" and target "families." Marketing must emphasize passenger comfort over cargo utility.
4.  **Competition:** Rivals copying the idea is a validation of the market, not a reason to abstain. First-mover advantage in a new segment is critical.
5.  **Space Limitation:** Reusing a compact platform limits absolute volume, but the proposal targets "home garage" fit, implying space efficiency is a feature, not a bug. If it fits a garage and carries a family, it meets the core requirement.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if marketing fails to distinguish the vehicle from commercial vans, causing families to ignore it. However, the physical attributes (FWD, car-like drive) support the positioning.

**Blockers:** None. The financial constraints dictate this specific approach; no cheaper alternative achieves the upside. The risks are executional, not fundamental flaws in the concept.

**Kill Criteria:** If pre-orders from family-focused demographics do not exceed commercial fleet inquiries by 2:1 within six months of reveal, pivot marketing or halt production expansion.

**Decision:** The plan turns constraints (low cash, existing platform) into strategic advantages (low cost, speed). It addresses an unmet need with minimal investment.

DECISION: GO
