First call: NO-GO. The proposal targets a shrinking trend with a product that likely satisfies neither core segment, facing immediate competition and price resistance.

**Steelman:** A mid-calorie cola captures health-conscious young men who reject artificial sweeteners but want fewer carbs than regular soda, leveraging a first-mover advantage before the rival launch.

**Attack:**
1.  **Fit & Trend:** The low-carb diet craze (Atkins) peaked in 2004 and is statistically likely a fad. Building a permanent SKU and premium pricing on a declining curve is capital destruction.
2.  **Segmentation Failure:** The "half-sugar" value proposition is weak. Dieters prefer zero-calorie certainty; regular drinkers want full taste. A middle ground often alienates both, creating a "nothing" product.
3.  **Price vs. Trial:** Premium pricing for an unproven category during a potential trend collapse creates high friction. If the trend fades, the inventory becomes stranded.
4.  **Competition:** A rival is already launching. Entering second with a higher price point against a similar product reduces the window for success to near zero.

**Alternatives:**
*   *Do Nothing:* Preserve margins on existing sugar/diet lines; wait to see if the rival fails (likely if the trend is a fad).
*   *Flank Attack:* Launch a zero-calorie natural sweetener variant instead of mid-calorie, addressing the "no artificial sweetener" complaint without the carb penalty.

**Pre-mortem:** One year later, sales are flat. The low-carb trend has collapsed, marketing spend yielded no retention, and the product is discounted to clear shelf space. The rival exited the market three months ago.

**Blockers:**
*   **Trend Viability:** The core premise relies on a specific diet trend showing signs of peaking exactly at launch time.
*   **Value Proposition:** No evidence suggests a large enough segment exists that refuses diet soda *and* regular soda but accepts a premium mid-calorie option.

**Risks:** Brand dilution, cannibalization of existing SKUs, wasted campaign spend.

**Kill Criteria:** If low-carb search volume or related food sales drop >10% in Q1 post-launch, terminate production immediately.

The proposal bets heavily on a fading fad with a confused product position.

DECISION: NO-GO
