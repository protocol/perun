First call: NO-GO. The proposal admits unproven economics and a large short-term margin hit without a cap, pilot, or kill criterion, which constitutes a blocker under the decision method.

**Steelman:** An annual membership eliminates repeat-purchase friction, locking in loyalty and increasing order frequency enough to offset shipping losses through volume and upfront cash flow.

**Blockers:**
1. **Unproven Economics:** The proposal explicitly states the economics are unproven. Launching a company-wide, hard-to-reverse financial commitment based on unverified assumptions is a factual failure of the plan as proposed.
2. **Missing Cap/Pilot:** There is no time-boxed test, user segment limitation, or spending cap to validate the "heavy buyer" risk. The plan demands an immediate, irreversible rollout despite known uncertainty.
3. **Margin Hit:** The admission of a "large short-term margin hit" without a defined runway or funding source creates an immediate liquidity risk that contradicts sound operator judgment.

**Risks:** Heavy buyers exploiting the fee, inability to reverse due to customer holdings, and competitive response. These are severe but would be manageable as kill criteria in a pilot; in a full launch, they are existential threats.

**Analysis:** The proposal fails the "cheaper alternative" test. A staged rollout (e.g., invite-only, specific ZIP codes, or a 3-month pilot with a hard stop) reaches the same learning upside with significantly lower downside. The current plan beats "do nothing" only if the unproven economic model holds true; since it is unproven, the plan does not clear the bar. The lack of a leading metric, a review date, or a pivot condition means there is no mechanism to stop the bleed if the premise is wrong.

A sound operator would demand a capped experiment first. Approving this as proposed ignores the stated lack of evidence and the admitted financial danger. The absence of a pilot is not just a missing feature; it is a blocker because the core claim (profitability via frequency) is explicitly unverified.

DECISION: NO-GO
