First call: NO-GO. The price is astronomical for an asset with zero revenue, negative margins due to bandwidth costs, and existential legal threats that could shutter the platform immediately.

**Steelman:** Acquiring the dominant cultural hub of online video now secures the category leader, prevents a competitor from owning it, and leverages the buyer's ad infrastructure to monetize traffic that no internal product can match.

**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Save $1.65B but cede the video market to the target or a rival; internal product remains niche.
2.  **Build/Buy smaller:** Cheaper, but fails to capture the network effect and user base of the leader.
3.  **Acquire (Proposed):** Massive immediate cost and legal liability, but guarantees market leadership if lawsuits are survivable.

**Fit:** Aligns with organizing the world's information, but assumes video is the future medium (unproven in 2006) and that copyright law will bend toward "fair use" or licensing rather than destruction.

**Pre-mortem:** The deal closes; six months later, major studios win an injunction shutting down the site due to copyright infringement. The $1.65B stock becomes worthless, and the brand is tainted by association with piracy. Bandwidth costs continue to outpace ad revenue growth.

**Evidence:** The proposal admits "no meaningful revenue," "high bandwidth bills," and "threatened lawsuits." The math fails: paying $1.65B for negative cash flow with a high probability of total asset loss is not an investment; it is a gamble with shareholder capital. The "unproven ad model" combined with "copyright litigation" creates a scenario where the core asset (the videos) is the very source of the liability.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Financial:** Price ($1.65B) vs. Revenue (~$0) implies infinite multiples with no path to profitability shown.
2.  **Legal:** Existential threat of lawsuits from media giants capable of shutting down the service entirely, rendering the acquisition valueless.

**Risks:** Monetization failure, bandwidth scaling costs, cultural integration.

**Kill Criteria:** If any single lawsuit results in a statutory damage award exceeding $500M or an injunction halting uploads within 12 months, the thesis is dead.

The risks are not merely executional; they are fatal. The legal exposure alone makes the asset potentially worthless overnight.

DECISION: NO-GO
