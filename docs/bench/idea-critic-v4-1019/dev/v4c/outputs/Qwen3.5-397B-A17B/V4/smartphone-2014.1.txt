First call: NO-GO. The proposal combines a premium price with a smaller app store, carrier exclusivity, and unproven 3D hardware in a saturated two-platform market, creating immediate structural friction against adoption.

Steelman: A differentiated device leveraging unique 3D interaction and seamless shopping could carve a high-margin niche among loyal customers despite the app gap.

Options:
1. **Do nothing:** Preserve capital; avoid entering a low-margin, high-churn hardware battle without ecosystem leverage.
2. **Low-cost entry:** Subsidize the device or target the mid-range to reduce adoption friction, accepting lower margins to build user base.
3. **Proposed plan:** High price, exclusive carrier, niche OS. This fails to beat "do nothing" because the app gap and price point contradict the mass-market reach required to sustain a third platform.

Fit: While shopping integration aligns with retail vision, the execution (forked OS, missing mainstream apps) actively blocks the primary utility of a smartphone for most users, misaligning with the "customer obsession" north star if it delivers a degraded core experience.

Pre-mortem: The device launches to initial curiosity but returns surge due to battery drain from 3D sensors, lack of essential apps (banking, social), and confusing pricing. Carrier exclusivity limits reach just as negative reviews solidify. The project becomes a costly inventory write-down within 18 months.

Evidence: The proposal admits the market is saturated, dominated by two platforms, and lacks mainstream apps on the proposed OS. Historical data (implicit) shows third platforms failing without massive ecosystem parity or disruptive pricing.

Blockers:
1. **Price vs. Value Mismatch:** Charging flagship prices for a device with a smaller app store and unproven 3D feature contradicts basic consumer behavior in a saturated market.
2. **Ecosystem Deficit:** A smartphone without leading mainstream apps is functionally incomplete for the target demographic, regardless of shopping features.

Risks: Hardware complexity of 3D tracking, carrier dependency, brand dilution if the device fails.

Kill Criteria: If pre-orders do not exceed 500k units in the first month (indicating strong loyalist pull despite deficits), halt production. Leading metric: Daily Active Users engaging with 3D features > 20%.

DECISION: NO-GO
