First call: NO-GO. The proposal ignores fatal market and social barriers explicitly listed in its own risk assessment.

**Steelman:** An invitation-only launch at $1,500 will validate demand and refine the product for mass market release by filtering for tech-tolerant users.

**Attack:**
1.  **Value Mismatch:** At $1,500 (2013 dollars), the device costs more than a high-end laptop or tablet, yet offers no "clear everyday use" for most people. Early adopters pay for novelty, but not for a product with undefined utility and social stigma.
2.  **Social Blocker:** Privacy objections and social acceptance are not minor risks; they are fundamental adoption barriers for a always-on camera. An invitation-only program does not solve the "creepy" factor; it may amplify it by creating an exclusive class of recorders.
3.  **Hardware Deficits:** Short battery life and high price make it unsuitable as an "everyday consumer product." Positioning it as such contradicts the hardware realities.
4.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" and wait for battery tech to improve and social norms to shift is superior. A cheaper, phone-tethered prototype would better test utility without the $1,500 barrier.

**Pre-mortem:** The product launches, sells a limited unit run to enthusiasts, faces immediate public backlash over privacy, gets banned from venues, and is discontinued within 18 months as a costly failure that damages the brand's reputation.

**Blockers:**
*   **No Core Utility:** The proposal admits "no clear everyday use," which is a blocker for a $1,500 consumer device.
*   **Social/Privacy Hostility:** Explicitly raised risks that invalidate the "everyday" positioning.
*   **Price vs. Value:** The price point is unjustified by the stated capabilities and battery life.

**Risks:** Execution difficulty, niche appeal, rapid obsolescence.

**Conclusion:** The proposal attempts to sell an incomplete, socially controversial, and overpriced device as a mainstream product. The lack of a defined use case alone kills the consumer pitch.

DECISION: NO-GO
