First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits to high-volume production and a premium price before resolving fundamental legal and market-fit uncertainties.

**Steelman:** Launching now capitalizes on first-mover advantage and publicity to establish the category before competitors emerge.

**Attack:**
1.  **Legal Blocker:** The proposal admits legal status on sidewalks and roads is "unclear." If regulators ban the device from primary transit zones (sidewalks/roads), the product becomes unusable for its core purpose. Launching before legal clarity risks massive recalls, stranded inventory, and reputational damage.
2.  **Market Fit vs. Price:** At $5,000, the unit is a luxury item, yet utility is limited (no cargo, short range implied by size). Institutional use is "unproven," and individual consumers may reject the high cost combined with social conspicuousness.
3.  **Capacity Risk:** Sizing a factory for 40,000 units assumes demand that has not been validated in a real-world environment. This is a one-way door; overbuilding creates fixed costs that cannot be reversed if adoption stalls.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Wait for regulatory frameworks to mature.
*   **Pilot:** Release a limited batch (e.g., 500 units) to institutional partners to test utility and legal pushback before committing to mass manufacturing.
*   **Price Reduction:** Not feasible immediately given hardware costs, reinforcing the need for a smaller initial run.

**Pre-mortem:** The product launches, cities immediately ban it from sidewalks due to safety concerns, sales collapse to near zero, and the company is left with a specialized factory and unsold $5,000 units.

**Blockers:**
*   **Regulatory Ambiguity:** The core use case depends on legal access that is currently undefined.
*   **Unvalidated Demand:** No evidence supports 40,000 units/year at $5,000 given the stated limitations.

**Risks:** Social stigma, limited utility compared to cost, potential safety liabilities.

**Conclusion:** The combination of unresolved legal status and unproven mass-market demand at a premium price point makes a full-scale launch irresponsible. The company must validate legality and utility with a limited pilot before scaling production.

DECISION: NO-GO
