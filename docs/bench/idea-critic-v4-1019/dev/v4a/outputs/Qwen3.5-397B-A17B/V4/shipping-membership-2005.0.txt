First call: NO-GO. The proposal admits unproven economics and a large short-term margin hit without defining the loss cap or the specific unit economics required to survive heavy buyer behavior.

**Steelman:** An annual membership drives retention and order frequency, offsetting shipping losses through volume and locked-in loyalty, provided the margin hit is temporary and calculable.

**Attack:**
1.  **Economics:** The proposal explicitly states economics are "unproven." Launching a company-wide financial model based on unverified assumptions regarding heavy buyer elasticity is a direct path to insolvency. Without a pilot proving that increased order frequency covers the $79 fee plus variable shipping costs, this is a gamble, not a strategy.
2.  **Irreversibility:** Once customers pay $79, reversing the program creates immediate liability and reputational damage. The "hard to reverse" constraint combined with "unproven economics" creates a one-way door into a potential loss spiral.
3.  **Margin Hit:** A "large short-term margin hit" is accepted without a defined tolerance limit or funding source. If heavy buyers exceed projections by even a small margin, the loss scales linearly with no natural brake.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Retain current friction; safe but limits growth.
*   **Pilot Program:** Limit to a specific geographic region or customer segment to validate the "heavy buyer" cost model before a full launch. This reduces the blast radius of the margin hit.
*   **Tiered Trial:** Offer a 3-month trial at a prorated rate to test elasticity before committing to an annual cycle.

**Pre-mortem:** The program launches, heavy buyers double their order frequency, shipping costs exceed revenue by 40%, and the company cannot cancel memberships without mass refunds, causing a cash flow crisis.

**Blockers:**
*   **Unproven Economics:** The core financial viability is admitted as unknown. Launching without a validated model is a fatal flaw.
*   **Undefined Loss Cap:** No maximum acceptable loss is stated for the "large margin hit."

**Risks:** Customer abuse of free shipping, inability to reverse due to prepaid contracts, sustained negative margins.

**Conclusion:** You cannot approve a financially transformative initiative with admitted unproven economics and high irreversibility. A pilot is required to convert "unproven" to "validated."

DECISION: NO-GO
