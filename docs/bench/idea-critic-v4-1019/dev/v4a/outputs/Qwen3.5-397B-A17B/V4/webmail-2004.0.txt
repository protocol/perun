First call: GO. The proposal trades a rapidly commoditizing asset (storage) for a defensible moat (search) and a high-margin revenue stream (ads), disrupting a complacent market.

**Steelman:** By offering 1,000x the standard storage, the service eliminates the user habit of deleting mail, creating a permanent data archive that locks users in and maximizes ad targeting surface area, funded by text ads that scale with usage.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
*   **Cost Risk:** Critics argue 1GB/user is financially suicidal. *Rebuttal:* Storage costs are falling faster than user acquisition costs; the marginal cost of idle storage is near zero, while the lifetime value of a retained user is high.
*   **Privacy Risk:** Scanning mail for ads invites backlash. *Rebuttal:* Automated scanning is distinct from human reading; users trading privacy for free utility is an established, accepted contract in free services.
*   **Market Risk:** Incumbents dominate. *Rebuttal:* Incumbents are constrained by their own low-storage business models; they cannot match this offer without cannibalizing existing paid tiers or breaking their infrastructure.
*   **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" cedes the search-data opportunity to competitors. "Paid only" limits scale and data volume, weakening the ad engine. "Low storage" fails to differentiate.

**Pre-mortem:** The project fails if privacy outrage becomes regulatory action or if storage costs plateau unexpectedly. However, the invitation-only launch controls growth to match infrastructure capacity, mitigating immediate cost shocks.

**Fit:** This aligns with a strategy of organizing information and monetizing attention. It turns email from a communication tool into a searchable data asset.

**Blockers:** NONE. The cited risks are manageable execution challenges, not fundamental flaws. The math works if storage costs continue their historical decline and ad relevance improves with data volume.
**Risks:** Privacy perception shifts; slower-than-expected storage cost declines.

**Kill Criteria:** If cost per user exceeds ad revenue per user after 18 months of active usage, or if regulatory action bans content-based scanning, pivot to a paid model or enterprise licensing.

**Leading Metric:** Daily active users returning to search historical mail (validates the "archive" value prop). Review in 6 months.

DECISION: GO
