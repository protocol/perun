First call: NO-GO. The price is 12.7x the prior valuation with no revenue path, violating basic capital discipline.

**Steelman:** Paying a historic premium is the only way to neutralize an existential mobile threat when organic build has failed.

**Options:**
1.  **Buy:** Immediate threat removal; extreme financial risk; irreversible capital deployment.
2.  **Do Nothing:** Risk displacement by the target; preserves $19B cash; allows organic mobile pivot.
3.  **Partner/Minority Stake:** Cheaper access to user base; lower control; fails to fully neutralize the competitive threat.

**Fit:** Assumes the north star is "mobile dominance at any cost." If the north star includes "profitable growth," this fails.

**Pre-mortem:** Integration fails due to culture clash (50 employees vs. massive corp); founders leave immediately post-vesting; regulators force a breakup due to privacy/data consolidation; the $1 fee model cannot be monetized without destroying the user base.

**Evidence & Math:**
*   **Valuation:** $1.5B (2013) to $19B (2014) implies a 1,166% increase in one year with minimal revenue change. This is speculative, not fundamental.
*   **Revenue:** ~$10M–$20M estimated revenue (hundreds of millions of users × $1, assuming <10% conversion or partial year). The Price/Sales ratio is roughly 1,000x. No historical precedent supports this multiple for a non-public, ad-free entity.
*   **Cost of Delay:** Waiting a quarter costs nothing compared to the risk of overpaying for a fad.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Valuation Disconnect:** The price relies entirely on the assumption that the current growth trajectory is permanent and monopolizable, which contradicts the volatile nature of social apps.
2.  **No Monetization Logic:** Acquiring a strictly anti-ad platform with no clear alternative revenue model creates a value destruction loop unless the buyer dismantles the very product they are buying.

**Risks:** Founder retention, regulatory antitrust action, culture clash, inability to monetize.

**Decision:** The financial terms are indefensible based on current facts. The "existential threat" argument does not justify a 1,000x sales multiple without a concrete integration and monetization plan.

DECISION: NO-GO
