First call: GO. The proposal addresses a verified market gap (garage-friendly family hauler) by leveraging existing assets (compact platform, converted factory) to minimize capital exposure during a cash-constrained recovery.

**Steelman:** Launch a car-based, front-wheel-drive minivan to create a new consumer category, utilizing idle capacity and existing engineering to achieve profitability with low upfront investment.

**Alternatives:**
1.  *Do nothing:* Continue selling declining station wagons; risk total insolvency as families shift to unwieldy full-size vans or imports.
2.  *New dedicated platform:* Prohibitively expensive and slow; contradicts the "little cash" constraint.
3.  *Commercial van only:* Limits market size and margins; fails to capture the family demographic seeking car-like comfort.

**Fit:** Aligns with the survival stage and the government guarantee's intent to preserve jobs via viable products. Reusing the compact platform is a feature (cost/space efficiency), not a bug, provided packaging is optimized.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if marketing positions the vehicle as a "work van," deterring families. The "no category exists" risk is actually an opportunity for first-mover advantage, not a blocker. Rivals copying is a sign of success, not an immediate threat, given our head start and retooled factory.

**Evidence & Math:** The government guarantee provides the necessary liquidity bridge. The converted factory eliminates new construction costs. The compact platform reduces R&D and parts inventory. The math works if the price point undercuts full-size vans while offering superior utility to station wagons.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks cited are market uncertainties inherent to innovation, not factual contradictions. The lack of an existing category confirms the blue-ocean potential rather than invalidating demand. The cash constraint is mitigated by the asset-light approach (reuse/convert).

**Risks:** Brand perception (commercial vs. family); competitor response. These are managed via targeted marketing and speed to market.

**Kill Criteria:** If pre-orders or early sales data (first 6 months) show >60% commercial fleet purchase vs. family retail, pivot messaging or discontinue. Leading metric: Retail family unit sales vs. station wagon cannibalization rate.

DECISION: GO
