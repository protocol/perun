First call: NO-GO. The proposal attempts to enter a saturated, two-platform duopoly with a higher-friction product (forked OS, missing mainstream apps) at a flagship price point, relying on a gimmicky 3D display and a niche shopping feature to drive adoption.

Steelman: A device leveraging unique hardware for shopping could create a high-margin niche for loyal customers. However, the attack reveals fatal flaws. The "app gap" on a forked OS is a known killer for mainstream smartphones in 2014; users will not pay flagship prices for a device that cannot run essential daily applications. The 3D display is a solution looking for a problem, likely increasing cost and battery drain without solving a core user need. Carrier exclusivity severely limits the addressable market, contradicting the need for massive scale to sustain a third ecosystem.

Alternatives: Doing nothing preserves capital. A cheaper, staged approach would be to launch the shopping software on existing iOS/Android devices first to validate the "one-touch" value proposition before committing to expensive hardware. The current plan fails to beat "do nothing" because the risks (market rejection due to app scarcity) are certainties, not possibilities.

Fit: This does not move the north star of customer convenience; it fragments it. The pre-mortem is clear: the phone launches, reviewers pan the lack of apps, early adopters return it due to battery drain from the 3D feature, and the carrier drops support within a year.

Blockers:
1. **Economic Viability:** Flagship pricing with a sub-par software ecosystem (no mainstream apps) contradicts basic market dynamics of 2014.
2. **Strategic Contradiction:** Exclusivity limits scale, yet a third platform requires massive scale to survive.

Risks: High manufacturing costs for unproven 3D tech; brand damage from a failed flagship.

The proposal relies on assumptions about user willingness to sacrifice app availability for 3D effects that are unsupported by market evidence. The "app gap" is a hard blocker that cannot be mitigated by a shopping feature.

DECISION: NO-GO
