First call: NO-GO. The proposal admits unproven economics and a likely margin hit where costs exceed revenue, which is a fatal flaw for a public retailer without a capped pilot.

**Steelman:** An annual fee locks in loyalty and increases order frequency, betting that lifetime value growth offsets immediate shipping losses.

**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Retain friction, protect current margins, risk churn to competitors.
2.  **Proposed Plan:** High risk of immediate cash burn; irreversible once sold; relies on unproven volume elasticity.
3.  **Capped Pilot:** Limit to 10,000 users in one region for 90 days to validate unit economics before scaling.

**Fit:** Assumes the goal is market share dominance over short-term profitability. If the north star is immediate margin protection, this fails.

**Pre-mortem:** Heavy users exploit the flat fee, driving shipping costs to 15% of sales while revenue remains fixed at $79/year. The company burns $50M in a year, cannot cancel due to customer backlash, and stock tanks. This is a one-way door with delayed feedback loops.

**Evidence:** The proposal explicitly states "economics are unproven" and "costs may exceed fee revenue." These are not risks; they are admitted gaps in the business case. No data supports the assumption that increased frequency will cover the marginal cost of two-day shipping.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Unproven Unit Economics:** The core claim (profitability via volume) contradicts the admitted fact that costs may exceed revenue.
2.  **Irreversibility:** Selling annual contracts creates a liability that cannot be undone without reputational damage if the model fails.
3.  **Margin Hit:** The plan accepts a "large short-term margin hit" without a defined cap or stop-loss mechanism.

**Risks:** Competitor price wars, supply chain inability to handle volume spikes, cannibalization of higher-margin expedited shipping.

**Decision:** No-go. The proposal asks to bet the company's margin on an unproven model with no exit strategy.

**Kill Criteria (if revised):** Run a 3-month, geo-fenced pilot with a hard cap on total subsidy cost. Stop if shipping costs exceed 110% of membership revenue collected.
