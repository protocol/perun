First call: NO-GO. The proposal bets $1B+ on unproven demand for premium short-form subscriptions amidst known fatigue, ignoring abundant free alternatives.

**Steelman:** A high-fidelity, mobile-native video service leveraging A-list talent and adaptive technology can carve a profitable niche by offering superior quality that free platforms cannot match, justifying a subscription despite market saturation.

**Attack:**
1.  **Demand Mismatch:** The core assumption—that users will pay for short-form content when free, abundant substitutes exist—is unproven and contradicts current consumption patterns. "Subscription fatigue" is cited as a risk but treated as surmountable without evidence.
2.  **Cost Structure vs. Value:** High production budgets and A-list talent create a massive fixed-cost burden. Short episodes (<10 mins) limit ad inventory for a hybrid model and reduce perceived value per dollar compared to feature films or long-form series.
3.  **Technological Moat:** Adaptive orientation (vertical/horizontal) is a usability feature, not a defensible monopoly. Competitors can replicate this quickly; it does not justify a premium subscription barrier.
4.  **Opportunity Cost:** Capital is better deployed validating demand via a low-cost pilot (e.g., a single show on an existing platform) before raising $1B. The "do nothing" or "small pilot" options offer similar upside with negligible downside.

**Blockers:**
*   **Economic Viability:** No evidence provided that Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) exceeds the acquisition cost (CAC) required to convert users from free tiers to a premium short-form service.
*   **Strategic Fit:** Launching a full-scale service without validated demand violates basic lean startup principles for a new category.

**Risks:** Market rejection, rapid competitor copying of tech features, inability to retain subscribers beyond initial A-list hype.

**Kill Criteria:** If a pilot episode fails to achieve a 5% conversion rate from free to paid in a controlled test, the full launch is aborted.

**Decision:** The proposal requests immediate approval for a massive capital raise based on unverified assumptions. The lack of a validated demand signal and the availability of cheaper, safer alternatives (pilots) constitutes a blocker.

DECISION: NO-GO
