First call: NO-GO. The proposal combines maximum engineering risk with maximum business risk by discarding the core value proposition (community voting mechanics) and eliminating any safety net (no rollback) for a loyal but slowing user base.

Steelman: A complete rewrite with a new discovery algorithm and publisher focus is necessary to reverse traffic decline and compete with a growing rival, requiring an immediate, total migration to realize benefits.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Contradictory Evidence:** The proposal admits the community values existing voting mechanics, yet the plan changes how stories surface and favors publishers. This directly attacks the stated source of loyalty, likely accelerating exodus to the rival rather than stopping it.
2.  **Operational Suicide:** Moving all users at once with "no rollback" violates basic risk management for a large engineering rewrite. If the new version fails (high probability given the scope), the site dies instantly with no recovery path.
3.  **Unproven Premise:** There is no evidence provided that "favoring publishers" drives traffic for this specific community; it is an assumption that contradicts the "social news" model where user votes drive visibility.

**Risks:**
*   Alienating the core community during a rewrite.
*   Engineering delays causing further traffic erosion.
*   Rival captures displaced users during the transition.

**Decision Analysis:**
The plan fails the "do nothing" comparison. Doing nothing preserves the community and allows for incremental testing. This proposal guarantees high friction, removes the ability to revert errors, and bets the entire company on an unverified hypothesis that contradicts user values. A cheaper, safer alternative exists: A/B test the new surfacing logic on a segment of traffic while keeping the core voting mechanics intact, allowing for rollback and iterative learning. The current plan offers no such safety.

The lack of a rollback mechanism alone is a fatal flaw for a "large engineering risk." Combined with the strategic error of ignoring community preferences, the proposal cannot be approved.

DECISION: NO-GO
