First call: NO-GO. An unprofitable retailer inviting competitors to undercut its own core inventory on the same page threatens its only path to margin without a verified mechanism to capture sufficient fee volume to offset lost direct sales.

**Steelman:** The platform becomes the definitive catalog where liquidity attracts buyers, generating high-margin fee revenue that exceeds lost direct sales and funds expansion beyond current inventory limits.

**Options:**
1.  **Proposal:** Open marketplace immediately. *Cost:* High cannibalization risk. *Risk:* Brand erosion, supplier revolt. *Reversibility:* Low (hard to un-mix trust).
2.  **Do Nothing:** Retain full control, grow inventory organically. *Cost:* Slow growth, capital intensive. *Risk:* Obsolescence if competitors launch marketplaces first. *Reversibility:* High.
3.  **Pilot (Better Way):** Test on out-of-stock or low-margin categories only. *Cost:* Limited revenue upside initially. *Risk:* Contained. *Reversibility:* High.

**Fit:** Assumes "selection" overrides "price/margin" for an unprofitable entity. If the core business bleeds cash due to undercutting, the platform fails regardless of selection depth.

**Pre-mortem:** Six months post-launch, direct sales of high-margin items collapse by 40% as competitors use the site's traffic to dump used inventory. Suppliers boycott due to price erosion. The fee income covers only 15% of the lost gross profit. The brand is associated with inconsistent shipping experiences.

**Evidence:** No data provided on fee elasticity, competitor willingness to join, or customer tolerance for mixed sellers. The claim that fee revenue will offset cannibalization is an assumption.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Profitability Conflict:** The firm is unprofitable. The proposal introduces immediate, high-probability revenue cannibalization (used vs. new, competitor vs. own) without a guaranteed fee volume to replace lost gross margin.
2.  **Missing Mechanism:** No stated safeguard to prevent total price wars on core SKUs that would destroy the retailer's own contribution margin.

**Risks:** Supplier retaliation, brand dilution, operational complexity in dispute resolution.

**Decision:** The risk of accelerating cash burn in an unprofitable company by inviting direct competition onto core product pages is a fatal flaw without a restricted pilot.

DECISION: NO-GO
