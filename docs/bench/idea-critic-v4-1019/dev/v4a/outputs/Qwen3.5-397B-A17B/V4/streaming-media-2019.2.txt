First call: NO-GO. The proposal substitutes a guaranteed, high-margin licensing revenue stream for a speculative, capital-intensive venture with unproven execution capabilities and a clear path to massive cash burn.

**Steelman:** Direct-to-consumer distribution captures full lifetime value per user and secures strategic independence, provided subscriber acquisition costs remain low and churn is manageable.

**Attack:**
1.  **Revenue Destruction:** Pulling content immediately eliminates billions in guaranteed licensing fees. Replacing this requires acquiring tens of millions of subscribers at a price point *below* the market leader, implying negative unit economics initially.
2.  **Execution Gap:** The conglomerate has zero history of running a global streaming technology platform. Building this while simultaneously marketing to millions introduces extreme operational risk; failure here means losing both the licensing revenue and the direct audience.
3.  **Cannibalization:** The plan explicitly risks degrading existing high-margin cable and theatrical windows without a guaranteed net gain in total profit.
4.  **Capital Intensity:** Funding multi-year operating losses requires immense capital reserves. If subscriber growth lags (a high probability given the competitive landscape), the cash burn becomes unsustainable before reaching the "tens of millions" target.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing/License Selectively:** Maintain licensing revenue while launching a smaller, premium-priced niche service to test technology and demand without burning cash on a mass-market price war.
*   **Joint Venture:** Partner with an existing tech giant to share platform risks and costs, rather than building alone.

**Pre-mortem:** Two years in, the platform suffers outages, subscriber growth stalls at 20% of target due to high churn, and the company faces a credit downgrade due to billions in accumulated losses, forcing a fire sale of assets.

**Blockers:**
*   **Financial Contradiction:** The math relies on replacing billions in *current* guaranteed revenue with *future* speculative revenue from a product the company cannot yet build, priced too low to be profitable.
*   **Capability Void:** No evidence exists that the organization can execute the required technology build at the necessary scale.

**Risks:** Market saturation, inability to reduce churn, prolonged cash burn exceeding reserves, and permanent damage to existing distribution relationships.

The proposal fails the basic test of replacing certain value with uncertain, costly speculation without a viable bridge for the execution gap.

DECISION: NO-GO
