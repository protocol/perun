First call: NO-GO. A loss-making manufacturer with low margins proposing high-fixed-cost retail expansion during a recession violates basic capital preservation logic.

**Steelman:** Direct retail is the only viable channel to demonstrate the integrated music strategy and control brand narrative, as third-party retailers actively undermine sales by pushing cheaper competitors.

**Attack:**
The proposal fails the "do nothing" and "cheaper alternative" tests. The stated risks are not merely potential; they are structural blockers given the current financial state.
1.  **Financial Suicide:** Operating with 3-5% share and recent losses means cash reserves are likely insufficient to absorb the high lease costs and inventory risks of prime mall locations, especially during a recession. One bad quarter could trigger insolvency.
2.  **Channel Warfare:** Opening stores directly competes with the existing reseller network. Resellers, already incentivized to push cheaper rivals, will retaliate by deprioritizing this brand entirely, causing an immediate sales cliff before new stores become profitable.
3.  **Execution Mismatch:** The admission of a "poor retail track record in the industry" suggests the organization lacks the core competency to execute this pivot. Retail operations require distinct skills from manufacturing; attempting to learn them while bleeding cash is reckless.
4.  **Cheaper Alternative:** The goal is product demonstration. Pop-up kiosks, dedicated sections within existing partner stores (paid for by the manufacturer), or online interactive configurators achieve the "demonstration" objective without the fixed cost burden of leases and full-store staffing.

**Blockers:**
*   **Cash Flow:** Recent losses + high fixed leases + recession = high probability of liquidity crisis.
*   **Competency:** Explicit lack of retail success history.
*   **Channel Conflict:** Immediate risk of alienating the primary existing sales channel.

**Risks:**
*   Inventory obsolescence in a fast-moving tech market.
*   Long-term lease liabilities if the concept fails.

**Conclusion:**
The proposal bets the company on a high-cost, low-margin venture in a downturn, despite admitted lack of expertise and active conflict with current revenue streams. The "digital music strategy" does not justify existential financial risk. The cheaper, safer path is to fund demonstrations within existing channels or use temporary formats.

DECISION: NO-GO
