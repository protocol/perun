First call: NO-GO. The proposal asks to launch a capital-intensive hardware company with no manufacturing track record, pricing a premium product against entrenched incumbents who can copy the feature and leverage superior distribution.

Steelman: A superior, bagless technology justifies a premium price and can disrupt the market if direct-to-consumer channels bypass retail gatekeepers.

Options:
1. Do nothing: Avoids total capital loss; incumbents may eventually adopt the tech.
2. License the technology: Generates immediate revenue without manufacturing risk or retail battles.
3. Proceed as proposed: High capital burn, high risk of being undercut or blocked from shelves.

Fit: Assuming the goal is market dominance, entering as an unknown manufacturer contradicts the constraint of having no distribution. A sound strategy must address the route to market before production.

Pre-mortem: The company burns cash building inventory, fails to secure shelf space due to incumbent pressure, and customers reject the high price without a trusted brand. This is a one-way door with massive downside.

Evidence: The proposal admits no manufacturing record and higher costs. Incumbents have confirmed distribution advantages. There is no evidence of customer willingness to pay a premium for an unproven brand.

Blockers:
1. No manufacturing record: Producing complex hardware without experience typically leads to quality failures and margin collapse.
2. Distribution gap: Premium pricing requires volume or high-margin direct sales; the plan relies on competing with incumbents in their stronghold (retail) without a foothold.
3. Copy risk: Without patent protection details, incumbents can release a cheaper version immediately.

Risks: Customer adoption friction, cash flow insolvency, supply chain breakdown.

The plan fails the basic operator test: do not start a factory before securing a route to market or validating the premium via a lower-risk channel. Licensing or a pilot run is mandatory before full company formation.

DECISION: NO-GO
