First call: GO. The shift from pure horology to accessible fashion aligns with market democratization trends, turning a potential reputation risk into a volume opportunity.

**Steelman:** Positioning the plastic watch explicitly as a disposable fashion accessory rather than a compromised timekeeper neutralizes the quality reputation risk by changing the evaluation metric from durability to style.

**Options:**
1.  **Proposed:** Launch 12 fashion models targeting 1M units. High upside, brand evolution risk.
2.  **Do Nothing:** Maintain status quo. Safe, but cedes the low-cost, high-volume segment to competitors.
3.  **Limited Pilot:** Launch 3 models. Lower risk, but fails to achieve the brand visibility required for a "fashion" statement.
The proposal beats "do nothing" by capturing a new demographic; a smaller pilot undermines the fashion narrative.

**Fit:** Assumes the strategic goal is volume growth and brand relevance among younger consumers. If the north star is exclusively "heritage prestige," this fails; however, the context implies a need for expansion.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the company markets these as "cheap watches" instead of "fun accessories," inviting direct comparison to their luxury goods. The one-way door is brand perception shift; however, this is manageable via distinct marketing channels. Cost of delay is losing the first-mover advantage in the emerging plastic watch category.

**Evidence:** No external data available; relying on the internal premise that plastic allows a price point capable of driving 1M units. The sales target is aggressive but necessary to justify the tooling and marketing spend for a fashion line.

**Blockers:** NONE. The reputational risk is a strategic choice, not a factual blocker. The sales target is a goal, not a current impossibility.
**Risks:** Brand dilution if messaging blurs between luxury and plastic; inventory obsolescence if fashion trends miss. These are mitigated by treating the line as a separate sub-brand or collection.

The plan addresses the core tension (quality vs. cost) by reframing the product category. The aggressive target is a feature of the fashion model, not a bug. Proceed with the launch as described, ensuring marketing strictly separates this collection from the core luxury identity.

DECISION: GO
