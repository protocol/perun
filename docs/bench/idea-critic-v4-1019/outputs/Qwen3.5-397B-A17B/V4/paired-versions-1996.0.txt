First call: GO. The mechanic directly converts a hardware limitation (single cartridge storage) and a market risk (declining console popularity) into a viral growth loop where every player becomes a recruiter.

Steelman: Releasing two distinct versions forces social interaction to complete the core gameplay loop, creating organic demand that marketing cannot buy, while doubling the addressable market per user session.

Options:
1. Single version: Lower friction, but eliminates the trading mechanic and viral coefficient; likely results in standard sales decay.
2. Do nothing: Game dies quietly with the handheld; zero upside.
3. Two versions (Proposed): Highest execution complexity and consumer skepticism risk, but the only path to creating a cultural phenomenon that transcends the hardware's lifecycle.

Fit: Assumes the goal is maximizing engagement and longevity over short-term unit efficiency. This aligns with building a franchise rather than shipping a product.

Pre-mortem: Failure occurs if the version differences are perceived as trivial or if the link cable penetration is too low to enable trading. However, the "fear of missing out" on exclusive creatures drives cable sales as a secondary revenue stream. This is a two-way door; if sales are weak, bundles or digital patches can mitigate fragmentation later. The cost of delay is high; launching while the handheld still has a critical mass of users is essential.

Evidence: The proposal relies on behavioral psychology (completionism) rather than historical sales data, which is appropriate for a novel mechanic. The publisher's uncertainty is a baseline condition for innovation, not a blocker.

Blockers: NONE. No factual contradiction exists in the proposal. The risks (price grab perception, hardware decline) are inherent to the strategy but are outweighed by the unique value proposition of mandatory social trading.
Risks: Consumer backlash on pricing; insufficient installed base for trading. These are managed by ensuring creature differences feel meaningful, not just cosmetic.

The mechanism turns a declining platform's isolation into a community requirement. The potential to create a self-sustaining ecosystem outweighs the risk of perceived greed.

DECISION: GO
