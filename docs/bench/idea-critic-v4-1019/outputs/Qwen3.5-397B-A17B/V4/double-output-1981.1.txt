First call: NO-GO. Doubling production while sales lag by 60% during a recession with rising interest rates destroys cash without a confirmed buyer.

Steelman: The claim assumes that flooding the market or achieving economies of scale now will force sales or lower unit costs enough to survive the downturn.

Attack:
1. **Cash Burn:** Producing 4,500 additional unsold units ties up massive capital in raw materials and labor. With high interest rates, financing this inventory is prohibitively expensive. The company has "limited cash"; this action accelerates insolvency.
2. **Demand Mismatch:** A recession suppresses demand; high interest rates make car loans unaffordable. Increasing supply does not create demand. Dealers already struggle to finance current stock; they cannot absorb double the volume.
3. **Alternative:** The "do nothing" or "reduce production" path preserves cash. A cheaper alternative is to halt production lines temporarily to match the 3,000-unit sales run-rate, preserving liquidity for marketing or dealer incentives once the economy stabilizes.
4. **Pre-mortem:** If we double production, we run out of cash within months. We are forced into bankruptcy or a fire sale of assets at the bottom of the market. The inventory becomes obsolete before it sells.
5. **Evidence:** The proposal's own facts state sales are 3,000 vs. production of 7,500. The gap is widening. Economic conditions (recession, high rates) are headwinds, not tailwinds.

Blockers:
- **Cash Flow:** Limited cash + high interest rates + doubled production costs = immediate liquidity crisis.
- **Channel Capacity:** Dealers cannot finance existing stock, let alone double it.
- **Market Reality:** Supply exceeds demand by 2.5x; increasing supply worsens the imbalance.

Risks:
- Inventory obsolescence.
- Brand damage from desperate discounting later.
- Total company failure.

This plan ignores the fundamental constraint: lack of demand and capital. It bets on a miracle rather than survival logic. There is no kill criterion that saves this; the premise itself is fatal.

DECISION: NO-GO
