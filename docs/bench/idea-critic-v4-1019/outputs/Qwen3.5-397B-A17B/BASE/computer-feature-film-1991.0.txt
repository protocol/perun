The proposal represents a pivotal moment to redefine the animation industry by committing to the first entirely computer-animated feature film. While the risks are substantial, they are inherent to pioneering any new medium. The concern that no all-CG feature exists is precisely the opportunity; being first creates an insurmountable brand advantage and establishes the company as the definitive leader in this emerging technology.

The fact that the company has only produced shorts is a valid operational concern, yet the core creative talent remains intact. The lead animator, whom the large studio attempted to poach, is the critical asset. Their presence validates the technical feasibility and artistic vision required to scale from short forms to a feature narrative. Losing this talent to a competitor would be a far greater strategic failure than the operational growing pains of expanding production scope.

Regarding rendering costs and speed, these are technical hurdles that will inevitably decrease over time as hardware improves. Delaying entry waits for costs to drop but cedes market ownership to others who might take the leap first. The high initial investment acts as a barrier to entry for future competitors, securing a long-term monopoly on the aesthetic and technical expertise.

The power imbalance with the large studio partner is real, but necessary. Without their distribution network and publishing muscle, the film would have no path to audiences. Accepting their stronger hand is the price of admission for a small company to access a global market. This partnership provides the financial shield needed to absorb the risks of unproven technology while the small company retains creative control over the unique "toy's point of view" concept.

Rejecting this deal preserves the status quo but guarantees eventual obsolescence as the industry shifts toward digital methods. The potential reward of creating a new genre and owning its origin story outweighs the financial and operational uncertainties. The window to define this medium is open now; waiting will only close it.

DECISION: GO
