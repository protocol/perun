Players may abandon the paid cooperative experience entirely for the free alternative, eroding the studio's current reliable revenue stream without guaranteeing sufficient new income from cosmetic sales. The studio has no prior history operating a free-to-play business model, meaning they lack the data, live-ops teams, and economic balancing expertise required to make microtransactions profitable. Launching without this foundational knowledge significantly increases the likelihood of financial failure.

The combination of a saturated market, an unrealistic development schedule, unproven monetization strategies, and the direct threat to existing paid sales creates a scenario where failure is more probable than success. Approving this now would likely waste resources and damage the brand's reputation if the product launches in a poor state. The risks regarding cannibalization and operational inexperience are too severe to ignore. The project requires a complete strategic overhaul, a realistic timeline extension, and a validated plan for live operations before any development begins.
