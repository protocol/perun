The proposed path is the only one that attempts to create a new market segment rather than fighting a losing spec-war.

Failure mode: Developers ignore the second screen, producing lazy ports that confuse users, leading to weak software sales despite hardware adoption. **Evidence & Calibration:** The proposal relies on the assumption that "broadening the audience" outweighs "core gamer satisfaction." There is no data provided on touch-screen usability or developer willingness to adapt. However, the strategy explicitly mitigates the "no software" risk by keeping the older, compatible handheld on sale, ensuring a baseline library exists while the new ecosystem grows.
