**Steelman:** Direct-to-consumer streaming captures higher lifetime value and strategic control than licensing, justifying short-term losses to build a scaled audience in a winner-take-most market.

**Skeptic Attack:** The proposal assumes the conglomerate can execute a global technology platform it has never run while simultaneously absorbing the immediate loss of billions in guaranteed licensing revenue. The "low price" strategy relies on burning cash to buy subscribers, yet the target (tens of millions) is a fraction of the incumbent's base, risking a perpetual loss position without network effects. Crucially, cannibalizing existing lucrative cable and theatrical windows creates a revenue hole that new subscriptions may not fill quickly enough, threatening core profitability before scale is achieved.

**Better Way:** A hybrid approach: launch a premium-priced niche service for super-fans while maintaining licensing deals for the long tail of content to preserve cash flow. Alternatively, partner with an existing tech giant for distribution rather than building a costly proprietary platform from scratch.

**Kill Criteria:** If subscriber acquisition costs exceed 3x the lifetime value within 18 months, or if licensing revenue drops by more than 40% without a corresponding 20% increase in direct subscribers, the strategy must pivot back to licensing.

**Pre-mortem:** The platform launches with technical failures during peak demand, driving churn; the cash burn exhausts reserves before reaching profitability, forcing a fire sale of the studio assets. The "low price" attracts low-loyalty users who churn immediately upon price hikes.

**Decision:** The proposal ignores the execution risk of building a tech platform from zero and underestimates the danger of destroying guaranteed revenue streams for speculative growth. Without a verified technology partner or a phased rollout that protects licensing income, the risk of catastrophic capital destruction is too high. The plan lacks a credible path to profitability given the incumbent's massive head start.

DECISION: NO-GO
