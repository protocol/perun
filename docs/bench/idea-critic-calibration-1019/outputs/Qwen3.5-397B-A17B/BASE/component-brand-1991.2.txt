The proposal addresses a critical vulnerability: the end-user is unaware of the product's identity, allowing clones to erode market share through confusion. By launching a consumer brand campaign, the company shifts power dynamics. Currently, computer makers hold all leverage because buyers are indifferent to the internal component. Creating consumer demand forces computer makers to include the specific chip to satisfy customer requests, effectively making the component a purchasing criterion rather than an invisible specification.

The risk of unprecedented consumer advertising for a component is precisely why this strategy offers a first-mover advantage. If successful, it creates a barrier to entry that clone makers cannot easily bypass, as they lack the brand equity to trigger consumer pull. While the program requires a significant portion of the marketing budget, this investment buys leverage over distribution channels. Without it, the company remains at the mercy of computer makers' pricing pressures and clone competition.

Concerns regarding computer maker resentment are valid but manageable. The offer to subsidize their advertising costs provides a direct financial incentive that outweighs the loss of creative control. Most partners will prioritize reduced marketing expenses over autonomy, especially if the campaign drives higher overall unit sales. The potential for sales benefits not showing immediately is a standard risk in brand building; however, doing nothing guarantees continued commoditization and margin erosion.

The status quo is unsustainable given the threat of clones using similar part numbers. Passive defense will fail. Active brand creation is the only viable path to differentiate the product in the minds of the ultimate decision-makers: the consumers. The high budget allocation is necessary to achieve the critical mass required for brand recall. Waiting for perfect certainty or revised plans allows competitors to solidify their foothold. The potential reward of transforming a commodity into a preferred brand justifies the immediate execution of this bold strategy.

DECISION: GO
