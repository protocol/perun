The proposal to instantly replace a decades-old logo with an untested design carries unacceptable risk. The brand's identity is deeply tied to the existing dark blue square, which serves as the primary recognition marker for loyal customers. Removing this anchor without warning ignores the emotional attachment the core customer base holds for the heritage symbol.

Executing a simultaneous rollout across every channel with no advance announcement eliminates any opportunity to manage the narrative or gauge reaction. In an era where social media amplifies dissent instantly, this approach invites immediate, widespread backlash that could damage reputation beyond repair. The lack of customer testing is a critical failure; launching a major visual rebrand without validating its reception is negligent, especially when the goal is to appear contemporary rather than alienate the existing market.

Contemporary branding requires evolution, not erasure. A sudden, total swap suggests a disconnect from the very people who sustain the business. The risks outlined—loss of recognition, customer alienation, and viral negative sentiment—outweigh the potential benefits of a fresher look. The strategy lacks safeguards, phased implementation, or validation data. Proceeding as proposed threatens the brand's stability and market position. Approval cannot be granted for a plan that disregards customer sentiment and bypasses essential testing protocols.

DECISION: NO-GO
