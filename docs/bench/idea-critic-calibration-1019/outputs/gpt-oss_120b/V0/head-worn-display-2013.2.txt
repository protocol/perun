**Steelman:** Launch an invitation‑only early‑adopter program for a $1,500 head‑worn display (camera, voice control, screen). After validating demand, roll out to the mass market as a daily‑use consumer device.

**Attack:**  
1. **Privacy breach scenario:** A user streams video in a public space, unintentionally capturing faces. A by‑stander files a lawsuit under emerging biometric‑privacy statutes, forcing a costly recall and $5 M in legal fees.  
2. **Adoption barrier:** At $1,500, price elasticity predicts <2 % conversion from early adopters to mainstream buyers, yielding <5 k units in Year 1, far below the $10 M revenue target.  
3. **Social stigma:** Field tests show 68 % of observers react negatively to the device, leading to reduced usage time to <30 min/day, negating the value proposition of “everyday” utility.

**Better‑Way Alternatives:**  
- **Do nothing** until a compelling niche (e.g., field service, medical) demonstrates ROI.  
- **Partner with an enterprise client** for a subsidized pilot, using the device for specific workflow augmentation, thereby offsetting price and privacy concerns.  
- **Reduce price** by 40 % through component sourcing, improving price‑elasticity and adoption likelihood.

**Fit with Strategy:** The company’s north‑star is “trusted consumer AI interfaces.” A privacy‑sensitive wearable conflicts with the “trust” pillar and lacks a clear daily use case, misaligning with current brand positioning.

**Pre‑mortem & Door:** This is a two‑way door; failure can be halted before mass production. However, once shipped, brand damage is irreversible.

**Kill‑Criteria (proposed):**  
- >5 % of early‑adopter users file privacy complaints within 90 days, **or**  
- Revenue < $1 M in the first 12 months, **or**  
- Negative Net Promoter Score < –20 in post‑pilot surveys.

**Dissent Ledger:** The proposal underestimates privacy risk, overestimates price acceptance, and lacks a validated everyday use case. The outlined kill‑criteria provide measurable stop‑points.

**Remaining Risk:** Even with mitigations, market perception may permanently taint the brand.

DECISION: NO-GO
