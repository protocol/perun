**Steelman** – Launch a free email service offering 1 GB storage per user, fast full‑text search, and a responsive web UI. Funding comes from contextual text ads matched to email content. Rollout begins invitation‑only to control growth and costs.

**Skeptic attacks**  
1. **Storage cost** – At 1 GB per user, even a modest user base (e.g., 10 k users) requires ≈10 TB of storage. In 2004, commodity disk cost was ~$30/GB, implying >$300 k just for raw capacity, not counting redundancy, backups, and I/O infrastructure. Without a clear path to revenue, the breakeven point is unclear.  
2. **Privacy** – Scanning users’ mail to serve ads raises legal (e.g., EU data‑protection) and reputational risks. Users accustomed to “free” services still expect confidentiality; a breach could trigger churn and lawsuits.  
3. **Market saturation** – Established players (Hotmail, Yahoo! Mail, Gmail soon after) already provide multi‑megabyte storage with ad‑supported models. Differentiation rests solely on storage size and search speed, which may not outweigh network effects and brand loyalty.  
4. **Revenue lag** – No direct income at launch means the service must be cash‑flow neutral for an undefined period, increasing exposure to the above cost and privacy risks.

**Better‑way alternatives**  
- Start with a modest storage quota (e.g., 50 MB) and scale as usage proves profitable.  
- Use non‑content‑based ads (banner, contextual to UI) to avoid privacy concerns.  
- Partner with an existing ISP or cloud provider to share infrastructure costs.

**Kill‑criteria**  
- If projected storage cost exceeds $1 M before reaching 100 k users, abort.  
- If privacy audit flags any non‑consensual content analysis, halt the ad‑matching model.  
- If user acquisition cost > $5 per user in the first six months, stop expansion.

**Remaining risk** – High capital outlay with uncertain monetisation and potential regulatory backlash.

Given the unmitigated cost, privacy, and competitive risks, the proposal should not be approved in its current form.  

DECISION: NO-GO
