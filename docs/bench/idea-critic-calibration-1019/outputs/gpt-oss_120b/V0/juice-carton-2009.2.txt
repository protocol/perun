**Steelman:** Launch a nationwide, all‑at‑once redesign of the leading chilled orange‑juice brand’s carton to a minimalist graphic (glass of juice, orange‑shaped cap, vertically rotated brand name) while keeping recipe and price, supported by a large advertising campaign.

**Skeptic assumptions**  
- Current carton image is the primary shelf cue. No data is provided on how strongly shoppers rely on it.  
- Redesign cost is “high” but no budget or ROI estimate is given.  
- Loyalty of an older buyer base is asserted without evidence of their sensitivity to packaging change.

**Better‑way alternatives**  
1. **Limited‑market pilot** – test the new design in a few regions before a full rollout.  
2. **Gradual refresh** – keep the orange picture but simplify other visual elements, reducing risk and cost.  
3. **Seasonal/limited‑edition packaging** – gauge consumer reaction without committing to a permanent change.  
4. **Status‑quo** – maintain the proven design while investing in other growth levers (e.g., product line extensions).

These options are cheaper, reversible, and provide real‑world feedback.

**Kill‑criteria (pre‑mortem)**  
- If first‑quarter sales fall ≥5 % versus the same period last year, revert to the original packaging.  
- If the advertising spend exceeds the projected budget by >10 %, pause the rollout.  
- If consumer surveys show a ≥10 % drop in brand‑recognition scores, abort the redesign.

**Risk summary**  
The proposal carries high brand‑recognition risk, unknown cost impact, and potential alienation of a loyal, older demographic. Cheaper, testable alternatives exist that satisfy the same strategic goal (modernising the brand) with far lower downside.

Given the lack of supporting evidence and the availability of lower‑risk paths, the proposal should not be approved as‑is.

DECISION: NO-GO
