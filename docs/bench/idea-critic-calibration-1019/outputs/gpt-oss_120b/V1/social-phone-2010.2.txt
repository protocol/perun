  - Teenagers will adopt a new OS despite lack of apps.
  - A single carrier can provide sufficient market reach.
  - Mandatory data plan cost is acceptable to the teen demographic.

  - Use the existing mobile platform (e.g., Android) to leverage its mature app ecosystem.
  - Partner with multiple carriers to offer subsidized data plans or allow BYOD (bring‑your‑own‑data) options.
  - Release an app store or web‑based app portal at launch to meet teen expectations.

  - App ecosystem deficit: fewer than 100 core apps available within 6 months leads to <10 % market share.
  - Pricing barrier: data plan cost > $30/month results in <5 % adoption among target age group.
  - OS adoption: < 5 % of installed base after 12 months triggers project termination.

  - How will the company acquire or develop a sufficient app catalog quickly enough to satisfy teen users?
