**Steelman**  
The company will design, manufacture, and sell a proprietary e‑reader featuring a paper‑like display and free built‑in cellular connectivity, allowing customers to browse and purchase e‑books directly from its store at a single low price per title, without needing a computer.

**Assumptions & Risks**  
1. **Hardware expertise** – The firm can develop reliable consumer hardware despite no prior experience.  
2. **Pricing model** – Low per‑title prices will not erode margins or provoke publisher backlash.  
3. **Market appetite** – Consumers will adopt a new device even though earlier e‑readers sold poorly.  
4. **Cellular cost** – Free connectivity can be subsidized sustainably.  
5. **Cannibalization** – Shift from print to digital will not offset revenue loss from lower‑margin print sales.

**Better‑Way Alternatives**  
- **Partner** with an established e‑reader manufacturer (e.g., Amazon, Sony) and focus on content integration and exclusive offers.  
- **Licensing** the device design to a third‑party, earning royalties while avoiding capital‑intensive hardware R&D.  
- **Software‑only** approach: launch a cross‑platform reading app with optional cloud sync, leveraging existing smartphones and tablets.

**Kill‑Criteria (Deal‑Breakers)**  
- Development cost > $150 M with projected breakeven beyond 2 years.  
- Publisher refusal to supply new releases at the proposed low price, leading to loss of ≥20 % of catalog.  
- First‑year market share < 5 % or unit sales < 100 k, triggering > $30 M inventory write‑off.  
- Measurable cannibalization of print sales reducing overall profit margin by >10 %.

**Premortem**  
Delays in hardware prototyping push launch past the holiday season; battery life falls short of expectations; network costs explode; negative press harms the brand’s reputation as a bookseller rather than a hardware maker.

**Recommendation**  
Given the company’s lack of hardware experience, the high financial exposure, and viable lower‑risk alternatives, proceeding with an in‑house e‑reader is not advisable at this time.

DECISION: NO-GO
