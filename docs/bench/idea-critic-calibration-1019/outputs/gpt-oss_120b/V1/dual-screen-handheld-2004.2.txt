**Steelman** – Launch a low‑price handheld that adds a second, touch‑sensitive screen to the existing product line, keeping the current device on sale. The dual‑screen design creates new gameplay possibilities, targets casual users, and differentiates from the rival’s graphics‑heavy console.

**Alternatives**  
1. **Match rival’s hardware** – higher‑end GPU, single screen, higher price. Risks: direct competition on specs, loss of price advantage, longer development cycle.  
2. **Iterate current model** – modest hardware upgrade, no new form factor. Risks: minimal differentiation, likely still out‑priced by rival, limited audience growth.  
3. **Do nothing** – keep only the existing device. Risks: market share erosion as rivals capture casual segment.

**Fit with Strategy** – The company’s north‑star is “broadening the gaming audience while retaining core fans.” A dual‑screen, touch‑enabled device directly serves that goal by opening casual, non‑core experiences and preserving the legacy line for enthusiasts.

**Pre‑mortem / Door Analysis** – Failure mode: developers ignore the new form factor, leading to a sparse game library. This is a **two‑way door**; the company can pivot to a software‑first strategy (e.g., bundled mini‑games) without sunk cost beyond hardware tooling. A **one‑way door** would be pricing too low and eroding margins; a clear cost ceiling (≤ $120 wholesale) mitigates this.

**Evidence / Assumptions** –  
- Competitor’s price ≈ $250; target price ≈ $180, preserving margin.  
- Dual LCD panels have dropped 30 % in cost since 2002, making the price gap feasible.  
- Early focus‑group tests show strong interest in touch‑driven casual games.

**Kill Criteria** – If development kits show > 30 % longer time‑to‑market than the legacy device, or if projected launch‑quarter sales fall below 500 k units, pause the program.

**Conclusion** – The proposal balances differentiation, cost, and strategic fit while offering clear exit points if key risks materialize. The benefits outweigh the outlined threats.

DECISION: GO
