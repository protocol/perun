verdict: REVISE
independence: inline
origin: agent-originated
claim: Launch a low‑price sporty two‑door coupe/convertible built on the existing compact car’s mechanicals, with a long hood, short deck, many options, and a massive launch advertising campaign.
steelman: The proposal aims to capture the emerging demand from post‑war baby‑boom youth for an affordable, stylish small car by re‑using proven mechanical components to keep costs low, offering extensive customization, and driving sales through an aggressive marketing push.
hats_run: skeptic, better-way, kill-criteria
assumptions:
  - There is a sizable, untapped market of young buyers who want sporty styling at low price.
  - Existing compact‑car mechanicals can support the performance expectations of a sporty coupe/convertible without compromising perceived quality.
  - A large advertising budget will generate sufficient demand to offset its cost before sales data are available.
better_ways:
  - Introduce a limited‑run “sport” trim of the existing compact model to test market response before committing to a new body style.
  - Offer a dealer‑installed sport package rather than a fully distinct coupe/convertible, reducing tooling and launch costs.
  - Phase the advertising spend, starting with regional campaigns and scaling up only after sales thresholds are met.
kill_criteria:
  - If first‑quarter sales of the new model fall below 10 000 units, halt production and revert to the base compact line.
  - If unit cost (including added tooling and advertising amortization) exceeds $1 200, stop further investment.
  - If brand perception surveys show a ≥15 % drop in perceived quality for the compact line within six months, discontinue the sporty variant.
strongest_attack_survived: The primary attack – “Unproven demand at volume means the massive ad spend could create a large loss if sales do not materialize” – survived because the proposal provides no market data, no pilot phase, and no sales‑threshold trigger; thus the risk remains unmitigated.
questions_parent_must_resolve:
  - What minimum quarterly sales volume is required to break even given the projected advertising spend?
  - How will the brand’s quality perception be measured and protected during the launch?
  - What is the incremental cost per vehicle for the new body styling and options package?
user_question: NONE
dissent_ledger: The proposal’s ambition is commendable, but it relies on unverified market demand and assumes that re‑using cheap mechanicals won’t hurt the sporty image. Without a staged rollout, sales‑threshold triggers, or a cost cap, the financial exposure is high.
remaining_risk: Market demand uncertainty, brand‑perception dilution, and potential overspend on advertising remain significant until mitigated by pilot testing and clear kill criteria.
