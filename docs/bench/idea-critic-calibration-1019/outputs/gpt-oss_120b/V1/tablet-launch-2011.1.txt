**Steelman** – Launch the tablet on the acquired OS at market‑leader price, with a modest app catalog, backed by aggressive developer outreach and advertising.

**Attack & Risks**  
1. **Market dominance** – The incumbent tablet controls >70 % of shipments; entering at the same price gives no price advantage.  
2. **App ecosystem** – A “small” catalog means fewer than 10 % of the leader’s apps; most consumers choose devices with rich app choices.  
3. **OS market share** – The OS has near‑zero presence in phones or tablets; brand‑recognition and user trust are minimal.  
4. **Leadership transition** – New executives may lack cohesion, increasing execution risk.  
5. **Consumer perception** – Early adopters will view the product as unproven, likely reducing launch‑phase sales.

**Better‑Way** – Start with a low‑cost, limited‑release pilot (e.g., education or enterprise niche) at a discount, using the same OS but without matching the leader’s price. This reduces sunk cost and provides real data on app ecosystem growth and consumer uptake.

**Kill‑Criteria** – If after six months the tablet’s market share <2 % or the app catalog remains <5 % of the leader’s, cease further investment and re‑allocate resources.

**Door** – This is a two‑way door: the pilot can be halted with limited loss; a full launch would be a one‑way commitment.

**Decision** – Given the high‑cost, low‑differentiation launch and the lack of a proven ecosystem, the proposal should not be approved as‑is.

DECISION: NO-GO
