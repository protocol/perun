**Steelman** – Launch a $89.95 handheld with a small monochrome screen, modest CPU, long battery life, bundled with a puzzle game, supported by a $20 M marketing push, to capture price‑sensitive gamers despite a rival color handheld.

**Skeptic attack** – The monochrome display may be perceived as outdated, limiting appeal to gamers who value graphics. Thin margins at $89.95 risk cash‑flow if component costs exceed $60. The $20 M spend could be oversized for a product that may sell modestly.

**Better‑way alternatives**  
1. **Do nothing** – Stay in the home‑console market; avoid inventory risk but cede handheld space to rivals.  
2. **Delay for color LCD** – Postpone launch 12‑18 months to introduce a low‑cost color screen, matching the rival’s spec but at higher price, risking missed market entry.  
3. **Bundle with multiple games** – Increase perceived value without raising price, but adds licensing cost.

**Fit with strategy** – The company seeks to diversify into portable gaming and leverage its strong brand. A low‑price monochrome device mirrors the successful Game Boy model of the era, aligning with a “mass‑market, long‑battery” positioning.

**Premortem / door** – If sales < 500 k units in six months, the product becomes a one‑way door: excess inventory and brand dilution. The $20 M marketing spend is a two‑way door; it can be scaled back if early demand signals are weak.

**Kill‑criteria** –  
- **Sales threshold**: < 500 k units sold within 6 months → halt production and liquidate inventory.  
- **Margin check**: Gross margin < 30 % after component cost and marketing → discontinue.  
- **Battery life**: < 10 h on a full charge → issue firmware update or recall.

**Remaining risk** – Competitive pressure from the color rival; potential perception of low quality; reliance on a single bundled game for launch momentum.

Given historical precedent that a well‑priced monochrome handheld succeeded despite color competition, and that the proposed margins and marketing budget are comparable to proven launches, the plan is acceptable provided the kill‑criteria are monitored.

DECISION: GO
