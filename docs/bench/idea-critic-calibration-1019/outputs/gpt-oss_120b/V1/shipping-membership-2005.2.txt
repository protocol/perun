**Steelman:** Launch a $79 annual membership that grants free two‑day shipping on all eligible orders and discounted one‑day rates, aiming to reduce shipping‑fee friction and boost repeat purchases.

**Attack / Skepticism**
- **Cost vs. revenue:** Without a detailed cost model, heavy users could generate shipping costs that far exceed the $79 fee. A 5‑order/month heavy buyer (average $50 order) would cost roughly $15 × 12 = $180 in two‑day shipping, a $101 loss per member.
- **Margin impact:** Current margin on shipped goods is unknown; a large share of orders may shift from paid‑shipping to free, eroding short‑term profit.
- **Reversibility:** Membership contracts are hard to unwind; churn could lock customers into a loss‑making tier.
- **Unproven economics:** No comparable baseline (e.g., prior pilot, competitor data) is provided.

**Better‑Way / Alternatives**
1. **Do nothing** – retain per‑order fees; avoid risk.
2. **Pilot a limited‑time free‑shipping trial** for a segment to collect cost data before committing to a paid membership.
3. **Tiered membership** (e.g., $49 for free two‑day on orders >$75) to shift cost to higher‑value purchases.

**Fit:** Aligns with goal of increasing repeat purchases, but conflicts with short‑term profitability targets.

**Premortem / Kill‑Criteria**
- **One‑way door:** If average shipping cost per member > $40 in the first quarter, abort.
- **Metric:** Membership uptake ≥ 30% of active customers and net contribution margin ≥ $10 per member within six months.

**Evidence Needed**
- Detailed shipping‑cost model (average weight, distance, carrier rates).
- Projected member uptake based on market research.
- Sensitivity analysis showing break‑even uptake and cost thresholds.

**Conclusion:** The proposal lacks essential financial validation and a safe‑exit condition. Approve only after a data‑driven pilot and defined kill criteria.

DECISION: NO-GO
