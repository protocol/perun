The acquisition pairs two declining businesses with overlapping operational challenges, yet offers few clear upside drivers.  

**Key risks**  
1. **Revenue drag** – Both chains are already losing market share; merging will not automatically reverse this trend.  
2. **Asset condition** – Stores are old and under‑invested, likely requiring substantial capex to meet modern shopper expectations.  
3. **Cost‑saving uncertainty** – Projected synergies are speculative; integration of corporate functions across divergent cultures often yields lower than expected savings.  
4. **Customer mismatch** – Discount‑store shoppers differ from department‑store buyers, making brand integration and cross‑selling risky.  
5. **Competitive pressure** – Big‑box rivals are expanding, further eroding the combined market.

**Missing evidence / kill criteria**  
- No quantitative target for sales rebound (e.g., >5 % growth in 24 months).  
- No firm capex budget for store upgrades or a timeline for required investments.  
- No validated synergy model with a minimum cost‑saving threshold (e.g., ≥10 % of combined operating expenses).  
- No pilot or staged approach to test the brand‑mix concept before full rollout.

**Better‑way alternatives**  
- Focus on organic turnaround of the discount chain: invest selectively in high‑potential stores, modernize the brand, and improve supply‑chain efficiency.  
- Pursue smaller, strategic asset purchases that complement the discount format without the heavy integration burden.  
- Form a joint‑venture or limited‑scope partnership with the department‑store chain to test brand‑mix concepts before a full merger.

**Pre‑mortem scenario**  
If integration costs exceed projected savings and sales continue to decline, the combined entity may face liquidity strain, forcing a distressed sale at a loss—an irreversible one‑way door.

**Conclusion** – Without concrete financial targets, a clear capex plan, and validated synergy estimates, the proposal lacks sufficient justification to proceed now.

DECISION: NO-GO
