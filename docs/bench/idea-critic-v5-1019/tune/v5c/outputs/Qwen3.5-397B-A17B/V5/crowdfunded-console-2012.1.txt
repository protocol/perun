First call: NO-GO. The proposal relies on a developer ecosystem and supply chain execution that the team explicitly admits it cannot secure, while entering a market dominated by incumbents with deep pockets.

Steelman: A niche, open-source console can succeed by targeting underserved hobbyists and indie developers ignored by major players, using crowdfunding to validate demand before mass production.

However, the attack reveals fatal flaws in the current plan:
1.  **Developer Chicken-and-Egg:** The proposal admits developers may not build for a new platform. Without exclusive hits or a massive installed base, the library will remain empty. Crowdfunding backs ideas, not empty ecosystems.
2.  **Hardware Reality:** The team acknowledges hardware delays are common but sets a rigid "ship within a year" deadline. In hardware, optimism kills companies. Missing this window burns backer trust and cash reserves instantly.
3.  **Marketing Void:** With no budget and established competitors (consoles + phones), discovery is impossible. "If we build it, they will come" is a strategy for failure in saturated markets.
4.  **Alternatives:** The "do nothing" or "software-first" approach is superior. Building an emulator or game engine for existing platforms (PC, Android) validates the software vision without the capital risk of custom silicon.

Pre-mortem: The campaign funds, but the hardware ships 18 months late with a limited game library. Backers demand refunds; the brand is toxic; the team dissolves.

Blockers:
-   **Ecosystem Gap:** No committed developer partners listed; the core value proposition (games) is unproven.
-   **Timeline Delusion:** A one-year ship date for new hardware with no buffer contradicts the stated risk of "common delays."
-   **Distribution Blindness:** Zero marketing budget against entrenched rivals makes public sales post-crowdfund unrealistic.

Risks: Supply chain cost overruns, feature creep, rapid mobile hardware improvement outpacing the static console specs.

The plan attempts to solve a hard distribution and ecosystem problem with a funding mechanism (crowdfunding) that does not solve either. It requires a pivot to a software-first validation or secured manufacturing/developer contracts before any hardware commitment.

DECISION: NO-GO
