First call: GO. The proposal secures the franchise's future by transitioning customers to an inevitable medium before competitors force them out, using the existing profitable base as a bridge.

Steelman: Leveraging seven million paying subscribers and a trusted brand to seed a streaming habit at zero marginal acquisition cost, treating the small catalog not as a product but as a proof-of-concept to lock in users before the market consolidates.

Alternatives:
1. Do nothing: Maximizes short-term DVD profit but guarantees extinction as broadband普及s and giants enter; high risk, zero reversibility once lost.
2. Spin-off streaming: Doubles costs, confuses branding, and slows adoption; higher risk, lower synergy.
3. Wait for better tech/content: Cedes the customer relationship to entrants; fatal delay.

Fit: Aligns with the north star of "home entertainment delivery," shifting the vehicle from physical to digital while the cash cow funds the transition.

Pre-mortem: Failure occurs if licensing costs bleed the DVD profits before streaming scales, or if quality issues drive users to pirates. However, the "no extra charge" model mitigates churn risk, and the small catalog limits initial exposure. This is a two-way door; features can be added or pricing adjusted later. The cost of delay is total market irrelevance.

Evidence: The proposal admits risks (cost, cannibalization) but relies on the verified fact of 7M subscribers. Cannibalization is a feature, not a bug: it is better to cannibalize oneself than be cannibalized by a competitor. The "poor quality" and "small catalog" are temporary constraints that improve with scale, whereas the subscriber base is an immediate asset.

Blockers: NONE. No fact presented proves the plan fails as proposed; the risks are execution hurdles, not fundamental flaws. The high licensing costs are offset by the zero customer acquisition cost.

Risks: Margin compression during transition; technology scaling issues. These become kill criteria: if streaming operating costs exceed 10% of DVD profits within 18 months without a 5% conversion rate, pivot pricing.

DECISION: GO
