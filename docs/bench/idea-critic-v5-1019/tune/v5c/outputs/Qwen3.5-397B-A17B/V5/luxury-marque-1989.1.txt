First call: GO. The proposal targets an unmet gap: luxury quality at a non-luxury price, leveraging existing engineering efficiency to undercut incumbents.

**Steelman:** A distinct brand identity, separated physically and operationally from the economy parent, can overcome brand stigma while delivering superior value through shared underlying engineering discipline, forcing incumbents to defend against a high-quality, lower-price disruptor.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
1.  **Brand Stigma:** Valid risk. Mitigation requires total separation (new name, new dealers). *Alternative:* Upgrading the main brand fails; the "economy" anchor is too strong. *Better way:* None; a clean break is the only path to credibility.
2.  **Cost of Dealers:** High fixed cost. *Counter:* Essential for the luxury service experience; skipping this guarantees failure against European rivals.
3.  **Thin Line-up:** High risk. *Counter:* A single, perfect flagship establishes the brand ceiling. Expansion follows proof of concept. Waiting for a full line-up delays entry and burns cash on unreleased models.
4.  **Sunk Costs:** Irrelevant to the forward decision; the heavy development spend signals commitment and reduces marginal launch costs.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the new brand is perceived as a "rich economy car" rather than a true luxury alternative. This happens if the dealer experience or build quality dips even slightly below the European benchmark.

**Blockers:** None. The proposal explicitly addresses the primary blocker (brand perception) via the separate network and brand strategy. The risks cited (cost, prestige, line-up) are execution challenges, not factual contradictions. The economic logic (high quality, lower price) creates a compelling value proposition that incumbents cannot easily match without destroying their own margins.

**Kill Criteria:** If initial pre-orders do not meet 60% of the first-year production capacity within six months of unveiling, or if independent quality reviews rate the flagship below the segment average, the program must pivot to a niche manufacturer or cease expansion.

**Decision:** The strategy correctly identifies that brand equity is mutable if the product and experience are sufficiently distinct and superior. The price advantage provides the wedge; the separate network provides the shield.

DECISION: GO
