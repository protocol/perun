2. **Do Nothing/Wait:** Preserve capital, observe market evolution. Zero cost, low risk, high opportunity cost of delay.
3. **Pilot First:** Perfect operations in the current metro, prove positive unit economics, then expand. Moderate cost, manageable risk, reversible.
