First call: GO. The hybrid form factor directly addresses the erosion of the handheld line by phones while leveraging the maker's strong first-party IP to overcome weak hardware specs, creating a unique market position that rivals cannot immediately replicate.

**Steelman:** A differentiated hybrid device leverages existing IP to create a new category, escaping the losing battle of raw power against rivals and phone-based handheld erosion.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Continued erosion of handheld revenue and irrelevance in the home market due to past failure.
*   **Power-Focused Home Console:** High risk of repeating previous financial losses; ignores the mobile chip constraint and phone competition.
*   **Handheld Only:** Cedes the living room entirely and fails to justify a premium price against phones.
The proposal beats "do nothing" by creating a unique value proposition. The "confusing positioning" risk is mitigated by the clear "hybrid" utility, distinct from the previous console's identity crisis. Weak specs are a feature, not a bug, enabling the mobile form factor and lower price point relative to performance.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if third-party support remains non-existent and first-party titles are delayed, leaving the device without software. However, the proposal explicitly relies on the maker's own studios for launch, directly addressing this. Supply constraints are a logistical hurdle, not a strategic flaw.

**Evidence & Fit:** The facts state the handheld line is eroding and the last home console failed. A hybrid solves both: it is a home console that cannot be confused with the previous failure (it's portable) and a handheld that offers more than a phone (detachable controllers, TV dock). The modest chip is necessary for the handheld mode.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks cited (weak specs, confusion, third-party support) are inherent to the strategy but are countered by the unique hybrid nature and reliance on first-party content. No fact provided proves the target users reject this specific combination; the "confusion" was tied to the *previous* console, which this proposal structurally differs from.

**Risks:** Third-party adoption lagging; supply chain inability to meet holiday demand. These become kill criteria: if third-party attach rate < X% by month 6, or if sell-through is capped by supply for two consecutive quarters, pivot strategy.

**Decision:** The plan turns weaknesses (low power, past failure) into a differentiated strength (portability, new category).

DECISION: GO
