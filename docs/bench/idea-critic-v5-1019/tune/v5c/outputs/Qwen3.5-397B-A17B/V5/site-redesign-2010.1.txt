First call: NO-GO. The proposal combines maximum technical risk (full rewrite) with maximum business risk (forced migration, no rollback) while alienating the core user base that drives traffic.

**Steelman:** A complete rewrite is the only way to fix structural debt and pivot to publisher content quickly enough to stop traffic decline.

**Attack:**
1.  **Fit:** The core community values current voting mechanics. Favoring publishers over users contradicts the "social news" value proposition, risking a revolt that accelerates traffic loss.
2.  **Options:** "Do nothing" preserves the loyal base. A "staged rollout" or "A/B test" allows measuring publisher content appeal without burning the bridge. The proposed "big bang" launch beats neither; it guarantees a single point of failure.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Launch day arrives; the new algorithm suppresses community favorites. Users feel betrayed, migrate to the rival site immediately. The "no rollback" clause means the team cannot revert before the exodus becomes permanent. The rewrite consumes all engineering capacity for months, leaving zero resources to react to the rival's growth.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal admits "no rollback" and "large engineering risk" while facing a "growing rival." Proceeding without a safety net against a known active threat is operational negligence.
5.  **Blockers:**
    *   **No Rollback:** A forced migration with no exit strategy is a blocker for any high-risk change.
    *   **Community Alienation:** Changing the core mechanic valued by the loyal base without validation threatens the primary asset.
    *   **Resource Lock:** A full rewrite prevents rapid response to the rival's moves.

**Risks:** Unproven demand for publisher-favored content; execution failure of the rewrite; total user churn.

**Kill Criteria:** If a pilot with 5% of users shows a drop in return visits >10% within two weeks, halt the rewrite.

**Decision:** The lack of a rollback plan and the antagonism toward the core community make this specific plan untenable.

DECISION: NO-GO
