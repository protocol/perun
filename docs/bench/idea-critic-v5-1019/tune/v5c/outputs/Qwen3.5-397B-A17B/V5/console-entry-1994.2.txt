First call: NO-GO. The proposal asks a hardware manufacturer with zero platform experience to enter a duopoly dominated by entrenched competitors with loyal user bases and iconic software libraries, while simultaneously launching against a rival's next-generation 3D system.

Steelman: A CD-based console could disrupt the market by offering significantly cheaper manufacturing and easier development tools than cartridges, attracting third-party developers ignored by incumbents.

Attack: The "cheaper disc" advantage is nullified if the hardware sells at a loss—a standard industry practice this company has no history of subsidizing. Without first-party hit games to drive hardware sales, courting external developers is a circular dependency: developers won't commit to a platform without an installed base, and users won't buy the hardware without games. The proposal admits no experience as a platform holder; this is not a minor gap but a fundamental incapacity to manage the ecosystem required for success. Furthermore, launching concurrently with an incumbent's 3D console invites direct comparison against a proven brand, likely resulting in a "also-ran" status. The risks listed (no experience, strong incumbents, timing conflict, margin pressure) are not merely potential downsides; they are structural blockers that the proposal offers no specific strategy to overcome. Relying on "easier tools" is insufficient when the competitors control the market's defining franchises.

Blockers:
1. No existing installed base or first-party software IP to drive initial hardware adoption.
2. Direct launch timing against an incumbent's 3D successor creates insurmountable marketing noise.
3. Lack of financial model or experience to sustain hardware losses required for market penetration.

Risks:
Developer apathy, price wars, brand dilution.

The absence of a concrete plan to secure killer apps or subsidize hardware costs renders the venture dead on arrival. The "cheaper manufacturing" benefit does not offset the cost of acquiring a user base from scratch against two fortified giants.

DECISION: NO-GO
