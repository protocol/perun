First call: NO-GO. The core value proposition (reliable handwriting recognition) is explicitly stated as unreliable in testing, and the form factor fails the primary constraint for a "handheld" device (pocketability).

**Steelman:** Launch a first-of-category pen computer at $700 to define the market early, leveraging CEO publicity to overcome initial technical roughness and size issues.

**Attack:**
1.  **Core Mechanism Failure:** The primary input method is admitted unreliable. Selling a computer where the main interface fails is not a "risk"; it is a product defect that guarantees negative reviews and returns.
2.  **Form Factor Contradiction:** A "handheld" that does not fit in a pocket contradicts the portability requirement of the category.
3.  **Price vs. Value:** At $700 (1993 dollars), the price is premium. Customers will not pay a premium for a device that is too large and does not work correctly.
4.  **Ecosystem Void:** Little software exists. Without a killer app or reliable input, the hardware is an expensive paperweight.
5.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" allows competitors to fail first while the company refines the technology. A cheaper, smaller prototype with verified recognition is a superior alternative to a full commercial launch.

**Blockers:**
*   **Fact:** Handwriting recognition is unreliable in testing.
*   **Impact:** The product cannot perform its primary function.
*   **Fact:** Device is too big for a pocket.
*   **Impact:** Violates the essential definition of the proposed category.

**Risks:** Unproven category, high price, lack of software. These are secondary to the blockers; even if the category were proven, this specific product fails on execution.

**Kill Criteria Check:** The proposal lacks any pilot, cap, or metric. It proposes a full launch despite known fatal flaws. The stated facts directly contradict the viability of the product. No amount of publicity can fix a broken core mechanism and poor ergonomics.

**Decision Logic:** A product that does not work as advertised and fails its basic physical requirements cannot be launched. The blockers are factual and present in the proposal itself.

DECISION: NO-GO
