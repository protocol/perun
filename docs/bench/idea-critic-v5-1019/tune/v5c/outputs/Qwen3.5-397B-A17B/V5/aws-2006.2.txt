First call: NO-GO. The proposal asks a retailer with no developer platform experience to enter an unproven market against entrenched incumbents while investors are already hostile to its capital spending.

**Steelman:** Leveraging spiky internal infrastructure to create a new revenue stream could theoretically improve asset utilization and diversify income if the market matures.

**Attack:**
1.  **Capability Gap:** Running a retail site and selling reliable, scalable compute to developers are fundamentally different businesses. The proposal admits zero experience in the latter. Building this capability from scratch while managing investor skepticism is execution suicide.
2.  **Market Risk:** The market is explicitly "unproven." Entering a nascent market requires immense patience and capital, directly contradicting the constraint of "thin margins" and investor pressure against heavy spending.
3.  **Strategic Distraction:** With "spiky" internal needs, prioritizing external reliability over internal stability risks the core retail business during peak loads. The "distraction" risk is not theoretical; it is immediate.
4.  **Competition:** Large incumbent hardware and software vendors already exist. They have the relationships, the support structures, and the brand trust that a retailer lacks. Competing on price alone with thin margins is a race to the bottom.
5.  **Capital Misalignment:** Investors are questioning heavy capex. This proposal doubles down on that exact weakness to chase an uncertain return, likely triggering a capital crisis.

**Alternatives:**
*   *Do nothing:* Preserves capital, focuses on core retail, avoids investor backlash.
*   *Optimize internal:* Use the spiky capacity to improve retail margins or site speed, directly benefiting the core business without external risk.
*   *Partner:* If the market proves itself, partner with an incumbent rather than building a competing platform from zero.

**Pre-mortem:** The project launches, suffers an early outage that hurts a high-profile developer, the core retail site slows during a spike due to resource contention, investors pull funding citing lack of focus, and the unit is shuttered within 18 months having diluted overall margins.

**Blockers:**
*   **Fact:** Investors already question heavy capital spending. **Impact:** This proposal requires heavy continued spending for an unproven return, directly violating current financial constraints.
*   **Fact:** Market is unproven. **Impact:** No evidence exists that customers will pay, making the ROI calculation impossible and the risk unacceptable for a thin-margin business.
*   **Fact:** No internal experience. **Impact:** High probability of operational failure against established competitors.

The risks are not merely "possible"; the stated facts confirm the company lacks the capital tolerance, experience, and market validation required to succeed.

DECISION: NO-GO
