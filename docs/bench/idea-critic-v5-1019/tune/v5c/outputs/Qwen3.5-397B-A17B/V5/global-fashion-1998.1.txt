First call: NO-GO. The proposal attempts to conquer two continents simultaneously with unproven unit economics and unlimited logistical complexity, a strategy that historically bankrupts retailers before finding product-market fit.

**Steelman:** Simultaneous global launch creates an insurmountable moat via brand dominance and network effects, preventing competitors from establishing footholds in either market while we scale.

**Attack:**
1.  **Options:** "Do nothing" preserves capital. A single-market pilot (e.g., UK only) validates the "unproven" online fashion model with 1/10th the burn rate. The proposed "all-in" approach lacks a cheaper alternative that reaches the same upside; it merely accelerates failure if the core hypothesis (online fashion works) is false.
2.  **Fit:** The vision is "largest in the world," but the stage is "1998 startup." North Star alignment requires survival. Expanding to "several countries" before proving retention in one violates the stage.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 18 months. Cause: Venture capital ran out while logistics costs in three time zones spiraled, and marketing spend failed to convert due to unproven consumer behavior. The "delay" risk is guaranteed, not potential, due to multiplied coordination overhead.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal admits online fashion is "unproven." Scaling an unproven model multiplies the error surface. No data exists to support concurrent execution; the plan relies entirely on assumption.

**Blockers:**
*   **Unproven Core Mechanism:** The proposal explicitly states online fashion buying is unproven. Scaling an unvalidated mechanism across multiple jurisdictions is a factual path to insolvency, not a calculated risk.
*   **Resource Mismatch:** Venture capital is finite. The plan commits to fixed costs (offices in several countries) before variable revenue is proven. This mathematically guarantees a cash crunch before sales can catch up, as admitted in the risks.

**Risks:** Logistics complexity, marketing inefficiency, competitor response. These are secondary to the primary blocker: the lack of validation for the core business model.

**Kill Criteria:** If a single-market pilot does not achieve positive unit economics within 12 months, the global vision is invalid.

The proposal fails the "cheaper alternative" test. A staged rollout beats "do nothing" and "global launch" on risk-adjusted returns. The current plan ignores the fundamental constraint of unproven demand.

DECISION: NO-GO
