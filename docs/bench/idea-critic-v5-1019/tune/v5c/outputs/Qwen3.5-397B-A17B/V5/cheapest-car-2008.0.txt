First call: GO. The stated facts describe a massive, unserved market segment (families on scooters) with a product priced to unlock it, creating a new category rather than fighting in an old one.

Steelman: Launching a dedicated, ultra-low-cost vehicle creates a new market segment for families upgrading from two-wheelers, leveraging volume to offset thin margins.

Attack:
1.  **Alternatives:** Used cars and scooters are cheaper. However, they do not offer the specific value proposition of a *new*, safe, four-seat enclosed vehicle at this price point. The "do nothing" option leaves the target demographic on scooters.
2.  **Fit:** The "cheapest" label risks brand stigma and safety concerns. Yet, for the target buyer, price is the primary feature, not a bug. Safety doubts are a risk, not a blocker, provided minimum regulatory standards are met (assumed).
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Failure could stem from land protests delaying the factory or cost-cutting causing fatal accidents. The land protest is a localized execution risk, not a product flaw. The "cheapest" perception is the core strategy, not an accident.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal cites a specific price point ($2,500) and a clear target (scooter families). No internal data contradicts the existence of this demand.

Blockers: NONE. The risks cited (protests, safety perception, alternatives) are execution challenges or market dynamics, not factual contradictions proving the plan fails as proposed. The alternatives (used cars) do not reach the same upside (new, reliable, 4-seat) at the same cost.
Risks: Land acquisition delays; safety incidents damaging brand; failure to maintain quality at price point. These become kill criteria: if the factory is not operational within 18 months, or if a safety recall exceeds 5% of units in year one, the strategy must pivot.

The plan beats "do nothing" by unlocking a new customer base. The cheaper alternatives do not offer the same utility (new car status, 4 seats, reliability). The "cheapest" marketing is a wedge, not a flaw.

DECISION: GO
