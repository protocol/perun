First call: NO-GO. The proposal combines a premium price with a fragmented ecosystem and a gimmicky differentiator in a saturated two-platform market.

**Steelman:** A device leveraging unique 3D navigation and instant shopping integration can carve a niche among high-value retail loyalists, justifying a forked OS if the shopping experience is seamless.

**Attack:**
1.  **Value Mismatch:** Charging flagship prices for a device lacking mainstream apps (social, banking, messaging) is economically irrational. Users pay for the ecosystem, not just hardware. The "shopping feature" is a single utility, not a platform replacement.
2.  **Carrier Constraint:** Exclusivity limits reach to one carrier's subscribers, while the high unsubsidized price removes the carrier's primary lever (subsidy) to move volume. This caps the install base below the threshold needed to attract third-party developers to the forked OS.
3.  **Differentiation Risk:** 3D head-tracking is a novel interaction model but historically a polarizing gimmick that increases battery drain and cost without solving a core user pain point comparable to app availability.
4.  **Market Reality:** In a duopoly, the penalty for being "the third option" without parity is total irrelevance, not a small niche. The app gap is a blocker, not a risk; without a bridge to run leading apps, the device is non-functional for mainstream users regardless of price or shopping features.

**Alternatives:**
*   *Do nothing:* Preserve capital; focus on selling goods via existing dominant platforms.
*   *Low-cost hardware:* Subsidize the device heavily to drive retail spend, abandoning flagship margins.
*   *Software-only:* Build the shopping features as an app for existing platforms to reach 100% of the market.

**Blockers:**
*   **Economic Contradiction:** The proposal demands flagship pricing while explicitly removing the primary value driver (mainstream apps) that justifies such pricing in this market.
*   **Distribution Ceiling:** Carrier exclusivity + High Price + No Apps = Near-zero addressable market for a general-purpose smartphone.

**Risks:** Execution complexity of 3D interface; developer apathy.

**Kill Criteria:** If pre-orders do not demonstrate willingness to pay full price despite the app gap, terminate immediately. However, the structural flaws suggest failure is inherent to the current configuration.

DECISION: NO-GO
