First call: GO. The marketplace model leverages existing traffic to generate high-margin fee revenue without inventory risk, addressing the core profitability gap.

**Steelman:** Transforming from a pure retailer to a hybrid platform maximizes selection and liquidity, making the site the definitive destination for buyers while monetizing competitors' inventory through fees.

**Attack & Rebuttal:**
1.  **Cannibalization:** While used items may undercut new sales, the alternative is losing the customer entirely to another site. Capturing a fee on a used sale is superior to zero revenue on a lost new sale.
2.  **Price Undercutting:** Lower prices on product pages increase conversion rates for the page overall, benefiting the retailer's own offers through improved search ranking and traffic volume.
3.  **Trust/Quality:** Risk is mitigated by conditioning platform access on performance metrics and holding funds until delivery confirmation, aligning seller incentives with customer satisfaction.
4.  **Supplier Objection:** Publishers may resist, but the expanded reach and data on used-market pricing dynamics offer a new value proposition they cannot access alone.

**Alternatives:**
*   *Do Nothing:* Continue burning cash on inventory with limited selection; high risk of insolvency.
*   *Exclusive Inventory:* Restricting to own stock caps growth and margin potential; fails to solve the profitability timeline.
*   *Acquisition:* Buying competitors is capital-intensive and integration-heavy compared to the asset-light marketplace model.

**Pre-mortem:** The initiative fails if customer service collapses due to bad actors. **Kill Criterion:** If seller-related customer support tickets exceed 5% of total orders or negative feedback regarding third-party sellers rises above 2% within six months, suspend new seller onboarding.

**Fit:** Directly addresses the "not yet profitable" constraint by shifting the cost structure from fixed (inventory) to variable (fees).

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks cited are operational challenges inherent to the model, not factual contradictions proving failure. The proposal correctly identifies them, implying awareness.
**Risks:** Supplier retaliation, brand dilution, operational complexity in dispute resolution.

The upside of immediate revenue diversification and selection expansion outweighs the manageable operational risks. The proposal represents a necessary evolutionary step for survival and scale.

DECISION: GO
