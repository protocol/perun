First call: NO-GO. The proposal states a massive need but offers no stated edge (cost, product, access, or timing advantage) over existing local retailers or catalog competitors to justify beating them globally on day one.

**Steelman:** Simultaneous global launch captures first-mover brand dominance in online fashion before incumbents react, leveraging VC capital to outspend local rivals everywhere at once.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves cash. A single-country pilot (e.g., UK only) tests the unproven "online fashion" hypothesis with 10% of the cost and complexity. The proposal admits logistics and marketing costs multiply with country count; a pilot isolates these variables. The pilot reaches the same upside (validating the model) cheaper and safer.
2.  **Fit & Evidence:** The claim relies on "venture money" lasting until "sales catch up." With unproven demand and multiplied operational costs across continents, the burn rate likely exceeds the runway before product-market fit is found in any single market. The proposal admits the risk of money running out but offers no mitigation other than speed, which the multi-country setup inherently slows due to complexity.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs when logistics collapse in one region, draining cash needed for others, while marketing spend yields no conversion because the "online fashion" behavior doesn't exist at scale. The company runs out of cash in 18 months with broken supply chains in four countries.

**Blockers:**
*   **Cost/Runway Mismatch:** The stated fact that costs "multiply" combined with "unproven" demand creates a mathematical blocker: the capital required to sustain multi-country losses until sales catch up is undefined and likely exceeds available VC funding.
*   **Superior Alternative:** A single-market entry achieves the learning goal (is online fashion viable?) with far less risk. Since the alternative reaches the primary objective (validation) cheaper, the current plan fails the "must beat do nothing/cheaper alternative" test.

**Risks:** (Converted to Kill Criteria for a revised plan)
*   *Risk:* Online fashion is unproven. *Kill Criterion:* If conversion <1% in the pilot market within 6 months, stop.
*   *Risk:* Logistics failure. *Kill Criterion:* If fulfillment cost >20% of GMV in month 3, halt expansion.

The proposal as written commits to a high-burn, high-complexity strategy without a validated core mechanism. It must be revised to a single-market pilot first.

DECISION: NO-GO
