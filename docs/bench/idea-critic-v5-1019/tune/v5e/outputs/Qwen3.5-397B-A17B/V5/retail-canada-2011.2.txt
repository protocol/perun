First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; higher local prices and an entrenched competitor negate the "discount" advantage without proof of a cost structure low enough to undercut incumbents.

Steelman: Rapid scale via lease acquisition creates immediate market presence, leveraging existing sites to bypass construction delays and capture share before the dominant rival can react.

Attack:
1. **Edge Failure:** Entering a market with "higher prices on some goods" is not an edge if the retailer cannot sustain lower prices overall. Without a verified cost advantage against the entrenched competitor, the value proposition collapses. Need is not strategy.
2. **Operational Overload:** Building a new supply chain and inventory system while simultaneously opening ~140 stores in 24 months creates a single point of catastrophic failure. Unproven systems supporting maximum velocity is a classic pre-mortem failure mode.
3. **Alternative:** A "do nothing" or "pilot" approach (5–10 stores) allows validation of cross-border logistics and price sensitivity without betting the entire expansion budget on unproven infrastructure. The current plan beats "do nothing" only if the speed premium outweighs the near-certain risk of systemic collapse, which the facts do not support.

Blockers:
- **No Stated Edge:** The proposal admits higher local prices and a dominant rival but offers no fact showing how the retailer wins on price or assortment.
- **Unproven Core Dependency:** The expansion relies entirely on a new supply chain and inventory system that are "not yet proven" at this scale.

Risks:
- Bilingual labeling and regulatory learning curves slowing rollout.
- Price perception mismatches despite "discount" branding.

Kill Criteria Check: The proposal names risks but offers no kill criteria, caps, or pilot phase. It commits to full scale before validating the core mechanism (cross-border supply chain).

The absence of a proven cost advantage to displace the incumbent, combined with the dependency on unproven systems for immediate massive scale, constitutes a factual failure of the plan as proposed.

DECISION: NO-GO
