First call: NO-GO. The proposal identifies a real need but fails to state an edge: it offers no fact showing why this plan beats established map sites given the stated costs, lack of revenue, and technical limitations of 2004 browsers.

Steelman: Leveraging the acquirer's search dominance to distribute a free, browser-based map tool with satellite imagery could capture users despite high data costs and slow browsers, monetizing indirectly through search integration.

Attack:
1.  **Economics:** Licensing map data and satellite imagery is explicitly "costly" with "no direct revenue model." Without a stated mechanism to convert free users to profit (e.g., specific search ad integration yields), the burn rate is a blocker, not a risk.
2.  **Competition:** "Established map sites already have users." A me-too product without a unique distribution advantage or feature monopoly cannot displace incumbents solely by being free if the experience is degraded.
3.  **Technology:** "Browsers of the time are slow at interactive graphics." Building a core value proposition (interactive maps) on a platform explicitly incapable of rendering it smoothly is a fatal flaw. The proposal does not address how to overcome this technical debt.

Alternatives:
*   **Do nothing:** Avoids costly licensing fees and technical failure.
*   **Desktop-only enhancement:** Add satellite imagery to the existing desktop program where performance is guaranteed, avoiding browser limitations.
*   **Data licensing:** Sell the map data to existing players rather than building a consumer frontend.

Pre-mortem: The project launches, users encounter laggy graphics in browsers, the company burns cash on licensing with no ad revenue to offset it, and the product is shut down within a year.

Blockers:
*   No revenue model to cover stated high licensing costs.
*   Technical platform (2004 browsers) contradicts core functional requirement (interactive graphics).
*   No stated edge over incumbents.

Risks: User adoption failure, cost overruns.

The proposal's own facts confirm the target platform cannot deliver the experience and the economics do not close.

DECISION: NO-GO
