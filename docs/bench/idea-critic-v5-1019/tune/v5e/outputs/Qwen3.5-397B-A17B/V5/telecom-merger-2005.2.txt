First call: NO-GO. The proposal admits the core assets (networks) are incompatible and the target is "limited for data," while the price ($36B) creates massive goodwill risk without a stated edge on how to fix the tech mismatch or monetize the legacy burden.

**Steelman:** The merger is justified solely by acquiring the target's high-value business customers who rely on push-to-talk, assuming their retention offsets the immense cost and complexity of integrating two incompatible networks.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" or a commercial MVNO agreement to resell the target's push-to-talk service avoids the $36B cash outlay and the technical nightmare of merging incompatible infrastructure. The proposal fails to explain why full ownership is required over a partnership.
2.  **Fit & Evidence:** In 2005, the industry north star is data growth (3G/4G transition). The target is explicitly "limited for data." Acquiring a legacy voice-centric network contradicts the strategic shift to data unless a clear, funded migration path exists, which is not stated.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Integration fails because the technologies cannot be unified without replacing the target's entire base, causing churn among the very business customers being bought. The $36B price becomes a sunk cost in stranded legacy equipment.
4.  **Blocker Test:** The proposal states the networks are "incompatible" and the target is "limited for data." These are facts, not risks. Paying a premium for assets that cannot be integrated with the core future-proof network and lack the primary growth vector (data) is a fundamental economic flaw. The "high-value customer base" is a risk if the service they rely on must be dismantled to achieve synergies.

**Verdict:** The plan lacks a stated edge to overcome the incompatibility and data limitations. It bets $36B on a legacy technology in a data-driven market without a viable integration mechanism. The existence of a cheaper alternative (partnership/MVNO) that captures the customer base without the balance sheet poison confirms the rejection.

DECISION: NO-GO
