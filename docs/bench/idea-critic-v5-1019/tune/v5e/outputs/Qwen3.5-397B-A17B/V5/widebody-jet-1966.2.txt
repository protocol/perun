First call: NO-GO. The proposal relies on a single customer order to justify an unprecedented capital outlay while facing an existential technological threat (supersonic replacement) and an unproven core component (engine). Need alone is not an edge; the stated facts show no diversified demand to absorb the risk if the sole airline cancels or if supersonic tech renders subsonic giants obsolete.

Steelman: Building a massive, convertible freighter jet now secures a long-term logistics monopoly and locks in the dominant carrier before competitors can react.

Attack:
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves capital while the supersonic question resolves. A "stretch" of existing models offers incremental capacity without factory-level risk. The proposal fails to prove why a 2.5x jump is superior to staged growth given the supersonic uncertainty.
2.  **Fit:** If supersonic travel becomes standard within the development cycle, this subsonic behemoth becomes a stranded asset before its first decade ends. The proposal admits this risk but offers no mitigation other than freighter conversion, which assumes cargo demand will offset lost passenger viability.
3.  **Evidence:** The engine is unproven. Relying on a supplier for a critical, novel component without a backup or proven prototype introduces a single point of failure that could ground the entire program.
4.  **Pre-mortem:** The program fails because the single anchor customer defaults during development, or supersonic flights capture the premium routes, leaving the huge jet with no economic niche. The factory debt bankrupts the firm.

Blocker: The combination of a single customer order and the credible threat of technological obsolescence (supersonic) means the financial math does not work as proposed. The upside is capped by one buyer; the downside is total insolvency. A cheaper, smaller alternative (stretching current jets) reaches similar immediate capacity gains without the existential bet.

Risks: Engine failure, supersonic displacement, cargo conversion viability. These are not merely risks; given the single order, they are blockers to a go decision.

DECISION: NO-GO
