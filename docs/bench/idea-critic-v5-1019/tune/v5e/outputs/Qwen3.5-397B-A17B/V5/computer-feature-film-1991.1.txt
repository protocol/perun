Options analysis confirms that "do nothing" leaves the company vulnerable to talent poaching without growth, while independent production is financially impossible given rendering costs. The studio deal is the only option that unlocks the upside.

Fit: This moves the north star from "short-form innovator" to "industry pioneer," aligning with the vision of proving computer animation as a legitimate medium for feature narratives.

The cost of delay is high, as the lead animator is actively being recruited by competitors; waiting allows the talent to leave.

Evidence: The offer itself is proof of market validation for the talent. The lack of prior features is a gap, but the short-form portfolio serves as the proxy evidence for capability. No internal data contradicts the feasibility; the risks are inherent to the industry, not specific flaws in this plan.
