First call: NO-GO. The proposal cites slowing traffic but offers no stated edge—no evidence that favoring publishers over the core community's voting mechanics will reverse the trend or beat the growing rival. Need alone is not an edge.

Steelman: A full rewrite with publisher-focused surfacing will re-engage lapsed users and attract new traffic, outweighing community friction.

Attack:
1. **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves the loyal base while testing small changes. A "cheaper alternative" is A/B testing the new surfacing algorithm on a subset of traffic before a rewrite. The proposal ignores these, choosing maximum cost and risk for unproven upside.
2. **Fit:** The core community values existing voting mechanics. Favoring publishers directly conflicts with this stated value, risking an exodus of the very users providing the "loyal" foundation.
3. **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure: The rewrite launches, the core community rebels against the altered mechanics and publisher bias, traffic plummets further, and the rival captures the displaced users.
4. **Door/Delay:** This is a one-way door; moving all users at once with no rollback means immediate, irreversible damage if the hypothesis is wrong. The cost of delay is low compared to the cost of a failed total launch.
5. **Evidence:** No data supports the claim that "favoring publishers" drives growth for this specific demographic. The only fact is "slowing traffic," which the proposed solution might accelerate.

Blockers:
- **No stated edge:** The plan relies on a guess (publishers > community) without evidence it beats current dynamics or the rival.
- **Irreversible risk:** "No rollback" on a "full rewrite" violates basic operational safety for a live business.
- **Contradictory incentive:** Alienating the core value system (voting mechanics) to fix traffic caused by slowing engagement is logically unsound without validation.

Risks: Community exodus, engineering overrun, rival acceleration. These are not just risks; given the "no rollback" constraint, they are probable outcomes of a blind leap.

Kill criteria missing: No pilot, no metric for success before total migration, no fallback. The proposal demands a binary bet on an unverified assumption.

DECISION: NO-GO
