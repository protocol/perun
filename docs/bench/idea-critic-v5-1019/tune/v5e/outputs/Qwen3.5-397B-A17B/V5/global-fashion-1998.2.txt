First call: NO-GO. The proposal states a massive need but offers no stated edge (cost, product, access, timing, or evidence) explaining why this plan beats existing local buyers or why simultaneous global launch is superior to a staged approach.

**Steelman:** Simultaneous global launch captures maximum market share before competitors react, leveraging venture capital to build an insurmountable brand presence in fashion-conscious demographics across Europe and America immediately.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves capital. A "single-market pilot" (e.g., UK only) offers the same upside potential with drastically lower logistics complexity, reduced marketing burn, and a proven feedback loop before scaling. The proposal fails to explain why the cheaper, safer alternative cannot reach the same upside.
2.  **Fit & Evidence:** The claim targets "young, fashion-minded buyers" but admits online fashion buying is "unproven." Launching in multiple countries simultaneously multiplies the risk of this unproven behavior without providing evidence that cross-border demand exists. The risks listed (logistics costs, delay, cash burn) are not merely possibilities; they are mathematical certainties given the scope.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 18 months. Cause: Venture capital ran out while logistics networks in five countries were underutilized because customer acquisition costs were 3x higher than projected due to fragmented marketing efforts. The "one-way door" of signing leases and hiring staff in multiple jurisdictions creates fixed costs that cannot be reversed when sales lag.
4.  **Blocker:** The proposal's own facts state that "online fashion buying is unproven" and "venture money may run out." Launching everywhere at once maximizes the burn rate while testing an unproven hypothesis. This contradicts the core requirement of survival. A cheaper alternative (single country) reaches the same learning upside without the existential cash-flow risk.

**Blockers:**
*   Unproven market demand combined with maximum possible fixed costs (multi-country offices/logistics) guarantees cash depletion before product-market fit is verified.
*   No stated edge justifies the complexity penalty of simultaneous launch over a staged rollout.

**Risks:** (All elevated to blockers due to lack of mitigation) Logistics failure, marketing inefficiency, total capital loss.

DECISION: NO-GO
