**Blockers:**
*   **No Stated Edge:** Need alone is not an edge. The proposal does not demonstrate why this specific $1B price beats the alternative of rapid internal iteration or smaller tuck-ins, given the target has no revenue moat.
*   **Self-Contradiction:** The plan risks destroying the culture that makes the target valuable, while simultaneously relying on that value to save the buyer's mobile strategy.

**Risks:** Regulatory blockade, mass exodus of talent post-acquisition, integration failure during IPO quiet period, setting a precedent of overpaying for user growth without monetization.

The absence of a business model and the extreme valuation relative to any tangible asset constitute a failure of the basic "beat the alternative" test. The risk of cultural destruction is a known negative that the proposal acknowledges but does not solve.
