First call: NO-GO. The proposal states a massive need but offers no stated edge (cost, product, access, or timing advantage) over existing local retailers to justify beating them, while explicitly admitting unproven demand and unlimited cost multiplication.

Steelman: Simultaneous global launch captures first-mover scale in fashion before incumbents react, using VC fuel to outspend local logistics constraints.

Options:
1. **Proposal**: High cost, extreme risk (logistics/cash burn), low reversibility (leased offices/hiring).
2. **Do Nothing**: Zero cost, zero risk, forfeits potential scale.
3. **Single-Market Pilot**: Moderate cost, contained risk, high reversibility; proves the "unproven" online model before scaling.

Fit: Aligns with the "largest in the world" vision but ignores the operational reality of 1998 logistics and unproven consumer behavior.

Pre-mortem: Failure occurs when VC runs out during complex multi-country logistics setup before unit economics are proven in even one market. This is a one-way door for capital; delay costs only opportunity, not survival.

Evidence: The proposal itself admits "online fashion buying is unproven" and that running many countries "multiplies logistics and marketing costs." No evidence suggests founders have solved these specific 1998 constraints.

Blockers:
1. **Unproven Core Assumption**: The proposal admits the fundamental activity (online fashion buying) is unproven. Scaling an unproven model globally multiplies the probability of total failure, not success.
2. **Resource Mismatch**: Simultaneous launch multiplies costs against finite VC funds which "may run out." The math of burning cash in multiple unproven markets simultaneously guarantees insolvency before traction.

Risks: Logistics delays, marketing inefficiency, cultural mismatches in fashion.

Kill Check: The proposal states "online fashion buying is unproven." This is not a risk; it is a stated fact that blocks a global rollout. A global launch requires proven demand to justify the multiplied costs. Since the proposal admits demand is unproven, the global scale is a blocker. The cheaper alternative (single-market pilot) reaches the same upside (validation) without the existential cost risk.

DECISION: NO-GO
