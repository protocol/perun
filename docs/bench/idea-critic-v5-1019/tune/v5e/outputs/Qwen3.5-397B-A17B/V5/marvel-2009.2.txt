First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; paying a $4B premium for a short, unproven film slate and encumbered characters offers no immediate advantage over existing market options.

**Steelman:** Acquiring the publisher secures long-term IP ownership to fix the buyer's demographic gap, betting that internal film production will eventually outperform current licensing deals despite short-term constraints.

**Attack:**
1.  **Valuation Mismatch:** The $4B price targets future film potential, yet the core asset (characters) is locked in long-term rival deals, and the new film division has only one data point. Paying for "potential" while blocked from exploiting it immediately is speculative, not strategic.
2.  **Integration Friction:** Creative-control disputes are flagged as a risk. Buying a creator-led studio to fix a demographic gap often fails if the acquirer imposes corporate oversight, destroying the very value sought.
3.  **Cyclical Exposure:** Comics-based films are cyclical. Entering at a peak price with an unproven internal slate maximizes downside risk.
4.  **Better Way:** Licensing specific boy-centric characters for targeted projects is cheaper, reversible, and avoids the $4B capital outlay and integration risk while the film slate matures.

**Pre-mortem:** The acquisition closes, but rival licensing deals prevent new merchandise or films for key characters for years. The internal film studio misses on the next two releases due to creative clashes. The $4B spend drains cash reserves, forcing asset fire sales during a genre downturn.

**Blockers:**
*   **No Stated Edge:** The proposal admits weak reach and unproven films but offers no fact showing this specific target beats "do nothing" or "selective licensing" today. Need without an edge is a NO-GO.
*   **Encumbrances:** Long-term rival deals negate the immediate utility of the IP, contradicting the urgency of a $4B spend.

**Risks:** Creative clashes, cyclical downturn, film slate failure. These become kill criteria if the deal proceeds, but the lack of a current edge blocks it now.

DECISION: NO-GO
