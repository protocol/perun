First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; matching the rival's price with a smaller screen, untested OS, and no native email removes the company's core advantage in business communications.

Steelman: A 7-inch device creates a new "phablet" category leveraging a unique OS, betting that portability outweighs app scarcity and that phone-pairing mitigates the lack of native PIM apps.

Options:
1. **Proposed Plan:** High cost, high risk (untested OS, no apps), low reversibility (brand damage). Fails to beat "do nothing" on business utility.
2. **Do Nothing:** Maintain dominance with phones; wait for OS maturity. Zero execution risk.
3. **Alternative:** Launch with native email via cloud sync or immediate port, even if delayed. Without this, the value proposition collapses for the target base.

Fit: Contradicts the "business email leader" north star by shipping a business device without built-in email.

Pre-mortem: The device fails because enterprise IT refuses to deploy hardware lacking native PIM, developers ignore the tiny install base, and consumers reject the price-to-screen ratio compared to the rival. This is a one-way door with high brand erosion.

Evidence: The proposal admits the OS is untested, the app store is missing, and email requires pairing. These are not risks; they are confirmed deficits in the current plan. The price parity with a superior (larger, established) rival product is a factual blocker.

Blockers:
1. **No Stated Edge:** Price matches rival, but screen is smaller and ecosystem is weaker.
2. **Core Feature Missing:** Shipping without built-in email directly contradicts the company's primary market strength.
3. **Untested Platform:** No evidence developers will support a new OS without an installed base.

Risks: Developer adoption, consumer confusion.

Kill Check: The proposal states users must pair with a phone for email. For a standalone tablet pitch, this is a functional failure, not a risk. The price parity with a better incumbent makes the value proposition negative.

DECISION: NO-GO
