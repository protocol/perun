First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge: high price, unproven demand, regulatory ambiguity, and social friction offer no fact-based advantage over existing transport for the mass market.

Steelman: A novel, self-balancing transporter leveraging brand prestige to create a new premium micro-mobility category before competitors.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" costs $0 and avoids regulatory battles. A cheaper, staged pilot (e.g., 500 units for institutional use only) tests the "unproven" utility and legal status without committing to a 40,000-unit factory. The proposed full-scale launch fails to beat the safer, cheaper alternative on stated facts.
2.  **Fit:** The risks (legal status, conspicuousness, limited utility) directly contradict a successful mass-market launch. Without evidence that these negatives are resolved or irrelevant, the plan assumes success where the proposal admits uncertainty.
3.  **Evidence:** The proposal itself admits demand is "unproven" and legal status "unclear." Committing to a large factory based on publicity rather than verified orders or regulatory clearance is a factual contradiction. The $5,000 price point is a barrier, not an edge, given the lack of proven unique value.
4.  **Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs because regulators ban sidewalk use, early adopters feel foolish, and the niche is too small to fill a 40,000-unit capacity, leaving massive stranded assets.
5.  **Blocker:** The proposal explicitly states core prerequisites (legal clarity, proven demand) are missing. Building a large factory before resolving these is a definitive failure condition inherent in the plan.

**Blockers:**
*   Unproven demand paired with a fixed 40,000-unit capacity commitment.
*   Unclear legal status preventing defined use cases.
*   No stated edge overcoming the high price and social friction.

**Risks:**
*   Regulatory bans.
*   Social stigma limiting adoption.
*   Limited utility restricting market size.

The plan commits massive capital to unverified assumptions. A capped pilot is the only logical first step; the current proposal is a blocker.

DECISION: NO-GO
