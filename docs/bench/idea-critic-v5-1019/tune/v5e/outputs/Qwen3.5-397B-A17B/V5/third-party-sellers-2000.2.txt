First call: NO-GO. An unprofitable retailer inviting competitors to undercut its own inventory on the same page destroys its only path to margin without a stated mechanism to capture sufficient fee volume to offset the loss of direct sales control.

**Steelman:** The platform becomes the definitive catalog for all conditions of a product, capturing total market liquidity and earning fees on transactions it would otherwise lose to other channels, while monetizing long-tail inventory it cannot stock itself.

**Attack:**
1.  **Cannibalization vs. Fees:** The proposal admits used copies cannibalize new sales. For an unprofitable seller, losing a high-margin new sale to a low-fee used sale is an immediate cash-flow negative. The math only works if the fee rate exceeds the margin lost on the displaced new unit, which is unlikely given the need to attract sellers with competitive rates.
2.  **Trust & Control:** The retailer's brand is its only asset. Allowing competitors and unknown sellers to fulfill orders on its pages introduces variable service quality. One bad experience (counterfeit, late delivery) tarnishes the host's reputation, driving customers away from the core business entirely.
3.  **Supplier Retaliation:** Publishers and suppliers, already wary of the retailer's margins, will view this as direct competition using their own product data. They may withhold stock or demand better terms, strangling the primary inventory source.
4.  **No Edge:** The proposal offers no "stated edge" (e.g., exclusive traffic, superior fulfillment integration) that forces sellers to accept these terms over building their own channels or using existing auction sites. Without an edge, the best sellers won't join, and the worst will dominate.

**Blockers:**
*   **Financial Suicide:** The core unit economics are undefined. If a used sale displaces a new sale, the net revenue impact is (Fee - Lost Margin). For an unprofitable company, preserving gross margin is critical; this model structurally erodes it.
*   **Brand Hazard:** There is no proposed filter or guarantee mechanism to prevent trust erosion.

**Risks:** Supplier revolt, customer confusion, operational complexity. These are secondary to the fundamental flaw: the plan monetizes the decline of the core business.

**Kill Criteria:** Not applicable as the decision is No-Go. The premise that "more listings equal more profit" fails when the listings actively displace the host's only profitable units.

DECISION: NO-GO
