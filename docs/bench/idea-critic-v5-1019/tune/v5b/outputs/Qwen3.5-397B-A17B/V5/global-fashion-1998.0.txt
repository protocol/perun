First call: NO-GO. The plan attempts to solve an unproven demand hypothesis (online fashion buying) with maximum operational complexity (multi-continent launch) and maximum capital burn, creating a single point of failure where logistics or funding delays kill the entire venture before product-market fit is established.

**Steelman:** Simultaneous global launch captures first-mover advantage across key wealthy demographics before competitors emerge, leveraging VC scale to dominate branding instantly.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves capital. A single-country pilot (e.g., UK or Germany) tests the core mechanism (logistics + online trust) at 10% of the cost. If the model fails locally, global expansion guarantees catastrophic loss. If it succeeds, expansion is funded by proof, not hope. The proposal ignores Chesterton's Fence: the friction of cross-border logistics is a barrier to entry only if the domestic model works first.
2.  **Fit:** The North Star is "largest retailer," but the path ignores the stage. At 1998 internet penetration levels, building trust is the bottleneck, not geographic coverage. Spreading thin across cultures dilutes the brand message needed to overcome skepticism.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 18 months. Cause: Cash ran out while fixing warehouse errors in three time zones simultaneously, with no single market generating enough volume to negotiate shipping rates or prove unit economics. The "unproven" nature of the market means every country presents unique, unpredictable regulatory and consumer behavior hurdles that a centralized team cannot solve in parallel.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal admits "online fashion buying is unproven" and "venture money may run out." These are not risks to be managed; they are contradictions to the strategy. You cannot test an unproven behavior with the most expensive possible experimental setup.

**Blockers:**
*   **Contradictory Facts:** The plan relies on unproven demand while executing the highest-cost validation strategy available. The stated risk of running out of money is guaranteed by the proposed burn rate before any learning occurs.
*   **Superior Alternative:** A staged rollout achieves the same upside (global dominance) with drastically lower ruin risk. The current plan offers no unique upside that justifies the existential risk; it only offers faster ruin.

**Decision:** The proposal fails the kill check. The core claim (simultaneous launch is necessary/viable) is contradicted by the proposal's own admission of unproven demand and limited runway.

DECISION: NO-GO
