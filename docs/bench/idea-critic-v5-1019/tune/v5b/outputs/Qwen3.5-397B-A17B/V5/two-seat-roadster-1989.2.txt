First call: GO. The market gap for an affordable, lightweight roadster is distinct, and the proposed specs directly address the void left by disappearing competitors.

**Steelman:** A focused, low-overhead product exploiting a neglected niche with high enthusiast demand, using existing mechanicals to minimize R&D risk.

**Attack & Options:**
The primary objection is the shrinking segment. However, this shrinkage reflects the exit of competitors, creating a monopoly-like opportunity for the sole remaining player rather than proof of zero demand. "Do nothing" guarantees zero revenue from this demographic. Building a heavy, complex GT car increases cost and misses the "lightweight" value proposition. The proposed front-engine, rear-drive layout leverages standard parts, avoiding the exotic costs that typically kill convertibles.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
Assuming the goal is brand revitalization and margin growth, this fits by creating a halo effect without halo pricing. If this fails, it will likely be due to over-engineering the safety features or pricing it above "affordable." The risk of fading sales after the initial enthusiast wave is real but manageable; the low development cost (relative to a new platform) ensures profitability even with a shorter product lifecycle.

**Evidence & Blockers:**
The proposal relies on the factual premise that competitors have exited. No internal data contradicts the feasibility of adapting existing drivetrains. There are no blockers: the lack of heritage is an asset (novelty), not a liability. Safety costs are a known variable, not an unknown blocker.

**Kill Criteria:**
Proceed only if development costs remain capped at 50% of a standard new model program. Stop if pre-orders do not cover tooling costs within six months of reveal.

**Verdict:**
The risks are executional, not fundamental. The market void is the strongest signal. The plan attacks the right problem with the right constraints.

Blockers: NONE
Risks: Market size miscalculation, cost overrun on safety compliance.

DECISION: GO
