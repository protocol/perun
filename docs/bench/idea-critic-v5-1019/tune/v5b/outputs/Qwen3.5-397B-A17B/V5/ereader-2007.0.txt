First call: GO. The proposal solves the core friction of digital books (instant access without a PC) and aligns with the company's catalog strength, despite hardware risks.

**Steelman:** A dedicated, cellular-enabled device removes purchase friction, unlocking latent demand for digital books that existing solutions failed to capture due to complexity and lack of connectivity.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
1.  **Do Nothing:** Cedes the digital future to competitors; print sales will eventually decline regardless.
2.  **Software Only:** Requires users to own PCs/laptops, maintaining the adoption barrier that killed earlier e-readers.
3.  **Hardware Risk:** Valid, but outsourcing manufacturing mitigates the lack of internal experience. The "paper-like" screen is the critical differentiator for long-form reading.
4.  **Cannibalization:** Inevitable but preferable to losing the customer entirely to a rival ecosystem. Margins shift from unit sales to lifetime content value.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the device is too expensive, the screen is poor, or publishers withhold content. The "free cellular" model is a high-cost bet that must be recouped via book sales; if attachment rates are low, this bleeds cash. However, the proposal's "single low price" directly addresses publisher piracy fears and consumer confusion.

**Evidence & Fit:** The proposal correctly identifies that earlier failures stemmed from poor UX (requiring a PC) and limited connectivity. The "paper-like" claim is assumed viable based on emerging tech trends. The risk of hardware inexperience is real but surmountable via partners.

**Blockers:** None. The stated risks (hardware experience, margin squeeze, cannibalization) are execution challenges, not factual contradictions. The market has not yet been served effectively; the "no buyers" history reflects bad products, not zero demand.

**Kill Criteria:** If the device cannot secure content from major publishers before launch, or if the unit cost exceeds the ability to subsidize connectivity via projected book margins, the project must stop.

**Decision:** The potential to own the entire digital reading value chain outweighs the operational stretch. The cellular feature is the killer app that justifies the hardware bet.

DECISION: GO
