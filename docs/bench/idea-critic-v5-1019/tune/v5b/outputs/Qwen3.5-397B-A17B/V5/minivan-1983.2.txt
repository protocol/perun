First call: GO. The proposal leverages existing assets (compact platform, converted factory) to address a clear market gap (garage-friendly family vehicle) created by a shift away from large vans, despite cash constraints.

**Steelman:** A capital-efficient launch of a novel "minivan" category using proven compact-car mechanics to offer car-like handling in a garage-fit package, rescuing market share from bulky traditional vans.

**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Remain dependent on declining large van sales; high risk of continued cash burn.
2.  **New large platform:** Prohibitively expensive given cash constraints; slow time-to-market.
3.  **Proposed approach:** Reuses existing R&D and tooling; fastest path to revenue with lowest capex.

**Fit:** Aligns with survival (cash preservation) and innovation (creating a new segment).

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if buyers reject the "compact" stigma, viewing it as a cheap commercial van rather than a family vehicle. However, the "car-like drive" differentiator mitigates this. It is a two-way door; if it fails, production can cease with minimal sunk cost compared to a clean-sheet design. Delay costs market first-mover advantage against rivals who may eventually copy the concept.

**Evidence:** The government loan guarantees survival capital. The compact platform exists. The factory conversion is feasible. Risks regarding category existence are actually opportunities for definition; the lack of competition confirms the white space.

**Blockers:** NONE. The cash constraint is solved by reusing existing platforms rather than developing new ones. The "commercial van" perception is a marketing challenge, not a structural blocker, addressed by positioning it as a family station wagon alternative.

**Risks:** Competitor copying (mitigated by first-mover brand association); platform space limits (accepted trade-off for garage fit).

**Kill Criteria:** If pre-orders or initial clinic testing show >60% of families perceive the vehicle strictly as a "work truck" despite marketing, pivot messaging or halt. Leading metric: Sales mix of family vs. commercial buyers in Q1.

The proposal turns constraints (cash, existing platform) into the very features (low cost, car handling) that define the new category.

DECISION: GO
