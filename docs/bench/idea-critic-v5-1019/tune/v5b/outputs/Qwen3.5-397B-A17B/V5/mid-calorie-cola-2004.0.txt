First call: NO-GO. The core claim—that a mid-calorie option captures value between sugar and diet—contradicts the stated risk that calorie-conscious consumers already migrate fully to diet, leaving the "half-sugar" niche as a solution for a problem no one has.

Steelman: A premium mid-calorie cola captures young men who reject the "diet" label but want fewer carbs than regular soda, creating a new category before rivals do.

Attack:
1.  **Fit & Demand:** The proposal admits the target audience (low-carb dieters) already buys diet cola. A half-sugar drink satisfies neither the taste seeker (who wants full sugar) nor the dieter (who wants zero). It risks cannibalizing full-sugar sales without converting diet drinkers.
2.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" allows the diet segment to grow naturally without brand confusion. A cheaper alternative is reformulating existing lines or limited-time tests rather than a full premium launch with a large campaign.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 12 months. Cause: The low-carb trend proves a fad (as suspected), leaving a premium product with no enduring demand. The rival's similar launch suggests a crowded, unproven space, not validation.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal itself lists "low-carb trend may be a fad" and "half-sugar may satisfy neither group" as facts. These are not mere risks; they are fundamental flaws in the value proposition. The premium price exacerbates the trial barrier for a product with an unclear benefit.

Blockers:
*   **Contradictory Value:** The stated fact that dieters already buy diet cola blocks the premise that they need a mid-calorie option.
*   **Trend Volatility:** Basing a major premium launch on a trend explicitly identified as a potential fad is a strategic failure.

Risks:
*   Cannibalization of core sugar soda.
*   Rival saturation in the niche.

The plan fails the "beat do nothing" test because the stated evidence suggests the target market is either non-existent or already served better by existing diet options. The "half-sugar" position is a compromise that solves no urgent pain point.

DECISION: NO-GO
