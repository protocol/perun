First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits to high fixed costs (new plant, dedicated network) before validating demand against entrenched rivals and uncertain buyer preferences.

**Steelman:** A dedicated 1100cc compact family car will capture sufficient market share to utilize the new plant and justify the exclusive dealer network investment despite low-price competition.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" or utilizing existing networks avoids the massive sunk cost of a dedicated dealer network. If the car fails, the dedicated network becomes a stranded asset; shared distribution limits downside.
2.  **Fit & Risk:** The core contradiction is building a *dedicated* network for a product where buyers "may prefer cheaper or larger." Without proof of distinct demand, this channel strategy inflates the break-even point beyond what the new plant's capacity utilization can sustain.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 18 months. Cause: The dedicated network failed to generate enough volume to cover its operating costs because the 1100cc segment was either too price-sensitive (buyers chose cheaper rivals) or too practical (buyers chose larger cars). The high fixed cost of the standalone network prevented profitability at moderate sales volumes.
4.  **Evidence Gap:** The proposal offers no data showing buyers specifically want a *new* brand/channel for this engine size. It assumes the plant must run at capacity immediately, forcing a high-risk sales target on an unproven channel.

**Blockers:**
*   **Economic Mismatch:** The requirement for a "dedicated dealer network" creates a cost structure that likely exceeds the margin potential of a "small car" sold against "rivals... at low prices." The math implies a loss unless market share is near-total, which contradicts the risk of buyer preference shifts.
*   **Unproven Demand:** Proceeding with full infrastructure (plant + network) while admitting buyers may prefer other segments is a capital allocation error.

**Risks:** Competitor price wars; shift to larger vehicles; underutilized plant.

**Kill Criteria:** If a pilot using existing channels cannot secure 5% segment share within 6 months, halt the dedicated network build.

**Decision:** The commitment to a dedicated network before validating the specific 1100cc demand against price and size preferences is a fatal financial flaw. The plan must be revised to test distribution via existing channels first.

DECISION: NO-GO
