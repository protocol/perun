First call: GO. The shift in buyer demographics toward American-brand customers seeking smaller sedans represents a clear market gap that the current lineup ignores; capturing this segment with a shared-platform vehicle maximizes margin while minimizing development risk.

**Steelman:** Leveraging an existing mass-market compact platform allows immediate entry into the growing small-luxury segment with superior unit economics, provided the premium trim successfully masks the shared underpinnings and protects the master brand's exclusivity.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
The primary failure mode is brand dilution if buyers perceive the car as a "badge-engineered" economy model. However, doing nothing cedes the emerging compact-luxury category to rivals who are already downsizing. Developing a clean-sheet small luxury platform is prohibitively expensive and slow, missing the current demand window. The only viable alternative is acquiring a smaller brand, which lacks the speed and margin control of internal development.

**Pre-mortem & Evidence:**
If this fails, it will be because sales channels refused to sell it or the pricing failed to distance it from the mass-market donor car. The proposal explicitly identifies these risks (salesperson behavior, price justification, platform visibility). These are execution risks, not fundamental flaws. The "shared platform" is a feature for profitability, not a bug, provided the trim differentiation is absolute. The risk to prestige is mitigated by maintaining a distinct model name and ensuring the vehicle is never marketed as an entry-point to the *existing* large sedans, but as a distinct product for a different buyer.

**Blockers vs. Risks:**
There are no factual blockers in the proposal. The stated facts show a demand shift the company cannot currently meet. The risks listed (perception, salesforce alignment, pricing) are manageable through training, marketing separation, and feature loading. No alternative reaches the same upside (immediate market capture with high margins) cheaper or faster.

**Decision:**
The strategic fit is precise: serve the new buyer without cannibalizing the core European owner base. The cost of delay is losing these customers to competitors permanently. The plan works if the differentiation is executed rigorously.

Blockers: NONE
Risks: Brand dilution, salesforce resistance, price justification (managed via kill criteria on sales mix and brand sentiment surveys).

DECISION: GO
