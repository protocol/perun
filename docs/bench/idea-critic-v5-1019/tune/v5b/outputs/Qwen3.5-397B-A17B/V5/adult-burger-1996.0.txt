**Steelman:** A massive ad spend can forcibly reposition a family brand as premium overnight, overcoming deep-seated consumer associations through sheer volume.

**Attack:**
1.  **Fit:** The strategy clashes directly with the "child-centered image." Adults do not visit this chain for premium dining; they visit for value and nostalgia. Repositioning risks alienating the core family demographic without guaranteeing adult adoption.
2.  **Evidence & Logic:** The proposal admits adults see the chain as "fast, not premium." No amount of advertising instantly erases years of brand conditioning. The "largest budget in industry history" amplifies a potentially flawed message rather than validating the product.
3.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves current margins. A "better way" is a limited regional pilot with a modest budget to test actual adult demand before committing industry-leading capital.
4.  **Pre-mortem:** The launch fails because families stop coming (feeling the brand isn't for them) and adults still don't buy (perceiving the high price as unjustified for the brand). The company burns cash on ads for a product nobody wants.
5.  **Blocker:** The plan commits to the "largest advertising budget" *before* demand is shown. This violates basic capital allocation logic for an unproven hypothesis in a hostile brand environment. The risk is not just failure; it is brand dilution and massive financial loss with no off-ramp.

**Decision Method:**
*   **Blockers:** Yes. Committing maximum capital to an unproven shift in core brand perception, ignoring stated risks that the price and image deter the target, constitutes a fatal flaw. The proposal offers no pilot, cap, or kill criteria, only a "go big or go home" gamble.
*   **Risks:** High probability of alienating the existing base and failing to convert the new target.

The proposal demands immediate, irreversible scale on a hypothesis that contradicts the brand's established reality. It lacks a staged approach to validate if adults will actually pay a premium at this specific chain.
