First call: NO-GO. The proposal bets the company's reputation on unproven consumer willingness to pay for online play in a market dominated by free alternatives, while lacking the operational backbone to deliver it.

Steelman: A unified, premium online ecosystem creates superior user retention and recurring revenue, differentiating the console through quality and community features that fragmented, free services cannot match.

However, the risks are not merely hypothetical; they are structural blockers based on the facts provided.
1. **Market Readiness:** With "few homes having broadband," the total addressable market is too small to sustain the high fixed costs of hosting and support operations. A mandatory subscription further shrinks this pool.
2. **Competitive Disadvantage:** Rivals offering free online play set a price expectation of zero. Introducing a paid wall without a proven track record of operational excellence invites immediate rejection.
3. **Operational Inexperience:** The company has "never run" hosting or support operations. Building this from scratch while launching a hardware product creates a massive execution risk. If the service fails at launch due to instability, it damages the core console brand.

Alternatives:
- **Do Nothing/Status Quo:** Allow publishers to handle online play individually. Cost: Low. Risk: Fragmented experience. Reversibility: High.
- **Hybrid Model:** Launch the infrastructure but make it optional or free for the first year to build the user base before monetizing. Cost: High initial burn. Risk: Delayed revenue. Reversibility: Medium.
- **Proposed Plan:** Mandatory paid subscription. Cost: High. Risk: Market rejection. Reversibility: Low (brand damage).

The proposed plan does not beat "Do Nothing" or a "Hybrid Model" because it forces a premium price on an infrastructure the company cannot yet reliably operate, for a market that barely exists. The lack of operational experience is a fatal flaw when combined with a paid mandate; failure in the first month is the most likely pre-mortem outcome. There is no evidence provided that players will pay for a service the company has no history of running.

Blockers: Lack of operational experience combined with a mandatory paywall in a nascent broadband market creates a high probability of service failure and brand damage.
Risks: Low broadband adoption, price sensitivity, execution failure.

DECISION: NO-GO
