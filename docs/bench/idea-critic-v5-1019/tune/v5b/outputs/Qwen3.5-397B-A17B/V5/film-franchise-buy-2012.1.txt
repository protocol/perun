The "mixed reviews" of recent entries suggest the brand is undervalued, not dead; a course correction often yields disproportionate upside. The lack of current tools prevents verifying exact licensing expiration dates, but the proposal states much value is *already* licensed out, implying ownership is the only path to recapture that margin.

*Blockers:* NONE. No fact presented proves the franchise is legally unsalable or that the seller refuses the offer structure.
