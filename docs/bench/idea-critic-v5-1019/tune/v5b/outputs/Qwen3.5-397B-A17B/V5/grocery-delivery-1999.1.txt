First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits irreversible capital before validating the core hypothesis that unit economics can be positive in a single market.

**Steelman:** Rapid nationwide warehouse construction creates an insurmountable moat via density and speed, preempting competitors before they can react.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" beyond the first city allows learning unit economics with minimal exposure. A "lighter" alternative using existing retail space or smaller hubs offers similar speed with 10% of the capital risk. The proposed $1B spend beats neither on risk-adjusted return given the unknown margins.
2.  **Fit:** The strategy contradicts the stated reality of "no operating track record" and "low margins." Scaling a broken or unproven model amplifies losses, not value.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs because customer density in new cities proves insufficient to fill expensive automated slots, leaving fixed costs uncovered while cash runs out. This is a one-way door; once built, warehouses cannot be easily undone or repurposed without massive loss.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal admits zero data on unit economics. Committing $1B without knowing if a single order generates profit is speculation, not operations. The risks listed (capital intensity, thin margins) are not mitigated; they are accelerated.

**Blocker:** The plan requires spending $1B before verifying if the business model works in the *first* city. The absence of validated unit economics is a factual blocker for irreversible expansion. No alternative reaches the same upside cheaper because the "upside" is currently theoretical; the only verified reality is the risk of total capital loss.

**Risks:** Low margins, high capital intensity, and unproven customer density are treated as hurdles to jump rather than filters to pass. Without a pilot, these remain fatal flaws.

**Kill Criteria:** None exist because the proposal skips the testing phase entirely. A sound plan would cap spend at the first city, define a break-even metric, and only expand upon hitting it.

**Decision:** The proposal fails the blocker test. It demands irreversible commitment based on assumptions rather than evidence.

DECISION: NO-GO
