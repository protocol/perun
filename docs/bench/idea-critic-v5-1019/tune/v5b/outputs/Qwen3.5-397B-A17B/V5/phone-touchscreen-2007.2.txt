First call: GO. The proposal trades initial unit volume for control of the user experience and a new revenue stream, directly defending the core music business from erosion while creating a premium anchor.

**Steelman:** A single-carrier, exclusive launch secures the unprecedented control required to deliver a differentiated, high-margin device that integrates the company's core strengths, accepting limited initial reach to prove the model before scaling.

**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Cede the mobile market to competitors; music player sales erode as phones become the primary music device.
2.  **Licensing:** License the OS to multiple carriers. *Risk:* Loss of design control, fragmentation, and brand dilution; likely fails to differentiate.
3.  **Proposed Path:** High risk due to inexperience and exclusivity, but offers the only path to the required integration and margin structure. It beats "do nothing" by creating a defensive moat and a new growth engine.

**Fit:** Aligns with the vision of integrating hardware, software, and services. The "no keyboard" and "premium price" are features of this integration, not bugs.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the single carrier underperforms in marketing/distribution, or if the lack of a keyboard and apps makes the device unusable for the target business/prosumer market. However, the full web browser and music integration may offset these deficits for early adopters. This is a two-way door; if the exclusive launch succeeds, scaling to other carriers is trivial. If it fails, the loss is contained to one partner.

**Evidence:** The premise relies on the company's brand strength and existing music ecosystem to overcome the lack of phone experience. The revenue share model is a novel incentive for the carrier.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks (inexperience, no keyboard, exclusivity, no apps) are valid but do not constitute factual contradictions or impossible constraints based on the provided text. They are execution risks, not blockers. The "no app" constraint is a launch condition, not a permanent flaw.

**Risks:** Market rejection of the form factor; carrier partnership failure; slow adoption due to price. These become kill criteria: monitor activation rates and churn in the first quarter.

DECISION: GO
