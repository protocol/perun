First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits to high fixed costs (dedicated dealer network, new plant utilization) without evidence that the specific 1100 cc segment offers sufficient margin or volume to offset rivals' existing low-price dominance.

Steelman: A dedicated 1100 cc car sold through an exclusive network will capture enough family buyers to fill the new plant and justify the dealer investment despite competitive pressure.

Attack:
1.  **Fit & Alternatives:** The "dedicated dealer network" is a massive capital expenditure that directly contradicts the need to minimize risk while filling a new plant. A cheaper, safer alternative exists: sell the new car through the *existing* network (if any) or partner with established distributors to test demand before building costly infrastructure. Building a network from scratch assumes immediate, guaranteed volume that rivals already fighting on price may prevent.
2.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 18 months. Cause: The dealer network burned cash due to low footfall, while buyers either chose cheaper rivals or traded up to larger cars, leaving the new plant underutilized. The "dedicated" constraint prevented flexible distribution.
3.  **Evidence Gap:** The proposal states risks (costly network, rival pricing, buyer preference uncertainty) but offers no data showing why this specific engine size wins against cheaper or larger options. It asserts a solution (new network) that amplifies the stated risks rather than mitigating them.
4.  **Blocker:** The commitment to a *dedicated* dealer network before validating demand is a factual blocker. It locks in high operating costs immediately, making the venture insolvent if sales ramp-up is slow—a likely scenario given the "buyers may prefer cheaper or larger" risk. The plan fails as proposed because it maximizes exposure to the very uncertainties it identifies.

Blockers:
-   Commitment to a costly dedicated dealer network prior to demand validation, ignoring cheaper distribution alternatives.
Risks:
-   Price war with established rivals.
-   Mismatch between 1100 cc offering and buyer preference for cheaper/larger cars.
-   Plant underutilization due to slow sales ramp.

Kill Criteria: None proposed; none can be added without revising the fundamental distribution strategy. The plan requires revision to decouple plant utilization from immediate network construction.

DECISION: NO-GO
