First call: NO-GO. Simultaneous transatlantic launch with physical offices in 1998 ignores proven unit economics and capital constraints for unproven online fashion markets.

**Steelman:** Aggressive global scaling captures first-mover advantage before incumbents react, leveraging VC fuel to dominate the nascent online fashion sector immediately.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves capital but cedes ground. A single-market pilot (e.g., UK or Germany only) offers lower cost, reduced logistics complexity, and faster iteration on the unproven "online fashion" behavior. The proposal fails to beat this cheaper alternative; it multiplies risk without guaranteeing proportional speed.
2.  **Fit:** The vision is "largest retailer," but the strategy assumes infinite capital elasticity. In 1998, logistics infrastructure was fragmented; managing returns and delivery across multiple continents simultaneously creates a high probability of operational collapse before product-market fit is verified.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 18 months. Cause: Cash burn rate from multi-country office leases and fragmented marketing spend exceeded customer lifetime value. Logistics nightmares in three time zones degraded customer experience, killing retention. Venture funding dried up due to missed milestones caused by over-extension.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal admits online fashion buying is "unproven." Scaling an unproven model across multiple jurisdictions is not a strategy; it is a gamble. No data supports the claim that speed of expansion outweighs the certainty of operational friction.
5.  **Blockers:** The core claim relies on venture money lasting until sales catch up. Given the admitted risks of multiplied costs and unproven demand, the capital runway required is speculative. The plan lacks a capped, time-boxed pilot to validate the model before full deployment.

**Blockers:**
*   Simultaneous launch multiplies fixed costs (offices, logistics) before validating the core assumption that users will buy fashion online.
*   No mechanism to stop bleeding cash if one region fails while dragging down others.

**Risks:**
*   Venture capital drying up prematurely.
*   Logistics failure damaging brand reputation globally.

**Kill Criteria:**
*   Metric: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs. Lifetime Value (LTV) in a single test market.
*   Threshold: If LTV < 3x CAC within 6 months in one country, halt expansion.
*   Action: Pivot to single-market dominance or shut down.

DECISION: NO-GO
