First call: GO. The proposal attacks the core weakness of current handsets (carrier control, poor web, fragmented music) by leveraging existing strengths in music and design, accepting high price and limited reach as necessary trade-offs for control.

**Steelman:** A premium, carrier-controlled device integrating a proven music ecosystem and real web browsing will create a new category, justifying exclusivity and high cost to establish a foothold without diluting the brand.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Risk music player erosion is high; waiting cedes the mobile interface to competitors.
*   **Keyboard/Enterprise Focus:** Matches current market leaders but sacrifices the differentiator (touch/web) and plays on competitors' turf.
*   **Multi-Carrier Launch:** Increases reach but surrenders design control and revenue share, replicating the failed status quo.

**Fit:** Aligns with the vision of integrating music, communication, and internet into a single seamless experience. The "no keyboard" risk is the primary differentiator, not a flaw, if the touch interface works.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the touch interface is unusable, the network is too slow for the "real web" promise, or the premium price yields zero volume. Exclusivity could limit brand visibility if the single carrier underperforms.

**Evidence & Constraints:** No external data available; relying on the premise that current phones are inferior for music/web. The lack of third-party apps is a temporary constraint that must be solved post-launch.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks (no experience, premium price, no keyboard, exclusivity, no apps) are execution challenges or strategic bets, not factual contradictions. The proposal explicitly accepts these risks to gain control.
**Risks:** Market rejection of touch-only interface; carrier relationship failure; slow app ecosystem growth.

**Kill Criteria:** If initial sales do not cover the exclusivity commitment within 12 months, or if user return rates due to interface issues exceed 15%, pivot to a keyboard hybrid or exit the carrier agreement. Leading metric: Activation rate and daily music usage per device.

The lack of phone experience is mitigated by the transferable skills in hardware and software integration. The premium price targets early adopters who value the integrated experience over cost.

DECISION: GO
