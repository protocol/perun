First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits irreversible capital before validating the core hypothesis that unit economics can be positive in a single market.

**Steelman:** Secure dominant market share in 12+ cities immediately by building insurmountable infrastructure moats, betting that speed and coverage will force customer adoption despite unproven margins.

**Attack:**
1.  **Options:** The "do nothing" alternative (optimize one city) costs negligible capital while revealing true unit economics. The proposed path burns $1B on assumptions. A cheaper alternative is leasing existing warehouse space and using third-party logistics to test density requirements without fixed asset risk. The proposal fails to beat "do nothing" because it risks total capital loss on an unproven model.
2.  **Fit:** Rapid expansion contradicts the stated risk of "no operating track record." You cannot scale a process you have not yet proven works in one location.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 2001. Cause: Massive overcapacity in cities where customer density never reached the break-even point required to cover thin margins and 30-minute slot logistics. The $1B sunk cost prevents pivoting.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal admits "no operating track record" and "low-margin business." These are facts, not risks. Scaling a negative or unknown margin by 12x accelerates bankruptcy; it does not fix the math.
5.  **Blocker Test:**
    *   **Blocker:** The plan commits $1B (irreversible) based on unproven unit economics (core claim failure). The stated facts confirm the team lacks the data to justify this spend.
    *   **Risk:** Capital intensity and thin margins are cited but ignored in the execution plan.

This is a one-way door with no data. The correct move is a capped, time-boxed pilot in the current city to prove a single warehouse can generate profit before replicating it. Committing $1B now is not a strategy; it is a gamble with someone else's money.

**Kill Criteria:** If the first city cannot show positive contribution margin per order within 6 months using temporary infrastructure, the model is invalid.

DECISION: NO-GO
