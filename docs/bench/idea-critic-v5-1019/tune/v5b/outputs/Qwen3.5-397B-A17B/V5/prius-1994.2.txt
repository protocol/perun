First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits to a fixed public schedule for unproven core technologies without a demand signal or regulatory mandate, creating asymmetric downside.

**Steelman:** A bold, fixed-date commitment forces the organization to solve novel battery and transmission challenges rapidly, securing first-mover status before regulators or competitors act.

**Attack:**
1.  **Evidence Gap:** Battery durability is explicitly "unproven." Committing to mass production by 1997 risks catastrophic warranty exposure if packs fail early. A single recall could bankrupt the program.
2.  **Market Mismatch:** With cheap fuel and no mandates, consumers lack financial incentive to pay the hybrid premium. There is no near-term demand signal to absorb the high unit cost.
3.  **Better Way:** The "do nothing" or "limited pilot" option preserves capital. A softer launch (e.g., fleet-only, limited region) tests durability and demand without binding the entire corporation to a public deadline that invites failure if slipped.
4.  **Pre-mortem:** It is 1998. The car launched late, batteries are degrading faster than expected, and the high price killed sales. The public schedule made the delay a news story, damaging brand credibility.
5.  **Blocker:** The combination of unproven battery durability and a fixed public mass-production date is a factual blocker. You cannot guarantee reliability by a specific date when the technology is unproven; the plan assumes a solution exists before it is validated.

**Kill Criteria Check:** The proposal lacks a cap on financial exposure, a pilot phase to validate durability, or a pivot trigger if demand remains absent. It bets the division on a "hope" strategy rather than a verified engineering milestone.

**Decision Logic:**
*   **Blockers:** Unproven battery durability + fixed mass-production date = high probability of warranty catastrophe. No demand signal + high cost = high probability of commercial failure.
*   **Risks:** Execution difficulty, competitor response (low, as none exist), regulatory shift (low, none mandated).
*   **Verdict:** The plan fails the basic operator test: do not scale unproven technology to mass production on a fixed schedule without data. Revise to include a durability pilot and flexible timeline.

DECISION: NO-GO
