First call: NO-GO. The proposal discards the brand's primary shelf cue—the orange with a straw—without evidence that the new minimalist design preserves recognition, directly threatening sales among the stated loyal, older demographic.

Steelman: A modernized package will attract new buyers while retaining existing ones through superior brand equity, justifying the nationwide cost.

Attack: The "better way" analysis fails because the proposal ignores Chesterton's Fence: the old image exists for a functional reason (instant recognition). Removing it to introduce abstract elements (vertical text, cap shape) creates a high risk of shelf invisibility. The pre-mortem is clear: shoppers walk past, sales collapse, and the massive ad spend cannot compensate for failed point-of-sale identification. The cost of delay is negligible compared to the irreversible cost of a failed nationwide launch.

Evidence: The proposal admits the old image is the "main shelf cue" and the base is "older." These facts contradict the claim that a minimalist redesign is safe. No pilot data, A/B test, or market research is cited to prove the new design works as a cue. The risk is not theoretical; it is a direct conflict with the stated facts of consumer behavior.

Blockers:
1. The plan removes the verified primary recognition signal (orange with straw) for a demographic explicitly described as loyal and older, with no evidence the new signal works.
2. The commitment is total (nationwide, all stores at once) with no staged pilot to validate the new cue, violating basic risk management for a core asset change.

Risks: High cost of reprinting; potential brand alienation. These become kill criteria if a pilot were attempted, but the lack of a pilot is a blocker.

The proposal demands a binary, irreversible bet on an untested visual language while dismantling the proven one. A sound operator would mandate a regional pilot with sales tracking before any nationwide commitment. As proposed, this is a reckless gamble with the brand's core revenue stream.

DECISION: NO-GO
