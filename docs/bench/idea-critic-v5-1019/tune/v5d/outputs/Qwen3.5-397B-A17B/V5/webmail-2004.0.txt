First call: GO. The stated edge is a 200x storage leap (1GB vs MBs) combined with fast search, turning email from a deletion-heavy chore into a permanent, searchable archive. This fundamentally changes the product category, not just the specs.

**Steelman:** By offering unprecedented storage and search, the service captures high-value users frustrated by incumbent limits, monetizing their attention via highly relevant text ads derived from content analysis, creating a defensible moat through data scale.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Cedes the "permanent archive" segment to a competitor who will eventually lower storage costs anyway.
*   **Cheaper Alternative:** Incremental storage increases fail to solve the "delete anxiety" problem; users still hoard cautiously. Only a massive leap changes behavior.
*   **Cost/Privacy:** Storage costs were dropping rapidly in 2004; the risk is temporal, not structural. Privacy objections are real but mitigated by the "free" value exchange and automated (non-human) scanning. The incumbent complacency is the opportunity.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if ad relevance is poor (spammy) or if the cost per user exceeds ad yield before scale is reached. However, the invitation-only launch caps initial blast radius, allowing yield tuning.

**Evidence & Fit:** The proposal relies on the known trajectory of storage commoditization and the proven efficacy of text ads in search. The "mature market" argument is a fallacy; maturity often signals ripe disruption via a step-change in utility.

**Blockers vs Risks:**
*   **Blockers:** NONE. No fact presented proves the unit economics fail immediately or that users reject the trade-off.
*   **Risks:** Privacy backlash, ad relevance tuning, initial cash burn. These are execution risks, not fundamental flaws. They become kill criteria: if ad CTR < X% or cost/user > $Y by date Z, pivot or cap growth.

The plan beats "do nothing" by seizing a first-mover advantage in the "infinite inbox" category. The risks are manageable via the proposed staged rollout.

DECISION: GO
