First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; high cost and poor indoor performance cannot beat rapidly expanding, cheaper ground cellular networks for the target demographic.

**Steelman:** This is the only solution for truly remote global connectivity, capturing high-value travelers who cannot rely on terrestrial gaps.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** Ground cellular is spreading fast. A "do nothing" or "wait" strategy allows terrestrial networks to cover most business routes at a fraction of the cost and with better indoor reliability. The proposal admits handsets are bulky and fail indoors, a critical flaw for business users.
2.  **Economics:** $5 billion in debt requires massive, immediate scale. At $3,000 per handset and several dollars per minute, the addressable market is too small to service the debt before technology obsolescence or terrestrial competition renders the service irrelevant.
3.  **Execution:** Sales channels are not ready. Launching a complex, expensive hardware ecosystem without established distribution is a fatal operational gap.
4.  **Pre-mortem:** The project fails because subscriber growth lags behind debt servicing requirements, forcing bankruptcy while ground networks achieve near-ubiquitous coverage.

**Blockers:**
*   **No Competitive Edge:** The proposal admits inferior user experience (indoor failure, bulk) and higher cost compared to the rapidly improving alternative (ground cellular). Need alone is not an edge.
*   **Financial Mismatch:** The debt structure demands speed and scale that the high-price/low-convenience product cannot generate.
*   **Operational Gap:** Unready sales channels prevent the rapid uptake required to survive the debt load.

**Risks:** Technology obsolescence, regulatory hurdles, further capital calls.

**Conclusion:** The plan relies on capturing a niche that is shrinking due to terrestrial expansion, using a product with known defects, funded by a debt structure that tolerates no delay. The absence of a ready sales channel confirms the inability to execute the necessary rapid scale. This is a financial trap disguised as a technological breakthrough.

DECISION: NO-GO
