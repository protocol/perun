First call: NO-GO. The proposal states a need (inexpensive watch) but fails to provide a *stated edge* explaining why this specific plastic fashion approach beats existing cheap watches or protects the brand's quality reputation.

**Steelman:** Launching 12 plastic models as fashion items leverages trend velocity to achieve 1M units, distancing the product from core quality expectations while capturing volume.

**Attack:**
1.  **Reputation Blocker:** The proposal admits the risk of harming the company's quality reputation but offers no mechanism (e.g., sub-branding, distinct distribution) to prevent brand cannibalization. Selling "plastic" under a "quality" name is a direct contradiction without structural separation.
2.  **Evidence Gap:** The 1M unit target is asserted without market sizing, price point validation, or distribution capacity evidence. In the absence of data showing demand for *fashion* plastic watches specifically from this maker, the number is a guess.
3.  **Alternative:** A "do nothing" or "sub-brand pilot" approach is safer. Launching 12 models simultaneously maximizes exposure to the "fashion tastes change" risk. A single model pilot would validate the fashion premise with lower reputational downside.
4.  **Pre-mortem:** The launch fails because established brands cut prices below our cost, or the fashion angle is rejected by consumers who view the brand as "serious," leaving us with unsold plastic inventory and a tarnished name.

**Blockers:**
*   **Reputation Contradiction:** The plan relies on the existing brand equity to sell fashion items while simultaneously risking that very equity with "inexpensive plastic." No mitigation is proposed.
*   **Unsubstantiated Volume:** The 1M target lacks a derived basis (price x market share x distribution), making the financial viability unproven.

**Risks:** Fashion volatility, price wars, brand dilution. These are significant but secondary to the lack of a defensive strategy for the brand core.

**Conclusion:** The proposal asks for a high-risk, high-volume launch without a stated edge on *how* it succeeds where others might fail or how it protects the core asset (reputation). It assumes success rather than engineering it.

DECISION: NO-GO
