First call: NO-GO. The proposal states a massive need but offers no stated edge (cost, product, access, or timing advantage) over existing local retailers to justify beating them, especially given the unproven nature of online fashion buying in 1998.

Steelman: Simultaneous global launch captures first-mover scale in online fashion before incumbents react, leveraging venture capital to outspend local competitors on marketing and logistics immediately.

Attack:
1. **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves capital until the model is proven. A "single-country pilot" (e.g., UK or Germany only) offers the same upside potential with drastically lower risk, allowing logistics and marketing costs to be validated before multiplying them across continents. The proposal fails to explain why a staged approach cannot reach the same upside safer and cheaper.
2. **Fit & Evidence:** The claim targets "young, fashion-minded buyers," yet admits online fashion buying is "unproven." There is no evidence cited that this demographic will trust online transactions for fit-sensitive goods in 1998. The risks (logistics complexity, cash burn) are admitted facts, not hypotheticals.
3. **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 18 months. Cause: Venture capital ran out while trying to fix logistics in five countries simultaneously, with no single market generating enough profit to sustain operations. The cost of delay (waiting to expand) is negligible compared to the cost of total insolvency.
4. **Blocker:** The proposal's own admitted facts create a blocker: "online fashion buying is unproven" combined with "running many countries at once multiplies costs." Launching globally before proving the unit economics in one market contradicts sound operational logic for an unproven category. The lack of a pilot or capped first step is a fatal flaw when the core behavior (buying fashion online) is unverified.

This is a classic case of scaling a hypothesis rather than a business. The absence of a stated edge, coupled with the admitted high burn rate and unproven demand, makes the global launch a bet on luck rather than strategy. A cheaper, staged alternative exists that reaches the same vision without the immediate risk of total capital depletion.

Blockers: Unproven core behavior (online fashion) + multiplied fixed costs = high probability of insolvency before product-market fit.
Risks: Logistics failure, marketing inefficiency, venture dry-up.

DECISION: NO-GO
