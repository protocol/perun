First call: NO-GO. The proposal offers a massive engineering risk and a forced migration without a stated edge over the current working product; "slowing traffic" is a problem, not a solution.

**Steelman:** A full rewrite with publisher-favored surfacing and forced migration will reverse traffic decline by engaging new users without alienating the core.

**Attack:**
1.  **No Stated Edge:** Favoring publishers directly contradicts the core community's value of user-driven voting mechanics. This risks driving away the loyal base before new users arrive.
2.  **Irreversible Risk:** Moving all users at once eliminates the ability to rollback if the new mechanics fail or the rewrite introduces critical bugs. With a rival growing, a failed launch could be fatal.
3.  **Alternative Ignored:** A staged rollout or A/B test on the existing codebase would validate the "publisher favor" hypothesis with minimal risk. A full rewrite delays learning and consumes all engineering capacity.
4.  **Pre-mortem:** The launch occurs, the core community revolts against the changed voting mechanics, traffic plummets further due to the exodus, and the rigid architecture prevents a quick fix.

**Blockers:**
*   **Contradictory Strategy:** The plan explicitly changes the core mechanic (voting) that the loyal community values, with no evidence this trade-off yields net growth.
*   **Zero Reversibility:** The "move all users at once" constraint creates a single point of failure with no escape hatch, violating basic risk management for a struggling product.
*   **Missing Validation:** There is no pilot or metric to prove "favoring publishers" actually works; the plan bets the company on an unproven assumption.

**Risks:** Engineering delays in the rewrite, rival capturing market share during the transition, total community alienation.

**Kill Criteria:** If a staged test of the new surfacing algorithm on 5% of users does not increase time-on-site within 2 weeks, the rewrite is cancelled.

The proposal fails because it trades a known asset (loyal community) for an unproven gain using a method (big bang rewrite) that removes all safety nets.

DECISION: NO-GO
