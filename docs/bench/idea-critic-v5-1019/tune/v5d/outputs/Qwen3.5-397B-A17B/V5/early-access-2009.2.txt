First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; "unfinished" is a liability, not a differentiator, and no fact suggests buyers prefer rough alphas over competitors in 2009.

Steelman: Releasing early monetizes development time and aligns features with actual user demand rather than speculation.

Attack: The claim fails the evidence and fit tests.
1. **Evidence Gap**: The proposal assumes buyers will tolerate an unfinished product without offering a price discount, exclusive access, or a unique value proposition that outweighs the "rough state." In 2009, the "Early Access" model was not an established consumer expectation; selling broken goods risks permanent reputation damage.
2. **Capacity Mismatch**: A solo developer explicitly cannot support a large audience. Selling immediately invites a volume of support tickets and bug reports that will halt feature development, creating a death spiral where the product never improves because the creator is drowning in support for a broken product.
3. **Missing Edge**: Need is not an edge. The proposal admits no marketing budget and no publisher. Without a mechanism to reach users who specifically want to co-develop, the default outcome is negative reviews from general consumers expecting a complete game.

Blockers:
- **Fact**: Solo dev cannot support a large audience + Action: Selling now guarantees more support tickets than can be handled, stopping development.
- **Fact**: No established market for unfinished games in 2009 + Action: High probability of negative reception killing future sales.

Risks:
- Players stop buying after seeing the state (validated by the lack of a "hook").
- Reputation destruction prevents a future successful launch.

The proposal offers no cap on audience size, no discount strategy to manage expectations, and no plan to handle support load. It relies on hope that buyers will be forgiving, which contradicts the stated risk of judgment. The cheaper, safer alternative is a free technical demo to gauge interest without the liability of a sales transaction.

DECISION: NO-GO
