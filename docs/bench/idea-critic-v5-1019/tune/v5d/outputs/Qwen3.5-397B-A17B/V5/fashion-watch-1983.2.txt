First call: NO-GO. The proposal identifies a massive volume opportunity but fails to state a defensible edge explaining why a traditional watch-movement maker, known for quality, would win in a low-cost plastic fashion segment against established competitors who may cut prices. Need alone is not an edge.

**Steelman:** Leveraging existing movement manufacturing to flood the fashion market with affordable, stylish plastic watches, capturing volume before competitors react.

**Attack & Options:**
1.  **Reputation Cannibalization:** The core risk is brand erosion. If the "quality" reputation is the company's primary asset, diluting it for a low-margin plastic play could destroy long-term value. The proposal offers no firewall (e.g., a sub-brand) to protect the core business.
2.  **Competitive Response:** Established brands cutting prices is a stated fact. Without a cost structure significantly lower than incumbents or a unique design moat, the company enters a price war it is not optimized to fight.
3.  **Fashion Volatility:** Betting on twelve specific models to hit one million units assumes static tastes. A miss here leaves massive inventory of a perishable good.
4.  **Alternative:** A "do nothing" or "pilot" approach (e.g., one model, limited region) tests the water without risking the brand equity or committing to an aggressive 1M unit target immediately. The current plan beats "do nothing" only if the fashion window is closing *now*, which is unproven.

**Blockers:**
*   **Missing Strategic Edge:** The proposal does not explain *why* this specific company wins. Is it distribution? Cost? Design? Without a stated advantage over existing plastic watch sellers, the plan relies on hope, not strategy.
*   **Unmitigated Brand Risk:** No mechanism is proposed to isolate the low-cost line from the premium reputation.

**Risks:** Inventory obsolescence, brand dilution, price war losses.

**Conclusion:** The plan lacks a stated edge to justify the aggressive target and ignores the critical need to protect the existing brand equity. It attempts to boil the ocean (12 models, 1M units) without a pilot or a defensive strategy against the stated risks.

DECISION: NO-GO
