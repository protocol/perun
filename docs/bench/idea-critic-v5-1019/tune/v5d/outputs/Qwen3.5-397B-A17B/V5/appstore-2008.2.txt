**Evidence:** The proposal relies on the stated facts: a existing phone base, a CEO ready to pivot from web-only, and a revenue model proven by other digital goods (implied). **Decision:** The plan beats "do nothing" (staying web-only limits functionality) and "open distribution" (invites the very instability/carrier issues the company fears). It turns the CEO's previous stance into a strategic pivot story. The 30% cut funds the review overhead.
