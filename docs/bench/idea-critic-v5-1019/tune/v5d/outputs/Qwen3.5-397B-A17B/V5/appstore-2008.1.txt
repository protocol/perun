First call: GO. The stated edge is converting a "web-only" device into a platform where the manufacturer controls distribution, quality, and revenue (30%), directly countering the CEO's prior stance by capturing developer value rivals already monetize via open but fragmented ecosystems.

**Steelman:** A curated, single-store model mitigates security/stability risks and carrier friction better than open sideloading while capturing network effects competitors enjoy.

**Attack & Options:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Retains polish but cedes the emerging app economy to rivals, risking long-term irrelevance as users demand native functionality web apps cannot match.
*   **Open Distribution:** Matches rivals but invites malware, instability, and carrier backlash, threatening the brand's premium positioning.
*   **Curated Store (Proposal):** Balances openness with control. The 30% cut funds review operations; strict approval protects the user experience and placates carriers by preventing service bypasses.

**Fit:** Aligns with shifting from a hardware-only vision to a platform ecosystem, essential for retention beyond first-generation novelty.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if review bottlenecks stall key apps or if the 30% fee drives developers to web-only workarounds. However, the "walled garden" creates a unique selling proposition (trust/polish) absent in rival open markets. Delay costs immediate developer mindshare; waiting a quarter allows competitors to lock in exclusive titles.

**Evidence:** Rivals already prove demand; the proposal's specific constraints (single store, review, fee) directly address the stated risks (security, carrier objection) without sacrificing the core value prop.

**Blockers:** NONE. The concerns (security, workload) are operational hurdles solved by the proposed review process, not fundamental flaws. The risk of hurting polish is mitigated by the gatekeeping mechanism itself.
**Risks:** Review scale-up speed; developer resentment of fees. These become kill criteria: if top-tier apps reject the store within 6 months, pivot to hybrid web-native support.

**Decision:** The plan turns a perceived weakness (late to apps) into a differentiated strength (curated quality), monetizing the ecosystem while protecting the brand.

DECISION: GO
