First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits irreversible capital to a scaled model before validating the core unit economics in a single market, violating basic capital allocation discipline for a low-margin industry.

**Steelman:** Securing infrastructure early creates an unassailable moat and prevents competitors from locking up real estate and logistics capacity in key metros.

**Attack:** The "moat" is illusory if the underlying business bleeds cash on every order. Groceries have notoriously thin margins; adding high-fixed-cost automation and a proprietary fleet before proving customer density or order frequency creates a catastrophic burn rate. The proposal explicitly admits to having "no operating track record" and ignores the need to learn unit economics. Scaling a broken or unproven model across a dozen cities multiplies the error, not the success. The 30-minute slot constraint further exacerbates logistics complexity and cost without evidence of customer willingness to pay the premium required to sustain it.

**Alternatives:**
1.  **Do Nothing/Wait:** Validate unit economics in the first city for 12–18 months.
2.  **Lease/Partner:** Use third-party logistics and leased warehousing to maintain flexibility.
3.  **Proposed Plan:** $1B fixed cost commitment immediately.
The proposed plan fails to beat "Do Nothing" because it removes the option to pivot or stop losses once data arrives.

**Pre-mortem:** The company runs out of cash within 18 months as expansion costs outpace revenue growth in new, unproven cities. The specialized warehouses cannot be repurposed, leading to total asset stranding.

**Blockers:**
*   **Fact:** The proposal states the company has "no operating track record" and has not yet "learning the unit economics."
*   **Contradiction:** Committing $1B to fixed assets requires proven unit economics to justify the ROI. The stated facts show this prerequisite is missing.
*   **Alternative:** Leasing achieves market entry with fraction of the risk and capital, reaching the same upside of market presence without the fatal downside of stranded assets.

**Risks:** Low margins, high capital intensity, unproven customer density. These are not merely risks; combined with the lack of data, they form a definitive blocker.

**Kill Criteria:** Not applicable as the decision is No-Go. If this were a pilot, a kill criterion would be negative contribution margin after 6 months in the first city.

DECISION: NO-GO
