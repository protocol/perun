First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; high cost and poor indoor performance are disadvantages, not advantages, against rapidly improving ground networks.

**Steelman:** This is the only solution for true global coverage where terrestrial cells cannot reach, serving a niche of desperate users regardless of cost.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" and wait for ground cellular expansion is cheaper, offers better indoor service, and requires no handset subsidy. The proposal admits ground networks are "spreading fast," directly eroding the unique value proposition before launch.
2.  **Fit:** The high price ($3,000 handset, high per-minute cost) contradicts the need for "fast subscriber growth" to service billions in debt. The addressable market (business travelers willing to carry bulky, indoor-deficient phones) is too small to sustain the capital structure.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 24 months. Cause: Ground cellular coverage reaches 90% of business travel destinations, rendering the expensive, cumbersome satellite handset obsolete for all but rare emergencies. The debt burden forces bankruptcy before the niche market can scale.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal's own facts contradict its viability: "debt payments require fast subscriber growth" vs. "price limits the market." These two statements cannot both be satisfied.

**Blockers:**
*   **Economic Mismatch:** The stated need for rapid scale to service debt is mathematically incompatible with the stated price point limiting the market.
*   **Superior Alternative:** Ground cellular offers a cheaper, higher-quality (indoor) experience that is rapidly closing the coverage gap, removing the primary reason to exist.

**Risks:** Execution difficulty, sales channel readiness, and technology bulkiness are secondary to the fundamental economic blocker.

**Decision:** The plan fails as proposed because its own facts show the target market cannot generate the revenue required to service the debt, while a cheaper, better alternative is expanding quickly.

DECISION: NO-GO
