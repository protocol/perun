First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; high price, legal ambiguity, and unproven consumer demand are admitted facts, not advantages over existing transport.

Steelman: A novel, self-balancing device creates a new micro-mobility category, leveraging inventor fame to overcome early skepticism.

Attack:
1. **Options**: "Do nothing" avoids massive capex on a factory for an unproven market. A cheaper, staged alternative (pilot production, third-party manufacturing) reaches the same validation upside with far less risk. Building a 40,000-unit factory upfront fails the reversibility test.
2. **Fit**: Without a confirmed north star or strategy context, assuming this moves a core goal is speculative. The stated risks (legal status, conspicuousness) directly block mass adoption.
3. **Pre-mortem**: Assume failure: Regulatory bodies ban sidewalk use; the $5,000 price limits sales to enthusiasts; the "conspicuous" factor kills mainstream utility. This is a one-way door with high delay cost if regulations shift post-investment.
4. **Evidence**: All claims rely on publicity, not verified demand or regulatory clearance. The proposal admits legal status is "unclear" and uses are "unproven." These are not risks; they are missing prerequisites.
5. **Blocker Test**:
   - **Blockers**: The plan commits to a large factory (irreversible capex) before resolving legal status or proving consumer demand beyond institutions. The stated facts (unclear legality, high price, unproven use) contradict the core claim of a successful mass launch. A cheaper, staged alternative exists that achieves validation without the factory risk.
   - **Risks**: Rider conspicuousness, limited cargo, regulatory shifts.

The proposal asks to bet the company on a hardware moonshot with known legal and market holes. The absence of a pilot, the commitment to a specific factory size, and the lack of regulatory clarity are fatal flaws in the current plan. The "edge" is merely novelty, which does not beat the "do nothing" or "staged pilot" options when the downside is a stranded factory.

DECISION: NO-GO
