First call: NO-GO. The proposal states smokers may dislike the taste and lighting mechanism, and that the product is complicated to use. Need alone is not an edge; a product inferior in core experience (taste, simplicity) to the incumbent has no stated advantage to overcome these deficits.

Steelman: A heated tobacco product offers reduced smoke and a familiar ritual, potentially satisfying health-conscious smokers without quitting.

Attack:
1.  **Fit & Evidence:** The proposal admits "smokers may dislike the taste" and "complicated to use." In 1988, without verified data proving a significant segment prefers "cleaner" over "tasty/simple," the core value proposition is assumed, not evidenced. The "very large sum" spent is a sunk cost, not a reason to launch a flawed product.
2.  **Better Way:** "Do nothing" or continue R&D to fix taste/usability beats launching a product likely to be rejected. Promoting a "cleaner" cigarette invites immediate regulatory scrutiny on health claims, a high-risk path with unproven consumer demand.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Launch fails because early adopters (smokers) reject the taste/complexity, regulators block "cleaner" marketing, and the brand suffers reputational damage from a failed "health-adjacent" product.
4.  **Blockers:** The proposal itself contains the blocker: stated user rejection risks (taste, complexity) and regulatory objections to the primary marketing angle ("cleaner"). These are not future risks; they are present negatives in the plan's own facts. No kill criteria or pilot can fix a product users "may dislike" before launch; the product must be fixed first.

Blockers: Stated user dissatisfaction (taste, complexity) and regulatory risk to core claim ("cleaner") with no evidence of demand overcoming these.
Risks: Sunk cost fallacy driving launch; brand damage from failure.

The plan fails as proposed because its own facts indicate the product is inferior to existing options and its marketing is legally vulnerable. Fix the product and validate demand before launching.

DECISION: NO-GO
