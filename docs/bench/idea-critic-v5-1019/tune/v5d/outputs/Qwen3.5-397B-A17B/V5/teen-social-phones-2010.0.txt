First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; it offers a high-cost data plan to price-sensitive youth while removing the primary drivers of smartphone adoption (apps and games), making it inferior to existing cheap phones and the company's own upcoming platform.

Steelman: A curated, distraction-free social device for youth could succeed if the carrier subsidizes the hardware deeply and the "no app store" constraint is marketed as a safety feature parents will pay for.

However, the attack reveals fatal flaws against the stated facts:
1.  **Economic Mismatch:** Requiring a full smartphone data plan for a device with no apps or games creates a terrible value proposition. Price-sensitive buyers will reject a high monthly cost for reduced functionality.
2.  **Strategic Cannibalization:** Launching this limited device months before the company's main new platform creates internal competition and confuses the market, likely depressing sales of the flagship launch.
3.  **Operational Fragility:** The history of internal conflict between the teams, combined with the recent delay caused by a software switch, suggests high execution risk. Without third-party apps, the device relies entirely on first-party execution, which is currently unstable.
4.  **No Alternative Advantage:** "Do nothing" and wait for the main platform launch is cheaper, avoids channel conflict, and leverages the full app ecosystem eventually. There is no evidence that a "walled garden" without games appeals to youth more than standard options.

The proposal fails the blocker test: the stated facts (price sensitivity, high data cost, no apps, upcoming internal competitor) directly contradict the path to success. The lack of a stated edge means the plan fails as proposed.

Blockers:
- Economic model contradicts target demographic (high cost vs. price sensitivity).
- Strategic timing conflicts with main platform launch.
- No stated edge over "do nothing" or waiting for the main launch.

Risks:
- Team conflict derailing execution.
- Market rejection of limited functionality.

DECISION: NO-GO
