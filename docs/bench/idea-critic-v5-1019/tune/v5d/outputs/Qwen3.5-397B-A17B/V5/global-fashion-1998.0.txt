First call: NO-GO. The proposal states a massive need but offers no stated edge (cost, product, access, timing, or evidence) explaining why this plan beats existing local retailers or why simultaneous global launch is superior to a staged approach.

Steelman: Simultaneous global entry captures first-mover advantage in online fashion before incumbents react, leveraging venture capital to build an unassailable scale moat immediately.

Options:
1. **Do nothing:** Avoids capital burn but cedes the emerging online channel to future competitors.
2. **Staged Launch (Single Country):** Drastically reduces logistics complexity and marketing spend; allows proof of concept before scaling. Cheaper, safer, reversible.
3. **Proposed (Global):** Maximum upfront cost, maximum operational risk, highest potential reward if logistics hold.

Fit: Aligns with the "largest in the world" vision but ignores the "unproven" nature of the market cited in the risks. A global bet on an unproven behavior is a strategy mismatch without a pilot.

Pre-mortem: The venture capital runs out within 18 months because logistics costs in multiple jurisdictions tripled projections while sales conversion remained low due to consumer distrust of cross-border fashion buying. The company collapses before achieving profitability in any single market. This is a one-way door; once capital is burned across five countries, retreat leaves no foothold. Cost of delay: Low, as the market is unproven; being second in a validated market is safer than first in a failed one.

Evidence: The proposal admits online fashion buying is "unproven" and venture money "may run out." These are internal contradictions to a "go" decision. No data exists to support the assumption that young buyers will purchase fashion online at a rate sufficient to cover multi-country overhead.

Blockers:
1. **Unproven Demand:** The core premise (online fashion buying) is explicitly stated as unproven. Scaling an unproven model globally multiplies the risk of total failure.
2. **Capital Mismatch:** Funding a multi-country launch with finite venture capital against "unproven" unit economics creates a high probability of insolvency before product-market fit is found.

Risks: Logistics complexity, marketing inefficiency, cultural mismatches in fashion.

Decision: NO-GO. The plan fails the blocker test: it commits massive resources to an explicitly unproven behavior without a staged validation step. A cheaper alternative (single-country pilot) reaches the same upside (validation) with significantly lower risk of total collapse.

DECISION: NO-GO
