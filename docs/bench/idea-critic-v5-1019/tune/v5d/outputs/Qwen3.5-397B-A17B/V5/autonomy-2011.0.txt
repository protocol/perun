4.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" or smaller, tuck-in acquisitions allow for organic capability building without the catastrophic downside of a $11.7B potential write-down. The cheaper alternative (organic build or smaller buys) avoids the specific risk of accounting fraud while achieving the same long-term goal.

**Blockers:**
*   **Financial Contradiction:** The core claim of value acquisition contradicts the stated fact of questioned accounting and internal finance objections.
*   **No Stated Edge:** Need is not an edge. The proposal fails to state how this specific target at this specific price beats the alternative of building or buying cheaper.

**Risks:** Integration failure, overpayment, hidden liabilities from accounting issues.
