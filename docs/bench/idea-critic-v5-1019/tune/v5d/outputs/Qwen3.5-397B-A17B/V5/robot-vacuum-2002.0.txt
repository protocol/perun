First call: NO-GO. The proposal admits zero market interest from the only two potential channel partners (floor-care companies) and highlights a complete lack of consumer experience, while offering no stated edge other than lower price to overcome the "dumber" random movement mechanism.

**Steelman:** A low-cost, randomized robot can succeed by targeting price-sensitive consumers who value convenience over perfect coverage, bypassing skeptical B2B partners via direct sales.

**Attack:**
1.  **Fit & Evidence:** The core evidence (two rejections) suggests the distribution strategy is broken, not just the product. Launching without channel support requires a direct-to-consumer machine the company has never built.
2.  **Better Way:** "Do nothing" avoids burning capital on an unproven consumer pivot. A cheaper alternative is licensing the tech to a toy manufacturer, not becoming a appliance brand.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** The product fails because "random" movement leaves visible dirty spots, leading to returns and brand destruction among early adopters who expect "clean," not "entertainment."
4.  **Blocker:** The proposal explicitly states the company has **no consumer-product experience**. This is a fatal capability gap for a hardware launch requiring retail logistics, warranty support, and consumer marketing. A lower price does not fix the inability to execute the business model.
5.  **Risks:** Random coverage is a functional defect, not a feature, in the floor-care category. Trust issues are compounded by the lack of a known consumer brand.

**Decision Logic:**
*   **Blockers:** Lack of consumer execution capability; negative validation from target industry experts.
*   **Risks:** Product performance perception; channel absence.

The plan relies on a "stated edge" (low price) that cannot compensate for the missing capability (consumer ops) and the negative signal from domain experts. The randomized motion is a technical compromise that becomes a liability without a strong brand to frame it as "good enough." As proposed, this is a capital burn with no route to market.

DECISION: NO-GO
