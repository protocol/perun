First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge: the target has no revenue, massive liabilities, and an unproven model, while the buyer already operates a smaller, presumably safer service. Need alone is not an edge; paying a premium for a liability-heavy asset without a clear path to monetization or legal safety fails the basic operator test.

Steelman: Acquiring the dominant traffic leader instantly solves scale and network effects, betting that legal risks can be managed post-close and monetization follows attention.

Alternatives: (1) Do nothing and grow the existing organic service (low cost, low risk, slow scale). (2) License content strictly from media companies (higher cost, lower risk, proven model). (3) This acquisition (extreme cost, extreme legal/financial risk, instant scale). The "do nothing" or licensing options reach similar upsides without the existential threat of copyright litigation that could bankrupt the combined entity.

Fit: Assuming the goal is safe, scalable growth, buying a lawsuit magnet contradicts a prudent north star. If the goal is "win at all costs," it fits, but the costs are undefined.

Pre-mortem: The deal closes; media companies sue immediately. Damages exceed the purchase price. The ad model fails to cover bandwidth. The site is shut down or stripped of content, leaving only debt and integration chaos. This is a one-way door with catastrophic downside.

Evidence: The proposal admits "no meaningful revenue," "large volume of copyrighted clips," and "threatened lawsuits." These are facts, not risks. The math does not work: $1.65B for negative cash flow and undefined legal exposure is speculation, not investment.

Blockers:
1. **Financial Suicide:** Paying $1.65B for an asset with zero revenue and high operating costs violates basic fiduciary duty without a guaranteed monetization path.
2. **Existential Legal Risk:** The core value proposition (user uploads of copyrighted material) is the very thing that will destroy the company via litigation. The proposal admits this threat is active, not hypothetical.

Risks: Bandwidth costs spiraling, ad model failure, cultural clash.

The stated facts show the target's primary asset is also its primary liability. No kill criteria or caps can mitigate a lawsuit that targets the entire business model.

DECISION: NO-GO
