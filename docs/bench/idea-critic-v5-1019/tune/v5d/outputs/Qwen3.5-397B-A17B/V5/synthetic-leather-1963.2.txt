First call: NO-GO. The proposal lacks a stated edge; it admits higher cost, potential comfort deficits, and fashion volatility without explaining why a buyer would switch from leather today.

Steelman: This synthetic offers superior durability that justifies a premium price if marketed as a long-life luxury good.

Attack:
1. **Options:** "Do nothing" (stick with leather) costs less and carries zero retooling risk. A cheaper alternative is targeting non-apparel uses (luggage, upholstery) where comfort and fashion matter less. The proposal fails to beat "do nothing" on the stated facts.
2. **Fit:** Launching a high-cost material into a fashion-driven market (shoes) contradicts the risk of shifting trends. Without a verified demand signal, this misaligns with a safe market entry.
3. **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure in 12 months. Cause: Shoe manufacturers refused to retool factories for a material that costs more and feels worse, while consumers rejected the "plastic" aesthetic. The door is one-way; heavy marketing spend is sunk if adoption fails.
4. **Evidence:** The proposal itself provides the blockers: higher cost, lower comfort, and manufacturer resistance. These are not hypothetical risks but stated negatives that remove the route to upside.

Blockers:
- **Cost/Value Mismatch:** The material costs more than leather while offering perceived lower comfort. No evidence suggests buyers will pay a premium for durability alone in 1963 footwear.
- **Adoption Friction:** Manufacturers are explicitly noted as unwilling to change factories. Without a subsidy or massive cost saving (which is absent), the supply chain will reject it.

Risks:
- Fashion trends shifting away from synthetics.
- Comfort complaints damaging brand reputation.

The plan commits real money to a strategy where the core value proposition (durability) is outweighed by immediate barriers (cost, comfort, retooling). A capped pilot in a non-fashion sector (e.g., industrial workwear) would be a valid first step, but launching broadly as "premium shoe-upper" is unsupported.

DECISION: NO-GO
