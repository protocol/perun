First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits the studio's entire steady income to a single unproven format with a cost structure (10x average short) that threatens insolvency if it fails, while key stakeholders explicitly advise against it.

Steelman: A full-length feature could unlock a new, higher-margin market and define the studio's legacy, provided audiences accept the format.

Options:
1. **Do nothing:** Continue steady, thin profits from shorts; zero risk of ruin.
2. **Pilot/Shorter Feature:** Produce a medium-length special (3-4x cost) to test audience endurance before committing to a full feature.
3. **Proposed Plan:** Bet the company on one 10x cost film immediately.

Fit: While ambitious, the plan ignores the current stage (thin profits) and lacks a safety net. It assumes the "north star" of feature dominance without validating the path.

Pre-mortem: The film releases to empty theaters because audiences find the format tedious or the story weak. The budget overruns by 20%, exhausting the cash reserve from shorts. The studio cannot fund the next year of operations and declares bankruptcy. This is a one-way door; failure is fatal. The cost of delay (waiting a year) is low compared to the cost of failure.

Evidence: The only evidence provided is negative: insiders and family advise against it, and the revenue model relies entirely on diverting existing fragile earnings. There is no data suggesting audiences will sit for 80+ minutes of animation.

Blockers:
1. **Fatal Risk Exposure:** The plan commits all operating capital to a single binary outcome with no intermediate validation. A single failure destroys the entity.
2. **Stakeholder Consensus:** Those with industry experience and personal stake advise against it; their concerns regarding audience endurance are unaddressed.
3. **No Stop Condition:** There is no pilot, cap, or kill criterion defined. If costs rise or early tests fail, the plan offers no off-ramp.

Risks: Audience rejection, budget overrun, production collapse.

Kill Check: The plan lacks a working stop condition. It demands full scale before proving demand. Without a capped pilot to validate audience endurance, the risk of total loss is unmanaged.

DECISION: NO-GO
