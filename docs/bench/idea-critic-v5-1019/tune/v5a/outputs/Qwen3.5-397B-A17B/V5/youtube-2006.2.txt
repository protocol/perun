First call: NO-GO. The price is astronomical for an asset with zero revenue, negative unit economics due to bandwidth costs, and existential legal threats that could result in the platform being shut down entirely.

**Steelman:** Acquiring the dominant cultural hub of online video now secures a monopoly on user-generated content and data, preventing a competitor from owning it, with the bet that ad technology and licensing deals will eventually monetize the massive traffic.

**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Continue growing the smaller, safer, licensed video service; avoid massive debt and litigation risk.
2.  **Build/Buy smaller:** Acquire a niche player or license content incrementally; lower cost, lower risk, but forfeits market leadership.
3.  **The Proposal:** Pay $1.65B for immediate dominance but inherit unlimited liability and unproven economics.

**Fit:** While this aligns with a vision of organizing the world's information, the specific mechanism (buying a lawsuit magnet with no revenue) contradicts basic fiscal sustainability and risk management principles required at this stage.

**Pre-mortem:** Six months post-acquisition, major studios win an injunction shutting down the site due to copyright infringement. The $1.65B stock value evaporates, leaving the buyer with massive bandwidth bills for a dead product and a shattered balance sheet. This is a one-way door; the integration and brand association are irreversible.

**Evidence:** The proposal itself admits "no meaningful revenue," "high bandwidth bills," and "threatened lawsuits." The math fails: there is no current path to ROI. The core asset (user uploads) is the primary source of legal liability.

**Blockers:**
1.  **Financial Viability:** The purchase price ($1.65B) cannot be justified by any current or near-term projected cash flow given the admitted lack of revenue model.
2.  **Existential Legal Risk:** The core functionality relies on copyrighted material owned by hostile third parties who have already threatened litigation; a single adverse ruling could reduce the asset's value to zero.
3.  **Unit Economics:** High bandwidth costs combined with zero revenue means every additional user increases losses, creating a scale disadvantage rather than an advantage.

**Risks:** Ad models may never work for short-form video; integration culture clash; continued escalation of licensing costs.

**Kill Criterion:** If the legal team cannot secure a binding indemnity or insurance cap against copyright damages prior to signing, the deal must terminate.

DECISION: NO-GO
