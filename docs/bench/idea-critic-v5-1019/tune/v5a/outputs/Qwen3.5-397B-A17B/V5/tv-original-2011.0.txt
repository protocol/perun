First call: GO. The data-driven bet on a full-season drop with top talent aligns with shifting viewer habits, despite high stakes.

**Steelman:** Leveraging proprietary viewing data to bypass the pilot system and greenlight a high-quality, full-season original series creates a unique value proposition that licensed content cannot match, securing subscriber retention against rising costs.

**Options:**
1.  **Proposal:** High cost, high differentiation, low reversibility (sunk production), immediate market shock.
2.  **Do Nothing:** Lower cost, zero differentiation, rising dependency on licensors, eventual margin collapse.
3.  **Pilot First:** Moderate cost, traditional de-risking, slower time-to-market, signals lack of confidence in own data.
The proposal beats "do nothing" by addressing the existential threat of licensing costs. A pilot is cheaper but fails to capture the "binge" advantage or signal a breakthrough platform shift.

**Fit:** Assumes the north star is transitioning from a distribution pipe to a premium destination. If the data confirms a hungry audience for this specific genre/talent combo, the fit is exact.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the data misinterprets niche interest as mass appeal, or if the "all-at-once" model confuses users accustomed to weekly schedules. This is a one-way door; the money is spent regardless of viewership. Cost of delay is high: competitors will eventually enter originals, and licensing leverage decays monthly.

**Evidence:** The claim rests entirely on internal viewing data. Without access to verify the specific metrics, we must assume the correlation between past viewing and future subscription retention is strong. The attachment of a film star and director mitigates execution risk.

**Blockers:** NONE. The lack of a pilot is a feature, not a bug, given the strategy. The cost is high but necessary for the strategic pivot.
**Risks:** Audience mismatch; cultural rejection of non-weekly releases; budget overrun.

**Kill Criterion:** If Season 1 does not drive a net new subscriber growth of >5% within 90 days of release, halt further investment in this specific production tier and revert to a mixed pilot/series model. Leading metric: Completion rate of Episode 1 vs. Episode 10.

DECISION: GO
