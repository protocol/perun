First call: GO. The proposal secures the franchise's survival by migrating its asset base (subscribers) to the inevitable distribution medium before competitors lock them out, despite near-term margin compression.

**Steelman:** Leveraging seven million paying subscribers as a captive pilot group validates the streaming model at zero customer acquisition cost, creating a defensive moat against incoming tech giants while the DVD cash cow funds the transition.

**Attack & Options:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Fatal. Retaining only DVDs ignores the broadband trajectory, inviting Amazon or Apple to capture the digital future with the company's own churned customers.
*   **Paid Tier:** Fails. Fragmenting the user base slows adoption data and invites competitors to undercut with bundled or free offerings.
*   **Wait for Quality/Broadband:** Fails. First-mover advantage in data algorithms and licensing relationships outweighs initial quality deficits.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:** This aligns with the north star of "home entertainment delivery," not "plastic disc logistics." A pre-mortem reveals failure if licensing costs balloon without corresponding retention gains. However, the "no extra charge" model mitigates churn risk immediately. The small catalog is a feature, not a bug: it limits initial capex while testing infrastructure.

**Evidence & Blockers:**
*   **Blockers:** NONE. The stated risks (cannibalization, cost) are inherent to strategic pivots, not factual contradictions. Cannibalization is preferable to obsolescence.
*   **Risks:** High licensing fees and tech debt. These are managed by the existing profit stream.

**Kill Criteria:**
If subscriber churn exceeds 5% within 12 months without a corresponding increase in lifetime value from retained users, or if licensing costs consume >40% of DVD profits without showing engagement growth, the initiative must pivot to a capped catalog or fee-based tier.

**Decision:**
The cost of delay is total market irrelevance. The proposal correctly trades short-term margin protection for long-term existence. The risks are real but manageable; the alternative is extinction.

DECISION: GO
