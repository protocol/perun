First call: NO-GO. The proposal launches a business-grade device without its core utility (email) unless paired with another product, directly contradicting the company's market strength and the stated goal of competing with a rival tablet.

Steelman: A 7-inch device on a new OS creates a distinct, portable category, using phone-pairing to leverage existing ecosystem loyalty while delaying native app development to accelerate time-to-market.

Options:
1. **Proposed Plan:** High risk. Matches rival price with inferior utility (no native email). Relies on unproven OS and uncertain developer support. Fails to beat "do nothing" on immediate business value.
2. **Do Nothing/Wait:** Retain brand integrity. Avoid launching a half-finished product that could tarnish the business email reputation.
3. **Alternative (Native/Integrated):** Delay launch to integrate native email or lower price significantly to reflect the missing utility.

Fit: The proposal misaligns with the "business email leader" north star. Shipping a business device without built-in email assumes customers will accept a degraded experience, which contradicts the company's value proposition.

Pre-mortem: The device fails because business users reject a tablet requiring a phone for basic functions, developers ignore the untested OS due to low adoption, and the price point makes it uncompetitive against the rival's superior app store and larger screen.

Evidence & Blockers:
- **Blocker 1 (Core Utility):** The proposal admits "initially without built-in email." For a business-focused manufacturer, this removes the primary purchase driver. The plan offers no compensation for this deficit other than pairing, which adds friction.
- **Blocker 2 (Price/Value Mismatch):** Pricing matches a rival's larger, established tablet with a full app store. The proposed device offers less hardware (size), less software (no native apps, untested OS), and equal cost. This is economically indefensible.
- **Blocker 3 (Developer Risk):** The plan acknowledges developers "may not commit" but proceeds anyway. Without apps, the tablet is a media viewer, not a business tool.

Kill Check: If business adoption is <5% in Q1 due to lack of native email, the brand damage exceeds any potential market share gain. The proposal lacks a stop condition or a capped pilot; it commits to a full launch.

Verdict: The plan fails on immediate value, pricing logic, and strategic fit. It attacks the company's core strength while offering no compensatory advantage.

DECISION: NO-GO
