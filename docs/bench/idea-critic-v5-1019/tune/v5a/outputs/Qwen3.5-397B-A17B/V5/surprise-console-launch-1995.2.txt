First call: NO-GO. Launching early with high price, scarce software, and alienated retailers contradicts basic market entry logic for a hardware platform dependent on ecosystem support.

**Steelman:** An early May launch at $399 secures a four-month head start to establish brand presence before the competitor arrives, capturing early adopters despite limited SKUs.

**Attack:**
1.  **Ecosystem Failure:** Console viability relies on software. Launching with "few games" while developers are unprepared for the early date guarantees a barren library. Early adopters who buy the hardware will have nothing to play, generating negative word-of-mouth that will poison the well before the competitor even launches.
2.  **Price Disadvantage:** At $100 above the rival, the value proposition collapses without a superior game library to justify the premium. The hardware is noted as "hard to program," suggesting the software gap will widen, not narrow, during the head-start period.
3.  **Channel Retaliation:** Shipping only to "a few retailers" intentionally burns relationships with the broader distribution network required for a mass-market September launch. Retaliatory exclusion or poor placement by frozen-out partners during the critical holiday season could be fatal.
4.  **Strategic Mismatch:** A four-month head start with a broken value proposition (high price, no games) does not build momentum; it demonstrates weakness. It signals to the market and developers that the platform is not ready, potentially causing developers to flock to the better-prepared competitor.

**Pre-mortem:** By September, the console is labeled "overpriced and empty." Retailers refuse prominent holiday display due to prior snubbing. Developers delay titles until the competitor's robust launch. The early launch burned cash and credibility, resulting in a distant second place.

**Blockers:**
*   **Ecosystem Void:** The stated lack of developer readiness and few available games directly contradicts the requirement for a functional entertainment product.
*   **Channel Hostility:** The plan explicitly creates enemies in the distribution channel required for survival.
*   **Value Gap:** No justification exists for a 33% price premium over a rival with better timing and likely better software support.

This plan trades long-term viability for a symbolic head start that highlights the product's deficiencies.

DECISION: NO-GO
