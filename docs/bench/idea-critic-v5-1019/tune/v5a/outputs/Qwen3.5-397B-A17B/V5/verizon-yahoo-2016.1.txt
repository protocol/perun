First call: NO-GO. The proposal attempts to solve a scale deficit by acquiring a second declining asset, creating a larger entity with accelerating negative momentum rather than a viable competitor.

Steelman: Combining two distressed media portfolios creates sufficient scale to negotiate better rates and cross-sell to the carrier's mobile base, halting revenue decline through synergy.

Attack: The core premise fails on momentum and fit. Both the acquirer's existing media unit and the target are in structural decline with falling traffic and revenue. Adding a shrinking asset to a shrinking division does not create growth; it aggregates decay. The carrier has no proven capability in consumer media, evidenced by the first acquisition's failure to stabilize. Integration risk is extreme given repeated management turnover at the target and the acquirer's lack of domain expertise. The $4.8 billion price tag assumes value in assets that the market has already deemed deteriorating. A "better way" exists: do nothing or invest the capital in native mobile infrastructure where the carrier has dominance, rather than fighting a war on enemy terrain with broken weapons.

Pre-mortem: Two years post-close, the combined unit misses revenue targets by 30%, key talent departs due to cultural clashes, and the carrier writes down the goodwill. The cost of delay is negligible because the ad market is shifting to programmatic platforms this conglomerate cannot match; waiting preserves cash.

Blockers:
1. **Momentum Mismatch**: The plan relies on combining two declining revenue streams to achieve growth, which contradicts basic arithmetic of compounding decay.
2. **Capability Gap**: The acquirer has no history of success in this sector, making the integration of a troubled target highly probable to fail.
3. **Valuation Disconnect**: Paying a premium ($4.8B) for a "declining portal" with falling traffic lacks economic justification without a specific, proven turnaround mechanism not present in the proposal.

Risks: Continued ad-revenue decline, culture clash, management distraction.

Kill Criterion: If the combined entity's ad revenue does not stabilize (year-over-year decline <5%) within 12 months, the strategy is invalid. However, the absence of a credible turnaround plan in the proposal itself blocks approval now.

DECISION: NO-GO
