First call: NO-GO. The proposal discards the brand's primary shelf recognition cue for an untested aesthetic, risking immediate sales collapse among a loyal, older demographic.

**Steelman:** A modernized package will attract new buyers and signal innovation without alienating existing customers if supported by heavy advertising.

**Attack:**
1.  **Fit & Evidence:** The proposal admits the old image is the "main shelf cue" and the base is "older." Removing the primary visual anchor for this demographic contradicts the stated facts. No evidence suggests the new "minimalist" design functions as a cue.
2.  **Options:** "Do nothing" preserves revenue. A "staged pilot" in limited regions would test recognition before a costly nationwide print run. The proposed "all stores at once" approach ignores the cheaper, safer alternative of testing.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Assume failure: Shoppers scan the aisle, miss the familiar orange-with-straw, assume the brand is out of stock, and buy a competitor. The ad campaign cannot compensate for failure at the critical 3-second shelf decision point.
4.  **Blocker Test:** The stated fact that the old image is the "main shelf cue" directly contradicts the plan to remove it. This is a factual blocker, not a risk. The plan offers no mechanism to verify the new design works as a cue before full commitment.
5.  **Kill Criteria:** The proposal lacks a stop condition. A nationwide launch with no pilot means if recognition fails, the loss is total and irreversible (printed cartons).

**Conclusion:** The plan relies on an unproven assumption (new design works) while violating a known constraint (old design is the cue). It rejects the cheaper, safer option (pilot) for a high-cost, high-risk big bang. The absence of a pilot or validation step for the core hypothesis (recognition) constitutes a blocker.

Blockers: Removes stated primary shelf cue without validation; rejects cheaper staged alternative for irreversible nationwide spend.
Risks: Older demographic rejection; high cost of wasted inventory if failed.

DECISION: NO-GO
