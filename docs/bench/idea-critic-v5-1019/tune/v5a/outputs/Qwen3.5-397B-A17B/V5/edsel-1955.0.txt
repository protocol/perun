First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits to a massive, irreversible infrastructure build (new dealer network) and a fixed 1957 launch date despite an explicit, known risk of a shrinking segment and potential recession.

**Steelman:** Launching a distinct mid-priced brand with dedicated dealers captures the growing "gap" market before competitors, leveraging extensive research to ensure product-market fit.

**Attack:**
1.  **Timing & Irreversibility:** The plan locks in high fixed costs (dealer network, tooling) for a 1957 debut while admitting the segment "could shrink" and a "recession risk exists." If the economy turns in 1957-58, the new division becomes a catastrophic drain with no easy exit, as dealer contracts and brand positioning are hard to reverse quickly.
2.  **Cheaper Alternative:** The proposal ignores entering the segment via existing dealerships or a sub-brand under current marques. This "better way" tests the market demand without the sunk cost of a standalone network, directly addressing the "cost of dealer infrastructure" risk.
3.  **Internal Cannibalization:** The risk of "overlap with the firm's own other brands" is stated but not solved. A new brand often steals share from the parent's low-end or premium lines rather than gaining net new customers, especially in a downturn.

**Pre-mortem:** It is 1958. A mild recession has hit. Car buyers are trading down to economy models or delaying purchases. The new division has 500 empty dealer franchises, huge overhead, and a brand image too expensive for the current mood. The company is bleeding cash it cannot stop because the network is contractually obligated.

**Blocker:** The commitment to a *new dealer network* prior to validating the segment's resilience against the stated recession risk is a factual blocker. The plan proceeds with maximum leverage (infrastructure spend) exactly when the environment suggests maximum caution. The "cheaper, smaller, staged" alternative (using existing dealers) reaches the same upside (market testing) with significantly lower risk and is not adopted.

**Kill Criterion:** If consumer confidence indices drop or a recession is declared before Q4 1956, the dealer network build must halt immediately. The current proposal lacks this stop condition, committing to full scale regardless of the economic shift.

DECISION: NO-GO
