First call: GO. The company leverages its dominant hardware footprint to solve the industry's existential threat: legal, reliable access.

**Steelman:** A seamless, integrated ecosystem where hardware sales subsidize a legal music distribution channel, converting pirates into paying customers through convenience rather than price competition.

**Options:**
1.  **Do nothing:** Hardware value erodes as piracy creates legal uncertainty and poor user experience.
2.  **Subscription model:** High friction for 2003 bandwidth/device limits; conflicts with "ownership" psychology.
3.  **Proposed (Per-song):** Low barrier to entry ($0.99), aligns with album mental models, secures label buy-in via DRM.

**Fit:** Directly protects the North Star: selling more portable players. The store is a feature, not a standalone profit center initially.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if labels demand unsustainable royalties or if DRM frustrates users enough to return to piracy. However, the alternative (no legal source) is worse for labels. The "own computers only" restriction is a strategic moat, not a bug, driving hardware differentiation.

**Evidence:** The proposal addresses the core friction of piracy (search time, virus risk, legal fear) with a superior product experience. Margins on songs are secondary to the lifetime value of a locked-in hardware user.

**Blockers:** NONE. The risks cited (margins, label relations, platform lock-in) are inherent to the strategy but do not invalidate the core logic that a legal, easy alternative will capture demand currently lost to piracy. The "rivals backing subscriptions" is a weak signal given the technical constraints of the era; ownership is the stronger consumer preference.

**Kill Criteria:** If major labels refuse licensing within 6 months, or if attachment rate (songs per device) remains below 10% after one year, the model requires pivot.

**Decision:** The plan turns a commodity hardware maker into a platform owner. It solves the label's distribution crisis and the user's access crisis simultaneously.

DECISION: GO
