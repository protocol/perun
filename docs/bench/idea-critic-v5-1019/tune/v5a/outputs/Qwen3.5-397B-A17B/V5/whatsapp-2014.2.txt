First call: NO-GO. The price is 12x the last valuation with no revenue path, violating basic capital discipline.

**Steelman:** Paying $19B is the only way to stop mobile messaging from cannibalizing the core business, buying immediate scale that cannot be built organically in time.

**Attack:**
1.  **Valuation Disconnect:** Jumping from $1.5B to $19B in 12 months without revenue or ads implies a speculative bubble, not fundamental value. The $1 fee is a friction point, not a business model.
2.  **Integration Risk:** The target's anti-ad stance and tiny team (50 employees) clash with the buyer's ad-revenue model. Retaining founders and culture post-acquisition at this price point is historically improbable; if they leave, the asset becomes just an app shell.
3.  **Opportunity Cost:** $19B could fund massive internal R&D or acquire multiple smaller competitors. Betting the company's largest ever spend on an unproven monetization strategy is reckless.
4.  **Regulatory & Privacy:** Acquiring a privacy-focused, fee-based service invites immediate regulatory scrutiny and potential user backlash if the buyer attempts to integrate data or inject ads.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Risk slow displacement, but preserve $19B cash.
*   **Build/Buy Smaller:** Develop own messaging or buy cheaper, monetizable apps.
*   **Partnership:** Integrate APIs without full ownership.

**Pre-mortem:** In two years, the founders have quit, users revolt against ads, regulators block data integration, and the $19B is written down as goodwill impairment while the core business continues to erode.

**Blocker:** The absence of any credible monetization plan combined with a price tag representing a 1200% premium in one year creates a financial hole that cannot be justified by "fear" alone. The math does not work; the upside is entirely theoretical while the downside is a guaranteed massive loss of shareholder value.

**Kill Criterion:** If user growth slows by >10% in the first quarter post-acquisition or if founders depart within 12 months, the thesis fails immediately.

DECISION: NO-GO
