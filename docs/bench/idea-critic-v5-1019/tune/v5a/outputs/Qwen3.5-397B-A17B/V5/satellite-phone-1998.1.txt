First call: NO-GO. The unit economics and market trajectory contradict the core premise.

**Steelman:** A premium, niche service for global travelers can generate sufficient margin before terrestrial networks achieve ubiquitous coverage.

**Attack:**
1.  **Market Shrinkage:** The target is "international business travelers," yet ground cellular is "spreading fast." Every month of delay shrinks the addressable market. The proposal admits sales channels are "not ready," guaranteeing a slow start that contradicts the need for "fast subscriber growth" to service borrowed capital.
2.  **Product-Market Fit:** At $3,000 per handset and high per-minute costs, the product is a luxury toy. Bulkiness and poor indoor performance are fatal flaws for a premium business tool, which demands reliability and convenience.
3.  **Financial Suicide:** With $5 billion mostly borrowed, the burn rate for debt service is massive. A slow ramp-up due to channel unavailability and price resistance will trigger a liquidity crisis before critical mass is reached. The "several dollars a minute" pricing accelerates churn once cheaper roaming agreements inevitably emerge between expanding ground carriers.
4.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" regarding this specific launch avoids sinking further capital into a dying niche. A cheaper alternative is waiting for technology to mature (smaller handsets) or pivoting to government/military contracts where price is less sensitive, though the debt structure likely prevents this pause.

**Pre-mortem:** The company launches, misses subscriber targets due to price and channel friction, cannot service debt, and declares bankruptcy within two years as ground coverage reaches 80% of business hubs.

**Blockers:**
*   **Financial:** Debt service requires immediate scale; sales channels are unready, making immediate scale impossible.
*   **Strategic:** The core value prop (global reach) is being eradicated by the rapid expansion of the competitor (ground cellular) faster than this network can monetize.
*   **Product:** High price + poor usability (bulk/indoor) = no sustainable demand in the target segment.

**Kill Criteria:** If the sales channels cannot be fully operational and stocked within 90 days, the cash burn will exceed recovery potential. The current state shows they are not ready.

The combination of unready distribution, prohibitive pricing, and a rapidly closing market window creates an insurmountable path to profitability before debt defaults.

DECISION: NO-GO
