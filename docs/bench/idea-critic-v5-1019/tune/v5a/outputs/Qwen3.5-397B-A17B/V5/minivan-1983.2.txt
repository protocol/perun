First call: GO. The proposal leverages existing assets to address a clear market gap for families needing garage-compatible capacity with car-like drivability, turning a survival situation into a focused product bet.

**Steelman:** Reusing the compact platform and converting a factory minimizes capital expenditure while creating a unique "garageable" family vehicle that rivals cannot immediately copy due to the specific platform integration.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Reliance on traditional wagons/vans risks irrelevance as competitors eventually downsize; cash burn continues without a differentiator.
*   **New Platform:** Prohibitively expensive given the cash constraint; too slow.
*   **Commercial Van Perception:** A valid risk, but mitigated by marketing the vehicle strictly as a family "mini-van" with car interiors, distinguishing it from utilitarian work vans.
*   **Space Constraints:** The "compact" limitation is the trade-off for garage fit; if the target is families (not large crews), the space is sufficient.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the brand cannot shake its "cheap commercial" image or if the converted factory faces delays. However, the government guarantee provides the runway to execute the conversion. The risk of rivals copying is low in the short term due to the lead time required to retool for front-wheel-drive family vans.

**Blockers vs. Risks:**
*   **Blockers:** NONE. The cash constraint is addressed by reusing assets; the "no category" issue is the opportunity, not a blocker.
*   **Risks:** Market education (family vs. commercial), platform space limits. These are managed by positioning and the specific "garage" value proposition.

**Kill Criterion:** If pre-orders from family-focused dealerships do not reach 5,000 units within 90 days of reveal, pivot to fleet sales or halt production expansion.

The proposal creates a new category using sunk costs and available capacity. It beats "do nothing" by offering a defensible niche with minimal incremental investment.

DECISION: GO
