First call: NO-GO. The plan attempts to solve multiple unproven variables (product-market fit, logistics, unit economics) across two distinct continents simultaneously, multiplying execution risk beyond the capacity of typical venture funding.

**Steelman:** Simultaneous global launch captures maximum market share before competitors emerge, leveraging first-mover advantage in online fashion.

**Attack:**
1.  **Fit & Evidence:** Online fashion buying is explicitly "unproven" (stated fact). Launching in Europe and America doubles the complexity of validating this single core hypothesis. If the model fails in one region, the capital burn from the second region may bankrupt the firm before learning occurs.
2.  **Options:**
    *   *Do nothing:* Miss the window, but preserve capital.
    *   *Single region (e.g., UK or Germany):* Validates logistics and demand with half the operational overhead. Reversibility is high; expansion is possible post-validation.
    *   *Proposed (Both):* Highest cost, highest risk, lowest reversibility. It does not beat the single-region alternative on stated facts because the upside (market share) is irrelevant if the company insolvates due to unproven demand.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** The company runs out of venture money 18 months in. Cause: Logistics costs in the US drained funds needed to fix conversion issues in Europe. Both markets stall.
4.  **Blocker Test:** The proposal states "online fashion buying is unproven." A plan that commits to massive fixed costs (offices in several countries) before proving the core transaction mechanism contradicts the stated reality. The risk of capital exhaustion ("venture money may run out") is not a mere risk here; it is a near-certainty given the multiplied costs and unproven revenue engine. This is a factual contradiction between the strategy (scale immediately) and the constraint (unproven model + finite capital).

**Kill Criterion:** If customer acquisition cost exceeds lifetime value in the first pilot region within 6 months, the global expansion must stop. The current proposal lacks this stop condition, committing to full scale before the metric can be read.

**Decision:** The plan fails the blocker test. It treats an unproven hypothesis as a reason for maximum scale rather than minimum validation.

DECISION: NO-GO
