First call: NO-GO. The proposal admits unproven economics and a large short-term margin hit while offering no cap on loss or mechanism to validate demand before full launch.

**Steelman:** An annual membership drives loyalty and order frequency, offsetting shipping costs through volume and retention, provided the margin hit is temporary and manageable.

**Attack:**
1.  **Economics:** The proposal explicitly states economics are "unproven." Launching a company-wide financial model without a pilot violates basic operational hygiene. If heavy buyers exceed the $79 threshold quickly, every additional order deepens the loss.
2.  **Reversibility:** The proposal admits the offer is "hard to reverse." Selling annual contracts creates a liability; cancelling them damages trust and invites backlash. This is a one-way door with no exit ramp.
3.  **Missing Kill Criteria:** There is no pilot, no cap on membership sales, and no defined metric to stop the bleed. The "short-term margin hit" is undefined in magnitude or duration.

**Better Way:** Run a limited beta (e.g., invite-only or single region) for 90 days. Cap enrollment to limit exposure. Measure actual shipping cost vs. fee revenue per user before scaling.

**Pre-mortem:** The program launches, heavy users immediately exploit free shipping, costs exceed revenue by 40%, and the company cannot cancel memberships without PR disaster, forcing a multi-year bleed to break even.

**Blockers:**
*   **Unproven Economics:** The core financial premise is admitted as unknown. Launching without validation is speculation, not strategy.
*   **No Stop Condition:** The proposal lacks a kill criterion (e.g., "if loss > X% after Y months, terminate"). Committing to an irreversible, uncapped loss is a blocker.

**Risks:** Margin erosion, brand damage if revoked, operational strain from increased volume.

**Kill Criterion Required:** A capped pilot with a specific loss threshold and date to pivot or stop. Without this, the risk is uncapped.

The proposal demands approval for an irreversible, uncapped financial experiment based on unproven math. It fails the kill check: it commits to full scale before a working stop condition exists.

DECISION: NO-GO
