First call: NO-GO. The unit economics and market trajectory described make immediate commercial launch financially unsustainable.

**Steelman:** A premium, niche service for global travelers who value ubiquitous coverage over cost and device ergonomics, capturing early adopters before ground networks mature.

**Attack:**
1.  **Market Erosion:** Ground cellular is spreading "fast." A high-cost, slow-build satellite network competes against a rapidly improving, cheaper substitute. By the time coverage is global, the addressable market of travelers willing to pay $3,000 for a bulky handset and high per-minute rates will have collapsed.
2.  **Debt vs. Growth Mismatch:** With $5 billion in borrowed capital, debt service requires massive, immediate subscriber growth. The proposed price point ($3,000 hardware + expensive calls) inherently limits the total addressable market to a tiny fraction of business travelers. The sales channels are "not ready," creating a fatal lag between launch revenue and debt obligations.
3.  **Product Deficits:** Bulky handsets that fail indoors severely limit utility. Business travelers need reliability in hotels and conference centers, exactly where this system fails.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing/Halt:** Stop commercial launch to avoid burning remaining capital on a failing go-to-market strategy.
*   **Pivot to Niche/Emergency:** Reposition strictly for government, maritime, or emergency use where price is secondary to existence, abandoning the mass business traveler segment.
*   **Delay:** Wait for handset miniaturization and cost reduction, though this conflicts with immediate debt pressure.

**Pre-mortem:** The company launches, misses aggressive subscriber targets due to price and device bulk, defaults on loan payments within 18 months, and enters bankruptcy while ground cellular achieves near-global roaming.

**Blockers:**
*   **Unit Economics:** The combination of high debt service, limited market size (due to price), and unready sales channels creates a mathematical impossibility for profitability as proposed.
*   **Product-Market Fit:** Indoor failure and bulk negate the core value proposition for the primary target audience.

**Decision:** The proposal ignores the speed of competitive displacement and the rigidity of debt schedules. Launching now guarantees insolvency.

DECISION: NO-GO
