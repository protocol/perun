First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits to a massive, irreversible infrastructure build (new dealer network) and a fixed 1957 launch date despite an acknowledged recession risk and a segment that "could shrink."

**Steelman:** Launching a distinct mid-priced division with dedicated dealers captures the growing market gap before competitors, leveraging extensive research to ensure product-market fit.

**Attack:**
1.  **Timing vs. Irreversibility:** The plan admits a recession risk and potential segment contraction yet locks in a high-fixed-cost dealer network and a hard 1957 debut. If the economy turns in 1957 (a known cyclical possibility), the brand launches into a shrinking market with unamortized infrastructure costs.
2.  **Cheaper Alternative:** The proposal ignores staging. A "go-if" approach using existing dealer networks or a limited model rollout would test demand without the sunk cost of a full network build. The current plan forces "all-in" capital expenditure before verifying the segment's resilience to a downturn.
3.  **Cannibalization:** With overlap risks noted against existing low/premium lines, a new brand dilutes focus. Without a confirmed economic upturn, internal competition for shelf space and marketing dollars becomes a zero-sum game during a contraction.

**Pre-mortem:** It is 1958. A recession hit in late 1957. Mid-priced sales collapsed as consumers traded down to economy cars. The company is stuck with hundreds of underutilized, contract-bound dealerships and a brand image associated with failure, draining cash from the core business.

**Blocker:** The commitment to a full dealer network and fixed launch date prior to resolving the recession risk constitutes a failure of the "stop condition" test. There is no capped, time-boxed first step; the plan bets the farm on a specific economic window that the proposal itself admits is uncertain. A sound operator delays the irreversible infrastructure spend until the economic outlook clears or adopts a reversible, low-capex entry strategy.

**Kill Criterion:** Do not sign dealer contracts or finalize the 1957 launch calendar unless leading economic indicators confirm zero probability of recession in the next 18 months. Since this cannot be guaranteed now, the plan as proposed fails.

DECISION: NO-GO
