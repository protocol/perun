First call: NO-GO. The proposal attempts to solve three hard problems simultaneously (new country, unproven supply chain, new inventory system) at maximum speed, creating a single point of failure for the entire expansion.

**Steelman:** Rapidly securing 130–150 sites creates an immediate scale advantage against the entrenched competitor, leveraging the retailer's domestic loyalty to disrupt a high-price market before rivals can react.

**Attack:**
1.  **Coupled Risk:** Launching a new international operation while simultaneously deploying an unproven inventory system and building a cross-border supply chain violates basic operational hygiene. If the new system fails (high probability for "not yet proven" tech), there is no fallback, and 150 stores face stockouts immediately.
2.  **Pace vs. Learning:** Opening nearly all stores within two years removes the ability to learn from early mistakes. Errors in bilingual labeling, regulation, or logistics discovered in month 6 will be replicated across 100+ subsequent openings, compounding losses.
3.  **Competitive Reality:** An entrenched dominant competitor with lower prices on some goods can easily engage in localized price wars to starve the new entrant during its fragile ramp-up phase. The retailer's "loyal domestic following" offers no shield in a new country where brand recognition is untested.
4.  **No Pilot:** The plan lacks a controlled pilot. A "big bang" approach means the first sign of trouble is a systemic crisis, not a correctable anomaly.

**Alternatives:** A staged entry (e.g., 10–20 stores) using existing or manual inventory processes would validate the supply chain and local demand before committing capital to 150 leases and custom software.

**Blocker:** The dependency on an unproven inventory management system for a launch of this magnitude is a factual blocker. The plan provides no evidence that the system can handle cross-border complexity, nor a rollback strategy if it fails. Launching 150 stores on unproven core infrastructure is not a risk; it is a predetermined failure mode.

**Kill Criterion:** If a pilot of 5 stores cannot achieve 95% inventory accuracy using the new system within 6 months, the expansion halts. This proposal skips the pilot entirely.

DECISION: NO-GO
