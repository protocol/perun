First call: NO-GO. The proposal bets $1B+ on unproven demand for premium short-form subscriptions amid known fatigue and abundant free alternatives.

**Steelman:** A high-fidelity, mobile-native platform with A-list talent could carve a premium niche if users value orientation-agnostic viewing enough to pay.

**Attack:**
1.  **Demand Contradiction:** The proposal admits "no proven demand" and "subscription fatigue" while requesting massive capital. Free, user-generated short-form dominates; charging a premium for professionally produced shorts lacks a validated wedge.
2.  **Feature Insufficiency:** Auto-orientation is a UI convenience, not a defensible moat or primary purchase driver against entrenched competitors offering vast libraries for free or lower costs.
3.  **Unit Economics:** High production budgets + A-list talent + $1B raise implies an unsustainable burn rate before achieving the scale necessary to offset churn in a saturated market.
4.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" or licensing content to existing platforms avoids the colossal risk of building a new distribution network from scratch. A cheaper pilot (single show, existing platform) would test demand without billion-dollar exposure.

**Blockers:**
*   **Fact:** The proposal explicitly states "no proven demand" as a core risk while asking for full-scale funding. This contradicts the requirement for a sound investment thesis.
*   **Fact:** "Subscription fatigue" is cited as a rising concern, yet the model relies entirely on new subscriptions.
*   **Missing Stop Condition:** No kill criterion exists for the $1B spend. If the pilot shows low conversion, the proposal commits to full scale anyway.

**Kill Criterion:** If a capped pilot ($10M) fails to achieve >5% conversion from free trial to paid subscriber within 90 days, the project stops. The current plan lacks this gate.

**Conclusion:** The plan fails the blocker test. It commits irreversible capital to a hypothesis explicitly admitted as unproven, with no working stop condition. The "orientation" feature does not solve the fundamental lack of demand.

DECISION: NO-GO
