First call: GO. The hybrid model directly addresses the erosion of the handheld line by phones and the failure of the previous home-only console by offering unique utility that rivals cannot match, despite lower raw power.

**Steelman:** A hybrid device leverages existing first-party IP to create a new market category, prioritizing versatility and unique gameplay (detachable motion controls) over graphical fidelity, thereby neutralizing the mobile threat while recovering home console relevance.

**Attack & Analysis:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" guarantees continued handheld erosion and irrelevance in the home market. Building a high-power rival console ignores the company's recent financial losses and inability to compete on specs. The hybrid approach is the only path to differentiation.
2.  **Fit:** This pivots from competing on specs (a lost battle) to competing on experience. The "confusing positioning" risk is mitigated if the hybrid nature is demonstrated clearly, turning a potential weakness into the primary value proposition.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if third-party support remains non-existent or if the "thin" first-party lineup fails to showcase the hybrid mechanic. However, the company's own studios are cited as the launch vehicle; if they fail to deliver quality, no amount of hardware power would have saved the platform.
4.  **Evidence/Constraints:** The proposal admits weak specs and limited launch titles. These are not blockers but defined constraints. The price parity with powerful rivals is justified only if the hybrid functionality is perceived as superior value. The financial losses from the previous console make a safe, iterative approach impossible; a bold pivot is required.

**Blockers:** NONE. The stated risks (weak specs, supply constraints, third-party support) are industry-standard challenges for a new entrant or pivot, not factual contradictions proving failure. The "short window" is a reason to act now, not to stop.

**Risks:** Third-party abandonment; consumer confusion regarding the form factor; first-party games failing to utilize motion/detachable features effectively.

**Kill Criteria:** If pre-orders do not exceed the install base of the failed previous console by 20% within the first month, halt production expansion. Leading metric: Software attach rate for first-party titles in Q1.

The plan survives the attack because the status quo is already a failure mode. The hybrid concept is the only viable differentiator available given the financial and technical constraints.

DECISION: GO
