First call: GO. A closed, curated store converts the CEO's prior "web-only" stance into a defensible product moat, directly addressing stability and carrier concerns while capturing revenue from a rival ecosystem's chaos.

Steelman: By restricting distribution to a single, reviewed store, the company ensures app quality and security, satisfying carriers and preserving device polish, while the 30% fee monetizes developer access to a premium user base that open platforms cannot guarantee.

Alternatives:
1. Do nothing (Web Apps only): Lowest risk, but cedes the high-value native ecosystem to rivals and validates the CEO's potentially outdated constraint.
2. Open Sideloading: Maximizes developer speed but invites the exact security breaches and carrier conflicts the proposal seeks to avoid; likely destroys product polish.
3. Proposed Store: Balances control with innovation. It beats "do nothing" by capturing the app market; it beats "open" by mitigating the stated blockers.

Fit: Aligns with a "premium, it just works" north star. Open distribution contradicts the brand's core promise of stability.

Pre-mortem: Failure occurs if review bottlenecks stall launch or if the 30% fee drives top developers to rivals. However, these are execution risks, not fundamental flaws. The cost of delay is losing the developer mindshare to established rivals before the platform matures.

Evidence: The proposal cites specific, solvable operational risks (security, carrier relations) that the closed model directly addresses. Rival open ecosystems exist but suffer from fragmentation and malware, validating the curated approach.

Blockers: NONE. No fact presented shows the model cannot work; the risks are manageable via staffing (review) and negotiation (carriers).
Risks: Review slowdowns, developer revolt over fees. Kill criterion: If >20% of top 100 web developers refuse to port within 6 months due to fees, revisit the revenue split.

The "web-only" argument is obsolete given the market shift; this proposal corrects course without sacrificing the device's integrity. The 30% cut is standard for the value provided (distribution + trust).

DECISION: GO
