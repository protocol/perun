First call: GO. The hybrid form factor directly addresses the erosion of the handheld line by phones while leveraging the maker's strong first-party IP, creating a unique market position that rivals cannot immediately copy despite their power advantage.

**Steelman:** A differentiated hybrid device captures both home and portable markets, relying on exclusive software to drive hardware sales despite inferior specs.

**Attack & Options:**
The primary risk is that "modest-power" alienates third-party developers and core gamers expecting parity with rivals. However, history shows unique gameplay often trumps raw power when first-party support is robust. The alternative—building a powerful home console only—ignores the failing handheld division and invites direct, unwinnable competition with established rivals. Doing nothing guarantees continued erosion by mobile devices. The "cheap alternative" of a handheld-only device fails to capture the living room revenue and brand prestige.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
This fits a survival strategy: pivot from losing two battles (home vs. rivals, handheld vs. phones) to winning a new one (hybrid). If this fails, it will be because the price point is too high for the specs, or the game lineup is too thin to justify the purchase. The "confusing positioning" risk is real but mitigated by the clear value proposition: play anywhere.

**Evidence & Blockers:**
The proposal admits weak specs and a thin launch lineup. These are risks, not blockers, provided the kill criteria are met. The financial loss from the previous console creates urgency, making a safe, iterative approach less viable than a bold pivot. No factual contradiction exists in the proposal; the risks are acknowledged and inherent to the strategy.

**Kill Criteria:**
Proceed only if pre-orders within the first month do not reach 60% of the previous console's launch window sales, indicating the market rejects the hybrid premise. If third-party support remains below five major titles by month six, pivot marketing strictly to first-party exclusives.

**Verdict:**
The potential upside of creating a new category outweighs the risk of competing directly on specs. The plan addresses the specific failure modes of the past (financial loss, handheld erosion) with a novel solution.

DECISION: GO
