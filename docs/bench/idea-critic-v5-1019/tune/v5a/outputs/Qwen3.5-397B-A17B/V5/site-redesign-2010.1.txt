First call: NO-GO. The proposal combines maximum engineering risk (full rewrite) with maximum deployment risk (big-bang migration) while altering the core mechanic that retains the loyal community, all without a pilot or rollback path.

**Steelman:** A complete rewrite with a publisher-focused algorithm and immediate full migration is the only way to reverse traffic decline and compete with a rival.

**Attack:**
1.  **Alternatives:** "Do nothing" preserves the loyal base while testing smaller changes. A "staged rollout" or "A/B test" allows validating the new surfacing logic without alienating the entire community at once. The proposal fails to beat "do nothing" on risk and ignores cheaper, safer alternatives that reach the same upside (traffic growth) with capped downside.
2.  **Fit:** Changing voting mechanics contradicts the stated value of the "loyal community." If they value existing mechanics, altering them threatens the primary asset.
3.  **Pre-mortem:** The rewrite takes too long; the rival captures the market during development. Upon launch, the community revolts against the new mechanics and publisher bias, causing an immediate traffic crash. With no rollback and a rewritten codebase, recovery is impossible.
4.  **Evidence:** The proposal admits "no rollback" and "large engineering risk" as facts. These are not hypothetical risks; they are stated constraints that guarantee failure if the new direction is wrong.
5.  **Blocker:** The plan commits to an irreversible, full-scale launch ("move all users to it at once") before verifying if the new mechanics work. There is no stop condition. If the community rejects the change, the damage is total and unrecoverable. This lack of a capped, time-boxed first step is a fatal flaw.

**Blockers:**
- Irreversible big-bang migration with no rollback path on a core mechanic change.
- No pilot to validate if "favoring publishers" actually drives traffic vs. alienating users.

**Risks:**
- Community revolt due to changed voting mechanics.
- Engineering delays allowing rival to win.
- Rewrite complexity introducing new bugs.

**Kill Criterion:** If a staged pilot of the new surfacing logic on 5% of traffic does not show a net positive in retention and story submission within 2 weeks, the rewrite must stop.

DECISION: NO-GO
