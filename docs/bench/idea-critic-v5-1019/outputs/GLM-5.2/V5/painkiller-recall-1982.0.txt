**First call: GO.** People are dying from your product through no fault of your own. The cost of withdrawal is finite and recoverable; the cost of inaction is unbounded — more deaths, criminal liability, and permanent brand destruction.

**Steelman:** A nationwide withdrawal and production halt, followed by a tamper-resistant relaunch, is the only response that (a) stops further harm immediately, (b) demonstrates to consumers that the company values their safety above profit, and (c) creates the conditions for brand recovery through a credible safety commitment.

**The hardest attack I can find:** The poisoning is local and post-factory. Withdrawing nationwide overreacts to a geographically limited crime, destroys shareholder value, cedes shelf space to competitors, and may itself amplify public fear — turning a contained incident into a national panic that needlessly kills the brand.

**Why that attack fails:** The "local" framing assumes the attacker's reach and intent are known. They are not. Every bottle is now a potential weapon and every consumer a potential victim. The company cannot verify the negative — that other metropolitan areas are safe — and cannot ask the public to bear that risk. The financial cost of withdrawal, however large, is a one-time charge against an asset whose value is trust. Competitors taking shelf space is a recoverable commercial risk; consumer deaths are not recoverable. And the concern that withdrawal "signals danger" is backwards: *not* withdrawing signals indifference to danger, which is the position from which brands do not recover. The relaunch in tamper-resistant packaging converts a crisis into a safety leadership story.

**Kill criteria already embedded:** The relaunch itself is the kill criterion — if tamper-resistant packaging cannot restore consumer trust, the product line ends. But that decision belongs after the withdrawal, not before it.

**Remaining risk:** The brand may not survive regardless. That is the cost of being the target. It does not change the call: you withdraw because people are dying, and you relaunch because trust, once earned through action, is the only asset worth recovering.

DECISION: GO
