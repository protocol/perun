**First call: NO-GO.** Spend and commitment are enormous relative to unproven demand; the economics must work first and they do not.

**Blockers:**

1. **Unit economics do not close.** ~$1.75B raised, high-budget commissions, two-country launch, 2020. Premium short-form at scale is unproven; recoupment on sub-10-minute episodes against nine-figure spend requires subscriber numbers the proposal does not substantiate. The cost-to-breakeven gap is the core claim, and it is not answered.

2. **Free substitute already dominates.** Social platforms serve free short video on phones — the exact use case. The proposal names this risk but provides no differentiation, lock-in, or conversion argument beyond "professionally made." That is a feature, not a moat against free.

3. **No TV at launch removes the premium anchor.** Premium streaming value perception is tied to the television. Launching phone-only with high-budget production creates a price-expectation mismatch: viewers compare it to free social content, not to Netflix. The proposal does not address this.

**Risks (kill criteria if pursued):**

- Subscriber acquisition cost vs. lifetime value: if CAC exceeds 12 months of revenue, kill or restructure.
- Monthly churn above 8% in first two quarters: indicates no retention loop.
- Ad-tier fill rate below 60% at six months: revenue model broken.

**Cheaper alternative:** Launch in one country with 5–10 commissioned titles, a capped budget (~$150–250M), and a 6-month pilot measuring conversion from free-to-paid, retention, and willingness to pay for phone-native premium content. If the pilot clears its metrics, scale to the full vision. This reaches the same learning at a fraction of the capital at risk.

**Pre-mortem:** The service launches, spends $1.5B+ on content and marketing, and discovers that phone viewers treat short video as free snack content — not a subscription category. Churn is high, ad rates are low, and the capital is gone. The one-way door on $1.75B is the problem; a staged pilot makes it two-way.

DECISION: NO-GO
