The proposal asks the executive team to commit to a January launch and large-scale mail-order sales of a novel vehicle with unresolved safety, performance, and market risks. The top speed of 15 miles per hour, short range, and lack of weather protection significantly limit the product's usefulness, especially in British winters. Safety groups may object to a low-sitting vehicle in traffic, creating reputational and regulatory exposure. The business model depends on large numbers sold by mail order, but the product's limitations and likely objections make that volume uncertain. Optional extras may push the effective price higher, further dampening demand. While the involvement of a large appliance maker and the sub-400-pound price are attractive, they do not offset the risk of launching a product that may be unsafe, seasonally impractical, and hard to sell in volume. The proposal should be revised to address safety certification, weather protection, realistic demand assumptions, and a more controlled rollout before approval.

DECISION: NO-GO
