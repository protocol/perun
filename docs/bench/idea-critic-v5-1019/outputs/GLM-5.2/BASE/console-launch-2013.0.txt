The maker's own studios having little new software at launch is a vulnerability, but the easy-to-build hardware should help attract third-party developers, filling the gap. The risk of consumers spending less on dedicated consoles is an industry-wide threat, but again, the aggressive price point and consumer-friendly policies give the maker the best chance to capture whatever demand remains.
