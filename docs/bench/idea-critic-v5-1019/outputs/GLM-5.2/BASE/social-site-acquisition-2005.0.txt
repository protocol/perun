The opportunity is a large, fast-growing audience that complements our existing advertising and media capabilities. Acquiring it would immediately strengthen our position with young users.

However, the price of 580 million dollars is steep for a two-year-old platform with no proven revenue model. The risks are substantial: user preferences shift rapidly, competitors are actively pursuing the same demographic, and converting audience growth into advertising dollars remains speculative. Paying this premium for an unproven asset is too aggressive.

A more prudent path is to revisit the deal with a structure that mitigates these risks—such as a lower valuation, earn-outs tied to revenue milestones, or a partnership that tests monetization before a full acquisition.

DECISION: NO-GO
