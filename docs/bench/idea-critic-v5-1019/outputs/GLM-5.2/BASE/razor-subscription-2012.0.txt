The dominant brands hold overwhelming advantages in scale, distribution, and marketing spend. Reselling a single outside supplier's products creates a fragile supply chain with no defensible intellectual property. A single low-budget video is an extremely thin acquisition strategy against entrenched competitors with massive advertising budgets. Subscription purchasing for razors is unproven and may face customer resistance. The threat of litigation or aggressive price cuts from incumbents could quickly erode any initial traction. The founders lack manufacturing control, brand differentiation, or a clear barrier to entry. Too many critical assumptions remain untested with no margin for error against well-funded competitors.

DECISION: NO-GO
