The proposal enters the most brutal consumer-hardware market with no phone experience, no app catalog, one carrier, and a price equal to incumbents with vastly larger app catalogs. The 3D head-tracking effect is a gimmick that risks nauseating users, and the camera-based product identification feature sounds like a solution searching for a problem. The membership bundling is a nice touch but insufficient to differentiate. The core issue is that a phone must win on the screen and apps buyers use most, and this proposal offers neither. The risks raised are not adequately mitigated. This is a one-way door with significant capital at stake and no clear path to winning against entrenched incumbents.

DECISION: NO-GO
