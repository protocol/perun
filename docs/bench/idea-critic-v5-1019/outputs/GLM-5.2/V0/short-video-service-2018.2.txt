**Claim.** Launch a paid, ad-supported short-form streaming service for phones, raising ~$1.75B, commissioning high-budget shows from known producers, spring 2020, two countries.

**Steelman.** Mobile video consumption is surging; premium short-form is an open lane between free social clips and long-form subscription services; marquee producers and a dual-revenue model (subscription + ads) could establish a defensible brand before incumbents notice.

**Skeptic.** The proposal assumes viewers will pay for short, phone-only content when free short video already dominates mobile attention. That is the core untested premise, and nothing in the proposal de-risks it before committing $1.75B. High-budget production for <10-minute episodes means per-minute costs rival prestige television while the format commands neither premium subscription pricing nor guaranteed ad inventory at launch.

**Better-way.** Cheapest alternative: commission a small slate of 3–5 shows on a fraction of the budget, launch in one country, test willingness-to-pay and ad-fill rates, then scale if metrics hold. This preserves capital, tests the riskiest assumption first, and avoids building full platform infrastructure before product-market fit exists.

**Kill-criteria.** The proposal names risks but no kill criteria, no capped first step, no leading metric with a review date. Without a defined "stop or pivot" threshold tied to a specific date and metric, $1.75B is committed blind.

**Strongest attack survived.** "Free social video already wins mobile attention, so paid short-form cannot compete." This *could* be true, but the counter—premium production values and known producers create a category free platforms do not serve—has not been tested and found false; it has merely been asserted. That is exactly why a smaller pilot must precede the full raise.

**Dissent ledger.** No willingness-to-pay evidence cited; no capped pilot; no kill criteria; no breakeven math against the $1.75B outlay; TV exclusion at launch narrows the addressable base further.

DECISION: NO-GO
