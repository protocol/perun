The proposal addresses previous failures by shifting from a standalone device to a low-cost, phone-dependent dongle. This eliminates the need for a remote and on-screen menu, directly mitigating the confusion risk cited in earlier attempts. By leveraging the user's existing smartphone or computer as the interface, the company bypasses the high development costs of building a proprietary navigation system.

The "very cheap" pricing strategy is critical. While margins are thin, the low entry price reduces consumer friction, encouraging adoption despite the lack of a dedicated remote. This approach prioritizes market penetration and ecosystem expansion over immediate hardware profitability. Given the company's core identity as a search engine, the true value lies in data acquisition and service integration rather than unit sales profit.

The risk of few supporting apps at launch is inherent to any new platform. However, providing an open kit for developers creates a pathway for rapid ecosystem growth without requiring heavy upfront investment from the company. This crowdsources content creation, potentially accelerating catalog expansion faster than negotiating individual partnerships. While established competitors have larger catalogs, their devices are likely more expensive and less integrated with the company's specific mobile ecosystem.

The lack of a remote is not a flaw but a strategic feature that forces integration with mobile devices, where the company already has strong user engagement. This aligns the television experience with current user behaviors. The financial risk is contained due to the low cost of goods; even if sales are modest, the capital exposure is minimal compared to previous device attempts. The potential to place video and music services directly into living rooms outweighs the initial limitations in app support and margin. The open developer kit offers a scalable solution to the content gap. Proceeding immediately allows the company to test this lean model in the market before competitors further solidify their dominance in the living room space.
