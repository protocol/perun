First call: NO-GO. The unit economics described guarantee that every small order loses money, and scaling this model multiplies losses rather than creating value.

**Steelman:** Rapid urban delivery of diverse goods creates indispensable convenience, securing dominant market share before competitors, with losses viewed as customer acquisition costs.

**Attack:**
1.  **Unit Economics:** Free delivery on small orders with no minimum means revenue per transaction is less than the cost of labor and transport. Unlike digital goods, physical delivery has high marginal costs. Every additional sale increases the cash burn rate linearly.
2.  **Operational Leverage:** Employing staff directly in multiple cities creates massive fixed costs and management complexity immediately. There is no asset-light phase to validate demand.
3.  **Behavioral Risk:** If usage relies on subsidies, the "product" is the discount, not the service. Removing subsidies likely collapses demand; maintaining them ensures insolvency.
4.  **Alternatives:** A "do nothing" approach avoids certain capital destruction. A better alternative is a single-city pilot with a minimum order value or delivery fee to prove positive unit economics before raising venture capital or expanding.

**Pre-mortem:** The company raises significant funds, expands to ten cities, and burns through capital within 18 months. They cannot raise more because the path to profitability is mathematically impossible under the current model (cost > revenue per unit). The brand becomes synonymous with wasteful spending.

**Blockers:**
*   **Negative Unit Economics:** The proposal explicitly states free delivery on small orders costs more than the margin. This is a factual contradiction of a sustainable business model.
*   **Premature Scaling:** Expanding to "more cities quickly" before proving a single profitable unit multiplies the confirmed loss mechanism.

**Risks:** Customer retention without subsidies, logistical complexity of diverse inventory (games, snacks, coffee) requiring different storage conditions.

**Conclusion:** The proposal asks to fund a mechanism designed to lose money on every transaction while scaling fixed costs. This is not a bet on uncertainty; it is a plan for guaranteed failure based on the provided facts. The model must be revised to demonstrate positive unit economics on a per-order basis before any capital is deployed.

DECISION: NO-GO
