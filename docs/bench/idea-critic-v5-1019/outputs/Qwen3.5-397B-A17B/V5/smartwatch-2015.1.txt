First call: GO. A phone maker extending its ecosystem to the wrist with a differentiated app store and material tiers is a logical defensive and offensive move, despite historical category weakness.

**Steelman:** This is not merely a watch; it is a mandatory accessory for the existing phone base that locks users deeper into the ecosystem while testing a new hardware category with manageable downside via multiple price points.

**Attack & Options:**
The "modest sales" of competitors reflect poor execution and lack of integration, not zero demand. A watch dependent on the phone is a feature, not a bug, if the phone base is large. Battery life (one day) is a known trade-off for functionality; it is not a blocker but a constraint to manage. Doing nothing cedes the wrist to competitors who may eventually decouple. The alternative—waiting for battery tech to improve—risks irrelevance.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
Fits the vision of a complete personal device ecosystem. Failure mode: The app store remains empty, making the device a expensive notification pager. However, the proposal includes "several materials and price levels," allowing a fashion-first entry even if apps lag.

**Evidence & Blockers:**
No internal data contradicts the claim that phone users want this. The risks (poor history, dependency, battery, need) are inherent to the 2015 context, not specific flaws in this plan. None are *blockers* because the plan addresses them: integration solves the "need" via convenience; price tiers address adoption; app store potential addresses utility.

**Kill Criteria Check:**
Risks are real but not proven failures. Demand is unproven but plausible given the phone base. Commitment is staged via materials/price tiers. No single risk meets the "three yeses" for a blocker (core claim contradiction, factual support of failure, irreversible commitment).

**Decision:**
Blockers: NONE.
Risks: Low app adoption, daily charging friction, category skepticism. These become kill criteria (e.g., if Q1 app downloads < X, pivot to notification-only focus), not reasons to stop. The plan is sound for the stated facts.

DECISION: GO
