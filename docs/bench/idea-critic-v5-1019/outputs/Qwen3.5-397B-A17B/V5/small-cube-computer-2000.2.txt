First call: GO. The company has momentum (11 profitable quarters) and needs a high-margin halo product to cement its premium recovery, even if unit volume is lower than towers.

**Steelman:** Launch a niche, design-forward desktop that prioritizes silence and aesthetics over expandability to capture a specific high-value segment willing to pay a premium, leveraging current brand momentum.

**Options:**
1.  **Proposal:** High price, fanless, non-upgradeable. High margin, strong brand signal, manufacturing risk. Reversible via price cut or discontinuation.
2.  **Do Nothing:** Maintain status quo. Safe, but misses the chance to define a new "prosumer" category and maximize margin on the recovery wave.
3.  **Cheaper Alternative:** Fanless version of the existing tower. Lower risk, but dilutes the premium signal and competes directly with the core volume driver.

**Fit:** Aligns with a "premium recovery" strategy by targeting users who value form factor and silence over raw specs/upgradability. It moves the north star of margin-per-unit.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if manufacturing defects in the acrylic case become visible at scale, destroying the "premium" perception, or if the price gap vs. the faster tower is too wide for the target niche to justify. This is a two-way door; if it fails, the line is killed with minimal long-term damage.

**Evidence:** The 11 profitable quarters provide the financial cushion to absorb a niche failure. The risks (cost, manufacturing, lack of slots) are inherent to the premium positioning, not contradictions of the business case.

**Blockers:** NONE. The cited risks are known trade-offs for the intended market positioning, not factual errors proving the plan cannot work. The higher price is a feature, not a bug, for this segment.

**Risks:** Manufacturing yield on acrylic; niche market size misjudgment. These become kill criteria: if defect rates exceed 5% or initial pre-orders miss targets by 50%, pivot or cancel.

**Decision:** The proposal correctly identifies a high-margin opportunity suited for a company in recovery mode needing to reinforce a premium brand image. The risks are manageable and the reversibility is high.

DECISION: GO
