First call: GO. The proposal directly attacks the previous generation's failures (late, expensive, hard to develop for) by prioritizing developer ease, lower price, and consumer-friendly policies (used games/offline play).

Steelman: By undercutting the rival's price and removing friction for developers and players, the console can regain market share despite a weaker online ecosystem and lower per-unit margin.

Alternatives:
1. Do nothing: Cedes the market to the rival; high risk of irrelevance.
2. Match rival features (online focus): Ignores the specific complaints about cost and restriction; likely repeats previous failure.
3. Proposed plan: Sacrifices immediate margin for volume and developer adoption.

Fit: Aligns with recovering lost ground by addressing the exact pain points of the prior launch.

Pre-mortem: Failure occurs if the price cut is insufficient to sway consumers who are already entrenched in the rival's online ecosystem, or if the lack of launch software renders the hardware useless regardless of price. However, the proposal explicitly allows used games and offline play, which mitigates the software gap better than a closed ecosystem would.

Evidence Check: The proposal relies on stated facts: previous gen was late/expensive, rival has head start. The logic holds: lower price + easier dev + consumer freedom counters "expensive/late/rigid."

Blockers: NONE. The risks (lower margin, strong rival online, weak launch library) are valid but do not invalidate the core strategy; they are trade-offs required to fix the previous generation's specific defects. Lower margin is the cost of entry; weak software is a temporary state solvable by the "easy to build for" promise.

Risks: Margin compression, slow online adoption. These become kill criteria: if unit sales do not offset margin loss within 4 quarters, or if developer uptake remains low despite eased restrictions, the strategy must pivot.

The plan addresses the root causes of the previous failure. Blocking it due to inherent risks would guarantee the status quo of decline.

DECISION: GO
