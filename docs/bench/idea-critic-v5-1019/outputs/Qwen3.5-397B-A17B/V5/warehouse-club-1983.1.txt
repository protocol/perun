First call: NO-GO. Entering a market against an entrenched incumbent with a copied model, unproven customer willingness to pay fees, and zero margin for error is financially reckless without validation.

**Steelman:** The model succeeds only if membership fees alone cover all operating costs and profit, allowing goods to be sold at near-breakeven prices to undercut the competitor on price while maintaining loyalty.

**Attack:**
1.  **Differentiation Failure:** The proposal admits a larger competitor already operates this exact format. Without a unique value proposition beyond "also low price," the new entrant faces a price war it cannot win due to the incumbent's scale and cash reserves.
2.  **Unverified Demand:** The core risk—shoppers refusing to pay upfront fees—is treated as a hurdle rather than a blocker. In 1983, the concept of paying to shop is unproven at this scale. If conversion is low, fixed costs of large warehouses will bankrupt the venture immediately given the "no room for errors" constraint.
3.  **Capital Intensity:** Large sites and bulk inventory require significant upfront capital. With thin margins, cash flow will be negative until massive volume is achieved. The incumbent can temporarily lower prices to starve the new entrant of cash flow before they reach critical mass.
4.  **Execution Risk:** The plan relies on perfection ("no room for errors") in a complex operation involving logistics, real estate, and member acquisition. History shows new retail formats invariably face unforeseen operational friction.

**Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing:** Preserve capital; avoid high probability of insolvency.
*   **Pilot First:** Open a single location with a strict kill criterion (e.g., 80% membership renewal rate within 6 months) before committing to "more warehouses."
*   **Niche Focus:** Target a specific demographic or product category the incumbent ignores, rather than a head-on collision.

**Blockers:**
*   **No Proof of Fee Willingness:** The proposal assumes customers will pay fees without citing evidence or a pilot result.
*   **Identical Model to Cash-Rich Incumbent:** Direct competition with a scaled player using the same low-margin strategy is a fatal strategic flaw.
*   **Zero Error Tolerance:** The financial structure cannot absorb the inevitable errors of a startup.

**Risks:** Customer rejection of fee model; price war bankruptcy; site selection errors; inventory mismanagement.

**Kill Criteria:** If a single pilot location fails to achieve 70% membership renewal within one year, or if customer acquisition cost exceeds 50% of the first year's fee, the project stops.

DECISION: NO-GO
