The claim is that a nationwide withdrawal, production halt, and advertising stop are necessary despite the poisoning being localized and post-factory. The steelman: immediate total withdrawal is the only action that guarantees no further deaths and preserves the brand's long-term viability by demonstrating absolute prioritization of consumer safety over short-term profit.

Alternatives considered:
1. **Local Recall Only:** Cheaper and preserves revenue, but risks missing contaminated bottles that traveled outside the metro area. If another death occurs elsewhere, the brand faces criminal negligence charges and total destruction.
2. **Public Warning Only:** Low cost, but relies on consumers noticing alerts. Given the product's ubiquity, this is insufficient to prevent accidental ingestion.
3. **Do Nothing:** Saves immediate costs but guarantees reputational collapse upon the next fatality.

Fit: This aligns with the north star of consumer trust. A painkiller requires 100% confidence; any doubt renders the product unsellable.

Pre-mortem: Assume the recall happens, yet the brand still fails. Why? Because the public perceives the poisoning as an inherent flaw in the product rather than external tampering, or competitors permanently lock in shelf space during the hiatus. However, the cost of delay (more deaths) is infinite in terms of liability and moral standing. The "signal of danger" is unavoidable; the choice is between signaling danger while taking responsibility or signaling indifference.

Evidence: The poisoning occurred after leaving the factory, meaning the supply chain is compromised unpredictably. Without knowing how far poisoned bottles traveled, a local recall is a gamble with human lives. The financial cost, while large, is recoverable; a loss of life trust is not. The tamper-resistant packaging relaunch addresses the root vulnerability exposed by the event.

Strongest attack survived: The argument that "competitors will take shelf space" assumes the brand will not return. If the brand is associated with death due to inaction, no shelf space will matter. If associated with a heroic safety stand, shelf space can be regained. The financial risk is real but secondary to existential survival.
