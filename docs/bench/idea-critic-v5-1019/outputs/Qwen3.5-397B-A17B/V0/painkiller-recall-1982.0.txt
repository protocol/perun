This over-correction treats a localized criminal act as a manufacturing defect. This contains the bleeding without self-inflicting a total market exit.

The pre-mortem reveals that halting production nationwide creates a supply vacuum competitors will permanently fill, making the "brand may not recover" risk a self-fulfilling prophecy. The cost of delay in launching tamper-resistant packaging is real, but the cost of a total shutdown today is existential. The proposal fails the fit test: it sacrifices the entire enterprise to solve a localized crime, rather than solving the crime's impact while protecting the broader business. The kill criterion for a total halt—evidence of widespread factory contamination—is explicitly stated as absent in the prompt.
