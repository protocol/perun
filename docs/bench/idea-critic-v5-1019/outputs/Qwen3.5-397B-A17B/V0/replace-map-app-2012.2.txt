The claim is that replacing a superior, established rival mapping application with an unproven internal alternative will increase control without causing unacceptable user backlash or functional degradation.

**Steelman:** Building an independent stack is the only path to long-term strategic autonomy, preventing a competitor from controlling a core user experience layer, even if short-term quality dips.

**Attack:** The proposal ignores the severity of the "less data" and "wrong information" risks. In mapping, utility is binary; a wrong turn or missing business destroys trust immediately. Unlike social features, maps cannot be "beta-tested" on millions of users without causing real-world harm (users getting lost, missing flights). The "annoyance" of removing a working app is not mere irritation; it is a functional downgrade that directly attacks the phone's value proposition. The reliance on "several data providers" does not instantly equal the rival's decade of ground-truth verification and traffic patterns.

**Better Way:** Maintain the rival app as default while launching the new app as an optional, high-performance alternative for early adopters. This allows data collection and refinement without forcing a broken experience on the entire base. Alternatively, delay the default switch until independent audits confirm parity in top 50 metropolitan areas.

**Kill Criteria:** If user support tickets regarding navigation errors exceed 5% of the installed base within 30 days, or if app store ratings for the map function drop below 3 stars, the default must immediately revert to the rival application.

**Verdict:** The proposal fails the "do no harm" test for a core utility. Swapping a mature product for an inferior one to gain "control" is a strategic gamble that treats the entire customer base as a testing ground. The cost of delay (waiting for parity) is lower than the cost of mass user frustration and brand damage. The plan lacks a phased rollout or a concrete parity threshold before the mandatory switch.

DECISION: NO-GO
