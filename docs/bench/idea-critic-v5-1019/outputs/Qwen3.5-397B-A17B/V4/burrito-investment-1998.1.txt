First call: GO. The asymmetric upside of a minority stake with follow-on rights, paired with independent operations, neutralizes the primary risks of a small, unproven concept.

**Steelman:** Acquire a low-cost option to validate a higher-price, made-to-order segment without diverting management attention or forcing immediate scale, preserving the right to dominate if the model proves viable.

**Options:**
1.  **Invest (Proposed):** Low capital exposure, zero operational drag, high strategic optionality.
2.  **Do Nothing:** Zero cost, but forfeits early access to a potentially disruptive format and cedes ground if competitors move first.
3.  **Acquire Majority/Full:** High capital risk, immediate management distraction, and forces premature scaling on an unproven 16-unit model.
The proposal beats "do nothing" by securing a foothold; it beats full acquisition by limiting downside.

**Fit:** Assumes the north star includes exploring adjacent price points and formats. A minority stake aligns with exploration without compromising core efficiency.

**Pre-mortem:** Failure occurs if the investor interferes despite the "independent" clause, slowing the chain's unique process, or if the "skilled worker" requirement proves impossible to replicate beyond 16 units. This is a two-way door; the stake can be sold or diluted if the model stalls. The cost of delay is losing the exclusive right to invest later if the chain succeeds independently.

**Evidence:** The facts state the chain is tiny (16 units) and slower. These are features of the pilot, not bugs. The higher price tests elasticity. No internal data contradicts the value of an option contract.

**Blocker Test:**
*   **Blockers:** NONE. No fact shows the investment creates immediate loss, violates a stated constraint, or that the "independent" clause is unenforceable. The risks (scale, labor, price) are hypotheses the investment is designed to test, not reasons to reject the option.
*   **Risks:** The concept may not scale; labor may remain too specialized. These are monitored via the minority position, not avoided by inaction.

**Decision:** Go. The structure (minority, independent, follow-on rights) specifically mitigates the raised risks while capturing the upside.

**Kill Criteria:** If, within 24 months, the chain fails to open 5 new locations organically or same-store sales drop >10%, exercise the right to sell or let the stake dilute.

DECISION: GO
