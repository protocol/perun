First call: GO. The immediate preservation of human life and the long-term viability of the brand outweigh short-term financial losses or local containment theories.

**Steelman:** A total nationwide recall and production halt is the only action that guarantees no further deaths, demonstrates absolute responsibility to the public, and preserves the brand's license to operate for a future relaunch in secure packaging.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
*   **Do Nothing/Local Recall:** Risks additional deaths if the poisoning spreads or if consumer panic triggers a de facto nationwide boycott anyway. One more death destroys the company.
*   **Competitor Threat:** Competitors gaining shelf space is irrelevant if the brand is legally or reputationally dead. Market share can be regained with trust; trust cannot be bought after preventable deaths.
*   **Cost vs. Survival:** The "very large sum" is an expense; a failure to act is an existential threat. The signal of danger is already present due to the deaths; withdrawing the product signals *safety prioritization*, not guilt.

**Pre-mortem:** If we limit the recall to the metro area and another bottle is found poisoned in a different state, the company faces criminal negligence charges, total brand obliteration, and likely bankruptcy. The cost of delay is measured in lives and the permanent loss of consumer confidence.

**Evidence & Logic:** The proposal admits the poisoner is unknown and the vector occurred post-factory. Therefore, the safety of any specific bottle outside the known zone cannot be verified by the company. The uncertainty is total. The stated risks (cost, market share) are financial; the counter-risk is human mortality and corporate extinction.

**Blockers:** NONE. No fact presented suggests a partial recall ensures safety.
**Risks:** High financial cost and temporary loss of market position. These are acceptable costs of survival.

**Kill Criteria:** If the company cannot secure funding for the recall, it must declare bankruptcy immediately rather than sell a potentially lethal product. The leading metric is zero new incidents post-recall announcement.

The proposal correctly identifies that the only path to eventual profitability runs through immediate, total altruism. Hesitation is fatal.

DECISION: GO
