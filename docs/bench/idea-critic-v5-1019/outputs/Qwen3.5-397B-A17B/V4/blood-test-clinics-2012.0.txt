First call: NO-GO. The proposal commits $350M to unproven technology with no independent verification, violating basic capital allocation discipline.

**Steelman:** If the device works as claimed, exclusive access could revolutionize preventative care and drive massive store traffic.

**Attack:** The claim rests entirely on the founder's word. In 2012, "many tests from a few drops" contradicts established physics and biology without peer-reviewed data. The refusal to share methods prevents due diligence. The supermarket lacks medical expertise to manage regulatory fallout or liability if tests fail.

**Alternatives:** 
1. Do nothing: Zero cost, zero risk.
2. Pilot: Fund a single clinic trial with strict milestones before scaling.
3. Wait: Let the tech company prove validity elsewhere first.

**Fit:** This diverges from core retail strategy into high-liability healthcare without capability.

**Pre-mortem:** Assume failure. Regulators ban the tests after false positives cause patient harm. The $350M is sunk into unusable clinic infrastructure. The brand suffers irreparable reputational damage.

**Evidence:** No published studies exist (stated). Methods are secret (stated). No medical expertise exists internally (stated). These are not risks; they are confirmed blockers to validating the core value proposition.

**Blockers:** 
1. **Unverified Core Claim:** The technology's efficacy is an assertion, not a fact. Investing $350M on an unproven assertion is financially indefensible.
2. **Information Asymmetry:** The vendor refuses to share methods, making independent verification impossible.
3. **Capability Gap:** The chain cannot safely operate or oversee medical diagnostics, creating immediate liability.

**Risks:** Regulatory rejection, false results causing harm, massive capital loss, brand destruction.

**Decision Logic:** A cheaper, safer alternative (pilot or wait) reaches the same upside potential without the catastrophic downside of a full rollout. The stated facts already show the target outcome (reliable, cheap tests) is unproven. The plan offers no route to verify the claim before spending the capital.

**Kill Criteria:** Not applicable as the proposal fails the initial evidence test. No metric can save a plan based on undisclosed, unproven science.

DECISION: NO-GO
