DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal fails on brand integrity, financial risk, and competitive reality. A loyal enthusiast base is the company's core asset; a heavy SUV directly contradicts the lightweight, agile DNA that defines the brand. This is a one-way door: a failed launch permanently dilutes brand equity, and even success risks alienating the very customers who sustain it. The first-year cost is high with no evidence of a profitable path against entrenched competitors who already own the luxury SUV segment. The proposal offers no kill criteria, no pilot, and no exit strategy. It is a bet on a market the company does not understand, using a brand it cannot afford to lose.
