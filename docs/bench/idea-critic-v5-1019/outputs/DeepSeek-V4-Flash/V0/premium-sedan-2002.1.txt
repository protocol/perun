DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal ignores the core value proposition of the premium segment: the badge itself. The risks listed are not hypothetical concerns but structural barriers. The mass-market brand name is the product's primary liability, and a dedicated factory with a worldwide launch locks in massive sunk costs before that liability can be tested. The low-volume, high-cost build guarantees a poor margin profile even in a best-case scenario, while the likely worst case—no badge acceptance—produces catastrophic losses. The proposal offers no staged entry, no brand-building bridge, no alliance with an existing premium player, and no exit strategy. It is a one-way door with no kill criteria. The correct move is to reject this as proposed.
