DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal relies on nostalgia and personalization to justify a price premium on a platform already sold cheaper. The stated risks are not merely possible—they are probable given the company's recent poor sales, which suggest weak brand pull. Buyers are unlikely to pay more for a retro-styled version of a car they can buy cheaper, especially from a financially weak maker. Thin margins on small cars amplify any miss, and the company cannot absorb a flop. The plan lacks a compelling, differentiated reason to buy beyond styling, which is subjective and quickly dated. Approving this now risks significant losses with no clear path to success.
