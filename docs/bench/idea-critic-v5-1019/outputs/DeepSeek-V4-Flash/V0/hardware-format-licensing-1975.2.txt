DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal underestimates the network effect of the rival’s open licensing. More manufacturers mean more players, lower prices, and greater consumer adoption, which attracts more pre-recorded titles. A proprietary format with few partners will struggle to match that ecosystem, regardless of superior picture quality. The one-hour tape is a hard limitation against a full film or sports event, a critical use case. The early lead is temporary if the rival’s format gains critical mass and becomes the standard, leaving the proprietary format stranded. The risk of being locked out of the market outweighs the short-term control of a closed system. A revised strategy—licensing broadly or matching the rival’s tape length—would be stronger. As proposed, this is likely to lose the format war.
