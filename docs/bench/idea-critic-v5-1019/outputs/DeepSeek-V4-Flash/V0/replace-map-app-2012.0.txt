DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal's core flaw is that it prioritizes control over user value. The established app has far more data, and the new maps risk being wrong in places—directly harming the core phone experience. Removing a working, popular default invites immediate user backlash, which outweighs the long-term strategic benefit of owning the mapping layer. The fall deadline leaves little room to fix data gaps or win back trust. The risk is high, the payoff uncertain, and the downside is a degraded product for millions of users. Approve only after data parity and a phased rollout are proven.
