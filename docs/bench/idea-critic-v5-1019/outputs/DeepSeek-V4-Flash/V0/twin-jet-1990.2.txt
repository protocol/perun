DECISION: NO-GO

The single-airline order of 34 aircraft is insufficient to justify the very large development cost. The program's viability rests on broad market demand, which one order does not prove. The unresolved regulatory approval for twin-engine long overwater operations is a critical, unmitigated risk: if denied, the aircraft's core value proposition fails. Airline co-design, while beneficial, introduces schedule and cost uncertainty that a launch decision should not absorb. The rival's competing models further fragment the market and increase the risk of a price war. The correct path is to secure additional airline commitments and regulatory clarity before committing the full program. Proceed only when those conditions are met.
