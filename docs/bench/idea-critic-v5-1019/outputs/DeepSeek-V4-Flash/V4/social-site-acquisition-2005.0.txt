DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal fails on its own stated evidence. The price is $580M for a two-year-old site with unproven revenue conversion. The risks listed are not hypothetical—they are the core facts: young audience, fast-changing tastes, direct competition, and no demonstrated path from growth to advertising dollars. The upside is speculative; the downside is concrete and large. The company has no evidence this acquisition will succeed where the risks suggest it may fail. The correct call is to decline as proposed.
