**First call: NO-GO** — the stated facts show a path that is late, thin on software, and margin-poor, but the attack below tests whether the upside still clears the bar.

**Steelman:** A cheaper, developer-friendly console with used-game support and offline play undercuts the rival on price and goodwill, wins back core gamers, and rebuilds mindshare despite a weak launch lineup.

**Options:**  
- *Do nothing*: cede the generation entirely; rival consolidates. Worst long-term.  
- *Proposal*: price below rival, PC-like hardware, used games/offline. Risks: low margin, no launch software, rival’s online lead.  
- *Alternative*: launch later with stronger first-party titles, accept higher price. Risks: rival grows further; misses holiday window.

**Pre-mortem:** It fails because consumers see no must-play titles, hardware margins bleed cash, and the rival’s online ecosystem locks in users. One-way door: this is a multi-year, capital-heavy bet; delaying a quarter costs momentum but not the market.

**Evidence check:** The proposal’s own facts — late, expensive prior gen, lost ground, strong rival online lead, little first-party software at launch, shrinking console spend — all point to a weak launch. The price advantage is real but insufficient without software. The numbers do not show a route to profitability or adoption within the stated constraints.

**Blockers:**  
1. Little new software at launch removes the primary reason to buy a console, regardless of price.  
2. Lower price plus thin margins, with no stated offset, fails the unit economics as proposed.  
3. The rival’s online head start directly counters the offline/used-game appeal for connected users.

**Risks (non-blocking):** consumer spend shift, execution delays, margin pressure.

**Kill criterion:** If first-party launch titles cannot be secured within 6 months, stop. Leading metric: pre-order uptake.

**Decision:** The plan as proposed does not overcome its own stated facts. It needs a revised approach — secure launch software or delay — before approval.

DECISION: NO-GO
