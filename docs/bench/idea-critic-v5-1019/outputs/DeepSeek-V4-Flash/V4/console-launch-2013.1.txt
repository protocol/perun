DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal repeats the previous generation's core mistakes—competing on price and hardware specs—while the stated risks show no plan to fix the real problem: a lack of exclusive, compelling software at launch. A cheap, developer-friendly box with used games does not counter a rival with a strong online ecosystem and a head start. Lower margin per unit plus weak first-party titles risks a slow start, and the consumer spending trend adds further headwind. The plan has no stated strategy to secure launch software, no timeline for online services, and no financial model to show how low price plus low attach rate is survivable. Approving this as proposed bets on price alone, which history in this proposal already shows failed.
