Evidence: The proposal states the site is fast-growing with a large young audience. No facts contradict the core claim that this audience is valuable to advertisers. The risks raised are speculative ("can change," "unproven"), not demonstrated failures.

Blockers: NONE. Risks: high price, taste volatility, competition, unproven ad conversion — all manageable through integration and measurement.
