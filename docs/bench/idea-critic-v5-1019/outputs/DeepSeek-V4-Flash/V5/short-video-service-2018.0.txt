First call: NO-GO. The economics and timing don’t hold up against the stated risks.

The proposal asks for $1.75B to launch in two countries in 2020, competing against free, established short-form video. The core claim—that viewers will pay a monthly fee for phone-only, under-10-minute shows—is unproven and directly contradicted by the popularity of free alternatives. The stated risks are not hypothetical; they are current market facts.

The production budgets are massive. Recouping that spend requires a subscriber base that is implausible given the free substitute and the lack of a TV option, which limits the service’s value for households that expect to watch on a big screen. The two-country launch doesn’t de-risk; it concentrates the bet.

The better path is a small, capped pilot in one market with modest budgets, testing whether anyone pays. The stated plan skips that validation and commits irreversible capital. The numbers don’t work as proposed.

DECISION: NO-GO
