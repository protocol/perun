DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal bets on picture quality and early lead, but the stated risks are not speculative—they are structural. The rival’s open licensing will flood the market with cheaper players, driving adoption faster than proprietary scarcity can. One-hour tapes fail the core use case (full films, sports), making the product inferior for the mass buyer. Fewer partners mean fewer titles, reinforcing the rival’s ecosystem advantage. The momentum is already against you: standards win on breadth, not just quality. Betting on a closed format against an open one, with a clear functional shortfall, is a losing position. Revise to pursue a longer tape and broader licensing, or pivot entirely.
