DECISION: NO-GO

Releasing in August to beat a rumored September rival sacrifices testing and validation on a thin-body, larger-battery, new-sensor device. A global volume launch with untested biometrics and compressed QA creates outsized defect risk. The upside of a few weeks’ head start does not outweigh a potentially costly recall or reputational damage. A safer path is to delay to September or later, or launch in fewer markets first.
