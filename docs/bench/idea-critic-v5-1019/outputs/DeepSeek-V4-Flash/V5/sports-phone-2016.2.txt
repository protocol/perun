The upside is clear: beating the rival to market could capture attention and sales momentum. The risks are real but not disqualifying—less testing time, a thermal/battery tradeoff, an unproven sensor, and high cost of a global defect.

The competitive pressure justifies the early launch; waiting would cede the narrative to the rival. The battery-in-thin-body and iris scanner are engineering challenges, but the company has shipped large-screen flagships successfully before, implying design and manufacturing maturity. Water resistance is a known feature. A defect at scale is costly, but the plan already flags it, and the company can mitigate with targeted testing on the highest-risk components and a staged rollout if needed. The downside is manageable relative to the strategic cost of letting the rival define the season.

The proposal is sound as a competitive move. The risks are operational, not fatal, and the company has the capability to address them. Approve as proposed.
