DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal's own facts fail to justify a nationwide withdrawal. The poisoning is confirmed only in one metropolitan area, and the company's plants are unaffected—meaning the risk is localized and post-factory. A nationwide recall, production halt, and ad suspension is a massive, irreversible response to a contained threat. The stated risks—huge cost, permanent market-share loss, competitor capture, and signaling danger—are not speculative; they are the near-certain outcomes of the action itself. The proposal offers no evidence that the contamination has spread beyond the one area, no plan to isolate and address the local problem first, and no kill criterion to reverse the recall if it fails. The upside (protecting public safety) is real, but it is already achievable by withdrawing only the affected metropolitan area's bottles, investigating the source, and communicating transparently with consumers there. A nationwide withdrawal sacrifices the entire brand and market position on a problem the facts say is local. The company can relaunch in tamper-resistant packaging later, but it cannot undo a self-inflicted national panic. The safer, cheaper, and more reversible path is a targeted recall plus a public statement that the threat is localized and being handled. The proposal's own numbers and scope contradict its justification.
