**Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Micro-Loan Auto-Rejection System**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record these specific risks for first-time borrowers in tier-two cities:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system penalizes cultural norms (e.g., shared device usage, community-focused posting, irregular internet access) as "erratic," systematically excluding specific castes, languages, or rural demographics.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Inferring "financial instability" from non-financial social signals (e.g., meme sharing, festival posting) without evidence.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are denied credit without knowing social media data was the decisive factor, preventing them from correcting misunderstandings.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Fully autonomous denial of essential credit (a high-stakes, life-impacting decision) without human review.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert fairness; measure it. Execute these tests immediately:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Create synthetic profiles representing distinct tier-two city demographics (varying language, religion, occupation). Inject known "stable" financial behaviors but "culturally distinct" social patterns.
    *   *Positive Control:* Profiles with known risky financial behavior should be rejected.
    *   *Negative Control:* Profiles with stable finances but high cultural distinctiveness must **not** be rejected.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure rejection rate disparity between demographic slices.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the rejection rate for any cultural subgroup exceeds the baseline by >5% without a verified financial cause, **block launch**.
*   **Data Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** and **Data Card**.
    *   Document intended use (micro-loan triage) and out-of-scope uses (general credit scoring).
    *   List data provenance: Which social platforms? What specific metrics define "erratic"?
    *   Mark unmeasured fairness metrics as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% bias" claim.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Critical):** Given the high stakes of denying essential credit, **disable full auto-rejection**.
    *   *Requirement:* Any application flagged for "social media instability" must route to a human underwriter for final review before denial. Autonomous denial of credit is the failure mode to prevent.
*   **Uncertainty & Override:** Do not display a raw confidence score (e.g., "87% risky") to users or underwriters, as this implies false precision.
    *   Use defined coarse tiers: "Verified Stability," "Corroborated," "Single-Source Signal," or "Unverified Social Signal."
    *   Provide a clear mechanism for applicants to appeal or provide alternative proof of stability.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly inform applicants that social media activity is part of the assessment. While legal necessity is a question for counsel, transparency is a mandatory safety control here.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name one engineer as the owner of the "Social Signal Bias" risk. They must re-evaluate the model if the data source or user demographic shifts.
*   **Regulatory Routing:** You mentioned a strict regulatory deadline. **Immediately route** the question of "automated decision-making compliance" and "mandatory AI disclosure" to legal counsel.
    *   Do not self-certify compliance with Indian digital lending guidelines or global frameworks (like the EU AI Act if applicable).
    *   Counsel must determine if this specific automated denial triggers regulatory reporting or prohibitions.

1.  Harm inventory signed off with worst-case scenarios documented.
2.  Auto-rejection disabled; all "social signal" denials require human approval.
3.  Adversarial testing completed with disparity metrics reported (or marked UNVERIFIED if testing was incomplete).
