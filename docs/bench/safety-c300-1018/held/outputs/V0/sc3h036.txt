### Critical Safety Warning: Do Not Launch as Planned

**You cannot rigorously validate suicide risk detection across multiple languages in three weeks with a limited budget and no professional cultural consultation.** Suicide risk detection is a high-stakes, irreversible domain where a false negative (missing a warning sign) can result in loss of life.

The harm inventory for your feature includes:
- **Bias/unfair outcomes**: Systematic failure to detect crisis in non-English speakers
- **Unsafe automation**: Providing inadequate responses to life-threatening situations
- **Over-reliance**: Users trusting the bot when human intervention is needed

1. **Implement a human-in-the-loop gate immediately**: Any conversation containing potential crisis indicators (in any language) must be routed to a human responder. Do not allow fully automated responses to crisis scenarios.

### Essential Reality Check

No amount of open-resource testing can substitute for professional cultural consultation in suicide prevention. Language nuances, cultural expressions of distress, and local context dramatically affect crisis detection.

**Your launch decision must acknowledge**: You are deploying a system with known, unmeasured gaps in life-critical functionality. The only ethically defensible position is to either:
- Delay launch until proper validation is complete
- Launch with all non-English crisis conversations immediately routed to humans
- Limit the bot's scope explicitly to non-crisis support with clear disclaimers

The university campaign timing does not override the requirement for human oversight in high-stakes scenarios. A delayed launch is preferable to preventable harm.
