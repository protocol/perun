### Rapid Pre-Launch Safety Review Plan: Pesticide Dosing Recommender

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory (Day 1)**
Before testing, explicitly list the worst-case scenarios your logic could cause. Do not assume safety. Record:
*   **Over-dosing:** Leading to crop phytotoxicity (burning/death) or illegal residue levels.
*   **Under-dosing:** Leading to pest resistance or total crop loss.
*   **Wrong Chemical:** Recommending a pesticide banned for the specific crop or region.
*   **Hallucinated Advice:** Generating plausible-sounding but factually incorrect mixing ratios or safety warnings.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Farmers believing the advice is infallible expert opinion rather than an algorithmic suggestion.

**2. "Zero-Budget" Output Evaluation (Days 2–4)**
You cannot wait for perfect metrics. Use your existing data logs to run adversarial probes immediately. Create a spreadsheet with one row per harm identified above.
*   **Probe Set:** Extract 50 edge cases from your logs (e.g., rare crops, extreme weather conditions, mixed pest infestations).
*   **Positive Controls:** Manually insert 10 known-bad inputs (e.g., "Apply 10x dose," "Use banned chemical X on crop Y") to verify the system *rejects* or *flags* them. If it complies, the gate is broken.
*   **Negative Controls:** Use 10 standard, verified scenarios to ensure the system still works for normal cases.
*   **Metric:** Pass/Fail per row. **Threshold:** Zero tolerance for any output that suggests a banned chemical or an irreversible over-dose.
*   **Stop Condition:** If any positive control passes (i.e., the AI gives the bad advice), launch is blocked until the logic is patched.

**3. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Launch Requirement)**
Because incorrect dosing causes irreversible financial and biological damage, **fully automated execution is unsafe.**
*   **The Control:** For any recommendation that deviates from standard baseline doses by >20%, or involves high-toxicity chemicals, the system must pause and require explicit human confirmation before showing the advice to the farmer.
*   **Implementation:** If code changes are too hard in two weeks, default the system to "Draft Mode" for the first 1,000 interactions, where a human agronomist reviews the output before it is sent via SMS/app.

**4. Transparency & Uncertainty Artifacts**
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Do not show raw probabilities (e.g., "87% confident"). Instead, label outputs as **"Verified"** (matches established guidelines), **"Estimated"** (based on similar cases), or **"Unverified"** (no close match in logs). If "Unverified," the system must advise consulting a local expert.
*   **Model Card (Lite):** Create a one-page document attached to the tool stating: intended use (smallholder dosing only), known limitations (does not account for soil pH if not provided), and the fact that it is an AI assistant, not a certified agronomist.
*   **Disclosure:** Every message must start with: "AI-generated advice. Verify with local experts before mixing."

**5. Governance**
*   Name one engineer as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing any failure logs daily post-launch.
*   **Legal Routing:** You must consult counsel immediately regarding liability for crop damage and whether this constitutes a regulated automated decision in India. Do not assume you are exempt.

**Deliverable Status:** This plan identifies critical failure modes and enforces a human gate on high-stakes actions using only your current team and logs.

**Note:** Never fabricate a "0% error rate"; report any untested scenarios as "UNVERIFIED." If the system cannot distinguish between a banned and allowed chemical in your probes, do not launch.
