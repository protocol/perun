### Immediate Risk Mitigation Workflow for EduSpark Beta

**1. Halt High-Stakes Automation (Do This Today)**
Immediately disable any feature that automatically displays generated stories to students without human intervention. Given the reports of inappropriate and factually distorted content regarding sensitive history, **fully autonomous delivery is unsafe**. Implement a mandatory "Human-in-the-Loop" gate: every generated story must be reviewed and explicitly approved by a team member before it is shown to a child. With a team of four, this is your primary defense; do not attempt to automate safety checks you cannot verify.

**2. Construct a Harm Inventory (Map)**
Before auditing logs, define exactly what you are looking for. Create a simple shared document listing these specific failure modes:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Does the narrative disadvantage or stereotype specific groups?
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Are fabricated events presented as historical truth?
*   **Inappropriate Content:** Is there violence, hate speech, or age-inappropriate themes?
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Is it clear to the teacher/user that this is AI-generated?
For each category, note the worst realistic case (e.g., "Student learns a fabricated atrocity as fact"). An empty list is not a pass; you must actively populate this based on your observed failures.

**3. Execute a Targeted Log Audit (Measure)**
Do not attempt to read every log. Instead, run a focused "red team" exercise using your existing team:
*   **Probe Set:** Have two team members generate stories using prompts specifically designed to trigger the known failures (e.g., "Tell a story about [Sensitive Event] from the perspective of [Controversial Figure]").
*   **Controls:** Include "known-bad" inputs (prompts you know previously failed) and "known-good" inputs (simple, non-sensitive topics) to verify the system's variance.
*   **Metric:** Record the pass/fail rate for each harm category defined in step 2.
*   **Stop Condition:** If *any* generated story contains severe inappropriate content or dangerous historical distortion during this test, the feature fails the audit and cannot be submitted in its current state.
*   **Reporting:** Report findings as "UNVERIFIED" for any metric you could not rigorously test due to time constraints. Do not invent a "99% safe" statistic; if you only tested 50 prompts, state that the risk remains unmeasured beyond those samples.

**4. Implement Uncertainty Tiers and Disclosure (Manage)**
*   **Confidence Display:** Do not show numerical confidence scores (e.g., "87% accurate") to users, as this implies a precision your system likely lacks. Instead, if you must show status, use defined coarse tiers like "Single-Source Draft" or "Needs Verification," accompanied by a clear visual cue.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label all outputs as "AI-Generated Historical Narrative" within the interface. While legal requirements for child-facing AI in the EU (such as the AI Act) must be confirmed by counsel, transparent disclosure is a critical safety control regardless of legal mandate.

**5. Create a Transparency Artifact (Govern)**
Draft a one-page "Model Card" for the school board submission. This document must include:
*   **Intended Use:** Generating draft historical narratives for classroom discussion.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state that the model may hallucinate facts and requires teacher verification.
*   **Measured Results:** Summarize the findings from your log audit (Step 3), clearly marking untested areas as "UNVERIFIED."
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for monitoring output quality post-launch.

**6. Route Legal Questions**
Do not assert internally whether your app legally qualifies as a "high-risk" system under the EU AI Act or if specific disclosures are mandatory. These are legal determinations. Note these questions explicitly in your submission materials as "Pending Legal Counsel Review" rather than guessing the compliance status.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes human review over automated fixes due to your frozen budget and high-risk domain.
**Note:** Never certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased"; only report the residual risks you have identified and the controls you have implemented.
