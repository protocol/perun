### Risk Assessment Checklist: Inactivity vs. Absence Simulation

To prevent the thermostat from misinterpreting inactivity as absence and causing dangerous overheating during winter storms, simulate the following behavioral patterns in your lab environment using internal telemetry proxies:

1.  **The "Stationary Elder" Scenario**
    *   **Simulation:** Generate telemetry showing zero motion sensor triggers for 4+ hours, but maintain consistent, low-level ambient noise or minute temperature fluctuations indicative of a living presence (e.g., breathing, small shifts).
    *   **Risk:** Algorithm interprets zero motion as "house empty" and ramps heat to "rapid warm-up" mode, risking hyperthermia or dehydration for an immobile senior.
    *   **Success Metric:** System maintains "Occupied-Eco" baseline rather than switching to "Away-Boost."

2.  **The "Deep Sleep" Winter Night**
    *   **Simulation:** Simulate a prolonged period (8+ hours) of absolute stillness during typical sleep hours, combined with a rapid external temperature drop (simulated storm).
    *   **Risk:** Algorithm confuses deep, cold-induced sleep with departure, triggering aggressive heating cycles that disrupt sleep or create unsafe humidity/temperature spikes.
    *   **Success Metric:** System recognizes the circadian context and external drop, maintaining steady, safe sleep temperatures without "absence" logic activation.

3.  **The "Illness/Recovery" Confinement**
    *   **Simulation:** Model a user remaining in a single room (e.g., bedroom or living room chair) for 12+ hours with no traversal between zones, typical of flu or recovery during a storm.
    *   **Risk:** Lack of whole-house movement triggers "Away" status, causing the occupied room to overheat while unvisited rooms freeze, or conversely, the whole house overheats trying to warm "empty" zones.
    *   **Success Metric:** System identifies single-zone sustained presence and adjusts only that zone safely, avoiding whole-house "empty" protocols.

4.  **The "Power Flicker" Data Gap**
    *   **Simulation:** Introduce intentional gaps in telemetry data (simulating storm-related connectivity loss or sensor lag) lasting 30–60 minutes, followed by resumed silence.
    *   **Risk:** The system treats the data gap + silence as confirmed absence, initiating a high-power recovery cycle immediately upon reconnection.
    *   **Success Metric:** System defaults to a conservative "Unknown/Safe" state rather than assuming "Away," preventing immediate aggressive heating.

5.  **The "False Negative" Motion Profile**
    *   **Simulation:** Feed data where motion sensors fail to detect very subtle movements (simulating sensor limitations with elderly users who move slowly), while power usage (lights, TV) remains active.
    *   **Risk:** Over-reliance on motion alone leads to false "Away" classification despite clear electrical signs of life.
    *   **Success Metric:** System fuses multiple data points (power draw, ambient sound proxies) to override missing motion data and retain "Occupied" status.

**Implementation Note:** Since external validation is prohibited, your "positive control" for these tests must be a hardcoded lab scenario where the ground truth is explicitly "User Present, Immobile." Any deviation into "Away" logic is an immediate fail.

**Safety Gate:** Do not deploy the "Away-Boost" heating function without a manual override or a secondary confirmation step (e.g., app notification) for users flagged as vulnerable in your pilot.
