### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Fitness Challenge App

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Psychological distress (disordered eating, severe anxiety) triggered by public shaming and social comparison on leaderboards.
*   **Affected Group:** Users with a history of body image issues or eating disorders.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Severe mental health crisis, hospitalization, or suicide linked directly to app usage; reputational destruction and legal liability for the team.
*   **Status:** This is a **high-stakes, irreversible harm**. The current design fails the safety floor.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
*   **Do not attempt to "identify vulnerable users" via segmentation filters.** Building a classifier to detect eating disorders without clinical validation is unsafe, likely biased, and creates a false sense of security. It also raises severe privacy concerns.
*   **Adopt a Universal Safety Approach:** Assume *any* user could be vulnerable.
*   **Testing Protocol:**
    *   **Red-Team Probes:** Run adversarial testing where actors with simulated body image histories interact with the leaderboard. Measure self-reported anxiety levels and urge to engage in harmful behaviors post-interaction.
    *   **Control Groups:** Compare engagement metrics between a "Public Shaming" leaderboard and a "Personal Best" only view.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If *any* participant in red-team testing reports severe distress or urges toward self-harm, the current leaderboard design **must not launch**.
    *   **Metric:** Zero tolerance for severe adverse psychological events. Do not invent a "safe" percentage of affected users.

**3. Mechanism Adjustments (MANAGE)**
Given the constraint of limited resources and the inability to build complex filters, you must change the **product behavior**, not the user screening.

*   **Immediate Action: Disable Public Shaming.** Remove features that highlight "lowest performers," "most weight lost," or negative comparisons. These are the direct causes of the identified harm.
*   **Replace with Personal Mastery:** Shift the leaderboard focus to **personal progress** (e.g., "Beated your own record") rather than social ranking.
*   **Opt-In Social Comparison:** Make public leaderboards **opt-in** only, with a clear, unavoidable warning about the nature of the comparison before joining.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any automated moderation of user-generated content on leaderboards, ensure a human review process exists for flagged terms related to eating disorders before content goes live.
*   **Uncertainty & Disclosure:** Clearly label the app as a motivational tool, not a medical or psychological device. Include a prominent, easy-to-access link to professional mental health resources (e.g., Swedish eating disorder helplines) within the app interface.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the explicit owner of "User Psychological Safety."
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Safety Card" for internal and external use. It must state:
    *   **Intended Use:** Motivation for general fitness.
    *   **Out of Scope:** Treatment of eating disorders or mental health conditions.
    *   **Known Limitations:** Social comparison can trigger distress in vulnerable individuals.
    *   **Measured Results:** "UNVERIFIED" for specific harm rates until red-team testing is complete.
*   **Legal Routing:** Immediately route the question of liability regarding mental health harms and required disclosures to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance internally.

**Definition of Done for Launch:**
The "public shaming" mechanics are removed or gated behind explicit opt-in; red-team testing shows no severe distress events; a Safety Card is published; and legal counsel has reviewed the liability landscape. If these cannot be met, the launch must be delayed regardless of the summer season deadline.

*Note: This plan prioritizes preventing irreversible harm over feature completeness. No metric certifies the app as "safe"; it only manages residual risk.*
*Legal determination of regulatory duties under EU AI Act or local health laws must be made by counsel.*
