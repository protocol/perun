**Residual Risk & Transparency Artifacts**
*   **Confidence Reporting:** Do not display raw model confidence percentages (e.g., "87% sure") to patients or clinicians. This implies a calibration unlikely to exist on constrained hardware. Instead, use defined coarse tiers: **Confirmed** (multiple corroborating signals), **Single-Source** (one signal, low power), or **Unverified** (data gaps due to power saving).
*   **Model/Data Card Requirement:** You must produce a versioned **Model Card** before the pilot. It must explicitly state:
    *   **Intended Use:** Adjunctive screening for silent ischemia in diabetics.
    *   **Limitations:** Explicitly list the specific older wearable models tested and the known performance drop-off in low-power modes.
    *   **Subgroup Performance:** Results sliced by device model and battery level.
    *   **Status:** Any metric not measured in the pilot is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a hallucination rate or bias metric.
