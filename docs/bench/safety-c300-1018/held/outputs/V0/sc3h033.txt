### Safety Protocol: Human-Gated Scholarship Selection

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition**
*   **Identified Harm:** Systemic bias against non-traditional educational backgrounds due to over-reliance on standard grading metrics.
*   **Affected Group:** Underrepresented students in Cape Town with valid potential but non-standard transcripts.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Deserving candidates are automatically rejected without review, reinforcing existing inequities and violating the program's core mission.
*   **Status:** **UNVERIFIED** (No external validation datasets available; internal tests indicate failure).

**2. Immediate Control: Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
*   **Rule:** No scholarship award or rejection is final until a human reviewer confirms the decision.
*   **Trigger for Manual Review:** **ALL** applications must undergo manual review given the known bias. Do not automate rejections based solely on the algorithm's score.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Reviewers are explicitly authorized to override the algorithm's recommendation if the candidate demonstrates merit through non-traditional indicators (e.g., portfolio, community impact, personal statement).

**3. Measurement Plan (Runnable within 2 Weeks)**
*   **Probe Set:** Isolate the top 20% of applicants flagged as "low score" by the algorithm but who self-identify as having non-traditional backgrounds.
*   **Positive Control:** Manually verify 5 known strong candidates with non-standard grades to ensure the review process catches them.
*   **Negative Control:** Manually verify 5 candidates with high standard grades to ensure the process doesn't introduce random noise.
*   **Metric:** Percentage of "low score" non-traditional applicants upgraded to "interview/award" status after human review.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the human review team cannot process 100% of the "low score" non-traditional cohort before the deadline, the automated scoring component is disabled entirely, and all applications default to full manual review.
*   **Owner:** Team Lead (name to be assigned).

**4. Transparency & Disclosure Artifact (Mini Model Card)**
*   **Intended Use:** Triage tool for scholarship applications; **NOT** a final decision maker.
*   **Known Limitations:** The model favors standard grading metrics and systematically under-scores non-traditional educational backgrounds. Accuracy for this subgroup is **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Data Provenance:** Trained on historical standard grading data; excludes external validation sets due to current constraints.
*   **Decision Process:** All outputs are subject to mandatory human review. The algorithm provides a suggestion, not a verdict.
*   **Version:** v1.0 (Pre-launch with known bias).

**5. Governance**
*   **Owner:** The Team Lead is accountable for ensuring the human gate is not bypassed.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any change to the selection criteria or the addition of new data sources requires re-running the measurement plan above.

**Note:** This protocol treats the algorithm as a flawed assistant, not an arbiter. Do not claim the system is "fair" or "unbiased" in any public communication; explicitly state that human review corrects for known data gaps. Legal counsel should verify if this automated triage triggers specific regulatory disclosures in South Africa regarding automated decision-making.
