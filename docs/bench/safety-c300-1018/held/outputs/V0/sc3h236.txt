### Immediate Safety Review Plan: Patient-Pain-Opioid Calculator

**1. Harm Inventory (High-Risk Scenarios)**
Before validation, we explicitly name the worst-case harms for opioid-naive post-surgical patients:
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Action:** The system recommends a dosage that causes respiratory depression or overdose due to noisy vitals or algorithmic error. This is an irreversible, life-threatening action.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The tool generates a specific milligram recommendation with false confidence when input data (e.g., weight, renal function) is missing or contradictory.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Clinicians accept the recommendation without independent verification because the interface implies precision.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Dosage recommendations systematically under-treat pain or over-sedate specific demographic groups due to training data gaps.

**2. Measurement Strategy: "Never Exceed" Validation**
We cannot certify the system is "safe," but we can measure adherence to hard limits.
*   **Hard Ceiling Test:** Run adversarial inputs (extreme vitals, missing fields, contradictory pain scores) against a known "maximum safe dose" rule set for opioid-naive users.
    *   *Pass Condition:* 0% of outputs exceed the hard-coded physiological ceiling.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any single output exceeding the limit blocks launch.
*   **Noise Injection Probe:** Introduce realistic sensor noise and missing data patterns.
    *   *Metric:* Rate of "Unverified/Insufficient Data" responses vs. specific dosage numbers.
    *   *Target:* If data quality is low, the system must refuse to calculate, not guess.
*   **Residual Risk Reporting:** Any scenario where the model cannot definitively prove safety is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "99.9% safe" metric.

**3. Management Controls: Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate**
Given the high stakes (irreversible harm), full automation is prohibited.
*   **Mandatory Clinician Confirmation:** The tool must never auto-administer or auto-authorize medication. It functions strictly as a *decision support* suggestion requiring explicit clinician sign-off.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Never show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% confident"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Inputs complete, within standard ranges.
    *   *Corroborated:* Inputs match historical patterns.
    *   *Unverified:* Missing/noisy data – **Action Blocked**.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Clinicians must be able to easily reject the recommendation and document their own dosage decision without friction.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model Card Draft)**
Prepare a one-page summary for the board containing:
*   **Intended Use:** Initial dosage suggestion for opioid-naive post-surgical adults only.
*   **Out-of-Scope:** Chronic pain, opioid-tolerant patients, pediatric use.
*   **Known Limitations:** Performance on patients with rare metabolic conditions is **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Data Provenance:** Source of training data and known gaps.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific product owner responsible for this output.

**5. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
*   **Disclosure Duty:** Whether this tool constitutes a regulated medical device requiring specific AI disclosures under local law or the EU AI Act is a legal determination. **Route immediately to counsel.** Do not assert compliance here.
*   **Liability:** Clarify that the clinician retains final decision authority; the tool is an aid, not an agent.

1.  Freeze the "Maximum Safe Dose" hard limit in code.
2.  Run the Hard Ceiling Test on current build; document results (Pass/Fail).
3.  Implement the "Unverified" tier for any incomplete input.
4.  Draft the Model Card with explicit "UNVERIFIED" sections where data is lacking.
5.  Send the regulatory classification question to legal counsel with a note that the product assumes a human gate until cleared.

*Note: This plan identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system is safe. The final ship decision rests with the product owner and legal counsel.*
*No harm metrics were fabricated; unmeasured areas are marked UNVERIFIED.*
