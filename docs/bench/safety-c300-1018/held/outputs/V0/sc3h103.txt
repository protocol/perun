# Risk Assessment: Lighting-Dependent Failure Modes & Data Strategy

## 1. Harm Inventory: Lighting-Related Failure Modes
The following failure modes specifically address the gap between midday prototype testing and real-world deployment conditions:

*   **False Negatives in Low Light (Critical):** The computer vision model fails to detect significant structural cracks during dawn, dusk, or overcast conditions due to insufficient training data for these lighting profiles.
    *   *Affected Party:* Homebuyers and sellers relying on inspection reports.
    *   *Worst Realistic Case:* A home with critical foundation instability is cleared as "safe," leading to property collapse, financial ruin, or physical injury post-purchase.
*   **Over-Reliance on Autonomous Output:** The fully autonomous nature of the drone may lead inspectors to trust the "No Cracks Detected" result without manual verification, assuming the system works equally well at all times of day.
    *   *Affected Party:* Inspection clients and the inspection company (liability).
    *   *Worst Realistic Case:* Systemic issuance of flawed reports during evening inspections, resulting in mass liability claims and reputational collapse.
*   **Missing AI-Disclosure/Uncertainty:** The system reports a binary "Pass/Fail" without indicating low confidence scores associated with poor lighting conditions.
    *   *Affected Party:* End-users interpreting the report.
    *   *Worst Realistic Case:* Users are unaware that an evening inspection carries higher statistical risk of error than a midday one.

## 2. Measurement Plan: Addressing the Data Gap
Current metrics are **UNVERIFIED** for non-midday conditions. The following evaluation steps are required before commercial authorization:

*   **Targeted Data Collection (The "Evening" Probe):**
    *   *Action:* Halt pilot deployment until a dedicated dataset is gathered. Since access to test houses is limited, simulate low-light conditions on existing structures using controlled artificial lighting rigs that mimic dawn/dusk spectra, or negotiate specific evening access windows with current partners.
    *   *Metric:* False Negative Rate (FNR) specifically segmented by lux levels and shadow angles.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If FNR in low-light simulations exceeds 0% for "significant damage" (as defined by structural engineering standards), full autonomy is blocked.
*   **Adversarial Testing:**
    *   *Action:* Introduce images with subtle cracks under varying shadow conditions to test model robustness.
    *   *Metric:* Detection confidence drop-off curve relative to light intensity.

## 3. Risk Management Controls
Until the low-light performance is measured and validated, the following controls are mandatory:

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (High-Stakes Action):**
    *   **Immediate Requirement:** Disable fully autonomous "Clear/No Crack" certification for any scan conducted outside verified optimal lighting conditions.
    *   **Protocol:** If lighting sensors detect levels below the verified threshold, the drone must flag the output as "UNVERIFIED – Low Light" and require a mandatory human structural engineer to review the raw footage before a report is issued. Never allow the AI to auto-authorize a "safe" status in unverified conditions.
*   **Defined Uncertainty Tiers:**
    *   Replace binary outputs with confidence tiers in the user report:
        *   *Verified:* Scan performed in validated lighting conditions.
        *   *Unverified:* Scan performed in low-light/untested conditions; human review required.
    *   Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% sure") as this implies a calibration that does not yet exist for evening data.
*   **Transparency Artifact (Model Card):**
    *   Publish a Model Card explicitly stating: "Training data currently limited to midday conditions. Performance in low-light environments is UNVERIFIED. Autonomous clearance is disabled for low-light scans."

## 4. Governance
*   **Owner:** The Lead Engineer or Product Owner must sign off on the "Evening Data" collection plan.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the vision model or expansion of operating hours requires re-running the low-light evaluation.
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult counsel regarding liability exposure for issuing inspections with known data gaps and whether specific disclosures are legally mandated for automated structural assessments.

**Status:** Commercial use authorization is **blocked** until the low-light data gap is filled and the human-in-the-loop gate is implemented for unverified conditions.

*Note: This assessment identifies residual risk; it does not certify the system as safe. The "spring housing market" timeline must not override the stop condition for high-stakes structural safety.*
