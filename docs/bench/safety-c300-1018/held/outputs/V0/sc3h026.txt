### Immediate Mitigation Strategy: Human-Gated Prioritization with Bias Safeguards

Given the confirmed use of flawed political affiliation data as a proxy for health behaviors, **fully automated deployment of this model is unsafe and must not proceed**. The risk of systematically excluding high-need neighborhoods based on non-medical criteria constitutes a high-stakes harm that cannot be corrected by software alone within ten days. The following strategy prioritizes human oversight and targeted evaluation to prevent unequal access to vaccines.

**1. Implement a Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate**
Do not allow the model to directly assign vaccine doses or lock out neighborhoods. Instead, reconfigure the system to output **priority recommendations only**. Establish a review board comprising local public health officials and community representatives to validate every neighborhood's priority tier before resources are allocated.
*   **Action:** The model generates a "Suggested Priority List."
*   **Control:** No distribution schedule is finalized without explicit human sign-off that cross-references the suggestion against known on-the-ground medical needs (e.g., hospital admission rates, clinic reports) that are independent of the flawed dataset.
*   **Safeguard:** If a neighborhood with known high infection rates is ranked low by the model, it triggers an automatic "High-Risk Override" flag requiring mandatory justification from the review board to maintain the low ranking.

**2. Targeted Adversarial Testing Framework (The "Bias Stress Test")**
Since new data cannot be sourced, you must measure the model's failure modes using the existing flawed data to understand the scope of the skew. Execute the following evaluation rows immediately:
*   **Probe Set A (Positive Control):** Input data for neighborhoods known to have high medical need but opposing political affiliations to the model's presumed bias. *Metric:* Rank deviation. *Stop Condition:* If these neighborhoods are ranked in the bottom 20% despite high medical need indicators, the model's output is blocked from auto-approval.
*   **Probe Set B (Negative Control):** Input data for low-need neighborhoods with favorable political affiliations. *Metric:* False Positive Rate. *Stop Condition:* If these are ranked in the top 20%, the weighting of the political proxy is confirmed as excessive.
*   **Probe Set C (Sensitivity Analysis):** Systematically vary the political affiliation input while holding medical indicators constant. *Metric:* Output volatility. *Goal:* Quantify exactly how much the political proxy distorts the ranking compared to medical inputs.

**3. Transparency and Uncertainty Disclosure**
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not present the model's output as a precise score (e.g., "Risk Score: 87%"). Instead, label outputs with coarse, defined tiers such as **"Model-Estimated (Unverified)"** vs. **"Human-Confirmed Need."**
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly document to all stakeholders that the current prioritization tool relies on imperfect proxies and that final allocation decisions are subject to human review to correct for known data biases.
*   **Artifact:** Create a temporary "Data Limitation Card" for this rollout, explicitly stating the use of political affiliation as a flawed proxy, the known risks of exclusion, and the specific human review steps taken to mitigate them.

**4. Governance and Ownership**
Assign a specific individual (e.g., the Lead Epidemiologist or Program Director) as the owner of the "Bias Harm." This person is responsible for reviewing every "High-Risk Override" flag and signing off on the final distribution list. Document this ownership formally; do not treat the review as a collective abstraction.

**Summary of Deployment Rules:**
*   **No** fully automated allocation.
*   **No** acceptance of model rankings for high-need areas without human verification.
*   **No** fabrication of accuracy metrics; report the bias as **UNVERIFIED** but **KNOWN**.

This approach accepts the data limitation but refuses to automate the resulting harm, placing the burden of correction on human judgment backed by targeted stress testing.

*Note: This strategy treats the political proxy as a known safety defect requiring a manual bypass, not a tunable parameter. Legal counsel should be consulted regarding potential liability for discriminatory allocation even with these safeguards.*
