### Safety Planning Document: CairoGut Fiber Tracker

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Definition**
*   **Primary Harm:** Unsafe automation of high-stakes medical guidance. Specifically, recommending high fiber intake to users with undiagnosed bowel obstructions, potentially causing perforation or severe distress.
*   **Affected Group:** Users with rare, undiagnosed gastrointestinal conditions who lack access to large-scale training data for detection.
*   **Worst Case:** Physical injury requiring emergency intervention due to algorithmic recommendation.

**2. Immediate Controls: The Human Gate (Pre-Launch)**
Given the three-week timeline and lack of training data for rare conditions, **fully automated recommendations for fiber intake are unsafe and must not ship.**
*   **Mandatory Screening Questionnaire:** Implement a strict pre-checklist. Any "Yes" to symptoms like severe abdominal pain, vomiting, inability to pass gas/stool, or history of obstructions triggers an immediate **hard stop**.
*   **Action on Trigger:** The app must **withhold** the fiber recommendation and display a static, non-negotiable message: *"Your responses indicate potential high-risk symptoms. Do not change your diet based on this app. Consult a physician immediately."*
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Requirement:** For users passing the screen but falling into borderline categories (e.g., known chronic issues without acute obstruction), the app must not auto-generate a plan. Instead, it must flag the profile for review by the partner clinic's medical staff before any advice is released. **No irreversible dietary action should be automated for high-stakes users.**

**3. Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
We cannot claim the system is "safe" without evidence. Before launch, execute the following:
*   **Probe Set A (Positive Controls):** Input 20 synthetic profiles mimicking bowel obstruction symptoms (pain, bloating, no output).
    *   *Pass Threshold:* 100% of these inputs must trigger the hard stop and withhold recommendations.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If even one profile receives a fiber recommendation, launch is blocked.
*   **Probe Set B (Negative Controls):** Input 20 profiles of healthy users or those with simple constipation.
    *   *Pass Threshold:* Recommendations are generated (subject to human review if borderline).
*   **Adversarial Probe:** Test if users can bypass the questionnaire by skipping steps or entering vague text. The system must default to "High Risk/Stop" on incomplete or ambiguous data.

**4. Transparency & Disclosure Artifacts**
*   **Model/Data Card:** Publish a one-page document visible to users and the clinic stating:
    *   *Intended Use:* Tracking fiber for general chronic digestive management, **not** for diagnosing acute conditions.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly state the system was **not** trained on rare obstruction data and relies on self-reported screening.
    *   *Uncertainty Tier:* Label all outputs as **"Unverified / Single-Source"** until reviewed by a clinician. Do not show confidence percentages (e.g., "87% safe"); use clear text warnings.
*   **AI Disclosure:** Clearly label all diet plans as "AI-Generated Draft – Requires Medical Confirmation."

**5. Post-Launch Monitoring & Governance**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the Safety Owner responsible for reviewing all flagged cases weekly.
*   **Adverse Event Loop:** Create a direct channel for the partner clinic to report any near-misses or adverse events. Any report triggers an immediate pause on automated features for similar user profiles.
*   **Legal Routing:** The requirement for specific medical disclaimers and whether this tool constitutes a regulated medical device under local law must be routed to legal counsel immediately. Do not assume the questionnaire absolves liability.

**Status:** The feature cannot launch with full automation. The "hard stop" on high-risk symptoms and the human review gate for borderline cases are mandatory floors.

*Note: This plan identifies residual risk as "UNVERIFIED" for rare conditions due to data gaps; do not fabricate safety metrics. Legal counsel must validate the screening questionnaire's liability implications before deployment.*
