### Mitigation Protocol: Human-in-the-Loop Verification for Wound Monitoring

**Objective:** Compensate for potential algorithmic bias (missed necrosis in darker skin tones) by enforcing a strict human verification gate before any "no intervention" decision is finalized.

**1. Mandatory Verification Steps (Per Patient Visit)**
*   **Blind Initial Assessment:** The nurse must visually inspect and document the wound status *before* viewing the AI's analysis. This prevents automation bias where the nurse unconsciously aligns their judgment with the tool's output.
*   **Dual-Channel Documentation:** Record findings using both structured data (AI score) and unstructured narrative notes describing color, texture, and odor specifically for areas of concern. If the AI reports "healthy" but the nurse observes discoloration inconsistent with the patient's baseline skin tone, the nurse must flag this as a "Visual Discrepancy."
*   **Explicit Override Mechanism:** If the AI indicates "No Intervention Needed" but the nurse suspects early-stage necrosis (especially in pigmented skin), the nurse must manually override the status to "Requires Clinician Review." This override triggers an immediate alert to a senior clinician, bypassing standard triage queues.
*   **Confidence Tiering:** The system must not display raw probability percentages (e.g., "87% healthy"). Instead, display defined coarse tiers: **Confirmed Healthy**, **Corroborated**, **Single-Source**, or **Unverified**. Any output labeled "Single-Source" or "Unverified" automatically mandates a second human review.

**2. Frequency of Manual Checks**
*   **High-Risk Cohort (Darker Skin Tones / History of Rapid Deterioration):** 100% of AI "Negative" results require manual confirmation by a senior nurse or clinician within 4 hours. No automated "all clear" is sent to the patient or primary care provider without this signature.
*   **Standard Cohort:** Randomized audit of 20% of AI "Negative" results daily. If any false negative is found in the audit, revert the entire cohort to 100% manual review until retrained.
*   **Longitudinal Comparison:** Nurses must compare current photos against the last three visits side-by-side, regardless of the AI's current snapshot analysis, to detect subtle progression the model might miss due to lack of temporal context or bias.

**3. Transparency and Disclosure**
*   **Patient/Family Notification:** Explicitly inform patients that the AI tool is an assistive device with known limitations regarding specific skin tones and that all diagnoses are ultimately confirmed by a human nurse.
*   **Internal Logging:** Every instance where a nurse overrides the AI must be logged with a photo annotation explaining the discrepancy. This creates the "harm inventory" data needed for future model retraining.

**4. Stop Condition**
*   If the rate of "Visual Discrepancy" flags exceeds 5% in any 48-hour window, the automated alerting feature is suspended entirely. All cases revert to full manual clinician assessment until the root cause is investigated.

**Note:** This protocol treats the AI as a "second opinion" rather than a primary screener for high-risk groups, ensuring the human remains the final decision-maker for irreversible health outcomes. Legal counsel should review the specific disclosure language required by the regional health authority.
