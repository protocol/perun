**Harm Inventory: Matching Logic Ignoring Safety Reports**
Your proposed feature automates high-stakes introductions while deliberately bypassing known risk signals. This creates the following inventoried harms:
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions:** Connecting users with individuals who have prior safety violations is an irreversible action with potential for physical or psychological harm. Automating this without a human-in-the-loop gate is a critical failure mode.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system systematically disadvantages users who have filed reports by exposing them to repeat offenders, while unfairly advantaging bad actors by granting them access to new victims.
*   **Over-Reliance/Automation Bias:** Users will trust the platform's "match" as an implicit endorsement of safety. By ignoring flags, the design invites users to trust the output past its reliability.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are unaware that the matching algorithm explicitly excludes safety report data from its decision logic.

**Measurement and Residual Risk**
You cannot launch with an unmeasured risk profile. To proceed, you must:
1.  **Run Adversarial Evaluations:** Create test cases where known bad actors (positive controls) are matched against vulnerable users. Measure the rate at which the system ignores flags.
2.  **Define Stop Conditions:** Establish a threshold where *any* match between a flagged user and a new user triggers an automatic block until reviewed.
3.  **Report Residual Risk:** If you cannot measure the failure rate of ignoring reports, the risk is `UNVERIFIED`. Do not invent a safety metric.

**Required Controls (The Floor)**
Regardless of your growth targets or team size, the following controls are the minimum floor for high-stakes automation:
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Any match involving a profile with a prior safety report must be halted and routed for manual review before connection is allowed. Fully autonomous execution of matches involving flagged accounts is the failure this design must prevent.
*   **Uncertainty and Override:** If a profile has ambiguous reports, the system must surface this uncertainty to the user or withhold the match, rather than presenting it as a standard recommendation.
