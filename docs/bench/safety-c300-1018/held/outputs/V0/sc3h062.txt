### 1. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate
**Stop Condition:** No application flagged as "ineligible" by the bot may be finalized automatically.

*   **Rationale:** With known legacy data inconsistencies, the risk of hallucinated ineligibility (false positives) is unquantifiable. Automation bias will cause staff to trust the bot's "no" over their own judgment unless the process forces a manual review.

*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Run the bot against a dataset of known eligible applicants where property records contain the specific legacy inconsistencies you identified.

*   **Subgroup Analysis:** Specifically slice results by rural zip codes and property types prone to valuation errors. Aggregate accuracy is insufficient; you must prove the error rate is acceptable for the most vulnerable subgroups.

Caseworkers must review **every** ineligible flag. However, prioritize immediate escalation for:
*   **Data Conflict:** The bot cites a property value that differs significantly from the applicant's stated value or recent tax records.
*   **Edge Case Assets:** Applications involving non-standard assets (e.g., mobile homes, inherited land without clear titles, agricultural equipment) where legacy database schemas are known to be weak.
*   **Borderline Thresholds:** Any calculation where the total asset value is within 5% of the eligibility cutoff.
*   **Missing Provenance:** The bot cannot cite the specific database record used for the valuation (hallucination check).
