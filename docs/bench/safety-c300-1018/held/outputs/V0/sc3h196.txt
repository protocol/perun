*   **Harm:** Unsafe automation of a high-stakes action (insulin dosing) leading to physical injury (hypoglycemia).
*   **Affected Group:** Patients consuming high-fat meals (e.g., pizza, fried foods).
*   **Worst Case:** Severe hypoglycemic event requiring emergency intervention due to over-correction or timing errors caused by the model's parameter limitations.
*   **Root Cause:** Model architecture inability to account for delayed gastric emptying in high-fat contexts via simple parameter tweaks.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure the failure rate specifically on this slice.

*   **Metric:** Percentage of high-fat meal calculations resulting in a dose deviation >10% from the clinically accepted standard.
*   **Threshold:** **Zero tolerance** for deviations that risk hypoglycemia. If *any* test case in the high-fat subset produces a dangerous under/over-dose, the automated feature fails the launch criteria for that segment.

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any input detected as high-fat (via keyword tag or low-confidence image classification), the app must **not** auto-calculate or auto-deliver a dose.
