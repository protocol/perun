**Pre-Launch Safety Protocol: MobilityFirst Balance Test**

**Critical Constraint Acknowledgement:** Computer vision (CV) systems can hallucinate stability or fail to detect subtle loss of balance. **No virtual confirmation can replace physical safety measures for high-risk fall prevention.** Given the limited inventory of harnesses and spotters, the system must operate under a "Safety-First Stop" logic where uncertainty defaults to halting the test, not proceeding.

### 1. Decision Logic Review Process (The "Virtual Gate")

Do not rely on a single confidence score. Implement a multi-factor gate that requires **all** conditions to be met before any exercise begins. If any condition fails, the system must lock the test and alert staff.

**Gate Conditions (All Must Be True):**
*   **Environment Verification:** CV confirms clear floor space (no obstacles) and adequate lighting *before* the patient steps into the zone.
*   **Spotter Presence Check:** CV must detect a human figure standing within the immediate "reaction zone" (arm's reach) of the patient. **If no spotter is detected, the test cannot start.** This compensates for limited harnesses by mandating human proximity.
*   **Baseline Stability Threshold:** The patient must demonstrate 10 seconds of static stability *without* assistance before dynamic exercises begin.
*   **Uncertainty Handling:** If the CV confidence regarding the patient's center of gravity drops below a conservative threshold (e.g., due to loose clothing, poor lighting, or occlusion), the system must immediately pause and request manual staff verification.

**Fail-Safe Logic:**
*   **Default State:** STOP.
*   **Trigger:** Any ambiguity in CV data = Immediate Pause + Audio Alert ("Staff Assistance Required").
*   **Override:** Only a human staff member can resume the test after physically verifying stability. The software cannot auto-resume.

### 2. User Instruction Set (Clinic Staff & Patients)

**For Clinic Staff (Mandatory Pre-Test Checklist):**
1.  **Physical Triage First:** Before turning on the system, manually assess the patient. If the patient requires a harness based on clinical judgment, **do not use the automated tool** until a harness or dedicated spotter is secured. The tool is only for patients already deemed low-to-moderate risk by a human.
2.  **The "Arm's Reach" Rule:** Regardless of what the screen says, a staff member must remain within arm's reach of the patient throughout the entire test. The CV system is a monitoring aid, not a supervisor.
3.  **Emergency Stop Protocol:** Keep a physical emergency stop button (or tablet kill-switch) accessible. If the patient wavers, hit stop immediately. Do not wait for the AI to detect the fall.

**For Patients:**
1.  **Disclosure:** "This tool uses camera technology to guide your movements. It is not perfect and may miss signs of instability."
2.  **Instruction:** "Perform movements slowly. If you feel unsteady, stop immediately and call for help. Do not try to 'beat' the test or rush through exercises."
3.  **Consent:** Explicitly confirm they understand a human is watching them, not just the computer.

### 3. Residual Risk Statement

*   **Unverified Metrics:** We have not measured the false-negative rate of this CV model in Cairo clinic lighting conditions. Assume the system **will** miss some instability events.
*   **High-Stakes Gate:** Because a fall is an irreversible, high-harm event, **full automation is prohibited.** The "human-in-the-loop" (the spotter) is the primary safety control; the software is secondary.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** A simple "Model Card" must be printed at each station stating: "Intended for low-to-moderate risk patients only. Requires constant human supervision. Not a substitute for physical safety harnesses."

**Note:** This protocol treats the CV output as advisory only. The lack of physical equipment increases the necessity for strict human oversight, not more automation.
