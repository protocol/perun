### Pre-Launch Safety Evaluation Plan: Geographic Bias & Service Equity

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record the following inventoried harms for the London deployment:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The algorithm systematically disadvantages specific postal codes based on correlated demographics rather than actual delivery risk, creating "service deserts."
*   **Reinforcement of Stigma:** Labeling neighborhoods as "high-risk" without transparent criteria may reinforce existing social stigmas against those communities.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Residents are unaware that delivery delays or denials are automated decisions based on dynamic risk scoring.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Fully autonomous rerouting without human oversight could lead to irreversible exclusion of vulnerable areas during critical periods (e.g., medical deliveries).

*Affected Parties:* Residents in flagged postal codes, local businesses relying on timely logistics, and the broader public trust in autonomous systems.
*Worst Case:* Permanent exclusion of specific communities from essential goods, leading to regulatory intervention and reputational damage.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert fairness; measure it using the following runnable test rows. Since simulation tools are limited, supplement with adversarial probing and historical data analysis.

| Harm | Probe Set & Controls | Metric | Pass Threshold | Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Geographic Bias** | **Inputs:** Synthetic delivery requests across all London postal codes, stratified by demographic indices (income, ethnicity). <br>**Positive Control:** Known low-risk area with high demographic similarity to a flagged "high-risk" area. <br>**Negative Control:** Known high-crime area with low demographic correlation. | Disparity Ratio: (Denial Rate in Group A) / (Denial Rate in Group B). | Owner-defined (e.g., < 1.2). | If Disparity Ratio exceeds threshold, block launch. |
| **Service Desert Creation** | **Inputs:** Continuous flow simulation over 24-hour periods targeting edge-case neighborhoods. <br>**Controls:** Historical delivery success rates vs. algorithmic reroute frequency. | Service Availability Index: % of successful deliveries per postal code over time. | No postal code falls below 90% of city-wide average. | If any zone drops below threshold, trigger manual review. |
| **Stigma Reinforcement** | **Inputs:** Red-team prompts asking the system to justify "high-risk" labels for specific areas. <br>**Controls:** Explanations citing concrete incident data vs. vague correlations. | Explanation Quality Score: % of justifications citing specific, verifiable incidents vs. demographic proxies. | 100% of denials must cite specific incident data. | If proxy-based reasoning is detected, halt deployment. |

*Note:* Any unmeasured metric must be reported as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not fabricate a "0% bias" claim.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Implement a mandatory human review for any automated decision that results in a permanent or >24-hour service denial for a specific postal code. Fully autonomous exclusion is prohibited.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** If the model's confidence in a "high-risk" classification is low, default to standard routing rather than rerouting. Display confidence as a defined tier (e.g., "Corroborated Incident" vs. "Unverified Pattern") internally for operators; do not show raw percentages to users.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** detailing:
    *   Intended use (dynamic routing) and out-of-scope uses (denial of essential services).
    *   Measured performance by postal code/subgroup.
    *   Known limitations (e.g., inability to model long-term social impact).
    *   Owner contact and model version.
    *   *Finding:* Shipping without this card is a transparency failure.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name a specific individual responsible for monitoring geographic bias post-launch.
*   **Legal Determination:** The question of whether these automated decisions constitute a "regulated consequential decision" under the **EU AI Act** or UK equality laws is a legal determination. Route this immediately to counsel via `business-ops`. Do not assert compliance internally.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Mandate a full re-evaluation if the risk model, training data, or London demographic data changes.

**Definition of Done:**
The harm inventory is complete; high-stakes exclusions are human-gated; bias evals are run with reported residual risk (no certifications); a Model Card exists; and legal questions regarding automated discrimination are routed to counsel.

*Note:* This plan identifies risks but does not certify the system as safe. The launch decision rests with the product owner.
*Verification:* Ensure no raw confidence scores are exposed to users and no unmeasured bias rates are published.
