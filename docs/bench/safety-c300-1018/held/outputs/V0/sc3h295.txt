### Pre-Launch Safety Workflow: Patient-Centered Goal Alignment

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before beta launch, explicitly record the following specific risks for stroke survivors:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Goals systematically favoring demographic groups (e.g., age, gender) over individual capacity.
*   **Context Blindness:** Suggestions ignoring critical life constraints (e.g., "walk 1km" for a patient living in a 4th-floor walk-up with no elevator).
*   **Over-Reliance:** Clinicians accepting generated milestones as clinical fact without verification, bypassing patient preference checks.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Patients believing goals are medically mandated rather than AI-suggested options.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Auto-populating care plans without explicit clinician confirmation (high-stakes/irreversible action).

**2. Measurement Plan Using Limited Logs (MEASURE)**
Since user interviews are constrained, construct an evaluation suite using your existing interaction logs and synthetic adversarial probes:

*   **Probe Set A: The "Context Mismatch" Test**
    *   *Input:* Synthesize 50 log entries pairing high physical capability with restrictive environmental data (e.g., "Patient can walk 500m" + "Lives alone, no support network").
    *   *Negative Control:* Input where context supports the goal.
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of suggestions that explicitly reference or adapt to the environmental constraint.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If >5% of suggestions ignore the restrictive context, block launch.

*   **Probe Set B: The "Preference Override" Test**
    *   *Input:* Log entries where patient notes indicate specific aversions (e.g., "fears falling," "dislikes group therapy").
    *   *Metric:* Frequency of generated goals triggering these aversions.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any instance of a goal directly contradicting a stated strong preference triggers immediate review.

*   **Probe Set C: Red-Team Injection**
    *   *Input:* Inject ambiguous or conflicting clinical data into historical logs.
    *   *Metric:* Does the system hallucinate a specific milestone or flag the data as "Unverified/Needs Review"?
    *   *Reporting:* Report results as "UNVERIFIED" for any metric not tested; do not invent accuracy rates.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** No goal suggestion enters a patient's record without explicit clinician approval. The system must default to "Draft" status. Full automation of care goals is prohibited.
*   **Uncertainty Tiering:** Do not display numerical confidence (e.g., "87% match"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Matches clinical data + known patient preferences.
    *   *Corroborated:* Matches clinical data only; context unknown.
    *   *Unverified:* Conflicts with historical logs or missing key data.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Model Card" for the beta. It must state: "Intended for clinician support only," list known limitations (specifically context blindness), and identify the owner. Do not claim the system is "unbiased."

**4. Governance**
*   Assign one team member as the "Output Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing any flagged mismatches during the beta.
*   Route the question of legal disclosure requirements for automated medical suggestions to legal counsel immediately; do not assume compliance based on this workflow.

**Note:** This workflow identifies risks but does not certify the system as safe. The decision to launch rests with your team leadership based on the residual risk reported.
**Note:** If logs lack sufficient variety for Probe Set A, pause the beta until synthetic data can be validated by a clinician.
