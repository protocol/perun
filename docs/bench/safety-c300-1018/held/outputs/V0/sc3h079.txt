### Testing Scenario List: Voice Assistant Notification Narration

**Objective:** Identify edge cases where notification narration diverts driver attention during critical driving moments, specifically under latency constraints that prevent real-time context analysis.

#### 1. High-Cognitive Load Maneuvers
*   **Scenario:** Vehicle is navigating a complex, unprotected left turn at a busy intersection.
*   **Trigger:** Incoming message arrives 2 seconds before the gap in traffic closes.
*   **Risk:** Auditory interruption breaks concentration during the precise moment of decision-making.
*   **Edge Case:** Message is short (<5 seconds) but arrives exactly as the driver begins the turn initiation.

#### 2. Sudden Hazard Response
*   **Scenario:** Driver is braking hard for a pedestrian stepping off a curb or a sudden stop by the lead vehicle.
*   **Trigger:** Non-urgent notification (e.g., social media like, newsletter) arrives during the braking event.
*   **Risk:** Startle response or divided attention delays reaction time or causes over-braking.
*   **Edge Case:** System latency causes the narration to start *after* the hazard has passed, creating confusion about the source of the sound during recovery.

#### 3. Emotionally Charged Content
*   **Scenario:** Vehicle is traveling at highway speeds (65+ mph) in moderate traffic.
*   **Trigger:** Message containing keywords indicating urgency, distress, or conflict (e.g., "Emergency," "Call me now," "We need to talk").
*   **Risk:** Emotional arousal causes the driver to disengage from the driving task mentally, even if eyes remain on the road.
*   **Edge Case:** The assistant reads the sender's name which is recognized as a strained relationship, triggering anxiety before the message content is fully spoken.

#### 4. Audio Masking and Clarity
*   **Scenario:** Driver has windows down in a noisy urban environment or loud music playing.
*   **Trigger:** Standard notification tone followed immediately by narration.
*   **Risk:** Driver strains to hear the message, increasing cognitive load; or misses critical external auditory cues (sirens, horns) due to the assistant's volume ducking logic failing.
*   **Edge Case:** Narration cuts off mid-sentence due to a new incoming call, leaving the driver distracted by the incomplete information.

#### 5. Repetitive Notification Storms
*   **Scenario:** Driver is merging onto a freeway ramp.
*   **Trigger:** Group chat activity generates three notifications within 10 seconds.
*   **Risk:** Continuous auditory stream prevents the driver from focusing on speed matching and gap selection.
*   **Edge Case:** The system queues these messages, causing a long narration block that extends well past the completion of the merge maneuver.

#### 6. False Positive Urgency
*   **Scenario:** Driver is parking in a tight space requiring precise spatial awareness.
*   **Trigger:** Automated system message (e.g., "Your package has shipped") misidentified or read with an intonation resembling urgency.
*   **Risk:** Unnecessary stress response during a low-speed but high-precision task.

**Implementation Note:** Since real-time context checks are disabled due to latency, these scenarios must be tested via recorded drive logs where notifications are injected at precise timestamps relative to vehicle telemetry (speed, braking, steering angle).

**Safety Gate:** Any scenario resulting in a measurable delay in brake reaction time >200ms or a deviation from lane centering >1 foot during the narration window must trigger a mandatory human-in-the-loop review before release.
