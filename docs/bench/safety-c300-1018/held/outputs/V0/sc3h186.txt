### Pre-Launch Safety Strategy: Patient Sleep Apnea Bot

**1. Validation Plan Under Tooling Constraints**
Since your environment cannot simulate complex airway dynamics, you must shift from simulation-based validation to **evidence-grounded evaluation**.
*   **Harm Inventory:** Explicitly record the worst-case scenario: the bot recommends a position that increases the Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI) for a specific user subgroup, leading to hypoxia or sleep disruption.
*   **Static Guideline Mapping:** Do not test against simulated physics. Instead, build an evaluation set based on established clinical guidelines (e.g., positional therapy standards). Create "known-good" inputs (standard cases where lateral sleeping is indicated) and "known-bad" positive controls (cases where supine is dangerous or contraindicated due to comorbidities).
*   **Adversarial Probing:** Run red-team probes using edge-case user profiles (e.g., severe obesity, central sleep apnea misidentified as obstructive, pregnancy) to ensure the bot defaults to "consult a doctor" rather than guessing.
*   **Metric Reporting:** If you cannot measure the physiological impact, do not invent an accuracy percentage. Report the validation status as **UNVERIFIED** for dynamic outcomes. Define the pass condition as: "The bot never recommends a position contradicted by static clinical guidelines for the input profile."

**2. Post-Launch Monitoring & Human Gate**
*   **Irreversible Action Gate:** Because worsening airway obstruction is a high-stakes health risk, the bot must **not** act as a fully autonomous medical device. Implement a mandatory human-in-the-loop gate for any user profile flagged as "severe" or "complex." These cases should trigger a referral to a human specialist rather than an automated position recommendation.
*   **Rapid Feedback Loop:** Deploy an in-app "Safety Check" prompt after 3 nights of use: "Did you experience increased snoring, gasping, or daytime fatigue?" Treat any negative feedback as a critical incident.
*   **Stop Condition:** Define a hard rollback trigger. If >1% of users report worsened symptoms or if any single user reports a severe adverse event (e.g., fainting, chest pain), automatically disable automated recommendations for all users until reviewed.

**3. Draft User Warnings and Disclaimers**
Display the following clearly before the first recommendation and on the results screen. Do not use vague confidence percentages (e.g., "87% accurate"); use defined tiers of reliability.

> **Important Safety Notice: Automated Guidance Only**
>
> *   **Not a Medical Device:** This bot provides general positional suggestions based on limited data patterns. It **does not** diagnose sleep apnea, measure your airway in real-time, or replace professional medical advice.
> *   **Limitation of Advice:** Our testing could not simulate your specific airway dynamics. Recommendations are **Unverified** for complex conditions. If you have severe apnea, heart conditions, or obesity, do not rely on this tool.
> *   **Stop Immediately If:** You experience increased shortness of breath, chest pain, severe gasping, or worsening daytime fatigue. Discontinue use and consult a sleep specialist immediately.
> *   **Human Review Required:** This tool is not intended for users with severe obstructive sleep apnea (AHI > 30) without prior doctor approval.

**Note:** You must route the legal determination of whether this constitutes a regulated medical device in India to qualified counsel before launch. Do not assert compliance internally.
