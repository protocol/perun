**Pre-Launch Safety Protocol: Body Image & Teen Safety**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Systematic disadvantage and psychological harm to teenage users via promotion of extreme body-image standards.
*   **Affected Group:** Users under 18, specifically those vulnerable to body dysmorphia or eating disorders.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Triggering or exacerbating an eating disorder, leading to severe health consequences or self-harm.
*   **Status:** This is a high-stakes risk. The current algorithm behavior constitutes a failure condition for safe launch in its present form.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Action:** Do not rely on aggregate accuracy metrics. You must run adversarial probes specifically targeting body-shape queries across different demographic slices before rollout.
*   **Metric:** Rate of recommendations promoting "extreme" or "unsafe" body standards vs. diverse/healthy representations.
*   **Threshold:** Propose **zero tolerance** for outputs flagged as promoting dangerous body standards for teen segments.
*   **Reporting:** Any unmeasured bias rate must be labeled **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% bias" figure for stakeholders.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** You must draft a **Model Card** immediately. It must list "fashion recommendation for teens" as the intended use, explicitly state "risk of promoting unhealthy body image" as a known limitation, and document that bias metrics are currently UNVERIFIED pending the specific evals above. Shipping without this card is a transparency failure.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Since you cannot redesign the algorithm in time, you must implement a **human review gate** for any content served to users identified as teenagers. Fully automated delivery of these specific recommendations is unsafe.
*   **Uncertainty & Override:** Do not present recommendations as absolute facts. If the system confidence is low or the content borders on sensitive topics, surface a disclaimer or offer an easy "not helpful/unsafe" override for the user.
*   **Disclosure:** We strongly recommend disclosing that styles are AI-generated, but the legal necessity of this disclosure is a question for counsel (see below).

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific individual (e.g., Lead Data Analyst or Product Owner) as the accountable owner for this harm inventory.
*   **Re-review:** This protocol must be re-evaluated immediately if the model, data source, or user demographic shifts.

***

**Regulatory & Certification Clarification**
Regarding your request to confirm if regulations require stating the AI is "certified free from bias": **No such certification exists, and you must not make this claim.** No test proves a system is unbiased; asserting this would be a fabrication. Whether an AI disclosure is legally required under the **EU AI Act** or other regimes for this specific use case is a legal determination that must be routed to **legal counsel** immediately. This protocol identifies the risk and mandates measurement; it does not and cannot certify safety or compliance.

**Note:** The tight timeline increases risk; if the human-in-the-loop gate cannot be implemented for teen users, delaying the rollout for that segment is the only safe option.
