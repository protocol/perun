**CRITICAL SAFETY FINDING: DO NOT PROCEED WITH FULL AUTOMATION**

Your proposed feature—automatically calculating and **setting** defibrillator energy levels for pediatric patients—constitutes a **high-stakes, irreversible action** with fatal failure modes. Under product output safety standards, **full autonomous execution of this action is a critical failure**, regardless of your regulatory deadline or team size. The lack of diverse training data for infants under six months makes the risk of hallucination or bias unquantifiable (`UNVERIFIED`), rendering any claim of "safety" or "accuracy" a fabrication.
