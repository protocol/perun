### Pre-Launch Review Strategy for Nile MedTech Nutrition Tracker

**1. Maximizing Existing Dataset for Stress-Testing**
To address the overestimation bias in severe cases without new data collection, implement the following synthetic stress-testing protocols on your current dataset:

*   **Severity Stratification & Resampling:** Isolate the bottom 10% of your current biomarker performance data (representing the most severe malabsorption). Use bootstrapping techniques to oversample this specific subset, creating a "severe-case" validation set. Train a temporary diagnostic model to identify which features correlate most strongly with the overestimation errors in this group.
*   **Synthetic Edge-Case Generation:** Since you cannot collect new patients, generate synthetic patient profiles by mathematically perturbing existing severe cases. Apply noise factors to dietary logs (e.g., simulating unreported snacking or measurement errors) and biomarker trends (e.g., simulating lab variability) to create "fringe" scenarios. Specifically, construct hypothetical profiles that combine rare syndrome attributes found separately in your data to test how the algorithm handles complex, multi-factorial inputs it hasn't seen before.
*   **Adversarial Validation:** Split your data not randomly, but by severity. Train the model on mild/moderate cases and validate exclusively on severe cases. This quantifies the "domain shift" error and highlights exactly where the model fails when extrapolating beyond the average patient.
*   **Conservative Bias Injection:** As an immediate mitigation, apply a post-hoc correction factor to the model's output for patients falling into the severe biomarker threshold. If the model historically overestimates by 15% in this cluster, hard-code a safety buffer that reduces the reported absorption estimate by that margin until further clinical data is available.

**2. Risk Communication Draft for Pilot Participants**

*Subject: Important Information Regarding Your Nutrition Tracker Results*

Dear Pilot Participant,

As we launch this pilot for our nutrition absorption tracker, your safety and informed participation are our top priorities. This tool uses artificial intelligence to estimate how well your body absorbs nutrients based on the logs and biomarkers you provide. While designed to assist your care team, it is vital to understand the current limitations of this technology.

**Potential for Estimation Errors:**
Our testing indicates that in cases of severe malabsorption or complex digestive disorders, the AI may occasionally **overestimate** your nutrient uptake. This means the tool might suggest your body is absorbing more nutrients than it actually is.

**What This Means for You:**
*   **Do Not Adjust Medication or Supplements Independently:** Never change your dosage of supplements, medications, or dietary plans based solely on the tracker's estimates.
*   **Clinical Oversight is Essential:** These estimates are a supplementary data point for your clinician, not a diagnostic result. Your care team will verify these figures against your clinical symptoms and standard lab results before making any treatment decisions.
*   **Reporting Discrepancies:** If you feel your symptoms worsen or do not align with the tracker's "positive" outlook, please contact your clinician immediately. Your lived experience takes precedence over the algorithm's output.

We are committed to refining this tool with your help. Your feedback on any discrepancies is the most valuable data we can collect to improve safety for future users.

Sincerely,
The Nile MedTech Clinical Team
