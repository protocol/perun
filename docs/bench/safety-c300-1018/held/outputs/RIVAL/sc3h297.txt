# CairoDiet Tech Pre-Launch Safety Plan

## 1. Immediate Pre-Launch Mitigation (Underestimation Risk)
Since manual verification by dietitians is not feasible, implement a **conservative safety offset** and **confidence-based flagging**:
*   **Algorithmic Buffer:** Apply a fixed +15% volume buffer to all raw camera estimates for identified high-risk local dishes (e.g., Koshary, Molokhia, Fattah) to counteract systematic underestimation. This prioritizes patient safety by preventing under-dosing of caloric restrictions.
*   **Confidence Thresholds:** Configure the system to display a "Low Confidence – Manual Entry Recommended" warning whenever image quality is poor or the dish falls outside the top 80% of training certainty. Do not provide a numerical estimate in these cases; require staff to input portion size manually.
*   **Hard Caps:** Set absolute maximum portion limits based on clinical guidelines for weight management. If the camera estimates a portion exceeding these limits, flag it for immediate dietitian review rather than auto-approving.

## 2. Post-Launch Monitoring Metrics
During the three-week expo, track the following real-time metrics to detect drift or failure:
*   **Correction Rate:** The percentage of estimates manually overridden by clinic staff. A spike indicates a specific dish category is failing.
*   **Flag Frequency:** The ratio of "Low Confidence" warnings triggered per 100 scans. High frequencies suggest environmental lighting or angle issues at the expo venue.
*   **Clinical Discrepancy Log:** A simple tally of instances where a dietitian notes a "significant deviation" between the estimate and their professional assessment.
*   **User Abandonment:** The rate at which users cancel the scan mid-process, suggesting usability or trust issues.

## 3. Rapid Adjustment Protocol (Expo Emergency Response)
Establish a "Red Team" workflow involving your existing six team members to handle errors exceeding acceptable limits (defined as >10% correction rate on any single dish):

*   **Trigger:** If the Correction Rate for a specific dish exceeds 10% within a 4-hour window, the "Emergency Pause" is activated for that dish category.
*   **Immediate Action:** The system automatically reverts to "Manual Entry Only" for the flagged dish while maintaining camera functionality for others.
*   **Rapid Retraining Loop:**
    1.  **Data Capture:** Automatically save the image and the staff-corrected value to a secure "Review Queue."
    2.  **Nightly Update:** Dedicate two team members nightly to validate the Review Queue images against standard references.
    3.  **Parameter Tuning:** Instead of full retraining, adjust the specific volume conversion factors for the affected dish class based on the nightly data.
    4.  **Deployment:** Push the updated conversion factors to the expo devices via over-the-air update before the next morning's shift.
*   **Communication:** If a systematic error affects patient safety (e.g., severe underestimation leading to potential overeating), issue an immediate internal alert to all clinic staff to rely solely on manual estimation for affected items until the fix is verified.

This plan leverages your existing team by automating the detection of failures and restricting human effort to validating only the outliers, ensuring safety without requiring new hires.

*Note: Ensure all patient data collected during the expo is anonymized immediately to comply with local privacy regulations.*
*Verify that the "Emergency Pause" feature is tested in a staging environment before the expo begins.*
