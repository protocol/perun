# Pre-Launch Safety Evaluation Plan: Geographic Bias & Service Equity

**Objective:** Quantify and mitigate the risk of "service deserts" and stigmatization caused by dynamic rerouting in the London delivery network before the holiday launch.

### Phase 1: Baseline Equity Audit (Static Analysis)
Before testing dynamic rerouting, establish a fairness baseline using historical or static routing data.
*   **Demographic Overlay:** Map proposed "high-risk" zones against London's Index of Multiple Deprivation (IMD), ethnicity data, and disability prevalence by postcode. Identify if high-risk flags correlate strongly with protected characteristics rather than objective safety metrics (e.g., crime rates vs. socioeconomic status).
*   **Service Density Heatmap:** Generate a heatmap of simulated delivery frequency per capita across all London boroughs. Flag any postcode where projected service drops below 80% of the city average without explicit physical impossibility (e.g., no-fly zones).
*   **Stigma Proxy Check:** Review the labeling of "high-risk" neighborhoods. Ensure internal documentation and public-facing communications do not use language that reinforces negative stereotypes about specific communities.

### Phase 2: Dynamic Simulation & Stress Testing
Since current tools cannot model social impact, augment simulations with specific "equity stress tests."
*   **The "Persistent Detour" Scenario:** Run simulations where risk algorithms trigger maximum avoidance for specific postcodes over a 30-day period. Measure the cumulative delay and cost increase for residents in those zones compared to control zones.
*   **Feedback Loop Modeling:** Simulate a scenario where reduced delivery frequency leads to less data collection, which the algorithm interprets as "unknown/high risk," further reducing service. Identify if the system creates a self-fulfilling prophecy of exclusion.
*   **Edge Case Injection:** Introduce synthetic events (e.g., localized protests, temporary road closures) to see if the algorithm disproportionately abandons vulnerable areas while maintaining service in affluent ones.

### Phase 3: Impact Assessment & Mitigation Strategy
Translate simulation data into actionable policies.
*   **Service Level Agreement (SLA) Floors:** Define a mandatory minimum service frequency for every active postcode, regardless of risk score. If a route is deemed too risky for autonomous flight, trigger a human-in-the-loop review or a ground-handoff protocol rather than a total bypass.
*   **Bias Correction Factors:** Implement a "fairness weight" in the routing algorithm that penalizes solutions creating significant disparities in delivery times between neighboring postcodes with similar physical risk profiles.
*   **Transparency & Redress:** Draft a public explanation for delays that cites specific, temporary safety hazards rather than vague "neighborhood risk." Establish a rapid response channel for community leaders to report perceived discrimination.

### Phase 4: Go/No-Go Criteria
Establish clear thresholds for the holiday launch:
*   **Go:** No postcode experiences >15% average delay compared to the city median; no correlation found between risk flags and protected demographic groups exceeding statistical noise.
*   **No-Go:** Evidence of systematic exclusion of specific communities or inability to guarantee minimum service levels in designated "high-risk" zones.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1.  Pause dynamic routing updates for identified high-risk postcodes until the Baseline Equity Audit is complete.
2.  Form a cross-functional review board including an external ethics advisor to validate the "fairness weights" before code freeze.

*Note: This plan relies on simulation augmentation and policy guards since real-world social impact cannot be fully modeled in a beta environment.*
*Ensure all data used in the audit complies with UK GDPR and data protection standards.*
