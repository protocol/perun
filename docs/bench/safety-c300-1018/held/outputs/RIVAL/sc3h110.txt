# Haiti ReliefMatch: Manual Verification and Feedback Protocol

## 1. Pre-Dispatch Manual Verification Checklist
Before any automated shipment is authorized, the following manual steps must be completed by at least two team members to prevent resource clogging:

*   **Cross-Reference Static Baselines:** Compare the requested location against the last known verified inventory list. If a region was marked as "fully supplied" within the last 72 hours, flag for immediate human review regardless of new requests.
*   **Triangulate Low-Bandwidth Signals:** Since satellite imagery is unavailable, validate needs using three distinct low-data sources:
    1.  **Local Partner Confirmation:** Require a direct voice call or SMS confirmation from a designated local lead using a pre-agreed code word for "urgent need."
    2.  **Community Feedback:** Check for incoming distress patterns via established community radio logs or WhatsApp group summaries from trusted ground contacts.
    3.  **Transit Visibility:** Confirm no other shipments are currently en route or recently delivered to the same coordinate cluster.
*   **Standardized Data Entry:** To address format inconsistencies, all manual findings must be entered into a single, simplified template (e.g., a shared spreadsheet with strict dropdowns: "Confirmed Need," "Already Supplied," "Unverified"). Do not proceed if data remains in unstructured text or varying formats.
*   **Dual-Signoff Rule:** No dispatch occurs without two separate logisticians signing off on the "Verified Need" status in the central tracker.

## 2. Rapid Feedback Loop for Error Correction
To quickly identify and correct allocation errors during active responses:

*   **Immediate "Stop-Work" Signal:** Establish a universal distress code (e.g., "RED HOLD") that any team member or local partner can trigger via SMS if they observe supplies arriving where they are not needed. This signal immediately pauses all automated dispatches to the affected zone.
*   **Daily "Lessons Learned" Huddle:** Conduct a mandatory 15-minute stand-up at the start of each shift. Review only two metrics: (1) Shipments sent vs. confirmed received/needed, and (2) Any reported misallocations. Adjust the verification checklist immediately based on these findings.
*   **Partner Simplification Initiative:** Assign one team member to work exclusively with local partners to co-create a single, ultra-simple reporting format (e.g., a specific SMS keyword structure) that replaces varied data submissions. This reduces validation time for future cycles.
*   **Post-Mission Audit:** After each major dispatch wave, manually trace three random shipments to confirm they reached the intended beneficiaries and were actually required. Document any breakdown in the verification chain and update the protocol accordingly.

## 3. Implementation Constraints
Given the team size of eight and severe bandwidth limits:
*   Rotate the "Verification Lead" role daily to prevent burnout.
*   Rely on asynchronous updates (SMS/Spreadsheet) rather than real-time video calls.
*   Prioritize accuracy over speed; a delayed correct shipment is preferable to a fast, useless one that clogs logistics.

***

*Note: This protocol assumes access to basic SMS and a shared digital spreadsheet; if these are unavailable, revert to paper logs consolidated hourly.*
*Ensure all local partners are briefed on the new "RED HOLD" signal immediately.*
