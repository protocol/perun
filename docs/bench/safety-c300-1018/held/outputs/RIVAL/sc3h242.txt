### Internal Clinical Validation Workflow for Protein Calculation Tool

**Objective:** Manually verify algorithm outputs against clinical guidelines to detect underestimation errors using existing beta-phase data, without external costs.

#### Phase 1: Guideline Standardization & Data Preparation
1.  **Define the "Gold Standard" Protocol:**
    *   Convene the two senior clinicians to codify a single, written reference protocol based on current guidelines (e.g., ESPEN or local Ministry of Health standards) specifically for pressure ulcer protein requirements.
    *   Create a simple lookup table or decision tree that maps specific input variables (wound stage, weight, albumin levels, inflammation markers) to the expected protein range. This eliminates interpretation variance during review.

2.  **Stratified Sampling Strategy:**
    *   Do not review randomly. Extract the existing beta dataset and stratify patients by risk factors where underestimation is most dangerous:
        *   **High Severity:** Stage 3 and 4 ulcers.
        *   **Complex Comorbidities:** Patients with renal impairment or severe inflammation (where calculations often fail).
        *   **Edge Cases:** Patients at the upper and lower bounds of weight and age ranges.
    *   Select a sample size of 50–100 cases, ensuring 40% come from the "High Severity" group. This focuses limited human hours on the highest-risk outputs.

#### Phase 2: The Blind Dual-Review Process
1.  **Blind Calculation:**
    *   Assign three junior clinicians/dietitians to review the selected cases.
    *   Provide them *only* with the patient input data and the written "Gold Standard" protocol. **Hide the algorithm's suggested output.**
    *   Ask them to manually calculate the required protein intake for each assigned case.

2.  **Adjudication:**
    *   If the three reviewers agree within a tight margin (e.g., ±5g), establish this as the "Manual Consensus Value."
    *   If there is disagreement, the senior clinician acts as the tie-breaker to set the final Manual Consensus Value.

#### Phase 3: Discrepancy Analysis
1.  **Comparison:**
    *   Reveal the algorithm's output and compare it against the Manual Consensus Value.
    *   Flag any case where the Algorithm Output is **lower** than the Manual Consensus Value by more than 10% (or your defined clinical safety threshold).

2.  **Root Cause Categorization:**
    *   For every flagged underestimation, the developers and clinicians jointly categorize the error:
        *   **Logic Error:** The formula itself is flawed for this specific condition.
        *   **Data Input Error:** The tool misinterpreted a lab value or unit.
        *   **Guideline Gap:** The algorithm lacks a rule for a specific comorbidity present in the patient.

#### Phase 4: Iterative Correction & Sign-off
1.  **Patch and Re-test:**
    *   Developers adjust the algorithm logic based on the categorized errors.
    *   Re-run the *failed* cases only to confirm the fix resolves the underestimation without breaking previous correct calculations.

2.  **Safety Sign-off:**
    *   The team leads sign off on the trial expansion only when the rate of critical underestimation errors in the high-severity sample drops to 0%.

**Note:** Ensure all patient data used in this internal review is anonymized to maintain privacy compliance during the manual handling process.
