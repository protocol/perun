### Safety Protocol: Equitable Scholarship Review

**Objective:** Mitigate algorithmic bias against non-traditional educational backgrounds within a two-week deadline without external data integration.

**1. Immediate Algorithmic Adjustment (Days 1–3)**
*   **Implement a "Context Flag" Trigger:** Modify the initial screening logic to automatically flag any application where the applicant's school type is listed as "alternative," "homeschool," "vocational," or "non-accredited," or where the grading scale differs from the national standard.
*   **Suspend Auto-Rejection:** Configure the system so that any flagged application bypasses the automatic rejection threshold based solely on GPA or standardized test scores. These candidates must proceed to human review regardless of their numeric score.

**2. The Manual Review Panel (Days 4–10)**
*   **Panel Composition:** Assign two reviewers per flagged application to reduce individual bias. If possible, include at least one reviewer familiar with non-traditional education pathways.
*   **Holistic Rubric:** Replace numeric grading for these cases with a binary "Potential/Readiness" assessment based on:
    *   **Trajectory Evidence:** Look for upward trends in performance rather than cumulative averages.
    *   **Non-Academic Metrics:** Prioritize documented community impact, self-directed projects, or vocational certifications over standard grades.
    *   **Personal Statement Weight:** Increase the scoring weight of the personal statement by 40% for flagged candidates to allow explanation of educational context.
*   **Blind Review:** Remove names and specific school identifiers from the manual review packet to prevent unconscious bias regarding the institution's reputation.

**3. Calibration and Consistency (Days 11–12)**
*   **Shadow Review:** Select 10% of traditionally qualified applicants (high GPA) and run them through the same manual holistic rubric anonymously. Compare the pass rates. If the manual process rejects high-GPA students at a significantly higher rate than the algorithm did, recalibrate the holistic rubric to ensure rigor is maintained while expanding inclusion.
*   **Dispute Resolution:** Establish a third senior reviewer to adjudicate cases where the two primary reviewers disagree.

**4. Final Integration (Days 13–14)**
*   **Merge Lists:** Combine the algorithmically approved list with the manually approved "flagged" list.
*   **Audit Trail:** Document every instance where the manual review overturned an algorithmic exclusion. Record the specific qualitative factor (e.g., "vocational certification equivalent to Grade 12 Math") that justified the inclusion. This creates an internal dataset for future model retraining.

**5. Post-Award Monitoring**
*   **Cohort Tracking:** Tag awarded scholars who entered via the manual review path. Monitor their first-semester performance specifically to validate that the holistic criteria successfully predicted success, providing evidence for next year's algorithm updates.

***

*Note: This protocol prioritizes human judgment for edge cases where data patterns are known to fail, ensuring no candidate is excluded solely due to formatting or institutional bias.*
*Ensure all reviewers sign a confidentiality agreement given the sensitive nature of manual data handling.*
