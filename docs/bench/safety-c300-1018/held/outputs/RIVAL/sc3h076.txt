### Manual Curation and Rapid Response Plan for Child-Safety Filter

**Phase 1: Pre-Launch Whitelist Curation (Days 1–10)**

1.  **Assemble the Seed List:**
    *   Assign two engineers to extract high-priority terms and URLs from established authoritative sources (e.g., CDC, WHO, local Canadian mental health organizations like CMHA).
    *   Focus categories: "Suicide Prevention," "Crisis Hotlines," "Mental Health Education," and "Counseling Resources."
    *   Create a master spreadsheet with columns: `Term/URL`, `Category`, `Source Authority`, `Status (Pending/Approved)`, and `Date Added`.

2.  **Manual Verification Sprint:**
    *   Divide the remaining four team members into pairs.
    *   Pair A reviews terms; Pair B reviews URLs.
    *   Test each entry against the current filter logic manually (using the limited API calls strictly for verification of borderline cases only).
    *   Flag any entry that triggers a "harmful" block despite being educational.
    *   Finalize the "Golden Whitelist" containing only verified safe entries.

3.  **Integration:**
    *   Engineers hardcode or load this "Golden Whitelist" into the filter's exclusion logic with highest priority (check whitelist *before* applying block rules).
    *   Document the exact format required for new entries to ensure consistency.

**Phase 2: Deployment & Rapid Response Workflow (Days 11–14 + Pilot)**

1.  **Reporting Channel Setup:**
    *   Establish a dedicated, high-visibility channel (e.g., a specific Slack thread, email alias, or simple web form) for teachers to report blocked resources.
    *   Required report format: `Blocked URL/Term`, `Context/Class Subject`, `Teacher Name/School`.

2.  **Triage Protocol (The "Swarm" Method):**
    *   **Step 1 (Immediate Acknowledgement):** Assign one moderator as the "Triage Lead" for the day. They acknowledge receipt within 1 hour.
    *   **Step 2 (Rapid Review):** The Triage Lead performs a manual check. If the resource is clearly educational/safe, they flag it as "Urgent Whitelist Candidate."
    *   **Step 3 (Engineering Update):** An engineer on standby adds the entry to the whitelist configuration immediately. Given the small team size, this should happen within 4 hours of the report.
    *   **Step 4 (Verification & Notification):** The moderator re-tests the specific resource to confirm it now passes. The teacher is notified that the resource is unblocked.

3.  **Daily Consolidation:**
    *   At the end of each pilot day, the full team reviews all added entries to ensure no false negatives (harmful content) were accidentally whitelisted.
    *   Update the master spreadsheet to track the evolution of the whitelist during the pilot.

**Constraints Management:**
*   **API Limit:** Do not use automated regression scans. Rely entirely on the manual "Triage Protocol" for new content. Use API calls only for the final verification step of a specific teacher report.
*   **Team Size:** With six people, rotate the "Triage Lead" and "Engineer on Standby" roles daily to prevent burnout while maintaining coverage.

**Deliverable:** A living "Golden Whitelist" document and a defined 4-hour turnaround protocol for teacher reports.

*Note: Ensure all whitelisted URLs are HTTPS and terms are context-insensitive where possible to maximize coverage.*
*Review the whitelist daily during the pilot to catch any pattern-based evasion attempts.*
