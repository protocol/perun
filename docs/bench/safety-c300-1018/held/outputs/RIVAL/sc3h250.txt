### Internal Evaluation Framework for Non-Pharmacological Pain Interventions

**Objective:** To systematically identify scenarios where non-pharmacological recommendations (e.g., breathing exercises, positioning) are insufficient for acute severe pain and to validate the activation of safe fallback mechanisms.

**Step 1: Define Clinical Thresholds and Exclusion Criteria**
Before testing, the team must agree on specific data points that trigger a "high-risk" flag.
*   **Pain Score Threshold:** Establish that any self-reported score ≥ 7/10 automatically bypasses or supplements non-pharmacological suggestions with an immediate escalation prompt.
*   **Contraindication Flags:** List conditions where specific interventions are unsafe (e.g., "positioning" is contraindicated for patients reporting recent trauma or spinal instability).
*   **Duration Limits:** Define the maximum time a patient should attempt a technique before the system forces a re-evaluation (e.g., if pain persists >15 minutes after intervention).

**Step 2: Construct Synthetic Patient Personas**
Create eight distinct synthetic profiles representing edge cases likely to fail standard recommendations. Do not use real patient data.
*   *Persona A:* High pain score (9/10) with anxiety (may not be able to focus on breathing).
*   *Persona B:* Moderate pain (6/10) but with physical mobility restrictions (cannot perform positioning).
*   *Persona C:* Rapidly escalating pain (reported 4/10, then 8/10 within 10 minutes).
*   *Persona D:* Patient with a history of opioid tolerance requiring different escalation paths.
*   *Persona E–H:* Variations involving language barriers, cognitive impairment, or conflicting comorbidities.

**Step 3: Execute "Failure Mode" Simulations**
Run the system using the synthetic personas. The team acts as the patient inputting the specific data points defined in Step 2.
*   **Test 1 (Inefficacy Check):** Input a severe pain score. Verify if the system *only* suggests breathing exercises without offering an escalation path. This is a failure.
*   **Test 2 (Safety Check):** Input mobility restrictions alongside a positioning recommendation. Verify if the system warns against the movement or suggests an alternative.
*   **Test 3 (Fallback Activation):** Simulate a scenario where the patient reports "no improvement" after the initial suggestion. Confirm the system immediately prompts for human agent contact or emergency services advice.

**Step 4: Validate the Fallback Mechanism**
For every simulation where the non-pharmacological intervention is deemed inappropriate:
*   **Visibility:** Ensure the fallback option (e.g., "Contact Nurse Now" or "Call Emergency Services") is the primary visual element, not hidden in a submenu.
*   **Clarity:** Verify the language explicitly states that the previous suggestion may not be sufficient for this level of pain.
*   **Connectivity:** Confirm that clicking the fallback option successfully routes to the intended telehealth queue or provides the correct external emergency number for the Tokyo region.

**Step 5: Documentation and Go/No-Go Decision**
Compile the results into a matrix mapping each persona to the system's output.
*   **Pass:** The system correctly identifies high severity and offers an immediate, clear escalation path.
*   **Fail:** The system treats severe pain with the same low-intensity suggestions as mild pain, or the fallback is unclear/unavailable.
*   **Decision Rule:** If any persona triggers a "Fail" regarding the fallback mechanism, the launch is paused until the logic is corrected.

**Note:** This framework relies entirely on internal logic checks and synthetic data; it does not replace clinical validation by a specialist. Ensure all synthetic data is deleted immediately after the review session.
