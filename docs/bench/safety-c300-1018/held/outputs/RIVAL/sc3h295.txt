### Pre-Launch Safety Preparation Workflow: Patient-Centered Goal Alignment

**Phase 1: Define "Non-Aligned" Heuristics (Days 1–3)**
Since clinician interview time is scarce, codify explicit rules for what constitutes a misaligned goal based on your existing clinical data schema. Create a "Negative Constraint List" that flags suggestions violating basic patient-centered principles, such as:
*   **Temporal Impossibility:** Suggesting milestones faster than average clinical recovery curves for the specific stroke severity.
*   **Contextual Blindness:** Recommending activities requiring equipment or environments not present in the patient's logged home context.
*   **Preference Contradiction:** Suggesting goals that conflict with previously logged patient refusals or stated dislikes (e.g., suggesting "group swimming" when logs indicate "fear of water").
*   **Agency Reduction:** Goals phrased purely as compliance tasks rather than functional independence markers.

**Phase 2: Retrospective Log Audit (Days 4–10)**
Apply these heuristics to your existing limited interaction logs using a hybrid manual-automated approach:
1.  **Automated Pre-Screen:** Run scripts to flag any generated goal that triggers the Negative Constraint List.
2.  **Targeted Clinician Review:** Instead of reviewing all logs, present *only* the flagged cases to your clinical team. Ask them to binary-classify these as "Safe False Positive" or "Critical Misalignment." This maximizes the value of their limited time by focusing solely on edge cases.
3.  **Pattern Clustering:** Group flagged errors by type (e.g., "ignores home setup" vs. "unrealistic timeline") to identify which data inputs the system is under-weighting.

**Phase 3: The "Shadow Mode" Simulation (Days 11–20)**
Before beta launch, run the system in "Shadow Mode" against historical cases where the final outcome is known:
*   Input the initial patient data from past successful rehabilitations.
*   Compare the AI's suggested milestones against the *actual* milestones achieved by humans.
*   Calculate a "Deviation Score." High deviation does not always mean error, but deviations where the AI suggested harder tasks that led to patient dropout in historical records are critical failures.

**Phase 4: Beta Launch Guardrails (Days 21–28)**
Deploy the beta with embedded safety nets designed to capture alignment data without extra clinician burden:
*   **One-Click Friction Logging:** Add a simple "👍/👎" button next to every suggested goal for the clinician. A "👎" triggers a mandatory but single-drop-down selection for the reason (e.g., "Too hard," "Irrelevant to patient life," "Medically unsafe").
*   **Confidence Thresholding:** If the system's internal confidence score for a goal is low (based on sparse patient data), force the output to be a "Question" rather than a "Suggestion" (e.g., "Consider walking 10 meters?" vs. "Goal: Walk 10 meters").
*   **Daily Drift Alert:** Aggregate the "👎" feedback daily. If misalignment reports exceed 5% of total suggestions, automatically pause non-critical suggestions and notify the development team.

**Deliverable Summary:**
This workflow shifts the burden from proactive, time-intensive interviews to reactive, high-yield analysis of existing data and targeted beta feedback. It ensures that by launch day, you have quantified specific failure modes and established a real-time feedback loop to catch non-aligned goals immediately.

*Note: Ensure all log analysis complies with local patient data privacy regulations before processing.*
*This workflow assumes your existing logs contain structured data on patient context and prior preferences.*
