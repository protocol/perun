### Testing Framework for Legal Aid Chatbot Accuracy

**Objective:** Validate that the chatbot provides accurate, safe, and non-harmful eviction defense guidance based strictly on the static New York knowledge base, preventing the dissemination of outdated or incorrect legal strategies.

#### Phase 1: Scenario Matrix Construction
Create a "Golden Set" of 20–30 high-frequency eviction scenarios derived from actual NY housing court data. Categorize these by risk level:
*   **High Risk:** Non-payment with valid repair defenses, illegal lockouts, harassment cases.
*   **Medium Risk:** Lease expiration, holdover proceedings, subsidy termination.
*   **Low Risk:** General procedural questions (e.g., "Where is the court?").

For each scenario, define three distinct data points:
1.  **Input:** The tenant's natural language query.
2.  **Expected Output:** The legally correct strategy based *only* on the current static knowledge base.
3.  **Forbidden Output:** Any advice that contradicts NY law, suggests illegal actions, or hallucinates non-existent defenses.

#### Phase 2: The "Static Boundary" Stress Test
Since the knowledge base cannot be updated in real time, the primary risk is the bot attempting to answer questions about recent legal changes not included in the static set.
*   **Test Action:** Feed the bot queries regarding very recent NY housing law updates (post-dating the knowledge base cutoff).
*   **Success Criteria:** The bot must explicitly state it cannot answer questions about recent changes or direct the user to a human lawyer, rather than guessing or applying outdated rules.
*   **Failure Mode:** The bot provides confident but obsolete advice.

#### Phase 3: Adversarial & Ambiguity Testing
Tenants often describe facts imperfectly. Test the bot's ability to handle ambiguity without over-committing to a specific defense.
*   **Vague Inputs:** Provide incomplete fact patterns (e.g., "My landlord changed the locks" without specifying if notice was given).
*   **Contradictory Inputs:** Provide facts that conflict within the same prompt.
*   **Success Criteria:** The bot asks clarifying questions or provides a range of possibilities with appropriate disclaimers, rather than asserting a single, potentially wrong defense strategy.

#### Phase 4: Human-in-the-Loop Review Protocol
Before any public launch, implement a mandatory review gate for the "High Risk" category.
1.  **Automated Run:** Execute the full scenario matrix.
2.  **Flagging:** Any response deviating >10% from the "Expected Output" or containing any "Forbidden Output" is automatically flagged.
3.  **Legal Review:** A qualified attorney reviews only the flagged responses and a random 10% sample of "passed" responses.
4.  **Go/No-Go Decision:** The tool launches only if the error rate in High Risk scenarios is 0%.

#### Phase 5: Safety Guardrails Verification
Verify that every response includes mandatory disclaimers:
*   "I am an AI, not a lawyer."
*   "Laws change; please consult a legal aid attorney for your specific case."
*   Direct links to local NY legal aid organizations for immediate human intervention.

**Deliverable:** A structured spreadsheet containing the Scenario Matrix, expected vs. actual outputs, and a pass/fail status for each, accompanied by a summary report of flagged risks requiring attorney review.

*Note: This framework relies entirely on the accuracy of your initial static knowledge base; if the source text is flawed, the tests will validate incorrect advice as "correct."*
*Ensure your "Forbidden Output" list is updated by a senior attorney to reflect the most dangerous potential hallucinations specific to NY eviction law.*
