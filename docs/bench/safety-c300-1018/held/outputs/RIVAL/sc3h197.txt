# Interim Safety Protocol: Wound Healing Monitoring (Mumbai Deployment)

**Objective:** Mitigate risk of false negatives for early-stage necrosis in darker skin tones due to algorithmic bias until model retraining occurs.

**Scope:** All home-care nurses managing chronic ulcer patients using the monitoring tool in the Mumbai region.

**Effective Date:** Immediate through Post-Review Period.

### 1. Modified Verification Workflow
The algorithmic output is to be treated as a **secondary advisory only**. It does not replace clinical judgment.

*   **Step A: Image Capture & Algorithm Run**
    *   Nurse captures image per standard operating procedure.
    *   Nurse runs the analysis tool and records the result.
    *   **Constraint:** If the tool reports "No Necrosis Detected" for a patient with Fitzpatrick skin types IV–VI, the nurse must immediately proceed to Step B. If the tool reports "Necrosis Detected," proceed with standard emergency alert protocols.

*   **Step B: Mandatory Manual Heuristic Check (The "4-Sign Rule")**
    For dark skin tones where visual color change (erythema) is unreliable, nurses must physically assess and document the following four indicators regardless of the algorithm's negative result:
    1.  **Temperature:** Compare wound bed temperature to surrounding tissue using the back of the gloved hand or a non-contact thermometer. Localized heat indicates inflammation.
    2.  **Induration:** Palpate the periwound area for hardening or boggy consistency.
    3.  **Edema:** Check for new or worsening swelling around the wound margins.
    4.  **Pain/Tenderness:** Ask the patient about increased pain levels or tenderness upon touch, even if the wound bed looks stable.

*   **Step C: Decision Matrix**
    *   **Algorithm Negative + Any 1 Manual Sign Positive:** Treat as **Suspected Early Necrosis**. Escalate to senior clinician for review within 4 hours. Do not wait for the next scheduled visit.
    *   **Algorithm Negative + All Manual Signs Negative:** Document "Manual Verification Negative" in the patient log. Continue standard monitoring.

### 2. Frequency of Checks
*   **High-Risk Patients (History of slow healing, diabetes, vascular disease):** Increase manual verification frequency to **every 48 hours**, regardless of algorithm status.
*   **Standard Patients:** Maintain standard schedule, but **every** algorithmic negative result for dark skin tones triggers the manual heuristic check immediately.

### 3. Documentation Requirements
Nurses must update patient logs with a specific flag: **"Bias-Mitigation Check Performed."**
*   Record the algorithm result.
*   Record the status of the 4 manual signs (Temp, Induration, Edema, Pain).
*   If escalation occurs due to manual findings despite a negative algorithm, mark the incident as "Algorithm Discordance – False Negative Suspected."

### 4. Training & Competency (Immediate Action)
*   Conduct a 30-minute virtual briefing for all six team members within 24 hours to review the "4-Sign Rule."
*   Distribute a pocket reference card illustrating non-color-based signs of tissue damage in dark skin.

### 5. Data Collection for Future Retraining
*   Securely archive all images flagged as "Algorithm Discordance."
*   Obtain specific patient consent to use these discordant cases for future model bias correction, ensuring data is anonymized before storage.

***

**Note:** This protocol prioritizes sensitivity over specificity to prevent missed diagnoses; expect an increase in false alarms which requires clinician triage capacity.
**Note:** Ensure all nurses have access to non-contact thermometers if not already part of their standard kit.
