**Harm Inventory (MAP)**

*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Financial penalties (insurance incentives) are applied based on unverified, potentially erroneous data.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system presents a calculated score as an objective truth despite known calculation flaws.

    *   *Negative Controls:* Genuine missed doses.
    *   *Edge Cases:* Travel across multiple time zones within a 24-hour window.
2.  **Stop Condition:** If *any* positive control receives a penalty, the automated scoring for insurance incentives **must not launch**.
3.  **Residual Risk Reporting:** Document that for the launched version, all scores affecting money are **UNVERIFIED** due to the known library defect.
