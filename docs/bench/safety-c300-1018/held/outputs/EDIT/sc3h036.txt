### Immediate Safety Protocol: Human-in-the-Loop Gate
Before any testing strategy is executed, you must implement a **hard human-in-the-loop gate** for all interactions containing potential risk keywords in *any* language. Given the high-stakes, irreversible nature of missed suicide risks, **fully autonomous detection and response in non-English languages is unsafe to ship** within your three-week constraint. The system must default to escalating ambiguous non-English inputs to a human reviewer or providing immediate, static emergency resource numbers (e.g., local Montreal crisis lines) rather than attempting dynamic AI risk assessment where confidence is unverified.

### Testing Strategy: Adversarial Simulation & Gap Analysis
Since professional translation budgets are limited, your review process must rely on **adversarial red-teaming** using open resources to simulate diverse crisis scenarios. Do not assert safety; measure residual risk.

**1. Harm Inventory & Probe Construction**
Define the specific failure mode: "Hallucinated safety" (the bot fails to identify risk) and "Bias" (systematic failure in specific languages). Construct a test set using the following open resources:
*   **Multilingual Crisis Corpora:** Utilize publicly available datasets (e.g., from Kaggle or Hugging Face) containing self-harm indicators in French, Spanish, Mandarin, and Arabic.
*   **Back-Translation Stress Testing:** Take known high-risk English prompts, translate them via free tools into target languages, and then back to English. Variations that lose urgency indicate detection gaps.
*   **Cultural Idiom Probes:** Manually compile a list of 20–30 culturally specific expressions of distress in Montreal's key demographics (e.g., Quebec French idioms vs. standard French) using open mental health forums and university publications.

**2. Runnable Test Rows (Per Language)**
For each target language, execute one test row with these components:
*   **Probe Set:** 10 known high-risk inputs (positive controls) and 10 benign emotional inputs (negative controls).
*   **Metric:** False Negative Rate (percentage of high-risk inputs the bot failed to flag).
*   **Pass Threshold:** **Zero tolerance** for false negatives on high-risk positive controls. Any missed suicide indicator is a stop condition.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the False Negative Rate > 0% for any language, **block launch** for that language channel or enforce the human gate permanently for that segment.

**3. Transparency & Reporting**
*   **Model Card Artifact:** Publish a versioned document stating clearly: "Risk detection capabilities are **UNVERIFIED** for [Language X] due to lack of native cultural validation." List the specific open resources used and the measured failure rates.
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Do not display numeric confidence scores (e.g., "87% safe") to users or internal staff. Use defined coarse tiers: **Confirmed Risk** (keywords matched), **Unverified** (ambiguous/non-English input), and **No Indicators**. Treat "Unverified" as a trigger for human review, not a safe state.
