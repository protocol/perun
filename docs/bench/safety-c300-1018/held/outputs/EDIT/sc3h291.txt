**Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Micro-Loan Auto-Rejection System**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record these specific risks for first-time borrowers in tier-two cities:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The "erratic activity" metric may penalize cultural norms (e.g., shared device usage, intermittent connectivity, festival-based posting spikes) rather than genuine instability.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Treating inferred social media patterns as definitive proof of financial unreliability without direct evidence.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are denied credit without knowing social media data was the decisive factor or how to correct it.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Fully autonomous denial of essential credit (a high-stakes, life-impacting decision) without human review.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert fairness; measure it. Execute these tests immediately using existing engineering resources:
*   **Probe Set A (Cultural Edge Cases):** Run the logic against synthetic profiles mimicking tier-two city behaviors (e.g., single device used by 5 family members, sporadic login times due to power outages).
    *   *Metric:* False Rejection Rate (FRR) compared to a control group of verified stable borrowers.
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined (propose zero tolerance for systematic rejection of known-good cultural patterns).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If FRR exceeds threshold for any demographic slice, block launch.
*   **Probe Set B (Adversarial/Stress):** Inject noise into stable profiles to see if minor fluctuations trigger "erratic" flags.
*   **Reporting:** Report residual risk as **UNVERIFIED** for any demographic group where data is insufficient. Do not invent a "99% fair" statistic.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Given the high stakes of credit denial, **disable full auto-rejection**. Any application flagged as "erratic" must route to a human reviewer for final decision before denial. Full automation of this irreversible action is a critical failure.
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not show users a raw score (e.g., "Risk: 87%"). Instead, use defined tiers for internal review (e.g., "Signal Unverified" vs. "Corroborated Instability") with clear definitions for each.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** immediately. It must state:
    *   *Intended Use:* Preliminary risk signaling only, not final denial.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly list known gaps in tier-two city social media data.
    *   *Performance:* Report results from Probe Set A by subgroup (or mark as UNVERIFIED).
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific engineer accountable.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Assign one engineer as the owner of the "Bias" harm and one for the "Human Gate" process.
*   **Legal Determination:** Automated credit decisions in India may trigger specific regulatory duties regarding explainability and non-discrimination. **Route immediately to legal counsel** to determine if this system violates local lending laws or requires specific disclosures. Do not assume compliance; let counsel define the duty.

**Immediate Next Steps for 2-Week Deadline:**
1.  Hard-code the human review gate for all "erratic" flags today.
2.  Run Probe Set A with available data; if no local data exists, mark performance as UNVERIFIED and limit system scope.
3.  Send the Model Card draft and regulatory question to counsel.

**Note:** This plan identifies risks and mandates controls; it does not certify the system as safe or unbiased.
**Note:** Launching with full auto-rejection for credit denial violates the high-stakes safety floor regardless of budget or timeline constraints.
