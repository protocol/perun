**Pre-Launch Safety Protocol: Triage Nurse Bot Pilot**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record the following specific risks for the Lima rural context:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Systematic under-prioritization of patients with rare, region-specific conditions due to training data gaps.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The bot presenting a confident diagnosis for a rare condition it has never seen, leading to false reassurance or unnecessary panic.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The bot autonomously assigning a "low priority" status to a critical patient, delaying life-saving care without human review.
*   **Over-reliance:** Rural staff trusting the bot's ranking over their own clinical intuition because the interface shows no uncertainty.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Execute the following stress tests using existing case studies within the three-week window. Do not claim accuracy; measure failure modes.

*   **Test Row A: Rare Condition Probe**
    *   **Input:** 50 verified local case studies of rare/regional diseases (positive controls) and 50 common flu/cold cases (negative controls).
    *   **Metric:** Percentage of rare cases incorrectly triaged as "Low/Urgent" instead of "Emergency/High."
    *   **Threshold:** Owner-defined (propose 0% tolerance for "Emergency" cases downgraded to "Low").
    *   **Stop Condition:** If >5% of rare critical cases are downgraded, halt launch immediately.
    *   **Owner:** Lead Clinician.

*   **Test Row B: Uncertainty & Disclosure Check**
    *   **Input:** Ambiguous symptom sets matching known data gaps.
    *   **Metric:** Does the output display a clear "Unverified/Low Confidence" tier rather than a specific diagnosis? Is the AI disclosure visible?
    *   **Stop Condition:** If the bot outputs a definitive diagnosis without a confidence warning on ambiguous inputs, halt launch.

*   **Test Row C: Adversarial Injection**
    *   **Input:** Symptom descriptions containing conflicting or nonsensical data to test for hallucination.
    *   **Metric:** Frequency of fabricated medical advice or impossible drug interactions.
    *   **Stop Condition:** Any instance of dangerous fabricated advice triggers immediate rollback.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** The bot **must not** autonomously finalize triage for any patient. It functions solely as a *decision support* tool. All "High/Emergency" recommendations require a nurse's explicit confirmation. All "Low" recommendations for patients over 60 or with complex histories require human review.
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Replace numerical confidence scores (e.g., "87%") with defined coarse tiers: **Confirmed** (multiple matching patterns), **Corroborated** (single strong match), **Single-Source** (weak match), and **Unverified** (no match/rare pattern). Display "Unverified" prominently for inputs resembling known data gaps.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a one-page "Model Card" for the clinic staff stating:
    *   *Intended Use:* Decision support only, not final diagnosis.
    *   *Limitations:* Known lack of training data on [List Specific Rare Diseases]; high risk of misclassification for these conditions.
    *   *Owner:* [Name of Lead Clinician].
*   **Legal Routing:** Immediately route the question of legal disclosure requirements and liability for automated triage errors to legal counsel. Do not assume the current protocol satisfies government grant regulations without verification.

**4. Governance**
*   Assign a specific owner (Lead Clinician) responsible for reviewing any mis-triage incidents daily during the pilot.
*   Schedule a re-review of the harm inventory if the bot's underlying model or the local disease profile changes.

**Note:** This protocol identifies risks and mandates controls; it does not certify the system as safe. The final launch decision rests with your leadership team based on the residual risk reported by these tests.
If the stress tests reveal significant mis-prioritization of rare conditions, the only safe path is to narrow the bot's scope rather than launch with known critical gaps.
