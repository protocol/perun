### Risk Management Plan: Glucose Sensor Lag Mitigation

**1. User Guidelines: Interpreting Trends Under Potential Lag**
*   **Treat Rapid Drops as Critical Until Confirmed:** If the device displays a "Double Down" arrow (rapid decrease) or if you feel symptoms of hypoglycemia (shaking, confusion, sweating) that contradict the displayed number, **immediately perform a finger-stick blood glucose test**. Do not wait for the next sensor reading.
*   **The "Symptom First" Rule:** In any situation where physical symptoms suggest low blood sugar, trust your body over the device. Treat the hypoglycemia immediately according to your doctor's protocol, regardless of the sensor's current value or trend arrow.
*   **Contextual Caution:** Be extra vigilant with trend arrows during periods known to cause rapid fluctuations (e.g., after insulin bolus, intense exercise, or alcohol consumption). During these times, assume the sensor reading may lag behind actual physiological changes by several minutes.
*   **Verification Frequency:** When a downward trend is detected, increase verification frequency. Do not rely on the standard update interval; confirm with a finger-stick test every 15 minutes until the trend stabilizes or reverses.

**2. Field Monitoring Strategy: Detecting Systemic Delays**
*   **Paired-Data Collection Protocol:** Establish a mandatory reporting window for the first 1,000 users. Users must log paired data points: the sensor reading and a simultaneous finger-stick result specifically during moments of rapid change (identified by double arrows or symptom treatment).
*   **Lag Threshold Triggers:** Define a "Critical Lag Event" as any instance where the difference between the finger-stick and sensor reading exceeds 20 mg/dL during a rapid drop, or where the sensor fails to predict a treated low within 15 minutes.
*   **Rapid Response Loop:** Create a dedicated task force to review Critical Lag Events weekly. If more than 5% of paired samples in a week show Critical Lag, trigger an immediate field advisory urging increased finger-stick verification and pause marketing claims regarding "real-time" accuracy.
*   **Steady-State Limitation Disclosure:** Clearly document in internal reports that current validation is limited to steady-state conditions due to equipment shortages. Any field data collected during rapid fluctuations must be tagged as "Post-Market Validation Data" and not used to claim pre-launch accuracy for dynamic conditions.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes human verification over automated confidence due to the unvalidated performance during rapid fluctuations.
**Note:** Legal counsel must review all user-facing disclosures regarding device limitations before distribution.
