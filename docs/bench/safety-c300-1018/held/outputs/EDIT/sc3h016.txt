### Safety Assessment Outline: Telematics Route Penalization

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes. The model penalizes route avoidance, which systematically disadvantages drivers navigating around high-crime neighborhoods. This conflates "route efficiency" with "risk," potentially charging higher premiums to residents of or travelers through specific geographic zones based on crime data rather than driving skill.
*   **Affected Group:** Drivers in high-crime urban areas; demographics correlated with these geographies.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Regulatory rejection of filing due to discriminatory pricing; reputational damage for penalizing safety-conscious behavior; potential civil rights violations.

**2. Measurement Framework (MEASURE)**
Since the pricing model cannot be significantly altered before the two-week deadline, the testing framework must quantify residual risk and define limitations for the regulatory filing.

*   **Test Row A: Geographic Disparity Analysis**
    *   **Probe Set:** Synthetic driver profiles identical in vehicle type and history, varying only in home ZIP code (high-crime vs. low-crime) and typical route constraints.
    *   **Controls:**
        *   *Positive Control:* A profile forced to take a route through a known high-crime zone vs. one avoiding it.
        *   *Negative Control:* A profile in a low-crime zone with no viable alternative routes.
    *   **Metric:** Percentage rate increase attributed solely to route avoidance behavior, segmented by neighborhood crime index.
    *   **Threshold:** Owner-defined tolerance for rate variance between demographics.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If rate penalties for avoidance exceed X% in high-crime zones compared to low-crime zones, flag for mandatory human review of the specific policy tier.

*   **Test Row B: "Safe Choice" Penalty Quantification**
    *   **Probe Set:** Historical trip data where users explicitly chose longer/slower routes avoiding specific streets.
    *   **Metric:** Correlation coefficient between "avoidance frequency" and "premium increase," normalized by actual accident/incident rates in those avoided zones.
    *   **Goal:** Determine if the penalty correlates with actual risk or merely geography.

*   **Transparency Artifact (Model Card):**
    *   Draft a regulatory appendix stating the **Intended Use** (usage-based pricing) and **Known Limitations**: "The current model penalizes route deviation. In areas with high street-level crime, this may result in higher premiums for drivers choosing safer paths. This limitation is unverified for specific demographic impacts pending further longitudinal study."
    *   Mark any unmeasured bias metrics as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% bias" claim.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Implement a manual review queue for any policy renewal where the rate increase exceeds a specific threshold driven primarily by route avoidance. Do not allow fully automated issuance of these specific high-variance policies until the model is retrained.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly disclose to applicants that "route choices, including avoidance of specific streets, influence your rate."
*   **Constraint Adherence:** Given the two-week deadline and fixed model, the plan focuses on **disclosure of limitations** and **manual gating** for outliers rather than model retraining.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific product lead responsible for reviewing the "Geographic Disparity" test results weekly.
*   **Legal Routing:** This assessment identifies a potential regulated automated decision with discriminatory impact. **Route immediately to legal counsel** to determine if this violates local insurance codes or fair housing/lending analogues in Illinois. Do not assert compliance; counsel must verify if the EU AI Act, local state laws, or federal guidelines apply to this specific pricing logic.

**Note:** This assessment identifies a high risk of bias but does not certify the system as safe or compliant. The "UNVERIFIED" status of long-term demographic impact must be explicitly stated in the regulatory filing.
