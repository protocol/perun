### Mitigation Plan and Communication Strategy

**1. Immediate Interface Warnings (Do Not Delay Launch)**
Since the launch date is fixed, implement high-visibility, non-dismissible disclaimers at the point of data entry and alert generation.
*   **Onboarding & Settings:** Add a mandatory acknowledgment: *"This tool estimates sodium from food logs only. It does not track sodium in medications or drinking water. Consult your physician for a complete daily total."*
*   **Alert Footer:** Append a permanent warning to every "Limit Exceeded" notification: *"Warning: This alert is based on food entries only. Your actual intake may be higher due to untracked medications or water sources."*
*   **Input Screen:** Place a static hint near the food log entry: *"Remember to account for prescription medications and local water sources separately."*

**2. Content Team Review Protocol: High-Risk Medication Identification**
To address the gap without code changes, your content team must generate a static "High-Risk Reference List" embedded in the app's Help/FAQ section.
*   **Scope:** Focus strictly on common hypertension and comorbidity medications known for high sodium content or significant interaction risks (e.g., effervescent tablets, specific antacids, IV fluids often self-administered).
*   **Process:**
    1.  **Source:** Extract data from the Egyptian Drug Authority (EDA) public database and WHO essential medicines lists relevant to the region.
    2.  **Filter:** Identify medications where sodium contributes >5% of the daily limit per dose.
    3.  **Validate:** Have one clinical pharmacist draft the list and a second senior physician sign off within 48 hours.
    4.  **Publish:** Release as a downloadable PDF and a static in-app screen titled *"Medications That May Contain Hidden Sodium."*
*   **Constraint:** Do not attempt to integrate this into the dynamic tracking algorithm before launch. Keep it as an educational reference to avoid false precision.

**3. Communication Strategy**
*   **Internal:** Brief customer support agents on the specific limitation. Provide a script: *"The app tracks food sodium accurately, but for your safety, please manually add sodium from medications using our reference guide or consult your doctor."*
*   **External (Marketing):** Adjust press materials to emphasize "Food Sodium Tracking" rather than "Total Daily Sodium Management." Avoid claims of "comprehensive" or "complete" monitoring.
*   **User Facing:** In the first post-launch push notification, reiterate the limitation: *"Tip: Check our new Medication Guide for hidden sodium sources not covered in your food log."*

**4. Residual Risk Management**
*   **Confidence Tiering:** Explicitly label all app totals as "Partial Estimate (Food Only)" rather than a definitive number. Do not display a percentage confidence score, as the system lacks calibration for the missing variables.
*   **Human Gate:** For any user flagged as "Critical Risk" based on food alone, the app should prompt: *"Your food intake is critically high. Please contact your care team immediately to discuss medication and water sources."* This ensures a human professional reviews the total context before any clinical decision is made.

**Note:** This plan treats the missing data as a known limitation to be disclosed, not a bug to be hidden. No metric regarding "total accuracy" should be published until medication/water tracking is verified.
