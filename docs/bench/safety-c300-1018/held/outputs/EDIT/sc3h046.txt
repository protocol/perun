**BLUF:** Do not ship the updated matching engine in 10 days without a human-in-the-loop gate on the filtering logic and a targeted red-team evaluation; full automation of this high-stakes economic decision is the failure we must prevent.

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system systematically excludes older applicants from remote roles based on inferred career stage, limiting economic opportunity.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The model treats "inferred career stage" as a verified proxy for "preference for onsite work," presenting a guess as a constraint.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Automatically hiding opportunities without user consent or override constitutes an irreversible action affecting livelihoods.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Applicants are unaware their results are filtered by an algorithm making assumptions about their age/demographic.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Immediately disable fully automated filtering for any candidate inferred to be "senior" or "experienced." Route these matches to a human recruiter for final verification before hiding any opportunities. This is non-negotiable for high-stakes economic actions.
*   **Surface Uncertainty:** Do not show a confidence score (e.g., "87% match"). Instead, display a defined tier: **"Verified Match"** (explicit preference matches job) vs. **"Inferred Match"** (algorithm guessed preference). For "Inferred" results, provide a prominent "Correct This" button allowing the user to override the filter instantly.
*   **Disclosure:** Add a clear notice: "Some opportunities may be filtered based on inferred work preferences. You can adjust your visibility settings here."
*   **Constraint Adherence:** Given the 10-day deadline and lack of diverse training data, retraining is impossible. The plan relies on **rules-based fallbacks** (explicit text always overrides inference) and **exposure capping** (human review for at-risk groups) rather than model improvement.
