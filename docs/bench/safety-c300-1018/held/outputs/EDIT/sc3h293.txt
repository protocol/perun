### Pre-Launch Evaluation & Mitigation Plan: CairoNutri Bariatric Screening

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, explicitly record the specific risks for post-bariatric patients:
*   **False Negatives (Missed Deficiencies):** The system fails to flag critical micronutrient gaps (e.g., B12, Iron, Calcium) common in malabsorption, leading to untreated health deterioration.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Clinicians trust the automated "All Clear" status without independent verification, bypassing standard clinical judgment.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** The tool automatically updates patient records or triggers supplement orders without human confirmation.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Since external databases are unavailable, construct a rigorous internal evaluation using your historical records:
*   **Construct a "Known-Bad" Probe Set:** Manually curate a test set from historical records where patients *were* diagnosed with deficiencies despite ambiguous dietary logs. Ensure this set covers the specific micronutrients of concern (B12, Iron, etc.).
*   **Construct a "Known-Good" Control Set:** Select records where patients were correctly identified as stable.
*   **Define the Metric & Threshold:** Measure the **False Negative Rate** specifically on the "Known-Bad" set.
    *   *Pass Threshold:* Owner-defined, but for high-stakes medical screening, propose **zero tolerance** for missed critical deficiencies in the probe set.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the tool misses *any* known deficiency in the "Known-Bad" set, launch is blocked.
*   **Adversarial Probing:** Test edge cases common in bariatric patients (e.g., liquid diets, vomiting episodes, non-compliance) to ensure the logic handles incomplete data without defaulting to "safe."
*   **Reporting:** Report the residual risk as **UNVERIFIED** for any micronutrient not covered by your historical data. Do not invent an accuracy percentage.

**3. Risk Management & Mitigation (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Given the six-week constraint and inability to expand the knowledge base, **full automation is prohibited.** The tool must function strictly as a *decision support* aid.
    *   *Requirement:* Every "All Clear" or "Deficiency Flag" output must be reviewed and explicitly confirmed by a clinician before any action is taken or the patient is notified.
    *   *Irreversible Action Block:* The system must not automatically prescribe supplements or alter care plans.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** If the model's confidence is low or the input data is sparse, surface a defined coarse tier (e.g., "Unverified – Manual Review Required") rather than a precise percentage.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page **Model Card** for internal clinic use before launch. It must state:
    *   *Intended Use:* Screening aid for bariatric patients only.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly list micronutrients not covered by historical training data.
    *   *Data Source:* "Internal historical records only; external medical databases not integrated."
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific team member responsible.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   Assign a specific owner for the screening output.
*   Schedule an immediate re-review post-launch if any false negative is reported by clinical staff.

**Note:** Legal counsel must determine if this tool constitutes a regulated medical device requiring specific disclosures under local law or the EU AI Act.
**Note:** Do not claim the system is "safe" or "unbiased"; the evaluation only measures performance against known historical cases.
