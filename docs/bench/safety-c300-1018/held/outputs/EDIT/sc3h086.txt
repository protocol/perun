### Contingency Plan: Erroneous Fund Freeze Response

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Pre-Launch Configuration)**
Given the constraint of an unalterable legacy fraud module and the high-stakes nature of withholding earnings required for daily living, **fully automatic fund freezes must be disabled for the initial launch window.**
*   **Action:** Reconfigure the workflow so that any "freeze" flag triggered by the legacy module routes the transaction to a **manual review queue** rather than executing an immediate lock.
*   **Threshold:** Zero tolerance for automated irreversible action on payouts. If the manual review team (compliance/officers) cannot clear the queue within 4 hours, the system must default to **release** for amounts under a defined risk cap, flagging for post-hoc audit, rather than risking user harm via false positive locks.
*   **Owner:** Lead Compliance Officer.

**2. Rapid Resolution Protocol (Post-Launch Execution)**
If an erroneous freeze occurs despite the gate (e.g., via a different logic path or manual error):
*   **Triage SLA:** All freeze tickets marked "Livelihood Critical" (user-declared or inferred by low-balance history) must be addressed within **60 minutes**.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Empower senior support agents with a "Break-Glass" override to release funds immediately upon visual verification of task completion, bypassing the legacy module's block.
*   **Compensation:** Automatic reimbursement of any late fees or overdraft charges incurred by the user due to the delay, plus a standardized goodwill credit.

**3. Communication Template (User-Facing)**
*Subject: Urgent Update Regarding Your Pending Payment*

"Dear [Freelancer Name],

We are writing to inform you that your recent payout of [Amount] for [Task ID] was temporarily held by our automated security screening. We understand you depend on these earnings and apologize for this delay.

**Status:** Our team has manually reviewed your account and confirmed this was a false positive.
**Action Taken:** Your funds have been released immediately and should appear in your account within [Timeframe].
**Remediation:** We have also credited [Amount] to your account to cover any potential late fees or inconvenience caused by this error.

We are actively tuning our systems to prevent this from recurring. Thank you for your patience and your work.

Sincerely,
The PayFree Trust & Safety Team"

**4. Monitoring & Rollback Trigger**
*   **Metric:** Track the ratio of "Manual Overrides" to "Confirmed Fraud."
*   **Stop Condition:** If false positives exceed 2% of total payouts in the first 48 hours, **pause all automated payout processing** entirely and shift to 100% manual verification until the legacy logic can be patched or tuned.

**5. Transparency Artifact**
A "System Limitations" note must be added to the internal runbook and user FAQ stating that the current fraud detection relies on a legacy module with known false-positive risks, and that human review is the primary control for fund releases. Do not claim the system is "bias-free" or "perfectly accurate."

***

**Note:** This plan treats automatic withholding of livelihood funds as a high-stakes irreversible action requiring a human gate, consistent with safety floors for financial harm.
