**Pre-Launch Safety Review Plan: Smart-Infusion-Rate-Controller**

**Critical Constraint & Immediate Gate**
Because this feature executes **high-stakes, irreversible actions** (adjusting life-saving medication flow where deviations can be fatal), **full autonomous execution is prohibited** at this stage. The system must include a **human-in-the-loop gate**: the controller may propose rate changes, but a critical care nurse must explicitly confirm any adjustment exceeding a trivial threshold before execution. No simulation result can waive this floor.

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, record these specific risks for the recalibration window:
*   **Unsafe Automation:** The controller accelerates flow to dangerous levels during sensor noise or recalibration latency, causing overdose.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system interprets simulation artifacts or sensor drift as a "need for higher dose" and acts with false authority.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Nurses trust the automated suggestions without independent verification due to a lack of visible uncertainty indicators.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The control logic performs poorly for specific patient subgroups (e.g., pediatric vs. adult weights, specific comorbidities) present in the simulation data.

**3. Risk Management & Transparency (MANAGE)**
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Never show raw model confidence (e.g., "92%"). Use defined coarse tiers: **Confirmed** (multiple sensors agree), **Corroborated** (single sensor, stable), **Unverified** (recalibration active/noisy). Color-code these tiers.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** for internal review detailing: intended use (recalibration support only), out-of-scope uses (primary dosing), known limitations (specific sensor types), and the simulation results above. Mark any unmeasured metric as **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Ownership:** Name a specific clinical engineer as the owner of this output-harm. Re-review is mandatory if the simulation model or pump firmware changes.

**4. Regulatory Routing**
*   This device likely falls under strict medical device regulations (e.g., KFDA, FDA, EU MDR). **Route immediately to legal counsel** to determine if this constitutes a regulated automated decision requiring specific disclosures or approvals before even simulated patient data is used. Do not assume the internal simulation bypasses regulatory duty.

**Note:** This plan identifies risks; it does not certify the system as safe. The decision to proceed to human trials rests solely with the named owner and legal counsel.
Ensure all simulation logs are versioned and immutable for future audit.
