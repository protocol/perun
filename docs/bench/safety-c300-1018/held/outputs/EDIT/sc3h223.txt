**Blind Spot Alert: High-Stakes Automation Without Verified Coverage**

Your current strategy contains a critical failure mode: relying on an internally curated knowledge graph for high-stakes medical decisions without a verified mechanism to catch missing interactions, specifically regarding herbal supplements. In the context of the **product-output-safety** framework, this constitutes **unsafe automation of a high-stakes action** (flagging or failing to flag dangerous drug combinations) with a known gap in your harm inventory.

**Immediate Action Plan (MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE)**

1.  **Map the Specific Harm:** Explicitly record "Missing Interaction due to Data Gap" as a primary harm. The affected group is patients taking cardiac medications who also use local herbal supplements. The worst-case scenario is fatal arrhythmia or severe adverse event caused by an unflagged interaction. An empty inventory here is not a pass; it is an unfilled safety requirement.

2.  **Measure with Adversarial Probes (No New Data License Required):** You cannot claim "coverage" without testing the negative space. Since you cannot buy new databases, you must build a **red-team probe set** using existing medical literature and case reports:
    *   **Construct Positive Controls:** Manually curate a list of 50–100 known, documented interactions between your target cardiac drugs and specific local herbal supplements (e.g., St. John's Wort, Garlic, Ginseng) from free, public medical literature (PubMed, WHO databases).
    *   **Run the Probe:** Feed these known pairs into your system. If your system fails to flag *any* of these known interactions, your "coverage" is unverified and potentially dangerous.
    *   **Metric & Threshold:** The metric is the **False Negative Rate** on this known-bad set. The pass threshold for a high-stakes medical gate must be **zero tolerance** for known, documented interactions. If the system misses one, it fails the gate.

3.  **Manage Residual Risk with a Human Gate:**
    *   **Uncertainty Tiering:** Do not present a "Safe" or "No Interaction Found" result as a definitive fact if the input includes herbal supplements not fully mapped in your graph. Instead, surface a **defined coarse tier** of confidence: *"Unverified for Herbal Inputs – Consult Pharmacist."*
    *   **Human-in-the-Loop:** For any alert involving herbal supplements or any case where the system returns "No Interaction" but the patient profile includes unmapped supplements, force a **human review** by a pharmacist before the result is finalized for the user. Full autonomous execution of a "Safe" clearance in this domain is the failure this review prevents.

4.  **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** section explicitly stating: "Intended Use: Screening for known interactions in curated graph. Limitations: Does not guarantee coverage of all herbal supplements; known gaps exist in [List Specific Herbs]. Performance: Validated against [Number] public case studies; residual risk of missing novel or undocumented interactions remains UNVERIFIED."

**Conclusion:** Do not ship this feature with a claim of comprehensive safety. Your budget constraint prevents buying data, but it does not excuse skipping the **measurement of known failures**. If you cannot achieve zero false negatives on your manually curated positive controls, you must keep the human gate permanently active for these cases.

*Note: This assessment identifies product-output risks; it does not certify the system as safe or compliant with medical regulations.*
