### Pre-Launch Review Plan: Elderly Fraud Detector Safety

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition**
*   **Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes (Systematic disadvantage to elderly users with atypical but legitimate patterns).
*   **Affected Group:** Customers aged 65+ making large donations, travel bookings, or medical payments.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** A vulnerable senior is locked out of funds during a medical emergency or while traveling abroad due to a false positive freeze, causing physical harm or financial distress.

**2. Synthetic Data & Evaluation Strategy (MEASURE)**
Since historical data is limited, construct a **Synthetic Adversarial Probe Set** specifically for the 65+ demographic. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy; measure performance by this subgroup.

*   **Probe Construction:** Generate synthetic transaction streams representing "Safe Atypical" behaviors:
    *   *Scenario A:* Large one-time charitable donation.
    *   *Scenario B:* Sudden high-value travel booking (flight + hotel) from a home location.
    *   *Scenario C:* Emergency medical payment to an unfamiliar provider.
    *   *Controls:* Include known fraud patterns (positive controls) and routine spending (negative controls) for the same demographic.
*   **Metric:** False Positive Rate (FPR) specifically for the 65+ "Safe Atypical" segment.
*   **Pass Threshold:** Owner-defined. Given the high stakes, propose **zero tolerance** for irreversible freezes on these specific synthetic probes without human review.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the system auto-freezes any "Safe Atypical" synthetic probe without triggering a human review workflow, **block launch**.
*   **Injection/Leakage Check:** Verify that synthetic user data does not leak into other user contexts and that the model cannot be manipulated via transaction descriptions (prompt injection via memo fields).

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** Because account freezing is a **high-stakes, irreversible action** for the user, full autonomous execution is unsafe given the current data gaps.
    *   *Requirement:* Any freeze trigger generated for a user aged 65+ (or matching the "Safe Atypical" pattern) must route to a human agent for confirmation before the account is locked.
    *   *Fallback:* If real-time human review is impossible within the transaction window, the system must default to **allowing the transaction** and flagging it for post-hoc review, rather than risking a false freeze.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not display raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% fraud risk") to internal agents or customers. Use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed Fraud* (Multiple strong signals)
    *   *Suspicious – Review Required* (Atypical pattern, single source)
    *   *Unverified* (Insufficient data)
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a preliminary **Model Card** section documenting:
    *   *Intended Use:* Fraud detection for general population.
    *   *Known Limitations:* Limited historical training data for 65+ atypical spending; high risk of false positives for this group.
    *   *Status:* Mark performance metrics for the 65+ subgroup as **UNVERIFIED** until the synthetic evals are run. Do not invent an accuracy rate.

**4. Governance & Next Steps**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific product owner responsible for reviewing the synthetic eval results before the 5-day deadline.
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult counsel immediately regarding regulatory obligations for automated decisions affecting vulnerable populations (e.g., EU AI Act implications for high-risk systems) and disclosure requirements. Do not assert legal compliance internally; route the determination.
*   **Execution:** Run the synthetic probe set immediately. If the stop condition is met, the go-live date must be delayed or the scope restricted to non-freeze alerts until the HITL gate is fully operational.

**Note:** This plan identifies residual risk; it does not certify the system as safe or unbiased. The decision to ship rests with the assigned owner based on the eval results.
Legal determination on regulatory duties (EU AI Act, etc.) must be confirmed by counsel, not assumed.
