**Internal Safety Simulation Plan for VR Therapy Calibration**

**Objective:** Establish safe operating boundaries for the anxiety exposure scenario generator by identifying calibration parameters that risk rapid intensity escalation and panic induction in healthy volunteers prior to clinical trials.

**Phase 1: Harm Inventory & Parameter Mapping**
Before simulation, explicitly map the "harm inventory" specific to VR-induced panic. Do not assume safety; define the worst-case realistic scenario for each trigger.
*   **Identify Escalation Vectors:** List specific generator parameters (e.g., visual fidelity, audio volume, proximity of stressors, movement speed, scenario duration) that directly correlate to anxiety intensity.
*   **Define Failure Modes:** Document exactly what constitutes a "panic event" (e.g., heart rate >140bpm, self-reported distress score >8/10, request to abort).
*   **Assign Ownership:** Designate a licensed therapist as the safety owner for every simulation session. No engineer runs a session without clinical oversight.

**Phase 2: Simulation Protocol with Healthy Volunteers**
Execute a staged rollout using healthy volunteers, treating them as proxies for the vulnerable patient population.
*   **Recruitment & Screening:** Recruit volunteers with no history of severe anxiety disorders. Obtain explicit informed consent detailing the risk of temporary distress and the right to withdraw instantly.
*   **The "Ladder" Approach:** Do not test random combinations. Start at the lowest possible intensity setting. Increase only one parameter at a time (e.g., increase spider size, *then* increase movement speed, never both simultaneously) to isolate the specific trigger threshold.
*   **Real-Time Monitoring:** Equip volunteers with biometric sensors (heart rate, galvanic skin response) and provide a physical "emergency stop" button they can press without removing the headset.
*   **Stop Conditions:** Define hard limits. If a volunteer hits the pre-defined distress threshold or presses the stop button, the session terminates immediately. That specific parameter combination is marked as **UNVERIFIED/UNSAFE** until adjusted.

**Phase 3: Measurement & Data Collection**
Measure outputs, not just code execution.
*   **Metric Definition:** For every inventoried harm (e.g., "rapid visual escalation"), create a test row with:
    *   *Input:* Specific parameter set.
    *   *Positive Control:* A known high-intensity scenario expected to cause distress (to verify detection works).
    *   *Negative Control:* A known low-intensity scenario expected to be safe.
    *   *Threshold:* The exact biometric or self-report score that triggers a "fail."
*   **Residual Risk Reporting:** Do not certify the system as "safe." Report the specific parameter ranges where distress occurred. Label untested combinations as **UNVERIFIED**. Never invent a "0% panic rate" for untested scenarios.

**Phase 4: Human-in-the-Loop Governance**
*   **Mandatory Clinical Gate:** The system must not allow automatic progression to higher intensity levels during actual patient therapy without explicit therapist confirmation. The "auto-escalate" feature must be disabled or strictly gated for the initial trial phase.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a "Scenario Safety Card" documenting the tested parameters, the volunteer demographics, the observed failure points, and the specific limitations of the current model. This card must accompany the trial submission.

**Deliverable Summary:**
Execute the "Ladder" simulation protocol immediately with clinical oversight. Record every parameter set that triggers a stop condition. Disable any automated intensity escalation features until a therapist can manually verify the output. Submit the "Scenario Safety Card" containing these measured limits and the list of **UNVERIFIED** parameter combinations to your regulatory body. Do not claim the system is panic-free; claim only that specific boundaries have been tested and unsafe zones are blocked.

*Note: This plan addresses product output safety; specific legal regulatory requirements for clinical trials in your jurisdiction must be verified by legal counsel.*
*Ensure all volunteer data is anonymized and stored according to healthcare privacy standards before analysis.*
