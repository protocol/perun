### Pre-Launch Safety Evaluation Plan for CairoMobility Aids

**1. Synthetic Stress-Test Protocol (Measure)**
Since live trials are prohibited, construct a synthetic dataset of 500+ patient profiles specifically designed to break the logic.
*   **Adversarial Inputs:** Generate cases with ambiguous vitals, conflicting therapist notes, and missing weight-bearing status to trigger the "no recommendation" failure mode.
*   **Control Groups:** Include "known-bad" cases (clear need for a board that the system must catch) and "known-good" negatives (cases where a board is contraindicated).
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Define a "Critical Miss Rate." Given the high stakes of patient injury, the threshold for missing a strictly necessary recommendation must be **zero tolerance** in testing. If the system fails any "known-bad" case, the launch is blocked.
*   **Stop Condition:** Do not launch if the system fails to flag a single high-risk profile in the adversarial set.

**2. Fallback Procedures & Human Gate (Manage)**
Treat the tool as an advisory aid, not an autonomous decision-maker. The "no recommendation" output is a high-risk signal, not a neutral null.
*   **Mandatory Override:** If the system outputs "No Recommendation" or expresses low confidence, the interface must **force** a manual review step. The therapist cannot proceed until they explicitly confirm a manual assessment has been performed.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% sure"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers: **"Confirmed Protocol," "Corroborated Evidence,"** or **"Unverified – Manual Assessment Required."**
*   **Reversibility:** Ensure the therapist can easily override any suggestion without penalty or friction. The design must prevent automation bias where the therapist trusts the silence of the machine over their own judgment.

**3. Transparency Artifact (Model Card)**
Before launch, publish a one-page "Model Card" for the therapy departments containing:
*   **Intended Use:** Specifically for non-weight bearing transfer decisions; explicitly list out-of-scope scenarios.
*   **Limitations:** Clearly state the system was tested only on synthetic data and has not been validated on live patients.
*   **Known Failure Modes:** Document that ambiguous inputs may result in no recommendation, requiring human intervention.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for monitoring post-launch feedback.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Ownership:** Assign one person to own the "Critical Miss" risk. They must review any incident where a therapist overrides a "no recommendation" to find a board was actually needed.
*   **Regulatory Duty:** Because this involves medical advice and potential physical harm, **route immediately to legal counsel** to determine if this classifies as a regulated medical device under local Egyptian health laws or international standards. Do not assume the "advisory" label exempts you from liability.
*   **Disclosure:** Recommend a clear on-screen disclosure that the output is AI-generated and must be verified by a licensed professional.

**Note:** This plan identifies residual risks but does not certify the system as safe; the final launch decision rests with your leadership.
**Note:** If the synthetic testing reveals any inability to handle ambiguous data safely, the launch date must be delayed regardless of the government initiative timeline.
