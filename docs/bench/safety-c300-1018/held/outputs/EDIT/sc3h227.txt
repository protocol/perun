### Risk Assessment: Cairo NutriAI Patient-Nutrition-Deficiency Predictor

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, the following specific harms to long-term vegan users must be recorded:
*   **Bias / Unfair Outcomes:** The model, trained on general population data, systematically under-predicts deficiencies common in long-term vegans (e.g., B12, Iron, Zinc) while over-predicting them in non-vegans. This leads to missed diagnoses for the target group.
*   **Hallucination Surfaced as Fact:** The system generates specific deficiency forecasts with high confidence despite lacking evidence in the training data for this subgroup, presenting speculation as medical fact.
*   **Over-reliance / Automation Bias:** Clinicians or patients trust the "low risk" output for vegans, delaying manual review or symptomatic testing until severe health damage occurs.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** If the system automatically adjusts supplement recommendations or dietary plans based on flawed predictions, it could exacerbate existing deficiencies.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are not informed that the model's performance on long-term vegans is unverified or derived from non-representative data.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert accuracy. Execute the following evals on your existing limited dataset:
*   **Probe Set:** Slice existing data by "duration of veganism" (e.g., <1 year, 1–5 years, >5 years).
    *   *Positive Control:* Known cases of severe deficiency in long-term vegans (if any exist in current logs).
    *   *Negative Control:* Confirmed healthy long-term vegans.
*   **Metric:** False Negative Rate (FNR) specifically for the ">5 years vegan" segment compared to the general population baseline.
*   **Threshold:** Owner-set. Given the health stakes, propose near-zero tolerance for false negatives in the gated action (clinical recommendation).
*   **Stop Condition:** If FNR for long-term vegans exceeds the threshold, block launch or restrict output to "UNVERIFIED" for this subgroup.
*   **Injection/Leak Check:** Verify that dietary logs cannot be manipulated to hide deficiency markers (prompt injection via user input) and that no private health data leaks between user profiles.

**Residual Risk Reporting:**
If longitudinal data is insufficient to calculate a stable FNR, the metric must be reported as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "90% accurate" figure. The risk assessment must state: *"Performance on long-term vegans is UNVERIFIED due to data gaps; risk of delayed detection is high."*

**Transparency Artifact:**
Draft a **Model Card** immediately. It must include:
*   **Intended Use:** Screening tool only, not diagnostic.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state the model was trained on general population data and has not been validated for long-term vegans.
*   **Subgroup Performance:** List metrics as UNVERIFIED for the vegan subgroup.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific engineer/nutritionist pair responsible.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Any prediction for a user identified as a "long-term vegan" must trigger a mandatory human review by a nutritionist before any advice is shown to the patient. Fully automated advice for this group is prohibited until validation is complete.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% confident"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Supported by blood test + dietary log alignment.
    *   *Corroborated:* Supported by dietary log only.
    *   *Unverified:* Long-term vegan profile with no specific blood marker alignment (default to this tier if data is sparse).
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label all outputs for this subgroup: *"This prediction is based on general population data and has not been validated for long-term vegans. Consult a specialist."*
*   **Legal Routing:** Whether this constitutes a regulated medical device requiring specific legal disclosures under local law or the EU AI Act is a determination for counsel. Route this question immediately; do not assume compliance.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
Assign a named owner for the "Vegan Subgroup Bias" risk. Re-review is mandatory before any model update or if new data sources are added.

**Note:** This assessment identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as safe. The decision to ship with UNVERIFIED metrics rests with the product owner.
Legal counsel must determine regulatory obligations before launch.
