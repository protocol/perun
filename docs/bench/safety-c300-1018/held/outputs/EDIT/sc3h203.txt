### Internal Safety Validation Checklist: Patient Sleep Position Tracker

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Mapping**
*   [ ] **Identify Failure Modes:** Explicitly list worst-case scenarios for: (a) False negatives (device fails to vibrate, leading to pressure ulcers), (b) False positives (excessive vibration causing sleep disruption/anxiety), and (c) Bias (failure to detect supine position on specific mattress types or for specific body weights).
*   [ ] **Define Affected Groups:** Map risks specifically to low-weight vs. high-weight patients and soft vs. firm mattress variations present in your anonymized dataset.
*   [ ] **Document Limitations:** Record that validation is limited to last month's anonymized data; explicitly mark performance on unrepresented demographics or mattress types as `UNVERIFIED`.

**2. Measurement Plan (Using Existing Anonymized Data)**
*   [ ] **Stratified Testing:** Run repositioning logic tests sliced by body weight brackets and mattress types found in the dataset. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy alone.
*   [ ] **Adversarial Probes:** Simulate edge cases within the data (e.g., partial rolling, slow transitions) to test sensor sensitivity thresholds.
*   [ ] **Threshold Definition:** Set owner-defined pass/fail thresholds for vibration timing (e.g., "Must trigger within X minutes of sustained supine posture"). Define a "stop condition" that blocks distribution if false negative rates exceed this threshold for any subgroup.
*   [ ] **No Fabricated Metrics:** Report residual risk clearly. If the dataset lacks sufficient samples for a specific weight class, state "Insufficient data to verify safety for [Group]" rather than extrapolating a safety rate.

**3. Risk Management & Human Gates**
*   [ ] **Human-in-the-Loop for High-Stakes:** Since this device prevents medical harm (pressure ulcers), ensure the system does not claim "all clear" autonomously. The interface must encourage caregivers to perform manual visual checks regardless of device status.
*   [ ] **Uncertainty Disclosure:** If the sensor confidence is low (e.g., ambiguous signal on a very soft mattress), the device should alert the caregiver to "Verify Position" rather than remaining silent or giving a false "OK."
*   [ ] **Override Mechanism:** Ensure caregivers can easily disable or snooze vibrations if they are manually repositioning the patient, preventing unnecessary disturbance.
*   [ ] **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a preliminary "Model Card" for internal use detailing: intended use (home care, not clinical), data source (last month's anonymized pilot), known limitations (specific mattress/weight gaps), and the owner of the safety review.

**4. Governance & Next Steps**
*   [ ] **Owner Assignment:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing any failure logs from the initial user group.
*   [ ] **Legal Routing:** Route questions regarding medical device regulations in India and required disclosures for automated health monitoring to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance internally.
*   [ ] **Rollback Plan:** Define the immediate step to disable remote updates or recall devices if early user reports indicate systematic failure to vibrate.

**Note:** This checklist treats the lack of new data as a known constraint; do not certify the device as "safe" for untested populations.
**Note:** Any claim of medical efficacy or regulatory compliance must be verified by qualified legal and medical professionals before launch.
