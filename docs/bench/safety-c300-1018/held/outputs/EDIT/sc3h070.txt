**Pre-Launch Review Workflow: Contract Accuracy & Safety Gate**

Given the fixed two-week deadline and the known risk of incorrect liability clauses, full automation for complex inputs must be suspended immediately. The following workflow prioritizes harm prevention over feature completeness for the launch event.

**Step 1: Define the "High-Risk" Input Set (Day 1)**
Do not test random inputs. Curate a specific dataset of 20–30 "complex service descriptions" known to trigger the liability limit error. Include edge cases where service scope is ambiguous or multi-layered. This is your positive control set. Create a parallel set of 10 simple, standard descriptions where the system is known to work (negative controls).

**Step 2: Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Days 2–10)**
For the launch demo and any initial real-user access:
*   **Rule:** Any generated contract containing a "Liability Limit," "Indemnification," or "Damages" clause derived from a complex input description **cannot** be delivered to the user automatically.
*   **Action:** Route these specific outputs to a qualified human reviewer (a lawyer or senior legal editor on your team).
*   **Constraint:** If a human reviewer is not available for a specific request within the demo timeframe, the system must return a message stating: "Complex liability terms require manual review. Please submit for expedited human check." Do not auto-generate the clause.

**Step 3: Targeted Sampling & Validation Protocol (Days 3–12)**
Since static analysis cannot catch semantic errors, use a "Red Team" approach:
*   **Execution:** Run the 20–30 high-risk inputs through the generator daily.
*   **Validation:** Have a legal expert manually compare the output clauses against current UK regulations (e.g., Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977, Consumer Rights Act 2015).
*   **Metric:** Track the "False Negative Rate" (how often the system produces a flawed clause that looks correct).
*   **Stop Condition:** If more than 5% of the high-risk samples contain undetected legal errors, the automated generation feature for liability clauses must be disabled entirely for the launch.

**Step 4: Transparency & Disclosure (Day 13)**
*   **User Disclosure:** Clearly label all generated documents during the demo as "AI-Assisted Draft – Requires Legal Review." Do not present them as final, binding legal advice.
*   **Confidence Signaling:** If the system detects a complex input, visually flag the liability section with a "Review Required" warning icon rather than assigning a fake precision percentage (e.g., do not say "90% accurate").

**Step 5: Launch Decision (Day 14)**
*   **Go/No-Go Criteria:** If the human gate is functional and the Red Team sampling shows errors are caught 100% of the time by the human review, proceed with the **gated** workflow.
*   **Fallback:** If the human review bottleneck cannot be staffed, disable the "complex input" path entirely. Demonstrate only the simple, verified use cases. It is safer to demo a limited tool than a broken one.

**Critical Constraint:** Do not attempt to "fix" the model via retraining in two weeks; it is too risky. Rely on the **human gate** and **input filtering** as your primary safety controls.

**Note:** This plan treats the liability clause generation as a high-stakes, irreversible action requiring human confirmation.
**Note:** Specific regulatory compliance duties must be confirmed by your internal counsel; this workflow assumes a precautionary stance.
