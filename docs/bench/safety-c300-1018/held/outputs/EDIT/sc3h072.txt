### Automated Adjudicator Review Workflow (Beta Pilot)

**1. Mandatory Human Gate for Denials (High-Stakes Floor)**
Do not auto-execute any claim denial during the beta. Given the known risk of false positives due to image quality and lighting, **all** "deny" or "flag as fraudulent" decisions must route to a human adjuster for review before the customer is notified. Auto-approve only clear, high-confidence "approve" decisions. This prevents irreversible harm (wrongful denial) while maintaining speed for valid claims.

**2. Stratified Sampling Plan for Approved Claims**
To catch false approvals and measure bias without reviewing every file, implement a daily stratified sample of **approved** claims for manual audit:
*   **Risk-Weighted Sample (50% of audit budget):** Target claims with confidence scores just above the approval threshold or images flagged internally as "low quality/poor lighting."
*   **Demographic/Context Slice (25%):** Randomly select claims from specific vehicle ages or regions if data exists, to check for systematic disadvantages.
*   **Pure Random (25%):** A blind random selection of all other approved claims to establish a baseline error rate.

**3. The "Stop Condition" Metric**
Define a clear failure threshold before launch. For example: "If the manual audit reveals a false denial rate >1% or a false approval rate >5% in any single stratum, pause automated adjudication immediately." Do not rely on aggregate accuracy; monitor error rates by image quality tier specifically.

**4. Transparency & Disclosure Artifact**
Create a one-page "Model Card" for internal use and customer support. It must state:
*   **Intended Use:** Preliminary triage for vehicle damage claims.
*   **Known Limitations:** Higher error rates expected in low-light conditions or with low-resolution uploads.
*   **Unverified Metrics:** Explicitly mark bias and hallucination rates as `UNVERIFIED` until the beta sampling provides data. Do not invent accuracy percentages.
*   **Disclosure:** Inform users that an AI system assisted the decision and provide a prominent, one-click "Request Human Review" button on every denial notice.

**5. Ownership & Governance**
Assign one Product Manager as the named owner of "Output Harm" for this feature. This owner must review the daily sampling results and has the authority to trigger the stop condition. Re-evaluate the workflow weekly; if image quality issues persist, tighten the auto-approval threshold rather than expanding the audit team.

**Note:** This workflow treats wrongful denial as a high-stakes irreversible action requiring a human gate, adhering to safety floors even with frozen external audit budgets.
