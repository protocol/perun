# Pre-Launch Safety Review Checklist: Pawsitive Health Pilot

**1. Harm Inventory: Severity Overestimation**
*   **Risk:** Limited training data causes the model to flag common, treatable conditions (e.g., minor skin irritations) as fatal or severe (e.g., necrotizing fasciitis) due to a lack of "negative" or "benign" examples.
*   **Affected Users:** Pet owners facing unnecessary panic, emergency vet visits, and financial distress.
*   **Worst Case:** Owner euthanizes a pet or incurs ruinous debt based on a false positive for a fatal condition.

**2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
*   **Probe Set:** Curate a small set of 20–30 images of common, benign conditions (known-good negative controls) and 5–10 images of actual severe conditions (known-bad positive controls).
*   **Metric:** False Positive Rate for "Fatal/Severe" classification on the benign set.
*   **Threshold:** Owner-defined zero tolerance for fatal classifications on benign inputs during pilot.
*   **Stop Condition:** If *any* benign image is labeled "fatal" or "emergency," the automated suggestion feature is disabled immediately.
*   **Status:** Until this test is run with your specific pilot data, the severity accuracy is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a success rate.

**3. Immediate Manual Oversight (Human-in-the-Loop)**
*   **The Gate:** No output suggesting a "fatal," "critical," or "emergency" condition may be shown to the user without explicit review and approval by a licensed veterinarian.
*   **Implementation:** Route all high-severity predictions to a dashboard for vet sign-off before the user sees the result. If a vet is unavailable, the system must default to a generic "Consult a vet" message without specifying a diagnosis.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show percentage confidence (e.g., "87% fatal"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Vet-reviewed.
    *   *Unverified:* Algorithmic guess only (must trigger the human gate above).
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly state on the interface: "This tool is an experimental assistant. Its outputs are not a veterinary diagnosis. Always consult a professional for medical decisions."

**4. Transparency Artifact (Draft Model Card)**
*   **Intended Use:** Triage support for pet owners; not a replacement for veterinary care.
*   **Limitations:** Trained on a small, unverified dataset; high risk of overestimating severity for rare or ambiguous symptoms.
*   **Data Provenance:** List the specific sources of your limited prototype images. Acknowledge gaps in benign cases.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing pilot incidents.

**5. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner:** Assign one founder as the safety owner for the pilot duration.
*   **Legal Duty:** Whether this tool constitutes a regulated medical device in Australia or requires specific statutory disclosures is a legal determination. Route this question to legal counsel immediately; do not assume the pilot is exempt due to size.

**Notes**
*   This checklist refuses to certify the system as "safe"; it provides the controls to manage residual risk.
*   If you cannot staff the vet review gate, the high-stakes automated output must remain disabled.
