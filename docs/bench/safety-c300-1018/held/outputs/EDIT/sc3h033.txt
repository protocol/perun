### Safety Protocol: Equitable Scholarship Evaluation

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Scenario**
*   **Identified Harm:** Systemic bias against non-traditional educational backgrounds due to over-reliance on standard grading metrics.
*   **Affected Group:** Underrepresented students in Cape Town with valid potential but non-standard academic records.
*   **Worst-Case:** Deserving candidates are automatically rejected, reinforcing existing inequalities and violating the scholarship's core mission.
*   **Status:** **UNVERIFIED** (No external validation datasets available; internal tests indicate failure).

**2. Immediate Control: Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory)**
Given the high-stakes nature of denying educational opportunity and the identified bias, **fully automated rejection is prohibited.**
*   **The Gate:** Any applicant flagged by the algorithm as "Low Probability" or "Below Threshold" based primarily on grading metrics must trigger an automatic hold.
*   **Action:** These applications are routed to a manual review queue. No rejection letter is sent until a human reviewer explicitly confirms the decision.
*   **Override:** Reviewers are empowered to override the algorithm's score if non-traditional achievements (portfolio, community impact, vocational training) demonstrate merit.

**3. Measurement Plan (Runnable within 2 Weeks)**
Since external data is unavailable, we will test the *process* using internal adversarial probes.
*   **Probe Set:** Construct 10 synthetic "positive control" profiles representing high-potential students with non-traditional backgrounds (e.g., gaps in schooling, vocational certs instead of grades).
*   **Test:** Run these through the current algorithm.
*   **Pass Threshold:** 100% of positive controls must be caught by the "Low Probability" filter and routed to manual review. If any are auto-rejected, the gate is broken.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the manual review team cannot process the projected volume of "held" applications within 48 hours, the launch is paused to adjust staffing or criteria.

**4. Transparency & Uncertainty Artifacts**
*   **Internal Data Card:** Draft a one-page document listing:
    *   *Intended Use:* Streamlining initial sorting only.
    *   *Known Limitation:* High false-negative rate for non-traditional backgrounds; grading metrics are poor proxies for this group.
    *   *Mitigation:* Mandatory human review for all low-score outputs.
    *   *Owner:* [Name of Team Lead].
*   **Confidence Reporting:** Do not display algorithmic scores (e.g., "87% match") to reviewers, as this invites automation bias. Instead, present a coarse tier: **"Requires Manual Context"** for any application lacking standard grade completeness.

**5. Governance & Ownership**
*   **Owner:** One specific team member is named as the "Bias Mitigation Owner" responsible for the manual review queue and the final ship decision.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** If the volume of manual overrides exceeds 30% of total applications, the selection criteria must be revised post-launch.

**6. Legal & Regulatory Note**
*   Automated decisions regarding education and opportunity may fall under specific local regulations or broader frameworks like the EU AI Act (if applicable via partners). **Route to legal counsel** immediately to confirm if a specific AI-disclosure statement is legally required for applicants. This protocol recommends disclosure as a best practice but does not assert legal duty.

***

**Note:** This protocol treats the algorithm as a flawed assistant, not a decision-maker. The "safety" comes from the human gate, not the code.
**Note:** Do not fabricate a "fairness score" for the launch report; state clearly that equity is being managed via manual review due to unverified model performance.
