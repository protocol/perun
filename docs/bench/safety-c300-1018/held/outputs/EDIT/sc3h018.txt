*   **Probe Set:** Simulate user requests from affluent zip codes targeting restaurants in low-income zones. Include positive controls (known cases where hiding occurs) and negative controls (areas where no hiding should occur).
*   **Metric:** Measure the disparity in visibility rates for low-income zone restaurants when queried by affluent vs. non-affluent users.
*   **Threshold:** Your team must define a pass/fail threshold. Given the potential for systematic exclusion, consider if *any* statistically significant disparity is acceptable.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the disparity exceeds your threshold, the launch must be blocked or rolled back until mitigated.
