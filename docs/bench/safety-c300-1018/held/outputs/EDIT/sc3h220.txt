**Safety Validation Plan: Patient Pain Medication Optimizer**

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Definition**
Before simulation, explicitly record the worst-case harm: **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions**. The specific failure mode is recommending dosing intervals that lead to overdose or severe side effects due to hallucinated patterns or bias against specific patient subgroups (e.g., age, weight, comorbidities).
*   **Affected:** Patients with chronic pain, specifically those with atypical metabolism or complex medication histories.
*   **Worst Case:** Fatal overdose or permanent organ damage due to an AI-generated schedule executed without human verification.

**2. Simulation & Measurement Strategy (Using Historical Data)**
Since large-scale trials are prohibited, you must construct a "Synthetic Patient Cohort" from your historical data to stress-test the model.
*   **Probe Set Construction:** Segment historical data into distinct profiles: typical responders, slow metabolizers, patients with comorbidities, and edge cases (extreme pain scores vs. low reported pain).
*   **Adversarial Testing:** Run the optimizer against these profiles using "known-bad" inputs (e.g., conflicting pain reports, missing weight data) to see if the model defaults to unsafe frequencies.
*   **Positive/Negative Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Input cases where historical records show a safe, doctor-approved interval. The AI must match or be more conservative than the historical standard.
    *   *Negative Control:* Input scenarios that historically led to adverse events. The AI must **refuse** to generate a schedule or flag for review, not optimize a dangerous pattern.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Define a "Safety Violation Rate." For any high-stakes recommendation (intervals < X hours), the tolerance for error is **zero**. If the model suggests a frequency tighter than the historical safe maximum for a specific profile without explicit justification, it is a failure.
*   **Reporting:** Do not claim a "99% safe" rate. Report residual risk as **UNVERIFIED** for any patient subgroup not adequately represented in the historical data.

**3. Management Controls: The Human Gate**
Because this feature executes high-stakes, irreversible actions (altering biological intake), full automation is prohibited.
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:** The system must **never** auto-dispense or auto-confirm a dosing schedule. Every AI recommendation must be presented as a "Draft Proposal" requiring explicit confirmation by a licensed clinician before being shown to the patient.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% confident"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Matches multiple historical precedents for this specific profile.
    *   *Corroborated:* Matches general guidelines but lacks specific profile history.
    *   *Unverified:* No close historical match; requires senior clinician review.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Clinicians must have a one-click option to reject the AI suggestion and input a manual schedule without friction.

**4. Initial Rollout Monitoring Protocol**
*   **Shadow Mode Launch:** For the first two weeks, run the AI in "shadow mode" where it generates recommendations that are logged but **not** shown to clinicians or patients. Compare AI suggestions against actual clinician decisions.
*   **Anomaly Detection:** Set automated alerts for any recommendation that deviates >15% from the clinic's historical average dosing frequency for similar profiles.
*   **Kill Switch:** Establish a hard stop condition. If >1 "Unsafe Recommendation" is detected in shadow mode, the feature launch is paused immediately for retraining.

**5. Transparency Artifact**
Create a **Model Card** before launch. It must list: intended use (adjunct to clinician judgment, not replacement), data provenance (historical records + focus groups), known limitations (specific subgroups with sparse data), and the owner of the safety gate.

**Note:** Legal counsel must review whether this constitutes a regulated medical device decision under local jurisdiction; do not assume compliance based on this plan.
**Note:** No metric here certifies the system as "safe"; it only provides a framework to measure and manage residual risk.
