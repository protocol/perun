### Immediate Safety Review Plan: Patient-Pain-Opioid Calculator

**1. Harm Inventory (Top Priority Risks)**
Before validation, we explicitly name the worst-case harms for opioid-naive post-surgical patients:
*   **Over-dosing due to noisy inputs:** Noisy vitals or pain scores leading to a recommendation exceeding safe limits, causing respiratory depression or acute addiction onset.
*   **Hallucination surfaced as fact:** The tool generating a specific dosage with high confidence despite missing critical contraindication data (e.g., undisclosed liver issues).
*   **Automation bias:** Clinicians blindly accepting the recommended dose without independent verification because the interface implies authority.
*   **Unsafe high-stakes automation:** The system directly populating a prescription order without a mandatory human confirmation step.

**2. Measurement Plan: Runnable Tests (48-Hour Sprint)**
We will execute three specific test rows to validate safety limits. We do not assert "0% error"; we measure residual risk.

*   **Test Row A: Noise Injection & Safe Limits**
    *   **Probe Set:** 50 synthetic patient profiles with intentionally noisy vitals (±15% error) and extreme pain scores. Includes positive controls (known overdose triggers) and negative controls (standard safe cases).
    *   **Metric:** Percentage of recommendations exceeding the established maximum safe dose for opioid-naive users.
    *   **Threshold:** **Zero tolerance.** Any single output exceeding the hard cap is a critical failure.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If any overdose occurs, the feature is blocked from the board demo until the hard cap logic is refactored.
    *   **Owner:** Lead ML Engineer.

*   **Test Row B: Missing/Incomplete Data Handling**
    *   **Probe Set:** 30 profiles with missing fields (e.g., weight unknown, age missing).
    *   **Metric:** Rate of "Unverified" or "Refusal" responses vs. specific dosage numbers.
    *   **Threshold:** 100% of incomplete critical inputs must result in a "Cannot Calculate – Manual Review Required" flag.
    *   **Stop Condition:** Any specific dosage generated from incomplete critical data triggers an immediate rollback of the calculation module.
    *   **Owner:** Product Owner.

*   **Test Row C: Adversarial Prompt/Input Injection**
    *   **Probe Set:** Inputs designed to manipulate the logic (e.g., entering text in numeric fields, extreme outliers).
    *   **Metric:** System stability and adherence to safety guards.
    *   **Threshold:** No system crash; no bypass of safety limits.
    *   **Owner:** QA Lead.

**3. Management Controls: Human-in-the-Loop Gates**
Given the high-stakes nature (irreversible health impact), the following controls are mandatory immediately:
*   **Hard Cap Enforcement:** Code-level absolute maximums that cannot be overridden by the model's output. If the model suggests >X mg, the system caps it at X mg and flags for review.
*   **Mandatory Human Confirmation:** The tool **never** auto-submits a prescription. It must present the recommendation as "Suggested Dosage (AI-Generated)" requiring explicit clinician signature and override capability.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If input data quality is low or the model confidence is not "Confirmed" (based on internal checks), the UI must display a prominent warning: "Low Confidence – Clinical Verification Required." Do not show raw percentages (e.g., "87%"); use defined tiers like "Verified," "Corroborated," or "Unverified."

**4. Transparency Artifact (Draft Model Card)**
For the board review, prepare a one-page "Model Safety Statement" including:
*   **Intended Use:** Initial dosage suggestion for opioid-naive post-surgical adults only.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state: "Not validated for patients with known substance use disorder, liver failure, or pediatric users."
*   **Measured Results:** Report the pass/fail status of Test Rows A, B, and C. If tests are ongoing, mark results as **UNVERIFIED** and list the scheduled completion time.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific clinician and engineer responsible for this version.

**5. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
*   **Disclosure:** Recommend clear UI labeling that the dosage is AI-assisted.
*   **Regulatory Duty:** Whether this tool classifies as a regulated medical device requiring specific FDA/PMDA approval or specific legal disclosures is a determination for **Legal Counsel**. This plan assumes the strictest safety posture but does not assert legal compliance.

**Next Steps for 48-Hour Deadline:**
1.  Run Test Rows A, B, and C immediately.
2.  Implement the "Hard Cap" and "Human Confirmation" gates in the staging environment.
3.  Draft the Model Safety Statement with "UNVERIFIED" markers where data is pending.
4.  Route the final regulatory classification question to counsel with the draft documentation.

*Note: This plan prioritizes harm prevention over feature completeness. Do not remove the human gate to meet the deadline.*
*Residual risk remains until live monitoring is established post-launch.*
