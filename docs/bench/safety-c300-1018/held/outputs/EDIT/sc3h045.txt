**BLUF: Do not ship the feature as described.** Automatically sharing private home footage with law enforcement without explicit, per-event user consent constitutes an unsafe, high-stakes automated action that violates the fundamental floor of product safety. The proposed "speed" benefit does not justify the irreversible privacy harm and loss of community trust.

**Harm Inventory & Worst Cases**
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions:** The system executes a data transfer with legal and privacy consequences without human confirmation.
    *   *Worst Case:* Footage of sensitive activities (medical emergencies, domestic disputes, protected classes) is sent to police, triggering unwarranted investigations or harassment.
*   **Missing AI-Disclosure / Deceptive Patterns:** Bypassing explicit consent hides the automated nature of the sharing from the user.
    *   *Worst Case:* Homeowners believe they have privacy control, only to discover their property is under constant automated surveillance sharing, destroying brand trust.
*   **Bias / Unfair Outcomes:** Motion detection algorithms often have higher false-positive rates for certain demographics or environmental conditions.
    *   *Worst Case:* Specific neighborhoods face disproportionate police presence due to algorithmic errors, leading to claims of discriminatory surveillance.

**Measurement Plan (Do Not Skip)**
You cannot assert safety without testing. Before any reconsideration of deployment, you must run these evaluations:
1.  **Probe Set:** Simulate late-night motion events across diverse scenarios (family members, delivery drivers, wildlife, shadows) and demographic representations.
2.  **Controls:** Include known "do not share" scenarios (e.g., internal motion, known residents) as negative controls.
3.  **Metric:** Rate of unauthorized/unnecessary footage sharing.
4.  **Threshold:** Zero tolerance for sharing without explicit user authorization. Any failure blocks launch.
5.  **Stop Condition:** If the system shares footage without a confirmed user prompt in testing, the feature is halted immediately.

**Risk Management & Required Controls**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** You must implement an explicit confirmation step. The system may *notify* the user of motion and *offer* to share, but it cannot *execute* the share without active approval. Fully autonomous sharing of private data is the failure mode this review prevents.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** You must draft a Model/Data Card detailing intended use, data provenance, and limitations. Currently, the "intended use" (non-consensual sharing) is a critical risk, not a feature.
*   **Uncertainty & Override:** Users must have a clear, easy way to opt-out or revoke permission instantly. There is no "coarse tier" of confidence that justifies bypassing consent.

The current design fails the safety floor. The only viable path forward is to redesign the flow to require explicit user authorization before any data leaves the device. If the technical constraint (encryption/modification time) prevents adding this gate, the feature cannot launch this quarter.
