The opportunity is real but the plan as stated is too risky to approve immediately.

The rival's success proves strong demand, and our existing assets give us a rare speed advantage. However, the risks are not equally weighted. The two-month timeline is the most serious concern: building, hardening, and scaling a separate online title that quickly invites technical failure. Equally, we have no proven experience operating item-based economies, so launching this as our first attempt compounds execution risk. Cannibalization is manageable if the new title expands the audience rather than splitting it, but that requires deliberate design choices not specified here.

A better path is to proceed with urgency but under a revised structure: treat it as a time-boxed prototype using our paid game's assets, validate engagement and monetization assumptions on a smaller scale, and only then commit to a separate launch. We should also secure experienced guidance on the economy design before going live.

Given the clear upside and our asset advantage, this should move forward, but not as proposed.

DECISION: NO-GO
