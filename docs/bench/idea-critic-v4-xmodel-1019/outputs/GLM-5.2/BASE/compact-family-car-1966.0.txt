This addresses the risk that buyers may prefer either cheaper or larger cars by offering a middle ground.

The new plant provides modern production capacity specifically designed for this model, making near-capacity operation achievable if launch timing aligns with market growth. Building a dedicated dealer network is costly, but it ensures the new car receives proper sales and service support rather than being overshadowed by existing models in a shared network. Rivals already selling small cars at low prices is a concern, but the proposal's car is differentiated by size, engine, and family positioning rather than competing solely on price.
