**Steelman.** Reuse paid-game assets to ship a free last-player-standing mode in two months; monetize cosmetics. The strongest version: sunk-cost assets make the build cheap, the genre is validated and growing, and a free title expands the audience rather than cannibalizing it. If the rival's lead is decisive and the studio lacks F2P muscle, this is a bet on speed and asset-leverage against a competitor already running the playbook.

**Skeptic.** Four claims are unsupported as stated. (1) "Two months" assumes the asset reuse path is known and the netcode scales to large sessions; neither is shown. (2) "Earn money from cosmetics" assumes a store, payment rails, and anti-fraud exist or can be built inside the window; none is confirmed. (3) "Cannibalization risk" is named but not priced: no estimate of paid-game units at risk versus free-mode upside. (4) "Rival owns the genre" is treated as a risk to list, not a barrier to measure: no data on whether late entrants in this genre have retained players.

**Better-way.** Two alternatives beat the proposal as written. (A) **Pilot first, separate title later**: ship the mode as a free weekend/event inside the paid game, measure retention and cosmetic conversion on real players, then decide on a standalone title. Reversibility is high; the studio learns F2P economics before committing. (B) **Do nothing**: keep selling the paid cooperative game and avoid entering a genre dominated by a rival with more F2P experience. Cheapest; avoids the cannibalization and capability-gap risks entirely. The proposal must beat both, not only the status quo.

**Kill-criteria.** The proposal names no stop condition. A defensible version would pre-commit: if the free mode does not reach a named DAU/retention threshold by week N post-launch, or if paid-game weekly sales drop more than X% for M consecutive weeks, sunset the free title and revert. Without that, the studio is making an open-ended bet with no exit.

**Verdict.** The idea has real upside, but as proposed it commits to a two-month standalone launch with no pilot, no priced cannibalization, and no kill criteria. That is a one-way-door bet treated as reversible.

DECISION: NO-GO
