Strongest version: a once-in-a-career chance to define a new medium, with a deep-pocketed partner de-risking distribution.

But the proposal asks for approval *as proposed now*, and the facts given contain no kill criterion, no cap, no leading metric, no review date, and no named pivot or stop action. That is the bar, and it is not met.

Concrete failure scenario: the company commits to a multi-year production schedule with no defined stop condition, the studio controls distribution and holds the stronger hand, rendering is slow and costly, and the company has never produced anything longer than a short. If rendering costs spiral or the story does not land, there is no pre-committed tripwire to catch it before sunk cost locks the company in.

One-way or two-way door: multi-year feature production with a controlling partner is a one-way door for a small company that has never done this. One-way doors do not get approved without a kill criterion.

What would settle it: a capped, time-boxed first step (a single sequence or reel test), one observable metric with a numeric threshold, a date inside that first window, and a named pivot or stop action when it fires.

The idea may be right. The proposal as written is not approvable.
