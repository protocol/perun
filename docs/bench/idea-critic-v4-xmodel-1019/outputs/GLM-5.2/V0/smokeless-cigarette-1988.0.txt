**Steelman:** A heated-tobacco product could capture health-conscious smokers and open a regulated-reduced-harm category before competitors.

**Origin:** agent-originated (an executive proposal, not a direct owner mandate).

**Blast:** very high — sunk development cost is large, a wide launch commits manufacturing, marketing and regulatory capital, and health-claim promotion invites liability that can attach to the whole firm.

**Reversibility:** low. A wide launch with health-claim promotion cannot be cleanly unwound; reputational and regulatory exposure persists after withdrawal.

**Alternatives vs do-nothing:**
1. Do nothing / shelve: avoids pouring more capital after a large sunk cost; loses any chance of recouping development.
2. Limited pilot, no health claims: cheaper, reversible, tests taste/lighting/usage objections directly; defers regulator-facing claims.
3. Wide launch, no health claims: lower regulatory risk than the proposal, but still high capital exposure on an unproven product.

**Pre-mortem (why it fails badly):** Smokers reject the taste and the carbon-tip lighting ritual; the product is fiddly versus a normal cigarette, so repeat purchase collapses. Meanwhile, the "cleaner way to smoke" promotion triggers regulator objections and liability, tainting the brand while the core product has already failed in market.

**Fit and evidence:** The proposal asserts a "cleaner way to smoke" without supplying evidence regulators accept, and it promotes widely before the named taste, lighting and usability risks are tested. The large sunk cost is cited, but sunk cost does not justify a higher, less reversible bet.

**Kill criteria (go-if):** Launch only as a time-boxed, capped pilot in defined test markets; make no health or "cleaner" claims until regulators approve specific language. Kill/pivot if repeat-purchase rate falls below a set threshold by a fixed date, or if regulators challenge any health-adjacent claim.

**Strongest attack survived:** That the product is complicated and smokers may dislike taste/lighting — this is exactly why a wide launch now is premature; a pilot tests it cheaply and reversibly.

**Remaining risk:** Even a clean pilot may reveal that the heating mechanism and lighting ritual make the product non-viable at scale.

DECISION: NO-GO
