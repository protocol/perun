**Steelman:** Simultaneous US+Europe entry creates a defensible first-mover position at global scale before competitors fragment the market.

**Strongest attack survived:** None. The capital-burn attack was not refuted by the proposal.

**Assumptions:**
1. Sufficient VC funding exists to sustain multi-country logistics and marketing simultaneously — *unverified*.
2. Young fashion buyers will purchase apparel online at scale — *unproven in 1998*.

**Better ways:**
1. Launch in ONE region (US or Europe), prove the model, then expand — cheaper, reversible, preserves capital.
2. License or partner for international presence rather than building offices in several countries.

**Kill criteria:**
1. If runway drops below 12 months before break-even in the first region → stop expansion, contract to one market.
2. If online conversion rate is below X% after 6 months in the pilot region → pivot the channel assumption before scaling.

**Dissent ledger:** Simultaneous launch maximizes burn and complexity before the core hypothesis (online fashion buying works) is validated. The proposal treats geographic scale as the priority when product-market fit is the actual risk.

**Remaining risk:** Multi-country simultaneous entry with unproven online fashion demand and finite VC runway is a one-way-door bet on scale before proof.

DECISION: NO-GO
