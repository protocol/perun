First call: NO-GO — irreversible privacy exposure for upside that an opt-in variant reaches.

**Claim:** Beacon will increase ad revenue by broadcasting users' partner-site purchases and actions to friends' feeds.

**Steelman:** Implicit social proof of purchases drives higher conversion than generic ads, and friends' buying signals are more trusted than platform targeting.

**Blockers:** The proposal's own stated risks constitute the blocker. It names "users may see it as an invasion of privacy" and "purchases can be sensitive" as facts, not contingencies — and the mechanism (broadcast to friends' feeds) directly triggers both. The feature is one-way-door: "hard to withdraw once launched." A one-way-door privacy feature whose own stated risks are the core mechanism, and that the plan does not answer with an opt-in default, fails as proposed.

**Risks (kill criteria):** Opt-out-by-default means the first purchase a user didn't realize was shared is the PR crisis. Kill criterion: if negative-press volume or user-deactivation rate exceeds [threshold] within 14 days of launch, pause the feed and revert to explicit opt-in. Leading metric: opt-out rate within first session — if >40% of users shown the notification opt out, the default is wrong.

**Better way:** Make Beacon **opt-in by default** (user actively enables sharing for each partner or globally). This reaches the same ad-revenue upside on the stated facts, preserves the social-proof mechanism, and removes the "invasion of privacy" blocker. The cost is lower initial adoption — acceptable for a one-way-door feature.

**Strongest attack survived:** "Even opt-in Beacon fails because purchase-sharing is inherently sensitive and users won't enable it." This fails because the proposal's upside (social proof driving conversions) doesn't require universal participation — even a subset of opted-in users generating purchase signals in feeds delivers the ad-revenue lift, and partner sites can seed it.

**Assumptions:** Ad-revenue lift depends on purchase visibility reaching friends; no stated revenue model beyond "earn more from advertising."

**Dissent ledger:** Default-opt-out on a one-way-door privacy feature is the flaw; opt-in default is the fix that unblocks GO.

DECISION: NO-GO
