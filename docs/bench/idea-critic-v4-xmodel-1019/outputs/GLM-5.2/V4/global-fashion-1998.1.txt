**First call: NO-GO.** Simultaneous multi-country launch multiplies burn across unproven demand before any market validates the model.

**Steelman:** Fashion-conscious youth are early internet adopters; being first and pan-regional locks in brand before competitors scale.

**Skeptic:** The core assumption — that young buyers will purchase fashion online in 1998 — is untested at scale. Launching in Europe AND America simultaneously means learning nothing from one market before committing capital to the next. The proposal states its own blocker: venture money may run out before sales catch up. This is not a risk to manage; it is the plan's own numbers failing. Multi-country logistics, marketing, and offices across several countries compound fixed costs faster than any 1998 e-commerce revenue curve supports.

**Better-way:** Pick ONE market — the one with highest fashion-buying youth density and lowest logistics friction. Prove conversion, average order value, and repeat purchase there. A single-market pilot reaches the same upside (validated online fashion demand) at a fraction of the burn, and preserves capital for a proven expansion. The stated ambition (largest in the world) is not served by going bankrupt across five countries simultaneously.

**Kill-criteria:** If the single-market pilot does not hit a defined conversion rate and repeat-purchase threshold within a fixed window, stop and do not expand. Expansion into a second market only after the first reaches unit economics that survive without new venture funding.

**Pre-mortem:** The plan failed because it spent pan-regional fixed costs against unproven 1998 online demand, ran through venture funding across multiple unvalidated geographies, and never reached the sales velocity needed to justify simultaneous launch. The proposal's own listed risks are not mitigated — they are the direct consequence of the simultaneous-launch decision.

**Blockers:** The proposal's own evidence contradicts its core claim — it states venture money may run out before sales catch up, yet the plan structure (simultaneous multi-country) is precisely what accelerates that outcome. No mitigation is offered for the cost multiplication the plan itself creates.

DECISION: NO-GO
