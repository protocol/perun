**First call: NO-GO.** Simultaneous multi-country launch with unproven online fashion demand and venture funding is the highest-cost, highest-burn path available.

**Steelman:** The internet is a land-grab; whoever moves fastest and biggest wins the category. Fashion-conscious youth are early adopters. European and American markets are large enough to justify the burn.

**Better-way:** Launch in ONE country first (or one city). Prove the unit economics — conversion rate, return rate, average order, customer acquisition cost — before multiplying. The proposal's own risk list acknowledges this: "many countries at once multiplies costs." A single-market pilot reaches the same learning at a fraction of the burn. The land-grab thesis survives a 6-month pilot in one market; it does not require simultaneous launch everywhere.

**Kill criteria:**
- **Cost of delay is real but asymmetric.** Waiting 6 months to validate one market costs some first-mover advantage. Launching everywhere at once and burning through venture funding before any market reaches profitability costs the entire company.
- **The proposal states its own blocker:** "venture money may run out before sales catch up." This is not a risk to manage — it is the plan failing as proposed. Multi-country simultaneous launch maximizes the burn rate that creates this failure.
- **Unproven demand is the core uncertainty.** Will young buyers purchase fashion online without touching it? This question is answerable with a single-market launch and is not resolved by launching in more markets simultaneously.

**Strongest attack survived:** The land-grab argument — that speed and scale win internet categories — is the sharpest objection to a staged launch. It fails here because the proposal itself identifies cash burn as the existential risk, and simultaneous multi-country launch is the highest-burn option available. A land-grab you cannot fund is not a land-grab.

**Dissent ledger:** The simultaneous-launch thesis has merit if funding is truly unlimited and demand is proven. Neither condition holds per the proposal's own stated risks.

DECISION: NO-GO
