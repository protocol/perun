DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal to produce a full-length animated feature is financially reckless under current conditions. A budget ten times that of a short, funded entirely by thin short-film profits, creates existential risk: one overrun or box-office failure could bankrupt the studio and end the short-film line that provides steady income. The founder's family and industry insiders—who understand audience expectations and production realities—advise against it. The core risk is untested: audiences have never proven they will sit through a feature-length cartoon, so the revenue assumption is speculative. There is no evidence of a market-demand signal, no contingency for cost overruns, and no fallback if the feature fails. Approving this now would gamble the studio's survival on an unproven concept with no safety net. The prudent path is to reject this proposal as presented.
