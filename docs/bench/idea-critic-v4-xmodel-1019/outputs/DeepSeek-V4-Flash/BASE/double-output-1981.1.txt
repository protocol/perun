The proposal asks to double monthly production despite 4,500 unsold cars, a recession, rising interest rates, and thin cash. Doubling output will worsen inventory, tie up more cash in unsold units, and strain dealer financing precisely as demand contracts. The company lacks the buffer to absorb this. No evidence suggests demand will catch up; recession and high rates are headwinds, not tailwinds. The only rational move is to cut production, not increase it. Approving this now risks bankruptcy.  

DECISION: NO-GO
