The risk of existing owners and software makers not migrating is mitigated by backward compatibility if the new machine runs the same software, which is not stated but is a reasonable assumption for a “successor” in this context. The biggest flaw is the tight schedule combined with complexity—rushing could yield a buggy launch that damages the brand. Given the clear demand and the ability to keep the old product alive during transition, the proposal’s direction is correct. The risks are known and mitigable, not fatal. The executive team should approve now to capture momentum, with a mandate to monitor reliability and sales milestones closely.
