The proposal leverages existing assets and a proven genre, but the competitive landscape is hostile. The rival’s dominance makes market entry costly, and cannibalization of the paid game is a real financial threat—especially since the studio lacks free-to-play monetization experience. Two months is insufficient to design, balance, and launch a polished mode that can compete on quality and retention. The revenue model (cosmetics) is unproven for this studio and may not offset losses from the paid title. While speed is an advantage, a rushed, unproven entry into a saturated market with internal inexperience likely leads to poor user acquisition and brand dilution. The proposal fails to address how to differentiate from the rival or protect the existing revenue stream. Without a clear mitigation plan for cannibalization or a pilot for monetization, the risk outweighs the reward. The studio should not commit resources now; instead, it needs a focused strategy for market entry and a tested revenue model.  
DECISION: NO-GO
