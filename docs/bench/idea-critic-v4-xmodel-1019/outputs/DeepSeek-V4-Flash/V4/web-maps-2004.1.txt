First call: This is a bold, high-risk bet against established players with no revenue model, but the upside of owning web mapping could be massive.

**Steelman:** A free, browser-based map with satellite imagery and routing could leapfrog existing products by offering a superior user experience with zero friction, capturing massive adoption and strategic value even without direct revenue.

**Options:** Do nothing (safe, but the acquired firm stagnates). Sell the technology (one-time gain). Build a paid desktop upgrade (limited reach). The proposal is the only path to transformative scale.

**Fit:** Assumed north star is market leadership in consumer mapping; this aligns.

**Pre-mortem:** It fails because data licensing costs explode, browser performance is too poor for a good experience, and users don't switch from established sites. This is a two-way door—can be shut down if it fails.

**Evidence:** All facts are as stated. The risks are real but not proven fatal. "Costly" and "slow" are challenges, not certainties. No evidence shows users definitively reject a better product.

**Blockers:** NONE. The stated facts do not prove the plan fails. The risks are unproven.
**Risks:** High licensing costs, performance issues, strong incumbents, no revenue model. Mitigations: start with a limited region, optimize aggressively, and define a kill metric (e.g., under 1M users in 12 months → pivot or shut down).

**Decision:** This is a high-risk, high-reward bet. The potential to define a new category outweighs the unproven risks. It should proceed as a capped, time-boxed initiative with clear kill criteria, which the plan implicitly allows.

DECISION: GO
