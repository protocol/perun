DECISION: NO-GO

The proposal asks for a large, irreversible bet—new plant, new dealer network—against documented market headwinds. Rivals already compete on price; buyers may prefer cheaper or larger options. The plan offers no evidence that demand exists at the volume needed to run the new plant near capacity, no pilot, no staged rollout, and no defined kill criterion if sales lag. The cost of building a dedicated network is real and front-loaded, while the upside relies on unproven assumptions about buyer preference and competitive response. Approval now, as proposed, commits resources before validating the core demand thesis. The correct move is to start smaller: test demand with a limited launch through existing channels or a single region, set a numeric sales threshold, and only then scale to the dedicated network and full plant capacity. This preserves the option while capping downside.
