**Approve the genetic algorithm (Approach B) for the new shift-bidding module to reduce agent idle time from 18 to 12 minutes per shift.**

This PR introduces the new shift-bidding module. Two implementation approaches are proposed:
*   **Approach A:** Greedy nearest-neighbor algorithm.
*   **Approach B:** Genetic algorithm with a fitness function.

**Key figure:** 12 minutes average idle time per agent per shift (Approach B) vs. 18 minutes (Approach A).

While Approach A is viable, Approach B delivers superior efficiency by reducing idle time by 6 minutes per agent per shift. The author leans toward Approach B based on this metric.
