**Dana, please choose by Thursday, March 15: (A) replace all 4,300 pre-1995 meters over 18 months for $2.1M, or (B) phase replacement of the worst 1,200 meters now and re-audit in Q3.**

**Key figure: 19.7% non-revenue water vs. 12% target.**

The leak-detection audit confirms aging brass meters in the Elmwood district are under-registering flow by roughly 8%, driving losses above target. These 4,300 pre-1995 units are the primary cause. Additionally, calibration contractor AquaMetrix missed their February 28 deadline, leaving some data unverified until resolved.

**Option A (Finance preference):** Replace all 4,300 meters.
*   **Cost:** Estimated $2.1M.
*   **Timeline:** 18 months.
*   **Rationale:** Captures bulk pricing discounts; resolves the root cause completely.

**Option B (Report recommendation):** Phased replacement.
*   **Scope:** Replace the worst 1,200 meters immediately.
*   **Next Step:** Re-audit in Q3 to determine remaining needs.
*   **Rationale:** Lower immediate spend; allows data-driven decisions for the remaining 3,100 units.

**Decision Required:**
Select A or B by Thursday, March 15, for inclusion in the council packet.
*   **If you choose B:** Explicit approval is required to issue an RFP to three vendors for the initial phase.

**Additional Findings:**
*   **Minor Leaks:** 37 identified; none urgent, but collectively losing ~40,000 gallons/day.
*   **Data Context:** Consumption data reliability is impacted until the new billing software rolls out in April.
