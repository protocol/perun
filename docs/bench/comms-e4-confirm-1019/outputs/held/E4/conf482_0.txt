**Dana, please approve Option A (redo gaskets for $260k) or Option B (replace units for $740k) by Friday, Dec 6, to capture the $410k/quarter power savings before the maintenance window closes.**

**Key figure: 1.52 average PUE vs. 1.38 target (0.14 gap).**

**The Problem**
Q3 efficiency audits confirm hot-aisle containment retrofits are underperforming, driving the data center's average Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) to 1.52 against the 1.38 target. This 0.14 gap, applied to the 10MW load, costs $410k extra per quarter ($1.7M projected annual loss). While the east wing performs well at 1.31 PUE, the west wing is dragging the average down. Two of six west wing CRAC units are running at 78% load, exceeding the recommended 70% threshold.

**Root Cause**
The retrofit vendor installed incorrect gasket material on 14 of the 20 rows, causing the containment failure and subsequent efficiency drop.

**Options for Approval**
*   **Option A: Redo Gaskets.** Replace the incorrect material on the 14 affected rows with the specified material.
    *   **Cost:** $260k.
    *   **Outcome:** Restores design containment efficiency.
*   **Option B: Replace Units.** Remove affected units and install new modular coolers.
    *   **Cost:** $740k.
    *   **Outcome:** New hardware, higher capital expenditure.

**Recommendation Context**
The audit suggests Option A addresses the specific root cause (gasket material) at a significantly lower cost ($260k vs $740k) while resolving the PUE gap. Option B incurs nearly triple the cost for a problem isolated to sealing materials rather than unit failure.

**Decision Required**
Select Option A or Option B by **Friday, Dec 6**. Missing this date forfeits the upcoming maintenance window, delaying efficiency recovery and incurring another quarter of $410k in avoidable power costs.

*Note: East wing PUE (1.31) remains within target and requires no action.*
