*   **Placement Mismatch:** 34% of new starters are placed above their ability level, leading to dropouts by week 4.
*   **Buddy System Failure:** Only 22% of students met their assigned buddy more than once.

The report presents two distinct paths to fix the placement issue for the April intake:

*   **Option A: Overhaul Placement Test**
    *   **Action:** Retrain all 15 assessors and update the testing protocol.
    *   **Cost:** ~$18k in training time.
    *   **Deadline:** Must be completed by March 12.
    *   **Impact:** Directly addresses the root cause of mismatched levels without delaying student start dates.

*   **Option B: Mandatory Leveling Bootcamp**
    *   **Action:** Keep the current test but require a 2-week bootcamp for anyone scoring below 70%.
    *   **Cost:** Cheaper than Option A.
    *   **Impact:** Delays start dates for affected students.
