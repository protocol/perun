*   **ML forecast model:** Reduces nightly rebalancing miles by 18% versus current operations. Requires an additional $30k in compute costs per quarter. A 14-day pilot in Austin demonstrated 22% fewer empty docks using this model.

The author is leaning toward the heuristic model due to cost efficiency, though the ML model offers higher accuracy and dock availability.
