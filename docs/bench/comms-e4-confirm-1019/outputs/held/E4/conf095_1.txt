**Context:**
*   **Baseline:** The June 30 load test reached 1.8 MW using the old controller.
*   **Recent Fixes:** The draft includes the sensor latency fix from last week and addresses the c7000 firmware issue.

The decision hinges on accepting the retrofit risk of the fuzzy-logic implementation to achieve the simulated 12% PUE improvement.
