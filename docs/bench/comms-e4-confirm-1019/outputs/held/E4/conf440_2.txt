**VPD controller: approve hybrid-always (A) or temp-gated hybrid (B) by Friday, April 11.**

This PR replaces PID-only logic with a hybrid model-predictive controller and PID fallback. It also fixes the March 14 sensor drift calibration bug and adds a high humidity alarm at 85% RH.

**Options for VPD control:**
*   **Option A:** Keep hybrid mode always on. Costs ~12% more compute per cycle but cuts overshoot to under 0.3 kPa.
*   **Option B:** Gate hybrid mode to run only when outside temp > 28°C or < 5°C. Saves energy but risks overshoot on mild days.

**Context:**
Bench 3 testing showed positive results, though the April 2 log file revealed a glitch in the fallback trigger. I lean toward Option A for the consistent overshoot protection, but the final call is yours.
