**Approach: fuzzy-logic controller (12% lower PUE in sim) vs. PID with new feedforward term (lower retrofit risk); Priya, please approve the approach by Thursday the 9th.**

This PR swaps the PID loop for a fuzzy-logic controller on CRAC units in Hall B or adds a feedforward term to the existing PID using outside air temperature.

*   **Option A: Fuzzy-logic controller.** Simulation on the 16-rack testbed showed a 12% lower PUE compared to the baseline. This is the primary efficiency gain but carries higher retrofit risk across all 48 units.
*   **Option B: PID + feedforward.** Retains the current control loop architecture while incorporating outside air temperature data. This path presents less risk for a site-wide retrofit but lacks the simulated efficiency upside of Option A.

**Context:**
*   The June 30 load test reached 1.8 MW using the old controller.
*   This change includes the sensor latency fix implemented last week for the c7000 firmware.

I am leaning toward the fuzzy-logic approach based on the simulation results, but the final decision rests on your assessment of the retrofit risk versus the efficiency gain.
