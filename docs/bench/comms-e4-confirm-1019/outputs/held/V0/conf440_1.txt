This PR replaces the PID-only logic with a hybrid model-predictive controller and a PID fallback. It also fixes the March 14 sensor drift calibration bug and adds a new high humidity alarm at 85% RH.

**Control Strategy Options**
*   **Option A:** Keep the hybrid model always on.
    *   **Impact:** Cuts overshoot to under 0.3 kPa.
    *   **Cost:** Increases compute usage by ~12% per cycle.
    *   **Author Lean:** Leaning toward this option for consistent performance.
*   **Option B:** Gate the hybrid model to run only when outside temperature is > 28°C or < 5°C.
    *   **Impact:** Saves energy but carries a risk of overshoot on mild days.
