**Decision: Hold release 2.11.0 until Thursday to include the Apple Pay fix; do not ship Monday.**

Shipping as-is risks a broken checkout for 38% of crash-reporting users on iOS 17.2, directly impacting revenue despite the 94% overall crash-free rate. While marketing has a Tuesday press slot, releasing a known payment failure contradicts the "win" of the new dark mode and checkout flow. The fix is ready; delaying four days prevents an immediate hotfix cycle and ensures the Friday release train carries a stable build.

**Context & Rationale**
*   **Critical Defect:** Apple Pay fails on iOS 17.2 due to a race condition (confirmed by Dan, QA).
*   **Impact:** 38% of current crash reports stem from this checkout flow bug.
*   **Status:** Fix is ready but unmerged. Android version is unaffected.
*   **Trade-off:** Missing the Tuesday press slot is preferable to launching with a broken payment method for a significant user segment.
*   **Author Position:** You leaned toward shipping, but the severity of the payment failure (38% of crashes) overrides the desire to maintain the schedule.

**Required Actions**
1.  **Release Plan:** Update plan to target Thursday build inclusion for the Friday train.
2.  **Dan (QA/Eng):** Merge the race condition fix immediately; verify Apple Pay on iOS 17.2 before Thursday EOD.
3.  **Marketing:** Notify of the shift from Tuesday press to Thursday/Friday announcement; frame as "ensuring full payment compatibility at launch."
4.  **Board:** Inform by Wednesday that the decision prioritizes transaction integrity over the original date, utilizing the buffer before the Friday train.

**Metrics**
*   **Current Stability:** 94% crash-free (beta).
*   **Defect Concentration:** 38% of crashes isolated to checkout flow.
*   **Target:** 100% functional checkout flow before sign-off.

**Sign-off**
Approved with the condition that the Apple Pay fix is merged and verified by Thursday. Proceed with the hotfix prep only as a contingency for post-launch issues unrelated to this known blocker.
