**Dana, please approve Option A (rule-based) or Option B (ML scorer) for the TriageFlow module by Friday, Feb 6 to lock the design for the Feb 12 release.**

This PR introduces the new triage queue module (renamed "TriageFlow" per Marketing) and includes the updated dashboard mockup with the new color scheme. The spike identified two implementation approaches:

*   **Option A: Rule-based Prioritizer**
    *   **Mechanism:** Uses static severity weights.
    *   **Trade-off:** Fast implementation with low infrastructure overhead.
    *   **Risk:** Predicted false-negative rate of **18%** (misses 18% of at-risk urgent cases).

*   **Option B: Lightweight ML Scorer**
    *   **Mechanism:** Uses past visit outcomes to score triage.
    *   **Trade-off:** Better accuracy but requires a new nightly batch job.
    *   **Risk:** Predicted false-negative rate of **7%** (based on a 4,300-case historical sample).

**Decision Required:**
Please review the trade-offs between infrastructure cost (Option A) and patient safety/accuracy (Option B) and comment with your approval of one option.
