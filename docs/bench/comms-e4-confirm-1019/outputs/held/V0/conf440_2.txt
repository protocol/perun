**Priya, please approve Option A (always-on hybrid) or Option B (temperature-gated) for the VPD controller by Friday, April 11, to meet the June 1 firmware freeze.**

The proposed change replaces PID-only logic with a hybrid model-predictive controller featuring a PID fallback. This update also resolves the March 14 sensor drift calibration bug and implements a new high-humidity alarm at 85% RH.

**Control Logic Options**
*   **Option A:** Keep the hybrid model active continuously.
    *   **Impact:** Increases compute cost by ~12% per cycle.
    *   **Benefit:** Limits overshoot to under 0.3 kPa in all conditions.
*   **Option B:** Activate the hybrid model only when outside temperature is >28°C or <5°C.
    *   **Impact:** Reduces energy consumption compared to Option A.
    *   **Risk:** Potential for overshoot on mild days when the hybrid model is inactive.

**Testing Status**
Bench 3 testing showed positive results overall. However, the log file from April 2 indicates a glitch in the fallback trigger mechanism that requires attention regardless of the selected option.

**Recommendation**
The author leans toward Option A to ensure consistent overshoot protection, but requests your decision on the trade-off between compute cost and risk exposure.
