**Option B: Temperature-Gated Hybrid**
*   **Logic:** Runs hybrid mode only when outside temperature is > 28°C or < 5°C.
*   **Impact:** Saves energy during mild conditions.
*   **Risk:** Potential overshoot on mild days.
