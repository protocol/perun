**PR: Scheduling Algorithm Update – Queue-Based vs. Predictive Overflow**

**Summary**
This PR introduces two candidate models for the call-centre staffing bot's new scheduling algorithm: Option A (queue-based) and Option B (predictive overflow). Both were tested against the March 3–7 dataset using a 1,200-call mock.

**Comparison**
*   **Option A (Queue-Based):** Delivers lower peak wait times during high-volume intervals.
*   **Option B (Predictive Overflow):** Reduces weekly overtime per agent by 18% (from 42 hours to 34 hours), with a modest trade-off in peak wait latency.

**Recommendation**
We recommend proceeding with **Option B**. While Option A improves immediate response metrics, the 18% reduction in overtime offered by Option B provides greater long-term operational efficiency and cost savings without violating service level agreements.
