**PR: VPD Controller – Hybrid MPC + PID Fallback**

This update replaces the PID-only logic with a hybrid model-predictive control (MPC) system that falls back to PID when necessary. The change also resolves the sensor drift calibration bug identified on March 14 and introduces a new high-humidity alarm triggered at 85% RH.

Bench testing on unit 3 showed positive results, though the April 2 log file revealed a glitch in the fallback trigger that needs attention.

We need your decision on the deployment strategy:

*   **Option A (Recommended):** Keep the hybrid model active at all times. This increases compute costs by approximately 12% per cycle but limits overshoot to under 0.3 kPa.
*   **Option B:** Gate the hybrid model to run only when outside temperatures exceed 28°C or drop below 5°C. This saves energy but carries a risk of overshoot during mild weather conditions.

Given the stability benefits, I lean toward Option A, but I need your approval to proceed. Please confirm your choice by Friday, April 11, so we can meet the firmware freeze deadline for the June 1 deployment.

Note: The April 2 fallback glitch will be investigated parallel to your decision to ensure it doesn't block the freeze.
