**The Problem**
The meter replacement program review confirms that aging analog meters are under-registering consumption by approximately 9%. This technical failure results in an annual revenue loss of **$1.2 million** in unbilled water.

**Options Analysis**
We evaluated two approaches to address the loss:

*   **Option A (Full Swap):** Replace all 40,000 meters over 3 years. Total cost: $6.8 million.
*   **Option B (Targeted Replacement):** Replace the 15,000 worst-performing meters over 18 months. Total cost: $2.4 million.

**Recommendation**
We recommend **Option B**. This approach recovers the largest volume of lost revenue in the shortest time frame with significantly lower upfront capital. Evidence from the East Ward pilot supports this strategy; targeted replacements there reduced leak detections by 22%.
