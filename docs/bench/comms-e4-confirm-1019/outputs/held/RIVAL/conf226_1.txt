### PR Description

**Title:** Implement demand-sensing model: Rolling 28-day window (Option A)

**Summary**
This PR introduces the new demand-sensing model with two implementation paths. Based on recent testing, we recommend **Option A** (rolling 28-day window with exponential smoothing) over Option B (90-day seasonal baseline with regression).

**Key Metrics**
*   **Accuracy:** Option A reduces MAPE from 8.4% to **6.1%** on the last three months of test data. Option B achieves 7.2%.
*   **Scope:** Pilot covers 214 SKUs.
*   **Operational Cost:** Option A requires weekly retraining at ~$1.2K per run. Option B runs monthly.

**Decision Context**
While Option A incurs higher operational costs due to weekly retraining, the accuracy improvement (6.1% vs 7.2% MAPE) is critical for the upcoming audit. We are prioritizing forecast precision for this cycle.

**Timeline**
Sign-off is required by Wednesday, 14 Aug, ahead of the audit next Thursday.
