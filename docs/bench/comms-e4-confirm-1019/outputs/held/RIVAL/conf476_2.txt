**Subject: Summary of Q3 Onboarding Audit & Decision Required**

Dana, the final onboarding audit for the 12-week program shows a sharp decline in completion rates, dropping to 61% this quarter from 78% last quarter. Two primary factors are driving this drop:

1.  **Placement Test Mismatch:** 34% of new starters are placed in levels above their actual ability, leading most to leave by week 4.
2.  **Buddy System Failure:** Only 22% of students report meeting their assigned buddy more than once.

The audit team recommends two immediate options to address the placement issue:

*   **Option A:** Overhaul the placement test and re-train all 15 assessors.
    *   **Cost:** ~$18k in training time.
    *   **Deadline:** Must be completed by March 12.
*   **Option B:** Keep the current test but add a mandatory 2-week "leveling bootcamp" for anyone scoring below 70%.
    *   **Cost:** Lower upfront cost.
    *   **Impact:** Delays student start dates.

A decision is needed by **Tuesday, March 4**, to update the course catalog for the April intake.

*Note: The admin team has flagged the full report as overly long; this summary covers the critical data points. Minor issues regarding 9 complaints from the French cohort about the homework app were noted but are not central to the completion rate decline.*
