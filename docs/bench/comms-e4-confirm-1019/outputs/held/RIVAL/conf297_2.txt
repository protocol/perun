**PR Description: climate-vent-prediction**

This pull request introduces two options for vent control to reduce temperature overshoot and improve energy efficiency. We validated both approaches during a 3-week trial in bay 4.

**Option A: Predictive Model (ML)**
*   **Performance:** Reduces temperature overshoot by 38% (from 2.1°C to 1.3°C).
*   **Efficiency:** Energy use was 4.2% lower than the baseline.
*   **Requirements:** Requires additional sensor data.
*   **Known Issues:** Maintenance flagged potential sensor drift during the trial.

**Option B: Fixed Hysteresis**
*   **Performance:** Standard control logic without predictive adjustments.
*   **Requirements:** No new sensors needed; cheaper to implement.
*   **Efficiency:** Baseline energy consumption.
