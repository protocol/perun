**To:** Dana, Deputy City Manager
**From:** Lena

**Subject:** Decision Needed: Water Leak Audit & Meter Replacement Strategy

The leak-detection audit results are in, and they require immediate attention before we finalize the council packet. Our non-revenue water currently sits at 19.7% of total flow, significantly exceeding the 12% target.

Two primary factors are driving this loss:
1.  **Aging Infrastructure:** Approximately 4,300 brass meters installed before 1995, concentrated in the Elmwood district, are under-registering flow by an average of 8%.
2.  **Contractor Delay:** AquaMetrix missed their February 28 calibration deadline, leaving gaps in our current data reliability.

Additionally, the audit identified 37 minor leaks. While none are urgent, they collectively account for roughly 40,000 gallons of lost water per day. Please note that full consumption data reliability will also depend on the new billing software rollout, now scheduled for April.

**Recommendation and Options**

The audit report recommends a phased approach, but Finance advocates for a full immediate replacement to secure bulk pricing. We need your direction by Thursday, March 15, to include the chosen path in the council packet.

*   **Option A (Finance Preference):** Replace all 4,300 pre-1995 meters over the next 18 months.
    *   *Estimated Cost:* $2.1 million.
    *   *Benefit:* Captures bulk pricing discounts and resolves the Elmwood issue comprehensively.

*   **Option B (Audit Recommendation):** Execute a phased replacement, starting with the 1,200 worst-performing meters immediately, followed by a re-audit in Q3.
    *   *Benefit:* Lower initial outlay and allows us to validate results before committing to the full scope.
    *   *Requirement:* If you select this option, I need your approval to issue an RFP to three vendors immediately.

**Decision Required**

Please let me know by Thursday whether to proceed with **Option A** or **Option B**.

If you choose Option B, confirm that I have authorization to release the RFP to vendors. Once decided, I will draft the corresponding section for the council packet.
