**PR: Payroll Vendor Migration – Approach Selection for Review**

**Reviewer:** Dana
**Deadline for Decision:** Thursday, Feb 20
**Goal:** Select migration approach to meet employee notice deadline (Feb 28) and contract end date (May 31).

### Context
We are switching payroll vendors. Two implementation approaches are proposed below. The HR system integration dependency has been validated with 200 mock records, and the EDI feed test succeeded with a 50-employee sample.

### Options

**Option A: Big Bang Cutover**
*   **Scope:** Migrate all 4,200 active employees simultaneously.
*   **Date:** March 1, 2025.
*   **Estimated Cost:** $1.8M (one-time conversion).
*   **Risk Profile:** Higher risk of pay errors due to single-point failure potential; however, it ensures full transition well before the current contract expires.

**Option B: Phased Rollout**
*   **Scope:** Salaried staff in March; Hourly staff in April/May.
*   **Duration:** 3 months.
*   **Estimated Cost:** $2.3M (one-time conversion).
*   **Risk Profile:** Lower risk of pay errors by isolating groups, but extends the timeline closer to the May 31 contract expiration.

### Financial Considerations
Option A saves $500K in immediate conversion costs compared to Option B. The CFO has confirmed a $500K contingency reserve is available should unforeseen issues arise during the chosen migration path.

### Request
Please review the cost vs. risk trade-offs and approve either **Option A** or **Option B** by Thursday, Feb 20. Once approved, I will finalize the detailed migration plan and issue the required employee notices by Feb 28.

---
*Current vendor contract ends May 31; any delay beyond the proposed timelines jeopardizes compliance.*
*Integration tests (200 records) and EDI tests (50 records) are complete and passed.*
