**To:** Dana Rossi, Deputy City Manager
**From:** Lena
**Date:** March 12, 2024
**Subject:** Decision Needed: Water Leak Audit Results & Meter Replacement Strategy

The leak-detection audit results are in, and they require your direction before we finalize the council packet. Our non-revenue water currently sits at 19.7% of total flow, significantly missing the 12% target.

Two primary factors are driving this loss:
1.  **Aging Infrastructure:** Approximately 4,300 meters installed before 1995, concentrated in the Elmwood district, are under-registering flow by an average of 8%.
2.  **Contractor Delay:** AquaMetrix missed their February 28 calibration deadline, leaving gaps in our current data validation.

Additionally, the audit identified 37 minor leaks. While none are urgent, they collectively account for roughly 40,000 gallons of lost water per day. Please note that our consumption data will remain partially unreliable until the new billing software rolls out in April.

**The Decision**
We need to select a replacement strategy for the aging meters by **Thursday, March 15**. There are two options:

*   **Option A (Finance Recommendation):** Replace all 4,300 pre-1995 meters over 18 months.
    *   *Estimated Cost:* $2.1 million.
    *   *Benefit:* Captures bulk pricing discounts and resolves the issue comprehensively.
*   **Option B (Audit Recommendation):** Phase the replacement, starting with the worst 1,200 meters immediately, followed by a re-audit in Q3.
    *   *Benefit:* Lower upfront cost and allows us to validate results before committing to the full swap.

**Recommendation & Next Steps**
The audit report recommends **Option B**, while Finance leans toward **Option A** due to long-term savings.

If you choose **Option B**, I need your approval to issue an RFP to three vendors immediately. If you prefer **Option A**, we will begin drafting the capital appropriation request.

Please let me know your decision by Thursday so we can update the council packet accordingly.
