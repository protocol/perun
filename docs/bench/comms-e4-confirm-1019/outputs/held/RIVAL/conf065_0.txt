**SUBJECT:** Approval Request: Targeted Meter Replacement Program (Option B)

**Decision Required**
Please sign the attached approval form by **Friday, March 14**, to authorize procurement for Option B of the meter replacement program. This action is required to secure vendor warranty coverage before it expires on March 31, 2025.

**The Problem**
The review of our meter replacement program confirms that aging analog meters are under-registering consumption by approximately 9%. This technical failure results in an annual revenue loss of $1.2M in unbilled water.

**Recommendation: Option B (Targeted Replacement)**
We recommend proceeding immediately with Option B rather than the comprehensive swap outlined in Option A.

*   **Option A (Full Swap):** Replaces all 40,000 meters over 3 years. Cost: $6.8M.
*   **Option B (Targeted):** Replaces the 15,000 worst-performing meters over 18 months. Cost: $2.4M.

Option B is the superior choice because it addresses the largest sources of revenue loss in the shortest timeframe. By focusing on the worst offenders, we stop the bleeding faster without the upfront capital burden of a city-wide swap. Evidence from the East Ward pilot supports this approach; targeted replacements there reduced leak detections by 22%.

**Financial and Operational Notes**
*   **Warranty Deadline:** The current vendor warranty expires on March 31, 2025. Failure to order by this date forfeits coverage.
*   **Finance Coordination:** The Finance Office has requested a change to the invoice coding structure; this will be updated during procurement setup.
*   **Communications:** The upcoming press release regarding this initiative will explicitly mention the concurrent tariff review to provide full context to ratepayers.

**Next Steps**
Upon your signature by March 14, we will initiate the procurement process for the 15,000 units. This timeline ensures installation begins well before the warranty cutoff, securing the $2.4M investment and beginning recovery of the $1.2M annual loss immediately.
