**Subject:** Decision Required: Routing Algorithm Change for Afternoon Pickup Window

**Context**
We are finalizing the routing algorithm update to address last week's three late bus complaints. With snow forecast for Friday, implementing a reliable solution before then is critical. We require your approval on the preferred approach before the district board meeting on Thursday to finalize the vendor contract.

**Proposed Options**
The operations team has evaluated two approaches for the afternoon pickup window:

*   **Option A:** Maintains the current 2:45 PM school dismissal time. This approach adds three new geofenced bus stops near the rec center, reducing walk distances for approximately 40 students.
    *   **Cost:** $12,400 one-time hardware expense.
    *   **Operational Impact:** No schedule conflicts; no additional staffing required.

*   **Option B:** Shifts school dismissal to 3:15 PM and adds two new stops.
    *   **Cost:** $8,900 one-time hardware expense, plus $2,100/month for an additional driver.
    *   **Operational Impact:** Creates a direct conflict with the middle school bus schedule at 3:05 PM. Marcus (Ops Lead) favors this option due to the lower upfront capital requirement.

**Recommendation**
While Option B reduces initial hardware spend, the recurring monthly cost for an extra driver exceeds the upfront savings of Option A within five months. More critically, the 3:05 PM conflict with the middle school route introduces significant reliability risks, particularly given the impending winter weather. Option A resolves the walk-distance issue and addresses the recent lateness complaints without disrupting existing schedules or incurring recurring labor costs. Therefore, Option A is the recommended path.

**Action Required**
Please review the cost and operational implications above and confirm your selection by end-of-day Wednesday. We need your formal approval to present the final contract to the district board on Thursday.

Reply with "Approved Option A" or "Approved Option B" so we can proceed immediately.
