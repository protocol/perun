This season's wheat pool net margin fell to $2.10/bushel, an 18% drop from last year's $2.56. The primary driver was $340,000 in storage overrun penalties incurred because our elevators reached capacity during harvest. A weather-related delay pushed our delivery window to October 15, creating a bottleneck that coincided with the corn harvest. We also recorded an $18,000 overrun in trucking costs due to these logistical constraints.

To prevent a recurrence next season, we must address our storage capacity. We are weighing two options:

*   **Option A:** Contract additional off-site storage at a fixed rate of $0.12/bushel/month.
*   **Option B:** Implement a quota system to stagger farmer deliveries.

Two board members, Ed and Priti, are advocating for a hybrid approach combining elements of both. However, given the immediate need to secure capacity and the complexity of administering quotas during a volatile harvest, I recommend proceeding with **Option A**. Securing off-site space now provides certainty and avoids the administrative burden of enforcing delivery windows on farmers.

We need to lock in these storage rates before the December 4 board meeting. To do so, I need your approval on Option A by **November 30**. Please let me know if you support moving forward with this recommendation or if you would like to discuss the hybrid proposal further before the deadline.
