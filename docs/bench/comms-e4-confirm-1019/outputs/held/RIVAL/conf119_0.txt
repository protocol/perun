### Context
We are finalizing the forecasting logic for the pharmacy stock replenishment module. The goal is to meet the Q2 forecast accuracy target of 95% while ensuring stability for all item types, including cold-chain products. With Marcus out until Monday, you are the designated owner for this decision.

### Options Evaluated
We tested two approaches against last year's data:

*   **Option A: Simple Moving Average (28-day)**
    *   **Accuracy:** 6.2% MAPE
    *   **Compute:** Baseline
    *   **Stability:** Works across all SKUs, including cold-chain items.

*   **Option B: Hybrid Model (ARIMA + Seasonal Adjustment)**
    *   **Accuracy:** 4.8% MAPE (Meets Q2 95% accuracy target)
    *   **Compute:** Requires 15% more resources than Option A.
    *   **Known Issue:** Currently fails on cold-chain items (~3 SKUs) due to outlier sensitivity.

### Recommendation
We recommend proceeding with **Option B**, contingent on applying a patch to the outlier filter to handle the 3 cold-chain SKUs. This approach delivers the required accuracy for the Q2 target and supports the upcoming VP demo on March 1.

If the patch cannot be validated in time, we will fall back to Option A to ensure the March 4 go-live is not delayed.
