Subject: Decision required: Revised ferry timetable (starts Mon 14 Apr)

Dear Harbour Master,

We have received the consultant's revised schedule and need your approval on a conflict in the morning slot before we print new leaflets. The deadline for a decision is Thursday 10 April.

**The Conflict**
The new morning passenger sailing is set for 06:50, which clashes with the existing freight slot. We propose two options:
*   **Option A:** Shift freight to 05:40. This adds 20 minutes to their turnaround time.
*   **Option B:** Keep freight at 06:10 and move the passenger sailing to 07:05.

I recommend **Option B** to minimize disruption to freight operations, but this requires your sign-off.

**Other Schedule Changes**
*   **Afternoon:** The 16:30 sailing moves to 16:45. This affects the school run, though we have received no complaints so far.
*   **Sunday:** The 10:00 sailing moves to 10:30.

**Concerns & Constraints**
The latest survey indicates only 68% of regular passengers support these new times. Given this lukewarm reception, we must ensure the final schedule is robust.

Please note that the Easter weekend runs from 18–21 April. We must avoid implementing any changes during this period; the new timetable should commence strictly from Monday 14 April, respecting the Easter holiday window.

As you have the final call, please let us know your preference between Option A and Option B by Thursday 10 April.

Best regards,

Marta
