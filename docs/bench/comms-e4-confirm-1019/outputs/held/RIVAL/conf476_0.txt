**Onboarding Audit Summary for Dana**

1.  **Placement Test Mismatch:** 34% of new starters are placed in levels above their actual ability, leading to a high dropout rate by week 4.
2.  **Inactive Buddy System:** Only 22% of students report meeting their assigned buddy more than once.

*   **Option A:** Overhaul the placement test and re-train all 15 assessors.

    *   *Deadline:* Implementation by March 12.
*   **Option B:** Retain the current test but mandate a 2-week "leveling bootcamp" for anyone scoring below 70%.
    *   *Cost:* Lower financial cost.
    *   *Trade-off:* Delays student start dates.
