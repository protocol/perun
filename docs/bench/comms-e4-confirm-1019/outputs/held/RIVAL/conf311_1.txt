**PR: Select Onboarding Flow for Fall Intake (Option A vs. Option B)**

**Summary**
This PR proposes adopting **Option A** (6-week blended program) for the upcoming fall intake of 128 students. This approach prioritizes student completion and leverages our existing infrastructure under current hiring constraints.

**Context & Data**
We piloted both flows with 28 students to determine the best path forward:
*   **Option A (Blended):** 3 live lessons/week + LinguaSoft app.
    *   **Completion:** 87%
    *   **Satisfaction:** 4.1/5
*   **Option B (Intensive):** 5 live lessons/week.
    *   **Completion:** 71%
    *   **Satisfaction:** 4.6/5

While Option B yielded higher satisfaction scores and aligns with Marta's CEFR curriculum goals, Option A demonstrated a significantly higher completion rate (+16%). Given the hiring freeze, we cannot staff the higher lesson frequency required by Option B without risking quality or burnout. The blended model also maximizes the value of our current tooling.

**Changes Included**
*   Updated onboarding checklist to reflect the 6-week blended timeline.
*   Revised lesson templates for the 3-day/week live schedule.
*   Integrated the new quiz module within the app workflow.
*   Removed intensive-track specific assets.

**Licensing Note**
Our LinguaSoft deal expires in March. Extending it for Option A requires a $12k renewal fee. This is budgeted and recommended to maintain the blended component that drives our higher completion rates.
