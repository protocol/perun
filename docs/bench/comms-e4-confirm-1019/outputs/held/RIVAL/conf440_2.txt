**PR: VPD Controller Hybrid Model & Calibration Fixes**

**Owner:** Priya
**Sign-off Deadline:** Friday, April 11
**Target Deployment:** June 1 Firmware Freeze

**Summary**
This update replaces the legacy PID-only logic with a hybrid model-predictive controller that uses PID as a fallback. The change addresses overshoot issues and includes critical calibration fixes.

**Decision Required: Control Strategy**
We need your approval on one of the following approaches before the April 11 freeze:

*   **Option A (Recommended):** Keep the hybrid model active at all times.
    *   **Impact:** Reduces overshoot to under **0.3 kPa**.
    *   **Cost:** Increases compute usage by approximately **12%** per cycle.
*   **Option B:** Gate the hybrid model to activate only when outside temperature is >28°C or <5°C.
    *   **Impact:** Saves energy during mild conditions.
    *   **Risk:** Potential for overshoot on mild days when the system reverts to PID-only.

I recommend Option A to guarantee stability, but please confirm which approach you prefer.

**Additional Changes**
*   **Calibration Fix:** Resolves the sensor drift bug identified on March 14.
*   **New Alarm:** Triggers a high humidity alert at **85% RH**.

**Testing Notes**
Bench 3 testing showed positive results overall. However, the log file from April 2 indicates a glitch in the fallback trigger mechanism that we are monitoring.
