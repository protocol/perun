Subject: Decision: Chemistry in Focus (3rd ed) Print Run

Go ahead with the 8,000-copy run for the May 15 shipment.

While the 12,000-unit option covers the full 9,300 sales forecast, the financial risk outweighs the benefit of avoiding a mid-semester reprint. The 12k scenario leaves us exposed to 2,700 unsold units if returns hit the projected 15%, tied up in capital at a total cost of $87,500. The 8k run costs $61,200 and utilizes existing paper stock, ensuring we meet the critical May 15 shipping deadline without the four-day delay required for a new paper order.

We should prioritize securing inventory for the start of the term over perfect coverage. If sales outpace the initial 8,000 copies, we can trigger a second, smaller print run based on actual sell-through data rather than a forecast. This approach minimizes upfront spend and eliminates the risk of significant overstock.

Regarding the corrupted cover art file, proceed with the 8k commitment now. We will integrate the corrected file from the designer once received on Friday, assuming this falls within the pre-press window for the smaller run. If the file delay threatens the May 15 date, we will reassess, but the current plan assumes the Friday delivery is sufficient for the 8k production timeline.

Please book the press for 8,000 copies.

Note: This decision locks in the lower cost and earlier ship date; monitor weekly sales closely to time the reorder.
