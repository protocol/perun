**Subject:** URGENT: Sensor Outage Cluster (Downtown) – Decision Needed by Friday

We have a significant sensor outage in the downtown core affecting the 300 block of Elm and the 400 block of Maple. Currently, 142 of 310 sensors are reporting stale data (last ping > 6 hours). This represents the highest failure rate we've seen this quarter.

We've ruled out network issues. The root cause is the new firmware v2.4 rollout that began Tuesday. Approximately 60% of the units that updated are now stuck in a boot loop.

We need a decision on the path forward by Friday to prevent data gaps during weekend congestion. We have two options:

1.  **Rollback to v2.3** across the affected blocks immediately. (Recommended)
2.  **Push a vendor-provided patch** that claims to fix the issue but has not been tested in our environment.

Given the risk of untested code, I recommend we proceed with option (a) and rollback to v2.3. Please approve this approach or advise if you prefer we attempt the untested patch.
