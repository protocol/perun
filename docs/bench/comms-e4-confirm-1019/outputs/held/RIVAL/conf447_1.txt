Subject: Approval Request: North Quay Ferry Timetable Change (07:15 Departure)

Dear Harbour Master,

We are seeking your approval for a minor adjustment to the North Quay ferry schedule to address crowding on the 08:00 departure. The current 07:15 run averages 214 passengers daily, making it our busiest service.

We propose shifting the 07:15 departure to 07:05 (Option A). This change is projected to reduce crowding on the subsequent 08:00 ferry by approximately 12%. While this option requires a corresponding adjustment to the bus link at the pier, it offers the most significant relief to passenger volume.

Please note that we are aware of potential tidal constraints for a 07:05 departure and request your confirmation that this slot is viable before we finalize the printed schedules. A separate proposal regarding a 10-minute delay for the 17:40 return service to connect with rail replacements is under review but is not included in this specific request.

We require your decision by Thursday to meet our printing deadline.

Regards,

Marta
