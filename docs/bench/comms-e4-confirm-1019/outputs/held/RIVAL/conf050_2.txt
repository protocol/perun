### Cold-Chain Storage Decision: Retrofit vs. Lease

**Context**
The frozen meal program for senior kits faces spoilage risks without immediate cold-chain upgrades at the Maplewood hub. We must select a storage approach by **Friday, March 28** to ensure material availability.

**Option A: Retrofit Existing Warehouse**
*   **Action:** Install two modular freezer units in the current dry warehouse.
*   **Cost:** $210,000 total.
*   **Timeline:** Installable by April 12.
*   **Impact:** Projects a **38% reduction in spoiled produce**, validated by the Riverside site pilot.
*   **Funding Note:** The city grant covers up to $150,000. Proceeding requires reallocating the remaining $60,000 from other budget lines.

**Option B: Lease Refrigerated Fleet**
*   **Action:** Lease three trailers from ColdHaul.
*   **Cost:** $3,200/month per trailer ($9,600/month total).
*   **Timeline:** Deployment starts May 1.
*   **Impact:** Provides immediate capacity but lacks the specific spoilage reduction data associated with the modular retrofit pilot.

**Budget & Approval Status**
*   **Priya (Ops Lead):** Pending final budget impact analysis and sign-off.
*   **Decision Required:** Marcus to review both options and approve one path.
*   **Deadline:** Friday, March 28.

**Recommendation**
Option A offers a permanent solution with proven spoilage reduction (38%) and an earlier completion date (April 12 vs. May 1). However, it requires bridging a $60k funding gap beyond the city grant. Option B avoids capital expenditure but delays implementation and lacks the pilot-backed efficiency gains.
