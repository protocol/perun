**PR: Select Option A (6-week blended) for Fall Onboarding Flow**

**Summary**
This change implements Option A as the standard onboarding flow for the fall term, updating the onboarding checklist, lesson templates, and adding the new quiz module.

**Context & Decision**
We must finalize the course catalog by Friday, Sept 12, to accommodate 128 enrolled students. Due to the current hiring freeze, we can only support one delivery model.

Based on pilot data from 28 students, Option A (6-week blended: 3 live lessons/week + app) delivered an **87% completion rate**, significantly higher than Option B's 71%. While Option B (4-week intensive) achieved slightly higher satisfaction (4.6 vs 4.1), the completion metric is the primary indicator of student success and retention for this intake.

Although the curriculum team (Marta) advocates for Option B to align with new CEFR standards, the risk of lower completion in a resource-constrained environment makes Option A the safer choice for this term. We can address CEFR alignment in future iterations once staffing constraints ease.

**Changes Included**
- Updated onboarding checklist to reflect the 6-week schedule.
- Revised lesson templates for the blended format (3 live sessions/week).
- Integrated the new quiz module into the app workflow.

**Side Note**
Our licensing deal with LinguaSoft for the app component expires in March. Extending it requires a $12k payment, which should be processed separately to ensure continuity for the blended model.

**Action Required**
Please review and approve this approach by Friday, Sept 12, so we can finalize the course catalog and notify enrolled students.
