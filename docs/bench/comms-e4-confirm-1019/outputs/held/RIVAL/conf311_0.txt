**PR: Select Onboarding Flow for Fall Intake (Option A vs. Option B)**

This PR proposes adopting **Option A** (6-week blended program) for the upcoming fall term involving 128 enrolled students. The decision balances completion rates, resource constraints, and curriculum alignment ahead of the September 12 catalog deadline.

**Pilot Data Summary**
A recent pilot with 28 students compared two approaches:
*   **Option A:** 6-week blended (3 live lessons/week + app). Result: **87% completion rate**, 4.1/5 satisfaction.
*   **Option B:** 4-week intensive (5 live lessons/week). Result: 71% completion rate, 4.6/5 satisfaction.

While Option B yielded higher satisfaction scores, Option A demonstrated significantly higher student retention. Given the hiring freeze limits our capacity to support complex scheduling or high-touch intensive models, the higher completion rate of Option A presents the lower operational risk for the larger fall cohort.

**Curriculum & Licensing Context**
Marta and the curriculum team prefer Option B for its alignment with new CEFR standards. However, Option A utilizes the existing LinguaSoft app integration. We currently hold a licensing deal with LinguaSoft that expires in March; extending this license requires a $12k investment. Selecting Option A maximizes the value of this existing asset before the renewal decision point. If we pivot to Option B, we risk underutilizing the app while still facing the renewal deadline.

**Changes Included in This PR**
The attached diff implements the infrastructure for Option A, including:
*   Updates to the student onboarding checklist.
*   Revised lesson templates for the blended 3-day schedule.
*   Integration of the new quiz module within the app workflow.
