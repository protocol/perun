**PR Description: climate-vent-prediction**

This branch introduces two approaches for vent control to reduce temperature overshoot and optimize energy use. We evaluated both options during a 3-week trial in bay 4.

**Option A: Predictive Model (ML)**
*   **Performance:** Reduces temperature overshoot by 38% (from 2.1°C to 1.3°C).
*   **Efficiency:** Energy use was 4.2% lower than the baseline.
*   **Requirements:** Requires additional sensor data.
*   **Notes:** Maintenance flagged potential sensor drift during the trial.

**Option B: Fixed Hysteresis**
*   **Performance:** Standard control logic.
*   **Requirements:** No new sensors needed; lower implementation cost.
*   **Notes:** Avoids the sensor drift issues observed in Option A.
