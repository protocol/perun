**Title:** Parking Sensor Gateway: Poll-to-Event Rewrite

**Summary**
This PR replaces the legacy polling loop with an event-driven MQTT architecture to resolve sensor dropouts and reduce power consumption. The diff includes a critical fix for heartbeat timestamps.

**Impact**
*   **Reliability:** Eliminates polling-induced dropouts (3 observed this week in the pilot zone of 340 sensors).
*   **Efficiency:** Reduces sensor battery drain from 12% to 8% per month.
*   **Budget:** Vendor quote is $205K against a $180K budget ($25K overage).

**Decision Required**
We propose two implementation approaches:
*   **A)** Single shared broker with per-zone topics (Recommended).
*   **B)** Per-zone brokers with a local aggregator.
