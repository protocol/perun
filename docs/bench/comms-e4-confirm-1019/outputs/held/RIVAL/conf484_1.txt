**Executive Brief: Parking Sensor Pilot Decision**

**Decision Required By:** Friday, 14th
**Action:** Select one option below and sign the attached approval form.

**Pilot Performance Overview**
The pilot covered 3,200 spaces. While sensor accuracy sits at 92.4%, vendor uptime dropped to 88% in February. The system currently generates 214 misreads daily, resulting in false violation notices. Testing confirms the detection algorithm fails during heavy rain and near construction sites.

**Financial Context**
- **Current Cost of Errors:** $38,000/month in dispute processing for false fines.
- **Proposed Switch Cost:** $1.2M one-time investment for hybrid model.

**Options for Approval**

**Option A: Retain Current Vendor (Patch Algorithm)**
Continue with the existing vendor while they update the detection algorithm to handle rain and construction zones.
*Pros:* No immediate capital expenditure.
*Cons:* Uptime issues (88%) remain unaddressed; false fines continue until patches are verified.

**Option B: Switch to Hybrid Camera+Sensor Model (Recommended)**
Replace the current setup with a hybrid system combining cameras and sensors.
*Pros:* Eliminates specific environmental failure points; likely reduces false fines immediately.
*Cons:* Requires $1.2M additional budget.
*Rationale:* The $38k/month loss in disputes accumulates quickly. Given the algorithm's specific failures in common conditions (rain, construction), a hardware change offers a more permanent fix than a software patch on unreliable infrastructure.

**Option C: Pause Rollout (6 Weeks)**
Halt implementation for six weeks to conduct further testing or seek alternative vendors.
*Pros:* Prevents further false fines during the pause.
*Cons:* Delays project momentum; no guarantee of better results post-pause.

**Immediate Next Steps**
1. **Approve Option:** Sign the approval form for your chosen option by Friday, 14th.
2. **Council Update:** A progress note is due to the city council by the 20th. Your decision will dictate the content of this update.
3. **Vendor Communication:** Note that the vendor's representative is currently on vacation, which may impact the speed of negotiation for Option A or C.

**Recommendation**
Proceed with **Option B**. Despite the $1.2M upfront cost, the current system's inability to handle standard environmental factors and its low uptime suggest that patching will not resolve the root causes of the 214 daily errors. Stopping the bleed on dispute costs and securing reliable data justifies the investment.
