# Cold-chain storage decision: retrofit vs. lease

**Context**
The frozen meal program for senior kits faces spoilage risks without immediate cold-chain upgrades at the Maplewood hub. We must select a storage approach by Friday, March 28, to ensure materials arrive before the volunteer orientation next Tuesday.

**Option A: Retrofit existing dry warehouse**
- **Scope:** Install two modular freezer units in the current dry warehouse.
- **Cost:** $210,000 one-time.
- **Timeline:** Installable by April 12.
- **Impact:** Projects a 38% reduction in spoiled produce, validated by the Riverside site pilot.
- **Funding:** The city grant covers up to $150,000. This option requires reallocating the remaining $60,000 from other budget lines.

**Option B: Lease refrigerated trailer fleet**
- **Scope:** Lease three trailers from ColdHaul.
- **Cost:** $3,200/month per trailer ($9,600/month total).
- **Timeline:** Fleet available starting May 1.
- **Impact:** Provides immediate capacity but lacks the specific spoilage reduction data tied to the modular retrofit pilot.
- **Funding:** Fully operational expense; no capital reallocation needed.

**Recommendation**
Option A offers a permanent infrastructure upgrade with a proven 38% spoilage reduction, directly protecting the senior meal program. However, it requires bridging a $60,000 funding gap and must be approved by March 28 to meet the April 12 installation target. Option B avoids capital expenditure but delays availability until May 1 and does not guarantee the same spoilage metrics.

**Action Required**
Priya is finalizing the detailed budget impact analysis for the $60,000 reallocation and will provide sign-off shortly. Please review both options and approve one by Friday, March 28, so we can order materials or secure the lease in time for program continuity.
