**Summary**
This PR introduces two candidate models for the quarterly pharmacy stock forecast. We need your approval to proceed with Option B (with a specific patch) or fall back to Option A.

**Approaches & Results**
We tested both options against last year's data:

*   **Option A (Simple Moving Average, 28-day):**
    *   MAPE: 6.2%
    *   Compute: Baseline
    *   Status: Stable across all SKUs.

*   **Option B (Hybrid: ARIMA + Seasonal Adjustment):**
    *   MAPE: 4.8% (meets Q2 accuracy target of 95%)
    *   Compute: +15% over baseline
    *   **Known Issue:** Fails on cold-chain items (~3 SKUs) due to outlier sensitivity.
    *   **Proposed Fix:** Apply a patch to the outlier filter to handle these SKUs.

**Recommendation**
We recommend **Option B with the outlier filter patch**. It delivers the required accuracy for Q2, though it requires a modest increase in compute resources. If the patch proves unstable during your review, we will revert to Option A to ensure the March 4 go-live is not jeopardized.
