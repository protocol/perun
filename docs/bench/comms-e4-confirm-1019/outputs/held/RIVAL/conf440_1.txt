**PR: Hybrid MPC + PID Fallback for VPD Controller**

This update replaces the existing PID-only logic with a hybrid model-predictive control (MPC) system that uses PID as a fallback. The change also resolves the sensor drift calibration bug identified on March 14 and introduces a new high-humidity alarm triggered at 85% RH.

Bench testing on unit 3 showed improved stability, though the April 2 log file revealed a glitch in the fallback trigger mechanism that requires attention.

We need your decision on the deployment strategy for the hybrid model:

*   **Option A (Recommended):** Keep the hybrid model active at all times. This increases compute costs by approximately 12% per cycle but limits overshoot to under 0.3 kPa.
*   **Option B:** Gate the hybrid model to run only when outside temperatures exceed 28°C or drop below 5°C. This saves energy but carries a risk of overshoot during mild weather conditions.

While Option B offers energy savings, Option A provides more consistent control. Please approve your preferred approach by Friday, April 11, so we can meet the firmware freeze deadline for the June 1 deployment.
