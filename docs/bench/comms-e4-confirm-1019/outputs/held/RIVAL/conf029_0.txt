This exposure presents a significant legal threat. This risk has grown since our last audit in March 2023, during which time we added 80 new dependencies without corresponding license reviews. The current compliance team is understaffed and unable to absorb this backlog without directed intervention. We have three options to address this:
1.  **Remove and replace** the risky components by the end of Q3.
2.  **Purchase commercial licenses** or obtain a formal legal opinion for each component.
3.  **Accept the risk** and document the exposure.

Option (a) is the recommended path. It eliminates the root cause of the liability rather than managing symptoms or accepting potential catastrophic loss. Replacing these components is feasible within the quarter if we begin immediately.
