**PR: Introduce TriageFlow module – select prioritization approach for Feb 12 release**

This PR introduces the new **TriageFlow** module (formerly "triage queue") to VetDesk, updated with the new dashboard color scheme. It outlines two implementation options identified during the spike phase and requests a design decision to lock the approach for the February 12 release.

**Context**
We need to finalize the prioritization logic for TriageFlow by **Friday, Feb 6**, to ensure inclusion in the upcoming sprint. The core trade-off is between infrastructure simplicity and prediction accuracy, specifically regarding the false-negative rate for at-risk urgent cases.

**Options**

| Feature | Option A: Rule-Based Prioritizer | Option B: Lightweight ML Scorer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Logic** | Static severity weights | Historical visit outcomes |
| **Infrastructure** | Low (no new jobs) | Requires nightly batch job |
| **Speed** | Fast implementation | Higher initial setup |
| **Accuracy** | **18%** false-negative rate | **7%** false-negative rate |
| **Data Basis** | N/A | 4,300-case historical sample |

**Recommendation & Request**
Option B reduces the risk of missing urgent cases by more than half (7% vs 18%) but introduces a dependency on a nightly batch process. Option A is faster to ship but carries a significantly higher risk of mis prioritizing at-risk patients.

**@Dana** – Please review the trade-offs above and approve one approach for the sprint. We need your sign-off by **Feb 6** to finalize the design before the Feb 12 release window.
