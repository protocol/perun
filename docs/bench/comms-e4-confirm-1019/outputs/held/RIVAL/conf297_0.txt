**PR Description: climate-vent-prediction**

This branch introduces two strategies for greenhouse vent control to reduce temperature overshoot and optimize energy use. We evaluated both approaches during a 3-week trial in bay 4.

**Option A: Predictive Model (ML)**
*   **Performance:** Reduces temperature overshoot by 38% (from 2.1°C to 1.3°C).
*   **Energy:** Consumed 4.2% less energy than the baseline during the trial.
*   **Requirements:** Requires additional sensor data.
*   **Risks:** Maintenance flagged potential sensor drift issues during the trial.

**Option B: Fixed Hysteresis**
*   **Performance:** Standard control logic without predictive adjustments.
*   **Requirements:** No new sensors needed; utilizes existing infrastructure.
*   **Cost:** Lower implementation cost compared to Option A.
