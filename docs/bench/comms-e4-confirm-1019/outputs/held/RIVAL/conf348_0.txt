**PR: Depot Charger Scheduling – Approach Selection for Birmingham Pilot**

Hi Priya,

Could you please review and approve the scheduling approach for the upcoming Birmingham depot pilot? We need a decision by Friday, 14th, to ensure the code is merged by 20th Feb for testing before the 1st March start date.

We are deciding between two implementations for the 42-bus e-fleet:

**Option A: Fixed Time-Slot Batching**
*   **Logic:** Assigns charging in 15-minute granular slots.
*   **Pros:** Aligns directly with the ops team's shift patterns (night shift ends at 6am) and is simpler to maintain.
*   **Cost:** 12% cheaper to implement.
*   **Effort:** +340 lines of code.

**Option B: Dynamic Priority Queue**
*   **Logic:** Prioritizes charging based on State of Charge (SOC) and departure time.
*   **Pros:** Reduces peak charger contention by 18% compared to Option A.
*   **Cost:** Higher implementation cost.
*   **Effort:** +520 lines of code.

**Recommendation:**
I recommend **Option A**. While Option B offers better peak load management, Option A's simplicity and alignment with current shift operations reduce implementation risk and cost. Given the tight timeline for the pilot, the 18% contention reduction in Option B may not outweigh the added complexity and cost at this stage.

Please let me know if you approve Option A or if you'd prefer to proceed with Option B.
