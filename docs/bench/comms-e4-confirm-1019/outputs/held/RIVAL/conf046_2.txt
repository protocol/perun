**PR: AGV Charging Scheduler – Dynamic vs. Fixed Slot Allocation**

**Summary**
This PR updates the AGV charging scheduler to reduce charger idle time and average wait times. We are evaluating two approaches:
*   **Approach A (Recommended):** Dynamic slot allocation with predictive demand.
*   **Approach B:** Fixed slot scheduling with a simpler heuristic.

**Key Metrics & Impact**
While both approaches pass the current test suite, Approach A delivers significant efficiency gains:
*   **Idle Time:** Reduces total daily idle charger time by **18%** (primary metric).
*   **Wait Time:** Cuts average charging wait from 14 minutes to 9 minutes.
*   **Performance:** Benchmark results for 10k and 20k order volumes are included in the attached report; both approaches scale, but A maintains lower latency under the 20k load.

**Dependencies & Constraints**
*   **Telemetry:** Approach A requires the new telemetry feed (v2.1), which is currently deployed in staging.
*   **Hardware:** Note that chargers 12–14 are offline until 5 Oct due to hardware maintenance. This constraint is external to this diff but should be considered during deployment planning.

**Rollback Plan**
If Approach A exhibits instability in production, the rollback procedure involves reverting to the previous scheduler version (effectively Approach B logic) via the standard deployment pipeline. No data migration is required as the configuration is stateless.
