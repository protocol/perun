# Cold-chain storage decision: retrofit vs. lease

**Context**
The frozen meal program for senior kits faces spoilage risks without immediate cold-chain expansion at the Maplewood hub. We must select a storage approach by Friday, March 28, to meet installation or delivery windows.

**Option A: Retrofit Existing Warehouse**
- **Scope:** Install two modular freezer units in the current dry warehouse.
- **Cost:** $210,000 total.
- **Timeline:** Installable by April 12.
- **Impact:** Projected 38% reduction in spoiled produce (based on Riverside pilot data).
- **Funding Gap:** The city grant covers $150,000. This option requires reallocating $60,000 from other budget lines.

**Option B: Lease Refrigerated Trailers**
- **Scope:** Lease fleet from ColdHaul.
- **Cost:** $3,200/month per trailer (3 trailers = $9,600/month).
- **Timeline:** Service begins May 1.
- **Impact:** Provides immediate capacity but lacks the specific spoilage reduction data associated with the modular retrofit pilot.
- **Funding:** Fully operational expense; no capital reallocation needed.

**Recommendation**
Option A offers a permanent solution with a verified 38% spoilage reduction, directly protecting the senior meal program. However, it requires approving a $60k budget reallocation beyond the city grant. Option B avoids capital expenditure but delays implementation until May 1 and lacks the pilot-backed efficiency gains.

**Action Required**
Priya (Ops Lead) is finalizing the detailed budget impact analysis for the reallocation scenario. Once she provides sign-off, we need your approval to proceed.

**Decision Deadline**
Please review and approve one option by **Friday, March 28**. This deadline is critical to order materials for the retrofit or secure the trailer lease before the May 1 start date.
