Our wheat pool net margin for this season settled at $2.10 per bushel, an 18% drop from last year's $2.56. Two primary factors drove this decline. Second, weather delays pushed our delivery window to October 15, colliding with the corn harvest and complicating logistics. We also recorded an $18,000 overrun in trucking costs.

Board members Ed and Priti are advocating for a hybrid approach that combines elements of both. However, given the urgency of securing capacity, I recommend we proceed with Option A. Locking in off-site storage now provides immediate certainty and avoids the administrative friction of enforcing delivery quotas on farmers.
