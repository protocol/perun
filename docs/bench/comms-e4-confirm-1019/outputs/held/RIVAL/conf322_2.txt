Subject: Decision needed: GH-3 climate control trial results

Dana,

The GH-3 trial (ending Mar 14) confirms we cannot maintain both lower heating costs and maximum yield simultaneously.

Running CO2 at 900 ppm cut heating costs by 18% compared to the 600 ppm baseline. However, yield per tray dropped 11% (from 42 kg to 37.4 kg) over the 8-week run. Although sensor drift in GH-2 affected data collection during weeks 3–5, the overall trend remains valid.

We have two paths forward:

1.  **Adopt 900 ppm:** Save $12,400/year on gas but accept an approximate 9% revenue loss due to lower yield.
2.  **Adjust to 700 ppm:** Heating costs rise 9% compared to the baseline, but yield recovers to 41 kg per tray.

The fan retrofit remains scheduled for April 2. To proceed with ordering the necessary controllers, please let me know your decision by Friday, March 28.

Note: Sensor data from GH-2 weeks 3–5 was excluded from the final average due to drift.
Revenue loss estimate assumes current market pricing remains stable.
