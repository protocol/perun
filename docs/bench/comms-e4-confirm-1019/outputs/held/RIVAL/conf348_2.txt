**PR: Depot Charger Scheduling – Approach Selection for Birmingham Pilot**

**Context**
Prepares the Birmingham depot pilot (starts 1 March) for the 42-bus e-fleet. Code must merge by 20 Feb to allow testing before the night shift handover at 6 am. We need sign-off by Friday 14th on the scheduling algorithm.

**Proposed Approaches**
We have two viable implementations:

*   **Approach A: Fixed Time-Slot Batching**
    *   **Logic:** Assigns charging in 15-minute granular slots.
    *   **Pros:** Aligns directly with current ops shift patterns; simpler logic.
    *   **Cons:** Higher peak charger contention.
    *   **Cost/Effort:** +340 lines; 12% cheaper to implement than B.

*   **Approach B: Dynamic Priority Queue**
    *   **Logic:** Prioritizes based on State of Charge (SOC) and departure time.
    *   **Pros:** Reduces peak charger contention by 18% compared to A.
    *   **Cons:** More complex; higher implementation cost.
    *   **Cost/Effort:** +520 lines.

**Recommendation**
I recommend **Approach A**. While Approach B offers better load balancing, Approach A's simplicity reduces implementation risk ahead of the tight February deadline. Crucially, the fixed slots map cleanly to the ops team's existing shift structure (ending at 6 am), which should minimize friction during the pilot phase. The 18% contention reduction in B is valuable, but the cost savings and operational alignment of A make it the stronger choice for this initial rollout.

**Action Required**
Please review and approve either Approach A or B by **Friday 14th** so we can finalize the merge by 20th Feb.
