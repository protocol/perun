**Subject:** Decision Needed: Northgate Middle Bus Routing (Due Thu 3/14)

We need to finalize the bus routing strategy for Northgate Middle by this Thursday, March 14, to ensure shuttle orders or schedule retiming are completed before spring break. The Bellweather routing study presents two viable options, each addressing our current driver shortage (7 vacancies, plus 2 retirements in June) differently.

**Option A: Add Express Shuttles (Current 3-Tier System)**
*   **Approach:** Maintain the existing three-tier bell times and deploy four express shuttles specifically for the east side.
*   **Cost:** $182,000 annually.
*   **Impact:** Reduces average ride time by 11 minutes.
*   **Pros:** Lower immediate financial complexity; no structural schedule changes.
*   **Cons:** Does not address the underlying driver shortage; ongoing annual cost.

**Option B: Shift to 4-Tier System**
*   **Approach:** Move Northgate to a four-tier bell schedule.
*   **Cost:** $310,000 one-time implementation cost (impacting Q3 budget) + $64,000 annually.
*   **Impact:** Returns 14 buses to the fleet and cuts 38 minutes off the longest route.
*   **Pros:** Directly alleviates driver pressure by reducing fleet needs; significant time savings for students on long routes.
*   **Cons:** Higher upfront cost; requires a 3-week transition plan if selected.

**Additional Context**
The data team noted that 22% of buses run more than 60% empty on Fridays. While not part of these two primary options, this suggests potential for future efficiency adjustments.

**Recommendation**
I lean toward **Option B**. Despite the Q3 one-time cost hit, returning 14 buses to the fleet directly mitigates our critical driver shortage and offers substantially better ride times for students. However, if preserving the Q3 budget is the priority, Option A provides immediate relief for east-side riders.

Please let me know your decision by Thursday so we can proceed with the necessary orders or scheduling updates.
