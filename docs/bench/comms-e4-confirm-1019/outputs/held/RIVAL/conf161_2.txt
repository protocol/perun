Hi Dana,

We need to choose a rollout path for the new timetable tool by this Friday, 5 PM, before the development freeze begins on June 12.

The current system supports 1,400 active users and is generating about 12 daily complaints regarding scheduling clashes. While the student union has noted separate mobile app bugs, our immediate priority is avoiding disruption to these 1,400 users during the transition.

Here are the two options:

**Option A: Phased Rollout**
*   **Timeline:** Spread over 3 terms.
*   **Cost:** $48k (requires additional development).
*   **Risk:** Lower risk of widespread user disruption.

**Option B: Big Bang Rollout**
*   **Timeline:** Single term.
*   **Cost:** $72k.
*   **Requirement:** Requires Registrar sign-off on a hard cutoff date.
*   **Risk:** Higher risk; any issues affect all users immediately.

Option A costs $24k less than Option B, but the primary driver for this recommendation is user stability. Given the volume of active users, a phased approach minimizes the chance of breaking the workflow for the entire campus at once.

I recommend **Option A**.

Please let me know which option you approve. Once you decide, I will own the implementation.
