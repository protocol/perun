**Subject:** Proposal: Fuzzy-Logic Controller Retrofit for Hall B CRAC Units

Requesting your review and approval on the control strategy for the Hall B CRAC unit retrofit.

We are evaluating two approaches to replace the current PID loop:
1.  **PID + Feedforward:** Retains the existing loop but adds a new term based on outside air temperature. This is the lower-risk option for retrofitting across all 48 units.
2.  **Full Fuzzy-Logic Controller:** Replaces the PID loop entirely.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with the **Full Fuzzy-Logic Controller**.

While the PID+feedforward approach offers a safer deployment path, simulation results on the 16-rack testbed indicate the fuzzy-logic controller delivers a **12% reduction in PUE**. This efficiency gain is the primary driver for the upgrade. For context, during the June 30 load test, the hall reached 1.8 MW using the old controller; the fuzzy approach directly targets the inefficiencies observed at that load level.

This proposal also incorporates the sensor latency fix deployed last week and accounts for the c7000 firmware constraints.

Given the potential efficiency gains, I recommend accepting the slightly higher retrofit complexity of the fuzzy-logic approach. Please let me know if you approve this direction or if you would prefer to limit the rollout to a pilot group first.
