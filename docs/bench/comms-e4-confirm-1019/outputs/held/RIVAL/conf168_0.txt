Subject: Decision needed: Q3 co-op grain pricing options

Marcus,

Please select one of the following three options for the Q3 premium pool by Friday, June 14. We need your choice confirmed to update the member portal before the July 1 contract renewal notices are sent.

Note that while the board mentioned a $4.5M reserve at the May 28 meeting, those funds are not available for this initiative. Additionally, the April weather delay impacted early deliveries, but that issue is being handled separately from this pricing decision.

**Option A: Raise Base Price**
*   **Action:** Increase the base price to $14.20/bu.
*   **Cost:** $1.8M.
*   **Impact:** Applies to all bushels uniformly.

**Option B: Loyalty Bonus**
*   **Action:** Maintain the base price at $13.50/bu and add a $0.30/bu bonus.
*   **Eligibility:** Members who delivered more than 5,000 bu last year.
*   **Cost:** $2.3M.
*   **Impact:** Rewards high-volume producers from the previous year.

**Option C: Equal Split**
*   **Action:** Distribute $2.0M equally among the membership.
*   **Distribution:** Approximately $1,667 per member across all 1,200 members.
*   **Cost:** $2.0M.
*   **Impact:** Provides a flat payment regardless of volume.

My recommendation is Option A, as it offers the broadest support at the lowest total cost. However, please let me know which direction you prefer so we can proceed with the necessary updates.
