**Executive Brief: Parking Sensor Pilot Decision**

**To:** Dana
**From:** [Your Name]
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Subject:** Approval Required: Parking Sensor Pilot Path Forward

**Context**
The pilot program covered 3,200 spaces. While sensor accuracy stands at 92.4%, vendor uptime dropped to 88% in February. We are currently seeing 214 misreads daily, generating false violation notices. Testing confirms the detection algorithm fails specifically during heavy rain and near construction sites.

**Financial Impact**
*   **Current Cost:** False fines cost the city $38,000/month in dispute resolution.
*   **Proposed Investment:** Switching to the recommended hybrid model requires an additional $1.2M upfront.

**Options for Approval**

**A. Keep Current Vendor (Patch Algorithm)**
*   **Action:** Retain existing hardware; vendor updates software to handle rain and construction noise.
*   **Pros:** Lowest immediate cost; no hardware changes.
*   **Cons:** Relies on vendor performance (88% uptime); does not address hardware limitations; false fines likely continue until patch is proven.
*   **Timeline:** Patch deployment estimated at 4–6 weeks.

**B. Switch to Hybrid Camera+Sensor Model (Recommended)**
*   **Action:** Replace current sensors with a hybrid system using cameras for visual verification.
*   **Pros:** Eliminates most false positives caused by environmental factors; reduces dispute costs long-term; higher reliability.
*   **Cons:** Requires $1.2M additional budget; longer implementation time.
*   **Timeline:** Full rollout delayed, but pilot can transition immediately upon approval.

**C. Pause Rollout**
*   **Action:** Halt all expansion for 6 weeks to re-evaluate technology.
*   **Pros:** Stops further accumulation of false fines and bad data immediately.
*   **Cons:** No progress on parking management goals; potential contractual penalties; delays revenue optimization.
*   **Timeline:** 6-week hold, plus time to source new solutions.

**Recommendation**
Proceed with **Option B**. Although the $1.2M switch cost is significant, the current rate of 214 daily misreads creates ongoing operational friction and reputational risk. At $38k/month in dispute costs alone, the current system is unsustainable. The hybrid model addresses the root cause (environmental interference) rather than treating symptoms.

**Deadlines & Next Steps**
*   **Decision Deadline:** You must approve one option by **Friday, 14th**.
*   **City Council:** A progress note is due by the **20th**; your decision will dictate this update.
*   **Vendor Status:** The vendor representative is currently on vacation, which may delay negotiations for Option A or C, but should not impact the procurement path for Option B if we move quickly.

**Action Required**
Please review the options above and sign the attached approval form indicating your choice. Once signed, we will initiate the necessary procurement or pause protocols immediately to meet the council's timeline.
