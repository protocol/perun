**SUBJECT:** Approval Request: Targeted Meter Replacement Program (Option B)

**Decision Required**
Please sign the attached approval form by **Friday, March 14**, to authorize procurement for Option B of the meter replacement program.

**Background and Financial Impact**
The review of the meter replacement program confirms that our aging analog meters are under-registering consumption by approximately 9%. This discrepancy results in an annual revenue loss of roughly $1.2M in unbilled water.

**Options Analysis**
We evaluated two approaches to address this loss:

*   **Option A (Full Swap):** Replace all 40,000 meters over 3 years. Total cost: $6.8M.
*   **Option B (Targeted Replacement):** Replace the 15,000 worst-performing meters over 18 months. Total cost: $2.4M.

**Recommendation**
We recommend **Option B**. This approach addresses the most significant sources of revenue loss immediately while requiring less than half the capital investment of a full swap. Data from the recent pilot program in the East Ward supports this strategy; targeted replacements there reduced leak detections by 22%.

**Critical Timing**
Immediate action is necessary to secure the vendor's warranty, which expires on **March 31, 2025**. Failure to place the order before this date will void coverage for the new units.

**Next Steps**
Upon your signature by March 14, we will initiate procurement. Note that the Finance Office has requested a minor update to the invoice coding structure, and the upcoming press release will incorporate details regarding the concurrent tariff review.
