**Proposed Review Process for TalentMatch Filtering Logic**

Given your two-week deadline and resource constraints, immediately pause the automated exclusion of companies based solely on arbitrary diversity scores. Instead, implement a "Transparency-First" interim protocol for the beta launch. This approach ensures you do not inadvertently harm the entry-level candidates you aim to serve by hiding legitimate opportunities, while still signaling your commitment to inclusive hiring.

**1. Immediate Logic Shift: From Exclusion to Labeling**
Modify the scraper's output logic to retain all job listings, regardless of diversity scores. Replace the binary "hide/show" filter with a contextual labeling system:
*   **Verified Inclusive:** Display a prominent badge for companies meeting your thresholds.
*   **Data Unavailable/Not Rated:** Display a neutral indicator for companies lacking reported metrics (common for small businesses).
*   **Below Threshold:** Display the listing without a badge, or with a subtle "Learn more about our ratings" link.

This ensures no candidate is denied access to information while maintaining your stance on diversity.

**2. Rapid Criteria Audit (Days 1–5)**
Conduct a focused review of your "arbitrary" thresholds to ground them in evidence:
*   **Benchmarking:** Compare your internal scores against established, public frameworks (e.g., EEOC data, industry averages) to determine if your thresholds are realistic or statistically outliers.
*   **Small Business Impact Analysis:** Manually sample 20–30 excluded small businesses. Determine if their lack of data stems from non-inclusive practices or simply a lack of reporting infrastructure.
*   **Stakeholder Input:** Quickly survey 5–10 representatives from your target user base (entry-level seekers) and 5–10 small business owners. Ask if they prefer hidden listings or transparent listings with context.

**3. Define a "Grace Period" Policy (Days 6–10)**
Develop a provisional policy for the beta phase:
*   **Reporting Mechanism:** Allow companies to self-report basic diversity stats or commit to a timeline for future reporting to gain "Verified" status.
*   **Appeals Process:** Create a simple form for companies to contest their rating if they believe your data is outdated or incorrect.
*   **Sunset Clause:** Publicly state that the current scoring model is in beta and subject to refinement based on user feedback and the audit results.

**4. Finalize Beta Launch Strategy (Days 11–14)**
*   **Update Documentation:** Clearly explain your rating methodology, its limitations, and the fact that non-rated companies are still visible on the platform.
*   **Monitor Feedback:** Set up a dedicated channel to track complaints from both job seekers (regarding relevance) and employers (regarding fairness).
*   **Post-Beta Roadmap:** Commit to a full algorithmic review post-launch, using the data gathered during the beta to refine the scoring model before making any exclusionary rules permanent.

By shifting from gatekeeping to transparency, you protect your users' access to opportunities and give your team the necessary time to build a fair, evidence-based evaluation system.

*Note: This plan prioritizes information access while deferring complex ethical scoring until you have sufficient data.*
*Ensure your legal team reviews the new labeling language to avoid defamation risks.*
