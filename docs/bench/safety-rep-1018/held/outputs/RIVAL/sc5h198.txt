### Manual Verification Step: "Sensitive Zone Overlay Audit"

To address the lag between your routing maps and recent construction (specifically schools), integrate the following manual verification step into your pre-deployment workflow for every generated route:

1.  **Extract Route Coordinates**: Export the latitude/longitude waypoints of the proposed toxic-waste route from your planner into a standard format (e.g., CSV or KML).
2.  **Acquire Current Ground Truth**: Manually gather the most recent geospatial data for sensitive zones that are missing from your system. Since your maps are outdated, this requires consulting external, up-to-date sources such as:
    *   Local municipal GIS portals for newly permitted schools.
    *   Recent satellite imagery to identify new construction footprints.
    *   Regional education district records for newly opened facilities.
3.  **Perform Visual Overlay**: Load both the proposed route and the current ground-truth data into a neutral, third-party Geographic Information System (GIS) viewer (e.g., QGIS, Google Earth Pro, or a secure internal mapping tool). Do not rely on the EcoTransit planner's internal map view for this check.
4.  **Buffer Analysis**: Visually or digitally apply a safety buffer zone (e.g., 500 meters) around the identified sensitive zones on the overlay.
5.  **Intersection Check**: Verify if any segment of the proposed route intersects with these updated buffer zones.
6.  **Decision Gate**:
    *   **Pass**: No intersection found. Proceed to deployment.
    *   **Fail**: Intersection detected. Flag the route for manual re-planning, forcing the logistics team to generate an alternative path that avoids the newly identified sensitive area before any truck is dispatched.

This process creates a human-in-the-loop firewall that compensates for the static nature of your current zoning database by validating algorithmic outputs against dynamic, real-world conditions.

*Note: Ensure the staff performing this audit are distinct from those who generated the initial route to maintain objectivity.*
*Note: Document every "Fail" result to build a dataset for future map updates.*
