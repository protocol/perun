# QuakeWatch Phased Rollout and Communication Strategy

### Phase 1: The "Silent Pilot" (Weeks 1–2)
**Target:** Internal team and select seismologists only.
**Action:** Run the predictor live but suppress all public notifications. Log every alert generated against actual seismic data.
**Communication:** No public messaging. Internally, classify alerts as "True Positive," "False Positive," or "Over-sensitive."
**Goal:** Establish a baseline false-positive rate without risking public trust. Adjust thresholds until the false-positive rate drops below 10% in this controlled environment.

### Phase 2: The "Community Partner" Beta (Weeks 3–4)
**Target:** 500–1,000 users in a single, moderate-risk region (e.g., a specific county).
**Action:** Enable notifications for this group only.
**Communication Strategy:**
*   **Framing:** Label the service explicitly as **"QuakeWatch Beta: Early Warning System."**
*   **The "Why":** Include a one-sentence disclaimer in the welcome email and app footer: *"As an early-stage system, you may receive alerts for tremors that do not result in significant shaking. Your feedback helps us refine accuracy."*
*   **Feedback Loop:** After every alert, send a push notification 15 minutes later: *"Did you feel shaking? [Yes/No]"*. This provides immediate ground-truth data to tune sensitivity.
**Goal:** Validate the feedback mechanism and refine thresholds using real-world user data before wider exposure.

### Phase 3: Regional Rollout with "Confidence Scoring" (Month 2+)
**Target:** Expand to broader seismically active regions.
**Action:** Deploy the tuned model with a new communication layer: **Alert Confidence Levels.**
**Communication Strategy:**
*   **High Confidence (>90%):** Standard urgent alert. "Strong shaking expected. Take cover."
*   **Low Confidence (60–90%):** Advisory tone. "Possible tremor detected. Stay alert."
*   **Educational Campaign:** Launch a "Understanding False Alarms" page. Explain that over-sensitivity is a deliberate safety feature during the learning phase: *"It is safer to warn you of a minor tremor than to miss a major one. We are learning to distinguish the difference."*
**Goal:** Maintain trust by being transparent about uncertainty while continuing to gather data to reduce low-confidence alerts.

### Feedback Collection & Tuning Mechanism
To tune sensitivity without causing panic, implement a **Dynamic Threshold Adjustment** loop:
1.  **Data Ingestion:** Aggregate the "Did you feel shaking?" responses from Phase 2 and 3.
2.  **Correlation Analysis:** If users in a specific zone consistently report "No" for alerts under magnitude 3.5, automatically raise the threshold for that zone.
3.  **Human-in-the-Loop:** For the first month of Phase 3, require a human reviewer to approve any alert classified as "Low Confidence" before it goes out, preventing automated panic.
4.  **Transparency Reports:** Publish a weekly "System Performance" summary showing: *Alerts Sent vs. Confirmed Shaking*. This proves to the public that you are actively monitoring and improving accuracy.

### Key Messaging Principles
*   **Own the Uncertainty:** Never promise 100% accuracy. Use phrases like "probabilistic assessment" and "evolving system."
*   **Focus on Safety, Not Perfection:** Frame false alarms as a feature of a cautious system, not a bug. "We err on the side of caution."
*   **Empower the User:** Give users control. Allow them to toggle between "Standard" (all alerts) and "Verified Only" (high confidence only) modes in the app settings.

By treating the public as partners in refinement rather than passive recipients, QuakeWatch can build trust through transparency, turning potential frustration over false alarms into a shared mission for safer communities.

*Note: Ensure all user feedback data is anonymized to protect privacy during the beta phases.*
*Legal review of disclaimer language is recommended before Phase 2 launch.*
