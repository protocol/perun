# ChainID Contingency Protocol & Communication Strategy

## Immediate Recovery Process (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1: Automated Detection & Triage**
When a user fails facial recognition three consecutive times within a 10-minute window, the system automatically flags the account as "Environmental Lockout Suspect" rather than "Security Breach." The user receives an immediate on-screen notification: *"Verification interrupted by lighting conditions. Your funds are secure. Initiating alternative recovery path."*

**Step 2: Multi-Modal Fallback Verification**
Trigger a secondary verification layer that does not rely on real-time facial matching:
- **Historical Selfie Comparison:** Request a new selfie but compare it against the user's last 5 successfully verified images (stored with timestamp/lighting metadata) rather than the original enrollment image. Use a lowered similarity threshold (e.g., 75% instead of 95%) for this specific fallback.
- **Challenge-Response Liveness:** Ask the user to perform a specific, random head movement (e.g., "Turn left and blink twice") to prevent photo spoofing while accommodating poor lighting.
- **Device Fingerprint Cross-Check:** Verify the login attempt originates from a previously trusted device ID and IP geolocation. If the device is known and the location is consistent, weight this heavily toward approval.

**Step 3: Time-Limited "Grace Access" Token**
If the fallback verification passes, issue a 24-hour limited-access token. This token allows:
- Viewing of all account balances and transaction history.
- Execution of withdrawals up to a conservative daily cap (e.g., 10% of total holdings or $5,000 USD equivalent).
- **Restriction:** No changes to security settings, no new device linking, and no transfers to new, unwhitelisted addresses.

**Step 4: Manual Review Escalation (High Value)**
For accounts exceeding the grace cap or failing the automated fallback:
- Route the case to a dedicated "Rapid Response" queue within your existing six-person team.
- Require the user to submit a short video statement holding a handwritten note with the current date and "ChainID Recovery" while stating their username.
- Assign one team member to manually compare this video against enrollment data. Aim for a 2-hour SLA for these reviews.

**Step 5: Post-Recovery Re-Enrollment**
Once access is restored, prompt the user (when they are in better lighting) to update their facial biometric profile with 3 new images under varied lighting conditions to prevent recurrence.

## User Communication Strategy

**Core Message Pillars:**
1.  **Security First:** "Your assets are never at risk; the lock is a feature, not a bug."
2.  **Temporary Friction:** "This is a momentary hurdle due to environmental factors, not a system failure."
3.  **Active Resolution:** "We have activated an emergency recovery lane specifically for you."

**Communication Channels & Templates:**

*   **In-App Alert (Immediate):**
    > "Lighting conditions prevented a confident match. For your security, access is paused. Click here to start our expedited recovery process. Your funds remain 100% secure."

*   **Email Notification (If email is verified):**
    > **Subject:** Action Required: Quick Verification Recovery for Your ChainID Account
    >
    > "We noticed you had trouble verifying your identity. This often happens due to lighting changes. We have unlocked a special recovery path for you. Please follow the link to verify via our secure fallback method. No funds are lost, and our team is standing by to assist personally if needed."

*   **Social Media/Status Page (Public Transparency):**
    > "ChainID is experiencing higher-than-normal verification friction due to lighting sensitivity updates. If you are affected, please use the 'Recovery Path' button in the app. Our team is manually reviewing cases every 2 hours. Funds are safe."

**Team Allocation for Launch Window:**
- **2 Members:** Monitor automated fallback success rates and adjust thresholds in real-time.
- **2 Members:** Handle manual video reviews (Rapid Response Queue).
- **1 Member:** Manage user communications (email responses, social media updates).
- **1 Member:** Oversee system stability and coordinate with the exchange listing partners to report status.

This protocol ensures zero permanent loss of access while maintaining a robust security posture acceptable for the exchange listing.

*Note: Ensure all manual review data is encrypted and deleted immediately after the case is resolved to maintain privacy compliance.*
*This plan assumes your current architecture supports storing historical selfie metadata and device fingerprinting.*
