### Data Validation Review and User Warning Strategy

**Current Process Gaps**
Your current validation process relies on a static weekly update cycle without an intermediate verification layer. Because the legacy system cannot support real-time API calls, the app currently presents stale data as fact. The primary failure point is the assumption that a location listed in the database is active at the moment of user arrival. Without a mechanism to flag locations as "unverified since [Date]" or to ingest rapid user feedback, the system inevitably directs users to closed or non-compliant sites.

**Immediate Mitigation: User Warnings**
To minimize risk immediately, you must alter the user interface to manage expectations regarding data freshness. Implement the following warnings on the location detail screen and the final navigation confirmation:

1.  **Freshness Banner:** Display a prominent notice at the top of every location card: *"Warning: Recycling center status was last verified on [Date]. Hours and acceptance policies may have changed."*
2.  **Mandatory Pre-Visit Check:** Add a bold instruction before the "Get Directions" button: *"Please call this facility to confirm they are currently accepting hazardous batteries before traveling."* Include a clickable phone number if available in your dataset.
3.  **Liability Disclaimer:** In the app's footer or terms of use, explicitly state that GreenCycle Co. provides directory information only and is not liable for trips made to locations that no longer accept specific waste types.

**Alternative Instructions for Users**
Since you cannot guarantee location accuracy, provide users with a safe fallback protocol if they arrive at a closed or non-compliant site:

*   **"Plan B" Guidance:** On the location screen, list general alternatives such as: *"If this location is closed, do not leave batteries unattended. Take them home and check our app again next week, or contact your local municipal waste authority for emergency drop-off options."*
*   **Unsafe Disposal Warning:** Explicitly warn users: *"Never dispose of lithium or hazardous batteries in regular trash bins or leave them at closed facilities, as this creates fire hazards and environmental risks."*

**Process Improvements for the Next Cycle**
While awaiting legacy system upgrades, your team should implement a low-tech validation loop:
*   **Crowdsourced Reporting:** Add a simple "Report Issue" button in the app allowing users to flag a location as "Closed," "No Longer Accepting Batteries," or "Hours Incorrect." Even without real-time API updates, your team can manually review these reports daily and temporarily suppress flagged locations from the weekly export.
*   **Targeted Verification:** Instead of verifying all locations weekly, rotate verification calls. Focus on high-traffic areas or locations with previous user reports.
*   **Data Aging Logic:** If a location has not been verbally confirmed within 30 days, automatically append a higher-risk warning label to that specific entry, distinguishing it from recently verified sites.

By shifting the burden of final verification to the user through clear warnings and providing safe alternative instructions, you can significantly reduce frustration and prevent unsafe disposal practices despite the technical constraints.

*Note: These measures rely on UI changes and content updates only; no backend integration is required. Prioritize the "Call First" warning as it offers the highest immediate risk reduction.*
