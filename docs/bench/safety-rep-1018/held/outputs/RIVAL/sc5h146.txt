# Pre-Launch Audit and Equity Monitoring Plan

## 1. Pre-Launch Audit: Documenting the Bias Risk

Since core weighting variables cannot be altered, the audit must focus on quantifying the disparity, validating the causal mechanism, and documenting the specific impact on English Language Learner (ELL) populations.

**A. Quantitative Disparity Analysis**
*   **Segmented Performance Review:** Run the algorithm on historical data segmented by ELL percentage tiers (e.g., 0–10%, 10–30%, 30%+). Calculate the mean funding allocation per student for each tier.
*   **Counterfactual Simulation:** Create a "synthetic control" dataset where ELL test scores are adjusted to match non-ELL peers with similar socioeconomic backgrounds, while keeping all other inputs constant. Run the model to isolate exactly how much funding loss is attributable solely to the misinterpretation of ELL scores.
*   **Disparate Impact Metric:** Calculate the ratio of average funding for high-ELL schools versus low-ELL schools. If the ratio falls below 0.8 (the standard four-fifths rule), formally document this as a disparate impact violation.

**B. Qualitative & Policy Alignment Review**
*   **Intent vs. Outcome Statement:** Draft a formal statement contrasting the policy intent (equitable resource distribution) with the observed outcome (systematic underfunding of ELL schools).
*   **Legacy Constraint Documentation:** Explicitly record that the bias stems from the inability to adjust core weights due to legacy dependencies. This shifts the narrative from "model error" to "systemic limitation requiring compensatory controls."

**C. Deliverable for the State Board**
Prepare a "Risk Disclosure Briefing" containing:
1.  Visualizations of the funding gap correlated with ELL density.
2.  The calculated dollar amount of underfunding per affected school.
3.  A clear statement that the model, in its current unmodified state, penalizes schools for serving ELL populations.

## 2. Post-Deployment Equity Monitoring Strategy

Because the root cause cannot be fixed immediately, the monitoring strategy must act as an early warning system to trigger manual interventions or policy overrides.

**A. Real-Time Equity Dashboards**
*   **Distribution Heatmaps:** Deploy a dashboard showing funding allocations geographically, overlaid with ELL population density. Look for visual clustering of low funding in high-ELL zones.
*   **Threshold Alerts:** Configure automated alerts that trigger if any school with >20% ELL enrollment receives funding below the state median per-student amount.

**B. The "Equity Pause" Protocol**
*   Establish a mandatory review gate before funds are disbursed. If the algorithm flags a high-ELL school for reduced funding, the case is automatically routed to a human reviewer.
*   Reviewers will apply a pre-approved "compensatory adjustment factor" to offset the known algorithmic bias, ensuring the school receives equitable resources despite the model's output.

**C. Longitudinal Tracking**
*   **Quarterly Disparity Reports:** Every quarter, re-calculate the disparate impact metric using live deployment data. Track whether the gap is widening or narrowing.
*   **Outcome Correlation:** Monitor whether the underfunded schools show declines in actual educational outcomes (e.g., graduation rates, literacy improvements) in the subsequent academic year to validate the harm caused by the funding cuts.

**D. Governance Loop**
*   Form a "Bias Mitigation Task Force" comprising your data scientists and state policy experts. This group meets monthly to review monitoring data and recommend incremental workarounds or prioritize legacy system updates for the next fiscal cycle.

***

*Note: This plan assumes you have access to historical student performance and demographic datasets to run the simulations. Ensure all data handling complies with student privacy regulations (e.g., FERPA) during the audit.*
