### Verification Workflow for Form Library

**1. Source Identification and Scheduling**
Designate one team member as the "Compliance Lead" for each target jurisdiction. This individual must manually visit the official government immigration portal (e.g., USCIS, IRCC, Home Office) on a fixed schedule: weekly for high-volume forms (work visas, family sponsorship) and monthly for low-volume forms. Create a shared tracking spreadsheet logging the URL, form number, edition date, and revision date found on the official site.

**2. The "Three-Point" Manual Check**
For every form in your library, the Compliance Lead must perform a three-point visual comparison against the official PDF:
*   **Edition Date:** Verify the date located at the bottom left of the official form matches your stored version.
*   **Form Number:** Ensure the alphanumeric code (e.g., I-130, IM5708) is identical.
*   **Barcode/QR Structure:** Visually confirm the presence and placement of any 2D barcodes, as these often change between versions even if the text looks similar.

**3. Content Spot-Check**
Do not re-read the entire legal text. Instead, scan the "Instructions" page header and the first three questions. Government agencies often update guidance notes or question phrasing slightly while keeping the form number static. If any discrepancy is found in these areas, flag the entire form version as "Suspect" until a full line-by-line review is completed.

**4. Library Quarantine Protocol**
If a mismatch is detected:
*   Immediately move the digital asset to a "Quarantine" folder in your content management system.
*   Update the system status to "Unavailable" so users cannot select it.
*   Log the discrepancy in your tracking sheet with a timestamp and the name of the reviewer.

### Contingency Plan for Mid-Process Version Changes

**1. User Notification Trigger**
If a form version changes while a user has an active, incomplete application in your system:
*   **Immediate Banner:** Display a high-visibility alert on the user's dashboard upon their next login: *"Important Update: The government has released a new version of Form [Number]. Your current draft may be invalid."*
*   **Email Alert:** Send an automated email with the subject line: *"Action Required: Visa Form Update for [Application ID]."*

**2. Data Migration Strategy**
*   **Minor Updates (Typos/Instruction only):** If the government confirms the old form is still accepted for a grace period, allow the user to finish but display a warning recommending the new version.
*   **Major Updates (Field changes):** If fields have been added, removed, or renumbered, the system must prevent submission of the old version. Provide a "Migrate Data" button that attempts to map answers from the old draft to the new form fields. Clearly highlight any fields that could not be mapped automatically for manual user review.

**3. Grace Period Communication**
Include a standardized disclaimer in the notification explaining the government's grace period policy (if known). If no grace period exists, state clearly: *"Applications submitted with the previous version after [Date] will be rejected by the government."*

**4. Audit Trail**
Maintain a log of every user notified, the time of notification, and whether they migrated to the new form or abandoned the process. This data is critical for liability protection and demonstrating due diligence to stakeholders.

*Note: Since your team lacks API access, rely strictly on the "Edition Date" printed on official PDFs as the single source of truth; do not trust third-party legal blogs for version numbers.*
*Ensure all manual checks are double-signed by a second developer before marking a form as "Verified" in your system.*
