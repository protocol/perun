### Manual Audit Plan for Educational Bias at Apex Talent

**1. Immediate Suspension of Automated Filtering**
Halt the automatic rejection rule regarding community college attendance immediately. Until the audit is complete, route all applications flagged by this criterion to a manual review queue. This prevents further loss of qualified candidates during the assessment period.

**2. Retrospective Data Sampling**
Since new software cannot be purchased, utilize your existing Applicant Tracking System (ATS) or spreadsheet records to create a control group.
*   **Extract Data:** Pull records for the last 500 applicants for entry-level roles.
*   **Categorize:** Create two columns: "Education Path" (Community College vs. Four-Year University) and "Outcome" (Rejected by Filter, Rejected by Human, Interviewed, Hired).
*   **Isolate Variables:** Identify candidates who were rejected *solely* based on the community college flag. Exclude those rejected for lack of experience or skills mismatches unrelated to education.

**3. The "Blind Re-evaluation" Protocol**
To test the quality of the filtered-out candidates without bias:
*   **Select a Sample:** Randomly choose 30–50 resumes previously rejected due to the community college filter.
*   **Redact Education:** Remove all mentions of specific schools, degrees, and graduation dates from these resumes. Keep only work history, projects, certifications, and skills.
*   **Manual Review:** Have two senior engineers or recruiters independently score these anonymized resumes based strictly on role requirements (e.g., coding ability, project relevance). Use a simple 1–5 scale.
*   **Comparison:** Compare these scores against the average scores of candidates who passed the initial filter. If the "rejected" group scores similarly to the "accepted" group, the filter is discarding viable talent.

**4. Impact Calculation**
Calculate the "False Negative Rate" to present to leadership:
*   *Formula:* (Number of blind-reviewed community college candidates deemed "Interview Ready") / (Total number of community college candidates reviewed).
*   *Diversity Check:* If demographic data is legally permissible and available in your records, cross-reference the rejected pool to see if the filter disproportionately affects specific underrepresented groups. This quantifies the diversity impact.

**5. Revised Criteria Proposal**
Based on the findings, draft a new policy:
*   **Replace Degree Requirements:** Shift from "Must have a 4-year degree" to "Must demonstrate proficiency in [Specific Skill] via portfolio, assessment, or equivalent experience."
*   **Skills-Based Screening:** Implement a brief, standardized technical task or work sample review as the first hurdle for all applicants, regardless of education background. This ensures objective measurement of capability.

**6. Pre-Drive Validation**
Before the major recruitment drive next month:
*   Run a pilot on the next 50 incoming applications using the new "skills-first" approach.
*   Track the time-to-screen and the quality of candidates moving to the interview stage.
*   Document the cost savings (time spent) and the increase in pool diversity to justify the permanent removal of the educational filter.

This process requires no external tools, only internal data access and staff time, fitting your budget constraints while addressing the ethical and operational risks of the current system.

*Note: Ensure all data handling complies with local labor laws and privacy regulations regarding candidate information.*
*Document every step of the blind review to protect the team from claims of inconsistent hiring practices.*
