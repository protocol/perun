**Immediate Manual Override Protocols for Childcare Matching Launch**

Given your ten-day timeline and resource constraints, implement the following three-tier manual fallback process to handle uncertain automated ID verifications. This ensures safety without halting operations.

**1. The "Hold-and-Human" Triage Queue**
Configure your system to automatically flag any application where the government database returns a "no match," "partial match," or times out. Do not reject these applicants immediately; move them to a "Pending Manual Review" status visible only to your internal team.
*   **Action:** Assign one developer to act as the rotating "Safety Officer" for the launch period. Their sole task is to review flagged profiles before any parent can see them.
*   **Protocol:** The Safety Officer must manually cross-reference the uploaded digital ID photo with a live video call (recorded) with the applicant. They must verify that the name and ID number on the document match the government portal's public search interface (if available) or request a secondary government-issued document (e.g., passport or driver's license) via a secure upload link.

**2. The "Two-Reference" Mandatory Gate**
For any caregiver whose automated verification is uncertain, bypass the single-source dependency by enforcing a strict human reference check before activation.
*   **Action:** Require the applicant to provide two non-family references who have previously employed them for childcare.
*   **Protocol:** The Safety Officer must physically call (no emails or texts) both references using a standardized script. The script must confirm: (a) the identity of the caller matches the applicant, (b) the duration of previous employment, and (c) a explicit "yes/no" on whether they would rehire the individual for childcare. If either reference cannot be reached or hesitates, the application remains suspended.

**3. The "Probationary First Match" Restriction**
If an applicant passes the manual video check and reference calls but the digital ID uncertainty remains unresolved due to database limitations, allow activation only under a "Probationary" label.
*   **Action:** Limit these caregivers to a single, short-duration trial job (e.g., max 2 hours) with a parent who has explicitly opted into the "Verified Plus" program (parents willing to accept a slightly higher risk for faster availability).
*   **Protocol:** The platform must mandate that the parent meets the caregiver at a public location or a community center for the first 15 minutes before the job proceeds to the home. The platform retains the right to immediately deactivate the caregiver if the parent reports any discrepancy in identity upon meeting.

**Documentation Requirement**
Create a simple internal log (a shared, access-controlled spreadsheet is sufficient for now) recording every manual override: the applicant's name, the specific automated failure reason, the name of the Safety Officer who performed the check, the time of the reference calls, and the final decision. This creates an audit trail should any issues arise post-launch.

*Note: These protocols rely entirely on your existing team's time; ensure the "Safety Officer" role rotates daily to prevent developer burnout during the launch sprint.*
*Do not store raw images of IDs in unsecured channels; use your existing encrypted upload infrastructure for all manual evidence.*
