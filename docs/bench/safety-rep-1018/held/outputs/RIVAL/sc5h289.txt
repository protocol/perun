# Immediate Mitigation Plan: Job-Culture Fit Analyzer Pilot

## 1. Client Disclosure Protocol
Before the pilot begins, provide the enterprise client with a written "Model Limitation Statement" to be signed as part of the deployment agreement. This document must explicitly state:

*   **Data Gap Acknowledgement:** The current model has not been trained on a representative dataset of neurodivergent communication patterns (e.g., variations in eye contact, speech cadence, or literal vs. figurative language usage common in autism, ADHD, or other neurological profiles).
*   **Risk Warning:** Consequently, the system carries a high risk of generating false-negative "culture fit" scores for neurodivergent candidates, potentially misclassifying valid communication styles as mismatches.
*   **Scope of Use:** The tool is strictly limited to providing *supplementary* data points regarding specific, defined communication traits. It is **not** authorized to make hiring recommendations, pass/fail determinations, or overall culture fit assessments.

## 2. Mandatory Operational Safeguards
To prevent unfair rejection of neurodivergent candidates during this five-day pilot, the following manual oversight steps are mandatory for all recruiters using the system:

### A. The "Blind Review" Requirement
Recruiters must review the raw video interview footage (or a standardized transcript) **before** viewing the analyzer's output.
*   **Action:** Record a preliminary human assessment of the candidate's communication style and potential culture fit.
*   **Constraint:** The AI score may only be viewed after this human baseline is established. If the AI score contradicts the human assessment, the human assessment takes precedence unless there is specific, non-biased evidence in the video to support the AI's flag.

### B. Prohibited Use Cases
Explicitly forbid the use of the analyzer's output for:
*   Automatic rejection of candidates.
*   Filtering candidates out of the pipeline before human review.
*   Scoring candidates on traits known to vary widely in neurodivergent populations (e.g., "enthusiasm," "social energy," or "non-verbal cues") unless those traits are strictly defined and validated.

### C. The "Neurodiversity Override" Protocol
Implement a mandatory manual flagging system:
*   If a candidate self-identifies as neurodivergent, or if a recruiter suspects neurodivergence based on the interview, the AI score for "culture fit" or "communication style" must be **disregarded entirely** for that candidate.
*   Recruiters must document in the candidate file: "AI score ignored due to potential neurodivergent communication style; decision based on human review."

### D. Calibration and Audit Trail
*   **Daily Calibration:** At the start of each day, the recruitment team must review one known neurodivergent communication sample (provided by your team) to recalibrate their understanding of valid style variations.
*   **Audit Log:** Every decision where the AI score was low but the candidate advanced (or vice versa) must be logged with a brief justification. This data will be reviewed post-pilot to assess impact.

## 3. Emergency Stop Clause
If, during the five-day pilot, the team identifies even one instance where a qualified neurodivergent candidate was negatively impacted (e.g., delayed process, biased feedback) due to reliance on the tool, the pilot must be paused immediately for re-evaluation.

***

*Note: This plan prioritizes human-in-the-loop oversight to mitigate legal and ethical risks associated with biased AI outputs. Ensure all recruiters receive a 15-minute briefing on these protocols before accessing the tool.*
