### Rapid Appeals and Review Workflow for QuickBite Lagos

**1. Triage and Action Thresholds**
To balance fraud prevention with driver livelihoods, the system will operate on a two-tier response model based on evidence confidence:

*   **Temporary Hold (Low/Medium Confidence):** Triggered by isolated anomalies (e.g., single unusual order pattern, minor GPS discrepancy).
    *   *Action:* Account is paused for 4 hours maximum. Driver receives an immediate SMS with a unique link to upload context (e.g., photo of receipt, explanation).
    *   *Review:* A human reviewer assesses the uploaded context against the flag. If the explanation is plausible, the ban is lifted instantly. If no response is received within 4 hours, the hold converts to a formal investigation.
*   **Automatic Ban (High Confidence):** Triggered only by coordinated indicators (e.g., multiple accounts sharing device IDs, impossible travel speeds combined with completed orders, known fraud ring patterns).
    *   *Action:* Immediate suspension. Driver receives an SMS stating the specific reason for the ban and a direct link to the "Emergency Appeals" portal.
    *   *Review:* These cases bypass standard queues and enter a "Critical Review" lane staffed by senior investigators.

**2. The Rapid Human Reinvestigation Process**
Given the two-week pilot and limited team size, the review process prioritizes speed and fairness:

*   **Step 1: Automated Notification (Instant):** Upon flagging, the driver receives a clear notification detailing *what* triggered the flag (without revealing detection logic) and a deadline for response (2 hours for Holds, 24 hours for Bans).
*   **Step 2: The "One-Click" Appeal Portal:** Drivers access a mobile-optimized form to submit counter-evidence. Accepted formats include geotagged photos, screenshots of customer chats, or voice notes explaining the situation. This lowers barriers for drivers with limited data plans or literacy challenges.
*   **Step 3: Tiered Human Review (SLA: <2 Hours):**
    *   *Junior Reviewers:* Handle "Temporary Holds." They check if the driver's submitted evidence contradicts the automated flag. If the evidence creates reasonable doubt, the account is reinstated immediately.
    *   *Senior Investigators:* Handle "Automatic Bans" and failed Hold appeals. They cross-reference the driver's history, check for correlations with other flagged accounts (to identify false positives from ring-detection algorithms), and make a final binding decision.
*   **Step 4: Resolution and Feedback:**
    *   *Reinstatement:* If cleared, the driver receives an apology, a small credit for downtime, and immediate access restoration.
    *   *Upheld Ban:* If the ban stands, the driver receives a detailed breakdown of the unrebutted evidence and instructions for a secondary, manual review by an external ombudsman (to ensure due process).

**3. Evidence Threshold Definitions**
*   **For Automatic Ban:** Requires two or more correlated high-risk signals (e.g., Device ID match with a known fraudster + 500% average order value spike) OR one undeniable proof of coordination (e.g., identical bank details across 3+ driver accounts).
*   **For Temporary Hold:** Requires a single moderate-risk signal (e.g., GPS drift, first-time high-value order) or a behavioral outlier that lacks corroborating fraud indicators.

**4. Pilot Safeguards**
During the two-week pilot, any driver banned automatically is eligible for a mandatory "Second Look" by a different senior investigator within 12 hours, regardless of their appeal status. This ensures the algorithm's error rate is captured and corrected before the holiday surge.

*Note: This workflow assumes the existence of an SMS gateway and a basic web form for evidence submission. Ensure all reviewers are trained specifically on local Lagos delivery contexts to avoid cultural misunderstandings.*
