# Pre-Launch Verification Protocol: Critical Facility List Validation

**Objective:** Identify and correct outdated entries in the critical facility database within four weeks to ensure accurate valve control during drought rationing.

### Phase 1: Data Triangulation (Week 1)
Cross-reference the existing six-month-old database against three independent, high-frequency data sources to flag discrepancies:
1.  **Utility Connection Records:** Compare facility addresses against the latest water meter installation and active service connection logs from the billing department. New facilities usually have active meters before appearing in master asset lists.
2.  **Emergency Services Dispatch Logs:** Request a list of all dispatch calls from the fire and medical coordination center for the past six months. Any dispatch location not in the current database is a priority candidate for a new or unregistered facility.
3.  **Building Permit & Occupancy Certificates:** Review municipal building department records for "Certificate of Occupancy" issuances for healthcare, emergency response, and large-scale residential structures issued in the last seven months.

### Phase 2: Targeted Field Verification (Weeks 2–3)
Deploy a rapid verification team to physically inspect only the "flagged" locations and a randomized 10% sample of existing entries.
*   **Protocol for Flagged Sites:** Verify physical existence, operational status, and critical classification (e.g., is it a full hospital or a closed clinic?). GPS-tag the entrance and main water intake valve.
*   **Protocol for Existing High-Risk Sites:** Re-verify coordinates for facilities near recent construction zones where access roads or infrastructure may have shifted.
*   **Stakeholder Direct Confirmation:** Email/phone the facility managers of all identified critical sites with a "Confirm Your Status" form requiring them to validate their address, criticality level, and primary contact within 48 hours. Non-respondents trigger an immediate site visit.

### Phase 3: System Shadow Mode & Logic Test (Week 4)
Before activating automatic valve closures, run the system in "Shadow Mode" for seven days.
*   **Simulation:** Feed real-time drought triggers into the system but disable actual valve actuation.
*   **Verification:** Generate the hypothetical "Closure List." Manually review this list against the updated database to ensure no critical facility appears on the restriction list.
*   **Fail-Safe Check:** Confirm that any facility marked as "Unverified" or "Data Conflict" defaults to an "Open/Protected" state rather than a "Closed" state.

### Phase 4: Final Sign-Off (Days 1–2 before launch)
*   **Freeze Database:** Lock the critical facility list. Any new discoveries after this point must be handled via manual override procedures, not automated updates.
*   **Emergency Override Drill:** Conduct one live test where a manual "Open All" command is sent to ensure human operators can bypass automation instantly if a critical facility is incorrectly targeted.

**Deliverable:** A validated, GPS-confirmed list of critical facilities with a "Confidence Score" for each entry, and a documented manual override procedure for any anomalies discovered post-launch.

*Note: Prioritize false negatives (missing a new fire station) over false positives (temporarily protecting a non-critical site), as the latter is easily corrected manually while the former poses immediate safety risks.*
