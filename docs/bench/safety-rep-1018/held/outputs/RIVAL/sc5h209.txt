### Comprehensive Safety Review Plan for TerraStable

**1. Data-Driven Sampling Optimization**
Conduct a retrospective analysis of historical landslide data specific to your target mountainous regions. Identify the precursory signals (micro-tremors, soil moisture spikes, tilt changes) that typically precede rapid onset events.
*   **Action:** Correlate these precursors with your current aggressive sleep cycles to determine the maximum allowable sleep duration that still captures the earliest warning signs.
*   **Goal:** Shift from fixed-interval sleeping to "risk-adaptive" sleeping. During high-risk weather windows (e.g., heavy rainfall forecasts), manually or remotely command sensors to enter a high-frequency monitoring mode, accepting higher battery drain for critical periods.

**2. Edge-Logic Threshold Tuning**
Since hardware cannot be replaced, optimize the firmware logic to maximize the utility of every wake-up cycle.
*   **Action:** Review the current trigger thresholds. If sensors wake up only when a hard limit is breached, lower these thresholds to capture "near-miss" data that indicates accelerating instability.
*   **Goal:** Implement a "wake-on-anomaly" protocol where a minor detection triggers an immediate secondary check within seconds, rather than returning to sleep. This creates a virtual high-frequency sampling burst only when necessary, preserving battery during stable periods.

**3. Networked Triangulation Protocol**
Leverage the network effect to compensate for individual sensor blind spots.
*   **Action:** Develop a protocol where a warning from one sensor forces neighboring sensors to wake up and verify conditions, even if those neighbors are in their sleep cycle.
*   **Goal:** Reduce the reliance on single-sensor detection rates. If Sensor A detects a potential slide, it alerts the network, causing Sensors B and C to activate and confirm the event, significantly reducing false negatives caused by sleep cycles.

**4. Hybrid Human-in-the-Loop Verification**
Integrate low-tech verification to bridge the gap during the highest risk windows.
*   **Action:** Establish a protocol with local government planners to conduct visual inspections or deploy temporary, powered monitoring stations in known high-risk zones during peak monsoon forecasts.
*   **Goal:** Use human observation or temporary high-power devices as a "safety net" during the specific hours when battery constraints make automated detection most vulnerable.

**5. Failure Mode Simulation**
Before launch, simulate the worst-case battery depletion scenarios.
*   **Action:** Run software simulations using recorded rapid-onset event data against your proposed sleep/wake algorithms.
*   **Goal:** Quantify the exact probability of missed detection under various weather conditions. Use this data to define clear "Red Zones" where the system explicitly warns users that detection confidence is lowered due to power-saving modes, managing community expectations transparently.

**6. Emergency Communication Drills**
Since technical perfection is constrained by battery life, procedural robustness must be higher.
*   **Action:** Design evacuation drills that assume the system *might* miss the initial signal. Focus on community response to secondary indicators (e.g., unusual sounds, visible cracks) rather than waiting solely for an automated alert.
*   **Goal:** Ensure that the "last mile" of warning does not rely 100% on the sensor network, creating a resilient safety culture that accounts for technical limitations.

**Note:** Prioritize firmware updates that allow remote adjustment of sleep schedules immediately upon deployment.
