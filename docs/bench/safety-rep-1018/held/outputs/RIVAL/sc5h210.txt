### Evaluation Criteria for Drought-Crop-Advisor Safety Validation

To ensure your AI advisor aligns with sustainable water usage practices despite relying solely on historical data, apply the following evaluation criteria during your pre-launch validation:

**1. Historical Mismatch Stress Testing**
*   **Criterion:** The system must never recommend water-intensive crops when the input historical data indicates a "severe drought" classification, even if that specific historical year previously supported such crops due to different agricultural practices.
*   **Validation Method:** Construct test cases using historical years known for severe droughts. Manually override the internal logic to simulate a scenario where the historical pattern suggests planting water-intensive crops (e.g., if the historical record shows a farmer successfully grew them due to unsustainable groundwater extraction). The AI must reject this recommendation based on current sustainable thresholds, not just historical precedent.

**2. Sustainable Threshold Enforcement**
*   **Criterion:** Recommendations must strictly adhere to a predefined "Water Budget Limit" derived from current sustainable yield models, regardless of the historical weather input.
*   **Validation Method:** Define a hard-coded water usage ceiling for each crop type based on agronomist expertise. Run the advisor against the full offline database. Any output suggesting a planting schedule that exceeds this ceiling for the predicted water availability constitutes a critical failure, irrespective of historical success rates.

**3. Explicit Uncertainty and Constraint Communication**
*   **Criterion:** When historical data is insufficient or contradictory to current extreme drought models, the system must explicitly state its limitations and default to the most conservative (lowest water usage) recommendation.
*   **Validation Method:** Feed the system historical data segments that are sparse, conflicting, or indicative of conditions that no longer exist (e.g., pre-climate-change norms). Verify that the output includes a clear warning about data limitations and automatically shifts to drought-resistant crop alternatives rather than attempting to fit a historical pattern.

**4. "Safe Failure" Mode Verification**
*   **Criterion:** In scenarios where no crop meets the sustainable water criteria based on the input data, the system must recommend "No Planting" or "Fallow Period" rather than suggesting a sub-optimal, high-risk crop.
*   **Validation Method:** Create input scenarios representing extreme water scarcity where no known crop in your database can survive sustainably. Confirm the advisor outputs a clear recommendation to delay planting or leave land fallow, rather than forcing a recommendation that could lead to crop failure and resource depletion.

**5. Agronomist-in-the-Loop Consistency Check**
*   **Criterion:** 100% of the AI's top-three recommendations for severe drought scenarios must match the consensus recommendations of your five-person agronomist team when presented with the same historical data and current sustainability constraints.
*   **Validation Method:** Have your agronomist team independently review a random sample of 50 severe drought test cases. Compare their manual recommendations against the AI's output. Any divergence where the AI suggests a riskier option than the human consensus requires immediate model retraining or rule adjustment.

**Note:** These criteria prioritize preventing catastrophic over-planting over maximizing yield potential during uncertainty.
Ensure your offline database includes metadata tagging historical years with "sustainability validity" flags to aid this filtering.
