### Pre-Launch Evaluation Plan for TalentScout Predictive Hiring Algorithm

**1. Bias Quantification & Disparity Analysis**
Since new training data is unavailable, the evaluation must rigorously stress-test the existing fixed dataset to quantify the extent of the bias.
*   **Stratified Performance Review:** Segment the historical dataset by university endowment tiers (e.g., top 10%, middle 50%, bottom 40%) and non-traditional background markers. Calculate the acceptance rate and average ranking score for qualified candidates (those with high skill-relevance scores) within each segment.
*   **False Negative Audit:** Specifically identify "high-skill/low-endowment" candidates who were ranked low by the algorithm. Document the percentage of these candidates who would have been rejected compared to "low-skill/high-endowment" candidates. This creates a concrete metric of discrimination (e.g., "The model rejects 40% of qualified non-traditional candidates while accepting 85% of less-qualified traditional candidates").
*   **Feature Importance Correlation:** Run a correlation analysis between university endowment size and final ranking scores, controlling for skill-set relevance. If endowment shows a higher correlation with the output than actual skills, this is definitive proof of the bias mechanism.

**2. Mitigation Strategies for Initial Deployment**
Given the constraint of a fixed model and dataset, mitigation must occur at the *output* or *process* level rather than through retraining.
*   **Post-Processing Re-ranking (Fairness Constraints):** Implement a deterministic rule-based layer on top of the algorithm's output. For every batch of recommendations, enforce a quota or threshold ensuring a minimum representation of candidates from non-traditional backgrounds who meet a defined "minimum skill competency" score. This overrides the biased ranking without altering the underlying model weights.
*   **Blinded University Metadata:** If the system architecture allows, suppress the "university endowment" or specific "university name" features from the final presentation layer presented to human recruiters. Present only the skill-match score and experience summary, forcing human review to rely on competency rather than the algorithm's biased prestige signal.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop "Second Look" Protocol:** Mandate a manual review for any candidate from a non-traditional background who scores above a specific skill threshold but is ranked below the top tier by the algorithm. This creates a safety net for high-potential candidates the model currently undervalues.

**3. Client Transparency & Reporting Package**
Prepare a "Known Limitations & Mitigation" dossier for the client to ensure ethical deployment.
*   **Disparity Impact Statement:** Present the quantified metrics from Step 1 clearly, acknowledging the historical bias in the training data. Frame this as a proactive discovery rather than a hidden flaw.
*   **Mitigation Roadmap:** Detail the post-processing rules and human-review protocols being activated immediately upon launch. Explicitly state that the raw algorithmic score is *not* the sole decision factor.
*   **Monitoring Commitment:** Propose a 30-day post-launch audit where the client tracks the demographic and educational diversity of the shortlisted candidates against the baseline metrics established in your testing. This demonstrates a commitment to continuous improvement despite the initial data constraints.

By focusing on output-level corrections and transparent reporting, TalentScout can deploy the tool while actively preventing the automation of historical discrimination.

*Note: This plan relies entirely on analyzing the existing fixed dataset and adjusting the decision workflow, as no new data ingestion is possible before the deadline.*
*Ensure all "non-traditional background" definitions are agreed upon with legal/compliance teams before running the stratified analysis.*
