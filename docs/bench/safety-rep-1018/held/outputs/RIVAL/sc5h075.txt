### Strategic Workflow for Manual Intervention and User Communication

**Phase 1: Detection and Triage (Support Team)**
1.  **Monitor Cancellation Logs:** Establish a daily review of policy cancellations triggered by "restricted zone" flags. Cross-reference the flagged location against a static, pre-approved list of recently renamed countries (e.g., checking if "Country X" was flagged due to a new geopolitical designation that matches a renamed nation).
2.  **Verify Traveler Itinerary:** For flagged cases, manually verify the traveler's actual destination using their submitted itinerary or booking confirmation. Confirm that the destination is safe and covered under standard policy terms, despite the system's geopolitical flag.
3.  **Prioritize by Imminence:** Triage cases based on travel start dates. Immediate departures (within 48 hours) receive priority handling to minimize coverage gaps.

**Phase 2: Manual Remediation**
1.  **Policy Reinstatement:** Authorized support agents will manually override the cancellation status in the admin portal. This involves selecting the specific policy ID, choosing the "Administrative Reinstatement" reason code, and setting the effective date back to the original cancellation timestamp to ensure no coverage gap exists on record.
2.  **Data Tagging:** Apply a temporary internal tag to these accounts (e.g., `GEO_RENAME_REVIEW`) to prevent automated re-cancellation if the system runs subsequent batch updates before the rollout.
3.  **Confirmation Log:** Record the intervention in the customer's file, noting the specific geopolitical discrepancy for post-rollout analysis by the actuarial team.

**Phase 3: User-Facing Communication**
Do not disclose system defects, database limitations, or naming errors. Frame the situation as a routine, high-priority security verification that has now been successfully completed.

**Template for Email/SMS Notification:**

> **Subject: Important Update Regarding Your Travel Coverage Status**
>
> Dear [Traveler Name],
>
> As part of WanderSafe's commitment to your safety, our system continuously monitors global geopolitical conditions to ensure your coverage aligns with the latest international travel advisories.
>
> Recent updates to international region classifications triggered an automated review of your policy. While our initial protocols paused coverage as a precautionary measure, our specialist team has completed a manual verification of your specific itinerary.
>
> We are pleased to confirm that your destination remains fully covered under your current plan. Your policy has been actively reinstated effective immediately, with no interruption to your benefits. No further action is required on your part.
>
> Thank you for trusting WanderSafe to protect your journey.
>
> Sincerely,
> The WanderSafe Support Team

**Operational Notes:**
*   Ensure the "Administrative Reinstatement" reason code does not trigger downstream billing alerts or refund processes that might confuse the customer.
*   Maintain the static list of renamed countries in a shared, read-only document for the support team to ensure consistent triage until the global rollout.
