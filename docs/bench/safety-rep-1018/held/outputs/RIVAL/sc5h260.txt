### Pre-Launch Price Anomaly Monitoring Dashboard

**Dashboard View: Real-Time Rate Integrity Monitor**

**1. Primary Alert Panel (Top Row)**
*   **Metric:** "Active Price Gouging Incidents"
*   **Visualization:** Large red counter displaying the number of active quotes exceeding the defined threshold.
*   **Trigger Logic:** Alert fires immediately when `Generated Rate` > (`Regional Average Base Rate` × 1.5) for any quote originating from a designated "High-Tourist Zone."
*   **Action:** Clicking the counter drills down to the specific transaction IDs.

**2. Live Rate vs. Market Baseline Chart (Center)**
*   **Visualization:** Dual-line time-series graph.
    *   *Line A (Blue):* Rolling 15-minute average of standard regional rates (static baseline).
    *   *Line B (Orange):* Real-time average of generated rates for high-tourist zones.
*   **Shaded Zone:** A red background highlights the area where Line B exceeds 150% of Line A.
*   **Purpose:** Provides immediate visual confirmation of divergence trends without needing to alter the underlying algorithm.

**3. Geographic Heat Map (Right Panel)**
*   **Visualization:** Map of Sydney with hotspots overlay.
*   **Data Points:** Glowing red circles indicate specific GPS coordinates where exorbitant rates are currently being generated.
*   **Tooltip Data:** Hovering reveals the specific `Vehicle ID`, `Demand Multiplier` applied, and the `Final Quote Price`.

**4. Transaction Log & Alert Feed (Bottom Panel)**
*   **Format:** Scrolling table of the top 10 most severe anomalies in the last hour.
*   **Columns:** Timestamp, User Location Zone, Vehicle Availability %, Calculated Rate, Deviation % from Baseline.
*   **Status Column:** Flags entries as "Unverified" until an operator acknowledges them.

**Operational Workflow**
Since the algorithm's location weighting factors cannot be changed until next quarter, this dashboard serves as a manual intervention trigger. When the "Active Price Gouging Incidents" counter rises:
1.  The operations team identifies the specific vehicle classes and zones involved via the Heat Map.
2.  Operators manually flag these specific inventory items as "Unavailable" in the rental management system to prevent booking at gouged rates.
3.  Customer service is alerted to honor quoted rates only if they fall below the threshold, or to offer manual overrides for affected users.

**Configuration Requirements**
*   **Baseline Definition:** The "Regional Average" must be hardcoded as a static configuration file updated weekly, independent of the dynamic pricing engine, to ensure the monitoring logic itself is not influenced by the gouging algorithm.
*   **Zone Tagging:** Ensure all incoming user location data is instantly tagged as "High-Tourist" or "Standard" based on a fixed geofence list before entering the pricing logic, allowing the dashboard to filter specifically for the problematic zones.

This approach isolates the monitoring layer from the rigid pricing engine, allowing your team to detect and manually mitigate exorbitant charges without violating the data provider contract or requiring code changes to the core algorithm.

*Note: This plan assumes your logging infrastructure can emit real-time events for every quote generation. If latency exists, the "Live" chart may need to be adjusted to a "Near Real-Time" (1-2 minute delay) view.*
