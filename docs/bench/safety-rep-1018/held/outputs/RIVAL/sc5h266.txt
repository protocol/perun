### Inclusive Language Safety Plan for Job-Description Writer

**1. Immediate Baseline Audit (Days 1–2)**
*   **Static Pattern Review:** Compile a comprehensive list of known gender-coded terms (e.g., "ninja," "dominate," "supportive," "sensitive") and ability-biased phrasing. Cross-reference your current generation logs against this list to quantify the frequency and context of biased outputs.
*   **Prompt Template Inspection:** Manually review the system prompts and few-shot examples provided to the model. Identify any inherited biases in the instructions or sample job descriptions that may be steering the model toward non-inclusive language.

**2. Dynamic Adversarial Testing (Days 3–4)**
*   **Synthetic Role Generation:** Create a diverse set of 50 synthetic job roles spanning various industries, seniority levels, and technical requirements. Ensure these cover stereotypes prone to bias (e.g., nursing vs. engineering, leadership vs. support roles).
*   **Automated Scoring Pipeline:** Run these synthetic roles through the writer. Implement an automated evaluation step using a secondary AI instance or a rule-based scorer specifically instructed to flag gendered, ageist, or ableist language.
*   **Edge Case Probing:** Test the system with ambiguous or conflicting requirements to see if it defaults to stereotypical assumptions when guidance is unclear.

**3. Remediation and Guardrail Implementation (Day 5)**
*   **Negative Constraints:** Update the system prompt to explicitly forbid identified biased terms and concepts. Include a "do not use" list directly in the generation instructions.
*   **Positive Reinforcement:** Inject examples of neutral, inclusive phrasing into the few-shot training data. Instruct the model to prioritize action verbs and skill-based descriptions over personality traits often associated with specific genders.
*   **Real-Time Filtering:** Implement a post-generation validation layer. If the output triggers the bias scorer above a certain threshold, automatically trigger a regeneration request with a specific instruction to "remove biased language and focus on skills."

**4. Human-in-the-Loop Verification (Day 6)**
*   **Diverse Review Panel:** Assemble a small group of internal reviewers with diverse backgrounds to manually inspect a random sample of 100 generated job descriptions. Automated tools miss nuance; human judgment is critical for tone and cultural context.
*   **Client-Specific Calibration:** If the major client has specific diversity guidelines or terminology preferences, run a dedicated test set against these criteria to ensure contractual compliance.

**5. Deployment Monitoring & Feedback Loop (Day 7 + Post-Launch)**
*   **Shadow Mode Logging:** For the first week of live deployment, log all generated descriptions and their bias scores without blocking them (unless a severe violation is detected). This establishes a real-world baseline.
*   **User Feedback Mechanism:** Add a simple "Report Issue" flag for client users to mark job descriptions that feel exclusionary.
*   **Weekly Retraining Schedule:** Commit to a weekly review of flagged content and new bias patterns to update the negative constraint list and few-shot examples continuously.

**Deliverables Checklist:**
*   [ ] Biased term dictionary specific to your domain.
*   [ ] Audit report of current generation logs.
*   [ ] Updated system prompt with inclusive constraints.
*   [ ] Automated bias scoring script.
*   [ ] Human review sign-off document.

Note: Given the one-week timeline, prioritize the "Negative Constraints" and "Real-Time Filtering" steps as they offer the highest immediate risk reduction. Do not delay launch for perfect coverage; ensure a robust feedback loop is active on day one.
