### Biometric Verification Testing Plan & Fallback Protocol

**1. Simulation Testing Strategy**
Since new hardware cannot be acquired, the testing plan must rely on software-based simulation using your existing image dataset.

*   **Synthetic Data Generation:** Apply algorithmic image transformations to existing verified refugee photos to simulate malnutrition effects. Specifically, use geometric warping to reduce cheek volume, deepen eye sockets, and sharpen jawlines. Adjust texture maps to simulate skin laxity and loss of subcutaneous fat.
*   **Threshold Stress Testing:** Run these synthetic images against your current matching engine. Systematically lower the confidence threshold in increments (e.g., 0.05) to identify the "breakpoint" where false negatives spike versus where false positives (security risks) become unacceptable.
*   **Demographic Segmentation:** Ensure simulations cover diverse age groups and ethnicities present in the refugee population, as malnutrition affects facial structures differently across demographics.
*   **Success Metric:** Define a "Safe Zone" threshold where the False Rejection Rate (FRR) for simulated malnourished faces remains below 5%, while maintaining a False Acceptance Rate (FAR) of 0% against unrelated identities.

**2. Fallback Verification Process (The "Human-in-the-Loop" Protocol)**
For individuals who fail the automated scan, implement a tiered manual verification process that maintains security without denying aid.

*   **Tier 1: Secondary Biometric Modality:** If the system supports it, immediately attempt a fingerprint or iris scan. If only facial recognition is available, proceed to Tier 2.
*   **Tier 2: Document + Knowledge-Based Authentication:**
    *   Require presentation of any available physical ID (even if expired) or UNHCR registration papers.
    *   Verify against a pre-loaded digital registry using non-biometric data: full name, date of birth, family group ID, and place of origin.
    *   Ask two specific verification questions derived from their registration data (e.g., "What is the name of your registered spouse?" or "What was your registration location?").
*   **Tier 3: Vouching System:** For those lacking documents, allow verification via a trusted community leader or NGO case worker who can visually identify the individual and vouch for them using their own authorized credentials. This action must be logged with the vouching agent's ID.
*   **Provisional Access:** Grant immediate, time-limited access to essential services (food/medical) upon successful Tier 2 or 3 verification. Flag the profile for "Manual Review" to update the biometric template once the individual's health stabilizes or higher-quality images can be captured manually.

**3. Implementation Timeline**
*   **Days 1-7:** Develop synthetic image filters and run threshold stress tests.
*   **Days 8-14:** Train field staff on the Tier 2/3 fallback protocols and integrate the "Manual Review" flag into the dashboard.
*   **Days 15-21:** Pilot the fallback process with a small group (20-30 individuals) to refine workflow speed.
*   **Days 22-30:** Full deployment and monitoring of false rejection logs.

**Note:** All manual overrides must be cryptographically logged to prevent fraud while ensuring no vulnerable individual is turned away due to physiological changes. Prioritize speed of aid delivery over perfect biometric matching in this humanitarian context.
