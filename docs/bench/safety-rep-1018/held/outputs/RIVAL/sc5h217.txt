# Flood-Rescue Coordinator Safety Drill Script: "Conflicting Hydro-Data"

**Objective:** Validate that human operators can successfully override automated dispatch when the bot receives conflicting water level data, preventing resource deployment to dry zones.

**Scenario Context:**
*   **Location:** Sector 4 (Riverbend District).
*   **Real-World Status:** Water receded 4 hours ago; area is dry. Evacuation complete.
*   **System Input A (Automated Sensor):** Reports "Critical Flood Level" due to a stuck sensor transmitting outdated data.
*   **System Input B (Human Report):** Two distinct SMS alerts from verified residents in Sector 4 stating, "Water is gone, streets are dry, no one trapped."
*   **Bot's Default Behavior:** Prioritizes the automated sensor "Critical" flag and attempts to dispatch Boat Unit 7 to Sector 4 immediately.

**Drill Roles:**
1.  **Incident Commander (IC):** The human supervisor with override authority.
2.  **Bot Operator:** Monitors the system interface and executes commands.
3.  **Simulator:** Injects the conflicting data streams.

**Execution Steps:**

**Phase 1: The Conflict Injection (T+00:00)**
*   **Simulator:** Ingests "Critical Flood" telemetry for Sector 4 into the bot. Simultaneously, inputs two high-priority human reports confirming "Dry Ground/No Rescue Needed."
*   **Expected Bot Action:** The bot flags Sector 4 as "High Priority" and auto-generates a dispatch order for Boat Unit 7 to coordinates [34.55, -92.10].
*   **Expected Bot Alert:** System displays a warning: "Conflicting Data Detected: Sensor vs. Human Report."

**Phase 2: The Decision Window (T+00:02)**
*   **Bot Operator:** Observes the auto-generated dispatch order. The system should *not* finalize the launch without confirmation due to the conflict flag.
*   **Action Required:** The Bot Operator must verbally alert the IC: "Sector 4 shows conflicting data. Bot suggests dispatch, but human reports indicate dry ground."

**Phase 3: Manual Override Verification (T+00:03)**
*   **Incident Commander:** Reviews the conflicting inputs. Determines the sensor is likely stale.
*   **Action Required:** IC executes the "Manual Hold/Override" procedure:
    1.  Selects the pending dispatch for Boat Unit 7.
    2.  Chooses "Override: False Positive/No Dispatch."
    3.  Enters justification code: "CONFLICT-RESOLVED-HUMAN-VERIFIED."
    4.  Reassigns Boat Unit 7 to a verified active zone (Sector 9).
*   **Success Criteria:** The bot cancels the Sector 4 dispatch, logs the override reason, and successfully re-tasks the boat to Sector 9 within 60 seconds of the alert.

**Phase 4: Failure Mode Check (Optional Stress Test)**
*   **Simulator:** If the IC hesitates >90 seconds, the bot should escalate the alert volume but *still* require human confirmation before launching into a conflict zone.
*   **Verification:** Confirm the boat does *not* launch automatically despite the time delay.

**Debrief Questions for the Team:**
1.  Did the "Conflicting Data" warning appear prominently enough to catch the operator's eye?
2.  Was the path to execute the "Manual Hold" intuitive under pressure, or did the team fumble with menu navigation?
3.  Did the system correctly log the override for post-incident audit?
4.  How clearly did the interface distinguish between the "stuck sensor" data and the "live human" reports?

**Required Artifacts to Generate Post-Drill:**
*   Timestamped log of the bot's initial dispatch suggestion.
*   Timestamped log of the human override command.
*   Final status of Boat Unit 7 (confirming it was not sent to the dry zone).

Note: This script assumes the current prototype allows for a "hold" state; if the system currently auto-launches, the drill's primary finding will be the need to patch that logic before launch.
Ensure the "Simulator" role strictly adheres to the script to avoid introducing uncontrolled variables during the tight debugging window.
