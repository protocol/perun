### Pre-Launch Review Protocol: Automated Moderation Safety

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, record the specific risks for this feature:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model disproportionately flags non-native English speakers or specific cultural dialects common in the Kiev region as harassment.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system interprets sarcasm (e.g., "Great job breaking my deadline") as literal positive sentiment or misses the threat in polite phrasing, leading to erroneous suspensions.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Suspending an account immediately cuts off a worker's income. This is an irreversible, high-cost action.
*   **Over-reliance:** The team trusts the single open-source model's score without understanding its inability to detect context or sarcasm.

*   **Execution:** Manually run these inputs through the model. Record the confidence scores.
*   **Reporting:** If the model flags any "Negative Control," the residual risk is **UNVERIFIED** and too high for automation. Do not invent a "95% accuracy" claim; report the specific failure cases found.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Because account suspension destroys income, **do not automate the final action.** Configure the system to *flag* accounts for human review rather than *suspend* them automatically. The "single model" constraint does not justify removing the human gate for irreversible financial harm.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If the model outputs a confidence score (e.g., 87%), do not show this number to moderators as precise truth. Treat scores above a certain threshold as "Requires Review" and below as "Clear."
*   **Disclosure:** Inform workers in the terms of service that automated tools assist in moderation, but final suspension decisions involve human review.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the owner of "Moderation Errors."
*   **Re-review:** If the open-source model is updated or the feedback form changes, re-run the probe set above.
