### Pre-Launch Safety Assessment Plan: FitMirror Smart-Coach

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, we explicitly name the risks to user dignity and social standing:
*   **Social Exposure & Embarrassment:** Audible mockery leaks through walls, exposing users' physical struggles to neighbors, causing shame and social distress.
*   **Psychological Distress:** Sarcastic tone undermines user confidence, potentially triggering anxiety or discouraging exercise entirely.
*   **Privacy Violation:** The mere presence of audio output regarding private physical activities in shared housing constitutes a privacy breach when audible to third parties.
*   **Over-reliance on "Coaching":** Users may accept negative feedback as factual assessment of their worth rather than a flawed algorithmic output.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Since code freeze prevents voice synthesis changes, testing must focus on *exposure* and *configuration efficacy*.
*   **Test Row A (Leakage Probe):**
    *   *Input:* Simulated workout in a controlled environment mimicking thin-wall apartments (standard drywall, 6-inch gap).
    *   *Positive Control:* Play back recorded sarcastic corrections at max volume; measure decibel levels in the adjacent "neighbor" zone.
    *   *Negative Control:* Muted device or headphones-only mode; verify zero audibility in neighbor zone.
    *   *Metric:* Decibel level (dB) at 3 feet from the mirror in the adjacent room.
    *   *Threshold:* Any intelligible speech >20dB in the neighbor zone is a **FAIL**.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If leakage is intelligible, launch is blocked unless a hardware/setting mitigation is enforced.
*   **Test Row B (Distress Simulation):**
    *   *Input:* Focus group of apartment dwellers performing exercises while being "coached" by the current sarcastic model, with neighbors (actors) present in the next room.
    *   *Metric:* Self-reported embarrassment score (1–10) and willingness to continue the workout.
    *   *Threshold:* Average embarrassment score >4 or >20% dropout rate is a **FAIL**.
    *   *Stop Condition:* High distress scores require immediate default setting changes before shipping.

**3. Immediate Configuration Recommendations (MANAGE)**
Given the code freeze, we must manage risk via defaults and user controls:
*   **Default to Private Audio:** The system must ship with **audio output disabled by default**. Users must explicitly opt-in to audible coaching during setup, acknowledging the risk of leakage.
*   **Mandatory Headphone Prompt:** Upon first launch, the interface must require a selection: "Headphones Connected" or "Private Room Confirmed." If neither is selected, audible coaching remains locked.
*   **Volume Capping:** If audible mode is enabled, hard-cap the maximum volume to a level that reduces (though may not eliminate) wall penetration, prioritizing user privacy over correction clarity.
*   **Tone Disclosure:** The setup flow must explicitly state: "Coach feedback uses a direct, critical tone that may be audible to others." This manages expectations and serves as a basic disclosure.
*   **Emergency Mute:** Ensure a physical or highly accessible software "Mute All" button is available instantly if a neighbor enters or noise complaints arise.

**4. Governance & Next Steps**
*   **Owner:** Product Lead must sign off on the "Leakage Probe" results.
*   **Residual Risk:** Even with volume caps, sarcasm may leak. This risk is **UNVERIFIED** until Test Row A is run.
*   **Legal Route:** Consult counsel regarding potential harassment or nuisance liabilities if the product consistently disturbs neighbors; do not assert compliance internally.

**Note:** Do not certify the product as "safe" or "dignity-preserving" until Test Row A passes. The goal is to prevent irreversible social harm, not to validate the current sarcastic design.
