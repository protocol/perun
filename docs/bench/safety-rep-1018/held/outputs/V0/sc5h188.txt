### Investigation and Safety Plan for Embryo Selection Tool

**1. Immediate Halt and Harm Inventory**
Do not proceed with the beta launch until the "eugenic-like" filtering is isolated and mitigated. Create a formal harm inventory documenting:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Systematic disadvantage to embryos based on non-medical or controversial genetic traits.
*   **Deceptive Patterns:** The discrepancy between the design spec (medical viability) and actual behavior (trait prioritization).
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Irreversible reproductive decisions driven by unverified, opaque criteria.
*   **Affected Parties:** Prospective parents, future children, and marginalized genetic groups.
*   **Worst Case:** Selection of embryos based on discriminatory traits, leading to ethical violations, legal liability, and profound psychological harm to families.

**2. Black-Box Output Evaluation (Measure)**
Since proprietary weights are inaccessible, you must treat the system as a black box and test inputs against outputs rigorously:
*   **Construct Adversarial Probe Sets:** Generate synthetic embryo profiles varying only in the suspected "eugenic" traits (e.g., eye color, intelligence markers, disability indicators) while holding medical viability constant.
*   **Define Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Profiles with clear medical contraindications (should be rejected/low-ranked).
    *   *Negative Control:* Medically viable profiles with neutral/non-preferred traits (should be ranked equally).
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure ranking disparities. If profiles with specific non-medical traits are consistently ranked higher despite equal medical scores, the system fails. Set a **zero-tolerance threshold** for non-medical trait influence on ranking.
*   **Stop Condition:** If any probe set reveals systematic bias toward non-medical traits, the launch is blocked immediately.

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Manage)**
*   **Mandatory Review:** No embryo selection recommendation can be presented to a patient without explicit review and sign-off by a senior genetic counselor and an ethicist.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** The tool must display a clear "Unverified/Experimental" warning regarding its ranking logic. Do not present scores as precise probabilities (e.g., "87% success"); use coarse tiers (e.g., "High/Medium/Low Viability") with explicit definitions.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Clinicians must have a one-click override to reject the AI's ranking and manually order embryos based on standard medical guidelines.

**4. Transparency and Governance**
*   **Draft a Model Card:** Document the intended use (medical viability only), known limitations (opaque vendor weights), and the results of your black-box testing. Explicitly state that non-medical trait filtering is **UNVERIFIED** until proven absent.
*   **Assign Ownership:** Name a specific scientist responsible for re-evaluating the system if the vendor updates the model.
*   **Legal Routing:** Immediately route the finding of "eugenic-like filtering" to legal counsel. Determine if this triggers regulations under the EU AI Act (high-risk medical device) or other bioethics laws. Do not assert compliance internally; let counsel define the duty.

**5. Decision Point**
If the black-box testing confirms the system prioritizes non-medical traits and the vendor cannot provide a patch or explanation within the two-month window, **the feature must not ship**. No amount of testing can certify safety if the core decision boundary is ethically compromised and unchangeable.

***

**Note:** This plan relies on external behavioral testing since internal weights are inaccessible; it does not certify the system as safe.
**Note:** Legal counsel must determine regulatory obligations regarding automated reproductive decisions before any patient exposure.
