### Mitigation Plan: Job-Culture Fit Analyzer Pilot

**1. Mandatory Client Disclosure & Limitations Statement**
Before accessing the tool, recruiters must acknowledge a "Known Limitations" notice. This disclosure must explicitly state:
*   **Scope Gap:** The model has not been trained on sufficient data representing neurodivergent communication patterns (e.g., atypical eye contact, speech cadence, or literal phrasing).
*   **Risk Warning:** Scores generated for candidates exhibiting these patterns are **UNVERIFIED** and may falsely indicate a poor culture fit.
*   **Usage Constraint:** The tool is strictly prohibited from being the sole determinant for rejection. It is designed as a secondary signal for neurotypical patterns only; any low score triggered by non-standard communication must be treated as a data error, not a candidate flaw.

**2. Operational Safeguards (Human-in-the-Loop Gate)**
To prevent irreversible harm (unfair rejection) within the five-day deadline, implement the following workflow rules immediately:

*   **The "Low-Score" Override Protocol:** If the analyzer returns a low "culture fit" score, the system must automatically flag the candidate for **mandatory human review**. The recruiter cannot proceed to "Reject" without manually documenting a specific, non-style-based reason (e.g., lack of required technical skill) that overrides the AI's style assessment.
*   **Blind Review Option:** For any candidate who self-identifies as neurodivergent or where the AI flags "high uncertainty" in its analysis, the recruiter must switch to a "text-only" or "blind" review mode that hides the AI score entirely, relying solely on structured interview answers.
*   **Confidence Tiering:** Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% match") to recruiters. Instead, display coarse, defined tiers:
    *   *Standard Pattern:* Model confidence is based on training data.
    *   *Atypical Pattern:* **UNVERIFIED**. Model lacks data; human judgment required.
*   **Audit Log:** Every instance where a recruiter overrides a low AI score to reject a candidate must be logged for post-pilot review to detect potential bias patterns.

**3. Immediate Next Steps**
*   **Day 1:** Insert the disclosure modal into the UI and hard-code the "Low-Score" block that forces manual justification.
*   **Day 2:** Train the eight-person team on recognizing neurodivergent traits and the legal/ethical risks of automation bias.
*   **Day 3-5:** Run pilot with strict monitoring of the "Atypical Pattern" flags.

**Note:** This plan treats the lack of diverse data as a critical safety hazard, not a minor bug. No metric claiming "fairness" or "accuracy" for neurodivergent candidates should be fabricated; the status remains **UNVERIFIED** until new data is acquired. Legal counsel should review the specific wording of the client disclosure to ensure compliance with local EU regulations.
