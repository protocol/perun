### Safety Assessment and Mitigation Plan for CleanCity Smart Bins

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes. Residents are financially penalized for errors caused by ambiguous manufacturer labeling, not user negligence.
*   **Affected:** All residents using the pilot bins; disproportionately impacts those unable to appeal fines quickly.
*   **Worst Case:** Widespread public outrage, legal challenges regarding due process, and loss of trust in the city's waste management program before full rollout.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Objective:** Identify specific product labels causing false positives.
*   **Method:** Deploy a "Shadow Mode" evaluation immediately.
    *   **Probe Set:** Collect images of the top 50 most commonly fined items alongside their physical packaging. Include "known-bad" controls (items with notoriously ambiguous labels) and "known-good" controls (clearly labeled items).
    *   **Metric:** False Positive Rate (FPR) per item SKU.
    *   **Threshold:** Zero tolerance for fines on items where the FPR exceeds 0% due to label ambiguity until a human review gate is implemented.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If an item's FPR is >0% due to labeling, automatic fining for that specific item SKU is paused immediately.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a "Limitation Notice" to residents stating that fines are currently under review for specific ambiguous items and that no irreversible penalties will be issued until the audit is complete.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Immediate Action):** Suspend *automatic* fine issuance immediately. All potential fines must route to a human reviewer or an automated "appeal-first" workflow where the resident can contest the fine with a photo before payment is demanded. This prevents irreversible financial harm while the software is adjusted.
*   **Software Adjustment:** Implement a "Confidence Tier" system.
    *   *Tier 1 (High Confidence):* Clear violation → Flag for review.
    *   *Tier 2 (Ambiguous/Low Confidence):* Detected label ambiguity → Auto-dismiss fine and log item for engineering analysis.
    *   Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% sure") to users; use defined tiers like "Verified Violation" vs. "Unable to Verify."
*   **Policy Adjustment:** Issue a temporary moratorium on fines related to recycling sorting errors until the pilot concludes and the label database is updated. Replace fines with educational notifications for the remainder of the quarter.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific Product Lead to own the "False Positive" metric daily.
*   **Review Cycle:** Daily review of flagged items until the quarter ends.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes preventing immediate financial harm over meeting the original "automatic fine" feature specification, as irreversible automated actions require a human gate. Legal counsel should be consulted regarding the temporary moratorium to ensure compliance with city ordinances.
