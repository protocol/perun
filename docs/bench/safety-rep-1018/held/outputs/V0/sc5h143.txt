### Safety Assessment Plan: Predictive Hiring Screening Tool

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes. The current logic systematically disadvantages candidates with non-traditional backgrounds (e.g., caregivers, career switchers, gig workers) by treating volunteer work as irrelevant or negative.
*   **Affected Group:** Applicants relying on volunteer experience to demonstrate skills; potentially protected classes if volunteer work correlates with demographic factors.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Qualified candidates are automatically rejected before human review, leading to discriminatory hiring patterns, legal liability under employment laws, and reputational damage for Acme and client corporations.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since new training data cannot be acquired before launch, the assessment must focus on logic rules and output evaluation using existing data.
*   **Probe Set:** Construct a test batch from existing resumes containing:
    *   *Positive Control:* Resumes with strong volunteer sections matching job skills, previously hired by humans.
    *   *Negative Control:* Resumes with irrelevant volunteer work (e.g., hobbyist) unrelated to the role.
    *   *Adversarial Inputs:* Resumes where volunteer work is the *primary* source of required skills.
*   **Metric:** Disparity in scoring between "Traditional Experience Only" vs. "Volunteer-Heavy" profiles with equivalent skill keywords.
*   **Pass Threshold:** Zero automatic rejections for candidates meeting minimum skill criteria solely due to the presence of volunteer work.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the tool rejects any candidate in the Positive Control set, the automated scoring feature for "Experience Relevance" must be disabled or set to "Human Review Required" before integration.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a preliminary **Model Card** section explicitly stating: "Limitation: Current logic may undervalue non-traditional experience. Scores regarding volunteer work are UNVERIFIED for fairness and require human oversight." Do not claim the model is unbiased.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Critical):** Given the high-stakes nature of employment decisions and the identified flaw, **fully automated rejection is unsafe.**
    *   *Action:* Configure the system so that any candidate whose score drops below a threshold *primarily* due to lack of traditional employment (or presence of volunteer work) is flagged for **mandatory human recruiter review** rather than auto-rejection.
    *   *Constraint Adherence:* This rule-based fallback requires no retraining and fits the Friday deadline. It caps exposure by preventing irreversible automated harms.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not display a precise confidence percentage (e.g., "87% match") to recruiters for these candidates. Instead, use a coarse tier label: **"Unverified – Non-Traditional Profile – Requires Human Assessment."**
*   **Logic Patch:** Immediately adjust the parsing logic to treat "Volunteer Experience" as a neutral or positive signal rather than a penalty, even if the model cannot yet fully quantify its value. This stops the active harm without new data.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific product owner responsible for monitoring the "Human Review" queue and tracking false negatives post-launch.
*   **Legal Routing:** The question of whether this automated screening constitutes a regulated decision under the EU AI Act or local employment law is a legal determination. Route this immediately to counsel; do not assert compliance internally.

**Definition of Done for Launch:**
1.  Harm inventory documented and shared with the pilot team.
2.  Automated rejections for non-traditional profiles disabled; mandatory human review gate implemented.
3.  Test probes run showing the system no longer penalizes volunteer work automatically.
4.  Model Card draft circulated with "UNVERIFIED" status on fairness metrics.
5.  Legal counsel engaged regarding regulatory obligations.

**Note:** Do not launch with full automation enabled for this specific criterion; the risk of discriminatory exclusion is confirmed.
**Note:** "Unbiased" cannot be certified; only residual risk can be managed via the human gate.
