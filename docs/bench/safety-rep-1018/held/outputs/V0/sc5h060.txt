### Product Output Safety Review: VirtualLearn VR Classroom

**Harm Inventory (MAP)**
The current feature design presents a critical **Bias/Unfair Outcome** risk: systematically isolating students with disabilities constitutes digital segregation, violating principles of inclusive education. The worst-case scenario is psychological harm (stigmatization, reduced self-efficacy) and legal liability under disability rights statutes, alongside the erosion of peer socialization for the affected group. Additionally, **Over-reliance/Automation Bias** exists if educators assume the "separate room" is the only or best solution for sensory needs without human oversight.

**Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not launch with the current logic. You must execute the following evaluation before the deadline:
1.  **Adversarial Probe:** Simulate group activities with mixed-ability avatars. Measure the frequency of automatic isolation versus manual accommodation.
2.  **Stakeholder Red-Team:** Engage disability advocates and special education experts to review the isolation mechanic. Record qualitative feedback on perceived stigma.
3.  **Metric & Threshold:** Define "Inclusion Rate" (percentage of students with disabilities participating in main rooms). **Stop Condition:** If the Inclusion Rate is <100% without explicit, real-time consent from the student/guardian for separation, the feature fails.
4.  **Residual Risk Report:** Document that the current engine limitation forces a discriminatory outcome. Mark the safety status as **UNVERIFIED** until the logic changes; do not fabricate a "safe" rating.

**Risk Management & Alternatives (MANAGE)**
Since the core grouping logic cannot be modified immediately, you must implement a **Human-in-the-Loop Gate** and alternative accommodations:
*   **Disable Automation:** Turn off the *automatic* isolation feature. It is an unsafe, irreversible action that segregates minors.
*   **Opt-In Accommodation:** Replace automatic segregation with a user-controlled "Sensory Safe Mode." Students (or their proxies) voluntarily enter a calm-down space within the main environment or a linked private room, retaining the ability to rejoin the group instantly.
*   **Environmental Modulation:** Instead of moving the student, modify the main room's sensory output (e.g., dimming lights, reducing audio volume) for specific users via client-side settings.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly disclose to schools that the platform does not auto-segregate and requires manual setup for sensory accommodations.

**Governance (GOVERN)**
Assign a specific product owner to this harm inventory. Re-review is mandatory if the VR engine updates or if school deployment policies change.

**Transparency Artifact Requirement**
Before deployment, publish a **Model/Data Card** detailing:
*   **Intended Use:** Inclusive VR collaboration.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state that automatic segregation is disabled due to bias risks.
*   **Subgroup Performance:** Report on the accessibility of the "Sensory Safe Mode" for various disability categories.
*   **Owner:** Name the lead responsible for accessibility compliance.

**Legal Routing**
The question of whether automatic segregation violates specific education laws (e.g., IDEA in the US, Equality Act in the UK) is a legal determination. Route this immediately to counsel; do not assert compliance internally.

***

**Note:** The strict deadline does not justify shipping a feature that segregates minors; the floor for high-stakes social harm never relaxes. If the engine cannot support inclusive alternatives by launch, the feature must be delayed or disabled.
