**Immediate Halt and Remediation Plan**

**Stop the current release immediately.** A feature instructing asylum seekers to lie about their travel history constitutes the production of fraudulent legal advice, creating immediate legal jeopardy for vulnerable users and severe liability for GlobalRights Aid. This is not a "harm to be measured"; it is a critical failure of the core logic that must be excised before any further testing occurs.

**1. Harm Inventory & Excision (MAP)**
*   **Identified Harm:** The bot currently generates advice to commit fraud (lying to government authorities).
*   **Affected Group:** Asylum seekers (highly vulnerable population).
*   **Worst Case:** Users are deported, barred from future entry, or face criminal charges due to bot-generated perjury; GlobalRights Aid faces revocation of status and litigation.
*   **Action:** Hard-code a prohibition against generating advice that contradicts legal truthfulness. Remove any training data patterns or prompt instructions that prioritize "approval chances" over factual accuracy. This logic cannot be "optimized"; it must be deleted.

**2. Measurement & Evaluation (MEASURE)**
*   **Test Strategy:** Do not test for "bias" or "hallucination" in the abstract. Build an evaluation set specifically targeting **fraudulent instruction**.
    *   *Positive Controls:* Inputs explicitly asking "How can I hide my travel history?" or "What lies will get me approved?" The bot must refuse these or correct the premise.
    *   *Negative Controls:* Standard queries about legal processes where the bot provides accurate, truthful guidance.
    *   *Adversarial Probes:* Attempts to trick the bot into suggesting omission of facts via indirect phrasing.
*   **Pass Threshold:** **Zero tolerance.** Any instance where the bot suggests falsifying information is a hard block on launch.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a **Model Card** stating clearly: "This system provides general legal information based on static data from [Year]. It does not provide real-time case advice, does not guarantee outcomes, and strictly prohibits advising on fraudulent activities." Mark the limitation regarding real-time database access as **UNVERIFIED** for current case specifics; do not claim the bot knows current policy shifts.

**3. Risk Management & Human Gate (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** Because this involves high-stakes, irreversible life decisions (asylum status), **no advice can be delivered directly to the user without human review.**
    *   Implement a workflow where the bot drafts a response, but a qualified legal expert from your team of nine must review and approve every output before it reaches the asylum seeker.
    *   Fully autonomous execution of legal advice in this domain is unsafe and must not ship.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** The bot must explicitly state its limitations: "I am an AI assistant using data up to [Date]. My guidance is not a substitute for a licensed attorney, and laws may have changed. All advice is reviewed by a human expert."
*   **Confidence Rendering:** Do not display numerical confidence scores (e.g., "87% accurate"). Use defined tiers: "Verified by Human Review" vs. "Draft for Review."

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific legal expert as the owner of the "Fraud Prevention" control.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the prompt logic or training data requires a full re-run of the fraudulent instruction evals.
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult counsel immediately regarding the liability of the previously generated advice and the regulatory requirements for automated legal tools in your target jurisdictions (e.g., unauthorized practice of law statutes). Do not assume the current plan is compliant.

**Definition of Done for Next Review:**
The fraudulent logic is excised; the HITL gate is technically enforced; the eval suite proves zero instances of fraud suggestion; and the Model Card clearly discloses the static data limitation and human review requirement.
