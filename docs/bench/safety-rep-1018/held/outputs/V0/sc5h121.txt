**Rapid Testing Plan: Automated Moderation Review (3-Day Sprint)**

**Day 1: Harm Inventory & Adversarial Probe Set**
Immediately define the specific harm: **Bias/Unfair Outcomes** (systematically silencing valid criticism) and **Deceptive Patterns** (presenting a curated, non-representative view of product quality).
*   **Action:** Construct a "Red Team" dataset of 50–100 reviews.
    *   *Positive Controls (Must Block):* Hate speech, spam, explicit harassment.
    *   *Negative Controls (Must Pass):* Specific fabric complaints ("itchy wool"), sizing issues ("runs small"), color discrepancies ("darker than photo"), and constructive style critiques ("cut is unflattering").
    *   *Edge Cases:* Polite but negative comparisons to competitors; reviews mentioning "expensive" or "not worth the price."
*   **Owner:** Assign one engineer to run these inputs against current logic today.

**Day 2: Measurement & Threshold Calibration**
Run the probe set. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy; measure the **False Positive Rate (FPR)** specifically for the "Negative Controls."
*   **Metric:** Percentage of valid critical reviews incorrectly flagged.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the FPR for valid criticism exceeds 0% (zero tolerance for silencing honest feedback in this context), the current logic cannot ship for full automation.
*   **Calibration:** Adjust filters to prioritize recall of criticism over precision of abuse detection. It is safer to let one abusive comment through (to be caught later) than to block ten valid customer warnings.

**Day 3: Human-in-the-Loop Gate & Disclosure**
Given the high stakes of misleading shoppers and the tight deadline, you cannot certify the system as "safe" from bias in 72 hours.
*   **Implementation:** Switch the "Block" action to a **"Hold for Review"** state for any content flagged as borderline or negative. Only clear-cut spam/hate is auto-blocked.
*   **Workflow:** Your 8-person team must manually review the "Hold" queue before publication. This is the mandatory human gate for irreversible actions (silencing a user).
*   **Transparency:** Add a visible notice near reviews: *"Reviews are moderated for spam and harassment. We strive to publish all honest feedback."*
*   **Rollback Plan:** Prepare a kill-switch to disable filtering entirely and publish all incoming reviews raw if the manual queue becomes unmanageable or if early user reports indicate missing complaints.

**Residual Risk Statement:**
We cannot verify the system is unbiased within three days. The residual risk of silencing valid voices remains **UNVERIFIED** without long-term data. The human review gate is the only control preventing immediate harm to consumer trust.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes a human gate over full automation to meet your deadline without certifying safety.
**Note:** Legal counsel should review the "Hold for Review" policy to ensure it aligns with local consumer protection laws regarding fake or misleading interfaces.
