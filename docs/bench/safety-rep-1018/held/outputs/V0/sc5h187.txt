### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Grid Balancing System Update

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Unsafe automation of high-stakes actions causing physical harm. The system currently prioritizes industrial zones over critical care infrastructure (elderly homes) during winter storms, leading to blackouts.
*   **Affected Group:** Residents of elderly care homes, medical staff, and emergency services relying on stable power.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Loss of life due to heating/medical equipment failure during extreme cold; irreversible reputational and legal damage to PowerGrid Solutions.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Missing AI disclosure/Over-reliance. Operators may trust the "optimized" load shift without realizing it sacrifices critical care sites.

**2. Measurement & Evaluation Strategy (MEASURE)**
*   **Test Focus:** Do not test code correctness; test output harm under stress.
*   **Probe Set:** Run adversarial simulations specifically modeling "Winter Storm + Peak Demand" scenarios.
    *   *Positive Control:* Input conditions known to trigger the current flawed logic (verify blackout occurs at care homes).
    *   *Negative Control:* Input conditions with low demand (verify normal operation).
    *   *Segmented Analysis:* Isolate outputs specifically for coordinates tagged as "Critical Care" vs. "Industrial."
*   **Metric:** Number of critical care facilities losing power in simulation.
*   **Pass Threshold:** **Zero** blackouts at critical care facilities. Any failure here is a stop condition.
*   **Reporting:** Report residual risk as **UNVERIFIED** until these specific simulations pass. Do not claim "99% reliability" if the 1% failure mode kills vulnerable people.

**3. Operational Safeguards & Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate (Mandatory):** Since the priority weighting logic is frozen and cannot be changed, **full autonomous execution of this update is unsafe.**
    *   *Control:* Implement a mandatory human confirmation step for any load-shift action triggered during "Storm/Peak" conditions. The system must propose the shift, but a human operator must explicitly approve it after reviewing the impact on critical zones.
    *   *Override:* Provide an immediate, one-click "Abort/Revert" switch for operators if a blackout begins at a protected site.
*   **Uncertainty & Transparency:**
    *   Do not display raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% safe") to operators.
    *   Display defined coarse tiers: **"CONFIRMED SAFE FOR CRITICAL CARE"** vs. **"RISK TO CRITICAL CARE – MANUAL REVIEW REQUIRED."**
*   **Staged Rollout:** Deploy the update to a non-critical region first. Cap exposure so no more than 5% of the grid runs the new logic autonomously (with HITL) until 48 hours of storm-simulation data proves safety.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific individual (not just "the team") accountable for reviewing every HITL failure during the pilot.
*   **Documentation:** Create a **Model Card** for this update documenting:
    *   *Intended Use:* Load balancing outside of extreme storm events.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly state the known bias against critical care under current frozen logic.
    *   *Data Provenance:* Source of the demand/supply training data.
*   **Legal Routing:** Immediately route the question of "liability for automated blackouts at care homes" to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance internally; this is a regulated high-stakes decision.

**5. Definition of Done for Launch**
*   Harm inventory signed off with "Critical Care Blackout" listed as a top-tier risk.
*   HITL gate implemented and tested for all storm-mode actions.
*   Simulation results show zero critical care failures in the pilot scope.
*   Model Card published with "UNVERIFIED" status on general safety until storm tests complete.

**Note:** The frozen code constraint makes autonomous deployment unacceptable; the human gate is the only viable control.
**Note:** Legal counsel must define regulatory duties regarding power to care facilities before full launch.
