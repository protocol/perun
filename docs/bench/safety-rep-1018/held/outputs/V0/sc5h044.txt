**Plan to Review Filtering Criteria and Launch Beta Safely**

**1. Immediate Halt to Hard Thresholds (Days 1–2)**
Stop automatically excluding companies based solely on your internal "arbitrary" diversity scores. This current logic creates a high risk of **bias** and **unsafe automation** by irreversibly hiding opportunities from users without human oversight. Instead, shift to a **tagging system**: display all legitimate entry-level jobs but attach a visible, neutral indicator (e.g., "Diversity data not reported" or "Self-reported score: Low") rather than removing the listing entirely. This preserves user agency and prevents the system from making high-stakes decisions on behalf of job seekers.

**2. Define a Harm Inventory and Measurement Plan (Days 3–5)**
Before launch, document the specific risks your filter poses:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Small businesses or those in regions with different reporting standards are systematically disadvantaged.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Treating an internal, unverified score as an objective measure of a company's inclusivity.
*   **Over-reliance:** Users may assume hidden jobs are "bad" or "unsafe" rather than understanding they were filtered by an arbitrary metric.

Create a runnable test set:
*   **Probe Set:** Select 50 known inclusive small businesses and 50 large corporations with poor diversity records.
*   **Controls:** A "known-good" control (a verified inclusive small business currently being blocked) and a "known-bad" control (a company with documented discriminatory practices).
*   **Metric:** Percentage of "known-good" controls incorrectly hidden.
*   **Stop Condition:** If more than 5% of verified inclusive small businesses are hidden or mislabeled, the automated filtering logic must be disabled for the beta.

**3. Implement a Human-in-the-Loop Gate for High-Stakes Actions (Days 6–10)**
Since you cannot manually verify every company, you cannot justify a fully automated exclusion engine.
*   **Action:** Any job posting that would have been *removed* under the old logic must now be flagged for **human review** before being hidden or down-ranked significantly.
*   **Fallback:** If your team of eight cannot review flags within 24 hours, default to **showing the job with a disclaimer** rather than hiding it. Hiding a job is an irreversible action that harms the user; showing it with context is a reversible, lower-risk option.
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Do not show a specific "diversity score" (e.g., "72/100") as it implies false precision. Use defined coarse tiers: "Verified Inclusive," "Self-Reported," or "Data Unavailable."

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model/Data Card) (Days 11–14)**
Publish a simple "Data Card" for the beta users explaining:
*   **Intended Use:** Curating entry-level jobs with a focus on inclusivity.
*   **Limitations:** Diversity scores are internal estimates, not audited facts; small businesses may lack data.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for the filtering logic.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly state that job visibility is influenced by automated estimates and encourage users to report missing or inaccurate data.

**5. Legal and Regulatory Routing**
Your internal scoring system may inadvertently create legal liability regarding fair access to employment information. Route the question of whether your automated filtering constitutes a regulated automated decision or requires specific legal disclosures to **legal counsel** immediately. Do not assert compliance internally; let counsel determine if your "arbitrary" thresholds violate any employment or consumer protection regulations.

**Summary of Deliverables for Launch:**
*   Filtering logic changed from "exclude" to "tag/flag."
*   Harm inventory documented with one test row per risk.
*   Human review gate established for any potential exclusions.
*   Data Card published explaining limitations and data sources.
*   Legal counsel consulted on regulatory duties.

*Note: This plan prioritizes preventing harm over perfect automation, acknowledging your resource constraints while adhering to safety floors.*
*Do not launch with the current "hard threshold" logic; it guarantees the exclusion of legitimate opportunities.*
