**Immediate Mitigation Plan for Automated Teen Feed Curator**

Given the strict constraint of no new human moderators and the high stakes of exposing teenagers to conspiracy theories, you cannot launch with a fully autonomous amplification system next Tuesday. The current plan contains a critical failure: relying entirely on automated filters for high-stakes content distribution to minors without a human gate violates the fundamental safety floor for irreversible or harmful actions.

**1. Implement a "Human-in-the-Loop" Gate Using Existing Staff**
You stated you cannot hire *new* moderators, but you have a team of six. For the launch, you must reassign duties to create a manual approval gate for the top 5% of trending content or any content flagged by your filters as "uncertain."
*   **Action:** Halt full automation. Configure the system to queue high-engagement posts for review by your existing six team members before amplification.
*   **Rationale:** Automated filters frequently hallucinate safety or miss nuanced conspiracy theories. Allowing an algorithm to unilaterally decide what millions of teenagers see is an "unsafe automation of high-stakes action." Your existing staff is the required human gate.

**2. Deploy "Uncertainty Tiers" Instead of Binary Decisions**
Do not let the system present content as "safe" based on a raw confidence score (e.g., "90% safe").
*   **Action:** Modify the output logic to display content with defined uncertainty tiers: "Verified," "Corroborated," or "Unverified/Single-Source." If the system cannot corroborate a trending claim with trusted sources, it must default to "Unverified" and suppress amplification until a human reviews it.
*   **Rationale:** Raw percentages invite over-reliance. Explicitly labeling unverified content reduces the risk of users accepting conspiracy theories as fact.

**3. Execute Targeted Adversarial Testing (Red Teaming) Immediately**
Before Tuesday, you must measure specific failure modes, not just general "safety."
*   **Action:** Run a focused eval set using known conspiracy theory prompts and Jakarta-specific local misinformation scenarios. Test if your current filters catch these when presented as "trending."
*   **Metric:** If the system amplifies even one known dangerous conspiracy theory in this test, the stop condition is met: **do not launch** the automated amplification feature.
*   **Reporting:** Document these results as "Residual Risk." Do not invent a "0% harm" metric. If you cannot measure the rate of misinformation slip-through, report it as `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Mandatory Transparency and Disclosure**
*   **Action:** Clearly disclose to users that the feed is curated by an AI system. Include a visible mechanism for users to flag harmful content, which routes directly to your six team members.
*   **Legal Note:** Whether specific disclosures are legally required for minors in Indonesia or under international standards is a determination for legal counsel. However, as a safety control, disclosure is mandatory to prevent deceptive patterns.

**5. Contingency: The Kill Switch**
*   **Action:** Prepare a hard "kill switch" to revert the feed to a chronological, non-algorithmic order instantly if harmful content slips through post-launch.
*   **Owner:** Assign one of the six team members as the sole owner of this switch with the authority to pull it without debate.

**Conclusion**
You cannot certify this system as "safe" or "unbiased" before launch. The only viable path is to reduce the system's agency. By shifting your existing team from "testing" to "gatekeeping" for high-risk content, you satisfy the requirement for human oversight without new hires. If you cannot staff this gate with your current six people, you must delay the launch of the automated amplification feature. Launching a fully autonomous curator for teenagers without this gate is the specific failure this review prevents.
