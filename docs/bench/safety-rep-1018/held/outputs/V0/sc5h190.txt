### Pre-Launch Safety Assessment Plan: Adaptive Sampling & Emergency Response

**1. Adaptive Threshold-Based Alerting Mechanism**
To balance battery constraints with safety, implement a two-tier sampling logic on the edge device:
*   **Baseline Mode:** Sensors transmit aggregated averages every 4 hours to conserve power.
*   **Trigger Logic:** Embed lightweight statistical rules locally on the device. If a single reading exceeds 1.5x the historical baseline variance or crosses a static "warning" threshold (e.g., turbidity > 5 NTU), the device immediately switches to **High-Frequency Mode**.
*   **High-Frequency Mode:** The sensor samples every 60 seconds for a 15-minute window. If three consecutive samples confirm the anomaly, the device bypasses aggregation and transmits an immediate "Critical Alert" packet.
*   **Fail-Safe:** If the device cannot confirm the spike due to noise but readings remain elevated, it defaults to sending the alert rather than suppressing it to save battery. Safety overrides efficiency.

**2. Instant Notification Response Plan**
Since rural connectivity may be intermittent, the notification chain must be redundant and human-centric:
*   **Primary Channel:** Upon receiving a "Critical Alert," the central server instantly pushes an SMS and voice call to the designated Village Water Officer and District Health Official. Do not rely solely on app notifications or email.
*   **Secondary Channel:** If the primary contacts do not acknowledge receipt within 10 minutes (via a reply code or button press), the system automatically escalates to a secondary list of regional supervisors.
*   **Local Signal:** If the sensor hub supports it, trigger a local audible siren or flashing light at the water source to warn residents physically before digital confirmation arrives.
*   **Protocol:** Pre-draft "Stop Use" advisories for local leaders to broadcast via community radio or loudspeakers immediately upon alert receipt, pending lab verification.

**3. Pre-Launch Validation Steps**
Before going live next month, execute these specific tests:
*   **Injection Testing:** Physically introduce safe tracer contaminants (e.g., saline solution for conductivity, non-toxic dye for turbidity) to verify the edge device detects the spike and switches sampling rates within 2 minutes.
*   **Blackout Simulation:** Cut power and network connectivity during a simulated event to ensure the device queues the alert and transmits immediately upon restoration.
*   **Drill:** Conduct a full end-to-end drill with local authorities to measure the time from "sensor spike" to "official acknowledgment." Target: <15 minutes.

**Note:** This plan treats false positives as acceptable costs to prevent false negatives (missed contamination). Ensure local authorities have printed copies of the response protocol in case digital systems fail during an actual event.
