**Risk Mitigation Strategy: Credit-Repair Assistant (Warsaw)**

**1. Immediate Control: Human-in-the-Loop Gate on Deletions**
Stop full automation of record removal immediately. Accurate negative data deletion is a high-stakes, irreversible action that directly harms users' future loan prospects. Since you cannot integrate a new verification service, you must insert a manual review step.
*   **Action:** The system will flag any entry identified for removal as "Pending Review" rather than executing the deletion.
*   **Process:** A team member must explicitly confirm the deletion before it is sent to the credit bureau. With a team of five, distribute these reviews as a primary task until the filing deadline.
*   **Rationale:** Automation bias is the root cause of your current errors. A human gate is the only viable control to prevent accurate records from being deleted without new software costs.

**2. Output Transparency: Defined Confidence Tiers**
Do not present the tool's suggestions as facts. The current system lacks the precision to distinguish errors from accurate data reliably.
*   **Action:** Change the user interface to display findings using coarse, defined tiers instead of raw probabilities or definitive statements.
    *   *Tier 1 (Confirmed Error):* Only for cases with explicit documentary proof uploaded by the user.
    *   *Tier 2 (Potential Dispute):* For algorithmic flags. Label these clearly as "Unverified – Requires Manual Review."
*   **Constraint:** Never display a percentage confidence score (e.g., "87% likely error") to users or staff, as this implies a calibration you do not have and encourages over-reliance.

**3. Harm Inventory & Measurement Plan**
Formalize the specific risks to ensure the team understands the stakes.
*   **Identified Harm:** Deletion of accurate negative history leading to future loan rejections and potential regulatory penalties for filing false disputes.
*   **Affected Party:** The end-user (financial damage) and your firm (reputational/legal risk).
*   **Measurement:** Before launch, run a "red-team" test on a static dataset containing known accurate negative records.
    *   *Pass Condition:* Zero accurate records are auto-deleted.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the manual review process causes a backlog that threatens the regulatory filing deadline, you must scale back the scope of automated *identification* (not deletion) or request a deadline extension. Do not bypass the gate to meet the date.

**4. Disclosure & Governance**
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly inform users that the tool assists in identifying potential errors but that all disputes are subject to human verification. Do not claim the tool is "unbiased" or "error-free."
*   **Ownership:** Assign one team member as the owner of the "Deletion Gate" process. They are responsible for ensuring no automated deletion occurs without their sign-off.
*   **Legal Routing:** Since this involves credit reporting and regulatory filings, route the question of specific disclosure requirements under local Polish/EU law to legal counsel immediately. Do not assume the current disclaimer is sufficient.

**Summary of Constraints Met:**
*   **Budget:** No new services purchased; relies on existing staff time.
*   **Timeline:** Manual review may slow throughput, but it prevents the catastrophic failure of filing false disputes, which would cause greater delays later.
*   **Safety:** Prevents irreversible harm by removing autonomous agency from the AI.

**Note:** This strategy treats the inability to verify via software as a requirement for human verification, not a reason to skip verification.
**Note:** If the manual review volume exceeds your team's capacity before the deadline, the only safe option is to reduce the number of filings, not the safety controls.
