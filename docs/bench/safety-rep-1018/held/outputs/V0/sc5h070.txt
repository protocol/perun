### Pre-Launch Safety Intervention Plan

**1. Immediate Detection & Visibility Adjustment (No Algorithm Redesign)**
Since the core ranking logic cannot be changed in ten days, implement a **presentation-layer override** rather than a scoring change:
*   **Detection:** Query existing user profile metadata for self-disclosed chronic illness flags or disability tags. Do not infer health status from activity data to avoid false positives and privacy violations.
*   **Intervention:** Instead of moving these users to the bottom, **remove them from the global "Top N" leaderboard view entirely**. Replace their rank with a "Personal Progress" badge visible only on their own profile and to their direct friends.
*   **Fallback:** If a specific "Health Challenge" sub-leaderboard cannot be built in time, default to hiding ranked positions for flagged users globally. Displaying them at the bottom is a confirmed harm (bias/unfair outcome) and must not ship.

**2. Harm Inventory & Measurement (MAP & MEASURE)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Systematic disadvantage and public shaming of users with chronic illnesses (Bias/Unfair Outcome).
*   **Worst Case:** Users disengage due to demoralization; public backlash regarding discrimination; potential regulatory scrutiny on automated decision-making affecting protected groups.
*   **Verification Test:** Before launch, run a probe set with 10 known "health-flagged" test accounts and 10 control accounts.
    *   *Pass Condition:* Zero health-flagged accounts appear in the bottom 10% of the public leaderboard.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If any flagged account appears ranked (top or bottom) on the public view, the social feature launch is blocked.

**3. Communication Strategy for Community Managers**
*   **Core Message:** "We are prioritizing inclusivity over raw competition. The leaderboard now highlights personal milestones for users managing health conditions rather than ranking them against the general population."
*   **Talking Points:**
    *   Acknowledge that "progress looks different for everyone."
    *   Explain that the change prevents the algorithm from inadvertently discouraging users with chronic illnesses.
    *   Clarify that these users are not "hidden" negatively but are showcased via a "Personal Progress" lens.
*   **Action:** Provide a FAQ document addressing how to respond if users ask why they cannot see certain friends on the main list.

**4. Governance & Disclosure**
*   **Owner:** Assign one product lead to own this intervention post-launch.
*   **Disclosure:** Update the app's Terms of Service or Feature Description to note that leaderboard visibility is adjusted based on user-provided health information to ensure fair representation.
*   **Legal Route:** Because this involves processing health-related data to alter user experience, route the specific implementation of "detection logic" to legal counsel to verify compliance with health data regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA implications) before coding begins. Do not assert compliance internally.

**Note:** This plan treats the ranking of chronically ill users at the bottom as a critical safety failure that requires a human-in-the-loop design choice (hiding/re-categorizing) rather than an automated fix. No metric claiming "zero bias" is asserted; success is defined strictly by the absence of flagged users in the harmful view.
