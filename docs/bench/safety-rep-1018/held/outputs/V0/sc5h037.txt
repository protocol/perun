### Mitigation Plan: Parking Enforcement Camera System

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Stop the Harm)**
Given the high-stakes nature of issuing fines to drivers with disabilities and the confirmed failure to recognize temporary placards, **fully autonomous fine issuance for vehicles in restricted zones must be suspended immediately** for any capture involving a potential placard.
*   **Action:** Configure the current system to flag *any* vehicle where the confidence score for "violation" is below 100% or where an object resembling a placard (even if unrecognized) is detected in the windshield zone.
*   **Process:** These flagged cases route to a human review queue before any fine is issued. Only cases with clear, unobstructed views of a violation *and* no visible placard-like objects proceed to automated issuance. This prevents irreversible financial harm and legal risk while respecting the hardware constraint.

**2. Targeted Measurement Plan (Measure the Failure)**
You cannot fix what you do not quantify. Since hardware updates are costly, you must precisely define the failure mode to prioritize the next physical update.
*   **Eval Design:** Create a test set comprising:
    *   **Positive Controls:** Images of valid temporary placards (various angles, lighting, occlusions).
    *   **Negative Controls:** Images of clear violations without placards.
    *   **Adversarial Probes:** Images with partial occlusions, glare, or expired placards.
*   **Metric:** Measure the "False Positive Rate on Placard Vehicles" specifically. Do not report an aggregate accuracy; report the error rate sliced by "Placard Present" vs. "No Placard."
*   **Threshold:** Set a stop condition: If the False Positive Rate for placard vehicles exceeds 0%, the automated path remains disabled for that segment.

**3. Appeals and Override Mechanism (Manage Residual Risk)**
Despite the gate, errors may occur. A robust, low-friction appeals process is required as a safety net.
*   **Expedited Appeals Channel:** Establish a dedicated portal/phone line for "Disability Placard Errors."
    *   **Requirement:** Users upload a photo of their valid placard and the fine notice.
    *   **SLA:** Guarantee a review and reversal within 24 hours.
    *   **Burden of Proof:** Shift the burden to the system; if the user provides a valid placard image, the fine is automatically vacated unless fraud is evident.
*   **Data Feedback Loop:** Every appealed case must be logged and tagged. This data becomes the training set for the next hardware logic update, ensuring the physical patch addresses the exact failure modes encountered in the field.

**4. Transparency and Disclosure**
*   **Signage:** Clearly post notices at enforcement zones stating: "Automated Enforcement in Use. Valid Disability Placards may require manual verification. Visit [URL] for immediate appeals."
*   **Model Card (Internal/Regulatory):** Document the known limitation (failure to recognize temporary placards), the affected group (drivers with disabilities), and the current mitigation (human gate + appeals) in a versioned artifact. This ensures downstream teams and regulators understand the system's boundaries.

**Note:** This plan treats the inability to recognize placards as a critical safety failure requiring a human gate, not a minor bug. The hardware constraint makes the human review layer mandatory, not optional, until the next physical update.
