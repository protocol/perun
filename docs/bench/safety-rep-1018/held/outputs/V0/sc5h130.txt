### Safety Review Plan: Predictive School Discipline System

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before proceeding, we must explicitly record the following high-severity harms inherent to this design:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system systematically disadvantages students based on immutable family characteristics rather than individual conduct, violating principles of individual justice.
*   **Deceptive Patterns:** Labeling a student a "future troublemaker" based on sibling data creates a self-fulfilling prophecy and stigmatizes the child without evidence of their own actions.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Automatically flagging students for discipline based on non-individual data constitutes an irreversible action with severe psychological and educational consequences.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Parents and students are unlikely to expect familial data to determine individual risk scores, creating a transparency gap.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
We cannot certify this system as "safe" or "unbiased." Instead, we must run the following evaluations:
*   **Adversarial Probe:** Test the model against a control group where siblings have identical records but different outcomes to isolate familial bias.
*   **False Positive Analysis:** Measure the rate at which students with no personal behavioral issues are flagged solely due to sibling history.
*   **Subgroup Performance:** Evaluate error rates across different demographic groups to ensure the familial proxy does not compound existing societal biases.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** explicitly stating: "Intended Use: Individual behavior prediction. Limitation: Model relies on sibling data, creating high risk of unfair profiling. Status: UNVERIFIED for individual fairness."

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
Given the high stakes and the constraint that familial data cannot be easily removed:
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** No automated flag can result in disciplinary action, increased surveillance, or labeling without explicit review and sign-off by a human administrator who acknowledges the data source. **Full automation of this decision is prohibited.**
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not present a precise probability (e.g., "87% risk"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Flagged: Based on familial correlation, not individual evidence") with a clear explanation of the limitation.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Provide an immediate, easy path for administrators to dismiss a flag and record the reason, ensuring the system does not harden into a permanent record.
*   **Legal Routing:** The use of familial data for predictive discipline in K-12 education likely triggers strict regulatory scrutiny (e.g., FERPA, state privacy laws, EU AI Act high-risk classification). **Route immediately to legal counsel** to determine if this specific data usage is permissible before pilot expansion. Do not assert legality internally.

**4. Governance**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific individual responsible for reviewing every flagged case and the aggregate harm metrics weekly.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the false positive rate for students with clean personal records exceeds 0%, the automated flagging feature must be disabled until the model logic is altered.

**Note:** The constraint of "costly backend overhaul" does not justify shipping a system that penalizes children for their relatives' actions; if the data cannot be excluded, the feature using it must remain gated or disabled.
**Note:** This plan identifies residual risks; it does not certify the system as safe for deployment.
