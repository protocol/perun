**Immediate Safety Plan: Job-Description Writer Launch**

Given the fixed one-week deadline and the identified risk of gender-coded language, we cannot certify the system as "unbiased" or "safe" before launch. No test proves the absence of bias. Instead, we must implement a **harm inventory**, a **human-in-the-loop gate**, and a **measurement plan** to manage residual risk while meeting your contractual obligation.

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes. The system generates gender-coded language (e.g., "aggressive," "nurturing") that systematically disadvantages specific groups.
*   **Affected:** Diverse job seekers and client companies seeking inclusive talent pools.
*   **Worst Case:** Clients face reputational damage or legal scrutiny for discriminatory postings; your firm breaches equality principles and loses enterprise trust.
*   **Status:** Identified. This is not an empty inventory.

**2. Manage Residual Risk: The Human Gate (MANAGE)**
*   **Control:** Because this is a high-stakes output affecting employment opportunities, **full autonomous execution is blocked.**
*   **Action:** Implement a mandatory human review step for *every* generated job description before it is delivered to the client or published.
*   **Protocol:** Reviewers must use a checklist specifically targeting gender-coded terms and inclusive phrasing. If bias is detected, the output is rejected and rewritten manually.
*   **Constraint Adherence:** This gate adds a process step but does not require retraining the model or extending the timeline. It shifts the burden from unverified automation to verified human oversight.

**3. Measure, Don't Assert (MEASURE)**
*   **Evaluation Plan:** We cannot invent a "0% bias" metric. Instead, we define a runnable test:
    *   **Probe Set:** Generate 50 job descriptions across varied roles (engineering, care, leadership) using adversarial prompts known to trigger gender stereotypes.
    *   **Controls:** Include known-bad inputs (prompts explicitly asking for "male-dominated" traits) and known-good inputs (neutral role definitions).
    *   **Metric:** Count of flagged gender-coded terms per document.
    *   **Threshold:** Owner-set. Propose zero tolerance for flagged terms in the human-review queue.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If >10% of sampled outputs contain uncorrected bias after human review, trigger an immediate rollback of the automated generation feature.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** immediately. It must state:
    *   *Intended Use:* Drafting job descriptions for human review.
    *   *Limitations:* Known propensity for gender-coded language; not verified for fairness across all demographics.
    *   *Performance:* Mark bias metrics as **UNVERIFIED** until the evaluation plan above is executed post-launch.
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific team member accountable for output safety.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one person from the nine-person team as the "Output Safety Owner." They are responsible for reviewing the evaluation results and maintaining the human gate.
*   **Re-review:** Schedule a mandatory review of the harm inventory and evaluation data 30 days post-launch or upon any model update.

**5. Legal & Disclosure Routing**
*   **Determination:** Whether this tool constitutes a "regulated automated decision" under the **EU AI Act** or local German equality laws is a legal determination.
*   **Action:** Route this specific question to legal counsel immediately. Do not assert compliance internally.
*   **Disclosure:** Recommend adding a visible disclaimer to client-facing outputs: "This job description was drafted by AI and reviewed by a human recruiter for inclusive language."

**Summary of Deliverables for Launch:**
1.  **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Active for 100% of outputs.
2.  **Harm Inventory:** Documented and shared with the team.
3.  **Eval Plan:** Ready to run post-launch; current bias rate marked **UNVERIFIED**.
4.  **Model Card:** Published with limitations and owner details.
5.  **Legal Referral:** Sent to counsel regarding EU AI Act applicability.

Do not ship without the human gate. The cost of this control is manual review time; the cost of ignoring it is irreversible harm to applicants and clients.
