### Immediate Mitigation Plan for TalentFlow

**1. Halt Automated Rejections (Human-in-the-Loop Gate)**
Effective immediately, disable the rule that auto-rejects candidates based solely on employment gaps. Any application triggering this flag must be routed to a human recruiter for manual review before a decision is rendered. Automating irreversible exclusions based on gap duration is a high-stakes action that currently lacks sufficient safeguards against bias. This requires no new budget, only a workflow adjustment.

**2. Implement a "Context Override" Field**
Modify the application intake form to include a mandatory, optional-to-fill text field: "Reason for Employment Gap (e.g., caregiving, education, health)."
*   **Logic Change:** If this field is populated, the system must bypass the gap-penalty algorithm entirely for that candidate.
*   **Disclosure:** Add a brief note near this field stating, "Providing context helps our review team understand your full career story." This satisfies the need for transparency without requiring complex legal determinations at this stage.

**3. Interim Scoring Adjustment (Rule-Based, No Retraining)**
Since retraining is not feasible within your budget and timeline, apply a deterministic code patch to the scoring logic:
*   **Current State:** Gap > X months = Automatic Penalty/Rejection.
*   **New State:** Gap > X months = Neutral Score (0 impact).
*   **Rationale:** It is safer to let a human review a candidate with a gap than to systematically exclude a protected class (caregivers) via an unverified model weight. Treat the gap as "Unverified" data rather than negative evidence.

**4. Harm Inventory & Measurement Plan**
You must document the following before launch:
*   **Identified Harm:** Systematic disadvantage against caregivers (bias) and over-reliance on automated gap detection (automation bias).
*   **Measurement:** Do not claim the system is now "fair." Instead, run a retrospective test on historical data:
    *   *Probe:* Run the old vs. new logic on a sample of 500 past applicants with known caregiving gaps.
    *   *Metric:* Compare the pass-through rate of this group.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the new logic still filters out >5% of known caregivers due to other correlated factors, trigger a manual audit of those specific cases.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a one-page "Model Limitation Note" for internal use. State clearly: "This model does not account for caregiving gaps; human review is required for all candidates with employment history interruptions." Do not invent an accuracy percentage for this feature.

**5. Governance & Next Steps**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member to own the "Gap Logic" risk. They must re-review this rule if the model or data sources change.
*   **Legal Routing:** Whether filtering based on gaps violates specific employment laws in your target regions is a legal determination. Route this specific question to counsel immediately; do not assert compliance internally.
*   **Post-Launch:** Plan to collect labeled data on "gap reasons" post-launch to enable proper model retraining in the next budget cycle.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes stopping immediate harm over optimizing model precision, adhering to the constraint that high-stakes exclusions require human oversight when algorithmic fairness cannot be mathematically guaranteed.
**Note:** Never present the adjusted score as "bias-free"; explicitly label it as "rule-adjusted pending further data."
