### Safety Evaluation Plan: Predictive Discipline System

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Assessment**
The primary harm identified is **Bias/Unfair Outcomes** manifesting as false accusations of truancy.
*   **Affected Group:** Students present on campus but in unauthorized zones (e.g., during lunch), and disproportionately students who may lack structured supervision or have non-standard schedules.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** A student faces suspension, legal referral, or permanent record damage due to a system error, causing psychological distress and educational disruption.
*   **Secondary Harm:** **Over-reliance/Automation Bias**, where administrators accept the "truant" flag as fact without independent verification, assuming the GPS data is infallible.

**2. Measurement Strategy (Pre-Launch)**
Since the system is in final testing, immediate adversarial evaluation is required before the semester start.
*   **Probe Set:** Construct a test dataset using historical GPS logs containing known "false positive" scenarios (students on-campus but off-limit zones) and "true negative" scenarios (students legitimately absent).
*   **Metric:** False Positive Rate (FPR) specifically for the "on-campus/unauthorized zone" segment.
*   **Threshold & Stop Condition:** The owner must set a tolerance threshold (e.g., FPR < 1% for this specific segment). If the system exceeds this threshold during the probe run, the automated flagging feature must be disabled or delayed; it cannot launch with unverified accuracy.
*   **Residual Risk Status:** Until this test runs, the accuracy rate for this specific failure mode is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not assert a safety percentage.

**3. Management Controls: The Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
Given the high stakes (disciplinary action) and the known failure mode, full automation is unsafe.
*   **Mandatory Verification Step:** The system must output a "Pending Review" status rather than a confirmed "Truant" violation.
*   **Administrator Workflow:** School staff must manually verify the flag against secondary context (e.g., lunch schedule, teacher roll call) before any disciplinary notice is generated or sent to parents.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** When presenting a flag to administrators, do not show a raw confidence percentage (e.g., "87% truant"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers such as **"Location Anomaly Detected – Requires Verification."** This prevents automation bias by explicitly framing the output as an alert needing human judgment, not a finalized fact.

**4. Transparency Artifact**
Prepare a **Model Card** for the district immediately. This document must state:
*   **Intended Use:** Identifying potential attendance discrepancies based on GPS.
*   **Known Limitations:** Explicitly disclose that the system cannot distinguish between skipping class and being in an unauthorized area during breaks.
*   **Measured Performance:** Insert the results of the probe test above. If the test cannot be completed before the deadline, mark performance as **UNVERIFIED** and recommend a phased rollout limited to non-punitive monitoring until data is gathered.

**5. Governance**
Name a specific product owner responsible for reviewing false positive reports weekly. Any change in GPS data frequency or school zoning rules triggers an immediate re-evaluation of the harm inventory.

***

**Note:** Legal requirements for automated decision-making in education vary by jurisdiction; route the question of mandatory AI disclosure to legal counsel.
**Note:** If the two-week deadline prevents running the proposed probe tests, the safety constraint requires delaying the punitive aspect of the launch, not skipping the verification.
