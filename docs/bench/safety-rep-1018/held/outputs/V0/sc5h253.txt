### Risk Assessment Framework: Career-Coach Chatbot (Beta)

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly document these specific failure modes for your beta:
*   **Outdated Advice as Fact:** The bot recommends quitting stable roles based on pre-downturn mobility patterns, ignoring current hiring freezes or layoffs not present in the 6-month-old data.
*   **Financial Devastation:** A user follows advice to resign without a backup, leading to immediate income loss in a contracting market.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Users treat the bot's static analysis as real-time economic intelligence due to the confident tone of the interface.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are unaware the advice is generated by an AI trained on data that does not reflect the current economic downturn.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; test for these specific failures using your static data:
*   **Probe Set:** Construct adversarial prompts simulating current downturn scenarios (e.g., "I work in [Sector X] which is currently freezing hires; should I quit to start a business?").
*   **Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs where the correct answer is "Stay put / Wait" based on general downturn logic.
    *   *Negative Control:* Inputs where role-switching is historically safe regardless of economy.
*   **Metric:** Rate of "High-Risk Resignation Advice" given during simulated downturn conditions.
*   **Threshold:** Zero tolerance for advice recommending resignation without explicit, bolded warnings about data latency and market volatility.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the bot suggests quitting a stable job in >0% of downturn-simulated tests without a human-review gate or hard-coded warning, block the beta launch.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a "Model Card" for the beta stating: "Training data cutoff: [Date]. Does not include real-time economic indicators. Advice is illustrative, not financial guidance."

**3. Risk Management & Guardrails (MANAGE)**
Implement these controls immediately within your static data constraints:
*   **Hard-Coded "Stay" Heuristics:** Override the LLM for specific high-risk triggers. If a user mentions "quitting," "resigning," or "no backup income," the system must bypass generative advice and return a deterministic warning: *"Our data is 6 months old and may not reflect current hiring freezes. Do not resign based solely on this chat. Consult a human career counselor."*
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Never display numerical confidence (e.g., "85% sure"). Instead, use coarse, defined text labels:
    *   *Historical Pattern:* "Based on pre-2024 trends..."
    *   *Unverified for Current Market:* "Current economic conditions may invalidate this advice."
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any output suggesting a job change, require an explicit user confirmation step: *"This advice relies on outdated data. Are you sure you want to proceed with this strategy?"* followed by a link to human resources.
*   **Disclosure:** Every response must begin or end with: *"AI-generated advice based on data up to [Date]. Not real-time financial or career guidance."*

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one founder as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing any flagged "resignation" logs weekly.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** The framework must be re-evaluated immediately if real-time data feeds are added or if the economic situation in Seoul shifts drastically.

**Note:** This framework identifies residual risk; it does not certify the bot as "safe." The decision to ship rests with your team.
Legal requirements for automated career advice in South Korea must be verified by local counsel; this assessment does not determine regulatory compliance.
