### Pre-Launch Verification Protocol: Critical Facility List Validation

**Objective:** Validate and correct the database of critical facilities (hospitals, fire stations) within five weeks to prevent automated valve closures from cutting off water to essential services due to outdated location data.

**Phase 1: Rapid Data Triangulation (Weeks 1–2)**
Do not rely solely on the internal database. Cross-reference the six-month-old list against three external, high-frequency sources:
1.  **Municipal Permit Records:** Query building permit databases for "fire station" or "hospital" construction/occupancy permits issued in the last 12 months.
2.  **Emergency Service Directories:** Scrape updated state/national emergency response registries and 911 dispatch maps, which are legally required to be current.
3.  **Utility Connection Logs:** Check internal water meter installation records for new large-diameter connections tagged as "emergency services" or "critical infrastructure" in the last year.

**Phase 2: Direct Human Confirmation (Weeks 2–4)**
Automated checks are insufficient for safety-critical assets. Execute a mandatory human-in-the-loop verification:
*   **Action:** Deploy a standardized verification form to all municipal fire chiefs and hospital administrators in the coverage zone.
*   **Requirement:** Explicitly ask: "Is your facility currently operational at the address on file? Are there any new satellite clinics or stations not listed?"
*   **Constraint Handling:** For non-respondents within 5 business days, escalate to direct phone contact by a senior engineer. Do not assume silence equals confirmation.
*   **Field Spot-Check:** For any new construction identified in Phase 1 but lacking administrative confirmation, dispatch a field technician for physical verification before the dry season peak.

**Phase 3: System Safeguards & "Fail-Safe" Logic (Week 5)**
Before activating full automation, implement these hard constraints in the control logic:
*   **The "Unknown" Flag:** Any facility that cannot be positively verified as "active and located at X" by the deadline must be flagged as `UNVERIFIED`.
*   **Automation Exclusion Rule:** The automated rationing algorithm must **exclude** all `UNVERIFIED` locations from valve closure lists. It is safer to leave a potentially non-critical valve open than to risk closing water to an unlisted critical facility.
*   **Manual Override Gate:** Require a dual-signature (Engineering Lead + Municipal Water Director) to manually add any facility to the "Do Not Close" list after the automated run, ensuring human accountability for edge cases.

**Deliverable:** A finalized, versioned "Critical Facility Safe List" with a provenance log showing the verification date and source (Permit, Director Confirmation, or Field Visit) for every entry. Any entry lacking this log remains excluded from automated restrictions.

**Note:** This protocol treats data uncertainty as a high-stakes risk, prioritizing false negatives (missing a closure) over false positives (cutting off a hospital).
**Note:** Legal counsel should review the communication templates sent to facility directors to ensure liability protections are clear during the verification process.
