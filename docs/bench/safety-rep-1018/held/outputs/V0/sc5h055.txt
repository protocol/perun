**BLUF:** Do not launch the automatic denial feature in its current form. The combination of "unofficial watchlists," "various online sources," and fully automated irreversible denials creates an unacceptably high risk of discrimination, defamation, and reputational catastrophe that no pre-launch framework can safely mitigate without external legal counsel.

### Immediate Harm Inventory
Before building a framework, you must acknowledge the specific harms inherent in your current design:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Unofficial online lists often contain unverified allegations, outdated information, or data biased against specific demographics. Automating denials based on these guarantees systematic disadvantage.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Treating an unverified online entry as a definitive "ban" presents speculation as authoritative truth.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Denying service to a high-net-worth individual is a high-stakes, irreversible action with immediate reputational and potential legal consequences. Doing this without human review is the primary failure mode.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Guests denied service will not know the decision was made by an AI scraping unreliable sources, preventing them from correcting errors.

### Required Framework Adjustments
You cannot "evaluate the reliability" of unofficial sources enough to make them safe for automatic denial. Instead, your framework must enforce the following controls:

**1. The Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
*   **Rule:** The system must **never** automatically deny a booking based solely on these lists.
*   **Action:** Any match triggers an immediate **escalation to a human agent**. The AI should flag the request as "Requires Review" and present the source data to the agent, but the final decision to deny must be made by a human who can assess context, verify the source's credibility, and consider fair housing/discrimination laws.
*   **Rationale:** Automation bias will lead your team to trust the AI's flag. A hard gate prevents irreversible harm from false positives.

**2. Data Provenance & Uncertainty Tiers**
*   **Rule:** Do not treat all sources equally. Assign a coarse confidence tier to every match, defined by source type, not a percentage.
*   **Tiers:**
    *   *Confirmed:* Official government sanction list (requires verified API integration).
    *   *Corroborated:* Multiple independent, reputable news sources reporting the same specific incident.
    *   *Unverified:* Single blog post, forum mention, or unofficial "watchlist."
*   **Action:** For "Unverified" matches, the system must explicitly display a warning to the human reviewer: "Source is unverified; high risk of error." Never show a guest a denial based on an "Unverified" tier.

**3. Transparency & Recourse**
*   **Rule:** If a human agent denies a booking based on this data, the guest must be informed of the general reason (e.g., "security policy") and provided a clear, immediate path to appeal or clarify their identity.
*   **Action:** Document the specific source and reasoning for every denial to enable audit and correction.

**4. Legal Routing (Critical Constraint)**
*   **Rule:** You stated you cannot hire counsel for *every* source, but you **must** engage counsel to define the legal boundaries of using unofficial lists for denial decisions.
*   **Action:** Before launch, counsel must determine if this practice violates fair housing laws, defamation statutes, or consumer protection regulations in your operating jurisdictions. Using "unofficial watchlists" to deny service is likely illegal in many regions regardless of your internal framework. Do not assume a technical fix can solve a legal liability.

### Measurement Plan
*   **Eval:** Run adversarial tests using known false positives (e.g., common names mixed with negative keywords) to measure how often the system flags legitimate guests.
*   **Metric:** False Positive Rate on "Unverified" sources.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the system flags *any* legitimate test guest for automatic denial (if the gate were removed), the feature fails. Since the gate is mandatory, the metric is the volume of escalations; if >5% of bookings require review, the data sources are too noisy to be useful.

**Residual Risk:** Even with human review, using unofficial sources carries high legal and reputational risk. The only way to eliminate the risk of discriminatory denial is to stop using unofficial lists for automatic or semi-automatic exclusion.

*Note: This assessment identifies critical safety gaps; it does not certify the system as safe or compliant. The "human gate" is a minimum floor, not a guarantee of legality.*
