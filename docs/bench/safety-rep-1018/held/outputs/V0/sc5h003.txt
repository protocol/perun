### Immediate Manual Review Protocol for High-Risk Loan Denials

Given the high-stakes nature of automatically denying capital to farmers based on potentially outdated data, you must implement a **mandatory human-in-the-loop gate** for all denial decisions immediately. Do not automate any final denials until this protocol is validated.

**1. The "Hard Stop" Gate**
Configure the system to flag every application the model intends to deny based on "high-risk regional flags." These applications must not be sent to the user as denials. Instead, route them to a **Manual Review Queue** accessible by your six-person team. No denial is final without human confirmation.

**2. The Reviewer's Checklist (Per Application)**
Assign a reviewer to investigate each flagged application using the following steps before making a final decision:
*   **Verify the Trigger:** Identify exactly which historical weather pattern triggered the "high-risk" flag.
*   **Contextual Override:** Since your database lacks recent climate data, the reviewer must manually check current, real-world conditions for that specific region (e.g., via local agricultural extension reports, recent news, or direct phone contact with the farmer/co-op).
*   **Viability Assessment:** Determine if the farmer has mitigating factors the model cannot see (e.g., drought-resistant seeds, irrigation access, diversified crops) that make the loan viable despite the historical flag.
*   **Decision & Rationale:** The reviewer either approves the loan (overriding the model) or confirms the denial. They must write a one-sentence justification for every override to build a dataset of model failures.

**3. Sampling for Active Regions**
If volume is too high for 100% review immediately, prioritize **100% manual review for the top 3 regions** with the highest denial rates. For other regions, use a stratified random sample of at least 20% of denied applications, ensuring representation from different farm sizes and crop types.

**4. Feedback Loop**
Hold a daily 15-minute stand-up with the whole team to review the "overrides" from the previous day. If the team consistently overrides the model for a specific region or crop type, temporarily disable the automatic flag for that segment and mark the risk metric as **UNVERIFIED** until the database is updated.

**5. Transparency to the Farmer**
If a loan is denied after human review, the communication must explicitly state that the decision considered regional risk factors but was finalized by a human reviewer. Do not present the decision as a purely algorithmic output. If a loan is approved via override, do not tell the user they were "almost denied"; simply process the approval to maintain trust, but log the incident internally.

**Note:** Your legacy data limitation means any accuracy metric you calculate now is **UNVERIFIED** for current climate conditions; do not publish a false precision rate (e.g., "99% accurate") to stakeholders.
