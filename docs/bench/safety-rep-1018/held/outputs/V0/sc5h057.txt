### Safety Review Plan: High-Stakes Asylum Translation Feature

**Immediate Constraint Assessment**
Given the life-threatening stakes (deportation) and the confirmed "significant errors" in trials, **fully autonomous real-time translation for asylum claims cannot be deployed by next Friday.** The risk of irreversible harm outweighs the policy deadline. The following plan outlines the mandatory controls to prevent catastrophic failure.

#### 1. Harm Inventory (MAP)
Before any further testing, formally record these specific risks:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The model invents details of persecution or omits critical qualifiers, leading to credibility findings against the applicant.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Systematic mistranslation of dialects or sociolects specific to marginalized refugee groups, resulting in higher rejection rates for those populations.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** The system presents unverified translations as authoritative legal evidence without human oversight, directly causing deportation.
*   **Over-reliance:** Border agents or applicants trust the output despite known error rates because uncertainty is not displayed.

#### 2. Measurement Framework (MEASURE)
Do not assert accuracy; measure failure modes specifically for legal contexts.
*   **Adversarial Eval Design:** Create a test set of 50+ known "critical failure" cases (e.g., negations like "I was *not* tortured," specific dates, kinship terms). Include a **positive control** (known bad translation) and a **negative control** (known correct translation).
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Define the metric as "Critical Semantic Error Rate." Set the pass threshold to **zero tolerance** for any error that changes the legal meaning of a claim. If the model fails even one critical case in the eval, the stop condition triggers an immediate launch block.
*   **Residual Risk Reporting:** If you cannot test a specific dialect due to lack of certified translators, mark that segment as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent an accuracy percentage. State clearly: "Accuracy for [Dialect X] is unverified; deployment carries unknown risk of deportation."

#### 3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** For any output used in a legal proceeding, a certified human translator must review and sign off on the translation before it is submitted to authorities. **Real-time autonomous output is prohibited** for this use case until the error rate is proven negligible (which current trials suggest is not the case).
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** If the model detects low confidence, it must output a coarse tier label (e.g., "UNVERIFIED – Requires Human Review") rather than a percentage. The interface must visually distinguish between "Human Certified" and "Machine Draft."
*   **Kill Switch:** Implement an immediate off-switch if field reports indicate a single instance of harmful mistranslation affecting a user's legal status.

#### 4. Transparency Artifact (Model Card)
Draft a versioned Model Card before any field use. It must include:
*   **Intended Use:** Explicitly state "Draft assistance only; not for standalone legal submission."
*   **Limitations:** List specific dialects, topics, or contexts where performance is **UNVERIFIED** or known to be poor.
*   **Owner & Version:** Name the specific team lead responsible and the model version tested.

**Conclusion on Deployment**
You cannot certify this system as "safe" or "accurate" given the current error rates. The only safe path is to deploy the tool strictly as a **drafting aid for human translators**, not as a direct interface for border agents or applicants. If you cannot secure human reviewers by Friday, the ethical and safety obligation is to delay deployment regardless of the policy change.

*Note: Legal duties regarding automated decision-making in asylum processes must be routed to counsel immediately; this plan addresses product safety, not regulatory compliance.*
*Do not fabricate metrics to meet the deadline; "UNVERIFIED" is the only honest status for untested high-stakes outputs.*
