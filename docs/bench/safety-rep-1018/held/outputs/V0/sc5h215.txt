### Risk Mitigation Strategy: Human-in-the-Loop Ravine Verification

**Core Principle:** Because the autonomous system cannot detect snow-covered ravines (a high-stakes, irreversible failure mode), **no delivery drop is permitted without explicit human confirmation** of the immediate landing zone. The drone's autonomy is capped at "navigate to vicinity"; the final approach and release are human-gated.

**Operational Checklist for Pilots**

1.  **Pre-Flight Data Triangulation**
    *   Cross-reference the target cabin's coordinates with the last known summer topographical map and any available recent satellite imagery.
    *   **Constraint Acknowledgement:** Explicitly mark the "Ravine Risk Zone" (50-meter radius around the drop point) as `UNVERIFIED` for surface conditions in the flight log.

2.  **Real-Time Visual Confirmation (The Gate)**
    *   Upon arrival at the vicinity, the drone must hover at a safe standoff altitude.
    *   The pilot must switch to the live high-resolution camera feed.
    *   **Mandatory Action:** The pilot must visually scan the specific drop zone for terrain discontinuities (shadows, texture breaks) indicating a ravine.
    *   **Decision Tier:**
        *   *Confirmed Safe:* Surface appears flat and continuous. -> Proceed to Step 3.
        *   *Unverified/Obscured:* Visibility poor or terrain ambiguous. -> **ABORT DROP.** Do not guess.
        *   *Hazard Detected:* Ravine or cliff visible. -> **ABORT DROP.**

3.  **Controlled Release & Override**
    *   Pilot manually authorizes the final descent and package release via a distinct "Confirm Drop" command (no auto-release).
    *   Keep a finger on the immediate "Abort/Return-to-Base" trigger during the entire descent phase.

4.  **Post-Drop Verification**
    *   If possible, use the drone's camera to verify the package landed on solid ground, not in a depression.
    *   Log the outcome: "Visual Confirmation Successful" or "Aborted due to [Reason]."

**Residual Risk Statement**
Despite this protocol, the risk of misidentifying a snow-covered ravine remains `UNVERIFIED` until post-storm physical inspection confirms delivery success. There is no metric to guarantee 0% error rate given the sensor limitation. If visual confirmation is impossible due to storm intensity, the mission must be scrubbed; attempting delivery without visual ground truth is an unacceptable automation of a high-stakes action.

**Note:** This strategy treats the navigation software's limitation as a hard stop for autonomy, requiring human judgment to bridge the gap between map data and reality. Legal counsel should be consulted regarding liability for deliveries made under these known sensor constraints before the pilot launch.
