### Immediate Contingency Protocol: "Fail-Safe Access Recovery"

**Objective:** Prevent permanent asset lockout due to environmental false negatives while maintaining security posture for the exchange listing.

**Step 1: Implement a Hard Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate**
Do not allow the automated system to permanently deny access. Configure the system so that after **two consecutive failed verification attempts**, the user is automatically routed to a manual review queue rather than being locked out.
*   **Action:** Pause the automated "permanent lock" trigger.
*   **Workflow:** Failed selfie → Prompt user to retry once with lighting tips → Second failure → Trigger "Manual Review Request."
*   **Staffing:** Assign at least two of your six team members exclusively to this queue during the launch window. They must verify identity using alternative, non-biometric proofs (e.g., historical transaction signatures, email confirmation codes, or pre-collected KYC document re-validation) before restoring access.

**Step 2: Deploy a "Grace Period" Security Mode**
For users stuck in the review queue, place their accounts in a "Withdrawal-Limited / Deposit-Open" state.
*   **Constraint:** Users cannot move funds out until manual verification is complete, preventing potential exploitation during the delay.
*   **Allowance:** Users can still view balances and receive deposits, reducing panic and demonstrating system liveness to the exchange auditors.

**Step 3: User Communication Strategy (Transparency & Guidance)**
Communicate clearly that the safety mechanism is active, not that the system is broken.
*   **In-App Message:** "For your security, additional verification steps are required due to lighting conditions. Your funds are safe. A team member is reviewing your request manually. Estimated wait time: <X> minutes."
*   **Proactive Tips:** Display dynamic advice before the camera activates: "Ensure even lighting on your face," "Remove glasses if glare is present," "Background should be plain."
*   **Status Page:** Maintain a public status indicator showing "Manual Verification Active" to manage volume expectations without admitting to a "bias" or "failure" rate you have not measured.

**Step 4: Post-Event Audit & Rollback Plan**
*   **Logging:** Record every manual override, including the environmental factor cited (e.g., "low light," "shadow").
*   **Kill Switch:** If the manual queue exceeds a manageable threshold (e.g., >15% of total login attempts), trigger a pre-defined "Maintenance Mode" that pauses all automated logins and requires manual sign-in for everyone, preventing a cascade of failures during the exchange listing.

### Critical Notes
1.  **Do not certify the system as "unbiased" or "safe"** in any public documentation; explicitly state that manual review is a required security layer for edge cases.
2.  **Route legal disclosure requirements** regarding automated decision-making and biometric data usage to counsel immediately; do not assert compliance internally without their validation.
