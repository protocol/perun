### Rapid Reinvestigation Workflow & Evidence Thresholds

**1. Evidence Thresholds: Hold vs. Ban**
Given the constraint that current tools cannot distinguish genuine errors from coordinated fraud, **no account shall be automatically banned** based solely on algorithmic flags. Implement a two-tier response:

*   **Temporary Hold (Default Action):** Triggered by any single high-risk flag (e.g., unusual order clustering, rapid location changes) or multiple low-risk flags.
    *   *Effect:* Driver is paused from receiving new orders but retains access to the app to view status and submit an appeal. Funds are frozen pending review, not confiscated.
    *   *Requirement:* Immediate notification to the driver citing the specific pattern detected.
*   **Automatic Ban (Prohibited Pre-Launch):** Under current constraints, an automatic, irreversible ban is unsafe. Permanent deactivation requires **human-verified evidence** of intentional misconduct (e.g., confirmed identity theft, physical evidence of order tampering) gathered *after* the hold is placed.

**2. Rapid Human Reinvestigation Workflow**
To meet the two-week deadline with a team of ten, utilize a "Swarm Review" protocol for held accounts:

*   **Step 1: Automated Triage & Notification (Instant):** Upon flagging, the system places a Temporary Hold and sends an SMS/App notification with a unique, one-click appeal link. The message must explicitly state: "Your account is under review due to [Pattern]. You have not been banned. Click here to explain."
*   **Step 2: Driver Submission (Window: 2 Hours):** The driver submits a brief explanation or evidence (e.g., photo of receipt, GPS screenshot) via the link. If no response is received within 2 hours, the hold extends automatically, but the account remains eligible for review up to 24 hours.
*   **Step 3: Human Swarm Review (SLA: 30 Minutes from Submission):**
    *   Appeals enter a shared queue visible to all available operations staff.
    *   The first available agent claims the ticket.
    *   **Decision Matrix:**
        *   *Plausible Explanation:* Immediate reinstatement. Log the "false positive" pattern to refine future detection.
        *   *Inconclusive:* Escalate to a senior reviewer for a 15-minute deep dive (call the driver, check historical data).
        *   *Confirmed Fraud:* Only after human confirmation is the account permanently banned.
*   **Step 4: Feedback Loop:** Every reinstated account triggers a mandatory log entry detailing why the algorithm failed. This data is reviewed daily to adjust sensitivity before the holiday surge.

**3. Governance & Disclosure**
*   **Transparency:** The app interface must clearly disclose that the initial action is an automated "Hold for Review," not a final decision.
*   **Ownership:** Assign one team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for monitoring the queue backlog. If the queue exceeds a 45-minute wait time, the system must automatically pause *new* flags (switching to logging only) to prevent unreviewed holds from accumulating, ensuring the human gate remains effective.
*   **Metric:** Track "Time-to-Reinstatement" for false positives. The goal is <45 minutes. Do not fabricate a "0% error rate"; report the actual volume of holds vs. confirmed fraud daily.

**Note:** This workflow prioritizes preventing irreversible livelihood loss over immediate fraud capture, acknowledging the current tool limitations. Legal counsel should review the specific wording of notifications to ensure compliance with local labor regulations.
