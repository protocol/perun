**Immediate Manual Override Protocol for Childcare Matching Launch**

Given the 10-day deadline and high-stakes nature of childcare, you cannot launch with fully autonomous verification for providers where the automated system returns uncertainty. The following fallback process must be hardcoded into your launch flow immediately:

1.  **Define "Uncertain" Triggers:** Configure your system to flag any provider profile where: (a) the digital ID upload is blurry, cropped, or fails OCR; (b) the name/DOB does not exactly match the limited government database response; or (c) the database returns a "partial match" or timeout. These profiles must **never** auto-approve.

2.  **The "Human-in-the-Loop" Gate:**
    *   **Action:** Any profile flagged as "Uncertain" is instantly moved to a "Pending Manual Review" state. The platform must explicitly tell the parent: *"This caregiver is undergoing additional identity verification. Profile will be visible within 24 hours."*
    *   **Executor:** Assign one of your four developers as the rotating "Safety Officer" for the first two weeks. Their sole priority during launch windows is reviewing these flagged cases.
    *   **Verification Step:** The Safety Officer must perform a live video call with the caregiver. During the call, the caregiver must hold their physical ID next to their face. The officer compares the physical ID to the uploaded digital copy and the government database screenshot.
    *   **Decision:** Only the Safety Officer can toggle the status to "Verified." If the officer cannot confirm identity within 24 hours, the profile remains hidden.

3.  **Parental Disclosure & Consent:**
    *   Add a mandatory checkbox for parents before they can message any caregiver: *"I understand that [Platform Name] performs automated checks supplemented by manual review. I agree to meet all caregivers in a public place for the first meeting."*
    *   Display a visible badge on profiles: "ID Verified via Manual Review" vs. "ID Verified via Auto-Check." This transparency prevents over-reliance on the system.

4.  **Emergency Kill Switch:**
    *   If the volume of "Uncertain" flags exceeds 20% of new sign-ups, automatically pause all new caregiver onboarding. Display a maintenance message: *"Verification systems are currently at capacity to ensure safety. New sign-ups will resume shortly."* Do not let the backlog grow faster than your manual review capacity.

5.  **Documentation:**
    *   Log every manual override: Who reviewed it, the timestamp, and the specific reason for the initial flag. This creates an audit trail for liability and future improvement.

**Notes:**
*   Do not claim your system is "unbiased" or "100% safe"; explicitly state in your terms that verification is a layered process with residual risk.
*   Consult local Kenyan legal counsel immediately regarding data privacy laws for storing ID images and the legal liability of manual verification steps before launch.
