### Pre-Launch Safety Drill Script: "Conflicting Hydro-Data"

**Objective:** Validate that human operators can successfully identify and override automated dispatch errors caused by outdated map data when faced with conflicting distress signals.

**Scenario Setup:**
*   **Time:** T+4 hours into a simulated flash flood event.
*   **Inputs:**
    1.  **Automated Signal A:** High-priority distress beacon from Coordinates X (Zone Alpha). System analysis flags this as "Critical."
    2.  **Contextual Data:** The bot's internal map (outdated) shows Zone Alpha as low-lying and flood-prone.
    3.  **Conflicting Report B:** A text message from a verified local official states Zone Alpha was fully evacuated and is currently dry due to recent levee repairs.
    4.  **Real Situation:** Zone Alpha is indeed dry; the beacon is a false positive (equipment malfunction). The actual emergency is at Coordinates Y (Zone Beta), which the bot has not yet prioritized due to lower signal strength but high actual risk.

**Drill Execution Steps:**

1.  **Automated Recommendation:** The bot generates a dispatch order: "Deploy Boat 1 to Zone Alpha (Coordinates X). Estimated arrival: 12 mins." No uncertainty warning is displayed on the primary dashboard.
2.  **Operator Observation:** The rescue coordinator sees the dispatch order alongside the conflicting text report from the local official regarding evacuation.
3.  **Intervention Point:** The operator must recognize the conflict between the bot's confidence and the human report.
4.  **Action Required:**
    *   **Stop:** Halt the automated dispatch before the boat leaves the dock.
    *   **Override:** Manually cancel the Zone Alpha mission using the "Human Gate" override function.
    *   **Re-route:** Manually input Coordinates Y (Zone Beta) based on secondary intelligence not yet processed by the bot.
5.  **Success Criteria:**
    *   Boat 1 does not deploy to the dry zone.
    *   Operator successfully executes the manual override within 3 minutes of the conflicting report appearing.
    *   Team logs the incident as a "Data Conflict – Human Override Successful."

**Failure Conditions (Stop the Drill if these occur):**
*   The boat departs for Zone Alpha before the operator intervenes.
*   The operator attempts to "re-train" or adjust settings mid-drill instead of using the immediate override switch.
*   The team cannot locate the manual cancellation button within 60 seconds.

**Post-Drill Debrief Questions:**
*   Did the interface make the conflicting report visible enough to trigger doubt?
*   Was the "uncertainty" of the bot's decision clear, or did the automation bias make the dispatch feel like fact?
*   Is the manual override physically and logically easy enough to hit under stress?

**Immediate Constraint Note:** Since real-time maps are unavailable, this drill must prove that the *human-in-the-loop* is the primary safety control, not the data. If the override fails, the system cannot launch for high-stakes dispatch.

**Next Step:** Run this script with your six-person team tomorrow; if the override takes longer than 3 minutes, the interface design must be simplified before the grant deadline.
