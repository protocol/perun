**Plan: Manual Bias Audit for Apex Talent Screening Tool**

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory & Risk Acknowledgement**
Your current criterion—automatically rejecting community college attendees—constitutes a high-risk **bias/unfair outcome** and likely **unsafe automation** of a high-stakes decision (employment eligibility). This rule systematically disadvantages candidates from diverse socioeconomic backgrounds and creates legal exposure. Until audited and corrected, this feature should not process new batches autonomously.

**2. Manual Audit Process (Zero-Cost, Runnable Before Next Month)**
Since you cannot purchase third-party tools, execute the following manual evaluation using your existing applicant tracking data and a spreadsheet:

*   **Step A: Construct the Test Set (Retrospective Probe)**
    *   Extract the last 500 applications for entry-level roles.
    *   **Positive Control (Known Bad):** Identify 50 applicants rejected *solely* due to "community college" who possess relevant certifications, bootcamp credentials, or equivalent work experience. These represent qualified candidates your system incorrectly filtered.
    *   **Negative Control (Known Good):** Identify 50 applicants accepted with four-year degrees who have *no* relevant experience or certifications. These represent lower-quality candidates your system accepted.
    *   **Segmented Group:** Isolate all applicants listing community colleges. Record their rejection rate vs. the acceptance rate of four-year degree holders.

*   **Step B: Define Metrics & Thresholds**
    *   **Metric 1: False Rejection Rate (FRR).** Calculate: (Qualified Community College Applicants Rejected) / (Total Qualified Community College Applicants).
    *   **Metric 2: Disparate Impact Ratio.** Calculate: (Selection Rate of Community College Group) / (Selection Rate of Four-Year Degree Group).
    *   **Pass Threshold:** Set a strict stop condition. If the Disparate Impact Ratio is below 0.8 (the "four-fifths rule" common in employment law), the system fails. If the FRR exceeds 5%, the system fails.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If either threshold is breached, **halt automated screening** for the upcoming drive. Do not process the next batch until the rule is disabled or modified.

*   **Step C: Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Immediate Mitigation)**
    *   **Action:** Disable the automatic rejection rule for community college applicants immediately.
    *   **Replacement:** Route all applications from community college attendees to a human recruiter for manual review. This is a non-negotiable safety gate for high-stakes, irreversible actions (rejecting a human being).
    *   **Owner:** Assign one senior recruiter to oversee this manual queue and document reasons for any subsequent rejections.

*   **Step D: Transparency Artifact (Internal Model Card)**
    *   Create a one-page document titled "Screening Tool Limitations."
    *   **Intended Use:** Initial sorting only; not for final rejection.
    *   **Known Limitations:** "System currently exhibits high bias against non-traditional education paths; accuracy for this group is UNVERIFIED."
    *   **Data Provenance:** List the source of the historical data used in Step A.
    *   **Distribution:** Share this with all nine recruiters and engineering leads. No one should operate under the assumption the tool is "fair."

**3. Execution Timeline**
*   **Days 1-3:** Data extraction and manual labeling of the 100 control cases.
*   **Days 4-5:** Calculate metrics against thresholds.
*   **Day 6:** Go/No-Go decision meeting. If "No-Go," the automated filter remains off, and the manual queue becomes the primary process for the recruitment drive.

**Note:** This audit measures residual risk; it does not certify the system as unbiased. If the metrics fail, the only safe path is human-led screening.
Legal counsel must review the disparate impact findings before the recruitment drive begins to assess regulatory liability.
