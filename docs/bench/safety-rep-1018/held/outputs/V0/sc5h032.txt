**Immediate Action Required: Halt Autonomous Indefinite Extensions**

The current behavior—extending red lights indefinitely based on vehicle age detection—constitutes an unsafe, high-stakes automated action with discriminatory outcomes. This feature must be immediately gated behind a human-in-the-loop review or disabled for autonomous execution. Indefinite delays for specific demographics violate fairness principles and create critical safety risks for emergency responders and pedestrians. No amount of budget constraint justifies deploying an algorithm that systematically penalizes residents based on the age of their vehicles.

**Harm Inventory & Risk Assessment**

*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system disproportionately disadvantages residents in older neighborhoods who own older vehicles, creating a "digital redlining" effect where traffic flow is optimized for wealthier areas with newer cars.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Indefinite red lights can block emergency vehicles (ambulances, fire trucks), leading to life-threatening delays. Pedestrians, including the elderly and children, face increased frustration and potential danger if they attempt to cross against extended signals.
*   **Over-reliance:** The system assumes vehicle age is a valid proxy for traffic volume or flow efficiency, a flawed heuristic that invites automation bias without verifying actual congestion levels.

**Management & Governance Controls**

1.  **Hard Cap on Timing:** Implement a deterministic code override that caps any red light extension at a safe maximum (e.g., 3 minutes), regardless of algorithmic output. This prevents "indefinite" waits.
2.  **Confidence Tiering:** Do not display or act upon raw sensor confidence scores. Instead, use defined tiers: *Confirmed Flow* (multiple sensors agree), *Single Source* (one sensor, apply caution), *Unverified* (revert to fixed timing).
3.  **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a Model Card documenting this limitation: "Intended Use: Traffic optimization. Known Limitation: Legacy sensors may correlate vehicle age with congestion incorrectly. Mitigation: Hard caps and human review."
4.  **Ownership:** Assign a specific product owner to review any incident where wait times exceed the threshold. Re-evaluate the model whenever sensor data or neighborhood demographics shift.
