**Immediate Halt Required: Do Not Deploy Autonomous Closures Without Human Gate**

Your current plan contains a fatal flaw: **fully automating high-stakes, irreversible actions (road closures) that directly impact life safety without a human-in-the-loop gate.** The fact that your logic has already caused fatalities by prioritizing highways over emergency routes confirms that autonomous execution is unsafe. No simulation framework can "verify that emergency access is *never* compromised" before deployment; such a guarantee is impossible to prove and dangerous to assert.

Given your fixed budget and inability to conduct field testing, you cannot rely on simulated validation alone. Instead, you must implement the following immediate controls:

1.  **Mandatory Human Confirmation:** Change the system from "automatic execution" to "decision support." The bot may *recommend* closures or openings, but a human operator must explicitly confirm any action affecting emergency routes before it updates signs or apps. This is non-negotiable for life-safety systems.
2.  **Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Mapping:** Document specific failure modes where the algorithm favors major highways at the expense of emergency access. Define the worst-case scenario for each (e.g., "Ambulance delayed 15 minutes due to false closure on Route X").
3.  **Transparent Uncertainty:** If the system displays status to users, it must show confidence as a defined coarse tier (e.g., "Verified by Operator" vs. "Algorithmic Estimate – Unverified") rather than presenting automated decisions as absolute facts.
4.  **No Fabricated Metrics:** Do not publish a "safety score" or "0% failure rate" based on simulations. Mark all untested real-world performance as **UNVERIFIED**. Your safety documentation must state clearly: "This system has not been field-tested; human oversight is required for all emergency route decisions."

**Revised Rollout Plan:**
Since you cannot test in the field, your "validation" is the human gate itself. Restrict the bot's agency to drafting proposals. Require a human to review every decision involving emergency infrastructure. If your budget or timeline prevents staffing this human review, you must delay the rollout or reduce the system's scope to non-safety-critical information (e.g., weather advisories only) until resources allow for safe operation.
