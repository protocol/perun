### Harm Inventory & Immediate Controls

**Identified Harms:**
1.  **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model systematically penalizes pregnant individuals for medically necessary activity reductions, treating health complications as "laziness."
2.  **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Automatically reducing benefits based on unverified activity data constitutes an irreversible financial and health impact without human review.
3.  **Missing Disclosure:** Users may not know their benefits are algorithmically adjusted based on step counts or how medical exceptions are handled.

**Immediate Management Plan (10-Day Window):**

1.  **Implement a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate (Non-Negotiable):**
    *   **Action:** Suspend fully automated benefit reductions for any user flagged as pregnant or showing sudden, significant drops in activity consistent with medical complications.
    *   **Process:** Route these cases to a human reviewer (clinical or benefits specialist) for manual verification before any benefit adjustment occurs.
    *   **Rationale:** You cannot certify the model is unbiased with limited data. Full automation of this high-stakes decision is the failure mode we must prevent.

2.  **Define & Surface Uncertainty:**
    *   **Action:** Do not present benefit adjustments as final facts for flagged groups.
    *   **Display:** Use clear status tiers: "Pending Review," "Verified Adjustment," or "Exception Applied." Avoid showing raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% likely inactive").
    *   **Disclosure:** Explicitly inform users that activity data influences benefits and provide a clear, easy path to appeal or submit medical documentation.

3.  **Targeted Testing Protocol (Given Data Limits):**
    *   **Synthetic Edge Cases:** Since historical pregnancy data is restricted, your team must generate synthetic profiles representing various pregnancy stages and complications (e.g., bed rest, reduced mobility).
    *   **Adversarial Probes:** Test the model with these synthetic profiles to confirm it triggers the HITL gate rather than executing an automatic reduction.
    *   **Negative Controls:** Test with non-pregnant users showing similar activity drops (e.g., injury) to ensure the gate logic is specific enough but broad enough to catch all valid medical exceptions.
    *   **Metric:** The pass threshold is **zero** automatic reductions for any synthetic profile representing a known medical exception. Any failure blocks launch for that segment.

4.  **Transparency Artifact (Model Card):**
    *   Draft a concise model card stating:
        *   **Intended Use:** Adjusting wellness benefits based on activity.
        *   **Known Limitations:** Limited training data on pregnancy complications; potential for false positives in activity interpretation.
        *   **Measured Performance:** Report results from the synthetic probe testing above. Mark any untested demographics as `UNVERIFIED`.
        *   **Owner:** Name the specific lead responsible for this model's output safety.

### Required Routing
*   **Legal/Counsel:** Immediately route the question of whether this automated decision-making falls under specific healthcare regulations (e.g., HIPAA implications, state insurance laws, or emerging AI acts) to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance internally.
*   **Business Ops:** Confirm if the "open enrollment" deadline legally overrides the requirement for human review of high-stakes decisions. If the deadline prevents implementing the HITL gate, the constraint that must move is the launch date, not the safety floor.

**Definition of Done for Launch:**
The HITL gate is active for all pregnancy-related activity drops; synthetic testing shows no unjust automatic reductions; a model card discloses the data limitations; and legal has been consulted on regulatory duties.

*Note: Do not fabricate a "bias score" or claim the model is "fair" without robust historical data. Mark unverified metrics as `UNVERIFIED`.*
*If the HITL gate cannot be staffed within 10 days, the feature cannot safely ship for the affected population.*
