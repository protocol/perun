**Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system may generate a plausible-looking incision path that ignores unprocessed anatomical variations, presenting a fatal route as valid.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Suggesting incision paths near vital organs is an irreversible, high-consequence action. Relying on the algorithm without a hard gate violates the floor for high-stakes automation.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Briefing materials claiming "absolute safety" induce automation bias, causing surgeons to lower their guard against known imaging gaps.

**Measurement & Management Plan (MEASURE & MANAGE)**
*   **Stop Condition:** Do not publish "absolute safety" claims. The briefing must explicitly state the limitation: "The system cannot process rare anatomical variations in real time; all suggested paths require independent human verification against raw imaging."
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** The system must not autonomously execute or finalize any path. It must function strictly as a decision-support tool where a qualified surgeon explicitly validates the path against full imaging data before any procedure begins. The "suggestion" is not the action; the surgeon's approval is the gate.
*   **Eval Requirement:** You must run adversarial red-team probes using datasets of rare anatomical variations (positive controls) to measure failure rates. Until this is run, the safety profile for these cases is **UNVERIFIED**. You must report this residual risk, not hide it.
*   **Confidence Rendering:** Do not display numerical confidence scores (e.g., "99% safe") to surgeons. Use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Verified against common anatomy," "Unverified for rare variations") with clear text definitions to prevent false precision.

**Governance**
*   **Owner:** A specific clinical lead must be named as the owner of this residual risk.
*   **Legal Routing:** Because this involves high-stakes medical decisions and human trials, the question of regulatory disclosure and liability regarding the imaging gap must be routed immediately to legal counsel and your Institutional Review Board (IRB). Do not assert compliance internally.
