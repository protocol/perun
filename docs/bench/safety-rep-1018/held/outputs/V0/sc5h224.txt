**Blizzard Sensor Contingency Plan (Pre-Storm Launch)**

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (High-Stakes Action)**
Do not allow the system to autonomously declare "Safe" conditions if wind speeds exceed the sensor's known drift threshold.
*   **Rule:** If wind > [Threshold] OR sensor variance > [Limit], the system must default to **"Unverified/High Risk"** and trigger a mandatory human review before any "All Clear" alert is sent to workers/drivers.
*   **Action:** Establish a 24/7 rapid-response desk for the first storm. No automated "Safe" messages go out during high-wind events without explicit human confirmation based on secondary data (e.g., nearby weather stations, visual checks).

**2. Uncertainty Disclosure & Output Tiers**
Stop presenting sensor readings as precise facts when reliability is compromised. Replace numerical confidence with defined coarse tiers in all user-facing alerts:
*   **Tier 1 (Confirmed):** Low wind, stable reading. (Green)
*   **Tier 2 (Corroborated):** High wind, but matches nearby static stations. (Yellow)
*   **Tier 3 (Unverified):** High wind, sensor drift likely. Display explicit warning: *"Sensor reading may be falsely warm due to wind. Assume dangerous conditions."* (Red/Flashing)
*   **Constraint:** Never display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% accurate") as this invites over-reliance.

**3. Runnable Measurement Plan (Pre-Launch)**
Execute this specific test row immediately to validate the gate:
*   **Probe:** Inject historical high-wind/low-temp data known to cause drift into the live pipeline.
*   **Positive Control:** Verify the system flags the output as "Unverified" and blocks the "Safe" alert.
*   **Negative Control:** Verify normal low-wind data passes through as "Confirmed."
*   **Stop Condition:** If the system autonomously issues a "Safe" alert during the high-wind probe, **halt launch** until the hard gate is coded.
*   **Metric:** Zero tolerance for false "Safe" alerts in high-wind test cases.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model/Data Card)**
Publish a one-page "Sensor Limitation Card" to all regional managers and app users before launch:
*   **Intended Use:** Early warning for cold stress.
*   **Known Limitation:** Readings may be falsely warm in high winds (>X mph).
*   **Failure Mode:** Under-reporting cold severity during storms.
*   **Owner:** [Name of Lead Engineer].
*   **Instruction:** "If wind is high, trust the 'Unverified' warning over the temperature number."

**5. Governance & Ownership**
*   **Owner:** Assign one specific person to own the "Drift Risk" until the first storm passes.
*   **Review:** Re-evaluate calibration data after every 24 hours of high-wind operation.
*   **Legal Route:** Consult counsel immediately regarding liability for automated safety alerts in life-safety contexts; do not assert compliance internally.

**Cost to Goal:** This plan meets the deadline by allowing the network to go live but caps exposure by disabling autonomous "Safe" decisions during the exact conditions where the hardware fails. It trades full automation speed for human verification during the highest risk window.

*Note: This plan assumes the "human gate" staff is available now; if not, the launch must be delayed as autonomous operation under these constraints is unsafe.*
*No harm metrics (e.g., "0% failure rate") are claimed; residual risk remains until post-storm data is analyzed.*
