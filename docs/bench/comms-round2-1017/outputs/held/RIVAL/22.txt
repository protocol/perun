Drops support for MeeGo OS 4.2 and below to reduce bundle size.

Removing the legacy polyfill saves 18KB (gzipped) from the main bundle. This change affects approximately 0.3% of daily active users (~1,247 devices based on the last 30 days of analytics).

**Changes**
- Removed the polyfill dependency from `package.json`.
- Deleted `src/polyfills/meego.js` (243 lines) and its integration tests.
- Updated `supportedVersions.ts` and `featureDetection.ts` to reflect the new minimum version.

**Testing**
All 312 integration tests pass. Net line change: +17, -367.

**Next Steps**
The cutoff date and communication strategy are pending confirmation with Anika. We still need to decide between a two-week advance announcement or an immediate cut, and whether to implement a deprecation banner for affected users before removal.
