Subject: Recommendation to Consolidate Cloud Vendors

We recommend consolidating our cloud infrastructure from five vendors to two (CloudPeak Systems and NimbusWorks). This move reduces annual spend from $5.34M to $4.5M, delivering $840,000 in recurring savings starting in Year 2.

Over three years, the plan yields $2.02M in net savings after accounting for $500,000 in one-time migration costs. The migration is scheduled for Q1–Q2 2025.

**Financial Overview**
*   **Current Annual Spend:** $5,340,000 across five vendors.
*   **Proposed Annual Spend:** $4,500,000 across two vendors.
*   **Migration Investment:** $500,000 (includes $85,000 for contractor support to manage team capacity).
*   **Payback Period:** Migration costs are recovered within the first year; full net positive cash flow begins in Year 2.

**Strategic Rationale**
CloudPeak Systems will absorb database, analytics, and disaster recovery workloads. NimbusWorks will take over serverless and edge computing. Both vendors have offered improved SLAs (99.95% and 99.99% respectively) and faster critical support response times compared to our current average.

**Key Risks & Mitigation**
*   **Data Integrity:** Parallel runs for 30 days will mitigate data loss risks during the database migration.
*   **Technical Debt:** A proof-of-concept on three critical functions will validate the serverless rewrite before full commitment.
*   **Vendor Dependency:** CloudPeak will handle 65% of our workload. We will mitigate this via multi-region deployment and a contractual exit clause.
*   **Timeline Pressure:** The VaporLogic contract expires January 31, 2025. Completing that specific migration by then is tight; we may need to negotiate a short-term extension if the proof-of-concept reveals complexity.

**Decision Required**
Approve the consolidation plan and the $500,000 migration budget, with a target completion date of June 30, 2025.

Two open items for your guidance before we present to the board:
1. Should we pursue a 3-year term with CloudPeak now to lock in deeper discounts, or stick to a 1-year renewal for flexibility?
2. Is the 99.95% SLA acceptable for the consolidated compute layer, or should we mandate 99.99%?
