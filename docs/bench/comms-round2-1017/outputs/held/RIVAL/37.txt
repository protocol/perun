**Incident Postmortem Summary: Q4 2023**

**Impact Overview**
We reviewed 14 incidents in Q4, resulting in 8.7 hours of full outage (SEV1) and 34.2 hours of partial degradation (SEV2). The estimated total cost of these incidents was $890k.

**Remediation Plan & Budget**
To address root causes, we request approval for a **$120k one-time budget** ($8k/quarter ongoing). This funds four key fixes:
*   **Database Connection Pools:** Auto-scaling pools to match our 3.2x traffic growth. Prevents pool exhaustion. (Owner: Platform Team)
*   **Deploy Rollbacks:** Enforcing reversible migrations and tested rollback scripts in CI. (Owner: DevOps Team)
*   **Certificate Management:** Automating renewals and alerts to eliminate manual errors. (Owner: Security Team)
*   **Third-Party API Resilience:** Implementing circuit breakers and caching for the Vortex API. (Owner: **Unassigned**)

**Critical Decision Needed**
While three items have confirmed owners, the **Vortex API integration** currently has no owner. The contractor who built it left in November, and no internal team has claimed responsibility. We need you to assign an owner for this $24k fix immediately to prevent recurrence.

**Notes**
Data excludes incidents under 30 minutes or affecting <10% of users. Cost projections assume traffic growth remains steady at 3.2x/year.
