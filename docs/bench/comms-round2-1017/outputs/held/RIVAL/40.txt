**Subject:** Decision Required: Facilities Consolidation Proposal

We recommend consolidating from four offices to two (Portland and Austin) to cut annual real estate costs by $2.1M. This move addresses our current 38% occupancy rate and aligns with hybrid work patterns.

**The Plan**
*   **Portland:** Relocate HQ to a smaller, hybrid-ready space in South Waterfront (28k sq ft). This reduces footprint while increasing collaboration capacity for ~250 daily occupants.
*   **Austin:** Expand the engineering office by 5k sq ft to accommodate ~180 daily occupants.
*   **Denver:** Close the sales office. We can either break the lease ($405k penalty) or sublease (reducing the hit to $216k).
*   **Memphis:** Sell the owned operations building (est. value $3.8M). We will need to relocate or release approximately 40 of the 143 staff members; the remainder would transition to remote work.

**Financial Impact**
*   **One-time costs:** $1.94M – $2.13M (includes build-outs, moving, relocation packages, and lease penalties).
*   **Annual savings:** $2.1M.
*   **Payback period:** Approximately 12 months.

**Key Risks**
*   **Austin Timing:** The adjacent suite is only held until April 30. We need a signed letter of intent to secure it.
*   **Memphis Sale:** Commercial sales are slow; we may carry the building for 12–18 months.
*   **Staffing:** Losing the Denver footprint may impact sales recruiting, and Memphis staff turnover could disrupt operations.
*   **Timeline:** Build-out takes 14–16 weeks; earliest move-in is August.

**Decision Needed**
Please approve proceeding by **April 10** to secure the Austin expansion space.

*Note: Subleasing Denver saves $189k compared to breaking the lease but adds management overhead. Memphis staff relocation packages are estimated at $340k total.*
