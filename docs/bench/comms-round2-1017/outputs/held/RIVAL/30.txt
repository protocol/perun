**Recommendation: Enter Vietnam First**

Vietnam offers the optimal balance of growth potential, manageable risk, and resource efficiency for our initial Southeast Asian expansion. While Indonesia presents the largest total addressable market ($520M), its high setup costs ($610k), extended time to revenue (7 months), and complex regulatory requirements make it a riskier first step given our current team constraints.

Vietnam projects $7.2M in revenue by Year 3 with a moderate $420k setup cost and a 5-month timeline to first revenue. Crucially, the competitive landscape is fragmented with no dominant player, unlike Thailand where a single competitor holds 35% market share. Vietnam also provides the strongest talent pipeline for our specific tech stack and benefits from favorable tax incentives for foreign SaaS companies.

The primary risk involves potential data residency laws expected mid-2025; however, the details remain unclear, and our 5-month launch window allows us to establish operations before regulations solidify. Currency fluctuation (VND down 3.2% this year) is a monitored variable but does not outweigh the strategic advantages.

Choosing Vietnam enables a sequential expansion into Thailand by Q3 2025 and Indonesia by Q1 2026. This path secures early revenue ($1.8M Year 1) while building regional expertise before tackling Indonesia's higher complexity. Three existing multinational clients have already requested local Southeast Asian support, providing immediate pipeline validation.

**Target Launch:** March 1, 2025.
**Required Approval:** $420,000 initial setup budget.

Note: This summary excludes Thailand as the primary choice due to its smaller deal sizes and entrenched local competitor. Indonesia remains the priority for the second phase of expansion.
