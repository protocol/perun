**Executive Summary: Search Platform Build vs. Buy**

**Recommendation:** Proceed with the **Buy Option (Vortex Search)**, contingent on resolving data privacy approval and securing written scalability guarantees.

**Rationale**
The current custom search platform is failing, with low user satisfaction (34%) and high latency (p99: 4.8s). While building "Project Lighthouse" offers long-term control, it presents immediate execution risks we cannot absorb. We have failed to fill three critical search specialist roles since October, making the proposed 8-month MVP timeline unrealistic. The build option requires $1.8M upfront and carries significant key-person risk.

In contrast, Vortex Search delivers core functionality in 3 months versus 8–14 months for building. Although the 5-year TCO ($2.3M) is comparable to the build option's initial outlay, buying reduces time-to-value and eliminates hiring dependency. The hybrid approach is discouraged as it introduces architectural complexity and inconsistent user experiences without solving the core hiring bottleneck.

**Critical Risks & Mitigation**
Two blockers must be cleared before signing:
1.  **Data Privacy:** The data team review is scheduled for March 15. If customer data cannot reside in Vortex's cloud, this option is void.
2.  **Scalability:** Vortex verbally confirmed support for our projected 5M documents by 2026 but refuses written commitment. We must mandate this guarantee in the contract.

Secondary concerns include a 3-year minimum contract with 8% annual price increases and a recent 6-hour vendor outage. However, these are outweighed by the certainty of delivery compared to the high probability of missing build targets.

**Decision Required**
Approach Vortex negotiations with strict clauses on scalability and data residency. If the March 15 privacy review fails or Vortex refuses written scalability terms, we must revert to a scoped-down Build plan, acknowledging the delayed timeline.
