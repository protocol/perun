**Executive Summary: Q3 2024 Reliability Review**

Northwind missed its Q3 uptime SLA (99.87% actual vs. 99.95% target), driven by three SEV1 incidents totaling 4.2 hours of downtime. While detection and acknowledgment metrics met targets, mean time to resolve (47 mins) exceeded the 30-minute goal, and on-call burden remains unsustainable at 2.3 pages per night. Two engineers have indicated they may leave if the on-call situation does not improve.

We spent $42,700 in engineering time this quarter on reactive fixes, including database health check improvements and payment circuit breakers. However, critical gaps remain: auto-scaling cannot handle sudden traffic spikes, database failover procedures are untested, and we lack proactive chaos testing.

To prevent repeat incidents during Q4 holiday traffic and stabilize the team, we propose the following actions totaling $454,000 ($74k one-time, $380k annual):

1.  **Hire 2 Engineers ($380k/year):** Essential to expand the on-call rotation to 8 people, reducing page frequency to under 1 per night and preventing attrition.
2.  **Predictive Auto-Scaling ($25k):** Reduces capacity-related SEV2 incidents by an estimated 60%.
3.  **Chaos Testing Program ($40k first year):** Proactively identifies vulnerabilities before they cause outages.
4.  **Failover Drills & Load Testing ($14k):** Validates runbooks and circuit breakers under production conditions.

*   **Budget Approval:** Do we proceed with the $380k annual investment for two new hires? Without this, we risk losing key staff and repeating Q3 failures during peak traffic.
*   **Project Funding:** Should we fund the $74k in one-time reliability improvements (auto-scaling, chaos testing, drills)?
