Subject: Recommendation on Easton Warehouse Cutover – June 24 vs. July

Martin,

I recommend we postpone the Easton facility go-live from June 24 to early July.

While we have successfully migrated 847 SKUs (73% of the total), we are behind the 85% target set for this week. Of the 312 remaining SKUs, approximately half have data quality issues that block migration. The slip stems from the legacy export from Apex Systems, which took four days instead of one due to what appears to be a network throttle; we are still investigating this with their infrastructure team.

The new system is performing well technically. Our parallel run showed an average pick time of 2.1 seconds versus 1.8 seconds in the old system, which is within our acceptable range. However, proceeding on June 24 with incomplete data poses an operational risk.

We face a trade-off regarding training. Sessions are scheduled for June 18–20 with 42 staff members signed up. Pushing the date may forfeit $18,000 in facilitator fees, but holding the training without a confirmed go-live date creates confusion for the staff.

One alternative is a partial cutover involving only the clean SKUs. The operations team has not yet weighed in on whether this is viable for daily workflows.

Given the data gaps and the unresolved export issue, a short delay allows us to clear the backlog and confirm the root cause of the slowdown. I propose we hold the June 24 date tentatively while we seek operations' feedback on a partial cutover by tomorrow. If they cannot support a partial launch, we will formally move the date to July and reschedule training.
