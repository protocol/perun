**Executive Summary: Q4 Pricing Strategy**

To maximize Q4 revenue, we recommend raising the Northwind Professional tier to **$59/month** effective October 15.

While the $39 option drove 24% more signups, it projects a 7% revenue decline due to lower per-user value and slightly weaker early retention. The $59 variant, conversely, projects a 1% revenue uplift ($29,330 vs. $29,016 monthly) despite an 18% drop in conversion volume. This price point also aligns better with our enterprise goals: companies with 100+ employees converted 14% better at the higher price, suggesting strong value signaling.

**Recommendation on Tiered Pricing:**
Do not approve the $28,000 investment in tiered pricing for Q1 yet. The build would take six weeks, missing the critical early Q4 budget window. Furthermore, current segment data has wide confidence intervals; we need 2–3 more months to confirm if the higher retention seen in the $59 group is statistically significant or just early noise.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1.  **Implement $59 price** by October 15 to capture Q4 budget spending.
2.  **Monitor support tickets:** The high-price variant saw a 12.3% ticket rate (vs. 9.1% control). Prepare customer success teams for higher expectations from enterprise buyers.

This approach prioritizes immediate revenue stability and enterprise positioning while avoiding premature engineering spend on unproven segment differences.
