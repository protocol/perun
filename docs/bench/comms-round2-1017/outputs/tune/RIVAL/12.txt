This PR shifts rate limiting from per-IP to per-account to address the brute-force vulnerability you flagged. The previous threshold (10 attempts per 5 minutes per IP) was trivial to bypass using rotating IPs.

**Changes:**
- **Logic:** Accounts are now locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed attempts within a 15-minute window.
- **Files:** Updated `auth_middleware.py`, `rate_limiter.py`, and `login_handler.py`.
- **Visibility:** Added `X-RateLimit-Remaining` and `X-RateLimit-Reset` headers to help clients track their status.
- **Tests:** Added 12 new cases covering direct login, SSO, and multi-device scenarios. All tests pass.
