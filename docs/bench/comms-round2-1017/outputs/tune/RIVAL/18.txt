**Executive Summary: Data Retention Audit Q3 2024**

Northwind faces immediate regulatory exposure with GDPR corrective actions due December 15, 2024. We currently retain 34,200 customer records beyond the 24-month limit and lack litigation holds on 890 mailboxes. While most findings involve straightforward remediation, two decisions require your direction regarding funding and vendor strategy.

**1. Transaction Record Recovery ($45,000)**
Our migration in 2019 created a 1.8-year gap in transaction records, violating our 7-year retention policy.
*   **Recommendation:** Attempt recovery.
*   **Trade-offs:** Spending $45,000 engages three engineers for four weeks to extract data from archives that may be corrupted. However, accepting the gap creates a definite failure for upcoming financial audits and invites tax authority penalties. Given the low probability of successful recovery versus the certainty of audit failure without it, the investment is justified as a mitigation effort. If recovery fails, we will document the loss formally to demonstrate due diligence to regulators.

**2. Vendors in Non-Compliant Regions (Acme Analytics, DataSync Corp)**
These two vendors store customer data in regions that violate our data sovereignty obligations. Their DPAs are also expired or missing.
*   **Recommendation:** Issue immediate termination notices contingent on data migration.
*   **Trade-offs:** Terminating these contracts may disrupt analytics and synchronization workflows. However, maintaining them breaches our own DPA obligations to customers and exposes Northwind to fines up to 4% of annual revenue ($3.2M). We cannot renew DPAs while data resides in non-compliant regions. The legal risk of inaction far outweighs the operational friction of migrating or replacing these services.

**Financial Overview**
Total remediation requires $183,000 one-time and $16,000 annually. The highest risk items—over-retained data and unencrypted backups—must be resolved by November 30 to meet the December 15 GDPR deadline. Delaying the $45,000 recovery or the vendor migrations increases the likelihood of regulatory fines exceeding $150,000 per incident.

**Decision Required**
Please authorize the $45,000 recovery effort and approve the issuance of termination notices to Acme Analytics and DataSync Corp pending data migration plans.
