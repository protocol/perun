**Executive Summary: Q3 2024 Churn & Critical Decisions**

Q3 churn rose to 15.2% (from 12.8% in Q2), costing $2.1M in ARR. The net customer base shrank by 660. While Enterprise churn remains low (7.9%), SMB churn hit 15.4%, driven primarily by onboarding failures, and Mid-Market churn jumped to 11.7% due to pricing pressure.

Three immediate decisions are required to stop revenue bleed:

**1. Mid-Market Pricing Strategy**
Twenty-two Mid-Market accounts left citing the January 15% price hike; eight stated they would stay if we matched Competitor X's pricing (now 22% cheaper).
*   **Decision:** Authorize loyalty discounts or price-matching for at-risk Mid-Market accounts?
*   **Impact:** Retaining 15–20 accounts saves $195k–$260k ARR.
*   **Cost:** ~$150k in reduced revenue.
*   **Risk:** Sets a precedent for future negotiations.

**2. Onboarding Overhaul (SMB)**
Nearly half (48.5%) of SMB churn stems from users failing to complete onboarding. Completion rates dropped after April flow changes, and average completion time slipped to 23 days. Users who don't finish in 14 days have a 73% churn probability.
*   **Decision:** Fund the redesign ($85k for 2 engineers over 6 weeks)?
*   **Impact:** Potential 30–40% reduction in SMB churn, saving $210k–$280k ARR.
*   **Risk:** May not resolve underlying product complexity.

**3. Compliance Feature Acceleration (Enterprise)**
Three Enterprise clients ($620k ARR) left due to missing compliance features. The current roadmap targets Q2 2025 delivery.
*   **Decision:** Pull resources to move delivery to Q4 2024?
*   **Impact:** Secures $620k ARR and prevents further Enterprise loss.
*   **Cost:** $120k + an 8-week delay to other roadmap items.
*   **Risk:** Delays dependent projects; requires immediate resource reallocation.

**Recommendation**
The compliance acceleration offers the highest immediate ROI ($620k saved vs. $120k cost) and protects our top-tier segment. The onboarding fix addresses the largest volume of churn (SMB) with a strong payback period. Price matching should be applied selectively to the 8 identified at-risk accounts rather than a broad reversal, minimizing precedent risk while testing price elasticity.
