**Executive Summary: Q3 2024 Cloud Cost Review**

Northwind's cloud spending reached $2.34 million in Q3 2024, an 18% increase from the previous quarter. We have identified $293,000 in potential quarterly savings (12.5% of current spend) through infrastructure optimizations. Realizing these savings requires funding approximately 3–4 engineering sprints.

**Key Decisions Required**

1.  **Reserved Instance Commitment ($67k/quarter savings):**
    Purchasing reserved instances for our compute capacity would save $268,000 annually but requires a one-year financial commitment.
    *   **Recommendation:** Defer this decision. Current growth trajectories are uncertain, and locking in capacity for 12 months carries a risk of over-provisioning if demand slows. We should revisit this once we have clearer visibility into next year's workload.

2.  **Engineering Investment for Immediate Savings ($226k/quarter savings):**
    The remaining savings opportunities involve right-sizing underused resources, upgrading storage, and removing unnecessary network traffic. These changes carry low financial risk but require engineering time to implement safely.
    *   **Recommendation:** Approve funding for the required engineering sprints immediately. These "quick wins" can be implemented in 2–6 weeks without long-term contracts, yielding $226,000 in quarterly savings ($904,000 annually) with minimal downside.

**Risk Overview**
The primary risks are operational, not financial. Right-sizing databases or changing storage types requires careful testing to ensure performance remains stable during traffic spikes. The engineering team has estimated the effort required to mitigate these risks safely.

**Conclusion**
We recommend approving the engineering work to capture $904,000 in annual savings immediately while postponing the reserved instance purchase until growth patterns stabilize. This approach reduces costs now without locking capital into uncertain future capacity.
