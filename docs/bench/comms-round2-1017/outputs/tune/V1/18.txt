**Fund the $45k recovery and mandate data migration for Acme Analytics and DataSync Corp to avoid $3.2M in fines and audit failure.**

**The Decision**
You must approve two actions by November 30 to meet the December 15 GDPR deadline:
1.  **Authorize $45,000** to recover missing transaction records (2019–2020).
2.  **Require Acme Analytics and DataSync Corp** to migrate data from non-compliant regions or face contract termination.

**Trade-offs: Transaction Records**
*   **Recover ($45k):** Restores the required 7-year history. Prevents financial audit failure and tax penalties. Cost is fixed; success is probable but archives are aged.
*   **Accept Gap ($0):** Saves immediate cash. Guarantees audit failure and exposes the firm to tax penalties and regulatory scrutiny for missing mandatory records.

**Trade-offs: Non-Compliant Vendors**
*   **Migrate Data:** Maintains service continuity while achieving compliance. Requires vendor coordination and potential short-term disruption.
*   **Terminate Contracts:** Eliminates compliance risk immediately. Creates operational gaps in analytics and sync capabilities; replacement vendors require new procurement cycles.

**Financial Context**
*   **Total Remediation:** $183,000 one-time + $16,000/year.
*   **Risk Exposure:** Fines up to $3.2M (4% of revenue) for over-retained data; breach notification costs of $150k–$500k per incident for unencrypted backups.
*   **Deadlines:** GDPR corrective actions due December 15, 2024 (78 days remaining).

**Recommendation**
The $45k recovery cost is 1.4% of the potential fine exposure and essential for audit validity. Vendor data migration is the only path to retain critical services without violating data residency laws. Delaying either action jeopardizes the March 1, 2025 certification renewal.
