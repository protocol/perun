### Delivery Plan: Refine On-Call Alerting Logic

**Binding Facts & Constraints**
*   **Locked Files:** `global-thresholds.yaml` (shared across regional clusters).
*   **Approvers:** Site Reliability Lead (SRL) and Product Owner (PO) required before merge.
*   **CI Cost:** 45 minutes full regression; iterative testing discouraged.
*   **Validation Gap:** Staging synthetic traffic cannot replicate burst patterns; validation must rely on historical log replay.
*   **Deployment Window:** Tuesdays/Thursdays, 10 AM – 2 PM only.
*   **Irreversible Risk:** False negatives (missing real outages) if thresholds are too tight; continued fatigue if too loose.

---

### Lanes & Serialization

**Lane 1: Historical Data Extraction & Replay Harness (Serial - First)**
*   **Actor:** Builder
*   **Work:** Extract last 7 days of incident logs. Build a local replay script to simulate the *new* logic against this historical data.
*   **Output:** A report proving the new rule would have remained silent during the 7-day false positive window while catching known real incidents.
*   **Dependency:** None. Must complete before Lane 2 to define the exact threshold values.

**Lane 2: Alert Logic Implementation (Parallel to Lane 1 setup, waits on Lane 1 data)**
*   **Actor:** Builder (Different worktree than Lane 1 if parallel, otherwise sequential)
*   **Work:** Update `global-thresholds.yaml` with the compound condition: `error_rate > 5% (3min)` AND `checkout_success < 98%`.
*   **Output:** Patch file and updated YAML.
*   **Constraint:** No commit until Lane 1 confirms the specific values prevent the reported noise.

**Lane 3: Independent Verification (QA/SRE)**
*   **Actor:** QA (Independent of Builder)
*   **Work:** Review the replay report from Lane 1. Verify the logic covers the "transient blip" scenarios mentioned. Run a dry-run of the CI pipeline locally (if possible) or on a throwaway branch to check syntax only (skipping full 45m regression if syntax is trivial, pending approval).
*   **Output:** Verification receipt confirming the change meets the "silent during false alarms" criteria.

---

### Gates & Checks

*   **G1 Spec (Now):** Define the exact compound logic and the success metric (zero pages during the 7-day false positive window).
*   **G3 Design (Post-Lane 1):** Review the replay data. If the new logic misses a *real* past incident, the design fails. Adjust thresholds if necessary.
*   **G4 Implement:** Commit change to a dedicated feature branch. Base SHA pinned.
*   **G5 Verify:** **Critical Gate.** Since staging is unreliable, verification is the **historical replay receipt**. The gate passes only if the replay shows 0 false positives for the last 7 days and 100% capture of known real incidents.
*   **G6 Review:** Independent review by **Site Reliability Lead** (logic safety) and **Product Owner** (business impact of potentially delayed alerts). Both must approve.
*   **G7 Integrate:** Merge to main. Re-run full 45-minute CI suite *once* on the exact merge SHA.

---

### Landing & Human Decisions

**Merge Strategy:**
*   Create a Draft PR immediately after G4.
*   Attach the Lane 1 replay report to the PR description.
*   Request reviews from SRL and PO explicitly citing the "staging unreliability" and reliance on historical replay.

**Human Decisions Required:**
1.  **Approval to Merge:** SRL and PO must explicitly approve the trade-off (suppressing noise vs. potential delay in detection) based on the replay data.
2.  **Deployment Execution:** A human must trigger the deployment strictly within the **Tue/Thu 10 AM – 2 PM window**. Automated deployment is forbidden outside this window.

**Rollback Plan:**
*   **Trigger:** If a real incident occurs post-deploy and the alert fails to fire within 5 minutes of the threshold breach.
*   **Action:** Immediate revert of `global-thresholds.yaml` to the previous SHA.
*   **Preparation:** The revert commit should be prepared locally and ready to push; no new CI run should block a safety rollback (emergency bypass pre-authorized by SRL).

**Schedule:**
*   **Target Merge:** Monday (to allow review) or Tuesday morning.
*   **Target Deploy:** Tuesday or Thursday between 10 AM and 2 PM.
