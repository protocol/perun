# Delivery Plan: Overnight Kiosk Configuration Update

## Lanes and Parallelism
**Lane A (Preparation & Validation):** The Configuration Engineer prepares the payload containing the new pricing matrix, loyalty banner config, and tax rates for the three new jurisdictions. This lane runs independently of Lane B but must complete before the "Pre-Flight Gate."
**Lane B (Compliance & Security):** The Compliance Officer and Operations Lead review the legal team's signed artifact checksum and the dual-approval authorization. This lane runs in parallel with Lane A.
**Lane C (Monitoring Setup):** The Operations team prepares the real-time validation dashboard to monitor the 02:00–04:00 window. This runs in parallel with A and B.

*Serialization Point:* Lanes A and B must both complete and pass the Pre-Flight Gate before Lane D (Deployment) begins. Lane C must be active before Lane D starts.

## Gates and Checks
**Gate 1: Pre-Flight Validation (Deadline: 01:30 Local Time)**
*   **Revision:** The specific commit hash containing the prepared config payload.
*   **Check 1 (Automated):** Verify the payload checksum matches the signed artifact provided by the legal team.
*   **Check 2 (Manual):** Dual approval signature from the Operations Lead and Compliance Officer recorded in the deployment log.
*   **Check 3 (Constraint):** Confirm the shared certificate file is *not* included in the rotation list for this deployment.
*   **Action:** If any check fails, abort immediately. Do not trigger the CI pipeline unless syntax changes were made outside the standard template; rely on local schema validation to preserve compute credits.

**Gate 2: Deployment Window (02:00 – 04:00 Local Time)**
*   **Trigger:** Automated queue activation precisely at 02:00.
*   **Check:** Real-time monitoring of kiosk handshake and config acceptance.
*   **Failure Condition:** Any unit failing validation must automatically revert to the previous stable config before 04:00.
*   **Hard Stop:** No manual interventions or queue modifications after 03:45 to allow automatic reverts to complete.

## Review Strategy
**Independent Reviewer:** A Senior Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) not involved in preparing the payload (Lane A) or granting compliance approval (Lane B).
**Review Scope:**
1.  Verify the checksum matching process was logged correctly.
2.  Confirm the dual-approval signatures are present and valid.
3.  Validate that the rollback logic is armed and tested in the staging environment prior to the window.
4.  Ensure the certificate exclusion constraint is explicitly documented in the deployment ticket.
**Timing:** Review occurs immediately after Gate 1 passes, before the 02:00 window opens.

## Landing and Merge Strategy
**Merge Mechanism:** This is a configuration deployment, not a code merge. The "landing" occurs when the queue successfully distributes the config to the fleet during the 02:00–04:00 window.
**Human Decision Points:**
1.  **Go/No-Go at 01:45:** The Operations Lead makes the final call to proceed based on the Independent Reviewer's sign-off.
2.  **Emergency Abort:** If >5% of units fail validation by 03:00, the Operations Lead must decide whether to halt the queue entirely or allow individual reverts.

**Rollback Protocol:**
*   **Automatic:** Individual units failing validation revert instantly to the previous stable config.
*   **Irreversibility Warning:** Once the 04:00 cutoff passes, the rollback script becomes irreversible. Units that fail validation after this point will remain offline until the next night cycle. No manual rollback attempts are permitted after 04:00.
*   **Post-Mortem:** If any unit remains offline after the window, a fault ticket is generated automatically for the morning shift. No further deployment attempts are made until the root cause is identified.

**Success Criteria:** 100% of the fleet either accepts the new config or successfully reverts to the stable config by 04:00, with zero units left in an indeterminate state.
