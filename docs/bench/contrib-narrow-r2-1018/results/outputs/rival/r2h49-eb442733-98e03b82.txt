The safest initial step is to send a minimal, non-revealing inquiry to the security email address listed in the readme. Do not open a public issue yet.

Draft an email that states you have identified a potential cryptographic weakness affecting a specific, rarely used configuration (128-bit key size in a specific mode) but explicitly withhold the mathematical details, the proof of concept, and the specific mode name. Ask the maintainers to confirm their preferred process for validating whether this constitutes a reportable vulnerability under their policy. This approach protects your intellectual property and the users by keeping the flaw private while forcing the maintainers to define the severity and engagement protocol before you invest further time or risk exposure.

Note: If they do not respond within 48 hours, follow up once; if still silent, consider contacting the Rust Security Team as a fallback. Never disclose the specific vector until a private channel is established and acknowledged.
