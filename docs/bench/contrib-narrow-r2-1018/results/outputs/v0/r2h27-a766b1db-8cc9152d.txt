Instead, take the following immediate steps:

2.  **Engage Internal Legal:** Forward the specific CLA text and the maintainers' request to OmniSoft's legal department. Explain that this is a minor fix but requires their review to confirm whether OmniSoft supports contributing under this specific agreement and whether they authorize you to sign on their behalf.
3.  **Communicate with Maintainers:** Once legal is engaged, ask the maintainers if there is an interim process (such as a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off on the commit) that allows the code review to proceed while the corporate CLA is being processed. Many projects allow technical review to continue even if the final administrative merge is blocked by pending legal paperwork.

Attempting to bypass this by omitting the name or providing personal details when the work belongs to your employer risks invalidating the contribution later, potentially causing legal disputes over ownership or licensing rights. The "minor" nature of the fix does not reduce the legal weight of the CLA; it is a permanent assignment or license grant that requires proper authorization.

Note: Do not attempt to contribute as an individual if the code was written as part of your employment duties, as your employer likely already owns the copyright.
