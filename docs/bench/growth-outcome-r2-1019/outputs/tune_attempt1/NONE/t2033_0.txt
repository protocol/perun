**SRM**: The difference in users is $|39,234 - 39,192| = 42$. The denominator is $\sqrt{39,234 + 39,192} = \sqrt{78,426} \approx 280$. The ratio is $42 / 280 = 0.15$. Since $0.15 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: The target lift is 10% on a 5.0% baseline, so $p_1 = 0.05$ and $p_2 = 0.055$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.05(0.95) + 0.055(0.945)}{(0.005)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0475 + 0.051975}{0.000025} = 7.85 \times 3979 \approx 31,235$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 31,235 \approx 28,112$. The smaller arm (control) has 39,192 users, which is well above the threshold. The test is adequately powered.

**PEEK**: The experiment ran from June 30 to July 23, which is 23 days. The planned duration was 21 days. Since $23 > (0.8 \times 21 = 16.8)$, the test was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: The reported control rate is $1,960 / 49,576 \approx 0.03954$ (3.954%). The reported treatment rate is $2,022 / 45,198 \approx 0.04474$ (4.474%). These match the readout exactly. However, the randomization unit was **users**, so the denominator should be users, not sessions. The correct control rate should be $1,960 / 39,192 \approx 5.00\%$. The reported metric uses the wrong denominator.

**SIMPSON**: 
Pooled lift: Treatment ($2022/39234 \approx 5.15\%$) > Control ($1960/39192 \approx 5.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop lift: Treatment ($1547/20208 \approx 7.66\%$) > Control ($1389/17351 \approx 8.01\%$). Lift is negative.
Mobile lift: Treatment ($475/19026 \approx 2.50\%$) > Control ($571/21841 \approx 2.61\%$). Lift is negative.
Since the pooled lift is positive but negative in every segment, Simpson's Paradox is present.

**NOVELTY**: 
First 3 days mean: $(13.6 + 12.8 + 11.1) / 3 = 12.5\%$.
Last 3 days mean: $(14.2 + 15.4 + 10.7) / 3 \approx 13.4\%$.
The last mean (13.4) is not below half the first mean (6.25). The lift is not fading by this definition.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
