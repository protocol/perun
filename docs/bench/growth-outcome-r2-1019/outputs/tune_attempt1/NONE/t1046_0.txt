**SRM**: The difference in users is $|230,309 - 233,567| = 3,258$. The denominator is $\sqrt{230,309 + 233,567} = \sqrt{463,876} \approx 681.1$. The ratio is $3,258 / 681.1 \approx 4.78$. Since $4.78 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.04$. Target $p_2 = 0.04 \times 1.05 = 0.042$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.04(0.96) + 0.042(0.958)}{(0.002)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0384 + 0.040236}{0.000004} = 7.85 \times 19,659 \approx 154,323$.
The threshold is $0.9 \times 154,323 \approx 138,891$. The smaller arm (treatment) has 230,309 users, which is well above the threshold. POWER is not flagged.

**PEEK**: Duration from March 25 to April 6 is 12 days. Planned is 21 days. Threshold is $0.8 \times 21 = 16.8$ days. Since $12 < 16.8$, PEEK is flagged.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is $9,343 / 344,202 \approx 0.027$ (2.7%). The readout reports 4.0%. However, $9,343 / 233,567 \approx 0.040$. The reported rate matches orders/users. DENOM is not flagged.

**SIMPSON**: 
Desktop Control: $6,361/102,918 \approx 6.18\%$. Desktop Treatment: $6,606/101,483 \approx 6.51\%$ (Lift +).
Mobile Control: $2,982/130,649 \approx 2.28\%$. Mobile Treatment: $3,097/128,826 \approx 2.40\%$ (Lift +).
Both segments show positive lift. SIMPSON is not flagged.

**NOVELTY**: 
First 3 days mean: $(13.2 + 10.0 + 8.3)/3 = 10.5$. Half is $5.25$.
Last 3 days mean: $(2.0 + 1.7 + 2.1)/3 = 1.93$.
Since $1.93 < 5.25$, the lift is fading significantly. NOVELTY is flagged.

Three errors were found: Sample Ratio Mismatch indicates potential randomization issues; stopping early (Peeking) invalidates the p-value; and the fading lift suggests the initial effect was transient, making the 12-month projection unreliable. These must be resolved before rollout.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
