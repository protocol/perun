**SRM**: The difference in users is $|37,342 - 37,274| = 68$. The denominator is $\sqrt{37,342 + 37,274} = \sqrt{74,616} \approx 273.16$. The ratio is $68 / 273.16 \approx 0.25$. Since $0.25 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.05$. Target $p_2 = 0.05 \times 1.05 = 0.0525$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.05(0.95) + 0.0525(0.9475)}{(0.0525 - 0.05)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0475 + 0.04974}{0.00000625} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.09724}{0.00000625} \approx 122,327$.

**PEEK**: Duration from Oct 26 to Nov 4 is 9 days (inclusive). Planned duration is 21 days. Threshold is $0.8 \times 21 = 16.8$ days. Since $9 < 16.8$, the test stopped early. **Flag PEEK**.
