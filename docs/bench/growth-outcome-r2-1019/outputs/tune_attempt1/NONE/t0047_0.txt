**SRM**: The difference in users is $|237,254 - 233,136| = 4,118$. The denominator is $\sqrt{237,254 + 233,136} = \sqrt{470,390} \approx 685.85$. The ratio is $4,118 / 685.85 \approx 6.0$. Since $6.0 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.05$. Target $p_2 = 0.05 \times 1.05 = 0.0525$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.05(0.95) + 0.0525(0.9475)}{(0.0025)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0475 + 0.04974}{0.00000625} \approx 122,328$.
Threshold is $0.9 \times 122,328 \approx 110,095$. The smaller arm (control) has 233,136 users, which is well above the threshold. POWER is not flagged.

**PEEK**: Duration from June 4 to June 13 is 9 days (inclusive of start/end or simply counting days run: 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, 13th = 10 days? Let's count strictly: 13-4 = 9 days of data if readout is end of day 13, or 10 days if inclusive. Even assuming 10 days: $0.8 \times 21 = 16.8$. Since $10 < 16.8$ (and $9 < 16.8$), PEEK is flagged.

Orders/Users: $11,656 / 233,136 \approx 5.00\%$.
Orders/Sessions: $11,656 / 334,910 \approx 3.48\%$.
The reported rate matches Orders/Sessions. Since randomization was by user, DENOM is flagged.
