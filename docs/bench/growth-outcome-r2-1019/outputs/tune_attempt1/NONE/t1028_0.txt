**SRM**: The difference in users is $|11,746 - 11,840| = 94$. The denominator is $\sqrt{11,746 + 11,840} = \sqrt{23,586} \approx 153.6$. The ratio is $94 / 153.6 \approx 0.61$. Since $0.61 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: The target lift is 10% on a 6.0% baseline, so $p_1 = 0.06$ and $p_2 = 0.066$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.06 \times 0.94 + 0.066 \times 0.934) = 7.85 \times (0.0564 + 0.0616) = 7.85 \times 0.118 \approx 0.926$.
Denominator: $(0.066 - 0.06)^2 = 0.006^2 = 0.000036$.
Required $n$ per arm: $0.926 / 0.000036 \approx 25,722$.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 25,722 = 23,150$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 11,746 users. Since $11,746 < 23,150$, the test is underpowered. **Flag POWER**.

**PEEK**: Duration from June 8 to June 30 is 22 days (inclusive). Planned duration is 21 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 21 = 16.8$ days.
Since $22 > 16.8$, the test did not stop early.

**DENOM**: Randomization was by user. Reported control rate: $710 / 15,092 \approx 0.04704$ (4.704%). Reported treatment rate: $751 / 13,133 \approx 0.05718$ (5.718%).
The reported rates match Orders/Sessions, not Orders/Users. Since users are the randomized unit, this is the wrong denominator. **Flag DENOM**.

**SIMPSON**:
Pooled lift: Treatment ($751/11,746 \approx 6.39\%$) > Control ($710/11,840 \approx 6.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop: Control $469/4,383 \approx 10.70\%$; Treatment $496/4,348 \approx 11.41\%$. Lift is positive.
Mobile: Control $241/7,457 \approx 3.23\%$; Treatment $255/7,398 \approx 3.45\%$. Lift is positive.
Lift is positive in all segments, so no Simpson's paradox.

**NOVELTY**:
First 3 days: 20.9, 20.8, 23.8. Mean = $65.5 / 3 \approx 21.83$. Half mean = $10.92$.
Last 3 days: 22.0, 20.4, 16.6. Mean = $59.0 / 3 \approx 19.67$.
Since $19.67$ is not below $10.92$, the lift is not fading by this definition.
