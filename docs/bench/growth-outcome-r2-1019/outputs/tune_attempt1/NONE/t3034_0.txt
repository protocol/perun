**SRM**: The difference in users is $|43,304 - 43,566| = 262$. The denominator is $\sqrt{43,304 + 43,566} = \sqrt{86,870} \approx 294.7$. The ratio is $262 / 294.7 \approx 0.89$. Since $0.89 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: The baseline rate $p_1 = 0.06$. The target lift is 8%, so $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.08 = 0.0648$.
The required sample size per arm is:
$n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.06(0.94) + 0.0648(0.9352)}{(0.0648 - 0.06)^2}$
$n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0564 + 0.0606}{0.00002304} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.117}{0.00002304} \approx 39,886$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 39,886 \approx 35,897$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 43,304 users. Since $43,304 > 35,897$, the test is not underpowered.

**PEEK**: The experiment ran from July 30 to August 19. Counting inclusive days: 2 days in July (30, 31) + 19 days in August = 21 days.
The threshold is $0.8 \times 28 \text{ (planned)} = 22.4$ days.
Since $21 < 22.4$, the experiment stopped early. **PEEK is flagged.**

**DENOM**: The reported control conversion rate is 6.0%.
Calculating orders/users: $2,614 / 43,566 \approx 0.0600$ (6.0%).
Calculating orders/sessions: $2,614 / 59,215 \approx 0.0441$ (4.4%).
The reported rate matches orders/users. Since users were the randomized unit, the denominator is correct.

Pooled lift: Treatment ($2,650/43,304 \approx 6.12\%$) > Control ($6.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop lift: Treatment ($1,986/22,446 \approx 8.85\%$) vs Control ($1,851/20,296 \approx 9.12\%$). Lift is negative.

**NOVELTY**:
