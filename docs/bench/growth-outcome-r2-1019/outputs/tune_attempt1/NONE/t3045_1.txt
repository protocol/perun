**SRM**: The difference in users is $|83,197 - 83,615| = 418$. The denominator is $\sqrt{83,197 + 83,615} = \sqrt{166,812} \approx 408.4$. The ratio is $418 / 408.4 \approx 1.02$. This is less than 3.5. No mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.05$. Target $p_2 = 0.05 \times 1.05 = 0.0525$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.05(0.95) + 0.0525(0.9475)}{(0.0525 - 0.05)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0475 + 0.04974}{0.00000625} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.09724}{0.00000625} \approx 122,133$.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 122,133 \approx 109,920$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 83,197 users. Since $83,197 < 109,920$, the test is underpowered. **Flag POWER**.

**PEEK**: Duration from Oct 19 to Oct 29 is 10 days. Planned was 14 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 14 = 11.2$ days.
Actual (10) < Threshold (11.2). The test stopped early. **Flag PEEK**.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate: $4,181 / 121,506 \approx 0.03441$ (3.441%).
Correct user-based rate: $4,181 / 83,615 \approx 0.0499$ (4.99%).
The reported rate matches orders/sessions, not orders/users. Since randomization was by user, this is the wrong denominator. **Flag DENOM**.
