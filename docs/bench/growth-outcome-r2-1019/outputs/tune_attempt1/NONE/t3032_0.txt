**SRM**: The difference in users is $|133,561 - 133,687| = 126$. The denominator is $\sqrt{133,561 + 133,687} = \sqrt{267,248} \approx 517$. The ratio is $126 / 517 \approx 0.24$. This is less than 3.5. No SRM.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.05$. Target lift 5% means $p_2 = 0.05 \times 1.05 = 0.0525$.
Numerator: $0.05(0.95) + 0.0525(0.9475) = 0.0475 + 0.04974 = 0.09724$.
Denominator: $(0.0525 - 0.05)^2 = 0.00000625$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times (0.09724 / 0.00000625) \approx 122,327$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 133,561 users. The threshold is $0.9 \times 122,327 \approx 110,094$. Since $133,561 > 110,094$, the test is sufficiently powered. No POWER error.

**PEEK**: Start date Oct 15, readout Oct 31. Duration is 16 days (inclusive counting or simply $31-15=16$ depending on convention, but clearly $>14$). Even if strictly $31-15=16$, planned was 14. $16 \ge 0.8 \times 14 = 11.2$. The test ran longer than planned, not shorter. No PEEK error.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is 5.0%.
Orders/Users: $6,685 / 133,687 \approx 0.0500$ (5.0%).
Orders/Sessions: $6,685 / 186,038 \approx 0.0359$ (3.6%).
The reported rate matches Orders/Users. Since users were the randomized unit, this is the correct denominator. No DENOM error.

**SIMPSON**:
Pooled lift: Treatment rate $6,814/133,561 \approx 5.10\%$. Control $5.00\%$. Lift is positive.
Desktop lift: Control $4,898/64,579 \approx 7.58\%$. Treatment $5,255/71,414 \approx 7.36\%$. Lift is negative ($7.36 < 7.58$).
Mobile lift: Control $1,787/69,108 \approx 2.59\%$. Treatment $1,559/62,147 \approx 2.51\%$. Lift is negative ($2.51 < 2.59$).
The pooled lift is positive, but both segments show negative lift. This is Simpson's Paradox. Flag SIMPSON.

**NOVELTY**:
First 3 days: 3.6, 3.1, 3.2. Mean = $9.9 / 3 = 3.3$.
Last 3 days (of 16): 1.4, 1.3, 0.8. Mean = $3.5 / 3 \approx 1.17$.
Half of first mean = $1.65$.
$1.17 < 1.65$. The lift is fading significantly. Flag NOVELTY.
