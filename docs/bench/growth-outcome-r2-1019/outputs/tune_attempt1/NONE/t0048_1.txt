**SRM**: The difference in users is $|5,540 - 5,612| = 72$. The denominator is $\sqrt{5,540 + 5,612} = \sqrt{11,152} \approx 105.6$. The ratio is $72 / 105.6 \approx 0.68$. Since $0.68 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.08$. Target $p_2 = 0.08 \times 1.10 = 0.088$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.08(0.92) + 0.088(0.912)}{(0.008)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0736 + 0.080256}{0.000064} = 7.85 \times 2404 \approx 18,871$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 18,871 \approx 16,984$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 5,540 users. Since $5,540 < 16,984$, the test is **underpowered**.

**PEEK**: Duration from June 16 to July 16 is 30 days. Planned duration is 28 days.
Threshold is $0.8 \times 28 = 22.4$ days. Since $30 > 22.4$, the test was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: Control reported rate is $449 / 8,091 \approx 5.549\%$. Treatment reported rate is $470 / 6,406 \approx 7.337\%$.
The readout divides orders by sessions, but randomization was by user. This is the **wrong denominator**.

**SIMPSON**: 
Desktop lift: Control $339/2682 \approx 12.64\%$, Treatment $396/3403 \approx 11.64\%$. Lift is negative.
Mobile lift: Control $110/2930 \approx 3.75\%$, Treatment $74/2137 \approx 3.46\%$. Lift is negative.
Pooled lift (using users): Control $449/5612 \approx 8.00\%$, Treatment $470/5540 \approx 8.48\%$. Lift is positive.
Since pooled lift is positive but segment lifts are negative, **Simpson's paradox** is present.

**NOVELTY**: 
First 3 days mean: $(32.0+34.9+35.6)/3 = 34.17$. Half is $17.08$.
Last 3 days mean: $(31.2+30.7+30.8)/3 = 30.9$.
Since $30.9$ is not below $17.08$, the lift is not fading by this metric.
