**SRM**: The difference in users is $|285,063 - 285,589| = 526$. The denominator is $\sqrt{285,063 + 285,589} = \sqrt{570,652} \approx 755.4$. The ratio is $526 / 755.4 \approx 0.70$. Since $0.70 < 3.5$, there is no SRM.

**POWER**: The smaller arm has 285,063 users. The required sample size per arm uses $p_1 = 0.04$ and $p_2 = 0.04 \times 1.05 = 0.042$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.04 \times 0.96 + 0.042 \times 0.958) = 7.85 \times (0.0384 + 0.040236) \approx 0.6173$.
Denominator: $(0.042 - 0.04)^2 = 0.000004$.
Required $n = 0.6173 / 0.000004 \approx 154,325$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 154,325 \approx 138,893$. Since $285,063 > 138,893$, the test is not underpowered.

**PEEK**: The experiment ran from March 29 to April 19. March has 31 days, so March 29–31 is 3 days. April 1–19 is 19 days. Total days = 22. (Note: If inclusive counting of start/end dates implies 22 days, or if strictly "duration" implies 21 days elapsed, let's look closer. Start 29th, End 19th. Days = (31-29+1) + 19 = 3 + 19 = 22 days? Since $21 \ge 16.8$, it was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: Randomization was by user. Reported control rate: $11,424 / 422,501 \approx 0.02704$ (2.704%). Reported treatment rate: $12,078 / 339,448 \approx 0.03558$ (3.558%). The reported rates match Orders/Sessions. Since the unit of randomization is Users, using Sessions as the denominator is incorrect. This is an error.

**SIMPSON**: 
Pooled lift: Treatment ($12,078/285,063 \approx 4.24\%$) > Control ($11,424/285,589 \approx 4.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop: Control $7,236/103,360 \approx 7.00\%$. Treatment $9,011/139,960 \approx 6.44\%$. Lift is negative.
Mobile: Control $4,188/182,229 \approx 2.30\%$. Treatment $3,067/145,103 \approx 2.11\%$. Lift is negative.
Since the pooled lift is positive but negative in every segment, Simpson's paradox is present.
