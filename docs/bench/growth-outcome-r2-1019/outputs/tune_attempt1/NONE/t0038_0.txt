**SRM**: The difference in users is $|28,954 - 30,420| = 1,466$. The denominator is $\sqrt{28,954 + 30,420} = \sqrt{59,374} \approx 243.7$. The ratio is $1,466 / 243.7 \approx 6.02$. Since $6.02 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.06$. Target $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.05 = 0.063$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.06 \times 0.94 + 0.063 \times 0.937) = 7.85 \times (0.0564 + 0.0590) = 0.908$.
Denominator: $(0.063 - 0.06)^2 = 0.000009$.
Required $n = 0.908 / 0.000009 \approx 100,889$ per arm.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 100,889 \approx 90,800$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 28,954 users. Since $28,954 < 90,800$, POWER is flagged.

**PEEK**: Start date June 24 to end date July 8 is 14 days inclusive (June 24–30 is 7 days; July 1–8 is 8 days; total 15 days? Or simply July 8 minus June 24 = 14 days duration). Let's count days run: 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30 (7 days) + 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 (8 days) = 15 days. Even if strictly 14 days, $14 \ge 0.8 \times 14$ (11.2). The experiment ran the full planned duration or longer. PEEK is not flagged.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is 5.999%.
Orders/Users: $1,825 / 30,420 \approx 0.05999$ (5.999%).
Orders/Sessions: $1,825 / 39,089 \approx 0.0467$.
The reported rate matches Orders/Users. Since users were the randomized unit, this is the correct denominator. DENOM is not flagged.

**SIMPSON**:
Pooled Lift: Treatment rate $1,862/28,954 \approx 6.43\%$. Control $5.999\%$. Lift is positive.
Desktop: Control $1,107/10,701 \approx 10.34\%$. Treatment $1,129/10,186 \approx 11.08\%$. Lift is positive.
Since the lift is positive in the desktop segment, it is not negative in *every* segment. SIMPSON is not flagged.

First 3 days: 19.5, 15.3, 11.8. Mean = $46.6 / 3 \approx 15.53$. Half mean = $7.77$.
Last 3 days: 2.1, 1.3, 1.5. Mean = $4.9 / 3 \approx 1.63$.
$1.63 < 7.77$. The condition holds. NOVELTY is flagged.
