**SRM**: The difference in users is $|29,761 - 29,693| = 68$. The denominator is $\sqrt{29,761 + 29,693} = \sqrt{59,454} \approx 243.8$. The ratio is $68 / 243.8 \approx 0.28$. Since $0.28 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.05$. Target $p_2 = 0.05 \times 1.08 = 0.054$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.05 \times 0.95 + 0.054 \times 0.946) = 7.85 \times (0.0475 + 0.051084) \approx 0.774$.
Denominator: $(0.054 - 0.05)^2 = 0.000016$.
Required $n = 0.774 / 0.000016 \approx 48,375$ per arm.
The smaller arm has 29,693 users. The threshold is $0.9 \times 48,375 = 43,537.5$.
Since $29,693 < 43,537.5$, the test is **underpowered**.

**PEEK**: Duration from Sept 21 to Oct 13 is 22 days (inclusive). Planned duration is 21 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 21 = 16.8$ days.
Since $22 > 16.8$, the test was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is $3.885\%$.
Orders / Users: $1,484 / 29,693 \approx 0.0500$ ($5.00\%$).
Orders / Sessions: $1,484 / 38,194 \approx 0.03885$ ($3.885\%$).
The reported rate matches Orders/Sessions, but randomization was by User. This is a **wrong denominator** error.

**SIMPSON**:
Pooled Lift: Treatment ($1,572/29,761 \approx 5.28\%$) vs Control ($1,484/29,693 \approx 5.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop: Control ($1,002/11,512 \approx 8.70\%$), Treatment ($1,061/11,538 \approx 9.20\%$). Lift is positive.
Mobile: Control ($482/18,181 \approx 2.65\%$), Treatment ($511/18,223 \approx 2.80\%$). Lift is positive.
Since segment lifts are not negative, there is no Simpson's paradox.

**NOVELTY**:
First 3 days mean: $(27.0 + 26.2 + 26.5) / 3 = 26.57$. Half is $13.28$.
Last 3 days mean: $(7.6 + 5.5 + 5.9) / 3 = 6.33$.
Since $6.33 < 13.28$, the lift is fading significantly compared to the start. This flags **NOVELTY**.

ERRORS: POWER, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
