**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.04$. Target lift 10% implies $p_2 = 0.044$.

Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 39,500 = 35,550$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 26,942 users. Since $26,942 < 35,550$, the test is **underpowered**.
