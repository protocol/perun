**SRM**: The difference in users is $|107,098 - 108,818| = 1,720$. The denominator is $\sqrt{107,098 + 108,818} = \sqrt{215,916} \approx 464.67$. The ratio is $1,720 / 464.67 \approx 3.70$. Since $3.70 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.04$. Target $p_2 = 0.04 \times 1.05 = 0.042$.
Required $n$ per arm: $7.85 \times \frac{0.04(0.96) + 0.042(0.958)}{(0.002)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0384 + 0.040236}{0.000004} = 7.85 \times 19,659 \approx 154,323$.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 154,323 \approx 138,891$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 107,098 users. Since $107,098 < 138,891$, POWER is flagged.

**PEEK**: Duration from March 8 to April 7 is 30 days inclusive (or 30 days elapsed depending on interpretation, but let's count days run). March has 31 days. March 8 to March 31 is 24 days. April 1 to April 7 is 7 days. Total = 31 days? Let's count simply: $31 - 8 + 1 = 24$ days in March + 7 in April = 31 days. Even if exclusive (30 days), planned is 28. $0.8 \times 28 = 22.4$. The actual days (30 or 31) are greater than 22.4. PEEK is NOT flagged.

The reported rate matches Orders/Users. Since users were the randomized unit, this is the correct denominator. DENOM is NOT flagged.
