**SRM**: The difference in users is $|34,827 - 35,005| = 178$. The denominator is $\sqrt{34,827 + 35,005} = \sqrt{69,832} \approx 264.3$. The ratio is $178 / 264.3 \approx 0.67$. Since $0.67 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.08$. Target $p_2 = 0.08 \times 1.10 = 0.088$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.08(0.92) + 0.088(0.912)}{(0.008)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0736 + 0.080256}{0.000064} = 7.85 \times 2404 \approx 18,871$.
The threshold is $0.9 \times 18,871 \approx 16,984$. The smaller arm (treatment) has 34,827 users, which is well above the threshold. The test is not underpowered.

**PEEK**: Duration from July 3 to July 17 is 14 days. Planned duration is 14 days. $14 \ge 0.8 \times 14$ (11.2). The test did not stop early.

**DENOM**: The readout reports control conversion as $5.541\%$.
Calculating Orders/Users: $2,801 / 35,005 \approx 0.0800$ ($8.00\%$).
Calculating Orders/Sessions: $2,801 / 50,553 \approx 0.05541$ ($5.541\%$).
The reported rate matches Orders/Sessions, but users were the randomized unit. This is a wrong denominator error.

**SIMPSON**:
Pooled Lift: Treatment rate ($2,973/34,827 \approx 8.54\%$) > Control rate ($8.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop Lift: Treatment ($2,152/15,134 \approx 14.22\%$) > Control ($2,027/15,211 \approx 13.33\%$). Lift is positive.
Mobile Lift: Treatment ($821/19,693 \approx 4.17\%$) > Control ($774/19,794 \approx 3.91\%$). Lift is positive.
Since the lift is positive in all segments, there is no Simpson's paradox.

**NOVELTY**:
First 3 days mean: $(32.3 + 32.3 + 34.0) / 3 = 32.87\%$. Half is $16.44\%$.
Last 3 days mean: $(26.4 + 34.1 + 30.7) / 3 = 30.4\%$.
$30.4\%$ is not below $16.44\%$. The lift is not fading significantly by this rule.

Only the DENOM error is present. The reported metrics are fundamentally flawed because they divide by sessions instead of users, invalidating the p-value and lift calculation relative to the experimental design.
