**SRM**: The difference in users is $|32,618 - 32,864| = 246$. The denominator is $\sqrt{32,618 + 32,864} = \sqrt{65,482} \approx 255.9$. The ratio is $246 / 255.9 \approx 0.96$. Since $0.96 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: The baseline rate $p_1 = 0.06$. The target lift is 10%, so $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.10 = 0.066$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.06(0.94) + 0.066(0.934)}{(0.066 - 0.06)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0564 + 0.061634}{0.000036} = 7.85 \times 3278.7 \approx 25,738$.
The threshold is $0.9 \times 25,738 \approx 23,164$. The smaller arm (treatment) has 32,618 users. Since $32,618 > 23,164$, the test is not underpowered.

**PEEK**: The experiment ran from June 15 to July 14. This is exactly 29 days (inclusive) or 28 days duration. The planned duration was 28 days. Since $28 \ge 0.8 \times 28$, the test was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: The reported control rate is 4.642%. Calculating orders/sessions: $1,972 / 42,478 \approx 0.04642$ (4.642%). Calculating orders/users: $1,972 / 32,864 \approx 0.0600$ (6.00%). The reported rate matches orders/sessions, but users were the randomized unit. This is a denominator error.

**SIMPSON**: 
Pooled lift: Treatment ($2,015/32,618 \approx 6.18\%$) vs Control ($1,972/32,864 \approx 6.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop: Treatment ($1,408/14,336 \approx 9.82\%$) vs Control ($1,251/12,145 \approx 10.30\%$). Lift is negative.
Mobile: Treatment ($607/18,282 \approx 3.32\%$) vs Control ($721/20,719 \approx 3.48\%$). Lift is negative.
Since the pooled lift is positive but negative in every segment, Simpson's Paradox is present.

**NOVELTY**: 
First 3 days mean: $(29.9 + 25.6 + 25.9) / 3 = 27.13$. Half is $13.57$.
Last 3 days mean: $(5.5 + 9.1 + 8.7) / 3 = 7.77$.
Since $7.77 < 13.57$, the lift is fading significantly compared to the start.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
