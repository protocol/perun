**SRM**: The difference in users is $|30,531 - 30,459| = 72$. The denominator is $\sqrt{30,531 + 30,459} = \sqrt{60,990} \approx 247$. The ratio is $72 / 247 \approx 0.29$. Since $0.29 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.06$. Target $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.05 = 0.063$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.06 \times 0.94 + 0.063 \times 0.937) \approx 7.85 \times 0.1155 \approx 0.907$.
Denominator: $(0.063 - 0.06)^2 = 0.000009$.
Required $n \approx 0.907 / 0.000009 \approx 100,778$ per arm.
The smaller arm has 30,459 users. The threshold is $0.9 \times 100,778 \approx 90,700$. Since $30,459 < 90,700$, the test is underpowered. **Flag POWER**.

**PEEK**: Duration from April 14 to April 30 is 16 days. Planned duration is 14 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 14 = 11.2$ days. Since $16 > 11.2$, the test did not stop early.

**DENOM**: Control reported rate is $4.326\%$.
Orders/Users: $1,827 / 30,459 \approx 6.00\%$.
Orders/Sessions: $1,827 / 42,232 \approx 4.33\%$.
The reported rate matches Orders/Sessions, but randomization was by User. **Flag DENOM**.
