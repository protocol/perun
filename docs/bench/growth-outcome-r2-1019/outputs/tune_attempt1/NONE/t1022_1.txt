**SRM**: The difference in users is $|149,334 - 149,820| = 486$. The denominator is $\sqrt{149,334 + 149,820} = \sqrt{299,154} \approx 547$. The ratio is $486 / 547 \approx 0.89$. Since $0.89 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: The baseline rate $p_1 = 0.06$. The target lift is 5%, so $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.05 = 0.063$.
The required sample size per arm is $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.06(0.94) + 0.063(0.937)}{(0.063 - 0.06)^2}$.
Numerator: $0.0564 + 0.0590 = 0.1154$.
Denominator: $(0.003)^2 = 0.000009$.
$n = 7.85 \times (0.1154 / 0.000009) \approx 7.85 \times 12,822 \approx 100,653$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 100,653 \approx 90,588$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 149,334 users, which is greater than 90,588. The test is adequately powered.

**PEEK**: The experiment ran from July 27 to August 12. Counting inclusive days: July (27–31) is 5 days; August (1–12) is 12 days. Total = 17 days.

Since $17 < 22.4$, the experiment stopped early. **PEEK** is flagged.
