**SRM**: The difference in users is $|123,691 - 125,747| = 2,056$. The denominator is $\sqrt{123,691 + 125,747} = \sqrt{249,438} \approx 499.4$. The ratio is $2,056 / 499.4 \approx 4.12$. Since $4.12 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.06$. Target $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.05 = 0.063$.
Variance sum: $0.06(0.94) + 0.063(0.937) = 0.0564 + 0.0590 = 0.1154$.
Difference squared: $(0.003)^2 = 0.000009$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times (0.1154 / 0.000009) \approx 100,654$.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 100,654 = 90,589$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 123,691 users, which is greater than 90,589. POWER is not flagged.

**PEEK**: Start date Feb 16, readout Feb 25. Duration is 9 days (inclusive counting or simply $25-16=9$). Planned is 14 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 14 = 11.2$ days.
Since $9 < 11.2$, PEEK is flagged.

**DENOM**: Control reported rate is $5.999\%$.
Orders/Users: $7,544 / 125,747 \approx 0.05999$ ($5.999\%$).
Orders/Sessions: $7,544 / 162,623 \approx 0.04639$ ($4.64\%$).
The reported rate matches Orders/Users. Since users were the randomized unit, this is the correct denominator. DENOM is not flagged.

**SIMPSON**:
Pooled Lift: Treatment rate ($7,644/123,691 \approx 6.18\%$) > Control rate ($5.999\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop Lift: Control $5,747/62,802 \approx 9.15\%$. Treatment $6,211/71,104 \approx 8.73\%$. Lift is negative.
Mobile Lift: Control $1,797/62,945 \approx 2.86\%$. Treatment $1,433/52,587 \approx 2.73\%$. Lift is negative.
Since pooled lift is positive but negative in every segment, SIMPSON is flagged.

**NOVELTY**:
First 3 days: 3.3, 2.7, 3.1. Mean = $9.1 / 3 \approx 3.03\%$.
Last 3 days: 3.1, 3.6, 2.4. Mean = $9.1 / 3 \approx 3.03\%$.
Is $3.03 < 0.5 \times 3.03$? No. NOVELTY is not flagged.
