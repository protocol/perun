**SRM**: The difference in users is $|37,195 - 36,941| = 254$. The denominator is $\sqrt{37,195 + 36,941} = \sqrt{74,136} \approx 272.3$. The ratio is $254 / 272.3 \approx 0.93$. Since $0.93 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.04$. Target $p_2 = 0.04 \times 1.08 = 0.0432$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.04 \times 0.96 + 0.0432 \times 0.9568) \approx 7.85 \times (0.0384 + 0.0413) \approx 0.626$.
Denominator: $(0.0432 - 0.04)^2 = 0.0032^2 = 0.00001024$.
Required $n \approx 0.626 / 0.00001024 \approx 61,133$ users per arm.
The smaller arm (control) has 36,941 users. The threshold is $0.9 \times 61,133 \approx 55,020$. Since $36,941 < 55,020$, the test is underpowered. **Flag POWER**.

**PEEK**: Duration from March 10 to March 19 is 9 days (inclusive of start and end dates for data collection). Planned duration is 14 days. Threshold is $0.8 \times 14 = 11.2$ days. Since $9 < 11.2$, the test stopped early. **Flag PEEK**.

**DENOM**: Control reported rate is $1,477 / 51,056 \approx 0.02893$ (2.893%). Treatment reported rate is $1,584 / 47,394 \approx 0.03342$ (3.342%). The readout matches orders/sessions. Since randomization was by user, this is the wrong denominator. **Flag DENOM**.

**SIMPSON**: 
Desktop Lift: Control $942/14,413 \approx 6.54\%$; Treatment $1,010/14,512 \approx 6.96\%$. Lift is positive.
Mobile Lift: Control $535/22,528 \approx 2.37\%$; Treatment $574/22,683 \approx 2.53\%$. Lift is positive.
Since lifts are positive in both segments, there is no Simpson's Paradox.

**NOVELTY**: First 3 days mean: $(13.6+13.5+15.9)/3 = 14.33$. Last 3 days mean: $(15.5+13.3+15.5)/3 = 14.77$. Since $14.77$ is not below half of $14.33$ ($7.16$), this rule does not hold.

ERRORS: POWER, PEEK, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
