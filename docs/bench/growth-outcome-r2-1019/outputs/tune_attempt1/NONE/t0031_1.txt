**SRM**: The difference in users is $|8,451 - 8,581| = 130$. The denominator is $\sqrt{8,451 + 8,581} = \sqrt{17,032} \approx 130.5$. The ratio is $130 / 130.5 \approx 0.996$. This is less than 3.5, so no SRM.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.08$. Target $p_2 = 0.08 \times 1.08 = 0.0864$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.08 \times 0.92 + 0.0864 \times 0.9136) = 7.85 \times (0.0736 + 0.0789) \approx 1.199$.
Denominator: $(0.0864 - 0.08)^2 = 0.0064^2 = 0.00004096$.
Required $n = 1.199 / 0.00004096 \approx 29,272$ per arm.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 29,272 \approx 26,345$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 8,451 users, which is well below 26,345. **POWER flagged**.
