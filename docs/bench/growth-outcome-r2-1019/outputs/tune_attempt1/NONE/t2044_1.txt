**SRM**: The difference in users is $|32,618 - 32,864| = 246$. The denominator is $\sqrt{32,618 + 32,864} = \sqrt{65,482} \approx 255.9$. The ratio is $246 / 255.9 \approx 0.96$. Since $0.96 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.06$. Target $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.10 = 0.066$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.06(0.94) + 0.066(0.934)}{(0.006)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0564 + 0.0616}{0.000036} = 7.85 \times 3277.8 \approx 25,731$.
The threshold is $0.9 \times 25,731 \approx 23,158$. The smaller arm (treatment) has 32,618 users. Since $32,618 > 23,158$, the test is not underpowered.

**PEEK**: Duration from June 15 to July 14 is 29 days (inclusive). Planned duration is 28 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 28 = 22.4$ days. Since $29 > 22.4$, the test was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: Randomization was by user, so the denominator must be users.
Control reported rate: $1,972 / 32,864 \approx 6.00\%$. The readout reports $4.642\%$.
Check sessions denominator: $1,972 / 42,478 \approx 4.642\%$.
Treatment reported rate: $2,015 / 32,618 \approx 6.18\%$. The readout reports $5.35\%$.
Check sessions denominator: $2,015 / 37,662 \approx 5.35\%$.
The reported rates use sessions as the denominator instead of users. This is an error.

**SIMPSON**:
Pooled lift: Treatment ($2,015/32,618 \approx 6.18\%$) > Control ($1,972/32,864 \approx 6.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop lift: Treatment ($1,408/14,336 \approx 9.82\%$) vs Control ($1,251/12,145 \approx 10.30\%$). Lift is negative.
Mobile lift: Treatment ($607/18,282 \approx 3.32\%$) vs Control ($721/20,719 \approx 3.48\%$). Lift is negative.
The pooled lift is positive while both segments show negative lift. This is Simpson's Paradox.

**NOVELTY**:
First 3 days mean: $(29.9 + 25.6 + 25.9) / 3 = 27.13\%$. Half is $13.57\%$.
Last 3 days mean: $(5.5 + 9.1 + 8.7) / 3 = 7.77\%$.
Since $7.77 < 13.57$, the lift is fading significantly. This flag applies.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
