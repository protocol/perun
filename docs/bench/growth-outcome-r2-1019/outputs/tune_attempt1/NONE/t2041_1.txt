**SRM**: The difference in users is $|58,111 - 59,497| = 1,386$. The denominator is $\sqrt{58,111 + 59,497} = \sqrt{117,608} \approx 342.9$. The ratio is $1,386 / 342.9 \approx 4.04$. Since $4.04 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.06$. Target $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.05 = 0.063$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.06 \times 0.94 + 0.063 \times 0.937) \approx 7.85 \times 0.1155 \approx 0.907$.
Denominator: $(0.063 - 0.06)^2 = 0.000009$.
Required $n \approx 0.907 / 0.000009 \approx 100,778$ per arm.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 100,778 \approx 90,700$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 58,111 users. Since $58,111 < 90,700$, POWER is flagged.

**PEEK**: Start date May 18, end date June 2. Duration is 15 days (inclusive count or simply comparing dates: May 18 to June 2 is 15 days if counting both ends, or 14 days difference). Even assuming 14 days run vs 14 planned, $14 \ge 0.8 \times 14$ (11.2). If 15 days, $15 \ge 11.2$. The rule requires actual < 80% of planned. The experiment ran for the full or extended duration. PEEK is not flagged.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is 6.0%.
Orders/Users: $3,570 / 59,497 \approx 0.0600$ (6.0%).
Orders/Sessions: $3,570 / 85,369 \approx 0.0418$ (4.2%).
The reported rate matches Orders/Users. Since users were the randomized unit, this is the correct denominator. DENOM is not flagged.

**SIMPSON**:
Pooled Lift: Treatment rate $3,695/58,111 \approx 6.36\%$. Control $6.00\%$. Lift is positive.
Desktop Lift: Control $2,709/29,662 \approx 9.13\%$. Treatment $2,804/28,971 \approx 9.68\%$. Lift is positive ($9.68 > 9.13$).
Mobile Lift: Control $861/29,835 \approx 2.89\%$. Treatment $891/29,140 \approx 3.06\%$. Lift is positive ($3.06 > 2.89$).
Since segment lifts are positive, there is no paradox where pooled is positive but segments are negative. SIMPSON is not flagged.

**NOVELTY**:
First 3 days: 12.1, 9.8, 10.4. Mean = $32.3 / 3 \approx 10.77$. Half mean = $5.38$.
Last 3 days: 3.3, 2.8, 2.1. Mean = $8.2 / 3 \approx 2.73$.
Is $2.73 < 5.38$? Yes. The lift is fading significantly. NOVELTY is flagged.

ERRORS: SRM, POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
