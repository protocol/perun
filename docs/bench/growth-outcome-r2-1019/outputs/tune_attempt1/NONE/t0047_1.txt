**SRM**: The difference in users is $|237,254 - 233,136| = 4,118$. The denominator is $\sqrt{237,254 + 233,136} = \sqrt{470,390} \approx 685.85$. The ratio is $4,118 / 685.85 \approx 6.0$. Since $6.0 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.05$. Target $p_2 = 0.05 \times 1.05 = 0.0525$.
Numerator: $0.05(0.95) + 0.0525(0.9475) = 0.0475 + 0.04974 = 0.09724$.
Denominator: $(0.0525 - 0.05)^2 = 0.00000625$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times (0.09724 / 0.00000625) \approx 122,133$.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 122,133 \approx 109,920$.
The smaller arm (control) has 233,136 users, which is well above 109,920. POWER is not flagged.
