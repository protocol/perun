**SRM**: The difference in users is $|37,620 - 37,564| = 56$. The denominator is $\sqrt{37,620 + 37,564} = \sqrt{75,184} \approx 274.2$. The ratio is $56 / 274.2 \approx 0.20$. Since $0.20 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.03$. Target $p_2 = 0.03 \times 1.08 = 0.0324$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.03(0.97) + 0.0324(0.9676)}{(0.0324 - 0.03)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0291 + 0.03135}{0.00000576} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.06045}{0.00000576} \approx 82,544$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 82,544 \approx 74,290$. The smaller arm (control) has 37,564 users. Since $37,564 < 74,290$, the test is underpowered. **Flag POWER**.

**PEEK**: Duration from July 31 to August 21 is 21 days inclusive (or 22 days depending on counting, but clearly $\ge 21$). Planned duration is 21 days. $0.8 \times 21 = 16.8$. Since actual days $\ge$ planned days, it was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is $1,127 / 48,876 \approx 0.02306$ (2.306%). Reported treatment rate is $1,180 / 40,908 \approx 0.02885$ (2.885%). The calculation uses sessions as the denominator, but randomization was by user. **Flag DENOM**.

**SIMPSON**: 
Pooled lift: Treatment ($1,180/37,620 \approx 3.14\%$) > Control ($1,127/37,564 \approx 3.00\%$). Lift is positive.
Desktop: Control $846/17,651 \approx 4.79\%$; Treatment $965/21,414 \approx 4.51\%$. Lift is negative.
Mobile: Control $281/19,913 \approx 1.41\%$; Treatment $215/16,206 \approx 1.33\%$. Lift is negative.
Since pooled lift is positive but negative in all segments, **Flag SIMPSON**.

**NOVELTY**: 
First 3 days mean: $(25.9 + 31.0 + 28.6) / 3 = 28.5$. Half is $14.25$.
Last 3 days mean: $(22.3 + 21.9 + 24.5) / 3 = 22.9$.
$22.9$ is not below $14.25$. No novelty effect flagged by this rule.
