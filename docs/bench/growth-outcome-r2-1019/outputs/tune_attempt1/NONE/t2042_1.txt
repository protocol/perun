**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.06$. Target $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.05 = 0.063$.

Required $n = 0.906 / 0.000009 \approx 100,667$ per arm.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 100,667 \approx 90,600$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 123,691 users, which is greater than 90,600. POWER is not flagged.
