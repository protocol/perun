**SRM**: The difference in users is $|58,091 - 56,045| = 2,046$. The denominator is $\sqrt{58,091 + 56,045} = \sqrt{114,136} \approx 337.8$. The ratio is $2,046 / 337.8 \approx 6.06$. Since $6.06 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

Required $n = 1.197 / 0.00004096 \approx 29,224$.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 29,224 = 26,302$.
The smaller arm (control) has 56,045 users. Since $56,045 > 26,302$, the test is sufficiently powered. POWER is not flagged.
