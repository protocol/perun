**SRM**: The difference in users is $|58,719 - 58,375| = 344$. The denominator is $\sqrt{58,719 + 58,375} = \sqrt{117,094} \approx 342.2$. The ratio is $344 / 342.2 \approx 1.01$. This is less than 3.5, so no mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.04$. Target $p_2 = 0.04 \times 1.10 = 0.044$.
Variance term: $0.04(0.96) + 0.044(0.956) = 0.0384 + 0.042064 = 0.080464$.
Difference squared: $(0.004)^2 = 0.000016$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times (0.080464 / 0.000016) = 7.85 \times 5029 = 39,478$.
The smaller arm (control) has 58,375 users. Since $58,375 > 0.9 \times 39,478$ ($35,530$), the test is sufficiently powered.

**PEEK**: Duration from May 8 to May 29 is 21 days inclusive. Planned duration is 21 days. $21 \ge 0.8 \times 21$ (16.8). The test ran the full duration.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is 4.0%. Calculated orders/users: $2,335 / 58,375 \approx 0.0400$. Calculated orders/sessions: $2,335 / 74,027 \approx 0.0315$. The reported rate matches orders/users. No error.

**SIMPSON**: 
Pooled lift: Treatment ($2,453/58,719 \approx 4.18\%$) > Control ($4.00\%$). Positive.
Desktop lift: Control $1,638/24,795 \approx 6.61\%$. Treatment $1,906/30,685 \approx 6.21\%$. Negative.
Mobile lift: Control $697/33,580 \approx 2.08\%$. Treatment $547/28,034 \approx 1.95\%$. Negative.
The pooled lift is positive, but both segments show negative lift. This is Simpson's Paradox.

**NOVELTY**: 
First 3 days mean: $(4.1+3.8+4.2)/3 = 4.03\%$.
Last 3 days mean: $(4.2+4.6+4.6)/3 = 4.47\%$.
The last mean (4.47) is not below half the first mean (2.015). The lift is not fading by this metric.

Only SIMPSON is flagged. The aggregated data suggests a benefit, but every user segment (desktop and mobile) actually performed worse in treatment. This indicates a severe confounding variable (likely uneven device distribution across arms despite randomization claims, or interaction effects) making the top-line result misleading. Rolling out based on the pooled positive lift would likely decrease overall conversions.
