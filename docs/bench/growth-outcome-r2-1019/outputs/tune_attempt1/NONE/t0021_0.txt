**SRM**: The difference in users is $|234,169 - 234,533| = 364$. The denominator is $\sqrt{234,169 + 234,533} = \sqrt{468,702} \approx 684.6$. The ratio is $364 / 684.6 \approx 0.53$. This is less than 3.5, so no SRM.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.05$. Target lift 5% means $p_2 = 0.05 \times 1.05 = 0.0525$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.05(0.95) + 0.0525(0.9475)}{(0.0525 - 0.05)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0475 + 0.04974}{0.00000625} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.09724}{0.00000625} \approx 122,133$.
The threshold is $0.9 \times 122,133 \approx 109,920$. The smaller arm (treatment) has 234,169 users, which is well above the threshold. No POWER issue.

**PEEK**: Planned duration is 14 days. The experiment ran from May 12 to May 18. Counting inclusive start and end dates (12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18), this is 7 days. Even if exclusive of the start date, it is 6 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 14 = 11.2$ days.
Actual days (6 or 7) < 11.2. The test stopped early. **PEEK** is flagged.

The reported rate matches Orders/Users. Since users were the randomized unit, this is the correct denominator. No DENOM error.
