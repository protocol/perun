**SRM**: The difference in users is $|37,146 - 36,074| = 1,072$. The denominator is $\sqrt{37,146 + 36,074} = \sqrt{73,220} \approx 270.6$. The ratio is $1,072 / 270.6 \approx 3.96$. Since $3.96 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.08$. Target $p_2 = 0.08 \times 1.08 = 0.0864$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.08 \times 0.92 + 0.0864 \times 0.9136) = 7.85 \times (0.0736 + 0.0789) \approx 1.199$.
Denominator: $(0.0864 - 0.08)^2 = 0.0064^2 = 0.00004096$.
Required $n = 1.199 / 0.00004096 \approx 29,272$.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 29,272 \approx 26,345$.
The smaller arm (control) has 36,074 users. Since $36,074 > 26,345$, the test is adequately powered. POWER is not flagged.

**PEEK**: Duration from Aug 26 to Sep 9 (inclusive) is 15 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 21 = 16.8$ days.
Since $15 < 16.8$, the test stopped early. PEEK is flagged.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is 8.0%.
Orders/Users: $2,886 / 36,074 \approx 0.0800$ (8.0%).
Orders/Sessions: $2,886 / 48,871 \approx 0.059$ (5.9%).
The reported rate matches Orders/Users. Since users were the randomized unit, this is the correct denominator. DENOM is not flagged.

Pooled Lift: Treatment ($3,155/37,146 \approx 8.49\%$) > Control ($8.00\%$). Positive.
Desktop Lift: Treatment ($2,108/13,885 \approx 15.18\%$) > Control ($1,928/13,484 \approx 14.30\%$). Positive.

**NOVELTY**:

Last 3 days mean: $(6.1 + 6.1 + 7.2) / 3 = 19.4 / 3 \approx 6.47$.

Since $6.47$ is not below $3.15$, the lift is not fading by this metric. NOVELTY is not flagged.
