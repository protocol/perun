**SRM**: The difference in users is $|34,723 - 34,539| = 184$. The denominator is $\sqrt{34,723 + 34,539} = \sqrt{69,262} \approx 263.2$. The ratio is $184 / 263.2 \approx 0.70$. Since $0.70 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.05$. Target $p_2 = 0.05 \times 1.10 = 0.055$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.05(0.95) + 0.055(0.945)}{(0.005)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0475 + 0.051975}{0.000025} = 7.85 \times 3979 \approx 31,235$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 31,235 \approx 28,112$. The smaller arm (control) has 34,539 users. Since $34,539 > 28,112$, the test is sufficiently powered.

**PEEK**: The experiment ran from Aug 14 to Aug 24 inclusive, which is 11 days. The planned duration was 14 days. The threshold is $0.8 \times 14 = 11.2$ days. Since $11 < 11.2$, the test stopped early. **Flag PEEK**.

**DENOM**: The randomization unit is users. The reported control rate is $3.795\%$.
Calculating orders/users: $1,727 / 34,539 \approx 0.0500$ ($5.00\%$).
Calculating orders/sessions: $1,727 / 45,507 \approx 0.03795$ ($3.795\%$).
The reported rate matches orders/sessions, not orders/users. **Flag DENOM**.
