**SRM**: The difference in users is $|42,401 - 43,791| = 1,390$. The denominator is $\sqrt{42,401 + 43,791} = \sqrt{86,192} \approx 293.6$. The ratio is $1,390 / 293.6 \approx 4.73$. Since $4.73 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

Denominator: $(0.0864 - 0.08)^2 = 0.0064^2 = 0.00004096$.
Required $n \approx 1.197 / 0.00004096 \approx 29,224$.
Threshold is $0.9 \times 29,224 \approx 26,302$. The smaller arm (treatment) has 42,401 users, which is above the threshold. POWER is not flagged.
