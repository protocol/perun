**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.04$. Target $p_2 = 0.04 \times 1.08 = 0.0432$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.04(0.96) + 0.0432(0.9568)}{(0.0032)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0384 + 0.0413}{0.00001024} \approx 7.85 \times 7783 \approx 61,097$.
The threshold is $0.9 \times 61,097 \approx 54,987$. The smaller arm (control) has 36,941 users. Since $36,941 < 54,987$, the test is underpowered. **Flag POWER**.
