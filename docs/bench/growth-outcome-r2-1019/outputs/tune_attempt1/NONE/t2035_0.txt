**SRM**: The difference in users is $|48,543 - 48,643| = 100$. The denominator is $\sqrt{48,543 + 48,643} = \sqrt{97,186} \approx 311.7$. The ratio is $100 / 311.7 \approx 0.32$. Since $0.32 < 3.5$, there is no Sample Ratio Mismatch.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.03$. Target lift 8% implies $p_2 = 0.03 \times 1.08 = 0.0324$.
Required $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.03(0.97) + 0.0324(0.9676)}{(0.0324 - 0.03)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0291 + 0.03135}{0.00000576} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.06045}{0.00000576} \approx 82,445$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 82,445 \approx 74,200$. The smaller arm has 48,543 users. Since $48,543 < 74,200$, the test is **underpowered**.

**PEEK**: Duration from Oct 8 to Nov 6 is 29 days (inclusive). Planned duration is 28 days.
Threshold: $0.8 \times 28 = 22.4$. Since $29 > 22.4$, the test was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: Reported control rate is $1,459 / 70,038 \approx 0.02083$ (2.083%). Reported treatment rate is $1,551 / 62,261 \approx 0.02491$ (2.491%).
The calculation uses sessions as the denominator, but randomization was by user. This is a **wrong denominator** error.

**SIMPSON**:

Mobile Control: $539/31,116 \approx 1.73\%$. Mobile Treatment: $573/31,052 \approx 1.85\%$. Lift is positive.
Since lifts are positive in both segments, there is no Simpson's paradox.
