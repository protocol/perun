**SRM**: The difference in users is $|153,707 - 153,595| = 112$. The denominator is $\sqrt{153,707 + 153,595} = \sqrt{307,302} \approx 554.3$. The ratio is $112 / 554.3 \approx 0.20$. Since $0.20 < 3.5$, there is no sample ratio mismatch.

The required sample size per arm is $n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.06(0.94) + 0.063(0.937)}{(0.063 - 0.06)^2}$.
Numerator: $0.0564 + 0.0590 = 0.1154$.
Denominator: $(0.003)^2 = 0.000009$.
$n = 7.85 \times (0.1154 / 0.000009) \approx 7.85 \times 12,822 \approx 100,653$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 100,653 \approx 90,588$.
The smaller arm (control) has 153,595 users, which is well above 90,588. The test is adequately powered.
