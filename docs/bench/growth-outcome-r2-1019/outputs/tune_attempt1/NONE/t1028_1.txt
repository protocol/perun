**SRM**: The difference in users is $|11,746 - 11,840| = 94$. The denominator is $\sqrt{11,746 + 11,840} = \sqrt{23,586} \approx 153.6$. The ratio is $94 / 153.6 \approx 0.61$. Since $0.61 < 3.5$, there is no sample ratio mismatch.

**POWER**: The baseline rate $p_1 = 0.06$. The target lift is 10%, so $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.10 = 0.066$.
The required sample size per arm is:
$n = 7.85 \times \frac{0.06(0.94) + 0.066(0.934)}{(0.066 - 0.06)^2} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.0564 + 0.061644}{0.000036} = 7.85 \times \frac{0.118044}{0.000036} \approx 25,771$.
The threshold for being underpowered is $0.9 \times 25,771 \approx 23,194$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 11,746 users. Since $11,746 < 23,194$, the test is underpowered. **Flag POWER**.

**PEEK**: The experiment ran from June 8 to June 30 inclusive. Count: $30 - 8 + 1 = 23$ days.
The planned duration was 21 days. The threshold is $0.8 \times 21 = 16.8$ days.
Since $23 > 16.8$, the test was not stopped early.

**DENOM**: The reported control conversion rate is $4.704\%$.
Calculating orders/users: $710 / 11,840 \approx 0.0599$ ($5.99\%$).
Calculating orders/sessions: $710 / 15,092 \approx 0.04704$ ($4.704\%$).
The reported rate matches orders/sessions, but users were the randomized unit. **Flag DENOM**.
