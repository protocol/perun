**SRM**: The difference in users is $|23,213 - 24,105| = 892$. The denominator is $\sqrt{23,213 + 24,105} = \sqrt{47,318} \approx 217.5$. The ratio is $892 / 217.5 \approx 4.1$. Since $4.1 \ge 3.5$, SRM is flagged.

**POWER**: Baseline $p_1 = 0.06$. Target $p_2 = 0.06 \times 1.08 = 0.0648$.
Numerator: $7.85 \times (0.06 \times 0.94 + 0.0648 \times 0.9352) \approx 7.85 \times (0.0564 + 0.0606) = 0.9183$.
Denominator: $(0.0648 - 0.06)^2 = 0.0048^2 = 0.00002304$.
Required $n \approx 0.9183 / 0.00002304 \approx 39,857$.
Threshold for underpowered: $0.9 \times 39,857 \approx 35,871$.
The smaller arm (treatment) has 23,213 users. Since $23,213 < 35,871$, POWER is flagged.

**PEEK**: Start date June 5, end date June 21. Duration is 16 days (inclusive) or 17 days depending on counting, but definitely not fewer than planned. Even if strictly counting full days between dates ($21-5=16$), the planned duration is 14 days. $16 \ge 0.8 \times 14$ ($11.2$). The test ran longer than planned, not shorter. PEEK is not flagged.
