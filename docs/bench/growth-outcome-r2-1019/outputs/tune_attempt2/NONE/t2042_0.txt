**SRM**: The total users are 249,438. Expected per arm is 124,719. Observed: Control 125,747, Treatment 123,691.
Difference is ~1,000 users. Standard error for proportion $p=0.5$ is $\sqrt{0.5 \times 0.5 / 249438} \approx 0.001$.
Observed split: $125747/249438 \approx 50.41\%$. Deviation is $0.41\%$, which is ~4 standard errors away from 50%. This exceeds chance limits ($p < 0.0001$). **Flagged.**

**PEEK**: Planned duration was 14 days. Final readout date is 2026-02-25, start date 2026-02-16.
Duration = 9 days (inclusive) or 10 days max. The test stopped early (Day 9/10 vs Day 14) while checking significance ($p=0.0593$ is close to 0.05). Stopping before planned duration when nearing significance suggests peeking. **Flagged.**

**POWER**: Baseline 6%, target lift 5% (to 6.3%).
Current sample size ~124k per arm.
Standard error of difference $\approx \sqrt{\frac{0.06(0.94)}{124000} + \frac{0.06(0.94)}{124000}} \approx 0.00095$.
Detectable lift at 80% power, alpha 0.05 requires $\approx 2.8 \times SE \approx 0.27\%$ absolute. Target is $0.3\%$ absolute.
The study appears adequately powered for the target given the final N, though it failed to reach significance. However, since the sample size was achieved (despite SRM), and the target lift is within detectable range for this N, POWER is not the primary numerical error shown by the counts themselves compared to SRM.
