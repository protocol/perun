**POWER Check**: Baseline 5%, Target lift 10% (to 5.5%). Alpha 0.05, Power 80%.
Required sample size per arm $\approx 2 \times (1.96+0.84)^2 \times (0.05 \times 0.95) / (0.005)^2 \approx 29,700$.
Actual sample size ~22,300 per arm.
The study is underpowered to detect the target lift. However, the result was not significant ($p=0.186$), so the team isn't falsely claiming success based on low power alone, but the design flaw exists. Usually, POWER is flagged if a *significant* result is found in an underpowered study (indicating inflated effect size) or if the failure to detect is the issue. Given the prompt asks to flag if numbers show the error: The sample is indeed too small for the stated design parameters. **POWER flagged**.
