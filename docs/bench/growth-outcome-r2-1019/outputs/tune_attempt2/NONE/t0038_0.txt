**SRM Check**: Planned split is 50/50. Total users = 30,420 + 28,954 = 59,374. Expected per arm = 29,687.
Difference = |30,420 - 29,687| = 733.
Standard Error (SE) for binomial split ≈ $\sqrt{n \cdot p \cdot (1-p)} = \sqrt{59374 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.5} \approx 122$.
Z-score = $733 / 122 \approx 6.0$. A Z-score > 1.96 indicates a significant mismatch. The arms differ significantly from 50/50. **Flag SRM.**

**PEEK Check**: Start date 2026-06-24, end date 2026-07-08. Duration = 14 days. Planned duration = 14 days. The test ran for the full planned duration. **No PEEK.**

**POWER Check**: Baseline 6%, target lift 5% (absolute 0.3%). Sample sizes ~30k.
Approximate SE for difference in proportions $\approx \sqrt{\frac{0.06(0.94)}{30000} + \frac{0.06(0.94)}{30000}} \approx 0.0019$.
Detectable lift at 80% power $\approx 2.8 \times SE \approx 0.53\%$. Target is 0.3%. The study is technically underpowered for the *target* lift, but it found a significant result for a *larger* observed lift (0.43%). However, the definition provided flags if the arm is "too small to detect the target lift". Since $N$ yields a minimum detectable effect (MDE) of ~0.53% which is greater than the target 0.3%, the design is underpowered for the stated goal. **Flag POWER.**

**SIMPSON Check**:
Control Rate: Desktop $1107/10701 \approx 10.34\%$, Mobile $718/19719 \approx 3.64\%$.
Treatment Rate: Desktop $1129/10186 \approx 11.08\%$, Mobile $733/18768 \approx 3.91\%$.
Segment lifts: Desktop (+7.1%), Mobile (+7.4%). Pooled lift is +7.2%. Directions match. **No SIMPSON.**

**DENOM Check**: Randomized by user id. Metric is order conversion rate.
Control: 1,825 orders / 30,420 users = 6.00%. Reported 5.999%.
Treatment: 1,862 orders / 28,954 users = 6.43%. Reported 6.431%.
Denominator matches randomized unit (users). **No DENOM.**

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift drops from 19.5% to 1.5%. Clear fading trend. The team projects the current reported average (7.2%) for 12 months, ignoring the decay to ~1.5% by the end. **Flag NOVELTY.**

ERRORS: SRM, POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
