**SRM Check**: Total users = 156,402 (Control) + 156,746 (Treatment) = 313,148. Expected per arm = 156,574.
Difference = 156,746 - 156,574 = 172.
Standard Error for proportion $p=0.5$ is $\sqrt{n \cdot p \cdot (1-p)} = \sqrt{313148 \cdot 0.25} \approx 279$.
The difference (172) is less than 2 standard errors ($172 < 558$). The split is statistically consistent with 50/50. No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: Planned duration was 21 days. The readout date is 2026-03-29, and the start date was 2026-03-19.
Duration elapsed = 10 days.
The test was stopped at 10/21 (~48%) of the planned duration. Stopping early based on significance inflates false positives. This is a clear PEEK error.

**POWER Check**: The smaller arm (Control) has 156,402 users.
Baseline = 3.0%, Target Lift = 8% (absolute change = 0.24%).
Using standard approximation $n \approx 16 \cdot p \cdot (1-p) / \delta^2$:
$n \approx 16 \cdot 0.03 \cdot 0.97 / (0.0024)^2 \approx 0.4656 / 0.00000576 \approx 80,833$.
The actual sample size (156k) exceeds the required size (~81k). The test is adequately powered. No POWER error.

**SIMPSON Check**:
Desktop Conversion: Control = 3088/59999 ≈ 5.15%; Treatment = 3257/60131 ≈ 5.42%. (Lift positive)
Mobile Conversion: Control = 1604/96403 ≈ 1.66%; Treatment = 1692/96615 ≈ 1.75%. (Lift positive)
Both segments show positive lift, matching the pooled result. No SIMPSON error.

**DENOM Check**: Randomization was by user id. The metric is "order conversion rate" (Orders/Users). The denominator matches the randomization unit. No DENOM error.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift trends: 13.6% → 0.8%. There is a sharp, consistent decay over the 10 days.
The team projects the current reported lift (5.2%) for the next twelve months. Given the rapid fade to near-zero by day 10, projecting the average lift as a permanent effect ignores the novelty wear-off visible in the data. This is a NOVELTY error.

ERRORS: PEEK, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
