While the study failed to detect the target lift, "underpowered" usually refers to a design flaw where the sample size was insufficient *a priori*. The failure to find significance is due to the true effect being small, not necessarily a design power error. However, the prompt asks if the smaller arm is too small to detect the *target* lift.
