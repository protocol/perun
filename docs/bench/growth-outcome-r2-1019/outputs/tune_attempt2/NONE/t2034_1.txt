First, I check for Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The total users are 76,123 (control) + 76,269 (treatment) = 152,392. The expected split is 50%, so 76,196 per arm. The treatment has 73 more users than expected. The standard deviation for a binomial distribution here is $\sqrt{152392 \times 0.5 \times 0.5} \approx 195$. The difference (73) is less than 2 standard deviations ($73 < 390$), so the split is within chance expectations. No SRM.

Next, I check the denominator. Users were randomized by user ID. The metric is "order conversion rate." The readout provides users and orders. Calculating the rates: Control $3,045 / 76,123 \approx 4.00\%$. Treatment $3,220 / 76,269 \approx 4.22\%$. However, the reported rates are 2.996% and 3.433%. Let's check against sessions. Control: $3,045 / 101,641 \approx 2.996\%$. Treatment: $3,220 / 93,806 \approx 3.433\%$. The reported metric uses sessions as the denominator, but randomization was by user. Since users can have multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption and mismatches the randomization unit. This is a DENOM error.

I check for Simpson's Paradox.
Desktop Control Rate: $2,335 / 36,977 \approx 6.31\%$.
Desktop Treatment Rate: $2,469 / 37,048 \approx 6.66\%$. (Lift positive)
Mobile Control Rate: $710 / 39,146 \approx 1.81\%$.
Mobile Treatment Rate: $751 / 39,221 \approx 1.91\%$. (Lift positive)
Both segments show a positive lift, matching the pooled direction. No Simpson's paradox.

Regarding PEEK: The test ran from Feb 25 to Mar 25 (28 days), matching the planned duration. No evidence of early stopping.

Regarding POWER: The sample size (~76k per arm) is large. With a 4% baseline, detecting an 8% lift requires roughly 30k-40k per arm typically. We have double that. It is not underpowered.

Regarding NOVELTY: The daily lift data shows a clear downward trend: starting at 27.7% and ending at 6.4%. Despite this fade, the team projects the overall average lift (14.6%) for the next twelve months. This ignores the decay pattern evident in the data. This is a NOVELTY error.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
