**SRM Check**: Total users = 302,754. Expected per arm = 151,377.
Control deviation: $|152,488 - 151,377| = 1,111$.
Treatment deviation: $|150,266 - 151,377| = 1,111$.
Standard Error for 50/50 split $\approx \sqrt{302754 \times 0.5 \times 0.5} \approx 275$.
Z-score $\approx 1111 / 275 \approx 4.0$. This exceeds the typical threshold of 2 (or 3), indicating a significant Sample Ratio Mismatch. Assignment or logging is likely broken.

**PEEK Check**: The test ran from March 1 to March 15 (14 days), matching the planned duration exactly. No evidence of early stopping.

**POWER Check**: Both arms have ~150k users. With a 5% baseline and 80% power at alpha 0.05, the required sample size per arm for a 5% relative lift is roughly 300k–600k total (depending on variance assumptions), but 150k per arm is generally sufficient to detect a 5% lift if the effect exists. However, since SRM is already flagged, the effective power calculation is compromised by the broken randomization. Strictly looking at size alone, it is not obviously underpowered for the stated target, but the SRM invalidates the comparison.

**SIMPSON Check**:
Control Desktop Rate: $5,185 / 63,311 \approx 8.19\%$.
Treatment Desktop Rate: $5,394 / 62,388 \approx 8.65\%$. (Lift positive)
Control Mobile Rate: $2,440 / 89,177 \approx 2.74\%$.
Treatment Mobile Rate: $2,538 / 87,878 \approx 2.89\%$. (Lift positive)
Both segments show a positive lift consistent with the pooled result. No Simpson's Paradox.

**DENOM Check**: Randomization was by user id. The metric is order conversion rate. The readout implies orders per user ($7,625/152,488 \approx 5.0\%$). The denominator matches the randomized unit.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift starts at 11.8% and decays steadily to 1.9% by day 14. The team projects the current reported average lift (5.6%) for the next twelve months. This ignores the clear fading trend, assuming a lasting effect despite data showing a novelty decay.

ERRORS: SRM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
