**SRM Check**: The planned split is 50/50. Total users = 92,186 (Control) + 92,068 (Treatment) = 184,254. Expected per arm = 92,127.
Difference = |92,186 - 92,127| = 59.
Standard Deviation ≈ $\sqrt{184254 \times 0.5 \times 0.5} \approx 215$.
Z-score = $59 / 215 \approx 0.27$. This is well within chance limits (typically |Z| > 2 or 3 flags SRM). No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: Planned duration was 14 days. The readout date is 2026-02-10, and the start was 2026-02-04. This covers only 6 days of data (confirmed by the daily lift list ending at day 6). The test was stopped/analyzed at 6/14 (~43%) of the planned duration. Stopping early to check significance inflates false positives. **PEEK flagged.**

**POWER Check**: We must verify if the sample size is sufficient for the target lift.
Target relative lift = 8% on 5.0% baseline = 0.4% absolute increase (to 5.4%).
Using standard approximation for proportions ($n \approx 16 \times \sigma^2 / \delta^2$ for 80% power, 5% alpha):
$\sigma^2 \approx p(1-p) \approx 0.05 \times 0.95 = 0.0475$.
$\delta = 0.004$.
Required $n \approx 16 \times 0.0475 / (0.004)^2 = 0.76 / 0.000016 = 47,500$ per arm.
Actual n ≈ 92,000 per arm. The study is adequately powered for the *target* lift, even if the *observed* lift was smaller. No POWER error based on design parameters.
