First, I check for Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The total user count is 294,708 (control) + 299,412 (treatment) = 594,120. The expected split is 50%, so 297,060 per arm. The treatment has 2,352 more users than expected. The standard deviation for a binomial distribution here is $\sqrt{594120 \times 0.5 \times 0.5} \approx 385$. The difference is $2352 / 385 \approx 6.1$ standard deviations. This is far beyond chance; assignment or logging is broken. **SRM** is flagged.

Next, I check the Denominator (DENOM). The metric is "order conversion rate." The randomization unit is "user id." The readout provides user counts and order counts. Conversion rate is typically Orders/Users. Since the denominator (users) matches the randomization unit, there is no DENOM error based on the provided text.

I check for Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON).
Control Desktop Rate: $8,447 / 139,238 \approx 6.07\%$
Treatment Desktop Rate: $8,990 / 141,460 \approx 6.36\%$ (Lift present)
Control Mobile Rate: $3,341 / 155,470 \approx 2.15\%$
Treatment Mobile Rate: $3,555 / 157,952 \approx 2.25\%$ (Lift present)
Both segments show a positive lift, consistent with the pooled result. No Simpson's paradox.

I check for Novelty (NOVELTY). The daily lift starts at 11.6% and decays steadily to ~1.4% by day 28. The team recommends rolling out based on the aggregate lift (4.8%) and projecting it for twelve months. Ignoring the clear fading trend and projecting the inflated early lift as lasting constitutes a novelty error. **NOVELTY** is flagged.

Regarding Power (POWER): The sample sizes are large (~300k per arm), baseline is 4%, and target lift is 5%. The observed p-value is significant. The study is not underpowered.

Regarding Peeking (PEEK): The test ran from Aug 19 to Sep 16. This is 28 days. The planned duration was 28 days. The test ran for the full planned duration. No peeking error is evident from the dates.

The presence of SRM invalidates the statistical validity of the test entirely, as the groups are not comparable. The NOVELTY error suggests the long-term projection is incorrect. Both require fixing before a rollout decision.

ERRORS: SRM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
