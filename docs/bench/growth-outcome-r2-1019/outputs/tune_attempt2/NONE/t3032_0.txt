**SRM Check**: Total users = 267,248. Expected per arm = 133,624. Observed: Control 133,687, Treatment 133,561. Difference is negligible (<0.1%), well within chance for this sample size. No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: Planned duration 14 days (Oct 15–Oct 28). Readout date Oct 31 implies 16 days of data. The test ran longer than planned, not shorter. No PEEK.

**POWER Check**: Baseline 5%, target lift 5% (absolute 0.25%). Alpha 0.05, Power 80%. Required sample size per arm ≈ 2 × (1.96 + 0.84)² × [0.05(0.95) + 0.0525(0.9475)] / (0.0025)² ≈ 310,000 users total (approx). Actual total users ~267k. Slightly underpowered but not severely; however, the p-value (0.23) is far from significance, so low power isn't the primary issue here. Not clearly POWER error based on numbers alone.

Direction reverses in segments vs. pooled. Clear SIMPSON.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift declines from 3.6% to 0.8% over 16 days, showing clear fade. Yet the team projects the final 2.0% lift over 12 months. This ignores the decay trend. Clear NOVELTY.
