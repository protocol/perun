**SRM**: The planned split is 50/50. Total users = 32,864 + 32,618 = 65,482. Expected per arm = 32,741.
Control deviation: |32,864 - 32,741| = 123.
Treatment deviation: |32,618 - 32,741| = 123.
Standard Error for difference in proportions ($\sqrt{n \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.5}$) $\approx \sqrt{16370} \approx 128$.
The deviation (123) is less than 2 standard errors (~256). The split is statistically consistent with 50/50. No SRM.

**PEEK**: The test started 2026-06-15 and ended 2026-07-14.
Duration: June 15 to July 14 is exactly 30 days (inclusive) or 29 days elapsed. The planned duration was 28 days. The test ran for the full planned duration (actually slightly longer). No PEEK.

**POWER**: Baseline 6.0%, target lift 10% (to 6.6%).
Sample size per arm $\approx$ 32,700.
Using standard approximation for proportion test: $n \approx \frac{16 \cdot p(1-p)}{(p_1-p_0)^2}$.
$p \approx 0.06$, $\Delta = 0.006$.
$n \approx \frac{16 \cdot 0.06 \cdot 0.94}{0.000036} \approx \frac{0.9024}{0.000036} \approx 25,066$.
The actual sample size (32,700) exceeds the required size (~25,100). The test is adequately powered. No POWER error.

**SIMPSON**: Check segment conversion rates.
Control Desktop: $1,251 / 12,145 \approx 10.30\%$.
Treatment Desktop: $1,408 / 14,336 \approx 9.82\%$. (Treatment is lower).
Control Mobile: $721 / 20,719 \approx 3.48\%$.
Treatment Mobile: $607 / 18,282 \approx 3.32\%$. (Treatment is lower).
Both segments show negative lift, yet the pooled result reports positive lift (+15.3%). This is a clear reversal. **SIMPSON** flagged.

**DENOM**: Randomization is by user id. The metric is "order conversion rate".
Usually, conversion rate is Orders / Users or Orders / Sessions.
If Orders / Users: Control $1,972/32,864 = 6.00\%$. Treatment $2,015/32,618 = 6.18\%$.
The reported rates are 4.642% and 5.35%.
Let's check Orders / Sessions: Control $1,972/42,478 = 4.64\%$. Treatment $2,015/37,662 = 5.35\%$.
The reported metric uses Sessions as the denominator. Users were randomized, not sessions. If the treatment affects session count per user (which it clearly does: Control 1.29 sess/user vs Treatment 1.15 sess/user), using sessions as the denominator biases the result. This is a denominator mismatch relative to the randomization unit. **DENOM** flagged.

**NOVELTY**: Daily lift starts at 29.9% and trends downward to ~8.7% by day 29. The team projects this high lift for 12 months despite the clear fading trend. **NOVELTY** flagged.

ERRORS: SIMPSON, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
