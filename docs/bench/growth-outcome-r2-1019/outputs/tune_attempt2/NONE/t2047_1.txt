**SRM Check**: The total users are 42,512 (Control) + 43,676 (Treatment) = 86,188. Expected per arm is 43,094.
Difference = |42,512 - 43,094| = 582.
Standard Error ≈ $\sqrt{N \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.5} = \sqrt{21,547} \approx 147$.
Z-score = $582 / 147 \approx 3.96$. A Z-score > 1.96 indicates a significant deviation from the 50/50 split. The assignment or logging is broken.

**DENOM Check**: Randomization was by **user id**. The reported metric is "order conversion rate."
Control Rate Calculation: $3,401 \text{ orders} / 42,512 \text{ users} = 8.00\%$.
However, the readout reports **6.167%**.
If we divide orders by sessions: $3,401 / 55,150 = 6.167\%$.
The reported metric uses **sessions** as the denominator, not the randomized unit (users). This violates the experimental design and biases the result if session counts differ between arms (which they do: 55k vs 50k).

**Other Checks**:
- **PEEK**: The test ran from Jan 10 to Feb 9 (30 days), exceeding the planned 28 days. No evidence of early stopping.
- **POWER**: With ~43k users per arm and an 8% baseline, the test is well-powered to detect a 5% lift.
- **SIMPSON**: 
  - Desktop Control: $2,359/19,548 = 12.07\%$. Treatment: $2,556/20,084 = 12.73\%$. (Lift positive)
  - Mobile Control: $1,042/22,964 = 4.54\%$. Treatment: $1,128/23,592 = 4.78\%$. (Lift positive)
  - Both segments show a positive lift; no reversal exists.
- **NOVELTY**: Daily lift fluctuates (13.9% to 25.0%) but shows no clear fading trend over the 30 days; the average remains high.

Two critical errors are present: Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) invalidates the randomization integrity, and Denominator Mismatch (DENOM) means the reported metric does not match the randomized unit.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
