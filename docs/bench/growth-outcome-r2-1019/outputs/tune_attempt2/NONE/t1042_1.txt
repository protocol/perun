**SRM Check**: Total users = 146,340 + 146,746 = 293,086. Expected per arm = 146,543.
Difference = |146,746 - 146,543| = 203.
Standard Error ≈ $\sqrt{293086 \times 0.5 \times 0.5} \approx 270$.
Z-score = 203 / 270 ≈ 0.75. This is well within chance (|Z| < 1.96). No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: Planned duration was 14 days; readout is at day 8 (Oct 7 to Oct 15). The test was stopped early. The daily lift shows a sharp decay (10.9% → 0.7%), suggesting the team may have called it once significance was hit or panic set in. Stopping before the planned duration inflates false positives. **PEEK flagged.**

**POWER Check**: Both arms are ~146k. With a 6% baseline and 5% target lift, standard calculators indicate ~130k-140k users per arm are needed for 80% power at alpha 0.05. The sample size is sufficient. No POWER error.

**SIMPSON Check**:
Control Rate Desktop: 5,841 / 57,398 ≈ 10.18%
Treatment Rate Desktop: 6,891 / 72,124 ≈ 9.55% (Lift is negative)
Control Rate Mobile: 2,940 / 88,942 ≈ 3.31%
Treatment Rate Mobile: 2,316 / 74,622 ≈ 3.10% (Lift is negative)
Both segments show a decrease in conversion, yet the pooled result reports a 4.6% increase. This is a classic reversal. **SIMPSON flagged.**

**DENOM Check**: Randomization was by user ID. The metric is order conversion rate (Orders / Users). The denominator matches the randomization unit. No DENOM error.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift decays from 10.9% to 0.7%. The team projects the current reported average (4.6%) over 12 months despite the trend nearing zero. This ignores the fading effect. **NOVELTY flagged.**

ERRORS: PEEK, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
