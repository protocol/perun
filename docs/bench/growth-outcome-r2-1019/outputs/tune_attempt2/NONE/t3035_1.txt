**SRM Check**: Total users = 28,193 (control) + 28,351 (treatment) = 56,544. Expected per arm = 28,272.
Difference = 79 users. Standard error for proportion $p=0.5$ is $\sqrt{N \cdot p \cdot (1-p)} = \sqrt{56544 \cdot 0.25} \approx 119$.
The difference (79) is less than 2 standard deviations ($2 \times 119 = 238$). The split is statistically consistent with 50/50. No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: Started 2026-04-24, planned 28 days. Expected end: 2026-05-22. Readout date: 2026-05-23.
The test ran for 29 days (one day over), not stopped early. No PEEK.

**POWER Check**: Baseline 6.0%, target lift 8% (absolute difference 0.48%).
Using standard approximation for sample size per arm: $n \approx \frac{16 \cdot p(1-p)}{\Delta^2}$.
$n \approx \frac{16 \cdot 0.06 \cdot 0.94}{0.0048^2} = \frac{0.9024}{0.00002304} \approx 39,167$ users per arm.
Actual users per arm (~28,200) are significantly lower than the required ~39,200. The study is underpowered to detect the target lift. Flag POWER.

**SIMPSON Check**:
Desktop CR: Control $1193/12578 \approx 9.48\%$, Treatment $1272/12649 \approx 10.06\%$. Lift positive.
Mobile CR: Control $499/15615 \approx 3.19\%$, Treatment $532/15702 \approx 3.39\%$. Lift positive.
Both segments show positive lift, matching the pooled direction. No SIMPSON.

**DENOM Check**: Randomized by user id. Metric is order conversion rate.
Implicit denominator is users (orders/users). Control: $1692/28193 \approx 6.001\%$. Treatment: $1804/28351 \approx 6.363\%$.
The reported percentages match the user-based calculation. The denominator matches the randomization unit. No DENOM.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift starts at 11.5% and trends downward to 1.9% by day 29.
The readout reports a total aggregate lift of 6.0% but the team projects this lift for the next twelve months.
Given the clear fading trend (novelty effect), projecting the current average or initial lift forward without adjustment is erroneous. The numbers show fading, yet the projection assumes persistence. Flag NOVELTY.

ERRORS: POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
