**SRM Check**: Total users = 118,656. Expected per arm = 59,328. Observed: Control 59,288, Treatment 59,368. Difference is negligible (~0.07%), well within chance. No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: Planned duration 14 days; readout at day 8 (April 16 to April 24 inclusive is 9 days, or roughly day 8/9). Stopping early (8/14 ≈ 57% of duration) inflates false positive risk. **PEEK flagged**.

**POWER Check**: Baseline 4.0%, target lift 10% (to 4.4%). With ~59k users/arm, standard error for difference in proportions is roughly $\sqrt{2 \times 0.04 \times 0.96 / 59000} \approx 0.00114$. Detectable lift at 80% power/5% alpha is roughly $2.8 \times SE \approx 0.32\%$ absolute (8% relative). The study is adequately powered for a 10% lift. No POWER error.

**SIMPSON Check**: 
Control rates: Desktop $1443/22432 \approx 6.43\%$; Mobile $928/36856 \approx 2.52\%$.
Treatment rates: Desktop $1768/29234 \approx 6.05\%$; Mobile $713/30134 \approx 2.37\%$.
Segment lifts: Desktop $(6.05-6.43)/6.43 \approx -5.9\%$; Mobile $(2.37-2.52)/2.52 \approx -5.9\%$.
Both segments show negative lift, yet the pooled result reports positive lift (+4.5%). This is a clear reversal. **SIMPSON flagged**. (Cause: Treatment arm has higher proportion of high-converting desktop users: 49% vs 38% in control).

**DENOM Check**: Metric is order conversion rate. Randomization unit is user id. The counts provided are users and orders. If the rate is Orders/Users, the denominator matches the randomization unit. No obvious DENOM error from the numbers alone.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift: 4.9, 4.9, 4.3, 4.2, 4.1, 3.6, 5.3, 4.8. There is no clear fading trend; it fluctuates. Projecting 12 months is aggressive but the "fading" condition for NOVELTY isn't strictly met by the data sequence.

The critical errors are stopping early (PEEK) and the severe Simpson's paradox where every segment performs worse, but the aggregate looks better due to imbalanced segmentation.

ERRORS: PEEK, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
