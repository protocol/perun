**SRM**: The planned split is 50/50. Total users = 43,791 + 42,401 = 86,192. Expected per arm = 43,096.
Control deviation: (43,791 - 43,096) / 43,096 ≈ 1.6%.
Treatment deviation: (42,401 - 43,096) / 43,096 ≈ -1.6%.
For N ≈ 86k, the standard error for a 50% proportion is $\sqrt{0.5 \times 0.5 / 86192} \approx 0.0017$ (0.17%). A 1.6% deviation is roughly 9.4 standard errors away, which is statistically impossible by chance. This indicates broken assignment or logging.

**PEEK**: The planned duration was 21 days. The readout date is April 22, and the start was April 10. This is only 12 days of data. The test was stopped 9 days early. Stopping early based on interim results inflates the false positive rate.

**DENOM**: Users were randomized by user ID. The reported metric is "order conversion rate."
Control rate calculation: 3,503 orders / 58,937 sessions = 5.944%.
Treatment rate calculation: 3,574 orders / 50,265 sessions = 7.11%.
The denominator used is **sessions**, not **users**. Since the unit of randomization is the user, analyzing at the session level violates the independence assumption unless sessions per user are identical across arms (which they are not: Control ≈ 1.35 sess/user, Treatment ≈ 1.18 sess/user). This biases the result.

**NOVELTY**: The daily lift data (20.8% down to 18.7%) shows minor fluctuation but no clear fading trend over the 12 days. There is insufficient evidence in these numbers to claim the lift is fading.

**POWER**: With ~42k users per arm and a baseline of ~8%, the test is likely adequately powered to detect an 8% lift, especially given the massive observed effect size (though the effect is likely artificial due to DENOM/SRM). No clear underpowering error is visible from the counts alone.

**SIMPSON**:
Desktop Control: 2,267/17,752 ≈ 12.77%
Desktop Treatment: 2,313/17,189 ≈ 13.46% (Lift)
Mobile Control: 1,236/26,039 ≈ 4.75%
Mobile Treatment: 1,261/25,212 ≈ 5.00% (Lift)
Both segments show a positive lift, consistent with the pooled direction. No paradox.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
