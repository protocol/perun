Here, Control > Treatment (Control wins).

Mobile Control: $1,711 / 59,338 \approx 2.88\%$.

Since the direction of the effect reverses when pooling the data (Treatment appears better overall, but worse in every segment), this is a clear case of Simpson's Paradox. This usually indicates a weighting issue where the treatment arm received disproportionately more traffic from the high-converting segment (Desktop: 55k vs 50k) compared to the control arm.
