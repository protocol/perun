**SRM Check**: Total users = 148,729 (Control) + 148,841 (Treatment) = 297,570. Expected per arm = 148,785. The difference is only 56 users (~0.04%), well within chance variation. No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: Started June 2, planned 14 days, readout June 16. This matches the planned duration exactly. No PEEK.

**POWER Check**: Baseline 5%, target lift 5% (to 5.25%). With ~148k users per arm, the sample size is sufficient to detect this lift at 80% power and 0.05 alpha. The p-value (0.0657) indicates the result is not statistically significant, but the study was not underpowered by design; it simply failed to find significance. No POWER error based on design numbers.

**SIMPSON Check**: 
Control Rate Desktop: 4,890 / 55,332 ≈ 8.84%
Treatment Rate Desktop: 5,485 / 65,082 ≈ 8.43% (Treatment worse)
Control Rate Mobile: 2,547 / 93,397 ≈ 2.73%
Treatment Rate Mobile: 2,178 / 83,759 ≈ 2.60% (Treatment worse)
Both segments show the treatment performing worse than control, yet the pooled result claims a lift (5.148% vs 5.0%). This is a clear reversal. **SIMPSON** paradox is present. The aggregation is misleading because the treatment arm has a higher proportion of Desktop users (who convert at higher rates naturally), artificially inflating the pooled treatment average.

**DENOM Check**: Randomized by user id. Metric is order conversion rate (orders/users). Denominator matches randomization unit. No DENOM.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift drops from 5.8% to 0.6%. The trend is clearly fading. However, the readout reports the aggregate 3.0% lift and the team projects this for 12 months. While the fade is evident, the primary analytical failure causing the incorrect "lift" conclusion is the aggregation error (Simpson's). The projection assumes the current aggregate holds, which is wrong, but the root cause of the positive signal is Simpson's paradox. If we strictly look at "lift fading... projected as lasting," NOVELTY applies. The daily data shows a decay to near zero, yet the recommendation rolls out based on the average. I will flag NOVELTY as the projection ignores the decay trend visible in the daily numbers.
