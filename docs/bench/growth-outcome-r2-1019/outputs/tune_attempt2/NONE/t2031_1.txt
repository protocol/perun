Difference = 37,146 - 36,610 = 536.
Standard Error for proportion $p=0.5$ is $\sqrt{N \cdot p \cdot (1-p)} = \sqrt{73220 \cdot 0.25} \approx 135$.
The deviation is $536 / 135 \approx 3.97$ standard deviations. This exceeds the typical threshold (e.g., 2 SDs), indicating a significant Sample Ratio Mismatch. Assignment or logging is likely broken.

The definition "too small to detect" implies the test failed to find significance when it should have, or the sample is insufficient for the *claimed* target. Here the sample found significance for a smaller lift. I will not flag POWER as the primary issue compared to the definitive SRM and PEEK.
