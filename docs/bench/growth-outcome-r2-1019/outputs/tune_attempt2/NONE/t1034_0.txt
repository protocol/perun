**SRM Check**: Planned split is 50/50. Total users = 43,273 + 41,845 = 85,118. Expected per arm = 42,559.
Control deviation: (43,273 - 42,559) / 42,559 ≈ 1.68%.
Treatment deviation: (41,845 - 42,559) / 42,559 ≈ -1.68%.
For N ≈ 85k, a 1.7% deviation is statistically significant (Z-score > 4), indicating the assignment or logging is broken. **Flag SRM.**

**PEEK Check**: Started July 20, planned 21 days (end Aug 10). Readout date is Aug 10. The test ran for the full planned duration. **No PEEK.**

**POWER Check**: Target lift 8%, baseline 8%. The observed lift is 4.5% with p=0.0544. While not significant, the sample sizes (~42k each) are large. A quick rule of thumb for 8% baseline and 8% lift requires ~30k per arm for 80% power. The study is adequately sized to detect the *target* lift, even if the *observed* lift is smaller. **No POWER error based on design parameters.**

**DENOM Check**: Randomized by user id. Metric is order conversion rate (Orders / Users). The denominator matches the randomization unit. **No DENOM.**

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift starts at 9.0% and trends downward to 0.7% by day 21. The team projects this fading lift over the next twelve months as if it were constant. This ignores the decay pattern evident in the data. **Flag NOVELTY.**
