**SRM Check**: Total users = 17,296 (Control) + 17,138 (Treatment) = 34,434. Expected per arm = 17,217.
Difference = |17,296 - 17,217| = 79.
Standard Deviation ≈ $\sqrt{34434 \times 0.5 \times 0.5} \approx 92.7$.
The difference (79) is less than 2 standard deviations (~185). The split is within chance expectations. No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: Planned duration was 14 days. The readout date is 2026-01-25, and the start was 2026-01-16. This covers only 9 days of data (Day 1 to Day 9 listed). The test was stopped 5 days early. Stopping before the planned duration based on interim results inflates false positives. **PEEK flagged**.

**POWER Check**: The smaller arm is Treatment (17,138 users).
Baseline = 8.0%, Target Lift = 8% (absolute rate 8.64%).
Using standard approximation for sample size $n \approx \frac{16 \cdot p(1-p)}{\Delta^2}$ where $\Delta$ is the absolute difference (0.0064).
$n \approx \frac{16 \cdot 0.08 \cdot 0.92}{0.0064^2} \approx \frac{1.1776}{0.00004096} \approx 28,750$ per arm.
The actual sample size (~17k) is significantly lower than the required ~29k to detect an 8% relative lift with 80% power. The test is underpowered. **POWER flagged**.

**SIMPSON Check**:
Desktop Control Rate: $1040/8313 \approx 12.51\%$. Treatment: $1097/8237 \approx 13.32\%$. (Lift positive)
Mobile Control Rate: $343/8983 \approx 3.82\%$. Treatment: $362/8901 \approx 4.07\%$. (Lift positive)
Both segments show a positive lift, consistent with the pooled result. No Simpson's paradox.

**DENOM Check**: Randomization was by user ID. The metric is order conversion rate (Orders/Users). The denominator matches the randomization unit. No DENOM error.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift drops steadily from 13.6% to 1.0% over 9 days. Despite this clear fading trend suggesting a novelty effect, the team projects the current reported lift (6.5%) for the next twelve months. This ignores the decay. **NOVELTY flagged**.

ERRORS: PEEK, POWER, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
