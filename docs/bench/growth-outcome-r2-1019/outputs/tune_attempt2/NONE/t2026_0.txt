**DENOM Check**: Randomization unit is "user id". The metric is "order conversion rate".
Calculated Control Rate from readout: $2,025 \text{ orders} / 67,514 \text{ users} \approx 3.00\%$.
However, the readout reports Control Conversion Rate as **2.394%**.
Let's check sessions as the denominator: $2,025 / 84,569 \approx 2.394\%$.
The reported metric uses **sessions** as the denominator, but users were randomized. If a user has multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption of the randomization unit. This is a denominator mismatch. **DENOM** flagged.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift starts at 23.7% and trends downward consistently to 4.0% by day 28.
The projection applies the current average (or final) lift to the next 12 months. Given the clear decay trend (23.7% $\to$ 4.0%), assuming the current lift persists without decay for a year ignores the novelty effect wearing off. The numbers show fading; the projection assumes stability. **NOVELTY** flagged.
