**SRM Check**: The planned split is 50/50. Total users = 30,420 + 28,954 = 59,374. Expected per arm = 29,687.
Difference = |30,420 - 29,687| = 733.
Standard Error (SE) for binomial split ≈ $\sqrt{n \cdot p \cdot (1-p)} = \sqrt{59374 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 0.5} \approx 122$.
Z-score = $733 / 122 \approx 6.0$. A Z-score > 1.96 indicates a significant mismatch. The assignment or logging is broken.

**DENOM Check**: Randomization unit is "user id". The metric is "order conversion rate".
Control Rate calculation: Reported 5.999%.
If denominator is users: $1,825 / 30,420 \approx 6.00\%$. Matches reported.
If denominator is sessions: $1,825 / 39,089 \approx 4.67\%$. Does not match.
The denominator matches the randomized unit (users). No DENOM error.

**SIMPSON Check**: Compare segment lifts to pooled lift (7.2%).
Desktop Control Rate: $1,107 / 10,701 \approx 10.34\%$.
Desktop Treatment Rate: $1,129 / 10,186 \approx 11.08\%$.
Desktop Lift: $(11.08 - 10.34) / 10.34 \approx 7.1\%$.
Mobile Control Rate: $718 / 19,719 \approx 3.64\%$.
Mobile Treatment Rate: $733 / 18,768 \approx 3.91\%$.
Mobile Lift: $(3.91 - 3.64) / 3.64 \approx 7.4\%$.
Both segments show positive lift (~7%) consistent with the pooled result (7.2%). No reversal occurs. No SIMPSON error.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift starts at 19.5% and decays steadily to 1.5% by day 14. The reported aggregate lift (7.2%) is an average of this decaying trend. The team projects this 7.2% lift for the next twelve months. This ignores the clear fading trend (novelty effect), assuming the initial high lift or the average will persist indefinitely despite the drop to near-zero by the end of the test. This is a NOVELTY error.

**PEEK Check**: The test ran from June 24 to July 8, which is exactly 14 days, matching the planned duration. No PEEK error.

**POWER Check**: The test detected a significant result (p < 0.05) with the observed sample size. While the treatment arm is smaller due to SRM, the test was not underpowered to the point of invalidating the detection of the observed effect, though the SRM is the primary validity threat here.

The critical errors are the Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM), which invalidates the causal inference due to broken randomization/logging, and the Novelty error (NOVELTY), where a fading effect is projected as permanent.

ERRORS: SRM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
