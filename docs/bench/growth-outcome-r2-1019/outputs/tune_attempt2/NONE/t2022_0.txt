**SRM Check**: Total users = 24,291 (control) + 24,103 (treatment) = 48,394. Expected per arm = 24,197. Difference = 94 users. Standard deviation for binomial split $\approx \sqrt{48394 \times 0.5 \times 0.5} \approx 110$. The difference (94) is less than 2 standard deviations ($< 220$), so the split is within chance expectations. No SRM.

**PEEK Check**: The test started 2026-07-30 and ended 2026-08-13. This is exactly 14 days, matching the planned duration. No PEEK.

**POWER Check**: Baseline 6.0%, target lift 8% (absolute difference 0.48%). With ~24k users per arm, the standard error for the difference in proportions is roughly $\sqrt{\frac{0.06(0.94)}{24000} + \frac{0.06(0.94)}{24000}} \approx 0.00217$. The detectable effect at 80% power ($Z_{\beta} \approx 0.84$) and $\alpha=0.05$ ($Z_{\alpha/2} \approx 1.96$) requires a difference of $(1.96+0.84) \times 0.00217 \approx 0.61\%$. The target (0.48%) is smaller than the detectable limit (0.61%), suggesting the test might be underpowered for the *target*, but the category definition requires the arm to be too small to detect the lift *at the stated alpha and power*. While tight, the primary issue here isn't a calculation error in the readout itself but a design constraint. However, looking closer at the definition: "smaller arm is too small to detect the target lift". Since 24k users cannot reliably detect a 0.48% lift with 80% power (requires ~38k users), this fits POWER.

**NOVELTY Check**: Daily lift values: 5.7, 4.6, 6.3, 5.2, 6.4, 5.3, 5.9, 5.1, 6.3, 5.9, 6.3, 5.4, 6.2, 5.2. The values fluctuate between 4.6% and 6.4% with no clear downward trend indicating fading. The projection assumes the reported lift lasts, but the data does not show fading. No NOVELTY.

The only clear error based on the numbers provided is POWER. The sample size (~24k) is insufficient to detect the specific target lift (8% relative on 6% base = 0.48% absolute) with 80% power. The minimum detectable effect for this sample size is approximately 1.0% relative lift, not 8%. Wait, re-calculation: $SE \approx 0.00217$. MDE $\approx 2.8 \times 0.00217 = 0.0061$ (0.61% absolute). Target is 0.48% absolute. Since $0.48 < 0.61$, the test is indeed underpowered for the stated target.
