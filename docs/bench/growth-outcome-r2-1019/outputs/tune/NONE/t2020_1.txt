The analysis contains two critical errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and metrics are reported in sessions. Since a single user can generate multiple sessions, the session counts (15,132 vs 15,969) do not reflect the randomized unit, invalidating the statistical test. Second, PEEK: the design specified a 14-day duration, but the daily lift data includes Day 15, and the readout date matches the end of Day 15, indicating the test ran longer or was analyzed after the planned stop, suggesting potential peeking or protocol deviation. The SRM check on sessions shows a mismatch, but this is expected due to the DENOM error, not necessarily assignment breakage. Power is sufficient given the large N. Simpson's paradox is not present as segment lifts align with the total.

ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
