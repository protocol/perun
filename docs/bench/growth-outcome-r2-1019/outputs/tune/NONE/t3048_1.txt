The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: with ~63k total users, a 50/50 split expects ~31,700 per arm. The observed counts (31,160 vs 32,244) differ by 1,084, which exceeds the threshold for chance variation (approx. 250), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, PEEK is evident: the test stopped on Nov 6, six days before the planned Nov 12 end date, likely due to early data inspection. Third, DENOM is violated: randomization was by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions; users can have multiple sessions, biasing the result. Finally, NOVELTY applies: the daily lift clearly fades from ~9% to ~2%, yet the team projects the current average as lasting twelve months.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
