The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized, but the metric "order conversion rate" uses sessions as the denominator (e.g., 11,297 orders / 313,939 sessions ≈ 3.6%), not users. Second, SIMPSON: the pooled data shows Treatment > Control (4.8% vs 3.6%), yet within both Desktop (8.3% vs 7.8%) and Mobile (2.9% vs 2.8%), the Treatment lift is much smaller than the aggregate suggests, indicating aggregation bias driven by session counts. Third, NOVELTY: daily lift drops steadily from 81.9% to 7.7%, yet the team projects the current high average for twelve months. Finally, PEEK: the test ran 22 days against a 21-day plan, likely stopping early due to significant results.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY, PEEK
