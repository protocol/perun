The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 34,886 vs 32,410 sessions. A chi-squared test confirms this deviation from 50/50 is statistically significant, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since users can have multiple sessions, this mismatches the unit of randomization with the unit of analysis, invalidating the p-value. Additionally, the readout includes data for 16 days despite a planned 14-day duration, suggesting PEEKing, though SRM and DENOM are sufficient to halt the decision. The segment data does not show Simpson's paradox (both devices show treatment > control), and daily lift is stable, ruling out NOVELTY.
