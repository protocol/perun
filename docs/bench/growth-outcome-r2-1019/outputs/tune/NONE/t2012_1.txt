The arm sizes (102,825 vs 108,689) deviate significantly from the expected 50/50 split given the total volume, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id." This mismatch (DENOM) violates the unit of analysis, as multiple sessions per user are not independent. While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a clear fading trend required to flag NOVELTY. The test ran for the planned duration (PEEK not flagged), and segment data does not explicitly show a reversal (SIMPSON not flagged). Due to SRM and DENOM, the results are unreliable.
