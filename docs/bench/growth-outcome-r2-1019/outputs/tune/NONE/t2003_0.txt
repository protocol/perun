The arm sizes (62,101 vs 57,556) show a significant deviation from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id." This mismatch violates the unit of analysis, flagging DENOM. Although the test ran for the planned duration and daily lift appears stable, these two structural errors invalidate the statistical results. The p-value and lift calculations are unreliable due to biased assignment/logging and incorrect denominators.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
