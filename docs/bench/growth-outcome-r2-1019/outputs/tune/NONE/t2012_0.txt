The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 102,825 vs 108,689 (48.7% vs 51.3%). With ~211k total users, the standard error for a 50/50 split is ~0.54%; the observed 1.3% deviation is roughly 2.4 standard errors away, indicating a significant mismatch unlikely due to chance. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the arm counts and metric (order conversion rate) are reported in sessions. Since users can have multiple sessions, analyzing sessions instead of users violates the independence assumption of the randomization unit. The daily lift fluctuates but does not show a clear fading trend required for NOVELTY, and segment math roughly aligns, ruling out SIMPSON.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
