The readout contains two clear errors. First, SRM: with ~300k total users, a 50/50 split should yield ~150k per arm. The observed counts (151,425 vs 147,057) differ by 4,368. The standard deviation for this sample size is ~274; the difference is ~16 standard deviations away from expected, indicating a broken assignment or logging issue.
