The test stopped on day 14 of a planned 21-day duration, indicating early stopping (PEEK). The arm sizes (52,103 vs 48,554) differ significantly from the 50/50 split; with ~100k users, a deviation this large exceeds chance expectations, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," but randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). While daily lift shows some fluctuation, the primary errors are structural. The p-value (0.235) is not significant, yet the team recommends rollout based on a non-significant result projected over a year.
