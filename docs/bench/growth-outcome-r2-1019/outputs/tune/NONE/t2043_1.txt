The test ran for 9 days (Jan 16–25) instead of the planned 14 days, indicating early stopping (PEEK). The arm sizes (22,891 vs 21,910) differ by ~4.3%; a chi-square check confirms this exceeds chance expectations for a 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The metric uses "sessions" while randomization was by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Daily lift drops from 13.6% to 1.0%, yet the team projects the current lift for 12 months, ignoring the fading trend (NOVELTY). Although underpowered due to SRM and early stop, the primary flags are the structural errors.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM, NOVELTY
