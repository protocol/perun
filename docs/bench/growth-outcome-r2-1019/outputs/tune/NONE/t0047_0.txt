The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the treatment arm has 237,254 users versus 233,136 in control. Given the large sample size, this ~1.8% deviation from the 50/50 split is statistically significant, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, PEEK applies because the test ran for only 9 days against a planned 21-day duration, inflating false positive risk. Third, DENOM is violated: users were randomized, but the metric "order conversion rate" appears calculated as orders/sessions (e.g., 11,656/334,910 ≈ 3.48%), making sessions the denominator instead of users. Finally, NOVELTY is suspected as the team projects a 12-month lift from a short, incomplete test without evidence of sustained effect.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
