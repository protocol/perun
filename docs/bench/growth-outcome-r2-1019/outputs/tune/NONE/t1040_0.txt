The user split is nearly 50/50 (37,564 vs 37,620), ruling out SRM. The test ran for the full planned 21 days, so PEEK is not indicated. Daily lift fluctuates but shows no clear fading trend, making NOVELTY unsupported. However, Simpson's paradox is present: while the overall treatment lift is positive, the desktop segment shows a lower conversion rate for treatment (4.51%) than control (4.79%), and the mobile segment also shows a lower rate for treatment (1.33%) than control (1.41%). The aggregate result reverses the direction of the segments due to imbalanced device distribution across arms. Additionally, the metric uses sessions in the denominator (implied by rates not matching user counts), while randomization was by user ID, violating DENOM.
