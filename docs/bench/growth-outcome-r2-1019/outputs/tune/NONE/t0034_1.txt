The user split is nearly 50/50 (56,853 vs 56,747), ruling out SRM. The test ran for 23 days against a 21-day plan, so PEEK is unsupported. However, the device breakdown reveals severe imbalance: desktop is 43% control vs 57% treatment, while mobile is 60% control vs 40% treatment. This indicates broken randomization within segments, causing Simpson's Paradox: desktop lift is positive (~2.5%), and mobile lift is negative (~-15%), yet the pooled result shows a positive lift. Additionally, the metric divides orders by users, whereas conversion rates typically use sessions as the denominator; since sessions per user differ significantly between arms, the denominator does not match the randomization unit's exposure (DENOM).
