The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 51.9% control vs 48.1% treatment, a deviation likely exceeding chance limits for this sample size. Second, PEEK applies because the test ended on March 27, well before the planned April 3 end date. Third, DENOM is violated: randomization was by user ID, but the primary arm counts and baseline metric use "sessions" as the denominator, not users. Finally, NOVELTY is flagged because the daily lift shows no fading trend (fluctuating between 4.2% and 5.7%), yet the team projects this short-term lift over twelve months without evidence of sustainability. Due to these fundamental flaws in data integrity and projection logic, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
