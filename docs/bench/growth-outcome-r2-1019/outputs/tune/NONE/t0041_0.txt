The test ran for 9 days against a planned 21-day duration, indicating early stopping (PEEK). The reported metric divides orders by users (e.g., 4,483/56,045 ≈ 8.0%), but the readout claims 5.76%; this implies the denominator used was sessions (4,483/77,831 ≈ 5.76%), not the randomized user unit (DENOM). The arm sizes (56,045 vs 58,091) differ significantly from the 50/50 split expected for ~114k users, suggesting Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The daily lift shows no fading trend, ruling out NOVELTY. Segments do not show reversed effects compared to the total, ruling out SIMPSON. Given the multiple critical errors in analysis and execution, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
