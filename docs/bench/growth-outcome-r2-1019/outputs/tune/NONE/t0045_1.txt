The user split is 97,253 vs 99,997, a ~1.4% difference. With ~197k total users, the standard deviation for a 50/50 split is ~222 users; the observed difference (~2,744) is over 12 standard deviations away, indicating a severe Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the device breakdown reveals Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON): while the pooled result shows a lift, the treatment group has significantly more desktop users (who convert higher) and fewer mobile users than control. Calculating segment rates shows the treatment actually underperforms in both desktop (4.2% vs 4.5%) and mobile (1.7% vs 1.8%) individually. The aggregate lift is an artifact of the skewed traffic mix.
