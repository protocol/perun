The treatment arm has 28,630 sessions versus 25,987 in control, a ratio of roughly 52.4% to 47.6%. With this total sample size (~54k), a deviation of this magnitude is statistically significant and indicates a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM), suggesting broken randomization or logging. Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while users were randomized by "user id." If users generated multiple sessions, this violates the unit of randomization, constituting a Denominator (DENOM) error. The test ran for 28 days as planned, so PEEK is not evident. While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a clear fading trend required for NOVELTY. Segment data does not show reversed trends for SIMPSON. Due to SRM and DENOM, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
