The arm sizes (51,056 vs 47,394) show a significant deviation from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The test ran for only 9 days against a planned 14-day duration, constituting Peeking (PEEK). The metric denominator is "sessions" while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Additionally, the reported p-value of 0.0 is statistically impossible for finite samples and suggests calculation errors. The projection assumes the lift persists without evidence of fading, but the daily data shows fluctuation rather than a clear decay trend, so NOVELTY is not definitively flagged by numbers alone. However, SRM, PEEK, and DENOM are clear violations.
