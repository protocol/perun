The randomization unit is user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions (e.g., control has 88,619 sessions vs. 22,719+36,873=59,592 users). Since users can generate multiple sessions, using sessions as the denominator violates the independence assumption of the randomized unit, constituting a DENOM error. The arm sizes (88,619 vs 90,682) are close enough to 50/50 that SRM is not clearly indicated without a statistical test, and the test ran for the full planned duration, ruling out PEEK. The daily lift fluctuates but does not show a clear fading trend required for NOVELTY. Segments do not show reversal (both desktop and mobile show higher treatment orders relative to their sizes), so no SIMPSON. Power appears adequate given the significant p-value.

ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
