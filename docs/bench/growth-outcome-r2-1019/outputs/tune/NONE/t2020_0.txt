The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present: with ~31k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~15.5k per arm. The observed counts (15,132 vs 15,969) differ by 837 users. A chi-squared check confirms this deviation is statistically significant (p < 0.001), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM (Denominator Mismatch) is evident: users were randomized by user ID, but the primary arm sizes and conversion rates are calculated using "sessions" rather than unique users. This violates the unit of randomization. Additionally, POWER is an issue; the reported p-value (0.314) and small lift (4.6% vs 10% target) confirm the test failed to detect the target effect, though SRM and DENOM are the structural flaws invalidating the result.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
