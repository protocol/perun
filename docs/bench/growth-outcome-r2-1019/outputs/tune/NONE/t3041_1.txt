The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 64,060 vs 70,829 sessions. A 50/50 split of 134,889 total sessions expects ~67,445 per arm; the deviation (~3,385) is far too large for chance (p < 0.001), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metric (conversion rate) and arm counts are reported in sessions. Since users can have multiple sessions, the analysis denominator does not match the randomization unit, biasing the results. Additionally, the daily lift shows a clear downward trend (3.6% to 1.0%), suggesting a NOVELTY effect that makes the 12-month projection invalid.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
