The analysis contains two errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and metric calculations use sessions. Since users can generate multiple sessions, the session counts (46,874 vs 48,162) do not reflect the randomized unit, invalidating the p-value. Second, PEEK: the planned duration was 14 days, but the readout includes data through day 15 and was finalized on May 22 (15 days after start), indicating the test ran longer or was checked after the planned window, potentially inflating false positives if stopped based on interim looks. The daily lift does not show fading (NOVELTY is not flagged), and segment math does not clearly show reversal (SIMPSON not flagged).

ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
