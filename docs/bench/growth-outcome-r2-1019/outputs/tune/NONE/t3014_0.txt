The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 49.4% control vs. 50.6% treatment (246,660 vs. 252,527). With ~500k total users, a 1.2% deviation is statistically impossible by chance, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms and desktop/mobile segments are reported in "sessions" and "users" inconsistently, and conversion is calculated on sessions rather than the randomized user unit, risking inflated significance. Additionally, NOVELTY is evident as daily lift decays from ~10% to ~3%, yet the team projects the current average lift for twelve months. Because assignment integrity (SRM) and unit mismatch (DENOM) invalidate the causal claim, the results cannot be trusted.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
