The session counts (32,022 vs 34,787) deviate significantly from the expected 50/50 split given the randomized user IDs, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). This suggests broken assignment or logging, invalidating the results. Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id." Since users can generate multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption and constitutes a Denominator (DENOM) error. While the test ran for the planned 14 days and daily lift appears stable, the fundamental data integrity issues prevent a valid rollout decision.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
