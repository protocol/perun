The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~470k total users, a 50/50 split expects ~235k per arm. The observed difference (237,254 vs 233,136) is 4,118 users, far exceeding the chance threshold (~1,000 for this sample size), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized, but the reported metric (3.48% vs 4.745%) uses sessions as the denominator (orders/sessions), not users. Third, PEEK applies: the test ran for 9 days against a planned 21-day duration. Finally, POWER is an issue: the calculated baseline conversion (orders/users) is ~5%, but the reported rates are much lower, suggesting the power calculation based on the wrong denominator or metric is invalid.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
