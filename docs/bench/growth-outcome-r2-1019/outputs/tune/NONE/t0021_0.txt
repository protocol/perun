The user split is nearly 50/50 (234,533 vs 234,169), ruling out SRM. Segment results (desktop and mobile) both show positive lifts, so no Simpson's paradox. The denominator (users) matches the randomization unit. Daily lift fluctuates but shows no clear fading trend to indicate novelty decay.

However, the test ran from May 12 to May 18 (7 days), half the planned 14-day duration. Stopping early based on significant p-values inflates false positive risk. Additionally, with ~234k users per arm, a 5% relative lift on a 5% baseline requires roughly 300k+ users per arm for 80% power; the current sample is underpowered for the stated design parameters, making the significant result suspicious given the early stop.
