The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~179k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~89.5k per arm. The observed counts (92,653 vs 86,562) differ by over 6,000, which is statistically impossible by chance alone, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is flagged: users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and metrics are reported in sessions. Since users can generate multiple sessions, analyzing session-level data violates the unit of randomization, invalidating the p-value.

Because the assignment mechanism appears broken and the analysis unit mismatches the randomization unit, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
