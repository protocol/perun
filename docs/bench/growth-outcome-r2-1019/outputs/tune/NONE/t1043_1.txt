The user split (18,148 vs 18,012) is statistically consistent with a 50/50 randomization, so no SRM. The test ran for the planned 14 days (July 11–26), so no PEEK. Power calculations based on ~18k users per arm are sufficient for the target lift, so no POWER issue.

However, DENOM is flagged: users were randomized, but the metric "order conversion rate" appears calculated as orders/sessions (e.g., Control: 726/23,461 ≈ 3.09%), not orders/users. This mismatches the randomization unit.

NOVELTY is also flagged: daily lift drops sharply from 64.9% to 9.3%, yet the team projects the current high lift over twelve months, ignoring the fading trend.

SIMPSON is not present; both device segments show higher treatment conversion.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
