The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is ~52% control vs ~48% treatment, a deviation too large for chance given the sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized, but the primary metric uses sessions as the denominator. Third, PEEK applies: the test ended on Oct 29, well before the planned Nov 02 end date. Fourth, POWER is an issue: calculating based on the smaller arm (treatment sessions) and baseline, the sample is insufficient to detect the target 5% lift with 80% power. Finally, NOVELTY is flagged because daily lift fluctuates without a clear fading trend, yet the team projects this volatile short-term lift over twelve months without adjustment.
