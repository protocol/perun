The analysis contains two critical errors. First, DENOM: The experiment randomized users, but the arm sizes and conversion rates are calculated using "sessions" as the denominator. A single user can generate multiple sessions, violating the independence assumption required for standard proportion tests. Second, PEEK: The test ran for 14 days (Aug 26 to Sept 9) against a planned duration of 21 days. Stopping early based on observed significance inflates the false positive rate, making the reported p-value invalid. The daily lift data shows stability rather than fading, so NOVELTY is not flagged. Sample sizes appear sufficient for power, and arm sizes are balanced enough to rule out SRM. Segment data does not show reversal, ruling out SIMPSON.

ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
