The user split is nearly 50/50 (109,632 vs 109,376), so no SRM. The test ran 23 days (Jan 22 to Feb 13 inclusive), exceeding the 21-day plan, so no PEEK. Power appears sufficient given the significant p-value. However, Simpson's Paradox is present: Desktop shows a lift (~19.1% vs ~11.2% relative), but Mobile shows a massive negative lift (Control: 4.3%, Treatment: 4.1%). The aggregated positive result masks the severe degradation on mobile. Additionally, the metric denominator (sessions) differs from the randomization unit (users), violating DENOM, as sessions per user differ significantly between arms (1.40 vs 1.33).
