The arm sizes (294,708 vs 299,412) differ by ~1.6%. A chi-squared test confirms this deviation is statistically significant given the large sample size, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the daily lift shows a clear downward trend from 11.6% to ~1.4%, yet the team projects the current aggregate lift over twelve months, ignoring the fading effect (NOVELTY). The metric denominator (orders/users) matches the randomization unit, and the test ran for the planned 28 days. While power appears sufficient, the SRM invalidates the randomization assumption, and the novelty decay makes the long-term projection unreliable.

DECISION: HOLD
