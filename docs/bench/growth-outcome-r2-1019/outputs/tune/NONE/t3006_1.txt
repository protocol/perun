The arm sizes (163,900 vs 152,204) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although the test ran past the planned end date, the primary fatal flaws are the assignment imbalance and the denominator mismatch. The daily lift appears stable, ruling out NOVELTY, and segment data does not show reversed trends, ruling out SIMPSON. Due to SRM and DENOM, the results are unreliable.
