The arm sizes (232,556 vs 233,244) are nearly equal, ruling out SRM. However, the test ran for only 15 days against a planned 28-day duration, indicating PEEK. The daily lift data shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend, so NOVELTY is not flagged. Crucially, the pooled results show a positive lift (treatment > control), yet within both desktop and mobile segments, the treatment conversion rates are lower than control (Desktop: 5.8% vs 6.2%; Mobile: 2.1% vs 2.2%). This reversal confirms SIMPSON. Since the metric denominator (users) matches the randomization unit, DENOM is clear. The sample size appears sufficient for the observed effect, so POWER is not flagged.
