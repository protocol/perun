The arm sizes (221,023 vs 212,331) show a ~4% imbalance. With ~433k total, a 50/50 split should have ~216.5k each. The deviation (~4.5k) is far beyond chance (std dev ~330), confirming SRM.

The daily lift fluctuates (5.9% to 7.8%) but shows no clear fading trend; the final days are comparable to early days, so NOVELTY is not supported by the numbers.

The test ran for the planned 14 days, so no PEEK. Segment results (desktop/mobile) both show positive lifts, so no SIMPSON. Power calculation isn't directly verifiable without variance, but the large sample suggests adequate power.
