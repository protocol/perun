The readout contains two clear errors. First, SRM is present: with ~362k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~181k per arm. The observed counts (186,038 vs 176,223) differ by nearly 10k, a deviation far exceeding chance limits for this sample size, indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: randomization was by user ID, but the arm counts and metrics are reported in sessions. Since users generate multiple sessions, analyzing sessions instead of users inflates the sample size artificially and biases variance estimates. Additionally, the daily lift shows a downward trend (3.6% to 0.8%), suggesting NOVELTY effects, yet the team projects this fading lift over twelve months. Due to the fundamental data integrity issues (SRM, DENOM), the results are invalid.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
