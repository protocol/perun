The arm sizes (71,193 vs 75,857) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although the test ran past the planned end date, the primary fatal flaws are the assignment imbalance and the denominator mismatch. These errors invalidate the reported p-value and lift estimate, making the rollout recommendation unsafe without re-analysis using correct units and investigating the traffic imbalance.
