The analysis contains two critical errors. First, DENOM: Users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and metrics are reported in sessions. Since users can generate multiple sessions, the session counts (48,029 vs 46,972) do not reflect the randomized unit, invalidating the statistical test. Second, PEEK: The planned duration was 14 days, but the readout includes data through day 15 and was finalized on day 15, indicating the test ran longer than planned or was checked/extended post-hoc, which risks inflating false positives if stopping rules weren't pre-defined for this extension. While the p-value (0.1417) is not significant, the denominator mismatch makes the entire result unreliable.

ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
