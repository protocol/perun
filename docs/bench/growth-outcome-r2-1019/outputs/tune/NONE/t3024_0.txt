The readout shows a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~179k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~89.5k per arm. The observed counts (92,653 vs 86,562) differ by over 6,000, a deviation far exceeding chance limits for this sample size, indicating broken assignment or logging. Additionally, the analysis suffers from a Denominator error (DENOM): users were randomized by user ID, but the metric uses sessions in the denominator. This mismatch invalidates the statistical inference. While the p-value (0.0537) is slightly above alpha, the structural data integrity issues (SRM) and unit mismatch (DENOM) make the results unreliable. The daily lift appears stable, ruling out NOVELTY, and segment data does not show reversal, ruling out SIMPSON.

DECISION: HOLD
