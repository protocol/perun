The user split is 47,996 vs 47,844, which is consistent with a 50/50 randomization (no SRM). However, the metric denominator is sessions (63,977/55,175), while randomization was by user ID. Since sessions per user differ significantly between arms, the denominator does not match the randomized unit (DENOM).

Additionally, the overall treatment lift is +20.7%, but segment analysis shows desktop lift is ~15% while mobile lift is negative (~-23%: 2.69% vs 2.70%). The aggregate positive result reverses within segments due to the imbalance in session distribution across devices (SIMPSON).

The daily lift shows a clear downward trend from 43% to 6%, yet the team projects the current high lift for twelve months (NOVELTY).
