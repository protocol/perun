The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 48.6% control vs. 51.4% treatment (87,890 vs. 93,103). With ~181k total users, a 2.8% deviation is statistically impossible by chance, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms and desktop/mobile segments are reported in "sessions" and "users" inconsistently (e.g., desktop sums to ~45k users while total sessions are ~88k), and the primary metric uses sessions as the denominator instead of the randomized user unit. Additionally, PEEK applies because the readout covers 23 days (Jan 13 to Feb 5) against a planned 21-day duration, suggesting early stopping or extension based on results.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
