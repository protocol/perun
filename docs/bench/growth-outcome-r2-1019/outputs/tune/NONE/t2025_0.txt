The test ran for only 18 days (June 13 to July 1) against a planned 28-day duration, indicating early stopping (PEEK). The arm sizes are 99,791 (control) vs 93,273 (treatment). A 50/50 split of 193,064 total users expects ~96,532 per arm. The deviation is ~3,259 users. The standard deviation for a binomial split is $\sqrt{193064 \times 0.5 \times 0.5} \approx 220$. The observed difference is ~15 standard deviations away, which is impossible by chance alone, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," but randomization was by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM).
