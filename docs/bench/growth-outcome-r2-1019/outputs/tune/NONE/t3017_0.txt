The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: with ~51,600 total users, a 50/50 split expects ~25,800 per arm. The observed counts (26,753 vs 24,852) differ by 1,901, far exceeding chance expectations for this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, PEEK is evident: the test stopped on March 27, well before the planned April 3 end date, inflating false positive risk. Third, DENOM applies: users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metric uses "sessions" as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization. Finally, the recommendation projects a 4.9% lift despite the p-value (0.1429) being non-significant, and the daily data shows no fading trend to justify NOVELTY, but the projection itself is flawed due to the lack of statistical significance.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
