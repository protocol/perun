The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized, but the reported conversion rates (4.3% vs 5.6%) match orders divided by sessions, not users. Second, SIMPSON: calculating lift by device (the randomization unit) yields ~4.3% for both desktop and mobile, yet the pooled session-based metric shows a massive 29.5% lift, indicating the aggregate result is reversed or distorted by the wrong denominator. Third, PEEK: the test ran for 16 days despite a planned 14-day duration, and the "p=0.0" suggests premature stopping or infinite significance. Finally, NOVELTY: daily lift fluctuates wildly without a clear fading trend, yet the team projects this volatile, short-term spike as a stable twelve-month gain. These issues invalidate the reported lift.
