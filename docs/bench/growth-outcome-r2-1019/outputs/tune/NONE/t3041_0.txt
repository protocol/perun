The arm sizes (64,060 vs 70,829) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). The daily lift shows a clear downward trend (3.6% to 1.0%), yet the team projects this fading lift over twelve months, demonstrating a Novelty effect error (NOVELTY). While the test ran for the planned duration and the pooled result isn't reversed in segments, these three identified errors invalidate the current readout.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
