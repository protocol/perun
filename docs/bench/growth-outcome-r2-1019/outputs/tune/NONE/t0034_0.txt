The user split is nearly 50/50 (56,853 vs 56,747), so SRM is not present. The test ran for 23 days against a 21-day plan, so PEEK is not indicated by early stopping. However, the segment data reveals a severe imbalance: Desktop has ~9k more treatment users, while Mobile has ~9k more control users. Crucially, the treatment lifts conversion on Desktop but drastically reduces it on Mobile. This reversal between segment-level and pooled results indicates Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON), likely driven by the skewed device distribution within arms. Additionally, the daily lift shows a clear downward trend from 14.3% to near 0%, yet the team projects this transient lift for twelve months, constituting a NOVELTY error.

ERRORS: SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
