The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 48.4% control vs. 51.6% treatment (65,497 vs. 69,986). With ~135k total users, a 3.2% deviation is far beyond chance limits, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows a positive lift (6.12% vs. 6.00%), both segments show negative lifts (Desktop: 9.17% vs. 9.48%; Mobile: 3.57% vs. 3.68%). The aggregate result is reversed relative to the strata. Additionally, DENOM is flagged because the metric uses "sessions" while randomization was by "user id," violating the unit of analysis. These issues invalidate the results.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
