The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~232k total users, a 50/50 split should yield ~116k per arm. The observed counts (121,506 vs 111,204) differ by over 10k, which is statistically impossible by chance alone, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, PEEK applies because the test stopped on Oct 29, well before the planned Nov 2 end date. Third, DENOM is violated: users were randomized, but the primary arm sizes and conversion rates are reported in "sessions," not users. Finally, the p-value of "0.0" is impossible; it should be reported as <0.001 or similar. Due to the SRM, the results are untrustworthy regardless of the lift magnitude.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
