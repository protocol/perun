The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 51.4% control vs 48.6% treatment, a deviation too large for chance given the sample size (~360k), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies: randomization was by user ID, but arm sizes and desktop/mobile breakdowns are reported in sessions and mixed user counts, mismatching the unit of analysis. Third, SIMPSON is evident: while the pooled data shows treatment > control (5.1% vs 5.0%), calculating rates by device reveals control wins on both desktop (7.58% vs 7.36%) and mobile (2.59% vs 2.51%). Finally, NOVELTY is flagged as daily lift fades from 3.6% to 0.8%, yet the team projects this transient lift over twelve months.
