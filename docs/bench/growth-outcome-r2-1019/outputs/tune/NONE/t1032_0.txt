The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 50.4% control vs 49.6% treatment. With ~372k users, the standard deviation is ~305; the observed difference of ~3,000 users is nearly 10 standard deviations away, indicating broken randomization. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized, but the reported conversion rates (e.g., 9,385/242,116 ≈ 3.88%) use sessions as the denominator, not users. Third, PEEK applies because the test ran for 16 days against a planned 14-day duration. Finally, the reported lift (19.1%) vastly exceeds the target (5%), and daily data shows no fading, so NOVELTY and POWER are not flagged based on these numbers alone. However, the structural errors invalidate the result.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
