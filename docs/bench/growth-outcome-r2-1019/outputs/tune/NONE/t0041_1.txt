The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~114k total users, a 50/50 split should yield ~57k per arm. The observed counts (56,045 vs 58,091) differ by over 2,000, exceeding chance expectations for this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, PEEK is evident: the test ran for only 9 days (Jan 7–16) against a planned 21-day duration, inflating false positive risk. Third, DENOM applies: the metric is "order conversion rate," but the reported rates (e.g., 4,483/56,045 ≈ 8.0%) contradict the stated 5.76%; the calculation likely used sessions (4,483/77,831 ≈ 5.76%) as the denominator instead of randomized users. Finally, NOVELTY is flagged: daily lift fluctuates without a clear fading trend, yet the projection assumes sustained impact over 12 months without justification.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM, NOVELTY
