The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 233,688 vs 217,492 sessions. A 50/50 split of 451,180 total sessions expects ~225,590 per arm. The deviation (~8,100) is far beyond chance (p << 0.001), indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metric uses sessions as the denominator. This mismatch invalidates the p-value and lift calculation because sessions are not independent randomization units. While device-level data exists, the primary readout relies on the flawed session metric. The daily lift appears stable, ruling out NOVELTY, and the test ran for the planned duration, ruling out PEEK.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
