The arm sizes (38,058 vs 37,937) are nearly identical, consistent with a 50/50 split, so no SRM. The test ran past its planned end date (Feb 17 vs Feb 15), which does not indicate PEEKing. The sample sizes are large enough to detect the target lift, ruling out POWER issues.

However, Simpson's paradox is present. The overall treatment conversion (4.08%) appears higher than control (3.999%). Yet, looking at segments:
Desktop: Control 6.3% (716/11,368) vs Treatment 6.1% (777/12,714).
Mobile: Control 2.35% (372/15,836) vs Treatment 2.28% (326/14,322).
The treatment performs worse in both device segments individually, but better in the aggregate due to imbalanced segment sizes (more high-converting desktop users in treatment). This invalidates the pooled result. Additionally, DENOM is flagged because randomization was by user ID, but metrics are reported per session.

ERRORS: SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
