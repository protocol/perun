Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id." This mismatch (DENOM) violates the unit of analysis, as multiple sessions per user are not independent. While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a clear fading trend required for NOVELTY. Segment data does not show reversed effects (no SIMPSON). The test ran for the planned duration (no PEEK), and sample sizes appear sufficient for the target lift (no POWER). Due to SRM and DENOM, the results are unreliable.
