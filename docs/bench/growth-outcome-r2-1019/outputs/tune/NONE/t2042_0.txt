SRM: The split is 49.2% control vs 50.8% treatment. With ~330k total sessions, a 0.8% deviation exceeds chance limits (p < 0.001), indicating broken randomization or logging.
PEEK: The test ran for 9 days against a planned 14-day duration, constituting early stopping which inflates false positive risk.
SIMPSON: Desktop shows a positive lift (~8.1% vs ~9.1%), while Mobile shows a negative lift (~2.9% vs ~2.7%). The segments move in opposite directions, yet the pooled result averages them, masking the mobile degradation.
DENOM: Randomization was by user ID, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
