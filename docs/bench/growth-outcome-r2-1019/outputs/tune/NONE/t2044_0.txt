The data shows a severe Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With 50/50 randomization by user ID, the expected split is equal. However, the readout reports 42,478 sessions for control versus 37,662 for treatment, a difference far exceeding chance. Additionally, the "By device" breakdown lists users (12k+20k vs 14k+18k), while the top-level metrics use sessions, indicating the denominator (sessions) differs from the randomization unit (users), flagging DENOM. Finally, the daily lift drops from ~30% to ~9% by the end, yet the team projects this fading lift over twelve months, which is a NOVELTY error. These issues invalidate the reported significance.
