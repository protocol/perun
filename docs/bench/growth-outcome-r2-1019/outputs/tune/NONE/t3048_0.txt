The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: with ~63k total users, a 50/50 split expects ~31,700 per arm. The observed counts (31,160 vs 32,244) differ by 1,084, which exceeds the threshold for chance variation (approx. 250), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is flagged because the metric denominator is "sessions," but users were randomized by "user id"; multiple sessions per user violate independence assumptions. Third, PEEK applies because the test stopped on Nov 6, before the planned Nov 12 end date. Finally, NOVELTY is evident as daily lift decays from ~9% to ~2%, yet the team projects this transient lift over twelve months. Due to broken assignment validity and incorrect denominators, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
