The data reveals multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 335,229 vs 300,874 sessions, a deviation far exceeding chance for this sample size, indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, DENOM applies because randomization was by user ID, but the primary arm counts and conversion rates use sessions as the denominator. Third, SIMPSON occurs: desktop lift is ~5.3% and mobile lift is ~5.3%, yet the pooled report claims 17.4%, which is mathematically impossible unless the denominators are mismatched or weighted incorrectly relative to the segments. Finally, NOVELTY is evident as daily lift decays from ~33% to ~5%, yet the projection assumes the initial high lift persists for a year.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
