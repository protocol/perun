The analysis contains two clear errors. First, SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present: with ~71k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~35.8k per arm. The observed counts (36,640 vs 35,029) differ by ~1,600, which exceeds the expected statistical variance (approx. ±212 for 99% confidence), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON (Simpson's Paradox) is evident: while the pooled data shows a positive lift (4.078% vs 4.001%), both segments show negative lift when calculated correctly (Desktop: 6.56% vs 6.75%; Mobile: 2.06% vs 2.00%). The aggregate result is reversed relative to the segments.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
