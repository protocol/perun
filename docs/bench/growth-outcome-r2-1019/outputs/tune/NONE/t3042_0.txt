The analysis contains multiple errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms report "sessions," meaning the denominator does not match the randomization unit. Second, PEEK: the test ended on June 9th, six days before the planned June 15th end date. Third, SIMPSON: while the pooled treatment lift appears positive (2.1%), the desktop segment shows a negative lift (~9.6% vs ~8.6%), and mobile also shows a negative lift (~3.3% vs ~3.2%), reversing the direction in segments. Finally, NOVELTY: daily lift drops from ~2.7% mid-test to 1.5% at the end, yet the team projects this fading lift for twelve months.

ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
