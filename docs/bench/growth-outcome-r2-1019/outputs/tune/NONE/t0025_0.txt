The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized, but the reported conversion rates (4.326% vs 5.603%) match orders divided by sessions, not users. Second, SIMPSON: calculating lift by device (the randomized unit) yields ~4.3% for both desktop and mobile, yet the pooled result claims a massive 29.5% lift due to the session-count disparity. Third, PEEK: the test ran for 16 days (April 14–30), exceeding the planned 14-day duration, suggesting unplanned extension or peeking. Finally, NOVELTY: the daily lift fluctuates significantly without a clear fading trend, yet the team projects this volatile short-term lift over twelve months. These issues invalidate the reported result.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON, PEEK, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
