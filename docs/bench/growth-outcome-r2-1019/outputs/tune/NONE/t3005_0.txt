The arm sizes (40,390 vs 39,791) are within expected random variation for this sample size, so no SRM. The test ran the full planned duration, ruling out PEEK. The p-value (0.1032) exceeds the 0.05 threshold, meaning the result is not statistically significant; however, this is a lack of significance, not necessarily an underpowered design error based solely on the provided counts without calculating the exact MDE post-hoc, though the failure to reach significance suggests low power or no effect. Crucially, the metric denominator is "sessions" while randomization was by "user id." A single user can generate multiple sessions, violating the independence assumption and constituting a DENOM error. There is no clear fading trend in daily lift to suggest NOVELTY, and segment data aligns with the aggregate, ruling out SIMPSON.

DECISION: HOLD
