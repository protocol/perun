The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~61k total users, a 50/50 split should yield ~30.7k per arm. The observed counts (32,127 vs 29,279) differ by nearly 3k, far exceeding chance limits, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms report "sessions" and segments report "users," mixing denominators. Third, SIMPSON is evident: pooled data shows treatment winning (6.2% vs 5.3%), yet calculating per-segment rates reveals Desktop control (12.0%) beats treatment (12.6%? Wait, 1286/10714=12.0%, 1362/10804=12.6% - Treatment wins desktop. Mobile: 432/10760=4.0%, 458/10852=4.2% - Treatment wins mobile). Actually, Simpson's is NOT shown here as direction is consistent. However, SRM and DENOM are definite. The session/user mismatch invalidates the metric calculation.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
