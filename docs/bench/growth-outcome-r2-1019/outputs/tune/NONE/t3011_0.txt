The readout shows a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With 145,550 total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~72,775 per arm. The control has 75,220 and treatment 70,330. The difference (4,890) is far too large for chance (Z-score > 10), indicating broken assignment or logging. Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," but randomization was by "user id." This violates the unit of analysis, constituting a DENOM error, as multiple sessions per user inflate sample size artificially and bias variance estimates. The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to flag NOVELTY strictly from numbers. Segments do not show reversed signs to flag SIMPSON.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
