The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~44k total users, a 50/50 split should yield ~22k per arm. The observed difference (22,490 vs 21,492) is ~1,000 users, far exceeding chance expectations for this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized, but the reported conversion rate (orders/users) appears correct, however, the raw counts show sessions differ vastly (30k vs 25k), suggesting potential data integrity issues often linked to SRM. Most critically, the reported p-value of "0.0" is statistically impossible; it indicates a calculation error or misreporting. Finally, the daily lift shows no fading trend, so NOVELTY is not evidenced, but the SRM invalidates the entire test basis.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
