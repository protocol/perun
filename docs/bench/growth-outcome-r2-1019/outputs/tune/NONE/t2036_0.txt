The treatment arm has 28,630 sessions versus 25,987 in control, a deviation of ~10% from the 50/50 split. With these sample sizes, such an imbalance is statistically impossible by chance alone, indicating broken randomization or logging (SRM). Because the assignment mechanism is compromised, the resulting p-value and lift estimates are invalid. Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions" while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to confirm NOVELTY, and segment math cannot be verified without exact denominators per segment for SIMPSON. However, SRM and DENOM alone invalidate the results.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
