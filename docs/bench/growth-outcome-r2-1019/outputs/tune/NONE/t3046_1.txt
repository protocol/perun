The arm sizes (109,172 vs 104,833) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). The daily lift shows a clear downward trend (11.0% to 3.7%), yet the team projects this initial lift over twelve months without adjustment, demonstrating a Novelty effect error (NOVELTY). While Simpson's paradox appears in device splits, the primary fatal flaws are SRM, DENOM, and NOVELTY. These errors invalidate the statistical significance and the long-term projection.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
