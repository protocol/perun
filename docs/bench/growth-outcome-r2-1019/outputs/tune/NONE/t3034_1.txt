The readout shows clear errors. First, SRM: the split is 59,215 vs 55,064 sessions. A difference of ~4,000 users with a p-value likely far below 0.05 indicates a broken randomization or logging issue. Second, PEEK: the test ended on 08-19, eight days before the planned 08-27 end date, inflating false positive risk. Third, SIMPSON: the pooled data shows treatment > control (6.12% vs 6.0%), but segmenting by device reveals the opposite. Desktop: 9.1% (control) vs 8.8% (treatment). Mobile: 3.3% (control) vs 3.2% (treatment). The aggregate lift is an artifact of unequal device distribution between arms. Finally, DENOM: randomization was by user ID, but the primary arm counts and desktop/mobile breakdowns mix "sessions" and "users," obscuring the true denominator.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
