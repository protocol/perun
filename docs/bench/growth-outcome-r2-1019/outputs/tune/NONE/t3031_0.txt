The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~58k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~29k per arm. The observed counts (28,687 vs 29,796) differ by 1,109. The standard deviation is ~120; this gap is >9 standard deviations, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the data reports "sessions" and calculates conversion per session. Since users can have multiple sessions, the denominator does not match the randomization unit, invalidating the p-value. Additionally, device splits show imbalances consistent with the global SRM. Because assignment integrity and unit-of-analysis are compromised, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
