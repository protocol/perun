The arm sizes (202,544 vs 193,266) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although daily lift shows some fluctuation, it does not clearly fade to zero, so NOVELTY is not definitively flagged by numbers alone. The test ran past the planned end date, so PEEK is not evident. Segment data does not show a reversal of effects, ruling out SIMPSON. Given the clear SRM and DENOM errors, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
