The arm sizes are 30,112 (control) and 32,164 (treatment), a ratio of roughly 48.4% vs 51.6%. With ~62k total users, a 1.6% deviation from 50/50 is statistically significant, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). This suggests broken randomization or logging, invalidating the results.

Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," but users were randomized by "user id." If a user generates multiple sessions, the independence assumption is violated, constituting a Denominator error (DENOM).

The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to flag NOVELTY strictly from numbers. PEEK, POWER, and SIMPSON are not evident; the test ran the full duration, sample sizes appear sufficient for the baseline, and segment directionality matches the pooled result.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
