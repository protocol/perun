The analysis contains multiple errors. First, PEEK is evident because the test ended on 2026-08-12, eight days before the planned 2026-08-20 end date. Second, POWER is an issue: with a baseline of 6% and target lift of 8%, the required sample size per arm is approximately 53,000 users. The actual arms (~41,000 each) are too small to achieve 80% power, making the non-significant result (p=0.0604) inconclusive. Third, NOVELTY is flagged because the daily lift clearly decays from 11.6% to ~3%, yet the team projects this transient lift over twelve months. Finally, DENOM is present because randomization was by user ID, but the primary metric and arm sizes are reported in sessions, not users.

ERRORS: PEEK, POWER, NOVELTY, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
