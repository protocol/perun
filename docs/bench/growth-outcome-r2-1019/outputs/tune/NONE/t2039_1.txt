The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: with ~73k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~36.5k per arm. The observed counts (38,194 vs 35,162) differ by over 3,000, which is statistically impossible by chance alone, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies because users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metric uses sessions as the denominator; multiple sessions per user violate independence assumptions. Third, NOVELTY is evident as the daily lift drops steadily from 27% to ~6%, yet the team projects the current aggregate lift for twelve months, ignoring the fading effect. Finally, PEEK is likely since the readout date (Oct 13) is only 22 days in, and the stopping rule isn't justified against the plan.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
