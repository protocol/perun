The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: treatment has 118,440 sessions vs control's 113,987. This ~2% deviation from 50/50 is statistically significant given the sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: randomization was by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions; users with multiple sessions inflate variance and bias results. Third, PEEK applies: the test stopped on March 27, four days before the planned March 31 end date, inflating false positive risk. While the result is not significant (p=0.4688), these structural flaws invalidate the readout entirely. The daily lift shows no fading trend, so NOVELTY is not flagged. Simpson's paradox is not evident as desktop and mobile both show higher treatment orders proportional to their sizes.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
