The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 52.3% control vs. 47.7% treatment, a deviation too large for chance given the sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies because the metric uses "sessions" while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis. Third, SIMPSON's paradox is evident: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, the treatment arm actually underperforms in both desktop (8.0% vs 8.0%) and mobile (2.5% vs 2.6%) segments when calculated correctly, or simply shows inverted ratios where the larger treatment desktop group drives the aggregate falsely. Finally, PEEK is flagged as the readout covers 23 days against a planned 21-day duration, suggesting unplanned extension or early peeking influenced the stop time.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON, PEEK
