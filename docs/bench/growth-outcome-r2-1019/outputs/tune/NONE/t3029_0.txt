The arm sizes (87,823 vs 85,952) differ slightly but likely fall within chance variation for this sample size, so SRM is not clearly flagged. The test ran for the full planned duration, ruling out PEEK. The daily lift shows no fading trend, eliminating NOVELTY.

However, Simpson's Paradox is present. In the desktop segment, the treatment converts higher (~6.19% vs ~6.38%), and in mobile, the treatment also appears higher or comparable depending on exact calculation, yet the aggregate lift is minimal. More critically, checking the math: Desktop treatment rate (2090/33790 ≈ 6.19%) vs Control (1996/31282 ≈ 6.38%) shows treatment is actually *lower* on desktop. Mobile treatment (608/32309 ≈ 1.88%) vs Control (702/36167 ≈ 1.94%) is also lower. Both segments show negative lift, yet the reported aggregate shows positive lift (4.082% vs 4.0%). This reversal indicates SIMPSON. Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions" while randomization was by "user id," violating DENOM.
