The arm sizes (55,150 vs 50,108) deviate significantly from the 50/50 randomization plan, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although the test ran for the planned duration, the SRM and denominator mismatch invalidate the statistical results. The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to confirm NOVELTY. Segment data does not show a reversal of the effect, ruling out SIMPSON.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
