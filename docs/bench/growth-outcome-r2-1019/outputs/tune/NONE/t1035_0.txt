The arm sizes (27,993 vs 28,017) are nearly identical, ruling out SRM. The test ran for 28 days as planned, so PEEK does not apply. The sample sizes are large enough to detect the target lift, eliminating POWER concerns.

However, DENOM is flagged: the metric is "order conversion rate," but the reported values (5.82% and 7.129%) match orders divided by *sessions* (e.g., 2,239/38,471 ≈ 5.82%), not users. Since randomization was by user ID, the denominator must be users.

Additionally, NOVELTY is evident: daily lift drops sharply from 48.7% to single digits by day 27, yet the team projects this transient lift over twelve months.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
