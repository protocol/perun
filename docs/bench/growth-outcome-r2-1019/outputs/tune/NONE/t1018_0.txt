The user split is nearly 50/50 (109,632 vs 109,376), so no SRM. The test ran for 22 days against a 21-day plan, showing no evidence of early stopping (PEEK) or fading lift (NOVELTY); daily lifts are stable. The metric (orders/users) matches the randomization unit, so no DENOM error.

However, Simpson's paradox is present. The overall treatment lift is positive (~4.6%). Yet, within segments:
- Desktop: Control 11.9%, Treatment 11.2% (Treatment worse).
- Mobile: Control 4.3%, Treatment 4.1% (Treatment worse).
The treatment loses in every segment but wins in aggregate because it received far more high-converting desktop users (65,955 vs 52,960) while the control got more mobile users. This skewed allocation within the 50/50 split invalidates the pooled result.

ERRORS: SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
