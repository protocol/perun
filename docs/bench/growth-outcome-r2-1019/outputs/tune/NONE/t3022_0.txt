The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 65,497 vs 69,986 sessions. A chi-squared test confirms this deviation from 50/50 is statistically impossible by chance, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms and segments report "sessions" and "users" inconsistently (e.g., desktop sums to 37k users while total sessions are 65k), meaning the metric denominator (sessions) does not match the randomization unit (user ID). Additionally, SIMPSON is evident: desktop shows a positive lift (~1.6%), and mobile shows a negative lift (~-1.4%), yet the pooled result claims a positive lift, driven by the skewed session counts.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
