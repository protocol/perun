The sample sizes (28,687 vs 29,796) differ slightly but likely fall within chance variation for this volume, so SRM is not clearly flagged. The test ran past its planned end date (Oct 14) to Oct 15, but there is no evidence of early stopping or peeking, so PEEK is not flagged. The p-value (0.6177) indicates no statistical significance; the test was underpowered to detect the small observed lift, but POWER usually refers to inability to detect the *target* lift before running. However, the critical error is DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms report "sessions." If users have multiple sessions, the denominator is wrong. Additionally, SIMPSON is present: Desktop lift is ~1.5% ((883/11637)/(828/10599)-1), while Mobile shows a negative lift ((226/10102)/(255/11076)-1 ≈ -1.6%), yet the pooled result is positive. This reversal indicates Simpson's Paradox.
