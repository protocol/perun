SRM: The session split is 48% control vs 52% treatment (32,022 vs 34,787). A deviation of ~2,700 on ~67k total far exceeds chance for a 50/50 design, indicating broken assignment or logging.
DENOM: Randomization was by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since users generate varying session counts, this mismatches the unit of randomization, biasing variance and p-values.
PEEK: The test ran exactly the planned 14 days (Aug 7–21); no early stopping is evident from the dates.
POWER: With ~32k–34k sessions per arm, the test detects the target 8% lift; it is not underpowered.
SIMPSON: Desktop lift (~8.5%) and mobile lift (~8.6%) both align with the pooled ~7.6%; no reversal exists.
NOVELTY: Daily lifts fluctuate (5.8%–9.3%) without a clear fading trend; projecting 12 months is aggressive but not provably a novelty error from these numbers alone.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
