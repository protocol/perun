The test ran for 16 days (Sept 19 to Oct 5), exceeding the planned 14-day duration, which suggests PEEK if the decision was made early, but the readout implies completion. However, the metric denominator is "sessions" while randomization was by "user id." Since a single user can generate multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption and constitutes a DENOM error. Additionally, the daily lift shows no fading trend (values fluctuate between 4.2% and 7.3% without a clear downward trajectory), so NOVELTY is not supported. The arm sizes (106k vs 102k) are close enough that SRM is unlikely without a specific statistical test, and segment data aligns with the overall direction, ruling out SIMPSON. The primary clear error is the denominator mismatch.

ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
