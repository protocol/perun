The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: treatment has 41,653 sessions vs control's 40,614. A chi-square test confirms this deviation from 50/50 is statistically significant, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: randomization was by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since users can have multiple sessions, this mismatches the unit of analysis. Third, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, the mobile segment shows control (534/16078 ≈ 3.32%) outperforming treatment (477/14772 ≈ 3.23%), reversing the direction within a major segment. Finally, PEEK applies as the test ended June 9, before the planned June 15 end date.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
