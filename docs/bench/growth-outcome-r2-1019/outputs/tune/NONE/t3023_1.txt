The analysis contains two clear errors. First, SRM is present: with ~110k total users, a 50/50 split should yield roughly 55k per arm. The observed counts (56,177 vs 53,511) differ by 2,666, which is far beyond the expected statistical noise (~235 for 2 sigma), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the primary arm sizes and conversion rates are calculated using "sessions" as the denominator, not unique users. While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a consistent fade required to flag NOVELTY. The p-value (0.136) correctly indicates non-significance, so the lack of power is a result, not necessarily a design flaw detectable here without variance details, but the metric definition and assignment integrity are definitely broken.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
