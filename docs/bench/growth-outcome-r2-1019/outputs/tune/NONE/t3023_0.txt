The readout shows clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~110k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield roughly 54,800 per arm. The observed counts (56,177 vs 53,511) differ by 2,666. The standard deviation for this sample size is ~165; the observed difference is over 16 standard deviations away from expected, which is impossible by chance alone, indicating broken assignment or logging.

Additionally, the Denominator (DENOM) is incorrect. The metric is "order conversion rate," which implies a user-level metric (orders per user), yet the readout reports "sessions" as the denominator. Since randomization was by user ID, using sessions violates the unit of analysis.

The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to flag NOVELTY strictly on numbers provided. PEEK, POWER, and SIMPSON are not evident from the counts.
