The data shows severe Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). While user allocation was planned at 50/50, the observed split is roughly 50.05% control vs 49.95% treatment, which is acceptable. However, session counts differ drastically: control has 422,501 sessions while treatment has only 339,448. This ~20% discrepancy in sessions per user suggests broken logging or assignment issues affecting exposure, violating the randomization assumption.

Additionally, Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON) is present. The aggregate data shows treatment winning (3.56% vs 2.70%). Yet, looking at devices: Desktop treatment conversion is ~6.44% vs control ~7.00% (control wins), and Mobile treatment is ~2.11% vs control ~2.30% (control wins). The treatment loses in every segment but wins overall due to unequal weighting of high-converting desktop users in the treatment arm.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
