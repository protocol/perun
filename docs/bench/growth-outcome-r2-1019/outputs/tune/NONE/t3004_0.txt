The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~68.8k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~34.4k per arm. The observed counts (35,717 vs 33,085) differ by 2,632. The standard deviation is ~131; the difference is ~20 sigma, far exceeding chance, indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, DENOM is flagged: randomization was by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since users can have multiple sessions, this violates the unit of analysis, biasing variance and p-values. While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a clear fading trend required for NOVELTY. Segments do not show reversal (SIMPSON). The test ran past the planned date, so PEEK is not evident from dates alone. Due to SRM and DENOM, results are invalid.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
