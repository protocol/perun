Second, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, the desktop segment favors treatment (11.7% vs 12.6%), but the mobile segment heavily favors control (3.8% vs 3.5%), yet mobile has fewer treatment users, skewing the aggregate.
