The readout shows a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~84k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~42k per arm. The observed counts (44,123 vs 39,984) differ by over 4,000, a deviation far exceeding chance limits for this sample size, indicating broken assignment or logging.

Additionally, the Denominator error (DENOM) is present. Users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and metrics are reported in "sessions." Using sessions as the denominator instead of unique users violates the randomization unit, invalidating the statistical test.

The test ran for the planned 29 days (June 7 to July 6), so PEEK is not evident. Daily lift is stable, ruling out NOVELTY. Segment data aligns with the total, so no SIMPSON. While POWER might be a concern given the p-value, SRM and DENOM are definitive calculation errors based on the provided numbers.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
