The analysis contains two clear errors. First, SIMPSON: while the pooled result shows a positive lift (treatment > control), both segments show negative lift when calculated correctly (Desktop: 6.29% vs 5.99%; Mobile: 1.79% vs 1.80%). The aggregate result is reversed relative to the segments. Second, NOVELTY: the daily lift clearly decays from 6.0% to 1.5%, yet the team projects the current reported lift forward for twelve months without accounting for this fade. Additionally, the result is not statistically significant (p=0.4179), making the rollout recommendation unsupported regardless of the errors.

ERRORS: SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
