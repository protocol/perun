The analysis contains two clear errors. First, SRM is present: with ~765k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~382.5k per arm. The observed counts (393,773 vs 371,348) differ by over 22k, a deviation far exceeding chance limits for this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metric (conversion rate) and arm sizes are reported in sessions. Since session counts per user can vary, analyzing sessions instead of users invalidates the p-value. Additionally, Simpson's paradox appears in device segments (treatment wins on both desktop and mobile individually) yet the pooled data suggests a negligible/insignificant result driven by the session-level mismatch.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
