The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the treatment arm has 211,978 sessions versus 196,473 in control, a deviation far exceeding chance for a 50/50 split, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies because the metric (conversion rate) uses "sessions" as the denominator, while randomization occurred at the "user id" level; this mismatch invalidates the statistical test. Third, PEEK is evident as the p-value is reported as exactly 0.0, which is statistically impossible and suggests early stopping or improper calculation without correction. Finally, the device-level data reveals SIMPSON: desktop lift is ~6.9% and mobile is ~6.9%, yet the pooled lift is reported differently due to the session/user mismatch inflating the aggregate.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
