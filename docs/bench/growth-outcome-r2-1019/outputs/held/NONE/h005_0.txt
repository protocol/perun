The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 48.1% control vs. 51.9% treatment (771,210 total users). A deviation of ~14,000 users from the expected 50/50 split is statistically impossible by chance alone, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms are reported in "sessions." Since users can generate multiple sessions, analyzing at the session level violates the independence assumption of the randomized unit. While PEEK might be suspected due to the p-value of 0.0, the dates match the planned duration exactly. The daily lift appears stable, ruling out NOVELTY. Segments align with the aggregate, ruling out SIMPSON.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
