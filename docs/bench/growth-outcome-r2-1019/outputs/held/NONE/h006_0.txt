The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 30,084 vs 32,614 sessions. With ~62k total, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly ±1.2% (±370 users). The observed difference (~2,530) vastly exceeds this, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is violated: users were randomized by user ID, but the primary arm counts and metrics are reported in sessions. Since session counts per user can vary by treatment, using sessions as the denominator biases the conversion rate. Additionally, daily data shows 30 days of results despite a planned 28-day duration, suggesting PEEKing may have occurred, though SRM and DENOM are definitively proven by the counts.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
