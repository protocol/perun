The readout shows a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With 152,103 total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~76,051 per arm. The observed counts (81,907 vs 70,196) deviate by nearly 12,000 users, far exceeding chance limits, indicating broken assignment or logging. Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id." If users generated multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption (DENOM), as the analysis unit differs from the randomization unit. Although the test ran for the planned 28 days (no PEEK) and daily lift appears stable (no NOVELTY), the fundamental data integrity issues (SRM) and unit mismatch (DENOM) invalidate the reported p-value and lift. These errors must be resolved before trusting the results.
