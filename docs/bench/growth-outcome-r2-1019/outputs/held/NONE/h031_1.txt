The randomization unit is user ID, but the reported metric uses sessions as the denominator (64,866 vs 67,072 sessions), violating the unit of analysis. This is a DENOM error. Additionally, the daily lift shows a clear downward trend from ~12% to ~3%, indicating a novelty effect that is fading, yet the team projects the current average lift over twelve months. This is a NOVELTY error. The sample sizes are large enough for power, the split is roughly 50/50 (no SRM), and the test ran for the planned duration (no PEEK). Segment data does not show reversal (no SIMPSON). Because the denominator mismatch invalidates the statistical test and the novelty effect suggests the lift is temporary, the rollout decision is premature.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
