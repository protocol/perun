The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present: with ~128k total users, the expected split is ~64k per arm. The observed counts (62,403 vs 66,269) deviate by over 3,800 users, far exceeding chance variation for a 50/50 split, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM (Denominator Mismatch) occurs: users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and device breakdowns are reported in "sessions" and "users" inconsistently without clarifying if the metric denominator (orders per session vs orders per user) matches the randomization unit. The p-value (0.1029) is also non-significant, yet the team recommends rollout.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
