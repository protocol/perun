The arm sizes (96,728 vs 86,308) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the analysis uses "sessions" as the denominator while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of randomization (DENOM). Although the test ran for the planned 14 days, the severe SRM and denominator mismatch invalidate the statistical results regardless of duration or power calculations. The daily lift appears stable, and segment data does not show reversal, ruling out NOVELTY and SIMPSON based on provided numbers. However, the identified errors prevent trusting the reported lift.

DECISION: HOLD
