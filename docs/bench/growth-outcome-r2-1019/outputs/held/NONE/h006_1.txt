The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM: the split is 30,084 vs 32,614 sessions. With ~62k total, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly ±1.6% (approx. ±500 users). The observed difference (~2,500) vastly exceeds this, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms report "sessions" and the device breakdown mixes "users" and "orders" inconsistently. Calculating rates using sessions as the denominator when randomization occurred at the user level violates the unit of analysis, invalidating the p-value. Because the assignment mechanism appears broken (SRM) and the metric denominator mismatches the randomization unit (DENOM), the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
