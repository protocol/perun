The arm sizes (69,857 vs 66,228) deviate significantly from the expected 50/50 split given the total volume, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id." This mismatch (DENOM) violates the unit of analysis, as multiple sessions per user are not independent. While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a clear fading trend required to flag NOVELTY, and segment data does not demonstrate reversed signs for SIMPSON. The test ran for the planned 14 days (Feb 13–28), so PEEK is not evident. However, SRM and DENOM are critical validity failures.
