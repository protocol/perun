The arm sizes are 105,529 (control) and 100,649 (treatment). For ~206k total users, a 50/50 split expects ~103k per arm. The observed difference (~4.9k) is far too large to be chance (Z-score > 10), indicating broken randomization or logging. This is a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Because the assignment mechanism is suspect, the validity of the p-value and lift is compromised. Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization was by "user id." If users generate multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption and constitutes a denominator error (DENOM). Due to these fundamental flaws, the results cannot be trusted for a rollout decision.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
