The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present: with ~188k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~94k per arm. The observed counts (91,761 vs 96,656) deviate by nearly 5,000, which is statistically impossible by chance alone, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON (Simpson's Paradox) is evident: while the pooled data shows a positive lift (2.999% vs 3.087%), the segmented data reveals the treatment performed worse in both Desktop (4.55% vs 4.34%) and Mobile (1.81% vs 1.72%). The aggregate result is reversed within segments due to the skewed traffic distribution caused by the SRM. Additionally, PEEK applies as the test stopped at 18 days instead of the planned 28.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
