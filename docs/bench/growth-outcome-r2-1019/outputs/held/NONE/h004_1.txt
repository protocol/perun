The readout shows a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~283k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~141.5k per arm. The observed counts (145,998 vs 137,604) differ by ~8.4k, a deviation far exceeding chance limits for this sample size, indicating broken assignment or logging.

Additionally, the Denominator (DENOM) is incorrect. Users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metrics and arm sizes are reported in "sessions." Analyzing session-level conversion without adjusting for users who generate multiple sessions violates the randomization unit, biasing variance estimates.

While the p-value (0.0659) is non-significant, the structural data errors invalidate the result entirely. The daily lift appears stable, ruling out NOVELTY. Segment data is insufficient to confirm SIMPSON'S paradox definitively without recalculating rates, but the SRM and DENOM errors are fatal.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
