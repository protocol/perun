The arm sizes (52,058 vs 55,538) deviate significantly from the 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although the test ran for the planned 14 days and daily lift appears stable, these two structural errors invalidate the reported p-value and lift estimate. The segmentation data also reveals inconsistent denominators (users vs sessions), preventing a valid Simpson's paradox check, but the primary errors are sufficient to halt deployment.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
