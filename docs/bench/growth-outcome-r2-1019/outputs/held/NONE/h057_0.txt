The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 33,952 vs 31,009 sessions, a deviation far exceeding chance for this sample size, indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, DENOM applies because randomization was by user ID, but the primary metrics and arm counts are reported in sessions, mismatching the unit of analysis. Third, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows treatment winning (6.4% vs 5.6%), the desktop segment alone shows treatment losing (13.8% vs 14.5%). Finally, PEEK is evident as the readout date (May 15) is only 29 days after start, yet the planned duration was 28 days; however, the daily data lists 29 days, suggesting the test ran its course, but the SRM and denominator issues are fatal.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
