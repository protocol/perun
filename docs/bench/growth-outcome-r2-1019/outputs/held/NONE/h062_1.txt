The test stopped on day 18 of a planned 28-day duration, indicating early stopping (PEEK). The overall result is not significant (p=0.0563), yet segments show reversal: desktop favors treatment (4.38% vs 4.59%), while mobile favors control (1.66% vs 1.59%), demonstrating Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON). Additionally, the metric denominator is sessions, but randomization and segment breakdowns are by user ID; since users can have multiple sessions, this mismatches the unit of analysis (DENOM). The arm sizes are close enough to avoid SRM, and daily lift shows no clear fading trend to flag NOVELTY. Power appears sufficient given the large sample sizes.
