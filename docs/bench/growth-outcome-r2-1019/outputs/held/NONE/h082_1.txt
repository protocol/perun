The arm sizes (42,740 vs 38,525) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). The daily lift shows a clear downward trend (32.3% to 5.6%), yet the team projects this initial lift for twelve months, ignoring the fading effect (NOVELTY). Finally, the pooled result shows a positive lift, but segmenting by device reveals the treatment performed worse on mobile (3.0% vs 3.1% for control), indicating Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON). These errors invalidate the reported significance.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
