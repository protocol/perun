The data reveals multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~81k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~40.5k per arm. The observed counts (42,740 vs 38,525) deviate by over 4k, far exceeding chance expectations, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: the pooled data shows treatment winning (5.1% vs 4.4%), but within segments, desktop treatment converts lower (9.8% vs 10.3%) and mobile treatment converts lower (3.0% vs 3.1%) than control. The aggregate result is reversed. Third, PEEK is evident: the test ran 23 days against a planned 21 days, and the "p=0.0" suggests early stopping or infinite significance, which is impossible. Finally, NOVELTY is shown by the daily lift fading from 32% to 5%, yet the team projects this as lasting.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
