The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 50.2% control vs 49.8% treatment. With ~760k users, the standard deviation is ~435; the observed difference of ~2,200 is ~5 standard deviations away, indicating broken randomization. Second, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows treatment winning (3.09% vs 3.0%), segment data reveals treatment loses on both desktop (4.38% vs 4.59%) and mobile (1.59% vs 1.66%). The aggregate result is reversed within segments due to imbalanced traffic distribution across devices.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
