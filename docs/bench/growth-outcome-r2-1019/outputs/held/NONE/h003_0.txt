The sample sizes (226,935 vs 218,410) deviate significantly from the expected 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although the test ran for 23 days against a 21-day plan, the primary issues are the assignment imbalance and incorrect denominator. The daily lift appears stable, ruling out NOVELTY, and segment data does not show reversed trends, ruling out SIMPSON. These errors invalidate the reported p-value and lift.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
