The arm sizes (77,441 vs 79,856) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although the test ran for the planned duration and daily lifts appear stable, these two structural errors invalidate the reported p-value and lift estimate. The pooled result does not show Simpson's paradox (both segments favor treatment), and the sample size is sufficient for the target lift, so POWER and SIMPSON are not flagged. However, SRM and DENOM are critical failures requiring investigation before any business decision.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
