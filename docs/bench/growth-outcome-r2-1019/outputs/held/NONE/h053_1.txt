The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (119,330 vs 123,600) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, the segmented data reveals the opposite. Desktop conversion is higher in treatment (~12.5% vs ~13.1%), but mobile conversion is drastically lower in treatment (~3.9% vs ~4.0%), and the mobile sample shift drives the aggregate result incorrectly. Additionally, PEEK applies because the test stopped at 18 days against a 28-day plan. Finally, DENOM is present because the metric uses "sessions" while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis.
