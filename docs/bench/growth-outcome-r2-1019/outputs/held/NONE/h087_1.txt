The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: with ~39,885 total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~19,942 per arm. The observed counts (19,610 vs 20,275) deviate by 665, which exceeds the threshold for chance variation (~284 at 95% confidence), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM is flagged because users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metrics and arm sizes are reported in sessions; multiple sessions per user violate the independence assumption required for this unit of analysis. Third, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, the desktop segment shows a lift (~2.5%), but the mobile segment shows a significant treatment loss (~-12.5%), reversing the direction within segments.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
