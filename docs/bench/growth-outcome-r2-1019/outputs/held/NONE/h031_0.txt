The readout shows clear evidence of two errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes are reported in sessions. Since session counts per user vary, using sessions as the denominator for conversion rate violates the randomization unit. Second, NOVELTY: the daily relative lift shows a strong downward trend from ~12% in the first week to ~3% by day 29, yet the team projects the current aggregate lift over twelve months without accounting for this fade. The test ran for 29 days against a planned 28, so PEEK is not indicated. Arm sizes are reasonably close to 50/50 given the session metric noise, so SRM is not clearly flagged by counts alone.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
