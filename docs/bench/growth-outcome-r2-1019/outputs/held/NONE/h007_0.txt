The randomization unit is user ID, but the reported metric uses sessions as the denominator (control: 69,857 sessions vs. treatment: 66,228 sessions), while user counts differ slightly less. This mismatch between randomization unit and analysis unit violates the DENOM principle. Additionally, the daily lift shows a clear downward trend from ~8.6% to ~5.5%, yet the team projects the current average lift over twelve months without accounting for this decay, indicating a NOVELTY error. The sample sizes appear sufficient for power, and no Simpson's paradox is evident as both segments show positive lift. The test ran for the planned 14 days (plus one day of data), so PEEK is not clearly indicated. SRM is borderline but likely due to the denominator issue rather than assignment failure.
