The arm sizes (95,250 vs 89,274) deviate significantly from the 50/50 split, indicating SRM. The test ran for 9 days instead of the planned 14, suggesting PEEK. While the pooled result shows a positive lift, the device segments reveal reversed effects: desktop favors treatment (8.2% vs 8.6%), but mobile favors control (2.5% vs 2.4%), confirming SIMPSON. The denominator is sessions while randomization was by user ID, violating DENOM. Finally, projecting a 9-day result over twelve months ignores potential NOVELTY effects, though the daily data is stable; however, the other errors are definitive.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
