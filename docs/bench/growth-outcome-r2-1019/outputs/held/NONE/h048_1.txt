The test ran for 16 days (Sept 19 to Oct 5), exceeding the planned 14-day duration, indicating PEEK. The arm sizes are 129,630 vs 124,630. A 50/50 split of 254,260 total users expects 127,130 per arm. The difference (2,500) is roughly 2% of the total. Using a quick z-test approximation for proportions, the standard error is ~0.0031, making the observed difference (~0.0196) over 6 standard errors away, which is statistically significant. This indicates SRM. The metric is orders/sessions, but randomization was by user ID; if users have multiple sessions, this creates DENOM. Daily lift shows no fading trend, ruling out NOVELTY. Segment data (desktop/mobile) both show positive lifts consistent with the aggregate, ruling out SIMPSON. Given SRM and DENOM, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
