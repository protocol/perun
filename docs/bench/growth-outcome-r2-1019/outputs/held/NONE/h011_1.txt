The randomization unit is user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since a single user can generate multiple sessions, using sessions as the denominator violates the independence assumption and mismatches the randomization unit. This is a DENOM error.

Additionally, the test ran for only 30 days (June 2 to July 2) against a planned duration of 28 days, which appears compliant, but the daily lift data shows 30 data points while the dates suggest a 30-day run. However, the primary clear error is the denominator mismatch. The sample sizes (277k vs 271k) are close enough that SRM is not obviously violated without a strict calculation, and the daily lift does not show a clear fading trend indicative of NOVELTY.

ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
