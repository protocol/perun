The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 152,495 vs 143,030 sessions, a significant deviation from 50/50 that suggests assignment issues. Second, DENOM is violated: randomization was by user ID, but the primary arm counts and metrics use sessions, mismatching the unit of analysis. Third, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows treatment winning (2.24% vs 2.00%), the desktop segment shows treatment losing (5.14% vs 5.38%) and mobile shows treatment winning; the aggregate direction is driven by the mix of users, not a consistent effect. Finally, PEEK is indicated as the readout date (June 10) is only 15 days after start, potentially stopping early based on significance.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
