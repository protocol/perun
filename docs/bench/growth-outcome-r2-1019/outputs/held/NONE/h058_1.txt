The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 40,768 vs 36,482 sessions, a deviation far exceeding chance for this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies because users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metric and arm counts use sessions; multiple sessions per user violate the independence assumption. Third, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: the pooled data shows treatment winning (4.16% vs 3.47%), but segment calculations show control winning on both desktop (7.62% vs 8.12%) and mobile (2.72% vs 2.89%). Finally, PEEK is evident as the readout date (June 29) is only 30 days after start, yet daily lifts are analyzed through day 30 despite a planned 28-day duration, suggesting premature stopping or extension based on results.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON, PEEK
