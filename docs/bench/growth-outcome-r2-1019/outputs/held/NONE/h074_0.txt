The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: treatment has 233,182 sessions vs. control's 216,400, a deviation far exceeding chance for a 50/50 split, indicating broken assignment. Second, PEEK applies because the test stopped at 14 days against a planned 21-day duration. Third, DENOM is violated: randomization was by user ID, but arm sizes and desktop/mobile breakdowns are reported in "sessions" and "users" inconsistently, while the metric denominator implies sessions. Finally, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled result shows a treatment lift, the mobile segment shows control outperforming treatment (2.60% vs 2.48%), and desktop drives the aggregate.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
