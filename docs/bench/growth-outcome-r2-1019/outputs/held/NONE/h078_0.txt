The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 49.2% control vs 50.8% treatment. With ~157k total sessions, the standard deviation for a 50/50 split is ~198; the observed difference of ~2,400 is over 12 standard deviations away, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, segment-level conversion rates show control winning on both desktop (9.24% vs 8.82%) and mobile (2.95% vs 2.82%). The aggregate result is reversed within segments due to the skewed traffic distribution across devices.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
