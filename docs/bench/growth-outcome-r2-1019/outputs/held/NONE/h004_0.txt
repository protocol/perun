The treatment arm has 137,604 sessions versus 145,998 in control, a deviation exceeding 5% from the 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id." This mismatch violates the unit of analysis, constituting a Denominator error (DENOM). Although the p-value is non-significant (0.0659), these structural flaws invalidate the result's reliability regardless of statistical significance. The daily lift appears stable, ruling out NOVELTY, and segment directionality matches the aggregate, ruling out SIMPSON. The test ran for the planned 21 days, ruling out PEEK. Due to SRM and DENOM, the data cannot support a rollout decision.
