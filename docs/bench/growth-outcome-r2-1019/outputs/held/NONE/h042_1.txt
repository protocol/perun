The test ran for 16 days against a planned 14-day duration, indicating PEEK. The arm sizes (200,821 vs 212,099) differ significantly from the 50/50 split expected for ~413k total users; a chi-square check confirms this deviation is too large for chance, indicating SRM. The metric denominator is "sessions," but randomization was by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Daily lift shows a clear downward trend (8.9% to 1.2%), yet the team projects the current average over twelve months, ignoring the fading effect (NOVELTY). Simpson's paradox is not present as both device segments show higher treatment conversion. The sample size is sufficient for power.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
