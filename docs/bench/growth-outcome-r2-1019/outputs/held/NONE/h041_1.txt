The readout shows a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~206k total sessions, a perfect 50/50 split expects ~103k per arm. The observed counts (105,529 vs 100,649) differ by ~4,880. The standard deviation for this sample size is ~227; the observed difference is over 21 standard deviations away from expected, which is impossible by chance alone. This indicates broken assignment or logging, invalidating the results.

Additionally, the denominator is "sessions" while randomization was by "user id." If users generated multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption and constitutes a Denominator (DENOM) error.

The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to flag NOVELTY strictly on numbers alone, and segment math roughly aligns, so SIMPSON is not clearly evidenced without recalculating exact rates. However, SRM and DENOM are fatal.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
