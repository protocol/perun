The test ran for 14 days (Apr 28–May 12) against a planned 21-day duration, indicating early stopping (PEEK). The arm sizes are 248,528 vs 267,931; a chi-square check confirms this deviation from the 50/50 split is statistically significant, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The metric denominator is "sessions," but randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a clear fading trend required to flag NOVELTY. Segment data aligns with the pooled direction, ruling out SIMPSON. Given the multiple structural errors in assignment and measurement, the results are unreliable.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
