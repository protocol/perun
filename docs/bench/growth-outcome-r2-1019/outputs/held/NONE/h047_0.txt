The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 67,763 vs 71,534 sessions. With ~139k total, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly ±0.4% (approx. ±550 users). The observed difference (~3,771) vastly exceeds this, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: the pooled data shows Treatment > Control (4.12% vs 4.00%). However, segmenting by device reveals Treatment < Control on Desktop (5.70% vs 5.97%) and Mobile (2.20% vs 2.30%). The aggregate result is reversed within every segment. Additionally, DENOM is flagged because randomization was by user ID, but the primary arm counts and metrics are reported in sessions, mismatching the unit of randomization.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
