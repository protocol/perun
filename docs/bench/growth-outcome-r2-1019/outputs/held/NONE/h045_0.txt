The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 48,067 vs 47,606 sessions. While close, the device breakdown reveals a massive imbalance (Desktop: 14.8k vs 17.5k; Mobile: 22.6k vs 20.3k), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: the pooled data shows treatment higher (8.25% vs 8.0%), but within segments, Desktop treatment is lower (13.0% vs 13.6%) and Mobile treatment is lower (4.1% vs 4.3%). The aggregate lift is an artifact of the skewed traffic mix. Finally, DENOM applies because the metric uses "sessions" while randomization was by "user id," violating the unit of analysis. Due to these fundamental flaws, the results are invalid.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
