The arm sizes (113,562 vs 107,406) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although the test ran for 23 days against a 21-day plan, the daily lift shows no clear fading trend to suggest a Noveltly effect, and segment data does not show reversed trends for Simpson's paradox. The primary issues are the assignment imbalance and the mismatched denominator.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
