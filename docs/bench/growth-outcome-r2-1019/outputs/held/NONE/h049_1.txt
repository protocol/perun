The analysis contains two critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is 54,538 vs 56,204 (49.2% vs 50.8%). With ~110k total users, a deviation of >0.8% is statistically significant, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows treatment higher (5.15% vs 5.00%), segment-level math reveals the reverse. Desktop: Control 8.01% vs Treatment 7.65%. Mobile: Control 2.65% vs Treatment 2.53%. The treatment performs worse in every segment but appears better overall due to imbalanced segment sizes. Additionally, DENOM is flagged because randomization was by "user id" but metrics and splits are reported in "sessions," violating the unit of analysis.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
