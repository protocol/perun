The analysis contains two critical errors. First, DENOM: Users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and metric calculations use sessions and mixed user/order counts per device, mismatching the randomization unit. Second, PEEK: The test ran for only 18 days (Oct 14 to Nov 1) against a planned 28-day duration, yet results were reported and a rollout recommended, inflating false positive risk. While SRM appears present (51.2% treatment share), the denominator mismatch makes assignment validation unreliable without user-level counts. The non-significant p-value (0.23) already suggests caution, but the methodological flaws prevent a valid decision.

ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
