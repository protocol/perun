The arm sizes (75,479 vs 65,657) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the test ran for only 18 days against a planned 28-day duration, constituting Peeking (PEEK), which inflates false positive risk. The reported p-value of 0.0 is suspicious given these errors. While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a clear fading trend required to flag NOVELTY strictly from these numbers. The denominator (sessions) aligns with the randomized unit (users) closely enough that DENOM is not clearly violated without session-per-user data. Simpson's paradox is not evident as segment lifts appear consistent with the aggregate direction.

DECISION: HOLD
