The data reveals multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: the split is ~52.5% control vs ~47.5% treatment, a deviation too large for chance in this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies because users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metrics and arm counts are reported in sessions, mismatching the unit of randomization. Third, SIMPSON's paradox occurs: while the pooled data shows treatment winning (5.14% vs 4.35%), calculating per-user conversion rates reveals control wins on both desktop (~9.3% vs ~10.0%) and mobile (~3.3% vs ~3.5%). Finally, NOVELTY is evident as the daily lift clearly decays from 35% to ~8%, yet the projection assumes the initial high lift persists for a year.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
