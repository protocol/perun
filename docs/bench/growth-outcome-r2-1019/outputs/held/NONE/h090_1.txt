The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM is present: with ~259k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~129.7k per arm. The observed counts (136,104 vs 123,494) deviate by over 12k, far exceeding chance limits, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM applies because users were randomized by user ID, but the primary metrics and arm sizes are reported in sessions, mismatching the unit of randomization. Third, PEEK is evident as the test ran only 14 days against a planned 21-day duration. Finally, NOVELTY is flagged because the daily lift shows a clear downward trend (34.5% to 6.7%), yet the team projects this fading lift as constant for twelve months.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
