The test stopped on day 14 of a planned 21-day duration, indicating early stopping (PEEK). The arm sizes (216,400 vs 233,182) differ significantly from the 50/50 split; with ~450k total users, a difference of ~17k is far beyond chance expectations, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions" while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). While mobile/desktop splits look odd, the primary clear errors are SRM, PEEK, and DENOM. These issues invalidate the statistical results.
