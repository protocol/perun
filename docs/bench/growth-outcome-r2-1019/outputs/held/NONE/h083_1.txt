The test ran for the planned 14 days (Jan 29–Feb 12), ruling out PEEK. The arm sizes (46,180 vs 47,762) are close enough to 50/50 that SRM is unlikely without a specific p-value indicating otherwise. The metric (orders/sessions) matches the randomized unit (users/sessions), so DENOM is clear.

However, Simpson's paradox is evident: the overall treatment lift is positive (+2.9%), yet within segments, Desktop shows a lift (~13.2% vs ~12.2%) while Mobile shows a significant drop (~3.9% vs ~4.9%). The aggregated positive result masks the negative impact on mobile users. Additionally, the daily lift fades consistently from 5.9% to 0.9%, suggesting a NOVELTY effect rather than a sustained improvement, making the 12-month projection unreliable.
