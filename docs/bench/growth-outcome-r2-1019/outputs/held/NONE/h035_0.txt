The randomization unit is user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions, violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Additionally, the device-level data reveals Simpson's paradox: while the pooled treatment appears higher (6.18% vs 6.0%), the treatment conversion rate is actually lower within both Desktop (8.44% vs 8.83%) and Mobile (3.26% vs 3.41%) segments compared to control (SIMPSON). The test ran 22 days against a 21-day plan, so PEEK is not indicated. The sample sizes are large enough for the target lift, ruling out POWER. Daily lift is stable, ruling out NOVELTY. The arm sizes differ slightly but likely within chance limits given the huge N, so SRM is not clearly flagged by counts alone without a specific test statistic, though the other two errors are definitive.

DECISION: HOLD
