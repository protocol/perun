The arm sizes (371,170 vs 400,040) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). This suggests broken assignment or logging, invalidating the results. Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id." This mismatch (DENOM) violates the unit of analysis, as multiple sessions per user introduce correlation that standard tests ignore. While the test ran for the planned 14 days and daily lift appears stable, these two fundamental errors render the reported p-value and lift unreliable. The data cannot support a rollout decision until the data collection integrity (SRM) and unit of analysis (DENOM) are corrected.
