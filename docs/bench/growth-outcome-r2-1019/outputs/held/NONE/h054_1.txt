The analysis contains multiple errors. First, SRM is present: the treatment arm has 267,931 sessions versus 248,528 in control, a deviation far exceeding chance for a 50/50 split, indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, PEEK applies because the test ran only 14 days against a planned 21-day duration, inflating false positive risk. Third, DENOM is violated: randomization was by user ID, but the primary arm counts and metrics use sessions, mismatching the unit of analysis. Finally, NOVELTY is flagged as the team projects a 12-month lift despite daily data showing volatility and no evidence of sustained stability.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM, NOVELTY
