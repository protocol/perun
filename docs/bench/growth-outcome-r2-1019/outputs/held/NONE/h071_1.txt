The arm sizes (49,510 vs 52,006) differ significantly from the 50/50 split; with ~100k total users, a deviation of ~2,500 exceeds chance expectations, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). The daily lift shows a clear decay from 17.1% to 2.8%, yet the team projects the current lift for twelve months without adjusting for this fading effect (NOVELTY). Simpson's paradox is not present as segment directions align with the pooled result. The sample size appears sufficient for power given the counts.
