SRM: The allocation is 48.9% control vs 51.1% treatment (122,949 vs 128,468). With ~250k total users, a 2.2% deviation from 50/50 is statistically significant, indicating broken randomization or logging bias.

DENOM: The metric is "order conversion rate," but the denominators provided are "sessions." Since users were randomized by user ID, the denominator must be unique users, not sessions. Using sessions inflates the sample size and invalidates the p-value.

PEEK: The test ran exactly 14 days (April 21 to May 7) as planned, so no peeking occurred based on the dates. However, the SRM and DENOM errors invalidate the result regardless.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
