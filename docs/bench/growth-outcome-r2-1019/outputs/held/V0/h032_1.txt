The readout shows a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~529k total sessions, the expected split is ~264.5k per arm. The observed difference (265,873 vs 263,078) is 2,795 sessions. The standard deviation for a 50/50 split is ~363; the observed deviation is ~7.7 sigma, far exceeding chance limits, indicating broken assignment or logging.

Additionally, the metric denominator is sessions, but randomization was by user ID. If users generate multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption (DENOM), biasing variance and p-values.

The daily lift fluctuates but ends at 3.0% on day 21, lower than the average. While this hints at novelty or variance, the SRM and denominator errors are fatal to validity. The p-value is unreliable given these flaws.
