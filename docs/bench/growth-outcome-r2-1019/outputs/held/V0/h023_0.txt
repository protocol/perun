SRM: Control (90,377) vs Treatment (88,218) is a ~2.4% imbalance. With ~178k total users, the standard error for a 50/50 split is ~0.37%. The observed difference is ~6.5 standard deviations away, indicating a significant Sample Ratio Mismatch.

PEEK: The test ran 23 days against a planned 21 days. While slightly over, the primary issue is the SRM invalidating the randomization assumption, making the p-value unreliable regardless of timing. However, strict adherence to "planned duration" suggests potential protocol deviation, but SRM is the definitive statistical failure here.

POWER: The sample size is sufficient for the baseline and target lift; the issue is data integrity, not size.

SIMPSON: Desktop lift (~4.7%) and Mobile lift (~4.6%) align with the pooled lift (~4.8% calculated from raw counts, though reported 4.8% vs calculated 4.84%). No reversal observed.

DENOM: Randomized by user ID, metrics use sessions/users consistently.

NOVELTY: Daily lift fluctuates (3.4% to 5.7%) but shows no clear monotonic fade; projecting 12 months from 23 days is risky but not strictly "fading" in the data provided.

ERRORS: SRM
DECISION: HOLD
