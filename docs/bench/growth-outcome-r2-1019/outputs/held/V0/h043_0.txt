The readout shows a severe Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM): planned 50/50 split vs. actual 52.7% control / 47.3% treatment (123,385 vs. 110,774 sessions). This difference is far beyond chance, indicating broken randomization or logging, which invalidates all causal claims. Additionally, the denominator is "sessions" while randomization was by "user id," creating a Denominator (DENOM) mismatch if users generated unequal session counts. The daily lift shows no fading trend, ruling out NOVELTY. Segments do not reverse the aggregate direction, ruling out SIMPSON. Because SRM breaks the experiment's validity, the result cannot support a rollout.
