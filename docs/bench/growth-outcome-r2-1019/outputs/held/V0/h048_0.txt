The readout contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~254k total sessions, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly ±0.2%. The observed split is 51.0% control vs 49.0% treatment, a 2% deviation far exceeding chance, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since users generate varying session counts, this violates the unit of randomization, biasing the variance and p-value. While **PEEK** is suspected (reading on day 17 of a 14-day plan), the SRM and DENOM errors alone invalidate the statistical claim. The projected lift is unreliable because the underlying assignment mechanism is flawed and the metric definition mismatches the experimental unit.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
