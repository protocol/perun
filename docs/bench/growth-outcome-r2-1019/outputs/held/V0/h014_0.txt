ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The arm sizes (196,473 vs 211,978) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, randomization was by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions; since users generate varying session counts, this violates the unit of randomization (DENOM). These flaws invalidate the p-value and lift calculation. Do not roll out until assignment integrity is verified and the metric is recalculated per user.
