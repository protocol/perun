The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since users can generate multiple sessions, this violates the unit of randomization and biases variance estimates. Second, **NOVELTY**: the daily lift shows no fading trend (values fluctuate between 3.8% and 7.3% without a downward slope over the 22 days), yet the team projects this transient lift permanently for twelve months without accounting for potential decay or seasonality. While the p-value (0.0644) exceeds alpha, the primary blockers are the metric definition and the unrealistic projection horizon.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
