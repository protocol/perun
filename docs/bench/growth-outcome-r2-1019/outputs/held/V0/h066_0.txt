The readout shows a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM): with ~188k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~94k per arm. The observed 91,761 vs 96,656 differs by ~4,900, exceeding the chance threshold (~430 at 95% confidence), indicating broken assignment or logging.

Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON) is present: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, the segmented data reveals the treatment lost on Mobile (1.73% vs 1.81%) and only won on Desktop. The aggregate win is driven by the uneven device mix between arms, not a true effect.

The test ran only 18 days of a planned 28, but since p=0.35 (not significant), calling it early didn't create a false positive win, so PEEK is not the primary blocker compared to SRM. However, SRM invalidates the randomization entirely.
