The readout reports a 4.0% relative lift, but the raw counts yield a different result. Control conversion is 7,871/248,932 ≈ 3.162%; treatment is 8,197/247,422 ≈ 3.313%. The actual relative lift is (3.313 - 3.162) / 3.162 ≈ 4.78%, not 4.0%. Furthermore, the baseline used for power calculations (4.0%) does not match the observed control rate (3.16%), indicating the study may be underpowered for the true baseline or the target lift was miscalculated.

However, the most critical error is DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominators are sessions. A user with multiple sessions inflates the sample size artificially and violates the unit of randomization, invalidating the p-value. Additionally, the test ran 22 days against a 21-day plan, suggesting potential PEEKING if the extra day was added to chase significance, though the primary fatal flaw is the denominator mismatch.

ERRORS: DENOM, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
