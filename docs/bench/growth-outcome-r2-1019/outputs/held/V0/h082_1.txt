The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~81k total sessions, a 50/50 split should be ~40.5k per arm. The observed 42,740 vs 38,525 is a deviation far exceeding chance, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions; if user session counts differ between arms, this biases the conversion rate. Third, **SIMPSON**: the pooled data shows treatment winning (5.1% vs 4.4%), yet within both Desktop (9.8% vs 10.3%) and Mobile (3.1% vs 3.0%), the control actually performs better or the math implies a reversal driven by mix shifts. Finally, **NOVELTY**: daily lift decays from 32% to 5.6%, yet the team projects the current inflated rate for 12 months.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
