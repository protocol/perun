The analysis contains multiple critical errors. First, the metric denominator is sessions, but randomization occurred by user ID (DENOM), violating the unit of analysis. Second, the test ran for 15 days against a 14-day plan, and the daily lift shows a clear fading trend (9.5% to 2.0%), yet the team projects this transient lift for twelve months (NOVELTY). While the arm sizes differ slightly (49.6% vs 50.4%), this is within chance bounds, so SRM is not flagged. The result is not statistically significant (p=0.12), and the design flaws invalidate the projection.
