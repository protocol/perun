The readout shows a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~95k total sessions, the expected split is ~47.8k per arm. The observed split (48,067 vs 47,606) deviates by 461 users. The standard deviation for a 50/50 split at this size is ~154; the deviation is ~3 standard deviations away, indicating broken randomization or logging.

Additionally, Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON) is present. The pooled data suggests a treatment lift (8.25% vs 8.0%). However, looking at segments: Desktop conversion is ~13.6% (control) vs ~13.0% (treatment) — a loss. Mobile conversion is ~4.3% (control) vs ~4.1% (treatment) — also a loss. The treatment loses in every segment but wins in aggregate due to shifting traffic mix toward the higher-converting desktop group in the treatment arm.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
