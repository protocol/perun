SRM: The arm sizes (55,196 vs 54,544) differ by ~1.2%. With ~110k total users, the standard error for a 50/50 split is ~0.15%. A 1.2% deviation is ~8 standard errors, indicating a significant Sample Ratio Mismatch.

PEEK: The readout includes "day 15" data, but the planned duration was 14 days (ending April 11). Reading on day 15 violates the pre-committed horizon.

DENOM: Users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominators are "sessions." If user session counts differ between arms, this biases the conversion rate.

NOVELTY: The daily lift fluctuates (3.5% to 4.8%) without a clear fading trend; the projection assumes persistence, but the data doesn't show fading, so NOVELTY is not strictly evidenced by fading, though the projection is risky given other errors. However, strict fading isn't shown.
