The readout contains two critical errors. First, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. If user session counts differ between arms, this biases the conversion rate. Second, **NOVELTY**: the daily lift shows a clear fading trend (dropping from ~5.6% early on to ~3.9% mid-test) before recovering slightly, yet the team projects this volatile, potentially novelty-driven lift flatly for twelve months. The sample sizes are sufficient for power, the split is roughly 50/50 (no SRM), and the aggregated data does not show Simpson's paradox (both segments show positive lift).

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
