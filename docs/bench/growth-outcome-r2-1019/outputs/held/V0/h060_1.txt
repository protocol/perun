The readout contains critical flaws. First, the arm sizes (42,370 vs 39,780) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM), which invalidates the randomization assumption. Second, the reported metric uses "sessions" as the denominator, but users were randomized by user ID; if users generated multiple sessions, this violates the unit of randomization (DENOM), risking pseudoreplication. While the test ran for the planned 14 days (avoiding PEEK), the SRM and denominator mismatch make the p-value and lift unreliable. The daily lift shows some fluctuation but no clear fading trend to confirm NOVELTY, and segment data does not show a reversal (SIMPSON). Due to the broken randomization signal and incorrect denominator, the result is unverifiable.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
