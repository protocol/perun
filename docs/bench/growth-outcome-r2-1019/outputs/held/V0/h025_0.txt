The arm sizes (219,984 vs 206,839) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Although the test ran for the planned 28 days (avoiding PEEK), the SRM and DENOM errors invalidate the reported p-value and lift. The daily lift shows variance but no clear fading trend to trigger NOVELTY. Segments do not show reversal (no SIMPSON). Due to broken assignment/logging and incorrect denominators, the results are unreliable for a rollout decision.
