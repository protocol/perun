The readout contains multiple analysis errors. First, **PEEK**: the test ran 15 days against a 14-day plan, and the daily lift shows a clear fading trend (9.5% to 2.0%), yet the team projects this transient lift for twelve months (**NOVELTY**). Second, **DENOM**: randomization was by *user id*, but the metric uses *sessions* as the denominator; if users generate multiple sessions, this violates the unit of analysis. Third, **SIMPSON**: aggregating sessions hides that desktop conversion actually *dropped* in treatment (9.21% vs 9.66%), while mobile rose; the pooled "win" is driven by mix shifts, not universal improvement. Finally, the result is not statistically significant (p=0.1232), yet a rollout is recommended.

ERRORS: PEEK, NOVELTY, DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
