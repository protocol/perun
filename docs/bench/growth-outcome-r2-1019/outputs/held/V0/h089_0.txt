The arm sizes (152,495 vs 143,030) deviate significantly from the 50/50 split, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). The metric denominator is "sessions," but randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). Desktop and mobile segments show opposite effects (desktop favors treatment; mobile favors control), yet the pooled result hides this reversal (SIMPSON). The test ran exactly to the planned date (May 26 to June 10 is 15 days inclusive), so PEEK is not flagged based on dates alone, though the daily lift shows no clear fading trend to confirm NOVELTY. Power cannot be definitively calculated without variance, but the sample size appears large.
