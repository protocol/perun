SRM: The arm sizes (98,326 vs 102,397) differ by ~4.1%. With ~200k total users, the standard error for a 50/50 split is ~0.35%. A 4.1% deviation is >10 standard deviations, indicating a broken assignment or logging mechanism.

NOVELTY: The daily lift fluctuates significantly (5.9% to 9.7%) without a clear fading trend, but the projection assumes the reported average lift persists for twelve months without accounting for potential novelty effects or seasonality, which is an unjustified extrapolation given the volatility.

POWER: The sample size is sufficient for the target lift given the baseline and alpha.

PEEK: The readout occurred on day 23 of a 21-day planned test. While slightly overdue, it does not constitute early stopping.

SIMPSON: Aggregating desktop and mobile user counts roughly matches the total sessions; no reversal is evident in the provided segment data.

DENOM: Randomization was by user ID, but the metric uses sessions. If users have multiple sessions, this violates the unit of randomization.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
