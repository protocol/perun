The readout contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: the arms are 48.2% control and 51.8% treatment. With ~770k total sessions, a 3.6% deviation from 50/50 is statistically impossible by chance, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since users generate varying session counts, this violates the unit of randomization, biasing the variance and p-value. The reported p=0.0 is therefore invalid. While daily lift appears stable (no NOVELTY) and segment trends match the aggregate (no SIMPSON), the foundational data integrity issues prevent a valid decision.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
