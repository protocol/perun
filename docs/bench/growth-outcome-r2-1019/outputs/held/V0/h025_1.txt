SRM: The split is 51.5% control vs 48.5% treatment. For ~427k users, a 1.5% deviation exceeds chance (p<0.001), indicating broken randomization or logging.
DENOM: Users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Multiple sessions per user violate the independence assumption, inflating false positives.
PEEK: The readout date (Oct 21) is 29 days post-start, exceeding the planned 28-day duration, suggesting an uncommitted extension or peek.
SIMPSON: Calculating segment rates: Desktop (Control 8.42%, Treat 8.77%); Mobile (Control 3.15%, Treat 3.28%). Both segments show positive lift consistent with the pooled result; no reversal exists.
POWER: With ~200k per arm, the test is sufficiently powered for a 5% lift on a 5% baseline.
NOVELTY: Daily lift fluctuates (3.0%–5.2%) without a clear fading trend; projecting 12 months is risky but not strictly a novelty error based on the data provided.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
