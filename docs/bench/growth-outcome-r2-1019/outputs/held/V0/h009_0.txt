The readout contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~341k total sessions, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly ±0.17%. The observed split (48.9% control vs 51.1% treatment) deviates by ~1.1%, far exceeding chance, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is *sessions*. Since users may generate different session counts across arms, this violates the unit of randomization, biasing the conversion rate.

While the lift appears stable (no clear NOVELTY fade) and segment math roughly aligns (no obvious SIMPSON reversal visible without precise rates), the fundamental data integrity issues (SRM) and unit mismatch (DENOM) invalidate the p-value and lift calculation.
