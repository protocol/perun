The readout shows a Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~206k total sessions, the expected split is ~103k per arm. The treatment arm has 100,649 sessions, a deviation of ~2.4k. The standard deviation for a 50/50 split is ~227; the observed difference is ~10.6 standard deviations away, which is impossible by chance, indicating broken assignment or logging.

Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions" while randomization was by "user id." If users generated different session counts across arms, this violates the unit of analysis (DENOM), biasing the conversion rate.

Because the randomization integrity is compromised (SRM) and the analysis unit mismatches the randomization unit (DENOM), the reported p-value and lift are invalid. The projection to twelve months is also unsupported given these foundational errors.
