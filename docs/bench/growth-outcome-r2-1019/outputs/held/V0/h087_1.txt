The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since session counts per user can vary, this violates the unit of randomization and biases variance estimates. Second, **SIMPSON**: while the pooled treatment lift is positive (+3.0%), the segmented data shows treatment underperforming control on mobile (2.93% vs 3.06%) and desktop (10.26% vs 10.73%). The aggregate gain is an artifact of the treatment arm having a higher proportion of high-converting desktop users, not a real effect.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
