The test stopped on day 18 of a planned 28-day duration because p < 0.05, which is **PEEK**ing; this invalidates the significance claim. The unit randomized was "user id," but the metric denominator is "sessions," creating a **DENOM** mismatch (users ≠ sessions). Additionally, projecting an 18-day lift over twelve months ignores potential **NOVELTY** effects, as early lifts often fade. The sample sizes are sufficient for power, the split is acceptably close to 50/50, and device segments do not reverse the pooled trend.

ERRORS: PEEK, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
