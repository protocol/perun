The readout contains two clear errors. First, **SRM**: with ~326k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~163k per arm. The observed difference (167,270 vs 159,241) is ~8k, far exceeding chance limits (~600 for p<0.05), indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is *sessions*. Since users generate varying session counts, this violates the unit of randomization, biasing the variance and p-value. While daily lift fluctuates, it does not show a clear fading trend required for **NOVELTY**. Segments (desktop/mobile) both show positive lift, ruling out **SIMPSON**. The test ran the full 14 days, so **PEEK** is absent. Given the massive sample, **POWER** is sufficient.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
