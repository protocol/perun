The arm sizes (277,127 vs 271,361) differ by ~2.1%. With ~550k total users, the standard error for a 50/50 split is ~0.07%; a 2.1% deviation is ~30 standard errors, indicating severe Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). This suggests broken randomization or logging, invalidating the p-value.

Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions," but users were randomized by "user id." If users generate multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption (DENOM), as sessions within a user are correlated. The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to confirm NOVELTY. Segments (desktop/mobile) both show positive lift, so no SIMPSON. Power is sufficient given the large N.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
