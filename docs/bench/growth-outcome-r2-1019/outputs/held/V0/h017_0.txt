The readout shows two critical errors. First, SRM: with ~123k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~61,500 per arm. The observed 63,084 vs 59,926 differs by 3,158. The standard deviation is ~175; this gap is ~18 sigmas away, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. If users generate different session counts across arms, this biases the conversion rate. Additionally, PEEK applies: the test ran 22 days against a 21-day plan, suggesting the end date was chosen based on results rather than pre-commitment. Due to SRM invalidating the causal claim, the rollout cannot proceed.
