SRM: Control has 125,950 sessions vs. treatment 121,801 (48.4% split). A 4,149 difference on ~248k total far exceeds chance for 50/50 randomization, indicating broken assignment or logging.

PEEK: Planned duration was 21 days; final readout occurred on day 22 (Oct 11 minus Sept 19). While technically one day late, the daily lift chart shows a clear fading trend (11.8% → ~3%), yet the team projects this transient lift for twelve months. This is NOVELTY, not strictly PEEK based on the date alone, but the fading lift invalidates the projection.

POWER: With ~120k per arm, the test is vastly overpowered for a 5% lift detection, not underpowered.

SIMPSON: Desktop lift: (2590/36837) / (2443/36874) - 1 ≈ 6.0%. Mobile lift: (1362/56254) / (1285/56311) - 1 ≈ 5.9%. Both segments show positive lift consistent with the aggregate; no reversal.

DENOM: Randomized by user ID, metric uses sessions. If users have multiple sessions, this mismatches the randomization unit. However, without user-level session counts, we assume sessions proxy users reasonably here, though it's a minor risk. The SRM is the critical failure.

ERRORS: SRM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
