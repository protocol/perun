DENOM is flagged: randomization was by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions, violating the unit of analysis. PEEK is not flagged as the readout occurred on the planned end date. SIMPSON is not flagged; while device splits are uneven, the direction of effect does not reverse in segments. NOVELTY is not flagged; daily lift is stable, not fading.

Because SRM and DENOM invalidate the causal claim, the rollout cannot proceed.
