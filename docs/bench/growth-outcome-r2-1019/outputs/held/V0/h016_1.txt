The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. A user with multiple sessions inflates the sample size artificially, violating the unit of randomization and biasing variance estimates. Second, **NOVELTY**: the daily lift shows a clear fading trend (starting ~4-5%, dipping to 3.0% near the end), yet the team projects this transient lift over twelve months. While **PEEK** is suspected due to the readout date (day 21 of a 21-day plan) potentially indicating early stopping if significance was hit earlier, the p-value (0.20) suggests they waited, so PEEK is not definitively flagged by the numbers alone. However, DENOM and NOVELTY are sufficient to halt the decision.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
