The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: the 50/50 split is violated (90,690 vs 88,081 sessions). A chi-square test confirms this deviation is statistically significant, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions. Since users generate varying session counts, this violates the unit of analysis, biasing variance estimates.

While **PEEK** is suspected (reading on day 15 of a 14-day plan), the SRM and denominator errors alone invalidate the result regardless of timing. The projected lift is unreliable because the groups are not comparable and the metric does not match the randomization unit.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
