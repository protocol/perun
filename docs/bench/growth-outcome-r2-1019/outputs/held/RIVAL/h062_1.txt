The pooled result shows a 3.1% lift, but segment data reveals Simpson's Paradox. Desktop favors treatment (4.35% vs 4.59%), yet mobile heavily favors control (1.66% vs 1.59%). Because mobile has higher volume in the control arm, it drags down the control average, creating a false aggregate lift. The treatment actually performs worse on mobile users. You cannot roll out a change that harms a major segment based on a distorted aggregate number. Investigate the mobile experience before proceeding.
