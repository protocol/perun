ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD

The randomization unit is user ID, but the arm sizes and order counts are reported in sessions, making the conversion rate denominator inconsistent with the randomized unit (DENOM). Additionally, the test ran for 30 days against a planned 28-day duration, indicating the analysis occurred after the pre-specified stopping rule, which invalidates the p-value due to optional stopping (PEEK). The daily lift shows no clear fading trend, ruling out NOVELTY. Segment math confirms no reversal, ruling out SIMPSON. The sample size is sufficient, ruling out POWER. SRM cannot be definitively flagged without user-level counts, though the session imbalance is notable. Because the metric definition and stopping rule are compromised, the statistical validity is unknown.
