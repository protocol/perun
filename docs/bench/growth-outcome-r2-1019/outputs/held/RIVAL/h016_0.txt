The analysis contains two critical errors. First, the unit of randomization is the user, but the metric denominator is sessions (DENOM). Since users can generate multiple sessions, this violates the independence assumption required for the t-test, invalidating the p-value. Second, the test ran for 21 days against a plan, but the readout provides no evidence of early stopping; however, the daily lift shows no fading trend, ruling out NOVELTY. The sample sizes are large enough to detect the target lift, ruling out POWER. The arm sizes (30,609 vs 31,994) differ slightly but likely within chance for this volume, so SRM is not clearly flagged without a formal test, though the DENOM error is definitive. Because the metric denominator does not match the randomization unit, the statistical result is unreliable.

ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
