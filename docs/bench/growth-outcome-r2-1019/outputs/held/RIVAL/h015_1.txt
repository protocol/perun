The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **DENOM**: Users were randomized by user ID, but the metrics use sessions in the denominator. If users generate different session counts across arms, this biases the conversion rate. Second, **NOVELTY**: The test ran exactly 14 days (March 29 to April 13), yet the readout presents 15 days of daily lift data and projects this short-term lift over twelve months without evidence of sustained impact. The p-value (0.1851) correctly indicates the result is not statistically significant, but the structural errors in measurement and projection invalidate the rollout recommendation.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
