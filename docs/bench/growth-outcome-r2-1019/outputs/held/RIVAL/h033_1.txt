The analysis contains critical errors preventing a rollout decision.

First, **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch): With ~785k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~392.5k per arm. The observed split (388,466 vs 397,215) differs by ~8,750 users. The standard error is ~445; the deviation is ~19.6 standard errors, indicating broken randomization or logging.

Second, **DENOM** (Denominator Error): The metric is "order conversion rate," but the denominators are "sessions" while randomization was by "user id." Sessions are not the randomized unit, invalidating the statistical test.

Third, **PEEK**: The test ran 18 days of a planned 28. Calling significance at p=0.0011 before the planned duration inflates false positive risk.

Fourth, **NOVELTY**: Daily lift fluctuates without a clear fading trend in the provided data, but projecting 18 days of data to 12 months assumes permanence without evidence of stability.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
