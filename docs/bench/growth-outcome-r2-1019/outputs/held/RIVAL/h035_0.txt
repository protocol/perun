The readout contains critical analysis errors preventing a rollout decision.

First, **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present. With 129,561 total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~64,780 per arm. The observed split (66,308 vs 63,253) deviates by over 3,000 users. A chi-square test confirms this imbalance is statistically significant (p < 0.001), indicating broken randomization or logging.

Second, **SIMPSON** (Simpson's Paradox) is evident. While the pooled data shows a treatment lift, the segmented data reveals the opposite:
- Desktop: Control 8.83% vs Treatment 8.44% (Treatment loses)
- Mobile: Control 3.41% vs Treatment 3.26% (Treatment loses)
The treatment underperforms in every segment yet appears to win in aggregate due to the skewed traffic distribution (more high-converting desktop users in the treatment arm).

Additionally, **DENOM** applies because the metric uses "sessions" while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
