DENOM: The metric denominator is "sessions," but users were randomized by "user id." This mismatch violates the unit of randomization.

SIMPSON: Desktop conversion favors treatment (6.85% vs 6.36%), and mobile favors treatment (2.40% vs 2.23%). The pooled result is not reversed within segments; Simpson's paradox is not present.

PEEK: The test ran for 23 days against a 21-day plan. While slightly over, the readout does not explicitly state it was stopped early due to peeking, though the SRM is the primary structural failure.

POWER: With ~100k sessions per arm, the test is adequately powered for the stated parameters.

NOVELTY: Daily lift fluctuates (5.9% to 9.7%) but shows no clear fading trend over the 23 days.
