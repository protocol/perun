ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains two critical errors. First, SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch): with ~178k total sessions and a 50/50 plan, the observed split (90,690 vs 88,081) deviates significantly from expected (~89k each), suggesting assignment or logging issues. Second, DENOM (Denominator Error): users were randomized by user ID, but the analysis uses sessions in the arms and device breakdowns, violating the unit of randomization. Additionally, the result is not statistically significant (p=0.1419 > 0.05), yet the team recommends rollout. The fading daily lift is minimal and stable, not indicating a novelty effect requiring a hold on its own, but the structural data integrity issues (SRM, DENOM) and lack of significance mandate holding the decision.
