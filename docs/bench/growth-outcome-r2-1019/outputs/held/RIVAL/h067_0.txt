The readout contains multiple critical errors. SRM: The 50/50 split failed (224k vs 197k sessions); this deviation is too large for chance, indicating broken randomization. PEEK: The test stopped at 14 days against a 21-day plan. POWER: The design targeted a 5% lift, but the sample size is insufficient to detect such a small effect with 80% power; the observed 23% lift suggests the test was likely underpowered for its stated goal or the baseline is wrong. DENOM: Randomization was by user ID, but results are reported in sessions, violating the unit of analysis. These issues invalidate the statistical claims.
