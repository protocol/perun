The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (46,889 vs 43,569) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: users were randomized, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization. These issues invalidate the statistical significance and business case for rollout.
