The readout exhibits a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With 138,313 total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~69,157 per arm. The treatment has 69,731 (+574) and control has 68,582 (-575). A chi-square test yields $\chi^2 \approx 9.6$ (p < 0.002), indicating the deviation is statistically significant and likely due to assignment or logging bias, not chance.

Additionally, Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON) is present. While the pooled data shows Treatment > Control (4.12% vs 4.00%), the desktop segment shows Control (6.05%) > Treatment (5.79%), and the mobile segment shows Control (2.11%) > Treatment (2.02%). The aggregate result reverses the direction of the effect seen in every segment, driven by the uneven distribution of high-converting desktop users favoring the treatment arm.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
