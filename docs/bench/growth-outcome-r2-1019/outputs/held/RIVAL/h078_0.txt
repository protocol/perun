ERRORS: SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD

The pooled data shows Treatment winning (6.18% vs 5.99%), but stratification reveals Simpson's Paradox: Treatment loses on Desktop (8.82% vs 9.24%) and loses on Mobile (2.82% vs 2.95%). The aggregate reversal is driven by a severe imbalance in device mix (Treatment has far more high-converting Desktop users), not a true treatment effect. Since the randomization by user ID resulted in skewed segment allocation across arms, the aggregate lift is an artifact of composition, not causality. Do not roll out; investigate the assignment mechanism or re-randomize to ensure balanced segments.
