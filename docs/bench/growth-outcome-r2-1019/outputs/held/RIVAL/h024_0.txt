ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains two critical errors. First, SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch): with ~326k total sessions, a 50/50 split expects ~163k per arm. The observed difference (167,270 vs 159,241) is ~8k users, far exceeding chance limits for this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM (Denominator Error): users were randomized by user ID, but the arms report "sessions" and orders are divided by sessions to calculate rates. This mismatches the unit of randomization. Additionally, daily lift shows no fading trend (fluctuating 5.6–9.1%), so NOVELTY is not flagged. Due to SRM and DENOM, the results are invalid for decision-making.
