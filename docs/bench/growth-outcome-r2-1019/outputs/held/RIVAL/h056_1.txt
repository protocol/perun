ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (144,938 vs 142,174) differ significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the metric divides orders by sessions; since users generate multiple sessions, the denominator does not match the randomized unit. Third, NOVELTY: daily lift fades from 17.5% to 3.3% by day 16, yet the team projects the early average as a permanent twelve-month gain. These issues invalidate the statistical claim and the business projection. Do not roll out until assignment is fixed, metrics align with randomization units, and projections account for decay.
