ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (152,495 vs 143,030) deviate significantly from the 50/50 randomization plan, indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, SIMPSON: while the pooled data favors treatment, the segmented data shows treatment winning on desktop but losing on mobile; the aggregate result is reversed within segments due to imbalanced traffic distribution. Third, DENOM: users were randomized, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization. Additionally, the test ran 15 days against a 14-day plan (PEEK risk), and the projection assumes sustained lift despite daily fluctuation. Do not roll out until assignment integrity is verified, analysis is re-run at the user level, and segment discrepancies are resolved.
