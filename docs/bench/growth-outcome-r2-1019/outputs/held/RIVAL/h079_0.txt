ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The arm sizes (59,559 vs 64,680) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON) is present: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, both desktop (13.3% vs 12.7%) and mobile (4.8% vs 4.5%) segments show the control outperforming treatment. Additionally, the daily lift trends downward from 5.9% to ~1.3%, yet the projection assumes this fading effect persists (NOVELTY error). These fundamental analysis flaws invalidate the reported result and the rollout recommendation.
