DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains four critical errors. First, **SRM**: the arm sizes (90,690 vs 88,081) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **PEEK**: the test ran for 15 days against a planned 14-day duration, suggesting unplanned extension or peeking. Third, **DENOM**: randomization was by user ID, but the metric denominator is sessions, violating the unit of analysis. Fourth, **NOVELTY**: daily lift fluctuates without a clear fade, yet the team projects this transient result as a permanent 12-month gain without justification. These issues invalidate the statistical conclusion and the rollout recommendation.
