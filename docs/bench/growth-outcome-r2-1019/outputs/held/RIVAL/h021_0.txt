ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (122,949 vs 128,468) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: the metric divides orders by sessions, but users were randomized by user ID; this mismatches the denominator to the unit of randomization. Third, NOVELTY: while daily lift data is provided, the team projects this temporary lift over twelve months without accounting for potential decay or novelty effects. These errors invalidate the statistical significance and business case for the rollout.
