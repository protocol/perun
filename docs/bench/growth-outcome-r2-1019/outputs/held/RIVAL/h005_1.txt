ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains two critical errors. First, SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch): with ~771k total sessions, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly ±0.11%. The observed split (48.1% control vs. 51.9% treatment) deviates by nearly 2%, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator. Since users can generate multiple sessions, this violates the unit of randomization, invalidating the p-value. Additionally, the reported p=0.0 is impossible; with these counts, p would be extremely small but non-zero. Do not ship until assignment integrity is verified and metrics are recalculated per user.
