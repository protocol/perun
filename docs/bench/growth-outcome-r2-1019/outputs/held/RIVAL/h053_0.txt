The readout shows a clear Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). With ~243k total users, a 50/50 split expects ~121.5k per arm. The observed split (119,330 vs 123,600) deviates by ~2.1%, which exceeds the threshold for chance variation at this scale, indicating broken assignment or logging.

Additionally, Simpson's Paradox (SIMPSON) is present. While the pooled data shows a treatment lift, the desktop segment shows a ~2.6% lift, whereas the mobile segment shows a significant negative lift (~-13.5% relative to control). The aggregate positive result is driven entirely by desktop, masking severe mobile degradation.

The test also stopped early (PEEK) on day 18 of a planned 28-day duration, inflating false positive risk.
