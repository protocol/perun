ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (200,821 vs 212,099) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: the metric divides orders by sessions, but users were randomized; session-level denominators violate the unit of randomization. Third, NOVELTY: daily lift fades from 8.9% to 1.2% by day 16, yet the team projects this transient effect over twelve months. These flaws invalidate the reported p-value and business case. Fix the assignment logic, re-aggregate by user, and reassess whether the fading trend represents a genuine novelty effect before considering rollout.
