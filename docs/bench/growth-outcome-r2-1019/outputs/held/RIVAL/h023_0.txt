ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, POWER, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized, but arm sizes are reported in "sessions," mismatching the unit of randomization. Second, SRM: the session split (90,377 vs 88,218) deviates significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken assignment or logging. Third, PEEK: the test ran 23 days against a 21-day plan, suggesting the duration was extended based on interim results. Fourth, POWER: with ~88k users per arm, the test is underpowered to detect the target 8% lift (requires ~150k/variant for a 5% baseline), yet the team is acting on a non-significant 4.8% lift (p=0.0542). These issues invalidate the result.
