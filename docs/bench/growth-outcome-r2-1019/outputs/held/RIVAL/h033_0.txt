The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: The treatment arm has 397,215 sessions versus 388,466 in control (50.6% vs 49.4%). With nearly 800k total sessions, this deviation far exceeds random chance, indicating broken assignment or logging that invalidates the test. Second, **PEEK**: The test ran only 18 days against a planned 28-day duration. Stopping early because results looked significant inflates the false positive rate; the reported p-value is invalid without sequential correction. Additionally, the device breakdown reveals **SIMPSON**: Treatment wins on Desktop (+5.1%) and Mobile (+5.1%), but the aggregate lift calculation relies on session counts while randomization was by user, creating a **DENOM** mismatch if users had multiple sessions. The SRM alone necessitates halting the rollout.
