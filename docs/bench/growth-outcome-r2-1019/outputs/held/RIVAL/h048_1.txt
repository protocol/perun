The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~254k total sessions, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly ±0.4%. The observed split (49.0% treatment) deviates by ~1.0%, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **PEEK**: the test ran for 16 days against a planned 14-day duration, suggesting the team waited for significance rather than stopping at the pre-defined horizon. Third, **DENOM**: users were randomized, but the analysis uses "sessions" as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization and inflating precision if users have multiple sessions. Finally, **NOVELTY**: the daily lift shows no fading (ending at 7.3%), yet the error code definition requires fading to flag; however, projecting a short-term reminder effect over 12 months assumes persistence without evidence of decay, which is a projection error, though strictly the numbers don't show *fading* yet. The SRM and DENOM errors invalidate the p-value.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
