The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **DENOM**: Users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and conversion metrics are reported in sessions. Since users can generate multiple sessions, the denominator does not match the randomization unit, violating the statistical assumptions of the test. Second, **NOVELTY**: The daily lift data shows no fading trend (fluctuating between 3.8% and 7.3% with no downward slope), yet the team projects this short-term lift over twelve months without accounting for potential novelty effects or habituation, which is an unjustified extrapolation.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
