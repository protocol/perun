The analysis contains critical errors preventing a rollout decision.

First, **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch) is present. With ~760k total users, the expected split is ~380k per arm. While the total sessions appear close (380,947 vs 378,709), the device-level breakdown reveals a severe imbalance: Desktop is skewed heavily to treatment (117k vs 139k), while Mobile is skewed heavily to control (140k vs 119k). This indicates broken randomization or logging, invalidating the test.

Second, **SIMPSON** (Simpson's Paradox) is evident. The aggregated data shows a slight lift (~3.1%). However, looking at segments:
- Desktop CVR: Control 4.59%, Treatment 4.38% (Treatment loses)
- Mobile CVR: Control 1.66%, Treatment 1.59% (Treatment loses)
The treatment performs worse in both segments, yet appears better overall due to the skewed traffic allocation favoring the higher-converting desktop segment in the treatment arm.

ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD
