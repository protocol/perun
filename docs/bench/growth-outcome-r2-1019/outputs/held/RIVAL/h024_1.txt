The readout contains critical analysis errors. First, **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch): with ~326k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~163k per arm. The observed difference (167,270 vs 159,241) is ~8k users, far exceeding chance limits for this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM** (Denominator Error): the metric is "order conversion rate," but the arms report "sessions" while segments report "users." Since randomization was by user ID, the denominator must be users, not sessions. Mixing these units invalidates the rate. Third, **NOVELTY**: daily lift fluctuates without a clear fading trend, yet the projection assumes the 6.8% lift persists for 12 months without accounting for potential novelty decay or seasonality.
