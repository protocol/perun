The readout contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~445k total sessions, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly ±0.15%. The observed split (51.0% control vs. 49.0% treatment) deviates by ~1.0%, far exceeding chance, indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, **POWER**: the test was designed to detect a 5% relative lift but found only 4.1%. Since the observed effect is smaller than the minimum detectable effect, the study was underpowered to validate this specific result reliably, even with significance. Additionally, daily data shows no **NOVELTY** fade (lift remains stable), and segment math confirms no **SIMPSON** paradox (both devices show positive lift). However, the SRM invalidates the randomization assumption required for the p-value to hold.

ERRORS: SRM, POWER
DECISION: HOLD
