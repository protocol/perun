The readout contains two critical errors. First, DENOM: Users were randomized by user ID, but the analysis uses sessions in the denominator. Since users can have multiple sessions, this violates the unit of randomization and biases variance estimates. Second, NOVELTY: The daily lift shows clear volatility with a notable dip to 4.6% late in the test, yet the team projects the average 6.8% lift unchanged for twelve months, ignoring potential fade or learning effects.

While the arm sizes (42,581 vs 42,158) are slightly uneven, they are likely within chance expectations for this sample size, so SRM is not definitively flagged without a chi-square calculation. Segments (desktop/mobile) both show positive lift, ruling out SIMPSON.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
