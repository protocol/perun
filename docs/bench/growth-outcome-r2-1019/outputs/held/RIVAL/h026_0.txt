The readout contains two critical errors. First, **DENOM**: Users were randomized by user ID, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator. This violates the unit of randomization and biases variance estimates. Second, **NOVELTY**: The daily lift shows a clear decay trend (from 10.8% to 0.7% by day 23), yet the team projects the current average lift over twelve months. This ignores the fading effect, likely due to novelty, leading to an inflated revenue forecast.

While the sample sizes are large enough for power and roughly balanced (no SRM), and the test ran for its planned duration (no PEEK), the denominator mismatch and unrealistic projection invalidate the rollout recommendation.

ERRORS: DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
