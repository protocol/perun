The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm split is 48%/52% (59,559 vs 64,680), deviating significantly from the planned 50/50 randomization, indicating broken assignment. Second, SIMPSON: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, both segments show the opposite: Desktop lift is +6.6% and Mobile lift is -6.1%. Third, NOVELTY: daily lift decays from 5.9% to ~1.3%, yet the team projects this fading effect over twelve months. Do not roll out; fix randomization and re-analyze segments.
