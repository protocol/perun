Second, **SIMPSON**: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, both desktop (8.8% vs 8.4%) and mobile (3.4% vs 3.3%) segments individually show the control performing better than treatment. Third, **PEEK**: the test ran 22 days against a 21-day plan, suggesting potential early stopping or extension based on results.
