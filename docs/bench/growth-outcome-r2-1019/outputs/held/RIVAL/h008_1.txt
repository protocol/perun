The readout contains two critical errors. First, SRM (Sample Ratio Mismatch): with ~221k total users, the expected split is ~110,500 per arm. The treatment arm has 107,406 users, a deviation of ~2,800. The standard deviation for a 50/50 split is ~235; this deviation is ~12 standard deviations away, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM (Denominator Error): the metric is "order conversion rate" (typically orders/users), but the data provides "sessions" for the totals while providing "users" for segments. If the rate divides orders by sessions, the denominator (sessions) is not the randomized unit (users). Additionally, daily data shows 23 days despite a 21-day plan, suggesting PEEK (early stopping/extension), but SRM and DENOM are definitive.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
