ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm split is 49.1% control vs. 50.9% treatment, a deviation too large for random chance given the sample size, indicating broken assignment. Second, DENOM: users were randomized, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization. Third, SIMPSON: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, both desktop (13.1% vs 12.5%) and mobile (4.0% vs 3.8%) segments actually show the control performing better when calculated correctly (orders/users). The aggregate result is mathematically reversed by the segment realities. Do not roll out.
