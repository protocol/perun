The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~382k total users, a 50/50 split expects ~191k per arm. The observed difference (193,505 vs 188,723) is 4,782 users, far exceeding the chance threshold (~617 for p<0.05), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms report "sessions" while segments report "users." Since session counts per user vary, using sessions as the denominator violates the unit of randomization, biasing the conversion rate. Additionally, the test stopped at 31 days against a 28-day plan, but without knowing the *planned* sample size vs. actual, **PEEK** cannot be confirmed solely from dates. The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to confirm **NOVELTY**.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
