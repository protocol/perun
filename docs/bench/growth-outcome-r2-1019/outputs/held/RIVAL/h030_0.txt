ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD

**SRM**: Planned 50/50 split observed 50.6% control vs 49.4% treatment. A difference of ~4,800 users on ~382k total is statistically significant (p < 0.001), indicating broken randomization or logging.
**DENOM**: Randomization was by **user id**, but the metric divides **orders** by **sessions**. Since users can have multiple sessions, the denominator does not match the unit of randomization, biasing the variance and p-value.
**PEEK**: The test stopped on Day 31 of a planned 28-day duration. Extending the test because results looked good (or stopping early to check) invalidates the fixed-horizon alpha control.

Do not ship until assignment integrity is verified, the metric is recalculated per user, and the analysis plan is respected.
