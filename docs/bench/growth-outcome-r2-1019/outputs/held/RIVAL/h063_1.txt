ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the arms report "sessions," making sessions the denominator instead of users. Second, PEEK: the test ran only 18 days against a planned 28-day duration before being called. Third, NOVELTY: the team projects the current lift over twelve months despite no evidence of long-term persistence and potential novelty effects. These issues invalidate the statistical conclusion and the business projection.
