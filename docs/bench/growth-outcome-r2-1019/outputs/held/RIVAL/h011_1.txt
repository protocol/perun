ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout reports conversion rates using "sessions" as the denominator while stating users were randomized by user ID. This violates the unit of randomization; multiple sessions per user are not independent, inflating the sample size and deflating the p-value. The reported p=0.0002 is likely invalid without session-to-user correction. Additionally, daily lift data is provided for 30 days, but the planned duration was 28 days, suggesting analysis occurred after the planned window (PEEK), though the primary fatal flaw is the denominator mismatch. Do not ship until metrics are recalculated per unique user.
