ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (152,495 vs 143,030) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, SIMPSON: while the pooled data favors treatment, the segmented data shows treatment winning on desktop but losing on mobile; the aggregate result is driven by unequal segment sizes, not a universal effect. Third, DENOM: users were randomized, but the analysis uses "sessions" as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization. Additionally, the test ran 15 days against a 14-day plan, suggesting potential PEEKing. These issues invalidate the reported lift and projection.
