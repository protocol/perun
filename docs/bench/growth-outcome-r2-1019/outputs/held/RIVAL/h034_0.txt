The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, **SRM**: the arm sizes (393k vs 371k) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken randomization. Second, **SIMPSON**: while the pooled treatment rate appears higher, both desktop (4.96% vs 5.56%) and mobile (1.62% vs 1.55%) segments show the treatment performing worse than control in one case and better in another, but the aggregate is misleading due to the skewed traffic distribution across devices. Third, **PEEK**: the test ran for 29 days against a 28-day plan, but more critically, the p-value (0.0564) exceeds the 0.05 threshold, yet a rollout is recommended. Fourth, **POWER**: with a baseline of 3% and target lift of 5%, the required sample size per arm is ~150k; while totals exceed this, the imbalance and lack of significance suggest the test is underpowered to detect the specific effect size reliably given the variance. Do not roll out.
