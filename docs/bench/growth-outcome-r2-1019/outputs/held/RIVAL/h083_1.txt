Simpson's Paradox is present: while the pooled data shows a positive lift (8.24% vs 8.00%), both segments show negative lifts. Desktop: 11.6% (treatment) vs 12.2% (control). Mobile: 3.7% (treatment) vs 3.9% (control). The aggregate result contradicts the stratified results due to unequal segment weighting.

Novelty effect is evident: daily lift decays consistently from 5.9% on day 1 to 0.9% on day 14. Projecting the current average lift over twelve months ignores this fading trend, likely overestimating long-term value.

The recommendation to roll out is unsupported because the underlying effect is negative in all segments and diminishing over time.
