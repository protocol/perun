ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (122,558 vs 111,224) deviate significantly from the 50/50 randomization plan, indicating broken assignment or logging. Second, SIMPSON: while the pooled data shows treatment winning, segment-level analysis reveals the opposite. Desktop conversion is higher in treatment (12.1% vs 12.7%), but mobile conversion is drastically lower in treatment (4.3% vs 4.5%), yet the aggregate suggests a win due to uneven segment distribution. Third, DENOM: users were randomized, but the primary metric uses "sessions" as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization. Additionally, the test ran only 14 days against a planned 21-day duration (PEEK). Do not roll out; fix data integrity and re-analyze.
