ERRORS: DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout reports conversion rates using "sessions" as the denominator (e.g., control: 1,948/42,581 = 4.58%, not the reported 5.999%). However, users were randomized by user ID, not session. Using sessions violates the unit of randomization, inflating precision and risking false positives (DENOM). Additionally, the reported percentages (5.999%, 6.405%) do not match the raw counts provided, suggesting a calculation error or mismatched data source. Without correcting the denominator to match the randomized unit (users), the statistical validity is compromised. The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to trigger NOVELTY, and arm sizes are balanced enough to avoid SRM. Fix the metric definition before rolling out.
