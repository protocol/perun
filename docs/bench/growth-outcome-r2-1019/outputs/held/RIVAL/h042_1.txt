ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (200,821 vs 212,099) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: users were randomized, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization. Third, NOVELTY: daily lift fades from 8.9% to 1.2% by day 16, yet the team projects this transient effect over twelve months. These flaws invalidate the statistical significance and business case for rollout.
