The analysis contains critical errors preventing a rollout decision.

First, **SRM** (Sample Ratio Mismatch): With ~283k total sessions, a 50/50 split should yield ~141.5k per arm. The observed split (145,998 vs 137,604) deviates by ~4,000 users. A chi-square test confirms this imbalance is statistically impossible by chance alone, indicating broken randomization or logging.

Second, **DENOM** (Denominator Error): The metric is "order conversion rate" (typically orders/users), but the data provides "sessions" in the arms while breaking down by "users" in segments. You cannot calculate a valid user-level conversion rate using session counts as the denominator.

Third, **PEEK**: The p-value is 0.0659 (not significant at α=0.05), yet the team recommends rolling out. This implies stopping or deciding based on uncommitted results rather than waiting for significance or accepting the null.
