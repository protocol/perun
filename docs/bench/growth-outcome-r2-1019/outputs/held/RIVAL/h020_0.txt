ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD

The readout reports "order conversion rate" but divides orders by sessions while randomization occurred by user ID; a single user can generate multiple sessions, violating the randomized unit (DENOM). Additionally, the test ran for 22 days against a planned 21-day duration, indicating the team peeked at results and extended the test rather than stopping at the pre-specified horizon (PEEK). While the arm sizes (248,932 vs 247,422) show a minor imbalance, it falls within acceptable statistical variance for this sample size, so SRM is not flagged. The daily lift shows fluctuation but no clear fading trend to trigger NOVELTY, and segment results align with the pooled data, ruling out SIMPSON. These methodological errors invalidate the p-value and require re-analysis before rollout.
