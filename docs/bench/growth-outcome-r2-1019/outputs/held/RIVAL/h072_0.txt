ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, **SRM**: the arm sizes (79,206 vs 74,854) differ significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: the metric divides orders by *sessions*, but users were randomized by *user ID*; this mismatches the denominator to the unit of randomization. Third, **NOVELTY**: the daily lift clearly fades from ~12% to ~3% over the 28 days, yet the team projects the average lift as lasting for twelve months. These issues invalidate the statistical significance and the business projection. Do not roll out until assignment logic is fixed, metrics are recalculated per user, and projections account for the decaying effect.
