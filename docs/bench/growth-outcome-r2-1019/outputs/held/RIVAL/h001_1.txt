The readout contains two critical errors. First, **DENOM**: Users were randomized by user ID, but the arm sizes and conversion rates are calculated using sessions. Since users can have multiple sessions, the denominator does not match the randomized unit, violating the experimental design. Second, **SRM**: The split is 60,235 vs 61,545 sessions (49.5% vs 50.5%). With ~122k total sessions, the standard error for a 50/50 split is ~0.14%. The observed 0.5% deviation is over 3.5 standard errors away, indicating a statistically significant sample ratio mismatch likely caused by the session-vs-user counting error or logging issues.

ERRORS: DENOM, SRM
DECISION: HOLD
