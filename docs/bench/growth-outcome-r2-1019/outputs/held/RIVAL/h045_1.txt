ERRORS: SIMPSON, PEEK, POWER
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, **SIMPSON**: While the pooled result shows a treatment lift, the treatment actually lost on both Desktop (13.0% vs 13.6%) and Mobile (4.1% vs 4.3%). Second, **PEEK**: The test ran for 23 days against a planned 21-day duration, indicating the team extended the test hoping for significance. Third, **POWER**: With ~48k users per arm and an 8% baseline, the test is underpowered to detect the target 8% lift (requiring ~150k/variant) or even the observed 3% lift. The non-significant p-value (0.21) confirms this. Do not roll out based on a directionally positive but statistically insignificant result that reverses in all segments.
