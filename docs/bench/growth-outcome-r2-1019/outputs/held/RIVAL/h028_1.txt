DECISION: HOLD

The readout reports conversion rates using "sessions" as the denominator (e.g., 1,967/97,605 ≈ 2.01%, not the reported 3.0%), while randomization occurred at the "user" level. This violates the unit of analysis (DENOM). Additionally, the reported p-value (0.0644) exceeds the alpha (0.05), meaning the result is not statistically significant; rolling out based on non-significant data is premature. The daily lift shows no fading trend (ruling out NOVELTY), and arm sizes are reasonably balanced given the total volume (ruling out SRM). The projection assumes lasting impact without accounting for the lack of statistical significance.
