The analysis contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~206k total users, the 95% confidence interval for a 50/50 split is roughly 49.5%–50.5%. The treatment arm is 48.7% (100,649/206,178), a deviation indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized, but the report uses "sessions" as the denominator for conversion rates. This mismatches the unit of randomization, invalidating the p-value. Additionally, the test stopped at day 21 (June 20) despite a planned 21-day duration starting May 30 (which requires ending June 19), suggesting potential **PEEK** if the decision to stop was data-driven, though the date discrepancy is minor. The SRM and DENOM errors alone invalidate the result.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
