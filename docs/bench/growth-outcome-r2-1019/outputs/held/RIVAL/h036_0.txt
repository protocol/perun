ERRORS: DENOM, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD

The readout exhibits two critical errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized by user ID, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator. This violates the unit of randomization and invalidates the p-value. Second, PEEK: the test ran for 14 days against a planned 21-day duration. Stopping early based on significance inflates the false positive rate. Additionally, while not a formal flag here, the daily lift fluctuates wildly (4.0% to 6.7%), making the 12-month projection highly unreliable without accounting for variance. Do not roll out until the analysis is re-run using unique users as the denominator and the full duration is completed or properly adjusted via sequential testing methods.
