The readout contains critical analysis errors. First, **DENOM**: Users were randomized by user ID, but the arms report "sessions" and conversion is calculated as orders/sessions. A single user can generate multiple sessions, violating the independence assumption of the randomized unit. Second, **SIMPSON**: The aggregate data shows a treatment lift (6.18% vs 5.99%), yet within both segments, the treatment performed worse (Desktop: 10.26% vs 10.73%; Mobile: 2.93% vs 3.06%). This reversal indicates severe segmentation bias or data integrity issues. Finally, **PEEK**: The test ran for 19 days against a planned 28-day duration before being called, inflating false positive risk despite the non-significant p-value.

ERRORS: DENOM, SIMPSON, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD
