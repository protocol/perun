ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (41,548 vs 37,172) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: the metric divides orders by "sessions," but users were randomized by "user id," mismatching the denominator to the unit of randomization. Third, NOVELTY: daily lift decays sharply from 43.7% to 5.6%, yet the projection assumes the current inflated rate persists for twelve months. These issues invalidate the statistical significance and business case for rollout.
