The readout randomizes by **user id** but reports sessions and orders per **session** (DENOM). This breaks the unit of analysis.

Simpson's paradox is present: the pooled data shows Treatment winning (8.45% vs 7.99%), but within segments, Control wins on both Desktop (11.96% vs 12.64%) and Mobile (4.15% vs 4.39%). The aggregate reversal indicates a skew in session-per-user distribution between arms, invalidating the pooled result.

Additionally, the test stopped at day 14 against a planned 21-day duration (PEEK), and the daily lift fluctuates without a clear novelty fade pattern, making the 12-month projection risky. Do not roll out until re-analyzed by user.
