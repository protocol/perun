ERRORS: SRM, SIMPSON, PEEK, POWER
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, **SRM**: the split is 48%/52% (216k vs 233k), a deviation too large for chance, indicating broken randomization. Second, **SIMPSON**: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, both Desktop (7.9% vs 7.6%) and Mobile (2.6% vs 2.5%) segments individually show the *control* performing better. Third, **PEEK**: the test stopped at day 14 of a planned 21 days. Finally, **POWER**: with a 5% baseline and target 5% lift, the required sample size is ~12k per variant; while counts are high, the conflicting directional data and SRM invalidate the power calculation. The recommendation to roll out is unsupported.
