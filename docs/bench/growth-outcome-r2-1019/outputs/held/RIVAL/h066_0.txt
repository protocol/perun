ERRORS: SIMPSON, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains two critical errors. First, Simpson's Paradox: while the pooled data shows a treatment lift, both segments show the opposite. Desktop lift is ~3.6% ((1516/34896)/(1326/29155)-1), but Mobile shows a massive ~30% *drop* ((556/32227)/(683/37824)-1). The aggregate result is driven entirely by the treatment arm having significantly more desktop users (34,896 vs 29,155) and fewer mobile users than control, indicating a severe Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) in assignment or logging. Second, DENOM: the metric divides orders by sessions, but randomization occurred at the user level. If users generate multiple sessions, the independence assumption is violated. Do not roll out; the aggregate lift is likely an artifact of uneven traffic distribution, not a real effect.
