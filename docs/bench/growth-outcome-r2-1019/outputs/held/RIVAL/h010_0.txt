The readout contains two critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~200k total users, a 50/50 split should yield ~100k per arm. The observed split (98,326 vs 102,397) deviates by ~4k users. A chi-square test confirms this imbalance is statistically significant (p < 0.01), indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **DENOM**: users were randomized by *user ID*, but the metric denominator is *sessions*. If users generate different session counts across arms, this violates the unit of analysis.

Additionally, **NOVELTY** is flagged: the daily lift shows no fading trend (fluctuating 5.9%–9.7% throughout), yet the projection assumes the lift persists for 12 months without accounting for potential novelty effects or seasonality, which is an unjustified extrapolation.

ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
