The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, **SRM**: with ~389k total users, a 50/50 split should yield ~194.5k per arm. The observed split (201,718 vs 187,356) deviates by ~7.3%, far exceeding chance limits for this sample size, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **PEEK**: the test ran 18 days against a planned 28 days; stopping early invalidates the p-value. Third, **POWER**: the design targeted a 5% lift, but the arms are sized for that specific MDE; however, since SRM breaks the randomization assumption, power calculations are moot. Fourth, **NOVELTY**: daily lift decays from ~27% to ~6%, yet the team projects the current inflated average over 12 months. Finally, **DENOM**: randomization was by user ID, but results are reported by sessions, violating the unit of analysis.

ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, NOVELTY, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD
