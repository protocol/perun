ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, SIMPSON, PEEK
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, DENOM: users were randomized, but arm sizes are reported in "sessions," mismatching the unit of randomization. Second, SRM: the session split (49.2% vs 50.8%) and user split (desktop/mobile totals) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken assignment or logging. Third, SIMPSON: while the pooled data suggests a lift, both desktop (10.7% vs 10.3%) and mobile (3.1% vs 2.9%) segments individually show the control outperforming treatment, reversing the aggregate result. Finally, PEEK: the test ended at day 19 against a planned 28-day duration. These issues invalidate the results; do not roll out.
