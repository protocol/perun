ERRORS: SRM, DENOM
DECISION: HOLD

The arm sizes (42,370 vs 39,780) deviate significantly from the 50/50 randomization plan, indicating Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM). Additionally, the metric denominator is "sessions" while randomization occurred by "user id," violating the unit of analysis (DENOM). These errors invalidate the p-value and lift calculation. The daily lift shows minor fluctuation but no clear fading trend to flag NOVELTY. Segments do not reverse the effect direction, so SIMPSON is not present. The test ran for the planned 14 days, so PEEK is not evident. Fix the data integrity and unit mismatch before making a rollout decision.
