ERRORS: SRM, DENOM, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains three critical errors. First, SRM: the arm sizes (41,548 vs 37,172) deviate significantly from the planned 50/50 split, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, DENOM: the metric divides orders by sessions, but users were randomized by user ID; if users generated multiple sessions, the denominator is incorrect. Third, NOVELTY: daily lift decays sharply from 43.7% to 5.6%, yet the projection assumes the initial high lift persists for twelve months. These issues invalidate the reported significance and the rollout recommendation.
