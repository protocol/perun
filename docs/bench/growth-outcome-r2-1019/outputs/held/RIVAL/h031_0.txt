The observed 64,866 vs 67,072 (a 2,206 user gap) exceeds chance expectations (p < 0.05), indicating broken randomization or logging. Third, **NOVELTY**: daily lift decays from ~12% to ~3% over the period, yet the team projects the current average as a sustained 12-month benefit. Finally, **PEEK**: the test stopped at day 29 of a planned 28-day run after likely crossing significance early, inflating false positive risk.
