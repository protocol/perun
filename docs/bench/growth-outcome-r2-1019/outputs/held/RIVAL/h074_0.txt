ERRORS: SRM, PEEK, POWER, SIMPSON
DECISION: HOLD

The readout contains multiple critical errors. First, SRM: the split is 48%/52%, a deviation too large for chance given the sample size, indicating broken randomization. Second, PEEK: the test stopped at day 14 of a planned 21 days. Third, POWER: the design targeted a 5% lift, but the observed 3% lift with p=0.0573 is inconclusive; the test was underpowered to detect this smaller effect. Fourth, SIMPSON: while the pooled data shows a lift, both desktop (7.9% vs 7.6%) and mobile (2.6% vs 2.5%) segments actually show the treatment performing worse or flat when calculated correctly (Control Desktop: 5398/68040=7.93%, Treatment Desktop: 6095/80534=7.57%). The aggregate result is misleading. Do not ship.
