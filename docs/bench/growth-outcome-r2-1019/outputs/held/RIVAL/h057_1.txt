The readout contains three critical errors. First, **SRM**: the arm sizes (33,952 vs 31,009) deviate significantly from the 50/50 plan, indicating broken randomization or logging. Second, **SIMPSON**: while the pooled data favors treatment, the segmented data shows treatment winning on desktop but losing on mobile; the aggregate result is misleading due to uneven segment distribution. Third, **DENOM**: users were randomized, but the analysis uses sessions as the denominator, violating the unit of randomization. Additionally, the test ended at day 29 against a 28-day plan (**PEEK** risk), and the projection assumes sustained lift without checking for **NOVELTY** effects, though the daily data appears stable. Do not roll out until assignment integrity and metric definitions are fixed.
