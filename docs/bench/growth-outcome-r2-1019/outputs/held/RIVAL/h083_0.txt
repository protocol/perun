The readout shows clear Simpson's Paradox. While the pooled treatment lift is +2.9%, the segmented data reveals the opposite: Desktop lift is +1.5% (11.64% vs 12.19%), but Mobile lift is -19.4% (3.72% vs 4.63%). The treatment arm skewed heavily toward high-performing Desktop users (45% of treatment vs 39% of control), artificially inflating the aggregate result. Since the change harms the majority mobile experience, the aggregate lift is misleading.

Additionally, the daily lift shows a consistent decay from 5.9% to 0.9%, indicating a Novelty effect where the initial boost fades as users adapt. Projecting the current 2.9% lift over twelve months ignores this fading trend.

ERRORS: SIMPSON, NOVELTY
DECISION: HOLD
