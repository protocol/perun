**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **custodial control**. If your multi-sig setup requires your signature to release funds upon death, regulators view you as having "control" or "custody," triggering transmitter laws.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you sign a transaction or hold a key shard that prevents a user from moving funds without you, you are likely transmitting.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you can move the asset (even conditionally), you are a transmitter. If you only hold encrypted data that the beneficiary decrypts locally without your interaction, you are likely a data custodian, not a money transmitter.
