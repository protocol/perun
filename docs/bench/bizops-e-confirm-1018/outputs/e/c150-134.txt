**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The determining factor is whether the payment fits a specific **Safe Harbor** under the AKS. Cash rebates tied to outcome metrics often fail the "Discount Safe Harbor" because they are not reductions in the *price* of the item at the time of sale, but rather retrospective payments that can be interpreted as inducements.
*   **The Trigger:** If the rebate is characterized as "remuneration for referrals" rather than a "discount on goods/services," the deal is illegal.
