**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The switch from "data host" to "fiduciary/advisor" flips if your contract or marketing implies you *verify* the appraisal accuracy rather than merely *store* the dealer's input.
*   **Trigger:** If your Terms of Service (ToS) do not explicitly disclaim responsibility for the *accuracy* of stored content, or if you offer any "validation" feature, you lose the "mere conduit" defense.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In both UK and EU law, fiduciary duty usually requires a relationship of trust where one party acts *on behalf* of another with discretion. Passive storage is typically a bailment or service contract, not a fiduciary relationship. However, negligence (failing to secure the data) remains a valid claim even without fiduciary status.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current ToS lacks a specific "No Verification of Content" clause, consider pausing the enterprise deal until a solicitor drafts an indemnity clause limiting liability to the subscription fee amount.
