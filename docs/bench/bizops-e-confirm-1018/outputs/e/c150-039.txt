**The Numbers**
*Formula:* LTV = (Monthly Price × Gross Margin % × Customer Lifespan in Months) – Hardware Replacement Cost.
*Formula:* Payback Period = Upfront Hardware Cost / (Monthly Price – Variable Monthly Cost).

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The **replacement liability** flips the model from profitable to insolvent.
*Trigger:* If >15% of your base requires replacement in the next 6 months, you will breach your cash covenant or run out of funds before reaching profitability.
*Rule of Thumb:* Hardware cost must be <30% of total LTV. Currently, it is >80% in the failure scenario.
*Step this week:* If your failure rate data confirms the 18-month hard stop, consider pausing new "subscription-only" sales and launching a "Hardware + Service" bundle immediately.
