Stop installing in new states immediately and engage a multi-state sales tax CPA before your next shipment; your current "collect nothing" strategy creates a high-probability audit liability that standard manufacturing exemptions likely do not fully cover due to the service/installation component.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The determining factor is whether the state treats the transaction as a "lump-sum" sale of tangible personal property (often taxable) versus a separately stated non-taxable service or exempt manufacturing equipment.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you send a crew into a state where you are not registered, you establish nexus.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** "Dry" county laws restrict *alcohol production*, not the sale of *steel tanks*. Most states do not automatically exempt tanks unless the buyer provides a specific "farm/manufacturing exemption certificate." Without collecting these certificates *before* installation, the default is usually taxability.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you have installs scheduled in the next 30 days, consider pausing them until a CPA reviews your contracts for "separately stated" installation language, which can sometimes shield the service portion from tax even if the tank is taxable.
