**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **Commodity Jurisdiction (CJ) determination**.
*   **The Trigger:** You cannot self-certify this safely. The "defense contractor" client status does not *automatically* flag all your data as ITAR, but it shifts the burden of proof entirely to you to prove it is *not*. If the data helps a foreign national understand the performance or utility of a defense article, it is ITAR.
*   **Decision Rule:** If you cannot explicitly map every data field to a purely commercial ECCN (Export Control Classification Number) without referencing defense applications, you must treat it as ITAR.
*   **Step This Week:** If you have any data fields linked to specific defense projects or dual-use items with military applications, **do not build the export pipe**. Instead, draft a "Commodity Jurisdiction" request outline to send to counsel.
