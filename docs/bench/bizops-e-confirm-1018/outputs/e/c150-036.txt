**Mandatory Safeguards & Gaps (Tier 2 Explanation)**
Under HIPAA, as a Business Associate, you must implement the Security Rule's three pillars. Your current "different formats and protocols" approach is a gap; HIPAA requires *your* system to enforce uniformity regardless of the source.

1.  **Administrative Safeguards:** You need a designated Security Officer, risk analysis documentation, and workforce training. *Gap:* Do you have a written Risk Analysis specific to multi-source aggregation?
2.  **Physical Safeguards:** Controls for workstation use and device security. *Gap:* Are servers hosting this data physically or logically segregated from non-PHI data?
3.  **Technical Safeguards:** Access controls, audit controls, integrity controls, and transmission security. *Gap:* Your varying input formats threaten "integrity controls." You must prove data isn't altered or mismatched in transit. Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256) is mandatory.
