**The Numbers**
We compare annual infrastructure savings against the incremental insurance cost.

*   **Infrastructure Savings:** $15 per unit × $N$ units.
*   **Insurance Cost Increase:** Current Premium ($85,000) × 25% = $21,250 (fixed cost increase regardless of unit count, based on your estimate).
*   **Break-even Formula:** $15N = $21,250
*   **Calculation:** $N = 21,250 / 15 = 1,416.67$ units.

*Correction on Scaling:* Your prompt states the $15 saving is per unit, but the insurance hike is a flat 25% on the total policy ($85k). However, if the insurance hike scales linearly with the number of devices (which is typical for liability policies), the math changes.
*Scenario A (Flat Premium Hike):* If the $21,250 increase is fixed regardless of growth:
Break-even = $21,250 / $15 = **1,417 units**.
*Scenario B (Pro-Rata Premium Hike):* If the premium scales per device (current cost $170/unit/year), a 25% hike is $42.50/unit/year.
Comparison: Save $15/unit vs. Pay extra $42.50/unit.
Result: **Never break even.** You lose $27.50 per unit annually.

*Assumption Check:* You stated the hike is "due to physical theft risks." Insurers typically rate per endpoint risk. If the 25% applies to the *total* policy because *all* units are now higher risk, Scenario B applies. If the 25% is a one-time surcharge for the *system architecture* regardless of scale, Scenario A applies.
*Base Case (Most Likely):* Insurance scales with exposure (number of devices).
*   **Savings:** +$15/unit/year.
*   **Cost:** +$42.50/unit/year (25% of $170/base).
*   **Net Impact:** -$27.50/unit/year.
*   **Verdict:** Local storage destroys margin immediately at 500 units and every unit thereafter.

**What Flips It**
The single variable is the **insurance rating methodology**. If the insurer treats the architecture change as a fixed system-wide surcharge (Scenario A) rather than a per-device risk multiplier (Scenario B), you break even at 1,417 units.
*Trigger:* Request a written quote from your broker explicitly asking: "Is the 25% premium increase a fixed fee for the architecture or a per-device rate adjustment?"
*Rule of Thumb:* If the cost per device rises, do not switch until hardware margins exceed the insurance hike.
*This Week's Step:* If the broker confirms per-device rating, consider negotiating the hardware cost down by $50 instead of changing architecture.
