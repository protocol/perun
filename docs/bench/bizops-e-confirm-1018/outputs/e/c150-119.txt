**Pricing Strategy & Contract Framework:**
There is no standard industry practice for *absorbing* this level of regulatory volatility in agriculture; standard practice in logistics and aviation is to pass variable regulatory fees through to the customer.
1.  **Good-Better-Best Packaging:**
    *   **Standard (Variable):** Quote a base rate of $10/acre + "Actual FAA Waiver Fee." This anchors the farmer to a lower base price while explicitly isolating the regulatory risk.

2.  **The "Congestion Band" Clause:** Structure contracts with a defined band. "Pricing includes waiver costs up to $150/flight. Costs exceeding this threshold are billed as a line-item surcharge." This shares the risk rather than dumping it entirely on the farmer.

*   **Trigger:** If you cannot average your waiver cost below $2.00/acre through batching by the start of the next planting season, the "Standard (Variable)" model must become your only offering to prevent insolvency.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never fix a price on a variable cost that swings by >20% without a corresponding contingency clause.

**Routing Note:**
