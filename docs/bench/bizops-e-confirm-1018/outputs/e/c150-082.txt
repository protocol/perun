**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call from "monitor" to "emergency restructuring" is a tax authority asserting that automated validator nodes constitute a "dependent agent" or a fixed place of business under local law or relevant Double Taxation Treaties.
*   **Trigger Value:** Any formal inquiry from a jurisdiction where nodes reside, or a term sheet from an investor conditioning funding on a clean tax opinion regarding node locations.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In many jurisdictions, physical servers alone do not create nexus, but if the nodes perform core revenue-generating functions (validating paid transactions) without human intervention, some regimes may argue this creates a "digital permanent establishment." This is unsettled law.
*   **Step This Week:** If you are entering term sheet discussions, consider pausing node deployment in new jurisdictions until a tax counsel maps the specific "nexus" statutes of the Cayman Islands and Bermuda against your node operational logic.
