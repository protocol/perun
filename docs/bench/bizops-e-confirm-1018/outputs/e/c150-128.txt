**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that flips this from "tech platform" to "broker" is **discretion and control**. If your platform sets the price, matches specific orders automatically without user confirmation, holds customer funds even momentarily, or guarantees trade execution, regulators typically view this as brokerage activity.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you facilitate a trade where you act as the counterparty or agent rather than a passive bulletin board.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you touch the money or the order book logic, you are likely a broker.
*   **Step this week:** If you currently hold funds in a centralized wallet or bank account before disbursing to farmers, **consider** immediately restructuring to use a third-party escrow or direct settlement where funds never touch your balance sheet.
