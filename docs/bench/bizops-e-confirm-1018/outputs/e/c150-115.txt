**The Numbers & Criteria:**
The SEC defines an "investment advisor" under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 based on three prongs, all of which your current operation appears to trigger:
1.  **Engaged in the business:** You charge a 2% performance fee and manage $2.5M across 150 wallets as a regular course of business.
2.  **Providing advice/analysis on securities:** If the DeFi protocols you interact with involve tokens deemed "securities" (a fact-specific legal determination), your rebalancing constitutes advice.
3.  **Compensation:** You receive direct compensation (the 2% fee).

*Assumptions & Thresholds:*
*   **Asset Threshold:** The standard federal registration threshold is **$100 million** in assets under management (AUM). Below this, you typically register with *state* authorities, not the SEC, unless you qualify for a specific exemption. You are currently at $2.5M (ASSUMED), well below the federal threshold but likely above many state thresholds (often $25M–$100M depending on the state).
*   **Client Count:** There is no strict "client count" exemption for advisors managing assets for others; the "fewer than 15 clients" exemption generally does not apply if you hold yourself out to the public as an advisor.
*   **Algorithmic Exemption:** There is **no automatic exemption** for using algorithms. The SEC views the code as the mechanism of advice; if the code makes discretionary decisions for clients, the operator is the advisor.
