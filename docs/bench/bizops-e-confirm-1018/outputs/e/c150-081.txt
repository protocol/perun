**The Numbers & Regulatory Reality:**
You asked for a regulatory determination, which is a **Tier 3 (Route)** issue, but the general rule (Tier 2) is clear: OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration) generally does not "certify" or "accredit" private training programs for hazardous waste operations (HAZWOPER, 29 CFR 1910.120) in the way a university accredits a degree. Instead, OSHA requires that training be conducted by a "qualified trainer" and that the employer certifies the employee has met the training requirements.

*   **The Gap:** Your internal "digital certification credit" is currently a proprietary metric, not a federal compliance artifact.
*   **The Risk:** If you sell this as "OSHA Certification," you risk enforcement action for misrepresentation. OSHA explicitly states it does not approve, certify, or endorse specific training providers.
*   **The Usual Practice:** Successful VR training firms in this space partner with established safety consultancies or organizations that already have the "qualified trainer" status. They issue the certificate under the partner's authority, while your software provides the delivery mechanism and data proof.
