**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes your legal classification from "technology connector" to "seller" is the **degree of control over the transaction**.
*   **Trigger:** If your platform sets the price, mandates the packaging standards, or takes title to the goods (even momentarily) during payment processing, courts are increasingly likely to pierce the "marketplace" veil.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In many jurisdictions, including NY, facilitating the sale *and* handling the money creates a presumption of participation in the chain of distribution.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you currently dictate packaging requirements or hold funds in a merchant account under your own name before disbursing to restaurants, consider restructuring immediately to act strictly as a payment gateway agent where the merchant of record remains the restaurant.
