**Scenario A: Bundle Commission (High Risk in NY)**
If the law firm pays a single "all-in" per-page rate that includes your commission:
*Formula:* Reporter Net = All-In Rate × (1 - 0.15).
*NY Constraint:* If All-In Rate = $4.25 (max), Reporter Net = $3.61.
*Result:* The reporter earns below the mandated maximum, which is legal but may cause reporter churn. However, if you set the All-In Rate higher to ensure the reporter gets $4.25 (e.g., $4.25 / 0.85 = $5.00), the **total cost per page** presented to the consumer exceeds the statutory cap. In strict regulatory interpretations, any fee structure that causes the end-user to pay more than $4.25 for a "certified transcript" risks being deemed a violation of the rate cap spirit or letter.
