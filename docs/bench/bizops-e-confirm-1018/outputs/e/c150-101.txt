**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The trigger is not the *amount* of the fee ($49), but the *nexus* between the fee and the provision of legal advice.
*   **The Flip:** If regulators determine your AI output constitutes "legal advice" rather than "legal information," the fee becomes compensation for UPL.
*   **Threshold Rule of Thumb:** If your marketing or tool output suggests a specific course of action for a specific legal dispute (e.g., "This clause is unenforceable, remove it"), you have crossed from information to advice.
*   **Stop/Pivot Trigger:** If you receive *any* customer complaint alleging reliance on your tool led to a legal loss, or if a state bar association sends an inquiry, you must pause billing in that jurisdiction immediately.
