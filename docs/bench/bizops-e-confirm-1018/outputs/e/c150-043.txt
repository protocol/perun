**The Analysis & Triggers:**
In New York, the Department of State (DOS) and real estate licensing laws generally define "property management" and "real estate brokerage" broadly. Activities that typically trigger a licensing requirement include:
1.  **Collecting or holding funds** belonging to others (security deposits are strictly regulated trust funds in NY).
2.  **Negotiating** lease terms or settlements regarding deposits.
3.  **Acting as an intermediary** in a transaction involving real property rights for a fee.
