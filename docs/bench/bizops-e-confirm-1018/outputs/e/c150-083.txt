**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the regulatory call is the **method of compensation calculation**.
- **Trigger:** If the fee is defined as a *percentage of the rent collected or saved* (a share of the lease economics), regulators in CA and NY often view this as acting as an agent for a fee tied to the property's income, triggering property management or brokerage licensing.
- **Pivot Rule:** If the fee is a *fixed amount* per successful lease (e.g., "$200 per unit furnished and leased") regardless of the rent amount, it is more likely to be viewed as a marketing or staging service, though still risky.
- **Action this week:** If you must test value-based pricing, consider a "fixed fee tiered by unit size" (Studio/1BR/2BR) rather than a percentage of rent, and immediately consult counsel on this specific wording.
