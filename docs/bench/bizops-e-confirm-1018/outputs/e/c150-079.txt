**The Call:** Immediately cut staff by 30% and eliminate marketing spend to extend runway to ~18 months; this buys time for FDA clearance without the dilution and signaling risk of a down-round bridge, which should be reserved as a last resort if clearance slips beyond month 15.

**The Numbers:**
