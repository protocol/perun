**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding factor is whether you can technically prevent US authorities from accessing the raw video content even if they subpoena the US cloud provider.
*   **Trigger:** If you cannot implement encryption where the decryption keys are held exclusively outside the US (e.g., by the EU client or a trusted EU entity) within 30 days, you must route EU traffic to EU-hosted storage.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the data is readable by the US host, it is a high-risk transfer requiring a TIA. If the data is cryptographically unreadable to the host, SCCs are generally sufficient.
