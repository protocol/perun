**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the legal call is the **presence of interpretive conclusion**.
*   **Trigger:** If the output contains phrases like "you should," "this implies," "strategy recommends," or applies a rule to a specific user's fact pattern to reach a conclusion, you are likely practicing law.
*   **Safe Harbor Threshold:** The output must stop at "Regulation X states Y" or "Your transaction matches pattern Z defined in Regulation X," leaving the *application* of that match to the user's counsel.
*   **Decision Rule:** If your product answers "What does this mean for me?" it is legal advice. If it answers "What does the rule say?" it is information.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current draft reports contain any advisory language, consider halting the feature build and engaging external counsel to define a "information-only" disclaimer and content review workflow before writing another line of code.
