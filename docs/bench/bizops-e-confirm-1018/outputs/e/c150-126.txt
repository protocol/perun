**The Call:** Reject the flat $3,500 fee for all volumes; adopt a **tiered usage-based model** with a mandatory risk surcharge for records exceeding 2,500, and explicitly exclude liability caps in the service agreement rather than requiring client-side insurance. At your current cost structure, the flat fee leaves only a 1.4x margin on labor/infra, which is insufficient to self-insure against a $50k+ breach event in a regulated domain.

**The Numbers:**
*Formula:* `Profit = Price - (Fixed Labor/Infra Cost + Variable Risk Reserve)`
*User Inputs:* Price ($3,500), Direct Cost ($1,200), Breach Penalty (>$50,000).
*Assumptions (ASSUMED):*
1.  **Risk Probability:** 1% chance of breach per migration (industry baseline for unverified transfers).
2.  **Risk Reserve:** $500 per job (1% of $50k penalty) must be booked as a cost of goods sold.
3.  **Variable Cost:** Labor scales slightly with record count above 2,500 (ASSUMED +$0.10/record).

*Scenario Analysis (per migration):*
*   **Base Case (2,500 records):**
    *   Revenue: $3,500
    *   Costs: $1,200 (labor) + $500 (risk reserve) = $1,700
    *   **Margin:** $1,800 (51%) → *Viable.*
*   **High Volume Case (5,000 records - Current Plan):**
    *   Revenue: $3,500
    *   Costs: $1,450 (labor + variable) + $750 (increased risk reserve due to volume) = $2,200
    *   **Margin:** $1,300 (37%) → *Marginal; one breach wipes out 38 migrations.*
*   **Proposed Tiered Model:**
    *   Tier 1 (0–2,500 records): $2,500 flat.
    *   Tier 2 (2,501–5,000 records): $2,500 + $0.40 per additional record.
    *   *Result at 5,000 records:* Price becomes $3,500. Margin remains protected because the higher price exactly offsets the higher risk reserve and variable labor.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is the **breach probability**. If your encrypted tunnel process reduces breach risk to <0.2% (verified by third-party audit), the risk reserve drops to $100, making the flat fee viable.
*Trigger:* If you complete the 15 pilot migrations with zero security incidents by **Q3 2024**, reconsider flattening the fee for Tier 2.
*Rule of Thumb:* Never price a regulated service where the maximum loss exceeds 20x the average profit margin without a corresponding price tier or contractual limitation.
*Step this week:* If your legal counsel confirms you can cap liability at 2x the service fee in the contract, consider keeping the flat fee but lowering the record limit to 3,000.
