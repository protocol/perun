**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The trigger is the **specific wording of the subpoena** versus your **actual key architecture**. If the subpoena demands "all data in your possession" and you hold *any* master key, backup key, or metadata that aids decryption, you must produce it. If you truly hold zero keys, your obligation shifts to proving that impossibility to the court without waiving privilege.
