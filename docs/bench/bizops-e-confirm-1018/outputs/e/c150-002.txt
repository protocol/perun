**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The decision flips if the price increase exceeds the cost increase percentage. Currently, a 15% price hike matches a ~20% cost hike? No.
$0.09 * 1.20 = $0.108 cost.
$0.12 * 1.15 = $0.138 price.
Margin Old: $0.03. Margin New: $0.03.
The math shows the price hike merely maintains the status quo per unit; it does not improve margins.
*Trigger:* If volume drops by **any amount**, you lose money faster. The break-even volume decline is **0%**. If you cannot pass on *more* than the cost increase (e.g., raise price to $0.145 to expand margin), do not raise prices if volume will drop.
*Rule of Thumb:* If Price % Increase < Cost % Increase, volume loss is fatal.
