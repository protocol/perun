**The Call:** Reject the dynamic surcharge model; adopt a **Good-Better-Best tiered pricing structure** with a built-in volatility buffer in the base rate. Dynamic surcharges create friction and perceived unpredictability for farmers, risking churn to tractor competitors. Instead, absorb the risk into a higher "Peak" tier price while offering a discounted "Off-Peak" tier to incentivize scheduling flexibility, protecting your 15% margin without exposing customers to line-item volatility.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Baseline:** $40/acre.
*   **Current Margin:** 15% ($6/acre profit, $34/acre cost).
*   **Volatility Impact:** A 300% spike in waiver fees during peak harvest turns a profitable flight into a loss.
    *   *Assumption (Labelled ASSUMED):* If standard waiver cost is ~$2/acre (embedded in the $34 cost), a 300% spike adds ~$6/acre in extra cost.
    *   *Result:* New cost = $40/acre. Revenue = $40/acre. Profit = $0. If the spike exceeds this assumption slightly, you incur a loss.
*   **Proposed Tiered Model:**
    *   **Tier 1 (Off-Peak/Standard):** $38/acre. (Incentivizes booking outside congestion windows).
    *   **Tier 2 (Peak/Guaranteed):** $48/acre. (Builds in a $8–$10 buffer to cover the 300% spike + margin protection).
    *   **Weighted Average Target:** If 60% of flights shift to Off-Peak and 40% remain Peak, the blended rate is ~$42/acre, increasing overall margin while capping downside risk on peak flights.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is **Price Elasticity of Demand**. If farmers are unwilling to pay more than $42/acre regardless of timing, the tiered model fails, and you must cap peak volume rather than service it at a loss.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If >50% of your quote requests reject the $48/acre Peak tier in the first two weeks of harvest, pause peak bookings or require a 50% deposit non-refundable if airspace closure occurs.
*   **Step this week:** Survey your top 10 farm clients: "Would you shift 40% of your spraying to early morning/late evening for a 5% discount, or pay a premium for mid-day guaranteed slots?" Use their response to set the specific split between your $38 and $48 tiers.
