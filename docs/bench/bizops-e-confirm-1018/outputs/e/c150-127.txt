**The Call**
Reject the uncapped liability clause immediately; it exposes ShuttleGo to existential risk ($25M+ potential claims) far exceeding your insurance ($10M) and cash reserves ($400k). Propose a capped indemnity limit equal to your available insurance coverage ($10M) or a multiple of the contract value (3x–5x), whichever is higher, rather than accepting unlimited exposure that requires unaffordable excess insurance.

**The Numbers**

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deal breaks if the airport insists on uncapped liability for *autonomous system failures* specifically, as this transfers the core technology risk entirely to you.
*   **Trigger Value:** Any clause requiring coverage >$10M without a corresponding budget increase from the airport to cover the $150k premium difference.
