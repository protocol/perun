**Distinction Summary (General Rule):**
*   **Payment Processor:** Moves data/instructions; funds settle directly between banks or into a merchant account owned by the user. Usually not a transmitter if you never touch the money.
*   **Escrow Agent:** Holds funds neutrally. Some states exempt licensed escrow agents from MTLs, but many require a separate escrow license which still demands significant bonding.
*   **Money Transmitter:** Receives money from one person and transmits it to another. Holding funds in a pooled account where you control the timing of release is the classic trigger for this status.
