**The Numbers & Rules:**

*   **The "Deemed Export" Rule:** Under US law, releasing controlled technology or source code to a *foreign national* within the United States is "deemed" an export to that person's home country.
*   **The Ownership Threshold:** There is no automatic exemption because the entity is US-based. These categories generally require a license for export to most destinations, including France, depending on the specific technical parameters.
