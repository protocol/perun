**The Call:** Treat your "governance boost" tokens as unregistered securities immediately; halt all future sales until counsel confirms otherwise. Your current model—selling tokens to developers specifically to fund platform operations with an expectation of platform growth driven by those funds—triggers the highest risk factors under the *Howey* test.

**The Numbers & Risk Factors:**
You have raised $600,000 from 1,200 buyers at $500/token. With a $45,000/month burn, this capital provides exactly **13.3 months of runway** ($600,000 / $45,000).
The legal risk is not arithmetic but qualitative. The SEC typically applies the *Howey* test, which defines an investment contract (security) if there is: (1) an investment of money, (2) in a common enterprise, (3) with a reasonable expectation of profits, (4) derived from the efforts of others.
Your model presents three critical tier-3 risk triggers:
1.  **Efforts of Others:** You explicitly state the tokens are sold "to fund platform maintenance." Buyers are likely relying on your team's work (the 6 employees) to increase the platform's utility or token value, rather than using the token for immediate, static utility.
2.  **Expectation of Profit:** If developers buy these tokens anticipating that a successful, well-maintained platform will increase the token's resale value or governance power, this satisfies the "expectation of profit" prong.
3.  **Common Enterprise:** The $600,000 is pooled to support the general business operations (burn rate), linking the fortunes of the token holders to the success of CivicChain as a whole.

**What Flips It & The Trigger:**
The single factor that could potentially alter this classification is if the token has **immediate, consumptive utility** unrelated to speculation or platform success (e.g., it is strictly a non-transferable software license key used instantly and consumed). However, labeling it "governance" while using proceeds for general runway strongly suggests an investment vehicle.
**Trigger:** Do not conduct the next token sale event. The trigger for legal engagement is immediate, given the $600,000 already raised and the active SEC scrutiny of crypto assets.
