*   **The Trigger:** The shift from "spot" (current delivery) to "futures" (agreement to deliver later). Spot carbon offsets are often treated as voluntary commodity-like credits, whereas futures are classified as "specified investments" (derivatives) under the Regulated Activities Order (RAO).

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes your status from "potential exemption" to "definite violation" is the **instrument type**.
*   **Current State (Spot):** If you merely provide a bulletin board where Buyer A pays Seller B directly and you take a fee for matching, you *may* argue you are not "dealing as principal" or "arranging deals in investments" if the offset is not legally defined as a "specified investment." However, the FCA scrutinizes "arranging" heavily.
*   **Future State (Futures):** Once you introduce futures, you are dealing in derivatives. This is a tier-3 binding determination. There is no "small-scale" exemption for running a derivatives marketplace.
*   **Stop/Pivot Trigger:** If your terms of service or user flow involves holding client money (even briefly) or guaranteeing the trade execution, you are likely already acting as a broker.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you touch the money or set the price, you are likely arranging/dealing. If you only introduce parties and they contract directly, you *might* be exempt, but this is a narrow path.
