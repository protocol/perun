### Recommended Strategy: Hybrid Price Lock with Material Surcharge

Do not adopt a pure floating surcharge (which frustrates customers) nor a pure quarterly lock (which risks your cash reserves given the 20% volatility). Instead, implement a **Base Price + Variable Material Surcharge** model with a **volatility collar**.

**1. Restructure the Price Point**
Your current $140 price assumes a $35 material cost. To hit a 60% gross margin, your price must be 2.5x your total cost.
*   **New Base Price:** Set at **$155**. This covers your average operational costs and assumes a "standard" material cost of $35.
*   **Variable Surcharge:** Implement a transparent "Sustainable Wood Adjustment" line item.
    *   **The Collar:** If material costs stay between $32 and $38, you absorb the variance (protecting the customer from minor fluctuations).
    *   **The Trigger:** If costs exceed $38, the surcharge activates at 100% of the overage per unit. If costs drop below $32, you pass the savings to the customer (building trust).
    *   *Example:* If wood hits $42, the surcharge is $4. Final price: $159.

**2. Supplier Strategy**
Immediately seek a **fixed-price contract** for 3–6 months covering 50% of your projected volume. Use your $20,000 reserve to pre-pay or secure this contract if a discount is offered. This creates a "hedge" that stabilizes half your inventory cost, allowing you to keep the base price stable for the majority of orders while the surcharge handles the remaining volatile 50%.

**3. Customer Communication**
Frame this as a commitment to **quality and sustainability**, not just cost recovery.
*   **Narrative:** "To guarantee we only use premium sustainable maple despite global market swings, we are introducing a transparent Wood Adjustment. This ensures we never compromise on material quality."
*   **Transparency:** Publish a simple chart on your site showing the current wood index and the corresponding surcharge. Transparency reduces alienation; hidden fees cause churn.

**4. Weekly Data Points to Track**
Stop guessing; track these four metrics every Monday:
1.  **Spot Price of Sustainable Maple:** The current market rate per unit.
2.  **Weighted Average Cost (WAC):** Your actual cost of inventory on hand (blends old cheap stock with new expensive stock).
3.  **Surcharge Activation Rate:** The percentage of orders in the last 7 days that triggered the surcharge.
4.  **Conversion Rate by Price Point:** Monitor if orders drop when the surcharge pushes the total price above $160.

**Execution Summary**
Raise the base to $155 immediately. Announce the "Wood Adjustment" policy effective next month. Use cash reserves to lock in 50% of supply at a fixed rate. This protects your 60% margin target without exposing the customer to wild weekly price swings.

*Note: With $20k reserves and 300 units/month, a $7/unit margin erosion consumes $2,100 monthly; this strategy caps that risk.*
*Do not wait for the next cost spike to implement; announce the change before your margins turn negative.*
