*   **Price Changes:** For existing contracts, you cannot unilaterally raise prices mid-term unless your Terms of Service explicitly allow for price adjustments with notice. For month-to-month subscribers, you can typically raise prices with 30 days' notice, but sudden large hikes may trigger chargebacks or consumer complaints.
