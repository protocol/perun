### Summer Pricing & Production Strategy for Lumina Gems

**1. Current Cost Structure Analysis**
*   **Current Selling Price:** $1,200/carat
*   **Current Gross Margin:** 40% ($480 profit)
*   **Current Total Cost:** $720/carat
*   **Current Energy Cost:** 4,500 kWh × $0.12 = $540/carat
*   **Non-Energy Costs (Labor, Overhead, Materials):** $720 - $540 = $180/carat

**2. Summer Cost Scenarios**
*   **Peak Rate Energy Cost:** 4,500 kWh × $0.24 = $1,080/carat
*   **Off-Peak Rate Energy Cost:** 4,500 kWh × $0.12 = $540/carat (assuming night rates remain at current base)

**3. Production Schedule Constraints**
*   **Total Inventory:** 3,000 carats
*   **Night Shift Capacity:** 150 carats/day
*   **Summer Duration (June–August):** ~92 days
*   **Max Night Production:** 150 × 92 = 13,800 carats (Capacity exceeds inventory; constraint is the 60% limit you imposed, not time).
*   **Required Split:**
    *   Off-Peak (Night): 60% of 3,000 = 1,800 carats
    *   Peak (Day): 40% of 3,000 = 1,200 carats

**4. Weighted Average Cost Calculation**
To find the new break-even and pricing, we calculate the blended cost per carat based on the 60/40 split.

*   **Cost of Off-Peak Carats:** $180 (non-energy) + $540 (energy) = $720/carat
*   **Cost of Peak Carats:** $180 (non-energy) + $1,080 (energy) = $1,260/carat
*   **Weighted Average Cost:**
    *   (0.60 × $720) + (0.40 × $1,260)
    *   $432 + $504 = **$936/carat (New Average Cost)**

**5. Pricing Recommendation to Maintain 25% Margin**
To achieve a 25% *gross margin* (where Margin = (Price - Cost) / Price), the formula is:
*   Price = Cost / (1 - Margin Target)
*   Price = $936 / (1 - 0.25)
*   Price = $936 / 0.75
*   **New Required Retail Price: $1,248 per carat**

**Strategic Breakdown:**
*   **Price Increase:** You must raise prices from $1,200 to **$1,248** (a 4% increase) to maintain your 25% margin target under the 60/40 production split.
*   **Margin Risk:** If you keep the price at $1,200 with this production mix, your margin drops to ~22.5%.
*   **Operational Note:** Since your night shift capacity (13,800 carats potential) far exceeds your 60% target (1,800 carats), you are artificially limiting your efficiency. If you can increase night production to 100% (producing all 3,000 carats at night), your cost stays at $720, and your current $1,200 price yields a 40% margin. The 60% cap is the primary financial leak, not the utility rate itself.

**Unknown Variables for Competitor Research**
Before finalizing the $1,248 price point, investigate:
1.  **Inventory Hedging:** Did competitors stockpile finished goods during low-rate months to avoid summer production entirely? If so, they may not raise prices, making your increase a competitive disadvantage.
2.  **Energy Contracts:** Are larger competitors on fixed-rate industrial contracts that insulate them from the spot-market doubling you are facing?
3.  **Substitute Pressure:** Will a 4% price push customers toward moissanite or lower-clarity natural diamonds, or is demand inelastic in your specific segment?

*Note: The 4-hour maintenance window does not constrain your ability to run 100% night shifts given your total volume; re-evaluating the 60% cap could save the margin entirely without a price hike.*
*Ensure your non-energy costs ($180) do not fluctuate with overtime pay for night shifts, which would alter the break-even calculation.*
