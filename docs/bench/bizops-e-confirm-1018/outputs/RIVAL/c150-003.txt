### LTV Modeling and Unit Economics Analysis for ResumeRocket

Your current unit economics are indeed negative, but your LTV calculation is incomplete because it ignores the **re-subscription cycle** inherent to executive careers. Executives do not leave the workforce; they change roles every 3–5 years. Therefore, you must model LTV as a **multi-cycle relationship**, not a single churn event.

**1. The Corrected LTV Model**
Do not treat the 3-month usage window as the end of the customer lifecycle. Instead, calculate LTV using a **Cohort-Based Multi-Cycle Approach**:

*   **Cycle 1 Value:** $49 × 3 months = **$147** (Current realized value).
*   **Re-acquisition Probability:** Estimate the % of users who return for a new role. Even a conservative 20% return rate over 24 months adds significant value.
*   **Referral Value:** Senior executives have high-network value. If one user refers another, attribute a portion of that CAC savings to their LTV.
*   **Adjusted LTV Formula:** $147 (Initial) + ($147 × Re-subscription Rate) + (Referral Value).

If you achieve just a **30% re-subscription rate** over two years, your LTV jumps to **$191**. If you add referrals (e.g., 0.2 new users per executive), LTV exceeds **$250**.

**2. Current Financial Reality**
*   **Current LTV:** ~$147 (ignoring cycles/referrals).
*   **CAC:** $65.
*   **Gross Margin per User:** $147 - $65 = $82.
*   **Variable Costs:** Your server/API cost is ~$3.33/user/month ($4k/1.2k users). Over 3 months, this is ~$10/user.
*   **Contribution Margin:** $82 - $10 = **$72 per user**.

**Crucial Insight:** You are **not** losing money on every acquisition regarding variable costs. You are generating $72 in contribution margin per user. With 1,200 users, you generate ~$86,400 in annual contribution margin. However, your **fixed burn** is $18,000/month ($216k/year). You are currently covering only ~40% of your fixed costs. The problem is not unit economics; it is **scale vs. fixed overhead**.

**3. Strategic Recommendations**

**Priority A: Increase Price (Highest Impact)**
Executives are price-inelastic regarding career tools. $49 is likely too low, signaling "generic tool" rather than "executive advantage."
*   **Action:** Raise price to **$99–$149/month**.
*   **Impact:** At $99, Cycle 1 LTV becomes $297. Contribution margin nearly doubles. This immediately fixes the path to profitability without needing more users or longer retention.

**Priority B: Extend Duration via "Career Mode"**
Users cancel when they get the job. Prevent this by shifting value from "resume building" to "career management."
*   **Action:** Add features like "Interview Prep AI," "Salary Negotiation Scripts," or "Performance Review Builder" that remain useful *after* hiring.
*   **Goal:** Extend average life from 3 to 5 months.

**Priority C: Optimize CAC via Organic Loops**
$65 CAC is acceptable if LTV is high, but rely less on paid ads.
*   **Action:** Implement a "Powered by ResumeRocket" footer on exported PDFs. Executives sharing resumes creates free B2B exposure to other executives.

**Conclusion**
Stop viewing these as one-time customers. Model for a 5-year horizon with intermittent subscriptions. However, your immediate fix is **pricing power**. Double your price to $99+. Your target market (London executives) will not flinch, and this single move will likely make your unit economics strongly positive, allowing you to cover your $18k burn with fewer than 200 new customers per month.

*Note: Your server costs ($3.33/user) are low enough that margin expansion via price hikes flows almost entirely to profit.*
*Focus on positioning the price increase as "Executive Tier" exclusivity to maintain brand prestige.*
