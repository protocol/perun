You cannot make this decision based solely on the numbers provided. You must engage a qualified EU privacy lawyer before proceeding with any market entry activities in Germany or France.

The financial estimates you cited ($150k initial + $20k/month) are likely inaccurate because they assume a specific architectural approach (separate data silos) and staffing model (full-time internal DPO) that may not be legally required or commercially viable for a startup. GDPR compliance is not a fixed cost; it is a risk-based obligation that scales with your data processing activities. A lawyer is required to determine if you can leverage a "Virtual DPO" service (costing ~$2k–$5k/month) instead of a full-time hire, and whether data segregation can be achieved via logical separation within your existing cloud infrastructure rather than building expensive physical silos.

Furthermore, the genetic data you process is classified as "special category data" under Article 9 of the GDPR, triggering the highest level of regulatory scrutiny. In Germany and France, regulators are particularly aggressive regarding direct-to-consumer genetic testing. Entering these markets without a formal Legal Basis Assessment and a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) exposes GeneFit to fines of up to €20 million or 4% of global turnover, which would immediately terminate your runway. The $150k estimate might be an overestimate if you use compliant third-party processors, or a dangerous underestimate if it excludes the cost of obtaining explicit, granular consent mechanisms required for genetic data.

Do not attempt to calculate unit economics until you have a validated compliance roadmap. If the legal pathway requires the full $20k/month overhead you fear, your unit economics might indeed fail (adding ~$240k/year to fixed costs against a potential market). However, if a streamlined approach exists, your current margins (60%) could support expansion. Only a legal expert can define which scenario applies. Stop all technical planning for EU expansion until this legal validation is complete.
