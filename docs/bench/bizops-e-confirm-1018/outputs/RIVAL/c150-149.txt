**Florida Legal Thresholds for Price Gouging:**
Under Florida Statute 501.0117, price gouging occurs when a seller charges a price for "essential commodities" that "unconscionably exceeds" the average price charged in the usual course of business during the 30 days prior to the declaration of a state of emergency.
