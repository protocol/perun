### General Regulatory Distinctions (ITAR vs. EAR)

**ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations)**
ITAR controls the export of defense articles, services, and related technical data listed on the United States Munitions List (USML).
*   **Scope:** ITAR is strictly tied to the *nature* of the item or data, not just the identity of the customer.
*   **Technical Data:** Under ITAR, "technical data" includes information required for the design, development, production, manufacture, assembly, operation, repair, testing, maintenance, or modification of defense articles.
*   **The Defense Contractor Factor:** Having defense contractors as clients does *not* automatically classify all your data as ITAR-controlled. However, if your software or the specific simulation data generated relates to defense articles on the USML (e.g., specific military logistics systems, classified operational details, or technologies designed specifically for military use), it may fall under ITAR.
*   **Dual-Use Goods:** You mentioned the warehouses store "dual-use goods." If the simulation data reveals specific capabilities, vulnerabilities, or operational parameters of defense articles, it risks being categorized as ITAR technical data.

**EAR (Export Administration Regulations)**
EAR controls "dual-use" items (commercial items that could have military applications) listed on the Commerce Control List (CCL).
*   **Scope:** Most commercial software and logistical data fall under EAR, which is generally less restrictive than ITAR but still requires compliance.
*   **Classification:** Items under EAR are assigned an Export Control Classification Number (ECCN). Many commercial software products are classified as EAR99, which often requires no license for export to many countries, though restrictions apply to embargoed nations or specific end-users.
*   **Logistical Data:** Generic logistical data (throughput speeds, inventory levels of commercial goods, layout maps of commercial facilities) typically falls under EAR, provided it does not reveal sensitive defense-specific technical data.
