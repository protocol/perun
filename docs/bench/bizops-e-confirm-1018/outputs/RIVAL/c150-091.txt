**Recommendation: Do not lower prices. Implement a "Verified-Only" billing model immediately and raise the effective price for high-volume partners.**

Lowering your price to £1.50 or £1.00 is a strategic error. Your churn is not driven by cost; it is driven by **regulatory risk**. In the UK gambling sector, a false positive isn't a nuisance; it is a compliance failure that threatens your clients' licenses. No discount compensates for the risk of losing a license. Furthermore, with a variable cost of £0.40, dropping your price to £1.50 reduces your contribution margin from £1.60 to £1.10 (a 31% drop), accelerating your burn without solving the root cause.

**1. Immediate Structural Change: Bill on Success, Not Volume**
Shift your billing metric from "Per Check Submitted" to "Per Verified Identity" (or "Per Clear Pass").
*   **The Mechanism:** You continue to run the checks, but you only invoice for the results that do not trigger a false positive flag requiring manual review or causing user friction.
*   **The Price Point:** Raise the unit price to **£2.50 - £3.00 per successful verification**.
*   **The Logic:** With an 8% false positive rate, a client currently pays £2.00 for every attempt. If you charge £2.50 only for successes, their effective cost per *total* attempt remains roughly £2.25 (assuming 8% waste), but their **risk is transferred to you**. This aligns your incentives with theirs: you are now financially motivated to drive that 8% error rate to zero. This stops the bleeding of major clients by removing their regulatory exposure.

**2. Tiered Packaging for Stability**
Once the billing metric shifts, introduce tiers to stabilize revenue and fund your engineering team:
*   **Growth Tier (SMBs):** £2.75 per successful verification. Pay-as-you-go. Includes standard SLA.
*   **Enterprise Tier (Major Operators):** £2.40 per successful verification, but with a **£2,000 monthly minimum commitment**. This guarantees cash flow to cover your £35k burn while offering a volume discount. Includes a dedicated account manager and a joint quarterly compliance review.
*   **Accuracy Guarantee:** Explicitly state in the contract that if the false positive rate exceeds 5% in a given month, the overage checks are free. This acts as a safety valve for clients while setting a clear internal target for your six engineers.

**3. Financial Impact & Runway**
*   **Current State:** 45k checks × £1.60 margin = £72k contribution. Minus £35k burn = £37k profit (theoretically), but you are losing clients.
*   **Proposed State:** If you lose 8% of billable events due to the new model but raise the price to £2.75:
    *   Billable events: ~41,400 (92% of 45k).
    *   Revenue: 41,400 × £2.75 = £113,850.
    *   Variable Cost: 45,000 × £0.40 = £18,000 (you still pay the cost to run the check).
    *   New Contribution: £95,850.
    *   **Result:** You increase your contribution margin by ~33% while eliminating the primary reason for churn.

**Conclusion**
Your pricing problem is actually a product reliability problem disguised as a pricing question. Use the pricing model to **insure** the customer against your current product flaws. By charging more per *successful* outcome, you generate the extra capital needed to retain your engineering team and fix the algorithm, while simultaneously removing the regulatory threat that is causing your clients to leave. Do not compete on price; compete on risk mitigation.

*Note: Ensure your legal team reviews the "billing on success" terminology to ensure it aligns with UK gambling commission reporting requirements.*
