### Recommended Strategy: Hybrid Per-Check with Service Level Credits

Do not switch to a flat tiered subscription yet. In the UK gambling sector, where volume fluctuates wildly based on marketing campaigns and player acquisition spikes, operators prefer variable costs. A flat subscription creates friction during low-volume months and margin erosion during high-volume months for you.

Instead, adopt a **Per-Check model with a "Accuracy Guarantee" credit clause**. This directly addresses your churn driver (false positives) without capping your upside.

**The Financial Trade-Off Analysis**

Your current unit economics are fragile. At £2.50 per check with an 8% error rate, you are effectively selling a defective product that costs your clients regulatory scrutiny.

**Scenario A: Status Quo (Do Nothing)**
*   **Revenue:** 45,000 checks × £2.50 = £112,500
*   **Churn Impact:** 12% monthly churn is unsustainable. If this continues, you lose over half your base in six months.
*   **Outcome:** Revenue collapses as operators flee to competitors offering higher accuracy. The £2.50 price point is irrelevant if the product fails compliance.

**Scenario B: Improve Accuracy + Pass Through Cost (Recommended)**
*   **New Cost Base:** Current Cost + £0.60 infrastructure increase.
*   **New Price Point:** £3.25 per check.
*   **Rationale:** You are increasing price by £0.75 to cover the £0.60 cost, leaving a £0.15 margin buffer.
*   **Value Proposition:** You are selling a reduction in regulatory risk. For a UK operator, the cost of a Compliance Officer investigating a false positive or a minor regulatory fine far exceeds £0.75 per check.
*   **Projected Revenue (assuming stabilized churn):**
    *   If volume holds at 45,000: £146,250 (+30% revenue).
    *   If volume grows 10% due to retained customers: £160,875.
*   **Net Impact:** Even if you lose 5% of price-sensitive customers immediately, the stabilization of the remaining 95% yields higher Lifetime Value (LTV) than the current high-churn model.

**Why Not Tiered Subscriptions?**
Tiers work well for predictable usage. Gambling sites have volatile traffic (e.g., during major sports events). If you cap checks in a tier, operators face service stoppages during peaks, which is a non-starter for compliance. If you offer unlimited tiers, you invite arbitrage. Stick to pay-as-you-go but offer volume discounts only above 100k checks/month to incentivize growth without locking margins.

**The "Accuracy Guarantee" Clause**
Include a contractual clause: *"If false positive rates exceed 5% in a calendar month, the customer receives a 20% credit on that month's invoice."*
*   **Psychology:** This shifts risk back to you, proving confidence in your engineering fix.
*   **Financials:** If you hit your 4% target, you pay nothing. If you miss, the 20% credit is cheaper than losing the client entirely.

**Critical Legal Questions for Your Attorney**

Before finalizing the rate card, consult a UK gambling compliance attorney on these specific points:

1.  **Liability Caps:** Can you contractually limit your liability if a false positive leads to a player suing the operator for "unfair treatment" under UKGC (UK Gambling Commission) guidelines? Most operators will demand you indemnify them for regulatory fines; you must determine if this exposure is insurable.
2.  **GDPR & Automated Decision Making:** Since you are blocking users, does your service trigger Article 22 of the GDPR (right not to be subject to solely automated decision-making)? Your pricing contract may need to specify who bears the cost of manual review workflows triggered by your system.
3.  **Service Level Definitions:** How is "false positive" legally defined in your contract? It must align strictly with UKGC identity verification standards to prevent operators from claiming credits for errors that were actually their own data entry faults.

**Summary:** Raise prices to £3.25 to fund the accuracy fix. Use a credit clause to guarantee performance. Avoid subscriptions until usage patterns stabilize.

*Note: This analysis assumes your engineering team can deliver the 4% error rate on schedule. If delayed, the price hike will accelerate churn.*
*Ensure your legal counsel specializes in UKGC regulations, not just general SaaS law.*
