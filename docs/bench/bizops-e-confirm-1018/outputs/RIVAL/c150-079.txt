**Runway Modeling Framework & Scenario Analysis**

**Current Baseline:**
*   **Cash:** $1.8M
*   **Burn:** $150k/mo
*   **Runway:** 12 months
*   **Gap to Revenue:** 18 months
*   **Deficit:** 6 months of funding required immediately.

**Scenario Modeling:**

1.  **Staff Reduction (30% Cut):**
    *   *Assumption:* Labor is ~70% of your burn ($105k). A 30% cut saves ~$31.5k/mo.
    *   *New Burn:* ~$118.5k/mo.
    *   *New Runway:* ~15.2 months.
    *   *Outcome:* Still short ~3 months. This buys time but does not solve the 18-month gap. Morale and development speed risks increase.

2.  **Zero Marketing Spend:**
    *   *Assumption:* Marketing is likely a small portion of hardware/medical device burn (<10%).
    *   *Impact:* Negligible. If marketing is $10k/mo, runway extends only 1.2 months.
    *   *Outcome:* Not a viable standalone solution.

3.  **Bridge Round (Down Valuation):**
    *   *Requirement:* Raise enough to cover the 18-month gap plus a 6-month safety buffer post-clearance = 24 months total runway needed.
    *   *Target Raise:* $118.5k (optimized burn) × 24 = ~$2.8M.
    *   *Reality Check:* Raising $2.8M on a down round with zero revenue and regulatory delays is extremely difficult. Investors will demand significant equity dilution (potentially 40-60%) and strict milestones.

**Strategic Recommendation:**
None of these options alone works. You must combine a **30-40% staff reduction** (focusing on non-regulatory roles) to lower burn to ~$100k, extending runway to 18 months, while simultaneously pursuing a **bridge round** sized only for the remaining gap ($1M–$1.5M) based on the milestone of FDA clearance.

**Key Assumptions to Stress-Test for Lenders:**
1.  **FDA Timeline Sensitivity:** Model outcomes if clearance takes 24 months instead of 18.
2.  **Post-Clearance Sales Ramp:** How many units must you sell in Month 1 post-clearance to cover ongoing ops? (Lenders will scrutinize your pre-orders/LOIs).
3.  **Hardware COGS:** Can you manufacture the first batch without additional capital?
4.  **Reimbursement Codes:** Does Medicare/private insurance coverage lag behind FDA clearance? If so, add 6+ months to revenue recognition.

**Investor Disclosure Requirements:**
You are legally obligated to provide timely, accurate material information to existing investors.
*   **Material Adverse Change:** The 18-month delay is material. You must disclose this immediately in writing, not wait for a quarterly update.
*   **Revised Financial Projections:** Provide a updated cash flow model showing the 12-month runway reality.
*   **Mitigation Plan:** Present the specific cost-cutting measures and fundraising strategy you are executing.
*   **Board Approval:** If raising a down round, existing investors usually have pro-rata rights or anti-dilution protections that must be addressed formally before closing new money.

*Note: This analysis is based strictly on the figures provided; actual legal disclosure duties depend on your specific shareholder agreements and state laws.*
*Consult legal counsel immediately before sending investor communications regarding regulatory delays.*
