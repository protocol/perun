**Critical Legal Interpretations for Aviation Counsel**

Before hiring pilots or pivoting technology, you must verify the following specific interpretations of the proposed FAA language with an aviation attorney:

1.  **"Visual Line of Sight" (VLOS) Definition:** Does the regulation strictly require the *pilot* to maintain unaided visual contact, or does it allow for "Visual Observers" (VOs)? If VOs are permitted, can one VO monitor multiple drones, or must the ratio be 1:1? This determines if you truly need one pilot per drone or if a leaner staffing model is compliant.
2.  **Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) Waivers:** Does the proposed rule eliminate existing BVLOS waivers entirely, or does it create a pathway for renewed waivers based on specific technological mitigations (e.g., detect-and-avoid systems)? Your autonomous stack might qualify for an exception if framed correctly.
3.  **"In Command" vs. "On Site":** Does the rule require the pilot to be physically present at the inspection site, or merely "in command" from a remote station within a certain radius? Remote piloting could reduce travel time and allow one pilot to manage sequential flights more efficiently.
4.  **Grandfathering Clauses:** Are there transitional periods or grandfathering provisions for operations certified under previous rules? This could buy you 12–24 months to adapt your technology or business model.

**Strategic Recommendation**

Do not immediately raise prices to $250 or hire a full team of pilots. First, secure a legal opinion on the "Visual Observer" ratio and BVLOS waiver viability. If the law mandates a strict 1:1 pilot-to-drone ratio with no waiver path, consider a hybrid model: raise prices moderately (e.g., to $195) while targeting only high-value commercial clients (insurance firms, HOAs) rather than price-sensitive homeowners, effectively moving upmarket to justify the human-in-the-loop cost.

Your current runway is at risk if the rule passes without a strategic pivot; immediate legal clarification is your highest priority investment.
