### Criteria Distinguishing Utility vs. Equity

Regulators typically apply a "substance over form" test. The distinction often hinges on:
1.  **Expectation of Profit:** Do buyers expect profit primarily from the efforts of your team? (Yes = Equity/Security).
2.  **Current Functionality:** Can the token be used for its intended purpose (title registry access) immediately? (Yes = Utility).
3.  **Transferability:** Is the token tradable on secondary markets before the platform is live? (Yes = Security risk).
4.  **Rights Conferred:** Does the token grant voting rights, dividends, or ownership stakes? (Yes = Equity).
