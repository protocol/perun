**Strategic Pathways for Enhanced Reimbursement**
Standard Medicaid fee-for-service rates often lag behind the cost of hybrid digital-human care. You must pivot from selling "sessions" to selling "outcomes" or "care management" to access higher payment tiers. Investigate these specific avenues with a healthcare reimbursement expert:

1.  **Remote Therapeutic Monitoring (RTM) Codes:**
    Your AI monitoring component may qualify for CPT codes 98975, 98976, 98977, and 98980/98981. These Medicare-derived codes (often adopted by Medicaid) pay for the supply of devices/software and the time spent reviewing data. Crucially, these are often **add-on codes**, meaning they can be billed *in addition* to the standard coaching session codes, potentially adding $100–$200+ per month per patient to your revenue stream without increasing session counts.

2.  **Value-Based Care (VBC) & PMPM Models:**
    Stop billing per session. Propose a **Per Member Per Month (PMPM)** care management fee or a bundled episode-of-care payment to State Medicaid agencies or Managed Care Organizations (MCOs). In a VBC arrangement, you share the savings generated by successful smoking cessation (e.g., reduced ER visits, lower long-term medication costs). Your AI data provides the proof of engagement and efficacy required to negotiate these risk-sharing contracts, which often pay significantly higher effective rates than fee-for-service.

3.  **Health Home & Care Coordination Codes:**
    If your platform acts as the central hub for a patient's cessation journey, investigate "Health Home" state plan amendments or care coordination codes. These reimburse the *integration* of services (AI + Human) rather than just the human time. Some states have specific waivers for Digital Therapeutics (DTx) that allow for enhanced rates if clinical efficacy is proven.

4.  **Provider "Incident-To" or Supervision Billing:**
    If your coaches are not independent practitioners, ensure you are billing under the supervision of a physician or psychologist where state laws allow. This can sometimes unlock higher reimbursement tiers associated with the supervising provider's license level rather than the coach's.
