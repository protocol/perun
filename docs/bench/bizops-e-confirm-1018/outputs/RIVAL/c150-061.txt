### California Short-Term Vehicle Rental Tax Logic

In California, the tax on short-term vehicle rentals (leases of 30 days or less) is generally **destination-based**.

**1. Which Jurisdiction's Rate Applies?**
For your cross-county scenario (Owner in San Mateo, Pickup in Alameda), you must collect tax based on the **pickup location (Alameda County)**.
*   **The Rule:** The taxable event is deemed to occur where the vehicle is delivered to the renter.
*   **Application:** You apply the combined state, county, city, and district tax rates effective in Alameda County at the specific address where the renter takes possession of the vehicle. The owner's garage location (San Mateo) is irrelevant for the rental tax calculation.

**2. Collection Logic for Cross-County Transactions**
Your checkout flow must dynamically calculate tax based on the **pickup address**, not the listing address or owner's home address.
*   **Step 1:** Capture the exact pickup address during the booking process.
*   **Step 2:** Query a tax rate engine (e.g., Avalara, TaxJar, or CDTFA lookup) using that pickup address to get the current combined rate.
*   **Step 3:** Apply this rate to the taxable amount (the rental fee). Note that your 15% service fee may also be taxable depending on how it is bundled; if it is mandatory, it is generally part of the taxable gross receipts.
*   **Step 4:** Collect this total from the renter at checkout.

**3. Registration Requirements**
You likely **do not** need to register separately in every single county, but you must register with the **California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA)** as a single statewide entity.
*   **Statewide Simplification:** California administers these taxes centrally through the CDTFA. You file a single return (usually quarterly or monthly, depending on volume).
*   **Reporting:** On your single CDTFA return, you will report the total taxable sales and break down the tax collected by jurisdiction (county/city/district). The CDTFA then distributes the funds to the respective local agencies.
*   **Exception:** If you have a physical presence (nexus) in a specific city that imposes its own separate business license tax or transient occupancy tax outside the standard CDTFA framework, local registration might be required, but for standard vehicle rental tax, the CDTFA registration covers statewide collection.
