### Cash Flow Projection & Runway Analysis

**Current Financial Position**
*   **Cash on Hand:** $400,000
*   **Monthly Burn Rate:** $48,000 ($45k salaries + $3k hosting)
*   **Revenue per Deal:** $25,000
*   **Payment Lag:** 60 days post-signing (Revenue hits cash flow 2 months after close)
*   **Baseline Runway (0 deals):** ~8.3 months ($400k / $48k)

**Scenario A: Closing 1 Deal in Next 12 Months**
*   **Assumption:** Deal closes Month 6 (average sales cycle); Cash arrives Month 8.
*   **Cash In:** $25,000 (Month 8)
*   **Total Cash Out (12 months):** $576,000
*   **Ending Balance:** -$151,000
*   **Result:** **Insolvent by Month 9.** You run out of cash before the payment arrives.

**Scenario B: Closing 2 Deals in Next 12 Months**
*   **Assumption:** Deals close Month 4 and Month 9; Cash arrives Month 6 and Month 11.
*   **Cash In:** $50,000 total ($25k in Month 6, $25k in Month 11)
*   **Cumulative Deficit at Month 6 (pre-payment):** -$88,000
*   **Result:** **Insolvent by Month 8.** Even with two deals, the cash gap before the first payment arrives is too deep. You cannot survive the initial 6-month drought.

**Scenario C: Closing 3 Deals in Next 12 Months**
*   **Assumption:** Deals close Month 3, 6, and 9; Cash arrives Month 5, 8, and 11.
*   **Cash In:** $75,000 total.
*   **Cumulative Deficit at Month 5 (pre-payment):** -$240,000.
*   **Cash Balance Month 5 (post-payment):** -$165,000.
*   **Result:** **Insolvent immediately.** You do not have enough cash to operate for the first 5 months required to close and collect on the first deal.

**Verdict on Bridge Financing**
**Yes, you need emergency bridge financing immediately.**

Under your current model, you are mathematically insolvent regardless of sales performance. Your cash on hand ($400k) covers less than 9 months of operations, but your revenue collection mechanism (9-month sale + 2-month delay) guarantees a cash gap of at least 11 months before the first dollar enters your bank account. You cannot reach "profitability" or even "cash flow neutral" without external capital to cover the first year of operations.

**Immediate Recommendations**
1.  **Secure Bridge Loan:** You need at least $200,000–$300,000 to cover the 11–14 month gap until the first payments clear.
2.  **Restructure Payment Terms:** Stop accepting 100% payment upon completion. Demand a **40% upfront deposit** ($10k) upon signing and **40% at mid-project**. This brings cash in during the development phase, reducing the bridge requirement and shortening the cash conversion cycle.
3.  **Reduce Burn:** With a 9-month sales cycle, a $48k monthly burn is unsustainable. Consider contracting artists/developers per project rather than full-time salaries to lower fixed costs until revenue stabilizes.

*Note: These projections assume no other revenue sources or cost reductions. The 60-day government payment delay is the critical bottleneck destroying your liquidity.*
