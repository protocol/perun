### Unit Economics Analysis: QuitAI

**Current Financial Reality**
Under the existing fee-for-service structure, your unit economics are negative.
*   **Revenue per User:** $60/month (Medicaid reimbursement)
*   **Cost per User:** $50/month ($10 app + $40 coaching)
*   **Gross Margin:** $10 per user per month (20% margin)

**The Verdict:** You are technically profitable on a per-user basis, but the margin is dangerously thin for a digital health startup. While you cover your direct delivery costs, a $10 margin per user must fund your team of eight, overhead, technology development, and customer acquisition.

**Runway Calculation**
Assuming an average fully loaded cost of $8,000/month per employee (salary + benefits + overhead), your monthly burn for the team alone is approximately $64,000.
*   To break even on operational costs (ignoring growth capital), you need **6,400 active users** ($64,000 / $10 margin).
*   You currently have 500 users.
*   At your current scale, you are losing roughly $59,000/month.
*   With $100,000 in cash reserves, your runway is less than **two months** if you scale slowly, or even shorter if you attempt rapid growth without additional funding.

**Strategic Recommendation: Do Not Lobby for Rate Increases Yet**
Lobbying for a higher fee-for-service rate (e.g., asking for $80/month) is a slow, political process with uncertain outcomes. Given your 30% quit rate (significantly outperforming the industry average of ~15-20%), you possess a stronger lever: **Value-Based Contracting**.

**The Path to Profitability: Value-Based Care (VBC)**
Medicaid payers are increasingly motivated to shift from fee-for-service to outcomes-based models because smoking cessation directly reduces long-term cardiovascular and respiratory costs.

1.  **Propose a Shared Savings Model:** Instead of demanding a higher base rate, keep the $60 base but negotiate a performance bonus.
    *   *Example Structure:* $60 base + $200 one-time bonus for every member who remains smoke-free at 6 months.
    *   *Math:* If your 30% quit rate holds, for every 100 users, you earn $6,000 in base revenue + $6,000 in bonuses (30 users x $200). This effectively doubles your revenue per successful outcome, raising your blended ARPU significantly without requiring a legislative rate change.

2.  **Pilot the VBC Model Immediately:** Use your current 500-user pilot data to model the payer's ROI. Show them that for every $1 they spend on QuitAI, they save $X in avoided emergency room visits and chronic disease management. Frame the conversation around *their* savings, not your costs.

3.  **Optimize the Coaching Mix:** If the $40 coaching cost is rigid, explore a hybrid model where AI handles early-stage engagement and only high-risk users trigger human coaching. This could lower the blended cost per user, widening the $10 margin while maintaining the 30% quit rate.

**Immediate Action Plan**
1.  **Pause broad expansion** until a VBC term sheet is signed; scaling on a $10 margin with $100k cash is insolvency risk.
2.  **Draft a Value-Based Proposal** highlighting the 30% quit rate vs. the 15% standard, quantifying the payer's potential savings.
3.  **Target a specific Medicaid Managed Care Organization (MCO)** in Ohio for a pilot VBC contract rather than seeking a statewide rate change immediately.

Your superior outcomes are your pricing power; monetize the result, not just the activity.
