### Critical Compliance & Legal Questions (GDPR & Data Sovereignty)

Before making an architectural decision, you must engage a qualified data protection lawyer or DPO in the UK and Germany to address the following specific questions:

**GDPR & Data Classification**
1.  **Biometric Data Classification:** Under GDPR Article 9, facial templates are "special category data." Does storing this locally on a device physically located in a customer's data center change our status as a "Processor" versus a "Joint Controller," and how does this shift liability in the event of a physical breach?
2.  **Right to Erasure (Article 17):** If a customer requests the deletion of their biometric data, what is the technical and legal protocol for ensuring deletion across 500+ distributed, offline devices? Does local storage make compliance practically impossible within the statutory one-month timeframe?
3.  **Data Minimization:** Does local storage of raw templates violate the principle of data minimization compared to storing only irreversible hashes or encrypted vectors in a centralized, hardened environment?

**UK & Germany Specifics**
4.  **German BDSG (Federal Data Protection Act):** Does the German implementation of GDPR impose stricter requirements on biometric processing for access control that specifically mandate centralized audit logs which local storage might obscure?
5.  **UK Post-Brexit Regulations:** Are there specific UK ICO guidance notes regarding biometric data sovereignty that require data to remain within UK borders, and does a hybrid architecture (local storage with remote management from outside the UK) violate cross-border transfer rules?
6.  **Physical Security vs. Cyber Security:** In the eyes of local regulators, does shifting risk from "cyber attack" to "physical theft" alter the required Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs)? Specifically, does local storage require us to mandate specific physical security certifications (e.g., ISO 27001 physical controls) from our customers to remain compliant?

**Liability & Insurance Interplay**
