### Recommended Pricing Structure: Tiered Risk-Based Model

Do not charge a flat $3,500 fee. A flat fee forces you to subsidize high-risk, high-volume migrations with low-risk ones, and it fails to account for the catastrophic cost of a breach relative to your margin. With a $1,200 base cost and $50,000+ breach risk, your current model offers a 65% gross margin but exposes you to unlimited downside if a clinic has complex, dirty data that increases breach probability.

Adopt a **Tiered Record-Count Model with a Mandatory Compliance Audit**. This aligns price with value (number of records moved) and risk (complexity of data).

**1. The "Pre-Migration Audit" (Mandatory Gatekeeper)**
Before quoting a final price, require a paid technical audit ($500, credited toward migration). This assesses the legacy system's data integrity.
*   **Why:** You cannot price risk you haven't measured. If the legacy data is corrupted or unstructured, the breach risk spikes. This fee filters out non-serious clients and covers the initial compliance officer time.

**2. Tiered Pricing Tiers (Per Practice)**
Replace the flat fee with volume-based tiers that scale your revenue against the potential blast radius of a breach.

*   **Tier 1: Starter (Up to 2,000 records)**
    *   **Price:** $2,250
    *   **Logic:** Lower absolute risk due to volume. Price covers costs ($1,200) + $1,050 margin. Suitable for solo practitioners.
*   **Tier 2: Growth (2,001 – 5,000 records)**
    *   **Price:** $4,500
    *   **Logic:** Your current target. The price increases by >25% over the flat fee to account for the higher probability of data anomalies in larger sets. Margin: $3,300.
*   **Tier 3: Enterprise (5,001+ records)**
    *   **Price:** $4,500 base + $0.40 per additional record.
    *   **Logic:** Linear scaling prevents margin erosion. Large clinics have larger IT budgets and perceive higher value in a guaranteed switch.

**3. Risk Mitigation & Liability Strategy**
*   **Do Not Bundle into Subscription:** Keep migration as a distinct, one-time professional service. Bundling dilutes the perceived value of the migration and complicates churn metrics if a client leaves early.
*   **Liability Coverage:** Do not sell "additional liability coverage" as an upsell; this implies you are negotiating the safety of patient data. Instead, make **Proof of Cyber Liability Insurance** ($1M minimum) a mandatory prerequisite for the client *before* migration begins. Include a clause in the Master Services Agreement (MSA) that caps your liability at 2x the service fee, except in cases of gross negligence. This protects your balance sheet without appearing to "sell safety."

**Financial Impact on Pilot:**
Assuming a mix of clinic sizes, this tiered approach likely increases your average revenue per user (ARPU) from $3,500 to ~$4,100 while filtering out high-risk/low-budget clients during the audit phase. It ensures that every migration carries a margin buffer ($2,000+) that can be allocated to a self-insurance reserve fund for potential incidents.

This structure respects the $10k IT budget constraint for small players (Tier 1 is affordable) while capturing appropriate value from larger clinics that impose greater risk on your infrastructure.

*Note: Ensure your MSA explicitly defines "gross negligence" to prevent standard technical glitches from triggering uncapped liability. Consult legal counsel to finalize the liability cap language specific to NY health regulations.*
