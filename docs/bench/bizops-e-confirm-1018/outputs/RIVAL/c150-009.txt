### TrueCrimecast Risk-Based Cash Reserve Policy

**Recommended Reserve Allocation: 15% of Monthly Revenue**
You should immediately establish a restricted "Legal Defense Fund" and contribute **$6,000 per month** (15% of your $40,000 revenue) to this account.

**The Logic:**
Your primary risk exposure is not the judgment amount, but the defense cost. Your consultant estimates $50,000 per case. A plausible worst-case scenario for a niche network relying on anonymous tips is two simultaneous lawsuits within a short window, totaling **$100,000** in immediate legal fees before any court ruling.

At a contribution rate of $6,000/month:
*   **6 Months:** You will accumulate ~$36,000 (covering one strong defense).
*   **17 Months:** You will accumulate ~$102,000 (covering the dual-lawsuit worst case).

Given you currently hold $200,000, you can jumpstart this fund by moving **$100,000** (50% of current cash) into the restricted account immediately. This secures your "two-suit" survival cap on Day 1. You then replenish or grow this fund via the ongoing 15% monthly contribution. If the fund hits a cap of $150,000 (providing a buffer beyond the $100k estimate), you can pause contributions and redirect the 15% toward growth or dividends until the balance dips below the threshold.

**Why 15%?**
Setting aside less (e.g., 5% or $2,000/month) would take 50 months to build a sufficient shield, leaving you dangerously exposed during your growth phase. Setting aside more (e.g., 25%) would unnecessarily starve your production quality and marketing, which are your best defenses against obscurity and stagnation. Since your production costs are fixed at $25,000, this reserve comes from your $15,000 monthly surplus, leaving $9,000/month available for reinvestment or dividends while you build the shield.

### Triggers for Hiring a Specialized Media Liability Attorney

Do not wait for a summons to engage specialized counsel. Retain a media liability attorney on a **monthly retainer** immediately if any of the following three factors occur:

1.  **The "Pattern of Malice" Indicator:** You receive two or more formal cease-and-desist letters or credible threat-of-litigation notices from distinct subjects within a rolling 90-day period. This suggests a coordinated effort or a systemic flaw in your verification process that requires immediate procedural auditing.
2.  **Source Volatility Spike:** Your internal data shows that more than 20% of your published stories in a quarter rely on sources who subsequently refuse to go on record, retract statements, or cannot be reached for follow-up verification. This increases the probability of a "actual malice" finding in court, drastically raising your risk profile.
3.  **Content Expansion:** You plan to expand beyond "unsolved cases" into naming specific living suspects in active investigations or publishing unredacted documents. This shifts your risk from negligence to high-stakes defamation, requiring pre-publication review protocols that general counsel cannot provide.

If none of these triggers are present, continue building the cash reserve. The cash is your shield; the specialized attorney is the upgrade you buy when the enemy changes tactics.

*Note: This policy assumes your current $200k cash position is liquid and unrestricted. Do not dip into this reserve for operational costs unless absolutely necessary to keep the lights on.*
