1.  **UK/EU (GDPR & UK GDPR):** Voice data used for unique identification is often classified as **biometric data** (Special Category Data). Processing Special Category Data for children generally requires **explicit consent** (often from a holder of parental responsibility) and a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA). It mandates **verifiable parental consent** prior to collection and limits data retention to what is reasonably necessary.
