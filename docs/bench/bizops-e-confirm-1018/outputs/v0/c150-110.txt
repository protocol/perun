To make your engagement with counsel efficient and to clarify your internal risk posture without making legal conclusions, prepare the following artifacts:

1.  **Workflow Diagram:** Map the exact user journey from "AI Suggestion" to "Claim Submission." Highlight the specific click/action the doctor takes to "approve." Does the system force a review, or is it a default-opt-out? This distinction often drives the "knowing" standard in FCA cases.
2.  **Contract Audit:** Gather your current Master Services Agreements (MSAs). Specifically, isolate clauses related to:

3.  **Accuracy Data:** Compile your internal metrics on AI suggestion accuracy vs. doctor override rates. If your system suggests higher-paying codes at a statistically significant rate compared to human baselines, this is a key fact pattern for the attorney.
