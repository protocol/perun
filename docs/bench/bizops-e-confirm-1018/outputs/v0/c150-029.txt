**Regime Identification & Routing**
The relevant regime is **Florida Insurance Law**, specifically the statutory definition of an "insurance contract" versus a "service contract" or "product warranty." In many jurisdictions, including Florida, the legal distinction often hinges on whether the primary purpose of the contract is to indemnify against a fortuitous event (insurance) or to guarantee the quality/performance of the product itself (warranty). Promising to pay for *third-party damage* or consequential loss (water damage to the building) rather than just repairing or replacing the *device* frequently crosses the line into insurance, regardless of the payout cap.
