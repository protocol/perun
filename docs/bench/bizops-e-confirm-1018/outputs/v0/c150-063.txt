**Lane R: Regulation-Dependent Routing**
Your question regarding whether to carry inventory at cost or write it down based on regulatory risk is a **Lane R** matter. It depends entirely on the application of specific accounting standards (likely **US GAAP ASC 330** for Inventory or **ASC 905** for Agriculture/Biological Assets, and **ASC 450** for Loss Contingencies) to your specific facts.

*   **The Regime:** The determination of "impairment" relies on the "lower of cost or net realizable value" (LCM/NRV) rule under GAAP, or potentially fair value measurements under **ASC 820** if these are considered specialized biological assets.
*   **The Risk:** Incorrectly carrying assets at cost when impairment indicators (like long timelines or rejection risk) exist can lead to material misstatement, auditor qualification, and securities fraud implications during your Series A raise.
*   **Action Required:** You must route this immediately to your **external auditors** and a **specialized biotech CPA**. Do not set this policy internally.

**Preparation for Your Professional Consultation**
To make your engagement with the CPA/auditor efficient before your Series A close, prepare the following facts and documents. Do not attempt to calculate the impairment yourself; provide the inputs for their judgment:

1.  **Contract Terms:** Provide the specific clauses regarding title transfer and payment triggers for the three major contracts. Does payment occur upon *delivery*, *approval*, or *milestone*?
2.  **Probability Assessment:** Document any internal or external data regarding the historical approval success rate for similar enzymes in your specific therapeutic area. (Auditors will need a quantitative basis for "risk of rejection").
3.  **Net Realizable Value (NRV) Inputs:** Gather the estimated selling price upon approval minus the estimated costs of completion and disposal. If the regulatory timeline extends beyond the contract delivery date, are there penalty clauses?
4.  **Alternative Use Analysis:** Can the materials/labor tied up in these three contracts be repurposed for other clients if these specific approvals fail? (Assets with no alternative use face higher impairment risk).
5.  **Time-Sensitive Item:** Your auditors need the **valuation policy draft** immediately. Do not wait for the final number; ask them to review the *methodology* you propose to use for determining NRV given the 14–18 month horizon.
