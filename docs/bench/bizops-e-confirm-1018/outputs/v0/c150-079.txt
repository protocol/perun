### Lane A: Runway Arithmetic & Scenario Modeling

You can model your runway using the formula: `Runway (months) = Cash Balance ÷ Net Monthly Burn`. Your current baseline is `$1,800,000 ÷ $150,000 = 12 months`. Since revenue recognition is delayed 18 months, you have a 6-month funding gap under current conditions.

Apply your specific numbers to these scenarios:

**1. Staff Reduction (30%)**
*   **Assumption:** Staff costs are the primary driver of burn. If 30% of the team (6 employees) is cut, assume a proportional reduction in burn, less any severance costs (one-time cash outflow).
*   **Formula:** `New Burn = Current Burn × (1 - 0.30)` → `$150,000 × 0.70 = $105,000`.
*   **Result:** `$1,800,000 ÷ $105,000 ≈ 17.1 months`.
*   **Read:** This barely covers the 18-month delay. You must stress-test the "severance cost" input; a large one-time payout could reduce this runway significantly.

**2. Zero Marketing Spend**
*   **Assumption:** Marketing is a distinct line item within the $150k burn.
*   **Formula:** `New Burn = $150,000 - Marketing Spend`.
*   **Application:** If marketing is $20k/month, new burn is $130k. Runway becomes `~13.8 months`. If marketing is $50k, runway becomes `18 months`.
*   **Read:** This only solves the problem if marketing constitutes >33% of your current burn.

**3. Bridge Round (Down Valuation)**
*   **Arithmetic Limit:** You cannot calculate the runway impact without two user-supplied inputs: (1) the amount raised and (2) the new burn rate post-raise (often higher due to diligence/legal costs).
*   **Formula:** `New Runway = (Current Cash + Raise Amount) ÷ (Current Burn + Transaction Costs)`.
*   **Decision Aid:** If you raise $1M with $50k in transaction costs, and burn stays at $150k: `($1.8M + $1M - $50k) ÷ $150k = 18.3 months`.

**Key Assumptions to Stress-Test for Lenders:**
*   **FDA Timeline Variance:** Model 18, 24, and 30-month delays.
*   **Severance Cash Drag:** The immediate cash hit of layoffs vs. monthly savings.
*   **Post-Clearance Ramp:** Time from clearance to first dollar collected (sales cycles in elderly care can be long).

### Lane R: Regulatory & Disclosure Obligations

**Regime:** US Securities Laws (SEC Regulations D, S-K) and Fiduciary Duty principles.

**Routing Required:** You asked about "specific financial disclosures" to investors regarding delays and down rounds. This is a securities compliance matter.
*   **Why Route:** The content, timing, and format of disclosures depend on your specific charter, the terms of your original Seed SAFE/Equity agreements, and state corporate law. Incorrect disclosure in a down round or material delay scenario can trigger fraud allegations or breach of fiduciary duty.
*   **Action:** Engage securities counsel immediately. Do not draft investor updates regarding the valuation cap adjustment or material adverse changes (the FDA delay) without legal review.

**Design-Time Triage (Medical Device):**

However, any change to the device software or hardware intended to speed up clearance may require a new regulatory determination. Route any product modification plans to your regulatory consultant before engineering begins.
