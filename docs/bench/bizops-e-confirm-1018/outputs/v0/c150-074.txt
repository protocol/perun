### Lane A: Unit Economics & Break-Even Analysis

We can model the arithmetic to see where the insurance premium increase offsets your infrastructure savings.

**The Formula**
To find the break-even point, we compare the **Total Annual Infrastructure Savings** against the **Total Annual Insurance Cost Increase**.
*   `Infrastructure Savings = Savings per Unit × Total Units`
*   `Insurance Increase = Current Premium × Percentage Increase`
*   `Net Impact = Infrastructure Savings − Insurance Increase`

**Applying Your Numbers**
1.  **Infrastructure Savings:** You project $15 savings per unit annually across 500 units.
    *   $15 × 500 = **$7,500 total annual savings**.
2.  **Insurance Increase Scenario:** Your current premium is $85,000. You mentioned a reported 25% increase.
    *   $85,000 × 0.25 = **$21,250 total annual increase**.
3.  **Net Impact at 25% Hike:**
    *   $7,500 (savings) − $21,250 (cost) = **−$13,750**.
    *   *Result:* At a 25% hike, you lose $13,750 annually compared to the cloud model.

**The Break-Even Threshold**
To find the exact insurance hike percentage that wipes out your gains, we solve for $X$ (the percentage increase):
*   `$7,500 = $85,000 × X`
*   `X = 7,500 / 85,000 ≈ 0.088` or **8.8%**

**Decision Aid**
*   **Read:** If your cyber insurance premium increases by **more than 8.8%**, the local storage model destroys value based on these specific inputs. A 25% hike would result in a net loss of $13,750/year.
*   **Sensitivity:** The single input that flips this answer is the **insurance risk assessment multiplier**. If your insurer views local storage as low-risk (e.g., due to hardware encryption), the hike might be under 8.8%. If they view it as high-risk, it will likely exceed 25%.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If LTV impact is negative (as it is here at 25%), do not proceed with the architectural change unless non-financial factors (e.g., customer mandate for local data) override the unit economics.
*   **Next Step:** Before deciding, request a formal "what-if" quote from your broker for the local-storage architecture to get a verified percentage rather than an estimate.
