**2. Industry Standard Structures for Regulatory Lag**
B2B compliance software vendors typically handle the "legislative lag" risk (the time between a law passing and software updating) through specific contractual mechanisms rather than accepting unlimited liability. Common structures include:
*   **"Best Efforts" vs. "Guaranteed Compliance":** Contracts usually state the vendor will use *commercially reasonable efforts* to update systems within a defined window (e.g., 48–72 hours of official guidance), rather than guaranteeing instantaneous compliance.
*   **Liability Carve-outs:** Standard clauses often exclude liability for losses resulting from: (a) client failure to apply updates, (b) reliance on preliminary/unofficial guidance, or (c) legislative changes enacted with less than a statutory minimum notice period.
*   **Remedy Limitation:** Instead of cash damages for overpayments, contracts may limit remedies to: (a) re-processing the return at no cost, and (b) paying any resulting *penalties/interest* charged by the tax authority (capped), but not the tax itself (which the client legally owes regardless).
