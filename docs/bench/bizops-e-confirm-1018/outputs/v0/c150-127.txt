Your request asks for "standard" liability caps and specific indemnity language for autonomous vehicle (AV) contracts with municipal authorities. This falls squarely into **Lane R**. 2.  **Insurance Market Conditions:** What is "standard" shifts based on current insurer appetite for AV risk, which is highly volatile.
3.  **Specific Risk Allocation:** The definition of "catastrophic failure" vs. "operator negligence" changes the legal exposure.
