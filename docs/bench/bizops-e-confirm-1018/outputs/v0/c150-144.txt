**Regimes to Name:**
The processing of SSNs in New York commercial real estate likely triggers the following regimes:
1.  **New York SHIELD Act (Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security Act):** Mandates specific "reasonable safeguards" for the private information of NY residents, including SSNs.
2.  **NY General Business Law § 899-aa:** Requires notification to consumers and the state attorney general in the event of a breach involving private information.
3.  **GLBA (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act):** If your clients or their tenants are considered "consumers" obtaining financial products/services, this federal law may apply to the handling of nonpublic personal information.
4.  **Contractual Obligations:** Your enterprise client's demand for assurance creates a contractual compliance requirement that may exceed statutory minimums.

**Action Required:**
You must engage a privacy attorney immediately to address the client's demand and assess your exposure. Do not attempt to self-certify compliance.
