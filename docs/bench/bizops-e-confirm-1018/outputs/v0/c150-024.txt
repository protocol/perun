### Lane R: Regulation & Structuring
**Regime Identified:** Securities Law (Fundraising) and Tax/Commercial Law (Lease Structuring).

1.  **Securities/Fundraising:** You mentioned preparing for a Series B. Selling equity or SAFEs is a **securities offering**. While discussing valuation metrics (ARR) is strategic, the actual terms, disclosures, and instrument mechanics (caps, discounts, pro-rata rights) must be handled by **securities counsel**. Do not draft term sheets or offer letters without legal review.
2.  **Tax & Lease Structure:** You asked if you need a tax advisor. **Yes.**
    *   **Why:** Lease agreements trigger specific tax treatments (operating lease vs. capital lease/finance lease) affecting revenue recognition (ASC 842) and depreciation schedules. Incorrect classification can lead to restatements during investor due diligence.
    *   **Action:** Engage a **CPA or tax attorney** to draft the master lease agreement and determine sales tax nexus implications in Texas and future states. Do not rely on template contracts for hardware leasing.

**Summary Recommendation:**
Mathematically, leasing reduces immediate cash and increases burn unless you secure the $500k working capital. Strategically, it may boost ARR, but only if you survive the cash trough. Legally, you must route the lease structure and Series B preparation to licensed professionals before executing.
