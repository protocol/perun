### Cash Flow Projection & Runway Analysis

**Your Inputs:**
*   **Starting Cash:** $400,000
*   **Monthly Burn:** $48,000 ($45k team + $3k hosting)
*   **Revenue per Deal:** $25,000
*   **Collection Lag:** 9 months (sales cycle) + 2 months (payment delay) = **11 months from start of cycle to cash in hand.**
*   **Assumption:** For this projection, we assume any deal "closed" in the next 12 months was already in the pipeline at $t=0$. Therefore, cash from deals closed in Months 1–12 will not arrive until Month 12 or later (specifically, a deal closed in Month 1 pays in Month 12; a deal closed in Month 2 pays in Month 13).

**The Formula:**
`Ending Cash = Starting Cash - (Monthly Burn × Months) + (Cumulative Deals Closed × $25,000 collected after 11-month lag)`

**Scenario A: 1 Deal Closed (in Month 1)**
*   **Cash In:** $25,000 arrives in Month 12.
*   **Cash Out (12 months):** $48,000 × 12 = $576,000.
*   **Net Position at Month 12:** $400,000 - $576,000 + $25,000 = **-$151,000**.
*   **Runway:** You run out of cash in **Month 8** ($400k / $48k ≈ 8.3 months), well before the payment arrives.

**Scenario B: 2 Deals Closed (e.g., Month 1 and Month 6)**
*   **Cash In:** Deal 1 ($25k) arrives Month 12. Deal 2 ($25k) arrives Month 17 (outside 12-month window).
*   **Total Cash In (12 months):** $25,000.
*   **Net Position at Month 12:** Same as Scenario A (**-$151,000**).
*   **Runway:** Still runs out in **Month 8**. The second deal's revenue does not arrive in time to save the current runway.

**Scenario C: 3 Deals Closed (e.g., Months 1, 4, 8)**
*   **Cash In:** Deal 1 ($25k) arrives Month 12. Deals 2 & 3 arrive in Months 15 and 19 (outside window).
*   **Total Cash In (12 months):** $25,000.
*   **Net Position at Month 12:** Same as Scenario A (**-$151,000**).
*   **Runway:** Still runs out in **Month 8**.

**Sensitivity Check (Best Case: All 3 deals closed immediately and paid instantly)**
Even if you collected all $75,000 today: $400k + $75k - $576k = **-$101,000**. You still cannot survive 12 months on current economics without new capital or reduced burn.
