**Regime Identification:** The relevant regime is US State Money Transmission Laws (often modeled after the Uniform Money Services Act) and federal guidance from FinCEN regarding "money transmission" and "exempting mechanisms." The core legal determination is whether holding funds "momentarily" in a smart contract while awaiting a conditional release constitutes "accepting value for the purpose of transmitting" it, or if it falls under a specific exemption (e.g., as a software provider or an agent of the payee).
