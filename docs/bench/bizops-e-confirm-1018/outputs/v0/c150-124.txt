### Lane A: Unit Economics & Arithmetic Application

**Current Baseline (per unit):**
*   **Revenue:** $2,400
*   **COGS (Manufacturing):** $850
*   **Gross Profit:** $1,550 (64.6% margin)
*   **Allocated Insurance Cost:** $18,000 / 300 units = $60/unit
*   **Adjusted Gross Profit (with insurance):** $1,490
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $400
*   **Contribution Margin:** $1,090 ($1,490 - $400)

**Scenario: Local Storage (Premium Increase)**
*   **New Annual Premium:** $25,200
*   **New Allocated Insurance Cost:** $25,200 / 300 units = $84/unit
*   **Incremental Cost:** $24/unit ($84 - $60)
*   **New Adjusted Gross Profit:** $1,466 ($2,400 - $850 - $84)
*   **New Contribution Margin:** $1,066 ($1,466 - $400)

**Analysis of the "Kill" Threshold:**
The insurance hike reduces your contribution margin by **$24 per unit** (a 2.2% reduction in contribution, not the full 4% gross margin swing, because CAC is fixed per unit).
*   **Break-Even Logic:** To absorb this $24 cost without losing your current 8% net margin, you must either:
    1.  Raise price by $24 (to $2,424).
    2.  Reduce manufacturing COGS by $24 (to $826).
    3.  Reduce CAC by $24 (to $376).
    4.  Accept the margin compression if volume scales sufficiently to cover fixed overhead.

**Decision Aid:**
The $24/unit increase does not mathematically "kill" the unit economics in isolation, as the contribution margin remains positive ($1,066). The viability depends entirely on the **Cloud Alternative Cost**.
*   **The Flip Input:** If the engineering, infrastructure, and ongoing maintenance cost to securely encrypt and store templates in the cloud exceeds **$24 per unit sold** (amortized over 300 units), then the insurance hike is the cheaper option.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If Cloud Cost > $7,200/year ($24 × 300), local storage (even with higher insurance) is the strictly superior financial choice, assuming no other liability risks.

**Next Step:** Calculate the fully loaded annual cost of the cloud architecture (dev time + server costs + security audits). If that figure is under $7,200, the cloud route preserves more cash flow. If over $7,200, the insurance premium is the cost-effective path.

### Lane R: Regulation & Liability Routing

**Regime Identified:** Data Privacy & Security Liability (Virginia Biometric Data / General Liability Insurance Contract Law).

**Routing Required:**
The decision involves interpreting your insurance policy's definition of "physical security risk" versus "cybersecurity risk" and potential compliance with Virginia's biometric data laws (if applicable) or sector-specific federal regulations for data centers.
*   **Action:** Consult your insurance broker or coverage counsel to confirm if the 40% hike is a binding quote or a risk assessment, and whether cloud storage shifts liability to a "cyber" policy (which may have different limits/exclusions) rather than general liability.
*   **Action:** Consult legal counsel to determine if local storage of biometric templates violates any specific contractual obligations your data center customers require, which could trigger breaches larger than the insurance premium difference.

**Note:** The arithmetic above assumes volume stays constant at 300 units; if the storage method affects sales velocity, recalculate CAC impact.
**Note:** Verify if the "cloud" solution introduces new compliance regimes (e.g., data residency) that carry their own hidden costs.
