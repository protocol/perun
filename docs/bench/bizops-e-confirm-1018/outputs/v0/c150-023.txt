**3. Break-Even Analysis for EU Expansion**

*   **Formula:** `Monthly Units Needed = Monthly Fixed Cost ÷ Contribution Margin`

### Lane R: Regulatory Routing
You asked if you can decide based on these numbers. **No.** The inputs for the arithmetic above (specifically the necessity of a Data Protection Officer and the $150k infrastructure rebuild) are legal conclusions, not facts.

*   **Regime:** General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to processing genetic data of individuals in the EU.
*   **Genetic Data Status:** Under GDPR, genetic data is "special category data," triggering stricter processing conditions.
*   **DPO Requirement:** Whether you *must* hire a Data Protection Officer depends on whether your core activities involve "large scale" processing of special category data. This is a legal determination, not a revenue threshold. A lawyer must assess if 5,000 users (or projected EU users) constitutes "large scale" in the eyes of German/French regulators.
*   **Infrastructure:** The requirement for separate data silos vs. other compliance mechanisms (like Standard Contractual Clauses with specific technical safeguards) is a legal strategy, not a fixed cost.

**Actionable Next Steps:**
1.  **Do not spend the $150,000 yet.** Engage privacy counsel specializing in EU health/genetic data to validate the "DPO required" and "separate silo" assumptions.
2.  **Scenario Plan:** Ask counsel for a "minimum viable compliance" scope. If the legal requirement is less than your $20k/month estimate, the break-even volume (269 kits) drops significantly.
3.  **Decision Threshold:** Proceed with expansion only if counsel confirms the regulatory scope AND your sales forecast exceeds the break-even volume derived from the *verified* compliance costs.

**Note:** Genetic testing faces additional EU regulations beyond GDPR (e.g., In Vitro Diagnostic Regulation - IVDR) which may affect product market access entirely.
**Note:** Do not treat the $150k/$20k estimates as fixed until a professional validates the regulatory baseline.
