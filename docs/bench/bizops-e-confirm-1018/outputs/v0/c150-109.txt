The core legal question is whether your service constitutes "accepting value for the purpose of transmitting it" or exercising "control" over the assets.

4.  **State-Specific Exposure:** List your current user count and volume per state, specifically isolating New York and Texas, as these jurisdictions have the most aggressive enforcement and distinct licensing thresholds.
