**Lane R: Regulation-Dependent (Route Immediately)**

Your question regarding whether SafeSim VR's internal certification credits satisfy federal OSHA compliance reporting requirements is a **Lane R** matter. It depends entirely on a legal and regulatory determination by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) and potentially accredited third-party bodies.

**The Regime:** The relevant regime is the **Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970** and specific OSHA standards (e.g., 29 CFR 1910.120 for hazardous waste operations). OSHA strictly defines who can issue valid training certifications and what content/methods constitute compliant training. In many high-risk industries, OSHA requires training to be conducted by accredited organizations or to meet very specific curriculum and instructor qualifications that an internal software validation may not automatically satisfy.

**The Route:** You must consult with an **employment law attorney specializing in OSHA compliance** or a **certified safety professional (CSP)** with legal standing before making any claims to enterprise clients. Do not proceed with your current go-to-market plan assuming your internal credits are valid. If you market your certification as "OSHA-compliant" without explicit verification, you risk severe legal liability, fines, and the collapse of customer trust if the credentials are later deemed invalid.
