The spec demonstrates robust handling of most harm categories. It explicitly addresses **DISC** by labeling outputs as AI-generated, **RELY** by providing cited sources, confidence levels, and one-tap overrides, and **AUTO** by requiring human approval for high-value irreversible actions. Interface design avoids **DARK** patterns through opt-in consent and easy cancellation.

However, the spec admits a specific **BIAS** issue: error rates are "twice as high" for suspension-related quotes following heavy rain, disproportionately affecting riders with those specific symptoms. While the team plans to delay this category's launch, the harm is currently present in the system logic described. Additionally, while confidence levels are mentioned, the spec does not define them as coarse, calibrated tiers (e.g., "Verified" vs. "Unverified") rather than raw numbers, leaving a minor **RELY** risk if users misinterpret precision. Because the spec acknowledges an active, unmitigated disparity in output quality for a specific user segment at launch, the feature cannot proceed without resolving this known inequity.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
