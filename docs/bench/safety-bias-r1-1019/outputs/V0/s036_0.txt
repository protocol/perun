The spec explicitly describes two present harms. First, **RELY**: the design states users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing any easy override for a decision that incurs financial charges. Second, **AUTO**: the system "executes irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically charging fees and finalizing cancellations automatically at draw time, which constitutes high-stakes automation without a human-in-the-loop gate.

Other categories are not flagged because the spec indicates they are handled: **DISC** is addressed by the team's stance that the output is "purely random" (though this rationale may be legally insufficient, the spec asserts the omission is intentional based on this belief, not an oversight of AI generation); **DARK** is mitigated by "no timers and one-tap cancel"; **BIAS** and **HALLU** are claimed to be covered by testing and source labeling, respectively. However, the presence of un-gated irreversible financial actions and the lack of an appeal mechanism are critical failures under the safety floor.

HARMS: RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
