The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: it includes AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), provides citations and confidence levels with override/appeal mechanisms (mitigating HALLU and RELY), and uses consensual onboarding without pressure (mitigating DARK). The voting mechanism ensures humans make the final selection, preventing irreversible automated decisions (mitigating AUTO).

However, the spec describes a test where "11% of older users and 11% of younger users" were flagged for low engagement to test a nudge. While the percentages are identical, explicitly segmenting and targeting users by age group for engagement interventions raises a potential BIAS concern regarding unfair treatment or systematic disadvantage based on age, even if the outcome rates shown are equal. The spec does not explain why age-based segmentation was chosen or confirm that the nudge content itself was neutral across groups. Since the spec describes this age-based targeting without confirming fairness safeguards beyond the equal flag rate, BIAS is flagged as present in the design description.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
