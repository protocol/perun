The spec explicitly describes several unmitigated harms. First, **RELY** is present: while confidence labels exist, users "cannot override or appeal the result," preventing correction of errors. Second, **DISC** is confirmed: the system "does not tell users the content was written by AI." Third, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the app "executes irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically auto-ordering items and permanently deleting user data.

Regarding **BIAS**, the spec notes equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users for the *same* variety set. This describes a test outcome showing parity in this specific slice, not a systematic disadvantage against a group as defined by the harm category; thus, BIAS is not flagged based strictly on the provided text. **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in, unticked consent without timers. **HALLU** is mitigated by the requirement that "every claim shows its source."

However, the combination of undisclosed AI generation, the inability to override recommendations, and the execution of irreversible financial and data actions without human oversight creates unacceptable risk.

HARMS: RELY, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
