The spec explicitly presents several unmitigated harms. First, **HALLU** is present: the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," risking fabricated routes being treated as truth. Second, **RELY** is flagged because "users cannot override or appeal the result," creating dangerous automation bias where families must trust potentially erroneous logistics. Third, **AUTO** is critical: the tool "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Assigning bus routes is a high-stakes operational decision affecting child safety and attendance; executing this without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for irreversible actions.

While the spec addresses **DISC** (via AI labels) and **DARK** (via clear consent/no timers), and notes equal conflict flagging rates (suggesting **BIAS** is monitored), the combination of unverified outputs presented as fact, the inability to appeal, and fully autonomous execution of high-stakes decisions creates unacceptable risk. The pilot being a "live test" of operational limits does not justify removing human gates for actions impacting student transportation.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
