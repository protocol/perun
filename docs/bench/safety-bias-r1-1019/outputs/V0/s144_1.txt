The spec explicitly flags **BIAS**: the error rate is "twice as high" for self-reported beginners. While a recheck quiz is added, the systemic disadvantage is admitted in the text.

The spec confirms **RELY**: it states "Users cannot override or appeal the result." This creates a hard lock on automation bias with no human escape hatch.

The spec confirms **AUTO**: it describes executing "irreversible actions with no human approval" to lock inventory and send reservations. This is a high-stakes action (financial commitment/inventory allocation) running fully autonomously.

The spec indicates **DISC**, **HALLU**, and **DARK** are handled: outputs are labeled AI-generated, sources/confidence are cited, and consent is opt-in without dark patterns.

However, the combination of admitted bias, the inability to override, and irreversible automated execution without a human gate constitutes a critical safety failure under the product output safety framework. The "recheck quiz" does not mitigate the lack of a human-in-the-loop for the final irreversible action, nor does it resolve the underlying bias disparity.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
