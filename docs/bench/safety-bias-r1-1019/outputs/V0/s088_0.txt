The spec explicitly confirms several harms. The planner "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," creating a risk of **HALLU** (unverified claims presented as fact) and **RELY** (inviting over-trust by hiding uncertainty). The spec admits "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," triggering **DISC**. The accessible pickup feature uses a "ten-minute timer" with "pre-ticked" consent, a clear **DARK** pattern pressuring users against their interest.

While **BIAS** was investigated (usability review showed equal hesitation across ages) and **AUTO** is handled (human sign-off exists for irreversible steps), the four flagged categories represent active, unmitigated risks in the current design. Presenting unverified routing as flat fact without disclosure or confidence indicators, combined with deceptive consent mechanics for accessibility, requires remediation before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
