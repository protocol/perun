The spec explicitly acknowledges a systemic disadvantage: the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, and the team states they "accept for now" this discrepancy. This is a confirmed **BIAS** harm that must be addressed before launch; accepting known unfair outcomes violates the safety floor.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal; **RELY** is addressed by ensuring field staff keep final say; **DISC** is covered by labeling outputs as AI-generated; **DARK** patterns are avoided via unticked consent and no timers; **AUTO** is prevented by requiring dispatcher approval for escalations.

However, the explicit acceptance of biased outcomes without a remediation plan or measurement threshold constitutes a critical failure. The spec admits the harm exists and chooses to ship it anyway.
