The spec describes a feature with strong safeguards: cited sources, defined confidence levels, easy overrides/appeals, visible AI labels, and non-deceptive consent flows. These explicitly address HALLU, RELY, DISC, and DARK.

However, the spec confirms a critical failure in the **AUTO** category. It states the pilot includes an "automated triage flow that acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." While the spec frames the resulting demographic flags (11% of older and younger users) as a signal to "simplify the interface," it does not state that the automated action itself has been disabled or gated behind human review for the launch. Shipping a feature where an automated decision triggers immediate action without human oversight constitutes unsafe automation of a high-stakes process (triage affecting user experience/access), regardless of the demographic parity in the error rate. The lack of a human-in-the-loop for this specific flow violates the safety floor for automated actions.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
