The spec explicitly handles several harm categories: uncertainty is shown via stated confidence levels (mitigating RELY); overrides and appeals are one-tap (mitigating RELY); high-stakes actions like payments require human sign-off (mitigating AUTO); and dark patterns are avoided via unticked consent and no timers (mitigating DARK). The internal testing data regarding age groups appears to be a workload balancing metric rather than evidence of systematic disadvantage, so BIAS is not flagged based strictly on the text provided.

However, the spec states the system "consciously presents rankings without any model branding—nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Since this tool scores financial need and camp fit to determine scholarship eligibility, users cannot distinguish machine-generated decisions from human ones. This directly matches the DISC category definition.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
