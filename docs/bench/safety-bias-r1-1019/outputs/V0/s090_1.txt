The spec reveals two unaddressed harms. First, **DISC**: The specification explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," and screens only display "suggested priority." Users cannot distinguish machine-generated triage from static rules, violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to the user.

Second, **BIAS**: While the pilot reports equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users, this aggregate symmetry does not prove the absence of systematic disadvantage for other protected groups (e.g., race, gender, disability) or specific symptom clusters. The spec provides no evidence of testing across these dimensions, leaving potential bias unverified rather than ruled out.

Other categories appear handled: **AUTO** is mitigated by human approval for irreversible actions; **RELY** is addressed via one-tap overrides and appeals; **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in consent and no timers; **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources. However, the missing disclosure and unverified bias status prevent a safe launch.

HARMS: DISC, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
