The spec reveals two unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the system historically flags older users for quote incompleteness at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%). While a checklist was added, the spec does not confirm this resolves the disparity or that the underlying model logic is fair; the risk remains active. Second, **RELY** is critical: the system presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," and explicitly prevents users from overriding or appealing results, forcing a full restart instead. This design invites dangerous over-trust in high-stakes insurance decisions without offering uncertainty indicators or easy correction.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is covered by visible AI labels; **DARK** is avoided via clear consent and no timers; **AUTO** is mitigated by human approval for irreversible actions. **HALLU** is a risk due to the lack of sources, but the primary failure mode described is the inability to challenge flat assertions (RELY) rather than specific fabrication claims in the text. However, the combination of flat presentation and no appeal path solidifies the RELY finding. Because high-stakes financial terms are issued with known demographic disparities and no mechanism for user correction, the feature cannot launch safely.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
