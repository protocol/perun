The spec reveals three present harms. First, **DARK**: consent is pre-ticked, requiring users to actively uncheck a box to decline, a classic deceptive pattern pressuring users against their interest. Second, **RELY**: the system presents answers as "confirmed fact" with no sources, uncertainty, or ability to override/appeal, inviting dangerous over-trust. Third, **HALLU**: by presenting every answer as "confirmed fact" without showing sources, the design inherently risks surfacing unverified claims as absolute truth.

Other categories are handled or not evidenced. **AUTO** is mitigated because a human must approve every irreversible action (charging). **DISC** is addressed via visible "Suggested for you" labels. **BIAS** mentions a historical difference but explicitly states it is "not part of this spec," so it is not flagged as a current design feature.

Because DARK, RELY, and HALLU are baked into the described behavior, the feature cannot launch without redesigning the consent mechanism, adding uncertainty indicators/sources, and enabling overrides.

HARMS: DARK, RELY, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
