The spec demonstrates strong controls for most harm categories: sources and confidence labels address HALLU and RELY; visible AI labels cover DISC; consent mechanics mitigate DARK; and human sign-off on irreversible steps handles AUTO.

However, the spec explicitly admits a present harm under BIAS: "the error rate is twice as high for one group." While the team plans to track this and report it in release notes, shipping a system with a known, unmitigated disparity where one group receives significantly worse outcomes constitutes a present bias risk. The proposed mitigation (tracking and disclosure) does not fix the underlying unfair outcome before launch. Since the definition of done requires managing residual risk and the spec confirms the disadvantage exists without a pre-launch fix, this flagged harm must be addressed before full deployment to prevent systematic disadvantage.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
