The spec explicitly identifies several output-safety failures. First, the algorithm flags older users for review at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), indicating systematic disadvantage based on age (BIAS). Second, the specification states the ranking list is "final" and users "cannot override or appeal the result," creating a hard barrier to correcting errors and inviting dangerous over-reliance (RELY). Third, the product intentionally omits disclosure that the content is AI-generated to maintain a "frictionless" experience, violating transparency norms where algorithmic ranking occurs (DISC).

While the spec demonstrates good controls for high-stakes actions (human approval for swaps/releases) and data collection (no dark patterns), the combination of demonstrated bias, the inability to appeal decisions, and missing AI disclosure presents unacceptable risk to users before launch. The bias metric is measured but unmitigated; the lack of an appeal mechanism makes any hallucination or error irreversible for the user; and the hidden AI nature prevents informed consent.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
