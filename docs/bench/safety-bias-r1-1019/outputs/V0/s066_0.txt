The spec reveals two unmitigated harms. First, under **RELY**, the system explicitly states "users cannot override or appeal the result." This design invites over-trust by removing human agency and correction mechanisms, violating the requirement for an easy override. Second, while the spec claims confidence labels are shown, it does not define them as coarse, text-based tiers (e.g., "Verified" vs. "Unverified") rather than raw numbers. Without explicit confirmation that these labels avoid false precision, there is a risk of **HALLU** if users misinterpret a numeric score as factual certainty, though the primary failure is the lack of appeal.

The spec successfully addresses **DISC** (visible AI labels), **DARK** (opt-in, no timers, easy cancel), and **AUTO** (human sign-off for power tools). The bias testing regarding age cohorts appears to be a measured mitigation rather than an active harm in the final design, so **BIAS** is not flagged as a current blocker based on the text provided. However, the inability to override or appeal a matching decision is a critical safety gap for a feature assigning physical labor.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
