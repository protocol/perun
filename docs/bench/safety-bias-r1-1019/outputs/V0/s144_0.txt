The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: it mitigates **BIAS** for beginners via a recheck quiz, counters **HALLU** with cited sources, reduces **RELY** by showing alternatives and confidence, satisfies **DISC** with clear AI labeling, and avoids **DARK** patterns through unticked consent and easy cancellation.

However, the spec describes a critical failure in **AUTO**. It states that once confirmed, the system "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" by locking inventory and sending reservations. For a physical good reservation that locks inventory (an action costly or hard to undo if the recommendation is wrong), executing this fully autonomously without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for high-stakes automation. The fact that the error rate is known to be higher for a specific group (even with mitigation) increases the risk of these irreversible automated errors.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
