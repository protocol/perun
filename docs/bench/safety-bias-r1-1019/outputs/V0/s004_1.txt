The spec reveals several critical output-safety failures. First, **DISC** is present: the product generates offers and answers but contains no disclosure that a model produced the output. Second, **DARK** patterns are explicitly described: a ten-minute expiration timer creates manufactured urgency, and pre-ticked consent forces users to opt-out rather than opt-in. Third, **RELY** is a failure because the spec states users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing any mechanism to correct errors or challenge decisions.

While **AUTO** is handled (a human approves every irreversible action) and **BIAS** appears addressed via identical treatment across age groups in testing, the combination of hidden AI generation, coercive interface design, and the inability to appeal constitutes a high-risk launch state. The stated "confidence level" without defining the tiers or source reliability further exacerbates the over-reliance risk.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
