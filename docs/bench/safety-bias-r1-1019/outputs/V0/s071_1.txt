The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: AUTO is mitigated by human sign-off on irreversible steps; RELY is addressed via one-tap override/appeal and cited sources; DARK patterns are avoided with plain consent and no timers.

However, two critical harms remain unaddressed in the design:

1.  **DISC (Missing AI-disclosure):** The spec states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output; it reads as a straightforward utility." Users cannot distinguish machine-generated analysis from deterministic logic, violating transparency norms for automated decision support.
2.  **BIAS (Unfair outcomes):** The spec admits the "error rate is twice as high for one group." While an appeal mechanism exists, shipping a feature with a known, unmitigated disparate impact on a specific group constitutes a systematic disadvantage that requires remediation or explicit risk acceptance before launch.

HARMS: DISC, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
