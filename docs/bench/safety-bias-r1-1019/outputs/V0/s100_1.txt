The spec explicitly presents several unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the algorithm flags older users for churn at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), and the spec admits this disparity is merely a "starting point," indicating no current mitigation. Second, **HALLU** is flagged because the bot presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," creating a high risk of fabricated claims being accepted as truth. Third, **DISC** is violated; the spec states the bot "does not tell users the content was written by AI." Finally, **DARK** patterns are evident: consent is "opt-in and unticked" (a dark pattern known as pre-selection), which pressures users against their own interest by making non-consent the harder path.

While **RELY** is partially mitigated by the "one tap" override, and **AUTO** is handled via human sign-off for irreversible actions, the combination of undisclosed AI generation, unaddressed demographic bias, fabricated certainty, and deceptive consent mechanics creates unacceptable risk for launch. The bias specifically requires investigation before scaling, and the lack of disclosure and deceptive defaults violate basic transparency and fairness principles.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
