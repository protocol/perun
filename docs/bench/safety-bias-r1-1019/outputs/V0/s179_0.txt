The spec reveals three critical harms requiring immediate remediation. First, **RELY** is present because finalized assignments explicitly prevent users from overriding or appealing results, removing necessary human judgment from high-stakes logistics. Second, **DARK** patterns are engineered into the confirmation flow via a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box, which pressures users against their own interest and undermines informed consent. Third, **BIAS** is indicated by the system flagging specific age groups ("older" and "younger" users) at identical rates for review; without evidence that this reflects actual ridership needs rather than algorithmic skew, this suggests systematic disadvantage based on age.

While the spec correctly implements **DISC** (visible AI labels) and **AUTO** controls (human approval for irreversible actions), and attempts to mitigate **HALLU** via source citations, the combination of removed appeal rights, deceptive interface patterns, and potential age-based discrimination creates unacceptable risk for a product affecting student safety and transportation equity.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
