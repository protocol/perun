The spec reveals three critical harms requiring remediation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the system flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, and the team has "accepted" this disparity as a baseline rather than mitigating it. This systematically disadvantages a protected group.

Second, **RELY** is triggered because once a route is committed, the result is final with "no appeal path." This removes the ability to correct errors or override the system, inviting dangerous over-reliance on potentially flawed automation.

Third, **DISC** is a failure: the spec states "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," yet dispatchers are making operational decisions based on these machine-generated recommendations without knowing their origin.

While **AUTO** is handled for salt/sand deployment (human sign-off required), the route committing mechanism lacks a similar gate for an action described as "final." **HALLU** and **DARK** appear addressed via source labeling and fair consent flows, respectively.

Because the spec admits to unmitigated bias, missing AI disclosure, and a lack of override mechanisms for final decisions, the feature cannot safely launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
