The spec reveals three unaddressed harms. First, **DISC**: the lottery explicitly does not tell users when content (including status messages) is AI-generated, violating transparency expectations for automated decisions. Second, **DARK**: the entry flow uses a pre-ticked consent box and a countdown timer, classic deceptive patterns that pressure users against their own interest by manufacturing urgency and hiding defaults. Third, **RELY**: while the system shows sources and confidence labels, the spec states the result is "final" with "no appeal path." This removes any easy override or recourse for vendors who believe the random selection or eligibility check failed, inviting over-reliance on a potentially flawed automated outcome without recourse.

Although **AUTO** is handled (irreversible cancellations require human approval) and **BIAS** testing was performed (duplicate flags were equal across age groups), the presence of deceptive entry mechanics, missing AI disclosure, and a rigid no-appeal policy creates unacceptable risk for a feature determining economic opportunity for small growers.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
