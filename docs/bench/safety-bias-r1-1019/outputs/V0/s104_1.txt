The spec reveals three critical output-safety failures. First, **HALLU**: the tool presents explanations "flatly" with "no source or confidence," fabricating authority for unverified claims. Second, **RELY**: users "cannot override or appeal" results, and the output is "final," creating dangerous automation bias without an escape hatch. Third, **DISC**: the system explicitly "does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating transparency norms for machine-generated health information.

While the spec claims even performance across age groups (addressing BIAS), includes human review before visibility (mitigating AUTO), and uses fair consent patterns (avoiding DARK), the combination of unverified medical assertions presented as absolute fact without disclosure or user recourse creates unacceptable risk for a health feature. The human-in-the-loop gate prevents irreversible *automation*, but it does not fix the product's fundamental design of hiding uncertainty, denying appeals, and concealing AI origin from the patient.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
