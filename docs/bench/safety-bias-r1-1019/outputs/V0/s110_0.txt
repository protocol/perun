The spec reveals two present harms requiring attention before launch.

First, **BIAS** is indicated. While the pilot data shows an identical 11% flag rate for older and younger users, the specification explicitly states the tool "does not highlight that split anywhere in the interface." This omission prevents operators from monitoring potential disparate impacts on age groups, satisfying the condition where outputs could systematically disadvantage a group without visibility.

Second, **DISC** is present. The spec notes that "nothing in the product says a model produced the output—it reads like a checklist." Since this is an AI-driven screener making recommendations that affect livelihoods, the lack of disclosure that the output is machine-generated violates the transparency requirement for this category.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by source highlighting; **RELY** is addressed via one-tap overrides; **DARK** patterns are absent due to clear consent; and **AUTO** is prevented by the human sign-off requirement for denials.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
