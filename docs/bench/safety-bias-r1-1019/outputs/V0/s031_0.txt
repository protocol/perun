The spec reveals three present harms. First, **RELY**: the app "does not tell users the content was written by AI," failing to disclose machine generation where it influences group assignments. Second, **DARK**: the captain's dashboard uses a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box," manufacturing urgency and hiding defaults to pressure users against their own interest. Third, **BIAS**: the algorithm flagged 11% of older and 11% of younger users differently than self-selection; while visible, the spec describes this systematic divergence without confirming it was mitigated or justified, indicating a potential disadvantage based on age groups.

Although **AUTO** is handled (human sign-off exists) and **HALLU** is addressed (sources and confidence labels are shown), the undisclosed AI nature, deceptive interface patterns, and unmitigated age-based divergence constitute launch blockers.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
