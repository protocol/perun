The spec explicitly flags three present harms. First, **HALLU**: the system presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," risking fabricated claims being treated as truth. Second, **RELY**: despite override capabilities, presenting unverified outputs as absolute facts invites over-trust by hiding uncertainty. Third, **AUTO**: the feature executes matching "automatically... and nobody reviews it first." While roommate matching may not always be irreversible, locking users into lease-date-specific matches without human confirmation constitutes unsafe automation of high-stakes commitments in this context.

Conversely, **DISC** is handled (visible AI label exists), **DARK** is handled (plain opt-in, no timers), and **BIAS** is claimed handled via internal testing (though external validation is absent, the spec asserts coverage). However, the combination of presenting unverified data as fact and automating high-stakes matching without a human gate creates unacceptable residual risk. The "error rates within one point" claim does not verify safety against hallucination or bias in real-world deployment.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
