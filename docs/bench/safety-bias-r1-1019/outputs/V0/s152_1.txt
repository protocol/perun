The spec demonstrates handling for several categories: it acknowledges disparity for players under eight (BIAS), requires sources and confidence labels (HALLU, RELY), mandates AI disclosure (DISC), and allows one-tap overrides (RELY).

However, two critical harms remain unaddressed:
1.  **DARK**: The sign-up flow uses a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box, explicitly pressuring users and hiding defaults against their interest.
2.  **AUTO**: The tool executes roster assignments immediately ("acts the moment the model decides") with no human review. For youth sports, assigning a child to a team is a high-stakes decision affecting safety and fairness; full automation without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor.

These issues are described as present features, not mitigated risks.

HARMS: DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
