The spec presents three specific harms that must be addressed before launch.

First, **HALLU** is present: the interface explicitly "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Presenting unverified algorithmic decisions as absolute fact without citation creates a high risk of misleading users, regardless of pilot error rates.

Second, **DARK** patterns are admitted: the intake form uses a "countdown timer" (manufactured urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (hidden default). These are deceptive designs that pressure users against their own interests, violating the requirement for voluntary, informed consent.

Third, **RELY** is triggered by the combination of presenting outputs as "confirmed fact" and the lack of visible uncertainty. While the spec notes an override exists ("one tap"), the primary presentation encourages automation bias by hiding the probabilistic nature of the tool behind a facade of certainty.

The spec successfully handles **AUTO** (human sign-off required for irreversible actions) and **DISC** (outputs are labeled AI-generated). **BIAS** is not flagged because the spec claims error rates were consistent across groups, whereas the other harms are explicitly described as current features.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
