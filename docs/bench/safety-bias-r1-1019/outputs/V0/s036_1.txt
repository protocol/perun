The spec explicitly describes two unmitigated harms. First, **AUTO** is present: the system executes irreversible financial charges and finalizes cancellations "with no human approval." For a feature moving money and locking users into non-refundable obligations, the absence of a human-in-the-loop gate or a reversible window before finalization is a critical safety failure.

Second, **DISC** is flagged: the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Even if the logic is purely random, the system makes an automated decision affecting user access and finances. Transparency standards require disclosing that an automated system determined the outcome, rather than obscuring the mechanism.

While the spec notes that confidence labels are shown (addressing **RELY**) and validation testing occurred (addressing **HALLU** and partially **BIAS**), the combination of irreversible financial automation without human oversight and the lack of algorithmic disclosure creates unacceptable risk for users. The "purely random" claim does not negate the need for disclosure or safety gates on high-stakes actions.

HARMS: AUTO, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
