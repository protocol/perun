The spec explicitly addresses BIAS (balanced flagging rates), HALLU and RELY (sources, confidence labels, overrides, and appeals provided), DISC (AI-generated labeling present), and AUTO (human approval required for irreversible actions).

However, the spec describes a "pre-ticked consent box for location sharing" and a "countdown timer" on the offer page. Pre-ticked boxes violate the principle of affirmative consent by shifting the burden to the user to opt-out, and countdown timers manufacture urgency. Together, these constitute dark patterns that pressure users against their own interest regarding privacy and purchase decisions. Since the spec confirms these features exist in the build rather than describing them as removed or mitigated, the DARK harm category is flagged.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
