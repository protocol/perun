The spec presents critical safety failures despite some mitigations. While AI disclosure is present (addressing DISC) and a human approves payouts (addressing AUTO), other harms are explicit. The bot states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," creating high over-reliance risk (RELY) and inviting users to trust unverified data as fact (HALLU). The "no appeal path" for final results exacerbates this. Furthermore, the interface employs dark patterns: a "ten-minute timer" manufactures urgency, and "pre-ticked" consent forces users to actively opt-out rather than opt-in, pressuring them against their own interest (DARK). The claim that error rates were "within one point across every group" during a small pilot is insufficient to rule out systematic disadvantage for specific subgroups in broader deployment, but the spec does not explicitly describe a current bias incident, so BIAS is not flagged based strictly on the text provided. However, the combination of unverified assertions presented as fact, lack of uncertainty indicators, irreversible outcomes without appeal, and coercive interface design constitutes a dangerous launch state.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
