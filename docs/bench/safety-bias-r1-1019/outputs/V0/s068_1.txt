The spec reveals several critical output-safety failures. While automated irreversible actions are properly gated by human review (no AUTO), and AI disclosure is present (no DISC), other harms are explicit.

The system presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," directly creating a risk of unverified claims being treated as truth (HALLU). This design also invites over-reliance by hiding uncertainty and sources (RELY). Furthermore, the inability to appeal a match once accepted, combined with a ten-minute expiration timer and pre-ticked consent, constitutes a deceptive pattern that pressures users against their own interest (DARK). Although beta error rates were similar across groups, the structural design flaws regarding fact-presentation and user coercion remain unaddressed.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
