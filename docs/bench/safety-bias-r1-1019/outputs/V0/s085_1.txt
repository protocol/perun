The spec demonstrates strong safeguards for most harm categories: it includes AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), explicit confidence labels with override/appeal mechanisms (mitigating RELY), ethical consent patterns (mitigating DARK), and human gates for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO). It also cites pilot data regarding group performance (addressing BIAS concerns).

However, the spec explicitly states the UI displays raw model probabilities to users: "a predicted arrival says 'from live GPS, 92% sure.'" Presenting a precise percentage implies a calibration precision that the pipeline likely cannot guarantee, inviting over-trust despite the accompanying text label. This violates the safety principle of rendering confidence as defined coarse tiers (e.g., "High/Medium/Low") rather than false-precision numbers. Additionally, while pilot error rates were similar across groups, claiming the engine will "hold up at full scale" based on a 400-user pilot is an unverified assertion of fairness and accuracy (HALLU/BIAS risk) without a stated plan for continuous measurement post-launch.

HARMS: RELY, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
