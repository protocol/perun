The spec explicitly identifies a disparity where error rates are double for customers with large inventories or unusual items, satisfying the definition of **BIAS**. Furthermore, the estimate engine executes pricing decisions automatically without human review. Since moving quotes can lead to significant financial commitment and the system admits higher error rates for specific scenarios, this constitutes **AUTO** (unsafe automation of high-stakes actions without a human gate). The spec notes capping the pilot to monitor performance, but this is an observation strategy, not the required human-in-the-loop gate for high-stakes automated decisions. While the spec includes AI disclosure and avoids dark patterns, the identified bias and lack of human oversight on irreversible financial estimates require remediation before launch.

HARMS: BIAS,AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
