The spec explicitly describes three present harms. First, **RELY**: the design invites over-trust by treating the ranking as final with no option for users to override or appeal the result. Second, **DISC**: while answers cite sources, the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," missing required AI disclosure. Third, **DARK**: the consent mechanism for notifications is "opt-in and unticked," a deceptive pattern that pressures users against their interests by hiding defaults.

Other categories are handled: **AUTO** is mitigated because a person approves irreversible actions (deposits, closing lists). **BIAS** and **HALLU** are not flagged as present because the spec claims error rates were consistent across groups and answers carry cited sources, even if the reliance on those claims is risky without independent verification. However, the three identified harms (RELY, DISC, DARK) are explicit design choices in the spec that must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
