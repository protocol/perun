The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: it includes AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), cited sources with confidence levels and override/appeal mechanisms (mitigating HALLU and RELY), human approval for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO), and ethical consent design (mitigating DARK).

However, the spec flags BIAS. It claims "error rates were within one point across every group tested" based on a pilot of only "three mid-sized districts" to justify rolling out to 20 districts. This sample size and scope are insufficient to verify that outputs do not systematically disadvantage specific groups (e.g., rural vs. urban, specific demographics) in a broader deployment. The assertion of fairness ("we're comfortable") relies on limited data without a robust measurement plan for the expanded rollout, leaving the risk of systematic disadvantage unverified and present.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
