The spec confirms DISC is handled via visible AI labels and AUTO is handled via human approval for irreversible actions. DARK patterns are mitigated by the absence of timers and the presence of easy cancellation. However, three harms remain present in the design:

1.  **BIAS**: The spec explicitly states the system flags older users for incompleteness at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%). While a checklist was added, the underlying disparity remains unaddressed in the output logic described.
2.  **HALLU**: The system states answers "flatly" with "no source," presenting potentially unverified calculations as absolute fact without grounding.
3.  **RELY**: The design offers "no uncertainty shown" and explicitly prevents overrides or appeals, forcing users to restart the entire interview to dispute a result. This invites over-trust and removes recourse.

These issues constitute significant risks to fairness and user agency that are not resolved by the current controls.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
