The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: human approval gates irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO), every output is labeled AI-generated (mitigating DISC), overrides are one-tap (mitigating RELY), and consent lacks pressure (mitigating DARK). Sources and confidence are absent, but the spec does not claim the tool presents fabricated facts as verified truth, so HALLU is not flagged based solely on the text provided.

However, the spec admits a measured disparity where the tool flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users. Crucially, the team states they will "track but not act on yet." This confirms the presence of systematic disadvantage against an age group (BIAS) that is acknowledged but intentionally left unmitigated during the pilot. Since the feature ships with a known, unaddressed bias affecting a protected demographic, it fails the safety floor for equitable outcomes.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
