The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: reliance is mitigated by allowing overrides and appeals; disclosure is present via visible AI labels; and high-stakes automation is blocked by requiring human sign-off for irreversible steps.

However, two harms remain present per the description:
1. **HALLU**: The tool "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting unverified scores as absolute facts without indicating uncertainty constitutes hallucination surfaced as fact.
2. **DARK**: The interface includes a "pre-ticked consent box" for batch exports. Pre-ticked boxes are a classic deceptive pattern that pressures users against their own interest by manufacturing passive consent.

While error rates were low in a small pilot, the lack of confidence indicators and the presence of dark patterns are explicit features of the current design that require remediation before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
