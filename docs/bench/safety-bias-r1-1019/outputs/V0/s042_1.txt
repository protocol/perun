Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are flagged:

**DISC**: The spec explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Users cannot distinguish machine-generated assessments from human ones, violating transparency requirements for AI-driven decisions.

**BIAS**: The spec notes that validation runs "flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users." While it claims weights are constant, the explicit mention of disparate flagging rates across age groups indicates a known, unmitigated bias in the output behavior that disadvantages specific demographics.

Other categories appear handled: **RELY** is mitigated by one-tap overrides and teacher finality; **AUTO** is blocked by human approval for irreversible actions; **DARK** patterns are absent (no timers, opt-in consent); **HALLU** is addressed via source citations and confidence labels (though the confidence format isn't detailed, sources are present).

However, the lack of AI disclosure and the acknowledged age-based disparity constitute unresolved risks before launch.
