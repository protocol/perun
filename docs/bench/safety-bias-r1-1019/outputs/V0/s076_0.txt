The spec explicitly discloses AI usage and sources, addressing DISC and mitigating HALLU. Consent flows appear ethical, clearing DARK. However, three critical harms are present in the text:

1.  **BIAS**: The spec admits the error rate is "twice as high" for left-handed applicants. Logging this during a pilot without pre-launch mitigation constitutes a known, unaddressed disparate impact.
2.  **RELY**: The system makes results "final" with "no appeal path." Combined with the admitted bias, this removes the necessary override mechanism for incorrect or unfair allocations.
3.  **AUTO**: The system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Allocating physical property (garden plots) is a consequential decision. Executing this irreversibly without human review, especially while known biases exist, violates the high-stakes automation guardrail.

The small pilot size does not negate the presence of these specific, acknowledged risks in the design.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
