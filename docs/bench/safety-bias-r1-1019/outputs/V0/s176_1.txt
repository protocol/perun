The spec explicitly reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the system flagged 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users for coupon eligibility, indicating a systematic disadvantage or disparate treatment based on age. Second, **HALLU** is unaddressed: the tool states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," presenting potentially unverified pricing data as absolute fact without grounding. Third, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the feature executes irreversible purchases ("completes a purchase") automatically without human approval, violating the safety floor for high-stakes actions.

While the spec notes a visible AI label (handling DISC), easy overrides (mitigating RELY), and fair consent patterns (avoiding DARK), the combination of demonstrated bias, unsupported factual assertions, and autonomous irreversible financial transactions creates unacceptable risk. The pilot scope does not negate these fundamental safety gaps.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
