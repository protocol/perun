The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity where the error rate is "twice as high for one group," confirming **BIAS**. While the design includes source attribution and AI labels (addressing HALLU and DISC) and avoids dark patterns (addressing DARK), it fails to mitigate the identified bias because users "cannot override or appeal the result," triggering **RELY**. Furthermore, the system executes assignments immediately ("acts the moment the model decides") with "nobody reviews it first." Since assigning food resources is a high-stakes decision affecting access to necessities, and the spec admits a known failure mode for a specific group, this constitutes unsafe automation without a human gate, triggering **AUTO**.

The combination of a known biased outcome, the inability for users to contest the decision, and the lack of human review for a high-impact action creates an unacceptable risk of harm to the disadvantaged group.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
