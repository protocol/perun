The spec demonstrates handling for most harm categories: DISC is addressed via explicit AI labeling; RELY is mitigated by showing uncertainty (though the metric is questionable), providing overrides, and citing sources; DARK patterns are avoided through clear consent and cancellation; AUTO is controlled by requiring human approval for irreversible actions. BIAS is claimed to be tested with equal error rates across groups.

However, the spec explicitly states the system displays "90% confident" to users. Product output safety standards dictate that raw model confidence percentages must not be shown as they imply uncalibrated precision and invite over-trust. Confidence should be rendered as a defined coarse tier (e.g., "High/Medium/Low") with explicit definitions, not a specific number. This constitutes a reliance risk (RELY) where the design invites trust past reliability due to false precision. Additionally, while the spec claims equal error rates, it does not provide the specific harm inventory or runnable evals required to verify this, but based strictly on the text provided, the presence of the percentage is a confirmed design flaw.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
