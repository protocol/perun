The spec reveals two present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **DISC** (Missing AI-disclosure) is explicitly present: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Users cannot distinguish machine-generated placement from human assessment, violating transparency norms for automated decisions affecting education.

Second, **BIAS** (Unfair outcomes) is indicated by the validation data: "flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users." While the spec notes weights are "kept constant," identical flagging rates across disparate age groups without further subgroup analysis or justification suggests a potential systematic disadvantage or measurement error that hasn't been resolved, only observed. The spec describes the harm's presence (disparate impact observation) without confirming it is fixed.

Other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by one-tap overrides and teacher final choice; **AUTO** is covered by human approval for irreversible actions; **DARK** is avoided via unticked consent and no timers; **HALLU** is addressed by showing sources and confidence labels.

However, the lack of disclosure and the unresolved bias signal prevent a safe launch.

HARMS: DISC, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
