The spec reveals four active harms. **BIAS** is present: the tool explicitly flagged 11% of older and younger users differently, indicating systematic disadvantage by age group. **HALLU** exists because the system presents every answer as "confirmed fact" with no sources shown, surfacing unverified claims with undue authority. **DISC** is triggered as the interface fails to inform users that the content is AI-generated. **DARK** patterns are evident via the countdown timer (manufactured urgency) and pre-ticked consent box, pressuring users against their own interest.

While **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override and **AUTO** is handled by the human sign-off on irreversible steps, the remaining four categories represent clear, described failures in the current design. The bias finding alone requires investigation before exposing protected classes to disparate treatment. The deceptive patterns and lack of disclosure violate basic transparency and fairness principles. These issues must be resolved before launching to external customers.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
