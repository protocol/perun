The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: it includes AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), provides citations and confidence levels with override/appeal mechanisms (mitigating HALLU and RELY), and uses clear consent without pressure (mitigating DARK). The suggestion of book titles is not a high-stakes or irreversible action, so the lack of a pre-execution human gate does not trigger the AUTO flag in this context.

However, the spec describes a test where "11% of older users and 11% of younger users" were flagged for low engagement. While the percentages are identical, the explicit segmentation by age and the application of a "nudge reminder" based on this grouping indicates the system is making differentiated decisions based on demographic groups. Without evidence in the spec that this segmentation was tested for disparate impact or unfair disadvantage beyond simple engagement metrics, the potential for systematic disadvantage (BIAS) remains present and unverified in the description. The spec notes the measurement but does not confirm the harm is absent.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
