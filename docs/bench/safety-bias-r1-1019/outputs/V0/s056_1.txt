The spec explicitly identifies critical failures in three harm categories. First, **BIAS** is present: the team admits the error rate is "twice as high for one group," indicating systematic disadvantage, and while tuning is planned, the disparity exists in the current design. Second, **RELY** is flagged because the spec states "users cannot override or appeal the result," removing the ability to correct errors or challenge the AI's judgment. Third, **AUTO** is a severe violation: the tool "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" by directly advancing or cutting candidates. This high-stakes decision-making lacks the required human-in-the-loop gate.

Other categories appear handled: **DISC** is addressed via visible labels; **DARK** is mitigated by the absence of timers and presence of easy cancel; **HALLU** is partially addressed by cited sources, though the high error rate undermines this. However, the combination of uncorrectable bias, lack of appeal, and fully automated irreversible hiring decisions creates unacceptable risk.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
