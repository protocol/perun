The spec explicitly describes three present harms. First, **BIAS** is admitted: the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, and the team intends to ship this disparity "as-is." Second, **HALLU** is present because every answer is presented as "confirmed fact" with no sources shown, despite being generated by a bot. Third, **DISC** is confirmed; the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output."

Other categories are handled. **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override and appeal. **DARK** patterns are absent due to plain opt-in and no timers. **AUTO** is controlled by the human sign-off requirement before irreversible steps.

However, shipping known demographic bias without mitigation, presenting unverified outputs as absolute fact, and failing to disclose AI involvement constitute critical safety failures. These must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
