The spec explicitly identifies a disparity where error rates are double for out-of-state plates. While a secondary step is added, the persistence of this known disadvantage triggers **BIAS**. The system presents a "confidence label" but offers no definition of its tiers, risking **RELY** (over-trust), though the primary failure is the lack of recourse. Crucially, the spec states the result is "final and there is no appeal path." Combined with the capability to "void a charge directly" (an irreversible financial action) with "no human approval," this is a definitive **AUTO** violation. High-stakes, irreversible actions require a human-in-the-loop gate; full automation here is the failure this review prevents.

While **DISC** is handled (outputs are labeled) and **DARK** appears mitigated (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of unaddressed bias impact and un gated irreversible automation creates unacceptable residual risk. The "pilot" status does not exempt high-stakes automation from requiring human oversight.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
