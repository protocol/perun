Based on the spec provided, the following harms are present:

**DARK**: The interface employs deceptive patterns by setting consent as "pre-ticked" for follow-up reminders and imposing a "ten-minute timer" on offers, creating manufactured urgency and hidden defaults that pressure users against their own interest.

**AUTO**: The specification states the assistant "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." While the mechanic checks the draft *before* showing it to a rider, the system executes its generation and internal actions autonomously without an initial human gate. Given that financial estimates can drive immediate customer decisions and resource allocation, this lack of a pre-action human review constitutes unsafe automation of a potentially high-stakes action.

However, the presence of DARK patterns and the autonomous execution trigger a block.
