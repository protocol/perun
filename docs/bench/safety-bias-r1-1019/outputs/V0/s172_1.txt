The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the system flags older users at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), and the team has "accepted" this disparity rather than correcting it. Second, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the system executes high-stakes seating assignments automatically with "nobody reviews it first," and the result is "final" with "no appeal path," violating the requirement for human gates on irreversible actions. Third, **RELY** is evident because the design offers "no easy override or appeal," inviting over-trust in an automated decision that directly impacts user access.

While the spec correctly handles **DISC** (labeling outputs as AI-generated) and **DARK** (using opt-in consent without dark patterns), the combination of accepted bias, fully automated irreversible decisions, and the lack of an appeal mechanism creates unacceptable risk. The presence of cited sources and confidence levels does not negate the harm of removing human review from a decision that excludes specific demographic groups at higher rates.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
