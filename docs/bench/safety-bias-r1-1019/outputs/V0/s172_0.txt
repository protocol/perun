The spec explicitly reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the system flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, and the team has "accepted" this disparity without correction. Second, **AUTO** is triggered: the feature executes seat assignments—a high-stakes decision affecting access—automatically with "nobody reviews it first." Third, **RELY** is evident: the spec states results are "final" with "no appeal path," denying users an override despite the known error rate and bias.

While the spec handles **DISC** (outputs are labeled AI-generated) and avoids **DARK** patterns (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of automated irreversible actions, known biased outcomes, and a locked decision process creates unacceptable risk. The "accepted" bias ratio is not a mitigation; it is an admission of unresolved harm.

DECISION: BLOCK
