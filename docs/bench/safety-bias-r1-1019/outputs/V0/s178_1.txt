The spec demonstrates strong controls for bias (tested across groups), hallucination (cited sources), over-reliance (overrides/appeals available), disclosure (visible AI label), and dark patterns (no timers, easy cancel).

However, the spec explicitly states: "The automation acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Assigning bus routes and stops is a high-stakes operational decision affecting student safety, punctuality, and family logistics. Executing these assignments automatically without a human reviewing the specific daily output before it acts constitutes an unsafe automation of high-stakes actions. Limiting the rollout scope does not mitigate the fundamental lack of a human-in-the-loop gate for the action itself. This matches the AUTO harm category.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
