Based on the provided spec, the feature demonstrates strong safeguards for most harm categories: it includes human review for high-stakes actions (mitigating AUTO), explicit AI labeling (mitigating DISC), easy overrides (mitigating RELY), and non-coercive consent flows (mitigating DARK). Testing claims suggest bias is addressed.

However, the spec explicitly states: "The interface presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." This design choice directly creates a risk where unverified rankings or acoustic likelihoods are treated as absolute truth without evidence, fitting the definition of **HALLU** (unverified claims presented as fact). While the output is labeled as AI-generated, presenting the *content* of that output as "confirmed fact" without sources encourages the board to accept potential hallucinations or errors as ground truth, undermining the scrutiny the label is meant to provoke.

HARMS: HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
