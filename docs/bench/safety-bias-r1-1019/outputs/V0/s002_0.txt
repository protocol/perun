Based on the provided spec, the feature handles most safety concerns well: it shows sources and confidence labels (mitigating RELY and HALLU), allows easy overrides (mitigating RELY), uses fair consent patterns (mitigating DARK), and gates irreversible financial actions behind human approval (mitigating AUTO). The spec also claims equal flagging rates across age groups, suggesting BIAS is addressed in the design intent.

However, the spec explicitly states: "The draft text does not tell users the content was written by AI." Since this feature generates waiver requests on behalf of patrons, the absence of disclosure regarding machine-generated content constitutes a clear DISC harm. The spec presents this as a current state, not a handled control. Therefore, this specific issue must be resolved before launch.
