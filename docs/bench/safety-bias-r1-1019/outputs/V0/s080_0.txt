The spec explicitly describes three present harms. First, **BIAS**: an internal audit reveals the system flags older users for no-shows at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), indicating systematic disadvantage. Second, **DISC**: when a user hits the strike threshold, the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating transparency. Third, **RELY**: users "cannot override or appeal the result" of these automated decisions, inviting over-trust without recourse.

Other categories are handled: **AUTO** is mitigated because a human approves irreversible cancellation actions; **DARK** is addressed via unticked consent and no timers; **HALLU** is managed through cited sources and confidence levels. However, the combination of unexplained algorithmic bias, missing AI disclosure, and the inability to appeal creates unacceptable risk for the affected group.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
