The spec demonstrates robust handling of most harm categories: bias is addressed via testing across groups; hallucination is mitigated with citations and confidence levels; over-reliance is countered with overrides and appeals; disclosure is explicitly visible; and high-stakes automation includes a human-in-the-loop gate.

However, the spec describes a **DARK** pattern: "a pre-ticked consent box" for cancellations. Pre-ticked boxes are a classic deceptive pattern that nudge users toward consent they might not otherwise give, violating the principle of informed, active agreement. Additionally, the "countdown timer" may manufacture urgency, further pressuring the user. While the human approval gate mitigates the final irreversible action (AUTO), the interface design itself pressures the user against their own interest before that gate is reached.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
