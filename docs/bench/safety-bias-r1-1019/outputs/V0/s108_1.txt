The spec explicitly reveals two unmitigated harms. First, **DISC** is present: the document states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," intentionally masking the automated nature of the curation despite the feature being AI-driven. Second, **RELY** is flagged because the spec admits, "users cannot override or appeal the result," offering only a restart option rather than a meaningful correction mechanism for incorrect matches.

While the spec notes known bias against households with toddlers, it claims this will be adjusted before wider rollout; however, since the pilot launches March 1st with acknowledged higher error rates for this group, **BIAS** is also currently present in the shipped version. Conversely, **AUTO** is handled (human sign-off exists), **DARK** is handled (opt-in, no timers), and **HALLU** is partially mitigated by source citations, though the lack of model disclosure undermines trust.

Because the feature launches with known disparate impact on a specific demographic, hides its AI origin, and prevents user appeals, it cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
