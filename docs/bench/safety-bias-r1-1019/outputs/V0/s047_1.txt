The spec explicitly flags two harms. First, **DISC** is present: the system "does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating the requirement for machine-generated disclosure where the output drives operational decisions. Second, **DARK** patterns are described in onboarding: a "countdown timer" manufactures urgency, and a "pre-ticked consent box" hides defaults, nudging users against their own interest regarding data import.

Other categories appear handled. **BIAS** is addressed via beta testing claiming consistent error rates across groups (though independent verification is ideal, the spec asserts coverage). **RELY** is mitigated by showing sources, confidence labels, and enabling one-tap overrides/appeals. **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human sign-off on irreversible steps. **HALLU** is countered by sourcing claims and displaying confidence.

However, the presence of undisclosed AI generation and deceptive onboarding patterns constitutes a failure to meet safety floors before launch. These issues require immediate remediation to ensure transparency and user autonomy.

HARMS: DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
