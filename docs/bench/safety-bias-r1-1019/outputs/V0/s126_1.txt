The spec demonstrates active mitigation for several harm categories. Bias (BIAS) is explicitly described as an intended feature (segmenting older vs. younger users) rather than an unaddressed disadvantage. Over-reliance (RELY) is managed via a one-tap override and appeal mechanism. Disclosure (DISC) is satisfied by labeling all outputs as AI-generated. Deceptive patterns (DARK) are avoided through plain opt-in consent without timers. Unsafe automation (AUTO) is prevented by requiring human approval for all irreversible actions like refunds.

However, the spec notes that when customers ask why they received a deal, the assistant "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting explanatory reasoning without sources or uncertainty indicators constitutes presenting unverified claims as fact, triggering the Hallucination (HALLU) category. While the user can appeal the *result*, the *explanation* itself lacks grounding or confidence tiers, risking misinformation about how the decision was made.

HARMS: HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
