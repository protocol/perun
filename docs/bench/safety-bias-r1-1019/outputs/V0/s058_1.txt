The spec demonstrates handling for BIAS (tested across groups), HALLU (cited sources), RELY (confidence shown, one-tap override), DISC (visible AI label), and AUTO (human sign-off on irreversible steps).

However, the spec explicitly describes a DARK pattern: "consent is pre-ticked" for prescription refill offers. Pre-ticked defaults manipulate users into agreeing to actions against their interest by requiring effort to opt-out rather than opt-in. Additionally, the "ten-minute timer" creates manufactured urgency, further pressuring the user. These interface designs constitute deceptive patterns that disadvantage the user.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
