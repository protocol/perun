The spec explicitly describes several unmitigated harms. First, the system exhibits **BIAS**: it flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, and the team intends to ship this disparity "as-is." Second, it demonstrates **HALLU** by presenting every answer as "confirmed fact" with no sources shown, despite being a generative bot. Third, it fails **DISC** because "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," leaving users unaware they are interacting with AI.

While the spec notes positive controls for other categories—specifically **RELY** (users can override/appeal), **DARK** (no timers, plain consent), and **AUTO** (human sign-off on irreversible steps)—the presence of bias, hallucination presented as fact, and missing disclosure constitutes a critical failure in the harm inventory. Shipping a feature that systematically disadvantages a protected demographic (older users) while masking its AI nature and fabricating certainty violates the safety floor. These issues require immediate remediation before the pilot can ethically proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
