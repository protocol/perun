The spec demonstrates strong safeguards for most harm categories: outputs are labeled AI-generated (DISC handled), confidence and sources are provided with easy overrides (RELY handled), and irreversible actions require human sign-off (AUTO handled).

However, two harms are present in the described design. First, the onboarding flow uses a pre-ticked consent box for sharing pacing history. This is a classic dark pattern that pressures users into sharing data they might otherwise withhold, fitting the DARK category. Second, while the pilot reported low error rates, the spec provides no evidence of testing for systematic disadvantages against specific runner demographics (e.g., age, gender, or disability status) regarding group assignment. The absence of described bias testing or subgroup analysis means BIAS remains a potential unaddressed risk in the specification.

HARMS: DARK, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
