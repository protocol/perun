The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity where older users are flagged at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%) and states the team will "accept for now" rather than mitigate it. This is a confirmed **BIAS** harm that must be addressed before launch; accepting known discrimination is not a control.

While the spec correctly implements controls for other categories—explicit AI disclosure (**DISC**), one-tap overrides (**RELY**), human approval for escalations (**AUTO**), and non-coercive consent (**DARK**)—the presentation of answers as "confirmed fact with no source" risks **HALLU**. However, since the primary failure is the admitted, unmitigated bias, that is the critical blocker. The "accept for now" stance violates the requirement to manage residual risk for unfair outcomes.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
