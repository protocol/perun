The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **DISC**: the interface omits any indication that a model generated the output, presenting itself as a standard workflow tool. Second, **DARK**: the design employs deceptive patterns, specifically a manufactured urgency (ten-minute timer) and pre-ticked consent, pressuring users against their own interest. Third, **RELY**: while human sign-off exists for sending notices, failed applications are declared "final" with "no appeal path," removing the user's ability to override or contest the automated decision despite potential errors.

Although the spec claims age consistency, the sample size (40 users) is too small to verify bias mitigation, but per instructions, we flag only explicitly described harms. The lack of disclosure, presence of dark patterns, and absence of an appeal mechanism constitute clear failures in the current specification.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
