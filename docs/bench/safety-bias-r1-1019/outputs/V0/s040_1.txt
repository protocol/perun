The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity where the error rate is "twice as high for one group," confirming **BIAS**. While the design displays source and confidence labels, it states "users cannot override or appeal the result," which creates **RELY** (over-reliance/automation bias) by removing recourse for known errors. Furthermore, the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first" for assigning waitlist spots. Since these assignments determine access to essential resources and occur despite known higher error rates for specific groups, this constitutes **AUTO** (unsafe automation of high-stakes actions without a human in the loop).

Disclosure (**DISC**) and deceptive patterns (**DARK**) are addressed via visible AI labels and plain opt-in consent without timers. **HALLU** is not explicitly flagged as present in the text, though the high error rate implies potential accuracy issues; however, the inability to appeal is the primary reliance failure.

Because the feature automates resource allocation with known biased outcomes and no human review or user appeal mechanism, it violates the safety floor for high-stakes automation.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
