The spec reveals several critical output-safety failures. First, the interface employs **DARK** patterns: a countdown timer manufactures urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box hides data sharing defaults, pressuring users against their interests. Second, there is a **DISC** failure; the UI states "Recommended group" without disclosing that a machine-generated model produced the output, leaving users unaware of the automation. Third, the system exhibits **AUTO** risk: final rosters lock automatically 72 hours before the race with no human review, executing a high-stakes assignment that is difficult to reverse close to the event.

While the spec notes demographic flags (11% of older/younger users), it does not confirm these are corrected or justified, yet the primary blockers are the design and automation controls. The presence of cited sources and override buttons mitigates **HALLU** and **RELY** partially, but the lack of human gating on the final irreversible action and the deceptive onboarding flow are sufficient to halt launch. The missing AI disclosure further prevents informed user consent regarding the nature of the recommendation.

HARMS: DARK, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
