The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the system flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, a disparity the team "accepted" rather than addressed. Second, **RELY** is triggered because committed routes are "final" with "no appeal path," removing the ability to override high-stakes scheduling decisions. Third, **DISC** applies because the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," withholding the nature of the decision-making from dispatchers.

While **AUTO** is handled for salt/sand deployment (human sign-off required), the route commitment itself appears irreversible and lacks the described human gate for that specific action. **HALLU** and **DARK** appear addressed via source citations and lack of deceptive timers. However, the combination of acknowledged demographic bias, missing AI disclosure, and an irreversible workflow without appeal constitutes a critical safety failure.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
