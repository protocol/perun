The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: DISC is handled via AI-generated labels; DARK is mitigated by opt-in consent without timers; AUTO is controlled by human sign-off for irreversible swaps; and BIAS appears managed given the pilot's reported error rates across groups.

However, two critical harms remain present per the spec's description:
1.  **RELY (Over-reliance):** The system provides "stated confidence levels" and rationales but explicitly states "Users cannot override or appeal the result," deferring disputes to the next season. This lack of immediate recourse or easy override invites over-trust in potentially flawed automated decisions.
2.  **HALLU (Hallucination as fact):** While the spec claims answers carry "cited source[s]," it does not describe any mechanism to verify that the AI's interpretation of bylaws or records is accurate before presentation. Without a verification step or a way to flag incorrect citations immediately, unverified claims could still be presented as fact with false authority.

Because the design prevents immediate user correction or appeal (RELY) and lacks a described verification gate for its cited outputs (HALLU), these risks must be addressed before launch to prevent user harm.

HARMS: RELY, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
