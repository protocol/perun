The spec reveals several critical output-safety failures. First, **DISC** is present: the product generates offers but "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating transparency requirements. Second, **DARK** patterns are explicitly designed: the interface uses a "ten-minute timer" creating manufactured urgency and "pre-ticked" consent, pressuring users against their own interest. Third, **RELY** is a failure because "users cannot override or appeal the result," removing human recourse for automated decisions.

While **AUTO** is handled (a person approves irreversible actions) and **BIAS** appears addressed via identical treatment across age groups in testing, the combination of undisclosed AI, deceptive urgency, and locked-in decisions creates unacceptable risk. The stated confidence levels without defined tiers may also invite over-trust, but the missing disclosure and dark patterns are sufficient to block launch.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
