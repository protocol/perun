The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: sources and confidence labels mitigate HALLU and RELY; AI-generated labeling covers DISC; consent mechanics avoid DARK patterns; and human sign-off gates irreversible actions for AUTO.

However, the spec reports a measurement outcome where "11% of older users and 11% of younger users" were flagged for follow-up. While the absolute rates match, the explicit segmentation by age ("older" vs "younger") without stating that the *reasons* for flagging or the *consequences* (e.g., denial of care, delayed results) are identical across groups raises a BIAS concern. The spec notes this data will "tune thresholds," implying the current state may produce disparate impacts or treatment based on age groups, even if the raw percentage is coincidentally equal. Without explicit confirmation that outcomes are equitable across these demographics, the risk of systematic disadvantage remains present in the described pilot results.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
