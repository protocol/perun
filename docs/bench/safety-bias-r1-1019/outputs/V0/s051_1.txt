The spec explicitly flags three present harms. First, **HALLU**: the interface presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," surfacing unverified claims with unjustified authority. Second, **DARK**: settlement offers utilize a "ten-minute timer" (manufactured urgency) and "pre-ticked" consent (hidden default), pressuring users against their own interest. Third, **RELY**: by presenting outputs as absolute facts without showing uncertainty or sources, the design invites over-trust, even though an override exists.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed because the tool labels outputs as AI-generated. **AUTO** is mitigated because a person approves every irreversible action. **BIAS** is not flagged because the spec states error rates were consistent across groups, indicating the harm is managed rather than present in the design description.

Because HALLU, DARK, and RELY are described as active design choices that mislead or pressure users, the feature cannot launch in this state.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
