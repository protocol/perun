The spec demonstrates strong safeguards for most harm categories: it includes AI disclosure (handling DISC), easy overrides and appeals (handling RELY), transparent consent without pressure (handling DARK), and a human-in-the-loop for irreversible actions (handling AUTO). It also cites sources for claims.

However, the feature displays a raw model confidence percentage ("90% confident") directly to users. This violates the safety principle of rendering confidence as a defined coarse tier (e.g., "High/Medium/Low") rather than a precise number. A specific percentage implies a calibration precision that is rarely justified and invites over-reliance, directly contradicting the goal of preventing automation bias. Additionally, while the spec claims error rates were "within one point across every group," it does not provide the underlying harm inventory or specific metrics for bias, leaving BIAS as potentially unverified despite the claim. The display of false-precision confidence is a present design flaw described in the spec.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
