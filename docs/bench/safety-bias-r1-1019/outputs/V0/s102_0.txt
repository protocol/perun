The spec demonstrates handling for most harm categories: bias is monitored across age cohorts; hallucination is mitigated via citations and confidence levels; over-reliance is addressed with one-tap overrides; disclosure is explicit; and irreversible actions require human approval.

However, the spec explicitly describes **DARK** patterns: a "countdown timer" manufactures urgency, and a "pre-ticked consent box" hides defaults, both pressuring users against their own interest. Additionally, while confidence levels are shown, presenting them as raw numbers rather than defined coarse tiers risks **RELY** (over-trust), though the primary blockable finding is the deceptive interface.

Because the feature ships with confirmed deceptive patterns (dark patterns) that manipulate user consent and behavior, it cannot proceed without remediation.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
