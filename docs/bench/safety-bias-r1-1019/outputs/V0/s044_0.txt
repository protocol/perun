The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: validation data shows the model flags older users at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%). While logged, this disparity lacks a mitigation plan or threshold before launch. Second, **RELY** is triggered because users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing necessary human recourse for an impactful educational decision. Third, **DISC** is a clear failure; the spec explicitly states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating transparency norms for automated decisions.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is addressed via source citations and confidence labels; **DARK** patterns are absent due to fair consent design; **AUTO** is mitigated by the requirement for human approval on irreversible cohort moves. However, the combination of documented bias, lack of appeal, and missing AI disclosure creates unacceptable risk for learners.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
