The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: DISC (visible AI label), RELY (cited sources, confidence levels, one-tap override/appeal), DARK (opt-in consent, no timers, easy cancel), and AUTO (human approval for irreversible actions).

However, the spec reveals a potential BIAS issue. It states that during internal testing, the model flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users for follow-up. While the percentages are identical, the explicit mention of age-based segmentation in the output logic ("flagged... older users and... younger users") indicates the system is making decisions based on protected characteristics. The spec says they will "monitor" this in the pilot, but it does not confirm that the disparate impact (if any exists in the *nature* of the follow-up, not just the rate) has been mitigated or that the use of age as a signal is justified and non-discriminatory before launch. Shipping with a known, unmitigated reliance on age-based sorting without a pre-launch validation that this does not systematically disadvantage a group constitutes a present BIAS risk under the definition provided.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
