The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the algorithm flags older users for review at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age. Second, **RELY** is a critical failure: the spec explicitly states the list is "final" and users "cannot override or appeal," removing necessary human recourse for an automated ranking. Third, **DISC** is confirmed: the product intentionally "does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating transparency norms for machine-generated outputs.

While **AUTO** is handled (human approval exists for irreversible actions) and **DARK** appears addressed (no deceptive timers), the combination of demonstrated bias, lack of appeal mechanisms, and missing AI disclosure creates unacceptable risk for end-users. The bias metric suggests a flawed model, and the inability to appeal locks users into potentially unfair outcomes without recourse.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
