The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: DISC is handled via visible AI labels; RELY is mitigated by cited sources, confidence levels, and one-tap overrides/appeals; DARK patterns are avoided through clear consent and no timers; and AUTO is controlled by human sign-off on irreversible steps.

However, the spec admits a specific BIAS issue: "the error rate is twice as high for one group." While the team tracks this in weekly metrics, shipping a feature with a known, unmitigated systematic disadvantage for a specific group constitutes a present harm under the BIAS definition. Tracking is measurement, not mitigation. The spec describes the harm as present without detailing a pre-launch fix or gate to reduce this disparity.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
