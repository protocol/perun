The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity where the error rate is "twice as high for one group," confirming **BIAS**. While the system tracks this segment, the harm exists in the output itself. The feature presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," inviting over-trust despite known accuracy gaps, which flags **RELY**. Although an AI label is present (**DISC** handled) and consent mechanisms are sound (**DARK** handled), the specification states that SeatSift "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" for auto-issuing tickets. Since issuing a ticket changes the inventory state and potentially displaces other buyers, this qualifies as an irreversible action lacking a human gate, flagging **AUTO**. The high error rate for a specific group combined with automatic execution creates a significant risk of unfair, uncorrected outcomes before launch.

DECISION: BLOCK
