The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: BIAS is acknowledged and being tuned; HALLU and RELY are mitigated by showing sources, confidence labels, and easy overrides; DARK patterns are avoided via plain consent; and AUTO is controlled by requiring human sign-off for irreversible binding calibrations.

However, the spec states: "The app does not tell users the content was written by AI; it reads as a straightforward tool." This confirms the presence of missing AI disclosure (DISC). While legal necessity is a counsel matter, the product safety floor recommends disclosure where it matters to the user, and the spec admits this control is absent. Since the feature generates recommendations presented as authoritative advice without revealing its machine-generated nature, DISC is a flagged harm that should be addressed before launch to prevent over-reliance based on a false premise of human expertise.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
