The spec reveals three critical output-safety failures. First, **HALLU**: the tool presents scoring reasons "flatly" with "no source or confidence," fabricating authority for unverified claims. Second, **DISC**: the product "presents itself as a simple utility" despite using a model, hiding the machine-generated nature of the decision from users. Third, **AUTO**: the system executes irreversible high-stakes actions (purging entire contest data) with "no human approval," violating the mandatory gate for destructive operations.

While the spec notes an override exists for scoring (mitigating **RELY**) and deletion consent is opt-in (addressing **DARK**), the lack of disclosure, the presentation of unverified reasons as fact, and the fully automated irreversible deletion are unaddressed harms described directly in the text. The demographic data provided (11% flag rate) relates to a separate review check, not the scoring bias itself, so **BIAS** is not explicitly confirmed as a present harm in the scoring logic based solely on this text. However, the identified flaws are sufficient to halt launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
