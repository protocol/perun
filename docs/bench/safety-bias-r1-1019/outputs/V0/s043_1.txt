The spec reveals three present harms requiring mitigation before launch. First, **DARK**: the onboarding flow uses a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box, explicitly pressuring users against their own interest and manufacturing urgency. Second, **RELY**: while an override exists, the interface states answers "flatly" with no source or confidence shown, inviting over-trust despite the appeal mechanism. Third, **HALLU**: the system presents recommendations as flat facts without sources or confidence indicators, risking unverified claims being treated as authoritative.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed by explicit AI labeling; **AUTO** is mitigated by the human sign-off on irreversible registrar writes; **BIAS** is claimed handled via pilot validation (though ongoing measurement is prudent, the spec asserts coverage).

The dark patterns (DARK) constitute a definitive barrier to launch as they violate basic consent ethics. The lack of uncertainty visualization (RELY/HALLU) combined with high-stakes educational placement necessitates showing confidence tiers or sources to prevent automation bias, especially given the "flat" presentation style.

HARMS: DARK, RELY, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
