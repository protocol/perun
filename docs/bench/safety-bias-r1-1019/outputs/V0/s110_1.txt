The spec reveals two unaddressed output-safety harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the system flagged older and younger users at identical rates (11%), yet the interface fails to highlight this demographic split. Without surfacing this data, officers cannot assess potential disparate impact during decision-making. Second, **DISC** is triggered: the specification explicitly states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are unaware the status is machine-generated rather than a simple deterministic checklist.

Other categories appear handled. **HALLU** is mitigated because every claim shows its source. **RELY** is addressed via visible confidence labels and one-tap overrides. **DARK** patterns are absent due to plain consent without timers. **AUTO** is controlled since irreversible denials require human sign-off.

However, the missing AI disclosure and the hidden demographic data constitute material risks before launch. The team must add a clear indicator that the output is AI-generated and ensure demographic performance metrics are visible to operators to satisfy safety requirements.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
