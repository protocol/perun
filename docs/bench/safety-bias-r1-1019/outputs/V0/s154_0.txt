Based on the provided spec, the following harm category is flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec explicitly states the interface "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." While error rates were low in a pilot, presenting unverified directory matches or questionnaire interpretations as absolute fact without sourcing creates a risk of fabricated or unverified claims being treated as truth, fitting the definition of hallucination surfaced as fact.

Other categories appear addressed: **BIAS** is mitigated by testing across groups; **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal; **DISC** is handled by AI-generated labels; **DARK** is avoided via opt-in consent and no timers; **AUTO** is prevented by human sign-off on irreversible actions. However, the presentation of outputs as unsourced fact remains a critical flaw.

HARMS: HALLU
