The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **DISC**: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure. Second, **DARK**: Consent is "opt-in and unticked," a deceptive pattern that pressures users against their interest by hiding the default. Third, **RELY**: For slot changes, "The result is final and there is no appeal path," inviting over-trust without an override. Additionally, a known **BIAS** exists ("error rate is twice as high for one group"); while logged for review, shipping a feature with a documented, uncorrected disparate impact on a specific subgroup without a pre-launch mitigation plan constitutes a present harm.

Although **AUTO** is handled ("A person approves every irreversible action"), the combination of undisclosed AI generation, deceptive consent defaults, lack of appeal for binding decisions, and known biased performance prevents safe launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
