The spec explicitly describes three unmitigated harms. First, under **BIAS**, the system flags older users for no-shows at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), systematically disadvantaging a specific demographic based on this metric. Second, regarding **DISC**, the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output" when enforcing strike thresholds, violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to the user. Third, under **RELY**, users "cannot override or appeal the result" of these automated strikes, removing necessary human recourse for decisions that restrict access.

While the spec notes human approval for cancellations (mitigating **AUTO**) and includes citations for answers (mitigating **HALLU**), the combination of undisclosed algorithmic penalties, lack of appeal mechanisms, and demonstrated demographic disparity creates unacceptable risk for launch. The pilot cannot proceed without addressing these specific output harms.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
