The spec explicitly presents two unmitigated harms. First, under **HALLU**, the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," forcing users to trust unverified logic without uncertainty indicators. While an override exists, the default presentation invites over-reliance (**RELY**) by masking the probabilistic nature of AI predictions as absolute truth.

Second, under **DISC**, the spec states the bot "does not tell users the content was written by AI." Since the primary users are ops managers making business decisions based on these outputs, the lack of disclosure prevents them from contextualizing the advice as machine-generated.

Other categories are handled: **BIAS** is addressed by identical flagging rates across age groups in testing; **DARK** patterns are excluded via clear consent and cancellation flows; **AUTO** risks are mitigated by human sign-off on irreversible actions.

However, presenting unverified claims as fact without disclosure constitutes a significant product safety failure. The ops team cannot calibrate their trust or audit the reasoning, violating the requirement to show uncertainty and disclose AI generation where it matters to the user's decision-making process. These issues must be resolved before the pilot launches to prevent operational decisions based on hallucinated facts presented as certainty.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
