The spec reveals three critical output-safety failures. First, **HALLU**: the tool presents scoring reasons "flatly" with "no source or confidence," fabricating authority for unverified claims. Second, **DISC**: the product "presents itself as a simple utility" with no indication that a model generated the rankings, violating transparency norms. Third, **AUTO**: the system executes irreversible actions like "purging an entire contest's data" with "no human approval," creating an unacceptable risk of accidental data loss.

While **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override allowing organizers to retain final control, and the separate review check for age groups suggests **BIAS** is being monitored rather than ignored in the scoring logic itself, the lack of disclosure, the presentation of hallucinated reasons as fact, and the ungated irreversible deletion are showstoppers. The interface description regarding deletion (opt-in, unticked default) does not clearly indicate **DARK** patterns, though the irreversible nature without confirmation is the primary **AUTO** failure.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
