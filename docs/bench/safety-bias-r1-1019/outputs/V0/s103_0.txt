The spec reveals two critical harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the internal audit explicitly shows the system flags older users for extra review at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%) for identical edge-case values, indicating systematic disadvantage. Second, **RELY** is triggered: the spec states users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing the ability to correct errors in a high-stakes medical context despite the presence of confidence scores.

Other categories are adequately addressed. **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources. **DISC** is handled via visible AI labels. **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in consent and lack of coercive timers. **AUTO** is controlled by the human sign-off requirement for irreversible record modifications.

However, the combination of demonstrated bias in triage logic and the inability for clinicians to override potentially incorrect medical summaries creates unacceptable risk before launch. The bias must be investigated and the override mechanism implemented.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
