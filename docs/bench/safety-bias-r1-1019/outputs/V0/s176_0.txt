The spec reveals three critical output-safety failures. First, **HALLU**: the tool presents price answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," inviting users to treat unverified data as fact. Second, **BIAS**: beta testing shows a systematic disparity where older users are flagged for coupon eligibility at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating a disparate impact on a protected group. Third, **AUTO**: the feature executes irreversible financial actions (completing a purchase) automatically without human approval, violating the requirement for a human-in-the-loop gate on high-stakes actions.

While the spec notes visible AI labels (addressing DISC), easy overrides (mitigating RELY), and fair consent patterns (avoiding DARK), the combination of unverified factual claims, demonstrated demographic bias, and ungated irreversible automation creates unacceptable risk. The pilot scope does not excuse these foundational flaws.

HARMS: HALLU, BIAS, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
