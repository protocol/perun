The spec explicitly states the tool "does not tell users the content was written by AI," which directly matches the **DISC** (Missing AI-disclosure) harm category. While the spec notes usability disparities by age, it describes a remediation (simplified flow) rather than an active scoring bias in the output itself, so **BIAS** is not flagged based on the text provided. The feature includes cited sources, confidence levels, easy overrides, and human gates for fund transfers, addressing **HALLU**, **RELY**, **DARK**, and **AUTO**. However, the lack of disclosure regarding the AI-generated nature of the scores is a present harm that must be addressed before launch to ensure users understand the source of the decision support.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
