The spec explicitly flags two present harms requiring immediate attention. First, **DARK**: the signup flow employs a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box for location tracking, constituting deceptive patterns that pressure users against their own interest. Second, **HALLU**: the post-session summary presents answers as "confirmed fact with no source shown," surfacing unverified claims with unwarranted authority.

Conversely, the spec demonstrates handling for other categories. **DISC** is addressed via visible AI-generated labels. **RELY** is mitigated by allowing one-tap overrides and appeals. **AUTO** is controlled by requiring human approval for irreversible actions. Regarding **BIAS**, while the team claims comfort based on internal testing, the spec does not describe a systematic disadvantage occurring; thus, per instructions, it is not flagged as a present harm in the design itself, though the evidence cited ("within one point") is weak.

However, the presence of DARK patterns and unverified facts presented as truth constitutes a failure of the safety floor. Deceptive consent mechanisms and hallucination-as-fact cannot ship, even in a pilot.

HARMS: DARK, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
