The spec explicitly mitigates several risks: it sources claims and shows confidence labels (addressing HALLU), allows one-tap overrides and appeals (addressing RELY), and requires human approval for irreversible actions (addressing AUTO). The beta data cited shows equal flagging rates for older and younger users, indicating no described systematic disadvantage (no BIAS).

However, two harms are present in the description. First, the spec states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," which is a clear missing AI disclosure (DISC). Second, the auto-trade feature uses a pre-ticked consent box combined with an expiring ten-minute timer; this constitutes a deceptive pattern that pressures users against their interest (DARK).

Because the spec describes these specific failures—hiding the AI nature of the tool and using dark patterns for consent—the launch cannot proceed without fixing them.

HARMS: DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
