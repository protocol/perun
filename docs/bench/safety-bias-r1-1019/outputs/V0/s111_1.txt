The spec explicitly handles several categories: DISC is addressed by labeling outputs as AI-generated; RELY is mitigated by allowing one-tap overrides/appeals; and AUTO is controlled via human sign-off on irreversible steps. BIAS is claimed to be handled via pilot testing showing consistent error rates across groups.

However, two harms are present in the description:
1. **HALLU**: The interface presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown." This design choice risks surfacing unverified or fabricated claims as authoritative truth without evidence, directly matching the hallucination-as-fact definition.
2. **DARK**: The intake form uses a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box." Even if mimicking existing forms, these are deceptive patterns that pressure users against their own interest (manufactured urgency and hidden defaults).

These issues require remediation before launch to prevent misleading users and manipulating consent.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
