The spec explicitly reveals two present harms. First, **DISC** is flagged because the specification states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where machine generation matters to the user's understanding of the roster. Second, **AUTO** is triggered by the feature that "automatically... flags inactive profiles." Although the spec notes a human approves *irreversible* actions (like removal), the act of flagging users as inactive is an automated decision affecting their status that runs without a stated human-in-the-loop gate before the flag is applied. The spec mentions equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users, so **BIAS** is not flagged based on the provided text. **HALLU**, **RELY**, and **DARK** are addressed by the spec's inclusion of source labeling, confidence tiers, override capabilities, and ethical consent patterns. Because the automated flagging lacks a human gate and the AI nature is hidden, the launch cannot proceed.

HARMS: DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
