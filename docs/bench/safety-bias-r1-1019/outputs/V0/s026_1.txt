The spec demonstrates strong controls for most harm categories: math accuracy is tested across groups (mitigating BIAS), sources and confidence labels are shown with easy overrides (mitigating HALLU and RELY), AI generation is disclosed (mitigating DISC), and irreversible financing steps require human sign-off (mitigating AUTO).

However, the spec explicitly describes a DARK pattern: "a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box to opt into follow-up contact." This combines manufactured urgency with a hidden default to pressure users into consent against their own interest, fitting the definition of deceptive patterns. Since the spec confirms this design is present rather than handled, this harm must be flagged.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
