The spec reveals two present harms requiring attention before launch.

First, **DISC** is flagged. The specification explicitly states: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output—the copy uses only 'available upgrade.'" While the system functions correctly, the absence of AI disclosure where a model drives the decision matches the defined harm category.

Second, **HALLU** is flagged. The spec notes the engine "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting generated inventory suggestions as absolute facts without indicating uncertainty or sourcing fits the definition of unverified claims presented as fact, even if pilot error rates were low.

Other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by the "one-tap" override/appeal; **AUTO** is mitigated by the "human signs off on each irreversible step"; **DARK** is mitigated by opt-in, unticked defaults, and no timers; **BIAS** is not flagged as the spec claims error rates were consistent "across every group tested."

However, because the spec confirms the presence of undisclosed AI generation (DISC) and flat assertions without confidence indicators (HALLU), these must be addressed to meet safety floors regarding transparency and uncertainty.

HARMS: DISC, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
