The spec reveals two unaddressed harms. First, **DISC**: the specification explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," and screens only display "suggested priority." This lacks the required disclosure that the decision is machine-generated. Second, **DARK**: the consent mechanism is described as "opt-in and unticked." An unticked box for opt-in consent is a deceptive pattern that pressures users against their interest by hiding the default state or making active consent difficult to perceive, contradicting the claim of being truly opt-in.

Other categories are handled: **BIAS** is measured with subgroup data (11% flag rate for both age groups); **HALLU** is mitigated via cited sources; **RELY** is addressed with one-tap overrides and appeals; and **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human approval for irreversible actions. However, the missing AI disclosure and the deceptive consent default are present in the design and must be fixed before launch.

HARMS: DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
