The spec reveals three unaddressed harms. First, **BIAS** is present: validation data shows the model flags older users at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%). While logged, this disparity lacks a mitigation plan or threshold before launch. Second, **RELY** is triggered because users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing the human gate necessary for correcting erroneous high-stakes educational placements. Third, **DISC** applies explicitly: "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to the user.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by showing sources and confidence labels; **DARK** patterns are absent due to clear consent and no timers; **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human approval for irreversible actions. However, the combination of unmitigated bias, lack of appeal, and missing disclosure creates unacceptable risk for the pilot.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
