The spec explicitly flags a disparate impact where the model affects older users (18%) at twice the rate of younger users (9%), satisfying the definition of **BIAS**. While the spec notes transparency measures (source citations, confidence labels, overrides, and disclosures) that address HALLU, RELY, and DISC, and describes a fair enrollment flow avoiding DARK patterns, it confirms the system executes reordering immediately without human review ("nobody reviews it first"). Since waitlist reordering determines access to services and the spec admits a systematic disadvantage to a protected demographic (age), this constitutes an unsafe automated decision affecting user opportunity. The presence of unmitigated bias in an automated output requires remediation before launch.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
