The spec explicitly confirms several harms are present. First, **HALLU** is flagged because the interface presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," despite being a probabilistic triage tool. Second, **DISC** is present as the spec states Pawse "does not tell users the content was written by AI." Third, **DARK** patterns are described: follow-up offers use a "ten-minute timer" (manufactured urgency) and "pre-ticked" consent, forcing users to actively decline.

Conversely, **BIAS** appears addressed via testing across groups with stable error rates. **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override and appeal mechanism. **AUTO** is handled by the requirement that "a person approves every irreversible action."

However, the combination of presenting unverified medical triage as absolute fact, hiding the AI nature of the advice, and using deceptive consent flows creates unacceptable risk for a health-adjacent product. The hallucination risk presented as fact and the dark patterns must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
