The spec explicitly presents two unmitigated harms. First, under **HALLU**, the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," forcing the ops team to trust unverified logic without uncertainty indicators. While an override exists, the default presentation is deceptive regarding the claim's verification status. Second, under **DISC**, the spec states the bot "does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating the transparency requirement for machine-generated outputs reaching end-users (via ops managers).

Other categories are addressed: **BIAS** is mitigated by identical flagging rates across age groups in testing; **RELY** is partially handled by the "one tap" override, though the lack of uncertainty cues remains a concern tied to HALLU; **DARK** patterns are avoided via clear consent and no timers; **AUTO** is gated by human sign-off for irreversible actions.

Because the feature ships with fabricated certainty (no sources) and missing AI disclosure despite available controls, it cannot proceed until these transparency gaps are closed.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
