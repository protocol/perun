The spec reveals three unaddressed harms. First, **DISC**: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to users. Second, **RELY**: While sources are shown, "the result is final and there is no appeal path for a slot change," removing the user's ability to override or correct automated decisions. Third, **BIAS**: The spec explicitly admits the "error rate is twice as high for one group—patients using older phones." Although the team plans to "log that and review weekly," shipping a feature with a known, measured disparity affecting a specific subgroup without a mitigation plan constitutes a present bias harm.

Other categories are handled: **AUTO** is mitigated because "a person approves every irreversible action"; **DARK** is avoided via opt-in consent without timers; **HALLU** is addressed by showing sources. However, the combination of undisclosed AI generation, lack of appeal mechanisms, and known unequal performance creates unacceptable residual risk for launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
