The spec explicitly handles several harms: it reports subgroup testing for **BIAS**, provides sources and confidence labels to mitigate **HALLU** and **RELY**, enables one-tap overrides for **RELY**, and includes an AI-generated label for **DISC**.

However, two critical issues remain unaddressed in the design:
1.  **AUTO**: The spec states, "The tool acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Waiving fines is a financial decision impacting revenue and policy compliance. Executing this irreversible action without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for high-stakes automation.
2.  **DARK**: The interface includes a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box." These are classic deceptive patterns that manufacture urgency and hide defaults, pressuring users against their own interest rather than ensuring informed consent.

Because the feature automates financial actions without human review and employs deceptive interface patterns, it cannot proceed.

HARMS: AUTO, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
