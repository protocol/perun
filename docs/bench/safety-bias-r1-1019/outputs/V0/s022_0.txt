The spec explicitly discloses several mitigations: confidence levels and sources address over-trust (RELY); equal flagging rates (11%) and appeal options suggest bias (BIAS) is monitored; opt-in consent with no timers counters dark patterns (DARK); and human sign-off prevents unsafe automation (AUTO).

However, the spec states: "The kiosk does not tell users the content was written by AI, keeping the focus on utility." This is an explicit admission that machine-generated recommendations and decisions are presented without disclosure. Since the recommendation engine generates suggestions (outputs) that influence user choices, the lack of AI disclosure constitutes a present harm under the DISC category. The rationale ("focus on utility") does not negate the absence of the disclosure itself.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
