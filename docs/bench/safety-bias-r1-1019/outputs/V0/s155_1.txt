Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec states the app "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Despite low error rates, presenting outputs as absolute fact without sourcing or uncertainty indicators constitutes hallucination surfaced as fact.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly admits, "The product does not tell users the content was written by AI." This is a missing AI disclosure.

The spec indicates other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override/appeal; **AUTO** is mitigated by the nurse confirmation gate for irreversible actions; **DARK** is mitigated by plain consent without pressure; **BIAS** is addressed by testing showing consistent error rates across groups.

However, the presence of unaddressed hallucination presentation and missing disclosure requires remediation before launch to meet safety floors regarding transparency and truthfulness.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
