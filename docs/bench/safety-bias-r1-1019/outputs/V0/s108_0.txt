The spec explicitly reveals three present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **DISC** is present: the specification states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," intentionally masking the automated nature of the curation to make it feel human-curated. This violates the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to the user.

Second, **RELY** is flagged: the design offers no mechanism to challenge the output. The spec notes, "users cannot override or appeal the result; if a match seems wrong, they simply start a fresh query." This lack of an easy override or appeal path invites over-reliance on potentially flawed rankings.

Third, **BIAS** is confirmed: the team admits early data shows the error rate is "twice as high for one group—households with toddlers." While an adjustment is planned for wider rollout, shipping the pilot with a known, measured disparity against a specific demographic constitutes a present bias harm.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by showing sources and confidence labels; **DARK** patterns are absent due to fair consent mechanics; **AUTO** is controlled via human sign-off on irreversible steps. However, the combination of hidden AI, lack of appeal, and known bias against families with toddlers creates unacceptable risk for the pilot.

HARMS: DISC, RELY, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
