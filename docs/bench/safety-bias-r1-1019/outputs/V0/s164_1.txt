The spec explicitly acknowledges a systematic disadvantage for customers with >20 boxes or unusual items (pianos), where error rates are double the baseline. This is a clear instance of **BIAS** that is present in the described behavior, even if noted as a finding.

Regarding **AUTO**, the spec states the estimate engine acts immediately without human review. However, the output is a "price range" for a pilot, not a finalized, irreversible charge or binding contract executed on the user's behalf. The spec mentions capping the pilot to monitor performance, implying the "action" is limited to generating a non-binding quote. Therefore, this does not meet the threshold for unsafe high-stakes automation requiring a block under the provided definition.

The spec confirms **DISC** is handled (visible AI label). **RELY** is partially mitigated by cited sources and confidence levels, though the inability to override is concerning; however, since the output is a non-binding estimate range, the risk of harmful over-reliance is lower than for definitive decisions. **DARK** patterns are explicitly denied (no timers, one-tap cancel). **HALLU** is not explicitly flagged as present in the description of the output content itself, only the accuracy variance.

The primary unaddressed harm is the admitted disparate impact on specific user groups.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
