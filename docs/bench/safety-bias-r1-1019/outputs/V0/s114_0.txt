The spec demonstrates strong controls for most harm categories: it explicitly addresses bias testing across groups, prevents hallucination by citing sources, mitigates over-reliance with overrides and appeals, ensures disclosure of AI generation, and avoids dark patterns with clear consent.

However, the spec explicitly admits a critical failure in the **AUTO** category. It states that if a mortgage lender requests a rate lock, the system "executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Financial rate locks are high-stakes decisions with significant monetary consequences. Executing such irreversible actions without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the fundamental safety floor for agentic systems. This specific exception creates an unacceptable risk of financial harm before launch.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
