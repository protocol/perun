The spec describes a system that executes irreversible actions without human review. Specifically, it states the tool "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." In the context of municipal building and zoning permits, decisions can lead to costly construction errors, legal liabilities, or safety violations if incorrect. Treating a two-week pilot with only 40 applications as sufficient evidence to bypass human oversight for high-stakes regulatory actions constitutes a critical failure in risk management.

While the spec indicates that disclosure (DISC), override mechanisms (mitigating RELY), and dark patterns (DARK) are handled, and claims bias (BIAS) was tested, the absence of a human-in-the-loop gate for an action with potential irreversible consequences is a showstopper. The claim that low error rates in a tiny pilot justify full automation ignores the severity of potential failures in this domain. Additionally, stating answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence" while taking automatic action exacerbates the risk of hallucination (HALLU) causing real-world damage, even if HALLU isn't explicitly confirmed as occurring in the pilot. The primary block is the unsafe automation.
