The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: it includes human approval for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO), labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), allows one-tap overrides (mitigating RELY), and avoids deceptive patterns (mitigating DARK).

However, the spec admits a systematic disadvantage: internal data shows the tool flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users. The team plans to "track but not act on yet." This confirms **BIAS** is present and unaddressed before launch. Additionally, the tool "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," which presents unverified scores as absolute facts without uncertainty indicators, confirming **HALLU** (hallucination/unverified claims presented as fact) and contributing to **RELY** despite the override button, as the flat presentation invites over-trust in potentially flawed scoring.

Since the spec acknowledges a measurable disparity affecting a protected group (age) and commits to inaction during the pilot, the feature ships with known, unmitigated harm.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
