The spec presents three active harms. First, **HALLU**: the interface displays every verdict as "confirmed fact" with no sources, risking fabricated claims being treated as truth despite underlying uncertainty. Second, **DARK**: settlement offers utilize a ten-minute expiration timer and pre-ticked consent, explicitly pressuring users against their own interests. Third, **RELY**: while an override exists, presenting un-sourced answers as absolute facts invites over-trust, undermining the user's critical assessment of the AI's reliability.

Conversely, **DISC** is handled (outputs are labeled AI-generated), **AUTO** is mitigated (irreversible actions require human approval), and **BIAS** appears addressed (error rates were consistent across groups). However, the presence of deceptive patterns (DARK) and the assertion of unverified facts (HALLU) creates unacceptable risk for a financial dispute tool. These issues must be resolved before launch to prevent user harm and regulatory exposure.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
