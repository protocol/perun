Based on the spec provided, the following harms are present:

**HALLU**: The spec claims "performance is consistent across age groups" based solely on identical flag rates (11%) for older and younger users. This ignores other protected classes (race, gender) and potential false positive/negative disparities within those age buckets, presenting an unverified fairness claim as fact.

**RELY**: For failed applications, the result is described as "final" with "no appeal path." This removes the user's ability to correct errors or challenge the automated decision, inviting over-reliance on the system's initial judgment.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure when it matters to the user.

**DARK**: The interface employs a "ten-minute timer" for the offer and uses "pre-ticked" consent. These are deceptive patterns that manufacture urgency and hide defaults, pressuring users against their own interest.

While **AUTO** is mitigated by the human sign-off on irreversible steps, and **BIAS** is claimed to be handled (though the evidence is weak, the spec asserts it is handled rather than describing the harm as active), the four categories above are explicitly described as active features.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
