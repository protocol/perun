The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity where the error rate is "twice as high for one group." Although a confirmation step is added, the underlying systematic disadvantage remains present in the described behavior, triggering **BIAS**. The system presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," which constitutes **HALLU** (fabricated or unverified claims surfaced as authoritative). Furthermore, the design states "users cannot override or appeal the result, by design," creating a clear **RELY** risk by inviting over-trust without an easy override.

Conversely, the spec indicates other harms are handled: **DISC** is addressed via the "AI-generated" footer; **DARK** is mitigated by plain opt-in consent without timers; and **AUTO** is controlled by requiring a supervisor to confirm denials (irreversible actions) before they run.

Because the spec confirms the presence of bias, hallucination-as-fact, and over-reliance without describing sufficient controls to neutralize them (e.g., showing uncertainty sources or enabling appeals), the feature cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
