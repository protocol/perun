The spec reveals two present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **RELY** (Over-reliance) is evident: the tool "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." While moderators currently treat it as a suggestion, the lack of displayed uncertainty invites automation bias, contradicting the safety principle of showing defined confidence tiers rather than false precision.

Second, **DARK** (Deceptive patterns) is explicitly described: the interface includes a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box." These are classic dark patterns that manufacture urgency and hide defaults, nudging users against their own interest during batch exports.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is covered by the visible AI label; **AUTO** is mitigated by the human sign-off on irreversible steps; **BIAS** and **HALLU** are not described as present in the spec's narrative (the pilot data mentions error rates but does not describe systematic disadvantage or fabrication as an active, unmitigated risk in the design).

However, the presence of dark patterns and missing uncertainty indicators constitutes a failure of the product's output safety floor. The pre-ticked box and countdown must be removed, and uncertainty must be surfaced, to prevent user harm.

HARMS: RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
