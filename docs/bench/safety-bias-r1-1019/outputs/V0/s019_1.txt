The spec explicitly handles several harm categories: it includes an AI-generated label (mitigating DISC), provides one-tap override/appeal (mitigating RELY), and uses plain consent without pressure (mitigating DARK). It also claims internal testing showed consistent error rates across groups, suggesting an attempt to address BIAS, though the claim itself is unverified here, the spec asserts it is handled.

However, two critical harms are present in the description:
1.  **HALLU**: The spec states, "The app presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." This directly matches the definition of presenting unverified claims as fact.
2.  **AUTO**: The spec notes, "Matching happens automatically... nobody reviews it first." While roommate matching may not always be irreversible, entering a lease agreement is a high-stakes legal and financial commitment. Automating the final match decision without a human review step before the user acts on it constitutes unsafe automation of a high-stakes action in this context.

Because the feature presents outputs as absolute fact without sourcing and automates high-stakes matching decisions without a human gate, it fails the safety floor for these specific risks.

HARMS: HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
