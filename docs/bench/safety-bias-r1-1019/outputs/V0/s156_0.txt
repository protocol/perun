The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity where the error rate is "twice as high for one group." Although a second confirmation step is added, the underlying systematic disadvantage remains present in the described behavior, triggering **BIAS**. The system presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," which constitutes **HALLU** by surfacing unverified claims as absolute truth. Furthermore, the design states "users cannot override or appeal the result, by design," creating a clear **RELY** risk by inviting over-trust without an easy override.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed via the "AI-generated" footer; **DARK** is mitigated by plain opt-in consent without timers; and **AUTO** is controlled because a supervisor must confirm any denial (irreversible action) before it runs. However, the presence of Bias, Hallucination presented as fact, and enforced Over-reliance represents significant unresolved harm.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
