The spec explicitly addresses the primary harm categories with mitigations: uncertainty is shown via confidence labels; overrides and appeals are one-tap (RELY handled); AI generation is disclosed (DISC handled); consent is clear without pressure (DARK handled); and irreversible actions like denial require human approval (AUTO handled).

However, the spec describes a potential **BIAS** issue that is not resolved. It notes that pre-launch testing flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users, stating the team is "monitoring the pilot's distribution to confirm the pattern holds." While the rates are currently equal in this specific test, the explicit acknowledgment of demographic flagging differences (even if currently matched) combined with a plan only to "monitor" rather than validate fairness across all protected groups before launch constitutes an unresolved risk inventory item. The spec admits the system flags demographic groups but lacks a verified pass threshold or a completed fairness evaluation beyond this single data point. Per the safety floor, an unverified metric regarding demographic impact cannot be assumed safe, and shipping with a known potential for systematic disadvantage requires a completed measurement plan, not just ongoing monitoring during a pilot that affects license eligibility.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
