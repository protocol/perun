The spec demonstrates active mitigation for most harm categories: AI disclosure is present (addressing DISC), uncertainty and overrides are provided (addressing RELY), consent mechanisms avoid pressure (addressing DARK), and irreversible actions require human sign-off (addressing AUTO). While sources are cited for answers, the core matching output relies on a known defect: the spec explicitly states the error rate is "twice as high for one group." Despite tracking this in weekly reviews, shipping a feature with a known, uncorrected systematic disadvantage constitutes a present bias harm. The existence of a monitoring plan does not negate the presence of the bias itself at launch.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
