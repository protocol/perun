The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: it reports equal flagging rates for older and younger users (no BIAS), requires cited sources for claims (mitigates HALLU), displays confidence levels with one-tap overrides (mitigates RELY), labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigates DISC), and uses unticked consent without pressure (mitigates DARK).

However, the spec states the bot "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" once the user taps "submit claim." While user initiation is present, the system automatically files claims—actions involving financial liability and legal assertions—without a final human review gate or intermediate confirmation step after the initial trigger. Under the safety floor, high-stakes or irreversible automated actions require a human-in-the-loop gate by default; full autonomous execution of such actions is the failure this skill prevents. The spec describes this automation as a feature rather than a risk to be gated, indicating the control is missing.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
