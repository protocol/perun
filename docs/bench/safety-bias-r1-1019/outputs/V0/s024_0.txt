The spec reveals several unaddressed output safety harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the error rate is double for power tool borrowers, yet the fix ("tuning sensitivity") is planned for *after* the pilot, meaning v1 ships with this known disparity. Second, **RELY** is a critical failure; the spec states users "cannot override or appeal" predictions, removing human agency despite known errors. Third, **DARK** patterns are built into the interface: a countdown timer creates manufactured urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box hides loan terms by default. Finally, while **AUTO** is correctly handled for destructive actions (human approval required), the combination of high error rates, no appeal mechanism, and dark patterns creates a risky launch environment. The transparency efforts (confidence labels, AI disclosure) are undermined by the inability to act on that information.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
