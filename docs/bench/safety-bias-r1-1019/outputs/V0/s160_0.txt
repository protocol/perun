The spec explicitly describes several unmitigated output harms. First, the bot presents recommendations "flatly" with "no source or confidence," creating a high risk of **HALLU** (unverified claims presented as fact) and **RELY** (inviting over-trust by hiding uncertainty and offering no appeal path). Second, the design intentionally omits any indication that a model generated the output to maintain a "conversational" tone, which is a clear **DISC** failure. While the spec confirms that irreversible queue changes are gated behind human approval (mitigating **AUTO**) and that nudges are opt-in without dark patterns (mitigating **DARK**), the core recommendation feature ships with no honesty about its artificial nature, no way to verify its basis, and no mechanism to challenge it. The claim of low "error rates" does not verify the absence of bias across subgroups, but since the spec does not describe biased outcomes, **BIAS** is not flagged based strictly on the text provided. However, the combination of hidden AI, fabricated authority, and locked decisions requires remediation before launch.
