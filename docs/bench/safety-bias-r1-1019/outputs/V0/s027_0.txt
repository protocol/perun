The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: sources and confidence labels mitigate HALLU; override/appeal options mitigate RELY; AI-generation labels mitigate DISC; and human sign-off on irreversible steps mitigates AUTO. While a known error disparity for flat roofs is acknowledged, the spec states an adjustment is planned before full rollout, implying the bias is being managed rather than left present for the pilot's intended learning scope.

However, two dark patterns are clearly described as active features. The "ten-minute timer" creates manufactured urgency, and "pre-ticked consent" for emails hides defaults, both pressuring users against their own interest (DARK). Additionally, the spec admits the error rate is "twice as high" for homes with flat roofs during this pilot. Since the tool will ship to this group before the model is adjusted, it systematically disadvantages them with known inferior accuracy (BIAS).

HARMS: DARK, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
