The spec explicitly confirms several harms are present. First, **HALLU** is flagged: the planner "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified routing logic as absolute fact without uncertainty indicators. Second, **DISC** is present: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement to disclose machine-generated decisions where it matters to users. Third, **DARK** patterns are described for accessible pickup: the offer "expires on a ten-minute timer" (manufactured urgency) and "consent is pre-ticked" (hidden default), pressuring users against their own interest.

While **BIAS** was investigated (usability review), the spec indicates a fix was implemented (list view), so it is not currently flagged as an open harm. **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override. **AUTO** is handled via human sign-off for irreversible steps. However, the combination of undisclosed AI, fabricated certainty, and deceptive consent mechanisms creates unacceptable risk for launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
