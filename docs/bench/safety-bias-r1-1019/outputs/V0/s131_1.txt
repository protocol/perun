The spec explicitly flags two critical harm patterns despite some mitigations elsewhere. First, under **HALLU**, the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Presenting unverified algorithmic outputs as absolute fact without provenance creates a high risk of operators acting on errors as truth. Second, under **RELY**, the design states "Users cannot override or appeal the result—the route order is final once issued." This eliminates the human safety valve, inviting dangerous over-trust in the automation.

While the spec notes that irreversible actions have a human sign-off (mitigating **AUTO**) and that outputs are labeled as AI-generated (mitigating **DISC**), the combination of presenting outputs as absolute fact while removing the ability to override them constitutes an unsafe design for high-stakes infrastructure management. The claim of low error rates in pilot testing does not negate the structural inability to correct errors when they inevitably occur in production.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
