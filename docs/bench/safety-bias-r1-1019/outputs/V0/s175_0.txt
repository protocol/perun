Based on the provided spec, PillRoute explicitly addresses most output-safety concerns: it includes AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), easy overrides/appeals (mitigating RELY), ethical consent patterns (mitigating DARK), and human-in-the-loop gates for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO). The planned detail view addresses the current lack of sources.

However, the spec explicitly states: "In our beta, the error rate is twice as high for one group." While the team tracks this internally, shipping a feature with a known, unmitigated disparity in accuracy for a specific demographic—especially one serving vulnerable populations like seniors and the chronically ill—constitutes a present risk of systematic disadvantage. The spec acknowledges the existence of this bias but does not describe a fix or a public mitigation strategy before launch, only internal tracking. Therefore, the harm is present in the shipped product state described.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
