However, the spec reveals a clear disparity in system behavior: the suggestion engine flagged 18% of older users for follow-up versus only 9% of younger users. This describes a systematic disadvantage based on age, satisfying the definition of BIAS. The spec notes this finding but does not describe a fix or a human gate specifically for this disparate impact before the pilot ships.

Additionally, the statement "the system acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first" raises concerns. While releasing tools might be reversible, if the "follow-up question" triggers an account flag or restriction automatically without review, it could constitute an unsafe automated decision (AUTO) depending on the consequence. Given the explicit evidence of demographic disparity in the output, the BIAS category is flagged as present and unaddressed.
