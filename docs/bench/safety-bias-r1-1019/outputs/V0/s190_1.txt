The spec demonstrates strong controls for most harm categories: sources and confidence labels address HALLU and RELY; visible AI labeling covers DISC; consent mechanics and lack of timers mitigate DARK; and human sign-off on irreversible steps handles AUTO.

However, the spec explicitly admits a systemic failure: "the error rate is twice as high for one group." This is a confirmed instance of **BIAS**, where outputs systematically disadvantage a specific demographic. While the team plans to "track this separately," shipping a feature with a known, unmitigated disparity in error rates violates the safety floor. A measurement plan or release note disclosure does not fix the underlying unfair outcome before it impacts users. The harm is present and active at launch.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
