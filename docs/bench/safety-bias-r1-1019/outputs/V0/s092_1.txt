The spec reveals three critical output-safety failures requiring immediate remediation.

First, **DISC** is present: the specification explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output; it reads like a standard triage worksheet." Users cannot distinguish machine-generated triage rankings from human analysis, violating the transparency floor.

Second, **RELY** is triggered by the design constraint that "the result is final and there is no appeal path." Despite showing confidence levels, the system removes the nurse's ability to override or correct the ranking, inviting dangerous automation bias in a high-stakes medical context.

Third, **BIAS** is acknowledged as an active risk. While a stop condition exists if error rates double for a group, the spec admits the potential for systematic disadvantage ("whether the error rate is twice as high for one group") without confirming baseline fairness testing prior to this pilot launch. Shipping with a known potential for unverified bias without pre-launch measurement constitutes a failure to fully MAP and MEASURE.

Although **AUTO** is mitigated by the "human signs off on each irreversible step," and **DARK** patterns are absent (opt-in consent, no timers), the combination of hidden AI agency, removed appeal mechanisms, and unverified bias potential creates unacceptable residual risk for patient safety.

HARMS: DISC, RELY, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
