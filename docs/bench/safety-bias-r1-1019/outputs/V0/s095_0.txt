The spec explicitly describes several handled controls: overrides exist (mitigating RELY), human sign-off gates irreversible steps (mitigating AUTO), and the UI avoids pressure (mitigating DARK). However, two harms are present as described facts. First, the system "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting fabricated or unverified inventory logic as absolute fact without uncertainty markers, triggering HALLU. Second, the spec states "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning the machine-generated nature of the decision is hidden from the user, triggering DISC. While error rates were monitored during the pilot, the current design ships without disclosure or uncertainty visualization.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
