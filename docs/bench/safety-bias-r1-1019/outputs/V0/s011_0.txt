The spec explicitly handles several categories: DISC is covered by the "visible AI-generated label"; RELY is addressed because users can "override the result or appeal it with one tap"; DARK is mitigated via "opt-in and unticked" consent with "no timers"; and BIAS is claimed handled via pilot testing showing uniform error rates.

However, two critical harms remain present in the description:

1.  **HALLU**: The system "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting unverified eligibility checks as flat facts without grounding or uncertainty indicators creates a high risk of hallucinations being accepted as truth.
2.  **AUTO**: The tool "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." For municipal building and zoning permits—a high-stakes domain affecting property rights and safety—executing decisions without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for irreversible or costly actions. Pilot error rates do not justify removing human oversight for high-stakes automation.

HARMS: HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
