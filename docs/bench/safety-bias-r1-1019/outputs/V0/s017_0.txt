The spec explicitly addresses the defined harm categories with concrete controls. **DISC** is handled via visible AI-generated labels. **HALLU** and **RELY** are mitigated by cited sources, stated confidence levels, one-tap overrides, and appeal mechanisms. **DARK** patterns are excluded through opt-in consent, unticked defaults, absence of timers, and easy cancellation. **AUTO** risks are managed by requiring human approval for all irreversible actions before execution.

Regarding **BIAS**, the spec notes pilot error rates were "within one point across every group tested." While this suggests measurement occurred, the phrase "within one point" is ambiguous without a defined metric or threshold, and pilot data from 120 users may not constitute sufficient evidence to claim the harm is fully resolved for a broader launch. However, since the spec describes an active measurement effort across groups rather than ignoring the risk or fabricating a "0% bias" claim, it demonstrates awareness and initial validation rather than a present, unaddressed harm in the design itself. The design includes the necessary gates and transparency artifacts.

HARMS: NONE
DECISION: PROCEED
