The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity where the error rate is "twice as high for one group," satisfying the definition of **BIAS**. While the spec notes this segment is tracked, it does not describe a mitigation that prevents the systematic disadvantage from occurring during the pilot; measurement alone is not a fix.

Regarding **HALLU**, the system "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting probabilistic predictions as flat facts without uncertainty markers invites users to treat unverified outputs as ground truth.

The **AUTO** category is triggered because the spec states SeatSift "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" for auto-issuing tickets. Issuing a ticket (consuming inventory, creating a financial record) is an irreversible action executed autonomously. The spec mentions manager override for "results," but the execution itself lacks a pre-action human gate, violating the safety floor for irreversible automation.

Categories **DISC**, **RELY**, and **DARK** appear handled: AI labels are visible, overrides are one-tap, and consent mechanisms are described as non-deceptive (opt-in, unticked, no timers).

However, the presence of unmitigated bias, flat factual presentation of uncertain data, and autonomous irreversible actions constitutes significant residual risk that requires control adjustments before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
