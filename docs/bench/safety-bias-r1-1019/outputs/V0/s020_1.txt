The spec explicitly identifies several unmitigated harms. First, **DARK** patterns are present: the sign-up flow uses a countdown timer to manufacture urgency and a pre-ticked consent box, pressuring users against their own interests. Second, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the system executes irreversible actions (finalizing a lease match) without a human-in-the-loop gate, relying solely on user confirmation which is insufficient for high-stakes automation. Third, **BIAS** is admitted: the spec notes a disparate impact where older users are flagged for conflicts at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), with no fix planned before launch other than monitoring.

While the spec claims to handle transparency (showing sources/confidence) and disclosure (labeling AI outputs), the presence of active dark patterns, ungated irreversible automation, and acknowledged disparate impact constitutes a blocked launch. The "monitoring" plan for bias is not a mitigation.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
