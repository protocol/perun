The spec explicitly surfaces several product-output safety failures. First, the interface employs **DARK** patterns: a countdown timer manufactures urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box hides defaults, pressuring users against their own interest. Second, the system executes **AUTO** risks by finalizing lease matches—an irreversible, high-stakes action—without a human-in-the-loop gate, relying solely on user confirmation. Third, the spec admits a **BIAS** issue where older users are flagged for lease conflicts at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%); while the team plans to "monitor" this, shipping with a known, unmitigated disparate impact constitutes a present harm.

Other categories appear handled: the app labels outputs (**DISC** cleared), shows sources and confidence labels with easy overrides (**RELY** cleared), and grounds claims (**HALLU** cleared). However, the presence of dark patterns, ungated irreversible actions, and unaddressed bias requires intervention before launch.

HARMS: DARK, AUTO, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
