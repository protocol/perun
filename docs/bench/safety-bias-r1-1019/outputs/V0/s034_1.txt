The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: it labels AI outputs (mitigating DISC), shows odds and confidence with citations (mitigating HALLU and RELY), allows one-tap overrides and appeals (mitigating RELY), uses opt-in consent without pressure (mitigating DARK), and requires human approval for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO). Fairness is claimed as a goal, but the spec admits a specific failure: "The error rate is twice as high for one group—mobile users." This confirms a systematic disadvantage based on device type, satisfying the definition of BIAS. While the pilot limits exposure, shipping a feature with a known, uncorrected disparity where one group suffers double the error rate constitutes a present bias harm that requires mitigation before broader reliance, even in a pilot.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
