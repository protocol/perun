The spec explicitly describes two present harms. First, under **DISC**, the system "does not tell users the content was written by AI," failing to disclose machine-generated outputs. Second, under **DARK**, the premium features employ a "ten-minute timer" (manufactured urgency) and "pre-ticked" consent (hidden defaults), which are deceptive patterns pressuring users against their interests.

Other categories are handled: **BIAS** is addressed via pilot data showing equal flagging rates; **RELY** is mitigated by cited sources, confidence levels, and one-tap overrides; **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human approval for irreversible actions. **HALLU** is partially addressed by citations, though the reliance on AI without disclosure remains a transparency risk covered by DISC.

Because deceptive patterns and missing AI disclosure are active in the shipped design, the launch must be blocked to remove the dark patterns and implement appropriate disclosure.

HARMS: DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
