The spec demonstrates strong handling of several harm categories: it cites sources and shows data to mitigate **HALLU**; it provides confidence levels, one-tap overrides, and an appeal queue to address **RELY**; it includes visible AI labels for **DISC**; and it avoids dark patterns (**DARK**) with plain consent and no timers. Pilot results across groups suggest **BIAS** was considered, though subgroup specifics are summarized rather than detailed.

However, the spec explicitly states: "The automation acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Route assignments determine whether students arrive at school safely and on time; errors can leave children stranded or create unsafe walking conditions. This constitutes a high-stakes action with potential safety implications. Despite the limited rollout, the absence of a human-in-the-loop gate before execution violates the safety floor for **AUTO**. Limiting the scope to 12 schools is a risk mitigation strategy, not a substitute for a human gate on irreversible or high-stakes automated actions.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
