These are classic dark patterns that nudge users against their own interest by reducing friction for the business at the expense of informed user choice. Additionally, while pilot error rates were close across groups, a one-point difference does not guarantee the absence of systematic disadvantage (BIAS) at scale, though the spec does not explicitly describe a biased outcome, so BIAS is not flagged based strictly on the text provided.
