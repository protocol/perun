The spec explicitly describes a disparate impact where the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, confirming **BIAS**. While the spec notes confidence labels, overrides, appeals, AI disclosure, and non-deceptive consent (addressing RELY, DISC, and DARK), it states the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Reordering waitlists and denying access based on automated scoring without human review constitutes a high-stakes decision executed via **AUTO**. The presence of unmitigated bias and fully automated high-stakes actions requires blocking the launch until a human-in-the-loop gate is implemented and the disparate impact is addressed.

HARMS: BIAS,AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
