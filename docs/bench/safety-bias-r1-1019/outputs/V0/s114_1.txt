The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (handling DISC), shows sources and confidence labels with override/appeal paths (handling HALLU and RELY), reports tested error rates across groups (addressing BIAS concerns), and uses clear consent without pressure (handling DARK).

However, the spec admits a critical failure in the AUTO category. It states: "if the policyholder's mortgage lender requests a rate lock, FloodLine executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Executing irreversible financial actions without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for high-stakes automation. This specific exception creates an unmanaged risk of harmful automated decisions that cannot be easily undone before human review.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
