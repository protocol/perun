The spec explicitly presents several unmitigated risks. First, under **HALLU**, the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," creating a high risk of fabricated claims being trusted as truth. Second, **RELY** is triggered because "users cannot override or appeal the result," inviting dangerous over-trust without recourse. Third, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the engine "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." While offers may seem low-stakes, automated financial decisions (discounts/free cycles) affecting business budgets and user expectations without human oversight constitute unsafe automation in this context.

Conversely, **DISC** is handled; the spec states the app "labels every output as AI-generated." **DARK** patterns are absent; the spec describes "opt-in and unticked" consent with "no timers." **BIAS** is mentioned regarding different offer tiers for age groups (11% each), but the spec does not explicitly state this results in systematic disadvantage or unfair exclusion, only differentiation; however, the combination of unexplained differentiation with the inability to appeal reinforces the reliance and automation risks.

The primary blockers are the presentation of unverified outputs as fact, the lack of user override, and the fully automated execution of financial offers without human review. These must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
