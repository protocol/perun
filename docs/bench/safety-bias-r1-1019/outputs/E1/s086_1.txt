The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: it includes an AI-generated label (handling DISC), cites sources and states confidence levels (mitigating HALLU and RELY regarding information trust), and requires human approval for irreversible financial actions (handling AUTO). Location sharing uses clear opt-in without pressure (handling DARK).

However, the spec states: "users cannot override or appeal the result of a route calculation." While the system provides data to "judge reliability," removing the ability to appeal or override the core output creates a rigid dependency on the system's judgment. If the algorithm fails or provides a suboptimal path due to unmodeled constraints, the user is trapped with no recourse. This design invites over-reliance by removing the safety valve of user correction or formal appeal, fitting the definition of RELY (no easy override or appeal). Although error rates were close across groups during the pilot, the structural lack of an appeal mechanism remains a present harm in the design description.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
