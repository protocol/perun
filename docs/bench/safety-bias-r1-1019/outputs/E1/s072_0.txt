The spec reveals three critical harms. First, **HALLU**: the bot "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified claims as absolute fact. Second, **RELY**: by hiding uncertainty and providing "no appeal path" once a claim is filed, the design invites over-trust and removes recourse. Third, **DARK**: the interface uses a "ten-minute timer" and "pre-ticked consent," forcing users to actively cancel rather than affirmatively opt-in, which pressures them against their own interest.

While the spec notes a visible AI label (addressing DISC), human approval for irreversible actions (addressing AUTO), and claimed equal error rates (suggesting BIAS is considered), the combination of fabricated certainty, lack of appeals, and deceptive consent patterns creates unacceptable risk. The claim that error rates were "within one point" is an assertion without provided methodology or raw data, but the structural flaws in interaction design are explicit.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
