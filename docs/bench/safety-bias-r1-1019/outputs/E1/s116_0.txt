The spec reveals two present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted: the system flags older users for quote incompleteness at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%). The added checklist is a mitigation plan, but the spec confirms the disparity exists, constituting a present harm until re-measurement proves it closed.

Second, **RELY** is present by design. The system states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," inviting over-trust. Crucially, users "cannot override or appeal" a disagreeable result and must restart the entire interview. This lack of an easy override or correction mechanism forces blind acceptance or abandonment, violating the requirement to make outputs correctable.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is covered by visible AI labels; **DARK** is avoided via clear consent without timers; **AUTO** is gated by human approval for irreversible actions; **HALLU** is a risk due to flat statements, but the primary failure mode described is the inability to challenge those statements (RELY) rather than a specific fabrication claim in the spec.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
