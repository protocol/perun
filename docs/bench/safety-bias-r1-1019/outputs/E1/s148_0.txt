The spec explicitly confirms several harms that will ship unchanged. First, the system exhibits **BIAS**: it flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, and the team admits to "shipping that behavior as-is." Second, the bot presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," creating a high risk of **HALLU** (fabricated claims presented as authority). Third, **DISC** is present because "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," hiding the automated nature of the decision from users.

While the spec notes that users can override results (mitigating RELY), uses clear consent patterns (mitigating DARK), and requires human sign-off before irreversible execution (mitigating AUTO), the confirmed presence of bias, hallucination risks, and missing disclosure constitutes a failure of the product output safety floor. Shipping a feature with known disparate impact and no AI transparency violates the requirement to measure and manage harm before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
