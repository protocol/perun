The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: it labels outputs as AI-generated (addressing DISC), allows one-tap overrides/appeals (addressing RELY), and requires human sign-off before writing irreversible data to the registrar (addressing AUTO). While error rates were reported as consistent across groups during pilot validation, the spec does not describe any ongoing mechanism to detect systematic disadvantage in live deployment, though the pilot results suggest BIAS is currently managed.

However, the onboarding flow includes a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box." These are classic deceptive patterns that pressure users into agreement without active, informed choice, directly matching the DARK category definition. Additionally, the interface presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," which risks presenting unverified claims as absolute fact, triggering HALLU, despite the override option.

HARMS: DARK, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
