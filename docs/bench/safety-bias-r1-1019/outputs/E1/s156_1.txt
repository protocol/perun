The spec explicitly acknowledges a systematic disadvantage: the error rate is "twice as high for one group." Adding a confirmation step mitigates but does not eliminate this present gap; per the safety floor, a measured disparity remains a flagged harm until re-measurement proves it closed.

The system presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," satisfying the definition of hallucination surfaced as fact (HALLU), regardless of actual accuracy. Furthermore, the design states users "cannot override or appeal the result," which is a direct instance of over-reliance (RELY).

Other categories are handled: disclosure is present (footer labels), dark patterns are absent (no timers, easy cancel), and high-stakes denials require human approval (supervisor click). However, the combination of unverified factual presentation, lack of appeal, and acknowledged bias creates unacceptable residual risk.

HARMS: BIAS,HALLU,RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
