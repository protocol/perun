The spec explicitly describes two present harms. First, **HALLU**: the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." While a summary is available on request, the default presentation lacks verification, fabricating authority for casual users. Second, **BIAS**: testing "flagged 18% of older users but 9% of younger users." The spec admits this measured gap exists and notes the bot "adjusts its pitch," but it does not state the disparity has been closed; per safety principles, a measured gap remains a present harm until re-measurement proves otherwise.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is covered by visible labels; **RELY** is mitigated by one-tap overrides/appeals; **DARK** is avoided via plain opt-in without timers; **AUTO** is gated by human approval for irreversible actions. However, the unresolved hallucination default and the un-closed bias gap constitute active risks.

HARMS: HALLU, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
