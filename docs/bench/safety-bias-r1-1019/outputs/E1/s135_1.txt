The spec explicitly handles several harm categories: confidence labels and override/appeal mechanisms address over-reliance (RELY); visible AI-generated labels satisfy disclosure (DISC); and subgroup error rate reporting suggests bias measurement (BIAS), though "within one point" lacks specific thresholds. Claims are sourced, mitigating hallucination (HALLU).

However, two critical failures are present. First, the onboarding flow uses a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box, which are classic deceptive patterns pressuring users against their own interest (DARK). Second, and most severely, the system executes irreversible actions (finalizing and sending a locked summary) with no human approval. Given that these summaries influence housing decisions—a high-stakes outcome—fully automating this without a human-in-the-loop gate constitutes unsafe automation (AUTO). The spec states this behavior as a feature ("executes irreversible actions with no human approval"), confirming the harm is present rather than mitigated.

HARMS: DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
