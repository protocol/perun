The spec explicitly describes three present harms. First, while offer tier flagging rates are equal (11%) across age groups, the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," triggering **HALLU** (unverified claims as fact). Second, the design states "users cannot override or appeal the result," which is a clear **RELY** failure (inviting over-trust with no easy override). Third, the engine "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first," executing automated decisions without human oversight; given the potential for financial impact via incorrect offers or exclusion, this constitutes **AUTO** (unsafe automation of actions without a human in the loop).

Conversely, **DISC** is handled because the app "labels every output as AI-generated." **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in consent, unticked defaults, and easy cancellation. **BIAS** is not flagged based *strictly* on the provided text, which reports equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users, showing no described systematic disadvantage in the metric provided.

However, the presence of hallucination presented as fact, the inability to appeal, and the lack of a human gate on automated decisions creates unacceptable risk for launch.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
