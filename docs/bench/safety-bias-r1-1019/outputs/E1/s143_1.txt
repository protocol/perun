The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity in flagging rates between older (18%) and younger (9%) users, confirming **BIAS** is present despite plans to tune it. The app "does not tell users the content was written by AI," directly triggering **DISC**. While the spec describes mitigations for over-reliance (sources, confidence labels, overrides) and high-stakes automation (human sign-off for bindings), the admitted bias gap and missing AI disclosure remain unaddressed in the current description. The bias represents a systematic disadvantage for older users, and the lack of disclosure prevents users from understanding the nature of the advice. Since the spec admits these harms exist without confirming they are resolved before launch, the feature cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
