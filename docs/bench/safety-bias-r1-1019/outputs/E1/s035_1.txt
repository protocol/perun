The spec explicitly describes a present harm under **BIAS**: internal testing revealed a significant disparity where 18% of older users were flagged for duplicate-entry checks compared to only 9% of younger users. The team acknowledges this as a "known behavior" but has not demonstrated a fix or closed the gap before launch; per safety guidelines, a measured gap is a present harm until re-measurement shows it closed.

Other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by one-tap overrides/appeals; **DISC** is addressed by explicit AI labeling; **DARK** patterns are avoided via opt-in consent and no timers; **AUTO** risks are covered by human sign-off on irreversible steps; **HALLU** is not flagged as the spec describes deterministic flagging logic rather than fabricated facts, though the lack of confidence indicators is noted as acceptable given the override mechanism.

Because the bias against older users is measured, admitted, and unresolved, the feature cannot proceed without addressing this inequity.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
