The spec reveals three unaddressed harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the system flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, a disparity the team "accepted" rather than mitigated. Second, **RELY** is triggered because committed routes are "final" with "no appeal path," removing the ability to override potentially erroneous automated decisions. Third, **DISC** applies because the specification states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," leaving dispatchers unaware they are viewing machine-generated recommendations.

While **AUTO** is handled for salt/sand deployment (human sign-off required), the route commitment itself lacks a human-in-the-loop gate before becoming final, compounding the reliance risk. **HALLU** and **DARK** appear addressed via source citations and fair consent flows, respectively. However, the combination of unmitigated bias, missing AI disclosure, and irreversible automation without appeal creates unacceptable risk for a safety-critical municipal service.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
