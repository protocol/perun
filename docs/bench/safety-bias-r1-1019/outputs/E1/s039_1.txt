The spec explicitly flags three harms requiring mitigation before launch. First, **AUTO** is present: the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." While scheduling may seem low-stakes, the lack of any human gate on automated decisions violates the safety floor for automated actions, especially given the vulnerability of the user base. Second, **RELY** is confirmed: "Users cannot override or appeal the result," removing the ability to correct errors or contest unfair allocations. Third, **DARK** patterns are described: consent is "unticked," meaning users must actively opt-in via a pre-unchecked box, which contradicts the spec's own claim that clients join "only if they explicitly choose to" (an unticked box implies passive enrollment or confusion, creating friction against user interest).

Conversely, **DISC** is handled (visible AI labels exist), **HALLU** is mitigated (sources and confidence tiers are shown), and **BIAS** appears addressed during the pilot (error rates were consistent across groups). However, the combination of irreversible automation without review, no appeal mechanism, and deceptive consent mechanics creates unacceptable residual risk.

HARMS: AUTO, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
