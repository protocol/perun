The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms but leaves one critical gap. Bias (BIAS) is acknowledged and mitigated by separating age cohorts after pilot data showed disparate impact. Hallucination (HALLU) is handled via source citations and confidence labels. Disclosure (DISC) is satisfied with visible AI-generated labels. Dark patterns (DARK) are avoided through opt-in consent, no timers, and easy cancellation. High-stakes automation (AUTO) is gated by human sign-off for power tool tasks.

However, the specification states: "users cannot override or appeal the result." This directly creates an Over-reliance (RELY) harm. By removing the ability to correct or challenge the matcher's pairing, the design invites users to trust the output past its reliability, violating the requirement for an easy override or appeal mechanism. Since this feature dictates volunteer assignments based on proximity and skill, the inability to correct errors constitutes a present harm that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
