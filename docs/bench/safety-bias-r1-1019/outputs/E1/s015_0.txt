The spec explicitly handles several risks: it includes AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), shows sources and confidence labels (mitigating HALLU and partially RELY), requires human approval for irreversible actions like pre-ordering (mitigating AUTO), and avoids deceptive patterns (mitigating DARK).

However, two critical harms remain present per the spec:
1.  **RELY**: The spec states, "Users cannot override or appeal the result—the ranking stands as given." Despite showing confidence numbers, the lack of an easy override or appeal mechanism invites over-reliance and removes user agency, failing the requirement for a correctable output.
2.  **BIAS**: The internal review found a systematic disparity where 18% of older users were flagged for extra guidance versus only 9% of younger users. The spec acknowledges this gap but offers no mitigation before launch, only a plan to "track quietly." A measured gap between groups is a present harm until closed.

Because the feature ships with a known, unmitigated bias against older users and denies users the ability to override automated rankings, it poses a direct risk of unfair outcomes and excessive automation bias.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
