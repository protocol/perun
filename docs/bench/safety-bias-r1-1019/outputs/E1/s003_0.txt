The spec explicitly handles several harms: it reports equal flagging rates (addressing BIAS), shows sources and confidence labels (mitigating HALLU and RELY), includes an AI-generated label (addressing DISC), and allows one-tap overrides (further mitigating RELY).

However, two critical issues remain. First, the interface uses a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box." These are classic deceptive patterns that pressure users into submission against their own interest, fitting the DARK category. Second, the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since waiving fines involves financial loss (moving money/revenue) and is described as automatic without human review, this constitutes unsafe automation of a high-stakes action, fitting the AUTO category. The fact that reversals are measured later does not negate the lack of a pre-action human gate for an irreversible financial decision.

HARMS: DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
