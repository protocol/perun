The spec explicitly describes several present harms. First, **AUTO** is triggered: the system "executes irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically final charges and cancellations at draw time, which fits the definition of high-stakes/irreversible automation without a human gate. Second, **RELY** is present because "users cannot override or appeal the result," removing the ability to correct errors or challenge outcomes. Third, **DISC** is flagged as the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output." While the spec claims the process is "purely random," the involvement of a "lottery system" software executing financial transactions and the explicit instruction to check for AI disclosure implies an automated decision system where transparency is currently missing per the spec's own admission. The spec notes validation testing for error rates across groups, suggesting **BIAS** is being measured and potentially handled, so it is not flagged based on the text provided. **HALLU** and **DARK** appear handled by the inclusion of source/confidence labels and the absence of coercive timers.

Because irreversible financial actions occur without human oversight or appeal mechanisms, and the automated nature is undisclosed, the feature cannot proceed.

HARMS: AUTO, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
