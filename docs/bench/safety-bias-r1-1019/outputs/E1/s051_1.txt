The spec explicitly flags three present harms. First, **HALLU**: the interface presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," surfacing unverified claims with unwarranted authority. Second, **DARK**: settlement offers utilize a "ten-minute timer" (manufactured urgency) and "pre-ticked" consent (hidden defaults), pressuring users against their own interests. Third, **BIAS**: while error rates were "within one point across every group," a measured gap between groups constitutes a present harm until re-measurement shows it closed; the spec admits this gap exists rather than confirming it is zero.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed by labeling outputs as AI-generated; **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal; and **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human approval for irreversible actions.

Because HALLU, DARK, and BIAS are described as active features or unresolved metrics in the spec, they must be addressed before launch. Presenting hallucinations as fact and using deceptive patterns are critical failures, and the admitted bias gap requires closure or explicit residual risk reporting rather than shipping.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
