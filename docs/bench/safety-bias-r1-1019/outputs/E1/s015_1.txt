The spec explicitly handles several categories: DISC is addressed via visible AI labels; DARK is mitigated by plain consent without timers; AUTO is controlled by human approval for irreversible actions.

However, three harms are present:
1.  **RELY**: The spec states users "cannot override or appeal the result," creating a hard barrier to correcting errors and inviting over-trust despite confidence scores.
2.  **BIAS**: Internal review shows a disparate impact where older users are flagged for extra guidance at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%). The spec admits this gap exists and plans only to "track quietly" rather than mitigate it before launch.
3.  **HALLU**: While sources are shown, the system presents specific numerical confidence ("92%") which may imply false precision if the underlying model isn't calibrated, but the primary failure is the inability to challenge potentially hallucinated rankings due to the missing override.

Because the design prevents user correction (RELY) and ships with a known, unmitigated disparity affecting a protected group (BIAS), the feature cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
