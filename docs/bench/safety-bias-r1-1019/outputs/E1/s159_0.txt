The spec explicitly confirms handling for several categories: AI disclosure is present (visible label), over-reliance is mitigated (override/appeal available), dark patterns are avoided (plain opt-in, no timers), and high-stakes automation is gated (human approval for irreversible actions). Bias is acknowledged with measured disparity (18% vs 9%), and the spec states the bot "adjusts its pitch," indicating the harm is being managed rather than ignored as a fixed flaw.

However, the spec states: "The bot presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." While a summary is available on request, the default behavior surfaces recommendations without verification or sourcing, inviting users to treat unverified suggestions as absolute truth. This matches the definition of HALLU (unverified claims presented as fact) and contributes to RELY (no uncertainty shown by default). Since the default interface hides uncertainty and sources, the hallucination risk remains present for casual users who do not request the summary.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
