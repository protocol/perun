The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: AI disclosure is present (DISC handled), uncertainty is shown via sources and confidence levels with easy overrides (RELY handled), consent is opt-in without dark patterns (DARK handled), and irreversible actions require human approval (AUTO handled).

However, the spec reveals a potential **BIAS** issue. It states the model flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users for follow-up. While the rates are identical, the spec admits this disparity is something they "will monitor in the pilot" rather than a resolved metric with a defined pass threshold or stop condition. Per the safety framework, a measured gap or unverified equity status constitutes a present harm until re-measurement confirms it is closed; a plan to monitor is not a mitigation. Additionally, while confidence levels are stated, the spec does not confirm they are rendered as defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Corroborated") rather than raw percentages, which could invite over-reliance if not properly calibrated, though the explicit override mitigates this significantly. The primary blocking factor is the unverified nature of the bias monitoring plan without a pre-launch validation gate.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
