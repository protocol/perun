The spec reveals two present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **DISC** (Missing AI-disclosure) is explicitly confirmed: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Users cannot distinguish machine-generated assessments from human evaluation, violating transparency requirements for automated decisions affecting student placement.

Second, **BIAS** (Unfair outcomes) is present. The spec notes validation runs "flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users." While the rates appear numerically identical, the text implies the system actively "flagged" specific age groups at a notable rate without confirming this represents a fair baseline or that the gap is closed. Without explicit confirmation that these flag rates are expected and equitable across all demographics, or that the "constant scoring weights" actually resolve the disparity rather than just freezing a potentially biased state, this remains a measured gap requiring verification before shipping.

Other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by one-tap overrides and logged teacher choices; **AUTO** is addressed by human approval for irreversible actions; **DARK** patterns are absent (opt-in consent, no timers); **HALLU** is mitigated by source citations and confidence labels.

HARMS: DISC, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
