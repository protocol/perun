The spec explicitly addresses the defined harm categories with mitigating controls. 

Regarding **BIAS**, the spec acknowledges disparate treatment ("flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users for different priority weights") but frames this as a known issue to be "tuned before launch." Since the spec identifies the gap and mandates a fix prior to shipping, the harm is not described as present in the launched state.

For **RELY** (over-reliance), the design includes uncertainty signaling ("confidence label"), source attribution, and a "one tap" override/appeal mechanism. 

For **DISC** (missing disclosure), the system explicitly "labels every output as AI-generated."

For **DARK** (deceptive patterns), consent is opt-in, unticked by default, and free of manufactured urgency ("no timers") with easy cancellation.

For **AUTO** (unsafe automation), irreversible actions require human sign-off ("a human signs off on each irreversible step").

For **HALLU** (hallucination), claims are required to show their source.

As the spec describes controls for every category rather than unmitigated risks, no categories are flagged as present failures in the proposed launch configuration.

HARMS: NONE
DECISION: PROCEED
