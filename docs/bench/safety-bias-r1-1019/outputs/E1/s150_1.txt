The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: it includes an AI-generated label (addressing DISC), allows one-tap override/appeal (addressing RELY), avoids dark patterns with clear consent and no timers (addressing DARK), and cites sources for confidence (addressing HALLU). Bias mitigation is claimed via internal testing showing low error rates across groups.

However, the spec confirms a critical failure in the AUTO category. It states: "the tool runs on the coordinator's device... so it acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." While the output can be overridden *after* generation, the automated action of creating and presumably finalizing/sending the team rosters happens immediately without a human review gate before execution. For a feature determining team composition—a decision impacting child participation and fairness—executing the split without prior human confirmation constitutes unsafe automation of a high-stakes action, especially since the "send" action described ("hits send") appears coupled with the immediate model decision in the local execution flow. The ability to appeal *after* the fact does not satisfy the requirement for a human-in-the-loop gate *before* irreversible or high-impact automated actions occur.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
