The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparate error rate for out-of-state plates. While a secondary step is added, the underlying gap remains present, triggering **BIAS**. The system presents confidence labels but declares results "final" with "no appeal path," preventing users from correcting errors despite the known reliability gap, which constitutes **RELY**. Most critically, the tool "executes irreversible actions" (voiding charges) with "no human approval." This is a direct violation of the high-stakes automation floor, flagging **AUTO**.

Although the spec addresses **DISC** (AI labeling) and avoids **DARK** patterns (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of known bias, locked outcomes, and autonomous financial execution creates unacceptable risk. The secondary verification for the biased group does not negate the harm if the error rate remains higher, nor does it justify removing the appeal path. The autonomous voiding of charges without human oversight is a blocking failure regardless of other mitigations.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
