The spec describes two present harms requiring remediation. First, **DARK**: the interface employs deceptive patterns by presenting a "countdown timer" to manufacture urgency and a "pre-ticked consent box" that hides defaults, pressuring users against their own interest during data processing. Second, **AUTO**: while the spec claims a human approves "irreversible actions" before they run, it simultaneously states the tool "returns a 1–100 score" used by boards. If the score itself influences eligibility or prioritization before the human gate acts on the final letter/placement, the automated scoring constitutes a high-stakes decision lacking a confirmed human-in-the-loop at the point of impact. The description of the gate is ambiguous regarding the score's immediate effect versus the final letter.

Other categories appear handled: **BIAS** is addressed via tested error rates; **HALLU** and **RELY** are mitigated by source citations, confidence labels, and appeal mechanisms; **DISC** is covered by visible AI labels. However, the deceptive patterns and the potential gap in the automation gate for the scoring output itself block launch.

HARMS: DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
