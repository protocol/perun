The spec explicitly reveals several unmitigated harms. First, **DARK** patterns are present: a countdown timer manufactures urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box hides defaults, pressuring users against their own interest. Second, **AUTO** is critical: the system executes irreversible actions (finalizing lease matches) without human approval, violating the safety floor for high-stakes decisions. Third, **BIAS** is admitted: the spec notes a measured gap where older users are flagged for conflicts at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), yet the team only plans to "monitor" this rather than fix it before launch.

While the spec correctly handles uncertainty (showing sources/confidence), overrides (**RELY** mitigated), and labeling (**DISC** mitigated), the presence of active dark patterns, un gated irreversible automation, and an acknowledged but unaddressed bias gap creates immediate risk.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
