The spec explicitly acknowledges a systemic disparity: the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, and the team states they "accept for now." This is a confirmed present harm under **BIAS**.

Regarding **HALLU**, the interface presents answers as "confirmed fact with no source shown," creating a risk of unverified claims being treated as truth. However, the spec notes results can be overridden in one tap, which mitigates **RELY** (over-reliance) by providing an easy escape hatch.

The spec confirms **DISC** is handled (outputs labeled AI-generated), **DARK** patterns are avoided (opt-in, no timers), and **AUTO** risks are managed (no irreversible actions without human approval).

Despite the mitigation for reliance and automation, shipping with a known, unaddressed bias gap violates the safety floor. A measured gap between groups is a present harm until closed; accepting it for launch is insufficient. Additionally, presenting unsourced outputs as "confirmed fact" invites hallucination risks that the current override mechanism may not fully neutralize if operators trust the "confirmed" label implicitly.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
