The spec explicitly describes several present harms. First, **BIAS** is evident: the algorithm flags 18% of older users for review compared to only 9% of younger users, indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age. Second, **RELY** is a critical failure; the spec states the list is "final" and users "cannot override or appeal," removing necessary human recourse for an automated decision. Third, **DISC** is confirmed as the product "does not tell users the content was written by AI."

While the spec notes that high-impact actions have human approval (mitigating **AUTO**) and data collection avoids deceptive patterns (mitigating **DARK**), the combination of unexplained demographic disparity, lack of appeal mechanisms, and missing AI disclosure creates unacceptable risk. The confidence labels mentioned do not resolve the inability to override the decision or the undisclosed nature of the generation. These issues must be addressed before launch to prevent user harm and potential regulatory violations.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
