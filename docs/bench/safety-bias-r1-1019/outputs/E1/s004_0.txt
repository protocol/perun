The spec reveals several critical output-safety failures. First, **DISC** is present: the text explicitly states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure. Second, **DARK** patterns are hardcoded: the interface uses a "ten-minute timer" creating manufactured urgency and "pre-ticked" consent, nudging users against their own interest. Third, **RELY** is a confirmed harm because "users cannot override or appeal the result," removing the ability to correct errors despite the system claiming confidence levels. Finally, while **AUTO** (irreversible actions) has a human gate ("a person approves"), the combination of pre-ticked consent, urgency, and no appeal mechanism undermines the efficacy of that gate.

Although the spec claims age parity in testing (addressing **BIAS**) and cites sources for answers (mitigating **HALLU**), the presence of undisclosed AI generation, deceptive interface patterns, and the inability to appeal automated decisions constitutes unacceptable residual risk for a feature affecting user accounts. These are not unverified risks; the spec admits them as design choices.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
