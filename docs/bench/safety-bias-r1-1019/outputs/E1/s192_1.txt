The spec explicitly states the tool "does not tell users the content was written by AI," which directly triggers the **DISC** (missing AI-disclosure) harm category. While the spec notes a measured disparity in setup completion times between older and younger users (18% vs 9%), it describes this as a usability friction that was "simplified," not as a systematic disadvantage in the *output* or *decision* itself; thus, **BIAS** is not flagged based strictly on the provided text regarding decision outcomes.

The spec confirms human gates for fund transfers (**AUTO** handled), easy overrides (**RELY** handled), and non-deceptive consent flows (**DARK** handled). Sources and confidence levels are cited, mitigating **HALLU** risks in the description. However, the absence of disclosure for machine-generated content remains an unaddressed harm in the current specification.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
