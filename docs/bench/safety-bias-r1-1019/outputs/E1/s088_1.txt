The spec explicitly presents route answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," creating a high risk of users treating unverified optimizations as absolute facts (HALLU). Furthermore, the product fails to disclose that a model generates these outputs, presenting itself merely as a "utility" rather than an AI assistant (DISC). While the spec notes a human gate for irreversible accessible pickup steps, it does not describe human oversight for standard routing decisions that could strand users if hallucinated, nor does it address the pre-ticked consent and expiring timers for accessible requests, which pressure vulnerable users against their own interest (DARK). Although an override exists (mitigating RELY) and demographic testing occurred (addressing BIAS), the combination of undisclosed AI generation, lack of uncertainty indicators, and deceptive patterns for accessibility features constitutes unresolved safety risks.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
