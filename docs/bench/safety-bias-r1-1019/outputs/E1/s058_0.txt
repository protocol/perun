The spec demonstrates handling for most harm categories: bias is addressed via measured error rates across groups; hallucination is mitigated by cited sources; over-reliance is countered with visible AI labels, confidence levels, and one-tap overrides; and high-stakes automation (ICU booking) requires human sign-off.

However, the spec explicitly describes a **DARK** pattern: "consent is pre-ticked" for prescription refill offers. Pre-ticked boxes are a deceptive pattern that pressures users into agreeing against their own interest or without active consent. Additionally, the "ten-minute timer" creates manufactured urgency, further compounding this issue. Since the spec admits this design exists rather than describing a fix, this harm is present.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
