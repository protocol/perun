The spec explicitly describes three present harms. First, **RELY**: the system treats proposals as final because error rates were similar across groups, and users "cannot override or appeal the result." Second, **DISC**: the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," despite the system generating ranked lists and answers. Third, **BIAS**: the justification for removing overrides relies on aggregate error rates being "within one point across every group," which fails to prove the absence of systematic disadvantage or unequal impact on specific subgroups; treating this as "final" without deeper subgroup analysis or an appeal mechanism codifies potential bias.

Other categories are handled: **AUTO** is mitigated by human approval for irreversible actions (deposits/closing lists). **DARK** is avoided via opt-in consent and easy cancellation. **HALLU** is partially addressed by cited sources, though the lack of AI disclosure remains a transparency failure regarding the generation method.

Because the feature removes user agency (no overrides) based on insufficient bias evidence and hides the AI nature of the decisions, it cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
