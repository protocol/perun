The spec explicitly states the system executes "irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically charges and cancellations that are "final at draw time." Because financial transactions and reservation denials are high-stakes outcomes for users, the lack of a human-in-the-loop or an appeal mechanism constitutes a critical **AUTO** failure. Additionally, the spec notes that "nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Even if the logic is purely random, the use of an automated system to make binding decisions without disclosure triggers **DISC**. While the spec claims validation testing showed equal error rates (addressing **BIAS**) and sources are shown (addressing **HALLU**), the irreversible automation without oversight and the missing disclosure are present harms described in the text. The inability to override or appeal further compounds the automation risk.

HARMS: AUTO, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
