The spec explicitly surfaces several output-safety failures. First, the planner presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," creating a high risk of **HALLU** (hallucination surfaced as fact) and **RELY** (over-reliance), as users cannot gauge uncertainty despite the one-tap override. Second, the product fails to disclose its AI nature ("Nothing in the product says a model produced the output"), triggering **DISC**. Third, the accessible pickup flow employs **DARK** patterns: a pressing ten-minute timer combined with pre-ticked consent nudges users against their own interest.

While **AUTO** is mitigated for irreversible steps (human sign-off required) and **BIAS** appears addressed via the list-view fallback for age groups, the combination of undisclosed AI generation, lack of confidence signaling, and deceptive accessible-design patterns constitutes unacceptable residual risk for launch. The "clean interface" justification does not excuse missing safety guardrails or dark patterns.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
