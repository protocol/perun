The spec reveals multiple unaddressed harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the algorithm explicitly flags different groups for older versus younger users at a measurable gap (11%), indicating systematic disadvantage until proven otherwise. Second, **DARK** patterns are built into the onboarding: a countdown timer creates manufactured urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box hides defaults, pressuring users against their own interest regarding data sharing. Third, **DISC** is missing: the UI states "Recommended group" without disclosing that a machine-generated model produced the output, leaving users unaware of the automated nature of the decision. Finally, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the system locks final rosters 72 hours before the race—a high-stakes, hard-to-reverse action affecting race participation—without any human review or confirmation step, executing fully autonomously.

While the spec mentions cited sources and an override mechanism (mitigating RELY and partially addressing HALLU), the combination of undisclosed automation, biased outcomes, deceptive onboarding, and irreversible auto-execution creates unacceptable risk for a pilot launch involving real participants.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
