The spec reveals three critical harms requiring immediate remediation. First, **BIAS** is present: the system flags 11% of both older and younger users for review, indicating a potential disparate impact or systematic disadvantage based on age that requires investigation before launch. Second, **RELY** is a confirmed failure; the spec explicitly states users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing human agency and inviting dangerous over-reliance on automated decisions. Third, **DARK** patterns are hardcoded into the workflow: requiring users to click through a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box" constitutes a deceptive interface designed to pressure users against their own interest.

While the spec correctly handles **DISC** (via visible AI labels) and **AUTO** (by requiring human approval for irreversible actions), the combination of unappealable decisions, deceptive consent mechanics, and observed demographic flagging creates unacceptable risk. The inability to override assignments is particularly dangerous in a physical safety context like school transportation.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
