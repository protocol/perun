The spec explicitly describes three present harms. First, **BIAS** is evident: the algorithm flags 18% of older users for review compared to only 9% of younger users, indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age. Second, **RELY** is triggered because the list is declared "final," meaning users cannot override or appeal the ranking, inviting over-trust without recourse. Third, **DISC** is present as the product intentionally omits telling users that the content was generated by AI.

Other categories are handled: **AUTO** is mitigated by human approval for irreversible actions; **DARK** is avoided via plain opt-in consent without pressure; **HALLU** is addressed by showing sources and confidence labels. However, the presence of Bias, Over-reliance, and Missing Disclosure constitutes significant risk that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
