The spec explicitly describes several present harms. First, **HALLU** is present: the bot "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified claims as fact. Second, **RELY** is triggered because the design invites over-trust by hiding uncertainty and offering "no appeal path" once a pick is posted. Third, **DISC** is confirmed: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Fourth, **AUTO** is flagged; while queue changes require approval, the primary function (posting the final weekly pick) is described as "final" with no human-in-the-loop gate before execution, constituting an irreversible social commitment automated without review.

The spec indicates **BIAS** is handled (tested across groups with low error variance) and **DARK** is handled (opt-in nudges, no timers). However, the combination of undisclosed AI generation, presented-as-fact hallucinations, and irreversible automated decisions without human oversight creates significant risk.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
