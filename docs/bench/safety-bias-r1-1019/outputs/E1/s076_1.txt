The spec explicitly acknowledges a present **BIAS** (error rate twice as high for left-handed applicants) and states it will only be "logged," which does not mitigate the harm before launch. It also describes **AUTO**: the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." While the pilot is small, assigning physical resources via irreversible automated action without a human gate violates the safety floor for high-stakes decisions, especially when known bias exists.

Although the spec handles **DISC** (visible AI label), **RELY** (sources and confidence labels shown), and **DARK** (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of unmitigated bias and fully automated execution without appeal creates unacceptable risk. The statement "the result is final and there is no appeal path" further cements the lack of recourse for the disadvantaged group.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
