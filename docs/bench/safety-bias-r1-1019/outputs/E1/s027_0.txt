The spec explicitly handles several harms: sources and confidence labels address HALLU; override/appeal options address RELY; AI-generated labeling addresses DISC; and human sign-off on irreversible steps addresses AUTO. No evidence of BIAS is presented as an unhandled feature; the known error rate for flat roofs is acknowledged with a planned fix before full rollout.

However, the spec describes two active DARK patterns. First, a "ten-minute timer" creating manufactured urgency pressures users to decide quickly, potentially against their best interest. Second, "pre-ticked" consent for follow-up emails is a hidden default that nudges users into agreement they did not explicitly grant. These deceptive patterns are present in the described design and must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
