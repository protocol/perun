The spec reveals three present harms requiring remediation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: an internal audit shows a disparate impact where 18% of older users are flagged for no-shows versus only 9% of younger users. The system enables studios to enforce strike thresholds based on this uncorrected gap, systematically disadvantaging older members.

Second, **DISC** is violated. The spec states that when a user hits the strike threshold, "nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Users are misled into believing the decision stems solely from standard studio policy rather than automated analysis.

Third, **RELY** (over-reliance) is confirmed. The spec notes that users "cannot override or appeal the result" of the automated flagging. This removes human recourse for high-stakes decisions (potential banning or penalty), inviting blind trust in the system's accuracy without a safety valve.

While the spec correctly gates irreversible cancellation fees behind human approval (mitigating AUTO) and provides citations for general answers (mitigating HALLU), the combination of unaddressed bias, missing AI disclosure for penalties, and the lack of an appeal mechanism creates unacceptable risk for the affected users.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
