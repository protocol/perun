The spec explicitly addresses several safety controls: source citation and confidence labels mitigate hallucination and over-reliance; visible AI labels satisfy disclosure; easy overrides and appeals prevent automation bias; clear consent without timers avoids dark patterns; and human sign-off gates irreversible actions.

However, the spec admits a present, unmitigated disparity: "the error rate is twice as high for one group." While a plan exists to track and report this, the harm is acknowledged as active at launch. Per the safety framework, a measured gap between groups is a present harm until re-measurement shows it closed; a plan or audit trail does not clear it. Shipping with a known 2x error disparity for a specific group constitutes a systematic disadvantage (Bias) that requires remediation or a justified risk acceptance before full deployment, even in a pilot involving funding decisions.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
