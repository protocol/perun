Based on the provided spec, the following harm category is flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec explicitly states the interface "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Presenting ranked likelihoods as "confirmed fact" without evidentiary support constitutes surfacing unverified claims as fact, regardless of the testing metrics cited.

Other categories are addressed in the spec: **BIAS** is noted as tested with minimal variance; **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override/appeal; **DISC** is handled by labeling outputs as AI-generated; **DARK** patterns are avoided via opt-in defaults and lack of pressure timers; **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human sign-off for irreversible actions.

However, the presentation of probabilistic rankings as absolute facts creates a direct risk of misinformation and over-reliance that contradicts the stated override mechanisms. This specific failure mode must be corrected before launch to ensure users understand the tentative nature of the rankings.

HARMS: HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
