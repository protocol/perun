The spec presents unverified outputs as confirmed facts without showing sources, creating a risk of users treating hallucinations as truth (HALLU). Additionally, the interface employs a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box, which are deceptive patterns that pressure users against their own interests (DARK).

While the spec notes error rates were similar across groups during a small pilot, this does not definitively prove the absence of systematic disadvantage, but per your instructions to flag only harms the spec *describes as present*, BIAS is not explicitly admitted as a current failure mode in the text provided. Over-reliance (RELY) is mitigated by the explicit "one tap" override. Disclosure (DISC) is handled by labeling outputs as AI-generated. Unsafe automation (AUTO) is prevented by the human sign-off requirement for irreversible steps.

However, the presence of presenting unverified claims as fact and the use of dark patterns constitute active harms described in the specification that require remediation before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
