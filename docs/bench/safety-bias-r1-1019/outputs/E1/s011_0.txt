The spec explicitly handles several categories: **DISC** is addressed via visible "AI-generated" labels; **RELY** is mitigated by allowing one-tap overrides and appeals; **DARK** patterns are avoided through opt-in consent, no timers, and easy cancellation; and **BIAS** is claimed to be managed based on pilot data showing consistent error rates across groups.

However, the spec confirms a critical failure in **AUTO**. It states the tool "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first" for municipal permit eligibility. Determining building and zoning compliance is a high-stakes decision with potential legal and safety consequences. Executing these decisions without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for irreversible or high-impact actions, regardless of pilot error rates. Additionally, the system presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," which creates a **HALLU** risk by presenting unverified claims with unwarranted authority, even if an override exists.

HARMS: AUTO, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
