The spec reveals three present harms. First, **HALLU**: the tool states reasons for low scores "flatly" with "no source or confidence," presenting unverified claims as authoritative facts. Second, **DISC**: the spec explicitly notes "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating transparency requirements for machine-generated decisions. Third, **AUTO**: the system executes irreversible actions like "purging an entire contest's data" with "no human approval," creating a critical safety gap for high-stakes operations.

While the spec mentions an override for scoring (mitigating **RELY**) and balanced review flags for age groups (suggesting **BIAS** is currently monitored rather than present), the lack of disclosure, the authoritative presentation of unverified reasons, and the autonomous irreversible deletion are explicit failures described in the text. The deletion capability specifically violates the floor for high-stakes automation.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
