The spec explicitly admits a systematic disadvantage: error rates are twice as high for customers with >20 boxes or unusual items. This meets the definition of **BIAS**. While the spec notes a visible AI label (handling DISC) and non-coercive consent (handling DARK), it confirms the estimate engine acts immediately without human review. Although capped as a pilot, an automated financial quote that binds the company or misleads users on cost constitutes a high-stakes decision running without a human in the loop, fitting **AUTO**. The inability to override or appeal the result also flags **RELY**. The spec acknowledges the bias but ships the feature without describing a fix or a human gate for the high-error group, meaning these harms are present and unmitigated at launch.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
