The spec explicitly identifies two present harms. First, **DARK**: the signup flow uses a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box for location tracking, constituting deceptive patterns that pressure users against their interests. Second, **HALLU**: the post-session summary presents answers as "confirmed fact with no source shown," surfacing unverified claims with unjustified authority.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed via visible AI labels; **RELY** is mitigated by override/appeal options; **AUTO** is prevented by human approval for irreversible actions. While **BIAS** is mentioned, the claim that error rates were "within one point" is an internal assertion without subgroup detail; however, the spec does not describe a *present* systematic disadvantage, so BIAS is not flagged based strictly on the text provided. The identified DARK and HALLU issues are explicit design choices admitted in the spec that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: DARK, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
