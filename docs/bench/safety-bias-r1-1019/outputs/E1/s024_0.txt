The spec reveals several unaddressed harms. First, a known bias exists where error rates are double for power tool borrowers; while tuning is planned, the harm is currently present and unmitigated at launch (BIAS). Second, the system prevents users from overriding or appealing AI predictions, creating a high risk of automation bias without recourse (RELY). Third, the interface employs dark patterns: a countdown timer manufactures urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box hides default agreement to loan terms (DARK).

Conversely, the spec indicates that AI disclosures are present for suggestions, confidence labels are shown, and irreversible actions like flagging items lost require human approval. Therefore, DISC and AUTO are handled. However, the combination of confirmed bias, lack of override mechanisms, and deceptive interface elements creates significant user risk.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
