The spec reveals three present harms requiring remediation before launch.

First, **DISC** is explicitly admitted: "NoisePing does not tell users the content was written by AI." This violates the requirement for machine-generated disclosure where it matters to the user's understanding of the triage process.

Second, **DARK** patterns are described in the premium features: an expiring "ten-minute timer" manufactures urgency, and "consent is pre-ticked," hiding defaults and pressuring users against their own interest.

Third, **RELY** is triggered by the presentation of a "stated confidence level." Unless this is rendered as a defined coarse tier (e.g., "Corroborated" vs. "Single-source") with explicit definitions, presenting raw confidence invites over-trust and false precision, especially without knowing if the underlying calibration supports the number.

While **AUTO** is handled (human approval exists for irreversible actions) and **BIAS** claims parity in the pilot, the active presence of undisclosed AI, deceptive interface patterns, and potential false-precision confidence constitutes unacceptable residual risk.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
