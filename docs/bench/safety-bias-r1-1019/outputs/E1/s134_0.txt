The spec demonstrates handling of several harm categories: BIAS is addressed via consistent flagging rates across age groups; HALLU and RELY are mitigated by cited sources, stated confidence, and easy override/appeal mechanisms; DISC is covered by explicit AI labeling; and AUTO is controlled by human sign-off for irreversible actions.

However, the spec explicitly describes a DARK pattern: a "countdown timer" manufactures urgency, and a "pre-ticked consent box" hides defaults, pressuring users against their own interest regarding data ownership confirmation. Additionally, while confidence levels are stated, the spec does not define them as coarse, defined tiers (e.g., "Verified" vs. "Unverified") nor separate source reliability from claim corroboration, risking over-trust if raw model numbers are displayed, though the primary blockable finding is the deceptive interface.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
