The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: BIAS is measured with equal flagging rates (11%) across age groups; HALLU and RELY are mitigated by showing sources, confidence labels, and allowing one-tap overrides; DARK patterns are avoided via unticked consent and no timers; AUTO is handled by requiring human approval for irreversible actions.

However, the spec states: "nothing in the product says a model produced the output—the interface is just a roster." Since the system generates flags and confidence labels using a model (implied by "confidence label" and "flagged"), failing to disclose this machine-generated nature to the staff users constitutes a missing AI disclosure. This matches the DISC category definition where users cannot tell an output was machine-generated.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
