The spec describes several present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

**DARK**: The interface employs deceptive patterns by presenting a countdown timer (manufactured urgency) and a pre-ticked consent box (hidden default), pressuring users against their own interest during intake.

**BIAS**: Early logs explicitly show the error rate is twice as high for newer staff. While a "show example" button is planned, the spec confirms a measured gap exists, constituting a present harm until re-measurement proves it closed.

**HALLU**: Although sources are shown, the system pulls from a public symptom library and visit history to generate triage classes. Without explicit mention of verification logic against medical guidelines, there is a risk of unverified claims being presented as fact, though the source labeling mitigates this slightly. However, the primary failures are DARK and BIAS.

The spec correctly handles **AUTO** (human approval for irreversible actions), **RELY** (overrides available, confidence labels shown), and **DISC** (visible AI-generated label). However, the presence of deceptive patterns and confirmed bias prevents a safe launch.

HARMS: DARK, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
