The spec describes a feature that largely addresses common output-safety concerns through transparency, citations, confidence tiers, easy overrides, and explicit AI disclosure. The interface avoids dark patterns with opt-in consent and no manufactured urgency.

However, the spec explicitly states the bot "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" immediately after a user tap. While the user initiates the claim, filing a legal/financial claim is a high-stakes action that can have binding consequences or trigger complex disputes if erroneous. Relying solely on a single tap without a final confirmation step reviewing the specific claim details (an explicit human-in-the-loop gate for the *execution* phase) constitutes an unsafe automation of a high-stakes action under the `AUTO` category. The pilot data regarding age groups shows equal flagging rates (11%), so `BIAS` is not flagged based on the provided text. All other categories (`HALLU`, `RELY`, `DISC`, `DARK`) appear mitigated by the described controls.

Because the spec confirms irreversible actions occur without a final human approval gate beyond the initial trigger, this violates the safety floor for high-stakes automation.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
