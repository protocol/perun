The spec explicitly describes three present harms. First, **RELY**: the system treats the ranking as final, and users "cannot override or appeal the result," creating unchecked automation bias. Second, **DISC**: the spec admits "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," failing to disclose AI generation where it drives decisions. Third, **DARK**: consent for notifications is "opt-in and unticked" (a pre-selected default), which pressures users against their interest by manufacturing enrollment unless they actively deselect it.

While **AUTO** is handled (a person approves irreversible actions) and **HALLU** appears mitigated (citations provided), the combination of undisclosed AI, forced acceptance of rankings, and deceptive consent defaults creates significant user risk. The claim that error rates were "within one point" does not justify removing override mechanisms or disclosure.

HARMS: RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
