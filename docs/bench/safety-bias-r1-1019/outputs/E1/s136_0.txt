The spec explicitly describes several present harms. First, **BIAS** is flagged: the system "flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users," indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age groups that remains unaddressed in the launch plan. Second, **HALLU** is present: the tool "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," risking fabricated claims being treated as truth. Third, **DISC** is a failure: the interface "does not tell users the content was written by AI." Fourth, **DARK** patterns are active: the design uses a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box," pressuring users against their own interest.

While **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override and appeal, and **AUTO** is handled by the human sign-off for irreversible steps, the four identified harms constitute significant risks to users and fairness. The bias finding alone, described as a present gap without a closure plan, combined with deceptive interface patterns and missing disclosure, prevents a safe launch.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
