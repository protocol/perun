The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: claims include sources and confidence labels (mitigating HALLU and RELY), outputs carry AI-generated labels (mitigating DISC), consent is opt-in with easy cancellation (mitigating DARK), and results are overrideable (mitigating RELY).

However, the spec reports a measurable disparity in the suggestion engine: it flagged 18% of older users for follow-up regarding tool condition compared to only 9% of younger users. This systematic disadvantage based on age constitutes a present BIAS harm. The spec acknowledges this gap but offers no evidence it has been closed or mitigated before launch.

Additionally, the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." While releasing tools may be reversible, the combination of automated decision-making with a known, unaddressed bias gap creates a risk profile that requires resolution before shipping. The spec treats the bias metric as a finding rather than a blocked risk.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
