The spec explicitly acknowledges a systematic disadvantage for homes with complex rooflines (error rate twice as high). While flagged as "preliminary," the disparity exists in the output, satisfying the **BIAS** condition.

The spec states "Users cannot override or appeal the result." This lack of an easy override or appeal mechanism, combined with the presentation of savings and payment estimates, invites over-trust and prevents correction, satisfying **RELY**.

The interface employs a "ten-minute timer" for offers and "pre-ticked" consent. These are classic dark patterns that manufacture urgency and hide defaults, pressuring users against their own interest, satisfying **DARK**.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources; **DISC** is covered by the visible AI label; **AUTO** is prevented by the human approval requirement for irreversible actions.

However, the combination of known biased outcomes, the inability to appeal, and deceptive interface patterns presents significant user harm that must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
