The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), provides sources and an override mechanism (mitigating RELY and HALLU), uses clear consent without pressure (mitigating DARK), and requires human approval for irreversible actions like charges (mitigating AUTO). The spec also claims pre-pilot testing showed error rates "within one point across every group tested," which indicates the team has considered BIAS and performed measurement.

However, a critical failure exists in the confidence presentation. The spec states the bot displays "90% confident." Product output safety standards dictate that raw model confidence percentages must not be shown to users as they imply uncalibrated precision and invite over-trust. Confidence must be rendered as a defined coarse tier (e.g., "High," "Medium," "Low") with explicit definitions, not a specific number. This violates the safety floor regarding over-reliance and false precision, regardless of the other controls in place. Because the feature ships with this specific unsafe output format, it cannot proceed until the confidence display is changed to a defined tier.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
