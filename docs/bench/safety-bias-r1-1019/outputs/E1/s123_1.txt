The spec explicitly flags three present harms. First, **DISC**: the tool "does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating the requirement for machine-generated disclosure. Second, **DARK**: the interface employs a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box" to pressure users, constituting a deceptive pattern against user interest. Third, **RELY**: while overrides exist, the combination of hidden AI origin and dark patterns invites over-trust in the system's authority.

Other categories are addressed in the spec: **BIAS** is claimed handled via tested error rates; **HALLU** is mitigated by source citations and confidence labels; **AUTO** is gated by human approval for irreversible actions. However, the presence of undisclosed AI generation, deceptive interface elements, and resulting over-reliance risks misleading families and staff before launch. These issues require immediate remediation to align with safety floors regarding transparency and user autonomy.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
