The spec reveals three critical output-safety failures. First, **DISC**: the product "reads like a standard triage worksheet" with no indication that a model generated the ranking or reasons, violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to the user. Second, **RELY**: while sources and confidence levels are shown, the spec explicitly states "the result is final and there is no appeal path," removing the easy override necessary to prevent automation bias in high-stakes triage. Third, **BIAS**: although a measurement plan exists for the pilot, the harm is currently active and unverified; the spec admits the error rate *could* be twice as high for one group, which constitutes a present systematic disadvantage until proven otherwise by the running evals.

While **AUTO** is mitigated by the human sign-off on irreversible steps, and **DARK** patterns appear absent due to fair consent design, the lack of transparency regarding the AI nature of the tool and the inability to appeal a final medical prioritization decision create unacceptable risk before launch. The measurement plan for bias is a good start but does not negate the current unverified status of the disparity.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
