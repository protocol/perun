The spec reveals two present harms requiring attention before launch.

First, **BIAS** is indicated. While the pilot showed equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users, the spec explicitly states the tool "does not highlight that split anywhere in the interface." Transparency about demographic performance is a core component of managing bias; hiding this data prevents officers from contextualizing potential disparities, even if the current aggregate numbers appear equal. The harm here is the lack of visibility into fairness metrics for the operators.

Second, **DISC** is present. The spec notes that "nothing in the product says a model produced the output—it reads like a checklist." Since this is an AI-driven screener making recommendations that affect livelihoods (permit eligibility), users (the permitting staff) must know they are interacting with an automated decision support system to calibrate their trust appropriately.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by source citations; **RELY** is addressed via confidence labels and one-tap overrides; **DARK** patterns are absent due to clear consent; **AUTO** is prevented by the human sign-off requirement for denials.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
