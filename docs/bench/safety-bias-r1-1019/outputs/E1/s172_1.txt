The spec explicitly reveals three present harms. First, **BIAS** is admitted: the system flags older users at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), and the team has explicitly "accepted" this gap rather than mitigating it. Second, **AUTO** is present: the feature executes seat assignments—a high-stakes decision affecting access—automatically ("nobody reviews it first") with no human-in-the-loop gate. Third, **RELY** is triggered because the result is "final" with "no appeal path," denying users an override or correction mechanism despite the known error disparity.

While the spec handles **DISC** (outputs are labeled AI-generated) and avoids **DARK** patterns (consent is opt-in with easy cancellation), the combination of unmitigated bias, fully automated high-stakes actions, and the inability to appeal creates unacceptable risk. The floor for product safety requires a human gate on irreversible/high-stakes actions and a path to correct errors, neither of which exists here.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
