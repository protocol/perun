The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: it shows equal flagging rates for older and younger users (mitigating BIAS), displays sources and confidence labels with easy overrides (mitigating HALLU and RELY), uses clear consent without pressure (mitigating DARK), and requires human approval for irreversible actions like fee write-offs (mitigating AUTO).

However, the spec states: "The draft text does not tell users the content was written by AI." Since this feature generates waiver requests for patrons who may believe they are reading or writing standard forms, the absence of disclosure constitutes a present harm under the DISC category. The spec treats this as a design choice rather than a handled risk.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
