The spec reveals two present harms requiring attention before launch. First, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted: internal data shows a significant disparity where 18% of older users were flagged compared to only 9% of younger users. The spec states this will be "tracked but not act on yet," meaning the harm remains unmitigated during the pilot. Second, **HALLU** is present because the tool "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified scoring as absolute fact without indicating uncertainty or evidentiary basis.

Other categories are adequately handled: **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal; **DISC** is covered by explicit AI labeling; **DARK** is avoided via clear consent and no timers; **AUTO** is prevented by the human approval gate for irreversible actions.

However, shipping with a known, unaddressed demographic disparity and uncalibrated confidence presentation violates the safety floor. The bias gap cannot be ignored as merely "tracked," and flat assertions without confidence tiers invite over-reliance despite the override button.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
