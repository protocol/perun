The spec reveals two unaddressed output harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the internal audit explicitly shows a systematic disparity where older users are flagged for extra review at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%) for identical edge-case values. The spec notes this gap but offers no mitigation, leaving the unequal treatment active at launch.

Second, **RELY** (Over-reliance) is triggered by the explicit constraint that "Users cannot override or appeal the result—the summary stands as generated." Despite providing confidence levels, the design removes human agency to correct or challenge the AI's interpretation, inviting blind trust in potentially flawed medical guidance.

Other categories appear handled: **DISC** is satisfied by visible AI labeling; **DARK** patterns are avoided via opt-in consent without pressure; **AUTO** is mitigated by human sign-off for irreversible record modifications; and **HALLU** is partially addressed by cited sources, though the inability to override remains a risk if citations are hallucinated. However, the confirmed bias and lack of override capability constitute critical safety failures for a clinical tool.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
