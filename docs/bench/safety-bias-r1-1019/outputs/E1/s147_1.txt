Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are present:

**HALLU**: The spec explicitly states the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Despite other controls, presenting unverified outputs as absolute fact constitutes this harm.

**BIAS**: The spec admits the error rate is "twice as high for one group" among senior households. While a double-check mechanism is noted, the systematic disadvantage (the gap itself) is described as a present reality in the system's behavior.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed by AI labeling; **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal; **DARK** is avoided via opt-in/no timers; **AUTO** is gated by human sign-off for irreversible actions.

However, because the spec confirms the presence of hallucination (presented as fact) and a measured bias gap, these must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
