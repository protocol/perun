The spec explicitly handles several categories: AUTO is mitigated by human review before irreversible actions; DISC is addressed via visible AI-generated labels; and BIAS claims measured parity across groups.

However, critical harms remain present in the design:
1.  **HALLU**: The app presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown." This constitutes presenting unverified claims with unjustified authority.
2.  **RELY**: By hiding uncertainty and sources while presenting outputs as fact, the design invites over-trust. Furthermore, once a volunteer accepts, the result is "final" with "no appeal path," removing the ability to correct errors.
3.  **DARK**: The interface employs manufactured urgency (ten-minute timer), pre-ticked consent, and removes the ability to reverse decisions, pressuring users against their own interest.

These issues create significant risk for volunteers acting on potentially incorrect or fabricated task data with no recourse.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
