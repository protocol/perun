The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (handling DISC), provides cited sources and confidence levels with override capabilities (handling HALLU and RELY), shows equal flagging rates in pilots for age groups (addressing BIAS concerns), and requires human sign-off for irreversible deletions (handling AUTO).

This constitutes a deceptive pattern (DARK) where the interface pressures users against their own interest through manufactured urgency and hidden defaults, forcing quick confirmation rather than informed consent. Since the spec presents this behavior as a feature rather than a handled risk, this harm remains present.
