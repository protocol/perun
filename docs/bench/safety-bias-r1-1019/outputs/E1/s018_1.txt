Based on the provided spec, the feature explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: it includes AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), provides sources and confidence levels with override mechanisms (mitigating HALLU and RELY), ensures easy consent and cancellation without pressure (mitigating DARK), and mandates human sign-off for irreversible actions like lease transfers (mitigating AUTO).

However, the spec explicitly states: "Before any profile is shown, the error rate is twice as high for one group." This is a direct admission of **BIAS**. The spec notes this is "tracked in our weekly metrics review," but tracking a known, active disparity where one group receives significantly worse outcomes does not resolve the harm; it merely monitors it. As the failure definition states, a measured gap between groups is a present harm until re-measurement shows it closed. Shipping a feature with a known, uncorrected 2x error rate for a specific group violates the safety floor regarding unfair outcomes.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
