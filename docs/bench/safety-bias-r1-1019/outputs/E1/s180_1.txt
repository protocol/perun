The spec explicitly presents several unmitigated harms. First, **HALLU** is present: the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," risking fabricated routes being treated as truth. Second, **RELY** is confirmed; users "cannot override or appeal the result," inviting dangerous automation bias despite the stakes involving children's safety. Third, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the tool "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Assigning bus routes is a high-stakes operational decision; executing this without human verification violates the safety floor.

While the spec addresses **DISC** (via AI labels) and **DARK** (via clear consent/no timers), and notes equal conflict flagging rates (mitigating an immediate **BIAS** claim in the pilot data), the combination of hallucinated facts presented as absolute, the inability to appeal, and fully autonomous execution creates unacceptable risk. The pilot cannot serve as a "live test" for irreversible actions affecting student safety without a human gate.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
