The spec explicitly flags two present harms despite some mitigations. First, under **HALLU**, the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," directly satisfying the definition of unverified claims presented with unsupported authority. Second, under **RELY**, the design invites over-trust by ensuring "users cannot override or appeal the result," leaving no easy override for operators.

While the spec notes that bias was tested (addressing BIAS), AI disclosure is present (addressing DISC), consent is clear (addressing DARK), and irreversible actions require human sign-off (addressing AUTO), the combination of presenting outputs as absolute fact without sources and removing the ability to override creates a critical safety gap. Operators are forced to trust potentially unverified routing decisions without recourse.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
