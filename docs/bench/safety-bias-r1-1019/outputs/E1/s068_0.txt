The spec confirms human review for matches and approval for irreversible actions, addressing AUTO. It includes an AI-generated label, addressing DISC. Beta data suggests BIAS is measured and currently acceptable.

However, critical harms remain present in the design:
1.  **HALLU**: The app presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown." Fabricating authority for unverified suggestions is a direct hallucination risk.
2.  **RELY**: By hiding sources and presenting outputs as confirmed facts, the design invites over-trust. Furthermore, once accepted, results are final with "no appeal path," removing the ability to correct errors.
3.  **DARK**: The interface uses "pre-ticked" consent and a "ten-minute timer" to pressure users into fast confirmations against their own interest (manufactured urgency and hidden defaults).

These issues constitute active harms described in the spec that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
