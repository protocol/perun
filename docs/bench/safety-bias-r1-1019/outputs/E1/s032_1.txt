The spec reveals several unaddressed output safety harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the algorithm flags 11% of both older and younger users for different groups than their goals, indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age that requires measurement and mitigation before launch. Second, **DARK** patterns are explicit: the onboarding uses a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box for data sharing, pressuring users against their own interest. Third, **DISC** is missing: the UI states "Recommended group" without disclosing that a machine-generated model produced the output, despite this mattering to users trusting the assignment. Finally, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the system locks final group rosters 72 hours before the race without any human review. Assigning runners to specific pacelines is a high-stakes decision affecting safety and performance; fully automating this without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor.

While the spec notes that sources are cited and overrides exist (addressing HALLU and partially RELY), the combination of undisclosed AI, dark patterns, observed bias, and irreversible automation without human oversight creates unacceptable risk.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
