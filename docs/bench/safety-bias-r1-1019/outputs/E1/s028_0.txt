The spec reveals several unaddressed harms. While complex rooflines are flagged (mitigating some HALLU risk) and irreversible actions have human approval (mitigating AUTO), other issues remain. The spec explicitly states the error rate is "twice as high" for homes with complex rooflines; labeling them "preliminary" does not eliminate the systematic disadvantage, so BIAS is present. Furthermore, users "cannot override or appeal the result," creating a risk of over-trust without recourse (RELY). The interface employs a "ten-minute timer" for offers and "pre-ticked" consent, which are classic deceptive patterns pressuring users against their own interest (DARK). Although an AI label exists (addressing DISC), the combination of known disparate error rates, lack of user agency, and coercive interface elements constitutes significant residual risk.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
