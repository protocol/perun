The spec reveals three present harms requiring remediation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the system flags older users for coupon eligibility at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating a systematic disadvantage or disparate impact that remains unaddressed in the text.

Second, **HALLU** is a risk: the tool states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence" displayed. Presenting price data without provenance invites users to treat potentially fabricated or outdated figures as absolute fact.

Third, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the assistant executes irreversible financial transactions (completing a purchase) with "no human approval." High-stakes, irreversible actions involving money must have a human-in-the-loop gate; full automation here is unsafe by default.

Other categories appear handled: **DISC** is covered by the visible AI label; **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal; **DARK** patterns are absent given the clear opt-in, lack of timers, and easy cancellation.

However, the combination of unexplained bias, unsourced factual claims, and autonomous financial execution creates unacceptable residual risk. The bias gap requires investigation, sources must be surfaced to combat hallucination risks, and the checkout flow demands explicit human confirmation.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
