The spec explicitly mitigates several harms: it shows sources and confidence labels (addressing HALLU and RELY), allows one-tap overrides and requires human approval for irreversible actions (addressing AUTO), and reports equal flagging rates for age groups (addressing BIAS).

However, two harms are present in the description:
1. **DISC**: The spec states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Users cannot distinguish machine-generated suggestions from manual calculations, violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to decision-making.
2. **DARK**: The auto-trade feature uses a "ten-minute timer" (manufactured urgency) and "pre-ticked" consent (hidden default). These are deceptive patterns pressuring users against their own interest.

Because the spec describes these specific failures as current behaviors, they must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
