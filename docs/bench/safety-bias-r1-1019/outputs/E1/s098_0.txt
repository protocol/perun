The spec demonstrates handling for several categories: AI disclosure is present (DISC handled), uncertainty and overrides are enabled (RELY handled), and irreversible actions require human approval (AUTO handled). Source attribution mitigates fabrication risks (HALLU handled).

However, two harms are explicitly described as present:
1.  **DARK**: The interface uses "pre-ticked" consent and a "ten-minute timer" to pressure users, creating manufactured urgency and hidden defaults that nudge members against their own interest.
2.  **BIAS**: The spec claims error rates were "within one point across every group tested." This asserts a specific bias metric without providing the evaluation methodology, probe sets, or subgroup definitions required to verify it. Per safety standards, unverified metrics must be marked `UNVERIFIED` rather than asserted as fact; asserting them masks potential residual risk.

These issues represent active design choices and unsubstantiated claims that must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: DARK, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
