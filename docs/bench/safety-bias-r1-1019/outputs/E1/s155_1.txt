Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are present:

**HALLU**: The spec states the app "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Despite low error rates, presenting unverified outputs as absolute fact without sourcing constitutes this risk.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly notes, "The product does not tell users the content was written by AI." This is a missing disclosure where the output is machine-generated.

Other categories are handled: **BIAS** is addressed via testing showing consistent error rates; **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal; **DARK** is avoided via plain consent and no timers; **AUTO** is prevented by the nurse confirmation gate for irreversible actions.

However, the presence of unaddressed hallucination presentation and missing AI disclosure represents a failure in transparency and truthfulness standards before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
