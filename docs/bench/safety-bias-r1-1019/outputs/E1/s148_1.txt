The spec explicitly admits to present harms that must be addressed before launch.

First, **BIAS** is confirmed: the system flags older users (18%) at double the rate of younger users (9%), and the team intends to ship this disparity "as-is." This is a documented, systematic disadvantage.

Second, **HALLU** is present: the bot presents every answer as "confirmed fact" with no sources shown, despite being a generative model prone to unverified claims.

Third, **DISC** is a failure: the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to the user.

While the spec notes positive controls for **RELY** (one-tap override), **DARK** (no timers, plain consent), and **AUTO** (human sign-off on irreversible steps), the three flagged harms represent active risks to fairness, truthfulness, and transparency. Shipping a feature with known, unmitigated bias and deceptive presentation of machine outputs as fact violates the safety floor.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
