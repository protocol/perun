The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms but leaves one critical gap. Bias (BIAS) is acknowledged ("error rate is twice as high for one group") but managed via overrides and appeals, so it is handled rather than an unaddressed flaw. Reliance (RELY) is mitigated by showing confidence levels and easy overrides. Automation (AUTO) is controlled by a human sign-off on irreversible steps. Dark patterns (DARK) are avoided with plain consent and no timers.

However, the spec states: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output; it reads as a straightforward utility." This confirms the absence of AI disclosure (DISC). Even though other safeguards exist, presenting automated reasoning as a simple utility without indicating machine generation prevents users from appropriately calibrating their trust or understanding the source of the analysis. This matches the DISC definition directly.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
