The spec explicitly addresses the primary harm categories through designed controls. Confidence labels, source citations, one-tap overrides, and appeals mitigate over-reliance (RELY). Visible AI-generated labels satisfy disclosure (DISC). The opt-in flow without timers or pressure avoids deceptive patterns (DARK). Crucially, a human approves every final denial or revocation, gating high-stakes irreversible actions (AUTO).

Regarding bias (BIAS), the spec notes equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users in pre-launch testing. While it states the team is "monitoring" to confirm this pattern holds, the provided data shows no current systematic disadvantage, and the spec does not describe a present gap. Similarly, by requiring sources for claims, the design attempts to prevent hallucinations presented as fact (HALLU), and the spec does not describe unverified claims being surfaced as authority.

Since the spec describes controls for all listed harm categories and provides data indicating no current disparate impact, no category is flagged as present based strictly on the text provided. The decision relies on the assumption that the described controls function as specified during the pilot.

HARMS: NONE
DECISION: PROCEED
