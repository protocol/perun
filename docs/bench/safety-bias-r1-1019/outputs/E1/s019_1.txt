The spec explicitly handles several categories: it includes an AI-generated label (resolving DISC), offers one-tap override/appeal (resolving RELY), uses plain consent without pressure (resolving DARK), and claims internal testing showed equal error rates across groups (addressing BIAS per the spec's assertion).

However, two critical harms remain present based on the text:
1.  **HALLU**: The spec states the app "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Presenting algorithmic matches as absolute facts without sourcing or uncertainty indicators constitutes hallucination surfaced as fact.
2.  **AUTO**: The spec notes, "Matching happens automatically... and nobody reviews it first." For a feature resulting in housing matches—a high-stakes decision with significant financial and personal consequences—executing this without a human-in-the-loop gate before the action finalizes violates the safety floor for irreversible/high-stakes automation.

These issues represent active risks described in the spec that are not mitigated by the current design.

HARMS: HALLU,AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
