The spec explicitly identifies a present disparity: 18% of older users flagged for incompleteness versus 9% of younger users. Despite a new checklist, this measured gap constitutes **BIAS** until re-measurement shows it closed; a plan alone does not clear it. The system presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence" and offers "no override or appeal," forcing a restart to dispute results. This confirms **RELY** (over-reliance/automation bias) and **HALLU** (unverified claims presented as fact without sourcing).

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is covered by the visible AI label; **DARK** is mitigated by the lack of timers and easy cancel; **AUTO** is gated by human approval for irreversible actions. However, the combination of unaddressed bias, missing uncertainty indicators, and the inability to appeal creates unacceptable residual risk for a financial product.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
