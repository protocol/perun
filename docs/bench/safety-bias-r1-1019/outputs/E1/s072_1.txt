The spec reveals three critical harms. First, **HALLU** is present: the bot states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," presenting unverified claims as fact. Second, **RELY** is triggered because the design offers "no appeal path" and shows no uncertainty, inviting over-trust in a final decision. Third, **DARK** patterns are explicitly described: a "ten-minute timer" creates manufactured urgency, and "pre-ticked" consent forces users to actively cancel rather than opt-in, pressuring them against their own interest.

While **DISC** is handled (visible AI label) and **AUTO** appears mitigated by the stated human approval for irreversible actions, the combination of unverified facts, lack of recourse, and deceptive interface patterns creates unacceptable risk. The claim of equal error rates during a small pilot does not negate the structural harm of removing appeals or the deceptive consent mechanism. These issues must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
