The spec reveals two present harms. First, **DISC** is triggered because the text explicitly states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to users. Second, **RELY** is present because the design specifies "the result is final and there is no appeal path." This eliminates the easy override or appeal mechanism necessary to prevent automation bias, inviting users to trust the output past its reliability without recourse.

Other categories are not flagged based on the provided text: **BIAS** is addressed by the pilot data showing equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users; **AUTO** is handled by the requirement that "a person approves every irreversible action"; **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in consent, no timers, and easy cancellation; and **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources and stated confidence levels.

However, the lack of disclosure and the absence of an appeal path are critical failures in a health-context tool. These issues must be resolved before launch to ensure users understand the nature of the decision-making and retain agency over the outcomes.

HARMS: DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
