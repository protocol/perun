The spec explicitly identifies several present harms. **BIAS** is confirmed by the admitted disparity in lease conflict flags (18% older vs. 9% younger users); monitoring a known gap does not resolve it. **DARK** patterns are present via the countdown timer and pre-ticked consent box, which pressure users against their own interest. **AUTO** is a critical failure: the system executes irreversible actions (finalizing lease matches) without human approval, violating the safety floor for high-stakes decisions.

Conversely, **HALLU** and **RELY** appear addressed, as the spec notes sources are shown, confidence labels exist, and overrides/appeals are one-tap. **DISC** is also handled, as outputs are labeled AI-generated. However, the presence of Bias, Dark Patterns, and Unsafe Automation requires immediate remediation before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
