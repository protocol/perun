The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: it claims measured parity across groups (mitigating BIAS), requires sources and confidence labels for claims (mitigating HALLU), allows overrides and appeals (mitigating RELY), labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), and uses clear consent without pressure (mitigating DARK).

However, the spec admits a critical failure in the AUTO category. It states that if a mortgage lender requests a rate lock, the system "executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Rate locks and premium changes in flood insurance are high-stakes financial decisions that can be costly or difficult to reverse. Executing these automatically without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for irreversible actions, regardless of the pilot's previous success with error rates. This specific exception creates an unmanaged risk of immediate financial harm before launch.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
