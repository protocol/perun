The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: bias is measured with equal flagging rates (11%) across age groups; hallucination is mitigated by cited sources; over-reliance is countered by visible triggers, confidence levels, and one-tap overrides; disclosure is present via AI labeling; and dark patterns are avoided through opt-in consent and no timers.

However, the AUTO category is flagged. The spec states the bot "files claims automatically" and executes "irreversible actions" (submitting legal/financial claims) with "no human approval" after a single user tap. While the user initiates the flow, the actual filing is a high-stakes automated decision executed without a human-in-the-loop gate or explicit confirmation step at the moment of execution. The spec treats the initial tap as sufficient, but the definition of high-stakes automation requires a human gate for irreversible actions, which is absent here.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
