The spec explicitly reveals three present harms. First, **DISC**: the product hides its algorithmic nature ("Nothing... says a model produced the output"), preventing users from understanding the source of recommendations. Second, **RELY**: users "cannot override or appeal the result," forcing them to restart queries rather than correct specific mismatches, which invites over-trust in the initial ranking. Third, **BIAS**: the spec admits the error rate is "twice as high" for households with toddlers, a systematic disadvantage that persists until a future fix, making the current pilot harmful to that group.

While the spec handles **AUTO** (human sign-off exists), **DARK** (consent is clean), and partially addresses **HALLU** (sources shown), the confirmed bias against families with toddlers and the lack of transparency/appeal mechanisms constitute unacceptable risks for launch. The plan to "adjust inputs... before wider rollout" admits the harm exists now during the pilot.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
