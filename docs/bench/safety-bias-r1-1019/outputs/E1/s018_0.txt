The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: it includes AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), provides sources and confidence levels with override mechanisms (mitigating HALLU and RELY), ensures easy cancellation without pressure (mitigating DARK), and mandates human sign-off for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO). There is no mention of systematic disadvantage based on protected groups in the matching logic itself, so BIAS is not flagged based on the text provided.

However, the spec admits a critical unresolved issue: "Before any profile is shown, the error rate is twice as high for one group." While the team tracks this in weekly metrics, the feature is launching with a known, unmitigated disparity in performance affecting a specific user segment. This constitutes a present harm under the BIAS category, as the output systematically disadvantages a group with higher error rates. The existence of a tracking plan does not neutralize the active harm at launch.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
