The spec demonstrates robust handling of most harm categories: DISC is addressed via explicit AI labeling; DARK patterns are avoided through opt-in consent and lack of manipulative timers; AUTO is mitigated by human sign-off on irreversible swaps; and BIAS appears managed given the pilot's reported equitable error rates across groups. HALLU is countered by requiring cited sources from bylaws.

However, the spec explicitly states: "Users cannot override or appeal the result; disputes go to the next season's waitlist." This design invites over-trust in the system's output by removing any immediate mechanism for correction or appeal for the current cycle, directly triggering the RELY (over-reliance/automation bias) category. The inability to challenge an allocation before the season starts constitutes a present harm where users must accept potentially flawed automated decisions without recourse.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
