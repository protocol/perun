The spec reveals three present harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted: the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users. Logging this disparity in release notes does not mitigate the systematic disadvantage; it merely documents it. Second, **RELY** is present because users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing the human safety valve for a decision affecting their education path. Third, **DISC** is triggered as "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," denying users knowledge of the automated nature of their assessment.

While **HALLU** is mitigated by showing sources and confidence labels, and **AUTO** is handled via human approval for irreversible actions, the unaddressed bias, lack of appeal, and missing disclosure constitute significant risks before launch. The "closed pilot" status does not negate the need to fix these foundational output safety failures before exposing even a small group to potentially unfair, unappealable, and undisclosed automated decisions.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
