The spec reveals three present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is evident: the system flagged 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users for coupon eligibility, indicating a systematic disadvantage or disparate treatment based on age.

Second, **HALLU** is present. The tool states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence." Presenting price data without provenance or uncertainty indicators risks surfacing unverified or fabricated claims as absolute fact, misleading users making financial decisions.

Third, **AUTO** is a critical failure. The feature "completes a purchase" and "executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Financial transactions are high-stakes; fully automating them without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for irreversible actions.

While **DISC** is handled (visible AI label) and **RELY** is partially mitigated (one-tap override), the combination of unexplained bias, unsourced factual claims, and autonomous financial execution creates unacceptable residual risk. The pilot scope does not negate these fundamental output safety failures.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
