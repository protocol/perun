Based on the spec provided, the following harms are present:

**HALLU**: The spec states the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." This matches the definition of unverified claims presented with unsupported authority.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly notes "the bot does not tell users the content was written by AI." Since the output reaches ops managers who must trust its logic, the missing disclosure is a present harm.

Other categories are handled: **BIAS** is addressed by identical flagging rates (11%) and messaging across age groups in the shadow test. **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override/appeal mechanism. **DARK** patterns are avoided via plain opt-in and easy cancellation. **AUTO** is prevented by the human sign-off requirement for irreversible actions.

However, because the feature ships with unverified claims presented as fact and no AI disclosure, it cannot proceed without addressing these specific gaps.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
