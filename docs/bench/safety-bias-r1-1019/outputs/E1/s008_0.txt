The spec reveals several unaddressed output safety harms. First, **DISC** is present: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to users. Second, **RELY** is flagged because "the result is final and there is no appeal path for a slot change," inviting over-reliance without an override mechanism. Third, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted: "the error rate is twice as high for one group—patients using older phones." While logged for review, a measured gap constitutes a present harm until closed; shipping with known unequal performance is unsafe. Finally, **DARK** patterns are evident as consent is "opt-in and unticked," a hidden default that pressures users against their interest by assuming agreement.

Although **AUTO** is mitigated ("A person approves every irreversible action"), the combination of undisclosed AI generation, lack of appeal for decisions, known biased error rates, and deceptive consent defaults creates unacceptable risk for launch. The plan to "review weekly" does not neutralize these active harms before users are exposed.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
