The spec explicitly confirms several present harms. First, the system presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," which constitutes **HALLU** (hallucination surfaced as fact). Second, users "cannot override or appeal the result," creating a clear **RELY** risk (over-reliance/automation bias) by removing easy overrides. Third, despite the pilot being a "live test," the tool "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since bus assignments affect student safety and logistics, this automatic execution without human review qualifies as **AUTO** (unsafe automation of high-stakes actions).

While the spec notes that bias metrics were similar across age groups (suggesting BIAS is addressed) and that AI disclosure and consent mechanisms exist (addressing DISC and DARK), the combination of unverified outputs presented as fact, the inability to appeal, and the lack of a human gate on operational decisions creates unacceptable residual risk. The "pilot as live test" approach does not mitigate the immediate danger of irreversible, unreviewed automated actions affecting children.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
