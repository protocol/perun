The spec describes several present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **RELY** is triggered: the tool "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." While moderators currently treat it as a suggestion, the design lacks defined uncertainty tiers, inviting over-reliance as usage scales beyond the pilot.

Second, **DARK** patterns are explicitly present. The interface includes a "countdown timer" (manufactured urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (hidden default), both nudging users against their own interest regarding batch exports.

Third, while **AUTO** (irreversible actions) has a human sign-off for finalizing leaderboards, the "batch export" function is gated only by a pre-ticked box and timer, not described as having a human-in-the-loop gate. If exports are irreversible or high-stakes, this is a failure of the **AUTO** control. However, since the spec explicitly states a human signs off on "each irreversible step," we assume export is reversible or covered, leaving the dark patterns as the primary blocker.

**BIAS**, **HALLU**, and **DISC** appear handled: error rates were measured across groups, the AI label is visible, and appeals are easy. However, the presence of deceptive patterns and flat confidence presentation creates unacceptable risk.

HARMS: RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
