The spec describes several present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

**HALLU**: The tool "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting scores without uncertainty indicators invites moderators to treat fabricated or unverified assessments as absolute fact, even if they currently view them as suggestions.

**RELY**: While an override exists, the flat presentation of scores without defined confidence tiers (e.g., "Corroborated" vs. "Unverified") fails to adequately signal reliability limits, fostering automation bias.

**DARK**: The interface includes a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box" before batch exports. These are classic deceptive patterns that pressure users into actions against their interest or bypass informed consent.

**BIAS**: The pilot tested only "three volunteer judging boards." This sample is insufficient to verify the absence of systematic disadvantage across diverse demographic groups or photography styles. The claim of uniform error rates is likely **UNVERIFIED** for the broader population.

**DISC** and **AUTO** appear handled: the spec confirms visible AI labels and human sign-off on irreversible steps.

However, the combination of deceptive interface elements (DARK), lack of uncertainty signaling (HALLU/RELY), and unverified fairness claims (BIAS) creates significant risk. The deceptive patterns specifically violate the requirement to avoid nudging users against their interests.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
