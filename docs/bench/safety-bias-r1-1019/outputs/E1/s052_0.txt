The spec explicitly identifies a present **BIAS**: error rates are twice as high for out-of-state plates. While a secondary verification step is added, the spec defines this gap as a current reality, which constitutes a present harm until re-measurement proves it closed.

The spec confirms **AUTO**: the tool "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" by voiding charges directly. This violates the mandatory floor that high-stakes or irreversible actions require a human-in-the-loop gate. Full automation of such actions is the specific failure this review prevents.

The spec explicitly handles other categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (resolving **DISC**), shows sources and confidence labels (mitigating **HALLU** and **RELY**), and uses plain opt-in without timers (avoiding **DARK**). However, the presence of unmitigated bias and ungated irreversible automation blocks launch.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
