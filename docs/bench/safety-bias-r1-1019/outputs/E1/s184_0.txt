The spec explicitly acknowledges a measured disparity where older users are flagged at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%) and states the team will "accept for now" without mitigation. This is a confirmed present harm under **BIAS**.

While the spec demonstrates strong controls for other categories—explicit AI disclosure (**DISC** handled), one-tap overrides (**RELY** handled), and mandatory human approval for escalations (**AUTO** handled)—the admitted bias violates the safety floor. The framework dictates that a measured gap between groups is a present harm until re-measurement shows it closed; a plan to accept it does not clear it. Shipping with an acknowledged, unmitigated disparate impact requires a block.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
