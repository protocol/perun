The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: it labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), allows overrides and appeals (mitigating RELY), and requires human sign-off before writing to the registrar (mitigating AUTO). While error rates were reported as consistent across groups during pilot validation, the spec does not provide evidence of testing for systematic disadvantage against specific protected groups, leaving BIAS unverified rather than confirmed present; however, the interface presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," which creates a risk of HALLU where unverified claims are presented as fact without context. Additionally, the onboarding flow uses a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box," which constitutes a deceptive pattern pressuring users against their own interest (DARK).

HARMS: HALLU, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
