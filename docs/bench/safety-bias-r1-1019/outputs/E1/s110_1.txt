The spec reveals two present harms. First, **BIAS** is evident: the tool flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users, yet the interface fails to highlight this demographic split. While the rates appear equal here, the explicit absence of visibility into group performance constitutes a gap in fairness monitoring required for public sector tools. Second, **DISC** is triggered: the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output—it reads like a checklist." Since this is an automated screener influencing permitting decisions, users (both staff and applicants) cannot distinguish machine-generated assessments from manual checks, violating transparency norms.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by source citations; **RELY** is addressed via confidence labels and one-tap overrides; **DARK** patterns are absent due to clear consent and no timers; **AUTO** is controlled by mandatory human sign-off for denials/revocations. However, the missing AI disclosure and the un surfaced demographic data split represent unresolved risks before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
