The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: bias is claimed handled via trial data; hallucination is mitigated with citations; over-reliance is addressed with confidence levels and overrides; disclosure is present via labels; and high-stakes automation is gated by human approval.

However, the spec describes a **DARK** pattern: "a pre-ticked consent box" for cancellations. Pre-ticked boxes are a deceptive pattern that pressures users into consent against their interest by requiring them to opt-out rather than opt-in. Additionally, the "countdown timer" manufactures urgency, further pressuring the user. Since the spec admits this design element exists, the harm is present.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
