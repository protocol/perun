The spec explicitly identifies three present harms. First, while flagging rates for older and younger users are equal (11%), the system assigns them to *different* offer tiers based on age, which constitutes systematic disadvantage by group (BIAS). Second, the engine presents every output as "confirmed fact with no source shown," creating a high risk of unverified claims being accepted as truth (HALLU). Third, the design explicitly states users "cannot override or appeal the result," inviting dangerous over-reliance without recourse (RELY).

Other categories are handled: the app labels outputs as AI-generated (resolving DISC), enrollment is opt-in with no dark patterns (resolving DARK), and while actions are automated, monetary offers in a pilot context may not meet the "irreversible/high-stakes" threshold requiring a human gate compared to the clear evidence of bias and hallucination risks (AUTO is not flagged based strictly on the provided text's severity description).

Because the feature ships with confirmed bias in tier assignment, presents unverified data as fact, and removes user appeal mechanisms, it cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
