The spec demonstrates strong handling of most safety categories: it includes AI disclosure (DISC), easy overrides (RELY), ethical consent patterns (no DARK patterns), and human gates for high-stakes financial actions (AUTO). It also acknowledges a potential disparity in error rates for suspension quotes after rain.

However, the spec explicitly states that for this specific subgroup and condition, the "error rate is twice as high." While the team is delaying the category's launch, the specification describes a present, measured systematic disadvantage for a specific group (likely defined by bike type or riding conditions) that has not yet been resolved or proven closed by re-measurement. Per the safety guidelines, a measured gap between groups is a present harm until a re-measure shows it closed; a plan or delay alone does not clear it. Because the spec admits this unequal error rate exists in the system's current state, it meets the definition of BIAS. Since a measured bias is present, the feature cannot proceed until that specific gap is closed or verified as resolved.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
