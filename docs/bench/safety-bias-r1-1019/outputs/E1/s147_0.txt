Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec explicitly states the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Despite other controls, presenting unverified claims with absolute authority constitutes this harm.

**BIAS**: The spec admits the error rate is "twice as high for one group" among senior households. While a double-check mechanism is noted, the systematic disadvantage (the gap itself) is described as a present reality in the system's behavior.

All other categories are addressed in the spec: uncertainty/override is available (mitigating RELY), AI disclosure is present (mitigating DISC), no deceptive patterns are described (mitigating DARK), and irreversible actions have human sign-off (mitigating AUTO). However, the presence of unverified claims presented as fact and a known disparate error rate requires remediation before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
