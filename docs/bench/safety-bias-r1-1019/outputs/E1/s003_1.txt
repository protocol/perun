The spec presents mixed controls and active harms. Bias (BIAS) appears addressed via equal flagging rates across age groups in testing. Hallucination (HALLU) is mitigated by sourcing claims and showing confidence labels. Over-reliance (RELY) is countered by one-tap overrides and appeals. Missing disclosure (DISC) is handled by visible AI labels.

However, two critical harms remain unaddressed. First, the interface employs deceptive patterns (DARK): a countdown timer manufactures urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box subverts informed agreement, pressuring users against their own interests. Second, the system executes high-stakes financial decisions (waiving fines) automatically without human review ("acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first"). This violates the requirement for a human-in-the-loop on irreversible or costly actions (AUTO), regardless of the "pilot" status.

HARMS: DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
