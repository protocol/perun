The spec reveals two present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **DISC** (Missing AI-disclosure) is explicitly present: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output; screens read 'suggested priority.'" Users cannot distinguish machine-generated triage from static rules or human input, violating the transparency floor.

Second, **RELY** (Over-reliance) is indicated by the presentation of "a stated confidence level." Unless these levels are rendered as defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Confirmed" vs. "Unverified") rather than precise percentages, they risk implying uncalibrated precision, inviting automation bias despite the available override.

Other categories appear handled: **BIAS** is addressed via pilot data showing equal flag rates (11%) across age groups; **AUTO** is mitigated by the human-in-the-loop gate for irreversible actions; **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in consent and lack of timers; **HALLU** is countered by cited sources. However, the missing disclosure and potential precision bias in confidence reporting constitute unresolved risks.

HARMS: DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
