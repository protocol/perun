The spec explicitly describes a measured disparity where the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, confirming **BIAS** is present. While the spec notes confidence labels and override capabilities (mitigating RELY), clear AI disclosure (mitigating DISC), and safe enrollment patterns (mitigating DARK), it admits the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since reordering waitlists directly impacts member access to services based on a biased metric without human intervention, this constitutes **AUTO** (unsafe automation of a high-stakes action given the proven disparity). The presence of unmitigated bias combined with fully automated execution creates a direct harm vector that must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
