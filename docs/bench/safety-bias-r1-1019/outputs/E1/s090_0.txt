The spec demonstrates handling for most harm categories: bias is measured with matched rates (11%/11%) and high agreement (92%); reliability is addressed via cited sources, confidence levels, and easy overrides; dark patterns are avoided with unticked consent and no timers; and auto-harms are prevented by requiring human approval for irreversible actions.

However, the spec explicitly states: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output; screens read 'suggested priority'." This confirms the **DISC** (Missing AI-disclosure) harm is present, as users cannot distinguish machine-generated recommendations from standard protocol logic. While the system includes human gates for irreversible actions, the lack of disclosure regarding the automated nature of the triage ranking violates the transparency requirement for AI-assisted decision support.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
