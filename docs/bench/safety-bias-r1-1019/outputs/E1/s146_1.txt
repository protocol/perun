The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: bias is measured across groups (BIAS handled), hallucination is mitigated via citations and confidence levels (HALLU handled), over-reliance is prevented by showing uncertainty and enabling overrides (RELY handled), and high-stakes actions require human approval (AUTO handled). Dark patterns are absent due to clear consent and no timers (DARK handled).

However, the spec states: "The bot does not tell users the content was written by AI." Since this feature generates custom financial plans and interacts directly with customers facing financial distress, the lack of disclosure constitutes a present harm under the DISC category. The spec confirms this handling is missing rather than implemented.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
