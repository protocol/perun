The spec explicitly describes several present harms. First, **HALLU** is flagged: the bot "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified claims as fact. Second, **RELY** is present because the design offers "no uncertainty shown, no easy override or appeal," and the result is described as "final." Third, **DISC** is confirmed: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Finally, **AUTO** is triggered because once the bot posts a pick, "the result is final and there is no appeal path," constituting an irreversible action without a human gate.

While the spec notes that queue *changes* require approval, the initial recommendation—the core output—is automated and irreversible. The claim that error rates were "within one point" does not mitigate the lack of uncertainty signaling or the missing appeal mechanism for the primary action. The conversational tone intentionally obscures the AI nature (DISC), and the "final" status prevents correction (RELY/AUTO). These are structural flaws in the output behavior that must be addressed before launch to prevent user harm from unchallengeable, opaque automation.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
