The spec explicitly acknowledges a present bias: the error rate is twice as high for one group. Per the safety floor, a measured gap is a present harm until closed; tracking it does not clear it. The feature also presents answers flatly with no source or confidence, inviting over-reliance (RELY) despite the override capability, as users cannot gauge reliability. Finally, the system executes irreversible actions (issuing new tickets) automatically after payment without human approval. While payment is a precursor, the ticket issuance itself is an irreversible fulfillment action automated without a human gate, violating the high-stakes automation floor.

Disclosure (DISC) and dark patterns (DARK) are handled via visible labels and unticked, timer-free consent. Hallucination (HALLU) is a risk due to flat presentation, but the spec describes the *presentation* style rather than confirming fabricated facts as a current state, though it strongly implies RELY. The primary blockers are the unmitigated bias, the lack of uncertainty signaling fostering over-reliance, and the fully automated irreversible action.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
