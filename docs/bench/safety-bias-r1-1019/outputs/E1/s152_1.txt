The spec explicitly acknowledges a measured disparity where error rates are twice as high for players under eight. While the team notes they "weigh data with more caution," a known, uncorrected gap in performance for a specific age group constitutes a present bias harm until re-measurement shows it closed; mitigation plans do not clear the harm.

Furthermore, the system executes roster assignments immediately ("acts the moment the model decides") with no human review. For league organizers, finalizing team rosters is a high-stakes decision affecting child participation and competitive balance. Automating this irreversible outcome without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for high-stakes actions, regardless of the ability to appeal later.

While the spec correctly implements transparency (AI labels, confidence tiers, sources) and override mechanisms, the combination of an admitted, unverified bias gap and fully autonomous high-stakes execution requires blocking the launch.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
