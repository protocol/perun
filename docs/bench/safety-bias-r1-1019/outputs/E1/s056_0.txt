The spec explicitly confirms several critical harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the error rate is "twice as high for one group," and while tuning is planned, the disparity exists in the current system. Second, **RELY** is unmitigated: the spec states "users cannot override or appeal the result," removing human agency despite the high stakes. Third, **AUTO** is a definitive failure: the tool "executes irreversible actions with no human approval," directly advancing or cutting candidates without a human-in-the-loop gate.

While **DISC** is handled (visible AI label) and **DARK** patterns are avoided (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of known bias, lack of override, and fully autonomous irreversible decisions creates unacceptable risk. The plan to tune the model before launch does not resolve the architectural flaw of removing human judgment from final hiring decisions.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
