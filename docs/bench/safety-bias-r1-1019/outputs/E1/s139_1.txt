The spec presents several output-safety concerns. First, under **HALLU**, the eligibility check "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified claims as fact. Second, **DARK** patterns are explicitly described: new applicants face a "countdown timer" (manufactured urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (hidden default), nudging users against their interests. Third, **RELY** is triggered because success is measured only by "pickup rates and stall turnover, not by complaints," indicating a lack of monitoring for over-reliance or user harm despite the system's authoritative tone.

Other categories appear handled: **BIAS** is addressed by equal flagging rates (11% for both age groups); **DISC** is satisfied by the visible AI-generated label; and **AUTO** is mitigated by the human sign-off on irreversible steps. However, the presence of hallucination, deceptive patterns, and unmonitored over-reliance constitutes significant residual risk.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
