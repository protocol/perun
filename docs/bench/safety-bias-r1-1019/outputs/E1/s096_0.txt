The spec explicitly acknowledges a measured disparity where the error rate is "twice as high for one group." Under the product-output-safety framework, a measured gap between groups constitutes a present **BIAS** harm until re-measurement shows it closed; tracking the segment in a dashboard does not mitigate the harm itself. Additionally, the system executes "irreversible actions" (issuing a new ticket) with "no human approval" after payment, which triggers the **AUTO** failure condition for high-stakes or irreversible automation lacking a human-in-the-loop gate. While the spec addresses disclosure (DISC), override mechanics (RELY), and consent patterns (DARK), the admitted bias and ungated irreversible action remain unmitigated risks. Presenting answers "flatly" without source or confidence supports the override capability but does not resolve the underlying bias or the lack of a pre-execution gate.

HARMS: BIAS,AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
