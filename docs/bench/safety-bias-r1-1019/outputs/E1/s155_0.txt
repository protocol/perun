Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are present:

**HALLU**: The spec states the app "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Even with low error rates, presenting AI-derived conclusions as absolute facts without sourcing constitutes hallucination surfaced as fact.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly notes, "The product does not tell users the content was written by AI." This is a clear missing AI disclosure.

Other categories are mitigated in the spec: **BIAS** is addressed via validation testing; **RELY** is handled by the one-tap override to a nurse; **DARK** patterns are absent due to plain consent; **AUTO** is prevented by the mandatory nurse confirmation for irreversible actions during the pilot.

However, the presence of unverified claims presented as fact and the lack of AI disclosure represent unresolved risks that violate the safety floor for user-facing AI outputs.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
