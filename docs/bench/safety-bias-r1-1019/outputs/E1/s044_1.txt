The spec reveals several unaddressed output safety harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the model flags older users at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%). While logged, this systematic disadvantage remains active for the pilot. Second, **RELY** is a failure point; the spec states users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing necessary human recourse for an impactful educational decision. Third, **DISC** is missing: the spec notes "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating transparency requirements for automated decisions. Finally, **DARK** patterns are evident because consent is "unticked" by default, pressuring users into agreement rather than requiring active opt-in.

Although **AUTO** is handled (humans approve irreversible actions like cohort moves) and **HALLU** is mitigated (sources and confidence labels are shown), the combination of active bias, lack of appeal, missing AI disclosure, and deceptive consent mechanics creates unacceptable risk for learners. These issues directly affect user fairness and autonomy before the system can be considered safe for even a limited pilot.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
