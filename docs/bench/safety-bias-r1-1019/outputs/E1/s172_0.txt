The spec explicitly describes several present harms. First, **BIAS** is admitted: the system flags older users at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), and the team has accepted this gap rather than fixing it. Second, **AUTO** is present: the system automatically assigns seats—a high-stakes action determining access—with no human review before execution. Third, **RELY** is evident: the spec states results are "final" with "no appeal path," removing any easy override for users harmed by errors. Additionally, **DARK** patterns appear in the pilot design, where consent was "unticked" by default, manipulating users into agreement.

While the spec notes good practices like AI disclosure (**DISC** is handled) and cited sources (**HALLU** appears mitigated), the combination of unaddressed bias, fully automated high-stakes decisions without human oversight, and the lack of an appeal mechanism creates unacceptable risk. The acceptance of known demographic disparities and the removal of user recourse violate the requirement to manage residual risk before scaling to 40 clubs.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
