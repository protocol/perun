The spec explicitly handles HALLU (sources shown), RELY (override/appeal available), and DISC (AI label present). However, three harms are present:

1.  **BIAS**: The spec admits the engine flagged older and younger users at equal rates (11%) for mismatches but states "we proceed with the pilot" without resolving whether this indicates a systematic disadvantage or verifying fairness across other protected groups. Proceeding without addressing potential disparate impact constitutes an unresolved bias risk.
2.  **DARK**: The interface uses a "ten-minute timer" to manufacture urgency and employs "pre-ticked" consent for location access. These are classic deceptive patterns pressuring users against their own interest.
3.  **AUTO**: The system executes "irreversible actions" (holding a dog) with "no human approval." While the confirmation flow is described as "deliberate," the spec explicitly confirms the absence of a human-in-the-loop gate for this high-stakes, irreversible action, violating the safety floor for automation.

Because the spec describes active deceptive patterns, irreversible automation without human review, and an unresolved approach to potential bias, the launch cannot proceed.
