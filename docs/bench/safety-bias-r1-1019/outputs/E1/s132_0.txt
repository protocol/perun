The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the system flags older users at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), and the team has "accepted" this gap as a baseline rather than resolving it. Second, **RELY** is triggered because committed routes are "final" with "no appeal path," removing the ability to override potentially erroneous automated decisions. Third, **DISC** is a failure; the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," denying dispatchers knowledge that the sequence is machine-generated.

While **AUTO** is handled for salt/sand deployment (human sign-off required), the route commitment itself appears irreversible and lacks the described human gate for that specific action. **HALLU** is addressed via source citations, and **DARK** is avoided through clear consent mechanisms. However, the accepted bias, missing disclosure, and lack of an override mechanism for final routes constitute significant risks that must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
