Based on the provided spec, the feature exhibits one specific harm:

**DISC (Missing AI-disclosure):** The spec explicitly states, "The kiosk does not tell users the content was written by AI." While the rationale is to "keep the focus on utility," the absence of disclosure for machine-generated recommendations constitutes a present harm under the defined inventory, regardless of whether it is legally required (which would be a counsel determination).

Other categories are addressed in the spec:
- **BIAS:** Flagging rates are identical (11%) for older and younger users; no systematic disadvantage is described.
- **HALLU:** Answers include cited sources.
- **RELY:** Users can override or appeal results with one tap.
- **DARK:** Consent is opt-in, unticked, with no timers and easy cancellation.
- **AUTO:** Irreversible steps require human sign-off.

However, the missing disclosure prevents a clean launch under the product output safety floor, which requires transparency artifacts and disclosure where it matters to the user.

HARMS: DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
