The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: high-stakes automation is gated by human sign-off (mitigating AUTO); uncertainty is shown via confidence levels and sources, with easy overrides available (mitigating RELY); and the error rate disparity is acknowledged with appeal mechanisms, though the gap itself indicates a present BIAS harm until closed. However, the spec states "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," which constitutes a missing AI disclosure (DISC). While the feature reads as a utility, the generation of claim packets and delay analysis by a bot requires transparency about machine involvement where it matters to the user. The bias gap is noted as a present harm ("error rate is twice as high for one group"), even with appeals available; a measured gap is a present harm until re-measurement shows it closed.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
