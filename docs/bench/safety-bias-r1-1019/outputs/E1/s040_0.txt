The spec explicitly admits a measured disparity: "the error rate is twice as high for one group." This confirms **BIAS** is present. While the system shows source and confidence, it explicitly states "users cannot override or appeal the result," confirming **RELY** (over-reliance/automation bias). The system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first" for assignment decisions; given the admitted bias and the impact on food access, this constitutes **AUTO** (unsafe automation of high-stakes actions without a human gate). 

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed via visible labels; **DARK** is addressed via plain opt-in and no timers; **HALLU** is mitigated by showing sources, though the bias suggests the underlying model may still be flawed, the specific harm of presenting fabrication as fact is not explicitly described as occurring in the text, rather the error rate disparity is. However, the combination of known bias, lack of appeal, and automatic execution creates an immediate safety failure.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
