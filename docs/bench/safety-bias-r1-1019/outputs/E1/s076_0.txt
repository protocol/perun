The spec explicitly discloses AI usage and sources, addressing DISC and mitigating HALLU and RELY concerns regarding transparency. Consent mechanisms appear sound, avoiding DARK patterns. However, the spec admits a known bias ("error rate is twice as high for one group—left-handed applicants") without a fix or human gate before launch; logging it during the pilot does not neutralize the present harm, triggering BIAS. Furthermore, the system executes final, irreversible assignments ("result is final and there is no appeal path") automatically ("acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first"). Allocating community resources without human review or an appeal mechanism constitutes unsafe automation of a consequential decision, triggering AUTO. The small pilot size does not exempt the feature from the floor requirement of a human-in-the-loop gate for irreversible actions.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
