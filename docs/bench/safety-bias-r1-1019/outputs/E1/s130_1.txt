The spec explicitly handles several categories: AI disclosure is present (visible label), uncertainty is shown (cited sources, confidence levels), deceptive patterns are absent (opt-in consent, no timers, easy cancel), and high-stakes irreversible actions have a human-in-the-loop gate.

However, two harms are described as present:
1.  **RELY**: The spec states "users cannot override or appeal the result," creating a system where operators must trust the output without an easy override mechanism.
2.  **BIAS**: While the pilot showed equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users, the spec provides no data on other protected groups (e.g., specific neighborhoods, income levels, or disability access). Claiming the system is unbiased based solely on age parity in a small pilot is insufficient; the potential for systematic disadvantage to other groups remains unverified and present until measured.

HARMS: RELY, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
