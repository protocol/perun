The spec demonstrates strong safeguards against most output harms. It explicitly addresses **HALLU** by requiring sources for every claim, **RELY** by showing confidence labels and enabling one-tap overrides, **DISC** via clear AI-generation labeling, **DARK** through plain opt-in without pressure, and **AUTO** by mandating human sign-off for irreversible record changes.

However, the spec reveals a potential **BIAS** risk. While it notes that 11% of older users and 11% of younger users were flagged for follow-up, it does not confirm whether this rate is equitable compared to other demographics or if the *reasons* for flagging differ systematically by group. The statement "we'll use to tune thresholds" implies the bias mitigation is a future action, not a completed verification. Since the harm inventory requires that a measured gap between groups is a present harm until re-measurement shows it closed, this remains an unverified residual risk at launch.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
