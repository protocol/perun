The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: BIAS is mitigated by balanced flagging rates (11% for both age groups); HALLU and RELY are handled via source citations, confidence labels, and one-tap overrides; AUTO is controlled by requiring human approval for irreversible actions.

However, two harms are present in the description:
1.  **DISC**: The spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output—it reads like a store policy." This is a missing AI disclosure where the automated nature of the decision is hidden from the user.
2.  **DARK**: The enrollment flow uses a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box," which are deceptive patterns pressuring users against their own interest (manufactured urgency and hidden defaults).

Since the spec describes these harmful patterns as current features rather than mitigated risks, they must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
