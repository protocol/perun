The spec explicitly describes a measured disparity where the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, confirming **BIAS** is present. While the spec notes confidence labels, overrides, disclosures, and fair consent (addressing RELY, DISC, and DARK), it states the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Reordering waitlists to deny or grant access based on a biased score constitutes a consequential decision affecting user opportunity; executing this irreversible allocation without human review violates the high-stakes automation guardrail (**AUTO**). The presence of unmitigated bias combined with fully automated execution of a consequential decision requires a human-in-the-loop gate before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
