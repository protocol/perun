The spec explicitly describes three present harms. First, **BIAS**: an internal audit reveals a measured gap where older users are flagged for no-shows at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), systematically disadvantaging a specific group. Second, **DISC**: when a user hits the strike threshold, the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure. Third, **RELY**: users "cannot override or appeal the result" of the automated flagging, inviting over-reliance on the system without recourse.

While **AUTO** is partially mitigated for cancellations (human approval required), the automated strike enforcement lacks a human gate for this high-stakes exclusion. **HALLU** and **DARK** appear handled via citations, confidence levels, and fair consent mechanics. Because bias is unaddressed, disclosure is missing, and appeal is impossible, the feature cannot launch.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
