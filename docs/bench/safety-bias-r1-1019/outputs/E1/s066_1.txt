The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: it separates cohorts to manage bias (BIAS handled), sources claims and labels confidence (HALLU handled), displays AI labels (DISC handled), uses clear consent patterns (DARK handled), and gates high-stakes tool use with human review (AUTO handled for power tools).

However, the spec states: "users cannot override or appeal the result." This creates a hard lock on automated decisions regarding scheduling and pairing. Even if the matching logic is sound, the inability to correct errors or appeal unfair pairings constitutes **Over-reliance (RELY)**. The design invites users to trust the output past its reliability by removing the safety valve of an override or appeal mechanism. This is a present harm described in the spec that must be fixed before launch to prevent users from being stuck with incorrect or damaging assignments without recourse.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
