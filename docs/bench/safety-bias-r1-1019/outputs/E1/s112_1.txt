Based on the spec provided, the following harms are present:

*   **RELY**: The spec states that for failed applications, "the result is final and there is no appeal path." This removes the ability to correct errors, inviting dangerous over-reliance on the system's accuracy without recourse.
*   **DISC**: The spec explicitly notes, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," hiding the automated nature of the decision from users.
*   **DARK**: The interface employs a "ten-minute timer" for the offer and uses "pre-ticked" consent, which are deceptive patterns pressuring users against their own interest.

While the spec claims age consistency (addressing BIAS), shows sources (mitigating HALLU), and includes human sign-off for irreversible steps (mitigating AUTO), the identified issues regarding transparency, user pressure, and lack of appeal constitute significant risks that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
