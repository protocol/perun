The spec reveals two present harms. First, **DISC**: the text explicitly states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to users. Second, **RELY**: while sources and confidence are shown, the spec notes "there is no appeal path" for final results. This lack of an override mechanism invites over-reliance and prevents correction of potential errors, constituting a design flaw regarding automation bias.

Other categories are handled: **AUTO** is mitigated because a person approves every irreversible action; **DARK** is addressed via opt-in consent without timers; **BIAS** shows equal flagging rates in the pilot; **HALLU** is partially addressed by cited sources, though the lack of appeal exacerbates the risk if citations are wrong. However, the missing disclosure and absent appeal path are sufficient to block launch.

HARMS: DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
