The spec reveals three present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted: the algorithm flags older and younger users for different groups at equal rates (11%), indicating a systematic divergence from self-selection based on age. While visible, this constitutes a measured gap that disadvantages specific age cohorts until proven fair.

Second, **DISC** is a confirmed failure. The spec states, "the app does not tell users the content was written by AI." Since captains rely on these machine-generated rationales to manage groups of up to 200 runners, the lack of disclosure prevents informed trust.

Third, **DARK** patterns are present in the captain's dashboard. The combination of a "countdown timer" (manufactured urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (hidden default) pressures users into agreeing to assignments against their own interest or without full deliberation.

Other categories appear handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by sourcing and confidence labels; **RELY** is addressed via one-tap overrides; **AUTO** is prevented by the human sign-off requirement for irreversible steps.

However, the presence of Bias, missing Disclosure, and Deceptive patterns creates unacceptable risk for a feature influencing race logistics and participant safety.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
