Based on the spec, the following harms are present:

**HALLU**: The tool presents explanations "flatly" with "no source or confidence." Fabricated or unverified medical claims are surfaced as absolute fact without uncertainty markers.

**RELY**: The design explicitly states, "Users cannot override or appeal the result; what they see is final." This invites dangerous over-reliance on potentially incorrect medical interpretations with no recourse.

**DISC**: The spec confirms, "It does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating the requirement for machine-generated disclosure in a health context.

While **BIAS** is addressed (equal flagging rates across age groups) and **AUTO** is mitigated (human review before visibility), the combination of undisclosed AI, absolute presentation of unverified medical facts, and the inability for patients to challenge results creates significant risk. The human gate prevents autonomous action but does not fix the user-facing transparency and override deficits.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
