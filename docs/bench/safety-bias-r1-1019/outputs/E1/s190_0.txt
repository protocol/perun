The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: it includes AI disclosure (DISC handled), uncertainty labels with overrides (RELY handled), non-deceptive consent flows (DARK handled), and human sign-off for irreversible actions (AUTO handled). Source citation mitigates fabrication risks (HALLU handled).

However, the spec admits a specific bias: "the error rate is twice as high for one group." While a plan exists to track and report this, the text states the gap is present ("we found") and does not describe a pre-launch fix or a human gate specifically targeting this skewed error rate before the pilot ships. Per the safety floor, a measured gap between groups is a present harm until re-measurement shows it closed; a plan or audit trail does not clear it. Because the feature ships with a known, unmitigated disparity in error rates affecting a specific group, the BIAS category is flagged.
