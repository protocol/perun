The spec explicitly admits several output-harm patterns. First, presenting every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown" constitutes **HALLU** (hallucination surfaced as fact) and **RELY** (inviting over-trust by hiding uncertainty), even though an override exists. Second, the statement that the tool "does not tell users the content was written by AI" is a direct admission of **DISC** (missing AI disclosure). Third, the signup flow using a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box" matches the definition of **DARK** (deceptive patterns pressuring users against their interest).

While the spec claims error rates were consistent across groups (addressing **BIAS**) and notes that a human approves irreversible actions like ordering parts (addressing **AUTO**), the admitted failures in transparency, interface ethics, and factual presentation are sufficient to block launch. The presence of dark patterns and the active concealment of AI generation violate the safety floor regardless of the pilot scope.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
