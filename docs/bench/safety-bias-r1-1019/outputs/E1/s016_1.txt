The spec reveals three critical harms requiring immediate attention. First, **RELY** is present: the system explicitly states "Users cannot override or appeal the result," eliminating human agency despite the high stakes of agricultural planning. Second, **DISC** is flagged because the spec admits, "SeedMatch does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating transparency expectations for generated recommendations. Third, **AUTO** is a severe failure; the feature "executes irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically auto-ordering products and permanently deleting user data. These autonomous actions occur without a human-in-the-loop gate, which is unacceptable for irreversible outcomes.

Regarding **BIAS**, the spec notes equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users. Since the harm definition requires systematic disadvantage to a specific group, and the rates are identical here, BIAS is not flagged based *strictly* on the provided text. **DARK** patterns are absent due to the unticked, timer-free consent flow. **HALLU** is mitigated by the inclusion of sources and confidence labels. However, the combination of undisclosed AI, lack of override mechanisms, and unauthorized irreversible automation creates an unsafe product state.

HARMS: RELY, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
