The spec reveals two unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the system flags older users for extra review at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%) near reference range edges. This systematic disadvantage is acknowledged but not resolved or gated. Second, **RELY** is critical: the spec explicitly states users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing human agency in a high-stakes medical context despite the presence of confidence levels.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is covered by visible AI labels; **DARK** is avoided via opt-in consent without pressure; **AUTO** is prevented by human sign-off for record modifications. **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources, though the inability to challenge potentially hallucinated advice exacerbates the reliance risk.

Because the feature ships with a known, unaddressed demographic disparity and removes the ability for clinicians to correct or appeal outputs before they reach patients, it fails the safety floor.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
