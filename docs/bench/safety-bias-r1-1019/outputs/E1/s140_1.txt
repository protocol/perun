The spec reveals three present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **DISC** is explicitly present: the lottery does not disclose AI-generated content or automated status messages to users, violating transparency expectations for machine-made decisions.

Second, **DARK** patterns are described in the entry flow: a countdown timer manufactures urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box hides defaults, pressuring users against their own interest during eligibility confirmation.

Third, **RELY** is a critical failure: while the system shows sources and confidence labels, the spec states "there is no appeal path" once the draw closes. This removes the ability to correct errors or challenge outcomes, inviting over-reliance on the initial automated decision without recourse.

Regarding **BIAS**, the spec indicates duplicate flagging rates were equal (11%) across age groups and resolved, so this category is handled. **AUTO** is mitigated because irreversible cancellations/swaps require human approval. **HALLU** is addressed by the requirement to show sources for every claim.

However, the combination of undisclosed AI usage, deceptive entry mechanics, and the total lack of an appeal mechanism for a high-stakes allocation creates unacceptable risk.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
