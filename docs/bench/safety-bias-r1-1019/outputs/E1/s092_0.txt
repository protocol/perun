The spec presents two clear output-safety failures. First, **DISC**: the specification explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output; it reads like a standard triage worksheet." Users cannot distinguish machine-generated rankings from human analysis, violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to the user.

Second, **RELY**: while irreversible *actions* (referrals, transport) have human sign-off, the core *ranking decision* itself is described as "final" with "no appeal path." The design invites over-trust by removing any mechanism for a nurse to override or question the urgency score, even though the system admits potential bias (stopping only if error rates double). The presence of cited sources and confidence levels does not negate the lack of an override for the final queue order.

Although **AUTO** is mitigated for irreversible steps via human sign-off, and **BIAS** has a measurement plan (though the threshold is reactive), the missing disclosure and the inability to appeal the primary output constitute unmanaged harms.

HARMS: DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
