The spec indicates two present harms. First, **HALLU** is flagged because the engine "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified inventory or pricing logic as absolute fact without indicating uncertainty. Second, **DISC** is explicitly present: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," and the copy obscures the automated nature by using only "available upgrade."

Other categories appear handled. **BIAS** is addressed by the pilot showing error rates "within one point across every group tested." **RELY** is mitigated because results can be "overridden or appealed in one tap." **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in consent, unticked defaults, no timers, and easy cancellation. **AUTO** is controlled since a "human signs off on each irreversible step."

However, the lack of AI disclosure and the presentation of outputs as flat facts without confidence indicators constitute unresolved risks before launch. Users cannot distinguish machine-generated suggestions from static inventory rules, and the absolute tone invites over-trust despite the override mechanism.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
