The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: claims show sources and confidence labels (mitigating HALLU and RELY), outputs carry visible AI labels (mitigating DISC), consent is opt-in with easy cancellation (mitigating DARK), and results are overrideable (mitigating RELY).

However, the spec admits a measurable disparity: the engine flagged 18% of older users for follow-up versus only 9% of younger users. This is a present instance of **BIAS** where a group is systematically disadvantaged by higher scrutiny rates. The spec notes this fact but offers no mitigation, threshold, or human review process to address the gap before launch.

Furthermore, the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since the kiosk physically releases tools from a locked cabinet based on these biased suggestions without human intervention, this constitutes **AUTO**. The combination of automated physical action and known demographic disparity creates an unacceptable risk profile for launch.

HARMS: BIAS, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
