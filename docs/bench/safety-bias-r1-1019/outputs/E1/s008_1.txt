The spec reveals several unaddressed output safety harms. First, **DISC** is present: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to the user. Second, **RELY** is triggered because "the result is final and there is no appeal path for a slot change," denying users an override for an automated decision. Third, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted: "the error rate is twice as high for one group—patients using older phones." While the team plans to review this weekly, a measured gap disadvantaging a specific group constitutes a present harm until closed; logging it does not mitigate the immediate disparity at launch.

Other categories appear handled. **AUTO** is mitigated by the "human approves every irreversible action" gate. **DARK** patterns are avoided via opt-in consent without timers. **HALLU** is addressed by showing sources and confidence labels. However, the combination of undisclosed AI generation, lack of appeal for decisions, and confirmed disparate error rates creates unacceptable residual risk for the pilot.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
