The spec explicitly identifies several unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the error rate is "twice as high for one group," and while tuning is planned, the disparity exists in the current system described. Second, **RELY** is a critical failure: the spec states "users cannot override or appeal the result," removing human agency despite the high stakes. Third, **AUTO** is triggered: the tool "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" by directly advancing or cutting candidates, violating the requirement for a human-in-the-loop on high-stakes decisions.

While **DISC** is handled (visible AI label) and **DARK** appears mitigated (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of known bias, lack of override, and fully automated irreversible hiring decisions creates unacceptable risk. The plan to tune the model before launch does not resolve the architectural absence of a human gate or appeal mechanism in the shipped spec.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
