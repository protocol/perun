The spec explicitly addresses the defined harm categories with mitigations rather than describing them as present failures. Bias (BIAS) is handled via tested error rates across groups. Hallucination (HALLU) is mitigated by cited sources and confidence levels. Over-reliance (RELY) is countered by visible uncertainty, one-tap overrides, and appeals. Disclosure (DISC) is satisfied by visible "AI-generated" labels. Deceptive patterns (DARK) are absent due to opt-in, unticked consent, no timers, and easy cancellation. Unsafe automation (AUTO) is prevented by requiring human approval for all irreversible actions like reservations.

Since the spec describes controls for every category rather than unmitigated harms, no categories are flagged. The feature includes a harm inventory, measurement data, human gates for high-stakes actions, and transparency artifacts as required.
