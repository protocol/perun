The spec explicitly handles several harm categories: sources and confidence labels address HALLU and RELY; AI-generated labeling addresses DISC; human sign-off on irreversible steps addresses AUTO.

However, two harms are present:
1.  **BIAS**: The spec admits the error rate is "twice as high" for homes with flat roofs. This is a documented systematic disadvantage for a specific group that remains uncorrected at launch ("adjust... before full rollout").
2.  **DARK**: The interface uses a "ten-minute timer" to manufacture urgency and "pre-ticked" consent for emails, pressuring users against their own interest.

Since the bias is acknowledged but not fixed, and dark patterns are active features, the launch cannot proceed without addressing these specific risks.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
