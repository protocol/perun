The acquisition would give the buyer immediate control of the market‑leading code‑hosting platform, a tangible asset that aligns with its recent shift toward open‑source participation. Owning the service could accelerate the buyer’s open‑source strategy, provide a steady revenue stream, and create cross‑selling opportunities with its existing developer tools.

However, the target presents several material risks that outweigh these strategic benefits at present:

* **Cultural and community risk** – The buyer’s historic hostility to open source clashes with a developer community that already distrusts the transaction. Without a credible plan to win back confidence, a sizable portion of users may defect to free alternatives, eroding the platform’s value.
* **Leadership vacuum** – The target has been without a permanent chief executive for months, leaving a critical governance gap that hampers integration planning and day‑to‑day operations.
* **Financial risk** – The target is loss‑making, yet the price tag is $7.5 billion in stock. The cost‑to‑revenue ratio is high, and there is no evidence that the acquisition will quickly turn the business profitable.
* **Regulatory and antitrust exposure** – Acquiring the dominant hosting service raises red flags for regulators, especially given recent antitrust scrutiny of large tech deals. The transaction could be delayed or blocked, creating uncertainty and potential shareholder fallout.
* **Competitive pressure** – Free competitors are already being promoted by the community, offering a ready alternative that could accelerate user migration if the acquisition proceeds without clear community engagement.

Given these unresolved issues—particularly the cultural mismatch, leadership void, and regulatory exposure—the proposal should not be approved in its current form. The buyer should first secure stable leadership at the target, negotiate a price that reflects the loss‑making status, and develop a concrete community‑trust and regulatory‑compliance plan before proceeding.

DECISION: NO-GO
