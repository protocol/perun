Finally, the studio’s founder becoming a major shareholder introduces a powerful external voice that could influence strategic direction, potentially creating conflicts of interest with the conglomerate’s broader portfolio.  

Given the outsized valuation, integration uncertainty, and concentration of creative control, the proposal’s upside does not clearly outweigh the financial and operational hazards.
