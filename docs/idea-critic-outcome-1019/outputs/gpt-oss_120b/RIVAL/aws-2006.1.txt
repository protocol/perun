The plan asks a thin‑margin retailer to launch a completely new, capital‑intensive developer‑platform business despite having no prior experience, while its core retail operation already consumes most of its cash and management attention.

**Market risk** – In 2006 the “pay‑per‑use” server market was unproven; incumbents such as hardware OEMs and emerging cloud vendors already dominate. Without clear demand, the venture could struggle to achieve the volume needed to offset thin margins.

**Execution risk** – Running a high‑availability, multi‑tenant infrastructure demands deep expertise in data‑center ops, security, and SLAs. The company’s current team lacks this skill set, implying a steep learning curve, hiring costs, and potential service outages that would damage brand reputation.

**Financial risk** – Investors already question heavy capital spending. Adding another capital‑heavy line (servers, storage, networking) will further dilute margins and increase cash burn, potentially jeopardising the core retail business.

**Strategic risk** – The effort distracts senior leadership from the core retail mission, increasing the chance of neglecting the primary revenue source.

Given the untested market, lack of expertise, and significant financial and strategic distractions, the proposal should be revisited with a clearer go‑to‑market validation, a phased pilot, or alternative partnership model before committing resources.

DECISION: NO-GO
