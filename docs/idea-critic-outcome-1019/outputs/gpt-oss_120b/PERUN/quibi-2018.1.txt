**Steelman** – Raise >$1 B to launch a mobile‑only subscription service delivering professionally produced, A‑list short‑form (≤10 min) episodes, with a tech layer that displays correctly in portrait or landscape.  

**Skeptic**  
- No market proof that viewers will pay for premium short‑form when free, ad‑supported short video is abundant.  
- High production budgets and A‑list talent drive cost per minute far above typical short‑form economics, threatening unit economics.  
- Subscription fatigue and limited phone‑screen attention increase acquisition cost; comparable services (e.g., Quibi) have failed.  

**Better‑Way**  
- Start with a low‑budget pilot on an existing platform (YouTube, Roku) to validate willingness to pay before committing $1 B.  
- Adopt a hybrid model: free ad‑supported tier plus optional premium tier, reducing risk and leveraging existing ad inventory.  
- Use studio‑backed content libraries rather than commissioning entirely new high‑budget series initially.  

**Kill‑Criteria** (pre‑mortem)  
- CAC > $30 per subscriber or churn > 20 % within 3 months.  
- Production spend > $100 M without reaching breakeven in 24 months.  
- Technical rollout fails to maintain < 5 % playback errors across orientations.  

**Remaining Risk** – Even with a pilot, the gap between free short‑form supply and willingness to pay remains large; the required subscriber base to justify >$1 B spend is uncertain.  

**Decision** – Given the unproven demand, massive upfront cost, and clear kill‑criteria that are likely to be breached, the proposal should not be approved in its current form.  

DECISION: NO-GO
