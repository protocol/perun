**Steelman claim** – Acquiring the video‑sharing site gives the buyer the largest online video audience, a flood of user‑generated content, and a platform that could be monetised through advertising, providing a strategic edge over competitors.

**Skeptic assessment**  
- **Price**: $1.65 B for a company with *no meaningful revenue* and a *tiny staff* is hard to justify; there is no disclosed valuation basis.  
- **Litigation exposure**: The site hosts massive amounts of copyrighted TV and film clips, and media companies have already threatened lawsuits. Potential damages and injunctions could far exceed the purchase price.  
- **Bandwidth cost**: “Very high” bills are noted; scaling the buyer’s existing infrastructure to the site’s traffic could erode any future ad revenue.  
- **Unproven ad model**: No revenue stream is demonstrated, and the buyer’s own smaller video service has not validated a sustainable advertising business.

**Better‑way alternatives**  
1. **Do nothing** – retain the buyer’s existing video service and avoid exposure.  
2. **Strategic partnership/licensing** – share revenue with the site while limiting liability.  
3. **Acquire a smaller, cleaner platform** – lower price, fewer legal risks, and easier integration.  
4. **Negotiate a lower, earn‑out‑based price** – tie payment to future ad revenue or litigation‑free milestones.

**Kill‑criteria** (must be met to proceed)  
- Projected litigation costs > $200 M or a credible risk of an injunction that would cripple the service.  
- Expected ad revenue within 24 months < $150 M (i.e., price > 10× projected cash flow).  
- Bandwidth expenses projected to exceed 30 % of any ad‑generated revenue.  
- No agreement to reduce the purchase price or include escrow/earn‑out protections.

**Dissent ledger** – The proposal fails on price justification, litigation risk, and bandwidth cost without mitigation; better, lower‑risk paths exist.

**Remaining risk** – Even if price is reduced, the legal exposure and untested ad model remain significant obstacles.

Given the lack of revenue, high legal exposure, and absence of risk‑mitigation mechanisms, the acquisition is not advisable in its current form.

DECISION: NO-GO
