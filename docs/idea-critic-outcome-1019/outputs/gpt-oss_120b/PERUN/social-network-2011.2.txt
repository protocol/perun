verdict: REVISE  
independence: independent  
origin: agent-originated  
claim: Launch an integrated social network across search, mail, video, and photo products with an invite‑only beta, aiming to compete with the market leader.  
steelman: By embedding the new social layer into existing high‑traffic products, the company will quickly reach a critical mass of users, achieve adoption ≥10 % of the incumbent within 12 months, and generate net positive revenue that outweighs development costs and executive bonus payouts.  
hats_run: skeptic, better-way, kill-criteria  

assumptions:  
- Existing users will welcome forced integration of social features.  
- Friend‑grouping circles and video hangouts provide a unique value proposition strong enough to overcome incumbent network effects.  
- Bonus incentives will align employee effort without inflating costs.  

better_ways:  
1. Enhance core search/mail/video/photo experiences without social features, preserving user satisfaction and focusing on proven revenue drivers.  
2. Partner or integrate with an existing niche social platform to gain network effects without building from scratch.  
3. Acquire a smaller, specialized social startup to inherit its community and technology, reducing development risk.  

kill_criteria:  
- Adoption <5 % of incumbent’s active users after 12 months.  
- ≥3 % drop in core product usage (e.g., mail sends) attributable to the new social layer within the beta period.  
- Bonus payouts exceed projected incremental revenue by >20 %.  
- Negative NPS (<20) from beta participants indicating annoyance with forced integration.  

strongest_attack_survived: The claim that “friend‑grouping circles and video hangouts” will differentiate enough was tested by comparing publicly available feature sets of the incumbent; the analysis shows no substantive gap, so the differentiation argument fails.  

questions_parent_must_resolve:  
- What concrete metrics define “critical mass” and how will they be measured?  
- How will the impact of forced integration on existing product usage be quantified before full rollout?  
- What contingency plan exists if adoption targets are not met?  

user_question: NONE  

dissent_ledger: The proposal underestimates the power of incumbent network effects and the risk of alienating existing users through forced integration; alternative strategies with lower risk and clearer ROI exist.  

remaining_risk: High – network‑effect lock‑in, user churn from forced changes, and misaligned incentive structure.
