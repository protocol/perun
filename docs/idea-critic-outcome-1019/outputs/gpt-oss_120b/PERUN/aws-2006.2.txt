**Steelman** – “The retailer should launch a pay‑per‑use server and object‑storage rental service for external developers, creating a distinct, revenue‑generating business line.”

**Skeptic**  
1. **Unproven market (2006)** – No comparable demand data; early‑cloud adopters were still niche.  
2. **Zero operating experience** – No team has run a developer platform at scale; hiring/training costs are hidden.  
3. **Capital intensity** – Existing “spiky” infrastructure already strains cash; adding a new capital‑heavy line threatens investor confidence.  
4. **Margin pressure** – Retail margins are thin; cloud services typically run at lower gross margins, risking overall profitability.  
5. **Reliability expectations** – External developers will demand SLA‑grade uptime, far higher than internal retail ops can currently guarantee.  
6. **Strong incumbents** – Large hardware/software vendors already dominate the nascent market, creating a high barrier to entry.

**Better‑Way Alternatives**  
- **Internal cost‑recovery pilot** – Offer the service first to internal business units to validate demand and build expertise.  
- **Strategic partnership** – Resell capacity from an established cloud provider while learning the market without heavy CAPEX.  
- **Limited‑scope launch** – Start with object storage only, a lower‑complexity product, before adding compute.  
- **Asset‑light model** – Lease excess capacity from third‑party data centers rather than expanding own build‑out.

**Kill‑Criteria (must be met to proceed)**  
- Projected ROI < 5 % within two years.  
- Gross margin on the service < 10 % after 12 months.  
- Required additional CAPEX > $50 M.  
- Early‑stage churn > 20 % in the first six months.  
- Measurable dip in core retail EBITDA > 2 % attributable to resource diversion.

**Pre‑mortem** – If SLAs are missed, brand reputation suffers; capital burn accelerates; investors may demand cut‑backs, jeopardizing the core retail business.

Given the high capital risk, lack of market validation, and stronger, lower‑risk alternatives, the proposal should not be approved in its current form.

DECISION: NO-GO
