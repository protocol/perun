**Steelman:** A curated, single-store model with a 30% fee balances developer monetization and user trust, turning the CEO's previous "web-only" stance into a controlled evolution that prevents fragmentation while capturing ecosystem value.

**Attack & Alternatives:**
The primary failure mode is stagnation: if review bottlenecks delay updates or reject innovative apps that threaten carrier revenue, the platform loses momentum against open rivals. However, open distribution in 2008 invites malware and instability, which would destroy the brand's "polish" differentiator before network effects take hold. The "do nothing" (web-only) alternative fails to leverage device hardware, ceding high-value use cases to competitors. A multi-store approach fragments the user base and complicates security enforcement.

**Fit & Pre-mortem:**
This fits a strategy prioritizing user experience over immediate maximal growth. If we assume the review team scales poorly, the store becomes a choke point. Yet, the 30% fee funds the necessary curation infrastructure. The risk of carrier objection is real but manageable through revenue sharing or pre-installation deals, whereas unvetted apps causing network congestion would guarantee carrier backlash.

**Evidence & Kill Criteria:**
Given the 2008 context, users prioritize reliability over app quantity. The proposal's central gate (review) directly addresses the stated security/stability concerns. The 30% cut is standard for providing distribution and payment processing.

**Decision:**
The proposal converts a defensive posture (web-only) into an offensive moat (curated native apps) without sacrificing the core value proposition of stability. The risks of open distribution outweigh the benefits of speed at this specific maturity stage. The review workload is an operational hurdle, not a strategic flaw, and the single-store model ensures a unified quality bar that rivals cannot easily match.

**Leading Metric:** Monitor app approval turnaround time; if it exceeds 7 days consistently, the curation model is failing.

DECISION: GO
