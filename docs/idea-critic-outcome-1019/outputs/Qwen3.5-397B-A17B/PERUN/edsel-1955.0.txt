**Steelman:** Capturing the growing mid-priced segment with a distinct brand prevents customer defection to competitors and maximizes market share across all price points.

**Attack:** The proposal relies on linear extrapolation of current growth, ignoring the high probability of a cyclical downturn. Launching a capital-intensive venture requiring new dealer infrastructure exactly when consumer credit tightens and discretionary spending collapses is catastrophic timing. The "perceived gap" may vanish during a recession as buyers trade down to economy cars or delay purchases entirely, leaving the new division with high fixed costs and no volume. Furthermore, internal cannibalization is likely if the economic squeeze forces the company to discount the new brand, eroding the premium line's value without securing sufficient new volume. The massive upfront spend on advertising and dealer networks creates a high burn rate before revenue stabilizes, exacerbating liquidity risks during a downturn.

**Alternatives:** 
1. **Do Nothing:** Preserve capital and avoid fixed-cost expansion during uncertain times.
2. **Badge Engineering:** Introduce mid-priced models under existing brands/dealers to test waters without infrastructure costs.
3. **Delay:** Wait for economic clarity before committing to a standalone network.

**Fit:** While the strategic vision of a full spectrum portfolio is sound, the execution timing contradicts basic economic cycle management. The cost of delay is low compared to the cost of failure during a recession.

**Pre-mortem:** In 1958, the division is shuttered after losing hundreds of millions because dealers refused to stock unsellable cars during a recession, and the brand was perceived as overpriced relative to truncated household budgets.

**Decision:** The risk of launching a high-overhead division immediately prior to a potential recession outweighs the opportunity of current segment growth. The proposal fails to account for macro-economic volatility or offer a low-capital entry strategy.

DECISION: NO-GO
