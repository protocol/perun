The steelman: a single controlled store balances developer reach with the device's need for security, stability, and polish, converting the CEO's previous caution into a quality guarantee rather than a blockade.

Alternatives considered: (1) Do nothing and rely on web apps, risking inferior performance and developer abandonment to open rivals; (2) Allow open sideloading like competitors, inviting immediate security breaches and carrier backlash; (3) Delay launch until a perfect solution exists, ceding the entire first-mover window. The proposed model beats these by offering a viable middle path: native performance with centralized trust.

Fit check: This moves the north star from "phone as appliance" to "phone as platform," essential for long-term survival. Evidence is historical fact: the device lacked a SDK, carriers held veto power, and rivals had fragmented distribution.
