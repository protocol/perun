### Fixed
- The stop-starting hook counted ready PRs on the newest 100 open ones only, so with a longer queue the older ready PRs fell outside the count and a gated `gh pr create` could pass while the real queue was at or over the cap. When the first read holds 100 PRs and is under the cap, the hook now reads again with `--limit 500` within what is left of its budget (skipped when under 2 seconds are left) and uses that list and count for the deny, the throughput judgement and the denial record. With the per-lane cap set, that second read also lists each PR's files and the per-lane recount uses it instead of listing a third time, so a slow queue no longer spends the budget before the lane is counted. A failed or slow second read keeps the first read's count and prints one stderr line saying so: the same verdict as the first read alone, at most the rest of the budget later. `flow_alarm.py symptom` counts on a `--limit 500` read too (a failed read exits 2, as documented), and the per-lane compare now takes its timeout after its base lookup and is skipped when under 2 seconds are left. A repo with more than 500 open PRs is still counted on the newest 500.
