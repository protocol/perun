### Added
- `/critic`, `/council` and `/idea` slash commands. `/critic` runs the idea-critic on a claim or artifact; `/council` asks for stakes, reversibility and criteria in plain words, then runs `veche.py`; `/idea` asks one question per turn, offers 2-3 directions, and saves a one-page brief (problem, direction, assumptions with a check each, MVP scope, Not-Doing list, open questions) to `docs/ideas/` only after you confirm. The brief shape is `agentic-delivery/references/template-onepager.md`, routed from the G0 row. `install.sh` ships `/critic` and `/council` with `--with-critic`, and `/idea` when both idea-critic and delivery are installed.
