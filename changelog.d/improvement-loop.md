### Added
- `agentic-ceo` now owns a product improvement loop for any project: "what are the next most impactful items?" Read `references/improvement-loop.md` (routed from `SKILL.md`). It starts with a strategy context (stage from the existing `deep-code-review` STAGE model, vision, north star, direction, constraints; a missing field is marked `assumed` and costs the owner one batched question), gathers evidence (issues and PRs, changelog, feedback files, support threads, analytics if present, session logs and token reports, rival changelogs fetched live), right-sizes between direct analysis and read-only C-suite role lanes (CPO, CTO, CFO, CMO, CISO, COO, each with a short brief and one fixed return schema, cheap models for gathering), scores with a printed formula, attacks the top 3 with `idea-critic`, has the CTO role write short ADRs, and renders strategy context, rejected items, ranked roadmap, ADRs and tracker-ready issues in that order.
- `agentic-ceo/scripts/roadmap_rank.py`: stdlib scorer for the loop. Score is reach x impact x confidence / effort x stage weight x vision factor; every input is sourced or `assumed`, confidence is capped at 0.5 while reach or impact is assumed, a vision-misaligned item is rejected with its reason, `--strict` requires an `idea-critic` verdict on the top 3, `--update` rewrites a marked block in a doc. Reads JSON, or a CSV plus `--context`. Tests: `scripts/test_roadmap_rank.py` (32 checks, wired into CI).
- `docs/roadmap.md` now opens with the Perun product roadmap built through this loop from `docs/roadmap-input.json` (the 2026-10-16 peer survey, `docs/perun-vs-rivals.md`, open issues, the changelog and live rival changelogs): 13 ranked items, 6 rejected with reasons, 7 marked in flight, 4 ADRs and tracker-ready issues. Every reach input is assumed (the survey has no counts), so the order is stated as a hypothesis; a test fails if the page and its input drift. The 2026-09-11 suite design record follows unchanged.
