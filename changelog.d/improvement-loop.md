### Added
- `agentic-ceo` now owns a product improvement loop for any project: "what are the next most impactful items?" Read `references/improvement-loop.md` (routed from `SKILL.md`). It starts with a strategy context (stage from the existing `deep-code-review` STAGE model, vision, north star, direction, constraints; a missing field is marked `assumed` and costs the owner one batched question), gathers evidence (issues and PRs, changelog, feedback files, support threads, analytics if present, session logs and token reports, rival changelogs fetched live), right-sizes between direct analysis and read-only C-suite role lanes (CPO, CTO, CFO, CMO, CISO, COO, each with a short brief and one fixed return schema, cheap models for gathering), scores with a printed formula, attacks the top 3 open items with `idea-critic`, has the CTO role write short ADRs, and renders strategy context, rejected items, ranked roadmap, ADRs and tracker-ready issues in that order.
- `agentic-ceo/scripts/roadmap_rank.py`: stdlib scorer for the loop. Score is reach x impact x confidence / effort x stage weight x vision factor. Confidence is computed, never typed: 1.0 when reach and impact both cite a real locator (URL, existing repo path, issue or PR), else 0.5, so "estimate:" or "~assumed" cannot bypass it. Effort includes +2 lane-days per owner or external-send gate and +0.25 per calendar-wait day. Items already in flight are scored and flagged, not dropped. Equal scores rank fewer assumed inputs first. A vision-misaligned item is rejected with its reason, `--strict` requires an `idea-critic` verdict on the top 3 open items, `--update` rewrites a marked block in a doc. Reads JSON, or a CSV plus `--context`. Tests: `scripts/test_roadmap_rank.py` (41 checks, wired into CI).
- `docs/roadmap.md` now opens with the Perun product roadmap built through this loop from `docs/roadmap-input.json` (the 2026-10-16 peer survey and field measurements committed as `docs/peer-survey-2026-10-16.md`, `docs/perun-vs-rivals.md`, open issues, the changelog and live rival changelogs): 22 scored items (15 open, 7 in flight), 6 rejected with reasons, 4 ADRs and tracker-ready issues for the open items. Field-proven items are included with sourced inputs (delivery dedup, the autonomy advisor); inputs without a source are listed as assumed. A test fails if the page and its input drift. The 2026-09-11 suite design record follows unchanged.
