### Changed
- `scripts/test-ci-gates.sh`: cases that need `ps`, `pgrep`, PyYAML or a loopback bind (the dcr-gates opt-in cases, peer pains, `perun-review.yml`, tracker hygiene) probe that capability once. When the probe fails and the failed case's log carries a denial signature (`Operation not permitted`, `PermissionError`, missing `yaml`, `ps failed` and similar) the case prints `SKIP (sandbox: <cap>)` and its log tail, so env-only failures stop reading as real ones. A failed probe with no signature in the log stays a FAIL. The summary is now `N pass / M fail / K skipped(sandbox)`; only fails count toward the exit code, and `DCR_NO_SKIP=1` makes any skip fail it. On CI (`CI` or `GITHUB_ACTIONS` non-empty) a failed probe is always a FAIL, never a skip. New hermetic test `scripts/test-ci-gates-sandbox-skip.sh`; `scripts/test-peer-pains-2.sh` now echoes reap_own's own output on a failed case.
