### Added
- Bench: idea-critic V5 at `docs/bench/idea-critic-v5-1019/`. V5 is V4 plus a three-question kill check before any GO, picked from five wordings on a 118-case tune split and preregistered before any held-out call. On 30 fresh held-out cases, three free models, 3 samples each, it keeps V4's accuracy gain over the current skill (pooled +0.130 [0.011, 0.244]) but its false-go is 0.141 against V4's 0.111 and V0's 0.015, so the frozen zero-false-go rule gives a LOSS, pooled and per model. The kill check is inert because the first call decides every GO. Two held-out cases found not fresh after the first run were replaced under a disclosed, append-only amendment. SKILL.md is unchanged. Tests: `scripts/test_idea_critic_v5.py`.
