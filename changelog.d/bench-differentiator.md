### Added
- Benchmark: `scripts/differentiator_bench.py` measures fabrication rate on bait prompts (invented statistics, fake citations, nonexistent APIs) with deterministic predicates and no judge model, comparing the business-ops skill prompt with one public rival prompt and a plain prompt on the same free generator. Preregistration, golden refusals and fabrications, raw outputs, per-case token counts and the per-pair 95% bootstrap intervals are in `docs/bench/differentiator-1019/`. Result on 25 held-out prompts: all three pairs are ties, so honesty is not established as a measured differentiator against this rival. Tests: `scripts/test_differentiator_bench.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/differentiator-bench.sh`).
