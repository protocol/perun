### Changed
- Operating layer: `outcome_claim_guard.py` Stop check flags fewer honest replies, with laundering resistance first. A claim word is skipped only when an action idiom is bound directly to it: a deliverable as the subject of "done" ("the audit is done", "Done: the handoff note"), "fixed one error in the report", "resolved the merge conflicts" with no delivery word after them, "6 issues already fixed" in a sentence that closes or dedups, and a "pre-merge" check. A deliverable, conflict or issue elsewhere in the sentence does not launder a claim, hyphenated names such as "auto-merge" still count as subjects, and merged tallies are never skipped. On 438 replayed coordinator replies the flags fall 34 to 29 and every flag that was an unmeasured outcome claim is still flagged. Default mode stays `warn`. Tests: `scripts/test_outcome_claim_guard.py` (run in CI), including 26 must-flag laundering cases.
