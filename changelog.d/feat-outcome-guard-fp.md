### Changed
- Operating layer: `outcome_claim_guard.py` Stop check flags fewer honest replies. A claim word no longer counts when an action idiom sits right next to it: a finished deliverable as the subject of "done" ("the audit is done", "Done: the handoff note"), a fixed typo or error, a resolved git conflict, "already fixed" or closed-issue triage, or a "pre-merge" check; and a merged tally is skipped when it names a set ("the 40 PRs merged since Monday") or sits beside a backlog count ("12 merged, 30 still open") without a progress word. Each rule skips one claim, never the whole message, and a deliverable, conflict or issue elsewhere in the sentence does not launder a claim. On 438 replayed coordinator replies the flags fall 34 to 24; every flag that was an unmeasured outcome claim is still flagged. Default mode stays `warn`. Tests: `scripts/test_outcome_claim_guard.py` (run in CI).
