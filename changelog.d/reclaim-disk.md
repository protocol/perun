### Added
- `.claude/skills/agentic-delivery/scripts/reclaim_disk.sh` lists (dry run by default) linked worktrees that are unlocked, have no modified, untracked or ignored files, have a HEAD reachable from origin/main or another branch, and whose last reflog move is older than `RECLAIM_MIN_AGE_HOURS` (default 24), plus local branches merged into that base. `--apply` runs `git worktree remove` (never `--force`) and `git branch -D` on exactly those items.
