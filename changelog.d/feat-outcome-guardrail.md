### Changed
- Pipeline optimizer: `pipeline_profile.py compare` no longer KEEPs a stage proxy that got faster while delivery got slower. The default metric is now `lead.first_push_to_merge_s`, and any KEEP also requires `lead.first_push_to_merge_s` not to rise and the new `lead.merged_per_day` (merged PRs per calendar day, from `profile`) not to fall by more than `--min-gain` (5% by default, documented in `compare --help` as the noise band: a smaller rise or fall is not called a regression). `merged_per_day` counts only the merged PRs the lead metrics count. A regression is REVERT ("stage improved but delivery outcome regressed"); missing outcome data on either side is UNVERIFIED ("outcome not measured") unless `--stage-only` is passed, which KEEPs labelled stage-only. Tests: `scripts/test_pipeline_profile_outcome_guardrail.py` plus the selftest (run in CI).
