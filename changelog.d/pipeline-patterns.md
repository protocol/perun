### Added
- `merge-queue-worktrees.md` gains the section "Around the heavy slot: cheap checks before the wait, a settled base after the grant" next to "A heavy slot guards the contended stage, not the whole train", which is unchanged. Three rules: run the cheap, fast-failing preconditions (mergeability, receipts present, base fresh, lint) before queueing for a heavy slot; compose the tested union after the grant, once the previous holder's landing has settled (landed or abandoned) and with a wait bounded to one landing; end the stage early when the base moves on a path the union touches (this decides only when to abort, and landing still needs the proven base). The slot stays scoped to the contended stage, widened only by that wait and the compose. A closing paragraph says what the shipped scripts do (`train_land.sh` waits out a landing train's push lock once, at train start; `land_train.sh` only warns on a moved base for a non-split train) and what is doctrine only (recompose after the grant, a mid-stage head compare).
- `pipeline-optimizer.md` gains two bullets under "Gate cost, feeder order and ready-PR age": a rerun of only the failing tests merges into the original run's receipt (full count, each failure replaced by its rerun result, both runs' raw count strings kept beside it, same tree only) and never replaces it, because a smaller receipt makes the next full-count gate force a second full run; and a pre-merge check pool at 0 workers fails silently, so alarm on worker count 0 while ready work waits (no shipped script reads a pool's worker count, and `landing_watchdog.py` is the natural home).
- Test: five eval cases in `.claude/skills/agentic-delivery/evals/evals.json` covering the five rules: `rerun-receipt-merged-not-replaced` and `rerun-receipt-after-fix-commit-needs-full-run` (the rerun rule, on the same tree and on a different tree), `stale-union-built-before-previous-landing-settled` (compose after the grant, with the early end as one expectation), `zero-worker-check-pool-alarm` and `cheap-preconditions-before-heavy-slot`. The rules are doctrine plus evals, not new scripts: a moved base only draws a WARN at land time for non-split trains, and nothing checks it mid-stage.
