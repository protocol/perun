### Added
- Agentic-CEO: `token_report.py` now reports 5-hour usage windows (`burn_blocks` in `--json`, one summary line in the text report): weighted tokens, burn per minute, lane share and most lanes open at once per window, the peak window's multiple of the median of the others, and, for the peak window, a named driver with a concrete lever (main thread over the auto-compact window: `/compact` at a task boundary or `/clear` and resume from a handoff note; and, only when that window is at least 2x the median of the others, one lane at or above 40%: split or trim that lane; no spender at 40% with 3 or more lanes open at once: dispatch fewer lanes per wave). The window is modelled on the public `ccusage` blocks report and the Claude-Code-Usage-Monitor session model; it reads only timestamps, usage fields and lane labels, and is a pace signal, not the account's real quota window. Tests: `scripts/test_token_burn_blocks.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/token-burn-blocks.sh`).
