### Added
- Three owner rules are now default-on hooks in the operating layer. `reply_cap.py` (Stop and UserPromptSubmit) tells the agent every turn to reply in at most 3 lines and 500 characters, blocks an over-cap reply once per turn and appends a dated line to a counter log (`~/.cache/perun/reply-cap.log`). `decision_gate.py` (PreToolUse on `AskUserQuestion|ExitPlanMode`) fails closed unless a decision record with `evil_twin:` and `council:` verdict lines (HOLD, REVISE or PASS_TO_USER) is under 30 minutes old; on Stop it also blocks a final reply that asks the owner to decide in plain chat, once per turn, until a record exists; it checks the record exists, not its quality, and never gates Bash, Edit or merges. `handback_cap.py` now logs `narr_rate` per lane and sends a `systemMessage` when a lane wrote at least 100 estimated tokens of prose between tool calls (detect and measure, not prevention).
- `./install.sh --global-rules` copies those scripts to `~/.claude/perun-rules/` and idempotently merges their hooks into `~/.claude/settings.json` (backup first, existing hooks kept, needs `jq`). See `docs/owner-rules.md`.
