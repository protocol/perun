### Fixed
- `review_checks.sh` says its scanners run offline, but three of them phoned home. Run under a network-denying sandbox with their exact argv on a one-file fixture, trivy 0.76.1 `fs` contacted `check.trivy.dev` (telemetry, even with `--offline-scan`), grype 0.121.0 contacted `toolbox-data.anchore.io` (its own-version check, even with `GRYPE_DB_AUTO_UPDATE=false`) and checkov 3.3.30 contacted `pypi.org` (its own-version check, even with `--skip-download`). Each is now switched off by an environment variable and not a flag, so an older tool ignores it instead of crashing on an unknown flag: `TRIVY_DISABLE_TELEMETRY=true` (set once for the trivy block, so the secret-only rerun carries it too), `GRYPE_CHECK_FOR_APP_UPDATE=false` and `CKV_SKIP_PACKAGE_UPDATE_CHECK=true`. With the variables set, the three runs contact no host.
- The other scanners were probed the same way and contacted no host: zizmor, actionlint, bandit, hadolint, gosec, gitleaks, trufflehog, osv-scanner on a warm offline DB, and semgrep with the bundled rules plus a warm `p/default` snapshot. semgrep with the registry pack contacts `semgrep.dev` by design (a warm snapshot or `REVIEW_SEMGREP_REGISTRY=0` avoids it); that was already documented and is unchanged.
