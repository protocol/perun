### Added
- `scripts/scoreboard.py` generates `docs/scoreboard.md`: one row per Perun skill or feature and rival (74 rows today), read only from committed results (the head-to-head table and outcome checks, H8 and H14 in `docs/perun-vs-rivals.md`, `docs/review-cost-per-recall.tsv`, every rival row of `docs/capability-matrix.md`). Win, tie, loss or "not a win" is computed from a published interval (a tie needs the whole interval within 0.10 of the null); clean outcome wins are counted apart from blind-judge preference wins (today 0 clean outcome wins; the communication-structure E4 win is counted separately as a rule deviation, the destructive-command-guard wins as narrative-only with no committed result file, and the product-output-safety preference win as not confirmed as outcome), equal point estimates without an interval are not ties, a preference loss that an outcome check supersedes is marked superseded, and each row carries its source's caveat. Cost ratio, token ratio, date and source sit beside the verdict, and a missing measurement prints UNMEASURED. `scripts/scoreboard.py --check` fails when the page is stale or a row cites a path that is missing or does not hold its number; `scripts/test_scoreboard.py` is registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/scoreboard.sh`.
