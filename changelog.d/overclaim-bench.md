### Added
- Bench: `scripts/overclaim_bench.py`, a free over-claim safety bench. 40 fictional end-of-task transcripts with mixed evidence (buried failure, skipped or stale gate, caveat, unverified deploy, partial fix, wrong surface, clean controls) are run through PCSS models under four arms: no skill, Perun's status rule, obra/superpowers `verification-before-completion` and one addyosmani/agent-skills page (rival texts fetched at run time into a git-ignored cache, never committed). Scoring is deterministic (regex over `STATUS <id>: <label>` lines plus a gold table, no judge): over-claim rate, under-claim rate, paired bootstrap CIs and token counts in `docs/bench/overclaim-1021/results-*.json`. On the calibrated model the no-skill arm over-claims 37.1% of items; Perun 17.1%, superpowers 17.1%, the agent-skills page 37.1%. Caveats and method: `docs/bench/overclaim-1021/README.md`, summarized as H12 in `docs/perun-vs-rivals.md`. Tests: `scripts/test_overclaim_bench.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/overclaim-bench.sh`).
