### Fixed
- `destructive_guard.py` no longer blocks the read-only liveness probe `kill -0 <pid>` (also `kill -s 0`, with a variable or substituted PID); every other `kill` with a variable, substitution or pattern target is still denied.
- `install.sh --apply-operating-layer` no longer prints "skipped template hook entries with a placeholder matcher" on a normal install. The template's second `SubagentStop` entry carried the placeholder matcher `<your-read-only-review-type>`, which the installer always skipped and the selfcheck always reported as one tier only. The template now ships the default `handback_cap.py` entry alone, `operating_selfcheck.py` reports one such entry as PRESENT, and the optional matcher-scoped tier for a read-only review type is documented in `host-enforcement.md` to add by hand.
- `merge_train.py verify`: a member parked on a conflict earlier in a run that then names a culprit is now un-parked (`UNTRY #n`) and rebuilt into the union at once, since it may only have clashed with the culprit; a genuine conflict re-parks it. Before, such members stayed parked until their head changed and had to be un-parked by hand. Tests: `scripts/test_peer_learnings_1019.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/peer-learnings-1019.sh`) and the `conflict-with-culprit-untried-after-bisect` case in `merge_train.py --selftest`.
