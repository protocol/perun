### Fixed
- `destructive_guard.py` no longer blocks the read-only liveness probe `kill -0 <pid>` (also `kill -s 0`, with a variable or substituted PID); every other `kill` with a variable, substitution or pattern target is still denied, including a real signal after the `-0` (`kill -0 -s 9 $p`).
- `install.sh --apply-operating-layer` no longer prints "skipped template hook entries with a placeholder matcher" on a normal install. The template's second `SubagentStop` entry carried the placeholder matcher `<your-read-only-review-type>`, which the installer always skipped and the selfcheck always reported as one tier only. The template now ships the default `handback_cap.py` entry alone, `operating_selfcheck.py` reports one such entry as PRESENT, and the optional matcher-scoped tier for a read-only review type is documented in `host-enforcement.md` to add by hand.
