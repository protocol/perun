### Changed
- The real-hub CI job now also runs real gitleaks 8.30.1, trufflehog 3.99.9 (offline, no verification), checkov 3.4.5 and kube-score 1.20.0 through `review_checks.sh` and asserts each is reported as ran with its expected rule id on a fictional canary file. The GitHub-token-shaped canary is joined at runtime so no source line matches the privacy gate. Binaries are pinned by sha256 from each upstream checksums file; checkov joins the hash-locked `scripts/real-hub-requirements.txt` (re-resolved, so boto3, botocore, s3transfer, networkx and packaging take checkov's older pins). cppcheck and njsscan stay stub-only: neither ships a hash-pinnable Linux binary or a resolvable wheel set, and semgrep and trivy need a rule or DB download.
