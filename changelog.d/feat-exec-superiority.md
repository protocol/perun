### Added
- `docs/bench/exec-1017/`: a pre-registered, no-paid-call bench of the five executive skills (`agentic-ceo`, `idea-critic`, `business-ops`, `growth-analytics`, `positioning`) against the rival skills named in `docs/perun-vs-rivals.md`. Per skill: 30 fresh fictional decision scenarios (10 tune, 20 held-out), both arms generated by Qwen3.5-397B-A17B on PCSS from the skill text alone, judged blind and rubric-free in randomized order by DeepSeek-V4-Flash twice (order swapped) with a GLM-5.2 tiebreak. The preregistered keep rule is a held-out win rate whose bootstrap 95% confidence lower bound is above 0.5 with n=20. Held-out win rate, Perun against the rival, before and after one tune-driven edit round: `agentic-ceo` 0.35 to 0.35 (reverted), `idea-critic` 0.30 to 0.35 (reverted), `business-ops` 0.50 to 0.60, interval 0.40 to 0.80 (reverted), `positioning` 0.70 to 0.70, interval 0.50 to 0.90 (reverted, lower bound not above 0.5), `growth-analytics` 0.55 to 0.75, interval 0.55 to 0.90 (kept). The bench does not show any skill beating its rival on the preregistered bar before the edit, and shows one of five doing so after; the other four stay below the bar. Caveats are in the bench README: same-family open-model judges, no tools for either arm, API nondeterminism of about 0.1 between identical re-judgements, and a single edit round.

- `docs/bench/exec-1017/` round 2 for the four skills that failed round 1: three decision-memo variants per skill compared on the tune split, the best run once on a new frozen 20-scenario held-out set with v0 as a reference, judged by DeepSeek-V4-Flash, GLM-5.2 and MiniMax-M2.5 with every pair in both orders (a judge's verdict counts only when both orders agree, else a tie; majority of three decides). All three judges prefer the first slot on more than half of calls (slot-1 rate 0.525 to 0.825 across the held-out runs), which both-order judging absorbs. Held-out win rate, v0 to chosen variant: `agentic-ceo` 0.450 to 0.625, interval 0.475 to 0.750 (reverted, lower bound not above 0.5), `idea-critic` 0.275 to 0.325, interval 0.200 to 0.475 (reverted), `business-ops` 0.475 to 0.525, interval 0.375 to 0.675 (reverted), `positioning` 0.475 to 0.500, interval 0.350 to 0.650 (reverted). No round-2 variant cleared the keep rule, so those four skill texts are unchanged; only the round-1 `growth-analytics` edit ships.
