### Fixed
- `train_land.sh` (#1777): a busy gate no longer refuses or fails after doing work. A gate that finds the exclusive heavy lease held now waits up to `LEASE_WAIT_SECS` (default `LOCK_TIMEOUT_MIN` x 60) and then `DEFER`s with exit 4 and nothing built, instead of exit 1. A held `train-push.lock` is waited out (`PUSH_WAIT_SECS`, default raised from 600 s to `LOCK_TIMEOUT_MIN` x 60) before the fetch and compose, so a held or stale push lock fails before any gate work and the union is built on the base the landing leaves. The lease is released as soon as the push lock is held, so heavy commands are allowed while a train lands. Composing during another train's landing is deliberately not done: that union would be stale at its own land. Replay model (`scripts/test_train_lease_replay.py`, seeded, 4 drivers x 30 trains, a model not a production measurement): refused attempts 1138 to 0, idle gate time 5.2 to 0.0 min, merges/day 134.4 to 134.6. Tests: `scripts/test-train-lease.sh` (7), `scripts/test-train-resilience.sh` (exit 4 on a held lease).
