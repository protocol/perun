### Added
- `communication-structure`: `references/artifact-templates.md` gives each artifact one shape and a word and line budget (status update, PR body, release note, decision memo or executive summary, incident summary, README section, comment, meeting notes), and `scripts/comms_lint.py --kind <kind>` scores every rule and prints the exact fix (exit 1 below the bar). It reuses `status_update_lint.py` for the leadership form and `status_vocab_lint.py`; `--source` flags figures and recommendations the notes never state. Rules fail on a buried lead, greeting openers, filler, placeholders and stand-ins ("owner unassigned"), numberless adjectives, a weekday that does not fit its date, missing verification on a PR, clock times without a zone; ask (checked only against an ask in `--source`, and an owner or date only if the source gives one), check marks, length, numbers, verification and time zones warn only; nothing demands what the input lacks. The `meeting-notes` kind checks the Item | Needs | Notes | Next steps table, short numeric lines with no emoji or repeats, a DRI on every row, and links for issue, PR and tracker keys.
- `agentic-delivery/scripts/comms_guard.py`, a PreToolUse hook in the operating-layer template (matcher `^(Bash|mcp__.*[Ll]inear.*__save_(status_update|comment))$`, registered with `|| true`): it lints the body of Linear `save_status_update` and `save_comment`, `gh pr create|edit|comment`, `gh issue create|edit|comment`, `gh release create|edit`, and `curl` or `gh api` writes to `/pulls` and `/issues`, and adds the exact fixes to Claude's context (`additionalContext`); it is advisory and never blocks. It fails open and `PERUN_COMMS_GUARD=off` opts out. `perun_doctor.py` now expects it to be wired.
- `docs/bench/comms-quality-1016/`: preregistered 3-arm rubric-free bench (Perun after the fix, humanizer, plain model) on 10 fresh scenarios, plus the failure evidence (`evidence.json`). The preregistered result is a loss for the first version of the fix: 0.100 [0.000, 0.300] against both rivals ($2.84 of the $3 cap). The shipped revision removes the causes the judge named; its exploratory check against the plain model is 0.700 [0.400, 1.000], not shown superior (`docs/perun-vs-rivals.md` H1e).
- `docs/standards-index.md`: Claude Code hooks matcher syntax, MCP tool names, PreToolUse decision control and exit codes, verified 2026-10-16.

### Changed
- `communication-structure/SKILL.md` is shorter (7890 to under 7000 bytes) and now says: add nothing the source lacks, an ask names owner and date only when the source gives them, address the reader, no "Reply A or B" in a PR body, route to the templates and the linter. `references/leadership-status-update.md` example decision names an owner and date and points to `comms_lint.py`.
- CI also runs `test_status_update_lint.py`, `test_comms_lint.py` and `test_comms_guard.py`.
