### Added
- Bench: idea-critic V4 (decide first, then attack), preregistered held-out result at `docs/bench/idea-critic-v4-1019/`. V4 writes the model's own first call before any attack, lets the attack flip it only through a named blocker, and tells the model a missing pilot, cap or metric is never a blocker. On 30 fresh cases it cut false-no-go on good decisions from 0.911 to 0.378 and raised accuracy over the current skill from 0.544 to 0.778 (paired +0.233 [0.078, 0.400]) but approved a bad decision in 3 of 45 calls (false-go 0.067 against 0.000), so the frozen zero-false-go rule makes it a LOSS and SKILL.md is unchanged. No-skill accuracy is 0.644. Three dev wordings are reported. Tests: `scripts/test_idea_critic_v4.py`.
