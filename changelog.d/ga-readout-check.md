### Added
- `growth-analytics` ships `scripts/readout_check.py`: a stdlib-only check that reads a two-arm A/B readout JSON and prints PASS/FAIL/SKIP for sample ratio mismatch (chi-square against the input's own `srm_alpha` and `expected_split`), a Holm adjustment over the primary p-values at the input's `alpha` (guardrails reported unadjusted), observed n below the pre-committed `planned_sample_size`, and `run_days` below `planned_days`. It holds no thresholds of its own: a rule whose pre-registered input is absent is skipped. Bad input prints `ERROR <reason>` and exits 2; any FAIL exits 1; when every rule SKIPs it prints `INCONCLUSIVE: nothing pre-registered` and exits 3. Routed from the skill's SKILL.md. Tests: `scripts/test_ga_readout_check.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/ga-readout-check.sh`).
