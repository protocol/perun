### Added
- Every guard and hook script under `.claude/skills/*/scripts/` now records who owns it, why it exists and when it is next reviewed, as one header line: `# rail: owner=<skill or role>, why=<one clause>, review=YYYY-MM-DD`. 42 scripts carry it: those named `*_guard`, `*_gate`, `*_cap` or `*_hook`, those a shipped `templates/*.settings.json` runs as a hook, and `stop_reminder.py`. The first review date is 2027-01-30. Scripts that only the git pre-push template runs (for example `flake_holder.py`) are outside the required set; adding the line opts one in.
- `scripts/ci-gates.sh rails <root>` fails on a rail with no header, a missing or empty field, or a review date that is not a real calendar day, so a new guard cannot land unowned. `scripts/test-rails.sh` pins it with 46 cases (all red against a `ci-gates.sh` without the subcommand) and `scripts/ci-tests.d/rails.sh` runs both in CI (refs #1592).
- `scripts/ci-gates.sh rails --audit <root>` lists the rails past their review date for the owner to confirm (bump `review=`) or retire; it never removes one, and a past date does not fail the plain gate. It prints no firing counts: no log of rail firings is shared (`reply_cap.py`, `handback_cap.py` and `file_finding.py` each keep a private one), so no rail is called never-firing until a shared log exists.
