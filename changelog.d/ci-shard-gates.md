### Changed
- CI now runs the `gates` workflow as parallel jobs (gate helper self-tests in three shards, documented gates, per-PR tests, live eval and scorer tests, and one job for the cheap steps) behind an aggregate job still named `gates`, cutting wall time from the sum of the steps to the slowest job. Every original step is kept with unchanged commands, and `scripts/test-ci-shards.sh` fails if one is dropped. The workflow also accepts `merge_group` events so a merge queue can be enabled later.
