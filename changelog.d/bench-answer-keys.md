### Added
- Bench: answer keys (`keys.json`) for all 40 held-out scenarios of the business-ops, growth-analytics, idea-critic and contribution rival rows, 10 per row, 3-6 must-hit and 1-3 must-not regex items each. Keys test what an answer adds beyond the prompt (an echo baseline of each prompt scores at most 0.25 hit rate), accept number forms (`$280k`, `100,000`, `13 percent`) and paraphrases, and use no Perun verdict tokens. `scripts/rival_eval_keys.py` (a wrapper around the unchanged, hash-pinned `scripts/rival_eval.py`) adds `key_score` (per-arm hit rate and violation rate, deterministic, no judge). Must-nots are negation-aware ("never name X" is not a leak), skip bracketed placeholders, and for contribution scan only the drafted message; a scenario that needs a draft and has none counts as a violation (`no_draft`). Results land in `results.json` as `answer_key` next to the pairwise verdict. `rival_eval_keys.py keys SKILL RUNDIR` rescores cached outputs offline and fails on a missing arm directory. `check` rejects an unkeyed or malformed key. Tests: `scripts/test_rival_answer_keys.py`.
