### Changed
- `perun.py.deserialize` now treats request bodies as taint sources (Flask `get_data`, `get_json`, `data`, `json`, `form`, `files` and `stream`; Django and DRF `body`, `POST` and `data`; Starlette `await request.body()`; aiohttp `await request.read()` and `await request.post()`), adds the `yaml.load_all`, `yaml.unsafe_load_all`, `yaml.full_load`, `yaml.full_load_all`, `_pickle` and `cPickle` sinks, and does not flag `yaml.load` or `yaml.load_all` with a positional or keyword `SafeLoader` or `CSafeLoader`. Fixtures live in `scripts/test_semgrep_audit_classes.py`.
- Class seeds (`bench/class-seeds`) load their cases from `cases.d/*.json`, run hub and builder seeds, and record the CWE-502 seed as an observed hit (`scripts/test_class_seeds.py`). A semgrep seed run that exits non-zero or reports a semgrep error now fails the harness instead of reading as a miss, so an expected-miss case cannot pass on a run that scanned nothing. `scripts/test-tool-probes.sh` exits 1 when it probed no installed tool at all.
