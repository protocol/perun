### Fixed
- `stall_guard.py` now also blocks, once, a turn that only schedules a wake-up (`ScheduleWakeup` or `CronCreate`) while its last text mentions CI, checks, merge or waiting and nothing was launched (no `Agent`, `Task`, `Workflow` or background tool): "Waiting on CI is not a stop: launch the next priority lane now, then wake on the CI notification." `PERUN_STALL_GUARD=off`, the one-shot rule and fail-open are unchanged. The plugin now ships `hooks/hooks.json` registering `stall_guard.py` on Stop, so a plugin install wires it; `install.sh --with-delivery` already applies it idempotently through the operating layer.
- The idle-wait block now passes while background lanes from earlier turns are still open (more `run_in_background` launches than `<task-notification>` completions in the transcript tail), and only fires on forward-looking text (waiting on or for, CI or checks running, pending or in progress, until checks), so "PR merged, CI green" never matches. The plugin Stop hook skips itself when the project's `.claude/settings.json` or `settings.local.json` already registers `stall_guard`, so the plugin and `install.sh --with-delivery` never double-fire.
