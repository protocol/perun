The unused-MCP rule skips servers that settings already disable (`disabledMcpjsonServers`, or outside `enabledMcpjsonServers`); the short-lanes rule skips lanes still in flight; the cache rules need at least 10 turns. `/perun:cost-retro` now runs `--advise`.
