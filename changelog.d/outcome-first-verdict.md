### Added
- `scripts/bench_verdict.py`: outcome-first verdict for a Perun-vs-rival comparison. A Win needs the preference interval's lower bound above 0.5 (or a parity tie at lower cost), a measured cost with Perun no higher than the rival, and a measured outcome that is not worse; otherwise the label is downgraded to `PREFERENCE-ONLY WIN (cost/outcome unmeasured)`, `WIN ON PROXY, OUTCOME WORSE`, `WIN ON PROXY, COST HIGHER`, `PARITY` or `LOSS`. It reuses the bootstrap in `scripts/rival_eval.py`. `--check-doc` is a gate: a row under a `Verdict` header in `docs/perun-vs-rivals.md` that says Win must carry the preference-only label or name a `$` cost figure and an outcome metric. Tests: `scripts/test_bench_verdict.py`, run from `scripts/test-ci-gates.sh`.
