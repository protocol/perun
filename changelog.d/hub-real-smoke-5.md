### Changed
- The real-hub CI job now also runs real semgrep 1.157.0 (repo rules, no registry pack) and njsscan 0.4.2 through `review_checks.sh`, each on a fictional canary file; the job fails unless the tool ran and reported its expected rule on that file with no not-run or NOT CHECKED line. Both install from their own hash-locked `scripts/real-hub-requirements-semgrep.txt` venv, because semgrep pins click 8.1.8, jsonschema 4.25.1 and wcmatch 8.5.2, older than the versions the other tools resolve to. Not added: trivy (the hub reports its vuln scan NOT CHECKED without a downloaded DB, which has no pinnable hash) and cppcheck (no upstream Linux binary or checksum file).
- The trufflehog canary assertion now matches the exact detector name `Github` instead of the prefix `^Github`.
- `provision_tools.sh` pins checkov 3.4.5 (was 3.4.0), matching the real-hub job.
- `review_checks.sh` now runs njsscan with the same sandboxed `HOME`, cache dirs and `SEMGREP_SEND_METRICS=off` as its semgrep call (njsscan drives semgrep, which otherwise writes under the real home and may send metrics); the hub stub test asserts it.
