- Secrets wave 11: blind held-out-14 closes 11 leak shapes and 3 false positives (blind baseline 49/60 recall, 3/60 FP; now raw 57/60 recall, 0/60 FP, or 57/57 after excluding three rows after the fact, reasons in the corpus ledger). The Datadog rule now accepts 32 to 40 hex. Review fixes: the referrer-comment pass applies to `generic-api-key` only for values starting `AIz`, the `sk-test-` placeholder is anchored, and the login, SAS, NuGet, Doppler and client-secret rules cover more orders and refuse name suffixes such as `_ref`.
